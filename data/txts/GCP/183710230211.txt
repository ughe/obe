2415. JOHN WILCOX was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of Octo-
ber. 1 leaden pump, value 88., the goods of William Dawes, and fixed to a
certain building; against the Statute, &c.
WILLIAM SMITH (police-constable R 49.) On the 25th of October, in
the morning, I was on duty in Lewisham-road, and saw the prisoner carry-
ing a load on his shoulder-I asked what he had got-he said, "A roll of
lead " I asked him to let me look at it, and found it was the head of a
pump-I asked where he was going to take it-he said, "To New Cross,
to a plumber's named Thorpe"-I asked if it was his own-he said, no, it
belonged to Mr. Whomes who had sent him with it-I asked if the plumber
was aware he was going to bring it to him-he said he believed he was-I
went to Lewisham, and found, in an empty house adjoining the one the
prisoner lived in, a pump without a head to it, and it fitted it- we exa-
mined the prisoner's house, and found some more lead which fitted to the
same pump- he had 39lbs. weight of lead in his bag, and 27lbs. weight in
his house-he said he lived there-I inquired at New Cross, and could
find no Thorpe a plumber and glazier there—the whole of the pump was
taken away level to the ground all the leaden part of it was taken awayit
appeared as if it had been recently unfixed, by the white lead-it appeared
fresh moved, for it had rained that morning, and it was not wet- the house
is in Lewisham, and is the property of Mr. Dawes--I have known the pri-
soner two years, and never knew any thing against him.
GEORGE ABBOTT. I accompanied the officer to the room occupied by
the prisoner adjoining Mr. Whomes's house, and found a saw and some lead
hid under some shoes in the room--the lead appeared to have been cut
with a saw, and the saw I found had lead in the teeth I have fitted the
lead and the pump head, and can say they formed part of one pump.
ROBERT WHOMES. I am agent to Mr. William Dawes, of Lewisham,
The prisoner occupied a house adjoining the one the pump was taken from
-I saw the pump before it was fixed in the yard-it was fixed against
the house by a holdfast let into the brick-work-the prisoner had no busi-
ness to take it-I had not employed him to take it to Mr. Thorpe, of New
Cross—I do not know such a person.
Prisoner. The pump was not fixed to the house when I took it--the
boys had been all over the empty house and pulled the pump down.
Witness. It was fixed when I saw it last.
