Mary Ann CLIFFORD. I am niees to Mr. Battle, of the City of Salisbury
public-house, Fair Street, Horselydown, and I assist him-on Saturday
evening. 16th November, about 6.50 the prisoner came in and asked me
for 11d. worth of rum, and gave me a counterfeit shilling-I saw it was bad,
I showed it to my uncle and he broke it--the prisoner then gave me a good
shilling and I gave him 10 d. change.
WILLIAM HENRY LEONARD BATTLE. My niece gave me the shilling,
found it was bad; I broke it with my teeth--I said to the prisoner “This
is & bad shilling," and showed him the pieces-he said he was very sorry
it should fall on a working-man, he had got change for a sovereign on
Friday night in Fetter Lane-I gave him one part of the shilling and
retained the other; he threw several pieces of silver on the counter, and
asked if there was any more bad among that I just looked at them, and
said“I do not think so," he paid with a good shilling and left-I followed
him and saw him enter the Grapes, about 200 or 300 yards off--I went in
at a different door ; I spoke to the landlord, Mr Daniels, and showed him.
