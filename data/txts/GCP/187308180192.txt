Cross-examined by Mr. W. SLEIGH. I saw some wax in pieces, in a bag,
King's; some of them were as large as the palm of your hand, some larger,
and some smaller I can't say whose manufacture this piece produced ik
because it is in such a dirty condition-I don't know that I could recognise

pieces were clean, it is very likely I might pick out a great portion of them
I think I could recognise this piece without any difficulty, because no one
manufactures it in the same way as we do I know it by the beautiful way
in which it is got up; our wax is, I believe, got up better than anyone else's
--I don't think it is more difficult to identify white wax than yellow, if you
give me a piece of the proper size.
