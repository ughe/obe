   Cross-examined. Q.  When the prisoner produced her purse and chain, did she not say, "Take this; take all I have got; only leave me till the morn ing?" A.  Yes, she did—some keys of the prisoner's were found in her bed-room—the officer tried them to my box in which the little boxes were—none of them would fit it—when I said to the prisoner, "Give me those ten sovereigns," she said she had no ten sovereigns—I said, "Those ten sove reigns that were taken from my box." ELEANOR RUSSELL. I am single—I keep the house No. 15, Holywell-street, Westminster. Mr. and Mr. Roberts lodge in my two parlours—I use the front kitchen, which is under the parlour occupied by Mr. Roberts—I remember the prisoner coming there on Friday, the 4th of June—I recollect Mr. Roberts going out—directly after she was gone, I distinctly heard the prisoner walk across the room—I heard the footsteps till they got to the folding-doors—I could not hear them after they passed the folding doors—I then heard them again coming back from the folding-doors—I had missed hearing them for some minutes—I remember the prisoner coming again on Saturday, the 5th of June, and Mr. Roberts going out—immediately she had gone I heard the footsteps again—I heard them nearly all the time she was out—I could not hear them in the bed-room—I lost the trace of the foot steps for some minutes—I remember Mr. Roberts and the prisoner leaving the house together—they left me in the house—no one could have come in without my knocking it—I have a gentleman and lady lodging in the first floor—the gentleman was out—his wife and children were at home—I have a young man and his wife up stairs—the young man was away—my servant was in the house—there is a door from the passage to Mr. Roberts' front parlour, and from that to the bed-room—there is a door from the passage to the bed-room, but that door is kept locked—during the whole time after Mr. Roberts went out with the prisoner till Mr. Roberts' return, I was at home—no one passed across the front parlour while they were away—I am quite sure about that—Mr. Roberts left the servant up stairs when she went out, but she came down as soon as Mr. Roberts was gone—I am certain she did not go into the room—I do not believe anybody could have gone across the floor—I heard no one.
