MAXY ANN DARWIN. I live at 28, Page's Walk; the prisoner decupied
room there on 20th July with Greeson as Mr. and Mrs. Martin-
between 8 and 9 o'clock on 23rd July the prisoner asked me if I would.
lend him the street door key as his wife had gone out with the key on
the ring-shortly afterwards the constable came and took him--the bag
was locked up in the room till Sunday by the constable, who had my key
-I then saw the constable search the bag, and saw a piece of brown
paper taken out-I had not seen the bag open before.
Cross-examined. I had seen you about 7 or 8 o'clock that evening in
the back parlour-it was about 7.30 when you asked for the key-you
went out with Greeson before that--about 7.30 I said your missis bad
gone out, and you went out after her-I gave you the key-about an hour
after you came in, but I did not know you were in the house after 7.30
-I had been out all day, and came home about 4.15–I found the place
in a disgusting state of filth, and the woman drunk on the bed I saw
nothing of you between 5 and 7 o'clock-there is a factory clock outside
the window which we look at.
