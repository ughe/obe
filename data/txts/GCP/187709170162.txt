WHITE, Mayor.
NEW COURT.-Friday, September 21st, 1877.
Before Mr. Recorder.
711. HUGH DALY (23), WILLIAM NEWSHAM (14), burglariously.
breaking and entering in the dwelling-house of William Williams, and
stealing therein five corts and other articles, his property ; to which DALY
PLEADED Guilty-Eighteen Months' Imprisonment each.
MR. AUSTIN METOALPE conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM WILLIAMS. I am a dairyman of 94, Devonshire Street, Lison Grove,
on the night of 20th August I was the last person up-I examined the door
and windows at 12 o'clock, they were all safe-I got up at a few minutes
after 5 o'clock and found the back parlour window open and a ladder against
the house - all the drawers and boxes had been opened, and I missed clothes
and other things value 151.
John ROBINSON (Policeman D 123). On 21st August, about 1 am, I
was on duty near the prosecutor's house, and saw the two prisoners together
loitering about-I am certain that Newsham was one of the two-on 24th
August I met Newsbam in New Street, Salisbury Street, and told him I
should take him for being concerned with Daly, who was in custody, for
committing a burlary in Lisson Grove-he said Daly got in first and opened
the window, and I got in after him "--on the way to the station he said that
he bad done funr other jobs, but only two with Daly.
Cross-examined by N.wsham. You did not say that you were with Daly
when he had the things in the sack, you said that you followed him in a
window and that you fell down in the room-I did not say “If you want to
go to sea, say that you were concerned in the case."
Newsham's Statement before the Magistrate : "I told the officer 1 was con-
cerned in two other jobs besides Mr. Williams'."
Newsham's Defence. I wish Daly to be called.
Hugu Daly. I have pleaded guilty to this charge, I did it alone,
nobody was with me, it was between 1 and 2 a.m.--I had not seen Newsbam
that day till I had the things--I took them to a place in Marylebone till I
was caught taking them from there, Newsham had nothing to do with it-
I told him I had a little business on and parted from him that was after
I did it-I used to be about there all day long—I do not remember seeing
the prisoner before I did it-Newsham was not concerned with me in the case
in Harrow Road, I simply asked him to mind some pawn tickets.
NEWSHAM-GUILTY-Two Months' Imprisonment and Three Years' is a
Reformatory.
