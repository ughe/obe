Cross-examined. I went out for a policeman one day, and he said he

I could not see him, but I don't believe at these times that he had been out
of the house - I don't agree with Mrs. Cove in saying that he was never
out at work all the time he was there, because he sent the little girl up to
me one night asking me to call him up in the morning about 6 o'clock
- I don't think he went out to work above twice; he was more at home
than he was away--I don't think he was away more than two days, at
different times--I told the Coroner that he did not seem to have much
work to do—he did not go to work frequently during the first three weeks
he was there I never saw him with the other children on the Saturday
and Sunday before the child died I only heard him swearing at the woman
--she was at home all day Saturday and Sunday--I heard Mrs. Cove on
one occasion accuse him of knocking the child about, and the mother came
out and said she was mistaken, that he had not been doing so--that was on
the Friday week before the child died.
