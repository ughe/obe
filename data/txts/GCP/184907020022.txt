1440. THOMAS BYFORD and HENRY ARNOLD, stealing 1 box and
79 lockets, value 441. 10s. 60.; the goods of George Crouch, the master of
Byford.
MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE WAISTLE. I am in the employ of George Cronch, a carrier, d
Tudor-street, Blackfriars; Byford was his porter, and Arnold an acquaintance
of his. On 21st June I gave Byford seventeen parcels-I called them over to
him-he entered them himself, and put them into his bag—the consignes
have to sign the book-he left me about nine o'clock or a quarter past, and
returned about three-I looked at his book, and found one parcel
receipted for he said, “I have missed one of the parcels for Green and
Banks, of Hatton-garden ; have I left it behind me?"--I said no, and be
must remain in the place till Mr. Crouch came-he said he had lost the
parcel on his way: he did not know how the parcel was in brown paper-
he would know that Green and Banks were jewellers-when we wett
arranging them in the morning, he said, “Here are two for Green and
Banks," and he has entered two.
Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. Has property been missed
before? A. Not while I have been there --Mr. Crouch has three sons em-
ployed there-a parcel was not, to my knowledge, traced to one of them-
the prisoner said he bad lost the parcel as soon as he came in-he did not
offer me the book--I asked him who had been with him; he said Arnold,
and he had given him something to assist him, as there was so much to
carry the parcels were not above the size of my hand-Arnold was porter
to Mr. Crouch once.
