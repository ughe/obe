MR. GRAIN Prosecuted; Mr. BEsLeY Defended.
MATTHEW COLLINs. I am a porter at the Queen's Hotel, St. Martin's-
le-Grand-on the 7th January, about 145, a pareel which I had made
up by instructions was in a chair in the hall; it contained an old porter's
jacket and a few other things-I was watching, and 8aW the prisoner
come in and go to the billiard room, and then to the coffee room, where
he read the newspapers for three or four minutes, and came out into the
hall, took up the parcel quite carelesaly, put it under his arm, and went
out-I followed him through Gresham Street, and took a constable with
me, who stopped him-I told the constable that he had stolen the pareel
from the hotel-he took him back and confronted him with Mr. Best--he
was not staying in the hotel.
Cross-aramined. I did not see him enter the hotel-a number of
commercial travellers stop there-he said that he had seen somebody at
a refreshment place, who asked him to feteh the pareel.
JOSEPH HORNBY (City Policeman). Oollins spoke to me, and I stopped

him back to the hotel and then to the station, where I found on him
some duplicates relating to bags-he said that he met a gentleman, that
they went to the hotel together to have some refreshment, and the
gentleman asked him to take the paroel to some place for him.
Cros-ezamined. I found some letters in his hand, they were given up

fellowed him to the doori Carter Lane and saw him off the premises-I

December 22nd I saw him again in the print department between 1

coat; I did not wait to see if he put the eoat on, but went baok and got
