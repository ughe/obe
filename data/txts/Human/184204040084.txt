 1225. JOHN CLARK was indicted for stealing, on the   5th of March , 1 coat, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of David Stanley . DAVID STANLEY. I am a fruiterer, living at Marley, in Kent On the 5th of March I placed my cart in Covent-garden Market—my great coat was on my horse—shortly after, I missed it, and, on turning round, saw the prisoner going off with it, folding it up under his arm—I jumped off the cart, followed him, and got within four yards of him—he looked round, and dropped it—I am sure he is the man who took it—I never lost sight of him.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Was it blowing hard that day? A.  No. OLD ENGLAND GOODSON. I am constable of Covent-garden Market I heard a cry of "Stop thief," saw the prisoner running, and saw his stopped by a porter in the market.
 (  The prisoner received a good character. ) GUILTY. Aged 26.— Confined Two Months.
