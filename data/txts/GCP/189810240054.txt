103, New Road, Battersea Park Road-I have known Perry for some

Cross-examined. Letters were sent there to Perry for some time
afterwards, but they were not taken in by me- I did not see Perry and
his wife after February 19th till 1 saw them at Bow Street, except once
at & public-house.
