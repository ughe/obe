1222. WILLIAM LEWIN was indicted for feloniously and knowing
uttering a forged receipt for money, with intent to defraud John Oakley. I
MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
John OAKLEY. I am a black-lead manufacturer. The prisoner was i
my employ as a packer-on the 11th of March 1 sent him to Mr. March
for some labels-he was to pay for them I gave him a sovereign and
written order to get them on the following morning he produced the
labels with this bill and receipt, with Mr. March's name to it-I should
have required a similar receipt to this from him on his return.
Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON. Q. What was the nature of the
business he had to do? A. To pack black-lead at my house-it was pies-
work-I paid him every Saturday night—the course was to make out a
account for the work he had done in the week.
MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Had he any right to appropriate this money
for wages ? A. No; he brought his bill in every Saturday night, and wa
paid.
JAMES MARCH. The prisoner came to me, on the 11th of March, and
brought an order from his master, in consequence of which I delivered
him 15,000 labels- I did not receive any money from him for them !
knew him as the servant of Mr. Oakley, as he had been before--the upper
part of this bill is mine, " 15,000 bills, 18s. 9d.," but this " Paid, I.
March," is not mine, nor did I authorize any body to write it.
Cross-examined. Q. I believe it was paid shortly afterwards ? A. Yes
the prisoner called on the 4th of April, and paid it-he has been in
the habit of getting things before on three or four occasions, and on ote
occasion he paid for some goods which he had bad when he gave orders
for others.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 37.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury.-Confined
