 2094. MARY WELLS was indicted for stealing, on the   30th of August , 1 sheet, value 3 s  ., 6 s  ., the goods of Matthew Moriarty , her master. MARY MORIARTY. I am the wife of Matthew Moriarty, we live in the stable-yard at Greenwich. The prisoner was my servant—she worked about the house, and slept there—she did not pay for her lodging—I missed some bed-clothes—here is one sheet, that I know is mine by my work on it—and here is one which is the fellow to it.
   Prisoner.  I never was her servant—I lodged in her house—I was coming down the street to get a lodging in Deptford, and she said I stole the sheet. Witness.  She was my servant for two months—I gave her victuals and lodging. THOMAS M'GILL( police-sergeant R  19.) I took the prisoner for steal ing a sheet and two blankets—I found this sheet at Mrs. Gerrard's.
   MRS. GERRARD . I bought the sheet on the 30th of August of the prisoner, I believe—for 1 s . 6 d .
   Prisoner.  I am certain she never bought it of me—when the officer took me to her house she said I was not the person. Witness.  I mean to swear she was the person.
   COURT . Q.  Did you never in presence of the officer express any doubt on ths subject? A.  When the officer brought her to me, she looked dif ferent—I did not own to the officer that she was the person—I did express a boubt at that time—she was not dressed in the same manner—when I bought it, the person had a dirty cap on, and a dirty face; and when the officer brought her to me she had a clean cap on, and a clean face—I buy second-hand clothes.
   Q.  What makes you know her now, if you did not know her then? A.  I was not obliged to own to the officer that she was the person—I mean to swear she was the person.
   COURT to THOMAS M'GILL . Q.  What did Gerrard say respecting the prisoner? A.  She said she was rather dirtier in her appearance; but now that she had a clean cap on, she was not certain whether she was the per son; but afterwards, before the Magistrate, upon a sound recollection, she said she was the person.
   COURT to MRS. GERRARD . Q.  Was any body with you when you bought it? A.  No—I bought it in my shop, No. 4, Gale's-row, Greenwich—I do not buy every thing that is bought—I do not know what the sheet is worth. NOT GUILTY.
