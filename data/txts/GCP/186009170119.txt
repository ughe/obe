822. MARY LOW (17), Stealing a gown, skirt, and a bonnet, value 48.,
of Jane Ann Low.
Jane Ann Low. I am single; the prisoner is my sister-on Sunday,
26th August, I missed from a chest of my mother's a skirt of a gown and a
bonnet- I went home on the Sunday evening about 10 o'clook —I had pro-
hibited my sister from taking them-my mother did not know that she had
taken them-on missing the things I went in search of my sister-I walked
about till I found her half tipsy in Woolwich, entering a house-I asked
her to come home; she said she was not coming home any more--she said,
" It is not me you want, you want your things" I said, "I want you, and
the things too-she threw the bonnet at me I got her to come home, and
she was given in charge-she had left home before she had asked me to lend
her a dress, and I have lent her one.
Nor GUILTY.
