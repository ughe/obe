239. WILLIAM EDWARD SMITH, feloniously killing and slaying
James Price.
Mr. CAARTEN conducted the Prosecution.

the Brickmakers' Arms, at North Hyde, in the Precinct of Norwood. On
Monday night, 21st Jan., the prisoner came in a little after 6 o'clock-he
called for a pint of beer, I served him he had another pint of beer, one
before Price came, and one after he came the prisoner was quite sober-
