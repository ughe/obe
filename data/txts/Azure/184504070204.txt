999. CHARLES PENTON and ISAAC LOPAZ were indicted for
stealing 1 purse, value fid. ; 4 sixpences, and 1 farthing; the property of
Elizabeth Gleaves, from her person.
ELIZABETH GLEAVES. I am a widow and live in Caroline-street, Green-
wich. I was standing on Greenwich-hill on the 24th of March, in the after-
noon—I had a purse with four sixpences and a farthing in it—I had 3d.
in coppers—this is my purse.
JOHN CARPENTER (police-constable R 84.) I was on duty—I saw the
two prisoners on the hill—I saw them go behind several females, and each
of them try several pockets—I then saw Penton go behind Mrs. Gleaves
—Lopaz was behind Penton, with his hands in his pockets, spreading
out, and covering him—I then saw Penton lift up Mrs. Gleaves' dress,
put his hand into her pocket, and take out this purse—I caught his band—
he dropped the purse, but I caught one farthing in his hand.
GEORGE CARPENTER. I was called by John Carpenter—I saw Penton
throw the purse from his hand.
