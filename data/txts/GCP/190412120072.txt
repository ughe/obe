FLORENCE WALDEN. I am a domestic servant at Uxbridge-the
deceased was my sister she was a single woman she was 19- I last
saw her alive three weeks before the murder-I afterwards saw her body
at the mortuary-I gave this ring to her.
Joseph WILLIAM DRABBLE. I am a grocer living at Leytonstone, next
door to the prisoner and his brother-I saw the prisoner on October 26th,
about 8.30, outside his shop-he came to my shop about 8.45 for the
money for some coal he had sold me I paid him, he said to me, "What
do you think of them robbing me?"- I said " Who?"-he said, "There
is only Tom and the girl "-I said, “What of !”-he said, " £19 from
£200-I said, "Hold your lie"; I did not believe it-he then went
away-about 1 o'clock Thomas Hoffman came to me- I went into their
shop, looked down the cellar and saw a form-I did not then know
it was the girl-1 fetched a doctor.
GEORGE BROUGHTON. I am 15, and now work for Mr. Hoffman-on
October 26th, about 10.30, I passed the prisoner's shop—the door was
shut-I saw the prisoner bending down outside the shop by the door-
I said to him, “Will you tell Tom to wait for me till about half-past five,
and then I can go with him "-the prisoner went away quickly towards
the Mayfield Road.
Cross-examined. The prisoner was calm; he had one hand in his
pocket, fiddling about with it; that is all I noticed he never threatened
me-I know Colmer, but not by that name; he is called "Totraker"-
the prisoner said to me, "If I catch that "Totraker' at my place again I
don't know what I will do with him "-he suggested that "Totraker" had
robbed him.
Reexamined. The prisoner was angry because he thought Colmer had
robbed him.
