FREDERICK WARDALL. I fastened my sister's house on Sunday even-
ing, about ten minutes before six o'clock-1 pushed up the front parlour
shutters, pulled the street door after me, and tried it-it was quite fast-it
opens by a large key-I was present when my sister tried it as she passed
it to go to church-wben we came back in the evening it was open about
a foot-the lock was not injured it was pushed back.
EDWARD FUNNELL. I am a policeman. Between seven and eight o'clock
on Sunday evening, the prisoner Johnstone ran past me on London-bridge,
not very fast, from the Borough-in consequence of information, I followed
and took him at the corner of King William-street-he said nothing then,
but when we got about half-way over the bridge, he said he had just come
from Ratcliff-highway, and had been drinking there, and said, "I came up
the steps, did you not see me?"- I said, “No, I saw you on the bridge" -
I took him to a tobacco-shop, where the other prisoner was searched, and
then to the station.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Of course you have told us
exactly what occurred? 4. Yes, I remember every word he said to me,
and cannot be mistaken-be first passed me on the bridge, not a rod from
the corner nearest to Adelaide-place-there are steps leading up from the
water, and steps out of Thames-street.

2774. JOHN RICHARDS was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of
September, 1 knife, value 6d.; and 1 glass-rummer, value 1s. 10d. ; the
goods of Jonathan Hall.
Jonathan Hall. I keep the Ship and Whale public-house in Rother-
hitbe. On the 22nd of September I received some information from one
of the customers-in consequence of which I ran out and saw my daughter
alongside of the prisoner, bringing him back- I took this glass and knife
from him it was mine-be asked me to allow him to pay for the knife-
he had no business with either of them he had got about 200 yards.
Prisoner. I was not six yards from the house-I was very much in
liquor. Witness. He was not drunk.
John THOMAS (police-constable M 237.) I searched the prisoner, and
found five knives on his person, and this glass.
Prisoner. These knives were given me on board the George William, at
the Commercial-dock-there was no one belonging to them--Mr. Hall,
the ship's steward, said I might have them.
(The prisoner handed in a statement, partly in prose and partly in verse,
representing that he had fought under the late Admiral Nelson in various
actions, and begging for a lenient sentence.)
GUILTY. Aged 62.-Confined Seven Days.
