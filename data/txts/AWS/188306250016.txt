647. ERNEST ARCHER (19), WILLIAM ARCHER (17), and
GEORGE ILOTT (18), Robbery together with violence on William
Heap, and stealing his watch.
Mr. PURCELL Prosecuted; MR. GEOGHEGaN Defended Ilott.
WILLIAM HEaP. I live at 41, Sandringham Road-on Friday night,
25th May, about 10 o'clock, I was in Pond Lane Fields, some men came
up to me-I recognise the three prisoners-Iot asked me for some
tobacco-I told him I had none, he then asked me for some money for
beer, which I refused, upon which he snatehed at my chain and the
watch came out in his hand-he broke it from the chain and ran away,
I followed-one of them struck me and gave me a black eye-we were
fighting for some little time, and I fell to the ground-my stiok fell out
of my hand and another man not in custody hit me over the arm with
my stick and broke it-they all ran away, and we lost them in a brick.
field-I am certain the prisoners are three out of the four who attacked
me-on the morning of 27th May I saw the two Archers at the police
station amongst others and picked them out--I saw Ilott on the Friday
following at the station-he was placed with fifteen others and I picked
him out-this is my watch (produced).
Cross-eraminod by GEoGHEGaN. I was first examined at the police-cour
when the two Archers were in custody-the second time Ilott and
another man were in custody-they were. perfect strangers to me-
Pond Lane Fields is a place were there are ericketers-this happened at
10 at night-when I was first examined I said I believed that William
Archer was the man who snatched my watch, but I afterwards corrected
-I only said to the best of my belief-the detective took me to identify
Ilott about 12 at night to the Hackney Station-the others were all
about his own size, and some of them dressed in the same way-8 man
named Burridge was in eustody-at first I picked him out as the man
that took my wateh, but the Magistrate discharged him-it was a
mistake, and the Magistrate gaye him the advantage of it-I could
distinguish their faces when they attacked me, it was not perfectly dark
-it lasted altogether about a quarter of an hour.
JAMEs AOKERMAN. I am a coachman-on 25th May Ernest Archer
offered to sell me his watch, I refused it-soon after I saw a friend, and
from what he said I asked Ernest Archer for the watch to show him-I
took it to a pawnshop to see the value of it, and it was detained.
