622. JANE DOORS was again indicted for feloniously receiving, ob
the 30th of November, 1 pair of shoes, value 58., the goods of John
Matthews, well knowing them to have been stolen.
WILLIAM ROBERTS. I live at Messrs. Walmsley's, pawnbrokers, in
Drury-lane. I have a pair of new shoes pawned by the prisoner, on the
28th of November, in the name of Hutchings.
JOHN MATTHEWs. This prisoner came to my house about two months
before Christmas-these are my shoes; I have lost such--I have not sold
any of this make-they have my private mark on them.
JAMES ATFIELD. I am a police-constable. When the prisoner was
apprehended on the other charge, she told me she had taken a pair of
shoes from Mr. Matthews, and pledged them at Mr. Walmsley's, in
Drury-lane.
Prisoner. No; I said I received them.
WILLIAM HENRY BARRETT. I am a police constable. The prisoner
said she took this pair of shoes from the shop, and pledged them in
Drury-lane.
Prisoner. I had only had those which the boys gave me--they gave
me these--they told me they might take a hundred pair from the shop,
and nobody know it, as the master was in the habit of going out at seven
o'clock every evening, and not coming home till two o'clock in the morn-
ing, and then quite or nearly drunk.
MR. MATTHEWS. The prisoner had access to these shoes--she used to pass
through the shop many times a-day-my shop is very full of goods-
any body passing might take a pair here and there.
NOT GUILTY.
623. SARAH POPE and JANE SCOTT were again indicted for felo-
niously receiving, on the 22nd of December, 1 pair of boots, value 43., the
goods of John Matthews, well knowing them to have been stolen.
JOHN Ross. I am a pawnbroker, and live in the Blackfriars-road.
I have a pair of boots, pawned on the evening of the 22nd of December,
by the prisoners--they bo's came into the shop-I know them both
cannot tell which I took them of-they treated them as their own property
-they pawned them for 2s.--they are worth 3s. 6d., or 48.--the named
"Ann Clare, 19, Surrey-road," is on the duplicate.
NOT GUILTY.
