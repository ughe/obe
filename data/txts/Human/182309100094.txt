 1203.  WILLIAM COLLETT was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of August , a fixture, (i.e.) a copper, value 1 l., belonging to James Brown , and fixed to his dwelling-house.
   JOHN EWIN. I am a watchman, of Mile-end. On the 8th of August, about half-past four o'clock in the morning I stopped the prisoner in White Horse-lane, about one hundred yards from the prosecutor's, with this copper; he said, he kept a cook shop in the Borough, and being in debt, was getting his property away as well as he could.
 Cross-examined by MR. BRODRICK. Q. You took him to the watch-house, and he was allowed to go away - A. I believe he was.
   ROBERT LEWIS. I am constable of the night. Ewin brought the prisoner to my house, about a quarter past five o'clock in the morning; thinking his story might be correct, as two of the watchmen knew him, I detained the copper, and let him go. Next morning, his father-in-law came to inquire about it, and he was taken again.
   JOHN STONE. I apprehended him on the 9th, at his own door, in Dog-row; we knew where he lived.
   MARY EYERS. I am servant to James Brown, and know this copper to be his. I have cleaned it for three years, and know it by a mark of soap suds round it, which was there at the time I missed it, at seven o'clock in the morning on the 7th of August; it was taken out of the wash-house window.
 Cross-examined. Q. Do you swear to it by the soap suds - A. Yes, and its general appearance - my master lives in  Crown-row, Bethnal-green.
     GUILTY. Aged 25. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.

 1205.  SAMUEL JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of August , a coal-scuttle, value 15 s., the goods of Joseph Tunnell.
   JOSEPH TUNNELL. I live in Ironmonger-row, Old-street. On the 13th of August, about a quarter to seven o'clock, I heard the cry of Stop thief! and a neighbour brought the scuttle to me. I then ran out, and found a gentleman bringing the prisoner back. It was safe in my parlour the night before.
   JOHN BASWICK. On the 13th of August, about ten minutes to seven o'clock, I saw the prisoner and another young man walking about three doors from Tunnell's. I saw one of them lift the window up and take the scuttle out, and give it to the prisoner, who ran away with it. I ran after him, calling Stop thief! he threw it down, and I picked it up. Mitchell stopped him without losing sight of him.
   ROBERT MITCHELL. I was going up Waterloo-street, and heard a cry of Stop thief! and stopped the prisoner, who was running before the rest.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined Fourteen Days.
 1206.  THOMAS JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of July , four sheets, value 8 s.; six pair of stockings, value 6 s.; three handkerchiefs, value 5 s.; a shawl, value 18 d.; a waistcoat, value 2 s.; a pair of boots, value 3 s., and a frock, value 18 d., the goods of William Mason.
   WILLIAM MASON. I am a watchman. On Saturday morning, the 5th of July, I was on duty in Sloane-street, Chelsea, and on entering my room, about twenty minutes past five o'clock, I found a dirty shirt, a handkerchief, and an old pair of shoes on the floor, and went to the prisoner's mother - these things belong to him.
   LUCY MASON. I am the prosecutor's wife - we live in College-street, Chelsea. On Saturday, the 5th of July, about six o'clock in the morning, I missed all the linen off the horse, which I had ironed the night before. The prisoner had lodged with us, and came again that night; he slept in the parlour, and was gone when I got up.
 Cross-examined by MR. BRODRICK. Q. Was he in liquor the night before - A. I do not think he was - he left his old things behind.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   THOMAS PACE. On the 5th of July, about a quarter past six o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner in Dartmouth-street, Westminster, with these things in a bundle; he walked into Great Queen-street, and there I stopped him; he said he had a shirt, a pair of stockings, and a handkerchief; and I found he had several more things, and took him to the watch-house.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was very much intoxicated, and do not know whether I had a bundle or not. I brought this one out, thinking it mine, but when I came to my senses, I found I had it, and was going to take it back.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Seven Years.
