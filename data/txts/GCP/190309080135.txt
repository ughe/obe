CHARLES BEARD (Detective Sergeant.) On the night of July 11th I got
a message, and went and saw the prisoner at the police court-she said,
“Whatever sentence I shall get I hope I shall have it to-day; whatever
I done maliciously I done in my sleep; I do not care what sentence I get
as long as my husband or any of my family don't get disgraced owing to
my condition. The policy would not cover it because it is not made out
properly."
HARRY WOOD. I live at 19, Cornwall Road, Peckham-on September
14th, 1902, I was in Cornwall Road at 7.50 a.m. I saw smoke coming
from a shop at No. 6; I heard a female voice inside the gate say, "Open the
gates and let me out”-there are some carriage gates into a yard next
to No. 6-I got a hammer and broke the lock and opened the gates-I
saw the prisoner inside-she had a baby in her arms; she was fully dressed;
she seemed a little bit confused-I asked her if there was anybody in the
house-she said, “No”-I asked her where her husband was; she said
he had gone out to work at 5.30-I left her to go into a dairy opposite -
I broke the fire alarm and some constables came.
