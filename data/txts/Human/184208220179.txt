 2430. GEORGE COOPER was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of August , 1 hand-barrow, value 5 l  . 5 s  .; and 28 loaves of bread, value 11 s  . 3 d  .; the goods of Charles Stephen Moore . WILLIAM ROBERT BALDRY. I am in the employ of Charles Stephen Moore, a baker. On the 13th of August I was delivering bread for him—I had a barrow with me containing twenty-six half-quartern loaves—I went into a house in St. Michael's-place, Brompton—when I came out my barrow and bread were gone—I found the barrow, about eight o'clock the same evening, at North-end, but the bread was gone. JAMES STRATTEN. I keep the Seven Stars public-house. On Satur day evening, the 13th of August, I saw the prisoner drinking in the tap room—he was offering the barrow, which was at my door, for sale—he pointed it out, and asked 10 s . for it—I thought it was worth a great deal more—I persuaded him to put it in my yard, and come for it on Monday morning—he said it was his own, and he should sell it—he took it, and went to the Crown, below me—soon afterwards Mr. Moore came to me—I went and pointed out the barrow to him, which was then standing by the Crown. THOMAS EDOSON. I am a wheelwright, and live at North-end. On the 13th I saw a baker's barrow at the Seven Stan—the prisoner was with it—he was drinking outside the window—he said he would take 14 s . for it; he then said he would take 10 s .—he offered to sell it to me—I looked at it, and then he went to the Crown, and returned and said he would take 18 s . for it—I said it never was made for 6 l .—he said I was wrong; that he had had it for three weeks, and gave 3 l . 16 s . for it—I saw a ledger green baize in it. CHARLES BIRCH( police-constable V  92.) I took the prisoner in charge.
 (The prisoner pleaded distress.) GUILTY. Aged 39.— Confined Six Months.
