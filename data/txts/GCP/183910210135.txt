JOHN MONDAY. I am a carpenter, and live in Sheffield-street, Clare-
market. I was with Denton and Pape on the evening of the 28th of May,
returning down King-street-I stopped behind the party for a necessary
purpose, and the rest went on-when I came up they were fighting ---I was
not able to hear any thing sajd—I had scarcely time to say, "Halloo, what
is the matter?" before I received a blow, and was knocked down by one
of the party--the prisoner was one of the party-I saw him fighting --when
I came up Crowl was fighting-when the row was over he was sitting on
a stone.
