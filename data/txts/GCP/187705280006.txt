The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate. "I came from Paris three
weeks ago. As to the first witness, I was not aware this florin was bad ; as
to the second I know nothing about it, I never saw the gentleman before.
During the whole week I only had one piece of money."
Prisoner's Defence. I arrived in London about a month from the time I
was taken in custody. I was not very rich then and Mr. Laverne, of New
man Street, lent me 21. 108. ; my defence simply is that somebody asked
me to carry some boxes to that part of the town and gave me balf a crown
for my trouble ; as I returned home I recollected that I had no candle at
home and went into the grocer's, who examined the piece and bent it in his
teeth which made me believe it was not good, and for fear of being arrested
I ran away. I do not know the first gentleman; I never saw him before.
wish to call Mr. Laverne.
LAVERNE. The prisoner is the son of a gentleman-he came
five weeks ago to England without any money, and I gave him 21. 108., ten
shillings at a time I do not know how he spent it—the last money I gave
him was 58. on 16th April—I heard no more of him till I had a letter from
his mother saying, that he was arrested; he was employed at an hotel to
wash glasses, since he has been in England but only for one week-he lost
that situation about three weeks before the 28th.
Guilty-Nine Months' Imprisonment.
