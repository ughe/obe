JOHN WHISTLER. I assist my father, William Whistler, a draper at Greenwich. These silk handkerchiefs belong to him—I missed them, to the best of my recollection, on Tuesday evening, the 22nd of January—I had seen two riflemen in the shop that afternoon—I cannot recognise the prisoner as being one of them. JAMES GOODWIN. I am a private in the rifle brigade. On Tuesday afternoon, the 22nd of January, I went to the prosecutor's shop with the prisoner, and bought a pair of stockings—the prisoner was dressed in his undress clothing, as he is now—I went from there to Deptford, and got to the barracks about eight o'clock in the evening—the prisoner parted com pany with me for about three-quarters of an hour in Deptford—I did not notice the handkerchiefs in the shop.
   Prisoner. Q.  Was not I talking to you all the time I was in the shop? A.  Yon were in the shop with me, and spoke to me about the stockings you wanted—you went across to the other side of the shop, and asked the price of another pair—the gentleman said they were 2 s . 8 d ., and yon said it was too dear.
   COURT . Q.  Have the soldiers pockets in their clothes. A.  Only watch pockets, unless we have them put ourselves—his waistcoat was buttoned up in the shop.
   Prisoner.  I had these clothes on, and could not conceal any thing. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Transported for Seven Years.

 833. GEORGE SMITH  , alias    Batchelor, was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of January , 1 coat, value 20 s  ., the goods of Samuel Burrows . SAMCEL BURROWS. I keep a public-house in New-street, Deptford The prisoner came there on the 2nd of January) and bad a bed from Wed nesday till Friday—he did not come on Friday night, but came again on Saturday, and about six o'clock that afternoon went out, faying be was going a little way, and should return that night—he did not come back, and I missed my coat five minutes after he was gone—he owed me about 4 s . 9 d .—he was taken on the Wednesday following—be belongs to the 1st rifle brigade, and told me he was on furlough—this is my—( looking at  it.) SARAH W  ——. My husband is a pawnbroker at Greenwich The prisoner pawned this coat for 12 s . on Saturday night, the 5th of January, about eight o'clock—I asked particularly if it was his own, and he said it was. GUILTY. Aged 27.— Recommended to mercy. Confined One Month.
   Before Mr. Justice Vaughan.
