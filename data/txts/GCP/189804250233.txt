Ann ROBINSON. I am a tobacconist, of 29, Tulse Hill-on March 24th
I was in my shop—the prisoner came in and gave me a five-shilling piece
I am sure he is the man-I asked Mr. Groves to go out after him-he
was brought back by a policeman-I told him I did not like the five
shilling piece-he said that I had made a mistake, and that he had not
been near the shop-he was dressed as he is now.
HENRY WILSON (705 W). I met Groves and the prisoner on March
24th, at Tulse Hill, a little over a mile from the shop -- I told the prisoner
I should take him into custody for uttering counterfeit coin-.he said,
"You have made a mistake, I have not been near the shop "ZI took him
back to the shop, searched him and found four florins, a sixpence, and
fourpence in copper-he said he had no fixed abode and that he did
not smoke-I found this pipe on him (produced).
WILLIAM John WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to Her Majesty's
Mint--this crown is counterfeit.
Prisoner's defence : I never went into the shop. I have been to work
and have a character here. Guilty.--Six Weeks' Hard Labour.
342. ALFRED FENTON (24), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin
twice within ten days.
MR. WILKINSON Prosecuted.
LOUISE PERRY. I am barmaid at the Duke of York, Waterloo Road-
on March 29th, after two p.m., the prisoner came in for a small lemon, price
twopence -- he gave me a five-shilling piece-I put it in the till-there was
no other crown there—I gave him the change and he left-I left the bar
about three o'clock, and Mr. Hilliard sent up to me--I went down, and he
showed me a crown piece—I noticed that the prisoner had an impediment
in his speech-I was sent for to the station on the 31st, and saw a man
being locked up who I recognise 1-I was afterwards sent for to the station,
and picked the prisoner out from other men-I have no doubt of him-I
recognised him apart from the impediment in his speech.
AUGUSTUS HILLIARD. I am manager of this public-house-on March
29th, between two and three p.m., I found a crown piece in the till, and
only one—that was after Perry went upstairs--the till would be closed
on her leaving the bar-I sent for her and showed it to her, and after-
wards handed it to Lizall—this is it.
