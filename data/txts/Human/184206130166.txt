 1894. JAMES WEST was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of May 3 trusses of hay, value 6 s;.  the goods of John Hargood , his master; and WILLIAM ALLEN , for receiving the same, well knowing it to have been stolen, against the Statute, &c. JOHN HARGOOD. I am a tailor, and live at Stanmore. On the 24th of May I directed West to bind three quarters of a load of hay—two cuts of it were to go to a customer, and the rest to go to  my lawn—it was a little field of three acres, which I buy the crop of—he bound three quarters and three trusses—he accounted for three quarters only—the three trusses were con cealed in a loft just by—Allen lodged in Mr. Dorey's loft—I had not sold any hay to Dorey—West only charged for binding the three quarters, not for the three trusses. GEORGE SHORT. I am in Mr. Hargood's service. On the 24th of May I was sent to a Rick belonging to him, to take half a load down to Mr. Law ford—West sent me with three trusses to Mr. Dorey's, to  the shed, and he told me to tell Allen to take them in, and put them into the loft—I was not aware that it was wrong at that time—I saw Allen put one into the loft, and when I went back again the other two were gone—I took half a load to Mrs. Law ford, six trusses of hay to my master's stable, and three trusses of straw—my master gave West 1 s . for binding the hay—West put the hay on my back to take to the shed—he said he should send a truss or two to Mr. Dorey's; he should like to make a good half-day's work; he did not want to go on the common again.
