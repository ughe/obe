762. JOSEPH ANTOINE LOUIS LOBJOIS (38), Fraudulently
converting to his own use and benefit a cheque for £6 2s. 8d. received by
him for and on account of Jules Bengué. (See page 1456).
MR. HEMMERDE Prosecuted; MR. HOLLOWAY Defended.
