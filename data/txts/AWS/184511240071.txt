the blow on the ear-I cannot say whether it was a life-preserver or a stick
-it was about a foot or eighteen inches long-there are different sorts of
life-preservers-som are loaded with lead, and have a cord round them and
some catguti-some are dark, and some covered with leather--there was
sufficient light for me to see Barnett-there are six or seven lamps at the
turpike, which was not ten yards off-I never entertained any doubt of
Barnett being the man who struck me-as soon as I got to the station-house
I was asked who did it, and I directly described Barnett as being the man-
I gave his name as well.
