MARGARET HURLEY was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of March , 1 snuff-box, value 1 s  ., 3 keys, value 2 d  ., 1 split-ring, value 1 d  .; 2 shillings; and 3 1/4 d  . in copper, the goods and monies of John Hillier , and that he had been before convicted of felony. JOHN HILLIER. I live in New-street, Deptford. Between eleven and twelve o'clock, on the night of the 23rd of March, I was going down High-street to my mother's—I met the prisoner—we walked down Flagon-row—she asked me to treat her—I agreed to do so—we went to the Bee-hive—I took a glass of run an she took a glass, and I paid half-crown, and the publican gave me 2 s ., 1 d .,—we came out, and were going down New-street—I knocked at the door—my mother looked out of the window, and I put the two bundles down which I had—my mother opened the door—I felt the prisoner's hand at my side, and she took my money—I saw her put her hand down between her legs—I did not feel her hand going into my pocket—I missed a snuff-box, some keys, and a half-a-crown—if a man suffers a woman to put her hand into his pocket, these things might come out—I took the snuff-box from her hand, and the money fell from her hand after a minute or two—I do not know long her hand been in my pocket—I felt it come out of the pocket.
   Prisoner . he was rather tipsy—he put his hand into his pocket and took something out—and he gave me in charge. EDWARD CLEMENTS( police-constable R  29.) I am came up a little before twelve o'clock—the prosecutor was not in liquor at all—I found 1 s ., 3 1/4 d . in the area, and the snuff-box I got from the prosecutor. WILLIAM COLLINS. ( police-constable R  18) I produce the certificate of the prisoner's former conviction—the prisoner is the woman. NOT GUILTY.
