1289. SOPHIA WALLIS was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of
July, 1 carpet, value 103.; 6 blankets, value 30s. : 4 decanters, value Il.;
2 water-bottles, value 38.; 6 goblets, value 98.; and 2 salt-cellars, value
28.; the goods of John Herey, her master.
THOMAS TELLING. I am butler to Mr. John Hercy, of Baker-street. On
the 1st of July he and his family left town, leaving the prisoner in charge of
the house--they returned on the 4th of March, and then I missed the things
stated, which were left at home in her care these are them (examining
them.
WILLIAM JONES. I have two blankets, which were pledged by the pri-
soner or her daughter, I do not know whicha
JAMES REARDON. I live with Mr. Ross, a pawnbroker in East-street,
This carpet was taken in by a young man who has left the employ, in the
name of Jane Watson-here are some blankets and other things, all taken
in by the same person.
JONATHAN POLLARD (police-constable D 125.) I went to the prose-
cutor's house on the 12th of March, and saw the prisoner-I found forty.
nine duplicates, amongst which were those of this property in her room in
Baker-court-no one else lodged there.
(The prisoner received a good character, and pleaded poverty.)

the 12th these are them (looking at them.)

I knew a dog that he had with him to be the prosecutor's, and detained
