shop-on the 29th May I served the prisoner with a penny loaf-he threw
down this shilling (produced) -I put it in my mouth and it bent-I gave
it to my mistress, who said it was bad, and sent me to the bakehonse to
fetch a man-I saw a constable brought into the shop, and my mistress gave
the prisoner in charge with the shilling.
JAMES SHERRARD (Policeman 0 298). The prisoner was given into my
custody with this bad shilling-it is marked – I said "Have you any more
of that kind of coin about you "-he said "I have no more of that kind”
- he said he got it for doing some work in Park Lane-I asked him for
whom, but he could not name any one I found on him 6d. in silver, and
31d. in bronze--he was taken before the Magistrate and discharged.
