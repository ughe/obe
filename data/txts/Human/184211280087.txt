 81. THOMAS STOW was again  indicted for embezzlement. JAMES MORTIMER CARTER. I send out the articles I tell—I am in the shop, and generally weigh them myself—Mrs. Carter books them—sometimes I book them myself, I copy from the day-book into the ledger—the prisoner did not pay me 11 s . 3 d . on the 19th of Oct.; on the 26th of Oct 19 s .; nor, on the 10th of Nov., 1 l . 9 s . 3 d .—if he received them on either of those days, he ought to have paid me.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Your wife keeps the day-book? A.  Yet, generally—I have other persons in my employ, who occasionally account to my wife, NOT GUILTY.
 82. MARY PHILIPS was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of Sept , 1 bonnet, value 1 s  .; the goods of William Harding . MARY HARDING. I am the wife of William Harding, of New-street, Ken sington Gravel-pits. I went to gather hops in Sept., and left my bonnet in the bottom drawer of a chest of three drawers, in a small back-par lour—the prisoner lodged there—when I came home I missed it—I met Mary Johnson with it in the street. MARY JOHNSON. I bought this bonnet of Sarah Goodaere. SARAH GOODACRE. I bought this bonnet of the prisoner for 9 d  —she brought it into my shop, and said she had got it to sell for a person.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did I not come back, and ask you if you had sold it, as I heard there was a bonnet lost? A.  Yes, and I said I could not tell who I had sold it to.
   Prisoner's Defence.  The person I sold it for was an acquaintance of Mrs. Harding, she had sprained her uncle; she said she had taken this bonnet in lieu of 1 s . which was due to her. MARY HARDING re-examined.  I do not say but that the prisoner has re quired money of me, and I have allowed her to take even the quilt off my bed to save her from her husband—I have not drank with her.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 46 .— Confined Ten Days .
 83. WILLIAM KEEP and GEORGE PRESTON were indicted for stealing, on the   5th of Nov ., 1 pig, value 3 s  .; the property of George Pullen .—2nd COUNT  , stating it to be 1 dead pig: to which
   PRESTON  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months . GEORGE PULLEN. I live at Stanweli. I had a sow and six pigs—I misted one on the 6th of Nov.—I did not see them after the 4th—I should not know it again—it is cut to pieces—I believe this to be the head—( looking at it. ) EDWARD GODFREY( police-constable D  73.) I went to the prisoner's lodg ing, and asked Preston what he had done with the pig he brought home—he said he knew nothing about it—I then went to the next room, and found a pig in a basin—the head was wanting—I afterwards found the head in a boiler on the fire—I showed it to the prosecutor, and it corresponded with what he had lost as near as he could say—Keep was there, and said he was surprised that I should take him—I then examined, and there was one per son's foot-marks from the prosecutor's to the prisoners', and two persons' foot-marks to the prosecutor's house—I examined them with the prisoner's shoes, and they corresponded exactly—they went within 100 yards of the prosecutor's.
