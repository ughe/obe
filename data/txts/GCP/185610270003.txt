939. CHARLES ASHTON, embezzling 951.; the moneys of the
Governor and Company of Copper Mines in England, his masters.
MR. GIFFARD conducted the Prosecution.
CHARLES FREWER. I am secretary to the Governor and Company of
Copper Mines in England; their office is in Broad-street-mews. In Jan.
last the prisoner was in their service-he had authority to receive money
on behalf of the Company, and it was his duty to hand it over to me about
3 o'clock the same day-in Jan. last, Thomassett and Co. were indebted to
the Company-the prisoner has never accounted to me for 95l. received
from them on behalf of the Company.
