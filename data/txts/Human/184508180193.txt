Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1790. THOMAS BATT was indicted for stealing 1 rule, value 6 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of John Pegrum , his master. JOHN PEGRUM. I am a carpenter, and live in Robert-atreet, Brixton—the prisoner worked in my service. I missed a rule, which I had laid on the stone pavement where we were at work—I spoke to the prisoner about it—he said he did not believe I had had a rule there that morning—this it my rule.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Did you hear of it from Randall? A.  Yes—I expect I had laid the rule on the pavement—the prisoner came about nine or ten o'clock, and I missed the rule about one. WILLIAM RANDALL. I saw the prisoner have the rule between eleven and twelve o'clock that morning—he said he had found it in the drain—he afterwards said he had sold it for 1 s . and told me not to say anything about it.
   Cross-examined. Q.  He showed it you? A.  Yes—I saw it was my master's—I told him so, and read the name on it—he then said, "Don't say anything about it." JOHN PERKINS. I bought the rule for 10 d . of the prisoner.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you rub it with sand-paper, to erase the name? A.  Yes—I do not know the name—I cannot read. JOHN PEGRUM re-examined.  This rule had my name on it—it is partly erased, but you can see it now. RICHARD WEBB( police-constable P  228.) I took the prisoner.
   Cross-examined. Q.  In going to the station, did he not say he knew where the rule was, he would get it back, and you need not take him up? A.  Yes—he said he found it down the drain, and he thought all he found he could do as he liked with.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 32.— Recommended to mercy.— Confined Six Days .
 1791. ROBERT WHITBY  , alias    John Rock, was indicted for stealing 3 feet of leaden pipe, value 1 s  ., the goods of William Baker , and fixed to a building; and that he had been before convicted of felony. FRANCES JANE BAKER. I live with my father, William Baker, who keeps a beer-shop—the prisoner was a customer—he was there between three and four o'clock in the afternoon, on the 8th of Aug.—we had a piece of leaden pipe at the end of the passage—I saw the prisoner pat it into his pocket, and go away—it has not been found since—I am quite sure I saw him take it.
   Prisoner. Q.  Why did not you stop me? A.  My mother and father both being out, I had two businesses to attend to, and it quite slipped my memory—my father missed some taps, and he asked me about the pipe—I told him I saw the prisoner put it in his left-hand pocket—I swear he took it. CHARLES JOHN ROPER. I was in the house—I went into the ground to play at skittles—I saw the prisoner come, take two pieces of lead pipe in his hand, and leave the ground—I thought he was a plumber.
