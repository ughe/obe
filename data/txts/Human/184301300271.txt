 835. GEORGE FOULCHER was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of Jan ., 1 pair of boots, value 15 s  ., the goods of Edward Neale . WILLIAM DUNFORD. On the 14th of Jan. the prisoner put up at my mas ter's stable—I missed a pair of boots, an hour after he was gone, belonging to Edward Neale, my master—these are them—there was no one in the stable but the prisoner and me. WILLIAM SMITH. The prisoner came and asked me to buy the ticket of these boots—I said I had no money, but he put the ticket into my waistcoat-pocket—three days after that I heard of the boots being stolen—this is the ticket. JAMES CLIPSON. The prisoner lodged with me. On the 15th of Jan. he said he had an excellent pair of boots to sell—he brought them from the boot of his coach—these are them—I did not buy them. ROBERT ADAMS( police-constable E  109.) I took the prisoner, got the ticket from Smith, and these boots from the pawnbroker.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I brought a man from Norwood, and put him down at the corner of Westminster-bridge; I bought the boots of him for 4 s . GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Four Months .
