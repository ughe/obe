—I had a pot of beer with the prisoner, and gave him 6  d .—the beer did not affect me—I had taken nothing else—I had not been well for a month or six weeks—if he had received the money and paid it to Clark and Green that would have been sufficient, but I was his master, and I have to give it to them—I gave the prisoner a delivery-note to give to Mrs. Edwards—I received that delivery-note from Clark and Green—I told them the prisoner had not given me the money.
   COURT . Q.  Did Clark and Green look to you for the money? A.  Yes, and I have had to pay them 5 s . a week ever since, to make the money good. MARGARET EDWARDS. I am the wife of James Edwards, a baker, in Vernon's-buildings, St. Pancras. On the 26th of October, some flour was brought to me by a young man—I cannot say it was the prisoner—it was a much younger person than generally came—I had never seen the person before—he looks better than he did then, if he is the person—he brought me a note with the flour—it was written on the back that he was to take the money back with him, and I paid him 12 l . 15 s .—he wrote on the note, "Received 12 l . 15 s ., John Cook"—I put it on my file, after wards took it to Union-hall, and have since lost it—I have looked for it every where.
 Cross-examined.  Q.  Do you recollect whether the name of Green and Clark was on the note? A.  Yes, it was—I have plenty of their notes—I only saw one person—I did not notice any one else come in. JAMES CUDDY. I am a policeman. On Wednesday morning, the 2nd of February, I was on duty in the London-road—the prisoner came up to me and said he wanted to give himself up—I said, "What for?"—he said for stealing 12 l . 15 s ., that he was intrusted by Cook to  deliver fire sacks of flour at a baker's shop over against King's-cross, Battle-bridge, that he had received 12 l . 15 s ., and absconded, that he turned the horse and cart adrift—he said he was induced to do it by some harness-maker of the name of Hand fast, I believe, that he had given him a part of the money, and had spent the remainder—I said, "Recollect what you tell me, I shall have to state against you before the Magistrate"—he said "I have only told you the truth; you will find it is the truth; I have been to Bristol; I have done no work since; I am in a state of starvation; I I might as well be transported as be out of work, starving about the streets."
   Cross-examined. Q.  You recollect his saying he had spent some of the money? A.  Yes—he said the harness-maker induced him to  keep the money, and he had given 5 l . or 6 l . to the harness-maker—I did not take particular notice of the word "spent"—I will not be certain that he used the word "absconded," but he said he went away, and had spent all the money except what he had given the harness-maker.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY  . Aged 19.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Twelve Months.
   Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
