1618. HENRY TYRRELL was indicted for embezzlement,
GEORGE SCAMMELL. I am a baker, and live in Union-street, Spitalfields
the prisoner was my journeyman-it was his duty to receive money for me,
and to pay it to me the same evening—if he received 188. 5d. on the 25th of
June, 58. 78. on the 28th of June, or 9fd. on the 1st of July, he has not paid
it to me-he ought to have paid it on those days.
SARAR NOTLEY. I deal with Mr. Scammell-I paid the prisoner 58. 7d.
on the 28th of June for his master.
John Wilson. I deal with Mr. Scammell— I paid the prisoner 188. 5d. on
the 25th of June for his master.
JANE OAKEY. I paid the prisoner 9fd., on the 1st of July, for Mr.
Scammell.
Prisoner's Defence. I received these sums, but I had the misfortune to
lose half-a-sovereign-I was not able to pay them on that account-when
my wages came due on the Saturday I would have paid them.
GEORGE SCAMMELL re-examined. I did not speak to the prisoner on the
subject of his receiving these sums till I gave him into custody-he did not
give me any answer.
Guilty. Aged 34.-Confined Three Months.
