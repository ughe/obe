JAMES DUNT, I am a livery stable keeper at Elpha Row, Brixton. On
Monday, 8th June, at 11 o'clock, the prisoner rode into my yard on the pony,
and said that he wanted to sell it-I asked him where he brought it from
he said that he had won it in a raffle at Westminster---that he was a painter,
and rode it up to the Crystal Palace to seek for employment, and not getting
any wanted to sell it, as he had got no money and wanted to get something
to eat and drink-I lent him 58. on it, and he left it in my care till the
Tuesday evening, when he said that he would come for it and bring a proper
direction where it came from, if I liked to buy it-I showed it to Mr. Gillett
on the next day, and afterwards saw the prisoner in custody at Woolwich
- he gave his name George Smith.
Prisoner. This witness speaks the truth, and he is the only one who
does.
