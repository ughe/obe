WILLIAM COSTER( Policeman E  97). On Wednesday, 25th August, I went with Treacher to a public-house in Neale Street, Seven Dials—he went in and pointed out Knight as a man he had seen running away on the Tuesday—I took him into custody, and told him it was for highway robbery in Lincoln's Inn Fields the day before—he said, "I will go with you." EDWARD MOTT( Policeman C ). On 25th August 1 was with Policeman Coucher on an omnibus in Holborn, and saw Philps and Magrath attempting to open a lady's handbag at the corner of Chancery Lane—we got down and followed them for about 20 minutes, and arrested them and took them to the station, and this charge was afterwards brought against them—I was at the station when Mr. Burton identified them—they were placed among eight or nine men; Mr. Burton came in, and looked along and said, "That is the one that took my watch," meaning Philps—I do not think he said anything about the other two.
   By the COURT . I did not take Magrath and Philps in custody at the time I saw them trying to open the lady's bag, there were four of them together—I did not know them before—after that I saw them open a lady's handbag and take a purse out, and then we arrested them. NOT GUILTY.
 944. GEORGE PHILPS and THOMAS MAGRATH were again  indicted for stealing a purse of Adele Johnson from her person.
   PHILPS PLEADED GUILTY.
   MR. DOUGLAS Prosecuted. ESWARD MOTT repeated his former evidence as to stealing the purse, and added:  When Magrath took the purse I ran up and laid hold of him—he struggled violently for about five minutes, and kicked me, and in the struggle he threw the purse on the ground—it has not been recovered—the lady called out, "Oh, they have got my purse"—I seized Magrath; Couchman seized Philps—the lady was very excited; she came to the station: she said her purse contained a 5 l . note, 2 l . in gold, 8 s . in silver, and 2 d . in coppers—she has gone away to Hamburg—they were charged at the station, and made no reply—the other two men got away. GEORGE COUCHMAN( Policeman ). I took Philps—I have heard Mott's evidence, it is correct.
   Magrath's Defence.  I am innocent of the charge, and Philps can say so. GEORGE PHILPS( The Prisoner ). Magrath was not with me when the lady lost her purse.
   MAGBATH  — GUILTY.
