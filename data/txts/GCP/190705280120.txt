FRANK CONNELL, 79, Woodside, Wimbledon. In the early morning
of April 30, about four o'clock, I heard our dog growl in the hall.
My bedroom 18 on the ground floor, and I went out and spoke to
him, and at the same time I saw a light through some coloured glass
in a door which leads to the kitchen. I went back into my room and
got my revolver, unlocked the glass door, heard a noise in the
kitchen, went in there, and saw a man getting out of the window. I
went forward and fired at him. I then went nearer the window and
fired again. I saw the man bend forward and then recover himself
and run away out of my sight. I then went to the front door, un-
fastened it, blew my police whistle, and a policeman came. The man
got in by forcing open the kitchen window. I found it broken open
when I came to examine it.
