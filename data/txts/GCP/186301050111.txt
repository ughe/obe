former part of the year—I had been at my home, Poole, in Dorsetshire-1
left Poole in August, and then I stayed at a place called Bentley, about
thirty or forty miles from London-I was not in the fishmonger line there;
I and my wife were staying with Mr. Spiers, a land surveyor-I went hop-
ping for him, and had a guinea a week from him-I was picking hops, and
seeing the men measure them, and take them to the house where they were

that employment close about six weeks--I took my wife and child there--I
did not come straight to London-I stayed in Bentley a few weeks after
that, in lodgings, and then walked, with my wife and child, to London,
where I arrived in November, or the latter part of October—I was not poor
when I walked to London-I went to lodgings in London-it was not from
