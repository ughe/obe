(Wednesday, March 6.)
The indictment for forging and uttering a cheque of the value of
260 2s. was now proceeded with.
Mr. E. L. Hadfield prosecuted; Mr. St. John MeDonald defended.
Mr. MeDonald raised the plea of autrefois acquit, submitting that
prisoner had virtually been tried upon the same charge yesterday and
acquitted by the jury.
Judge Lumley Smith refused to accept the plea.
