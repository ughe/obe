   Cross-examined by SIR HENRY JAMES . I heard that Saffery had resold, but not till Mr. Longcroft called on me, which was about the time of the letter as to the requisitions of March 22, 1876—the conveyance came from Mr. Longcroft's office, and was approved by me on behalf of the vendor—Saffery was a party to the conveyance—I have sold three-quarters of an acre of this estate at the rate of 1,200 l . an acre—I told Mr. Longcroft I had sold some for l,000 l . an acre and I had done so—I asked 1,200 l .—I do not know that I would not have taken less—I have some left now.
