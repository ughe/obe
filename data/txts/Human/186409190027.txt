 869. JOHN WILLIAMS  (35) , Feloniously wounding Annie Williams , with intent to murder her. Second County  with intent to do grievous bodily harm.
   MR. BEST conducted the Prosecution., and MR. SLEIGH the Defence. ANNIE WILLIAMS. I am the prisoner's wife—about half-past 10, on the evening of 25th August, I was walking in Leicester-square—a gentleman was with me—the prisoner came up to me—I told him to go away, I did not want him—he said I was his wife, and where I was he had got the right to be—we had a few words together, and he cut me in the neck with a knife that he uses in his business as a saddler—I got it in my hand, and broke it in two, and threw it on the ground—I ran into the Sabloniere Hotel, and was there attended by a surgeon, and afterwards by a gentleman who was supposed to be my father.
