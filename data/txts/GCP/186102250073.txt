SURREY CASES.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
277. JEREMIAH MCARTHY (21), and WILLIAM FELLOWS (21),
Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MESSRS. CLERK and ORRIDGE conducted the Prosecution,
CHARLES TWILLEY. I keep the Fox beer-house, Frogmore-lane, Wands-
worth-on 24th January, about 3 o'clock in the afternoon, the two prisoners
came-M'Carthy asked me for a pint of beer, and offered me a half-crown-
I said, “This is a bad one "-he said, “Give it me back, and I will see if I
have got another one " I gave it him back-I tried it with my teeth; I
found it grit-I am sure it was bad—I saw a half-crown since, but cannot
swear that it was that one-be then gave me a 28. piece-I said, " This is
good I gave him 18. 10d. in change they drank the beer between them,
and left the house together-I gave information to the police-I believe this
(produced to be the half-crown.
Carthy. 0. When the policeman gave you a good half-crown at the
police-court, did you not try it with your teeth A. Yes I said, "No, this
in not the one " I bit the second one, and said, “This is a bad one," and
that it looked like the one you gave me.
MR. CLERK. 0. At the police-station did the policeman show you some
money! 4. Yes-he first gave me a good half-crown, which I tried with my
teeth-he then gave me a bad one which appeared to be like the one that
they had changed at my place.
JANE STYLES. My husband, John Styles, keeps the Cedar Tree public-
bouse at Putney-from there to Frogmore-lane, Walworth, is about three-
quarters of a mile on Thursday afternoon, 24th January, the prisoners came
to my house about half.past 4 o'clock in the afternoon—M'Carthy called for
half a quartern of gin, wbich is 20.-- he gave nue a half-crown- I examined
it-there was no one else in my house at the time—it appeared to be a bad
one at the time I took it-I gave him his change, 28. 4d.--they both drank
the gin, and left immediately-as soon as they had gone out a constable
named Johns came in- I gave him the half-crown which M'Carthy had given
me-he went out and followed the prisoners.
M'Carthy. Q. How did you know it was a bad half-crown! 4. I thought
it was bad, and when the constable came in I asked him-I did not try it
while you were in there, I did not let it go out of my hand-there were not
teeth marks on it when you gave it to me.
