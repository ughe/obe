of December, 1 half-crown, 1 shilling, 1 sixpence, 1 penny, 1 half-
penny, and 1 farthing, the monies of James Dempsey,
JAMES DEMPSEY. I live in Bow-yard, St. Giles's. On Sunday morn-
ing, the 8th of December, I went to bed in the house of a person named
Hoy the prisoner was in the same bed-I fastened the door inside
early in the morning, when I awoke, I observed be was gone, and the
door open-I missed from my jacket pocket the money stated.it was
safe when I went into the room the night before,
