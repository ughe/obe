   By the COURT . The goods given out were to be brought back com plete; the work was to be done off the premises—the arrangement was if the work was not brought back it was to be paid for—I always told them that if there was any lost they must make it good; I might not have done so with the prisoner; that was the arrangement made with the others who did the same kind of work; if they brought back the work completed they were entitled to what they had done for it.
   The RECORDER was of opinion, referring to the case of Reg. v. Davis and Warner , 12 Cox, Criminal Cases, p.  514, that this was not an indictable offence, but one that should be dealt with summarily by the Magistrate under the Act of George II. NOT GUILTY.
