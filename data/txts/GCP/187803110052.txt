WILLIAM LOVE (Policeman 464 T). I saw the prisoner on the morning

used to sleep in the same room at the station-just as I was going to bed
the prisoner said to me "I wonder what I shall get for calling the fire
engines to the fire last night”-I replied “Nothing, the police are not
allowed to receive gratuities for calling fire-engines; you ought to know
that"-he said that wouldn't matter, he could easily sign some other per-
son's name.
EDMUND BLAKE (Policeman 368 T'). I prepared the plans produced in
Court—they are an enlargement from the Ordnance Survey map-they
are accurate.
CHARLES ADAMS (Re-examined). The prisoner made a report to me in
writing-I tore it up-we generally afterwards destroy the papers, it is
no use keeping them—the report he gave me related to the fire and not
the cutting of the window-I destroyed another report which referred to
the cutting of the window-I destroyed the report because I thought it
was of no use I could scarcely understand it, it was so badly written.

up on Saturday night-the windows and doors were not in at that time
