WILLIAM WATTS re-examined.  I believe this to be the same shawl—I went on the Monday following to the pawnbroker's to inquire about the shawls, and, among others, to Mr. Parry's, who had previously denied having any shawl pawned at his house—the person I saw at Mr. Parry's said he was perfectly certain nothing of the sort had been taken in. RICHARD PARRY. re-examined.  When Mr. Watts came to my shop I was not at home—he asked for two shawls—this is called a scarf—if he had said a scarf, they might have looked for a scarf—I took the shawl in myself.
