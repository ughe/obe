 164. JANE MARSHALL , stealing 3 handkerchiefs, 1 pair of stockings, and other articles, value 5 shillings; the goods of John Whitcher , her master; having been before convicted. SARAH WHITCHER. I am the wife of John Whitcher, of Bengal-terrace, Old Kent-road—the prisoner came into my service about ten weeks ago—I wrote for her character, and did not get it—she remained a little more than two months—I missed three cigars out of the parlour—she denied having them—she afterwards gave them to me out of her pocket—next day I looked in her box, and found a pair of stockings, a piece of lace, and two handker chiefs—she said the stockings and lace were mine, but not the handkerchiefs—she packed up her boxes and went away without them—I opened the boxes and took the handkerchiefs out—I saw this satin handkerchief at the police-court—it is mine, and has my mark upon it—the things are worth 5 s . altogether. MARY ANN RANDALL. I am the wife of a policeman, and search females at the station—I searched the prisoner, and found two half-crowns in her hand, and afterwards a satin handkerchief in her box. WILLIAM ROSS( policeman ). I produce a pair of stockings, some lace, and three cigars. SARAH WHITCHER re-examined , I believe these to be mine, I swear to the stockings.
   Prisoner's Defence , I was keeping company with a young man, and tookc three cigars for him; I did not know it was wrong; my master forgave me, and told me to go on as though nothing had happened; I put things not belonging to my department into my box for security when I was left in charge of the house, and I had so much to do I had not time to look over my box; I had plenty of opportunity to be dishonest if I wished; I was left in charge of the house with money and a watch, and when my mistress came from the country she appeared very much pleased; I said, "If you will wait till I earn the money I will give it you;" she said, "That proves you guilty;" this is all done out of spite, because I left her service. SARAH WHITCHER re-examined.  When I went out of town I gave her 1 l . 17 s . 6 d ., in case she wanted anything; but the money was all marked, and she knew it; at least we showed her one marked coin—I have missed things and money before, but did not suspect her till I missed the cigars. ALEXANDER TAHOURDIN( policeman, L  57) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's conviction—( ready, Convicted Dec , 1847, of stealing from her master, having been before convicted; confined eighteen months )—she had been out of pri son three or four weeks when she went to this place—she was also convicted in Nov., 1846, for robbing her mistress. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined One Year, (There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
   Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
