RICHARD PATTERSON( continued. ) I heard nothing more of the prisoner after the receipt of that letter till Aug., 1843—in consequence of what I then heard I came to Newgate. and saw the prisoner there—he was therein the name of William Smith—I lodged a detainer against him about three or four months after, and on the 4th of Feb., when he was about to be discharged, he was taken on this charge, taken before Mr. Rawliuson the Magistrate, and committed.
