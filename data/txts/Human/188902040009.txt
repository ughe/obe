   Cross-examined.  Three or four persons can walk a breast on the pavement there—I did not take the coins from you because I had hold of you with both hands. WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. I am Inspector of Coin to H. M. Mint—these coins are counterfeit, and one of those dropped is from the same mould as the one uttered.
   The prisoner in his defence stated that he did not know the other man, that he merely stopped to allow the witnesses to pass him when they seized him; he denied attempting to throw the policeman or dropping the coin.
