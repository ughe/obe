 1891.  HENRY SCORNELL, GEORGE LILLEY, and JOHN STEVENS were indicted for stealing, on the 3d of September , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of a man unknown, from his person.
   JOHN GROOM. On the 3d of September, about nine o'clock at night, I was going along Queen-street, with my wife, and saw Scornell put his hand into a gentleman's pocket several times, and at last he got a handkerchief out; he put it under his coat, and then gave it to Stevens; I ran and collared Stevens - he dropped it; my partner took the others. Lilley and Stevens were close behind, covering him, when he took it; we had a scuffle with them, and could not find the gentleman; Lilley must have seen him pass it to Stevens.
   WILLIAM WHITTINGHAM. I was in Queen-street, and saw the three prisoners following the gentleman; I crossed over, and seized Scornell and Lilley, who were running.
 SCORNELL's Defence. The handkerchief was laying down - I was going to take it up, but Stevens did.
 STEVENS' Defence. I picked it up.
 Stevens received a good character.
 SCORNELL -    GUILTY. Aged 12. Whipped and Discharged.
 LILLEY -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined Six Months.
 STEVENS -    GUILTY. Aged 15. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 1892.  CATHERINE FITZGERALD was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of September , 1 half sovereign, 2 half-crowns, 10 shillings, and 10 sixpences, the monies of Thomas Goff , from his person.
 THOMAS GOFF. I am a  carpenter. On the 1st of September, between eleven and twelve o'clock at night, I met the prisoner in the street, and went to her room in Maynard-street, St. Giles'; I counted my money before I went to bed - I had one half sovereign, two half-crowns, and the rest in shillings and sixpences; I put it into my trouser's pocket, which I put under my pillow; I awoke about six o'clock in the morning - she was gone, and the money also - I rapped at the door, which was fastened with a padlock - I could make nobody hear - I got out at the back window, and waited there till about twelve o'clock; she then came home; I followed her up stairs, and accused her of it; she came down, and gathered about one hundred women, who covered me with mud.
 Prisoner's Defence. I have known him ten years; he went home with me; he gave me 1s., and I went away.
   THOMAS GOFF. I never knew her before; the door was locked inside when I went to bed.
 GUILTY. Aged 32.
 Of stealing, but not from the person. Transported for Seven Years.
 1893.  MARY SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of September , 5 shillings, the monies of James Donohue , from his person.
 JAMES DONOHUE. On the 16th of September, rather after ten at o'clock at night, I was at a public-house in  Catherine-wheel-alley- I was rather drunk; the prisoner and some men were there; I intended to call for a pint of beer; one of the men asked if I objected to make it a pot; I said No, and gave a 6d. - I had no change back; I had 5s. in my hand; I laid my hands on the table, and laid my head down, but did not go to sleep; the prisoner was at my left side; I felt her hand coming to mine; she made a grasp and got my money; I asked her for it; she got up and ran out of the house; I followed her into a house; she ran up stairs; I went up into the room with two watchmen, and found the 5s. in her hand.
   GEORGE DUNWORTH. I was at the public-house, and saw the prisoner sitting on the prosecutor's left side; he got up, and pursued the prisoner.
   GEORGE HUBBARD. I heard a cry of Murder! and went to No. 27, Rose-lane, where I found the prisoner and prosecutor - I found five shillings and a purse in her bosom.
   JOHN POWELL. I am a watchman. I found five shillings and a purse in the prisoner's bosom - one shilling was under her left arm, and four shillings under her right arm; the prosecutor stated what he had lost before I found it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I met him, and went to two houses with him; I took 1s. out of my purse to pay for some beer - he said it was his money; he then said, "Go on, and I'll forgive you;" he followed me home, and would have my money - I called Murder!
     GUILTY. Aged 27. Transported for Fourteen Years.
