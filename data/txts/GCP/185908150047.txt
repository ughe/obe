see whose writing it is? A. Yes, once; that was some time ago-Mr. Fryer

many bales I received on 517, Huddersfield, and if it was in the same state
as when I passed it to the reference clerk-he did not ask me in whose
writing the alterations were made; this is the first time I have been asked
