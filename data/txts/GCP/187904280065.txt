FREDERICK COLE. I am a grocer, of 19, Sidmouth Street, Gray's Inn
Road; on 13th December I received this cheque (produced) drawn by Toler
on the British Association--I paid 31. 108. for it-I gave it to my cheese
monger, Mr. West, who paid it into the bank, and it was returned dishonourad
- week after the first examination the 37. 108. was sent to me from
Batchelor, who cashed the cheque, not from Toler-I said that I thought
I ought not to receive it, as the case was pending, but I gave a receipt
for it.
Cross-examined by Toler. Mr. Myers, my solicitor, told me that some-
body came and tendered him the, 31. 108., which he refused to take, and
another cheque also found its way to Mr. Myers.
WILLIAM HENRY MARTIN. I am & waiter at the Stock Exchange, and
knew Barrett when he was a member-about February 5th, at 10a.m., he
brought me this order for 107. (dated February 5th), and asked me to get
it changed for him; I took it into Mr. Slaughter's office, where one of the
clerks gave me 10 sovereigas for it, which I gave to Barrett, who insisted on
giving me 108. for my trouble the cheque was returned dishonoured-I.
went once to the office in New Bridge Street and asked for Toler, but could
not see him; I saw a lady and a young lady-I also went to Barrett's office
in Eldon Street, Finsbury, but did not succeed in seeing him-I have had
to pay Mr. Slaughter the money I have received two prost-cards from Barrett
but no money.
