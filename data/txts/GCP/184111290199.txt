WILLIAM HENRY FINCH. I live at No. 5, Moon-street, Islington. I
was sent to pay some money for a coral necklace for the prisoner-it was
such a necklace as this-I brought it home for her-I have known her
about two years-I nerer heard any thing against her character till she
was apprehended-I knew her living at the Angel and Crown, Islington,
for twelve months.
MARY Lainson. I live at No. 27, Aldgate. These black earrings and
necklace are similar articles to what I sell I cannot positively say I sold
them-I never sold the prisoner a pair of black earrings like these, to my
recollection- I have no recollection of it whatever-it is impossible for me
to say whether I sold such articles on the 19th of August-I do not enter
articles in a book--I sell similar earrings to these-I see this is my box they
are in, and that is quite sufficient, for this box was given with the articles
-I am in the habit of placing articles in a box in this way-that is my
box, but that does not imply that they are my earrings.
Court to Mrs. BEAGLEY. Q. Can you state where you got these jet
ornaments ? A. I bought them in Tottenham-court-road, to the best of
my belief-I cannot tell the person's name-it is some years back-I
bought the brooch just on the other side of the New-road, at Mr. Baylis's,
I believe, some years back-I never wore it above once-I dare say per-
sons have seen me wear it-I generally kept my earrings in a box in cotton
- I bought this pair of earrings, I think, of a Mr. Harrison I have worn
them-it is more than two years ago since I wore them--they were perfect
-one top was not off-I believe I gave 4s. 6d. for them-- I cannot say
what I gave for the brooch-I bought the stays at the bar, over the counter
of the public-house-- I cannot positively say who of-I did not try them on
to see if they would fit-the general way of trying is by a piece of tape-
I cannot positively say when I bought them-it was of a woman I cannot
say in what year, or whether it is five years ago.
Q. On the solemn oath you have taken, do you mean to represent you
bought these stays over the bar; take warning before you answer? A.
It might be in the kitchen-I will swear I bought them either in the bar
or kitchen-I cannot say when I frequently buy things of people in
the kitchen-when people stand at the bar, we ask them to come into the
kitchen.
Q. Is there any human being who ever saw you in possession of the
stays? A. I do not know-on my oath the stays belong to me-I do not
know what letters are on them I tried them on, but not to wear them any
length of time-I never wore them-they did not fit then-I have not
tried them on since the prisoner has been apprehended-I do not think I
tried them on this year-I rather think it was last year.
