Mr. Way conducted the Prosecution.
LAWRENCE MURPHY. I am a boot-clicker, and live at 64, Sun-street,
Bishopsgate-on the morning of the 27th July, about 2 o'clock, I was at
the Swan public-house in Long-alley-the prisoner was there singing
invited him to take a glass of ale, which he secepted, and then I sung
then he sung againat about half-past 2, I left the house with him and
his wife, and we went to another public-house to have some spirits-1 was
never in that house before the prisoner suggested our going there-I paid
for some spirits- drank some, and the prisoner and his wife had the rest
between themwe did not remain there long, we all three came out
togetherat the top of Long-alley I put out my left hand to shake hands
with the prisoner, and with his other hand he took my ring off my finger
it was on the little finger of my left handI grasped him by the collar
and said, You vagabord, you have my ring, give it me back 3 do not
wish to hurt you struck me several times on the head with his fists
I bled very much from the nose-I still had hold of him, and I called out
