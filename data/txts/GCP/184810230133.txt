2364. WILLIAM HOGG was indicted for embezzlement.
MR. METOALPB conducted the Prosecution.
CHARLES TRIVETT. I am foreman to Mr. Defries; the prisoner was his
errand-boy. On 22nd Sept. he was sent with a gas-meter to Mr. Campbell, of
High-street, Shadwell-I gave him a bill of 21.4s. Id., and a return-ticket, to
be signed if the parties were not at home-it was the first errand he had been
sent on from that shop, but he had been employed at the other factory-he
did not bring back the money-he said Mr. Campbell was not at home-I
asked him for the ticket, and he said they would not sign it-that is the case
sometimes.
WILLIAM HOLMES. I am in the service of Mr. Campbell, of High-street,
Shadwell. On 22nd Sept. the prisoner brought a gas-meter-I paid him
21. 45.-- he signed this bill in my presence (produced).
Prisoner's Defence. I lost the money, and did not like to tell him; I had
saved it up all but 10s.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Four Months.
2365. GEORGE GROVES, stealing 1 dung-fork, value 28.; the goods
of James Bugbee; having been before convicted.
WILLIAM STERNEY. I am horsekeeper to James Bugbee, of Palmer's-
village, Westminster. On Saturday afternoon I saw the prisoner loading
dung at my master's the fork was missed this is it (produced), I know it
by the eye-it is James Bugbee's.
ROBERT HENRY RIGGLESFORD (policeman, T 26). I took the prisoner at
Stanwell, with this fork on his shoulder-I asked how he came by it-he
said it was his, that he had been at work for Mr. Price; then, that it be-
longed to Mr. Price; and then to Mr. Price's carter-I took him to the
carter, who denied it-I took it to Mr. Bugbee.
Prisoner's Defence. I laid it down, with three others, and did not know
which was Mr. Price's.
