   GEORGE AVERY. I am a Police-constable. I received information, and apprehended Rebecca Russell, who had the gown; I was looking for the prisoner for five weeks, and went to his lodging, but could not find him - I was in the habit of seeing him daily before.
   REBECCA RUSSELL. Mary Jones came to our house, and said she had a gown to sell, which would suit me; my mother gave her 1s. for it.
   ELIZABETH RUSSELL. I bought the gown of Mary Jones for my daughter; she sells things in the streets.
   MARY JONES. My husband travels the country - I have known the prisoner about nine years. I bought this gown of the prisoner opposite Shoreditch church, for 9d, about five weeks before he was taken up.
 Prisoner. Q. Do you recollect two or three people being round, and I told you I had just found it? A. I did not hear him say so; he asked me next day to buy a duplicate for a pair of shoes, and a gown, pawned for 5s. - he did not say who pawned them.
   WILLIAM CRAIGHILL. I am a pawnbroker, and live in the Hackney-road. I have a gown, a pair of shoes, and a piece of cotton, pawned by a woman, in the name of Ann Saunders, on the 5th of August, for 5s.
   WILLIAM BARROW. I am a Policeman. On the 5th of August, in the morning, the prosecutrix gave me information of the robbery; she described the prisoner by a mark on his left cheek, and gave a very clear account- I knew him before; I looked every where for him, and on the 9th of September I apprehended him at a publichouse in Clerkenwell - I said, "I want you, Symons;" he said, "What for?" I said, "For those two gowns, a pair of stockings, shoes, and a piece of print, from a young woman, in a coffee-shop;" he said, "Oh, I'll soon settle that" - I took him to the office; the girl identified him the moment she saw him - my brother officers had been looking for him night and day, but he changed his lodging.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. The morning I sold the gown to the witness I had occasion to go down a court, and saw that old gown on a dunghill, and inside it was the ticket of another gown; two or three people said, "It will suit Jones;" they called her, and asked what she would give for it - she gave me 8d.; I can prove I was never away from home - the witness has been and slept in the same place as I was since.
   JOHN SYMONS. I heard the Policeman was after my brother, and went to my mother's four days after the robbery; I there saw the prosecutrix, and asked her if she knew the young man who had robbed her - she said distinctly, "No, I do not;" she had met my mother, and stated to her that she was without a lodging - my mother took her in, and she stopped all night; my brother came in at ten o'clock, and he sat up in the room all night, not liking to go to bed - in the morning the prosecutrix had her breakfast at the same time with him; I told my mother it was strange she should come there, as she said he had robbed her - he did not leave home for three weeks after the robbery.
 COURT. Q. You knew the Police were after your brother? A. Yes, and so did he; I cannot account for his keeping away - I blame him very much; I do not live at home with my mother, but he slept at home - he says so, but I do not know where he was in the day time; I have not spoken to the prosecutrix but once - I heard something about a dunghill about a week after.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. - Transported for Seven Years.

 1916.  ANN FOX was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of July , 18 yards of ribbon, value 4s., the goods of George Evans.
   RICHARD HUGHES. I am in the employ of George Evans, in Tottenham-court-road. On the 5th of July the prisoner came in, and bought three-quarters of a yard of ribbon, which came to 21/4d.; while I was cutting it I saw her take a piece out of the basket, and put it under her shawl - she went out, after paying for what she bought; I told my master of it, and then went and overtook her about twenty yards off, and brought her back; she was taken up stairs - master asked if she had any property about her; she denied it - this eighteen yards of ribbon was found under her shawl.
   THOMAS SUMPTER. I took the prisoner in charge, and found thirteen duplicates for trifling things on her.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I saw it on the floor, and picked it up.
     GUILTY. Aged 34. - Confined Six Weeks.

   WILLIAM SMELLIE. I am brother-in-law of James Squire, a pork-butcher, of Tottenham-court-road.
