669. SAMUEL SUMMERS, stealing I metal tap, and 35 feet of leaden
pipe, value 78.; the goods of Robert Pool
WILLIAM SOUTH (policeman, K 233). I was on duty, in High-street,
Stratford, about 9 o'clock at night, on 29th April I saw the prisoner
coming towards Bow-when he passed me he turned a corner, and as he
turned I saw he had something under his smock frock-he went down a
street into the Marsh, and I concealed myself in a corner till he came up
again, and went towards Bow; I followed him, and took him into custody
I said, “Halloo, Sam / what have you got here "--I looked into a basket
which he had, and saw the tap and lead pipe in it-he said, "I found it.”
I locked him up, on suspicion--the pipe had the appearance of having been
cut or jammed off, and it appeared fresh done - I took the pipe to the
station, and the next morning we made inquiries.

-I had some conversation with him, and I took a piece of pipe which I

was left-it fits exactly with this other-it was broken off quite into the
ground.
