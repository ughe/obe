541. SARAH ANN FITZGERALD (31), Unlawfully attempting to
drown herself.
Mr. ELDRIDGE Prosecuted.
JOHN JACKSON. I am a carman, of 15, Haslewood Terrace, Kensing
ton-on 18th May, about 7.30, I was going over the bridge at Harrow
Road-I saw the prisoner walking across the wharf with a child in her
hands-she walked into the eanal and threw herself on her back with the
child on her breastshe was three or four yards from the bank and in
five or six feet of water saw her fetched out.
FREDERICK FARRANT. I am a waterside labourer, of 128, Kensal Road,
Paddington-on 18th May, about 7.30, I was standing at Collins' Wharf
-a tramear was passing, and the people shouted-] looked towards the
canal, and saw the prisoner floating on the water with a child on her
breast-the water is 6 ft. deep thereI jumped in and fetched them out
I rubbed the child and held it, and asked the prisoner what she had
done it for-she said she was in trouble-I took her to 8 doetor-they
were not very long in the water, but they were both under water before
I got to them.

and her baby out of the water aceompanied them to Dr. Griffiths, and
then home, and after the prisoner's clothes were changed I told her she
would be charged with attempting to commit suicide and to murder her
baby-she said, "I must have been silly : this is all through a little
trouble, a little she was charged at the station, and made no reply
-she fainted, and was assisted to a chair] should say she was sober,

ALFRED LEATH GRIFFITHs, M.D. I live at 606, Harrow Road-o
May 18th a boy about a year and eight months old was brought to me
