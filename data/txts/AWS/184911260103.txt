JOHN HARRISON. I am a carpenter, in the employment of Messrs. Ponti-
fex, at their manufactory, at Millwall, and was so in July, 1848-1 was
engaged in preparing the green-house, and afterwards in fitting it up at
Greenwich- was engaged about fourteen days in preparing it, and fourteen
or fifteen days in working on it at Greenwich-aI first received directions from
Spriggs about it-the deals and battens were then prepared-the prisoners
were in the habit of coming in to the carpenters' shop, sometimes together,
sometimes alone, while we were working-a man named Miller was also
employed at it-William Riddell did not give me any direetions-I went with
the green-house to Greenwich on the day it was taken-William Riddell was
at times present while I was fitting it up at Greenwich, and Joseph also-
William gave me no orders or directions about it while I was fitting it up
Spriggs was the foreman of the carpenters, I received my orders from him-
I never heard William Riddell give Spriggs orders, neither at Millwall or
Greenwich-Mr. Jones paid me my wages during this time have since
seen the materials that I fitted up at Greenwich-some of them are here.
Cross-examined. Q. Are you still in the employment of Messrs. Pontifex ?
A. Yes ; I did not give any information to Messrs. Pontifex while this work
was going on-I had no suspicion whatever.
