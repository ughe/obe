John HOUGHTON. I am the son of Mr. Shiell's gardener, and was
in his service the beginning of April. I slept in the room over between
the stable and harness-room-part of the room is over each-I get to it
by the stair-case outside- I do not pass through the stable--the prisoner
kept the key of the stable--it was always kept locked-I got up on
Tuesday, the 5th of April, at half-past six o'clock, and saw the prisoner
when I came down-I smelt a very unpleasant smell, and asked him if he
had been singeing-it smelt like the singeing of horses-he said he had been
in liquor overnight, and had been toasting some cheese for refreshment.
Q. Can you tell whether there was a fire in the harness-room at the
time? A. I heard the crackling as if there was a fire, but did not see
any fire-he told me at the time he bad a fire drying the harness- I have
seen a little boy with the prisoner-he told me he was his son, and that a
female had the care of him named Jane Jones, who he (the prisoner)
used to call his sister-I have seen her at Putney three times with the
prisoner-the last time I saw her there was on Easter Monday, about ten
o'clock in the morning-she was looking out at the stable gates, which go
down the lane-the prisoner did not then tell me who she was-he said
nothing about her-he had previously told me who she was I think she
wore a blue bonnet on Easter Monday.
Cross-examined. Q. Was Easter Monday the last time you saw her!
A. Yes I have seen a little boy there with a female- I have seen Susan
Butcher there-I am quite sure I have not seen any other females there-I
have been examined before-I never saw any other female at the stable with
the prisoner-I swear I have not seen Butcher or any other female there
since Easter Monday- I went to bed at this time between nine and ten,
or about ten o'clock- I went to bed on Sunday night about ten, and about
the same time on Monday- I got up about six o'clock, or a few minutes
after on Monday-the prisoner and I were on terms as fellow servants;
not good friends particularly-I was working in the kitchen garden
on Monday morning-it lies at the back of the stable-the room I slept
in was partly over the stable where the body was found the prisoner
slept at the house.
