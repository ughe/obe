had several friendly drinks together, and I was rather drunk. Pri-
soner and I went into a coffee-house, were put out, and we had a
bit of a jangle. We were both drunk. I cannot say what we were

the thigh. We were not jangling then. I was jangling with another
man. There were a lot of us together, and I got jangling with some
