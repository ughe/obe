ELIZABETH FANCHON. I am the daughter of Friend Fanchon. he keeps a public-house at Greenwich—I saw the prisoner come to the house, about a quarter past eleven o'clock on the night of the 18th—she had a pint of porter—I served her—she asked me to warm it, which I did, and she wanted me to warm it again—I said I could not, as the fire was too low—there were a great many galsses on the counter—I did not miss any till the officer brought this one—it is my father's—we only had two of this pattern—it has my father's name on it. FRIEND FANCHON. I keep the public-house. I had but two glasses of this description—this is mine, and has my name on it—I knew nothing of the prisoner before. GUILTY. Aged 33.— Confined Three Months.

 592. MARY ANN HOLLAND was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of January , 5 chairs, value 5 s  .; and 1 table. value 2 s  .; the goods of John Brewster Purkis . SOPHIA PURKIS. I am the wife of John Brewster Purkis. We live in High-street, Woolwich. On the 29th of December, we had five chairs and a table, in a house on Plumstead-common—I left that house at two o'clock that afternoon—I left the prisoner in care of it—I returned on the 18th of January—I found one door was locked, and the other two of the chairs prisoner was gone, and the chairs and table also—I found two of the chairs and the table at Mr. Martin's, and three chairs at Mrs. Davis's—these are the articles. EDWARD MARTIN. I am a broker, and live at Woolwich. I bought these chairs and table, among a number of other things, of the prisoner—she said they were her own—the whole of them came to 17 s .3 d .
