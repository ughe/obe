WILLIAM BELCHER. I am a grocer, in Holles-street, St. Marylebone.
On 15th Dec. Simpson came to my shop, about twelve o'clock, and asked for
a quarter of a pound of tea and one pound of sugar, it came to 18. 3 d. ---be
gave me a shilling and a sixpence in payment-I noticed that the shilling was
bad- I told him so-he said he was not aware of it, and he put down a good
one-1 detained the bad one-I gave him his change out of the good shil-
ling-he would have gone away, but a person came in and winked his eyes
and went out again, and I detained Simpson in conversation till the person
came back, and took him into custody -I asked him where he lived-he said
in Park-street, Dorset-square-I gave the shilling I received from Simpson
to the officer- this is it.
