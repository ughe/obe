ELIZABETH THOMAS. I am the mother of John Thomas. On Saturday
night, the 4th of February, the prisoner was sent to Mr. Sands with a
shoulder of mutton-it came to 38. 94d.--I recollect his coming back-
there was no one in the shop but me-my son was at supper in the little
room adjoining--the prisoner came into the shop, put the tray down, and
said, "Sands has not paid"--I said, “Very well, go in to your supper," and I
made the entry in the book on the following Saturday another person
was sent to Mr. Sands with a bill of the two, and brought word back
that this was paid.
