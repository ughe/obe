SaRaH PALMER, I am the prosecutrix's sister. I saw the prisoner the
evening previous to this circumstance-he called at my house to inquire
for my sister-on the day this happened he came about three o'clock, and
they went out together, as I understand, but I was not at home then-I had
left them together-I did not see him return-I knew he was visiting her,
but I knew nothing about it till the Tuesday night, after it was all done
I had heard him spoken of, but did not know he was paying his addresses
to her.
