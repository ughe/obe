 243.  WILLIAM TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of October , 7 knives, value 15s., the goods of William Day, his master.
   FREDERICK WILSON MILNER. I am a Policeman. On the 23rd of October I saw the prisoner in the Strand, showing some new knives to another boy, and having seen him before in bad company, I took him; he said his father made the knives, and sent him out to sell them - he begged hard of me to go to his father with him, to convince me that what he said was true; I went with him, and as soon as he got to the house he ran up stairs as fast as he could - I heard him say, "Mother - knives - say you gave them to me;" I went up, and he then said he found them in Covent-garden-market - I took him to the watch-house, and found these seven knives on him.
   WILLIAM DAY. I am a cutler, and live in the Strand. The prisoner has been in my service about three months - he slept in the shop, and had access to all my drawers; I had sent him on an errand about a week before he was taken, and he never returned; these are my knives - I have some others in my pocket; they correspond exactly.
 Cross-examined by MR. LEE. Q. Do you swear to them by the maker's name? A. I gave an express order to a man to make these particular knives for me, and they have the stamp on them - I swear they had not been sold.
 Prisoner's Defence. I never said such a word to my mother.
 Two witnesses gave the prisoner a good character, one of whom stated that he would take him as an apprentice.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. - Whipped and Discharged.
 244.  JOHN BOYD was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of November , 1 whip, value 1s. 6d., the goods of George Muir.
   GEORGE MUIR. I left my gig in the Edgware-road on the 12th of November - I left my whip within the apron of the gig, and the reins tied round it; I went into a shop at the corner of Store-street, and the officer produced my whip to me.
   RICHARD HANCOCK. I am a Police-constable. I saw the prisoner take the whip; I pursued, and took him with it under his right arm - I was in plain clothes.
 Prisoner. Q.Was there any person with me? A. There was another in company with you.
 Prisoner. It was the other boy took it; he gave it to me, and then ran away - I told the officer so.
   RICHARD HANCOCK. I saw the prisoner take it.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Transported for Seven Years.
 245.  WILLIAM ADAMS was indicted for stealing on the 10th of November , 1 pencil-case, value 2s, the goods of Samuel Avila.
   JAMES LOCKYER. I am shopman to Samuel Avila, a pawnbroker, of Mile-end-road. On the morning of the 10th of November I saw the prisoner at a broken pane of glass in our window; he had some kind of instrument - I saw him drawing out a pencil-case, and when he had very nearly got it out, I ran out, and pursued him; I saw about three others near him, as he was running - I took him; and as he was going to the watch-house, he threw away a pencil-case; he left this piece of whalebone, with a book to it, in the window.
   JOSEPH WILLIAMS. I was standing at my door, and saw the prisoner being taken to the watch-house; I saw him throw away this pencil-case.
   JOSEPH FRIANEL CRISTALL. I am a police-officer. I was at the station-house when the prisoner was brought in; I found on him a handkerchief and 1s.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 11. - Transported for Seven Years.
 246.  JOHN ARUNDEL was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of November , 1 cloak, value 35s, and 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of Susannah Bruce.
   JOHN ELLIS. I am a watchman on Westminster-bridge. I met the prisoner on the Middlesex end of the bridge on the 20th of November, after nine o'clock at night; I saw him go and take this bundle out of a four-wheeled chaise, it was behind the driver - he had his right hand on the spring of the chaise as it passed me, and then I saw his left hand draw the bundle out; I took him - the chaise went on, but I advertized the property, and found the owner.
   SUSANNAH BRUCE. I am a widow, and live in Alfredplace, Bedford-square; this cloak and handkerchief are mine - I was in the chaise, returning from Vauxhall, about nine o'clock in the evening; the parcel was in the back of the chaise - I did not miss it till I got home; I know no thing of the prisoner.
 Prisoner's defence. I saw the bundle in the road, and the watchman came and took me; I never had it in my hand.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. - Transported for Seven Years.
