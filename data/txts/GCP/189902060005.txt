Cross-examined. My attention was first called to the prosecutor when
he was fighting; he was not sober—the policeman was going to take the
prosecutor into custody, but his wife said, "Give him a chance," and he
said, “All right, take him away," and they got him away I have not

was convicted under that name on January 18th last year, that was for

vious convictions against me then I got twelve months then-in 1892 I

-- in October, 1892, I had two months for stealing-in February, 1893, I
was sent to a reformatory for ten days-since the last sentence of twelve
months I have had three months for being in possession of housebreaking
instruments in the name of John Ruley.

--I saw the beginning, but not the end of this row-I did not see any

Cross-examined. Shea said there was a drunken row—two young men
were fighting, and Shea said, “Let them have fair play;" and then
another lad struck him on the face and knocked him on the back of his

thirty people there-nobody robbed Shea, if they had I must have seen it
- I am an improver in the plaster trade; I live with my brother-when

public-house he was not near Shea ; he was about twenty yards away.
