DANIEL NICHOLLS. I am a butcher. On Saturday night, the 22d of September, I took same mutton-chops to the Hope and Anchor, about eight o'clock, to the best of my knowledge, I cannot be certain to a few minutes; I saw the prisoner there I am certain.
 MR. ADOLPHUS, Q. Did you enter it in your book? A. No; but on that day I had a goose sent up out of the country; we eat it on Sunday - it was not sent for Michaelmas-day, and we were remarking that we should have goose before Michaelmas-day; I had seen the prisoner before in Smithfield.
 MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did you observe his dress? A. I think it was the same as he has now, except that he had a coloured handkerchief.
 COURT, Q. You were there but a short time? A. Not a minute scarcely; Broom paid me 1s. 1d.
     NOT GUILTY.
 2216.  WILLIAM BRUNTON was indicted for a misdemeanor.
 MR. ADOLPHUS conducted the prosecution.
   RICHARD HULSE. I am in the employ of Messrs. Charles Butler and Richard Hayward, chemists, of Queen-street, Cheapside. On the 18th of July the prisoner came, and produced this note, signed "William Hopkins." I asked if he was to pay for the articles; he said No; I asked if he came from Mr. Hopkins; he said he did, and I gave him 3 lbs. of bergamot for Mr. Hopkins; he took it away. (Order read).
 July, 18, 1827.
 Please to send by bearer 3 lbs. of essence of bergamot for St. John-street,  WILLIAM HOPKINS.
 Cross-examined by MR. BARRY. Q. Did he give you the order? A. No; one of the porters brought it to me; I went to him with it.
 WILLIAM HOPKINS. I am a tallow-chandler, and live in St. John-street; the prisoner was in my service three years ago; this order was not written by me, or any body in my house; I never sent for the goods, and never had it.
 Cross-examined. Q. You have sent for such things? A. No; I have sent goods there, but never had any thing sent to me from there to my knowledge; I have two servants.
     GUILTY. Confined Nine Months.
 There was another indictment against the prisoner.
 2217.  ALEXANDER BARR was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   HENRY GRAVES. I live in Wood-street, Cheapside. On the 16th of October the prisoner came to my warehouse, and produced a pattern of sewing silk, and asked for 3 lbs. of green sewing silk for Messrs. Dickman and Co., of Newgate-street, who dealt with me; I let him have it; he had been in the habit of coming from them for goods within a very short time before - it was worth 3l. 18s.; he came again for 2 lbs. of silk to two patterns; I had not the precise pattern, and delivered him 3 lbs. 9 ozs. of silk.
 MR.  CORNELIUS JORDAIN. I am in partnership with Mr. John Dickman; the prisoner was in our employ till the 14th of October; we used to deal with Mr. Graves; we never authorised him to go on the 16th or 24th of October for any goods.
   HENRY HONEY. I am an officer, and apprehended the prisoner.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Confined Nine Months.
 2218.  HENRY HIND, JOHN LOCK, and JOHN WATSON were indicted for stealing, on the 1st of October , 1 set of chaise harness, value 3l.; 1 saddle, value 15s., and 1 bridle, value 5s., the goods of William Lack. The prosecutor did not appear. NOT GUILTY.
 2219.  WILLIAM REYNOLDS was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of October , two bells, value 16s., the goods of Benjamin Gould.
 BENJAMIN GOULD. I am a  scavenger. I use these bells when I go out with a cart. I saw them safe in a basket, in my stable, in Worship-street, on Saturday night, the 20th of October, and lost them next morning.
   ROBERT CHRISTIE. I work for Gould; last Saturday week, between two and three o'clock, I put the bells into a basket, in the stable - the door was not locked, and on Monday they were gone; a man could get in at the wicket, which was not fastened.
   JOHN HANDS. I live with Mr. Gould. I saw the bells put away, and missed them on Saturday morning. I do not know the prisoner.
   JOSEPH TAYLOR. I am an officer. I apprehended the prisoner at seven o'clock on Sunday morning, the 21st of October, in Petticoat-lane, on the City side, endeavouring to sell these bells,(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 36. Transported for Seven Years.

 2221.  JOSEPH GARDNER was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   THOMAS SMITH. I am servant to Henry and  Henry Charles Lee, who are butchers, of Leadenhall-market. On the 24th of September the prisoner came to me; I knew him to have lived with Messrs. Angels; he said, "Thomas, I want 10lbs. or 12lbs. of brisket of beef for stewing;" he said he came from Messrs. Angels and wanted it for them; thinking he still lived with them, I gave it to him.
   WILLIAM HENRY ANGEL. I carry on business with my father, William Sandal Angel, at Cornhill - the prisoner was in our employ about three years ago: we never sent him for this meat.
