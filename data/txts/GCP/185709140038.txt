930. MARY ANN PARKER (19) was indicted for a like offence,
MESSRS. Ellis and Payne conducted the Prosecution,
Johx SULLING REDGELL. I am & corn chandler, and live at Stepney.
On 5th Aug. the prisoner came for half a peck of oats--they came to 6d.;
she gave me a half crown-I put it into the detector, told her it was bad,
and asked her whether she wanted the oats for herself; she said, “No, a
man in the road gave the money to me"-I said I would go and see if it
was a man who had given me a 58. piece in the afternoon, as the descrip-
tion she gave me of the man very much resembled him-I went out, but
the man was gone I gave the prisoner into enstody, with the half
crown-the prisoner was taken to the police court, and discharged the
next day.
Prisoner. Q. Why did you not take that man, as you took met
A. He said that he had only 2d., and his brother was outside, and he would
go and find him, but he never returned.
• WILLIAM CREIGHTON (Policeman, K 63). I took the prisoner on 5th

ANN HERMAN. I am the niece of Mr. Giles. On 28th Aug. the prisoner
came to his shop for two penny buns, and gave me a shilling—I put it into the
till; there were several other shillings there she came back again in about
ten minutes, and in the mean time the till had been examined by my uncle
she then asked for 2d. worth of pastry, and she gave me a bad shilling
I gave it to my aunt, and told her it was bad- the prisoner said she did
not know it was bad, and she had got it from a gentleman in the Com-
mercial Road-I bent it this is it.
Maria GILES. I am the aunt of the last witness. I got this shilling
from her, and gave it to my husband.
GEORGE GILES. I am a baker. I got this bad shilling from my wife
before that, I had looked into my till, and saw a bad shilling amongst
some good ones I gave that shilling, and the one my wife gave me, to
the policeman.
JAMES ALLISON (Policeman, H 128). I received these two shillings from
Mr. Giles—I took the prisoner.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. This half crown is bad- these two shillings are
bad, and from the same mould
Prisoner. I was not in the shop till I went for the pastry.
