767. JOHN CANNON was indicted for embezzlement.
Messrs. CLARKSON and BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES Parr. I am porter at the Eastern Counties Railway-station, at
Shoreditch. There is a class of fares called "excess fares "-it was my duty
to take them on the 28th of Dec.-on the arrival of the luggage train from Col-
chester, I received for three persons and a child 14s. for excess fares-I
paid it over to the prisoner immediately, and produced one of the party who
had come up as acting-guard to the prisoner at the time-I received the 4o.
from that person-I told the prisoner the partieulars of these receipts.
Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. That 48. was paid in the
office? 4. Yes, I took the person into the superintendent's and inspector's
office, who was acting-guard to Salter's wagon I paid the prisoner 10s. first,
and then I received the 4s. from that other man in the office, which I handed
over to the prisoner-I had received the 108. on the line, but the 48. was paid
in the office in the prisoner's presence, and by me to bim-I have been about
six months in the company's service-I had been in the police before, and had
been in the army.
