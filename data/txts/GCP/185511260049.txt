TITUS FARMER. I live at No. 12, James-street, Haggerstone; I am
plasterer. I know the prisoner-I know where the prosecutor's house is
I recollect the night that somebody got into his house on that night I
went to the Black Bull, a few minutes before the half hour past 9 o'clock-
there is a clock in the public house, you can see it on entering when
I went in I saw the prisoner there, and the last witness I drank with
them--they were drinking together-I remained in the house about five
minutes I left them there together.
