John WHITTER SMITH. I was formerly manager of the Westminster
branch of the Imperial Bank-about the end of November last year the
prisoner called on me; at that time he had opened an account, and we
had received references to Mr. Davis and Mr. MoNalty-he said he had
a New Zealand bond for 1,0001., and that a friend of his, Mr. Davis, had
lent him 5001. on it; that he wished to sell that bond, and would be glad
if I would make a fresh advance on it; it was arranged that 8001. should
be advauced on the bond-I knew the prisoner as Walter Tait, 9, Victoria
Chambers, Westminster--the 1,0001. bond was afterwards brought to
the cashier of the bank; the 5001. was first paid to Dr. Davis, this
(produced) is the prisoner's pass-book-his account was opened on 27th
November with a deposit in cash of 501., and on the 2nd Dec. there appears
loan 8001., and on the other side Davis 5001, afterwards the prisoner
deposited at the bank two 2001. bonds, on 10th January, for a loan of 3001.,
and we advanced a further 1001. On the 13th-I remember receiving a
letter from the prisoner, but I do not recollect the date; it was lent to
the Treasury at the time of the prosecution, and we have not seen it
since; we were requested in that letter not to colleet the coupons as he
was going to redeem the bonds afterwards; it was signed in the usual
way, 4 Walter Tait towards the middle or end of January we wrote
