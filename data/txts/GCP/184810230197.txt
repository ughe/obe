station-the deceased's mother was ill in bed at the time-Solita's father is a
tailor, or draper-I suppose he lived with his father-he had nothing to do
with our boy- I swear that he had not been an ailing boy long before the
9th Sept.- he did not wear a handkerchief round bis head till after he
received the blow; never before that-I have brought him up-I hear that
a man has come to swear that he used to wear a handkerchief, and I have a
witness to prove he did not he never cried and complained of his head
before-- he has said he has had the ear-ache, but never of any consequence
he never cried about it-he has complained but very seldom, when he has
had a cold, as many children will-I have noticed a discharge of thick matter
from his ear sometimes, but very trifling--I have not done anything for it,
but left it to cure itself.
Mr. Platz. Q. Are you a mother? A. No, but I have always been
with children-it is not uncommon for children, when they have a cold, to
have a discharge at the ear-a healthy child will often have it.
