-I said he was very well, and did not want medicine-she said the doctor

Court. Q. What was the doctor to call on you on Monday for? A. To see
the baby, about five o'clock, and I waited till the doctor called before I would
give the child any thing-I was not waiting for the doctor's directions
particularly-I meant to ask him how it was to be taken if he came-I
am not sure she did not tell me to ask for the doctor's directions-she said
the doctor would most likely come to see the baby at five o'clock.
