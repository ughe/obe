2413. ISIDORE THEODORE KRAKAUER was again  indicted for stealing 1 coat, 1 pair of trousers, and one waistcoat, value 6 l  . 6 s  .; the goods of Robert Caspar .
   MR. PARNELL conducted the Prosecution . ROBERT CASPAR. I know the prisoner—I recollect his coming to my house in the beginning of Sept., and ordering a suit of clothes—he said he was going to get married, and they were to be sent home between nine and ten o'clock on Saturday morning—the money was to be paid on delivery—he told me he lived in Queen-square, Eldon-street, Finsbury—he did not give me the number—he told me he had got the house on a lease—he came to me on the Friday afternoon to make trial of his clothes; they fitted him very well, and he told me to bring them home on Saturday morning, between nine and ten o'clock—I went home with them—he came to me in the parlour, and tried them on—he said, "How much do they come to?"—I made a memo randum of them altogether, 5 l . 16 s .—he took the piece of paper, and said, "I suppose you want your money?"—I said, "Yes, I made an agreement for payment"—he said, "I have no English money; I have got a French note"—he showed it me—he said it was for 500 francs—I did not see that—he took it from a drawer—I refused the note, and said, I wanted English money—he said, "If you want your money so very particularly, come with me"—I went with him to King William-street—he said he was going to change the note, and give me the money for the clothes—he told me to want at the top of King William-street, and he would go into a house in Lombard-street—he pretended to go into a house, but he did not—I waited for him two hours—I never saw him again till he was in custody.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  What was the date of this transaction? A.  The 9th Sept., not 7th—I gave him a memorandum; I think I dated it—I believe I dated it the day I gave it, but I do not know exactly what day it was—I did date it—this is what I wrote ( looking at a paper )—I have made a mistake in the date—I swear now it was on the 9th—this 2 s . 6 d . at the bottom is not my handwriting—I do not know what 1: can mean—I never saw this 2 s . 6 d . before—I made for ready-money—I do not take discount off—I was to receive the whole of this—I wrote this at his house—I thought the prisoner would be an honest man, and give me the money directly—I put "Mr. Krakauer"—that is his name—I did not tale the coat back—it was not too large in the back—he came to try it on—I went in the cart with Marks and Sherwinski, from Houndsditch to Cler kenwell.
   COURT . Q.  Did you go to any office, or make any complaint? A.  I went to Mr. Barnett; the officer, Webb, was sent for—I do not know who sent for him—I saw him in the street—I said a man had robbed me of my clothes. ROBINSON WEBB( City-policcman . 658). On 10th Sept., the prosecutor asked me if I knew Krakauer's brother—I said yes, I knew them both, I had frequently seen them together—he told me if I saw him, to take him in custody—he said he had got a suit of clothes from him, and he was not able to find him—the prosecutor met me in the street—I do not not know that he was coming to me—I have known the prosecutor seven years; he is a very respectable man—I know Sherwinski and Cohen.
   JURY to ROBERY CASPAR . Q.  You deny this 2 s . 6 d . being your writing? A.  Yes, I do—I went to his house frequently, but he was denied—I wanted to give him in charge—I had not a policeman with me—I went for the money.
