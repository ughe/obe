129. DAVID HUMPHREYS was indicted for stealing, on the 19th
of November, 1 handkerchief, value 18., the goods of Charles Colyer, from
his person.
CHARLES COLYER. I live in Tavistock-street, Bedford-square, and am
an articled clerk. About twelve o'clock on Saturday, the 19th of Novem-
ber, I was in Great Russell-street-I felt a pull at my pocket-I felt for
my handkerchief, and it was gone-1 turned round, and saw the prisoner
and another boy behind me-the prisoner was directly behind me-not
more than two yards, I should think -I then saw the corner of my hand-
kerchief projecting from beneath his jacket-I charged him with taking it-
he said he had not-I took hold of the corner of it, and pulled it out-
found it was mine-- this is it-I kept him till the other boy went away,
Robert DUCKETT (police-constable E 96.) I was called, took the
prisoner, and have the handkerchief.
Prisoner. I saw two boys walking before me-they took the handker-
chief and threw it down-I put it into my coat, and they went round the
corner.
CHARLES COLYER. He said nothing about the two boys then.
GUILTY. Aged 13.-Confined Three Months; Three Weeks Solitary.
