Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
1539. CORNELIUS MURPHY was indicted for stealing, on the
22nd of April, 3 sovereigns, and 4 half-sovereigns the monies of Thomas
Jackson, his master.
JAMEs STARKBY. I live at Vincent-street, and am clerk to Mr. Thomas
Jackson. The prisoner was in his employ as a carpenter's laboureron
the 22nd of April I gave him a note sealed up with an order to Mr. Jones
to send me 51. back by him for Mr. Jackson-h did not return-] saw no
more of him till he was taken.
JOHN JoNes. I am clerk to Mr. Jackson. The prisoner brought me
the note from Mr. Starkey-Ipr up 51. in a piece of writing-paper, sealed
it up, and directed it to Mr. Starkey- told the prisoner to make haste
back with it.
Prisoner. I would have settled it with my master if he would have
taken ten shillings a-week-I was tipsy, and whether I had it stolen or
lost it I am not able to say.
WILLIAM UNDERHILL. I keep the Crown and Thistle, in Great Peter-
street. On the 22nd of April, between five and six o'clock in the evening,
the prisoner came and had change of half-a-sovereign of me.
JOHN CAMPBELL. About seven 'clock that evening I was at the Dog
and Stile beer-shopthe prisoner was there-he called for a pot of ale,
and paid for it with a sovereign.
RICHARD SADD (police-constable B 80). I took the prisonerhe said he
had the money stolen from him, and had his pocket cut,
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 40.-Confined Nine Months.
1540. GEORGE PUCILL was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of
April, 15 savoloys, value 1s. 3d. and 4lb. tripe, value 2d., the goods of
George Anderson, his master.
GEoRGe ANDERSON. I am a pork-butcher, and live in Whitecbapel-
road. The prisoner was in my employ a short time-I discharged him
because I wanted a larger boy- came again to assist me on Saturday the
13th of April, and as I was about discharging him he stooped to pick up
something, and I saw some savoloys projecting from a hole in bis smock-
frock--1 then found fifteen more round his body and over his arms-I am
confident they were mine-I gave him in charge-the officer found the
tripe in his bosom.
THOMAS ARNOLD (police-constable H 127.) I took the prisoner, and
found the tripe and savoloys-b said he was going to take them home for
his supper.
(The prisoner received a good character, and the prosecutor promised
still to employ him.)
GUILTY. Aged -Confined Five Days, and Whipped.
