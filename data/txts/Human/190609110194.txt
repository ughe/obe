I have not seen the letter dated March 17, purporting to be written to Dr. Sumraerskill by prisoner. Prisoner called on me some time in April, and wanted me to visit and examine Miss Jones. I asked him what was the nature of the illness, and he told me that an illegal operation had been performed. I advised him, "If what you say is true you had better consult the divisional police surgeon." I did not make any further remarks. It is not true that I stated that "any respectable doctor would inform the police of the case, as it was a very serious matter." I only advised him to see Dr. Wright.
 Cross-examined, By going to a divisional-surgeon I meant putting it into the hands of the police.
 Miss  MAUD HILLS , 43, Kenneth Road, Chadwell Heath. I knew prisoner about five years ago, and Alice Jones. I have only seen one bed, that is in the room I slept in. I slept there with Alice Jones. I noticed her night linen at the fime I used to go and see her.
