 210.  THOMAS WILLICOMBE was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of November , 6 loaves of bread, value 2s. 4d., and 1 piece of green baize, value 1s. 2d., the goods of Abraham Phillips Bromwich , his master.
   ABRAHAM PHILLIPS BROMWICH. I was a baker. On the 14th of November I lived in Tottenham-court-road, the prisoner had been in my employ about six months - he used to go out with bread in the day time; I cannot swear that I missed any loaves - the prisoner worked in the night time.
   EDMUND DAVIS. I am a Policeman. On the 14th of November, about half-past two o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner going up Tottenham-court-road, about two hundred yards from Mr. Bromwich's, with something under his arm - I crossed over to him, stopped him, and asked what he had got, and where he got it from; he said he got it from Mr. Bromwich's, and he was going to take it to a biscuit-baker - I asked where the biscuit-baker lived - he pointed to a mews; I asked him to show me where it was; I accompanied him there, and found a biscuit-baker there named Richard Shackelford- I took him back to his master's; he had these six loaves in a green baize - he offered me a crown to let him go; I showed his master the bread and cloth, he said it was his property - it was not new bread; he said he could swear to the green baize.
   ABRAHAM PHILLIPS BROMWICH. Shackelford had been a servant of mine - I never authorized the prisoner to take this bread to him; I have not a doubt that the bread is mine, and the green baize I can swear to - it had been a table-cloth, and was used for a roll-cloth; the prisoner lived in my house, but Shackelford lived at home.
   JAMES COLLINS. I am a Policeman. I was with Davis; his account is correct - I did not hear the prisoner offer a crown; I found two keys on the prisoner, and on searching his waistcoat-pocket found a key which belonged to the prosecutor's till.
 ABRAHAM PHILLIPS BROMWICH. This is the key of my till, which I lost a fortnight before - the other key belongs to a closet in his room, which he had the use of.
 GUILTY. Aged 18. Recommended to Mercy- Confined Three Months.
 211.  JAMES WALKER was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of November , 1 wheelbarrow, value 8s., the goods of William Burton.
   WILLIAM BURTON. On the 26th of November I left my barrow safe on the area-railing, in front of my door, about six o'clock in the evening - I went out; I returned at ten o'clock, and it was gone; I saw it about half-past ten o'clock that night at Mr. Brightman's, China-street, St. Pancras, and was quite positive of it.
   BENJAMIN BEIGHTMAN. I keep a coffee-shop in China-street, St. Pancras. About a quarter-past eight o'clock the prisoner came to my shop - I knew him by sight before - he pleaded distress, saying he had come from Highgate with a barrow, and had brought a man with him to wheel it for him, with a box, and he asked me to lend him a few shillings - I said I could not afford it, but he pleaded distress, and a man was with him, who he said he had got to wheel it, as he was a cripple, and he wanted to pay him; I at last lent him 2s. on the barrow, and he promised to pay me next day at twelve o'clock - I saw Mr. Burton about ten, and he claimed it.
   JOSEPH WOOD. I do porter's work. I was in the prosecutor's house - the prisoner came there about half-half-past six o'clock in the evening - the house was in Little Drummond-street, Somers'-town; he asked the price of 1 cwt. of potatoes - he came in and said he could get them in Chapel-street for 3s. 6d; the prosecutrix said her husband was out, and he had better come in the morning - he said, "Will you come and have a glass of gin?" but I refused, and about ten minutes after he was gone the barrow was missing; I afterwards saw it at the office, and knew it.
   EDMUND CANDY. I am a Policeman. I had information of this, and took the prisoner in Regent's-park; he said he met a man at Battle-bridge, with the barrow, who asked him if he knew where he could sell it.
 Prisoner's Defence. A person asked if I knew who would buy it - I said No; I was in liquor - I went with him, and offered it for sale.
   BENJAMIN BRIGHTMAN. He appeared to have had beer, but was not intoxicated.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. - Confined Fourteen Days.
