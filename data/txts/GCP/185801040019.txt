he was asked if he could dispose of the goods; he said that he could, and
took them away, not knowing how they were come by; the proceeded to the
Dover Road, where he could not get sufficient, and a message was sent to me
to that effect, and, through the messenger, I sent him a message not to dis-
pose of them; I then accompanied him to the Minories; I do not know the
