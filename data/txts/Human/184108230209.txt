   Cross-examined. Q.  How long had he been in your service? A.  Six or seven months—in that time I pose he bought six or eight gallons of oil for me—I did not see how much oil I had in my possession at this time—I cannot tell whether he had bought any within ten days or not—he gave me that account immediately on my speaking to him—according to the con sumption of oil in my house, it is not likely it should have been bought and used—I should say there ought not to have been any wanted—The prisoner said, "If you measure the oil in the house, yon will find there was the gallon I have just now bought, and about half a gallon, which was in the can before I went for it"—I did not measure—I had no particular reason for not doing so.
   Q.  Supposing you had measured, and found the gallon newly brought, and the half-gallon also remaining, would that have satisfied you that what he said was correct? A.  It would not certainly, because there would have been no reason for his getting the oil if there was some in the house—half a gallon will last us for some time—he never paid for oil out of his own pocket—I should say the two gallons we had before ought to have lasted—I do not, of my own knowledge, know when those two gallons were purchased, only from the books. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Three Months.
