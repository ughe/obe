MARY ANN MARTIN. I am an unfortunate girl, and live in Rupert-street.
On Friday night, the 17th of July, about a quarter to ten o'clock, I was at the
Garrick tavern, and saw the three prisoners and another man drinking--they
all four crossed the road, and went into a public-house opposite-I went
there and saw them I heard Webb say to Gill, "Will you bave any more drink"
she said, "No"-I heard her say, "Where shall I meet you"-I did not hear
the answer-I saw Gill and Jones together again about a quarter to two o'clock
that night, going up Christopher-court, Rupert-street, near Mrs. Barkley's house
-Jones had something square carrying by the side of him, wrapped up in
something dark, with one corner hanging down-they were going out of Ru:
pert-street, going in a direction from Mrs. Barkley's house, and not above three
minutes' walk from it—they were both together-I was standing at Mrs.
Smith's door, No. 1, Christopher-court-there was a gas-light opposite-Mrs.
Smith goes by the name of Bowner-I have no doubt of the prisoners Jones
and Gill being the parties-Jones had a black band round his hat, very
broad, and I knew Gill before.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'Brien. Q. Had you seen Jones before that
evening? A. Yes--they went right before me up the court, as I stood at
the door-I am living with Mrs. Barkley now I was not then-after I was
examined I went to live there, and am there still.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Where is the Garrick tavern. 4. In
Leman-street, Whitechapel-Cox's is opposite when Jones and Gill went
away and said "Good night," Webb and the other man remained at the
public-house-I had seen Jones three weeks before, coming out of the Low-
don docks-he told me he was a foreman in the docks-he did not speak to
me when I saw him in the public-house.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'BRIEN, Q. Were you ever taken up on any
charge ? A. No.
MR. BALDWIN. Q. Is it unusual for a girl in your situation to live in one
house more than another? A. No, I do not often move about, but my
landlady was in the hospital, and I staid there till she was better.
SUSANNAH WOODWARD re-examined. I did not notice Jones' hat that night.
