JOR WILLIAMSON. I am in the service of Mr. John Hackman Brown, a
linen draper, in High-street, Aldgate. On the 25th of Aug., about half-past
Javen o'clock, I was in the cellar, and saw the prisoner and two other women
come over the grating-they pulled a piece of cotton about which was on a
chair, and the prisoner drew it under her shawl-I ran up and took her-she
dropped the cotton--this now produced is it-I gave the prisoner to a police-
man-I had not lost sight of her.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Did you not lose sight of her while
you were coming up out of the cellar? A. Yes, I did then-this printed
cotton was on a chair over the grating-the chair did not cover the whole
grating-when I came up, the prisoner was about a dozen doors off-she
dropped the cotton about the same place-I stooped and picked it up, and
threw it into the shop.
WILLIAM Drew. I am a draper in Old Change-I was passing, and saw
the prisoner drop the print.
Cross-examined. Q. You did not know the print? A. No-the witness
put his hand on the prisoner's shoulder-she dropped it, and he took it up.
GEORGE NORDON. I am a tobacconist-I saw the prisoner take the print,
and put it partly under her shawl.
Cross-examined. Q. Did she take it up and put it down, and then take
it up again! A. Yes; and then the witness took her.

2146. ROBERT ROBINSON was indicted for embezzlement.
ROBERT REID. I am a baker, at No. 23, Berners-street, Russell-square
the prisoner was my servant-if he received money he was to pay it to me
u soon as he returned from the delivery of the bread--if he received these
sums of money he has not paid them to me-I asked him about them--he
said he had received them.
Cross-examined by Mr. ROBINSON. Q. He denied at first that he had
received them? A. No-he acknowledged at once to these and a great
many more sums-he might receive 21. or 31. in a week.
MARY DE LA CROSBET. I have three receipts for 28. 3fd., 28. 3d., and
21. 8d. -I paid those sums to the prisoner for his master.
Guilty. Aged 20.-Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor and Jury.
Confined Three Months.

want to search me you must call a policeman, and take me to the station,
and search me"-I did so, and found twenty-four cheroots in his two trowsers'
pockets-I can swear they are my property.
