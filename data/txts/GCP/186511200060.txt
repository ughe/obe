RICHARD GREEN. I am a grocer, at 254, High-street, Poplar-on the evet-
ing of 25th October, about half-pust ten clock, I was in Cheapside-I
saw the prisoner and another female--they asked me to stand something to
drink - I said, "No"--I was not talking to them two minutes--they then
left me in haste and went away down Gutter-lane-I theu missed my watch,
I was wearing it in my waistcoat pocket with a guard-I had my coat but-
toned—I followed the prisoner and a policeman followed me--I missed my
watch the soment they left me I followed them down Gutter lane, along
Carey-lane, to the corner, and when I came up to the prisoner I saw her
throw the watch down-I picked it up and gave it to the officer—the pri-
soner said, I was not to give her in charge, she never took the watch-I
gave her into custody.

Cross-examined. Q. How long were you talking to the two women alto.
gether A. Not two minutes, or about two minutes, there was no other

he was, perhaps, a yard from the woman when the watch was thrown down
-the women walked up to me and stopped me at the corner of the street,
