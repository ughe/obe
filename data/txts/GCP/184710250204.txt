2501. JAMES LEE was indicted for stealing 45lb. weight of lead pipe.
value 11s. ; 1 basket, 10d.; the goods of George Robinson, bis master.
GEORGE DAY. I am foreman to Mr. George Robinson--the prisoner was in
his employ. I was at work on the 4th of Oct. at Richmond, and about four
o'clock in the afternoon I saw the prisoner coming out of a shed where there
was lead kept he was bringing part of a coil of lead-he placed it at the
end of the shed, near a wall, where there was a path on the other side-he
went back to the shed-he had no business whatever with the lead-it was
my master's - he moved it about twenty yards.
THOMAS PRIES. I went into the shed on that day and saw the prisoner
bending a piece of lead backwards and forwards- I told the foreman and went
back to my work-in half an hour afterwards I saw the prisoner take a roll
of lead and put it behind a place on my master's premises -I am certain it is
my master's lead.
GEORGE Woods (policeman, V 209.) I took the prisoner and have the
lead-I found this basket at the prisoner's lodging.
GEORGE DAY re-examined. This basket is my master's property. W
Guilty. Aged 20.-Confined Three Months.
2502. ALEXANDER STORAR was indicted for stealing 1 barometer,
value 108., the property of Robert Bastow and others.
BENJAMIN WILLMOT WHEATLAND. I am an officer of the Southwark
Literary Institution. On the 18th of Sept. I missed a barometer-this is it
--it is the property of Robert Bastow and others.
JAMES WALTER. I am a pawnbroker-I have a barometer 1 took in-I
cannot say of whom--this is the duplicate I gave of it.
RICHARD MERRONY ( policeman, Ř 102.) I took the prisoner-I found this
duplicate on him of the barometer pawned on the 18th of Sept.
Prisoner's Defence. A man named Hardy owed me some money; I asked
him several times for it, and he told me to call on Saturday afternoon, and
he would give me part and security for the rest; I called, he gave me a
shilling, and said he had a barometer out of repair, I might pawn it; I did
80 : I sent my wife to the man afterwards, and he was gone away- I told the
officer where I lived and where I worked.
RICHARD MERRONY re-examined. Yes, he did I went to his masters, who
said he knew nothing dishonest of him.
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.
(There were two other indictments against the prisoner.)
2503. SARAH GROOM was indicted for stealing 1 shawl, value 23., the
property of Phæbe Pearce; having been before convicted of felony.
Phase PEARCE. I am single, and live at Mortlake. On the 18th of Oct.
I saw the prisoner at Richmond with my shawl on-I bad seen it safe at
home at four o'clock that afternoon-I went home, it was gone from the table
where I had left it-1 went back after the prisoner and gave her in charge-
the policeman took the shawl from her-this is my shawl.
Sophia Pearce. I know this shawl-it belongs to Phæbe Pearce.
JAMES STRUDWICK (policeman, V 205.) I found the prisoner lying in
privy with this shawl.
John Cannon (police-sergeant, V 6.) I produce a certificate of the pri-
soner's former conviction-read-Convicted Sept., 1845, and confined four-
teen days solitary)-the prisoner is the person.
Guilty. Aged 20.-Confined One Year.
