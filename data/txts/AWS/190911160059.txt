Cross-examined. In 1892 I was separated. I think the divorce
was in 1897. Miss Donisthorpe was the co-respondent. Under the
deed of separation he was to allow me 10s. a week. He did not
keep up the payments and I made him bankrupt.
Mr. Leycester submitted that there was not sufficient technical
evidence of the first marriage for the case to be allowed to go to the
jury.
Judge Lumley Smith held that, the certificate being shown to the
prisoner in the presence of the first and second wives and they both
saying That is all right," it meant that they were the people who
went through the ceremony, and he could not withdraw the case
from the jury.
Verdict, Guilty.
Prisoner was then charged with feloniously marrying Eliza Jane
Donisthorpe, his wife being then alive.
