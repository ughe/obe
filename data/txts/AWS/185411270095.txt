spoons, 1 tankard, and other articles, value 2400 the property of Sir
Hyde Parker, Bart. g and 1L salver, 4 pairs of sugar tongs, 2 sugar ladles,

LOUIsa ANN EDEN. I am the wife of General Eden. Last summer we
were oceupying the house of my uncle, Sir Hyde Parker, in Onslow-square
-we had a butler, named Baker-iwe left London on 12th Aug., but hear
ing of this robbery on the 19th returned, and found that all our own plate,
and Sir Hyde Parker's, which: had been left in the house, was gone, and
Baker, the butler, had absconded-the value of the plate was between 6001
and 700l., some of it was old family plate there were silver dishes, and a
tankard silver gilt, studded with coins, with a figure on the top, which had
been a present from Queen Anne.
