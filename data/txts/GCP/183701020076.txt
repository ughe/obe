391. JAMES CASTLE was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of
December, 1 mortar, value 2s.6d., the goods of Thomas Trevellian, and
that he had been before convicted of felony.
THOMAS TREVELLIAN. I am a furniture-broker, and live in Old-street.
On Monday, the 12th of December, this brass mortar laid in my shop,
about eighteen inches from the street-between eleven and twelve o'clock
in the morning I was called out of the shop by a man, and saw the mortar
in the prisoner's apron-the man said, “I have been watching this boy,
and saw him take this mortar from your shop."
BENJAMIN HAWKINS. I am a policeman. The prisoner and mortar ve
delivered to me by the prosecutor.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
Prisoner's Defence. It was outside the door-I took it up and put it
into my apron.
Mr. TREVELLIAN. I am quite positive it was inside the door.
WILLIAM ARNOLD. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former 600-
viction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office (read)-I was present at the
trial, and am certain the prisoner is the boy who was convicted.
GUILTY. Aged 14.- Transported for Fourteen Years.
