ALBERT HENRY DAVIS. I am a cashier at the London and South-
Western Bank at Wandsworth on 16th February, 1878, the prisoner
opened an account there in the name of Charles Leake; it continued till 30th
September, 1879, and was then closed by a charge of 9d.- in the ordinary
course he would have a cheque-book-these two cheques were presented
to us in January and July, and were returned.

gether 238l., which was all drawn out-we never allowed him to over-
draw; he then carried on business at Wandsworth, and afterwards at
Whetstone as a chemist-we had a satisfactory reference.
