GEoRGe PAXTON, 195, Great Portland Street, W. My premises
were left safe on May 2. When I arrived next morning I was informed
of the burglary by the caretaker. I afterwards saw the inspector and
caretaker examining the back. When I went in I found the place
very much upset, our work strewn all over the place. Entrance had
been effected through a fanlight. The burglars had broken open a
drawer, taking E8 or E9 in sovereigns, and cleared a drawer of stamps.
I identify Exhibits 1 to 6 as my property. The penknife belongs to
one of our girls.
