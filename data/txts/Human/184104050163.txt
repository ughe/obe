NEW COURT .— Monday, April  12 th,  1841.
   Sixth Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1200. GEORGE POND and ALEXANDER AGAR were indicted for stealing, on the   1st of April , 7 locks, value 4 s  .; and 7 keys, value 2 s  .; the goods of James Boobbyer and another; to which
   POND  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 13.
   AGAR  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 12. Confined Six weeks.
 1201. FREDERICK JAMES was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of April , 3 shillings, the monies of James Leach ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 11.— Confined Six Weeks.
 1202. EDWARD GIBBS was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 3 s  ., the goods of Frederick William Thorn , from his person; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Three Months.
 1203. JAMES SCOTT was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of March , 9 jacks, value 18 s  .; 2 lamp irons, value 10 s  .; 1 branding iron, value 1 s  .; 1 door-spring, value 6 d  .; the goods of James Cook , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Six Months.
 1204. JAMES BLANNING was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of April , 13oz. weight of brass wire, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Thomas Giles , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined One Month.
 1205. WILLIAM CLARIDGE was indicted for stealing, on the   28th of March , 7lbs. weight of mutton, value 5 s  .; 1 pie, value 2 s  .; and 2 dishes, value 1 s  .; the goods of Mary Jane Cole ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 41.— Confined Eight Days.
 1206. JAMES BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the   31st of March , 53lbs. weight of hay, value 3 s  ., the goods of William Culver , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Eight Days.
 1207. JAMES CLARK was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of March , 2 table-covers, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; 1 table-cloth, value 6 d  .; 4 trays, value 1 s  .; 10 counters, value 10 d  .; and 1 yard of dimity, value 2 d  .; the goods of Cuthbert George Brown ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 64.— Confined Three Months.
 1208. JOHN PRESLAND , THOMAS STONE, and ELIZA SMITH, were indicted for stealing, on the   28th of March , 4 tame rabbits, value 4 s  ., the goods of William Knight .
   JOHN PARKER ( police-constable K  320.)
