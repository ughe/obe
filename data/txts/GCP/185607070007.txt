George BRANSOME I am hall porter at the Great Northern HotelutI

when I first saw him he was in the entrance to the hall; he asked me to
brush him, I think he had no boots on when he first came down, and he
told the chambermaid that he wanted them he appeared in a hurry, and
went away in a cab, which was fetched for him-- he would not want a cab
