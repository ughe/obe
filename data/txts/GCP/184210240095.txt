2856. ROBERT SINCFIELD was indicted for stealing, on the 27th
of September, 10 coats, value 5l., the goods of Hannah Godby; and that
he had been before convicted of felony.
CORNELIUS RYAN. I am shopman to Hannah Godby, in Brooke-
street, Holborn. About half-past seven o'clock, on the 27th of Septem-
ber, I saw the prisoner running away with another person one of them
dropped ten coats--I never lost sight of the prisoner till I took him—the
coats had been on the counter, within three yards of the shop door.
Prisoner. Q. Did you see me with them? 4. I saw you running
after they were dropped- I cannot say which of you dropped them you
ran different ways.
WILLIAM Luck. I was sitting in the parlour, between seven and eight
o'clock, and I saw a person go out-I followed-I saw two boys, one of
whom was the prisoner-when I got to them, one of them chucked down a
lot of coats, 1 do not know which it was.
Prisoner. Q. How can you swear to me? 4. By your person and
your coat.
John BARNES (City police-constable, No. 273.) I heard a cry of
"Stop thief”-I stopped the prisoner, who was running-I got these coats
off the counter in the shop.
Prisoner's Defence. I was playing down Brooke's-market; I saw a
mob at the door, came up, and ran into the policeman's arms; the man
called out, "Take him."
THOMAS BRADLEY (City police-constable, No. 269.) I produce a certi-
ficate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's
office-(read)-the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY.* Aged 16.-Transported for Seven Years-Convict Ship.
