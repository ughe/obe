 1678. GEORGE RIXON was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of April , 2 sovereigns, the monies of Thomas Whitcomb Crispin ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined One Month .
   Before Mr. Recorder.
 1679. JOHN GLENNING was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of April , 1 paper-bag, value 1/2 d  ., and 1lb. weight of gingerbread, 1 s  ., of a man whose name is unknown, from his person. ROBERT BURNETT( police-constable C  120.) I was on duty at Greenwich fair, on the 18th of April—I saw the prisoner in company with some other boys—I saw him follow a gentleman, and take out of his pocket a bag of gingerbread nuts—I took him directly—the gentleman came part of the way to the station, but he had a ticket to go by the steam-boat—it was just getting dusk, and when I got near the station, he was gone—I had not asked his name, as I was not aware he was going—this is the bag of nuts—I asked the gentleman if he had lost anything of value, and he turned round, and said this was his property. GUILTY. Aged 13.— Confined Three Weeks .
 1680. CHARLES NICHOLSON and CHARLES ANSLOW were in-dicted for stealing, on the   18th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 6 d  .; the goods of John Foster Hillman , from his person; to which
   NICHOLSON  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Fourteen Days . JOHN FOSTER HILLMAN. I am clerk to Mr. Hillman, a merchant in the City. On the 18th of April, between nine and ten o'clock at night, I was in Greenwich fair—I heard some one call—I turned, and saw my handkerchief in the constable's hand—it was safe about a minute before—this is it. HENRY FOWLER( police-constable E  111.) I was at Greenwich fair, and saw the prisoners following the prosecutor, and talking together in an under tone—I saw Nicholson take this handkerchief out of the prosecutor's pocket, and give it to Anslow, who had been covering Nicholson, and was near enough to see what he did—I took him, and took the handkerchief from him.
   Anslow.  I had it not in my possession at all. Witness.  I took it from him.
 (The prisoner received a good character.)
   ANSLOW  — GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Fourteen Days .
