1674. WILLIAM WOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
April, 3 quarts of beer, value 18., the goods of Thomas Hayes.
EDWARD Evans. I am pot-boy to Thomas Hayes, who keeps a beer-
shop, at Sydenham, Kent. On the afternoon of the 20th of April, I was
in a cow-shed at the back of master's house, and saw the prisoner go to a
cellar and bring a bucket and bottle, take it to the pig-sty, and conceal it,
then return to the brewhouse, take a pint pot, and return to the cellar again
--there was about three pots of beer in the bucket altogether--he brought
some beer out in a pint pot, and offered it to a man named Darby, who
said, "I shan't have that," and he chucked it away against the coal-shed-
I told master, and the prisoner was given into custody-I saw the bucket
and the beer in the sty.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Did you see it yourself!
A. Yes-neither master por mistress are here-it was porter-only master
and mistress had access to the cellar-not the servants, unless they had
leave-the prisoner is a gravel-digger-he was not in master's employ.
ment-there was nobody but Darby present-he is a navigator.
COURT. Q. How did he get into the cellar ? A. He must have gone
round the back of the house, then closed the middle door, while mistress
was serving in the shop, and could get round to the back premises.
WILLIAM GLADWIN. I am a policeman. I apprehended the prisoner,
and asked him if his name was William Wood-he said, "Yes"--I said,
I apprehended him for stealing beer from Mr. Hayes—he said, "Arn't
you going to take more of them from work?"-I said no, there was no
more-he then said, “There was more concerned in it than me."
Prisoner's Defence. I did not go into the cellar at all; I was drinking
rum at the time it happened; Mrs. Hayes lent us the money to get the rum;
I did not go into the cellar except when she sent me to draw beer, because
she would not awake the baby; he swore at first he saw Darby go into
the cellar; I was not given in charge till next day; they gave other men
in charge that night, but he then said it was not me at all; there was only
five pints, not three quarts; the boy could not see me go, because the pig-
sty is not level with the cow-house.
