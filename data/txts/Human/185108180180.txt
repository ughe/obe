 1762. JOHN PRIOR was again indicted for embezzling, on   26th June . 2 l  . 12 s  . 6 d  .; the moneys of William Shaw , his master.
   MR. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution. WILLIAM HOOD. On 26th June, I directed the prisoner to take half a load of bay, which came to 1 l . 17 s . 6 d ., and half a load of straw, 15 s ., to Mr. Parson's, at Fish-street-hill—he was to receive the money and bring it back—he did not bring the money to me—I saw him when he returned, but had not time to speak to him.
   Cross-examined by MR. PARRY . Q.  You were very busy? A.  He went away before I came back out of the office—I asked him next morning for it—he remained in our service two days after that, and was taken into custody at Smithfield, where I attend.
   MR. ROBINSON . Q.  What did he say the next morning? A.  He told me he had lost it. WILLIAM SHAW. The prisoner has not paid me the 2 l . 12 s . 6 d . JOHN PARSON. I lived with John Connett, my son-in-law, a butcher of Fish-street-hill. I purchased some hay and straw in Smithfield; it was delivered at Dowgate-hill—the prisoner then came back to Fish-street-hill, and I saw him paid the money, 2 l . 12 s . 6 d . NOT GUILTY.
 1763. JOHN PRIOR was again  indicted for embezzling, on   21st June , 1 l  . 6 s  .; the moneys of William Shaw , his master.
   MR. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.
   This was the indictment to which the evidence given in the first case applied.
   MR. PARRY applied to withdraw the plea of NOT GUILTY , and put in a plea of AUTREFOIS ACQUIT , which he submitted he was entitled to do at any period within six days. MR. GURNEY , after having consulted MR. JUSTICE WIGHT  MAN, was of opinion that he could not receive the plea. WILLIAM HOOD. On 21st June I sold Joe  a load of clover, and sent the prisoner with him to receive the money—it was his duty when he had received it to pay it over to me—4 l . 6 s . was the amount—I next saw him in the Old Kent-road, when he paid me 3 l . for the clover, and said that was all the money he had got, the old woman had not got any more, and the old man was not at home, and he would pay it in a fortnight, when he went that way.
   Cross-examined by MR. PARRY . Q.  How long after that did be remain in Mr. Shaw's service? A.  Till 28th—it was Long Joe  who bought the hay—I do not know where he is—Mr. Ostler is not Long Joe  —I did not sell it to Ostler—I have dealt with Mr. Ostler before, but not with Joe.
   COURT . Q.  The prisoner had no authority to sell it to Mr. Ostler? A.  No.
   MR. ROBINSON . Q.  But he was to get the money and bring it back to you? A.  Yes.
   MR. PARRY . Q.  From Joe ? A.  He was either to bring back the money or the hay.
