 1914.  GEORGE GLOVER was indicted for stealing. on the 21st of July , 1 tea-kettle, value 8s., the goods of Uriah Bryant and another.
 URIAH BRYANT, JUN. I am in partnership with my father Uriah Bryant - we are  tin-plate workers; this kettle hung on our door-post. I did not see it taken.
   JAMES THORN. I am an officer. About half-past eleven o'clock on the 21st of July, I saw the prisoner running from Bryant's premises with this kettle in his hand - he was not two doors from the house - I followed - he dropped it. I took it up and secured him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. A young man hired me to carry it.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined One Month.
 1988.  THOMAS JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 22d of August , 1 handkerchief, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Joseph Sharpe , from his person.
 JOSEPH SHARPE. I am a  gentleman's servant. On the 22d of August, about half-past eight o'clock in the evening, I was in Smithfield- I felt a hand in my right-hand pocket - I turned and caught the prisoner close to me with his hand in my left-hand pocket. I lost my handkerchief from my right hand pocket, and have never seen it since - I had used it not a minute before. I charged him with it, but there was one on each side of me - the other got away.
   JOSEPH HORTON. I am an officer. The prisoner was given into my charge; he seemed agitated, but denied the charge.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Confined Three Months.
 1916.  JOHN WARE was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 1s. 6d., the goods of John Jones , from his person.
 JOHN JONES. I am  clerk to Mr. Goss. About nine o'clock in the morning I passed three lads just by the Fleet-prison, standing with their backs against the wall - they followed me till I got near the Old Bailey, on Ludgate-hill. I felt something touch my heel - I turned and saw the prisoner with my handkerchief inside his coat, under his arm - he ran across the street. I followed calling Stop thief! - he threw it down - I took it up, and he was taken- it was safe a minute before.
 Cross-examined by MR. J. ALLEY. Q. Did the others appear in his company? A. Yes - I believe they spoke together. I do not know what became of the others.
   ALEXANDER JOHNSON. I am an officer, and took him in charge.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner Defence. It was thrown down by me - I did not throw it.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 17. Strongly recommended to Mercy. - Confined Six Weeks.
 1917.  ELIZABETH KING and ANN LANE were indicted for stealing, on the 14th of August , 1 basket, value 2s. 3d., and 3 pecks of apples, value 4s., the goods of Theophilus Salmon.
 THEOPHILUS SALMON. I am a  ticket-porter, and attend Fleet-market. On the 14th of August, at a quarter to five o'clock in the morning, I had five baskets of apples in my care. I left them a short time, while I unloaded something at the top of the market, and heard an alarm - I missed one basket. I looked up Ludgate-hill, and saw the prisoners carrying it away - it contained about three pecks; each had hold of a handle, and had got about one hundred yards. I overtook them at the corner of St. Martin's-court - they said they had paid 6s. 6d. for it, but it was not for sale.
   JAMES FISHBURN. I am a watchman. I was at the market and saw the prisoners together, sitting on a basket, for about a quarter of an hour; I saw Lane take hold of this basket three times; then they both took it away - I gave an alarm.
   GEORGE GOUGE. I am an officer, and received them in charge.(Basket produced and sworn to.)
 LANE'S Defence. I had been out, and had been drinking.
 LANE -    GUILTY. Aged 29.
 KING -    GUILTY. Aged 29. Confined One Month.
