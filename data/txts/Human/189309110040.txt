   Cross-examined.  I had been in for three days—I had no unpleasant ness with the prisoner—I had no watch with me—I know the time we went to bed by the clock on the mantelpiece, and at half-past two I heard from the neighbours—I heard Gleeson use some bad language to the prisoner; that was after I got up, when we were downstairs—the prisoner did not seem dazed—he had not said anything to me about being a ruined man—I knew there was a bill for £10; that would about consume what there was there—I had gas in my bedroom—I locked the door each night I was there—I had tea with Gleeson about four—whatever cooking there was was done at the gas-stove.
   Re-examined.  I made the water hot for tea—I used a kettle, and then put the gas out—that was done safely and properly. JOHN WALTER KIRBY.
