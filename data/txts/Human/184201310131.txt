 Prisoner, I bought the newspapers of a man; the prosecutor was not in the room, and I wish to ask how he could see me take the papers. JOHN TILLMAN. I was not in the room—I was in the passage, with the door partly open—I could see him quite plainly.
 Prisoner. The door was shut; I bought the papers, at 2  d . each; I solemnly assert they are not his; I do not know the address of the person I bought them of; the knives were pallet knives, which I use, and never were in the prosecutor's possession. JOSEPH HOPKINSON re-examined.  There were nine duplicates in a box in his bag, and various newspapers, which were torn, and two penknives, as well as these table knives. JOHN TILLMAN re-examined.  I know the knives, by constantly using them—the prisoner was frequently at my house, more than two dozen times—there is no mark of the knives being used for mixing colour, which there would be if used as he says—one is an odd one, and the other like a set I have. GUILTY. Aged 41.— Confined Three Months.

SARAH WATTS was indicted for feloniously receiving, of a certain evil-disposed person, on the   14th of January , 1 shovel, value 8 d  ., the goods of James Bartley , well knowing it to be stolen. JAMES BARTLEY. I live in North-place, New-road. On the 14th of January I missed a shovel from my stable—it is worth 8 d .
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  Is it worth more than 6 d .? A.  Yes—it was broken by accident—I am a carman—I charged my son with stealing it—I could not buy a handle to it under 1 s . JAMES BARTLEY. I am sixteen years old. I took this shovel from my father's stable, and sold it to the prisoner, down in Church way, for 1 d .
   Cross-examined. Q.  You wanted to sell it to somebody else, did not you-?. A.  Another boy came up and said he would give me 2 d . for it, but she had it first—I had asked a baker at the door if he would buy it—he said would not give 2 d . for a dozen of them—I did not tell the prisoner I had my father's leave to sell it, nor that I was sent to sell it—I asked 2 d . for it—she said she would only give me 1 d .—I did not tell my father till next morning, when he asked me—he said if I did not tell where I sold it I should be punished—I was given into custody. WILLIAM HUNT I live in Brunswick-place, Clerkenwell. I went to the prisoner's shop, with two marked sixpences, which the officer gave me, to purchase the shovel—I went and asked the prisoner if she had a shovel—she said, "Yes," and asked 10 d . for it—I paid her the two marked six pences.
