some handkerchiefs and pawned them in London, and I will tell of it."
EDMUND CHAMPNESS (police-constable K 138.) On Wednesday I
went to Mr. Brass's, a pawnbroker's shop, and found the two prisoners
detained, for offering these breeches.
Watson's Defence. I never took them; Burns said to me, at the station,
"If they ask who took them, you say you did."
Burns. I did not say so; he took them, and gave them to me to pawn.

375. MARY KEMP, alias Smith, alias Vincent, alias Williams, was
indicted for stealing, on the 19th of June, 2 blankets, value 58.; 2 sheets,
value 48. ; 2 table-cloths, value 4s. ; 2 tumblers, value 1s. ; 2 egg-cups,
value 18.; three knives, value 18d. ; 3 forks, value 18d. ; 2 wine glasses,
value 1s. ; 1 basin, value 3d.; and 3 salt-cellars, value 6d.; the goods of
Eleanor Berry: also, on the 24th of November, 2 blankets, value 12s.;
3 sheets, value 98. ; 1 pair of snuffers, value 18d. ; 1 brush, value 1s. ;
and 1 basin, value 6d.; the goods of William Smith: also, on the 12th of
November, 1 blanket, value 58.; 1 key, value 6d.; and 1 ring, value 6d.;
the goods of Henry Lidney : also, on the 2nd of July, 1 blanket, value 58.;
1 towel, value 6d. ; 1 salt-cellar, value 6d.; 1 cup, value 3d.; and 1
saucer, value 6d.; the goods of Henry Booker ; to all of which indict-
ments she pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 26.-Transported for Seven Years on each of the
Two First Indictments.
