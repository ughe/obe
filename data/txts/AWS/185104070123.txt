DOLBY. I live at Stratford. I was with my sister, Mrs. Fordham,
and saw a man, who in my opinion is the prisoner, and another further
back-ithere were two or three boys also.
Cross-eramined. Q. Do you say the prisoner is the man 4. No;
be is like the man that stood behind my sister.
COURT. Q. In what respect is he like the man 4. In height, but
when we were first taken to the cell he had on an over-coat, which made
bim appear stouter; he is decidedly like the man, and I feel positive in
my own mind that he is the man.
