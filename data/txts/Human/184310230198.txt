CHARLES STURGE FLETCHER. I am inspector of police at Cambridge. On the 26th of Oct. I found the prisoner at a house at Cambridge, and told him I took him on suspicion of stealing Bank notes from Mrs. Lavallin, of Greenwich—he said he knew nothing of Greenwich, and I was deceived; he was not the person, his name was Arthur Wellesley, and he came from London—I found a box and bag there—I asked if they were his—he said, "Yes"-—I found bills of parcels amounting to 46 l ., and a gold watch in it—I asked him to write his name—he wrote "Arthur Wellesley, K. G. C., London," and said he was the son of the Duke of Wellington; and while in custody he wrote a letter to the Duke, begging His Grace to write a letter, that he might be released.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I did not know what I was doing; I had entirely lost my senses for some hours. GUILTY of Larceny only. Aged 21.— Transported for Seven Years .
 3088. THOMAS THORN was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of Dec ., at Woolwich , 1 watch, value 5 l  .; 1 guard, 1 s  .; and 1 key, 6 d  .; the good of William Daniels , his master, in his dwelling-house. MARY ANN DANIELS. I am the wife of William Daniels, and keep a public-house at Woolwich. On the 26th of Sept. I put my gold watch into the glass drawer on the table—I missed it about half-past two o'clock the same after noon—this is it—the prisoner was there, helping to wait.
   Prisoner.  I was not a servant in the house, I was discharged the day be fore. Witness.  I am sure he was in the house that day—he cleaned the pots. THOMAS MARSON. I am a pawnbroker. I produce the watch which I re ceived in pledge of the prisoner on the 26th of Sept.
   Prisoner. Q.  What dress had I on? A.  A white moleskin dress, but no jacket—it was between four and five o'clock in the afternoon—I swear you are the person.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I never saw the watch, nor ever had it in my posses sion. There was a man drinking in the house that day, dressed very much like me; I was at Woolwich all the day. GUILTY of Larceny only.  Aged 34.— Recommended to mercy. Confined Six Months .
 3089. WILLIAM FOSTER was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of Oct ., 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  ., and 2 shillings, the property of John Tester . CAROLINE TESTER. I am the wife of John Tester. On the 20th of Oct., a few minutes before ten o'clock, I was going by the Observatory, on Wool wich Common—I had a basket, containing a handkerchief and 2 s ., which I left at the gate, in the prisoner's care—I came back in about an hour and a half, and all the soldiers were gone—I saw my basket, but the handkerchief and 2 s . were gone—I am sure it was in it when I gave it to him. JOHN WARD. I am a bombardier, in the Royal Artillery. On the 20th of Oct., between twelve and one o'clock, I searched the prisoner's bed, and found this handkerchief—I found 1 s . 7 1/2 d . on him. WILLIAM GLADWIN( policeman. ) I took the prisoner—he said he could not imagine what possessed him to do it, he wished he had never seen it—I had cautioned him that whatever he said I should be obliged to use against him. CAROLINE TESTER re-examined.  This is my handkerchief. GUILTY.* Aged 42.— Confined Three Months .
