 1038.  HENRY THOMPSON was indicted for stealing, on 11th of March , 4 pewter pots, value 4s., the goods of John Cox.
   THOMAS GOOK. I am an officer. On Sunday morning, the 11th of March, I saw the prisoner and three others near one another - I knew them, and told Wood, who was with me, that something was wrong; I saw four pots standing at Mr. Ponsonby's rails - I watched some time, and saw the other three go away; I followed them some distance, and then ran round to find the prisoner - Wood gave me information; I took the prisoner, and found two pots in his hat and two on his person.
   JOHN COX. I keep a public-house, in Pall-mall. These pots are mine.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1039.  WILLIAM WYATT was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of March , 1 flageolet, value 4s, the goods of William Newton.
   FRANCIS FEATHERSTONE. I am an officer. I was on duty on the morning of the 29th of March, in Plummer's-row, City-road, and saw the prisoner in company with another; they went to several shops, and then to the prosecutor's; they stood ten minutes in front of it, then the prisoner put his hand into the shop window, and took something out, which he put under his apron, and walked away - I suspected he had stolen something, and went after him; when he saw me he ran down Park-street - I called a man to stop him, and when I came up I found this flageolet under his apron - he said he found it under a stall.
   GEORGE NEWTON. This is my property, but it was at my father's house; his name is William.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. - Confined Six Weeks,
 1040.  ELIZABETH WEBB was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of February , 1 ring, value 10s., the goods of John Smith.
   JOHN SMITH. I am a jeweller, and live in the Strand. On the 27th of February the prisoner came to my shop; my man was showing her some rings in a tray, which contained thirty or forty rings - I was looking from a back window, and saw her take a ring from the tray, and after attempting to look at it, put it into her month; the shopman then suspected she had done something with it, but his suspicions were removed by her returning him a brass one; I then sent for an officer, and with some difficulty this ring was got from her mouth.
   GEORGE NUTTAN AGERS. I am an officer. I was sent for, and this brass ring was produced at the shop; the shopman said she had stolen a gold one; I asked what she had one with it; she said she had not one - I asked her to open her mouth, which she would not do; I opened it by force and this ring was in it.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. When I left home I had a gold ring; I went to buy another - I had money enough to buy it, but I met a man, who took me to have something to drink - it took such effect on me, that I did not know what I did, nor where I was - I had no metal ring when I left home.
   GEORGE NUTTAN AGERS. She was no more in liquor than she is now.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1041.  GEORGE WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of February , 5 jackets, value 40s., the goods of John Driscol.
   JOHN DRISCOL. I am a tailor, and live in Four Awl-court, Rosemary-lane. On the 29th of February I had some jackets laying in my place; I missed them about ten o'clock in the morning - I know they had been safe shortly before; I cannot say whether the door was open or not - I went to look for them, and found the prisoner with the jackets, in custody of the officer.
   PEARCE DRISCOL. I am an officer. I saw the prisoner in Rosemary-lane - the prosecutor's wife had hold of him, and was crying out for an officer.
   CATHERINE DRISCOL. I am the prosecutor's wife. I found the prisoner with this bundle in Rosemary-lane; I asked what he had in it, for he had changed the wrapper; he had come to me two or three days before for a lodging.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was sitting at their house, and smoking my pipe; a shipmate came, and called me out; he had a bundle - he told me to hold it while he went to have a glass of grog; this woman then came up, and asked what I wanted with her bundle; I said, "There is the man who gave it me," but she would not go into the the house to see him.
   CATHERINE DRISCOL. He was walking along with the bundle when I came up; he said, "It is a fine morning;" I took hold of his bundle, and said, "I should like to see what is in your bundle, as there was a bundle taken from my house a quarter of an hour ago;" he did not point out any man, but said he did not take them.
     GUILTY. Aged 55. - Transported for Seven Years.
