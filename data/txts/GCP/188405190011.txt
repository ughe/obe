Cross-examined by MR. GRAIN. I parted with my money partly on the
faith of the agreement that had a great deal to do with it before
I parted with anything I went and made careful inquiries as to the
title of the property on the spot; I mean to say I made inquiries as
to whether the vendors had a good title to the property they had to
sell, and I found they had & sufficient title to warrant my advancing
the money-I took no measures to ascertain the value of the pro
perty-1 saw the property, and found that there was sufficient to
satisfy my amount of 5001.-I was satisfied that I would be safe to
take & second charge in respect of my 5001., and I took one I con
sidered the agreement was the security-I did not consider whether
the second charge on the property was sufficient to hold me safe as
regards the 5001.-I did not go into figures at all I was bound to
take the mortgage to secure the payment of the 5001, if I had found
the title was not good I certainly should not have lent it that was
the condition on which I agreed to lend it-I have not heard that.
the property has been since valued by a competent valuer-I have
never caused it to be valued-I wrote to the solicitors for the mortgagees
to inquire if they thought there was any margin, and the reply was to
the effect that there was not, in fact they had taken possession of the
property and sold some of it—they told me there was a mortgage of
1,8001.-Young told me that there was a margin of 2,0007.-he pointed
that out when the money was advanced- it was later than 29th June
when Young said "I am Murray" I think it was about the middle of
July, but I cannot speak without my book-I have sent for it-Young
