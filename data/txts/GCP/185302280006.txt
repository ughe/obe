John WILLIAMS. I am a working smith and engineer. I was at work
at Emscote the whole of June, and of course on the 9th and 10th- I did not
come up to the trial of Steadman D. Knight; I was not subpoenaed on that
trial-I never received 31. 10s, as my expenses on that trial; I did not
hear of the trial till after it was over-about a month afterwards I
was at Mr. Steadman's, with Sparks and Miller, and saw Mr. Steadman; he
did not say anything about my being a witness on the action-Sparks was
there before Miller and I arrived received this subpoena there that day
from Yorston, and 1s. with it; we were then taken by Yorston to Westmin-
ster, and he showed us the Court, and then took us to a public-house; he
had the expenses to pay for Mr. Steadman; I know that, because I saw
Mr. Steadman put some money into his hands to pay our expenses before we
started-Yorston paid for the whole of our refreshment.

Witnesses for the Defence.
WILLIAM SHADRAKE. I was foreman to Mr. Steadman about four years,
and was so at the time of the trial between Steadman and Knight, I have
since left-before that trial, Mr. Steadman's health had been very bad for
some time, and he was very ill then, from being thrown out of his chaise ; he
was not able to attend to business, and I attended to it for him-I remember
receiving some subpoenas from Mr. Sergeant-these are them (produced)
I received them two days before the trial-after I had received them, 1
received some money; it was either from Mr. Sergeant or Mr. Steadman-I
think it was about 51.-it was to subpoena the different witnesses- I was to
pay them when I served the subpoenas on them I had more subpoenas
than these-my directions were to subpæna those in town, and at Emscote,
in Warwickshire, also I did not go to Emscote; I had not time I went to
the railway station the day before the trial, but was too late for the first train,
and the next train would not have taken me in time to get them up for the
trial; I did not go-it was half-past 11 o'clock at night before I subpænaed
the last in town- I did not see Mr. Steadman afterwards till the morning of
the trial- I did not say anything to him about not having subpoenaed these
persons- I was a witness at the trial- the witnesses were not allowed to be
