ELLEN DESMOND. I am the wife of Daniel Desmond, and live in Col-
yer's-rents, Borough. The prisoner came to lodge at our house on
Thursday evening the 13th of October-a woman, a neighbour, brought
him-I was very ill in bed, having laid in only eight days, and I had a child
lying dead-I agreed to let him the lodging at 28. a week-he said he
might stop only a night or two, or a month-he slept there that night-we
had a wake I was in bed, and do not know what time he went to bed
The went away about seven o'clock in the moming, and these clothes were
gone-I had several other lodgers-the prisoner was taken about six weeks
Kfterwards-he went away without paying for his lodging, and did not say
he was going.
DANIEL DESMOND. I know the clothes were all missed five minutes
after the prisoner was gone-I had seen them the night before they were
all in my charge, and the lodgers look to me to pay for them.
JORN KEMP (police-constable M. 60.) I apprehended the prisoner last
Wednesday in Bird-cage-alley, and found a pair of braces on him.
DANIEL DESMOND re-examined. These are my braces, which I lost
when he went away--the things were worth 21.
GUILTY of stealing the braves.-Confined Twelve Months.
