ALFRED SANDE. I am in partnership with my brother Buchan and Mr
Sands, as ink manufacturers, in Cardington Street, Kennington Cros

him on Saturday, 23rd December-I saw him two or three times between
then and 6th January-on the Tuesday evening after bis discharge he came

having sent him away, and that we might find our chimney shaft blown of
