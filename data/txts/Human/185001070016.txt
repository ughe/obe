JOHN BOLTON re-examined.  It was last Nov. twelvemonths that the pri soner applied to me to endorse the bill, long before this loan—he knew me perfectly well by sight, as well as he knew any member of his own family. GUILTY. Aged 42.— Transported for Seven Years .
