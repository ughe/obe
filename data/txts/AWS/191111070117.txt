Cross-examined. The prisoner has been in trouble before and the

Prisoner's statement. All I can say is I have been getting a living

Verdict, Guilty.
Prisoner confessed to having been convicted at the Guildhall
Police Court on May 25, 1911, receiving three months' hard labour for
stealing a carriage bicyele and its contents. Other convictions
proved County of London Sessions, July 5, 1910, stealing two horses
and harness, eight months' hard labour; Guildhall, December 29,
1909, three months' hard labour for attempted fraud.
