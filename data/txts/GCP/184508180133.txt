for stealing 1 copper, value 12s., the goods of James James; and that
Hull had been before convicted of felony; to which
HULL+ pleaded Guilty. Aged 19.-Transported for Seven Years.

Highgate-I saw it safe between two and three o'clock, and missed it the
same day-this is it.

fetched this copper from the prosecutor's house, one of the witnesses had
taken it back there.

1719. JOHN FITZGERALD was indicted for stealing 1 purse, value
ls., the goods of a certain woman, whose name is unknown, from her
person.
WILLIÁM CHILD. I am beadle of Trinity-square. On the afternoon
of the 13th of July I was on duty on Tower-wharf, and saw the prisoner
walking behind the persons who were looking at the rowing-match-I saw
the prisoner and another go to a lady-the other took a purse from her
pocket and gave it to the prisoner-I went after them and took the pri-
soner-the other got away-the prisoner got the purse from his pocket
and shoved it into the river-I found the young lady, but she would not
appear, nor give her name.
Prisoner. Q. Was I not standing in front of the lady? 4. No, you
were walking away, and when I took you you took your hand out of your
pocket with the purse in it, and got it into the river.
GEORGE CLARK. I am a wharfinger. I was going along and saw a boy
rob a lady in front of the Tower-wharf-what he took I know not, but he
passed it to the prisoner, who put it into his pocket.
