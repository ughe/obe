Cross-examined. It was about nine days after the offence that prosecutor came to me, but there was a reason why he did not give informa  tion before.
 (Defence). ALBERT VINCENT(prisoner, on oath). I admit having drink with the prosecutor. we just went to the "Black Lion" and had five of six drinks there. We went to the "Green Gate" and had a tidy few, when prosecutor intermixed with four other men and two rough women. I said to him "Will you come home?" because I did not like the look of these people, they looked to me very bad, no I left his company at two o'clock in the afternoon. Prosecutor was drunk, and was remanded for attempting to commit suicide on the same day. I was only with him from ten o'clock till two.
 Cross-examined. I never saw the paper or the coins. I did not go into the "Queen's Hotel" with him. I left him at the "Green Gate." He did not knock me down; he just pushed me because I said I did not like the look of the men and women. That was about a mile and a half away from the "Queen's" public-house. I had not been near the "Queen's" that day. Prosecutor said "What is it to do with you, I can spend my money where I like and treat who I like." After he pushed me down I walked away and left him with the four men and two women. He was trean  ing them I was not drunk. When I walked away prosecutor did not make any attempt to run after me. The first public we went to was the "Black Lion." He was treating anybody who came in. He went away from there with me. The next was the "Prince Albert," Broadway, plastron, that is about five minutes' walk from the "Black Lion." None of the other people went there with us. The next was the "Green Gate." He one lese went with us there. There he treated there of four men and two rough women. I had two or three drinks and I left him with these other people. The women got hold of him and danced with him. I knew he had some money but I only saw the money that he paid for the drinks. I only saw silver, no gold at all. I saw no money wrapped in paper. He pushed me down because I wanted him to go home and he wanted to stop with these women. I did not give him halfpenny and a farthing.
 Detective-sergeant STEPHENS, recalled. Prosecutor tried to consults suicide late that night by taking poision. He was alleged to have taken prussic acid, but the doctor said there were no signs of it. He was not taken up for being drunk. The story he told me was that he and prisoner were alone, and they got to the public-house the other people got round him. JAMES CAMPIN, recalled. At the "Black Lion" I treated nobody but Vincent. We went from there straight to the "Green Gate" and had a glass each, came out and got on a tram. There were no other people at "Green Gate" that I know. We went from the "Green Gate" to the "Boleyn," and them up to the "Queen's Hotel. When I knocked him down the other men got round him. I had no beer in the "Queen's Hotel" at all. There were no women there.
 Verdict, Not Guilty.
