HANNAH TAYLOR. I am cook in the prosecutrix's family. I know the

Cross-examined. Q. How long had you been there A. Six months
-I had not had notice to quitthe box was one that the prisoner asked

ROBERT WHITE (police-constable T 119.) I took the prisonershe
