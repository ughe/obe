   Q.  Did Mr. Miers get any quantity of goods in after you went in No vember—was there any considerable quantities of goods brought into the shop? A.  No, I did not see any—I think if there had been any large quantity I should have seen them—I could see into the shop through the glass door, and when my master had no man I used to watch the shop door while master and mistress were at dinner—I did not serve in the shop—the shelves were not full, some of them had two or three things on and the others were not full—the shop was not well stocked.
   Q.  You say you did not see any large quantities of things come in; you know of any thing going out after you got there? A.
