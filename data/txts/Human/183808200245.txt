2076. JAMES TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of July 2 gowns, value 11 s  .; 1 cloak, value 10 s  .; and 1 pocket, value 6 d  .; the goods of Emily Manser ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. EMILY MANSER. I am servant to Mr. Hobbs, at Kew, Surrey. I had a merino cloak, two gowns, and a pocket—I saw them safe about five o'clock, on the 21st of July, in my box, in a passage even with the kitchen—I missed them on the 24th—these are my things—( looking at them )—I do not know the prisoner. THOMAS PARSONS. I am a market-gardener. On the 22nd of July I pursued the prisoner from Barnes, and took him at Putney, with a bundle, containing these things—he said it was his property.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  What day of the week was that? A.  Sunday, about twelve o'clock-Barnes is three miles from Putney, by the way he went—I was running—I was very little out of breath—I did not lose sight of him—I came up to him at the Half Moon, Putney—I found the bundle in the passage—I did not take it from him—he was standing by it—he said it was his, and I should not have it—he came through Barnes a little after twelve o'clock—I am not a constable. JOHN FRANKS( police-constable V  124.) I took the prisoner. JOHN PASCOE( police-constable T  19.) I produce the certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, from Mr. Clerk's office—( read )—the prisoner is the man who was tried and convicted.
   Cross-examined. Q.  When was that? A.  On the 22nd September, 1834—I have known him nine years. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Transported for Seven Years.
 2077. EDWARD THOMAS was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of July , 1 tarpaulin, value 1 l  . 15 s  ., the goods of Henry Dudin .
   MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution . HENRY DUDIN. I am a lighterman and wharfinger, at Horsleydown, and reside at Sydenham. On the 20th of July I saw two of my tarpaulins doubled up—I gave orders for their being put into the punt named the Hope, under my own eye—it was lying in the lighter-road, Horsleydown—the punt was shoved off, and remained there till the following Tuesday, when I received some information, and went along-side—I went to where the tarpaulins ought to have been, and the small one was gone—one of them is here, and it is mine—the prisoner is a lighterman—I do not know that he works any where—I have seen him at George Stairs—the premises ad joining these stairs—I never let or lent any one the tarpaulin.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Do you know a man named Stemp? A.  I never saw him till at the police-office, when he was in custody, as I was informed—I saw him at the bar—the prisoner and the wharfinger's man were in custody—I do not recollect that the prisoner said a word—he was not asked in my hearing of what size it was-at the first examination I asked the Magistrate as a particular favour to let it stand over—I was present during the first and second examination—I was not absent during any time.
