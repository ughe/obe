MARY ANN PRESLAND. I am the wife of John Presland, and keep
shoemaker's shop at Stratford, in the parish of West Ham ; My
is very ill, and has been so for some time. On Friday morning, the 10th

the morning—I found the show-room door open—the staple had been
wrenched of—the door of the show-room opens into the passage, anotber

41.178.—1 also missed a great coat of my husband's. and a great coat•d
