JOSEPH REDDEN. On the 8th of February I missed this cash, 21.90
I fancied I had not entered the cash in my payments, and in order to be
more particular, I put the different days' receipts in different places, in
recess--one of them was 21. 123. 60. in silver-it was in a cupboard, which
has no door to it-it was wrapt in a piece of sugar paper-I went up-stain
and forgot to take it to my cash-box-I did not think of it again till Mon
day morning, the 15th, when I went to balance, and found a deficiency of
21. 12s. 60.--I thought there must be something wrong, and I thought it
prudent to go to the police-station--the girl was to come for her wages
she came; and on account of her altered appearance, I suspected her more
-she brought a doll and a couple of tarts for my children, and had ons
new dress : a new shawl, a new apron, and something very pretty round
her neck-I sent for the police sergeant, and he asked her " Where did you
get the last money from ?"-she said, "I got 6d. from my mistress to buy
comb"-he asked if she received any other money 1-she said, la

“Where did you get the money to buy that doll ?”- she said, her

Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Had you known the girl and
her family? A. Yes, for eight years—her father is living in Bermondsey.

- I believe she left our service on the 13th of February it was on the
