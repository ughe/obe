Robert MATTHEWs. On the 21st of August I was one of the waiters at
the Saloon, in Piccadilly. About two o'clock in the morning of the 22nd
there were some words between two gentlemen there I know the two gen-
tlemen-they were Mr. Eliot and Mr. Broughton-I consider the quarrel
was between them—they were having words—there were several gentlemen
standing round at the time-I do not know any of them-I did not hear
anybody else take part in it.
