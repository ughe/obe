1649. JOHN RAYMENT was indicted for stealing 80 yards of silk,
value 101., the goods of Thomas Ballance and another.
WILLIAM SUNFORD. I am foreman to Thomas Ballance and Son, silk-
manufacturers, Gutter-lane, Cheapside. On the 4th of June I delivered
to the prisoner's daughter, Elizabeth Rayment, a cane, containing 110
yards of silk, to be manufactured-it would make about 100 yards of
work-on the 18th of July she came for a little shoot, and I gave her nine
bobbins, with about eight ounces of silk on them-shortly after she came
back, and in consequence of what she stated to me, Mr. Hughes was sent
to the prisoner's house-we have never got back the silk.
JOHN Huones. I am inspector of work to the prosecutors. On the
morning of the 18th of July I was sent to the prisoner's house I know it
was his house, as I had been there two or three times before to see that the
work was going on in a proper state-I found the manufactured silk had
been cut off, and taken away, leaving about twenty-two yards unmanufac-
tured on the cane roll-the prisoner was not there the next day I took
away wbat was unmanufactured, and gave information.
Betsy RAYMENT. (This witness, professing not to understand the
nature of an oath, was not sworn, and the prisoner was)
ACQUITTED,
1650. SARAH JULIAN was indicted for stealing 1 pair of trowsers,
value 178.; 1 waistcoat, 38.; and 1 plane, 6d. ; the goods of John Lewis.
her master.
John Lewis. I live in Lower Whitecross-street, and am a stay-maker.
The prisoner was in my employ for three days-she went away on the
24th of July-I was out at the time I came home soon after seven o'clock
in the evening-I found my box had been broken open, and a pair of
trowsers and a waistcoat taken from it-the box had been fast when I
went away, about a quarter before seven in the morning the prisoner
worked in the room where my box was-I also lost a small plane--this is
the plane, and these are the waistcoat and trowsers-I did not see the pri-
soner again till Jast Thursday night.
EDWARD SALTER (City police-constable, No. 603.) In consequence of
information, on the 24th of July, I searched the prisoner's lodgings, in
Type-court, Milton-street--I heard from a fellow-lodger that she lodged
there found in the room this plane in this basket-I know the basket
belonged to the prisoner, as I had her in custody once before, and returned
this basket to her.
James Howell. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Beech-street. This
waistcoat and trowsers were pawned on the 24th of July, by the prisoner, I
have no doubt of her.
John PITMAN (police-constable K 132.) I apprehended the prisoner in
Cambridge-road, Bethnal-green-I asked if she recollected living in White-
cross-street-she said, "Yes"-I asked if she recollected her mistress and
master losing anything-she said, yes, she did, perfectly well, but she
knew nothing of it herself.
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.
