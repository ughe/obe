JOHN STANFORD. I am foreman of the malt-loft at Messrs. Truman and Hanbury's. On the 16th of May eight bundles of sacks were sent to the Eastern Counties Railway, to go  to Mr. bedward, at Thetford—these two sacks produced are the sacks of Truman and Hanbury.
   Cross-examined. Q.  you do not know that they belonged to those bundles? A.  No. FRANCIS DAKIN. I am receiving clerk at the Eastern Counties Railway, On the 16th of May I received sixteen bundles of sacks, to be sent to Mr. bedward, of Thetford—they were forwarded by the train. WILLIAM JOHNSON, Sen., re-examined.  One of these sacks I will swear is a sack that I saw at Wellington-street. NOT GUILTY.
 1686. JAMES HAWKINS was indicted for embezzlement.
   MR. RYLAND conducted the Prosecution CHARLES WILLIAM DUNFORD. I am a printer, and live at Kensington— the prisoner was my errand-boy—it was his duty to receive money which he " was to pay to Mrs. Dunford or to me—Hemmings and Malcot owed me money—I sent the prisoner to receive it—I saw him on his return—he said Mr. Malcot had removed and he did not know where to find him, and Mr. Hem-mings would call and pay me.
   Cross-examined by MR. O'BRIEN . Q.  How long had he been with you? A.  Six weeks or two months—his father is a respectable man—my wife used to receive money—it  was on  the 15th or  16th of July the prisoner had these bills to take out—I have a son—he is not here—I have no complaints to make of him in the way of money—I had three charges against the prisoner at the police-office—it did not turn out that one sum had been received by my son. CHARLOTTE DUNFORD. I am the prosecutor's wife—the prisoner never paid these sums to me. MARIA HEMMINGS. I live with my father at Notting-hill—I do not know the prisoner—I paid a boy 4 s . on account of my father for Mr. Dunford—he wrote this receipt. JOHN ROWLES MALCOT. I am a merchant's clerk, and reside at Kensington—I do not know the prisoner—I paid a boy 2 s . for Mr. Dunford, and he wrote this receipt. CHARLES WILLIAM DUNFORD re-examined.  I believe these receipts to be the prisoner's writing. NOT GUILTY.
