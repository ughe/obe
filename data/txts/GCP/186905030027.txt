Armstrong's Statement before the Magistrate.--" I left work at 6.15, and

Witness for the Defence.
CHARLES MUNDAY (the Prisoner). The prisoner Armstrong was no party

before he was brought to the station--when I saw the lanterns over the

469. GEORGE SMITH (27), Feloniously assaulting George Davis, with
intent to rob him.
MR. COLLINS conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE Davis. I am a merchant, at 39, Cannon Street-about 2.30 on
Saturday, 3rd April, I was going to the Cannon Street Railway Station-I
was wearing & watch and obain, attached to my waistcoat-I received
blow in the stomach, which took the wind entirely away from me at the
time I received the blow the prisoner grabbed at my chain, but he could
not pull the watch through-he ran away and was pursued and brought
back-I am sure he is the man who grabbed my chain, and struck me in
the stomach.
Prisoner. Did I not come face to face with you as you came along
Witness. I did not see you coming towards mo at all—I saw you strike the
