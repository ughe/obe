service-if he received money for me it was his duty to pay it the same day
--if he received 4s. 9d. on the 12th of Aug. he has never paid it me
I asked him about it at the police station he said he had received the
money, but that his brother was as bad as he was, and had induced him to
do it and he would bring him in as well as himself.
Cross-examined by Mr. Crouch. Q. On the 12th of Aug. did you have
any arrangement with the prisoner about the payment of some money?
A. No; not on that day-on the Saturday before, he arranged to pay me
22. 105. Od. in various sums-that had nothing to do with this 48. yd.-it
