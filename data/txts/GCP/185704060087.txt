the payment of 51. 108., with intent to defraud.

FREDERICK JOHN PADLEY. I am a mining broker, of No. 4, Union
Court, Old Broad Street. The prisoner was employed by me as an errand
boy and clerk I was in the habit occasionally of sending him to my
bauker's with cheques on 14th Feb. I gave him a cheque for 51. on the
London and Westminster Bank, Lothbury branch, to get change-he
brought me back 51.- at the time I gave it him it was simply a cheque for
51., I am quite certain of that that cheque is not here,
JOSEPH BROWN (City policeman, 584). I had a cheque for 51. 108
which I received from Mr. Padley at the Mansion House I have since
had the misfortune to lose it out of my pocket when running after a thief
-I have a copy of it
