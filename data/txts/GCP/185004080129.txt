832. JONATHAN COE, WILLIAM HOWE, and WALTER PHIL-
LIPS, breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Richard Stowell, and
stealing 1 brooch and other articles, value 25s. ; his goods : 1 coat, value
30s.; the goods of Richard Stowell, the younger; and I waistcoat and 1 pair
of trowsers, value 10s.; the goods of Job Elliott. KOHAT IS..
MR. BIRNIE conducted the Prosecution.
CAROLINE STOWELL. I am the wife of Richard Stowell, of 6, Newcastle.
place, Mile-end, in the parish of St. Dunstan's, Stepney. On the evening of 20th
April the prisoner Howe came to my house he told me that my son Richard
had met with a serious accident, that two men had taken him up, and he was
to go to the London Hospital, but he refused to go till he saw me-he then
gave me a letter-I asked him to walk in I took him into the back parlour,
and read the letter by the fire-light-I said as soon as I had changed my
dress I would go-be begged me not to wait, but to go directly-I asked
him if he was going back--he said, Yes I told him to be kind enough
to tell my son I would come directly I went and asked Mrs. Love to come
over, and left her in my house-I went by the bus to Stratford, where my
son was- I found him quite well, and at work returned by the train, and
found my front-room had been stripped-four sheets, two dresses, two
petticoats, a frock-coat of my son's, a coat of Mr. Elliott's, a waistcoat, and
pair of trowsers, were all at the station when I returned, and a black bag
which was not mine--I never saw it before they had put the things into
that bag, and left it bebind-these are the articles--the letter was not my
son's writing- I stood at the door looking at Howe, and have no doubt he is
the man-there was a gaslight there.
