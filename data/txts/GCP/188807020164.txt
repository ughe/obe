12, Coleman Street-I produce a certified copy of Donaldson's account;

sum paid in, and it was all drawn out by March 15th-on 26th March
we sent him this letter: “We must request you will abstain from sending
cheques which are not provided for, or we shall have to close the
account"-four cheques, amounting to 151. 108., were presented and
dishonoured-there was one to Griffin on 10th March for 21.

Ebsworth's favour.
