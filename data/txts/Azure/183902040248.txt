845. ROBERT GEE was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of January,
1 box, value 58. 6d., the goods of Lewis Davis.
JOSEPH WEIGHT. I am a constable of Woolwich. On the 8th
January, about six o'clock in the evening, I saw the prisoner take a box
out of Mr. Davis's shop, and run away—I pursued, and brought him back
—he had to reach inside to get it.
JAMES LAMB. I am assistant to Mr. Lewis Davis, of Tower-street,
Woolwich, a pawnbroker. This box is his—we had some like this
standing inside the door—I could not swear whether it was sold or
stolen.
JAMES HENRY WATTS. I am an ironmonger. I was with Weight,
and saw the prisorier take the box from inside Mr. Davis's shop.
Prisoner. Q. Had I any thing with me? A. Yes, a bundle containing
a gown.
Prisoner's Defence. I went to that shop on some business of my own—
I cannot say how I got the box, but I must have paid for it—I had the
fever very bad in Jamaica, and since that I sometimes do not know what I
am doing.
GU ILTY. Aged Recommended to mercy by the Jury.
Confined Four Days.
Before Mr. Recorder.
