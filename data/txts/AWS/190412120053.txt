FOURTH COURT RT.-Friday December 16th, 1904.
Before Lumley Smith, Bag., K.C.
93. THOMAS BREADMORE (27) Feloniously obtaining from Keevil
& Weston, and from Joseph Eaton, two dead pigs by false pretences.
Mr. FITZGERALD Prosecuted; Mr. COHEN Defended.
JosEPH EATON. I am a licensed porter in the Central Meat Market,
Smithfield-in the early morning of November 4th, the prisoner, whom
I know well by sight, came up to me in the market and 8aid, 44 Are you
doing anything I said, No "-he said, 14 Do you mind doing me a
round and I said, said, Go and get two pigs out of
Keevil & Weston's, in the name of Cotton"- went there and Moses
Bernard gave me two dead pigs-I came outside the shop, and seeing
the prisoner, I said, Where are you going to have these two pigs
he said, Follow me, and I will show you took me to Eastman's
cold air store-there was a large truok outside, and he told me to put
them down there, which I did-he then gave me 3d., and I went to
where I generally work-I was arrested for having obtained the pigs by
false pretences; I was remanded first and eventually discharged.
Cross-eramined. The reason that I recollect that morning so well is
that I was taken to the Snow Hill police station on the Tuesday
morning after, in consequence of what happened- do not suggest that
the prisoner told me that the pigs were his.
CHARLES WRIGaT. I live at 1, Clifton Terrace, Finsbury Park, and

-I 8aw Eaton outside there, but I did not see him do anything- I saw

where to.
Cross-eramined. I have lost my license- good many men have lost
their licenses-there is a lot of pig lifting going on.
