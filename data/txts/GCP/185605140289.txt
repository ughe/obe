COURT. Q. Have you been long acquainted with Charlotte King A.
About four or five months, but I have known her since she was a ehid; we
were both children together in Norfolk-I lived at Market Dereham, and
she at Bargewell—I have been in London about three years. I knew that
she had this money. she was going to meet some friends, and took out the
money I wished her to leave it at home by friends, I mean females I
do not know what she was going to do, or any object which she had in
taking out the money--we lodge at Mr. Watkins's, we are the only two
girls in the house I have got my room, and she has got her's--we do not
live together_I know Mrs. Smith who keeps the coffee house, we were in
the habit of going there almost every night to have supper the Crown
public house, where we were going to meet, is not far, it is about: five
minutes walk-I was in the habit of going to the Crown as well as to this
place.
