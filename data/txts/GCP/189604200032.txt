cannot say how I got to the station; I believe the police took me there

complained to someone about my head, I cannot say to whom I think
the doctor must have attended to my head-I had between £3 and £4
when I went out that evening; I paid some money out of that, I don't
think the prisoners had more than from £1 to £2-I only paid twopence
for drink that evening-no one else paid anything for me-I only had
one glass of beer-I charged the prisoners on the Monday-I saw them
as they were being brought into the dock, and recognised them-I never
appeared before a Court before.
