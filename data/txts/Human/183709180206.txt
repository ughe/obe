WILLIAM TERRY. I am a butcher, at Clapham. The prisoner was in my service—he was in the habit of receiving money on my account—✗ was always a rule laid down to him, to take the money and bring the bills to me, or his mistress to write the receipt, and take them back—it was his duty to pay me the money—Mr. Cubitt was a customer—the prisoner did not account to me for 1 l . 10 s . 5 d ., paid on the 10th of August—he paid the sum of 2 l . 5 s . 8 d . after I challenged him with it in my own house, he gave me two sovereigns, two half-crowns, and two sixpences, and I found the bills in his cart.
   Prisoner . The 1 l . 10 s . 5 d . I spent—I paid away some money to my club. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prosecutor . Confined Three Months; Three Weeks Solitary.

 2172. FANNY REED was indicted for stealing, on the   28th of August 2 handkerchiefs, value 6 s  .; and 2 towels, value 1 s  .; the goods of James Bannatts James . JAMES BANNATTS JAMES. I am a mariner, and belong to the Exchange  of St. Ives. On the 28th of August she was lying in Alderman's tier—I had been at Waterford, and there by accident I met the prisoner—I took her on board, and she was three weeks on board—I had some business on shore, and left her the key of the state room—I returned at half-past eleven o'clock at night, and then she was gone, and the state room was locked—I waited, and she did not come—I dropped asleep, and the next morning I broke open the door and found my drawers were all open, and this property and some Irish notes gone—I heard she had gone off for Cork—I came to London, and found her lodgings, and went for an officer and found this property in her box—one of my towels has my name on it—the other has not.
   Prisoner . The handkerchief he gave me, and the towels were put in with them—he said he intended to marry me, but I found he had a wife at St. Ives, and I left him—he had promised me marriage. Witness . I am married—my wife is in London now. JOHN WOODHOUSE. I am a police-sergeant. I found the prisoner in Unicorn-square, Tooley-street—the prosecutor pointed her out—before I opened her box I found the two towels and handkerchief—she said she knew nothing about any of them—she did not then say that he gave them to her.
   Prisoner . The handkerchief he gave me—I met him in a public house, and he said he should like to be with me—he took me on board his vessel and kept me better than three weeks, and told every one that I was his wife—I found he was going after another woman, and then I left him—I took my things, but I did not know it—the handkerchiefs he gave me, and he might know that, as I wore them all the time I was in the ship.
