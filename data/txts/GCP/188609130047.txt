Eleventh Session, 1885–86.
910. EMILY SARAH HOGSDEN (22), Unlawfully endeavouring to
conceal the birth of her child.
MR. HORACE AVORY Prosecuted; Mr. BESLEY Defended.
GUILTY. Strongly recommended to meroy by the Jury. She received a good
character.-One Week's Imprisonment.
911. ALEXANDER ROSS (24), Feloniously forging and uttering an
order for the delivery of a cheque-book, with intent to defraud.
The prisoner stated that he wished to plead guilty to this indictment, upon
thich the Jury found a verdict of
GUILTY.
912. ALEXANDER ROSS was again indicted for feloniously forging
and uttering an order for the payment of 3001., with intent to defraud.
Mr. FULTON Prosecuted.
ROBERT FRITI WYATT. I am a clerk in the London and Westminster
Bank, Lothbury-on 11th March I received this order for a cheque-book
-I believed it to come from William Potter, a customer, and delivered
a book of 200 cheques—this cheque (produced) is the first cheque out of
that book.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I do not recognise you-the person
signed a receipt, "F. H. Smith for Mr. Potter."
ROBERT THOMPSON. I am a paying cashier at the London and West-
minster Bank-on 11th March I cashed this cheque, believing it to be
Mr. Potter's signature-I gave ten 51. notes, 02968 to 02977, eight 101.
notes, 60593 to 60600, two 107. notes, 60401 and 60402, and 150l. in
gold-I do not recognise the prisoner.
Cross-examined. The cheque is endorsed “George Brooks."
WILLIAM MOSELEY POTTER. I am a tea merchant, of 7, Mincing Lane
-on 10th March my father was living, and was at the head of the
business--some time after March my attention was drawn to a debit of
3001. in the pass-book, and I went to the London and Westminster Bank,
inspected these documents, and declared them to be forgeries--they are
a fair imitation of my father's writing-he was then alive, and saw them
-the prisoner was in his employ, but left in 1883--these documents,
1 to 9, are in his writing.
CHARLES JOHN WILLIAMS. I am a clerk in the accountants' branch
of the Bank of England-I produce ten bl. notes, 02968 to 02977, eight
101. notes, 60593 to 60000, and two 107. notes, 60401 and 60402-16 of
those notes are endorsed " Geo. Brooks," and two of them “F. Bowen,
37, Barnsbury Square," one " Geo. Dakin," and one is without endorse-
ment-the words "Mincing Lane" are on one note.
Cross-examined. We have marks on the notes to show from what bank
they come to us-notes 02968, 02970, 64294, and 64295 were paid in to
the account of the Postmaster-General.
WILLIAM WRIGHT (City Detective Sergeant). On 11th August I arrested
the prisoner at his lodgings, on a charge of forging the order for &
cheque-book, and found these documents, which I produced at the police-
court, and the clerk numbered them 1 to 9.
Cross-examined. I also found 16 pawnbrokers' duplicates, dated from
December 1st, 1885, to June 4th, 1886.
