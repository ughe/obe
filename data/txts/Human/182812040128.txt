256. ALEXANDER MORGAN was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of December , 2 sovereigns, the monies of Alexander Souter.
   ALEXANDER SOUTER. I am a sailor. Last Thursday I was in bed at a lodging-house, and about eleven o'clock in the morning I lost two sovereigns from my pocket by my bed-side - I can swear they were in it the night before; there was no other money in it - the prisoner slept in the same room; he was gone as well as the money - I have seen one sovereign and 3s. 2 1/2d. since - the prisoner came to the house before I got up, paid his lodging, and went away; five persons slept in the room - I do not know whether the prisoner had any money the night before.
 Prisoner. He had been out all night, and came in the morning very much the worse for liquor. Witness. I been over to the Borough, but came home at six o'clock sober, and went to bed - I had the two sovereigns then; I had my trousers on.
   THOMAS PITTLELADDS. I slept in this room - I saw the prosecutor come in; I then turned out, and never went to sleep any mone - I asked the prisoner if he were going to turn out; he said No - I went down, and came in again in a few minutes.
   ADAM HIGH. I took the prisoner into custody. On our way to the office my brother officer called out that he had got something in his hand; he shuffled it and tried to put it into his other pocket - I took hold of his hand at the office, and found a sovereign and 3s. 2 1/2d. - he had a new pair of shoes on, hardly soiled, and a second-hand hat, which he said he had bought that morning; he said he had been on board a ship in the London Docks, and had got the money for his work - I said I would go on board the ship; he then said he had it from Berwick, from his wife - I said I knew the Berwick smack, and I would go on board; he then said."No, no, I had it from a man you cannot find" - he was a little in liquor.
   ANN PUGH. The prisoner came to our house on the Monday night and slept there; on Tuesday morning he brought his chest to our house, and asked my mistress to lend him 1s. - she said she had no change; he came and asked me to lend him 1s. - I said I had but half a crown, and lent him that; on the Tuesday night he slept at our house - my mistress told me to call him up, and said he should not stop in the house - he asked her to let him stop that night, and said he would go away the next day, which he did.
   SAMUEL CROMARTY. I took the prisoner with the other officer; I saw his hand in his pocket; he drew it out, and, I saw a piece of paper, and called to the other officer - I seized his wrist, and he passed the paper out of one hand into the other, and clapped it into his pocket; my brother officer found in it one sovereign and 3s. 2 1/2d.
 Prisoner Defence. I went to Blackwall on Tuesday to get a vessel to the West Indies; I went into a public-house to get some bread and beer - a Scotchman there asked me if I knew where he could buy some tools - I got my chest, and sold him my tools for 1l. 15s. 4d.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. Confined Six Months.
 257.  JOHN KING was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of December , 2lbs. weight of butter, value 2s., the goods of Robert Cole.
   WILLIAM ALLARD. I am porter to Robert Cole, a cheesemonger, of Wigmore-street. This 2lbs. of butter was taken from my basket, last Wednesday, at a quarter before nine o'clock in the morning; I was in Wyndham-place serving a customer, and I saw the prisoner at the basket -I ran and pursued; he ran into Crawford-street, and he went off, I cried "Stop thief!" and did not lose sight of him but in turning the corner - there was one more person running after him with me. I am sure he is the person I saw at the basket; he had thrown the butter over the rails of an area, which he had passed.
   PHILIP WEBSTER. I took the prisoner.
   ELIZABETH BURGIN. I saw the boy that was running throw the butter over the rails of the area; I saw two men run after him, and I went over and saw the butter.
 Prisoner Defence. I went up the street, and saw the basket; I turned up another street; the man came and said I tried to take something out of his basket - I said I had not.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.
 258.  MARY GREEN was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of December , 1 shovel, value 2s., and 5 pieces of wood, value 2s., the goods of David Sage.
 DAVID SAGE. I am a  builder. I lost some wood, and a shovel, last Thursday, from a field where I am building; I did not see them taken - but I had been robbed before, and set a man to watch; these are the articles.
   THOMAS SAGE. I was desired to watch in the field, where there is a house in an unfinished state; it is called Stone-field, near White Conduit-house. About half-past eight o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner come into the field, and go into a cottage; she then came out, and took four pieces of wood - she then took the shovel out of the cottage; she came out with this wood and the shovel; when she got about one hundred yards, I took her with them.
   JAMES GRIFFITHS. I am a bricklayer's labourer. This shovel is mine; it was in the house that is building for Mr. Sage.
   GEORGE UPWARD. I took the prisoner: she said she hoped Mr. Sage would not be severe with her.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was coming from work - this young man seized me, and said, "I am looking out for you;" I said, "For what?" he said, "You want to steal that wood over the way:" I said, "No, I don't want to take it."
 GUILTY. Aged 38. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Fourteen Days.
