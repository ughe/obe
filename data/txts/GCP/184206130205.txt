1945. CORNELIUS SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing, on the
14th of June, 4 lbs. weight of lead, value 8d., the goods of Edward
Sayers, in a certain port of entry and discharge.
Joux Leith. I am chief mate of the barque James Cruikshanks, in the
West India Docks. The prisoner came on board on the afternoon of the
14th of June, between four and five o'clock-I was on the quay-I saw
him walk from the larboard side on the deck towards the starboard-I
followed, and as I went to the gangway, he met me, and went over the
side again I heard from those on board that he had something with him
- he ran round the course of the sheds, and heaved something into the shed,
then came and wished me to search him-I went to the place, and saw this
lead picked up-it was the apron of a gun--it came from the starboard-
side of the deck-it is the property of Captain Edward Sayers.
George GORDON. I was on board, and saw the prisoner take something
off the gun, and gave information to the carpenter, who told the mate-I
saw the apron was gone from the gun, and I had seen the prisoner kneel-
ing on the gun-this is the lead—it is used for the apron of the gun.
Prisoner's Defence. I was coming there; I passed by the ship; a man
opposite spoke to me, and about ten minutes afterwards the policeman
came and said, “Halloo, come back;" I stood a moment, and then went;

1946. HANNAH EDMUNDS and MARY NEWLAND were indicted
for stealing, on the 14th of June, 2 pewter pots, value 38., the goods of
Peter Richard James.
2
Peter Richard James. I am a victualler, and live in Charles-street,
Berkley-square-these two pots are mine.
CHARLES Davis (police-constable C 47.) About half-past two o'clock
in the morning of the 14th of June I was on duty in Hayes-mews,
Berkley-square-I saw Newland take these two quart pots from the cill of a
stable window—she ran round from the Mews in the direction of Berkley-
square-I took her-Edmunds was in Charles-street, not near enough
to see Newland-I asked Newland to give me the pots that she had just
stolen from the window-she gave them to me-Edmunds was there in
company with her--they directly ran away-Edmunds was not there when
Newland took them. se
Newland's Defence. I was out with Edmunds to look after a servant ;
we saw the pots, and thought we would take care of them till the morning,
and the publicans would give us a few halfpence.
