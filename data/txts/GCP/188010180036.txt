bedroom-I carried his things to No. 44 bedroom-he occupied that room
till he was taken into custody--the woman came the next morning-she

described the male prisoner-I told her he was out-she went away-
she gave no name, nor did the male prisoner-when Inspector Butcher
camo I showed him No. 44 room--he searched the prisoner's portmanteau
