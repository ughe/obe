1204. JULIA BURKE was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of March,
4 frocks, value 48.9; 3 pairs of shoes, value 18. 6d. 2 napkins, value 6d.
and 2) yards of carpet, value 4s.; the goods of John Sawyer, her master
ANBLIA SAWYBR. I am the wife of Join Sawyer, and live in Bulling-
lam-place, Kensington. The prisoner lived with me three weeks-I dis-
charged her, and paid her her wages in the presence of a policeman, as she
was very insolent to me-about half an hour after she was gone the police
man came-I went with him to his house, and saw these things--the are
mine-| had not missed them.
JANEs BANHAM. I am a policeman- live next door but one to the
prosecutrix. On the 25th of Mareh the prisoner left this bundle at my
house unknown to me-she came about seven o'clock, and said she had left
a bundle with my wife, and wanted it-ashe stood talking with me some
time-my wife came in, and asked her to stop to tea-she said she had no
objection-ghe went to the bundle, and got a little tea from it--I caught
sight of the things, and thought they were not hers, and gave notice to the
prosecutrix-the prisoner said at the station-house, that it was liquor had
done it-she said the nursery maid had given her a pair of shoes, sbe did
not know what was in the bundle, she was so intoxicated, she took all the
other things but the pair of shoes-she was in liquor-she gave my wife
1s. after tea to get some rum, but I did not allow ber.
