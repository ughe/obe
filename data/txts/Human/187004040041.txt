   MR. BESLEY . Q.  You say you were summoned for obtaining 7 s . by false pretences; when was that? A.  It was to have been heard last Saturday week, but they did not appear—I was informed that that charge was made through the prisoner by the person who took out the summons—no such charge was made till after I had appeared as a witness against him.
   COURT . Q.  Where did you get that staff? A.  It was presented to me—I took it with me for my own protection, for fear of being molested by the prisoner or his gang; I always carry it about with me at night. WILLIAM ROUSE.
