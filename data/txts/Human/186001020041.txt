   MR. HAWKINS . Q.  Will you undertake to swear that you ever spoke to him about that particular account? ( selecting one ) A.  Yes, I will, about this particular account that I have in my hand—I do not mean to say that this paper was produced—I do not know that any paper was produced at all; he did not always produce papers when he conversed with me about money—I cannot speak to any particular conversation—I cannot say whose hand writing—this is; I should suppose an accountant's—I believe it is either in Mellers handwriting, or Ellis's—should not like to swear to their hand writing.
