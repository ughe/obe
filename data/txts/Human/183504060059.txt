   Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant .
 935. JOHN WILLIAM STRUDWICK was indicted for embezzlement, to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Seven Days.
 (The prisoner received a good character, and was recommended to mercy.)
 936. ANN CLANCEY  , alias SULLIVAN, was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of March , 1 pair of shoes, value 4 s  .; the goods of James Rider . JAMES RIDER. I live in Dock-street, St. George's—the prisoner was invited to tea with my wife, on the the 14th of March—I went to get a candle—I returned in five minutes, and met the prisoner in my passage going away—I went up to my room and missed a pair of shoes, which I had left when I went out—I could not find the prisoner till Monday—these are my shoes—they are new ones. JOSEPH GERRARD. I am in the service of a pawnbroker. On the 14th of March, the prisoner pawned these shoes with me, for 2 s . 6 d .
   Prisoner . The prosecutor's wife sent me to pawn the shoes in my own name, while he was gone for the candle. ANN RIDER. I am the prosecutor's wife—I did not give her the shoes to pawn, or to take away. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined One Month.
 937. CHARLES DOWNES was Indicted for stealing, on the   10th of February , 2 coats, value 6 s  .; 1 frock, value 1 s  .; and 1 pair of shoes, value 2 s  .; the goods of Robert Downes . JANE DOWNES. I am the prisoner's mother. I live on Bethnal Green. On the 10th of February, I left the prisoner and my other, son to mind my child—I returned within an hour, and my other little boy said something—the prisoner was then gone—I missed the two coats, and the other articles—I did not see the prisoner again till the Saturday, when he was brought back for stealing a blanket—he said he had sold these things in Rosemary-lane, to a man who lived near St. Paul's. PATRICK LARKING( police-constable H  125.) I took the prisoner—I heard him say, he had sold the things in Rosemary-lane, to a man who lived near St. Paul's—I tried to trace them, but could not. GUILTY. Aged 14.— Transported for Seven Years.
