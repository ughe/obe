   MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  What are you? A.  A baker by trade—I kept a shop near him, about seven years—I was a publican, but I am now out of business—I had a little property when I left my house, and I am expecting a situation—when I let my house, I had money sufficient to keep me—I let it to another party for 150 l  —I let it to a person named Brown—his uncle or father keeps a public-house in the Kent-road—Brown has left the house, and I think he went to Chelmsford—I got about 150 l  —I cannot tell exactly—I let it for Messrs. Meux—they can tell—I will not swear I got that.
   Q.  What did you get? A.  136 l . 10 s . I think—I cannot tell exactly—it is twelve months ago—I might owe some debts—I did owe about 30 l  —I owed nothing to Meux—I owed my distiller 15 l  —his name is Mayston—he lives close by Lambeth-walk—I cannot tell where, upon my honour—I owed my ale brewer, Mr. Goding, of Knightsbridge, about 14 l  —his col lecting-clerk called on me once a month—I do not know his name, he lived at Vauxhall, but I do not recollect his number—it is close by the turnpike, just in the Wandsworth-road—I never was at his house—I now live at No. 24, John-street, New-road—I have my receipt at home, no doubt.
   Q.  What time did the prisoner leave this card party? A.  I think about two o'clock—we parted good friends—I do not recollect that we shook hands—we were all friendly—there was no complaint on either side—I did not see the bag in which the prosecutor had his money—he paid in sovereigns, and some silver, when the bet was 10 l ., there was a £5 note I saw—the pro secutor lost, altogether, about 16 l  —the prisoner's partner was a person named Shillitoe—he is a cigar merchant—I do not know where he lives—I have known him about a year—I lost about 2 l ., and paid it—I had about 12 s . of my own, and 30 s . I borrowed, and have not paid it—it has not been convenient to pay it.
   Q.  But you have money? A.  No—I did not say I have it now—I had it—I do not know exactly how I am living—I am in view of a situation—I am in no business—my property is nearly gone—I pay every body—I have not paid the prosecutor his 30 s .—he has not asked me for it—I never saw him till he took me no in Bow-street—he took me for this money—he said I had robbed him, and the Magistrate discharged me—I have not seen Shillitoe since—I do not know where he is.
