omnibus-Evans was conductor that day-we started from the Goose and
Gridiron, St. Paul's-churchyard, at twenty minutes to eleven o'clock, and

Court. Q. How long do you stay at the White Horse-eellar?
Sometimes it takes more time, as loading parcels is sometimes rather more,
but about ten minutes-he could not go from there to Oxford-street in ten
minutes, and he would be obliged to go and get his way-bill.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'Brien. Q. Have you any recollection of the
day? A. Yes, I recollect it well, because while we were changing horses I
and the landlord's son went and had a glass of stout each, at a quarter before

Richmond conveyance omnibus, belonging to the same Company-I never
drove him; I have known him two or three years-Evans has been a
conductor two years I have known him upwards of two years and a half.
