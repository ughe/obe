Cross-examined by MR. CHAMBERS. Q. As soon as you had signed this
for 148. 1d. did you see Mellish put his initials “T. R. M. ?" 2. Well, I

them into the desk-Mellish was not at the desk-he was at the other end of
the room, at the side of the large table by the window sometimes he was
there and sometimes at the desk-Douglas was at the desk, I believe, and

CHARLES HUNT. I am a gilder, of Suffolk-street, St. James's. I did
work for Varnish and Co., of Berners-street-1 had worked for Mellish when
he was carrying on business for himself-he sent me the work--this account
for 17. is in my writing (marked "Y") it is not receipted-this other
account for 17., due on 26th Oct. for work from Varnish and Co., is receipted
"Paid, Charles Hunt," in my son's writing.
CHARLES Hunt, Jun. This " Paid, Charles Hunt," on this paper is in
my writing- I received 178. on that occasion, not 31. 178., which this bill
purports to be for- I took the bill with me for 179. I cannot recollect who
wrote out the paper-I am quite sure I only received 178.
CHARLES HUNT re-examined. On 21st Dec, 21. 68, 10d, was due to me
from the firm--this is my bill for the amount, but it is now 27. 168. 10d.
(marked " Z")—the "1" has been put since I made it out-I sent my son to
receive it-he always brought me the sum I sent for--he brought me 21. 6s. 10d.
on this occasion.
