WILLIAM GEORGE WOOD. I am one of the principal warders at the Chatham
convict prison-I know the prisoner by the name of John Lundgren-he
was received at the Chatham prison on 7th August, 1868, under sentence
of seven years he was discharged on 25th March, 1873, on licence-I saw
him frequently every day for a considerable number of months or years, I
may say.
