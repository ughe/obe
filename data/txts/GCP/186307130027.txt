888. ELLEN ROBINSON (27), and EMMA DAVIS (28), Robbery on
Robert Smith, and stealing 1 watch, his property.
MR. DICKIE conducted the Prosecution.
ROBERT SMITH. I am mate of the Ann, lying at Limehouse-wharf,
Ratcliffe-about 1 o'clock, on the morning of 11th June, I was walking
down a street in Ratcliffe, towards my ship, and met the prisoners—Robin-
son asked me to treat them-I said I had got no money to treat them-I
turned myself round and was walking away, and I got a severe blow from
a fist, and was knocked down-I felt them take my watch from my pocket
--the chain broke--I seized one by the arm and one by the shawl, and she
got away and left her shawl in my handa policeman came up, and asked.
me to look in my waistcoat-pocket-I did so, and found my watch had been
put back-it was rather dark where I was knooked down.
Cross-examined by MR. TAYLOR. Q. Who was it took your watch! 4. I
don't know which of them took it; Robinson put it back—I was quite
sober-I had not been to a public-house that night-I did not run after
the other woman; the policeman did--they did not walk with me two
yards—I have not said it was the little woman that took my watch - this is
my signature to my deposition. (This stated, “The watch was taken by
the little one.") I can't say for a truth which took it, for I was half-stunned.
with the blow, and was bleeding terribly.
HENRY HAYNES (Policeman, 258). On the morning of 11th June, &
little past 1, I saw the prosecutor in the Broad-street, Ratcliffe-he was
just rising on his legs, holding the prisener Robinson, and she had hold of
him, and I saw her put the watch into his waistcoat-pocket-he had a shawl
in his hand.
Cross-examined, Q. How far were you from the spot when you spoke to
the prosecutor about his watch? A. It was before we removed from the
spot; I said, "What is that she has put into your pocket ?"-he pulled it
out, and said, “It looks like my watch, but it is broken if it is"-he looked
at it again at the station, and found that it was his.
ROBINSON-GUILTY.-Confined Six Months.
DAVIS-Nor Guilty.
