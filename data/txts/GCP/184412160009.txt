WILLIAM PAYNE. I am a farrier at Mr. Beck's, at No. 19, North-
wharf-road, Paddington. I remember Larkin bringing a horse to our shop
to have the sboes taken off-it was a grey horse, as far as I can describe
hím-I cannot exactly say the height-I have seen the horse since-Mr.
Larkin brought him last week, to know if we could recollect the horse
coming to have his shoes taken off-I cannot recollect when it was
brought, but my master has looked through the books-he is not here
my master has got a clerk that keeps the books they are not here, nor is
the clerk.
Court. Q. Who brought you here? A. I came here with Mr. Larkin
- it was in May that he brought the horse-I could not see that it had
any initial on it-the horse was new shod five days after, as near as we
can recollect--if there had been any brand-mark on the hoofs we should
have seen it when we clenched the horse's feet up-I did not do that my.
self, I only took the shoes off when the horse was brought I did not
notice any brand-mark-we have had plenty of other horses to shoe, but
Larkin did not bring any other-we do not write down the marks of all
the horses that come to us.
