3117. JOHN HARWELL was indicted for obtaining money by false
pretences.
HARRIET RBED. I am servant to Mr. Henry Gooch, at Camberwell. I
received this paper from the collector of the Vauxhall Water-works, for 11.
11s. 6d. due for water-the prisoner afterwards came-he said he had called
for the water-rate-] asked if it was right-he said, 64 Yes, quite right".
asked him to give me a receipt, or sign the bill-he said he could not write,
but he would put his cross-I believed he came from the collector, and had a
right to come from him, for the rate, and I paid him the money on that belief,
and he put his cross to this paper.
HENRY WALLER. I am the superintendant of the Vaaxhall Water-works
Our collector is Mr. Benjamin Walmsley-the prisoner had no right whatever
to call for this money-] is not connected with the Company.
Prisoner. I did not call for any rate for the water-it is for watering the
roads that I have got the contract for.
