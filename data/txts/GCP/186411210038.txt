came near him-he cut me on the back of the arm and the back of the wrist,
and made several stabs at my body-I called out, " Take the knife out of his
hand I" and a young man named Brown came up.
BENJAMIN BROWN. I live at 6, Pierpoint-terrace-on the night of 4th

THOMAS WILLIAM HARN. I am a surgeon of Pierpoint-terrace on the

came near him-I dressed the wound on the prosecutor's band-it was a
superficial cut. Guilty.**-Seven Years' Penal Servitude.-
41. HENRY SPARKS (25), embezzling the sums of 21. 78. 6d., 61. 88. Od.
and 158. 6d., the moneys of William Langley and another, his masters.
MR. LANGFORD conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN LONG. I am an artificial florist, and live at 57, Middleton-street,
Clerkenwell-I know the prisoner from his calling on me for orders for
Messrs. Langley and Young--on 22d July I owed them 21. 78. 6d., which I
paid to the prisoner, and for which he gave me this receipt (produced)-I
saw him write it
Thomas EDWARD HOBBS. I carry on business at 30, Great Pulteney-
street-on 6th October I owed Messrs. Langley and Young 6l. 148. 9d.-
know the prisoner from his calling on me for orders--I paid him that
amount by cheque, less discount—this is his receipt (produced).
BENJAMIN STACEY. I manage the business of Messrs. Stacey, of 3 and
4, New Inu-yard I know the prisoner as a travelleron 2d October we
owed Messrs. Langley and Young 158. 6d. - the prisoner called for it and I
paid bim--this bill (produced) is receipted by the prisoner.
WILLIAM LANGLEY. I am in partnership with Mr. Young at No. 3,
Salter's Hall-court--the prisoner was our traveller and collector--he has
been in our service sixteen or eighteen months it was his duty to solicit orders
and collect accounts when he was authorized to do so—the prisoner Las never
acoonnted to me for 21. 78. 6d. received of Mr. Long; be ought to have done
so, either to me or to my partner-he has not accounted to: 61. 8s. Od. received
from Mr. Hobbs on 6th October, nor for 158. 6d. received from Mr. Stacey
on the 22d of October.
GUILTY.-Recommended to mercy.--Confined Six Months.
