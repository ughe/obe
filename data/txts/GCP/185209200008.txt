told me they were perfectly respectable gentlemen-I told him as the Govern.
ment officer had not yet passed the ship, as soon as he had done so, if
he wrote to me I would come down and pay the money-he said the

to pay the money to secure a place I then paid half the passage money,

there-he spoke to me first-he told me he was going to write to me that
night, but he had been so very busy, or else he should have done so before;

ships, which were to take the name of “The Star of the South Packets;" that
I should see an advertisement in the paper the day following announcing the
fact, and that one, which was coming up from the Clyde, was to be called the
