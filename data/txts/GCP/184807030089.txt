HORACE HARDY (policeman, G 15.) I was present at the meeting at
Clerkenwell-green on 29th May. I heard two speakers--- procession was
afterwards formed they went through Finsbury-square, where they wound
ound several times, and they went through the streets towards the West, and
ultimately came to a coffee-shop in Redcross-street, from where people
addressed them--the City police then interfered, and dispersed the mob-I
unnot say whether there was any resistance; I went out of it.

about an hour after, and found that they were I should say there were
3,000 people-I do not know how many police there were, I was in the
