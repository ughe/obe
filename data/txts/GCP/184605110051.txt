GEORGE MADDOX. I am inspector of the Thames-police. I apprehended

blood on his frock, which he said was caused by a scuffle with some of the
workmen, which made his nose bleed.

1028. TIMOTHY COLEMAN was indicted for a robbery on William
Pursey, putting him in fear, and stealing from his person and against his will
1 hall-crown and 3 shillings; his monies and beating, striking, and using
other personal violence to him.
WILLIAM PURSEY. I am eighty-five years old. Between eight and nine
o'clock on the night of the 29th of April I was going along the Cambridge
Heath-road, and saw the prisoner outside a public-house with a pipe-I
heard him say he had been asleep all his lifetime-I said softly to myself,
"And so have I," but he heard me I had my hand in my pocket-he
turned round upon me and said, “What, have you been asleep?" and be
took and held me down by the arms, put his hand into my waistcoat pocket,
and took my money, I called out for help, "The fellow is robbing me"-he
said, "Hold your tongue, you old fool; I won't hurt you"-he attempted
to run away-1 put my stick in the collar of his coat-he got away from me,
and ran across the Mile-end-road-be put the money on a cask-a boy going
by took it up, and said, “I have got your money, he has put it down here"
-I had said to the prisoner, "You rascal, you have robbed me of half-a-crown
and three shillings."
