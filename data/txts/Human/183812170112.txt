   Cross-examined by MR. RYLAND . Q.  You were conscious of dropping your bag as you were stepping out? A.  I missed it in a minute after wards—I was not conscious of it at the moment—I went to the side of the horses to speak to the coachman, and continued there about a minute and a half—when I had done me—not more than two minutes elapsed between the girl speaking to me and my speaking to the prisoners—I found them talking together—I first stated that my bag contained sovereigns and half-sovereigns when I got to the station-house—no one asked before that what my bag contained—I know M'Gowan, a shop-keeper—after I left the prisoners in the street, and had gone back to my own house, I was passing spoke to him in his shop, nor he to me, nor at the door—when we were going to the station-house, I asked him what sort of a sovereign Mrs. Higgins had changed with him—he said, "A new one, to the best
