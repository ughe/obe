 366. JOHN HIGGINS  (35) was indicted for a rape on Deborah Withers .
   MR. POYNTER Prosecuted. GUILTY. — Five Years' Penal Servitude.
 367. EDWARD SIMMONS  (23) for a rape on Theresa Robinson .
   MR. LAWLESS Prosecuted; MR. ABINGER Defended. NOT GUILTY. He was again  indicted for a like offence upon Elizabeth Casey , upon which no evidence was offered. NOT GUILTY.

ALICE MARY PAYNE  (24) PLEADED GUILTY to stealing a brooch and ring, the goods of John Marks and others; also  to stealing a gold watch, the goods of James William Benson ; also  to stealing a gold and diamond ring, the goods of Alfred Shepherd and George Reed ; and also  to stealing a chain, the goods of Samuel Smith and others.
