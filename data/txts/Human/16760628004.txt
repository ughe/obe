I am killed ---- I am a dead man. In the mean time the Boy little thought what mischief he had done, and was only troubled at the loss of the Gunstick, which he missed assoon as he had discharged the Piece. But many people hearing the report of the Gun, and seeing from whence it proceeded, presently ran thither to enquire; going up stairs they finde the Boy with the Gun, who confessed he had shot it off; but when they told him, he had killed a Man, he struck his hand on his Breast, crying out, Ah! What shall I do, I am undone: testifying that he did not intend or imagine any hurt to any Creature, and expressing much sorrow for the same, as he did now likewise upon his Tryal.
