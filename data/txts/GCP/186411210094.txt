WILLIAM MARKWELL (Policeman, A 476). I was on duty in the Black
friars-road on Monday evening. 14th October--the prisoner came to me,

whom I found bleeding at the back of the ear, and he made a charge against
the prisoner for cutting him with a knife - I took the prisoner into custody,
and at the station house searched him, and found this knife (before produced)
in his pocket--the prisoner said he did not do it.
