 852. EDWARD HATTON was indicted for burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of John Dorrell , about the hour of five o'clock in the night, on the   31st of January , at   St. Saviour, Southwark , with intent to steal. JOHN DORRELL. I am a dealer in marine-stores, living in Redcross-street, in the parish of St. Saviour, Southwark—I have purchased things of the prisoner. Last Monday night I shut up at ten o'clock all safe—my wife and daughter and myself sleep in the house—my son and a friend sat up to watch—I came down about five o'clock on Tuesday morning—I thought it was six—I told my son it was six, and he might leave—he said it was only five—I went to bed again—I had not gone five minutes be. fore this happened—it was a quarter-past five—I had lost money before this, and could not account how it had gone. RICHARD BROWN. I am a warehouseman, living in Cross-lane—I sat up two or three nights at this house. Between five and six o'clock on Tuesday morn ing, I saw the prisoner get over the wall, which is ten feet high, and lower himself down into the back-yard—he then moved a tub which covered a grating, and lifted up the grating, and dropped himself down into the cellar—a friend, who was with me, let the grating down—we took a light and found the prisoner—he had gone from the back-cellar to the front-cellar, and had got his shoes off ready to come up Stairs—there is a way up from there. FREDERICK LONG( police-constable M  100.) I took the prisoner.
 (William Hatton, a labourer, gave the prisoner a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 24.— Transported for Ten Years.
 853. CHARLOTTE PENNYFATHER was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of July , 3 sheets, value 6 s  .; 1 table-cloth, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; 1 shirt, value 2 s  .; 3 waistcoats, value 6 s  . 6 d  .; 1 coat, value 8 s  .; and 12 yards of ticking, value 6 s  .; the goods of John Rye , her master. JOHN RYE. I am a coachman, in the service of Mr. Sutton, of Dulwich—the prisoner was my housekeeper—she had no right to dispose of, or to pawn any of my property. On the 11th of January I missed these things produced, which are all mine—the prisoner absconded that day without notice. RICHARD LAW. I am a pawnbroker. I have three sheets, two waist coats, and one table-cloth—they were pawned by the prisoner at various times. JOHN BRAGG. I am a pawnbroker. I have a bed-tick, a coat, and pair of trowsers pawned by the prisoner.
