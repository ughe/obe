877. CHARLES DAVIES, JAMES MILLS, and CATHERINE
NEALE, were indicted for stealing, on the 27th of February, 2 shirts,
value 5s., the good of William Murfit.
WILLIAM MURFIT. I live in the parish of St. George the Martyr,
Southwark. I had three shirts hanging in my yard on the 27th of Fe-
bruary-I saw them safe at three o'cloek, and missed them at a quarter
three-I found them in pawn the same evening-these are them.
CHARLOTTE PIGG. I know both these boys by sightI saw them on
the 27th of February-they went down the court where the prosecutor
lives about three o'clock-I had seen the shirts hanging there-I saw
them come up the court again, and a boy who is not present gave Davies
something white--they then went away together.
WILLIAM PARR. I am a pawnbrokery I took in two shirts, on the
27th of February, of the female prisoner, in the name of Ann Johnson-I
sent them the same evening to Union Hall.
JOSEPH CLOTHIER. I received the shirts from Parr, and gave them to
the officer.
GEoRGs SAWYER (police-constable M 61.) I took the two boys-
Davies said he gave the shirts to Neale-ishe was taken, and asked where
she pawned them, and she told.
Mills's Defence. I never saw the shirts I did not know what I was
taken for.
DAVIES-GUILTY. Aged 22.
MILLS - GUILTY. Aged 21.
Transported for Seven Years.
NEALE-NOT GUILTY.
878. JOSEPH BEADLE and WILLIAM BEADLE were indicted
for stealing, on the 5th of March, 500lbs. of coals, value 68., the goods of
Robert Wildman Parchard, in a certain barge upon a navigable river called
the Thames.
HENRY MAYNARD. I am a coal-merchant, and live at No. 9, Elm-
wharf, Battersea. On the 5th of March I saw the two prisoners in a boat,
reaching the coals out of a barge into the boatoI walked down my yard,
and jumped over the wharf on the barge-I asked what they were doing,
and tried to cateh James Beadle-he said, 44 Do not take any notice, we
have only got a few"-they then shoved the boat off-I took up some
coals to throw at them, but they still kept rowing on; and I called my
boy to bring me a gun, and said I would shoot them-I saw a police-boat,
and called it; the prisoners threw the coals overboard, and they were
taken-there was the name of 66 James" on the barge.
HENRY Cox. I am servant to Mr. Robert Wildman Parchard. He
had a barge in the river, called the James- was lying at the wharf of
the last witness- gave orders for her to go there to deliver seven tons of
coals.
Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. Do you know how much the
barge contained? A. Twenty-eight tons-she had delivered seven tons
there the night before ; the other twenty-one tons have not been de-
livered.
(Elizabeth Dunkley, Diana Curtain, and Charlotte Paris, gave the pri-
soners a good character.)
JAMES BEADLE-GUILTY. Aged 8.-Confined One Year.
WILLIAM BEADLE-GUILTY Aged 7.-Confined Three Months.
