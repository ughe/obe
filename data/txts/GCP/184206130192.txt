1924. JAMES FORD was indicted for embezzlement.
CHARLES SMITH. I am a baker, and live at Chiswick--the prisoner
was my servant. On the 16th of May I sent him to Strand-on-the-Green,
to Mr. Jeffries and Mr. Heman with bills-he was to receive the money
from them-he returned, and worked that week on the 23rd I sent him
out with bread, and to deliver bills again--he returned at half-past six
o'clock-next morning I met him, and asked him where he was going
and he said he was going to send a man to do his work-he had not
then paid me either of these sums.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Did you ask when he returned on
the 16th, if he had received the money? A. He did not receive these
sums till the 23rd, when he went out with his bread he returned, and ser
his sponge, but did not book his bread-1 did not see him again till the
Thursday when he was given into custody-a person named Burt offered
me the money that same afternoon, and I said I would settle it before the
Magistrate, and he would not allow it-I was on friendly terms with the
prisoner-1 gave him notice on Saturday, the 21st, to quit me on the
Saturday following.
COURT. Q. This offer of the money by Burt was after the prisoner was
in custody? A. Yes.
