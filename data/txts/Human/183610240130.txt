 3403. JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of October , one pair of trowsers, value 2 s  ., the goods of Richard Gray . ROBERT TAYLOR. I am a constable at the West India Docks. I stopped the prisoner passing out of the gate at six o'clock in the evening of the 21st of October—he had been employed as a lumper four days—I asked what he had got under his arm, he said a pair of trowsers—I looked at them, and found they were turned inside out—he said he picked them up on board the Sir Edward Hamilton  —I said I would go on board and inquire—is going along he said he would tell me the truth, that he picked them up on the jetty alongside the vessel. JOHN GRAY. I belong to the Sir Edward Hamilton.  These are my trowsers—I lost them out of my chest, in the forecastle—the prisoner was working on board. GUILTY. Aged 19— Confined Six Weeks.
 3404. JOHN ALLISON was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of October , 1 pillow, value 2 s  ., the goods of Thomas Pratt . HENRY ALLEN( police-constable N  250) I saw the prisoner, on the 22nd of October, loitering about the prosecutor's shop in the Liverpool road—he then stole this pillow from inside the door, and took it to the corner of Chapel-street, and then ran—I pursued and took him while he was stuffing it into this bag—he said he did not know what it was, that he picked it up. MARY PRATT. I am the wife of Thomas Pratt. This is my pillow—it was in the shop—I do not know the prisoner.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I picked it up, and walked down Chapel-street—the policeman came, and I gave it him—I said I did not know what it was he was welcome to took at it—it is not feasible that a policeman would allow a person to take a thing, and go down another street before he stopped him. GUILTY  . Aged 26.— Judgement respited.
