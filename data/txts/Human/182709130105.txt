ANDREW GARRETT. On the 4th of August, at a quarter before nine o'clock in the evening, I was in Elm-street, Gray's Inn-lane; the prisoner and another person had made an attempt at my pocket, while I was in Cold Bath-fields, but when I got to Elm-street, the prisoner snatched the handkerchief from my pocket; he ran away - I pursued him, and took him to the watch-house; when I turned round he was secreting it in his breast, but in running he threw it away, and I have never seen it since.
 Cross-examined by MR. QUIN. Q. Elm-street, I believe, is not a very light street? A. There are gas-lights in it. I am positive he is the person who made the two attempts - it was my handkerchief he was secreting; he turned several corners, but I did not lose sight of him till I secured him, in Laystall-street.
   JOHN HUTT. I received the prisoner at the watch-house, but I found nothing on him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going towards Spa-fields - I met a person I knew, and when I got to the corner of Elm, street I turned down a court leading to Mount-pleasant and some person called Stop thief! I never saw the handkerchief.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1782.  WILLIAM STEVENS and JOHN STRATFORD were indicted for stealing, on the 30th of July , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of Francis Weedon.
 FRANCIS WEEDON. On the 30th of July I was in  Goswell-street, with my wife, about half-past eight o'clock in the evening; she said she felt something at her arm - I felt my pocket, and my handkerchief was gone, which I had used within an hour. I saw two boys go by at the instant, but I cannot identify them - Roe immediately came up, and asked if I had lost any thing; he said, if I would follow him, he thought he could find the persons who had it; we went on; he turned a corner, and took Stevens - I followed him, and took hold of Stratford - I saw Stevens drop my handkerchief from his pocket - I took it up, and put it into my pocket; there were no other persons round at that time.
   JOHN ROE. I was in Goswell-street, and saw the prisoners, with two others, following this gentleman and lady; and saw the gentleman feeling his pocket - I asked if he had lost any thing; he said he had - I went on to Turk's Head-court, and found the two prisoners; the witness caught hold of Stratford - I am quite sure they were with two others whom I had seen altogether just before.
   JAMES FORDHAM. I received the prisoner, at the watch-house, and produce the handkerchief, which I received from Mr. Weedon.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 STEVENS -    GUILTY. Aged 15. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 STRATFORD - GUILTY. Aged 16. Recommended to Mercy- Confined Six Months.
 1783.  MARY LAWRENCE was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of August , 1 shawl, value 2s. 6d., the goods of George Yardley.
   JANE YARDLEY. I am the wife of George Yardley; we live in Church-street, Bethnal-green. On the 7th of August I went out about eleven o'clock, and left the key in my door - I returned in a quarter of an hour, and found the key had been turned, and my shawl gone off my table - I saw it again at Mr. Blitz's door, on the Friday following - I know it by a darn, which is my own work - I know nothing of the prisoner.
   JUDITH BLITZ. The prisoner brought this shawl to me on the 8th of August, and I bought it of her - I had not seen her before; she came to my shop again on the Sunday morning, and I sent for the officer.
   RACHAEL BLITZ. I was present when my mother bought this shawl of the prisoner.
   GEORGE KERSHAW. I am an officer, and took the prisoner.
     GUILTY. Aged 34. Transported for Seven Years.
 1784.  WILLIAM CROPLEY and SAMUEL GRIFFITHS were indicted for stealing, on the 1st of August , 4lbs. weight of bacon, value 2s., the goods of Ethelbert Holborow.
 EDWIN CLEWETT. I am shopman to Ethelbert Holborow,  cheesemonger of Clare-street, Clare-market. On Wednesday evening, the 1st of August, I was at the window, and offered two pieces of bacon to a woman; she refused them, and had some cut off; in about half an hour the officer came, and asked if I had lost any thing - I looked, and said I had lost two pieces of bacon, which I described - I know nothing of the prisoners.
   WILLIAM McGREGOR. I was on duty, and saw the two prisoners going by the shop - Griffiths took one piece of bacon, and as I was crossing to him, Cropley took the other; they ran down Stanhope-street, where I took them, and found it on them.
   DANIEL REARDON. I was with McGregor, and saw what he has stated.
 CROPLEY -    GUILTY. Aged 14. Confined Six Weeks, and Whipped.
 GRIFFITHS -    GUILTY. Aged 12. Confined Four Weeks and Whipped.
 1785.  JAMES CLARK was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of July , 10 yards of printed cotton, value 5s., the goods of William Dobinson.
   WILLIAM MACE. I am shopman to William Dobinson, linen-draper, of High-street, Shadwell. On the afternoon of the 31st July, I was serving in the shop, and heard a girl say a man had taken a piece of print - I jumped over the counter, and found the prisoner about twenty-five yards off, with the print in his hand.
   GEORGE SMITH. I saw the prisoner in the street; he was coming towards me - when he came up I told him to surrender himself, or I would knock him down; he would not - I knocked him down, and kept him till Mace came up and found the print.
   JOHN MIDDLEDITCH. I took up the prisoner; he said distress had tempted him to do it, and the print was outside the door, which was in his favour.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Fourteen Days.
