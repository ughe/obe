road to the station, he said if I would go to No. 100, High-street, there was
a person named Hammond, in possession under the Bankruptey Court, and

the whip-I said, "Where is your whip?" he said, "I have dropped it,
but that is of no consequence" -I took him to the station, and was ordered
to search him-he said I need not, he had nothing about him-I found this
strap, this brass box, and these other things in his coat pocket-I said, You
said you had nothing"-he said, “Mr. Hammond gave me these; it is of no
consequence."

on the counter-the dealer referred to his book, and asked for your name and
your card-I stated before the Magistrate that you said you had dropped the
whip.
