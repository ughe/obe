2125. JOSEPH BLACKBURN was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of August , 1 ring, value 6s.; 1 waistcoat, value 12s.; 1 knife, value 2s.; 1 half-crown, 5 shillings, 1 sixpence, and 2 pence, the property of Samuel Baker.
 SAMUEL BAKER. I lodge at No.1,  Tennis-court, Middle-row, Holborn; I am a tailor- the prisoner lodged in the same room. On the 20th of August he left the room at half-past five o'clock in the morning; I got up at half-past eight, and missed a ring, a waistcoat, a knife, a half-crown, and some halfpence - the prisoner was brought home the next night: he gave me the ring off his finger and the knife - he said they were my property; but the waistcoat he could not account for, as he had made away with it.
   AMBROSE MOSS. I met the prisoner in Gray's Inn-lane, and took him back to the prosecutor's; he gave up the ring and knife, and wanted to give up two duplicates of clothes of Mr. Haydon's, his master, to make it up with him.
   WILLIAM MASON(Police-constable G 15). I took the prisoner, and have the property.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 41. - Transported for Seven Years.

 2127.  ANN BRODIE was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of September , 1 purse, value 2d., and one 5l. Bank note, the property of William Rolfe , from his person; against the Statute, &c.
 WILLIAM ROLFE. I am a  seaman, and lodge at Poplar. On the evening of the 7th of September I was at the Coach and Horses, Shadwell; I went from there soon after nine o'clock; when I had been out about ten minutes, the prisoner came up to me, clasped me round, and took a red purse out of my pocket, with a 5l. note in it - there was no other person near me; I told her to go away, as I was going home to my wife and family - I am certain I had the purse when I left the house.
 Prisoner. Q. Was there nobody in company with you at the Coach and Horses, and the Bull's Head? A. I came out of the house with the money in my pocket, and you were the only person with me afterwards; I did not sleep with any girl that night - I roamed about the street.
   JAMES CHRISTIAN(Police-constable K 196). I saw the prisoner in company with the prosecutor at the Bull's Head, on the night of the 7th of September - he went out and she followed him; he stopped when he had got a few doors off, and said he had lost his bundle - I went out to him, and said, "I understand you have a 5l. note about you:" he said, "Yes, I have in this purse, and I mean to take it home to my wife and family" - the prisoner was close to him at the time; I pushed her away: a young girl came up to her, and said, "Nance, will you stand some gin" - she said, "I have not a blessed farthing, but I shall soon have some money, and I will treat you;" I said to her, "You had better let him alone, you know he has a 5l. note about him" - she said, "No, I will go home with him to night, so help me God."
   WILLIAM WHEELER(Police-constable K 160). I went to a house in Bluegate-fields the next morning - I found the prisoner there; I asked what she had done with the man's pocket-book and purse; she said she knew nothing about it - I said, "What have you done with the man's money?" she said, "I have got no money," but I found on the floor a sovereign and a sixpence, and a woman, who was there, said she had seen her that morning with four or five half-crowns in her hand.
   MARY BIGNELL. I was in the room where the officer apprehended the prisoner; she had come home that morning, and called me at half-past five o'clock or a quarter before six - she said God was good, and she was not without 1l. or some silver - she showed me a sovereign and three or four half-crowns in her hand; I asked her if she had got it honestly, and she said she was as right as the day - she asked me to go and buy some things; we then had a cup of tea, and the officer came and took her.
   DIANA CLIFFORD. I am the wife of Charles Henry Clifford; we keep the Coach and Horses, in Shadwell. -The prosecutor was at my house on the night of the 7th of September; he had a 5l. note, and I offered to take care of it for him.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you see me with him? A. Yes, you went out with him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went into the Coach and Horses, and saw the prosecutor and several persons drinking with him; he then went to the bar, and wanted this lady to give him his 5l. note; she refused - he then came out, and went to the Bull's Head; I went with him there, and saw no more of him.
     GUILTY. Aged 32. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
