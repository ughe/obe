375. HENRY JOHNSON was indicted for stealing 1 pair of boots, value
88. 6d., the goods of John Henderson.
HENRY DREwin. I am in the service of Mr. John Henderson, of Clapham.
road. On the 7th of Dec. I was in the shop-there were some boots outside
-I heard them snatched from the door-I ran out, and saw the prisoner-
overtook him with these boots under his arm, in less than two minutes--they
are my master's.
Prisoner's Defence. Another man took them, and gave them to me.
Guilty. Aged 28.-Confined Three Months.
376. ANN SKIPPER was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 3s.;
1 blanket, 5s. ; 2 shawls, 10s. ; 2 table-cloths, 8s.; and 1 night-gown, 2s.6d.;
the goods of Gregory Cording, her master.
CATHERINE CORDING. I am the wife of Gregory Cording--the prisoner
was in my service-she left without notice on the 10th of Oct. -I then missed
the articles produced-they are mine.
Prisoner. She lent me the things to pledge instead of paying me my
wages. Witness. I never lent her any of them.
THOMAS SPARKES, pawnbroker. I took these articles in pledge from the
prisoner.
GUILTY. Aged 56.-Confined Six Months.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
377. WILLIAM GREENOW was indicted for stealing 28lbs. weight of
sugar, value 11s. ; the goods of John Prior, his master.
John Prior, bottle-merchant, High-street, St. Saviour's. The prisoner was
in my service up to 11th of Nov.-- he left without notice-I missed some loaf
sugar from the cellar when he had gone-I cannot identify it.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. What wages did you pay him?
A. 25. a gross for all the bottles be washed-I had sugar for making British
champaigne-he was only in my service for washing and collecting bottles-
I did not enter into any agreement with him to share the profits--this is my
handwriting-there is nothing about 28. a gross in this paper-(read)
“Agreement between John Prior and William Greenow, that he John Prior
should find money, and pay all expenses, and aid and assist in the business.
Joun Prior, WILLIAM GREENOW.") I never signed this.
JAMES CHALLIS. I am employed on the prosecutor's premises, by the
prisoner-he acted as master--the cellar was his as well as the prosecutor's,
Not GUILTY.
378. HENRY MARRIOTT was indicted for stealing 1 tame rabbit,
price 48., the goods of Charles Edmonds.
CHARLES EDMONDS, labourer, Albany-cottage, High-street, Lambeth. I lost
a rabbit on Sunday morning about ten weeks ago - this is it-I swear to it by
three marks on the shoulder.
Joseph Young, Chapel-street, Stockwell. I bought this rabbit of the pri-
soner for 2s., on a Saturday, about three months ago.
Prisoner. I bought it of Mr. M'Cleod, of Stockwell-brewery.
Guilty.. Aged 14.-Confined Sir Months.
