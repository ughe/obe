WILLIAM EDWARD BALL (policeman, 365). I have known Wilks about
eight or nine months, and Matilda Ricketts also--I have seen them in com-
pany together-Ricketts lived in Cooper's Gardens, and previous to that, in
Austin-street-I have seen them in the street in Shoreditch, about the door
of No. 2, where the two Ricketts lived I cannot say that Wilks lived with
Matilda Ricketts- I have no means of knowing that all I know is that!
have seen them at the door of the house.
COURT. Q. Have you any means of knowing where Wilks lived on 20th
March ? A. I have not-Cooper's Gardens is about two miles from the Red
Lion-I believe it is about two miles from Smith's house, where Wilks was
found,
Cross-examined by MR. METCALFE. Q. When did you last see Wilks
and Matilda Ricketts together ? A. At the beginning of March, after the
February Session, they were talking together in Cooper's Gardens-I bad
seen them before that, two or three times I have known both the Ricketts
six or seven years I never knew anything wrong of them.
MR. WILLIAM WADHAM COPE. I am the governor of Newgate. The
prisoner Wilks has been recently under my care-he left on 1st March-be
was committed on 26th February-he was searched on coming in, and had
neither money or anything else about him-he took nothing out.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. He was charged with some offence,
and acquitted? Yes; he went out on 1st March-he was here a very
few days.
