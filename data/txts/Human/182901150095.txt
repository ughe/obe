The prisoner told the Magistrate that there were ten respectable people in the tap-room, where the harness laid for two hours, exposed, and that the landlord, landlady, and their neice saw it; he was remanded, to give him time to send for them - I said, "If you will give me their names I will go for them:" he said he would send for them himself: the next day he said he had been locked up, and could not send to them; I had a summons to get the publican - he said that the same two men had been there with a lot of things in a bag - his wife told them that the prisoner was taken, and they went away - that he himself never saw the men.

 MARY JONES. I am a widow, and live in Turnmill-street; I go out to work. I have known the prisoner three or four years - he lived in the next room to me; I was in the habit of making his fire and going to clean for him. -On the 9th and 10th of December he was in bed at nine o'clock in the morning, and as far as I can recollect, on the 8th the same; I remarked that he was later in bed than ever I knew him - the 8th was Monday; I was going out to wash at ten o'clock - he was not up at nine.
 HENRY SHELDRAKE. This is the prisoner's wife - she has been to me three times, for a bag and some other things - she told me she was his wife, and was sent by his authority for the things; I said I would have a note from the Magistrate before I would give them up.
   JOHN DUFFIELD. This woman has been to me, and represented herself as his wife.
 Prisoner. This woman was turned from house and home, and I gave her a room to live in, to do for me, and I have supported her.
     NOT GUILTY
 480.  WILLIAM STOCKDALE was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of December , 1 shawl value 30s., the goods of Lydia Perry.
 LYDIA PERRY. I am  single, and live in Lower Crown-street, Westminster. I had been to the Adelphi Theatre on the 5th of December, and the prisoner met me against the Ship public-house - I took him home; we were not in bed, but I had taken off my shawl, and as soon as he was gone I missed it; he had been ten or twelve minutes with me - he gave me no money: I did not ask for any - he asked me if he could stop all night; I said No, and he said that would not do - he said he had been to Richmond, and torn a piece out of the sleeve of his coat, which was neatly joined in.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q.Have you told all the story? A.No, not all: I did recommend him to a friend up stairs - he went up, and stopped about ten minutes; I was in my room that ten minutes, but did not miss my shawl from the sofa - my friend has absconded; she did not come to my door: I do not know that she is a woman of the town.
 COURT. Q.Did you go to a public-house and see him there? A. Yes; I went to the watchman, and asked if he could go in and take a felon out - he said, No, but he would take him when he came out; I went in, and the landlord insulted me grossly - he said he never saw me.
   GEORGE OWEN. I was sent for to a public-house in Pall Mall, and the prisoner was pointed out to me; I took him, but found nothing on him - he said he was not in London at the time.
     NOT GUILTY.
 481.  CHARLES BLADES was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of January , 3 pairs of boots, value 30s.; 1 coat, value 20s.: 1 cap, value 3s.; and 1 looking-glass, value 6d., the goods of Christopher Ihlen.
   CHRISTOPHER IHLEN. I missed these articles on the morning of the 15th of January, when I was going on board a ship in the river - I had seen them about five o'clock on the evening before; these are them - the prisoner was on board.
 JOHN SMITH. About seven o'clock last Thursday morning, the prosecutor went below, and missed these articles; he came up and said so; I said, "Then I will go and look for the boy," meaning the prisoner, who the Captain had allowed to stay on board for charity; he had been on board the same morning - I found him in Ratcliff-highway, about ten or eleven o'clock; he had the cap on his head, a pair of boots on his legs, and the looking-glass.
 BOVD SILVESTER. I am an officer. I took the prisoner; and took this cap from his head, and this pair of boots from his legs; I asked him where he had sold the other pair of boots: he said "That way for 6s.;" I went, but could not find the place - I afterwards got this coat, and this pair of boots in a public-house; the people recognised him, and said he left them; I asked him if these were the things he left; he said Yes - one pair of boots, which were quite new, have not been found.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. It is not true.
 GUILTY. Aged 14.
     Judgment Respited.
 482.  SARAH SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of January , 3 lbs, weight of pork. value 2s. the goods of James Gould.
 JAMES GOULD. I  keep a butcher's shop in Whitecross-street. On the 3d of January, between seven and eight o'clock, I was standing at my door; the prisoner came and took a piece of pork - I saw her take it; I called my man, then went and took it from her, two doors off, and gave her in charge.
 JAMES BROCK. I was in the shop: my master called me out - he had got hold of the prisoner, who had the pork - it had been on the board within the boundary of the shop some time; it was not so exposed so that any one might take it.
   SAMUEL YATES. I am a watchman. I took the prisoner - she said if I dared take her up, she would make me pay for it; the board is a very little way out of the window.
 Prisoner's Defence. My husband was dying at the time, and is since dead. GUILTY. Aged 36.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Seven Days.
