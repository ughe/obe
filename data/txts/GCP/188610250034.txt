1041, RICHARD FOOTER (35), Feloniously demanding money with
menaces from Julia Cormack, with intent to steal.
MESSRS. POLAND and GILL Prosecuted; MR. WALKER Defended.
JULIA CORMACK. I live at 15, Chapel Place, Belgrave Square; I am
a charwoman-on Thursday night, 26th August, at half-past 10 o'clock,
I was in Park Lane-I there got into conversation with the witness
Brown, a butler; he came and spoke to me in the street-I had seen
him before, I knew who he was- I chatted with him, and we went into
the park and sat down on a seat-we were not there five minutes, no
impropriety whatever took place between us, when the prisoner came
up, he was in plain clothes-he said he was a detective from Scotland
Yard, he said we were acting indecently, that Brown was disarranging
my clothes--I said there was nothing of the kind-he said, “Well, I
don't wish to give you in charge " Brown said he could do as he
