JOSEPH ADAMS. I keep the Three Kingdoms public-house, Lower Thames-
street-on 19th May, the prisoner came with another woman, between 2

for half a sovereign-I gave her a good crown, and two good half-crowns
she then said, “Will you please to give me change for this half-crown 1"
holding it out to me-she gave it into my hand-I gave her the change,
and kept it in my hand, as I was very busy, but somebody spoke to me,

and put it on a shelf in the bar; it remained there a few days, and I after-
wards gave it to the police—this is it (produced); here is my mark on it-
I went to look after the prisoner, but could not find her about a week
afterwards she came again with the same woman, and I gave her in
custody.
