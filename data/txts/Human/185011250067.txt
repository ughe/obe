   Cross-examined. Q.  Can you tell me whether the 10 l .-note, No. 58270, was paid in through a bank? A.  It was paid in across the counter; the name that is on it is the name that was given by the party to whom the change was given, in accordance with the custom of the Bank. WILLIAM BAILEY re-examined.  I believe the indorsement on this 100 l .-note to be the prisoner's writing—that on the 50 l .-note I am rather doubtful about—it is something of the character of his writing—that on the 10 l .-note, No. 58270, is his.
