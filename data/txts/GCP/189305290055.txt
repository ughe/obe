Mr. C. F. GILL Prosecuted, and MR. ARTHUR GILL Defended Ball.
Before Ball pleaded to the indictment, Mr. ARTHUR GILL moved
to quash Counts three and six (which charged the prisoner with having
unlarfully received goods, knowing them to have been obtained by false pre-
