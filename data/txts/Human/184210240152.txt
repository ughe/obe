 2951. FREDERICK WILD was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of October , 11 lbs, weight of lead pipe, value 1 s  .; and one metal cock, value 1 s  .; the goods of Ann Knight , and fixed to a building. CHARLES KNIGHT. My mother, Ann Knight, has a house in Goswell-road—this pipe and cock was fixed there on the 18th of October—I mind it on the 19th—I am sure it is hers. ROBERT NICHOLLS( police-constable G  142.) About a quarter before six o'clock on the morning of the 19th of October I was on duty in Gos well-street—I missed this pipe—I found the prisoner in the coal-cellar, and the pipe by the side of him—he had no business there—he had a box of lucifers and a candle. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Four Months.
 2952. THOMAS HUNT was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of Octo ber , 1 handkefckief, Value 1 s  ., the goods of Robert Banfield , from his person. ROBERT BANFIELD. I am a servant, living at Norwood, in Surrey. On the 7th of October I was in Holborn looking at some birds—I felt some thing at my pocket and missed my handkerchief—I turned and saw the prisoner close to my side, with it in his pocket—I asked him for it—he denied having it—I said I could see it in his pocket—he then ran away—I pursued, and caught him—he gave it to me—this is it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I saw the handkerchief down by my feet; I pick ed it up, and put it into my pocket; when the prosecutor asked me if I had got it, I said yes, and asked if it was his. GUILTY.** Aged 26.— Transported for Ten Years.
