was placed for the speakers- I saw the defendant, Fassell, in the van there
were eighteen or twenty persons in it and between 2000 and 3000 persons
were assembled.

Wiliama there-- he spoke first to the meeting he was on the van- I took

the bloody aristocracy have now done ils work at last," or " at least, I am not

while you remain here you will be peaceable, and obey my injunctions as
to the course you shall adopt-all the arrangements have been made ; and
when you have been addressed by two or three speakers, I shall give you a
signal, when you must fall into marching order, four abreast, and follow me
where I shall lead you; you will know where when you are there. I shall
take you then to a place where you will meet ten times as many as are here,
and then you will be told what to do. I have made up my mind on a course
of action. I fear banishment no more than that brave patriot and noble man,
