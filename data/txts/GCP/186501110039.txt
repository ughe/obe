WALTER REWEN FOWLER I am & stock-jobber on the Stock Exchange
--on 14th October, I had to deliver to the prisoner 3,0601. Spanish Passive
Bonds—the amount of the cheque I was to receive, was 9481. 128. -I ran
into the office, Mr. Burrows was sitting at the table, settling day is always
a busy day, I said, “Here is 3,060 Passive Bonds; I claim this amount,
9481. 128."-he wrote me a cheque directly, and gave it me-1 put it in my
pocket, and paid it in with others--I gave him the bonds--I stood at the
table with the bonds in my hand, and he wrote the cheque-whether I put
them on the table first, before I positively bad the cheque in my hand, I
cannot swear-I asked for a cheque, and I held the bonds in my hand.
Cross-examined. Q. You said, “Here are the bonds;" and held them out,
I suppose A. Yes ; if I come to refresh my memory, I should say that he
counted them before he gave me the cheque-- that is the usual custom.
Court, R. Did you see him writing the cheque before you parted with
the custody of the bonds ? 4. That I cannot swear to, and whether he
counted the bonds before he gave me the cheque, I cannot say it was all
done in a moment I should say, in all probability, he did count them
first.

--on 14th October, I had to deliver to the prisoner 400 Mexican Bonds I

COURT. Q. You had confidence in him ? A. Yes, that is the usual
