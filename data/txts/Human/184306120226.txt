CHARLES JOHNSON. I am a shoemaker, and live in Palmers-row. About half-past eight o'clock on Monday morning, the 15th of June, my girl beckoned to me—I went out—she ran out of the shop, and brought in Williams—she said, "This is the boy that stole the boots"—he said, "It is not me, it is the other"—Kent was running off—I held Williams, and called "Stop thief"—one of the boots were at Williams's feet when he was stopped—the policeman brought Kent and the other boot—these are my boots. EMMA HOLBORN. I am the prosecutor's servant. I saw the prisoners standing at the window—I am sure these are the two—Williams unhung the boots from the door—I got up, and beckoned my master out—I ran to the door and caught Williams—Kent received the boots from Williams, and ran away—one of them was dropped at Williams's feet—Kent was brought back.
   WILLIAMS  — GUILTY. * Aged 16.
   KENT  — GUILTY. * Aged 14. Transported for Seven Years.—Convict Ship.
 1990. JOHN PASCOE was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of June , 1 pair of trowsers, value 4 s  .; the goods of James Franklin . THOMAS ROCKCLIFFE. I live in Swan-yard, Shoreditch. On the 10th of June I was in Devonshire-street—I saw the policeman pursuing the prisoner—I saw the prisoner throw away this pair of trowsers—I took them up, and gave them to the policeman—I am sure he is the person. PETER ALLEN. I am shopman to James Franklin, of Tottenham Court-road. About eight o'clock on Saturday evening, the 10th of June, I missed a pair of trowsers from the door—I had seen them safe half an hour before—these are them. JAMES UTTING( police-constable E  34.) I saw the prisoner at the corner of Hanway-street—I turned, and missed him—Allen said something to me, and I followed the prisoner.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I never had the trowsers. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months.
 1991. MARY WILKES was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of June , 2 sovereigns, 3 half-crowns, 1 shilling, 1 penny, and 3 halfpence; the monies of Charles Cross , from his person. CHARLES CROSS. I am a hair manufacturer. About half-past twelve o'clock, on the night of the 13th of June, I was in the City-road—the pri soner was at the corner of Cowper-street—she wanted me to go home with her—I refused, and while she entreated me some money dropped—she said, "You have dropped some money"—I said, no I had not—she picked up a 6 d ., and gave it me—I had about 2 l . 10 s ., and missed all but 6 d ., and—I found my pocket was open—I took hold of her wrist, and gave her in charge.
   Prisoner.  You gave me the money out of your pocket. Witness.  I did not—I had not undone my trowsers—I had been drinking, but was senisible of what I was doing. HENRY KIDNEY( police-sergeant G  6.) I passed the prisoner and the prosecutor—he had got her by the wrist, and said she had robbed him of two sovereigns and some silver—she said, "I have not taken it"—the prosecutor was a little fresh, but seemed to know what he was about. HARRIET HAYWARD. I searched the prisoner—she had something in her mouth—she said she had not, and then she gave me this sovereign out of her mouth.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I had a shilling given me, and in the confusion I do not know what I put in my mouth. GUILTY.— Confined Eleven Days .
