255. GEORGE SMITH (37), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MessRs. CRAUFUID and LLOYD Prosecuted.
15) FREDERICK GRay. I am barman at the Railway Tavern, Fenchurch
Street-on 15th January, about 3.30 p.m., the prisoner and two other
men came in-the prisoner called for a pint of ale, and gave me a florin
-I gave him 18. 9d change- intended the florin to go into a half-pint
pewter measure, but it went on to the refrigerator- few minutes after.
wards the prisoner asked for half an ounce of tobacco and gave me
florin-I said What do you all this?"-he said "A two-shilling
piece"- said It is made of pewter pots; Frank, give me a knife"-
the barman did so, and I eut a piece out of it before the prisoner-
then found the one on the refrigerator was also bad-he said nothing-
I went round the counter, laid hold of him, and said "I shall detain
you"-he tendered a good sixpence, but I did not take it-I sent for a
constable-the other man had gone out-I said to the prisoner What
made you put down two florins in succession said Oh, any one
might do that' man who gave his name as Doon followed the
prisoner to the station, but he was discharged-I gave the two florins to
Rose-I cut a piece out of the second.
Cross-ecamined by the Prisoner. There were from 12 to 16 people in
the bar-some of them paid with silver, but not with a florin.
HENRY RosE. On 15th January Mr. Gray gave the prisoner into my
custody for uttering two florins-I heard him say to the prisoner 44 Why
did you pass those two florins when you had plenty of small change?
-he said Any one might do "-Davis followed us to the station
and he was ordered into the dock-he was remanded for a week and

sixpence-the prisoner made no answer to the charge-I found on him
three shillings, three pence, three halfpence, and a farthing.
WILLIAM WEB8TER. These florins are both bad, and from the same

The Prisoner called:
JoHN DAVIs. I am a labourer-I was charged with the prisoner at

-you called for it and threw down a florin.
Cross-eramined. It was a little after 3 o'clock-no one was with me-
Smith and I were alone, no one else drank with us-we did not have any

Prisoner's Defence. I did not know the florin was bad. He took s
knife and cut it, and took another coin from the refrigerator, and said
that I had given it to him. You don't suppose that I should go and
tender him a bad coin, and another two minutes afterwards, knowing
that it was bad.
