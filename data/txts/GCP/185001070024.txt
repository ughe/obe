FARNCOMB, Mayor.
HENRY WEBB. I am a City-policeman. On 3rd Jan., at half-past two
o'clock in the afternoon, I was in Long-lane, in a barber's shop-I saw the
prisoner and another walk behind the prosecutor, take a handkerchief from
his pocket, and run in amongst the pens-I pursued, and took him-he took
this handkerchief from his breast, and threw it amongst the pens.
George HENRY GREEN. On 3rd Jan. I was in Long-lane, about two
o'clock-I had this handkerchief in my pocket-when the policeman ran
after the prisoner I missed it- I took it up, and gave it to the policeman.
Prisoner. Is it possible for a man in a barber's shop, being shaved, to see
me, and the barber before him?
Guilty.* Aged 19.-Confined Twelve Months.
321. GEORGE WILLIAMS, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 6d.; the
goods of James Hawes, from his person : having been before convicted.
JAMES Hawes. I am a clerk. On 29th Dec., about half-past seven
o'clock, I was in Kingsland-road-I felt a tag at my coat behind, turned, and
saw the prisoner with my handkerchief in his band-I took hold of his hand
with the handkerchief, but he slipped his fingers through mine, and left the
handkerchief in my hand-he then bobbed behind a pork-butcher's awning,
and I went on, and about thirty or forty yards further I found the prisoner
was walking after me again-I spoke to a policeman-he started off-the
policeman took him.
JAMES HOCKING (policeman, N 181). I pursued the prisoner 200 or
300 yards--I told bim what I took him for-he said he knew nothing of it-
this is the handkerchief I got from the prosecutor.
Francis HALIFAX. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's conviction-
(read-Charles Jellett, Convicted Jan., 1848, and confined four months)—he is
the person-I have seen him frequently with thieves.
Guilty. Aged 21.- Transported for Seven Years.
