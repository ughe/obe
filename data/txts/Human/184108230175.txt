 2241. CHARLES HUGHES and JAMES BROWN were indicted for stealing, on the   7th of August , 2 breast-pins and 1 chain, value 5 s  ., the goods of a man unknown, from his person. CHARLES JACKSON( police-constable A  146.) On Saturday afternoon, the 7th of August, I saw the two prisoners, in company with two others, near Westminster-Bridge—there was a rowing-match—I saw them walk ing at the back of different people, up and down the bridge—after watch ing them some minutes, I saw one, not in custody, put his hand over, and take something from a gentleman's breast, which was immediately handed to Hughes, who was standing on the other side of the way—Brown was covering the man who took the pin—Hughes put something into his pocket—I immediately went over to him, took hold of his arm, and took this pin from his trowsers' pocket—the same pocket he had put the pin into—he had not got his hand out—at the same time another officer came up—I gave Hughes to him, and took Brown—I am sure he was covering the other man—my brother constable and I went to the spot where the pin was stolen from—I held it up, and asked what gentleman in the crowd had lost a pin—one gentleman turned round, and said, "This is my pro perty, drawn from my breast"—there were the marks of the pin in his stock—we asked him to accompany us to the station, but in going we missed him, or he missed us—we have heard nothing of him since.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Was there a good crowd of people? A.  Yes—Brown stood Behind the other—when we took Hughes, Brown had got eight or nine yards—he was within our sight, and could see us.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Where were you standing when you saw them first? A.  On the other side of the way—but when the boats were starting off, I saw them closing on a gentleman—I was half-way across when the pin was handed to Hughes.
