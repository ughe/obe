2449. JOHN BEARD, stealing 1 coal-scuttle, value 78.: the goods of
James Edward Lawrence; having been before convicted ; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 41.-Confined Six Months.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
2450. WILLIAM HISCOCK, stealing 2 shillings, 3 sixpences, 2 penee,
and 3 balfpence; the moneys of George Wells, his master.
GEORGE WELLS. I keep the Coopers Arms, at Putney. The prisoner
was in my service-on 26th Sept. I marked some money, and put it in dif-
ferent places where I had lost money from before I put 16s. 6d. into a
bag in a drawer, four sixpences in the till, and 18. worth of halfpence-I
locked up the bar and the places--the next morning, at eight o'clock, I came,
and missed two shillings and a sixpence out of one place, and 8jd. out of
another--the money was all found on the prisoner by the officer--we went up!
stairs, and found in his box brandy and wine and cheroots, and a great many
other things.
CHARLES CANHAM (policeman, V 58). I was secreted in the house-I
saw the prisoner come down at half-past seven o'clock in the morning-he
went into the bar-parlour-I heard a drawer and a till go-I waited till Mr.
Wells came-I told him, and this money was gone--we found the marked
money on the prisoner-I had seen it marked-it was the same money-
found this other property in the prisoner's box.
Prisoner's Defence. I opened the door : I saw some money down on the
mat, and took it up. (The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 21.-Confined One Year.
2451. MARY DONOVAN, stealing 6 sheets, and 1 gown, value 14s.;
the goods of William Poole and another; having been previously convicted.
WILLIAM POOLE. I am a pawnbroker, and have one partner. On 11th
Oct. the prisoner came to our house, and I saw ber take down a gown, and
conceal it beneath her clothes, and a bundle of sheets, and put them under
her shawl-she left the shop, saying she would call again in the afternoon-
followed her, and asked her to walk back with me, and I saw her drop these
things from under her shawl, and from under her petticoats--I had not sold
them to her-she had no right to them.
Prisoner's Defence. I had had a little too much to drink, and he chaffed
me; I could not bave stolen them; I beg for mercy, on account of my poor
children; I never was in the bands of a policeman but once before; I have
worked nine years for one master.
