   Cross-examined. Q.  Did it never occur that you received money and did not enter it? A.  No—I will not swear that I have never received money and not entered it in the presence of the man that paid it—it has not often occurred—it may have occurred—it is their duty to see it entered. JOHN JONES. I am clerk to the prosecutor. The prisoner has not accounted to me for either of these sums.
   Cross-examined. Q.  When did you receive money? A.  Just as it happened—I make the entry in the book when I receive money; I never missed—I never received money and did not enter it in the man's presence, since the prisoner has been there. THOMAS BARNES CALVERT. I am clerk to the prosecutor. The prisoner has not accounted to me for either of these three sums—I have the books here—they are not entered. ROBERT GEORGE THURSTON re-examined. Q.  Do the masters take money? A.  The young ones do; not the old gentleman—they enter it in the book—the youngest one receives money of the carmen—he is not here—both the young masters were before the Magistrate, but they were not called—the Magistrate would have called them, but Mr. Robinson opposed it.
   COURT  to THOMAS BARNES CALVERT . Q.  Are any writings of the money received by either of the Mr. Houghtons from the prisoner here? A.  No—I have known one of them to receive money from him—he is not here. NOT GUILTY.
 1988. JAMES ROBINS PRICE , stealing 1 loaf of bread, value 3 d  .; the goods of George Gregory , his master. GEORGE GREGORY. I am a baker—the prisoner was in my service. On 17th Aug. I saw him going out—I thought he looked bulky—I called him back, and found a half-quartern loaf under his coat—he said it was the only one he had taken.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 35.— Confined Nine Days.
 1989. EDWARD DUKE , stealing 30 yards of cotton plaid, value 24 s  .; the goods of George Hardin ; having been before convicted. THOMAS PLUMMER. I am assistant to Mr. George Hardin, a linen-draper in Hackney-road. On 11th Aug. I was in the shop, and my mistress was at the door—I heard a cry of "Stop thief!"—I ran out, and saw the prisoner running with this plaid under his arm—he dropped it, and was taken—this is it ( produced ). HENRY PENNY( policeman, N  152). I produce a certificate of the pri soner's former conviction at Clerkenwell—( read—Convicted April  1847, and confined two months )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined One Year .
