I saw some persons assembled, and I stopped for a moment on the pave-

my head round, and saw the prisoner take my handkerchief out of my right-
hand coat pocket--he ran off immediately into the crowd-I followed him,
and collared him-I said, "Give me my pocket handkerchief" he said, "I
have not got your handkerchief". I said, “You have got it in your left hand
breeches' pocket, and if you don't produce it I will take it out”-he immedi-
ately produced it out of his pocket I gave him in charge. sued e
