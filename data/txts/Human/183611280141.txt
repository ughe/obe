 117. AGNES ELIZA MELLINCH was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of November , 2 tea-pots, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Henry Howell . HENRY HOWELL. I deal in china, and live in Little New-street, Shoe-lane. The prisoner often passed through my shop to go to a lodger—as she passed through, on the 26th of November, I observed something under her shawl, and after she was gone I missed three piles of tea-pots. ELIZABETH SULLIVAN. I am a book-binder, and live in Charles-street, Hatton-garden. The prisoner had a furnished room of me—she broke a jug of mine, and gave me this tea-pots, instead of it—I saw the policeman find another in the room she occupied—they were wrapped in brown paper. JOHN FURNACE. I am a policeman. I received the prisoner in charge at her lodging—I found one tea-pot in her room, and one the landlady gave me,
 (  Property produced and sworn to. )
   Prisoner's Defence.  I bought them in White-cross-street. HENRY HOWELL re-examined.  I had sold none of them—they were in brown paper—I had lost fifteen or eighteen that day. GUILTY. Aged 54.— Confined One Month.
