Cross-examined. I have known the prisoner for some time-I believed
he was an inoffensive man-I did not strike anybody till I was struck

- I had not assaulted him that night-after he struck me I attempted

I could not hit hard enough to black the prisoner's eye-I am not aware

called Tiddles to ask the prisoner to let it all blow over-it is untrue
that he came back with the answer, "Yes, if you can give a reason for
knocking me about "-I hit a man with a cup at a coffee stall nine months
ago, I think-he was going to assault me, 80 I poked it at him in self
defence-Horncastle did not try to prevent me from fighting I did not
throw anything at Horncastle - I have never seen the prisoner with a
knife.
Re-examined. I did not fetch Stapleton, a cabman, from Drury Lane;
he was in Salisbury Square-no knife was used in Salisbury Square, that
I know of-that dispute was ended between 10 and 10.30-the other
dispute at Ormond Yard was between 1 and 1.30 a.m.--I did not see the
prisoner between those times—I did nothing to provoke a dispute at
Ormond Yard—I had earned 5s, 6d. between the disputes-I was taking
my cab home for the night.
