ANNE GRBEN. I was the wife of Richard Green, now deceased; he kept
the Queen's-head public-house at Uxbridge. On the night of the 13th of
Feb., I was in the inner room adjoining the bar -1 had only the fire-light-
heard a noise like the unlocking of the till, and then the rattling of halfpence
- I got up and went to the till, and saw the prisoner who is a stranger, lift-
ing his hand from the till, with the bowl in it, and he drew it across the
counter--there were some coppers in it-I had seen it a very few minutes
before-the till was locked, and the key in it-I went and took hold of the
prisoner-he tried to get from me, and pushed me against the window, and
broke two panes of glass-the policeman arrived, and I gave him in charge
I heard the bowl dropped when I took hold of him-I got a light, and the
policeman picked up the bowl and the coppers as described. Tam
Cross-examined by Me, HORRY, Q. This was half-past ten o'clock at
night? 4. Yes—there was a gas-light in the bar over the till, on the counter
--the prisoner dropped the bowl in the passage--I had some persons drinking
in a room opposite the bar--there was no one in the bar but the prisoner and
me-I know there were farthings and halfpence in the bowl, but I could not
swear how many.
