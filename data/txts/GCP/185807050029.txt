MR. T. SALTER. Q. Do you know that Milsom was pardoned, and that
he returned to this country under that pardon ? A. I do, he has since then
maintained a respectable position up to this time it is not true that I have
now in my hands any property belonging to the prisoner, and I never had,
except that singular document which he asked me to take care of this letter
is a very good imitation of my writing; the paper the prisoner has produced
is my writing, this is not--this “as” has been practised upon; I did not
see that before; it has evidently been altered—the letter produced from the
prisoner's possession, purporting to be signed White and Reynolds, is not my
writing; it is a clever imitation of it; there are two or three words in it
which are clumsy imitations; it is dated 14th June-I was not at Bristol on
the 14th ; I was on the 12th.
Court. Q. When did the prisoner come out of the Lunatic Asylum at
Salisbury ? A. I do not know the date. I have only known him out since May
last, or a short time earlier-since he has been out he has been in full posses-
sion of his senses—I have not observed any incapacity of mind about him.
