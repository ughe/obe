 1258.  JAMES DONELLY, and JANE, HIS WIFE , were indicted for stealing, on the 30th of May , 1 pair of shoes, value 3s., the goods of William Fears.
   MARGARET FEARS. I am the wife of William Fears, a shoemaker- we live in Tothill-street. The prisoners came in on the 30th of May - the man asked for a pair of boots to fit his wife; I tried on a pair - the man sat on a chair, and put his arm across; he took off a pair of boots, and put them into his basket, while I was trying a pair on his wife; he then objected that they were not a right and left - I showed a pair that were - they would not have them, and went away, saying they would come again in an hour; I said nothing to them, as I was alone; I followed them about four doors, then took hold of the man, and said he had a pair of shoes in his basket - he denied it; I brought them back, and the constable found them.
   JAMES PROBERT. I work for William Fears. I took these boots home that evening; I hung them on a chair, and as I came out I met the prisoners going in.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   JOSEPH HILL. I am a constable, and found the shoes in his basket.
 JAMES DONELLY's Defence. I bought them of a man on Westminster-bridge.
 JAMES DONELLY - GUILTY. Aged 20. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Six Months.
   JANE DONELLY-   NOT GUILTY.
 1259.  ANN AYRES was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of May , 1 piece of linen, containing 20 yards, value 36s., the goods of Thomas Stevens.
   JOHN PIKE. I am shopman to Thomas Stevens a haberdasher, of Islington. On the 29th of May, between five and six o'clock in the evening, the prisoner came into the shop with a box, and offered ribbons and velvets for sale - we have bought of her before - I told her nothing was wanted; she had opened her box on a chair, under some Irish linen; I was serving a customer - I looked up, and saw her drawing a piece of linen off the counter - she put it into her box - I jumped over the counter, and stopped her as she went towards the door; I said, "You have got something that don't belong to you;" she did not deny it; I told her to open her box, and found it; she seemed to feel her situation, and beggen for mercy.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   SAMUEL MOUNT STEVENS. I am an officer, and received her in charge.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 44. Strongly Recommended to Mercy- Confined Three Months.
 1260.  JOHN ROBINS was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of May , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of Plowden Presland , from his person.
 PLOWDEN PRESLAND, Esq. I am a  barrister. On the 26th of May, about one o'clock in the day, I was in Carey-street; I felt something at my left pocket - I put my hand down, missed my handkerchief, and saw the prisoner running from me, towards Shire-lane; I called Stop thief! and found him in custody immediately; I only lost sight of him for a moment; the officer took him across the street, and took the handkerchief from a door.
 Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. Were there any persons about? A. Not that I saw.
   JOHN CAPES. I was in Searl's-place, and heard a cry of Stop thief! I met the prisoner making towards me; I saw him throw this handkerchief into a passage; I secured him - the prosecutor came up - I gave him to an officer.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you lose sight of him? A. Not at all.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was running with the mob, and never had the handkerchief.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Confined Three Months, and Whipped.
 1261.  MARY BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of April , 5 pewter pots, value 7s., the goods of John Hatchard; and 1 pewter-pot, value 1s., the goods of James Goff.
   JOHN HATCHARD. I keep the Marquis of Granby public-house, at Pimlico. I lost these pots.
   THOMAS MORRIS. I am servant to Mr. Hatchard. On the 27th of April, about twenty minutes past eight o'clock, I was collecting pots - a person came to me. I went and saw the prisoner going down Raneleigh-walk, and asked if she had any pots; she said No. I turned her clothes aside, and found five.
   JOHN SOUTHERWOOD. I took her in charge; she said she lived in King-street, and then that she had no lodging - I took her to the watch-house, and found Goff's pot on her.
 JOHN GOFF. I  keep the Crown and Anchor public-house, Ebury-street, Pimlico- this pot is mine.
     GUILTY. Aged 57. Transported for Seven Years.
