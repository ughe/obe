1745. GEORGE MARCH was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of
May, 8oz. weight of tobacco, value 2s.; and 4 shillings; the goods and
monies of Robert John King, his master; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined One Month.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
1746. PHEBE MORRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of
May, i peck of potatoes, value 6d., the goods of Joseph Reynolds, her
master.
JOSEPH REYNOLDS. I am a farmer, and live at Harmondsworth. The
prisoner was in my employ to sort potatoes in the warehouse she lives
with a man named Charles Morris in consequence of suspicion, I had her
watched, and she was found with forty potatoes in her pocket-she had a
large pocket in front to put them in, and two pockets by her side.
Ann FRAZIER. I am the wife of William Frazier, a labourer. I saw
the prisoner searched-I took the potatoes out of her pocket-she bad
one pocket down in front, and two on her side the one before was
larger than usual, and was made of everlasting she was going home when
she was searched.
HANNAH REYNOLDS. I am the prosecutor's daughter. I watched the
prisoner, and saw her put potatoes in her round frock pocket-I gave
information, she was taken as she was going home, and the potatoes
found.
SARAH TAPPING. I am the wife of a labourer. I was present when
the potatoes were taken out of the prisoner's pocket.
WILLIAM THOMPSON. I am a horse-patrol. I received the prisoner on
the 1st of June. .
Prisoner's Defence. I had never taken any before, and then I should
not if I had not wanted them. .
GUILTY. Aged 31.-Confined one Week.
