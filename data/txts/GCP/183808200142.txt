robbery on James Stevens, on the 5th of August, at Christ Church,
putting him in fear, and taking from his person and against his will, 1 hat,
value 3s. ; 1 tobacco box, value 4d.; 1 comb, value 4d. ; 1 pencil, value
d.; 1 watch, value 21. 10s. ; 1 watch-chain, value 1s. 6d. ; i watch key,

and at the time of the said robbery, feloniously striking and beating him..
