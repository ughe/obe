Toler, in his defence, reviewed the evidence at great length, and asserted that
he had not obtained anything by fraud, and that Bryant was merely his
agent and not a conspirator. Bryant stated that he had morely acted as messenger
for Tolor.
- ILOTT (Constable, Maidenhead). I was present when Bryant
was tried before the Lord Chief Justice for obtaining money from a
Maidenhead baker-he had three months-it had nothing to do with
this—the Lord Chief Justice said he gave him & short sentence as he saw
there were other cases against him.
