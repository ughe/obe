HENRY JOHN MCCLEAN. I am assistant secretary to the “Civil Service
Supply Association Limited "—it is registered under the Friendly Societies
Act-I have not got the certificate of registration.
Edward Adre. Hardy is one of our drivers at the Parcels Delivery
Company-his duty at the Civil Service was merely to assist in loading
--he carried large quantities of goods, principally to and from the Civil
Service stores on other days he would go one of our general rounds-it
was not part of his duty to take any of these things to his own house.
Cross-examined by Mr. W. SLEIGH. Q. How long has he been in your
service 4. Twelve or fourteen years—he is one of our special picked
men, in whom trust could be placed many thousand pounds worth of
property passed through his hands in a year.
COURT to GEORGE Rows. Q. Did Hardy go into your stores and take
the things ? A. Yes, he loaded his van from the stores-I placed great
confidence in him, and allowed him to come on the premises--I had com-
plaints only in one or two instances that customers had not had their goods.
MR. GRIFFITHS. Q. Were there not numbers of other goods lying about
up packed! A. Yes it would not be difficult for him to take any of those
articles if he felt disposed.
The prisoners received good characters. HARDY—Guilty. Recommended
to mercy by the Jury-Nine Months Imprisonment. MARK and JANE
REYNOLDS--Nor Guilty.
