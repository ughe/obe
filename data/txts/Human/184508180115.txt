   NEW COURT .— Friday, August 22nd , 1845.
   First Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1681. CHARLES JOHNSON was indicted for stealing 1 coat, value 3 s  ., the goods of Daniel Sharp , and that he had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded GUILTY.*— Transported for Seven Years .
 1682. JOHN CROWLEY was indicted for stealing 1 drinking-glass, value 10 d  ., the goods of Samuel Thomas ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 28.— Confined Three Months .
 1683. JOSEPH BROWN was indicted for stealing two planes, value 7 s  ., the goods of Richard Coleman Nunn , hit master; also 1 pair of boots, 5 s  ., the goods of James Satchell ; to both which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Three Months .
 1684. THOMAS HOWELL was indicted for embezzling 6 s  . 9 d  ., the monies of John Davis , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 47.— Confined Three Months .
 1685. JAMES SHAMBROOK was indicted for stealing 2 1/2lbs., weight of bacon, value 1 s  ., the goods of Samuel Edmunds , and that he had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded GUILTY.** Aged 22.— Transported for Seven Years .
 1686. JOHN HENRY THOMPSON was indicted for stealing 90 cakes, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 2lbs. of sweetmeats, 1 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 handkerchief, 3 d  .; the goods of James Andrews , and that he had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined One Year .
 1687. RICHARD WILSON was indicted for embezzling 1 l  . 9 s  ., the monies of Alfred Tarrant , his master; to which be pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months .
 1688. ROBERT BROWN was indicted for stealing 7 shirts, value 12 s  .; 1 duck-frock, 1 s  .; 1 bag, 1 s  .; and 6 handkerchiefs, 3 s  .; the goods of Richard Wetherell Hammond , in a vessel, in a certain port of entry and discharge; to which be pleaded GUILTY. Aged 32.— Confined Six Months,
 1689. JONATHAN BURGIN was indicted for stealing 1 sovereign, the monies of John Gibson, his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Four Months .
