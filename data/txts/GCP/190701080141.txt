(Defence.)
ALFRED DENT (prisoner on cath). My real name is Alfred Fowler
and I am the other prisoner's brother. Dent is my mother's name;
when I went to America the people there always called me Dent;
I lived in Michigan with my uncle Isaac Dent; my return passage
was taken by him in the name of Dent, and all my luggage was in
that name. In America I became very familiar with & dairyman's
business. On my return to England in June, 1905, I brought with
me £100. My brother was then employed as an engineer at Tilling's,
at New Cross. My wife and child came back with me from America.
I got my brother to lend me £50; I was to repay him £3 a month
and a bonus of £20. I looked out for a place at which I could start
a dairy business, and ultimately went to Penge. I spent £93 in fitting
