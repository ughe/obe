I was with Mr. Hartley at the office-I saw him almost every day-on
one occasion I was with Mr. Hartley in Mansel-street, and saw the two
Caspars at the end of Somerset-street, which leads into Mansel-street-
one is a continuation of the other I was afterwards present at the count-
ing-house, where Roe put some questions to Lewin Caspar--I do not
remember Mr. Hartley putting any questions to him-Roe asked him if
he knew a person named Moss-he said "Yes, three or four"-after some
other questions had been put, and answered, he was given into my cus-
tody by Mr. Hartley-I afterwards took Mrs. Moss and Mrs. Levy into
custody- I have produced this box, which I got out of a little room in the
back bed-room in Mansel-street-1 produce the gold bars which are now in
this box-I received them from a person of the name of John Dean, at Mr.
Solomon's house, 53, Strand, on the 7th of May-I received these two
moulds from him at the same time (producing them) and half a dozen Rus-
sian copper coins, which I have not got with me I searched the house
of Davis, No. 8, Oxendon-street, and I went with Roe to the house in
Coventry-court-he searched it while I remained down stairs--I did not
see either of the prisoners at the house in Oxendon-street, when I searched
it-I saw Mrs. Abrahams in Coventry-court-she was sitting in the
room when I went in this was on Easter Tuesday, the 2nd of April,
when Roe went to search the house - I did not know Davis till I
saw him that evening--I did see him that evening-I saw him at the
office during the examination, and I saw him in company with Mrs.
Abrahams in a hackney-coach in Threadneedle-street-I have also seen
him at the house of the prisoner Moses--Moses keeps a public-house in
Vinegar-yard, Drury-lane I did not know who Mrs. Abrahams was at the
time I saw her at Davis's-she called me by my name, but I did not know
her-she told me she lived at her father's (Moses) house--I produce a £300
bank note, (marked No. 15,) which I received from Mr. De Mole, and two
£5 notes which I got out of the Bank of England.
Cross-examined by MR. SERGEANT BOMPAS. Q. What time on the 2nd
of April was it that you saw Mrs. Abrahams at Coventry-court? A. About
eight o'clock in the evening, as near as I can recollect-seven or eight
o'clock-I searched part of Solomon's house-Roe searched the upper part
the same day-I first searched it on the 16th of April-I consider that I
searched it thoroughly, as far as I had any thing to do with it I found
nothing there then- I went there again on the 7th of May I did not go
with Solomon-I did not make any arrangement with him.
