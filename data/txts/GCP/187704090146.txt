secretary-I remember on 19th December Mrs. Ramsden coming with a
constable Cowell, who said “Mrs. Moses, I have come about a very unplea-

truth"-I looked round, but did not see the servants-I said that I had

at the Guildhall on the 30th; it charges me with the larceny of 1,000 franc
bank-note-I appeared on the 30th November and was charged with larceny-
my daughter was not then included-Flatow, Vandervoort, and Frinberg were
not there-my daughter was called as a witness for me and I was discharged,
I afterwards received notice that a bill had been found against me at the
Surrey Sessions, and I stood my trial there for two days-my daughter was
charged with me. I never took a 1,000 franc note out of Ramsden's bor

my life.
