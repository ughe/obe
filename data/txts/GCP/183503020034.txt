MR. CLARKSON. Q. Have you ever been in the Insolvent Court ? A.
I have told you I was in the Marshalsea-I once came through the Insol.
vent Debtors' Court, four years ago. I was discharged under that writ-
described myself then as a corn and flour factor I became answerable for
Miss Bills two months ago I have not been distrained upon for taxes a
rates--I have kept a house since I was discharged under the Insolvent
Act-I have always paid my rates for thirty-five years I did not owe
a hundred pence when I was discharged-it was on account of the accom
modation bills I gave-there were three or four, and were given to a Quake
in Mark-lane-I had £5000 or £6000 in good debts, as I expected, but
they turned out bad I did not swear they were good in my schedule-
cannot tell what amount I expected under my schedule.
Court. Q. How long is it since you took the benefit of the Insolvent
Act? 1. Four years--I have paid many pounds of my debts since that-
I owed very little indeed, for I do not consider that I owe money where I
receive no value...I lent a man £900 worth of accommodation paper-
did not get into a third party's hands.
