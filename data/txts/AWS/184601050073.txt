DANIEL BRYANT. I am a tailor and draper, and live at Diss, in Norfolk.
deal with Messrs. Nash and Ely, of King-street, Cheapside--I bought a
quantity of goods of them on Monday, the 13th of Oct.- was promised
them on the following Thursday, but they never reuched me--l directed them
to be sent from the Blue Boar, Aldgate, by Reeve-this is the invoice of
them.
RICHARD NasH. I am in the employ of Messrs. Nash and Ely-I do not
know Mr. Bryant, personally, but I know him as a eustomer- remember
the order for goods for him coming from Mr. Ely-I superintend the selection
of the goods-I saw them selected, and ready for packing-these goods now
produced are of a similar description to the goods selected by me to be sent on
that oceasion--they appear exactly the same, except in length-they are prer
eisely the same quality with goods we had, and they correspond with dupli-
cates we have here-two of them are waistcoat-pieces-] did not make out
the invoice-these goods are of a similar description to what were sent, and
I believe are the same goods-this waisteoating is called toilenette--this other
is tweed, and this other is a diagonal alpaca--this tweed is made of wool.
Cross-examined by MR. PARRY. Q. These are the faney names for them,
but in reality they consist of wool and silk A. Yes-this one is supposed
to be made of Alpaca wool-Messrs. Nash and Ely are woollen-drapers-
these goods are made at Huddersfield, and Leeds, and other places--there
are great quantities of them made-I have been in the business nine or ten
years-such goods as these are to be found in various places, but different
pieces will vary a little-we have another piece or two of this one-we sell
these largely-we have not sold large quantities of this tweed-I can iden-
tify this more particularly- never saw two alike of this-it is a peculiar
mixture-w had such in our warehouse-I would not pretend to say that
this is the only piece ever made of this kind-other dealers have similar
goods.
Mr. BALDWIN. Q. Is this the invoice of the goods you selected on that
occasion A. This is the invoice-these two waistcoat-pieces correspond in
quantity with the invoice-the other pieces do not-they have been cut.
Cross-examined. Q. Are not similar waistcoat-pieces to be found in every
tailor's shop in London? A. Similar things are.
