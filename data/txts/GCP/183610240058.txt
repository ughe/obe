GEORGE COLLIER (police-constable E 38.) On Monday morning, the
19th of September, about ten o'clock, I received information respecting the
robbery, from the superintendent of the police-I went to Mr. Tullock

21st of September, in Leather-lane, Holborn-I followed him, and overtok
him in the lane I did not know him before-I only had a description
him-I said to him, “Halloo, Tom, I have got you at last," and took hin
