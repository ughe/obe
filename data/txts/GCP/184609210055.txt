MR. PARRY. Q. Was William running from or towards the policeman ?
A. I think he was running towards home, but he fell into the arms of the

THOMAS MEERES. I am a surgeon, and live in Brick-lane, Spitalfields. On
the morning of the 25th of Aug., Hussey was brought to me-I found a deep
contused wound on the top of his head-he was very faint and low from loss
of blood-the skin was cut through-he bled a good deal-he was suffering
for a week afterwards-he had severe constitutional symptoms-he is now
free from danger-he is still ill, and has not returned to duty-he is under
my care still he kept his bed nearly a fortnight-the wound might have
been inflicted with a policeman's truncheon.
