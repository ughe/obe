FOURTH COURT.-Thursday, July 27th, 1905.
Before J. A. Rentoul, Esq., K.C.
615. SAMUEL WILLIAM PARKER (39), Feloniously marrying!
Mary Ann Scanlan, his wife being alive.
Mr. PASMORE Prosecuted.
John HOWARD (Detective-Constable T.) I am stationed at Chiswick-
I arrested the prisoner on July 4th-I asked him if his name was Samuel
William Parker-he replied, “ Yes "-I said, “I shall arrest you for
bigamously marrying Ann Scanlan on September 7th, 1902, at the Catholic
Church, Stafford Road, Acton, your first wife, Louisa Caroline Maud,
being then and now alive"-he said, “Yes, that is right; I heard you
were making inquiries about it. I stopped to see it out”-I produce
copies of the certificates of the two marriages in the second he is de-
scribed as a widower—the second wife came to the police station and
said that she had heard that her husband had committed bigamy.
GEORGE NICHOLLS. I am a labourer at Brentford-I was present
