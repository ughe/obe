Inspector JOHN HOCKING, Y Division. I have been 15 years in the
police force. On June 28 1 was in charge of Somers Town Police
Station when Willson was brought in. It is absolutely false that I
told Hefford to say the man he saw was Willson. Butters asked
Hefford, Is this the man you saw this afternoon at the shop whom
you refer to as the governor? " Hefford said, Yes." No one has ever
suggested till now that I said any such thing to Hefford.
