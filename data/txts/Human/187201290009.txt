 196. ELIZABETH NICHOLLS  (27), and ELIZABETH BRANTON  (47) , Stealing two towels, one piece of repp, and other articles, of James Francis Lovell , the master of Nickolls. MR. THORNE COLE conducted the Prosecution; and MR. MOODY the Defence. WILLIAM FENELLEY( Policeman Y  449). On 7th January, about 10.30 I found Branton in the Ship public-house, Kentish Town, drunk—I took her to the station—she had these two bundles with her—I asked where she got them—she said she had been to Ludgate Hill to see a friend who gave them to her—I afterwards took Nicholls at the Daniel Lambert, Ludgate Hill.
   Cross-examined.  She gave me her right address. JOHN DALTON( Detective Officer Y ). On the morning of 8th January, I went with Fenelley to the Daniel Lambert, and told Nicholls I should take her in custody on a charge of stealing those articles, the property of her master—she said "You had better let me be till the morning"—I said "Well, it is morning now"—I searched her box in her presence, she said "There is nothing there belonging to my master, it all belongs to me"—I went on searching and found this pocket-handkerchief, marked "Lavell" this piece of repp, this towel marked "J. M. L." and three house cloths—I took her to the station—I afterwards searched Branton's lodging and found ten glasses, two plates, two dresses, a knife and fork, and twenty-four duplicates ( produced ). CLARA LAVELL. My father keeps the Daniel Lambert, Ludgate Hill—Nicholls was seven months in his service as cook—these towels are marked "Lavell"—I also identify this repp and this petticoat.
   MR. MOODY stated that he could not resist this evidence. GUILTY — Nicholls received a good character. Twelve Months' Imprison  ment each.
