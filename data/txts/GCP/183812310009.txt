428. WILLIAM COLEMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of
December, 5lbs. weight of mutton, value 2s. 6d. ; 4flbs. weight of pork,
value 2s. 4d.; 2}lbs, weight of beef, value 18, 3d.; and Alb. weight of
suet, value 6d.; the goods of Frances Somers.
MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
· SAMUEL ABORN SOMERS. My mother, Frances Somers, is a widow, and
carries on the business of a butcher in Somers Town. The prisoner was
in our service about four months this time, but prior to that he was four
years or more in our service in consequence of suspicions I gave instrue.
tions to Williams the policeman on the 23rd of December to watch, and
seeing the prisoner leave the house I made a signal to the policeman-
saw some mutton produced which I knew to be my mother's-I missed
it from the shop-I afterwards went to the prisoner's lodging and saw other
meat and suet.
