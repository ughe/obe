1698. JOHN CALVERT was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd bf May,
1 box, value 18.; and 14 medals, value 91. ; the-goods of Thomas Hock-
ings, in his dwelling-house.
JANE HOCKINGS. I am the wife of Thomas Hockings. We keep a
coffee-shop in Blackman-street-on the evening of the 2nd of May I was
called down into the shop- the prisoner was standing there, in great con-
fusion, having just let these medals fall from the box, which was kept in
the window, covered with glass—there was a curtain to the window-none
of my customers had any business at the window-the medals are worth
91.--they are silver gilt and metal-I have one here worth 11, that is
silver---they were there for sale they are called medals, and are worn as
such-he had only one cup of coffee, which he had partly drank, and paid
for-I had never seen him before.
