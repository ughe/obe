OLD COURT.--Wednesday, June 9th, 1869.
Before Mr. Justice Mellor.
540. ALICE BATES (34), was charged, upon the Coroner's Inquisition
only, with feloniously killing and slaying Michael Straub.
Mr. Daly, for the Prosecution, ofered no evidence, the Grand Jury having
ignored the Bill Not Guilty.
