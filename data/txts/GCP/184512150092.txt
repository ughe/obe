282. JOHN BIRCH was indicted for stealing 2 saws, value 158. ; 4 planes,
168. ; 4 chisels, 2s. ; and 1 basket, 2s.; the goods of Austin Piper; and that
he had been before convicted of felony.
Austin Piper. I live in Wenlock-street, North-road; I am a carpenter.
I was employed at an unfinished house, No. 9, Wenlock-street, on the 13th
of Nov.- I went to dinner at twelve o'clock, returned at one, and missed the
saws, planes, and other tools mentioned-they were worth about 358.- they
had cost me 21. 108.- I have seen two of my saws since I had left them in
the kitchen of the unfinished house.
William Pocock. I am in the service of Mr. Kemp, a pawnbroker. I pro-
duce a saw which was pawned with me on the 20th of Nov. in the name of John
Piper, Holywell-lane-it was pawned by somebody very much like the prisoner,
but I could not undertake to say it was him-bere is another saw pawned on the
22nd of Nov., which I did not take in the officer has the duplicate I made out
of the saw pawned on the 20th of Nov.--this is it.
Cross-examined by Mr. HORRY. Q. Have you a large business? 1. Not
very; forty or fifty persons come in a day-we had a number of persons there
the day I received this saw.
CHARLES COOPER (police-constable N 61.) I apprehended the prisoner on
the 8th of Dec.--I found on him this duplicate of a saw pawned at Mr.
Kemp's in the name of John Piper.
AUSTIN PIPER. This is one of my saws-the name of Piper is on the handle
of the other saw, but not on this-I lost all my tools together.
