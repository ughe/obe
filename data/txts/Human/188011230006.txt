When the prisoner came down without his shoes he did not go into the parlour—he went out to the back—the closet was at the back—sometimes it would be in the morning, sometimes in the evening—Mrs. Messenger told me that she had found this laudanum in the prisoner's pocket, and that she had accused him of buying it for her—she did not say why—I can't say for certain how soon that was before he left—I did not pay any attention to it at the time.
