 1288.  JAMES GAINSFORD was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of May , 1 bag, value 6d., and 100 lbs. of iron, value 5s., the goods of Michael Cohen.
 MICHAEL COHEN. I am an  old iron dealer, and live in Cowcross; the prisoner worked for me in my shed - he left on the 31st of May, about eight o'clock at night - this bag and iron are mine.
   CHARLES VINCENT. I am a shoemaker. On the 31st of May, about a quarter to eleven o'clock at night, I saw the prisoner come out of Cohen's yard, with this bag on his shoulder - I followed him, and called the watchman - we asked what he had got; he said he was going to take it to Mr. Cohen's - I said that was not the way, and took him to the watch-house.
   JAMES ALLIS. I assisted in taking him to the watch-house - it was not quite dark.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I saw the bag by the gate, and took it up to carry to Mr. Cohen's - being intoxicated, I went the wrong way.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Confined Three Months.
 1289.  JOSEPH SIBLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of May , 4 bells and 8 brass-nobs, the goods of Abraham Nonmus , and fixed to his dwelling-house; and JOSEPH ALLEN was indicted for feloniously receiving part of the same goods, knowing them to have been stolen.
 ABRAHAM NONMUS. The house,  No. 4, Middlesex-place, New-road, was in my care; it is empty - I had the keys of it, to let it, but did not reside there. I believe it is in Chancery.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1290.  MARGARET SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of May . 1 ham, value 10s., the goods of Robert Cox.
   BENJAMIN TIMBRELL. I am a watch-house-keeper. On the night of the 25th of May the prisoner was brought in, and the ham afterwards - I found only a few halfpence on her.
   ROBERT COX. I keep a shop in Tothill-street. On the 26th of May I lost a ham - I had seen the prisoner at the door, and saw my son take the ham from her - it was at the back of the shop. I have lost one entirely.
   CHARLES COX. I saw the prisoner sitting on the cheese in my mother's shop; she took up this ham, and went out- I followed her, and she dropped it from under her apron, a little way from the door.
   EDWARD COLYER. I am a watchman, and took her to the watch-house.
     GUILTY. Aged 25. Confined Three Months.
 1291.  SARAH HILLIER was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of June , 1 curtain, value 3s., the goods of Thomas Cowper.
 THOMAS COWPER. On the 1st of June this curtain was in my workshop, which is in the second floor - my girl came home, and said a strange woman had gone out with something - I went out, and took the prisoner with this curtain.
   MARY ANN LACK. I met the prisoner coming out, and told Cooper.
 THOMAS COOPER. I am an officer, and took her.
     GUILTY. Aged 55. Confined Three Months.
 1292.  JAMES GRAVES was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of June , 1 pair of reins, value 2s., the goods of Peter Winter ; 1 handkerchief, value 4d., and 1 waistcoat, value 1s., the goods of George Thomas Davis.
   MARY GLADE. I live in the Angel-inn-yard, Islington. On the 1st of June I saw the prisoner go into a stable of the inn, and take up this property - I went, and called the hostler, who stopped him, and took it from him.
   PETER WINTER. I am an hostler at the Angel. I stopped the prisoner with these things in his hat.
   GEORGE THOMAS DAVIS. This handkerchief and waistcoat are mine.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. Confined Three Months.
 1293.  LEONARD SMITH, EDWARD SMITH, and JOHN THOMAS, were indicted for stealing, on the 15th of April , 300 lbs. of oakum, value 3l., the goods of John Scott.
   THOMAS BUNN. I am clerk to John Scott, a ship-chandler, of Narrow-street, Limehouse. I was fetched to the warehouse on the morning of the 15th of April, about seven o'clock, and found Thomas in custody there; I said,"Have you got some oakum?" he said he knew nothing about it, but the two Smiths had asked him to bring two bundles of oakum to our warehouse, and they had each brought two themselves; I know the oakum by one bundle having been cut open on the Saturday before, and on the Monday morning they were all taken away.
 Cross-examined by MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. Is not Scott a dealer in marine-stores? A. Yes; I only know the oakum by the parcel which is cut open, and it is picked off Russian hemp; I am convinced of it; there is a good deal picked in that way.
 Cross-examined by MR. QUIN. Q. Has Scott any partner? A. Not in this trade; the oakum was taken away, and then brought back.
