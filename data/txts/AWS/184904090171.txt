1007. PATRICK CONNER, burglariously breaking and entering the
dwelling-house of Alfred Staniford, with intent to steal.
Mr. PAYNE, JUN., conducted the Prosecution.
ALFRED STANIFORD. I am a publican, in George-street, Lisson-grove St:
Marylebone. On 1st April, at a quarter past three o'clock in the morning, I
was awoke by my wife, who said she heard footsteps in the room-she jumped
up, and I did the same-I looked all round, and saw the prisoner under the
bed-I took hold of him and put him on the bed-I sent for a policeman,
and he was taken into custody--h said, 41 I hope you will forgive me this
time"- said, No, not if you were my own brother" knew him to be a
hardworking man a year and a balf ago.
JOHN MANNING (police-sergeant, D 44). I was called, and took the
prisoner-he had his shoes off-I searched about the yard, and found a ladder
against the washhouse-I went up the ladder, and found his shoes on the top
of the washhouse-1 asked him where his shoes were-he said he had lost
them-when he came down he said they were on the tiles.
Prisoner's Defence. Having been keeping company with the servant, I
went to the house for the purpose of seeing her, as I was going into the
country the next day and not wishing to disturb her master, and knowing
that the window was left unfastened, I thought I would avail myself of it to
get in to see her.
ALFRED STANIFORD re-examined. I hare no servant-m wife has a sister,
but she never had a word with the prisoner-my window was shut at night,
and it was open in the morning, when I found bim.
GUITY. Confined Nine Months.
