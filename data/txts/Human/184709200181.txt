 2218. JOHN PRITCHARD was indicted stealing 2 pewter pots, value 3 s  . 4 d  .; the goods of George Mason . GEORGE MATTHEWS. I am servant to Mr. George Mason, a market-gar dener and a publican; he keeps the Windmill, at Deptford. On the 1st of Sept. I saw two pots in a shed in his binding-ground—these pots produced are my master's property.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q.  When had you seen them in this garden? A.  At half-past seven in the evening—the garden adjoins the high road—I have seen the prisoner about three years before—he then used my master's house—I am foreman and salesman in the market-gardening business. JOHN PAVOR( policeman M  147.) I was on duty at half-past two o'clock in the night on the 1st. of Sept.—I came up to the prisoner, and asked what he had got under his jacket—he made no answer—I took him by the collar, put my hand under his jacket, and pulled out this quart pot—I put my hand under his other arm and pulled out this other—I asked whose they were—he said, "I believe they belong to Mr. George Mason, of the Windmill public-house"—he said he had picked them up under the hedge—I asked if he worked for him—he said, "No"—I asked how he came to take these pots, if he did not work for him—he then said he did work for him—I asked where he lived—he said, "In the brick-field," which is about a quarter of a mile from the spot—I said I must take him to the station—he became very violent—I threw him on the ground and made an alarm—my brother officer came, and we took him to the station.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did he not say he was going to take them home, and take them to Mr. Mason next day? A.  Yes, when I asked him, he said he was going to take care of them—he was not drunk—I could not say whether he had been drinking or not.
   JURY to GEORGE MATTHEWS. Q.  Were the post in the shed in the gar den? A.  Yes—the garden is enclosed by a hedge—the shed-door was shut—no one had any right there from the time I shut up the shed till the next morning.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY . Aged 31.— Confined Three Months .
