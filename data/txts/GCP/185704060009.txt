Q. Between the Friday when you had this conversation, and the Thurs-
day when you gave him into custody, did he not ask you to come and look
at the meat you had sold him 4. No, I never saw him-he never asked

I asked him why he had taken the sheep from my shepherd, and represented
to bim that they were paid for-he said that he had not done so I said,
"I shall lodge it in other hands”-that was all I said-he did not say,
"I was not to pay for them till next Thursday"- there was no arrange-
ment that he was to pay for them on the following Thursday.
Mr. COOPER. Q. How came it that he was not taken into custody
sooner A. Because it is my father's business I communicated with him,
he lives in Norfolk-I waited for his answer.
JEREMIAH RINGER. I am shepherd to the prosecutor. On 19th March
the prisoner came to me on the farm, at Ashford, about half past 3 o'dock --
my master was away at the time I do not know where he was the pri-
soner said he had bought three sheep and two skins of my master, and given
him 31. for them-I asked him for the money, he said that he had paid 31.
down to my master for the three sheep and two skins-I said that my master
left word with me not to let him have the sheep without the money-he
said that he had paid my master-I went and let him have three sheep, and
he put them on the cart.
Cross-examined. Q. You told him that your master bad instructed you
not to let him have them without he paid for them; did you ever state that
before! 4. No, sir—the prisoner said that he had paid 31 down for them
-I asked him if he had paid the money to my master, he said, “Yes, 32"-

ing two sheep from him in the autumn-I did not go to the prisoner's place
- I did not see him after the Thursday, when he got the sheep, till he was
taken- remember the prisoner being there in the morning-I saw him
with my master-I did not hear the conversation.
