JOHN RIDDELL. I occupied the top floor back room. I paid my
rent-5s. a week-to Mrs. Steele. On January 15 I went out about
fyr o'clock or ten past in the morning and returned about quarter
to four. I got in by the latch-key. When I got in I noticed smoke in
che passage-way; it was fairly thick. I went to my room and I found
smoke all the way upstairs. I came down again, threw off my over
erat and called loudly for Mrs. Steele. I got no answer. I went
up again, and looked into the kitchen; there was smoke there. The
doors of the rooms were standing open. I could see no actual fire
when I looked into the rooms. I did that three times. I then went
upstairs again and washed. I then heard a knock at the door, and,
Ct opening, found a constable there. He went into the front parlour.
The smoke was so dense I could not get in. Then the fire brigade
arrived and put out the fire.

police court at one of the hearings, but I was ill-the result of
drinking bout. I was so much upset by the fire that I took to drink.
