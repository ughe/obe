rnonies of George Fowler.
GEORGE FOWLER. I am a servant out of place, and live at Hescott,
near York, when I am at home. Between twelve and one o'clock on the

bridge—I went to bed with them both, and stopped till between five and
six o'clock next morning; and when I awoke, my watch and money were
gone, and the prisoners as well—it was a silver watch, worth 61. 108., a
sovereign, two or three half-crowns, and some Odd silver—I had put m
