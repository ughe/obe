2013. HENRY MAY, alias Harris, was indicted for feloniously killing
and slaying James Edward Herepath.
MR. JONES conducted the Prosecution.
John CLARK. I am a cabinet-maker. I saw a fight take place between
the deceased and the prisoner, at Lambeth-they fought about two rounds
--the first blow was struck by the prisoner-at the commencement of the
second round I saw the prisoner strike the deceased on the left side of his head
--the deceased continued to fight after that blow-after it was over I ob-
served him looking for his hat-he was standing by me, and I thought he
was comingtowards me, but he tumbled and fell, and pitched into the kennel
on his knee-he did not speak after that, but merely gave a deep sigh-his
head did not come in contact with the ground when he fell then-they had
been down together in the scuffle-he had been down twice, but was not
knocked down; it was struggling in the fight they were both down to-
gether-no blow was struck on either side after the fight had ceased.
Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. Did it appear to you that a push
of which the deceased complained before they fought was not intention-
ally done? A. It did not appear to me that he meant to insult him-it
was that the deceased was offended at, and then the prisoner offered to fight
-the deceased said " That is what I mean," and prepared to fight-the
deceased was sober-I consider the prisoner was in liquor-the deceased
remained for about ten minutes after the fight without any thing happening
to him.

the evening of the 14th of August, I was called to see the deceased-1
found him sitting on the curb-stone, with thirty or forty people round him,
supported by a policeman and others—I directed him to be taken to the
Dearest house-I felt his pulse and found it was fluttering-he was not able
to speak to me when I saw him in the house he appeared dead-I
