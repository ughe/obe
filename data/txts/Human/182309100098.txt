 1222.  HENRY WOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of July , fourteen umbrella covers, value 16 s.; twenty-one rings, value 1 s.; a flat iron, value 1 s., and an apron, value 6 d., the goods of James White.
   JANE WHITE. I am the wife of James White, who lives in Coleman-street, we lodge on the second floor. On the 24th of July, I went out about half past ten o'clock in the morning, returned in an hour - I had left the key with a lodger, for my husband; I found the door open and this property gone. I make umbrellas, the bolt of the lock was shot.
   JAMES WHITE. I am a carpenter, I was out till twelve o'clock, when these things were missed. I apprehended the prisoner on the 26th of July, in Type-street; he said, he would take me to where he sold the property, and took me to Needham's - he was a stranger.
   THOMAS NEEDHAM. I am an umbrella maker, and live in Rosemary-lane. On the 24th of July, between four and five o'clock, the prisoner brought me fourteen umbrella covers, and twenty-one rings, and asked 10 s. for them; I offered 5 s. - he came back and took the money, and brought White to me afterwards. He said, he took them for a debt of a man who was dead.
   MARY SHEW. I lodge with White, and had the key - I gave it to Maunders.
   MARY MAUNDERS. Shew gave me the key - I did not part with it till White came home.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing of the theft - I had them from a person who said a man was dead. I said I was doing it for another person.
     GUILTY. Aged 45. Confined Six Months and Whipped.
 1223.  JOHN WHITE was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of August , six table cloths, value 20 s., and three towels. value 1 s., the goods of Thomas Harrison, the elder, and Thomas Harrison, the younger.
   THOMAS HARRISON, JUN. I am in partnership with my father Thomas; we keep the  Tavistock-hotel, Covent-garden. The prisoner was in our service, as laundryman with his wife, and left about six months ago - we missed a large quantity of linen while he was with us; and in consequence of information, went to his lodgings in August; he was not at home, but his name was on the door, and his wife and daughter there - he keeps a chandlers-shop. On the first floor of the house I found six table cloths, and three towels, some marked Tavistock-hotel, at full length, and others with other initials.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. When did you miss them - A. I cannot say, as we missed a quantity at different times.
   JOHN PURTON. I am an officer. On the 6th of August, I went to the prisoner's house with a search warrant - I found a towel in the back parlour, a phosphorous box, and a bunch of keys, and the other things on the first floor - there were no lodgers in the house. I waited till he came in, and told him he was suspected of stealing these things; he was much agitated, and wished to put away a pocket book which he had. He afterwards said, he was in great trouble, and sorry for what he had done.
   RICHARD LIMBRICK. I went with Purton. The prisoner said he was in great distress, and his wife knew nothing about it. I found the duplicate of a table cloth on him.
   HENRY IVES. I am servant to Mr. Beecham, pawnbroker, Holborn. On the 29th of June, a table cloth was pawned in the name of Collins. I do not know who by.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 38. Transported for Seven Years.
 1224.  RICHARD ALDERTON was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of August , a saw, value 5 s. 6 d., and two squares, value 3 s., the goods of Joseph Dore.
   JOSEPH DORE. I am a journeyman carpenter. I lost my tools from a building in Kingsland-road on Saturday, the 8th of August, where I was at work. They were on the first floor at twelve o'clock when we went to dinner, and at one o'clock they were gone. The prisoner had no business there. I found my saw an hour after.
   BENJAMIN MATSON. I am a carpenter. I was working at this building, and left about twelve o'clock. I lost a saw. The prisoner had formerly worked on the premises. I have seen him lurking about since.
   WILLIAM BLENKHORN. I am a carpenter. I was at work about fifty yards from this building, and found the prisoner lying asleep among the shavings, about five o'clock in the morning, and turned him out.
   JAMES ESSEX. I am an officer. I took the prisoner on Sunday morning, in Bethnal-green, and found the duplicate of a saw upon him.
   RICHARD CARPENTER. I am shopman to Mr. Thimbleby. On the 9th of August, between twelve and one o'clock, the prisoner pawned the saw for 2 s.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined One Year and Twice Publicly Whipped.
 1225.  THOMAS BELFIELD was indicted for stealing on the 24th of June , a pocket-book, value 6 d.; a purse value 1 s., and two sovereigns and five shillings, the property of William Cansdale , from his person.
   WILLIAM CANSDALE. I live on Swan-terrace, Great Dover-road, and am collecting-clerk at a brewery. On Tuesday, the 24th of June, between nine and ten o'clock in the evening. I was sober. I was at the fire in Red Lion-street, Holborn. There was a great crowd. I got into the midst of it, and was endeavouring to make my way out. My pocket-book was in my left inside coat pocket, and my purse containing two sovereigns and five shillings in my left side breeches pocket. My coat was buttoned, and my breeches pocket also. They were safe when I entered the crowd. There was a general rush among the crowd; and immediately as I got out of the crowd, I found both my pockets had been robbed.
