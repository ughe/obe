Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. You were at the public-
house? A. Outside, not inside-I was looking for my youngest brother,
who was out selling work for me and Mrs. Johnson-I bad not been there
above two minutes when the fight commenced-it was among the parties who
came out of the public-house-it was turning out time-my husband is a
marble polisher-I am an artificial florist-we occupy one room--there was
no gin-drinking going on there-I have never seen my money since I have
known Mrs. Johnson for years-she is very respectable, as far as I know.
