WILLIAM GLADWIN( police-constable R  122.) On the 10th of Jan. I was in High-street, Woolwich—I stopped the prisoner with this pair of drawers under his jacket—I asked him where he got them—he gave me no answer—in going to the station he said he picked them up in the gutter, and then he said he took them out of a shop next to a baker's, and near the Dock-yard—the prosecutor's is near the Dock-yard, and next to a baker's. REBECCA FINCH( re-examined. ) There were three pairs of drawers pinned to a rail, and the pin with which this pair was pinned to the rail was bent, and was in the drawers when the officer brought them back, as if they had been pulled forcibly from the rail—these have, in every respect, the appear ance of those I lost. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined One Month .

MARGARET LAWRENCE was indicted for stealing 4 shawls, value 1 l  ., 1 spencer, 1 s  .; 2 gowns, 5 s  .; 3 shirts, 4 s  .; 1 pair of shoes, 6 d  .; 2 sheets, 5 s  .; 4 handkerchiefs, 6 s  .; 3 frocks, 2 s  .; 4 waistcoats, 2 l  .; 1 table-cloth, 3 s  .; 1 table-cover, 1 s  .; 1 cloak, 8 s  .; 1 painting and frame, 10 s  .; 1 tea-kettle, 9 s  .; 1 printed book, 1 s  .; 2 pairs of boots, 8 s  .; and 2 waistcoats, 6 s  .; the goods of Francis Lamburn Greenley ; and EDWARD LAWRENCE for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing them to have been stolen, against the Statute, &c.:—2nd COUNT  , for receiving the same of some evil disposed person. FRANCIS LAMBURN GREENLEY. I am a pawnbroker, and live at Broom field-place, in the parish of St. Paul's, Deptford. On the 21st of July Mar garet Lawrence came to my sale shop about a quarter before five o'clock in the afternoon—in consequence of what transpired there she was taken into custody—we went to her room at No. 3, Union-street, Deptford—she gave the officer the key of it, and said it was her room—we there found shawls, table-cloths, gowns, sheets, and the other things mentioned, which were my property; and a great many duplicates—we found this picture, which I had had safe on the Saturday previous to Margaret Lawrence being given in custody—this pair of boots were safe on the Monday—these are all my property, and have all my marks on them—they had been taken by somebody from my premises—I had not sold them. GEORGE HAY( police-constable R  91.) I went with Mr. Greenley to the prisoner's room—I witnessed the finding of these things in the room—they were all about the room, and some were in a box or a trunk—I went down and fetched Edward Lawrence her husband from his work—I took him to the lodging, and pointed out to him the things we had found—he said he could not account for them—I received the key of the trunk that was in the room from him, and my brother officers opened the trunk in my presence—these waistcoats and boots, and this lot of things were found in it—they are all identified by the prosecutor—we pointed out the heap of things to Edward Lawrence, and I heard my brother officer ask him how they came there—he said he did not know—he could not give any account of the possession of them—when we asked him for the key of the trunk, he said, "There are many more things there."
   Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON . Q.  I believe he said, "There are more in that chest?" A.  He said, "There is as many more in that trunk"—I think he has three children—I fetched him from his work—he is an engineer at the Steam Navigation Company's wharf—he told me he was out from morn ing till night, and did not know where these things came from.
