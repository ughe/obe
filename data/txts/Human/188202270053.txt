   Prisoner's Defence.  I was supposed to send in my advices every day to the chief office, and receive them back once a week to enter in my stock-book. I did not receive them for three weeks or a month. Childs said the last time I took them to do up that he had not time, and I left them for him to do. After he was dismissed I took my book back to Kensing-ton, and he had made the entries in pencil, which I inked over. I knew nothing of the erasures. GUILTY.— Fifteen Months' Hard Labour.
