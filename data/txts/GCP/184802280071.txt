JOHN SELLERS. I am a brewer, at Lyme Regis. I and my wife came up
by the mail-train to the Paddington-station, and were inside the cab, going as
Witnesses to the House of Commons-this basket was on the top of the cab
--it had a ham in it, a pair of lady's boots, and a pair of gentleman's slippers
-I missed it--this is it, and its contents are mine.

839. THOMAS SHEA, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 28.; the goods
d's man unknown, from his person.
WILLIAM BOSLEY. I live at 25, York-street, Commercial-road, and am a
porter. On 8th Feb., at a quarter past six o'clock in the evening, I was coming
down Holborn-hill-I saw the prisoner following a gentleman, and a young
lad of the same size was by bis side-the prisoner caught the tail of the
gentleman's coat with one hand, and with his other took a handkerchief from
bis pocket, and placed it in his breast I told a policeman--the prisoner saw
me speak to him, and ran away, and the officer after him-) told the gen-
tleman-he said yes, he had lost a handkerchief-he did not give his name
or address-hesaid he should not trouble himself about it.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE, Q. What sort of a handkerchief was it?
A. Rather dark-it looked worth about 28.- the policeman was a stranger to
me-I never spoke to him before-1 swear I live at 25, York-street East, Comu
mercial-road--the policeman has been there-I work for Mr. Marsh, of 84,
Houndsditch-he employs me regularly when he wants me I was working
for him all last week-I had been that night to Cursitor-street, to fetch a book
-I met the policeman afterwards-he said to me, “Do not forget to come
to-morrow"- he did not say, "I will give that fellow a jacketing"--we went
and had a drop of gin-I had not been often with him-I have seen him once
since I was in Holborn on business, carrying parcels for Mr. Marsh-1 had
not to pay 1s. 6d. a-week for hurting the arm of a boy named Sullivan-I
was not had up at Guildhall in Nov. last-I know Moor-lane-I did not say
I lived there--the gentleman who lost his handkerchief was rather taller than
myself; his coat looked like a dark olive-I do not know what buttons he
had-be had a black hat I did not notice whether he had a bat-band, or
whether he had boots or shoes-York-street East is the third or fourth
turning in the Commercial-road on the left hand-a person named Hodson
keeps the house, I have not been often with the same policeman up and
down Holborn-I never saw him before that nigbt, to my knowledge-I
never was with him at all.
WILLIAM Covill. I am fourteen years old - I live 45, Grosvenor-street,

prisoner lift a gentleman's coat up and take a handkerchief out-he put it

to the first policeman I saw-as soon as the policeman went up to the

e he is the same person I had never seen him before it was not a minute
