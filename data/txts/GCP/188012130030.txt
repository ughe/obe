Cross-examined by Mr. GEOHEGHAN. I found no bar of iron or any
revolvers--there was a lot of broken glass inside the bar where James
Gill was brought in by the policeman-I heard James Gill and Barrett
speak to each other; I could not hear what they said; it was after that
that I saw Gill's head bleeding-it is not correct to say that 10 or 12
people were beating him the first shot was fired in the private bar,
where he treated the female; it was when the prisoners were following
him up that he fired the second shot.
