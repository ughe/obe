2552. HERBERT GOMPERTZ was indicted for stealing, on the
26th of June, 1 sovereign, 4 half-crowns, 6 sixpences, 36 pence, and 134
halfpence, the monies of Robert Henry Davies, his master.
HOBERT HENRY Davies. I live in Fletcher-row, Clerkenwell. In June
last I kept a beer-shop at Vauxhall-the prisoner was in the habit of com-
ing there-on the 26th of June I left him in charge of the premises between
two and three o'clock, locked the desk, took the key with me, and left
the money stated in the indietment, safe-I went out to tea and left him
to mind the business; his master, next door, being a pious man, I thought
