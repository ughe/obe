577. SAMUEL ALLINGHAM, embezzling 41. 23. ; which he bad re-
ceived on account of James Piggott, his master.
JAMES PIGGOTT. I live at Richmond ; the prisoner was in my service. On
6th Jan., I gave him this check for 51., to get changed - I told him to take
his wages, 188., out of it—be was to return me 41. 25.-1 did not see him
again till the Wednesday following-he then wanted to know whether, if he
brought the money back, I would not proceed against him I told him I
could not receive it, the law must take its course.
Prisoner. It was on the Tuesday that I went back to my master; I told
him I had been in bad company, and lost the money; I would have returned
it, but I was taken the same evening, and bad not the opportunity : I wrote
to my master on the Monday, to tell him I would come back and work it
out; he told me to get it cashed on my way home. Witness. I received this
letter-(this being read, was dated 8th Jan., begging forgiveness for having lost
the money) - I gave him leave to go home that evening.
James DENMAN. I am parish-clerk of Petersbam-I cashed this check for
the prisoner, at the Dyson's Arms, on Saturday evening, 6th Jan.
GEORGE Wood (policeman, V 209). I apprehended the prisoner on the
Tuesday, at Kingston, at his own house-I had traced him to London, and
from London home again-he did not come home on the Saturday.
