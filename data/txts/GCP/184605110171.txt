THOMAS BENT (police-constable V 95.) On the 5th of May I was on duty
in the Wandsworth-road-I saw the two prisoners talking together a little
before two o'clock the man appeared to give her something - then sex
Hayes leave the man and cross the road, and the man sat down by some rail.
inge- saw Hayes afterwards in Mrs. Baughan's shop, and I heard the
dispute about the shilling- I received this shilling from Mrs. Baughan, and
Hayes was allowed to go away-I saw her go to the end of the lane-she
there joined Mayes, and they went on-I followed them to Vauxhall.gate-
they stood talking some minutes, and then went towards the Triangle-
Mayes then appeared to give her something, and Hayes went to Mr. Knap-
ton's shop-she came out-then she and Mayes walked away together-I
went into Knapton's and received a shilling from Weedon-I then followed
the prisoners up the Clapham-road-they parted-Hayes went into a shop,
and I went up to Mayes-he put something into his mouth, and held bis head
back - he said, "I have got nothing, I have not uttered any bad money,
have no bad money; all my money is good" - I said, “You have given the
woman some"-I found on him a sovereign and a ball good, and 7fd.-1
found no more bad money-I found part of a penny roll, and some mould,
apparently the same as had been rubbed over the shillings- it is the same
colour as they are—it is road dirt, apparently mixed with gunpowder-it was
wet, but it has got quite light by being dry--the shillings given me in the
shop were very dark, and appeared as if this stuff had been rubbed into the
letters--this is such stuff as would darken shillings. -
Court. Q. How near was Mayes to Hayes when she was in any shop!
A. He was only three doors from the shop where she was apprehended-he
was very nearly close--at the second shop she went into, be walked by while
she was in-I should think he was fifty yards from the first shop.
ROBERT MOORE (police-sergeant V 12.) On the 4th of May I saw Hayes
in Mr. Jennings's shop-I went in-Mr. Jennings gave her in charge, and
Mrs. Jennings gave me this shilling- I told her to mark it.
Mr. John Field. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint. These
shillings are all counterfeit, and cast in the same mould--they appear to have
had some black preparation rubbed over them.
Mayes's Defence. I met this young woman in Walworth-road: I asked
her where she was going; she said, “To Kennington-common;" 1 said I
would take a walk with her; I thought she was a servant; I had no bad
money.
HAYES-Guilty. Aged 20.-Confined One Year.
MAYES--Not GUILTY.
