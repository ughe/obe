JANE WILKS. I am a laundress, and the wife of Edward Wilks, of 58,
Canberry Park Road, Kingston-on Bank Holiday, August 3rd, the
prisoner Dix, whom I knew, came to my house about 7.10 a.m.; he said,
"Will you take this coat, Mrs. Wilks, and mind it?"-I said, "Yes"-
I dried it for him-he said he was very cold and wet, as he had been out
all night I gave him some breakfast- about three that afternoon he
asked me to pledge the coat-I took it to Carn's and pledged it for half a
crown, saying it was my husband's—this is it, and this is the ticket I
received from Mr. Carn-I gave the prisoner the half a crown and he
went away-he left the coat with me to get what I could on it.

in Dix's company.
