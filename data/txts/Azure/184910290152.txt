ELIZA JONES.
I am a widow, and live at 18, Fashion-street, Spitalfields.
The prisoner lodged with me, on and off, for five years, always as a single
woman—a man visited her a few times, but never came further than the door
—I have not seen him lately—she never said anything to me about him, or
any other man—she said she went out selling an article for a man, getting a
better price than he could get; she did not say what man.
