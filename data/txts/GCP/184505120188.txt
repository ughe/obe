Before Mr. Recorder.
1199. JOHN VOSPER was indicted for stealing 5 pairs of boots,
value 188., the goods of James Berry, his master.
JAMES Berry. I am a shoemaker, and live at Southwark. The pri-
soner was my journeyman for about twelve months, and lodged in the
house-my workshop is on the second floor-on the 9th of April, between
eleven and twelve o'clock at night, he asked me to sell him a candle,
knowing I was busy, he said he wished to do as much work as possible
in that week, and he would work late, or all that night-I allowed Mrs.
Berry to let him have a candle-I went to rest-he went up to the work
shop-he slept in the house, and could retire when he thought proper
-next morning I found he was gone, with another, who has not been
taken since-five pairs of boots were also gone, part his own work, and
part another work man's I have since seen three pairs of the boots, but
not the other two.
