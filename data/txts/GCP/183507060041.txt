David FLEMING. I keep a public-house at the corner of Gravel-lane.
On the 29th of June, I went out, leaving the prisoner and others in my
tap-room- returned in about twenty-five minutes I heard something
I went to the prisoner, and asked what business he had with my rabbits-
he said he had got no rabbits-he wanted to go away--I laid hold of him
he struggled, and I took two rabbits from his pocket-he took one out,
and another was found on him.
MARY CUTHBERTSON. I live with the prosecutor. I missed the rabbits
from the hutch-I did not see them taken from the prisoner-I know these
are my master's.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Was not the prisoner very
drunk? A. No; he had had some beer.
Prisoner's Defence. I was tipsy-I do not know how they came into my
pocket.
GUILTY. Aged 29-(Recommended to mercy by the Jury and the Pro-
secutor) Confined Two Days. .
1645. JOSEPH MONTGOMERY BENNETT was indicted for steal-
ing, on the 29th of May, 9 shirts, value 21.; 1 shift, value 48. ; 1 shawl,
value 98. ; 3 table-cloths, value 78.; 1 pair of stockings, value 18.; 1 flan-
nel shirt, value 28.; 3 printed books, value 78.; the goods of Robert Ro-
binson Alexander: and 2 shirts, value 58.; and 1 waistcoat, value 38.; the
goods of Samuel Waller.
MARY ALEXANDER. I am the wife of Robert Robinson Alexander ; we
keep the King of Prussia public-house, in Wych-street. The prisoner
lodged there some time he left in May last we then missed nine shirts,
a shift, and other articles—these are the things."
WILLIAM Watson. I am in the employ of a pawnbroker in Green-street,
Leicester-square. These things were all pawned by the prisoner between
March and May last.

1646. JOHN MORGAN was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of June,
three handkerchiefs, value 3s., the goods of George Cresey; and that he
had been before convicted of felony.
GEORGE CRESEY. I live in Lawrence-lane, St. Giles, and am a smith.
I had three handkerchiefs, and 30s. in silver, on the 9th of June-I laid
down on my bed, and fell asleep I had not laid long before I missed
them I know the prisoner; he lodged in the house for a little while.
Prisoner. Do you think I stole them? Witness. I do not know; I
swear these are my handkerchiefs.
MARY EAGLE. On the day of the robbery, the prisoner called me out,
and said he wanted to speak to me-I went with him to the pawnbroker,
and pawned one handkerchief, which he gave me- I then went to Rogers's
shop, where I sold the other two, and gave him the money-he put his
head in at the door as I was selling them, and said, “Do you think I stole
them."
Prisoner. Q. Had you not been drinking with the prosecutor that
morning? A. Certainly I had; I get my living by hard work, as can be
proved- I did not accuse any old man of it.
Prisoner. I did not know but that she was the prosecutor's wife; she
had been dining with him, and drinking with him.
GEORGE CRESEY. Yes, she had, but she is not my wife.
