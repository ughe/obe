645. ANN HICKS was indicted for stealing 2 frocks, value Is.; 1
dressing gown, 28. 6d. ; 1 glass cloth, 2d.; and 20 candles, 18.; the goods of
Edward Pain, her master.
EDNA PAIN. I am the wife of Edward Pain-we live at Battersea-the
prisoner was in my service-I had her boxes searched, and found a child's
frock, a dressing gown, and the other things stated--I can swear they art
ours.
FREDERICK LANGTON (police-constable V 323.) I was sent for to Mr.
Pain's-he asked the prisoner if she had any property in her boxes belonging
to him or Mrs. Pain-she said No-I searched her boxes, and found these
articles-she then said nothing--I also found some wine in a shed.
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor.-Confined
Four Months.
