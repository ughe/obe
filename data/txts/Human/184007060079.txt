   Q.  You know his hand-writing, of course? A.  Yes—( looking at the paper headed "Rules and Regulations" found in the prisoner's box )—I believe this paper is his hand-writing—all that is his writing—I have received two or three notes from him since he has been confined—I have seen him write many times—I have no doubt whatever these are his hand-writing—these three notes—( looking at them )—are also his hand writing.
