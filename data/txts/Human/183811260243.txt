ANNA MARIA WIS. I was in the shop on the 8th of November. The prisoner is the woman who came and passed a crown, I am certain—on the 13th my father called me down, and I saw her again. WILLIAM WEAVE. ( police-constable K  86.) I took the prisoner, and received this crown piece from Mr. Wise—he also gave me this other crown piece—I have had it ever since. MR. JOHN FIEL. I am Inspector of coin to the Royal Mint. These are both counterfeit, and both from the same mould.
   Prisoner's Defence . I met a man in the Borough—he gave me some gin and water, and gave me a crown piece, and told me to get him half a pound of candles, as he was a bricklayer, and going to set a copper—with respect to the other crown, I was not in the place. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months.
 262. WILLIAM SCOTT was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   MESSRS. CHAMBER . and SHE . conducted the Prosecution. CHARLES BURGBSS GOF. ( police-constable L  81.) On the evening of the 20th of November I was on duty in Tower-street, Blackfriars. I saw the prisoner in company with a young girl—I followed them to Union-street, Borough—I then went up to the prisoner, and took bold of both his hands—I called my brother constable, who took hold of Him by the throat—I saw six bad shillings fall from his mouth—I picked up five, and Brooks took up the other one—I looked at them at the light, and they were all bad. JAMES BROO. I was with Goff. I followed the prisoner, and throt tled him—I saw him trying to swallow something—six bad shillings fell from his mouth—I took up one, and on searching him I found another bad shilling in his pocket, and ten pence farthing of good moneys when the shil lings fell on the pavement, I said to Goff, "These dropped from his mouth"—the prisoner said he had none in his mouth—I am quite sure they came from his mouth. MR. JOHN FIEL. These seven are all counterfeits, and all cast in one mould.
   Prisoner's Defence . I was by Blackfriars-road, and saw a piece of dirty rag, and in it were these seven shillings—I put them into my pocket—one fell down the leg of my trowsers—I took them out, and put them into my mouth—I did not know whether they were good or bad. GUILTY.* Confined One Year.
