MERS. SLEIGH and Lewis conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. PALMER
the Defence. The particulars of this case were unfit for publication.
THE COURT considered that there was no evidence on the first second, and
third Counts, and MR PALMER contended that the fourth Count did not disclose
any offence. Tas COURT was of opinion that there was evidence to go to the
Jury upon the fourth Count, and the question of lar could be raised by motion
in arrest of judgment, if necessary. The Jury found the prisoner Guury on
the Fourth Count.
