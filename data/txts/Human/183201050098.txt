485. HANNAH GADSBY was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of January , 2 sheets, value 4s, and 1 counterpane, value 2s., the goods of Ann Cowell.
 ANN COWELL. On Monday, the 2nd of January, the prisoner hired a furnished room of me; a soldier took the room with her for a week, and they said they wanted clean sheets - I put them clean sheets; the soldier gave me 1s. earnest, and said the prisoner was his wife - the next morning they went out, and never returned; they left the door wide open - I fetched a Policeman, and missed two sheets, and the counterpane, which were let to them; I had the soldier apprehended the next morning.
   HENRY KEAN EDWARD NEWSTEAD. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Tothill-street. I took in a sheet and a quilt from a woman, on the 3rd of January.
   JAMES STANTON, I live at Mr. Morritt's - I have a sheet, pawned by the prisoner, on the 3rd of January.
   GEORGE PECK. I am a Policeman. I took the prisoner; she gave me these duplicates of the sheet and counterpane.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I slept there the next night.
   ANN COWELL. She never came back to my knowledge.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. - Transported for Seven Years.
 Fifth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
 486.  DANIEL JONES and JOHN PEDDRICK were indicted for stealing, on the 14th of December , 1 shirt, value 3s., the goods of Timothy White.
   ELIZABETH WHITE. I am the wife of Timothy White, and live in York-street, Kingsland-road. On the 14th of December I hung some things out to dry, and among them some shirts; I missed one of them.
   CHARLES BENFIELD. I keep cows, and live three doors from White's. I had seen the two prisoners within four or five yards of her garden; they were both together, and I told Waller of them.
   CHARLES WALLER. I am a Policeman. Benfield gave me information, and I took the prisoners; I found this shirt in Peddrick's jacket - he said he had picked it up.
 Jones' Defence. Peddrick told me he had picked up a shirt, and the officer took us into custody.
 Peddrick's Defence. I was going along, and picked it up.
 JONES -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 PEDDRICK -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Three Months.
 487.  WILLIAM LUCKER was indicted for embezzlement.
   SOPHIA SUSANNAH BYFIELD. I am the wife of Thomas Richard Byfield; we are chimney-sweeper s. The prisoner was in our employ - he had to receive money on our account.
   ELIZABETH MILLS. I live in Arlington-street. I paid the prisoner 1s. 6d. for sweeping a chimney on the 24th of December: he did not give me a receipt then, but he generally did.
   RICHARD DAWSON. I paid the prisoner 1s. 6d. for sweeping my father's chimney on the 24th of December.
 MRS. BYFIELD. He never accounted to me for these sums; he said Mrs. Mills would pay on a future day, when she wanted some more done.
 Prisoner's Defence. My mistress came to me, and asked me to go back and work the money out. Witness. Yes, I said he might come back and get my husband's money.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Fifth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 488.  MARY MORGAN was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of December , 10 handkerchiefs, value 5s., the goods of Henry Davis.
   GEORGE BALLINGER, I am shopman to Mr. Henry Davis, a hosier, in Oxford-street. On the 8th of December a man came into the shop, and in consequence of what he said. I went out and took the prisoner about fifteen yards off - I took her back, put my hand under her shawl, and found these ten handkerchiefs of my master's; she acknowledged her guilt, and asked forgiveness.
 Prisoner. I was in distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 46. - Confined Ten Days.
 489.  JAMES OLIVER was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of November , 1 bedstead, value 10s., the goods of Thomas Ridge.
   ANN RIDGE. I am the wife of Thomas Ridge- we live in Cumberland-street, Middlesex-hospital. On the 29th of November, between five and six o'clock in the evening, I lost a bedstead; I had seen it safe half an hour before - they left one leg behind, and the broker had one made.
   JOHN PARROTT. I am a broker, and live in Queen-street, Seven-dials. On the evening of the 29th of November, the prisoner came to my shop with another young man, who carried a bedstead; the prisoner said he had a bedstead for sale, and asked if I would buy it - he asked 3s. 6d. for it; I offered him 3s. - he went away, came back again, and said if I would stand a quartern of gin I should have it for 3s. - I gave him the 3s.; we all three then went to a public-house, and had the gin - the prisoner afterwards came with an iron pot; I gave him into custody - he said his father bought the bedstead for him six months before; it wanted a leg - I did not know him before.
   CHARLES PARROTT. I live with my father. I remember the prisoner bringing this bedstead.
   THOMAS MADAM. I am a Policeman. I took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I never saw the broker in my life till last Saturday month, when he gave me into custody.
     GUILTY. Aged 18 - Transported for Seven Years.
