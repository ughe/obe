FRANCIS GEORGE SAUNDERS, manager, London and South-Western
Bank, Clapham Junction. On September 7, 1904, David Myers, of 61,
St. John's Road, S.W., opened an socount by & payment of $12. On
April 18, 1905, the balance, £20.17s. 2d., Tas transferred to the Cam
den Town branch.
Cross-examined by Sir E. Carson. So far as I know, Joseph Myers
had nothing to do with the account. He introduced David to us.
The account was never overdrawn. (To the Recorder.) It was a
straightforward small account.
FREDERICK ADOLPH RICHARDS, manager, London and South-Western
Bank, Camden Town. David Myers opened an account with my bank
on April 17, 1905, by vransfer from the Clapham branch in the name
of the Camden Town Furnishing Company. I produce a certified copy
to December 30, 1905. When Mr. Baker took possession under a con.
sent order and the account, it showed a balance of £24 98., which is
still standing to his credit. The turnover is £2,691. The account
has always been in credit.
Cross-examined. My bank was aware that the Camden Town Fur.
nishing Company was David Myers. So far as I know, Joseph Myers
bad nothing to do with the account at any time,
