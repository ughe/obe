(Wednesday, October 20.)
HENRY Davis, head stableman to Wade and Co., Ltd., Station
Road, Walworth. On December 28, 1908, my employers had a Ralli
car in the stable-the cushions, back seat, and the lamps I had at home.
On January 5, 1909, I went to the stable, found it had been entered
with a key and the Ralli car stolen. It was brought back on Septem-
ber 24-the wing had been straightened and the monogram taken out;
it was not my employers monogram.
