ZECARIAH RICHARD CATCHPOLE. I am a cheesemonger and live in Cable-street. On the 4th or 5th of November George Palmer brought me a sample of paper; and in a day or two afterwards he brought me 59lbs., which I bought for 13 s  —he gave this bill of parcels—this is the paper I bought—I afterwards gave it to the officer. GEORGE SNELLING. I am a pork-butcher in the Minories. I bought nearly 30lbs. of waste paper in October, of young Palmer, at 3 d . or 3 1/2 d . a lb.—it was very small paper, and part of it was done up in bundles—I cannot say that I examined it—there were two or three small books I believe among it—I did not open it—there was no secrecy about it. JOSEPH MARTIN. I am a pork-butcher, in Somerset-street, Whitechapel. I bought some paper of young Palmer on a Sunday in November—on the Tuesday afterwards he came to me and told me he had heard the paper he had sold me was stolen, and he was very anxious to get it back for the prosecutor—I believe this to be part of it—I bought it as it is now.

ELIZABETH NAPP.
