HENRY PARKER. (police-sergeant K. 10.) I went to No. 14, John-
street, about eleven o'clock, on the 29th--the prisoner was at the door
with a gin-bottle in her hand, she was perfectly sober-I called the pro-
secutor, and asked if that was the girl-he said it was--I asked her what
place she occupied in the house---she said, “None at all"-I went into a
back room, where there was another woman- I turned up the bed, and
found this handkerchief-the prosecutor identified it-I said to the pri-
soner, “Where did you get this handkerchief?"-she said " It was given to
une by the sailor, in part payment"-I asked her if she had any money-she
said none but half-a-crown which she had in her hand-I saw the bon-
net and stays, and know the bonnet was hers-I had seen her in it before.
GUILTY. Aged 25.-Transported for Seven Years.
1704. GEORGE SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
July, 1 bag, value 18. ; 1 pair of stockings, value 58.; 1 pair of gloves,
value 2s. ; 1 buckle, value 18.; 1 cardcase, value 1s.; 1 purse, value ls.
3 half-crowns, and 1 shilling; the goods and monies of Rose Ann Ward,
from her person.
Rose ANN WARD. I live at No. 29, Charles-street. About two o'clock
on the 3rd of July, I was going into Hassel's, the comb-maker's, in St.
Paul's church-yard, in the middle of the day I had a reticule in my hand
containing all these things, and a man snatched the bag out of my hand-
I never saw his face-a gentleman passing cried “Stop thief"---the bag
was brought to me-this is the one.
Thomas Davis. I am an officer of Cheapside. I heard a cry of “Stop
thief"-I saw the prisoner running-I pursued and took him-- I took this
bag from his hand, and took him to the Compter, and waited till the even-
ing-the lady was alarmed, and went home to Berkeley-square till the
evening-the bag contained the things stated.
Prisoner. I have been in great distress for three or four weeks-1 had
been so pushed I did not know what to do to get a bit of bread-my wife
and child were very ill, and I was very ill through lowness of living.
GUILTY. Aged 23.--Recommended to mercy by the Proscculrix and Jury,
believing him to be in distress.--Confined Three Months.
