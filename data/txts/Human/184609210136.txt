 1911. THOMAS WHATMAN and EDWARD RICKARDS were in—dieted for stealing 1 bedstead, value 135.4 d  ., the goods of Alfred Webster , their master; to which
   WHATMAN  pleaded GUILTY. ALFRED WEBSTER. I am a bedstead-manufacturer, and live in Christopher-square, Long-alley. Both prisoners were in my employment—on the 14th of Sept. I received a communication from Mary Andrew, and asked Rickards where he had taken a bedstead to—he said, "b—you, what do I know about your bedstead? I will not work for you any longer if you accuse me"—I said he should not go unpunished—he said he would not do another stroke for me, because I accused him—I fetched a policeman, and gave him into custody—he said he would not go, and fought with the policeman—I was obliged to get another—he asked whether I was going to give him in charge alone—I went back, and charged Whatman with robbing me—he went out of the shop, and I saw him coming from Mr. Johnson's with my bed-stead, and gave him in charge—they had no right to take it THOMAS JOHNSON. This bedstead was taken from Whatman on his way from my house to Mr. Webster's—it had been brought to me by Whatman—Rickafds was not with him—I did not see him at all. ARTHUR BROWNE( policeman. ) I took the prisoners into custody.
   RICKARDS  — NOT GUILTY.
 1912. THOMAS WHATMAN and EDWARD RICKARDS were again  indicted for stealing the head and foot of a bedstead, value 13 s  . 6d, the goods of Alfred Webster , their master; to which
   WHATMAN  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 31 .— Confined Six Months . ALFRED WEBSTER. Rickards was in my service. On the 14th of Sept. this bedstead was brought to me—I had not missed it—I saw it safe to it. MARY ANDREWS. I live in Mr. Webster's house. On the 14th of Sept, about half—past seven o'clock in the morning, I heard somebody going out of the door—I got out of bed, looked out of the window, and saw the prisoners—I only saw Rickards's face—they were taking the head and foot of a bedstead away—each had a piece of it. THOMAS JOHNSON. Whatman brought me these parts of a bedstead on the morning of the 14th and sold them to me—he was alone—it was not found at my premises—I have never bought anything of Rickards.
