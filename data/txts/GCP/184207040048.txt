NEW COURT.-Tuesday, July 5th, 1842.
Sixth Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
2075. WILLIAM KEATE was indicted for embezzling, on the 21st
May, 158. 9d.; and, on the 31st of May, 51. 148. ; the monies of Hender
Treverton and another, bis masters; and that he had been before convicted
of felony; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Transported for Seven Years.
2076. FRANCIS STAGG was indicted for embezzlement.
FRANCIS ALLEN. I am a baker, and live in Mortimer-street, Maryle
bone. The prisoner was my journeyman for about eleven months-be used
to receive money for me, which he should pay me the same day that he to-
ceived it-he left me on the 20th of June, without notice-on the 25th
the policeman brought him to me- I told him he had taken 138. 5d, of one
customer, and 98. 11d. of another, and not paid it to me--he said he had
nothing to say.
JEMIMA Rook. I am the wife of Richard Rook, living in Paul-street,
Portman-market; I deal with the prosecutor. The prisoner brought
bread to my house-on Monday the 20th of June I paid him 4s. 6d. for
Mr. Allen-I saw him sign my book.
SARAH FREEMAN. I am the wife of Abraham Freeman, who keeps i
coffee-house in Blenheim-street. On the 20th of June I paid the prisoner
13s. 5d. for his master.
MARIA Woon. I live in Great Titchfield-street, and deal with the pro-
secutor. On Monday night, the 20th, I paid the prisoner 9s. 1d. for his
master, in six shillings, and 38. in copper.
FRANCIS ALLEN re-examined. The prisoner never paid me 4s. 6d. from
Rook, 13s. 5d. from Freeman, or 9s. id. from Wood-he did not account
to me for it.
Thomas WALLIS (police-constable E 130.) I was called to Mr. Allen's,
in Portland-street, and the prisoner was given in charge-he said he did not
mean to wrong Mr. Allen, and that he had been rather intoxicated
found 38. 31d. on him.

Prisoner's Defence. I had got in debt with my master; I was ill, and
was obliged to leave him ; I had but 58. a week, not enough to live upon;
and when I received this money from Mrs. Freeman there were two omni-
buses coming along; I ran, missed the half-sovereign out of my hand, and
was afraid to go back.

2077. THOMAS ELLIS was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of June,

WILLIAM FAIR. I am a boot-maker. and live in Fleet-street--the pile
soner was in my service about three months. On the 27th of June I lost
these four pairs of boots now produced I can swear to them by the marks
JOHN BARTON. I am foreman to the prosecutor. These boots were
my hand five minutes before I missed them I put them on the drawer
at the farther end of the shop,they were found in the wood-cellar, behind
