618. RICHARD BAILEY was indicted for unlawfully obtaining 25 yards
of canvas, and 24 skeins of twine, the goods of John Soulby, by false
pretences.
JOHN Thomas COMB. I am in the service of John Soulby, a ship-chandler,
residing in Wapping. I remember the prisoner coming to our premises on
the 23rd of Jan.-he said he wanted some half-bleached or brown canvas
told him that we had no half-bleached, but that we had got brown-he said
he wanted it for Captain Brown, of the William, of Kirkcaldy-I showed him
a piece of brown, and let him have twenty-five yards, because we believed it
was for the captain-I booked it-he came afterwards and had twenty-four
pieces of twine which he said were for the same captain, and he took them
away-I gave him a bill with the first, and then I put the other in the same
bill.
SAMUEL BRIDGE. I am in the service of Mr. Soulby. I remember the
prisoner coming and selecting the canvas-he said he wanted it for the William,
Captain Brown, of Kirkcaldy, lying in the St. Katherine's Docks, at the jetty
- he had the canvas-I said I would send it, but he said he had a person to
take it, I had better not send it I followed him, but I did not find the vessel
--I had been there two or three hours before, and there was no such vessel in
the dock- I have never found such a person as Captain Brown-I have
searched since.
od
SAMUEL KINSEY. I am a police-constable. The prisoner was given into
my charge on the 24th of Jan., near Bermondsey New Church-I asked
Bridge what was the charge-he said, robbing them of two bolts of canvas
this was said in the prisoner's presence-the prisoner said it was no robbery
-the acting inspector asked him, In my presence, where be lived-he said,
"Mill-lane, Tooley-street"-I went there, but found no such residence-I
found that he had occupied a loft there two year's back.
ROBERT WILSON. I am in the employment of Mr. Grass, a pawnbroker.
I produce two pieces of canvas which were pledged at my master's by Richard
Bailey.

of John Soulby by false pretences.

was not inside the house-he said he wanted it for Captain Brown, of the
William, of Kirkaldy-he called the other prisoner in, and wanted him to carry
it away-two bolts were given him, measuring eighty-yards-John Bailey
carried it away-he did not say anything about Captain Brown.
