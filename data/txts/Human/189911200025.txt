that wound might have been caused while they were in that position—the wound on the middle of the neck might have been fatal alone, and also the wound in the back, which injured the lung; that could have been caused by the prisoner while the deceased was in the position described, and I say also the same thing in regard to the wound on the right side of his neck—severe hemorrhage would result from the wounds on the face—I did not go to 1, Stephen Street.
