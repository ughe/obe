mental automatism. Sometimes the individuals who are suffering from
this perform violent acts ; they may set fire to things or commit almost
any crime. There may be also a certain amount of premeditation
and knowledge of the act with memory of the act subsequently. I
have known of cases of this condition lasting for a number Of years.
To the uninitiated a person in such a condition would appear to be
perfectly sane. The suddenness of the attack, carelessness, and a
feeling of relief after it is done are all symptoms of homicidal mania.
While in this condition of automatism the subject performs most extra.
ordinary things not appreciating the dangerous nature Of what he is
doing ; the parapet incident is an illustration Of this.
