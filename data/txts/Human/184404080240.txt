 1318. JAMES BOX and HENRY ADAMS were indicted for stealing 1 sixpence, 2 pence, and 1 halfpenny, the monies of George Sandiford ; from the person of Frances Sandiford . FRANCES SANDIFORD. I am the wife of George Sandiford, we live in Marsh-lane, Greenwich. On the 3rd of April I was at the Dockyard-gate at Woolwich—a ship was to be launched, and there was a crowd there—I did not feel anything, but I lost a sixpence, two pence, and a halfpenny, out of my pocket. JOHN CARPENTER( police-constable R  84.) I saw the two prisoners at the Dockyard-gate on 3rd April—I watched, and saw them attempt several gen tlemen's pockets—I saw them go behind Mrs. Sandiford—Adams went close to her, and Box was behind him—I saw Adams put his hand into her pocket, and take something out—he was in the act of passing it to Box, when I seized him—some money dropped on the ground I picked it up, and with the assistance of Gladwin took both the prisoners. WILLIAM GLADWIN( police-constable R  122.) I saw Adams put his hand into the woman's pocket, and take out something, which he banded to Box—I seized Box, and he dropped a sixpence, two pence, and a halfpenny.
   Adams's Defence.  The constable took me; he took another lad, let him go, then seized Box, and said he was with me; I saw some money on the ground, and they took it up.
   BOX  — GUILTY. Aged 18.
   ADAMS  — GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined six months .
   Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1319. ELIZABETH PERRY was indicted for stealing 1 gown, value 7 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Edward Charles Carpenter Nash . RICHARD MASON CLARK. I am shopman to Edward Charles Carpenter Nash, of London-street, Greenwich—I hung a gown on his door-post on the 1st of April—I did not miss it till the policeman came—this is it. JOHN BOVIS( police-constable R  139.) I watched the prisoner in the prosecutor's shop, on 1st April—I saw her come out, and took this gown from her, about 300 yards from the shop.
   Prisoner.  It was not me that stole it. Witness.  There were three per sons—I did not see her take it, but she brought it out, and she drew it from under her clothes.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 13.— Recommended to mercy.— Confined Ten Days .
