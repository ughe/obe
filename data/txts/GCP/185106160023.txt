were perfectly healthy, and so were the viscera of the chest and the beart-
there was congestion of both lungs-the liver was considerably enlarged-I
do not think he had any disease which would have killed him-I attribute
the enlargement of his liver to drinking: I do not think that had anything to
do with his death-the bowels were healthy-I examined the wound, and
found the bone bare-in my judgment, the cause of death was the
diarrhoea, which was produced by the typhoid fever, which was produced by
the low state he got into through the loss of blood.
17Cross-examined. Q. Are not congestion of the lungs and enlargement of
the liver, signs of a person being in the habit of drinking? A. Certainly-I
think I should have found the brain healthy.if the blow under the eye bad
been the cause of death-there is no reason why it should not be- I believe
all the symptoms were caused by the blow under the eye-I do not
remember such another case-the erysipelas and diarrhea would not have
followed a severe cold caught after drinking : as much as a blow under the
eye, not in the way they did follow-pus forming under the jaw was the first
thing: that might have resulted from a severe cold after drinking.
COURT. Q. Was the abscess on the neck on the gland? A. On one
gland-inflammatory action of the glands is a very common result from
cold, but not to that extent-I could not judge what blood had been taken
from him by the leeches-congestion of the lungs would be a likely conse-
quence of severe cold; typhoid fever sometimes results from severe cold-I
saw him in a state of delirium, he was delirious several days before he died
I cannot state positively that the cause of death was the blow under the eye.
Not GUILTY.
