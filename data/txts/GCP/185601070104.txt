THEOPHILUS Gooch (policeman, R 122). I was on duty at Deptford, on
the morning of 20th Dec.--the prisoner was given into my custody by the
last witness-while he was away, the prisoner asked me to let him go to
the water closet-I let him go to the water closet in the Fountain public
house yard-after he was taken to the station, I went with Chapman to that
water closet, and he got this lead out of the soil, and this key; and I found
this one piece of lead behind the door-I returned to the station, told the
prisoner what I had found, and asked if he knew anything about this piece
- he said, “Yes, I put it behind the door."
Cross-examined. Q. Did he say, "I put it behind the door;" or, "I have
put it behind the door ?" A. He said, "I put it behind the door "-I did not
tell him I found it behind the door-I asked him if he knew anything about
it-I told him afterwards- I did not tell him before-he was afterwards
searched in my presence by Chapman.
