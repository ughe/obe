Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
287. TIMOTHY DRISCOLL (2), Stealing a watch and chain and a
snuff box, goods of John Richard Adanis, from his person.
MR. SAUNDERS Prosecuted.
John RICHARD ADAMS. I live at Camelford Terrace, Brockley Road,
Lewisbam-I am a retired tradesman-on Friday evening, 5th January. I
was walking towards home opposite Brockley Cemetery-a woman cate
up and spoke to me-immediately afterwards a man came up and took from
my pocket my silver watch, my silver Albert guard, and also a snuff-box-the
swivel gave way--he then ran away-I gave information to the police-1
could not swear it was the prisoner because it was getting dark--the valve
of my watch and chain is about 31.
SAMUEL TAYLOR. I live at 4, Merton Terrace, Lee Bridge-I am 9 years
old-on Saturday, 5th January I picked up this chain in Ivy Lane, near
Brockley Cemetery, between 11 and 12.
HENRY GOODWIN (Police Sergeant R). On Saturday, 12th and 13th
January, I received information of a robbery in the Brockley Road, and
a description of a man-at 7 o'clock on 13th I went to a lodging-house
at Mill Lane, Deptford-I saw the prisoner-I took him out of the
lodging and said "I shall charge you on suspicion with being concerned
with others in committing a highway robbery in Brockley Lane"-be said
"I know nothing about it”-he was about to put his hand to his waistcoat
pocket when I seized it, and from that pocket I took this watch-on the
way to the station he, knowing me, said " You have got a good job this
time"-he was taken to the station-Sergeant Francis took this suff-
box from him-the charge was read to him-he said " I picked up the
watch in Brockley Lane."
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. You answered the description given to
me, and I found the watch in your pocket.
THOMAS FRANCIS (Police Sergeant R). I was with Goodwin when he
apprehended the prisoner-I searched him at the station-I found this
snuff-box on him I opened it and found it smelt of snuff-I said " This
looks like a snuff-box"-he said “That is a tobacco-box; I have had it
six months."
Prisoner's Defence. That is my box, I gave 2d. for it six months ago.
Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate. "I have nothing to say."
Join RICHARD ADAMS (Recalled). I swear that snuff-box is mine-
I recognise it by the make of it-I have had it some time- I bought it
as a large snuff-box down in Deptford.
Nor GUILTY.
