EDWARD SAMUEL LEE. I am a registered medical man, of Barking-
on 15th February, about 11.30 p.m., I went to a van in the New Road,
Barking, and found the prosecutrix suffering from a small punctured
wound at the back of her left side-it was bleeding, and she was in a
state of considerable collapse-it must have been made with a sharp
instrument; it penetrated into her ch-st, and injured her lungs, and
pneumonia supervened; she was in very great danger of her life for two
months—she gave evidence at the Police-court on April 25, but in my
opinion she was not able to go-she has not recovered yet-the wound
could be inflicted with a soldering-iron.
THOMAS MASON (Police Sergeant K). I took the prisoner on 16th February
