610. EDWIN THOMAS, Embezzling whilst employed in the
Post-office 198., 11. 18., and 61. 138., entrusted to him by virtue of his
employment. Other Counts for the fraudulent disposal of other sums.
MESSRS. Cowie and BAGGALLY Proseouted; MR. BESLEY Defended.
BENJAMIN WALKER. I live at Wixton Cottage, Hendon, and am a
grocer's assistant-I have a Post-office Savings Bank account at the
Hendon Post-office; this is my deposit-book-on 18th December I went
to that office and handed 11. to the prisoner, who was the clerk there,
and he made this entry in my book, December 18th, 11."-I received
an acknowledgment from the head office, but did not open it for eight
or ten days I then discovered that it was an acknowledgment for 1..,
and pot 11.-I spoke to the prisoner about it and he told me to write to
the secretary of the General Post-office and say there had been a mistake,
which I did.
Cross-examined. He entered it correctly in my book-when I told him
about it he said he knew I had paid him 11.
JAMES GEORGE THOMASIN. I reside at St. Anne's, Hondon, and am
honorary secretary of the Hendon Provident Coal Club, which keeps &
Post office Savings Bank account at the Hendon Post-office-on 19th
December I paid into that account at the Hendon Post-office 11.
which I handed to the prisoner, and he made this entry in my book,
" December 19th, 17. 18."--I received no acknowledgment from the
head office.
Cross-examined. I made no inquiries about it-I made another deposit
on the 23rd, and got an acknowledgment for that-I had made a deposit
on 9th December, and got one for that-26th January is the last entry-
I made several payments between 23rd December and then, one on the
8th and one on 13th January, and I drew out some on 22nd January
there was a balance left then-the prisoner put these initials here.

subsequently received an acknowledgment from the head office; I have
not got it now, I gave it up-I did not notice that it was for 78., and not
