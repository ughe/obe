JAMES BRANNAN (Police-sergeant, 21 G). I searched Pritchard, and in
his left-hand trousers pocket I found a forin, a sixpence, and a shilling, all
good-while I was examining it, Pritchard said, “O you will find that
good."
ARTHUR ELLIOTT (Policeman, 104 G.) I was with the other officers; I
seized Thomas wbile he was in the cart he did not get out till he was seized
by me and another officer-I saw him throw & packet on the floor of the
cart-I searched him but found no money whatever.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These six shillings found in the packet are counter-
feit; three are of the date of 1855, and two of 1866, from the same mould
--this half-crown and shilling found in the bag are counterfeit, and the
shilling is of the same mould as two of the other shillings.
Pritchard's Defence. I am guilty of having them, not knowing that they
were bad.
