George Maddox. I am a Thames Police inspector. I received in-
formation, and apprehended the prisoner-Bedell gave me a description of
him-I took him and his brother to Mr. Lucas's, and Bedell immediately
pointed out the prisoner-I asked if he knew any thing respecting the coals
in the boat-he said, “I don't know anything of it; I was at home, having
my breakfast, at the time;" I had two boats given me, with about 6 cwt. of
coals in them,
Edmund Lucas. I am a coal-merchant. I had a barge called the
Augusta, lying at the wharf, with some coals belonging to me--I saw the coals
which were found on the morning of the 15th, and believe them to be part
of the same as were in my barge.
Prisoner's Defence (written.) “William Bedell falsely swore to my
person before the committing Magistrate. At the same time, the two
parties who committed the felony were in the police-office, and ready to
give themselves into custody, knowing at the same time they alone were
the guilty parties; I being at home and in bed at the time; not having
arisen to go to my work ; but when I came out from home, I met James
Kates and James May, who are the two parties guilty of the offence; for
they had not long before told me of the whole transaction, when three of
the Thames Police came to where I reside, and took me."
