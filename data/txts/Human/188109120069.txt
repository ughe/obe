 804. JOHN ANDERSON REUST  (60) , Unlawfully obtaining 100 l  . from Thomas Lund by false pretences. Other Counts  for obtaining other sums from other persons by false pretences.
   MESSRS. MONTAGU WILLIAMS and GILL Prosecuted ; MR. EDWARD CLARKE , Q.C., Defended.
   The defendant purported to be carrying on business in the name of Anson and Co., at 34, Parliament Street, from whence he issued advertise  ments requiring partners, secretaries, &c., and the sums charged in the indictment were obtained under the assurance that the business in which the parties were engaged would be of a profitable nature.
   MR. CLARKE submitted that this would only amount to a promise for the future, and was not a false pretence of an existing fact within the statute He referred to a ruling of MR. JULTICE BYLES to that effect in the case of "Reg. v. Watson," 11 Cox, Criminal Cases, page 328 and the RECORDER , acting upon that ruling, held that there was no case for the Jury, and directed a verdict of NOT GUILTY.
