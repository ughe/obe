MARY ANN M'CARTHY. I live at 3, Thames-street, near William-street,
at the back of William-street-on Sunday afternoon, the 29th, between
2 and 3, I heard a disturbance there I went to the door, 17, William-
street, and saw the prosecutor on the ground, and the prisoner kicking
him-I saw two 28. pieces fall on the ground, and I saw the prisoner
pick them up-I did not observe where they fell from—they were both
struggling I heard Emma Shore say it was Bryan's money-I saw the
prisoner's left hand by the side of the prosecutor's pocket, and his trowsers
were torn down-the money fell to the ground at the same time the
trowsers were torn.
COURT.Q. Were you one of the women who had the stone in the pocket-
handkerchief? A. No; I saw the woman in the road getting a stone.
Not Guilty.
Before Mr. Common Serjennt.
499. WALTER GRIMSTONE BOND (32), Unlawfully obtaining 36
from James Mitchell by false pretences.
MR. SLEIGH conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES PEEK. I reside at Blackheath-my son, Edward Peek, and another,
carry on the business of bread and biscuit manufacturers at Mill-street,
Dockhead-I occasionally go to the premises, though not a member of the
firm—the prisoner was in the employment of the firm, and had the manage
ment of the stables- there is no Mr. James Peek in the firm I never
authorized the prisoner to shoot, or in any way to dispose of, the horses of
the establishment—there is no pretence for such an assertion-I cannot
imagine why he should have said so—he never asked me anything about
them, in the way of shooting or disposing of them.
Cross-examined by MR. LILLEY. Q. Although not a member of the firm,
are you accustomed to go there frequently? A. Yes; nearly every day of
the week when in town, on my way to town—I do not recollect going twice
a day-I may have done so— I had no distinct office there of my own-
there was a desk that I made use of, and of which I kept the key-it had
papers in it connected with the business of the firm I had some conver-
sation with the prisoner on one occasion with respect to the horses I think
he was there about three months, I received information that the horses
under his care looked rather better, and complimented him on it, by way of
encouragement-he did not exactly complain, but he mentioned that two
