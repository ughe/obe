Friday, the 19th of March, Harvey came in soon after twelve o'clock, with a kettle under her apron, which I believe to be this; she requested Lloyd to buy it - she said she would do no such thing; she was there ten minutes urging her to buy it - she said, "It is my own kettle, I have had it eight years - I bought it in East-street;" Lloyd said, "Keep it eight years longer" - she said,"For God's sake buy it, I have the broker in my house, and want 10s.; I have 6s. 6d., and if you will let me have 3s. 6d., I will leave the kettle - and come and fetch it again in a day or two;" I was there again on the Monday, when Harvey came and brought a box of different things - part of it was wearing-apparel, belonging to a man; some boots and some working-tools - she said, "I have brought these, won't you buy these;" Lloyd said,"No, I won't have any thing to do with you, or your things, put them in your box" - she put them in, and went away, saying, she would come for her kettle the next day.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Was there another person in the shop? A. Yes.
   EDWARD BURRIDGE. I was ordered to go to Lloyd's, which I did, and directed her to come to our office; I went again and she was getting ready to come - she said,"D-nation seize the woman;" I said, "What did you give for the kettle?" she said, "I did not buy it, I only lent 3s. 6d. on it."
 Cross-examined. Q. I believe you went away, and she came down to the office? A. Yes.
 Harvey's Defence. I met Mrs. Phipps in Exeter-street, and asked how she did; she said, "I have removed to North-street - my husband has had a little money left him, and built a house;" I went to her house, and said I had been out of work all the winter, and I owed 10s. for my lodging - she said, "If you like you may take the teakettle and get a shilling or two on it, till Saturday or Monday, without my husband knowing it - I took it to Mrs. Lloyd, who did not seem very willing to lend me 3s. 6d. on it, but at last she did; I met Mrs. Phipps - she said,"What have you done with the tea-kettle? my husband has found it out;" I said, "I will take you to the place;" we were going, and saw her husband, who used shameful language, and gave charge of me.
 MRS. PHIPPS re-examined. Q. Did she mention such a thing to you? A. No, it is not true - I do not know that she noticed it at all.
 HARVEY -    GUILTY. Aged 33. Transported for Seven Years.
 LLOYD -    NOT GUILTY.
 899.  SARAH QUIM was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of March , 1 gown, value 3s.; 6 bed-gowns, value 3s.; 2 pairs of shoes, value 5s.; 1 apron, value 1s. 6d.; 13 caps, value 13s.; 11 shirts, value 11s. 6d.; 5 handkerchiefs, value 2s.; 1 tea-canister, value 6d., and 2 frocks, value 2s., the goods of Edward Wynn , from the person of Sarah Wynn.
   EDWARD WYNN. I have a sister named Sarah - she is twenty-five years of age. On the 15th of March we were coming from Stafford to London; my sister had two bundles, which contained the articles stated - we met with the prisoner at Dunchurch, near Coventry; she came from there to London; she told us she would take us to the Town-hall, where they gave money to poor people - she took us about the streets, and took us to Worship-street Office- she said that was the Town-hall; she said we must not go in with a bundle, and my sister must wait in the street - she took me into the public-house, and said she would go and inquire when we could get in - she then went to my sister, and got the bundles from her.
 SARAH WYNN. I had the bundles while my brother and sister went into the public-house - the prisoner came and said they had given them some money, I was to give her the bundle, and go to the Castle to my brother; I gave them to her; I went, but could not see my brother - I returned, and the prisoner was gone; I found her on the Saturday night following, and asked her for my bundles - she took me from one place to another, said I was to go to a little street, and ask for James Quim, and tell him I was the person who was to have the bundles; I went, and there were some sailors in the house, making use of very bad language - I came out, and the prisoner was gone; I saw her again on the Monday, on London-bridge, and asked her for them - she said they were at Westminster, would I go there; I said Yes - she took me to Queen-square, and was going to give charge of me, and I gave charge of her.
   DAVID PHILLIPS. I am an officer. These two women were quarrelling, and the prisoner gave the witness into custody; I examined them, and took them into the office - the witness stated what she has now; I asked the prisoner where she lived - she said no where, and would not tell us any thing.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that she had come to London with the prosecutrix, who had been assisted by the different parishes on the road, and denied that they had any bundles with them to lose.
 GUILTY. Aged 27. -    Judgment Respited.
 900.  HENRY JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of April , 1 bag, value 6d.; 1 handkerchief, value 6s.; 1 card-case, value 1s.; 1 purse, value 6d.; 1 vinegarette, value 2s., and 1 bunch of keys, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Amelia Little , from her person.
   AMELIA LITTLE. I am single. I was in Portland-place last Friday, at a quarter before four o'clock, and a black silk bag was taken from me, which contained a handkerchief, a vinegarette, a purse, a card-case, and a bunch of keys - I do not know who took it, but I felt it touched, and felt the string, which was on my arm, and the bag, were gone; I saw a person running - there was a gentleman passing at the time.
   PETER DAVIS. At ten minutes past four o'clock on the 16th of April, I was on duty at the corner of Cavendish-street - I heard a cry of Stop thief! turned, and saw the prisoner running towards me; I made a grasp to catch him, and he threw this silk bag away - I saw my brother officer take it up; I found this blue handkerchief round the prisoner's body.
   JAMES HULLICK. I am an officer. I took up the bag.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I did not take it.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. -   Judgment Respited.
