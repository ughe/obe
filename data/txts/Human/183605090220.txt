   Witness for the Defence . REBECCA BLITS. I remember hearing to Mr. Lamert's death—I was the housekeeper when Mrs. Lamert went to see her husband—I was there before his death a long time—I remember, after the 4th of March, Mr. Lamert sending me for the prisoner—I went to him, and he came—she sent for the express purpose of settling with Mr. Gore—he came at the time Mr. Issacs happened to be at home—I told Mrs. Lamert he was come, in obedience to her direction—she said she could not see him then.
   MR. BODKIN . Q.  I believe you are on very intimate terms with the prisoner? A.  No—I never quarrelled with him—I was very friendly with him—I lived in the house with him when Mr. and Mrs. Lamert west away—I was then on friendly terms, but not more than before. NOT GUILTY.

JOHN CARTER was indicted for burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house, of John Glover , at   Little Ilford, Essex , about the hour of one in the night, on the   6th of April , with intents to seal, and stealing therein, 3lbs. weight of cigars, value 50 s  .; 2 coats, value £4; two handkerchiefs, value 10 s  .; 5 shillings, 30 pence, and 60 half-pence, his goods and monies: 1 coats, value 20 s  .; 1 umbrella, value 5 s  .; and 1 hand kerchief, value 7 s  ., the goods of William Webb . ELIZABETH GLOVER. I am the wife of John Glover, and live at the Coach and Horses, at Little Ilford, in the parish of Ilford in Essex. On the 6th of April I went to bed at eleven o'clock at night—I fastened the house up at eleven, but our lodger was a after me—my husband had gone to bed before that—I was called up next morning about a quarter before six o'clock by a person who was going by the house—I got up came down stairs—I found the bar-door wrenched open, and the lock lying on the floor—I am certain I had locked it the night before, and I had the key in my pocket—I missed 3lbs. of cigars from the bar, and 120 half-pence, 60 penny pieces, 5 s . in silver, and six silver tea-spoons—I am quite positive they were all there when I went to bed over night—I also missed three great coats, two neck-handkerchiefs, and a silk umbrella, from the her parlour when I had seen them the night before—I saw the prisoner a few days previous to the robbery—I served him with beer at our bar about eleven o'clock in the day—he was not above a quarter of an hour in my presence—I am certain of him—I have since seen the umbrella which belongs to William Webb, our lodger.
