SPENCER SELDEN. I am potman at the Bell public-house. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner on the 7th of October at the Bell public-house—I received a counterfeit shilling Porter, sealed up in paper, and the other from Spencer—I produce same shillings. MR. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to the Mint, and have been many years—these shillings are both counterfeit in all respect.
