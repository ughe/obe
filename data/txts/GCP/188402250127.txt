371. FREDERICK RUSSELL (28) and EDWARD SAUNDERS
(43) were indicted (together with Thomas Barron, not in custody) for
stealing a quantity of wooden staves, the property of Messrs. Champion,
the master of Russell.
After the Jury had been charged SAUNDERS stated that he desired to
PLEAD GUILTY, and upon hearing that statement the Jury returned a verdict of
GULTY as to him.
Mr. Grain Prosecuted; MESSRS. MONTAGU WILLIAMS and BURNIE
defended Russell.

staves--he drove up a van alongside the pile I was present at the de-
