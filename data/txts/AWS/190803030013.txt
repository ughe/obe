JAMEs SULLIVAN (prisoner, not on oath) stated that he had gone
home from his work and had then gone to his sister's (Harding's wife)
for tea; that as soon as he saw what Harding was doing he went
downstairs to go way, and that a girl had asked him to ask Mrs.
Harding if she would buy a washing dish, and that as he went upstairs
the officers followed him into the room.
Verdict, Not guilty.
Harding had been twice summarily convicted of lareeny. It WAS
stated that during the past three years he had been spending money
freely and doing little work and was associated with notorious. East
End coiners.
Sentence, Five years' penal servitude.
