Queen," belonging to prosecutors. On October 9 we tugged some

CHARLES HAwKs, lighterman, another deck hand on the 44 Queen."
I assisted to moor the barges at Beckton on October 9. On the 17th
I went to the police station and identified the three pieces of rope
produced as part of what we moored the barges with. On the 18th
was shown the fourth piece; it is Williams's rope, but was not used

Cross-examined. I am certain this is Williams's rope. We all
