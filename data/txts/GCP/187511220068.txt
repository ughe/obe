Cross-examined by MR. MOODY. I have stated that I took Thomas Wain-
wright into custody on the day of the first hearing of the charge against
Henry, but I find that it was a mistake; my meaning was the first day I
was at the police-court was on the Monday after the 13th September-I
knew the warrant was out against Thomas, but I did not arrest him until the
20th ; I cannot tell the day on which the warrant was actually issued-he
spoke to me about the spade and the chopper prior to going before Mr.
Handay, but I did not mention anything to him about the charge until after
he was discharged--Mr. Pettigrew who has been in business many years in
the Whitechapel Road, told me that he knew both the prisoners well—I did
not know Thomas Wainwright, but I have seen him once before I had his
address given to me by Mr. Mason, the warrant officer-I had not known it
before.
