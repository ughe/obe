JOSEPH WREN. I am the prisoner's son, and am twelve years old-I
lived with him and the deceased on this afternoon I was at home-my
mother kept on swearing at my father-she was my step-mother-be said
"I don't want no swearing at me "my father just hit her on the side of
her face with the palm of his hand-she got hold of his whiskers and
punched and scratched him-he said "Will you go away," and she said
No;" he then shoved her, and she fell down against the door--they had tos,
and went out together about 5 o'clock-I never saw her alive againsbe
fell right on her side-the door was.shut-she did not complain of any pain
when she got up.
By THE COURT. She got up by herself and sat in a chair and kept on
swearing-before she went out she complained of pain, and kept rolling all
over the place and swearing-she said “My side is bad," and then they went
out-she did not say how it came to be bad-I did not see her put her hand
there--my father was sober-she was half drunk.
ALPRED GEORGE BUOKLAND, M.R.C.S. I am house-surgeon to the London
Hospital-the deceased came there about 6 o'clock on the evening of 20th
January—she complained to me of intense pain in the lower part of her
obest-she had every appearance of suffering I admitted her as an in-
patient and examined her-her left eye was bruised, and she had a bruise
over the region of the heart-I saw her again about 10 o'clock-I saw she
was much worse, and I told her I was afraid she was dying-she made a
statement which I took down in writing-the impression that she was dying
appeared to be fully conveyed to her mind--the statement is signed by me
and two other witnesses. (Read: "I, Elizabeth Wren, believing myself
about to die, do hereby solemnly declare that I had been out with my hus-
band this afternoon, that we returned home between 4 and 6 pm, and that
we had a quarrel about some money affairs, my husband being in drink at
the time. I was quite sober. He was very angry indeed ; came up to me
while I was standing beside the table and struck me on the face and then in
the stomach with his fist. I fell on the floor, and then he struok me several
more blows in the stomach; cannot say whether be used his hand or bis fist.
I swear that I did not strike him first, but she flew at him afterwards and
scratched his face. Severe pain in the abdomen came on about half an hour
after the accident. My husband is frequently in the habit of beating me.
I left him in the house when I came away. Signed, Elizabeth Wren, X.
