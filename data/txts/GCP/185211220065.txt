FREDERICK LANE. I am surgeon of the Royal Free Hospital. I exa-
mined this little boy; he had a wound about half an inch long on the upper
part of the left eye, which appeared to be recently inflicted-it had wounded
the iris, and extended to the interior portion of the eye; he has lost the
sight of the eye for ever-an operation might be performed, but it is not
deemed advisable when the sight of the other eye is good; it might injure
the other eye, and the operation might not be successful--the lower lid of
the eye was also cut.
