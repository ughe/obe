 1625. EDWARD BURN was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of May , 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  ., the of Thomas Fauntleroy , from his person. THOMAS FAUNTLEROY. I live at Potter's-fields, and am ivory-mer chant. On Tuesday, the 13th of May, I was in Tooley-street, and felt a tug at my coat pocket—I turned round suddenly and caught the prisoner's hand enclosing my handkerchief—I took it from him—I caught his hand and the handkerchief and the tail of my coat together—the handkerchief was all outside the pocket—it was gone from person, because my coat pocket dropped, and I took the handkerchief his hand.
   Prisoner.  I told you a boy that went along took it—you know well it was not me took the handkerchief—I told you it was another boy—you would not go after him Witness . He told me it was put into his hand by another boy—he was close behind me. SAMUEL JAMES HALEY. I took charge of the prisoner, and have the handkerchief.
   Prisoner. Q.  Have you ever known me to dishonest in your life? Witness.  I have known him the last six or seven years residing on the spot—I never knew any thing against him—it is some length of time since I knew him to be in any service—he was then a pot-boy.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I am innocent—I was going for some soap for my mother at the same time. GUILTY. Aged 14— Recommended to mercy by the prosecutor. Confined One Month, and Whipped.
