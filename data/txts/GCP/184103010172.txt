Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
973. MARY DOYLE was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of Febru-
ary, 1 pair of stockings, value ls. 4d.; and 11 shillings, the property of
Joseph Johnson, from his person.
JOSEPH Johnson. I live in Orange-street, Bethnal-green. On the
morning of the 27th of February I went to the Black Swan public-house,
in Schoolhouse-lane, Limehouse, I got drunk, and fell asleep, I had
158. in my left hand trowsers' pocket-when I awoke a little after four
o'clock I had only 28.--I had spent 28., and had lost 118.--I had seen the
prisoner all day in the house-she was gone when I awoke-1 also lost a
pair of stockings from my coat pocket.
William Burn. I was at this public-house all day. I was going past
the tap-room door, and saw the prisoner's hands in the prosecutor's coat
pocket, and taking out a pair of stockings- I took them from her, and
put them into his pocket again-1 did not see her hand any where else.
Philip HENRY. I was in the tap-room. I saw the prisoner put her
hand into the prosecutor's coat pocket, and then into his trowsers-after
I heard some money jink, and she put her hand in her bosom.
GUILTY.- Recommended to mercy.-Confined Three Months.
974. MICHAEL WALSH was indicted for a misdemeanor.
GUILTY. Aged 35.-Confined One Year.
975. MARY ANN MIDDLETON was indicted for stealing, on the
30th of January, 2 blankets, value 5s. 6d. ; 2 pillows, value 4s. 6d.; 1
sheet, value 1s. 6d.; and I looking-glass and frame, value 6d.; the goods
of John Smith: and that she had been before convicted of felony.
John Smith. I keep part of a house in Vine-street, Saffron-hill. On
the 27th of January the prisoner hired the first floor of me-after I heard
something I went and missed the articles produced, from the room--they
are mine.
HENRY HALL. I am a pawnbroker in Baldwin's-gardens. These two
blankets and the other articles I took in pledge from the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. I did it through distress.
JOHN KENT. I am a policeman. I produce a certificate of the pri-
soner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office-(read)
the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 31.-Confined One Year.
976. JAMES FRANCIS was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
February, 1 bridle, value 14s. ; 1 pair of reins, value 28. ; I collar, value
108.; 1 saddle, value 138.; 1 pair of traces, value 58.; 1 martingale,
value 38.; 1 pair of hames, value 6s. ; and 1 crupper and breeching, value
78.; the goods of Thomas Kibble.
WILLIAM Pitt. I am horse-keeper to Thomas Kibble, a cab-master in
Portland-street. I left my stable about one o'clock on the 19th of Febru-
ary-I was coming back, and met the prisoner with this set of harness on
his back-it had been removed out of the stable-I thought a neighbour
in Wardour-street had borrowed them, and I watched the prisoner to see
whether he took them there, but he passed by-I then inquired at the
shop, and pursued the prisoner- overtook him in Rathbone-place-
