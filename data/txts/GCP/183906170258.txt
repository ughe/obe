1963. JAMES RAYNER was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of
May, 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of a man whose name is un
known, from his person.
John Archer. I am a policeman. On the 22nd of May I was at
Greenwich fair, in plain clothes. I observed the prisoner there for about a
quarter of an hour, and watched him-he went behind a gentleman, lifted
the tail of his coat, and took this handkerchief from it-I immediately
went to the gentleman, and asked if he had lost his handkerchief-he felt.
and said he had I said, “This boy has got it: come with me, and I
will take it from him"-the prisoner crept under a show-I fetched him
out, took the handkerchief from him, and the gentleman claimed it-he
would not come to the station-house, as he said the had so much trouble in
a case before-1 do not know his name.
Cross-examined by Mr. HORRY. Q. Was it this boy took it out of the
pocket ? A. Yes-there were two others in company with him at one
time, but he was alone when he did this-I found a quantity of toys on
him, which he said a boy had put into his pocket-I asked how he came
by the handkerchief-he said the boy had put that in also.
GUILTY. Aged 14.-Confined Three Months.
