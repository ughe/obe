2475. ELIZA VEINTER was indicted for stealing 1 ring, value 7s.; and
1 handkerchief, 60.; the goods of Thomas Timpson, from his person.
THOMAS TIMPSON. I live at Woolwich. On the 4th of Oct. I had
ring on my finger, and a handkerchief in my pocket-I met the prisoner
she caught me by my hand and asked what I was going to give her-I then
had the ring slightly on my finger--she left me, and as soon as she was gobe
I missed the ring and my handkerchief--these are them.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not give me the ring, and want me to go with you!
A. No1 did not bring a female who said it was hers.
Enos Cinch. I am a minister-I had been on duty, and in coming home
there was a noise-I stopped to hear what it was-some one said, "There is
a ring lost"-while I was there the prisoner dropped this handkerchief with
the ring in it from her shawl.
Prisoner's Defence. It does not stand to reason that I could take the ring
off his finger.
Guilty. Aged 32.- Confined Three Months.

Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
2477. ANN CASEY, alias MARY TAYLOR, was indicted for stealing
1 pair of stockings, value 1s. 3d., the goods of Richard Sears.
RICHARD SEARS. I live in Artillery-place, Woolwich--the prisoner came
to my shop on the 28th of Sept., and asked if I had a second-hand shift-she
turned and went out--there were two pairs of stockings hanging at the door
--after she was gone I missed one pair of them-I went after her, overtook
her, and found my stockings on her- these are them.
Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON. R. Have you told us all? 4. No
-- I took the prisoner to the station---she asked me the price of the stockings
in the shop-I went after her immediately, and overtook her about 200 yards
off, as she was going into a public-house-I cannot recollect exactly how she
was dressed-she had a bonnet and shawl on-I pulled that aside and found
the stockings under her left arm, loosely doubled up.
Guilty. Aged 30.--Confined Six Months.
(There were two other indictments against the prisoner.)
