(Wednesday, November 16.)
WARD, William (25, labourer), unlawfully uttering counterfeit
coin.
Mr. Beaumont Morice prosecuted.
WALTER BoND, 86, Lewisham Road, Greenwich, confectioner and
tobacconist. On October 5, at two p.m., prisoner asked for a penny
packet of Peters' chocolate, tendering counterfeit florin produced; I
told him it was bad; he handed me a good half-crown, received 2s. 5d.
change, and left. Being suspicious I followed him to Mrs. Wood's
shop, 28, Lewisham Road. I there spoke to Mrs. Wood, followed
prisoner, and gave him into custody. He was brought back to Mrs.
Wood's shop, where he admitted passing the coin and said he did not
believe it was bad.
Cross-examined. When I handed prisoner the coin he made no
reply.
