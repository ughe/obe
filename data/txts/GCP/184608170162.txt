1661. THOMAS STRATFORD was indicted for unlawfully obtaining
28. 2d., the monies of Sarah Brown, by false pretences.
SARAH TOWNSEND. I am the wife of Charles Townsend, and am servant
to Miss Sarah Brown, of Parson's-green, Fulham. The prisoner is a carrier,
in the service of Mr. Woods-I knew him by his coming backwards and for-
wards-on the 26th of July he brought a basket for Miss Brown-he said
there was 2s. 2d. to pay-Miss Brown said she was confident it had been
paid I went down and told the prisoner 80-Miss Brown said she never
knew Mr. Brown to send a parcel without paying before the prisoner said
the 2s. 2d. was right-Miss Brown came to the door and gave me the money
-the prisoner made out this bill, produced,) and I paid him two shillings and
two penny pieces, of Miss Brown's money, and he went away.
JOHN PAYNE. I am clerk at the Great Western Railway station at Pad-
dington. On the 25th of July I kept the books at the Bull and Mouth,
Regent-circus, and delivered the prisoner a basket, with “Miss Brown,
Fulham, G. W. R. carriage, paid," upon it-he paid me 2d. --we charge 2d.
for the transit through London.
HENRY Wood. I am a carrier between Fulham and London. The pri-
soner was in my employment. On Sunday, the 26th of July, he accounted
to me for a parcel to Miss Brown-he only gave me 6d.--that was the carriage
from London to Fulham.
ROBERT Culver (police-constable V 175.) I took the prisoner into custody.
SARAH TOWNSEND re-examined. This ticket was on the basket--the
“Carriage paid" was torn off-this ticket that the prisoner wrote is in pencil,
and cannot be read now
Prisoner's Defence. I did not receive the money.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Six Months.

property of Robert Richardson.

prisoner brought me a note from his captain-it is not here-he said it was from
the captain-I asked why the captain did not come himself-he said he was
ill in bed, and could not get up-I do not know the captain's writing read
the letter, and gave him two sovereigns and a common scrubbing-brush, be
lieving the letter to come from Captain Forman-he said he was mate of the
ship-- he came again next day, and presented another letter for 31, more!
did not let him have that, but gave him into custody-he had said the vessel
was at Deptford, and wanted me to go there with him.
