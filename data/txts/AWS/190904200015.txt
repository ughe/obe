LEESON, Thomas (73, butler), pleaded guilty of marrying Emily
Hawkins, his wife being then alive. The first wife of prisoner died
in 1895, and in May, 1890, he married a second time, the second
wife being, it was stated, a woman of very bad character, who was
sentenced at Clerkenwell to 20 months' imprisonment for obtaining
goods by false pretences. While she was undergoing sentenoes he
made the acquaintance of proseeutrix, who is a cook. He repre-
sented himself to be a man of means, and suggested that they should
travel about together. Ultimately he persuaded her to go through
the form of marriage. He was formerly in the army.
The Recorder directed prisoner to enter into recognisances to come
up for judgment if called upon, observing that he was a somewhat
ancient bigamist.
