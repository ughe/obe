1. He only came and took our statements-that is about three weeks ago
-my brother was present then-I had not made a deposition then-it was

me was a copy of the statement I made at Lambeth-street-I know it was
before I went to Lambeth-street that he took down my statement. .

shop? A. Occasionally, not always-I did not generally have them,
only sometimes--my father kept his gold, and bullion, and things of that
