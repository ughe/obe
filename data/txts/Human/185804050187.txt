On 2d March, about 12 o'clock in the day, the prisoner came for a pint of beans; it came to 2 1/4 d .—she gave me a half crown; it bent very easily, and I told her I thought it was bad, and she had better take it back—shesaid she should not take it back, her husband must take it back, as he had taken it from his master this morning—I gave it to my father. GEORGE SMITH. My daughter gave me this coin ( produced )—I fold the prisoner it was not good, and asked her where she got it—she said her hus band took it for his work in the market—I asked her name; she said, "Neale," or "O'Neale," and pretended to cry; I do not know whether it was real—I sent her to fetch her husband, and she went away—I followed her to the soup kitchen, Vauxhall Wharf, and called a policeman—She said, "Do forgive me, I know I have done wrong; do not take me to the station"—I said, "Where did you get it?" she said, "I dare not tell you"—I said, "Where do you live?" she said, "In the Mint"—on the road, she said that she was hungry and dry, and a gentleman gave it to her to get something—Isaid, "It is strange that you should go to a corn chandler's shop to get peas,"and that if she had gone tcr a baker's, it would have been more feasible—she said that she did not know what she was doing.
