1345. WILLIAM DEAR was indicted for stealing 1 gelding, price 15l.,
the property of Thomas Wheatley.
MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM LEWIS ROGERS. I am foreman to Thomas Wheatley, a stage
proprietor at Greenwich--the prisoner lived with him as horse-keeper, and
left him on the 24th of March-Mr. Wheatley had a gelding which I saw
safe on the 4th of April, at a quarter before nine o'clock at night in
his yard at Greenwich-1 missed it at half-past six the next morning
-I saw it again on Saturday morning, the 6th of April, at Mr. Giddens',
the slaughterer's, in Green-street, Blackfriars- I saw the prisoner at Union-
hall-he had lived with Mr. Wheatley two years, and knew his premises as
well as I did I always found him honest.
JAMES GIDDENS. I am a horse slaughterer, and live in Green-street,
Blackfriars--the prisoner came to my place about a quarter past six o'clock
on the morning of the 5th of April, with a bay gelding- I looked out of
window and asked where he brought it from-he said from Mr. Archer, a
fishmonger at Dorking-I got up and let him into the yard - I asked if
he knew anybody at Dorking-be said yes, and his master had told him to
bring the gelding to me, and get what he could I asked if he knew Mr.
Doudeney, a harness-maker at Dorking-he gave me an account of him that
I knew was not correct-I told him to wait till I went to get change-
went and called our inspector, Mr. Harris-he came, and we all walked
to the station house.
