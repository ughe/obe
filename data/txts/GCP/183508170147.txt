I gave the shilling to James King, after I had marked it—the prisoner had

JAMES KING (police-constable N 231.) I took the prisoner. I found

John FIELD. I am inspector of counterfeit coin to the Mint. These

1838. WILLIAM MALING was indicted for a misdemeanour.
JOHN CLARAGE (police-sergeant B 19.) On the 8th of August I saw
the prisoner in Palace-street, about twelve o'clock. I stopped him, and
took him by the neckcloth and the hand-he threw these six sixpences
from his mouth-he was smoking a pipe at the time.
WILLIAM CLIPTON (police-constable B 50.) I was with Clarage. I
took hold of the prisoner's neck, and saw him throw the six sixpences from
his mouth-he said, "I suppose, Clifton, it is all up with me this time."
JOHN FIELD. These are all counterfeit, and from the same mould.
GUILTY.-Confined Two Years.
