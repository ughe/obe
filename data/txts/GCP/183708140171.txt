JAMES HOLGATE were again indicted for stealing, on the 27th of July,
1 gown. value 18. ; 2 shirts, value 6s. ; 2 caps, value 3d. ; 1 neckerchief.
value 3d.; 1 pillow-case, value 2d. ; 1 pair of stockings, value 18.; 1
apron, value 2d.; and i handkerchief, value 2d. ; the goods of James
Bigsby.
MARY ANN Bigsby. I am the wife of James Bigsby, of Barking-
about half-past six o'clock in the morning, on the 27th of July, these
things were left in the shed--I came back in an hour-they were then gone
-these are the things.
GEORGE BIGGs. In consequence of information, I went after the
prisoners, and found all three a little after eight o'clock, with all these
things wrapped round them wet.
William Henry Hairs' Defence. I and my brother said we would go and
see the country, to get a living, and met this other boy-he asked whether
he might come with us, and I said “Yes"-about half past five o'clock in
the morning-and this boy said, “ Here is a tub I found."
WILLIAM HENRY HAIRS* -GUILTY. Aged 19.
Transported for Seven Years.
ABRAHAM HAIRS-GUILTY. Aged 17. Confined for Six Months.

1991. THOMAS SAUNDERS was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of
August, 1 wooden bowl, value 6d. ; 38. ; 4 sixpences; and 2 fourpenny-
pieces; the goods and monies of James Chapman.
ELIZABETH CHAPMAN. I am the wife of James Chapman. I keep the
Coach and Horses, at Stratford-I had a wooden bowl, with this property
in it. On the 8th of August the prisoner came in and opened the till, and
took the bowl out with the silver in it-I was not two yards from him-I
nearly caught him-I cried out “Stop thief," and he was taken by Willis
-I am positive he is the boy.
THOMAS WILLIS. I pursued and took the prisoner-he threw the bowl
and money away—this is it.
Prisoner. I was going to my uncle's, and meant to get a pint of beer,
and saw a boy come out of the shop at the time-a man came and took
me.
