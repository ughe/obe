798. WILLIAM MANN (22), Feloniously cutting and wounding Mary
Ann Inwood, with intent to murder her.-2nd COUNT, with intent to do
her some grievous bodily harm. MR. PHILIPs conducted the Prosecution.
MARY ANN INWOon. I live at No. 48, Booth Street, and work for Mr.
Freeman, a bootmaker, of No. 14, Westmoreland Street, as a boot stitcher.
On 26th June, about 6 o'clock in the evening, I was down stairs; I was sent
for to see the prisoner-Mr. Freeman came for me, and I went up stairs
with him-I saw the prisoner, he wished to shake hands with me as he was
going away-I shook hands with him, and after I had shaken hands with
him he took a knife out of his pocket--I saw just the point of the knifo
-he put his left arm round my neck, and stuck the knife in my face--I
screamed, a Murder er!"-my master came to my assistance- disengaged
myself from the prisoner, and ran into the landlord's room-on the night
before this the prisoner had asked me to leave my place and live with him
I told him I would not; and he said if I did not he would take my life
and his own afterwards-I went to the police station and had my wound
dressed by a surgeon.
