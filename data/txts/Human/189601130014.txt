CHARLES MATHEWS, for the Prosecution, called attention to the prisoner's mental condition, and before his plea was taken proposed to call medical evidence on that subject. MR. JUSTICE HAWKINS was of opinion that the plea should first be taken, it was right to the prisoner that that course should be taken, and right that the JURY should have the opportunity of observing his manner and demeanour.
