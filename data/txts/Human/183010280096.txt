2069. THOMAS SEATON was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of September , 1 pair of trousers, value 5s., the goods of John Boards.
   JOHN BOARDS. I am a pawnbroker. The prisoner was in the habit of pawning things almost every morning; I missed a great many things, and was at a loss to know where they went - one morning I missed a coat; I found these trousers at another pawnbroker's - I swear they had been stolen from my shop by somebody; they have my mark on them.
   WILLIAM ESTILL. I am a pawnbroker. I have a pair of trousers, pawned by the prisoner.
     GUILTY. Aged 77. - Confined Two Years.
 2070.  GEORGE SCUTT was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of September , 1 coat, value 10s., the goods of William Thirkell.
   MARY ROBINSON. I live opposite the house of William Thirkell. On the morning of the 27th of September I was looking out at window; I saw the prisoner get in at the window, and get out something - I gave an alarm; my son pursued him, and my husband went and got the Policeman.
 Prisoner. Q. How far is your window? A. Nearly opposite; I did not state it was one hundred yards from it- the window was partly open; I did not say I did not see your face; I am positive of your person.
   THOMAS ROBINSON. I am the son of the witness - she told me of this; I got up, and looked at the window - I saw the prisoner turn round, and pursued him a hundred yards or more - he was carrying this coat in a black bag; he gave it me out when I got up to him, but kept the bag himself.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you come up to me? A. Yes; you were stopped near the corner of Redmans-row; it was you that gave me the coat, and you asked me to let you go and said you had nothing; I let you go, and you were taken again.
   JOSEPH ALDRIDGE. I am an officer. I was in Stepney-fields; the prisoner was delivering up the coat when I first came in sight of him - he then ran when he saw me; I called Stop thief! a man laid hold of him, but he was very active and got away - I called after him again, and my brother officer turned a corner and took him.
   WILLIAM THIRKELL. This is my coat, it laid in a chair in my front room - it was not in any bag.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2071.  JOHN SHIRLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of September , 1 necklace, value 2s., the goods of Richard Robertson , from the person of Sarah Robertson.
   JOSIAH ROOME. I am nine years of age. On the 28th of September I was at Mr. Hughes', the grocer, at the corner of Seabright-street; I saw Sarah Robertson and another little girl with her - Sarah Robertson had a necklace on - she cried out, "Boy! beads!" and the prisoner took the necklace off her neck, threw it to another boy behind, and they ran different ways - I had seen the prisoner before - he wanted to take my marbles; I am sure he is the boy- I ran after him with the baby, and gave the alarm; I then went, and took home the baby.
 FRANCES ROBERTSON. I am the wife of  Richard Robertson; my little girl is two years old - she was at the door while I was in the shop; I heard the alarm, ran, and caught the prisoner - he had nothing then; he had ran perhaps, fifty yards - the necklace was worth perhaps 2s.
 Prisoner. I sat down on a stone, and that woman came and took me.
     GUILTY. Aged 11. - Whipped, and delivered to a person who engaged to employ him.
 2072.  EDWARD SANNAGAN was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of October , 1 necklace, value 5s., the goods of William Pinder , from the person of Caroline Pinder.
 JAMES DUNN. I was in  Brownlow-street on the 18th of October; there was a puppet-show - I saw a little girl, and heard a report that some beads were gone from her neck; I dived into the business, and took the prisoner and another into custody; while I had hold of the other, the prisoner stood by, and I saw the beads drop from his person - they had both been close behind the little girl; I found nothing on the other, and let him go - I was close to the prisoner, and am quite positive I saw the beads drop from him.
   WILLIAM PINDER. I am the father of Caroline Pinder. She came to me for a halfpenny to see the puppet-show - this necklace was then about her neck, tied with blue cotton and a common gilt snap - I had seen the prisoner before.
 Prisoner's Defence. I never was guilty of any thing before, and I do not know what I am brought here for.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 2073.  THOMAS ROBERTS was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of October , 1 hat, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Richard Osborn.
   RICHARD OSBORN. I was at the Macclesfield Arms public-house on the 22nd of October; I fell asleep, and when I awoke my hat was gone - the prisoner had been sitting a little distance from me; he returned, and I accused him of it - he denied it; Mr. Spring's young man said he saw a man with a hat on, which he thought was not his- and the next day the prisoner went and showed me where it was - this is the hat; I found it where the prisoner pointed it out.
 Prisoner's Defence. He said if I would give him his hat he would liberate me, and say nothing about it.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. - Confined Six Weeks.
 2074.  CHARLES WILLIS was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of October , 1 coat, value 12s., the goods of Charles Iliff.
   CHARLES ILIFF. I am ostler to a gentleman in St. John's-wood-road. I missed a coat out of his coach-house on the 24th of October, at seven o'clock in the evening - the door had been shut, but not latched; the prisoner had been living with my master for two years, but was discharged a week before - this is my coat.
   THOMAS SMITH. I am a pawnbroker. This coat was pawned with me by a woman, in the name of Ann Thomas - she is not here.
     NOT GUILTY.
