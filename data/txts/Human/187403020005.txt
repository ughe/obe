   Cross-examined.  Le Blanc was at my place from 15th September till he was taken, which, I believe, was on 8th November—I used to see him almost daily, but I never saw the prisoner there except on one occasion. ELIZA JOANNY. I am the wife of last witness—I remember Le Blanc being the tenant of our kitchen—I have never seen what was going on in the kitchen; Le Blanc always had the key, and he locked the door and took the key away with him—I have seen bottles and hampers taken out, and I have seen the baskets come back—I can't say that I have seen any thing brought in—I have never been in the kitchen since Le Blanc occupied it—he sometimes had the door a little open, but not so that I could see what he was doing—he has sometimes come to the door and closed it.
