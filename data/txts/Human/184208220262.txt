   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  How long had you been in the service? A.  Two years and three months—the prisoner was put as housekeeper over me—I did not dislike that—I have expressed some dis like because I saw some things which were not proper—I did not go to Lincolnshire this year—the prisoner went with the family, leaving me be hind—I opened the prisoner's room door, because I thought things were not right—I had not mentioned it to my master or mistress before—they were not in the house—I had been suspecting things were not right for about three weeks—I never said I would get the prisoner transported nor anything of the kind, nor that I wished she might be transported—I never said anything about it, nor heard anything said about it, before she was taken, and then Bodley, my fellow-servant, said she deserved to be trans ported.
   MR. DOANE . Q.  Was it a false key with which you opened the door of the prisoner's room? No, the key of the next room door. MARY BODLEY. I am servant to Mr. Forster—I had only been there about two months—I observed the prisoner to lock her room door, and she was the only person who did so—on the 7th of August I observed her go towards the water-closet several times within a short period, and as she went on one occasion I observed a piece of muslin hanging from the pocket hole of her dress—I then went into the water-closet and searched—I found some muslin, a cap border, and some other articles—they were cleansed and shown to my master and mistress.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Who directed your attention to the prisoner as she was going to the water-closet? A.  No one—Crow was then gone to church—it was Crow who told me the prisoner's locking her door was unusual—I went into the water-closet immediately after the prisoner came out—I was standing on the stain as she came out—I found the articles down the pan quite concealed, so that they would have been destroyed—Crow and I had been talking about this a few evenings before that—I forget whether Crow told me that she had opened the prisoner's room door with another key—she said she went into her room when she was gone to church—I do not know when my master returned from Lin colnshire—I was not then in the service—I said I thought the prisoner deserved to be transported, as Crow said she had things belonging to her master and mistress—we were all bound to tell our mistress.
