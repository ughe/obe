John Bonham (police-constable A 48.) I took the prisoner, and found

GEORGE SANDERSON. I was sitting in the tap-room the whole evening,

46. JAMES WHITE was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of Octo-
ber, 1 pewter pot, value is.; I quarter of a pint of gin, value 4d. ; 2 half-
crowns, and 15 shillings; the goods and monies of William Griggs, his
master.
WILLIAM Griggs. I keep the Tyssen Arms, at Dalston. The prisoner
had been with me six weeks, as pot-boy.
Ann Griggs. I am the wife of William Griggs. On the 3rd of October
the prisoner asked me for a quartern of gin in a pint pot, to take to a
customer-he then asked for change for a sovereign for Mrs. Noble, a
lady that lives in Mason-row, near us-he asked what she owed, and we
told him it was 38.; and then he asked for change for a sovereign to take
the bill out of it-he said he should get the bill paid he took the change
for that purpose-- I gave him two half-crowns and fifteen shillings- I am
sure there were some shillings- I cannot swear that there was a half-crown
-- he did not return-I never saw him till he was at Hatton-garden, about
three weeks after he took his clothes with him.
MARY NOBLE. I deal with the prosecutor I did not send for any gin
on the 3rd of October—the prisoner did not ask me to pay my bill, or offer
to give me change.
