I have known Perryman and Harris for about 17 years in connection with the "Financial Observer," of which I did the printing for about 18 months, getting the copy at a rule from a lady clerk at the office at Jewry Chambers. The money was usually left out for me. I have seen Perryman there, but I looked to Harris for the money. I met Tyler at the office of the Rand Roodepoort Company, 43, Moorgate Street. I used to work for the company. The name of Herbert Smith what afterwards put up at 43, Moorgate Street. I have seen Perryman and Hawke a there occasionally. I have printed circulars, for Herbert Smith, the copy being handed me by Harris or a lady clerk. Perryman approached me in 1905 about starting another paper called the "Financial Guardian" and asked me if I would print it and take an office for it, which I did at 27, Sun Street. I got the type on the hire system. Perryman paid me the rent of the office and the type. The paper was only set up twice—it was never printed or issued. The manuscript consisted of cuttings from the financial papers, which I received from Harriet or a lady clerk. Cir culars
