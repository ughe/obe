243. GEORGE LEGOW (34), Robbery, with violence, on Andrew Duthie,
and stealing from him seven sovereigns.
Mr. BRINDLEY conducted the Prosecution.
atual
ANDREW DUTHIE. I had been staying at the Sailor's Home, and on the
19th December I left that home, with a porter, who carried my chest con-
taining my clothing, &e.-I was taken to the Hermitage Stairs, and from
there to the Union Stairs-] saw the prisoner and another man at the Union
Stairs] engaged them to take me to the Dundee Boat for Sa.they took
me across, and upon landing we all went to a public-house--I was a little
resh"I had seven sovereigns in one pocket and some silver in the other-
did not put it out in the public-house-] treated the prisoner and the other
man to two glasses of ale-I left my things in the beaton leaving the
public house, we went down the stairs again, and as soon as I got at the foot
of the stairs I Was knooked down by one of the men, but I cannot say which
I was kicked in the face, and stunned for a little-my money was taken
out of my pocket, and they jumped in the boat and pulled away-I felt
them take the money out-it was safe in my pooket when in the public-
house-my box was in the boat-I called after them-I afterwards saw a
constable, and gaye him information--when I went to Leman Street station
I gave a description of the men-I do not know the officer's name-the
prisoner is one of the men who assaulted and robbed me-to my knowledge
there was no one there standing by me, except those two men.
