Laross's Defence (interpreted). I did not wish to go in the Dutch ship,

from another ship, and if I would do that he would replace him with that
money. I was to go in the name of Nelson. I then did not go in the
ship. I never signed that note, and never saw it; the second note I did
put my mark to, and handed it to Nelson, but I never saw a farthing of
the money.
