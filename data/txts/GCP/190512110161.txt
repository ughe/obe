Mint, E. Dear Miss Phillips, -I am sorry I have been so long in sending
you your references. We have had some trouble at home; the old gentle-
man, Mr. Fulton, has passed away. I herewith enclose your references,
and I shall be pleased to hear from you at any time. Affectionately yours,
T. Lewis." - I never saw that letter until it was handed to me at Greenwich
Police Court, and I had to say there and then, did I believe it was in my
husband's writing, so I could not have arranged with him whether it
was his or not-this letter marked Al is not my husband's writing and I
have never had since I was married a black edged sheet of notepaper in

Lordship's dictation taken from that letter-your name was not given
until you were at the police station, when you stated you were the father
of the young lady.
