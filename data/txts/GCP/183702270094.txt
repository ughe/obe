765. PETER GRAY was indicted for bigamy.
MARTHA HALFHIDE. I am the wife of Joseph Halfhide, of Friar's-alley.
I was at St. James's church, Clerkenwell when the prisoner was married
to Martha Whitbread, on the 15th of October, 1821-she is now alive and
in this Court-they lived together nearly seven years - she then went into
the country to her mother's.
Emma Dobson. I live in Playhouse-yard, Blackfriars. I was married
to the prisoner in July, 1835, at Newington church--I have not had any
child by him.
(The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that he had left his first
wife on account of her acts of dishonesty, and habits of intoxication.)
GUILTY. Aged 36.-Confined Three Months.
