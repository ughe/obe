1565. PETER SMITH, feloniously killing a lamb, value 258., the
property of John Mason; with intent to steal the carcass.-2 COUNt, for
feloniously and maliciously killing it.
WILLIAM JENNINGS. I am a labourer. On 11th July, about ten o'clock
at night, I was in the Green-park, near Piccadilly, and saw a man among the
sheep, ten or twelve yards from me-his back was towards me-I went rear
to him, and saw him jump at a lamb and struggle with it-he appeared
working his arms about-] went towards him-he got up and went away-
saw him take off his hat and wipe his hands-I afterwards saw the lamb he
had been struggling with, lying down bleeding from the throat-I do not
believe it was alive- followed him to the corner of the Park, near Burton-
street, where he got over the gate-I climbed over also, and followed him
into Bond-street, where I saw a policeman, and gave him into custody- had
not lost sight of him.
