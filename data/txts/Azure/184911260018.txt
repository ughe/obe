FARNCONB, Mayor.
WALTER LEE ( police-sergeant, T 7). I heard of the loss of this shovel—
I went to Mr. Young, and got it from him—it was covered with mud, and the
prosecutor said, " If it is mine there is a flaw in this part," which there is.
JOHN REED re-examined. This is my shovel—I broke a bit off this corner
when I was forming the foot-path along the Accommodation-road, and there
is a flaw in this part—I often noticed it myself, that it would be agood mark
o know it by.
Prisoner's Defence. It is my own shovel, I had had it three weeks; I
gave 38. 3d. for it; I had not used it for some time; here is my name on it;
I did not steal it, I bought it when I was at work on the line.
NOT GUILTY.
10. JOSEPH IRONS, stealing I scythe, value 58. ; the goods o!
William Wright.
WILLIAM WRIGHT. I live at Chesham. I had a scythe, which I was at
work with on 16th June, in the parish of Harefield—about six o'clock that
Saturday night I left it in the meadow. covered over—I went to work .in
on the Monday morning, about half-past three, and my scythe was gone—I
found it the same afternoon, with David Greige—l gave it to Brown, the
officer; this is it, I know it by this hook, which I ground down, and
ground a bit of the rabbit off with it.
DAVID GREIGE. I am a labourer, at Harrow. On Monday, 18th June,
borrowed a scythe of Thomas Bray.
THOMAS BRAY. I got this scythe from the prisoner on 17th June—I was
to give him 28. for it, but I bad no money then—I said I would pay him om
Monday night—I know the prisoner; he lived at Harefield—l saw bin
again on Monday, the 18th.
WILLIAM BROWN (policeman). This Wythe was delivered to me by
Wright—the prisoner ran away—I could not find him till 19th Nov., when
apprehended him.
Prisoner's Defence. I found it down in the field.
GUILTY. Aged 19.—Confned One Month.

11. MARGARET MITCHELL, stealing 3 half-crowns. 7 shillings, 2
pence, and 1 halfpenny, the moneys of Johan Kauth, from his person ;
having been before convicted.
JOHAN K AUTH (through an interpreter). I am a baker, and live at 2,
Tabernacle-square. On Saturday evening, 28th Oct., I Saw the prisoner on
Holborn. hill—she took me by the band, and put the other hand in my
pocket— she spoke to me, but I did not understand her—I held her, and
called out for the police—I had 158. 2}d. in my pocket—the prisoner took
out three half-crowns, seven shillings, and 21d., and left 6d. in my pocket—
I know I had my money in my pocket at the time—I had bought two hand-
kerchiefs in a shop on Holborn-hill only a few minutes before, end my
money was safe then.
