February, 1 handkerchief, value 4d. ; 2 aprons, value 1s.; 1 petticoat,
value 28. ; 1 shift, value 28.; and 2 caps, value 6d.; the goods of Sarah
Clarke.
SARAH CLARKE. I am single. I sent some clothes by Michael Collins
to be washed, on the 4th of February-I have known the prisoner some
time as a coal-whipperbe visited at my house.

George? 4. Yes--they were done up in a handkerchief.
MICHAEL Collins. Clarke left a bundle in my charge in the tap-room,
and in about twenty minutes I missed it went out for five or ten
minutes, leaving the bundle safe-- the prisoner was there I put it on the
side of the seat, alongside of my tin—when I came back he was gone and
the bundle also I went outside, and took it from him.
Cross-examined. Q. Was he sober? A. I do not know he was there
about half an hour I did not see him drink I was not in his company,
and did not exchange a word with him-he came in and spoke to a few of
his work-mates-there were five persons in the tap-room-I did not see
any bundle of the prisoner's there.
THOMAS GILBERT. I live at Shadwell. I was talking to the prisoner
