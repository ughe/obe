732. WILLIAM HARRIS was indicted for feloniously receiving 9 yards
of lace, value 241., the goods of James Marshall and another, well knowing
them to have been stolen.
MR. WILDE conducted the Prosecution
Joun SNELLGROVE. I am assistant to James Marshall and Co., of Vere-
street, Oxford-street-on the 31st of Jan. Williams produced these nine
yards of lace to me amongst others for sale--I am quite certain it is my
employer's-it is a very beautiful piece--it is the length of our piece, and
the colour and pattern-I am quite certain it had not been sold.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. How many persons are in your
master's service? 4. From fourteen to sixteen-I have not sold this, and
it is not entered in the books-the books are not here. I buy all the lace
goods- I did not miss this till it was offered for sale-I only know of this
lace by examining the books and the lace-box. am Nor Guilty.
