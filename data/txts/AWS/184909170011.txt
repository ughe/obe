WILLIAM BROoKs. I am a salesman in the employ of Robert Spence and

WILLIAM HaILe. I am warehouseman to Robert Spence, Baggally, and Co.

JAMEs GRIPFITHS. I am in partnership with my brother John, at 246,

GEORGE ARNISON HANSLER. I am warehouseman to William Carlisle and
others, of Bow-lane, Cheapside, wholesale baberdashers. On SOth Aug. the
prisoner came and asked for 1lb. of sewing silkI weighed it, and he asked

bill-head, from James Griffiths of the Strand-that would be understood as
an order in the trade-1 asked permission, and then entered the goods, and
gave them to the prisoner-h signed this book as receiving them-they were
worth 11. 5s. 6d.
Prisoner. Q. There is no signature to the order? A. There was another

to Spence's to get the satinet.
