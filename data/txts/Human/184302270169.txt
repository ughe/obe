 975. EDMUND SKINNER was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of Feb ., 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  ., the goods of James Francis Thompson . FREDERICK COLLINS. I am in the employ of Mr. James Francis Thompson, a pawnbroker, in East-street, St. Marylebone. On the 21st of Feb. the pri soner offered a handkerchief to pledge for 1 s . 6 d .—I lent him 1 s . on it—he then went to the back of the shop, and I served another person—he then came to the counter and inquired if I had given him the shilling—I said yes, and he left the shop—previous to his coming to the counter the second time I had seen him put something into his pocket—I missed a handkerchief—I followed, and overtook him about twenty-five yards off—I found this hand kerchief on him.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  Do you know it? A.  Yes—it is an unredeemed pledge, and hung at the back of the shop—I had noticed it two or three minutes before he came in—it is worth 2 s .—the hand kerchief was taken from his hand—I cannot say whether I took in this hand kerchief originally, but I have bad it in my hand several times—we have no other of the same description—I will swear that when the prisoner came into our shop it was hanging on the line—I had noticed it two or three minutes before, as I was looking round the shop—there was a woman and a policeman in the shop when the prisoner came in—there was another young man in the shop, who took the report of the policeman, and I served the prisoner, and then another customer—I cannot recollect whether the woman that I served went out before the prisoner—our shop had not been very full—I attended to a female after I had paid the prisoner—I think she pawned a shawl—I do not recollect what she wanted on it—I had some discussion with the prisoner—he asked 1 s . 6 d ., and I offered him 9 d .—he said that would not do, and I gave him 1 s .—I might have been a minute or two talking with him—I think it was not four or five minutes—I was serving him in front of the shop—there was a partition between him and the boxes—this handkerchief was in front of me as I was behind the counter, and this handkerchief was behind him—it was tied round a string—it would not undo with pulling—when I went after the prisoner I tapped him on the shoulder, and told him he must come back to the shop with me—he had this handkerchief in his hand—he said, "This is what you want, I suppose"—he added, at the police-office, that he had picked it up in the passage.
   COURT . Q.  Are you sure you saw this handkerchief two or three minutes before he came in, safe on the line? A.  Yes. ARTHUR GORE( police-constable D  199.) I took the prisoner.
