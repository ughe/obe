On the afternoon of the 9th of June I was called to St. Andrew's-terrace, Waterloo-road, and saw Trussell outside No. 5, in a chain, and bleeding very much from a wound on his head—he was insensible—a surgeon was sent for, and after some time he came to himself—I brought some women from the house No.5—the prisoner was not one of them, she made her escape from the back of the house before I was let in—she was not produced before the prosecutor that day at all.
   Cross-examined. Q.  When you got in, did you find Williams and three or four other females? A.  Yes—I found 2 s . on Williams—Trussell smelt of liquor—I saw some broken images in the parlour of the house—it was after the people said that Trussell broke all their images, that he said Williams took the half-crown.
   COURT. Q.  Was the original charge for the blow he had received? A.  Yes, and after that, he made the charge of the half-crown.

JOSEPH MAYNARD.
