ALEXANDER BRANDER. I am a teller and accountant in the Union Bank
of Scotland, Glasgow, Jamaica Street, branch--on 2nd October, Bale called
and said that he wanted to open an account, he gave his name, J. Elliott,
and said that he was an advertising agent, but had no office in Glasgow,
but was staying at the Union Hotel – he gave me a 204. note of the Clyde
dale Bank, and a cheque on the Hammersmith Branch of the London and
County Bank for 1031. 108., drawn by Jacob Francis, payable to self
bearer-Bale endorsed it in my presence, “J. Elliott "-I asked him to do
80—he signed the signature-book, and I gave him two books of thirty
cheques each, payable to bearer, and told him I would place the 201. to his
credit and send the cheque to London for collection-I filled up this paying
in slip, which he signed- he called again next day and said that he wanted
301., as he had some small payments to make-I had not received any
advice about the large cheque, and I gave him 201. in 11. notes, for which
he wrote this cheque for 201.-he called again next day, October 4th, and I
told him we had got an acknowledgment of our letter by post-card, but that
it would be another day or two before we heard-he said that he would call
again, but did not, and the balance is still in our hands- this “1 per cent
26." is the interest we allow on private accounts.
