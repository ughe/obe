Count, for killing the said ewe, with intent to steal the carcase.

& quarter from Harrow, and about a mile from the prosecutor's I went to

distance from the village- I concealed myself, having ascertained that the
mutton was there, concealed in an oak tree-there were two legs and a
shoulder, and liver-I concealed myself, to watch-Watson was in another
place, and in about two minutes, I saw the three prisoners come out
