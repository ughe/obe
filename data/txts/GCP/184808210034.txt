1839. MARY ANN JONES, stealing 6 sovereigns, and 98. 6d. in silver
the moneys of Thomas Trotman, in the dwelling-house of Sarah Labbett:
which she pleaded Guilty. Aged 27.-Confined Four Months.
1840. JAMES TERRY, embezzling 13 shillings; the moneys of Joseph
Sparks Hall, his master; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 15.-Confined Ten Days and Whipped.
1841. JAMES WHITBREAD, stealing fowling-piece, and other
articles, value 6l. 138.; the goods of William Gay, in the dwelling-house of
Stephen White; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Four Months.
1842. JOSEPH WILLIAMS, stealing 2 pence and 13 halfpence; the
moneys of William Clark, his master.
REBECCA FAIRCHILD. I am in the employ of William Clark, of the
Buffalo public-house-the prisoner was potman there. On 18th July be
came inside the counter about eight o'clock in the morning-I heard some
coppers rattle- I turned, and saw him shutting the till-in a minute or two
I saw he had something in his hand-I took 8 d. from his hand-I said I
should tell my master, for it was not the first time-he said, "Don't tell
him ; it is the first time."
Prisoner's Defence. I took a cloth off the counter; the halfpence were in
it; I deny taking it out of the till.
CHARLES WEBBER. I was at the house I saw FAIRCHILD take the
money from the prisoner. Guilty. Aged 34.-Confined Four Months.
1843. CHARLES WATERS, stealing 1 waistcoat, 1 jacket, and 2 hand-
kerchiefs, value 11. 2s. ; the goods of Matthew Bentley; and 1 pocket-book,
18.; the goods of William Michael Kelly, in a vessel in a port of entry and
discbarge.
MATTHEW BENTLEY. I am an apprentice on board the Agnes, which is
lying in the Docks- I was going on board on 10th July, and saw the
prisoner, who had no business there-he had my waistcoat on, and was
putting on my jacket-I had left them in my chest-I asked what he was
doing there- he said, "Nothing."
Guilty. Aged 25.-Confined Four Months.
1844. EDWARD PIGOTT, stealing 1 mare, price 91.; the goods of
Charles Batley.-(The prosecutor did not appear.)
Not GUILTY.
