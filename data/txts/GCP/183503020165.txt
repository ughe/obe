849. RICHARD DAVEY was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of
March, 19 spoons, value 358. ; 1 pair of sugar-tongs, value 10s. ; 2 milk.
jugs, value 11. 58.; and 2 pairs of spectacles, value 108.; the goods of
James Humphryes.
MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
2
JEMIMA HUMPHRYES. I am the wife of James Humphryes-he is in the
police, and lives in Swan-street, Minories. The prisoner was in the service
of Mrs. Riggs, and was minding a house at No. 19, Middlesex-street-I
had pawned the property stated in 1832-in 1838 I paid three months'
interest on it-and in 1834 I paid twelve months' interest, at Mr.
Whiskard's, in Bishopsgate-street--the duplicates were in a box, at No.
19, Middlesex-street-I had the key of the box, but I gave it to Mrs.
Riggs about the 5th of March, 1834, because I went into the country:
and about a month ago the prisoner's wife gave it to me at No. 19, Mid-
dlesex-street--I opened my box in presence of the officer, and missed my
duplicates-Mrs. Riggs is in Whitecross-street prison.
NOT GUILTY.
