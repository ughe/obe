scover whether they were making them or not? 4. Because I expected

M. BALLANTINE. Q. You did it for a trap then ? A. I did it to detect
the parties, if I possibly could, who were making these sort of things, to find
ant who they were-I did it knowing the solemn position I placed myself in
I do not think I ever said, “These are the things to throw among the
troops of the Government"-I do not think the word was used
at all I do not believe I said "the troops of the Government," or "the
troops of the Queen"-I might have said, "They would do to throw among
the troops," I do not say I did say so I said that I had a large quantity at
home-that was false- I wish to speak the truth now, and confess what I
have done- I dare say I told several falsehoods in the course of these pro-
ceedings-Gurney is in custody; I went to him in his cell, one of the turn-
Lepe let me in-I believe I said to Gurney, that if he would inform it would be
rester for his wife and children- I believe I did say those words--I am not
exactly positive, I am rather confused with so many questions-I cannot
wy whether any policemen were waiting outside-there were lots of police-
men in the yard—I did not, as I came out of Gurey's cell, say to a police-
man, “It is no go," or " It won't do"-nothing of the kind—never spoke
to any policeman coming out-I do not believe I said so to the turnkey or
w sybody-I believe I said to Gurney that he, his wife, and family would
all be made comfortable for their lives if he would inform, and I told him to
speak the truth, all he knew about it-I had no authority whatever for say-
ing that it was untrue it was done for him to state all he knew, and for
him to tell me what he knew of it-I did not mean to get all I could out of
him, and then tell it-I did not tell all that he said to me-- I wanted him to
till all he knew about it, because I thought he would be telling the truth and
getting himself out of a scrape-I cannot tell what made me think so I was
not sent in-I was not requested to go the turnkey let me in-J told him
what it was for ; that I thought he would confess, and tell all about it--I do
not think that I said when I came out that it was no go, but I will not swear
It-Gurney was put into a separate cell for me to speak to him he was by
himself when I saw him.
Q. Have you been in England all your life! 4. Except about three months,
about thirteen years ago I went over with a friend, named Rodder, to New
York, and stayed there I think about a month-I was certainly not away four
months altogether--my father was alive at that time-I did not know that
be had any money at the Savings' Bank--there was no charge against me
about that before I went to America-I was married at that time I did not
take my wife-I had but one child-I only let two or three persons know
that I was going--my father never made any complaint of some money having
been taken out of the Savings Bank--there was money of my owo, which I
dow out of the Funds-it was in my own name, and I used my own name
to get it out-I signed nobody else's name it was money that had been
riven me by my father, and put into the Stock-broker's hands in my name
I went to America a few days after I drew the money out my father was
very cross that I left my wife-when I came back I gave what money I had
into my father's hands to take care of-I got back every farthing of it from
him-his name was Howell Powell.
