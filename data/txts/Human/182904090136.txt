971. JANE CHAPPEL was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of April , 1 pewter pot, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Mark Cullingford.
   MARK CULLINGFORD. I am a publican, and live in York-street, Marylebone. The prisoner was brought to my house with this pot, which is mine; she was quite a stranger.
   CHARLES SAGE. On Thursday night last, I was informed a woman had taken a pot from the door; I went out, and followed the prisoner up William-street - I took her with this put under her shawl.
 Prisoner's Defence. I stopped to tie up my stocking, a man and a woman came by; the man put his face close to mine, and I took up the pot which stood there merely to pretend that I stooped for that - I had not gone many yards when this gentleman took me.
     GUILTY. Aged 29. Transported for Seven Years.
 972.  MARY CONNELLY was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of March , 2 half-crowns, the monies of John Manuel.
   JOHN MANUEL. I met the prisoner between nine and ten o'clock, on the 30th of March, in Sutton-street, Soho; I did not know her before; she said, "Well my dear," or something to that effect; we went into a passage together, but were not there more than half a minute; I then put my hand to my pocket, and my money was all correct - I had four half-crowns and 2s.; she said,"Let us go up stairs;" we went up, and she began to feel about my person, as we were on the first landing-place; we did not go into any room; I heard some money chink in her hand - I put my hand into my pocket, and missed two half-crowns; I accused her of the theft, and she dropped one halfpenny down the stairs, offered me another, and said there was my money; I said it was not, for I had lost two half-crowns; we came down stairs, and she called the watchman; I held her till the watchman came and searched her, but no half-crowns us were found; they had been in my right-hand waistcoat pocket, and could not have fallen out - I am quite sure she took them out; I had no halfpence about me; she said something about a shilling as we were going along; I was sober.
   JOHN FRENCH. I am a watchman. I searched the prisoner as far as decency would allow; but found nothing on her - the prosecutor was quite sober, and stated the same as he has now.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going for some starch; he dragged me on the stairs; I said it was not the first time he had accused me wrongfully; he said he would transport me; I had a 1d. in my hand - he said on the stairs he would give me so and so: he had me here before for 4s. a year and a half ago, when I first came from Ireland; he was dragging me along, and the halfpence fell from me.
   JOHN MANUEL. Upon my oath, I never saw the girl before; I never was before a Magistrate before in my life; there were a number of people round her.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years.
 973.  CHARLES DUNN was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of April , 1 ham, value 10s., the goods of Thomas Barford.
   GEORGE ALLEN. I know Mr. Barford, a cheesemonger of East-street. About ten minutes before ten o'clock, on the 9th of April, I saw the prisoner and another come by- the other lad crossed, took this ham, and gave it to the prisoner, who put it under his coat, and walked off with it; a neighbour gave information to the prosecutor, who came out and pursued him; I saw the prisoner throw down the ham in Spring-street, and the watchman took him.
   GEORGE HAWKER. I am a watchman. I took took the prisoner.
   THOMAS BARFORD. This is my ham.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years.
 974.  JOHN HUNT was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of April , 1 coat, value 5s. the goods of Richard Edwards.
   RICHARD EDWARDS. I drive a cabriolet. I lost my coat on the 5th of April, out of my master's stable, in John-street, Tottenham-court-road; the door was shut but not locked - the gates were locked; I left it safe about half-past ten o'clock at night - I have seen the prisoner in the yard several times - he went out two or three times as odd man for my master; he knew the yard very well - there were five or six coats taken at the same time, and I believe ten whips: I found mine on Salter.
   CHARLES SALTER. I am a hackney-coachman. I bought this coat of the prisoner last Monday week, in the evening - he wanted 5s., but agreed to let me have it for 3s. 6d.; it is old and torn.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I bought three coats and ten whips in the Old Bailey, on the Saturday night, of a man in a New market cut-off coat; I sold two coats to a Jew.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. Transported for Seven Years.
 975.  JAMES KNOWLES was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of February , 5 sovereigns and 1 half sovereigns, the monies of William Povey.
   WILLIAM POVEY, JUN. I drive the Stroundwater coach. Mr. Lawerence asked me to bring this money to Mr. Van, of Tooley-street: the prisoner had acted as porter to the coaches at the Bull inn, to which I came - I did not hear from Mr. Van, and on the Sunday morning I sent the prisoner to him to know if he would come; he returned and said, Mr. Van said if I would send the money over by him he would pay him - I gave him five sovereigns and one half-sovereigns, to take to Mr. Van; he did not return, and I wrote to Mr. Van to know if he had seen him.
 Prisoner. Q. Have I not a great many times been intrusted with your money? A. Yes.
   THOMAS VAN. The money never came to my hands; some of my family desired the prisoner to receive the money, but I was gone to church - I do not know whether the prisoner is the person who came; I do not know that any person authorised him to go for it on my account.
     NOT GUILTY.
