858. BENJAMIN SMITH and ANN JOHNSON, stealing 1 gown, and 2
shirts, value 6s. ; the goods of Mary Ann White : Johnson having been before
convicted.
ALFRED SPICE (police-constable, V 47). About half-past five o'clock in
the evening of 13th March, I saw the prisoners coming through Vauxhall-
gate-they went into the Brixton-road-I saw them at Vauxhall-gate again,
a little before eight-I had received information, and stopped Johnson, and
told her she must go to the station-I saw something hanging below her gown
-she said, “It is of no use ; you may as well take it"-I got this gown
and two shirts from her-they were wet--they appeared to come from between
ber gown and petticoat.
Cross-examined by Mr. WOOLLETT. Q. Did not she say that she found
them? 4. Yes.
WILLIAM GIBBS (policeman, V 77). I was with Spice, and saw something
drop froin Johnson's petticoats on to the ground-Smith picked it up, and
she drew it under her dress again-I took Smith, and found a whip on bim
- he said, “They can't hurt me for the whip, if they do for the other."
MARY ANN WHITE. I am single, and live at Halstead-street, Brixton-
road. This shirt and gown are mine-they were hung up upon a line to dry
about four o'clock in the afternoon-I missed them about ten.
Cross-examined. Q. How do you know them? A. I marked them
myself.
SAMUEL BRINE (policeman, B 33). I produce a certificate (read-Ann
Murray-Convicted August 1848, having been before convicted, confined one
year)- I was present, Johnson is the person.
SMITH-Not Guilty.
JOHNSON-GUILTY. Aged 19.-Transported for Seven Years.
