CHARLES PATRIOK KEMP. I am a surgeon, of Clarendon Road-on Good
Friday, I was sent for to Mr. Pino's, and saw the prisoner-she said nothing,
but unlocked her box, in which I found a child in a state of decomposition
-there were no external marks of violence the cord had not been tied, it
was torn off close to the navel-I should say the child had been dead about
three weeks.
Cross-examined. It was born dead, it had never breathed-she at once
opened the box for me she seemed very much distressed.
COURT. Q. Did the lungs float? A. No, they had never been inflated,
and therefore the child had never breathed; that is an infallible test.bid
