I carried on business as a brush-maker in Somersetshire for several years, and was cryer to the Court of Quarter Sessions there, but from unforeseen circumstances, was obliged to give up business and my situation; the pencils are part of the stock I had left, and the paper the goods are in I bought of Mr. Hasty, opposite where I lodge - the writing on it is my own - the four brushes are my own manufacture; as to the string, I bought it of a hawker in the street, in exchange for other goods.

 1083.  MARY HONER was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of April , 1 gown, value 1s.; 2 petticoats, value 1s., and 1 shift, value 6d., the goods of Eleanor Moore.
   ELEANOR MOORE. I live in Chamber-street, Whitechapel. On the 4th of April I was at dinner in the front kitchen; the prisoner came and looked into the kitchen; she was a stranger - I followed her, and asked her, on the top of the stairs, what she wanted; she said Nothing; I went down, and told master - he went up, and we found all these things of mine laying in the passage; she had them under her shawl - he ran out, and took her - she got from him; I followed into Rosemary-lane, and gave her in charge.
   JOHN FARNES. I am a Policeman. I took her in charge.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was taken ill, and seeing the door open, went in to look for a convenience.
     GUILTY. Aged 52. - Confined One Month.
 1084.  JOSEPH HUGHES was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of March , 1 bag, value 6d., and 19s. 10d. in copper money, the property of William Clay.
 SECOND COUNT, stating it to be the property of  George Tucker.
 GEORGE TUCKER. I am  carman to William Clay, saltmerchant, of Lower Thames-street. On the 4th of March, about five o'clock, I received four 5s. papers of copper from the soup-house in Brick-lane, and put them into a bag on a board, at the top of my cart - I rode in front; the cart was empty; I observed the prisoner following the cart, and kept looking behind now and then - he must have got into the cart to take it, and in Old-street I saw him in the cart; I came round, and called out,"Oh, you thief! you are there" - he had then got the bag of copper in his arms; he jumped out; I called Stop thief! he was stopped before I lost sight of him - he dropped the bag in the bed of the cart - I am sure he moved it from where I had placed it.
   EDWARD HARDY. I am a weaver, and live at Hoxton. I saw Tucker riding in front of his cart, and when he called out I saw the prisoner in front of the cart, with the money in his arms - he jumped out, leaving it behind.
   HENRY JAMES LARDNER. I am a gun-maker. I was in Old-street-road, and secured the prisoner.
   GEORGE KEMP. I am a Policeman. I received him in charge - the bag contains 19s. 11d. in copper.
   JAMES GLIBBERY. I am a sergeant of Police. When the prisoner was brought to the station, he said distress caused him to do it, and if he got through this he would not he in trouble again.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1085.  ALFRED HOLT was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of February , 1 knife-case, value 18d., the goods of John Pistor , and that he had before been convicted of felony.
   JOHN PISTOR. I am a broker, and live in Walbrook-place, Hoxton. On the 4th of February this knife-case was in the front part of the shop - I was absent about two hours, and did not miss it till the Policeman stopped the prisoner with it.
   JOHN COLLINS. I am a Policeman. On the 24th of February, about half-past six o'clock, I stopped the prisoner with the knife-case under his arm, about two hundred yards from Pistor's shop; he said he had brought it from his sister's, at Islington.
   GEORGE KEMP. I am a constable. I have a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction in this Court -(read); I know he is the man.
 Prisoner. I get my living by selling things in the street - the Police have disturbed me several times, which forced me to commit this crime.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
