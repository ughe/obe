of January, 1 cheese, value 14s., the goods of James Pearson.
THOMAS JOHNSON. I am shopman to Mr. James Pearson, of East-
street, Marylebone. About two or three o'clock, on the 28th of Janu-
ary, a neighbour opposite came and told me something, in consequence
of which I missed a cheese-I had seen it safe half an hour before the
prisoner was pointed out to me by somebody-I went and brought him
back-when the prisoner was in the shop, he said, " I do not want to
have any bother about it, I know where the cheese is; I will return it,
and make you a handsome present"-I said I could not do it, and gave
him into custody.
