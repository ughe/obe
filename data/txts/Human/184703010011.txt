 660. HENRY WHAYMAN was indicted for stealing 7 3/4 lbs. weight of lead, value 5 s  .; the goods of Charles Robinson and another, his masters. GEORGE HOUNSOM. I am gate-keeper to Charles Robinson and Son, engineers and iron-founders, of Eaton-lane, Pimlico. The prisoner has been their labourer for three or four months—on his leaving work, at half-past five o'clock, I noticed him pressing his arm against his waistcoat, to keep the lead up—I stopped him, and told him he had something concealed in his posses sion—he said he had nothing—I found on him a pig of lead—he said he found it in Ebury-street—we have a mould in which we cast our lead—I tried it to the mould, and it fitted. JOHN CULHANE( police-constable B  144.) I received the prisoner in charge, and have the lead.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY  .— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Fourteen Days; his uncle entering into recognizances for his good behaviour.
