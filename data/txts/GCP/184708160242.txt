1990. ANN BLACKMORE was indicted for feloniously killing and
slaying a certain male child; also charged on the Coroner's Inquisition
with the like offence.
Mr. Cooper conducted the Prosecution.
John ROBINSON LEADAM. I am a surgeon, in Tooley-street, Southwark.
In July last I was called to see a child of the prisoner's, it was just dead-I ex-
amined it I understood that it had been strangled-there were some crease
marks about the neck, in the situation of the cap-strings, which appeared to
have been tied rather tightly, but not sufficient to cause death-I made a
post-mortem examination, next day I discovered nothing particular externally
on removing the scalp I found extravasation of blood on the right side of
the head-I immediately examined the scalp, and then observed two or three
small bruises on it, very slight, not sufficient to be observed before I then
examined the skull, and found it fractured to the extent of about two inches
on the right side-in my opinion, that was sufficient to cause death, it might
be caused by a blow, or fall on any hard substance.
