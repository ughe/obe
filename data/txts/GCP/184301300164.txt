741. JEREMIAH DANIEL HEALEY and CATHERINE HEALEY
were indicted for stealing, on the 19th of Dec., 11 orders for the payment of
287. 11s.. the property of Peter Green.-4 other Counts, calling them war-
rants, securities, declarations, and pieces of stamped paper.
MR. CHARNOCK conducted the Prosecution.
PETER Grxen. I went to Oporto in 1832, and remained there some years
-on the 16th of Dec. last I obtained these orders from the Portuguese Com-
missioners-on the 16th of Dec. I saw Jeremiah Healey on the subject of
selling these warrants-he was recommended to me by a man-I saw him on
the day I received them-he was attending the plaee where the Commissioners
gave these warrants-he gave me his address, and said if I disposed of them
to any one he would give me as much as any man-on the 19th of Dec. 1
went to his house with my daughter, between nine and ten o'clock, with these
warrants- I was asked up stairs- I sat down, and saw the two prisoners-
they asked if I had got the bills-1 produced them-I said, "I called on you
to ascertain the amount you will give for these bills"-Jeremiah Healey said,
148. 6d. or 158. 6d. in the pound-the gross amount of them is 281. 11s.,
and there is five per cent. on them till they are paid I did not agree to sel]
them--he handed them back to me, and to the best of my belief I put them
into my pocket in my coat-I would not swear that I did I was then coming
out, and Jeremiah said, " I hope you will perform your contract to me, to let me
have them sooner than any one else"- he put his hand on my shoulder, and
we talked some time-I then bade him good night, and next morning I missed
them-I went to him that day, and he said he knew nothing of them-I went
again on the 21st-he said he knew nothing of them I heard no more of
them till Benwood called I went with him to Healey's-Jeremiah said he
would not give me 5s. for the chance of my payment, and I had better write
to the Commissioners-in consequence of information I went to Mount-street
- I found Jeremiah there- I called him on one side, and said I was given to
understand he had these bills, and he had best give them up I did not want
to have any more to do with him-he said, "D-n your eyes, do you think
you are going to bounce me out of them ?"-Catherine was with him-she
said she knew nothing at all about them- I called a policeman, and gave them
both into custody-these are the orders.

titulos ? A. No, I have not sold them since this-I did not sell them to
Silver-I received altogether by this affair, 411. 18.-I do not know that their
value is only 211.- I have heard many persons say they would give 14s. 6d. or
158. 6d. in the pound for them--they were issued on the 15th of December, and
carry interest at 5 per cent.-there are eleven of them-I understood them to
be worth 281. 118.-I cannot tell who I received 417. from-I saw a man on
the stairs, who gave me a paper with "201." in it, and asked me into a public-
house-I believe he was the prisoner's solicitor-my lawyer was at my hand,
and Mr. Strutt the lawyer was on the other hand, I believe I received 206
from the father of the prisoner-I believed that my titulos had been stolen
from me by one of the prisoners- I do not know what I received 201, for, but I
asked what it was for, he said, “ Put it into your pocket"-they got me to
write a receipt before I received 1s, of it-I believe it was Mr. Silver
put down 21l. more--I never saw the titulos from the time I lost them till
