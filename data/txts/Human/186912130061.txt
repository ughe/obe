137. WILLIAM JOHN EATON  (26), and JOHN FEMSOM  (42) , Stealing thirty-six pieces of wood, of John Dornett Bartlett , the master of Eaton. Second Count  Receiving.
   EATON PLEADED GUILTY.— Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
   MR. WOOD , for the prosecution, offered no evidence against Fensom  — NOT GUILTY.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY , 10 TH JANUARY , 1870.
