MR. Parry. Q. With whatever pack you were playing, you knew the
cards you were giving? 4. Yes—I stacked the cards each time, and each
time got that hand that the money was to be won on-I have described
myself as a gambler and a cheat-I am sorry I can give myself no other
name, since I have been connected with these men-I have been a gambler
and cheat about two years--I have not followed it as some have, I have
been attending to business- I was a house-decorator-I have been engaged
for some firms in London, and was in business for myself in 1842 and
1843, in New York, in America-I was upwards of three years in America
- I did not carry on the trade of & gambler and cheat there I was
respectable man in America-I left London for America, as I wished to see
it-1 had been working at my business-I wished to go into my busines
there- I had a shop at New York-while in London I was engaged for
Morant, of Bond-street, house-decorators-I might be with them three
years- left of my own accord, to go to America-Wales is my native
place-I come from Cardiff, in Glamorganshire-I there followed the busi-
ness of a painter- I was apprentice to Mr. Thomas, a painter- I was no other
