   MR. ADOLPHUS . Q.  Is it at all uncommon in a bookseller's shop for a stranger to come in? A.  No.—the edges of the book he sold appeared white to me—I saw it was a book—that was all the notice I took of it.
   COURT . Q.  Are you sure that the man was Holland? A.  Yes—I saw him come into the shop. DENNIS HOLLAND re-examined. Q.  Do you recollect seeing this man in Jolly's shop? A.  I cannot say that do—I took "Lindley's Botany" there, and sold it for 7 s .
