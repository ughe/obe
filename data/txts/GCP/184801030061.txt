COURT. Q. You sometimes put something in pills of opium, to make it
stick together! 4. I am not in the habit of making pills; I believe such:
to be the fact-the texture of opium is rather bard, and I do not think you
can roll it into the form of a pill without its being moistened with something
-it can be powdered; it does not crumble very easily--the pills would
not readily crumble; if broken it could be powdered if a pill were made with
something to stick it, I should say it would not crumble very easily, it
would not require a hammer-I do not think it could be done with the
fingers- I think it would require something harder, you might do it with
your nail - I do not think you could do it by squeezing and rubbing it,
it would depend a good deal on the hardness of the opium in the state
most favourable for crumbling, I do not think it could be crumbled by the
fingers.
HENRY PHILLIPS. I am one of the clerks at Marylebone Police-court. I
was present at the examination of the witnesses before the depositions were
taken---the prisoner's statements were taken down each time--they are not
signed by bim -- he could not write-they were read over to him, and signed
by the Magistrate-on 23rd Nov., after the witnesses were examined, the
prisoner said, "The roof of my mouth is now sore from the stuff I have
taken from her; the roof of my month has been off several times from what
she has given me; if there was anything in it she must have put it in"-on
1st Dec., after Lee's examination, he said, "I never saw that boy before
with my eyes" -on the same day he said, "I have been a good husband as
any one can be, and I have been as bad as any one since-on 8th Dec.
after Mr. Heisch's 'examination, he said, "Last Tuesday fortaight I went
into Mr. Watson's, in Edgware-road, and asked for two pills of opium ; he
asked me if anything was the matter, I said there was a great discharge in
front of me, and he gave me two pills ; I have been suffering from it for three
years and three months, and never divulged it till this day, I have been in
the habit of taking them, and must take the same again to-day, I have also
taken some in tea, and I put the pills into the cup myself to take into
my own body, and I have taken many a hundred, and I must take more
again; at half-past two o'clock that day, I had fifteen drops of laudanum in
me; I would not tell my wife about it; I took the cup from my wife, and
drank the contents, all but a little, and threw the rest under the grate/
GILBERT M'MURDO, Esq. Tam surgeon to the gaol of Newgato. I visit the
gaol every day I have seen the prisoner repeatedly--he complained of a little
cold, and on my putting some particular questions to him last week, in re-
lation to this trial, he told me he suffered from the effects of old venereal
disease, for which he had been in the habit of taking opium for a considerable
time-previous to that he had not made any complaint to me I talked to
him about his asserted complaint-I believe it did not exist I did not give
him any opium-he bas not taken any to my knowledge.
Prisoner's Defence. I took the pills into my own body to do myself good,
and no one else; I did not buy them to poison my wife; I put them into my
tea, and put them into my own belly : I was very happy and comfortable;
if she had poured me out another cup I should have had the grounds as well
as her; I am quite innocent; I have suffered in this prison more than I have
for the twenty-three years I have been married; it was when I was not able
to work that I was a bad husband, because I could not maintain my family
I have taken nine grains of opium; I asked them at Marylebone-office to
examine me, to see whether it was true or not, but there was not a doctor
made any answer; Mr. Faulkner was there.
