EDWARD PARIS. On the morning of 23rd Feb. there were white and
black peas in Messrs. Pickford's loft-I have compared the bag of peas produced
with them, and have not the slightest doubt about them-there is a peculiarity
about them-a few white ones have got among them-the black peas are
peculiarly large, and much darker than the generality-th black peas in the
loft are separated from the white by a partition-a quantity of white peas had
got among the black ones, but no black ones had got into the white-that was
the case in the bulk, and is so with the peas in the bag also.
