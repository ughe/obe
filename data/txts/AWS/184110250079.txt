2636. JANE DRAPER, SUSAN CAMPBELL, and MARY GILL
were indicted for stealing, on the 8th of October, 4 pairs of boots, value
138., the goods of William Bradford.
WILLIAM BRADFORD. 1 keep a boot and shoe shop in Kingsland-road.
On the morning of the 8th of October I left my shop to call on a neigh-
bour-J returned in ten minutes-] saw a vacancy in the window, and
missed four pairs of boots-the policeman afterwards showed these boots to
me-these now produced are them.
ROBERT QUINLAY (police-constable N 240.) About nine o'clock that
morning, I saw the prisoners at the corner of a street, half a mile from the
prosecutor's-Gil approached me in a very daring manner, and I saw some
boots under her shawl-the other two were crossing the road-I called to
them to stop, and come to me-they came, and I said to them, 44 I want all
three of "-they walked before me to the station-I found two pairs of
boots on Gill, and one pair on each of the other two-I took them to the
prosecutor, and he identified the shoes.
Draper. We bought them.
Gill. We bought them.
DRAPER'LIGUILTY.
Aged 14. Transported for Seven Years-
CAMPBELL'-GUILTY Aged 18. J Recommended to the Penitentiary.
GILL-GUILTY. Aged 13.-Confined Four Days.
2637. THOMAS FULFORD was indieted for stealing, on the 29th
of September, 1 handkerchief, value 18., the goods of Henry Barnes, from
his person.
HENRY BARNES. I am a dry-salter, and live in Long-acre. On the
29th of September I was under the arcade near Lincoln's-inn-fields-I
felt in my pocket, and I had no handkerchief-] believe this one now pro-
duced is mine-it is very much like mine.
WILtAM COLLINS. I am clerk to a house-agent. About half-past
nine o'clock, on the morning of the 29th of September, I saw the prisoner
walking behind the prosecutor-he lifted up the tail of his coat, and took
the handkerchief out-I eried 46 Stop thief," and followed him-he turned
up Little Queen-street, and there I lost sight of him-he was taken by the
policeman.
WILLIAM SINNOTT (police-constable 91.) I heard a cry of 44 Stop
thief," and the prisoner ran into my arms in Little Queen-street-he had
this handkerchief in his hand, and was in the act of dropping it in a door-
way-he did not see me till I caught him-he was in the act of looking
back after Mr. Collins.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Six Months.
