 811. JOHN JONES, WILLIAM WESTCOMB, and ABRAHAM WAFFINGTON, were indicted for stealing, on the   5th of February , 601bs. weight of rope, value 3 s  ., the goods of the London Dock Company .
   MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution. TIMOTHY CRAWLEY. I live in Angel-garden, Shadwell, and am a labourer in the London Docks occasionally. On the 5th of February I saw Waffington, who I had known five or six years, on the Eas-quay—he asked me to go and get some old junk, pointing to a shed close by—he said it would fetch 8 s . a cwt., and he would make a pass himself to get it out—I said I would not do such a thing for him, or any body else, as I had once been in trouble for beating a policeman—he said I was not game  —I then left him, and stood behind a crane, and watched—in about two hours I saw him come out of the shed, which he had pointed out to me—he had a piece of rope with him, which he put behind a pile of wood—he then went back, and got some more rope—the other two prisoners then came out, with each of them some rope, and chucked  it down in the same place, behind the wood—I saw them all talking together—I told the dock-officers of it—it was between half-past twelve and one o'clock.
   Waffington. Q.  How often have you been in the House of Correction? A.  Only once—I worked at the Docks last Monday week. GEORGE DIX. I am an officer of the London Docks. Between one and two o'clock, on the 5th of February, Crawley made a communication to me—I went to the East-quay, and he pointed out Jones and Westcomb—I took them into custody—they said they knew nothing about it—I went to the wood, and found 60lbs. weight of rope. CHARLES SLATER. I am an officer. I went in search of Waffington, and found him coming out of a cheque-clerk's box—I told him he was charged with stealing rope—he said he knew nothing about it. WILLIAM LANCASTER. I am foreman of the spirit quay. I examined the pile of foreign junk in the shed, and il looked as if the corner had been pulled down—this is part of it.
   Westcomb.  I went to see a cousin at the Docks, and was taken.
   Waffington.  I went there for work, and was taken. NOT GUILTY.
