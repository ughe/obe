to the ledger, journal, and cash-book-since that time they have been put
in order.
MR. METCALFE. Q. You say the prisoner came over to Paris in December
or the beginning of January ? A. Yes he went from Paris to Switzerland,
and then to Lyons, to buy-be then returned to England at the end of
January without seeing us-it was the 30th of January, I think, or the very
beginning of February—there was a man named Killingworth at the office
at times-he is in London-I don't know whether he is here.
MR. GIFFARD. Q. That person was employed by the prisoner himself!
