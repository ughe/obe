WILLIAM PERRY. I live at 30, Bath Street, City Road, and am a
master carmun-on Sunday, 3rd May, I was in my yard about 7 o'clock
in the morning, and the prisoner Mandall came to me I knew he worked
for Mr. Hunter-he asked me if I could do with two pieces of stuff-I
asked what sort of stuff it was-he asked whether I could go over and
have a look at it at his house-I asked him if it was all right; he said
it was all right, that a man named George gave it to him to sell - I went
over to his house and he cut me off two pieces as patterns-I showed
them to my mother on the Monday morning-next day I saw Mandall
again and gave him 108. for it; this is the stuff-I was taken into custody
I made a statement to the police and was allowed to go-I do not know
Allen.
JAMES NEWBY (Police Sergoant). On the 29th May I arrested Allen in
Murray Street, Hoxton-I told him I should take him into custody for
being concerned with Quilter and others in a robbery in a warehouse in
the City-he said "I know nothing about it; I have been in the country
for a week; I suppose I shall get over the job "-he was taken into
custody, and then Davidson saw him.
JOHN PEARCE (Ro-examined). The property produced is mine-those
two pieces that were sold for ten shillings are worth about one pound.
piece-I can swear to this canvas-this property was on my premises on
the 2nd May, and I missed it on the 4th.
Mandall's Defence. The first statement I made was false; the second
is quite true.
Allen's Defonos. I was hired on the Saturday morning to do this job
by Mr. Quilter. I always took him to be a respectable shopkeeper. He
asked me if I could get a van. I said “Yes." I asked Hunter could
he let me have a van. He said "Who for?” I said "Mr. Quilter."
Quilter was there, and we drove into Knightrider Street with Mr. Hunter.
I never moved away from him. I thought I was doing a job to earn
an honest shilling. I never thought of what it was. I am very sorry
indeed. I am quite innocent.
Valo'. Defonce. I wish to say a few words with respect to the evidence
of Mr. Davidson. He says when Hunter came into the shop he identified
me as the man that hired the van of him on the 2nd May. Mr. Quilter
says he never saw me before, and I did not hire it. I can provo I
never left home on Saturday till half-past 1. I was dressed as I am
now. I never wore a light coat in my life. I wish to call my mother a
& witness. All these men are entire strangers to me; I never saw them
before, or the man Perry either.
SUSAN VALE. I am the prisoner Vale's stepmother-on 2nd May be
never left home till & quarter-past 1 in the day-he was not well-
might have been nearer the half-hour, but I can swear he did not leave
his home till turned a quarter-past 1, and he was dressed exactly as he
is now-he possessed no overcoat.
Cross-examinod. I live at 42, Beeton Road, Bermondsey-it was not
& quarter past 12 when he left home-I fix the time & quarter past 1,
because I wanted to go out that day; and as he did not have his break-
fast till past 11, I could not get ready to go-he had no work that day-
I have seen him wearing this coat.
