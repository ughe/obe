WILLIAM COOPER. I am a brewer at Lower Shadwell, I have known
Mr. Rogers about twelve years--I am acquainted with his handwriting-I
believe the signature " Thomas Rogers," to this I O U for 17891. is not
Mr. Rogers's writing, nor this to the one for 4001.--I have seen it before
-I do not believe the endorsements on these three bills to be his writing
--it is not like it.
Court. Q. You apply your observation with equal confidence to the
