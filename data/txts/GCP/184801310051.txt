Mr. Bopkin. Q. Can you form any judgment how long he had had the
small-pox? 4. Taking the very lowest general estimate, the disease would
be latent at the time of the injury-he might have recovered from the injury,
but having the disease latent in the system, the shock of the attack killed him
-I am certain he would not have died from the small-pox if he had not had
the injury.
MARTIN MEOASIN BULL. I am a surgeon. I assisted Mr. Clark in the
post-mortem examination.
Cross-examined. Q. Your opinion I believe is, that the death was caused
by the combined effects of the injury and the small-pox; that he did not die
from the injury solely, but that it must have had a share in producing death?
4. Yes; he did not die from small-pox alone.
Kemp's Defence. They told the truth before the Magistrate, but they have
left part of it out, which has done me great injury.
