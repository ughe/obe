ESTHER CARTER. I am a barnaid at the Clarence Arms, Plumstead_
on 16th November Warren came there about 5.40, and asked for half a

came to 14d.; I gave him the change; I put the shilling into the till
there were other shillings there-he went away—in about twenty minutes
he came back and called for a glass of 6d. ale; I served him, he tendered a
