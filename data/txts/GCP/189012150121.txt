HENRY JOSEPH SARGEANT. I am a butcher, of 104, High Street, Lewis-
ham, within a minute's walk of the High Road, Lee-on 8th November
about 8 p.m. Clarke came and bought half a leg of mutton, price 29. 4ja.,
and gave me a florin and a sixpence-I gave her 1fd. change, and she
turned to go, when Barnett came in and spoke to me-I thon tried the
florin with my teeth-it was soft and it bent-my teeth went into it-
this is it-I found it gritty, and handed it to Barnett immediately--he left
the shop, and in about three minutes brought in the two prisoners and the
half leg of mutton.
Cross-examined by Mr. FRITH. I did not try the forin in Clarke's

to the opposite side, and went into Mr. Thomas's boot shop, and Nye
took & purse out of a black basket, took something out of it, and

fifty yards off from Mr. Sargeant's shop, stood looking into a furniture
