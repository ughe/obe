144. CHARLES JOHNSON (19) and EDWARD BRILL (21).
Robbery with violence on William Gordon and stealing 9d., his money.
WILLIAN GORDON. I am a stevedore of 4, Crawford Place, Tidal
Basin—I was with Barry on Christmas Eve--we went into a publie-
house and saw the two prisoners : Johnson said, "I will have a drop of
rum with you "-I said I would not drink with him-we left the house,
and Brill struck me a violent blow on my jaw and knocked me down ; I!
got up and Johnson struck me on my jaw and put his hand in my left
pocket and took out 9d. and showed it to Brill-there was a great crowd
of women and men-Michael Courtney came up and said, Have them.
locked up”; and they knocked him down-I went with a constable to
the public house and arrested Johnson; and he kicked me in the groin,
but did not hurt me, though he left the mark of his boot on me the
blow I had from Johnson caused blood, and my cheek was cut by my.
teeth:
Cross-examined by Brill. I had earned £1 28. the night previous-I
never stole a purse and silk handkerchief from a sailor.
PATRICK BARRY. On the night of December 23rd I was walking with
Gordon in High Street, Woolwich-We went into the Three Daws
Johnson was there Gordon said, “You will have nothing with me"-
Johnson said, "I will see you later on," and outside, Johnson said, "Why
did not you give me a drink 1"-Gordon said, "I do not associate with
your equals " -Johnson knocked him down with his left hand-he got up
again and stood against the wall, and Johnson put his hand in his pockets
-I did not interfere, as there was a strong gang there, and a lot of
women,
Cross-examined by Brill. I am a coal-porter-I do not steal-I do not
cut people's heads open with a pot, or knife them, to get money; I have not
been long in Woolwich.
MICHAEL COURTNEY. I live at 4, Crawford Street, Tidal Basin, with
the prosecutor -I am a coal porter on December 23rd I saw Gordon
outside the Three Daws, bleeding from his mouth-I asked him what
was the matter-he said that Johnson had been kicking him about and
Johnson came up and hit me on my mouth, and kicked me, and as I was
getting up he kicked me just below the temple, and made a great mark.
Cross-examined by Brill. I am not the terror of Woolwich I have
been convicted of assaults, but these men are called the Woolwich Arabs,
and it is not safe for a man to go out with a shilling in his pocket.
