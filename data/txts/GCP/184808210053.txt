1860. WILLIAM HARRIS, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 2a. 6d. ; the

CHARLES EDWARD STRUTT. I live at Chelsea. On the afternoon of the
29th July I was walking on Holborn-bill-I felt some one at my pocket-I
turned, and saw the prisoner close behind me, and no one else-my band-
kerchief was on the ground, between us--I took it up the prisoner ran
sway I ran after him-another man caught him, but I did not lose sight of
him-- he said it was not him. GUILTY. Aged 15.-Confined Six Months
