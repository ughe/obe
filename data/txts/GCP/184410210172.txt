Thomas Dice. I am a boot and shoemaker, and live at Clapham. On the
night of the 15th of Oct. I was sitting at work, and heard a noise outside
about eight o'clock-I got up and went outside- I went towards the persons
who were making the noise, but I did not go close-I saw the prisoner with
something in his hand, and he said to Wheeler, “I will break your head
this night before I leave you"-Wheeler said, “Don't you let me catch you,
my lad," and they all ran off-I did not see any more-the prisoner ran be-
fore Wheeler, and soon after they had ran I heard Wheeler groan-I was
going to his assistance, but I saw plenty of people round him, and I did not
go any further.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you see other boys running after Wheeler ? A.
Yes, there were other boys running up Clapham, and they said, “ Give it to
the be g ive it him"-I cannot tell how far those other boys were in the
rear of Wheeler-I know the prisoner's parents as neighbours- I never knew
anything against them or him.
COURT. Q. What is this bar for ? A. To prevent carts and horses going
through-it is like a gate, and it is kept locked--a person can get under it
by stooping very low-the boys get under it easily-they stoop and run under
it easily, they are so used to it.
THOMAS Bent (police-constable V 95.) I apprehended the prisoner about
balf-past eight o'clock the same evening- I told him Wheeler charged him
with throwing a stone at him-he said, “No, I know I had a stone in my
hand, but I did not throw it at bim"-he said, "I know who did do it"-I
took him to the station.
John PARRATT. I am a surgeon. I attended Wheeler-he had a com-
pound fracture of the bones of the nose, and a contusion of the left eye-
there was a slight laceration of the angle of the eye-it was a bad hurt-it was
such a one as would be inflicted by a stone or by a fall-I have attended him
since-his eye is still inflamed, and there has been a great deal of blood in-
fused-he could not open the eye for some days.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty of a common Assault. Aged 14.- Recommended to mercy by the Jury.
-Confined Fourteen Days.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, THE 25TH OF NOVEMBER.
