SAMUEL FINcH. I live in Canterbury-road Newington. In June last, I
was desirous of emigrating to Australia-I was acquainted with a person
named Waterford, who was going to emigrate, and in consequence of some-
thing he told me, I went to 6, Austin-friars-] do not recollect the day, but I
should say somewhere about 13th or 14th June; it was 10th July I was to
sail--I only saw a couple of youths at Austin-friars, but I saw Tripe the
same day at Captain Tomlins's, 68, Cornhill-Mr. Vallance, a solicitor, in
Essex-street, had money of mine to pay the passage--I told Tripe if he
would go up to Mr. Vallance he could receive the money for my passagemit
was not mentioned then what ship I was to go by--I did not go with Tripe
to Mr. Vallance's- did not pay the money; Tripe told me he had received
it-| saw him write this receipt, and believe this letter (produced) to be in
his writing-after I had read it, I saw him on the subject of it-(read)
Australian Gold and Emigration Company, 6, Austin-friars, June 23rd,
1852-Dear Sir, I beg to inform you that our ship, which sails on 15th July,
(the Camilla). is now in the West India Import Doek, which you can go and
see; she as just returned from the colony in ninety-one days, and a very
fine ship; I am, dear Sir, your obedient servant, C. J. Tripe, superintendent
of shipping"-afte I received that letter, I saw Mr. Montague in Austin-
friers-) asked him if I could get any passengers what he would allow me
I spoke to him and Tripe on that subject in what was considered the board
room, and took the letter I had received from Tripe with me-I did not show
it to Montague, but I spoke to him ob the subject-I asked him whether if
I could get them some passengers, as I was going by their vessel, what they
would allow me by way of commission--Mr Tripe appealed to Montague, and
asked what they could allow, and he said they could allow me 14. per head--I
did not succeed in getting any; the affair blew up soon after had received
a sort of printed receipt from Tripe, this is it (produced)-this is not for
