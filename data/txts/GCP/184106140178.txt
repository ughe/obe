John ATCHELOR. I am a horse-slaughterer. On Friday evening, the
21st of May, the prisoner brought a cow to my yard - I asked whether he
wanted me or Mr. Lansdowne-he said, No, he did not want Lansdowne,
he had orders to take it to the knacker's-yard--he said his governor was
coming-it rained very hard, and he waited for three hours-the cow was
not fit for a butcher-it was in a dreadful state-he said he knew what it
cost, and what he was to sell it for, if he could get a customer on the road,
but he did not meet a customer on the road, and so he brought it to my yard
he said he wanted 359, for it-I said I would not give any such money as
that for it, it was not worth it, I would give him 30s.-he said he did not
know whether it was right or not, would I return the cow in the morning
if his governor did not like to take the money-I said I would gave
him 30s. and he then left-the cow was in my place about a fortnight, and
then died-it was a complete bag of bones- I was not at home when the
prosecutor came and saw it-I boiled the carcase for the canine.

a different business to mine.
