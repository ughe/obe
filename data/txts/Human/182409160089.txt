 NEW COURT. (6th Day.)
 Middlesex Cases, Fourth Jury, Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1473.  WILLIAM PARRY was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of August , a handkerchief, value 3 s., the goods of Richard Attenborough.
   WILLIAM HAYES. I live with Mr. Richard Attenborough, pawnbroker, Sun-street. On the 2d of August, about half-past five o'clock in the evening, I observed the prisoner looking at some handkerchiefs which were pinned on a line. I went out, and came back in about a minute; he was then looking at some others, and asked the price of one. I told him 3 s. 6 d., but agreed to take 3 s. for one; and while he was buying it I observed that he did not use his left arm as he did his right - I then took the money, and laid hold of his left arm, and found this handkerchief under his arm, his coat was buttoned. I charged him with stealing it, he said he had found it.
   THOMAS VANN. I took him into custody.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that he found the handkerchief on the floor.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Six Weeks.
 1474.  JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of September , a printed bound book, value 18 d., the goods of Sarah Durham.
   SARAH DURHAM. I live at Stepney; I was in my room adjoining the shop on the 11th of September, and heard the moving of halfpence. I went into the shop, and saw the prisoner with his hand in my till; I said,
 "Here is a man robbing me:" he went away - I was much terrified, and made way for him to pass. I followed him two doors, and then could follow him no further: he was pursued, and brought in less than five minutes, and a book was found in his hat, which I had seen that day, and had not sold it.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 J. J. DOWNES. I saw the prisoner caught by a stranger, who gave him into my custody in the shop - I found the property on him.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined Six Weeks.
 1475.  GEORGE ELSEY was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of September , four table-cloths, value 5 s., the goods of George Robinson and Edward Robinson.
   HENRY MOSS. I am a porter to Messrs. George and Edward Robinson. Last Friday I went to fetch a pitcher of water; on my return I met the prisoner a yard from the door with these table-cloths in his apron. I collared him, and said he had something that did not belong to him. I put him into the passage - he said he was very sorry, and hoped I would forgive him. Another man came in, and wanted a ham bone; I told him we had not one - he said the prisoner was a poor man, and hoped I would be kind to him.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   JOHN SCOTT. I took the prisoner into custody.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. Transported for Seven Years.
 1476.  WILLIAM CARTER, EDWARD HARRIS, and CHARLES JONES were indicted for stealing, on the 11th of August two pairs of shoes, value 3 s., the goods of George Ferguson.
   GEORGE FERGUSON. I am a haberdasher, and live in Ratcliff-highway. I had some shoes out at my door on the 11th of August, and next morning I missed two pair.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   JAMES FOGG. I am a Thames police-officer; I saw the three prisoners watching about Mr. Ferguson's shop; I stood opposite them on the other side of the way; I was called aside by a gentleman, and while I turned my head, I missed them, and saw them going up the Highway; Carter was putting one of the shoes under his jacket; I followed till I got some person to assist in taking them; I stopped Carter and found one shoe on him, and at the watch-house I found three shoes on Harris; I asked Carter where he got the shoe, he said his father gave it him, he was a shoe-maker, in Rosemary-lane; I said I would go to his father; be then said he had no father, and that Jones gave him the shoe; Jones denied it at first, but at last said he had, and would not have taken them if he had not been hungry.
 CARTER'S Defence. We were standing round a pump, and Jones came and asked if there was any ladle; Jones was not near us at the time.
 CARTER -    GUILTY. Aged 14. Confined Three Months.
 HARRIS -    GUILTY. Aged 15. Confined Two Months.
 JONES -    GUILTY. Aged 14. Whipped, and discharged.
