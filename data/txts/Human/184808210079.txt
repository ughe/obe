1894. JOSEPH SUGG, and HENRY O'NEAL , stealing 1 rug, and 1 looking-glass, value 15 s  .; the property of John Adams , their master. JOHN ADAMS. I am a baker, of High-street, Camden-town—O'Neal has been in my service some time—I missed a hearth-rug—I called Sugg into the room, and was going with him to the pawnbrokers—I said nothing to him about the rug—he said, "It is about the hearth-rug, I hope you will for give me"—the pawnbroker said, "That is the young man who pledged the hearth-rug"—going back, I asked him how he could serve me so—he said "Henry gave them me, and we divided the money"—I asked O'Neal how he could give that man the hearth-rug—he said, "I did not give it him"—I gave them in charge. JAMES WEBB( policeman ). I produce two duplicates, found in O'Neal's room, for a hearth-rug and looking-glass. BENJAMIN JONES. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a corresponding dupli- cate to this one for the looking-glass. EDWARD ROBERTS. I produce the corresponding duplicate for this hearth-rug.
   SUGG  — GUILTY. Aged 56.— Confined Six Months .
   O'NEAL  — GUILTY. Aged 26.— Confined One Year .
 (There were two other indictments against the prisoners.)
 1895. JAMES MEAGAN , stealing 2lbs. of bread, value 3 d  .; the property of Elizabeth Coutts , and another; having been before convicted. HENRY DONIMORE. I live with Mr. Bailey, a grocer. On 24th July I saw the prisoner take a loaf from a baker's barrow in the street. ROBERT WILLIAM ESAU. I examined the loaf that was found—it is Elizabeth and Ann Coutts's property. CHARLES AUSTIN. I saw the prisoner throw something away, and run away—the loaf was picked up at that spot. ALFRED LINDSEY. I was present at the prisoner's trial, and produce a certificate—( read—Convicted Jan . 1847, and confined three months. ) GUILTY. ** Aged 16.— Confined Six Months .
 1896. WILLIAM ALLEN , stealing 1 waistcoat, and 72 buttons, value 9 s  .; the property of Henry Lewis Cæsar Langrish . HENRY LEWIS CæSAR LANGRISH. I keep a ready-made clothes shop, in St. John-street. I received information, followed the prisoner, gave him in charge, and found my waistcoat under his—he said his mother told him to bring a waistcoat to show her—we met his mother, before she knew anything about it; she said "I told him to bring home a waistcoat to show me"—half a gross of buttons were found at her house belonging to me. GUILTY  . Aged 15.— Recommended to mercy. Confined for Seven Days , and Whipped.
 1897. JOSEPH FEFUS , stealing 1 bag, value 1 d  .; and 15 l  . 9 s  . in silver; the property of John Sherran , in his dwelling-house. JOHN SHERRAN. I keep the Blue Anchor, at Ratcliff—the prisoner came to me for a lodging—I missed a bag containing crowns, haif-crowns, six pences and groats—I asked the prisoner if he had been in my bedroom, and how he liked it—he said he did not know what part of the house my bed-room was in—I took him there, and said "I am confident you have been here, and what have you done with the bag of money, have you spent it?"—he said no; that he had taken it and hid it—I asked where—he said, "Under neath a large tavern, at London-bridge"'—I went with him and found it there. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Recommended to mercy.— Confined One Month .
