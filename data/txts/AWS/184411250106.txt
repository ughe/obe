72. JOHN KENT was indicted for stealing 5 wooden fellies, value
78. 6d. the goods of Robert Webb and another,
WILLtam EvE. I am the son of Charles Eve. I was lying in Mr.
Webb's ground, between twelve and one o'clock, on the 24th of Oet., and
saw the prisoner come in-he looked round, but could not see me-he
went out again, to see if anybody was comingwhe came in again, went
to the end of the bench, and took a felly, and pushed it through the
fence-he then fetehed another, and put that throughhe then walked
out, to see if anybody else was comingth then walked in, took another
from a stack, put that through, and then another-1 took five in all
-I am sure the prisoner is the man.
Cross-examined by Ma. WILkins. Q. Your father is foreman of these
premises1 A. Yes-1 have worked in the rick-fields for nearly two years
-when I do not work in the fields; I am at home, and do nothing was
in custody once-l had got ttwo quartern loaves in my brother's name at a
shop--I gave some of them to a boy-I have plenty to eat at bome-I
do not remember about any pillowaI had a coat and sold it, but it
was my own--I left it at a baker's shop-I went and fetched it, and a
woman down Kingsland sold it-my father had bought it-it was not my
best coat-I took the mopey and spent it was before the Magistrate
about the bread-my father took me before the superintendent of Police-
I have sometimes slept away from home-I have slept under a shed, and
in another place in the yard, which is about twenty yards from my father's
house-] do not know how many times I have slept in the shed-I am
fourteen years old-g boy named Catling slept with me in the shed once-
Catling has been in prison-i I had gone some, I had a comfortable bed
at my father's house.
CHARLES Eve. I am foreman to Robert and William Webb. I know
their yard. On the 24th of October, about twelve o'clock in the day, my
son gaye me some information, and I went to look after five fellies-I
found them shoved in a hole where an old piece of brick wall had been
taken out-they were Messrs. Webb's property-I asked the prisoner if
he had been in the yard-he said he had never been in the yard, but the
coachman had met him coming out.
Cross-examined. Q. Have you had a good deal of trouble with your
son ?
A. have-I have taken a great deal of pains to make him
a good boy, but I have not been able-he is in the habit of telling lies
more than I am aware of-I have had him chained up for three weeks
together-) let him loose to come here.
