1233. JOHN PRICE was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of July , a necklace, value 3 s., and a piece of silver coin, value 4 d., the property of John Harris , from the pro- person of Sarah Harris , an infant.
   HANNAH HARRIS. I am the wife of John Harris; we live in Ogle-street, Mary-le-bone. On the 3d of July, I was in Cleveland-street with my infant; about half-past six o'clock in the evening, looking at a fire, my child had a necklace round her neck, with a coin fastened to it. I saw the prisoner, with two or three others, following and looking at me, so that I went to a doorway to get from them. I noticed the prisoner particularly; he crept behind the child and touched my shoulder - I turned round, and he was in the act of unclasping the necklace. I said,
 "You villain, are you taking my child's necklace" - he made no answer, but gave it a pull and got it - he snatched a piece of flesh off the child's neck, and gave the necklace to another man. I caught hold of him immediately, and held him; he was taken into a baker's shop, and the mob broke the shop window to get him out; but he was detained, and put into the watch-house.
 Prisoner. Q. Did I not come out of the court with other people - A. No one was in the court.
   CHARLOTTE ADNAM. I stood close to the prosecutrix - I knew her before - she called out - I saw the prisoner give the necklace to another man; she laid hold of him, and I ran for her husband. I am certain he is the man.
 Prisoner's Defence. I stood in Hampstead-road with my basket; a gentleman bought some barley-sugar, and told me to take it to Cleveland-street; seeing a mob, I went down there and stood by a baker's shop; this woman came out of the court, and said,
 "You have got my child's beads." I had my hands in my pocket, and said,
 "If you think so, search me."
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined One Year.
 1234.  JOSEPH WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of September , a watch, value 30 s., the goods of John Honghton.
   KETORAH HOUGHTON. I am the wife of John Haughton. On the 10th of September, about half-past ten o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner coming out of the area, and tapped at the parlour window twice, and asked him what he wanted; he made no answer. I flew to the door and called out Stop that man! - he was stopped and brought back. I went into the kitchen and missed my watch, which was there three minutes before. It was not found on him. Smith produced it; he had no business in the area.
   ANN SMITH. I was standing by the top of my place when the prisoner ran by me. The prosecutrix called out Stop that man! I saw him taken and brought back. I saw him take something from his pocket, and lay it on the window cill. I immediately said,
 "Let us see what he has put there," and found it was the watch.
   BENJAMIN PARROTT. I received him in charge with the watch.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I went down the area after a young man, whom I knew. I found it was the wrong place, and came up again. This woman called Stop thief! and I came back immediately.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 1235.  THOMAS THOMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of August , a handkerchief, value 2 s., the goods of William Brown , from his person.
   WILLIAM BROWN. I am a half-pay captain. On the 20th of August, about a quarter before nine o'clock, I was walking in Gower-street, and turning into Bedford-square, I felt somebody at my pocket - turned round, and found the prisoner with my handkerchief in his hand. I took him across the square, and met Leonard, who took him - he had got it out of my pocket.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 13. Transported for Seven Years.
 1236.  ELIZABETH SHEEN was indicted for stealing on the 28th of July , a set of bagpipes, value 2 l. 10 s., the goods of Martin Flannagan.
   MARTIN FLANNAGAN. I play the bagpipes. On the 28th of July, about seven o'clock in the evening, I was at Kennedy's public-house, in St. James's-street, playing. I tied my bagpipes up in a bag, and put them on the table, and went into the yard for a minute or two, and on returning they were gone. I had left the prisoner in the room. She was also gone; I gave an alarm, and have never found them. She came into the house half an hour after; I asked her for them, she said she did not take them. I did not detain her - but she was apprehended afterwards.
   ANN CRAWLEY. I was at the public-house, and saw the prosecutor there. There was a green parcel on the table; I did not know what it was; I saw the prisoner take it off the table and go out; but whether she took it out, I do not know; I mentioned it five or six days after. When I found Flannagan had lost his pipes, I told the landlady of it the same night; I was never taken up for it. I never saw her before.
   JOHN GREEN. I am beadle. I apprehended the prisoner - I took Crawley to appear against her; she was not charged with the theft.
   MARTIN FLANNAGAN. I do not remember seeing Crawley there.
 Prisoner's Defence. This woman tells falsehoods; she sleept at my place every night.
   ANN CRAWLEY. We both live in one house, but are not related.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1237.  JANE PARKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of August , a silver spoon, value 10 s. the goods of Thomas Castle.
   THOMAS CASTLE. I am a straw-hat manufacturer, and live in Church-street, Kensington. The prisoner lived servant with a person on the first floor, where I lodge; I have the ground-floor for a shop. I missed a table spoon on the 3d of August, and found it in pawn; nine or ten days after, I took the prisoner, which was on the Wednesday after, I missed it; she denied ever seeing it. I found it in pawn - she had no business in my room.
   JAMES BARNES. I am shopman to Messrs. Graham and Co., of Tothill-street. I have a spoon pawned by the prisoner, in the name of Mary Castle.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner pleaded Distress.
 GUILTY. Aged 19. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Two Months.
