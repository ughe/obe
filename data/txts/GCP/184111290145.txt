MATTHEW MAWson. I am under-butler to Lady Essex, and deal with
the prosecutor-the prisoner delivered bread to me. On the 30th of Oe-
tober I paid him, on the prosecutor's account, 18d.; on the 6th of Novem-
ber, 18d.; and on the 13th of November, 1s. 10 d., for bread I had re-
ceived from him.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. You do not know that it was the
30th of October, do you? 4. Yes I have recollected it since I was be-
fore the Magistrate-I paid the prisoner money every Saturday for the
Tast three months--I generally paid on Saturday-I sometimes paid on
Monday, when I was out on Saturday-he has been in the habit of leaving
bread at the house for six months, I should say, but I did not pay him
every Saturday all that time, only since Lady Essex has been out of town,
which was in September-I paid him from 18d. to 28. every Saturday-I
am quite sure I paid him on the three days in question-either I or one
of the servants took the bread in, but no one but me paid the money-I
am quite sure I paid it with my own hand.
John Mulwoo. I am clerk to the prosecutor, I keep an account of
the receipts of monies, and always know what is paid-the sums paid are
entered in this cash-book-the parties who carry out bread and receive
money do not give their accounts to me-it is accounted to me by my
master, who receives the money-(referring to the book) here is no entry
of 18. 6d. received by the prosecutor from Mawson on the 30th of October
-on the 6th of November, here is 18. 73. entered as received from Maw-
son---these entries are made by me.
Court. Here is an entry on the 30th of October, “Essex, Servant,
1s. 114d.; ditto, 1s. 7d.;" on the 6th of November, “ Essex, Servant,
1s. 70.;" and on the 13th of November there does not appear any entry.
Witness. Those entries are for money received for former weeks.
MATTHEW Mawson re-examined. Mrs. Langford, another servant of
Lady Essex, also has bread of the prosecutor-we are on board-wages, and
pay our own bills,
Court to Mrs. Klos. Q. Here is a larger sum entered on the 30th
of October than you charge him with embezzling? A. The reason of that
is, he left more bread with the servant than he accounted for at home, and
at a less price than we allowed, and that makes the difference in the money.
John Mulwoo re-examined. This entry certainly relates to former weeks
--it cannot relate to the week for which the bill had been made out-il he

week--if he received 1s. 2 d. on the 30th of October, he ought to account
