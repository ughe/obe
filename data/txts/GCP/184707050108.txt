1669. THOMAS SMITH was indicted for feloniously cutting and wound
ing Catherine Oliver on the head, with intent to do her some grievous bodily
harm.
CATHERINE OLIVER. I am a widow, and live in Ebenezer-place, Bunbill.
row. The prisoner lives with my daughter, Mary Montgomery-on the 28
of June, about ten o'clock in the morning, I went to the prisoner's house-
was not at home-I found my daughter there-I went away, and I went again
in the afternoon-when I got close to the house I heard my daughter cry out
- I went in, and found the prisoner and her quarrelling-he had her throttled
- I went to her assistance he had got hold of her by the neck, I cannot
exactly say whether with his hands or with anything else- I got her away
from him as well as I could, and put her into the passage-he told me seveni
times to let her go-after I had got my daughter away, the prisoner turne
round, and said, "You b y old cow, if I don't have satisfaction of her, I
will of you"- he had something in bis hand, but, in the height of my passion,
I cannot tell whether it was a stool or not-be had a stool in his hand, but
whether it was with that, or anything else, I know not, but he struck me on
the temple- I was taken to the doctor's, and then to the hospital - I remained
there from Monday evening till the Friday following-I had not struck him,
or done anything besides getting bim away from my daughter-I had said
nothing to him-I never had an angry word with him-I have known bin
twenty years, he is a very hasty young man-he had been living with my
daughter ten months-he was a little intoxicated.
MARY MONTGOMERY. I am the daughter of the last witness--I am married,
but I live with the prisoner-my husband lives with a female, a servant. On the
morning of the 28th of June the prisoner came home-- I was sober-he was
very drunk-he began to kick up a piece of work-he struck me with his fist
-my mother came in-she was not sober-she came to the door as he was
punching me he was very tipsy, and did not know what he was doing-my
mother interfered, and tried to save me--he turned round, and struck my
mother with a stool.
JAMES WHITE (police-constable H 210.) I took the prisoner into custody
-Mrs. Oliver was seen by the surgeon.

- it had been strapped up by a surgeon-she was suffering from slight come

Prisoner. I am very sorry; I will never do so any more.
