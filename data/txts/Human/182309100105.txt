 Prisoner LEVY. Q. What time was it - A. Between twenty minutes and a quarter past four o'clock.
   THOMAS HARRISON. I am shopman to Mr. Harris. About a quarter past four o'clock, Hanisel came in, and asked to look at some beads - I shewed him one row, which did not suit; he went out, and pointed to a particular row in the window, then came in, and I shewed them to him - he said he thought they would do, and he would go and fetch his woman; he did not ask about any watch. I saw nobody at the window. He went away, and in about a minute I missed the watch. I went out, but he was gone; he was brought to the shop next day, about two o'clock - I am certain of his person. Levy was charged with receiving it, and denied it at first; the girl said he was outside the window - he said it was false. Hanisel afterwards shewed me how he took it; he said he put his handkerchief over it, and took it up in it, and took it outside to Levy, who said at last, that he had bought it of him for 30 s.
   THOMAS HARRISON. I am a constable, and took Hanisel - he said Levy only gave him 8 s. for it.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 HANISEL'S Defence. (Through an Intrepreter.) I lodge at Levy's. Brant tied my hands together, and beat me, and I confessed that I took it - distress and hunger drove me to it. He beat me, and made me say I sold it to Levy.
   HENRY BRANT. I did not beat him; he kicked me, and I gave him a smack in the face.
 LEVY'S Defence. The prisoner came to me on Tuesday night, in new clothes, and said he had sold his watch for 28 s., in Rosemary-lane. When the officer came, I said I knew the person who bought it of him, and took them to the man, who delivered it up.

 LEVY -    GUILTY. Aged 55. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1255.  WILLIAM WEAL was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of September , a cage, value 5 s., the goods of William Craghill.
   WILLIAM CRAGHILL. I keep  the Greyhound, public-house, in Old-street. My cage hung in the parlour, with a bird in it. I saw it safe twenty minutes before it was stolen - the window was open - it hung in the centre of the room - they must have got into the room to take it - the prisoner was brought back with it ten minutes after - he had a handkerchief over it, which he claimed.
   HENRY HERBERT. I stood opposite the side of the house. I was informed a man had come out of the window. I looked and saw the prisoner twenty yards off with the cage; he threw it down when I got near him. I brought him back.
   JOSEPH HARDING. I saw him come out of the window; had another boy with him, who was sitting on these at under the window. I saw the cage on the table in the parlour. I afterwards saw him throw it down, and picked it up.
   THOMAS BENNETT. I am a constable. The cage was delivered to me; he claimed the handkerchief.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Six Months.
 1256.  ELIZABETH WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of August , 5 lbs. of bacon, value 3 s., the goods of Thomas Peters.
   JOHN OXMAN. On the 9th of August, I was servant to Mr. Peters, cheesemonger, Whitecross-street. On Saturday night, about eleven o'clock, somebody told me this bacon was taken from the window. I ran out and saw the prisoner putting it into her apron, and I secured her with it. She keeps a bad house. The watchman found another piece in her apron, which she also said she took from the window.
   JOHN BROWN. I am a watchman. I found another piece in her apron.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 43. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
