FULGENCE PIGARCHE. I am partner in the house of Veco, lace manu-
facturers, at Paris. In June, 1841, I was living at Wright's botel, in
London-I had a quantity of lace with me-in the beginning of June I had
occasion to send a box of lace and other things, worth about 1501., to the
West-end-I employed a porter to carry it-I went there, and found
neither the porter or the lace had arrived-amongst the contents of the box
was this lace shawl, and these other things--the amount of the property
here is 401. or 501.-the shawl is spoiled.
HENRY LAMBERT. In October last I was a jeweller, living in Cheap-
side. On Sunday, the 3rd of October, my premises were entered, and
nearly the whole of my property, from 7001. to 9001. worth taken away
four or five of these articles I can positively swear to as mine--they are
worth about 25l. or 301., and some others I believe to be mine from
having similar.

2525. EDWARD GIFFORD and SARAH ANN HUNT were again
indicted for feloniously receiving, on the 28th of February, 8 lace shawls,
value 13l. ; 26 lappets, value 81. ; 19 berthes, value 81. ; 81 capes, value
211.; 83 cambric caps, value 351.; 11 lace dresses, value 291.; 27 falls,
value 101.; 28 veils, value 191. ; 12 collars, value 41. ; 38 scarfs, value 301.;
and 10 muslin collars, value 41.; the goods of Samuel Lambert; well
knowing them to have been stolen ; against the statute, &c.; to which
GIFFORD pleaded GUILTY. Aged 33.-Transported for Fourteen Years.
MR. CHAMBERS conducted the Prosecution.
FREDERICK HERBERT HEMMING. I am agent to Samuel Lambert, a
lace-manufacturer-he has a place of business in Aldermanbury. In Feb-
ruary last I packed up a quantity of lace to go to the country-I delivered
it to Beale, a porter, to take to the Swan-with-Two-Necks-I can swear
to a great part of these things found, as being part of them.
RICHARD BEALE. I am a porter. I took this box of lace to the Swan-
with-Two-Necks--while I was engaged in putting up a carpet-bag, the box
of lace was stolen.
