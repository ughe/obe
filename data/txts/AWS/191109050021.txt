EDWARD TROTT, carman to James Garwood, 51, Randell Street,
e
Tidal Basin. On August 9 I was driving a van in which were three
kegs of oil to the Docks, I was accompanied by Mr. Garwood, prisoner,
and his son. On reaching Sparrow Court, about 200 strikers stopped
the van. A shot was fired from behind me over my left shoulder.
Cross-examined. We were taking the oil to a ship that was waiting
to sail. Prisoner had asked for a police escort, but the men could
not be spared. The strikers turned the van right round; they
threatened to throw glasses at us, and I heard shouts of 46 Out him 1
and Turn it over; and, when I was at the horses' head, Limb
him." The van was eventually turned over, and the oil was stolen.
Before the shot was fired I heard prisoner say Let the horse's head
go"; I did not hear him say anything about firing.
Re-examined. The van was overturned after the shot was fired,
but the threats eame before.
