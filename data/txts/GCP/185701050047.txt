WILLIAM FORD. I am employed at Mr. Benham's. On the night of
10th Dec., when I left, I left a drawer, which I use as a till, locked, and
about 298. in it-there was one sovereign, and 98. in silver on the following
morning I found the drawer had been forced open by some instrument, and
the money gone.
THOMAS LAKIN. I am employed at Mr. Benham's. When I left on
10th Dec., I left a drawer there locked, with 41. in gold in it-next morning
the drawer was broken open, and the money gone.
EDWARD CUNNINGHAM. I am employed at Mr. Benham's. I have a desk
there where I put money-on the night of 10th Deo, when I went away, I
left there 108. 7d. in silver, and seven penny pieces--it was not locked
when I came in the morning the 108. was gone, and the coppers left I saw
a shilling at the station house, which I certainly believe to be a shilling
that was in my drawer on the night of 10th Dec.--I noticed it when a
porter brought it to me--a piece was knocked out of the edge of it, and it
was rather black on the right hand side of the woman I certainly believe
it to be the one, but I did not swear to it at the police court-I think this
(produced) is the shilling.
