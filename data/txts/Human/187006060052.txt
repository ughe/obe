SAMUEL EDWARDS. JAMES SPRULES. I live at 31, Newton Terrace, Notting Hill, and am in the service of Mr. Baker, a butcher—I know the three prisoners—they lived at 15, Newton Terrace; I have seen them all there from time to time—I was present when the two male prisoners were taken into custody, at 2, Carlton Terrace, Bow—I knew them at Newton Terrace, in the names of Rumbelow and Edwards, and the female prisoner as Mrs. Edwards, and there was Mrs. Rumbelow, who js not in custody—15, Newton Terrace was a shop—it was never opened—they were there about a fortnight—we served them with meat rather more than a week—when they were taken into custody at Bow, Rumbelow said his name was Musk—there win place of business there with "Musk" put up—the female prisoner took the meat in at Newton Terrace.
