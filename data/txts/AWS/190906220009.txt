The Recorder said he had considered the question of costs. The

Mr. Lionel Benson said he was instructed that prisoner had
means to pay these costs, and asked that the case should stand out for
three days to enable him to pay them.
The Recorder said if the costs were not paid at once they could be
recovered in the same way as anything else, by execution. He had
no power to direct that Major MeKay should be imprisoned if the
costs were not paid.
Mr. Lionel Benson. I am instructed that he will never pay the
costs at all.
The Recorder. I cannot help that. I have nothing to do with
the drawing of the Act of Parliament. I have only got to adminis-
ter it.
Sentenee, One day's imprisonment, entitling prisoner to immediate
discharge.
VINCENT, Horace Samuel (29, agent) indicted for forging and
uttering, knowing the same to be forged, divers orders for the pay:
ment of money, to wit, cheques and orders for the several sums of
E9C 15s. 5d., e90 18s. 4d., E90 18s. 4d., and 77 12s. 6d., in each
case with intent to defraud; and forging and uttering, knowing the
same to be forged, the several endorsements on the said orders for
the payment of money and feloniously stealing the said cheques, the
goods of Robert Sehult and another, his masters; pleaded guilty to
stealing three cheques amounting together to E259 6s. 3d. Mr. Cecil
Fitch, for the prosecution, accepted the plea.
Prisoner was employed by a firm of West India merchants and was
entrusted with the duty of drawing cheques to corresponding invoices.
These cheques had to be signed either by a partner of the firm or two
head elerks. Prisoner drew a cheque for, say, E9 18s. 4d., and, after
hn had got it signed, altered the nine" in the written amount to
ninety and added "0" after the "9." He disposed of the cheques
by opening an account in the name of one of the firm's customers, at
the Birkbeck Bank. After the cheques were returned he abstracted
them so that they could not be traced. Altogether the defaleations
amounted to E533. When he found in November, 1908, that the
game was up he abseonded, and on April 29 was sentenced to six
months' hard labour at Nottingham for attempting to obtain 2100 by
false pretenees. Upon his identity being discovered a warrant was
sent down from the Home Office.
