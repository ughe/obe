BENJAMIN ROSENTHAL (prisoner, on oath). I carry on business
with my brother Isaac at Hanbury Street, Spitalfields. I have
known Gershon Warshawsky for some time, and have always found
him & respectable man. I have known nothing against him. I also
know his son, who left some samples of cloth at our place on Feb-
ruary 24 last. It was a job line. I was not there when the price
was discussed. I took the samples round to Russell and Co., with
whom we deal. I asked & price & little above the price in the
invoice. It was 3s. 1d., I think. They offered me & price which
I accepted. The cloth was then brought round by the younger
Warshawsky. He brought a paper with him with the measure
ments on it. This paper is copied off from his paper, with the
measurements and the lengths. There was a torn piece, which was
a bit of paper knocking about on the floor, and it was picked up to
put the measurements down (Exhibit 10). He handed the invoice
