   Cross-examined.  The gas was turned down but not put out—I lad taken a glass or two, and I had had two bottles of soda-water at different places—I took another woman, who was at the station-house door, into the station, and told her I thought she belonged to the party, but when I came to look at her I did not charge her—the Alderman at Guildhall said that she ought to have been detained, as she might have had the watch, or something of that sort. ARTHUR BARTON( City Policeman  889). About a quarter to one on the morning of 19th September Mr. Tatton spoke to me, and I went into one of the compartments and found the bow of a watch ( produced ).
   Cross-examined.  The prosecutor says that it is his—it is hall-marked. JOHN MINES. I am the prosecutor's potman—I asked some people to leave the compartment about 12.30, but they did not, and my master went to them—I saw him dragged into the street, holding the prisoner—I heard him say that he had lost his watch—I ran for a policeman, and as 1 came back I saw the prisoner running and stopped her—my master gave her in charge. CHARLES KING( City Policeman  933). I took the prisoner—the charge was read to her at the station, and she said "I know nothing about the watch—I never had it." GUILTY.  797. EUGENE JOHNSON, Stealing 18 s  . 7 d  ., the moneys of the churchwardens of St. Ann's Church, "Westminster , and afterwards break ing out of the church.
   MR. GILL conducted the Prosecution. RICHARD WILEY( Policeman C  250). On Saturday morning, 22nd Sep tember, I was in Dean Street, and saw the prisoner come out of St. Ann's Church, close the door after him, and walk away—I followed him and he ran—I ran after him—he got twenty yards in advance of me—I sprang my rattle, and another constable came towards me—before I got up to the prisoner he threw away this piece of steel ( produced )—I took him in custody, and at the station he tried to throw away this other piece of steel—both pieces have some sticky substance still attached to them—he said that he picked the steel up on the church steps—I found on him six florins, six shillings, a sixpence, and two halfpence—they have a sticky substance on them, and have evidently been wiped with this duster, which I found on him.
