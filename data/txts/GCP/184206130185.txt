1917. JOHN DOWSETT, alias Frederick Edwards, was indicted for
stealing, on the 30th of March, 1 spoon, value 108., the goods of William
Puncher, his master.
WILLỊAM PUNCHER. I keep the Red Lion, in Parliament-street, West-
minster--the prisoner was my pot-boy. In March, 1841, I lost this spoon.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. This occurred fifteen or six-
teen months ago, at the time the prisoner left you ? A. Yes-he was
taken a few days ago--I have no desire to press against him.
CHABLES MUNDAY. I was in the service of the prosecutor. I missed
this spoon directly after the prisoner had left-I took him the day after he
left, and found the spoon at his mother's house, but he ran from me-1
do not know where he has been since.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury.-Confined
Two Months.
