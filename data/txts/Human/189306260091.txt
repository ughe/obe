   The prisoner, in his defence, stated that they were having supper, and the deceased grumbled that the meat and potatoes were bad, and that he ( the prisoner ) lost his temper, got up, and flung down his knife, and turned to go away, when he was told his brother was bleeding from the neck; that he then ran round the table and tried to stop the bleeding, and so got blood on his hand He declared that he did not intend to do him any harm when he flung down the knife, and they were always the best of friends.
