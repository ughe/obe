   Cross-examined by MR. CHARNOCK . Q.  Where did you know Buckley? A.  He kept a public-house on our division seven or eight years ago—I have not seen him for several years. CHARLES PATRICK CONNELLY re-examined.  I think I had seen Wigzell on one or two occasions previous to the robbery—on the day I missed my money I inquired of Brown where Buckley lived—I went, and found her in Wigzell's house, in Cross-court, Drury-lane—I did not go up stairs—Buckley and Wigzell came down stairs together, and spoke to me respecting this affair, saying they were very sorry I had lost so much money—from the exterior appearance, I should say the apartments were rented at 4 s . 6 d . or 5 s . a week. NOT GUILTY.
