MR. BORNIE conducted the Prosecution. CHRISTIAN RIGER. I live at 3, London-buildings, Whitecross-street, on the first-floor. The street floor is usually left open—the landlord does not live there—on 10th April I went out with my wife, about half-past seven o'clock—she fastened the door—we returned about twelve, and found the door broken open, and missed these pictures and other things—they are mine. ESTHER RIOBR. I went out with my husband, and fastened the door with a key—I came hack, and missed these things. CATHERINE PEARCE. I live in this house, on the ground-floor. On the night of 10th April, between eight and nine, I saw two boys come out of Mr. Riger's passage—one escaped—I laid hold of the prisoner, and said, "You have been robbing the place"—he said, "Oh, Mrs. Pearce, let me go, I will bring the things back"—I gave him to my husband. JOHN PEDRO. I am an iron-dealer, of 5, London-buildings. I took the prisoner from Mr. Pearce—he asked to be let go, and he would show where the things were—I accompanied him to 4, Broad-yard, and he pro duced these things. GUILTY.* Aged 14.— Confined Three Months , and twice Whipped.
 1016. WILLIAM MARCH and THOMAS SMART , stealing 1 handker chief, value Is.; the goods of John Cotterill , from his person. JOHN COTTERILL. On 16th March, about a quarter-past eleven o'clock, I stopped in Long-lane to look at a print-shop—a lady said something—I missed my handkerchief—it was safe not a minute before—some one pointed out the prisoners—an officer seized them, and took this handkerchief from March's pocket—( produced )—it is mine. MICHAEL HAYDON( City-policeman , 2 l ). I saw the prisoners together for a quarter of an hour previous—I saw March put his hand into Mr. Cotterill's pocket, draw something out, and put it into his left trowsers pocket—I laid hold of his hand; he took it out, and I took this handkerchief from it—Smart stood so as to conceal him from the view of persons passing—they had made several attempts alternately before that. JOHN SPITTLE( City-policeman , 9). I was with Haydon, and saw what he describes—both, prisoners were trying to pick pockets.
   Smart's Defence.  March came running up to me, and said he had found a handkerchief, and put it into his pocket
   MARCH  — GUILTY. Aged 18.
   SMART  — GUILTY. Confined One Year .
