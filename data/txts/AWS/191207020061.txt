I understod from my brother that Mr. Strong had some 21,500 worth
of machinery in his premise which was up for sale. After calling
three times I saw him and told him that I understood his case was in
bankruptey and he told me it was. I looked over the machinery to
value it; I never gave any name or eard and I did not ask him for his
Lavington's address; I never saw Lavington until at the police court.
The endorsement on cheque (Exhibit 5) is not mine. In collecting
these moneys I fully believed that Downs was the trustee in bank-
ruptey. On March 22 he went with me to Busby's yard and was intro-
duced to his foreman. I have tried to find both him and Beckmann but
have not sueceeded in doing so.
