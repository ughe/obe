1590. JAMES WHITE and JANE BELL, stealing 4 salteellars, 10
smelling-bottles, and other articles, value 10s. the goods of James Anderson,
the master of White to which
WHITE pleaded GUILTY.
JAMEs ANDERSON. I am a glass-manufacturer, of Montague-street, White-
chapel. White was in my service-on the night of 19th July I watched with
a constable, and saw a person named Duval go down into the factory and
come up- spoke to her, and in consequence of what she said I gave White
in custody-I afterwards went to Bell's, and asked if she had anything of
mine-she hesitated, and said o"-afterwards she said she bad two or
three things, and showed me some decanter-stoppers, and one or two other
things in a cupboard-sh said Duval had given them to her-these are them
(produced)-they are mine.
JOHN POPLE (policeman). I went to Bell's, and told her Duval was
taken for stealing goods from Mr. Anderson, and asked if she had anything
belonging to him-she said she had not-we asked if she would allow us to
search the room-we did so, and found these things-she said they were
given her by Duval.
Bell's Defence. Duva made me a present of them; I asked if she got
them honestly, she said she had.
BELL-NOT GUILTY.
