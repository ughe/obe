685. JOHN THOMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of
January, I lamp, value 9$., the goods of William Wright.
WILLIAM W RIGHT. I am a broker, in King's-road—l lost this lamp
from within my doorway on the 25th of January—the policeman brought
it to the door with the prisoner in custody.
WILLIAM EDWARDS. I am a policeman. I met the prisoner in the
next street to the prosecutor's, and about two hundred yards from it—I
suspected, and followed him—he went to the prosecutor's shop—I saw a
boy, who was with him, pull the lamp out of the shop, and give it to the
prisoner—they both ran off together with it—the boy made his escape—I
took the prisoner with the lamp in his hand.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 34.—Confined Two Months.

SARAH NEALE. I am single, and live with Mrs. Traylors, at Ilanwell
—I missed a pair of stockings off the line by the side of the house—thae
are them—I know nothing of the prisoner.
CHARLES MILES. I live at Hanwell. On the 20th of January, at ten

nothing from there—there were footmarks on the snow, which were after•

particular—he lives at Southall.
JOHN DENTON. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner, and found this
pair of stockings on him—I matched the prisoner's shoes to the foot-marks,
and they tallied exactly.
