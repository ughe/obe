Kitty; she told me she had a parasol—I saw her sue it, nor the beads—she did not bring these things to me—she only told me she had got them, and then she said they were lost—the bundle was tied up with a cord, and sewn with some black thread—it was brought there on Sunday—the prisoner was to come again on Tuesday, but he came on Wednesday night, and was taken. BOYD SYLVESTER. I am an officer. I was called in, and found this parasol and beads in the bundle—the prosecutrix described the heads by the two knots, and the darn on the top of the parasol, before I opened it. JOHN NICHOLS( Police-constable K  88). I took the prisoner—he said the parasol and beads were his, he had brought them from Bomday.
   Cross-examined. Q.  When did he say this? A.  While I was taking him from Mrs. Mulvaney's to the station-house, about eight o'clock in the evening, on Wednesday.
   Prisoner  I did not tightly apprehend what I said when I went before the Magistrate—I took the parasol and the beads, but I did not exactly know what I was doing at the time—I had been on the spree  for a little time, and was a little groggy . NOT GUILTY.
 553. WILLIAM CALVERT was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of January , 4 handkerchiefs, value 16 s  ., the goods of Mary Redit and an other, and that he been before convicted of felony. HENRY PARNELL. I am assistant to Mary Redit and Emma Redit, Sidney-alley, Leicester-square, they are hosiers. On the evening of the 27th of January, the prisoner came into the shop—he asked if I had a handkerchief to match one which he brought in his hand—I showed him the nearest we had—he purchased one at 6 s . 6 d ., and as was going out, I lifted up a little apron he had on, and found these four handkerchief of ours—I took him into custody—he said he was very sorry but he admired the patterns—they are worth 16 s ., RICHARD DUDLEY( Police-constable C  56) I took the prisoner in charge and the handkerchiefs. WILLIAM PRAT( police-constable C  130) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction ( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 12— Transported for Seven Years.
