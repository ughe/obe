JOHN LANGRISH (Police Inspector, Criminal Investigation Department).
In the course of my duty I have attended a good many racecourses
including places like Croydon, where there are tickets for the enclosure-
prior to 6th August complaints were made with regard to the tickets
and in consequence, on 6th August Bank Holiday, I went with Denny
to the race meeting at Croydon and kept observation on people leaving
for Croydon at the Cannon Street Railway Station-I first saw Cooper
and Thomas Taylor together-I did not know them by sight-I say
them with a number of others whom I did not know and watched them-
I followed them to Woodside Railway Station adjoining the course alone
-Denny had left me at Cannon Street--they went on the course with
two or three others, and I saw them go into the 28. enclosure and come
out and go in again as the second race was run-the course was being
cleared and I saw Cooper pass something to some one else on the course,
and I gave the signal for their arrest-I did not know the man to whom
the thing was passed or what the thing was-that was outside the en-
closure, on the course I saw him go in and out of the enclosure twice-I
took Cooper with the assistance of Denny, who had joined me on the course,
to some buildings adjoining the racecourse-I had given the order for
both of them to be arrested-I searched Cooper first and found on him 10
of these Tom Hoodless's cards, these instruments, for printing, I believe,
