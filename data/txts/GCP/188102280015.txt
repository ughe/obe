GEORGE EGGLETON (Policeman X 787). On the morning in question
I was in Moscow Road, when I heard a rattle sprung-I ran to Ossington

they fell back, and I immediately apprehended them they said
nothing.
FRANCIS BEWSTER. I am a carpenter and builder, and occupy 53,
Ossington Street-I let apartments and use the lower part of the
house as a workshop I don't sleep there I left the previous evening
at 8.45 having shut up the place I was called up at 1.30 and met the
constable bringing the prisoners down the Moscow Road-in the shop I
found & desk wrenched open and different tools moved and other things
shifted; I missed my boots, which I put in the fender to air, a file moved
of the mantelpiece, and a five-inch mortice chisel off the rack the
window on the first landing was knocked out and the water-pipe pulled
down; they were safe the night before when I left.
Cross-examined. My boots were found in the closet.
