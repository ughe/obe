   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT .— Monday, March  1 st , 1847.
   PRESENT  —The Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; Sir C. MARSHALL , Knt., Alderman; Mr. RECORDED; WM. HUGHES , Esq.; and WM. HUNTER , Esq., Aldermen.
   First Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 643. RICHARD DONOVAN , JOSEPH HARRIS , PETER TOY, and ABRAHAM MANAGER were indicted for an assault on the high seas. NOT GUILTY.
   NEW COURT .— Monday, March  1 st , 1847.
   PRESENT  —Mr. Alderman JOHNSON EDWARD BULLOCK , Esq.
   Fifth Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
 644. FREDERICK BROWN was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 3 s  ., the goods of Thomas Richard Chidley , from his person; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Four Months .
 645. HENRY SHERIEF was indicted for stealing 1 1/2 pint of gin, value 2 s  ., and 1 glass bottle, 2 d  ., the goods of Benjamin Catchpole , in a port of entry and discharge; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 33.— Confined One Month .
 646. JOHN ALFRED BISHOP was indicted for embezzling 4 d  ., part of 1 s  . 9 d  .; the monies of William John Burton , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined One Month.
 647. MARY LEARY was indicted for stealing 2 rings, value 6 s  .; 1 ear drop, 6 d  .; 18 yards of lace, 2 s  .; 30 yards of ribbon, 2 s  .; 1 collar, 6 d  .; 2 pairs of gloves, 6 d  .; 1 pair of stockings, 6 d  .; and 12 yards of printed cotton, 2 s  .; the goods of Coleman Coleman , her master.
