MR. DALLETT re-examined by MR. BODKIN, I charge my customers with
the boxes, and they pay for them-sometimes they afterwards return them
to me, and get back the money, or deduct it in the next account-these
two boxes may have been charged and returned many times, and credit
given for them I cannot undertake to say they might not have been
charged and paid for.
