ELIZABETH TUCKER Nicks. I am a widow of 2, Clifton Villas, Leyton,
the house of my brother, William Jones; on the night of the 10th

property--they have no initials on them-I identify them by there being
the same quantity and the same description in every way-I cannot
positively swear to thein-the two pepper tops are mine, and also the
thimble.
