   Re-examined.  I was the principal witness against Julius for defrauding his creditors and committing perjury; he pleaded guilty to all those matters last Sessions—he was taken in custody about that time—it was a few days before that that I was charged with stealing a horse and cart; it was investigated by Mr. Montagu Williams, and I was discharged.
