1366. JOHN BARRELL was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of March , 2 nose-bags, value 3 s  ., the goods of John Edgington and another; and that he had been before convicted of felony. WILLIAM BOSWELL. I am carman to Mr. John Edgington and an other, coal-merchants, at Blackfriars Wharf. On the 12th of March I was unloading ray wagon, in Wentworth-street, Whitechapel—I had four nose-bags hanging on the side of the wagon—I left it for a short time, and when I came back I missed two nose-bags—these now produced are them, and are my master's property. WILLIAM BROOMCROSS. I am a policeman. On the 12th of March, about six o'clock in the evening, I met the prisoner in Shoreditch, offering these nose-bags for sale to a cab man—I took him into custody.
   Prisoner.  I was tipsy at the time. GEORGE COMARFORD. I am a policeman. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—I was present at the trial—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 40.— Transported for Seven Years.
 1367. FREDERICK TAYLOR was indicted for obtaining the sum of 7 s  . by false pretences; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 31.— Confined Two Months.
 1368. ANN COUTTS was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of April , 2 pillows, value 2 s  .; 1 tea-caddy, value 5 s  .; 2 pictures, framed and glazed, value 2 s  .; and 2 paintings and frames, value 2 s  .; the goods of Francis Joseph Green . FRANCIS JOSEPH GREEN. I am a carpenter, and live in Berwick-street, St. James. On Monday, the 4th of April, the prisoner came and took a furnished room in the name of Coutts, representing herself as a married woman—a/ man came as her husband on the Sunday following—she was there alone on Friday and Saturday, as far as I know—I did not see any one else—I saw the articles stated safe in the room between seven and eight o'clock on the Friday evening, when she came—on the Thursday morning following I looked through the window and missed the carpet—the door was locked, and the prisoner had the key, and also a latch-key of the street-door—I went into the room on the Thursday morning with my wife, and missed the articles—I sent for a policeman, and gave the prisoner in charge—she said she knew nothing about them—she afterwards delivered up three duplicates to the officer, and said she intended to re deem them—the things now produced are mine—I have never seen the caddy or picture-frames since. JAMES MOODY. I am in the service of James Aldous and Son, pawnbrokers, in Berwick-street. This carpet was pledged at my masters' by the prisoner—the duplicate now produced is what I gave her. JOHN CARVER. I am shopman to Mr. Ben ton, a pawnbroker, in Tot tenham-court-road. One of these pillows was pledged at my master's, (I believe by the prisoner,) for 1 s ., in the name of Ann Rogers, lodger, No. 10, Rouse-street—this is the duplicate I gave. THOMAS MARCHANT. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into custody—I told her what it was for—she gave me these three dupli catcs, one for a carpet, and the others for one pillow each.
