469. HENRY JONES and HENRY DANCE were indicted for a like
offence. MessRS, SCRIVEN and POLAND conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM Bush. I am ostler to Mr. Stephen Simpson, of the Crown and
Anchor, Brixton-road. On 25th March I saw the two prisoners standing
together about thirty yards from my master's—I saw Dance cross the road, and
saw no more of them till my master gave me information, when I went after
them, and saw them join company at Loughborough-toad - they went straight
up the road-I saw a policeman and gave them into custody- I saw Jones
come out of my master's house, but I had not seen him go in-that was about
two or three minutes after I saw them talking together.16 21.
STEPHEN Simpson. I keep the Crown and Anchor, Brixton-road. Or
25th March Jones came, asked for half a pint of beer, and tendered me a
shilling in payment-I tried it in the detector, bent it, and found it was bad
SI laid it on the counter-be picked it up, and said he had no more money
about him-he then left the house and turned towards the right - I went out
to see if I could see a policeman, Jones was still in sight, and met Dance at
the Loughborough-road about forty or fifty yards from my house, and joined
him--they appeared to talk together, and 1 directed Bush to give them into
custody.

ROBERT HODDER (policeman, P 10). I was on duty at Brixton on 25th
March, received information, went after the prisoners, took them, and charged
them with uttering a counterfeit shilling, and told them I suspected they had
bad money-Jones said they had not, they had no money at all – Jones then
put his left band in his left hand trowsers pocket, and I saw and heard six
shillings drop from his leg-1 picked them up, and while I was stooping
Dance ran away and I took him afterwards-I afterwards, examined Jones's
pocket, and found a hole towards the top of it large enough for the shillings
to have fallen through-I gave the shillings to the sergeant,
John PALMER SUMMERS (police-sergeant, P 24). I was on duty at the Brix-
ton police-station, saw Dance running up the road, and apprehended him-I
searched him and found on him this small piece of oilcloth made like a purse,
sixpence, and fourpence in halfpence, good money-I told him the charge,
and he said he picked up 11s. on Kennington-common that morning, wrapped
up in a piece of paper ; that he came up the road, and just before be came,
to Mr. Simpson's house, he fell in with Jones; they were both looking for
work, and he gave Jones the money, and that he never saw Jones before that
day- I received these shillings from Hodder, and one was brought to the
station afterwards, having been picked up by some one (produced ).
