 520. JOHN JAMIESON , stealing 1 spoon, value 3 s  .; the goods of Thomas Mason . GEORGE WRIGHT. I am waiter at the Jamaica coffee-house, St. Micnael's-alley; Mr. Mason is the proprietor. On 27th Jan., soon after six o'clock, the prisoner entered the coffee-room, sat down, and took a paper—he after wards went to a table where there was a glass; took a spoon out or it, ana was going out—I stopped him, and said, "You have stolen a spoon—he took it out of his pocket, and gave it me—I gave it to Mr. Mason—he gave it to the constable.
   Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON . Q.  Has Mr. Mason any partner? A.  No—persons are not allowed to come there to read the newspapers—it a subscriber wants to meet a person there, it is common to do so—the prisoner did not appear in the least intoxicated. THOMAS MASON. I saw the prisoner stopped, going out of my door the waiter gave me the spoon; I gave it to the officer—this it. JOHN LATHAM TURNER( City-policeman , 531). I took the prosoner, and produce the spoon.
 (The prisoner received a good character) GUILTY. Aged 42.— Recommended to mercy by, the Jury.— Confined One Month .
