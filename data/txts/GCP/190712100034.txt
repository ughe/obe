rice Bush, his daughter, a girl above the age of 13 and under the

Mr. W. B. Campbell prosecuted.

CRAWFORD, Henry (30, seaman); was indicted for attempting
an abominable crime and for gross indecency with various persons.
Mr. Arthur Gill and Mr. Boyd prosecuted.
Verdict, Guilty.
Sentence, Six months in the second division,
JONES, Charles (30, cook), pleaded guilty of breaking and enter-
ing the shop situate at 74, New Bond Street, and stealing therein
three wrist braces, the goods of Henry Joseph Williams and another.
ARTHUR WILLIAN MOORE, Liverpool City Police, detailed the antece-
dents of the prisoner. The prisoner stated to his lordship that he
had nine months of his tickel-of-leave unexpired, and that he would
leave the country if the Court dealt Teniently with him.
