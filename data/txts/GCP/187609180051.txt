floor in C, it is a wooden door, opening directly on to the stairs; it is never
closed--I saw the prisoner coming from No. 3 engine box through the iron
doors on the second floor, he came towards me, and went downstairs, he
passed me, I went upstairs to the third floor, and he went down-I told him
I had turned off the steam he said "All right!" I closed the iron doors on
the third floor, and then went to the fourth floor and closed the iron doors

I then returned to the second floor and there found Robins, I helped him in

in C; they were kept there to mend chests of tea, or anything that was required
-it was then from about 3.55 to 4 o'clook-while doing that, I saw the prisoner

same floor fronting the river-Robins and I then left that floor, leaving him
there ; Robins went downstairs, and I went up to the top floor-I there
found Attfield doing some work, I waited till he had finished; he was wipingout
the hand orane that they had been using in the day-he and I then closed
the iron doors on the top floor, they worked on rollers; they did not work
very well, they were rather out of repair, the stones had dropped along
Where the wheels ran-we could not get them quite close together one

one side-I am sure we closed them as far as they would go I and Attfield
then went down to the fifth floor, and closed the doors there; they closed
