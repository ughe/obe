HERBERT WILLIAM HALL. I am the prosecutor's clerk. The prisoner
was authorized to receive a sum on account of Colonel Ross, and various
sums on account of his master--he has never paid me any sum of money as
received on account of Colonel Ross-he has not accounted for a sum received
of Adland, and for only 3l. received from Volteur on the 4th of April--he paid
me no sum of money at all on the 2nd April, nor for any received on the 11th
of March-he did not mention at the time he paid the 31. that he had received
any other sum from Volteur-he said Mr. Volteur would not pay any more,
our charges were too high.
Cross-examined. Q. How many times were you before the Lord Mayor!
A. Perhaps six-it may have been ten-the Lord Mayor said the prisoner

none--it was not at my suggestion that I went before the Grand Jury without

lect being told to come before the Lord Mayor again-1 was not aware that I had
