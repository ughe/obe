MR. BALLANTINE. Q. You never saw that bill! A. No; or any bill

EDWARD LINDON. I am a creditor of Peters's to the amount of about
2901.-the last goods ordered was by letter on 23rd March, to the amount
of 631.; the goods were sent on 1st April - none of them have been
returned.
WILLIAM BUTCHER. I am agent to Messrs. Bright and Co., carpet
manufacturers-they are creditors of Peters's to the amount of 1121.-the
last parcel of goods was delivered on 8th Feb.; they came to about 1121.-
he had bought a lot of goods on 20th Jan. ; 1961. worth was sent on that
day, and the remainder are what I have mentioned.
Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON.Q. Are those goods part of what he
owed you for? A. That was the entire of the goods.
SAMUEL MORLEY. I am a creditor of Peters's to the amount of 2681.128.11d.;
goods to the amount of 521. 78. were supplied in Feb., 201. 188. in March,
and 21. in Jan.
Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Did he discount with you also, as
to the payment? A. He discounted the first purchase, 2001.mine are
hosiery goods—We understand by discount, the payment of cash within a
month-the cash was paid-we allow discount only for cash-he owes me
2601. now.
THOMAS FORBES. I am a creditor of Peters's to the amount of 2151, for
goods supplied in Dec., Jan., Feb., March, and April - in March the amount
was 6. 38., in April it was a returned bill, in Feb. 192. 18., and on 30th Jan.
65l. 2s.
THOMAS BAKER. I am a creditor of Peters's to the amount of about 250%.
--the last goods were supplied in Feb., they amounted to 211 148., 201. 38.
and 341. 38. 4d.; 751. altogether-in Jan. there were five parcels—the amount
of the whole is about 2501.
Cross-excamined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. When was your last dealing with
Peters? A. On 27th Feb. I received two bills from him, one for 2761. 118.,
and the other 1942. 128.; they have not been paid they were bills given in
discount of the time within which I should have been entitled to be paid
one was due on 16th June last, and the other on 13th of this month neither
of them have been paid.
WILLIAM LANGSTREE. I am a porter, at the railway at Southampton. I
do not book goods there I receive them on the stage-I recollect goods
coming there directed to Simeon and Hart the times and the weights will
appear by the book which Eggleston has here I used to receive the goods
from Mr. Peters's man.
THOMAS EGGLESTON. I booked some of the goods directed to Simeon
and Hart--the first of my booking there was on 5th Jan.--I have not
ascertained the weight of the goods sent to Simeon and Hart.
GEORGE JOHN HEWSON. I am the attesting witness to this assignment
- I saw it executed by Peters (this was an assignment of all his estate and
effects for the benefit of his creditors.)
SIMON HANARTY. I am clerk to Thomas and Henry Brett. I believe
they are the petitioning creditors in this matter-Peters owes them 2381.-
this is the signature of Mr. Brett, for himself and partner.
(Mr. BARON MARTIN being of opinion that there was no case to go to the
Jury as against HART and SIMEON, they were acquitted at the close of the
case for the prosecution.) PETERS-GUILTY.-Confined Fifteen Months.
