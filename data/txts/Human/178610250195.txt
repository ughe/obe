Then how is it possible you should know they were in the box? - I have since found a quantity of buttons, which answer the order I sent for to Birmingham.
 Court. These are buttons, you had ordered from Birmingham? - Yes, they came in a box, which was nailed up as common boxes are.
 Was it nailed down when it came to you? - Yes.
 Had you sent the particular pattern? - Yes.
 Did not the pattern come up with the box? - Yes.
 Was the pattern in the box, or on the outside of the box? - Inside the box; but I did not see the pattern.
 Had you any letter of advice? - No.
 How long have you had them? - They came up in November twelve month.
 Had you drawn the nails of that box? - No, I did not.
 What buttons were they? - A plain yellow metal, perfectly plain, and white shanks to them.
 Jury. Did you order your buttons all coat, or did you order them coat and breast? - Coat and breast.
   FRANCIS UMPAGE sworn.
 I searched the prisoner's lodgings, and found the box broke all to pieces, and the lid off.
 Have you all the pieces of the head? - No, there is one piece that I have not got; there wants a piece to complete the head of the box.
 Is there any mark upon it? - Here is a mark No. 7.
 What was in the box? - Only pieces of brown paper.
 Prosecutor. I cannot swear to the box; I never observed, nor looked at it.
 Umpage. There was no direction upon it.

 I am in partnership with Mr. Stiff, in New-street, Covent-garden; on Monday, the 6th of October, between the hours of ten and twelve, the prosecutor came to our shop for some buttons; he came behind to my partner and me, as we were sitting at the desk, and told us, he understood a person was to come to sell some buttons; about one the same day, the prisoner came to my partner and me, as we were sitting at the desk, and produced two buttons, one of each, and mentioned that he had a quantity to dispose of; we asked him if he was the manufacturer of them; he said no, he had them of a taylor who lodged in his house, who was gone to Liverpool, and that it was all he had for fifteen pounds; we asked him what price he asked for them; he said, he could not think of taking less than four shillings and sixpence; he did not say, nor we did not mention, whether he meant the single gross, or the double gross, as we did not want an explanation, only to get possession of the buttons; we appointed him to come at three; about ten minutes before that, he brought the buttons tied up in two handkerchiefs; he put them on the counter in the front shop; I desired him to step into the back shop, which had not the command of the shop door, as I expected the prosecutor with his friends, to come at three; the prosecutor came in with two gentlemen belonging to the Sun-fire-office; and the foreman of the fire men, when he came in, he gave me a small slip of paper, upon which was wrote the number of the buttons, and the description of the buttons, and the quantity;
