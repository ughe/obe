   Cross-examined by MR. M. WILLIAMS . I have been told that if I gave evidence I should be well paid for it—this was just after I had told about the wax—I think it was Sergeant Croome who said so—I have been convicted twice, once for striking a person with a poker, and once for receiving a stolen jacket—I got one month for that—after that I had six months' for taking lodgings in a false name—it was not for robbing my lodgings, they were un furnished—that was at Stone's End, Borough, and I think it was last July three years—my landlord prosecuted me, and I served the four months—I have received 5 s . from Mr. Brockwell, and 2 s . 6 d . was given to my husband—I have not had 2 l ., nor have my family—Mrs. Cook is my mother—I have had 10 s . a week, but not from Mr. Brockwell; it was given me by a gentleman at Mr. Field's; Croome has given me nothing—he gave my husband 2 s . 6 d . when Mrs. Castle was taken.
   Cross-examined by MR. CROOME . I worked some time for Mrs. Castle; some times in the yard and sometimes in the house—I took particular notice of Mrs. Scott, because people in the neighbourhood used to talk about her taking bundles out—I did not take notice of any other customer, because no other customer used to Come into the parlour—I used to do the house cleaning, and when she came I was always in the parlour—I was there from 8 in the morning till 7 at night—I saw her there on the Monday or Tuesday in the week I left, but not in the latter part of the week—I was first asked about this the end of April, after I left—I had not talked it over in—the meanwhile, and my attention had not been drawn to it—I don't know that Mrs. Scott brought a number of bags for Mrs. Castle in the bundle, as she finished them.
   Cross-examined by MR. LYON . I don't know that Mrs. Scott took in washing—I have not seen babies' petticoats hanging outside Mrs. Castle's door—Mrs. Scott used to take a bundle under one arm, and a basket on the other to Mrs. Castle's—I have known her three or four years, but never knew her to take in washing—she did wash for my mother three years ago, but not lately—I don't know that she washed for Mrs. Bitham, of the Carlisle Arms, for four years.
