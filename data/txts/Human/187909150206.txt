873. GEORGE WILLIAMS  (42), JOHN JONES  (24), and GEORGE KITSON  (38) , Burglary in the dwelling-house of Joseph George Stotten , with intent to steal.
   WILLIAMS  and JONES PLEADED GUILTY.
   MR. GEOGHEGAN Prosecuted : MR. Purcell Defended Kitson; and MR. POYNTER Defended Jones. MATTHEW FOX( Police Inspector M ). On Sunday night, 3rd August, I was in company with Walsh, Harvey, and Stretton, in Old Kent Road, and at 11.30—I then saw Williams and Jones—I saw them again in about half an hour, sitting on the railings in front of a house in the Old Kent Road—Harvey gave me some information at 2 o'clock, in consequence of which I went to the Duke of Kent public-house, in the Old Kent Road, with Harvey, Stretton, and Walsh—I placed a man at each door, and tried to rouse the proprietor, who leaned out of the window and spoke to me—while watching the house, the door was thrown open suddenly, and Jones and Williams attempted to rush out—I secured them, and left them in custody—I then went into the house, and found that an entrance had been effected through the fanlight—I did not see Kitson.
   Cross-examined by MR. PURCELL . The last time I saw Kitson he was about 200 yards from the house—it was a fine night, no rain. JOSEPH GEORGE STOTTEN. I am landlord of the Duke of Kent—I was aroused by the police—I cannot swear to having seen Kitson in the house before, I have seen the other two frequently. GEORGE HARVEY( Police Sergeant M ). I was in company with Walsh, and Fox in the London Road, on the night in question—I saw the three prisoners together about 9.30—I followed them to the Duke of Kent, and about 11.15 they went in at the corner door; the house was then open—in about 10 minutes or a quarter of an hour they came out and went to another public-house, where they remained till closing time, 11—they then came out and separated—I saw them again about 12 at a coffee stall, nearly opposite the Duke of Kent—I saw them again four or five times before 2 o'clock altogether; at one time the three were standing together—I went up Trafalgar Road, and saw a light flash inside the public-house—I was standing opposite, Kitson came across, looked into my face, and passed on—I passed a word to Fox, and he placed us round the house—I took Williams—we took Kitson; he gave us an address. JAMES WALSH( Police Inspector ). I was in company with Fox and Harvey on this night, about 9.30, in the London Road—I saw the prisoners together at 2 on the morning of the 4th outside the Duke of Kent—I saw Kitson coming from the Duke of Kent; I followed him twenty or thirty yards; he turned and looked at me; I walked on to get up to him; he walked quickly and then ran, and I after him for a considerable distance; he got into a hansom—I then turned back and assisted in taking the others—after that I was looking for Kitson,. and found him outside the Crown public-house—I took him to the station and charged him with the burglary—he said "If I knew what it was you wanted me for you should have brought me in pieces."
   Cross-examined by MR. PUROELL . The Crown is a mile and a half from the Duke of Kent.
   KITSON  — GUILTY.
   JONES and KITSON also PLEADED GUILTY to previous convictions.— Seven Years' Penal Servitude each. WILLIAMS  — Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
