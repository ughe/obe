come back till 6.
Cross-examined. Nobody spoke to me about this charge-my mother
has not told me what to say--I went to bed at 6 o'clock that might
with a bad faceache, and my father had not gone out then; whether
anything was put up for him I don't know, I did not put anything up
for him that night.

--there was no meat in the house but roast beef.
