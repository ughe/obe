Sergeant  SAMUEL GALE , 110 K. On July 26, at about 3.15 p.m., I was returning to East Ham Police Station, when I beard cries and saw the prisoners seated in a pony trap driving at an excessive pace. I gave chase on my bicycle, overtook them at Cyprus Place, New Beckton. and took them into custody for being drunk and furiously driving. I took them to the station. Brett was charged with being drunk in charge of the pony and cart; both with being concerned in furiously driving the pony. Between 4 and 5 p.m. I learnt that a child had been knocked down. I saw the prisoners again and said, I as going to charge you with causing grievous bodily harm to Minnie Slamovitz at about 3 p.m. in Queen's Road." They were then charged and made no reply. It was found the other charge was in west Ham, and they were recharged at West Ham Police Court.
 (Defence.) ALBERT HENRY HEATH(prisoner, on oath). I live at 67, Eastbourne Road, West Ham, and am a catsmeat man. On July 26, at 3 p.m., I was in Whitechapel, coming home from market, when I met Brett in the trap, which belongs to his uncle, and came home with him, we had one or two drinks and Brett had a row with a publican. I got him out into the trap and came with him to see him home. As we cane down Queen's Road the child ran across the road, ran back, and then dashed right into the pony. The pony shied, the little child was knocked down, the pony bolted, and it was some time before Brett could control him. After we had got some distance I heard a police man whistle. Both before and after I tried to stop the pony. At the up of the road the pony eased a bit, but Brett, being drunk and very excited, paid no attention and drove on. I tried to get the reins from him and got the whip away from him, but could not get him to stop. The police then chased us. I put the whip down in the cart and tried a got hold of the reins. I did not whip the horse at all. I tried my but to stop thepony. The Common Serjeant directed that the Jury should acquit the prisoners of intentional assault and causing grievous bodily harm, and put the question whether they caused injury by wanton or furious driving, by wilful misconduct or wilful neglect.
 Verdict (both), Guilty on the third count; not guilty on the first.
 and second counts.
 Sentence: Brett, Three weeks' imprisonment, second division.
 Heath, one month's imprisonment, second division.
