I did not try to persuade him to say he was mistaken.
EDWARD Brown re-examined. - The policeman came to my mother's
during the week, but I was engaged with my brother in Quaker-street, who
minds a small general shop-I do not sell nuts every day, and was not
selling them all that week I was in the same place on Sunday-I stand
there every Sunday-it was known where I was.
WILLIAM ARGENT re-examined. I inquired at Brown's mother's, and
was told he was not at home-she said, "It is quite uncertain when he will
be, he travels down the country"-I went several times, but could not see
him till after the prisoner was committed.
