 985.  ANN HOLLAND was again indicted for stealing, on the 25th of March , 1 stuff gown, value 3s., the goods of the   Mayor and Corporation of the City of London, Governors of St. Bartholomew's Hospital.
 Mr. WILBY. This gown was at the bottom of the prisoner's box - it is called a livery gown, and was worn by sister Morley; it is found by the Governors, and when a sister dies it becomes the property of the hospital - I said to the prisoner "You cannot say this was given to you, it is the property of the Governors;" she said, it must have been put there by somebody to implicate her.
   ANN ELDEN. I saw this gown found, and the prisoner said she did not know how it came there; her box was not locked; it was so full it could not be shut when I saw it.
 Prisoner's Defence. This gown laid on the bin - I never put it into my box, I only laid it on the top of the box with the other things - Morley told me to take it, and said when they had been there twelve months, it was their own.
     NOT GUILTY.
 FIFTH DAY. TUESDAY, APRIL 10.
 Second London Jury - Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 986.  MARY DUNN was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of February , 1 leghorn bonnet, value 10s., the goods of Thomas Joseph Jordan , privately in his shop.
   ANN MARY JORDAN. I am the wife of Thomas Joseph Jordan, a painter; I keep a bonnet shop in Leadenhall-street. On the 24th of February the prisoner came in with another person, to purchase a bonnet; I showed them ten or twelve - they then asked for one without a rim; they would not try them on as I wished, and while they were looking at them a bonnet dropped in front of the counter, opposite to where I was; I said, "My good woman you have dropped a bonnet;" the prisoner immediately ran towards the door - I then ran round - the other woman caught hold of me by my clothes, but I ran to catch the prisoner when another bonnet dropped from her clothes when she got to the door, which was open, and she ran up Leadenhall-street.
   THOMAS JOSEPH JORDAN. I was in the back-room, my wife called Stop-thief! I ran out and found the prisoner in the officer's arms, two or three hundred yards from the house.
   WILLIAM PLAISTOWE. I am an officer. I stopped the prisoner running fast towards the Exchange, in the middle of the road, about eight o'clock in the evening.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I went with another woman - she spoke to the lady as if she knew her - I was not near the counter, and had no bonnet in my possession - whether it fell from the counter I do not know.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 987.  ANDREW DODSON and THOMAS PRETTY were indicted for stealing, on the 5th of March , 1 box, value 2s.; 2 pairs of breeches, value 30s.; 3 shirts, value 21s.; 3 handkerchiefs, value 6s.; 4 shifts, value 12s.; 1 table-cloth, value 3s.; 2 petticoats, value 3s.; 1 sheet, value 4s., and 1 pair of trousers, value 2s., the goods of James Rapley.
 JAMES RAPLEY. I am a  carrier, and live at Edmonton. On the 5th of March I received a box from Mr. Johnson of Charterhouse-lane - I left my cart in Bread-street for four minutes, about half-past three o'clock; when I returned this box was gone - it is directed to Mr. Cox, Lower Edmonton.
   CHARLES HERDSFIELD. I was coming up Foster-lane on the 5th of March, between three and four o'clock, and saw Dodson with this box and a bundle on his shoulder; Pretty, who was a few yards down the lane, then took the box - I seized him and called Stop thief! after Dodson, who was secured.
   ANN COX. I live at Edmonton. This box is Mr. Johnson's property, and contains these articles.
 DODSON's Defence. The box was on the pavement in St. Martin's-le-Grand - a man asked me to carry it to the Bell public-house, Foster-lane; when I got to the corner of the lane I missed him, and asked this man to mind it for me, while I looked for him.
 PRETTY's Defence. He asked me to mind the box - I said Yes, if he would not belong.
 DODSON -    GUILTY. Aged 18.
 PRETTY -    GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Three Months.
 988.  RICHARD BEACH was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of March , 28 yards of printed cotton, value 9s., the goods of Robert Macglew.
 ROBERT MACGLEW. I am a  linen-draper, and live in the Minories. On the 7th of March, about two o'clock, the prisoner was brought into our shop by several persons, with this cotton, which was taken from the door - it must take some trouble to get it down.
   JAMES PEARCE. I was in the Minories, and saw the prisoner take this print from the door - he put it into his apron and was going off - I took him about forty yards off with it - two men were at the window, but I cannot say whether they were with him - he struck me and tried to throw me down.
   JAMES ROBERTS. I received him in charge.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. Two young men dropped it, and asked me to pick it up, as they had bundles to carry.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Two Months.
