Smith, of Birmingham-we expected some nails to be delivered by Boyd
in consequence of a transaction in the previous February; we had paid
him for them, and we ought to have received them within a few days of
28th February-they were Globe horse nails of Mr. Seeley's Company-
shortly before 10th April I pressed him for the nails several times; at
last I told him I thought he was going dangerously near to getting
them under false pretences-he called on me on Saturday, 11th April,

handwriting-the paper was handed over to Mr. Lawrence, one of our own
clerks, to obtain delivery of the nails-Boyd said, "I want you to get
these nails at once"-I said, “What is the hurry?”-he said, “Oh,
