Bank notes—this is the bill and notes—I received them of Ross, whose name is on them—I paid them to the prosecutrix for half a year's rent of the ground floor, at 120 l . a-year—I had purchased the premises for her—the lease is in the hands of Mr. Robson, the solicitor, to be transferred to her—I advanced 10 l . to make up 50 l . for the prosecutrix to get the lease—her mother was with her—the prosecutrix took possession of the premises six months ago—Ross was the tenant below—I seized on him for the rent in the prosecutrix's name, and he paid it—I let the upper part for 60 l .—the prose cutrix has received rent for that, and is acknowledged by the tenants as the owner—she is to pay 120 l . a-year rent.
