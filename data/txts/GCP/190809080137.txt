BEFORE THE COMMON SERJEANT.
(Wednesday, September 9.)
DYE, George Charles (26, painter), and DYE, Sarah Esther (24,
no occupation); both breaking and entering the dwelling-house of
Alfred Maul, and stealing therein one gold link, one locket, one!
ring, two gold pins, and divers other articles, his goods; feloniously
possessing three moulds, which would make and impress the figure of
both sides of c Es, pisce.
George Charles Dye pleaded guilty to both indictments.
Mr. Beaumont Morice stated that the prosecution did not propose
to offer evidence against the female prisoner on the coining charge,
and the Jury returned a verdict of Not guilty.
Sarah Dye was then tried on the housebreaking charge.
Mr. St. John Morrow prosecuted; Mr. Knight defended.
