FREDERICK WILLIAM LANCE. I am a clerk in the North British and
Mercantile Insurance Office--it is part of my duty to survey premises

roughly guessed the value- I roughly guessed that there were between
2,500 and 3,000 tins of capuala, they were in the top floor front room, I

and paraffin lamps-there was a place for heating water for use in the
