Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
1050. JAMES BURTON was indicted for stealing 70 lbs. weight of iron,
value 38., the goods of the Eastern Counties Railway Company.
David MOORE. I am in the employ of the Eastern Counties Railway at
Stratford. On the 1st April, while I was having my breakfast, I saw the pri-
soner pass my window twice with a bag on his shoulder, and something heavy
in it-I went and watched him, and saw him go to a marine store dealer's
yard, and take the bag from his back-he put it in the scales and weighed it
- I went and took the bag and found these three pieces of iron in it-they
are the property of the Eastern Counties Railway Company.
John WILLIAM MANNING ( police-constable K 5.) I took the prisoner into
custody, and now produce the iron.
Prisoner. I found the iron at the bottom of Little North-street, Stratford.
David MOORE re-examined. I do not know where he got the iron from
- it does not lay about the public ways, nor about Little North-stre et.
Guilty.* Aged 34.- Confined Six Months.
Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
1051. BENJAMIN SKIGGS and CHARLES ROOT were indicted for
stealing 398lbs. weight of lead, value 31. 6s. 4d., the goods of Benjamin
Nind, being fixed on and to a certain building; and that Skiggs had been
before convicted of felony.
JOHN WILLIAM MANNING (police-sergeant K 5.) I heard of some lead
having been stolen from Mr. Nind's portico, at Leyton - I watched near Cat-
Hall-lane, opposite the cage, in Harrow-green, Leyton--I saw a cart go down
the lane I watched till it returned in about a quarter of an hour-I, Benton,
and Potten, went to the cart-we found the two prisoners in it-Hall had
been driving it, but he got out and went to the public-house-the cart was
standing opposite the public-house--this was about nine o'clock at night-
laid hold of Root by the collar with one hand, and I felt the lead with the
other hand-I asked him how he came by the lead-he said he knew nothing
at all of it-he made great resistance, and attempted to throw me several
times after getting him out of the cart-another officer came, and we ulti-
mately secured him-Benton took Skiggs.
