ANTHONY Wilson MONGER (City Detective). I apprehended Goldsmid
I met Pollock in the street when I was with Goldsmid--I told him I was a
detective officer, and asked him if he had bought any stockings or under-
shirts of Goldsmid-he said that he had not_I then took Pollock to his
house, which was close by, and searching the place, I found this piece of
canvas-I said “This looks like a bag out open " he said "Oh, we use
that for shirts for the boys, and we have had it a long time I took him
to the station, and told him he was charged with receiving, at different times,
goods to the amount of about 601.- he said nothing.
Cross-examined. It was from inquiries I made that I took him to the
station, not because I found the bag.
