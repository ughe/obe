 1548.  BENJAMIN HUGHES was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of August , a handkerchief, value 5 s., the goods of Ann Garton and Eliza Garton.
   JOHN FAREY. I am in the employ of Ann and Eliza Garton - they are hosiers, and live in Cheapside; this handkerchief is their property. On the 10th of August, it laid on the right hand counter, where we expose goods for sale. I did not miss it, till the officer brought in the prisoner about twelve o'clock.
   DANIEL FORRESTER. On Wednesday, the 10th of August, I was going past the shop with a prisoner, and saw this lad come from the door with something yellow under his jacket; I went into the shop and said to the young man, "Have you sold a yellow handkerchief;" he said, No; I said, "If you will take care of my prisoner, I will go and see if I can get it;" I went to Bow-lane, and took the prisoner with this handkerchief under his coat.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was coming from Mitcham, and saw the handkerchief lying on the ground; I picked it up - the constable came and took me.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Transported for Seven Years.
 1549.  JOHN KIRKHAM was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of September , a handkerchief, value 6 d., the goods of Samuel Buckingham , from his person.
 SAMUEL BUCKINGHAM. On the 12th of September, about twenty minutes before seven o'clock in the evening, I was walking down  Holborn, an officer stopped me, and said I had been robbed of my handkerchief; I felt my pocket, and missed it, and saw the officer take it from under the prisoner's coat; I find he has a good character; his father is ill, and his mother in great distress.
   FRANCIS KEYS. I am an officer. On the 12th of September, I saw the prisoner and two others in Gray's-inn-lane - I watched them for half an hour - when they got into Holborn, they followed the prosecutor, who was coming toward the City - one of them made an attempt at his pocket, but did not succeed; the other two were close to him - when they came to Mr. Thompson's wine vaults, I saw one of them take the handkerchief from the prosecutor's pocket, and give it to the prisoner - I seized him and found it on him.
 The prisoner received an excellent character. GUILTY. Aged 18.
 Strongly recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor. Fined 1 s. and Discharged.
 1550.  SARAH KIRBY was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of August , a counterpane, value 3 s., the goods of Jacob Metzgar , in a lodging-room, in his dwelling-house.
   ELIZABETH METZGAR. I am the wife of Jacob Metzgar, he lives in Long-lane, West Smithfield. The prisoner came and hired a ready furnished room of me; there was another woman with her; this counterpane was a part of the furniture; I missed it from the bed on the 20th of August, and told her of it - she said she did it through distress.
   CHARLES SAUNDERS. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Long-lane. This counterpane was pawned on the 20th of August, by the prisoner.
   EDWARD JONES. I took the prisoner; I searched her lodgings, and found the duplicate of this counterpane, pawned in the name of Ann Kirby.
 Prisoner's Defence (written). Three weeks before my apprehension, I nursed Mrs. Turner, who occupied the prosecutor's furnished lodging; she gave me the things to pawn - I did not know but they were her own; and all the duplicates were found upon her.
 ELIZABETH METZGAR re-examined. Q. Did the prisoner say she pawned it for  Ann Turner? A. No; she took the room.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. Confined One Month.
