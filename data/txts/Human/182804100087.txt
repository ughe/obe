 949.  JOHN FERRIS and HYAM JACOBS were indicted for stealing, on the 26th of March , 26 lbs. weight of cheese, value 16s., the goods of Thomas Marshall and others.
 FERRIS pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17. Confined Three Months and Whipped.
   FRANCIS KEYS. I am an officer. On the 26th of March I met Jacobs and Ferris opposite the hospital in Whitechapel; they came on to the prosecutor's shop, and Ferris took the cheese - Jacobs was about six yards from him at the time; I took Ferris with the cheese, and gave him to my brother officer - I then pursued, and took Jacobs.
 Prisoner JACOBS. He never knew any harm of me. Witness. I have seen him with thieves in Whitechapel.
   JOHN STROTHER SHOTTER. I am shopman to Messrs. Thomas Marshall and Sons. On the 26th of March I put six cheeses on the stall-board of the window, and about a quarter before eleven o'clock, I went into the parlour to put on my boots; the officer brought the prisoner and Ferris in with the cheese, which I knew to be my masters.
   WILLIAM HARRIS. I was with Keys; I confirm what he has stated.
 JACOBS's Defence. I may walk up and down the road without doing any harm; I saw the other with the cheese - I do not know where he got it.
 JACOBS -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 950.  CATHERINE BAILEY was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of March , 1 handkerchief, value 4s., and 1 apron, value 1s., the goods of Sophia Lee.
 SOPHIA LEE. I  deal in fish; I have known the prisoner many years, and have employed her to bring up fish from Billingsgate. On the 19th of March, I sent her to my house, No. 2, Brazier's-buildings, Fleet-market, for a flannel, my servant who lives with me came and gave me information - I went home and missed a handkerchief and an apron; I have found the handkerchief at a pawnbroker's.
   SARAH TYER. I live with the prosecutor, the prisoner came for a flannel, and in about an hour I missed an apron and a handkerchief which I had put on the table.
 Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. There is no mark on it? A. No, but I can swear to it; I have two others of the same pattern - I never wanted 30s. to settle it; she said they must have got into the flannel, and she have dropped them - I found her at the pawnbroker's, where she had come for the rest of the money, and took her.
 GUILTY. Aged 34. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined for Fourteen Days.
 951.  JACOB BOWICK was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of December , 1 male ass, price 40s. the property of John Blakes.
 JOHN BLAKES. I am a  labourer. I left a male ass in the possession of Mr. Pottinger, at the Green Dragon at Stepney, for 1s. 6d. a week, the latter end of August; I went there on the 23d of December and paid a fortnight's keep; it was missed on the 24th, but I did not go again till the 30th, and when I went on the green, behind the Green Dragon, it was gone - I found it on the 18th of February in the possession of Mr. Smith; the man whom I bought it of stopped it coming from the West India Docks.
   MARY BLAKES. I am the prosecutor's wife; I saw the ass safe on the 23d of December, and we missed it on the 30th. On the 31st I met the prisoner in Ratcliff-highway and asked if he had it: he said he knew nothing of it; I described it to him, as having a scar on the off-side - he then said he knew it, and had seen it at the back of the Halfway-house; I went there, and it was in the possession of Smith - I saw the prisoner again on the 1st of January, and asked if he knew any thing about it; he said,"D-n your donkey and you to."
   JOHN KYFFIN. I live in Montague-place, Poplar. I bought the ass of the prisoner for 20s.; I cannot say on what day; I asked why he sold it; he said it was not strong enough - I think this was the beginning of January; he said he had bought it on the Friday week or fortnight before, at Smithfield, for 20s., and that it was a very good one; it was very ill, and I took it to Smith to sell it for me; it was a light brown colour - the prosecutor claimed the same ass as I bought of the prisoner.
   GEORGE DEVERELL. - I am night constable of Shadwell. I took the prisoner on the 26th of February; he said, "I suppose you came about the donkey;" he said the prosecutrix had told him she had lost it, and if he saw it to let her know, and she would give him 5s.; that on the Wednesday following he was going to Romford to buy a donkey and met a man with this one; that he bought it of him for 16s., and sold it a few days after; and if he could have deposited 30s. with Kyffen they would not have prosecuted him.
   JOHN BLAKES. When I found the donkey it was not worth so much by 1l. as when I lost it.; the hair had been cut off, but I knew it again - the prisoner at first said he would give me 10s., and then said he did not care what I did.
 Prisoner's Defence. What I have done was justly; I sold the donkey to the man, and when it was said that it was the prosecutor's; I said he should have it; I left my own for it, but Kyffin would have 30s., which I could not give him.
 - SMITH. I had an order to ship some donkies; Kyffin sent this one up, but the captain would not have it.
 GUILTY. Aged 45. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Six Months.
