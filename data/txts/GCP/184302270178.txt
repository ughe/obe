998. MARTHA BARRETT was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of
February, 2 cups, value 6d. ; 2 saucers, 6d.; 1 handkerchief, 6d. ; 1 jacket,
2s. ; 1 towel, 3d. ; 1 sheet, ls. ; 2 aprons, 18.; and 1 neckerchief, 6d.
the goods of Charles George Chapman, her master.
James ReynolDS (police-sergeant T 1.) On the 12th of February I was
on duty in Turnham-green, about a quarter before three o'clock in the mom-
ing- I saw the prisoner carrying this bundle and a basket-I asked where
she was going, she said to Colnbrook, and she had left a situation at Clapton
at four o'clock the afternoon before-that her father had come to meet her
he was gone on in a wagon, and had got her boxes-she said this bundle
which she had contained her clothes- I took her towards the station, and on
the road she told me that these cups and saucers she had taken from her mis.
tress—and the statement she had made respecting her leaving her place at
Clapton was all false-I examined the bundle at the station, and found these
articles in it.
ANN CHAPMAN. I am the wife of Charles George Chapman, we live in
Pelham-row South, Brompton-the prisoner came to me on the 3rd of Jan.
as a servant, and left me on the 11th of Feb.-I went to bed that night about
a quarter to twelve o'clock, also leaving her up and my husband-when I was
in bed she brought me the baby-she rubbed her eyes, and said she was very
weary-I told her to tell her master to come up stairs, and she could go to
bed-she slept in the kitchen I heard no more of her till a quarter before
five, when the policeman came to the door-I tben discovered the prisoner
was gone, and I missed this property—the cups and saucers had been in the
kitchen-cupboard--we had used some of them for tea on that afternoon-the
kitchen was in great confusion, and the window open-she had not given me
notice to leave.
Guilty. Aged 17.-- Confined Four Months.
