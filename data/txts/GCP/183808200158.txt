1946. ELIZABETH JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 11th
of July, 1 brooch, value 11.; 1 needle-case, value 28.; 2 yards of holland.
value Is.; and 1 frock, value is. 6d.; the goods of William Smith, ber
master.
MARY ANN SMITH. I am the wife of William Smith. The prisoner
was our servant-I missed the brooch and other things-we had dismissed
her before-this property is mine-(looking at it.)
ROBERT BACKHOUSE (police-constable H 92.) I went with Mr. Smith
to Brown's-lane, Spitalfields-I found this needle-case there, and in the
prisoner's box some duplicates of these articles—the brooch has not been
found I have produced the article.
Prisoner. There was a gold chain and brooch that I was taken up for,
and the chain has been found since.
MARY ANN SMITH re-examined. The chain has been found in the
country, but she was taken up for stealing my purse and money, which
she owned to, and I forgave her.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Three Months.
1947. JANE PEARSON was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
August, 1 pair of spectacles, value 168., the goods of John Newton.
JOHN NEWTON. I keep a chandler's shop in Buckeridge-street, St
Giles's. I placed a pair of spectacles about a foot from the shop
door on the 3rd of August-the prisoner came to my shop that day,
and within ten minutes I missed them-I went after the prisoner,
brought her in, and requested to know if she would suffer herself
to be searched, and she said " Yes"-I got a woman to search bet,
but nothing was found on her-she went past the window in going away,
and as I considered she must have them, I told a man of it-in about
two hours I saw the prisoner and the spectacles.
WILLIAM LEIGH Wood, (police-constable E 127.) I followed the prisoner
to a paynbroker's, and found these spectacles in her bosom.
Prisoner's Defence. I am a married woman with four small children-
my husband has been ont of employ for some time, having his arm broken
-I had been dismissed from the infirmary myself on the 1st of August
went to the prosecutor's shop to borrow a chopper, and seeing these
spectacles on the ground near the door, I took them up.
GUILTY. Aged 47.- Recommended to mercy.-Confined Ten Days.
