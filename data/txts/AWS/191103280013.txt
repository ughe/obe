HALEY, Patrick (32, hawker), unlawfully uttering counterfeit
coin twice within ten days.
Mr. Wilkinson prosecuted.
JoHN PAYNE, assistant, B. Hemming and Co., hosiers, 34 and 35,
Blackfriars Road. At 2.15 p.m. on February 25 prisoner came into
the shop and asked for a pair of black socks at 41d. I told him the
cheapest I had was 61d. and he bought a pair. He tendered this
half-crown, which I bent; it was bad. I sent for a constable and gave
him into custody. He was taken to the station, where I charged him,
but the inspector refused to take the charge as there was insufficient
evidence and he was liberated. On March 2 I identified him at the
Bridewell Police Station.
