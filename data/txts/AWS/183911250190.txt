273. CATHERINE M'MAHON was indicted for stealing, on the 9th
of November, 15 yards of printed cotton, value 108., the goods of John
Dennis and another; and that she had been before convicted of felony.
JAMEs STRUTT. I am assistant in the shop of John Dennis and another,
in Tooley-street. On the 9th of November the prisoner came and asked
for something, I could not tell what-I asked her again-she muttered
something, and was going out-I went to her and pulled up her cloak, and
saw this plece of print, which she had taken from the counter.
JAMes HIGHWOoD. I am a policeman. I received the prisoner into cus-
tody.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going into the shop for a skein of silk-my foot
kicked against this print-I took it up and was going to the light-this
gentleman came and said I had taken it off a pile.
JAMES STRUTT. It was close under her arm when she was going out.
GEORGE SAWYER. I am a policeman. I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's conviction, which I got from the Clerk of the Peace office for
Surrey-(read)-the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Transported for Seven Years.
