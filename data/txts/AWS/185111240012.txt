Before Mr. Recorder, and the Second Jury.
3. THOMAS ALLEN, stealing 1 carpet-bag, 6 towels, and other articles,
value 61. 1s.; and 6 sovereigns and 6 half-sovereigns ; the property of
Thomas Young.
MR. BRIARLY conducted the Prosecution.
ELLEN ELPHINSTONE WALKER. I am daughter-in-law of the person who
keeps the Puneh coffee-house, Bishopsgate-street. On Tuesday, 7th Oet.,
the prisoner and Young came to the coffee-house--they left a carpet-bag and
basket or bundle, and the prisoner said they would return for them in the
evening-th prisoner afterwards returned alone, and left a small bundle.
Cross-eramined by MR. COoPER. Q. I suppose you have a great many
people in and out in the course of a day A. Yes; there was one other
person there at the time, but I cannot say whether there were more-I am not
there every day, only on this day on account of the party being out-I had
never seen the prisoner before-I was not called on to see the prisoner again
for about a week.
Mr. BRIARLY. Q. How long did they stay A. Ten minutes or a quarter
of an hour-I am quite sure the prisoner is the man.
MaRY CONNOLLY. I am servant at this eoffee-house. On Tuesday, 7th
Oet., between 7 and 8 o'elock, the prisoner came and asked for a cup of coffee
served bim, and he asked me for a carpet-bag, basket, and bundle-Mr.
Walker, the master of the house, delivered them to him and he went away-
I have not the least doubt he is the man.
Cross-eramined. Q. Had you ever seen him before A. No; numbers
of persons come in day after day--1 cannot speak to every one who has been
there in the last three weeks-[ was taken to the Mansion-house to see the
prisoner about a week after that-there was a policeman at his side-I did
not pick him out from a dozen others.
MR. BRIARLY. Q. Did you recognise him immediately A. Yes; he
was not pointed out to me-I have no doubt about him.
