EDWARD PRESTON re-examined. Nowlan asked if he was a taller man than
me that meant Doyle ; and I said Doyle was taller than me it was not
Molloy, for I never saw him-I did not see him in Orchard-street,
WILLIAM Nowlan re-examined. I saw Mulloy in Orchard-street--I did
not ask Preston if he was one of them-I was asking him about Doyle at the
time-he did not point to Molloy, and say, "He was a taller man than
you"-Mulloy was five or six yards behind me, and had a short pipe in his
mouth, smoking--this was about half an hour after the robbery- he had had
plenty of time to go home and come out again he had been to the station-
house while the others were in custody-he appeared to be in liquor.
Haycock's Defence. I heard a cry of “Police l" met Preston at the bottom

watch ;" a policeman then came up; I followed them to where it was done,
and was talking to some person at the bottom of the court, when the prose-

Flynn's Defence. I am innocent; two days before I was given into cus-
tody I was lying in Jones's bed, when the constable entered the room, and if
I had been in trouble why did not he take me then I never saw the prose-
cutor in my life; I did live in the same house, but not in the same room;
when the prosecutor was at the station-house he only said, "I think I know
you."
Mark Looms re-examined. I was at the house three days after the robbery,
and saw Flynn; but I knew if I took her the other would escape, so I let
her alone till I could find them both together they made their escape from
that room the same morning-I know that the male prisoners are in the habit
of associating with the females--they are all associates together I believe
Molloy has not been about Westminster more than a month--I have seen
him in company with Doyle, and Haycock also Haycock cohabits with a
Noman who lives in the first floor front-room in that house,
Haycock. I did live in that street, but had left, and went to White Horse-
street; I was in Westminster that night ; I bave seen the other prisoners, but
do not associate with them.

EDWARD PRESTON re-examined. I am certain that Flynn returned into the
room with Molloy after I sent her out for the gin---the gin never came there
was not time for her to bave gone for it-I never expressed a doubt about her
being the person--she called me to the cell door, and said, "Why the b

MULLOY-Guilty. Aged 20.--Confined Eighteen Months,
JONES-Guilty. Aged 24.-Transported for Fifteen Years.
FLYNN—Guilty. Aged 19,Confined Eighteen Months,
HAYCOCK and DOYLE-Not Guilty; but were directed to be delained,
and indicted as accessories, at the next Session.
(The prisoner Jones was stated to have cohabited with Thomas Sale, who was
executed for murder in December last.)
