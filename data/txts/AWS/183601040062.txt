387. THOMAS RAINSBURY and WILLIAM JONES were indicted
for feloniously and burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-hous
of William Henry Gunn, at Lewisham, Kent, about one o'clock in the night
of the 13th of December, with intent to steal, and stealing therein 1
warming-pan, value 18d. 1 kettle, value 1s. 1 powder-flask, value 1a.;
1 cruet frame, value 2s. 1 telescope, value 3s. 1 soldering-iron, vale
6d. 12 lbs. of solder, value 3s. 32 glass bottles, value 4s.; 8 lbs. of
rags, value 2s. and 6 bell-cranks, value 1s.; his property; and that the
said William Jones had before been convicted of felony;-an JOHN
ROBERTS was indieted for feloniously receiving the same on the 14th
of December, well knowing them to have been stolen.
WILLam HENRY GUNn. I have a house on Dartmouth-hill, which is
empty-1 never let it-it was uninhabited on the 14th of December, and
had been so four months- lived in it for the last ten years, till some time
in August-] was not going back to it-I wanted to let it, but had no ser-
vant nor any body there-1 was there the last time about a fortnight before
the 14th of December-I merely went over the bottom part of the house-
I left the doors and every thing secure-I left a good deal of property in it
it was furnished nearly all through-on the 14th of December I beard
of the robbery, and went there that day-I found the place in a complete
state of confusion, and a great deal of property, as stated in the indictment,
gone away from the lower part of the premises-] found, on the 16th, at
Roberts's, at Deptford, a warming-pan, powder-flask, kettle, bell-cranks,
and cruet-frame.
