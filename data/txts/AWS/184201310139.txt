GUILTYConfined Three Months.
778. ANN MARIA EDSON was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
January, 1 gown, value 11. 1 boa, value 158, 1 pair of stoekings, value
6d.; 1 handkerchief, value 18. 1 brooch, value 6d. and 1 sixpence;
the property of George Davidson 1 gown, value 11. 1 bonnet, value 6a.
1 shawl, value 58. 1 sheet, value 1s. 6d.; 3 yards of printed cotton,
value 9d. 1 pair of bracelets, value 58.; 1 pair of gloves, value 1s.
and 1 handkerchief, value 6d.: the goods of Maria Crick; and that she
had been before convicted of felony.
ELIZA DAVIDSON. I am the wife of George Davidson, of Paul-street,
Portman-market-we have lived in the prisoner's mother's house about
nine months. One evening, as I was going home, she came out to me in
the street, and said she had been to her mother's, and she would not take
her in-she said she had been in prison-my sister and me took her in for
the night-next morning we went out, leaving her in the room till we came
home to dinner at one o'clock-I then found the room door open, and she
was gonewe had locked her inside-she had slept and breakfasted with
us-] missed a black dress, a handkerchief, brooch, and a pair of stock-
ings-1 found an old bonnet, handkerchief, and stockings, which belonged
to her, left behind-] gave information to the policeman.
Prisoner. My mother turned me out of doors. I did not know what
to do. I was almost driven mad.
MARIA CRICK. I am single, and lodge with my sister. I missed a
black dress, a bonnet, cap, shawl, handkerchief, pair of gloves, sheet, a piece
of cotton, a pair of bracelets, and a sixpence.
HENRY BILLING (police-constable A 137.) I received information of
the robbery, and a description of the articles lost-I afterwards saw the
prisoner at the bar of the Yorkshire StingoI asked her name-she said,
Miss Edson"- told her I took her in charge, and asked whether the
gown she had on was her own, and at the station she said she was very
sorry for what she had done-I fetched the prosecutrixI charged her
with having Mrs. Davidson's gown on-she had her gown, boa, stoekings,
bonnet, and cap on.
