367. ANN WEST  (50), Was indicted with James Field ( since deceased  ), for stealing one sack of oats of Thomas Stanley Wakeley and others. Second Count—Receiving  the same.
   MR. MEAD  for the Prosecution offered no evidence. NOT GUILTY.
 368. JOSIAH BUTTIFANT  (56), ( whose case was removed from Norfolk ) PLEADED GUILTY, before Mr. Justice Archibald , to embezzling 78 l  . and other sums received by him on account of Frederick Brown and others. He was then tried and convicted of forging and uttering a warrant for 34 l  . 11 s  . 4 d.— Fifteen Years' Penal Servitude , and to pay the costs of the Prosecution .
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, JUNE  7 TH , 1875.
