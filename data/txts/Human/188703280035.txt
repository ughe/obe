   MR. MATTISON Prosecuted ; MR. GEOGHEGAN Defended. CHARLES L'ENFANT. I am a clerk in the office of the London Bank ruptcy Court—I produce the file of proceedings in the bankruptcy of Robert Lewis—the petition is dated 25th October, 1884, the receiving order November, 1884, and the adjudication 29th December, 1884—it does not appear from the file that he has been discharged. GEORGE TAYLOR. I am clerk to Mr. Rosenfelt, who was solicitor in this bankruptcy—the prisoner is the man who was adjudicated bankrupt in December, 1884. WILLIAM MCDIARMID ARMSTRONG. I am manager to Messrs.
