   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT .— Monday, June  16 th,  1851.
   PRESENT  —The Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; Sir JAMES DUKE, Bart., Ald.; Mr. RECORDER ; Mr. Ald. HUNTER ; Mr. Ald. WIRE ; and Mr. Ald. CUBITT .
   Before Mr. Recorder, and the First Jury.
 1240. JAMES ISAAC SIDNEY was indicted for wilful and corrupt perjury; upon which MR. HUDDLESTON offered no evidence. NOT GUILTY.
 1241. JOHN HENRY GUEST, stealing 196 clipping-tools, 2 dye-blocks, 3 clipping-punches, and other tools, value 145 l  .; the property of John Warren .
   The COURT , upon Mr. Robinson's opening, considered that the facts did not amount to a felony, and directed a verdict of NOT GUILTY.
 1242. JAMES HEAD, embezzling 20 l  .; the moneys of James Smith , his master: to which be pleaded GUILTY.* Aged 40.— Confined Six Months .
 1243. JANE BROWN. stealing 2 shoes, value 6 s  .; the goods of Thomas Cove : to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 28.— Confined One Month .
 1244. WILLIAM FINISHER, feloniously assaulting Eliza Middleton , aged eight years, with intent, &c. GUILTY of a Common Assault. Aged 41.— Confined Twelve Months .
