Wheeler-street, Spitalfields. I was present at West Hackney Church, four
years ago, when the prisoner was married to Catherine Butt-I have seen
her since-she is here now.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'BRIEN. Q. Did you know her before her
marriage? 1. Yes, several years I do not know that she had a family
before marriage--they lived together till her confinement-he then left het,
and eighteen months after that she went to live with another man, and has
had two children by him--they are not alive.
ANN WEBB. I was married to the prisoner at St. John's Church,
Waterloo-road, three or four months ago I cannot tell the day-I lived
with him till he was taken.
Cross-examined. Q. He treated you kindly? A. Yes- I was an unfor-
tunate girl-he took me home to his uncle's house, and I lived with him
three nights-his uncle, Mr. Clay, gave him in charge.
ELIZABETH BAOGLEY. I live in King-street, Borough-road. I was present
