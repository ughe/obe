 710. WILLIAM GROSSERT JOHNSTONE  (35) , stealing 3 books value 1 l  ., the property of Robert Wyllie his master.
   MR. SERJEANT BALLANTINE and  Mr. F.H. LEWIS conducted the Prosecution. HERBERT CATLIN. I produce a deed; I witnessed the signature of the prosecutor to it—it is dated the 25th of January, 1858, by which William Grossert Johnstone agreed to be clerk to Mr. Wyllie, at the salary of 140 l . per annum; to devote his whole time to his business, and to account for and pay over all monies that might come to his hand as such clerk, without any deduction whatever.
