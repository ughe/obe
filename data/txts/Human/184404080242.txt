 1322. JOHN JACKSON and DANIEL GLANVILLE were indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 6 d  . the goods of a certain woman, unknown, from her person. JOHN WHITE( police-constable R  180.) On the 9th of April I was at Greenwich-fair—I saw Jackson put his hand into a woman's pocket, take something, and pass it to Glanville—I took them, and found this handker chief crumpled up in Glanville's pocket. JOHN SEAGER. I am a labourer, and live at Deptford. I have been in the police, but am superannuated—I was with White, and saw the prisoners go down the fair in company, about 150 yards—I saw Jackson take some thing from a woman's pocket, which appeared to be a handkerchief, and pass it to Glanville—I seized Jackson—he tried to get from me—I tried to get the woman, but the crowd was so great I could not get at her—I have been getting my living the last six months by attending at the police-court, and going on messages for Mr. Finch or the Magistrate.
   Jackson's Defence.  A boy took the handkerchief from a young woman, and threw it to me; Glanville was not there.
 (Glanville received a good character.)
   JACKSON  — GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months .
   GLANVILLE  — GUILTY. Aged 14.— Confined Ten Days , and Whipped.
 1323. JOHN COOK , JAMES GRACE, and JAMES WILSON, were indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 6 d  ., the goods of a certain man, unknown, from his person. JOHN CARPENTER( police-constable R  84.) I was at Greenwich-fair on the 8th of April—I saw Wilson go behind a gentleman, and take this hand kerchief out of his pocket, and pass it to Cook, under his arm—Cook took it, and put it into his hat, and at I was seizing him, he had hold of another gen tleman's pocket—I called to the first gentleman, but could not get at him—Grace was covering the other two—I had sees them all three together for an hour and a half. GEORGE MARTIN( police-constable R  93.) I saw the prisoners attempt the pockets of twenty different persons during an hour and a half—Grace was covering the others, but Carpenter was before me, so I did not see them take the handkerchief.
   Grace's Defence.  I know nothing of these two persons.
   Cook's Defence.  We had not been down in the fair about twenty minutes.
   COOK  — GUILTY.
   GRACE  — GUILTY.
   WILSON  — GUILTY. Confined Three Months .
