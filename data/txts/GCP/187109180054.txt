sleep in the house-on Monday night, 31st July, I was the last person up,
and before going to bed I saw that the doors and windows were fast-that

three waistcoats, some tobacco, and cigars—these' (produced) are two of
the waistooats-an entry had been made through the tap-room window,
which was wide open in the morning-it had been closed the night before
--the window was cut, to undo the latch, and anyone could get in—the
prisoners were customers, and knew the premises well.

not say I could not swear to the waistcoat.
