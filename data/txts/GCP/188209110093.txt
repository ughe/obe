GEORGE HOWARD. I am assistant to Mr. Digger, pawnbroker, South-
ampton Street, Camberwell—on 19th December the prisoner came to me
with two pairs of scales and offered them in pledge--I told him I should
require a receipted invoice, which he went to get; he came back in a short
time and produced this invoice, which appeared to be receipted-I then
advanced him 31. on the scales-I should not have done so if he had not
brought the receipt.
EDWARD RONALD THOMAS. I am invoice clerk to Mr. Bartlett, at
Bristol — I made out this invoice for Mr. Weaver--when it left our place
there was no receipt to it-I do not know the name of the party receipting
it it is not signed by anybody in our office.
