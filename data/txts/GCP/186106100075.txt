JOSEPH JOEL. I have been a bill-broker and discounter-in 1851 I had
an account at the Henrietta-street Branch of the Commercial Bank-on 30th
July, 1851, I drew a cheque for 5801. ; I do not find that debited to me in
my pass-book--the pass-book correctly represents my account,
COURT. Q. Did you get 5801. on 30th July, 1851, from the Commercial
Bank! 4. No; nor draw for that amount.
Cross-examined. Q. I suppose your only means of knowing that is by the
book ? A. Certainly-I am quite satisfied I did not get it ; I should say I
did not have it, because if I had it would be entered in this book ; independ-
ently of that, I know nothing of it-my account was a pretty fair one at
the bank at that time, a few thousands—I kept another account besides.
