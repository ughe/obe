2528. THOMAS CANEY was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of De-
cember, 7 printed books, value 148., the goods of Elizabeth Tomlin.
ELIZABETH TOMLIN. I keep a circulating library in Merlin-place, Clerken-
well-the prisoner came to me on the 10th of Dec., and had seven volumes.
Cross-examined by MR. JONES. Q. Is Mrs. Scriven your sister? A. Yes
--they are my books, and the premises are hers- I am single--I never saw
the prisoner before that day-persons pay according to the time they keep the
books-there was no time mentioned to return them that I recollect.
Court. Q. What is the usual time? A. A week or a fortnight-we
lend them for six or seven months, if they are going to take them into the
country-he never told me they were for the country-they have not been
returned.
Jane Scriven. The prisoner came for the books on the 10th of Dec.-I
am sure he is the man.
Ann Gunn. I am the wife of Mr. Gunn-I remember seeing the prisoner
that day, I have no doubt about of him.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you seen him before ? A. Yes, two or three times
in Clerkenwell.
Jury to ELIZABETH TOMLIN. In what name did he take the books ? A.
Mr. Scott-there was no deposit paid for them.

Mr. Jones conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY WILLIAM ALBERT. In 1841 I carried on business in London, as
a merchant-I had offices in Oxford-court, Cannon-street, City. On the 19th
of May I left this country-before I left, I engaged the prisoner as my clerk,
at 11. per week--I paid him 21. the day before I went to France-I was away
till August-in the interval some letters passed between me and him-I took
a list of every thing I had in my office--I had a trunk in my counting-house,
containing waistcoats, trowsers, handkerchiefs, stockings, and a ring, and I left
a guitar and time-piece in my private room-I locked the trunk, and put the
key into my cash-box, and the key of my cash-box I took with me-the
cash-box I put into the office-I did not give the prisoner authority to open
the trunk, and take any of the things, or to sell or pawn any-1 returned in
August, and saw the prisoner--he advised me not to go to my office, as I
should be arrested for debt--I saw Duke, who was a friend of mine, and the
prisoner together-I left it to them to try to arrange with my creditors-I
