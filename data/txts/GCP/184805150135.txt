Robert EDWARDS (policeman, K 311.) I was on duty in Mile-end-road,
on Wednesday night, 19th April, and in consequence of what Bartrup told
me I went to Messrs. Charrington's premises, and there found a bundle, con-
taining part of a sheep, quite warm, with a strap round it, which I gave to
Smith -1 afterwards saw the meat fitted to the skin, and it exactly matched.
WILLIAM SMITH (policeman, K 220.) I took King into custody on the
morning of 27th April-1 told him the charge-he said he knew nothing
about it; he would go with me anywhere; he was at home all that week, and
the people in the house knew it; he had not been out one night since last
barvest- produce a strap, which I got from Edwards.

MARY WRIGHT. I live at Playhouse-yard, Whitecross-street. In April
last King lodged at my house--I know this strap to be his; I mended it for
him two years ago; here is my work-he carried it about with him-I lived
with him for four years-on 19th April be went out about half-past nine
o'clock in the morning, and came home about half-past eleven at night.
Tuomas MUMFORD. I am a butcher. On 20th April I attended at Ilford,
when a sbeep skin was shown me by Corbyu-I afterwards fitted it to some
meat produced by Parsons, and it fitted-all the mutton that was produced
formed one carcass-it was not cut in a butcher-like way.
Georox CORBYN ( policeman, K 100.) I received a skin from Payne, the
bailiff, which I gave to Mumford.
