THOMAS FAIRCHILD. I live in Back-road, St. George's-in-the-East. On
3rd Oct., in consequence of something that was said to me, I watched the
prisoner-I saw her leave Mrs. Boyce's bouse at half-past 6 o'clock that
night, with a child in her arms-1 followed her to Florence-road, Deptford-
it was between 9 and 10 at night-she went out of the main road, and
turned into Florence-road, and into a lane-she went about forty yards, and
put the child down, and ran away immediately I went to the place, and
found the child on some bricks, close by the dungheap-it was an unfre-
quented place, and there was no light there--I saw a policeman I took the
child up-it was lying on its back, wrapped in this article, which I believe is
part of a cloak-the policeman took the child—I went with him to the
station-next morning 1 accompanied him, when he apprehended the prisoner
in her mother's house, I think about a mile from where the child was found
- pointed her out to the constable-he asked her if she knew anything
about dropping a child last night in the Deptford-road-she said, “Yes,"
she had done it because she was unable to keep it, and she was sorry for
what was done-she thought the child would not remain there long before it
was picked up.
