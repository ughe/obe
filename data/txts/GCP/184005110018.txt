1329. SAMUEL EAGLE was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of May,
1 101. Bank-note, the property of Arthur Edward Somerset.
ARTHUR EDWARD SOMERSET, Esq. I am a barrister--my chambers
are in Pump-court, Temple-I left them on the 30th of April, about four
o'clock, and left my note book there with a 101. note in it, which I had
received from Messrs. Hoare's, my bankers, two days before I returned
next morning about half-past twelve o'clock the book was there, but the
note gone - I sent to the Bank of England as soon as I got the number-I
did not know the prisoner.
