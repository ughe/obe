I asked if they knew any thing of those hearth-rugs--they said they
knew nothing about them they appeared quite alarmed - I looked over
the rugs, and found some keys on them, between the rugs and the chain

nothing about it--the rugs were piled on the top of the bedstead, and
the keys between them—there was nobody there besides the prisoners.
