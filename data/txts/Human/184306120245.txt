THOMAS SKILLIN , RICHARD SMITH, and SARAH DAVIS, were again  indicted for feloniously assaulting Stephen Skillin , on the   20th of May , and cutting and wounding him on his left arm, with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm. ESTHER HILL. I am the wife of Thomas Hill. On Saturday night, the 20th of May, I heard cries of "Murder," went to the bottom of the court, and took two policemen to Stephen Skillin's house—when the door was opened Davis was on the bed, on the top of Stephen Skillin, beating him with her hand—he was drunk—his wife was lying on the floor—Davis shut the door, and said, "There are only two of those b----s, we will soon do for them"—the other two prisoners were in the room with her—there was a great noise, and the policemen got in—after they were got out there were brick-bats flying—Mrs. Skillin is since dead—she was very ill after this, from a blow she received in the scuffle—her finger was broken. STEPHEN SKILLIN. I was at home on the night of the 20th of May, in bed alongside my wife—my son was out drinking with Smith and Davis, who is my daughter-in-law—she was the instigation of all this disturbance—I was not given to drink, but they coaxed me to come and have some beer with them that night—I was beat and pulled out of bed—I do not recollect being struck or beaten by my son. CAROLINE MACE. I heard Davis beating her father on the bed with her hand in the evening—Thomas Skillin and Smith were not in the room at that time. EDWARD DOWNING. I am a surgeon. I dressed four lacerated wounds on Stephen skillin's left arm.
   DAVIS  — GUILTY of an Assault. — Confined Six Months .
   SKILLIN  and SMITH  — NOT GUILTY.
 2027. THOMAS SKILLIN , RICHARD SMITH, and SARAH DAVIS were again  indicted for stealing, on the   20th of May , 2 shillings, 2 sixpences, 30 pence, and 46 halfpence; the monies of Stephen Skillin . STEPHEN SKILLIN. On the night of the 20th of May I had 7 s . 2 1/2 d . in copper, and 2 s . in silver, and two sixpences wrapped in a piece of paper, which I intended to give my old woman in the morning when she was sober—the 2 s . were black—I put it all in the cellar, behind the water-butt, in the evening—I afterwards went down to look at the rabbits, and the money was gone, but I was so intoxicated I did not know what I was about.
   Skillin. Q.  Did not I ask you for some money to get beer, and did not you say if I went down in the cellar, and looked behind the water-butt, I should find some there? A.  I do not remember, I was so tipsy. CAROLINE MACE. I heard the prosecutor say he should go and feed his rabbits—he went into the cellar, came up, and went in doors—the old lady fell down—I saw the three prisoners go down into the cellar, and come away—I think the old man told them the money was there. NOT GUILTY.
   Before Mr. Recorder.
