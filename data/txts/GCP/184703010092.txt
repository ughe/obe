2 squares, 2s. ; and 1 saw, 18.; the goods of John Hubbard, his master.
John HUBBARD. I am a cabinet-maker, and live in Norfolk-street, Mile-
end. I took the prisoner into iny service as journeyman-he worked with
me till the 8th of Aug., when he left me I then missed some tools--I met
him on the 7th of Feb. in Whitechapel-road-he denied having seen my tools
- I gave him into custody- I have seen three of my tools since at Mr. Dex-
ter's, a pawnbroker's, in Whitechapel-road-I got two of them, this plane
and this square, out of pawn at my own expense- they are mine, and were
amongst the tools I lost.
RICHARD WHITE. I am assistant to Mr. John Creed Dexter, a pawn
broker in Whitechapel-road. These articles were pawned by the prisoner on
the 8th of Aug.
Prisoner. I was in the habit of going to Mr. Dexter's shop for months
before that; this man knows my face: I did not pawn these tools. Witness,
I am positive he is the man- they were stopped a few hours afterwards-
gave a description of the prisoner to the policeman.
