173. GEORGE JOHN PAGE was indicted for stealing 4 feet of brass
tubing, value 4s. 6d.; 2 iron drills, 18.; and 2 iron brackets, 25.; the
goods of the London and South Western Railway Company, his masters.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution. d e
GEORGE WILLIAMS (police-constable V 97.) In the morning of last
Saturday week I went with Dalby and Ratcliff to No. 56, Wickham-street,
Vauxhall - Ratcliff went up stairs and brought the prisoner down-I staid
below-I then asked him to accompany me up stairs while I searched his
bedroom--I found nothing there, the prisoner said, " It is no use looking
here, you will find nothing ; if you don't look into the box that is down in
the back room I will give you a sovereign " I went down stairs where
Dalby and Ratcliff were placed, and found a box there it was locked-1
asked the prisoner if that was his box-he said it was I asked if he
would be so good as to unlock it-he said he had not got the key-1
said I should break it open-I did so, and found three large pieces of brass
tubing, which I produce, and some drills and a screw-wrench were taken
out of the box by Dalby I then took the prisoner into custody,
Cross-examined by MR. NBALE, Q. What was the exact hour when
you searched his bed-room? A. Between seyen and eight o'clock in the
morning-when Ratcliff brought bím, down, I told him what I came for
-I searched the lower part of the house -- there were two rooms in the
lower part--- his wife was there, and two children, I believe there was no
lodger that I know of - I partly searched the rooms -I had previously
gone up stairs with the prisoner, and searched his bedroom-I told him
we had come to take him into custody, for offering, some brass tubing for
sale, and we had come to search his house he then said, “It is no use
your searching here, you will not find anything, if you don't search the
box that is down stairs I will give you a sovereign" - no one was present
then but the prisoner and myself—the sovereign was not given to me-I
had just done searching the room when he said those words-he was pre-
sent when we searched below-I did not find any sovereign on his person,
or in the house the box, was in the back room, which is a kitchen or
workshop-I had been in the house about two minutes before I saw him
coming down stairs.
