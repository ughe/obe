Nuziato Meo, a girl under the age of sixteen years, out of the possession
and against the will of her father. Second Count-Out of the possession of
Gaetano Meo, a person having the lawful care and charge of her.
MR WHITELEY conducted the Prosecution; and MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS
the Defence.

MR. WILLIAMS submitted there was no proof of the taking out of the custody
of the father, and quoted the following authority from Archbold's Pleadings
and Evidence in Criminal Cases: “If the girl leaves her father without any
persuasion, inducement, or blandishment held out to her by the defendant, so
that she has got fairly away from home, and then goes to him, although it may
be his moral duty to return her to her father's custody, yet his not doing so is
no infringement of this statute." MR. COMMISSIONER KERR, having consulled
MR. JUSTICE LUSH, held that there was evidence to go to the Jury,
Not Guilty.
