546. JAMES HUNT (31) and THOMAS GOWLETT (36), Robbery
with violence on Thomas Herbert, and stealing a coat from his person.
Mr. ROACH Proseouted.
THoMas HERBERT. I am a water bailiff of Uxbridge-on May 2nd,
between five and six, I was in the bar of the Swan and Bottle, Uxbridge
the prisoners came in together, and Hunt offered a coat and waistcoat
to anyone who would buy it, as he was hard up-I (bought them for
3s. 6d., and left to go home, and Hunt came up and asked me for the
coat back-they both followed me on to the bridge would not give the
coat up, and Hunt struek me on my eye and knocked me down, and took
the coat from me, and made off with it; Gowlett had it in his hand-
while this was going on Mathews came up and Hunt struck him-I gave
information to the police-this is the coat (produced) : I had bought it
out right-he did not take the waistcoat-my eye was bad for more than
a week, and I lost a great deal of blood.
Cross-ezamined by Hunt. I might have drank out of your beer after I
bought the coat-you knocked me down, and Gowlett took the coat:
Cross-eramined by Gorolett. You did not strike me-I only noticed you
two--there were others on the bridge.
By the COURT. I carried the coat on my arm, and put the waistcoat
in my fish basket.
JomN MATHEWS. I am a boatman-on 2nd May I was in the Swan
and Bottle between five and six o'elock, and saw Herbert there-the two
prisoners same in-Hunt had a coat for sale and a waistcoat afterwards
the sold them to Herbert for 3s. 6d.--they went out, and I followed
them on to the bridge-they came up together, and Hunt said he meant
having the b-- coat-Herbert said he would give it to him if he would
give him the money-Hunt knocked him down and took the coat away,
and gave me a blow-they were both together pulling the coat, which
was in a strap they went away with it.
Cross-azamined by Munt. I drank out of your beer-there was only one
pot--I was not the worse for drink-Herbert went out first, and I fol-
lowed struck me with yourt fist; we were souffling together over
the jacket.
Cross-etamined by Gowlett. There were only us four on the bridge-I
was no worse for drink than I am now.
JOHN GODFREY. I am a labourer-on 4th May, between five and six
o'eloek, I was in the Wagon and Horsespublic-house-the prisoners same
in; they were strangers to me-they said they were hard up-Hunt
pulled his coat off, and I bought it for 2s.-I afterwards handed it to the
police-they said they were going to work, and would give me 4s. for it
back again.
GEoRge WHITLerr (Police Inspector F). On 10th May, after making
inquiries, I went to Chaltham St. Peters, about six miles from Uxbridge,

handed to me on the 12th.
