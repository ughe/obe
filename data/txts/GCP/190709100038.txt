JEAN FRANK DUBOIs, managing clerk to Dodd, Longstaffe, and Co.,
solicitors, deposed that his firm were consulted by the last witness in
June, 1906; they communicated with prisoner, but, receiving no
satisfactory reply, lodged a complaint with the Incorporated Law
Society. An inquiry was held by a committee of that society, and
in the result prisoner was struck off the roll of solicitors by order of
the Court on January 23 last.
