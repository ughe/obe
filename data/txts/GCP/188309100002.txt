THIE
WHOLE PROCEEDINGS
On the Queen's Commission of
OYER AND TERMINER AND GAOL DELIVERY
FOR
The City of London,
AND GAOL DELIVERY FOR THE
COUNTY OF MIDDLESEX, AND PARTS OF THE COUNTIES OF ESSEX.
KENT, AND SURREY, WITHIN THE JURISDICTION
OF THE
CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT.
Held on Monday, September 10th, 1883, and following days.
BEFORE the Hon. Sir WATKIN WILLIAMS, Knt., and the Hon. Sir JOHN
CHARLES DAY, Knt., two of the Justices of Her Majesty's High Court
of Justice; Sir ANDREW LUSK, Bart., M.P., Sir THOMAS SCAMBLER
OWDEN, Knt., and Sir CHARLES WHETHAM, Knt., Aldermen of the said
City; GEORGE SWAN NOTTAGE, Esq., Sir REGINALD Hanson, Knt., F.8.A.,
JAMES WHITEHEAD, Esq., HERBERT JAMESON WATERLOW, Esq., and
HENRY AARON ISAACS, Esq., other of the Aldermen of the said City; Sir
WILLIAM Thomas CHARLEY, Knt., Q.C., D.C.L., Common Serjeant of the
said City; and ROBERT MALOOLM KERR, Esq., LL.D., Judge of the
Sherifs Court: Her Majesty's Justices of Dyer and Terminer, and
General Gaol Delivery, holden for the said City, and Judges of the
Central Criminal Court.
POLYDORE DE KEYSER, Esq., Alderman,
JOSEPH SAVORY, Esq., Alderman,
Sheriffs.
...
MJREHALL PONTIFEX, Esq.,
BEAUMONT SHEPHEARD, Esq.,
Under-Sheriffs.
