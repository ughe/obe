 58. OLIVER KINGSLEY  (34), PLEADED GUILTY to the manslaughter of Eliza Jane Williams . He received an excellent character. Six months' hard labour.
 59. HENRY PAYS  (19) and WILLIAM JONES  (24) ( who is deaf and dumb  ), committing an unnatural crime with each other.
   PAYS PLEADED GUILTY.
   MR. ELLIOTT  and MR. FITCH Prosecuted.
   During the evidence the Court considered that Jones should be tried as to whether he was fit to plead to the indictment. The Jury were therefore discharged from giving a verdict, and were again sworn to try as to whether the prisoner could plead. On the evidence of Dr. James Scott, the medical officer at Brizton Prison, they found that the prisoner was not in a condition to give proper instructions for his defence or to defend himself. To be detained during His Majesty's pleasure. PAYS , against whom a former conviction was proved. Three years' penal servitude.
