Cross-excamined by Mr. SLEIGH. Q. How long had you been trying to
sell this pony and cart before Christmas ? A. About a fortnight or
three weeks—I had had no offer for them-I only offered them for sale-I
have known Bolter a very short time he was sober when he came to me
that morning-it was on the day before Christmas day-when he came
back about dusk, be brought me a piece of paper and a sovereign-I gave
the paper to Mr. Lewis - he did not tell me that he had sold the
pony and cart, he told me that the man had taken it away from him, put
it into a stable, and would not let him have it, and said, "Take this to your
master"-he was quite stupid and intoxicated when he came back-I saw
Day on Christmas morning at the stables in Agar Town-he represents
that he owns stables there, but they are not his premises -a policeman
found him at King-street, Clerkenwell—I was outside he did not tell me
