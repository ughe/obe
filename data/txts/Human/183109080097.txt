 First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Justice Patteson.
 1741.  JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of August , 1 watch, value 3l. 10s.; 2 seals, value 2l. 2s.; 2 rings, value 1l., and 2 keys, value 10s., the goods of William Henry Smith , in his dwelling-house.
   JOHN PHIPPEN. I am a Policeman. On the 4th of August, at half-past four o'clock in the afternoon, I saw the prisoner run out of Mr. Smith's shop, and run along the street - I followed him a long way, and caught him; he had his hand in his waistband - I pulled it out, and he had this watch and seals.
   WILLIAM HENRY SMITH. I am a butcher, and live in Back-road, Shadwell. On the 4th of August, about four o'clock in the afternoon, I went out, leaving this watch in the room behind the shop; I was fetched in about three quarters of an hour, and they were gone - I gave three guineas and a half for the watch four years ago, one for each seal, and 10s. for the keys; one is only gilt.(Property produced and sworn to.) GUILTY of stealing to the value of 99s. only. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.

 1742.  THOMAS WORLEY was indicted for feloniously assaulting James Tulloch , on the 9th of September , putting him in fear, and taking from his person and against his will, two 20l. Bank notes, his property.
   JAMES TULLOCH. I am carpenter to the Jane of London. Last Friday, about five o'clock in the afternoon, I was in company with Ellen Brooks at a house in Glasshouse-street, and asked her to go over the way to a publichouse to get a glass of liquor, instead of which we went to the prisoner's house; I had two 20l. notes in my fob, and a sovereign, a shilling, and a sixpence in my trousers pocket - the prisoner asked me for some gin; I gave him 1s. - he was not satisfied, and I gave him 2d. more, and then 6d.; he did not get any gin; I went to change my sovereign at the public-house - I returned in a few minutes, and he wanted me to give him money again; I would not- he went to put his hand into my pocket; I would not let him, and he knocked me down - he fell down on me, and while he was on me took my money; before I could get on my feet he had gone out of the house the back way - I had felt the notes safe in his house, and I felt him take them out; I staid in the house a little time, and said to Brooks, "I am done now, my two notes are gone;" I had received my wages, which came to 58l., about a week before - he was taken on Sunday; I had gone to his house twice that night, but he was out - I was as sober as I am now; I had been to a good many places, but to no bad place till I got to this lane.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Do you know the Compasses? A. Yes, I was there, and had some gin and rum; I cannot say how many glasses I might take - one or two of both, not more, and that was all the day; I had no brandy - I do not think I had any brandy and water, but will not swear it, as I was in the house in the day-time once or twice in the morning, about seven o'clock, and had half a pint of rum then, but only drank one glass; I was at the lodging-house the woman lived at almost all day - I did not go to any other public-house that day; I went there once after seven o'clock, but cannot tell when, and had two glasses of gin; I sent out at the lodginghouse for a good deal of gin, and gave it to the women, but did not take much myself - I spent 1l. at that house, and might have had six glasses myself in the course of the day- I will not swear I did not have a dozen; I did not fall down drunk in the street, nor did a sweep pick me up; nothing of the sort happened - I was found asleep in the privy of the house I stopped at, but that was at nine o'clock, after I was robbed; I was not drunk - they said I was asleep; I do not know whether I was - I never used the word rooping, I do not know what it means; I never said I was rooped - I think it was a young woman awoke me in the privy; it was not much after five o'clock when I went to the prisoner-house - I left my hat in his house, not at the public-house; I did not stay there five minutes after I was robbed - it was in a bed-room down stairs; I found my hat there next day - I had only been with one woman that day; I do not know how many were in the house - I treated about a dozen.
 COURT. Q. You treated women in the house on that day? A. Yes, I sent out for liquor for them from time to time.
