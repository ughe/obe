 1538. FREDERICA SOPHIA AUGUST was indicted for stealing on the   2nd of June , 3 half-boots, value 6 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of James Parsons . REBECCA PARSONS. I am the wife of James Parsons, a shoemaker. Susan Treacher is a customer of ours—on the 2nd of June, the prisoner, who had lived with her, came ask for three pairs of boots for Mrs. Treacher—I gave her three right-footed boots, for patterns—I did not sell them—these are them—they were new when I sent them out—they now appear to have been worn. JAMES PARSONS. These are my property—the prisoner's father brought them to my shop. SUSAN TREACHER. The prisoner lived with me, but was not in my service on the 2nd on June—she left the Saturday before—I never sent her for these boots. GUILTY. Aged 17— Judgment Respited.

I am single, and live in Charterhouse-lane.
