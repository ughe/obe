HENRY ELLISTON HUMPHRIS, bookseller, Norwich. I advertised in
the "Publishers' Circular" on July 18, 1908, “Harmsworth Self-
Educator” at £2 5s., received a postcard from "R. E. King and Coin
wholesale and export booksellers, 4, Eagle Street, Holborn," ordering
same to be sent by goods train. I forwarded the books, sent the bill,
have applied repeatedly for payment, which I have not received, and
I communicated with the police.
Cross-examined. I deal in old and new books. The "Publishers
Circular" is chiefly advertisements of books for sale.

me and said he wanted an address near the Law Courts as he was
carrying on a lawsuit, and I allowed him to use my room for a pay.

understood he was a wholesale bookseller. On the door there was a
