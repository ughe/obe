 1895.  WILLIAM SMITH, alias    SAMUEL ASHFORD, was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of September , 7 dwts. and 17 grs. of gold, value 20s., the goods of Francis Carpentier, his master.
 FRANCIS CARPENTIER. I am a  goldsmith, and live in Clerkenwell - the prisoner was in my employ. On the 13th of September he came to dinner as usual, but did not return to work - I found him in custody on Saturday. Mr. Simons produced some gold, which I compared with part of a diamond ring - they appeared to be the same; here is a piece which corresponds with it.
 Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. Was this standard gold? A. No, but it is jeweller's gold.
   JOHN JAMES SIMONS. I am a jeweller, and live on Clerkenwell-green - I saw the prisoner at my house on Thursday afternoon - he put these pieces of gold into the scale, and asked me to buy them. I asked where he got them; he said his mother gave it to him, that his name was William Smith, of Barbican-court, and that he worked at a rule makers; I said it had come from a goldsmith's, and if he did not tell me who his master was, I would give him in charge; he said he worked for no master - I then took him to the officer.
   JAMES WALBANCK. I am in the prosecutor's employ, and know this gold - I gave the prisoner part of it to work.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   GEORGE WADDINGTON. I took him in charge.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 13. Recommended to Mercy. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 1896.  GOODMAN SAUNDERS and JOSHUA LYONS were indicted for stealing, on the 11th of July , 1 watch, value 3l.; 1 chain, value 1l., and 2 keys, value 5s., the goods of George Russell , Esq. , from his person.
 GEORGE RUSSELL. ESQ. On the 11th of July I was in  Hyde-street, near Manchester-square, and lost my watch from my fob - I did not see it taken; the two prisoners came up to me in company; and one of them held up some lemons to sell, and at that time I missed my watch and key they were gone in a minute. I looked for it on the ground, but it was not there - Saunders held the lemons up almost close to my eyes - I had seen Lyons with him.
 Cross-examined by MR. BARRY. Q. Is your sight not defective, Sir? A. Yes; I missed it directly they were gone; nobody else had come against me - it was in my waistcoat pocket - it was safe a few minutes before - nobody came near me but them. I advertised it as lost, and offered 15l. reward for it.
   JOHN FORRESTER. I am a City officer. I received information of this, and took up Saunders.
   JOSEPH TAYLOR. I am a City officer. I received information, and apprehended Lyons, who I know goes about with lemons and oranges.
   ABRAHAM COHEN. I am a confectioner, and live in Petticoat-lane. On the 11th of July, about seven o'clock I saw Lyons give a watch into the hands of Henry Levy; he looked at it, went into his house, and returned with some sovereigns, which he gave into the hands of Saunders - they then all three went into a public-house, at the corner of Arrow-alley, Petticoat-lane. I heard some silver ringing - I went to Taylor's house, but he was out; and at night I saw him and gave him information.
 Cross-examined. Q. Have you known Taylor long? A. Yes; I knew he was at Aldgate watch-house - I was never locked up there; I have sometimes been out late, and Taylor let me in there; that is not once in a year - I do not go by the name of Tyo; they call my brother so. I work for my brother - I knew the prisoners by sight - I knew nothing of a reward till afterwards; I expect nothing - I do it for the good of my country - Levy lives at No. 1, Petticoat-square.
 SAUNDERS -    GUILTY. Aged 20.
 LYONS -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined Six Months.
 1897.  JAMES WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of September , 1 umbrella, value 5s., the goods of Thomas Hartley.
 THOMAS HARTLEY. I  mend umbrellas in the street. On the 14th of September I was in a skittle-ground near Frederick-street, St. Pancras- the prisoner was also there - he went away about eight o'clock, and soon afterwards I missed the best umbrella out of my bundle. I followed the prisoner and took him, and found the duplicate of it on him.
   DANIEL SALKELD. I was in the skittle-ground, and saw the prisoner take the umbrella from the bundle - I thought he was an acquaintance, but as soon as he got out he ran away with it - we afterwards pursued, and found him with the ticket; the pawnbroker is not here.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Fourteen Days.
