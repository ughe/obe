Fifth Jury, Before Edward Bullock, Esg.
419. MICHAEL WALDRON and THOMAS COCKLING were in-
dicted for stealing 48 spoons, value 38., the goods of Susannah Hyams; and
that Waldron had been before convicted of felony b
o l
SUSANNAH Hrams. I am a widow. I keep a general sale-shop at No. 135,
Whitecross-street-on Tuesday evening, the 29th of Dec., I was standing at
my door between seven and eight o'clock-I saw the two prisoners walk back-
wards and forwards--I told them to go away-they would not, and at last
they were very saucy-I thought they were gone, and I went in doors for
about ten or fifteen minutes-1 was then called by Bryant-I went to the door
to look for the prisoners, but they were gone, and I missed between three
and four dozen spoons, which had been lying on a table, amongst other
goods, about a yard inside the shop - I am quite sure it was the pri-
soners I saw-I have seen them in the street, and knew their persons-I
can speak positively to them-I went to my door about ten minutes after-
wards, and saw the two prisoners together-Cockling called to the other, “Here,
Mike, here she is" --Cockling ran off, I ran after him as fast as I could, but
could not catch him-in coming back I caught Waldron, and gave him into
custody-that was about twenty minutes after I lost my spoons-he was
taken to the station, and in coming back I met Cockling I said, " I have
caught you, what have you done with my spoons ?"-he said, “What do
you mean?"-I said, "You said, Mike, there she is,'"-he said, "I don't
know a boy of the name of Mike."
