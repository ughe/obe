596. THOMAS SMITH, ELLEN COURTS, and HENRY HALL
were again indicted for unlawfully and maliciously committing damage in
the house of Frederick Leaf.
FREDERICK LEAT. (This witness's evidence in the former case was read.)
That is all true-Hall threw glasses at me, and then put his hand in the
till the doors and shutters were burst in and broken-I did not see
Smith do anything except rob the till—I had 61. or 72. worth of glass
broken.
Courts. Q. Why did you not give me in charge when I returned from
the hospital ? A. Because there was not a policeman near-I am sure
you turned on the taps—I lost twenty or thirty quarts of spirits.
