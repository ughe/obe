1453. CATHERINE DENNY was indicted for unlawfully, malici-
ously, and feloniously, assaulting Bridget Santry, on the 15th of March,
and cutting and wounding her on the head, the left side of her face, and
left hand, with intent to maim and disable her.--2nd Count, stating her
intent to be to do her some grievous bodily harm.
BRIDGET SANTRY. I am the wife of Dennis Santry, and live at Rother-
hithe, in the same house as the prisoner, and Margaret Mulchannaugh.
About half-past seven o'clock in the morning of the 15th of March I was
lying on my bed—I heard the prisoner outside at the foot of the stairs,
calling me by an expression not fit to mention—in the course of ten mi-
nutes I heard her again addressing me with the same kind of expression as
before I went down stairs, and knocked at her door, and told her I
wanted to speak to her the second time I spoke she opened the door, and
let me in- I told her she deserved a good hiding for her filthy language to
me—she told me to hit her-I said I did not feel myself able to do it, but
I was, she deserved it for her abuse to me—she took hold of my hair
with her left hand, took her right hand from behind her back, and I felt
her stab me on the left side of my head with a knife, but I did not see the
knife till after Mrs. Mulchannaugh came up and took it out of her hand-
I was standing up at the time directly I saw the blood over me, I got so
weak I fell down directly-I had two wounds and a scratch-T put up my
left hand in my defence, and it cut through my fingers-I felt pain from
the cuts, and was very unwell indeed-I was attended by a surgeon for
ten days—the blood vessel then burst out again, and I sent for the doctor,
who is attending me still.
