. Tell us what you saw on the Thursday ? 4. I and my brother were
coming from chapel and saw M'Bride and Greeney go to Frame's shop with
the copper--they staid in about five minutes, they then came out, and all
three of the boys went in with the lead, which they fetched from a watering-

morning, about half-past nine, I told an officer, and I was called up at half-
past two on Saturday morning by M'Culloch, and went to the station.
