 RICHARD GRAYDEN. I am a brick-maker, and live at Lambeth. The witness Peel is my brother-in-law; I would not believe him on his oath.
 MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. Did he marry your sister, or you his? A. I married his - I do not live with her; I allow her a maintenance; I married her in 1818 - I live with no woman now; a woman lives with me.
 COURT. Q.How long have you been on bad terms? A. I have not spoken to him for nearly two years; I do not know where my wife lives - I pay the parish 3s. 6d. a week for her, and I have my six children at home to maintain; I think I heard Peel examined in a Court of Justice on the 30th of June, 1830 - I did not contradict what he swore.
   ISAAC LATIMER. I was apprenticed to Peel - I would not believe him on his oath.
 MR. ADOLPHUS. Q.Have you heard him examined in Courts of Justice? A. I have once, at Union-ball, about obscene prints; and once at the Court of Requests, where he recovered a debt.

 1482.  JOHN MEAKIN was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of June , 2 shirts, value 12s., the goods of William Terrier.
 WILLIAM TERRIER. I lodge at  Boston-place. The prisoner lodged in the same room; I missed two shirts on the 23rd of June.
   GEORGE ADNUM. I am shopman to a pawnbroker in Upper Park-place; I have a shirt pawned by the prisoner on the 18th of June, in the name of John Williams - he came again on the 21st, and had a fresh advance on it.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. It is my first offence, and I hope you will be merciful.
     GUILTY. Aged 29. - Confined One Month.
 1483.  WILLIAM KNIGHT was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of June , 2 shillings, and 4 sixpences, the monies of Eliza Wilkins.
 ELIZA WILKINS. I  keep a shop in Arthur-street, Chelsea. The prisoner came there with another man on the 27th of June - they had some beer, and then asked for some tobacco - the other man asked for a light; I went up stairs, and while there I heard footsteps - I came down; the prisoner and his companion were then gone - I looked in the bar, and missed, I think, about 4s., but I am positive of 2s. - I had seen it when I gave them a sixpence and 4d. change just before.
 Prisoner. Q.Can you swear you saw me take the money? A. No - I saw you coming from the bar as I came down.
   CHARLES HUGHES. I am an officer. In consequence of an alarm from a man named Gamble, I stopped the prisoner five or six hundred yards from the prosecutrix's house - he turned and said, "I will give up to you, but I know that man is not an officer;" I was going to search him - he threw down 18d., and said, "This is my 18d., if she can swear to it, but I am d-d if she can;" he said he came out for a stroll, and begged me to return him 6d. for drink.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was not in the bar at all; the other man left before this woman came down - I am innocent.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. - Confined Three Months.
