and metropolitan departments, in addition to the one I have spoken of-
I have been many years at the Post Office--I should not call it a rare
occurrence for a telegram to be sent to the wrong department-it happens
every day-a message for the London department, if delivered at the
Belfast table, could be sent upstairs to the London department by hand,
but that would not be the ordinary course-if it were taken upstairs
irregularly by hand the messenger would probably take it to the person
