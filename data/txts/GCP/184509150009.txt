Cheapside. On Friday, the 29th of Aug., I paid the prisoner 2s. for bread
supplied by Mr. Newman-I think I paid him about half-past eight
o clock in the morning.
Joun KERSHAW (policeman.) I took him into custody.
Mrs. COULLING cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. When was the
prisoner given into custody? A. On the evening of the 29th of Aug.-he
came home from his first round about half-past nine o'clock in the morn-
ing-he usually went out between eight and nine-we booked the bread as
usual on his return, and he said Mrs. Sparkball had not paid, on the
Thursday or Friday, and I put that down-I cannot tell how much he
paid for Mrs. Sparkhall altogether on the Wednesday-I think it was for
about two quarterns.
Court. Q. What wages had he? 4. I believe 168. a-week—they
were paid on the Saturday night-he always kept his accounts very cor,
rectly up to this time-there is no other charge of embezzlement against
him-his last week's wages have not been paid--118. is now due to him.

1801. MARY HORROGAN was indicted for feloniously assaulting
Ellen Welch on the 26th of Aug., and cutting and wounding her on the
forehead and face, with intent to do her some grievous bodily harm.
ELLEN Welch. I am the wife of Michael Welsh, and live at No. 20,
Great Saffron-hill. On Tuesday morning, the 26th of Aug., I went to the
prisoner's room, and knocked at her door-she told me to come in-I bid
her good morning, and she bid me good morning- I said, “Mary, you
have served me very pretty for the last two days, and abused me most
shamefully, and I do not deserve it from you: I have always been a good
friend to you"-she said, "Yes, that I acknowledge"-I said, "You said
yesterday you would rip my bowels out, and I don't deserve such state-
ments as you have made for the last two days; if you are a better woman
than me, come down into the court if you like, and we will decide it with-
out any further variance" --she said, "Go out of my room," and seized the
chopper from the corner of the room-I said, "I did not come into your
room to injure you"-she took the chopper, and immediately was going
to hit me-l caught the chopper and took it out of her hand, and
while doing so she made several blows at my shoulder-I said, "Take
care of the child"-she had her child in her arms-I took the chop-
per out of her hand and laid it on the table-Mary Leary, who was
in the room, took it off the table, and put it in one corner under-
neath the cupboard--the prisoner then ran and took either a jug or a basin
and threw it, but it did not touch me--she then took the chopper again
