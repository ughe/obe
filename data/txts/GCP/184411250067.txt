Q. Who was it thrown by? A. There was nobody in the room but Lynch
-Brugger then looked round the doorway, and another last came, which

fortune since June-my husband frequently lent him money.
MR. RYLAND. Q. When did you first tell this horrible story? A. At
Guildhall-I was examined there--when I saw this begin I called for
somebody to fetch the police---my little girl went up the court, but there
was no policeman-I screamed in the room-I did not go down stairs till
the police came- I returned to the room after taking the baby out, and did
not leave again- I was not absent a minute—there was nobody else in the
room but Brugger and Lynch.
