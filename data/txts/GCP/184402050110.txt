627. GEORGE MAWBEY was indicted for stealing 2 metal cocks,
value 9s., the goods of the Gas-light and Coke Company, his masters.
MATTHEW GARDNER. I live in Garden-row, London-road, Southwark. 1
am assistant store-keeper to the Gas Company-I have always understood it
to be the Chartered Gas Company-their works are in Horseferry-road—We
have an act to authorize the company to sue and to be sued in this name-
the prisoner was in their service as a gas-fitter-I missed some brass cocks out
of the store-room under my charge, and I had the remainder counted, but I
did not count them myself-they were lost from a room to which the pri-
soner had access, to get meters-be applied to me on the 12th of Jan, for a
meter, and I allowed him to go into the store-room for it-upon his coming out
I caused the cocks to be counted, and supposing that they were correctly
counted, I missed two-I called bim, and told him I missed two, and he said
he had not got them--the warehouseman asked if he had got them about him
--the prisoner said, "No"-it was proposed to search him, and I sent for a
policeman-before he had time to arrive, the prisoner pulled two brass cocks
from his coat-pocket, and put them on a shelf-he said he did not know
what induced him to take them.
Cross-examined by Mr. WILDE. Q. The prisoner has been in the employ.
upwards of nine years? A. I believe so, and during that time has given every
satisfaction-he has borne an unexceptionable character.
John Cutlippe police-constable B 153.) I received charge of the pri-
sor.er at the gas-works-he said he was sorry for what he had done, and he
did not know what induced him to take them.
Not Guilty.
