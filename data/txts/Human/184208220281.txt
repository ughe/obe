JOHN BROWN re-examined . I do not believe I was a yard from the pri soner when the stone was thrown—he had on a white smock-frock and a cap—I have not the least doubt of his throwing the stone—I saw one other man throw a stone a few yards before I came up to the prisoner—the pri soner drew his hand so near my face that I could not help seeing him—I was passing along to leave the ground. JOSEPH HOLDSWORTH re-examined . I parted with the prisoner at six o'clock that evening—he had a shiny cap and smock-frock on—it was not white—he had been working in it for a month or so.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY  on the 2nd Count.— Strongly recommended to mercy on account of his good character.— Confined Three Months.
