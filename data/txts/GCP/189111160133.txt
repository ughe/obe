70. BENJAMIN WILLIAMS and DENNIS COLLINS, Robbery with
violence on Edward Baker, and stealing part of his watch-chain.
MR. GREENFIELD Prosecuted.
EDWARD BAKER. I am a fishcurer, of 87, Tabard Street, Borough-
on 8th October I was in the prisoners' company, and about ten a.m. I went
into a urinal in Chapel Court-as I was coming out Williams seized me
by the throat, and said, “I have got you"-I said, “What are you
going to do ?"—he said, "I am going for them "--I said, “For what?”
-- he said, “I am going for your lot" -I struggled with him, and pushed
him out, and partly got out myself-he said, "Now then, come on,"
and he and Collins rushed at me and shoved me back again-I struggled,
and forced myself out into the open place, and should have got away but
for Collins crossing and preventing my getting away-Williams ran at
me, and said, "I will have you now"-he tried to get my watch and
chain, and seized me by the collar-I put my hand up to prevent him,
and he said, "Leave go "--I said, "I will not "_he said, I will gnaw
your hand off if you don't," and he began biting my hand-I
struggled with him, and got hold of his collar; someone behind pulled
my arm away--my hand was bitten-I halloaed out “Police ! ^ and
"Murder!” as loud as I could-Oollins, I am pretty near sure it was,
came and kicked my legs, and I fell, and as soon as I fell someone else
kicked me in the ribs-I struggled as well as I could, but I seemed to
lose all power, sense, and everything—this (produced) is a piece of my
chain; these are my watch and locket.
