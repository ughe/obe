PAUL CRONIN (policeman, C 158). I was on duty in Piccadilly on 22nd
Jan. I tried Mr. Skeffington's door two or three times in the course of the
night, and at three o'clock it was fast-I tried it again at five, and found it
was unfastened- I called Mr. Skeffington up-I took Lovell into custody
the same morning.
