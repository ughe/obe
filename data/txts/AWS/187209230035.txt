bad state-| thought all along that it would not live, it looked dying--she
Was not altogether a good mother to it, the man treated it well in regard
of buying it little nourishments, eggs, and a little wine, and arrowroot, and

but as for the mother I can say nothing for her-I have not seen her give
it food, without they were sitting together; but she gave no attention to it
-they seemed to have comfortable food-I think the child was greatly
neglected before this man took to the child; I don't think it was kept
without food.
