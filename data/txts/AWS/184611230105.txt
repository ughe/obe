148. MARGARET DIXON, MARY ANN FRANKLIN, and MARY
BOYD were indicted for assaulting George William Stripling, and putting
him in fear, and stealing from his person and against his will 1 watch, value
4l. and 1 swivel, 1s. his goods.
GEORGE WILLIAM STRIPLING. I live in Little Queen-street, Holborn.
On the 23rd of Nov.. about twelve o'clock at night, I was at the corner of
Gray's Inn-lane-the prisoners accosted methey all three took hold of me
at once-one of them took bold of my watch, and took it out by main force-
my watch-guard broke-I have got the remainder of it in my pocket now-]
called 44 Police," and held Dixon and Franklin till the policeman came-I am
sure of them-Boyd ran away, but not out of my sight--I can swear she is
one of the three who accosted me-they said nothing to me.
Cross-eramined by Mr. HORRY. Q. What were you doing there A.
I

on my oath I did not go home with her, nothing of the sort-she did not call

where I was-not a word passed-] did not hear one of them say, Why
don't you pay the girl the money ""-they said nothing to me-I had no dis-
pute with them at all-1 had had two small glasses of gin and water, and no-
