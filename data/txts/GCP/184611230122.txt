John PAYNE (police-sergeant B 11.) On the 1st of Nov. I was on duty

was there crying-after she became more collected I asked what charge she

do anything with that---he took another knife off the table and stabbed her
through the arm; that she took the knife outof his hand, and sent for a policeman
-the prisoner answered, "She has taken the knife to me scores of times"-

creature is innocent-he said nothing about the knife-he appeared to have
been drinking-the woman was much excited when she came in- I cannot
say whether she was affected with drink-I had no idea that she was the
worse for drink-the prisoner was decidedly so.
