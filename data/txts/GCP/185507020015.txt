JAMES HOCKINGS (police sergeant, N 44). I was called to go to Mr.
Thwaites's house; I stood by while the prisoner searched the box, she
seemed in a very agitated state-first of all she said there was nothing in
her box, afterwards she unlocked the box, and turned out this towel, and
these handkerchiefs, which were identified- she did not give any exeuse
when she took them out-Mr. Thwaites said, “ That is mine;" and then
the handkerchiefs, she said, “They are mine”--the prisoner did not say how
they got there.
Cross-examined. Q. Where was the prisoner A. Sitting in Mr.
Thwaites's parlour-Mr. Thwaites said he had suspicion he was being robbed
by some one, and he was anxious to look in the prisoner's box to see if there
was anything belonging to him—the prisoner said she was quite willing-
she took the key out of her own pocket.

the prisoner and the other servant-there were not many other things in
the prisoner's box—these things were just in this state,
