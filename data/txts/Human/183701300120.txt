The prisoner was in my service I went to the house of Mr. Marner, in Charles-street, Hatton-garden, tad saw these books on the counter with the covers torn off, except one which had my writing in it—we then went to the prisoner's residence and found the covers at his house—these are the books and covers—they are chiefly novels, part of Sir Walter Scott's, and some others—this is Lucan'g Phar  salia, it has my private mark in it. JOHN PIKE( police-constable F  105.)
