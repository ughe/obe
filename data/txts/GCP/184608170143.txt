Morris Duncan being called, did not appear.

the morning, the prisoner came with the prosecutor she owed me some
money, and came down and gave me 180.she said I was to give her the
key of the room-I saw her with some half-crowns, shillings, and sixpences
-I stopped her coming down stairs with the man's clothes-when I heard
that she had pledged a jacket, I went to see what she had been doing, and
found her with the man's shoes, stockings, and neck-handkerchief. be

1633. ROBERT ROSE and GEORGE HALL were indicted for steal-
ing 2 pence, 6 halfpence, and 3 farthings, the monies of Michael Byrne,
from his person.
MICHAEL BYRNE. I am a pauper in the City of London Union. As soon
as I came out of the gate on Monday morning, the prisoner and seven or
eight others came round me-Rose held my hands, and Hall laid hold of my
pocket, where the halfpence were, and tore it off-they had been in the work-
house and got out before me they go there every night.
WILLIAM GODFREY. I came out of the workhouse that morning, and saw
a lot of boys throwing stones at the prosecutor-the prisoners were there-
Rose said, "He has got half-a-crown in his pocket"-Hall said, "Let us go
and take it from him" -- they went up to him-Rose laid hold of his hands
-Hall tore the pocket off, and took pocket and money away.
