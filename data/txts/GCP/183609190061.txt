2083. WILLIAM GROVE was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of
August, 1 painting and frame, value 68., the goods of Robert Savage.
ROBERT SAVAGE. I am a general salesman, and live in Brick-lane. The
prisoner came, on the evening of the 30th of August, about seven o'clock
- he looked at several paintings, two of which he selected, and said he
had a very good customer for me--they were large, one a painting, and
one a print-one was of Thomas Belcher--he said he should like to
make a present of it to his brother, as he was very intimate with him--he
asked the price-I said I could not take less than a guinea for the two
- he said very well, he would see me the next day, and bring the cus-
tomer-1 had seen him put something into his pocket, which I thought was
his spectacles-I then missed the small painting, and called the boy to ask
him about it-the prisoner went away directly I called the boy I sent
after the prisoner to ask if he had taken it by mistake the boy came back
without it-I ran out after him the policeman saw me, and followed-
came up, and asked if he had taken it by mistake he said no, he would
come back-I said there was no need if he had not taken it-he said “No,
search me" I put my hand into his pocket and took it out-this is it.
JOHN Brooks. I am the officer. I saw the prisoner stopped, and the
picture taken from him.
(The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that he had been drink-
ing, and was unconscious of having the property in his possession.)
