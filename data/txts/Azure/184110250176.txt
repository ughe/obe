2777. ROBERT KING was indicted for stealing, the 26th of sep-
tember, 421bs. weight of copper, value 21.28., the goods of Thomas Crisp,
his master.
WILLIAM THOMAS JOYNER. J am foreman to Thomas Crisp, of Ber-
mondsey—the prisoner was in his employ as a blacksmith. I missed
421bs. of copper on the 26th of September—it was sent to repair the Flora
—l cannot swear to the copper produced—I had some of this description,
and to the best of my belief it is my master's—l missed it.
Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Have you reason to know
there was a person who you could not reach by law, who has been seducing
this boy away? A. I have, and am instructed by my master to recom-
mend the prisoner to mercy.
JOHN BUDD HENDLE (police-constable M 254.) On the 25th of Sep-
tember, I met the prisoner with the copper on his shoulder—I asked what
he had got in the bag—he said copper, which he was going to take to Capt.
Turner, and he brought it from the schooner Flora—I went with him to
Mill-pond—I saw where he said he was going, and could find no such
person as Capt. Turner.
Cross-examined. Q. He was taken to the Magistrate? A. Yes—be
made a discovery to me about a Jew named Isaacs, and gave me an account
of where I could find some property of his master's—lsaacs was taken and
allowed to go.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
