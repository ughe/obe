Prisoner's Defence. I was coming through Denmark-street, and heard

WALTER HOLMES re-examined. There was no one else in the street at

JOHN CHILCOT MURDON re-examined. Another man who was outside
the door picked up his hat in the road-he ran one way and the prisoner
another-I had the prisoner by the collar for two minutes before he hit
me the blow-I never lost sight of him-there were thirty or forty people
round the door when he was brought back.

479. THOMAS DURDEN, WILLIAM DICKENSON, and MAT.
THEW TARGETT, stealing a clock, value 121. ; and a glass shade, 12s.;
the goods of Caroline Johnston, in ber dwelling-house.
MR. WILDE conducted the Prosecution.
CAROLINE JOHNSTON. On 30th Nov. last, I lived at 19, Pelham-road,
Brompton. Mary Ann Durden, the prisoner's sister, was in my service-
I had a clock on the mantel-piece in the parlour-I saw it safe last when
I went to bed on the evening of the 29th-it was worth more than 5l.
MARY ANN DURDEN. I was servant to Mrs. Johnston, The prisoner
Durden is my brother-he called on me at Mrs. Johnston's house a little
before eight in the evening of 80th Nov. to fetch some covers to clean
I had occasion to leave the kitchen where the covers were kept, and was
away about five minutes-my mistress had a clock on the mantel-sbelf in
the parlour-I bad seen it on the mantel-piece not long before eight o'clock
that morning-I did not notice it when I came down from my mistress-
I missed it about a quarter of an hour after my brother was gone-nobody
had been in the kitchen or parlour during that time but my brother and
myself when I got down in the kitchen again, I believe my brother was
tying the bundle which contained the covers- I think they were in a dark
shawl.
Durden. Q. What time did I come that morning ? A. I think a little
before eight-I opened the door for you-you went along the passage and
down into the kitchen-I believe I followed you-you asked me what covers
you were to take, and I showed them to you hanging on the wall-my
mistress's bell rang very shortly after, and I took up the hot water-I was
absent about five minutes, as near as I can say I saw nothing inside the
covers when I came down-I was just against the parlour door when you
came up from the kitchen--the parlour door was ajar-I shut the street door
after you-I did not follow you out.
Dickenson. Q. Was I anywhere near the place when you opened the
door ? A. I did not see any one-I did not go out into the street.
