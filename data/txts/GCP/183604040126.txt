973. THOMAS GRANTHAM was indicted for feloniously breaking
and entering the warehouse of Kaufman Meyers, on the 26th of February,
and stealing therein 6 life protectors, value 12s.; 15 winches, value 17. 12.;
and 1 quire of paper, value 58.; his goods.
The same evidence was given in this case as on the prisoner's trial
on the 7th day, when he was convicted of feloniously receiving the above
articles.

that he had been before convicted of felony.

I saw the prisoner near the door of the shop, pulling at some jackets which
hung at the door-post-I called my uncle out-I had seen him before
that, kick some boots which were placed at the door- I have since seen
jacket which hung at the door in the prisoner's possession.
John SAVAGE. I am the last witness's uncle.. About seven o'clock al
night he called my attention to the jackets-I missed one-a policeman
came, and I told him what I had lost-I afterwards went to Aaron's,
pawnbroker, in Whitechapel-road, and saw the prisoner there--a jacket
was produced to me, which was the one I had lost--that was near eight
o'clock - I gave the prisoner in charge, and the policeman kept the jacket.
