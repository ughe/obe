WILLIAM EDMAN( police-constable V  179.) I received information, went to the station, and took this handkerchief from the prisoner's hat—he said he bought it in the New-cut—one corner of it has been hemmed for about an inch, but has been now unpicked.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I had the handkerchief in my hat when I was taken to the station, and I took it to wash myself with before all the policemen, which I should not have done if I had stolen it. NOT GUILTY.

 2578. WILLIAM HANDCOCK was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of June , 20 paving-stones, value 2 l  . 10 s  ., the goods of Joseph Sargent and ano ther, his masters. JOSEPH SARGENT. I am in partnership with Charles Denton. We em ployed the prisoner—I did not authorise him to sell any paving-stones—I have seen some stones since—they correspond with those we had at Paradise-street, but I could not swear to them—I never authorised the prisoner to part with them.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  You were directed to take up these stones? A.  Yes; we had a contract with the parish of Rotherhithe to take them up, and lay down new ones.
   COURT . Q.  Were they under your care and custody? A.  Yes; we were answerable to carry them to a laystall belonging to the commissioners—they had been in our carts—I had not the care, the custody, or the possession of them. NOT GUILTY.
 2579. WILLIAM HANDCOCK was again  indicted for stealing, on the   19th of June , 30 paving-stones, value 1 l  . 13 s  ., the goods of Joseph Sargent and another, his masters. JOSEPH SARGENT. I am in partnership with Charles Denton. We under took to take up the old paving at Rotherhithe, and lay down new—we took up the old ones—we employed the prisoner, but he was not authorised or allowed to sell them—I have seen the stones produced by Mr. Heath and Mr. Miller—they are similar to what were at Rotherhithe. WILLIAM HEATH. I bought about twenty pieces of stones of Mr. Winter, Mr. Sargent has seen them, and I think they are the stones he has iden tified. ROBERT WINTER. The stones I sold to Mr. Heath I bought of the prisoner—I found them at Rotherhithe—I paid him 2 d . a foot for them—they amounted to 1 l . 2 s . 6 d .
   Prisoner.  I had the stuff given me. Witness.  I believe Mr. Nottingham, the commissioner, gave the prisoner some stones; I was present, and believe I heard him give them to him—I told the Magistrate so—Mr. Nottingham, is since dead. NOT GUILTY.
