     GUILTY. Aged 56. - Confined One Month.
 1019.  JOHN JAMES was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of March , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of William Holland , from his person.
 WILLIAM HOLLAND. On the 19th of March I went to  Smithfield to select a horse, and while trying him in a cart I was pushed about, and felt a pull at my pocket - I put my hand, and missed my handkerchief, which I had safe five minutes before; it was then half-past three o'clock in the afternoon - the prisoner was going towards the pens- I followed, and took him; he was doubling up this handkerchief, which is mine.
   THOMAS BROWN. I saw the prisoner take the handkerchief from the prosecutor on the 19th of March - he rolled it under his apron, walked away, and the prosecutor took him with it.
 Prisoner. It was ten minutes before they took me.
 MR. HOLLAND. I took him directly they came to rescue him - one man said he was an officer, but I would not let him go.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Transported for Life.
 1020.  JAMES JEFFERY was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of Robert Penfold.
 ROBERT PENFOLD. On the 15th of April, about ten minutes past seven in the morning, I was unloading a coal-waggon in  Tower-street; I took off my hat, and my handkerchief was in it - I put it on the premises while I went down with a sack of coals; when I came up a gentleman's son said, "Either you, or your mate, have lost a handkerchief;" and he showed me the prisoner in Mark-lane - there were four persons in company; he was the stoutest of them - I took him, and found my handkerchief in his bosom; this is it - I know it by a darn, and by the pattern: I gave it to the officer - it was as I had pulled it off my neck; I suppose I found it on him in about twenty minutes after I first put it down - we had unloaded one waggon; the prisoner's companions had parted from him, before I got up to him.
 Prisoner's Defence. It was given me by the three men, who told me to put it round my neck; I put it in my bosom, and gave it to the gentleman.
   JOHN THOMPSON. I took charge of the prisoner - he said it was given to him by one of the three men he had come from Greenwich fair with.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1021.  ANN THOMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of March , 14 sovereigns, and three 10l. notes, the property of Richard Heaven , from his person; and GEORGE THOMPSON was indicted for feloniously receiving the same, knowing them to be stolen.
   RICHARD HEAVEN. On the 15th of March I had three 10l. notes and fourteen sovereigns in a purse in my breeches-pocket - about ten o'clock at night I met Ann Thompson in Thames-street, and went with her down an alley leading round to the Shades; we stopped two or three minutes at the bottom of the alley, and she gave me a slap on the face - my hat fell off; I caught her shawl and the shawl, came off - my purse was gone; I observed her particularly, and can swear she is the person - she got away; the officer has part of the money, and the pawnbroker has a 10l. note - I could swear to the purse, but that has not been found.
   JOHN HACKETT. I was sent for from the Grapes public-house between ten and eleven o'clock - the prosecutor said he had lost 44l., and he gave me this shawl, which he said he took from this woman's shoulders; I knew I had seen this woman with it commonly in the street, and I went with my partner the next day to a lodging where she had lived, but we could not find her - I went to a pawnbroker, and said if a woman named Thompson came there they were to stop her; I know nothing of the man.
   WILLIAM SOWERBY. I am a pawnbroker. I have a 10l. note, which I got from the female prisoner early in the morning of the 16th of March, she offered it to pay for some duplicates - having heard of this robbery; I sent for an officer and detained her.
   WILLIAM LAND. I got change of a check for the prosecutor on the 8th of March, in three 10l. notes and twenty sovereigns; I went to the banking-house on the 16th of March and got the numbers of the notes.
   MARY WEBB. I am niece of Mr. Rushmore, who keeps the White Bear. On the 16th of March the male prisoner came and had some liquor, and left two 10l. notes and nine sovereigns with me - he said it was a legacy left him in the country; he went out returned, and asked for them, and I gave them to him.
   DAVID HEALEY. On the 16th of March information was given at our office; I went and found the male prisoner at No. 73, Lambeth-street, in the privy - I was about to search him, and he dropped two 10l. notes and nine sovereigns; a person who is not here picked them up and gave them to me - he said they did not belong to him, and he knew nothing about them; I then searched him and found 18s.6 1/4d. on him, and 16s. 6 1/4d. on the woman.
   THOMAS RIGG. I am cashier at Key and Co.'s, I paid the witness three 10l. notes, Nos. 2882, 2883, and 2884; dated the 13th of February, 1820.
   WILLIAM LAND. I gave the prosecutor the same notes which I had from the banking-house.
 MR. HEAVEN. I lost the same notes as I got from Land these are them.
 Ann Thompson's Defence. I never set my eyes on the man till I was at Worship-street; I picked up that money on the pavement as I was returning home after selling my goods, I kicked against it, and then picked it up; there were nine sovereigns and three notes - I could not read, I went home and then went to the public-house; I told my husband I had picked up some money - I went to the pawnbroker's next morning to redeem some clothes with the note, ignorant like; the 16s. 6d. I had taken for my goods, I never had that shawl.
   George Thomspon's Defence. My wife came home and said,
 "I have been picking up a lot of money." and I found it was three 10l. notes and more sovereigns; the 18s. 6d. found on me was for three pairs of water-proof boots I had made.
   ANN THOMPSON-   GUILTY. Aged 26. Transported for Life.
 GEORGE THOMPSON -    GUILTY. Aged 24. Transported for Fourteen Years.
