WILLIAM CAIN. I live in Maypole-alley, High-street, Southwark; I am
a bell hanger. On Wednesday evening, 15th Nov., between 6 and 7 o'clock,
I went into M'Enoff's house, which is opposite to mine-I there saw my
cousin Kelly, and Frances M Enoff, and John M Enoff-I was given in
charge for a deserter, but I found it out, and I went into their house, and I

and I suppose she thought I came in for a row she up with a knife, and
stabbed me in the lip—the husband of the young woman put his hand round
me to hold me, and Frances struck me in the head with a knife; that wound
bled—I do not know; I suppose he was trying to take his wife away-he
struck me several times with a soldering iron, made out of a penny piece-he
was trying to keep his wife and daughter away-I do not wish to press the
charge against him.
Q. Was that all that happened? 4. Yes; he struck me with the sol-
