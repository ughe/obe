1700. MARY COSTER was indicted for stealing 5 shirts, value 21. ;
1 blanket, 2s. ; 1 coat, 10s. ; 1 scarf, 28.; and 1 fork, 6d.; the goods of
Charles Butcher, her master.
MR. PARNELL conducted the Prosecution.
CAROLINE BUTCHER. I am the wife of Charles Butcher, of Cheynes-
street, Tottenham Court-road. The prisoner was in my employ about two
months—the last time was about six weeks ago--I employed her as a char-
woman, to light the fire and clean the place the last time she was there I
missed a sovereign-shortly before her leaving I missed two shirts—I dis-
charged my other servant because I thought she was dishonest—the next
time I saw the prisoner after she left, I went to ask her to come to cook a
supper sbe was in liquor, and her husband would not let her come-sbe
ran away from her husband-on the 15th of Aug. I saw her-she said,
" Mrs. Butcher, how do you do?"-I said, " Very well, you are the person I
want "-she first seized me by the neck, she then let go her hold and ran up
stairs—I followed her, and sent for a policeman, and gave her into custody
next day I searched her premises with the policeman-I found a shirt on her
husband's back, marked with my husband's name- I found a coat hanging up
which I had not missed, and a black scarf in a band-box, a fork, and a blanket
on the bed-they are my husband's-I had not given the prisoner any of
these things--this is the coat-these are my articles.
Cross-eramined by MR. MELLOR. Q. This is an old coat? 4. Yes -
my husband worked about in the house in it-I know the shirt by the
name on it-it is old, but it is of use I was married at Northampton-I
have not been married twice--I have been married to Mr. Butcher between
three and four years, I have never been separated from him I had not em-
ployed any person but the prisoner as a charwoman-I gave her the gown
she has on now, and a bed-quilt-I have sold furniture to Mr. King, a broker,
with my husband's permission-I believe the prisoner lives with Mr. King-
my husband has not been in the habit of selling things- I have not given
anything away within six months, I gave her three blankets to wash, which
came home-this shirt is the worst that was found.
MR. Parnell. Q. At the time this shirt was missed other shirtswere
missed ? A. Yes, four-they were good ones- this is an old one-my hus.
band was not before the Magistrate-the prisoner stated that I gave her the
things—if Mr. Butcher had sold either clothes or furniture to Mr. King I
should have known of it.
MR. MELLOR. Q. There was some quarrel between you and the prisoner ?
4. Yes, I went to her house-she has not been living with her husband ever
since she left me I went after her several times the door was always locked
-she was away for six weeks from her lodging.
WILLIAM WILLIS (police constable E 64.) I took the prisoner- I went
with Mr. Butcher the next morning to the prisoner's lodgings, and found
these articles.
GUILTY. Aged 39.---Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor.-Confined
Three Months.
