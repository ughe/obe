Mr. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to the Mint. These half.

EMMA WINToN. I live with my uncle, Henry Luke Winton, who keeps
the Crown public-house, in High-street, Borough. On the Saturday be-
fore Christmas-day the prisoner came there, about ten minutes after two
o'clock in the afternoon, for half a quartern of gin-I served her-it came

out-the half-crown laid on the counter till Robert Nicholson came in,
and he said it was a bad one- saw it till he took it and said it was bad-
he wrapped it in a piece of paper, and gave it to me-I showed it to
Henry Luke Winton, and he gave it to Henry Winton, who gave it to me
again-] put it into my pocket-] gave it again to Henry Luke Winton,

RoBERT NICHOLSON. I saw the half-crown on the counter-I saw it

AMELIA RANDALL. My brother-in-law, Mr. Yearwood, keeps the Dun

payment a shilling-] thought it was a bad one, and I gave it to Mr. Year
wood-the prisoner was taken into custody.
