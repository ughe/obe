   Prisoner. Q.  Will you swear that I threw anything into the water closet? A.  I will; you took it from your pocket, one was a bill, and the other a piece of paper with a black lead address on it—you ran from my shop as fast as you could for a short distance, but I was too quick upon you—you talked to me in good English—you signed the name to the note directly you placed it on the counter, not after you returned to the shop. JOSEPH DEEBLE( policeman, H  195). I took the prisoner into custody—I asked him where he got the note from—he said from a friend in Liver pool, on the Friday or Saturday previous—I asked him whether he had ever been in London before—he said not before that morning, he came from Liverpool that morning.
   Prisoner. Q.  Could I talk English well? A.  Very well; I could under stand you perfectly—I cannot swear that you understood me—you answered what I asked you. REBECCA PRIOR. I live at No. 19, Little Alie-street. The prisoner lodged with me from 7th July, till about the 21st, when I missed him.
   Prisoner. Q.  Do you recollect a letter coming? A.  Yes, which I gave to you—I did not miss you directly after the receipt of that letter—I saw nothing wrong about your person or with me. JOSEPH BUMSTEAD. This is a forged note; it seems to be an impression from the plate produced in the last case.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was discharged at Plymouth, and went to Liver pool, where I received my money, and bought some things; I must have got the bad note there, or some one cheated me in some way with it. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined One Year .
