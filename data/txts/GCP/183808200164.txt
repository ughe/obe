the 1st of June, 6 shirts, value 11. 12s., the goods of James Adams.
JAMES ADAMS. I live in Portman-mews, and am a cow-keeper. The
prisoner came to live with me in May, and on the 3rd of June I missed
these six shirts-(luoking at them.)
WILLIAM THOMAS BARTON. I live with Mr. Cotterell, a pawnbroker.
in Oxford-street. I have these shirtsI do not know by whom they
were pawned, but on the day the prisoner was committed, he came to our
shop and said he had lost the duplicates of two parcels of shirts which be
had pledged-he saw these shirts, and said he had pledged them.

August, 16 sheets of paper, value 1s., the goods of Barclay Farquharson
Watson, from the person of Robert Johnson.
ROBERT JOHNSON. I live in Lion-street, Kent-road. On the 16th of

have been taken out by Mr. Watson--they were the Abstract of some
property in Essex.
