JAMES MASON (Policeman, L 57). On the morning of 18th September,
about a quarter past three, I was on duty near the proseoutor's house, and
heard a poise like the crash of a door I went and looked through the window
over the door, and saw three lights as if struck by lucifers I listened for
some moments at the door a few moments after, a sergeant and constable
came to the corner of the street, a few yards from me I called them to my
assistance, and we got on the tops of the houses--I got in from the skylight
at 82, Garden-alley-I saw two persons come out of the street-door-I can
identify Allen, but I cannot identify Pitts, as he was so soon out of my sight
--be only had to run just across the street, which is not very wide I WA
not near enough to see how he was dressed-I only identified Allen, he had
no cont on when he ran out at the door-I did not take either of them.
David PEORAN (Policeman, L 161).- On the morning of 18th, between
9 and 3 o'clock, I was in West-square, near the prosecutor's house I heard
the spring of a rattle, and went into Garden-row-I herrd two men running
over the houses of Garden-row- I could not see them I saw two come out
of the house I can identify Allet-I took him, and charged him with
breaking into the public-house-he made no reply-I asked him where his
cont was, and he at first said he had got none-I took him to the station
house, searched him, and found 88 and 3 farthings in copper, and a white
handkerchief, a duplicate for a suit of clothes, a knife, and there were seven
lucifer matches, I think-two coats were taken to the station-houss, and
Allen asked the inspector to give him his cost-the coats were at that time
in the inspector's room-he said the old coat was his he gave me an address
-I went there--I have a white handkerchief here, the property of the pot-
boy (produced)--the spoons were ordered to be given up by the Magistrate
on Friday, 20th, I went to 13, Market-street, London-road, and found
Fitts there. I charged him with being concerned with Allen in the robbery
at the Albert Arms be said, “I have got no guernsey" - I had not suid
anything to him about a guernsey before that I took him to 32, Garden
row, and put him with two men-Mrs. Kenny lived at that house-I went
upstairs for her-she identified him directly.
