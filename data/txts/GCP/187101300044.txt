JOHN READ. I am a potato merchant, of Bonny Street, Blackpool,
Lancashire-I have known the defendant three years, I have had dealings
with him in the way of trade, and continued to do so until 9th May last
year, my account against him was then 302. 18. 6d. - I received this telegram
from him on 9th May, and on 12th May I received this cheque for 301.
(produced) on account of the balance of 301. 1s. 60.--in consequence of
that telegram I sent off 18 tons 19 cwt. of potatoes-the price was 51. 158.
per ton, altogether 1071. some shillings--I sent those from the 9th to the
13th, a truck at a time I afterwards received a letter from the defendant,
which I have mislaid-it contained an order for a few trucks more, and in
consequence of that I sent 17 tons more, amounting to 1001.- altogether
2071. 98. 8d.-when I received the 301. cheque I paid it into my banker's
the same day-I sent off the last of the 18 tons on the 14th-on 20th
May I received this cheque for 1001. (produced)-I also paid that into my
banker's— I received both these cheques back dishonoured – I received the
301. cheque back on the 19th, and the 1001. cheque on the 25th--it was
issued on the 19th ; I got it on the 20th, and got it back on the 25th-I
should not have allowed my potatoes to go if I had known the cheques
would have been dishonoured, or if I had known the 301. cheque would
have been dishonoured-in consequence of the dishonour of those cheques,
I came up to London to see the defendant--and saw him on the 26th, at
his house, at 256, Caledonian Road-my brother was there with him before
I got to see him I went in and bid him "Good morning," and I said
