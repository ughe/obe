right to reply is in the nature of a prerogative right; it is a right on the part
of the Crown, exercised by the officer of the Crown, the Attorney-General, and

to reply, and that right is not affected by the question of whether or no the

NEW COURT.-Wednesday, June 12th, 1872.
Before Mr. Justice Blackburn.
472. EDWARD BREWIS (33), Unlawfully obtaining by false pretences
three cheques for the payment of 1501., 1501., and 751., and 3751. in money,
with intent to defraud. The prosecutor, by the consent of the Court, withdrew
from the prosecution, and the prisoner was discharged.
472. TIMOTHY LORDAN (36), Feloniously cutting and wounding Mary
Lordan with intent to murder her. Second Count-with intent to do her
some grievous bodily harm.
MR. HARRIS and Mr. D. MICHels conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM DRISOOLL. I live at 2, Baker's Arms Alley, Whitechapel-on
10th June, about 2 p.m., I was going in to my dinner and saw Mrs. Lordan
coming down the court from her dinner-I live at No. 2 and she lived at
No. 3-her throat was cut, and she turned round to me and told me to
take hold of her husband, as he did it-he was coming out of their own
door, following her-I took hold of him and held him till the policeman
took him.
PATRICK NORMAN. I live at 5, Hayes' Court, Whitechapel, and am
scavenger-I was taking stuff out of & gulley at the bottom of Baker's
Arms Alley, and saw Mary Lordan come out with blood flowing from her
throat-she cried out "Murder I”—I saw the prisoner coming down with
Driscoll, who asked me to take hold of him-I took hold of his arm-he
asked me to let go of him-I said not till I gave him to a constable-
he said "Let go of me or I will have the
