WILLIAM LOVEGROVA, I am a cow-keeper, of 8, Denmark Road, Camber
well-in May last I had two cows for sale-Palmer called on 9th May and
sreed to buy them for 381. 108., and gave me 308. deposit-he called next
day and took one of the cows away, and gave me a cheque for 167. drawn
by Frederick Clement, in part payment-he took the other cow away on
the following Monday, and on the Tuesday I had the cheque returned.
upon which I sued Palmer in the County Court, and got judgment, upon
which he came and offered to pay 41. down and 41. & month he paid the
first 46., but he did not keep up his payments I got a judgment summons
sgainst him, and he agreed to pay 158. a month he paid one month, and
never paid any more-- on the same morning that I received the cheque
back, 16th May, I saw Palmer- I said "I have come to see you about the
cheque, which has been returned" he said " It is a great shame a party
came in and bought a joint of meat of me, and I gave him change for the
cheque.

I know him as a butcher, and at the time believed him to be a respectable

it-if Palmer had presented anybody else's cheque I daresay I should have
taken it in the same way.
