in barges-Harrison raised the skips by a steam crane—he counted them
as they came up, and put them down on a slate provided for the purpose
- I have seen him mark them-I obtained the weights of the skips from

they were full-the sago was weighed atter it had buen unloaded, and in

son has been employed by the company about three years—he was a hard-
working industrous man—my object in sending for him was to give him
