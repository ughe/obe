bling a half-sovereign, with intent to defraud.
MR. GOODRICH Prosecuted.
FRANCIS TUCKER. I keep the New Barge beerhouse, Canal Bank,
Peckham-about 11.30 a.m. on 12th October the prisoner came in for 8
pint of beer, and tendered this coin, which I took for a half-sovereign-I
gave him 98. 100.-I said “Give me back my change, and take this coin
or half-sovereign back to Mr. Sheppard,” who he said he got it from for
his wages-he said “You have got it, and you will have to keep it. I
have got the change," and went out, and before I could get my assistant
downstairs to go after him he was out of sight-I identified him at
Kennington Lane Police-station next day-he said he had half a sovereign
and a shilling for his wages I gave the medal to the policeman.
Cross-ecramined by the Prisoner. I told you it was a bad one at once-I
showed you the difference between this and a good half-sovereign-I left
the bad one on the bar-I used to keep brass tickets for beer for working
men, but I left it uff a fortnight ago.
