JOHN THOMAS GROTTICK. On Sunday morning, the 27th of December
about one o'clock, I hired a cabriolet at the corner of Hart-street, Long
Acre-James Day was the driver-I am sure he is the person-I had,
that time, a silver watch in my pocket, and a seal, key, and ribbon, withi
guard-chain- James Day placed himself in the cab, by my side, I made no
objection to it, he was in the cab when I got in-I directed him to go to No
66, Wood-street, Cheapside-I had been drinking in the evening, but was
sober-I fell asleep, and on awaking, I found myself at Battle-bridge
my watch was gone- just at that time Day stopped the cab, and got out,
and went round the back of the cad to the horse's head-I found my watch
was gone, and desired him again to drive me to Wood-street-he said he
would not, and was very impertinent I would not leave the cab, 1
called a policeman, and gave him in charge--my guard-ebain was cut
- part of it remained round my neck-here is the seal, the key, and the
ribbon.
James Day. He was in liquor when I took him up, and the policernas
told me to take him to Wood-street-I took him there he got out, and

took him there, and he then said he meant Maiden-lane, New North-road.
