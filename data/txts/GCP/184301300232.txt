Q. Did she say who were the parties to that deed ? A. As far as my me-
mory serves me, I understood that she had witnessed a deed conveying this
property, which ought to be filled up-that she was a party to the deed -I
will not say a witness-that she had witnessed, or was a party to a deed which
required to be filled up, I believe, for the amount of 8001, and upwards (I had
not at that time put an execution into the prisoner's house)-the prisoner or
Miss Constable, when both were present, in the room, informed me that
such a deed was in the hands of Mr. Birch, and I believe the prisoner said he
had not the means to settle Mr. Birch's account-I went to the prisoner's
house again, and saw Miss Constable, I believe about a week or ten days after;
during that time I had advanced the prisoner the money to take the deeds out
of Mr. Birch's hands-on the second occasion that I saw Miss Constable, I made
a memorandum- this is it-(looking at the account produced)-it is my writing:
I copied that from receipts which were placed before me, which I was informed
Miss Constable was in possession of-Miss Constable and the prisoner together,
informed me so- I believe the prisoner produced the receipts-he laid them
before me, that they were from Miss Constable--that was not in Miss Con.
stable's presence-I copied them in another room--they were both present
after I copied them, when I laid the copy of the receipts before them- I can-
not say in whose handwriting the part beginning, "I bave carefully compared,"
is-I saw Miss Constable write the words “Elizabeth Hollis Constable" to
this account—that was done after I had copied this from the receipts, and after
she had seen it-I believe she stated that the deed I have referred to was
as a security for sums of money which Mr. Stanley had paid on behalf of her
father, and that the receipts were for those sums-I left this account at South-
ampton-street on that occasion-I do not recollect having it in my possession
after that evening-I have never seen it since, to the best of my recollection,
till this day.
