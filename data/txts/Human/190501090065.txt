JULIUS ADOLPHUS EHRENEEST. I reside at St. Dorothy, Barnes, and am a chemical merchant in West Smithfield—in March, 1899, I received a prospectus similar to the one produced, of Low, Sons & Bedford, Limited—I read the statements in it with regard to the assets the company would acquire and the assets and alleged earnings of the old firm—after studying those statements very carefully I subscribed for fifty pre ference shares and sent the application money, a cheque of March 23rd, for £18 15s. for the second instalment, and on April 23rd a cheque of £25 for the third instalment—in July, 1900,1 received a copy of the report and accounts issued by the directors—except dividends I have received nothing for my capital since the company has fen in liquidation.
