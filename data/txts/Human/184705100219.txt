 1324. WILLIAM WESTON was indicated for stealing two same rab bits, value 4 s  .; the goods of JANE WEBB ; and that he had been before con victed of felony. JANE WEBB. I am the wife of John Webb—we live at Lambeth—we had two tame rabbits safe on the 7th of May—we missed them—this is one of them—I can swear to it—they were safe on Friday night, and gone on the Saturday. RICHARD HOLWAY. I live in East-street, Lambeth. About five o'clock on Saturday afternoon, the 8th of May, the prisoner came to me, and asked if I would buy two rabbits—I said I could sell them for him—I took them to Ingram, and he bought them. WILLIAM INGRAM. I bought these rabbits of Holway.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I bought them on the Saturday, between three and four o'clock; I gave 1 s . 6 d . for them; I asked Holway to buy them; he said he had left off keeping them, but he could sell them for me. HENRY BUCKLEY( police-constable G  73.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction at this Court—( read—Convicted 30th March , 1846, having been before convicted of felony, confined one year )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 25.— Transported for seven Years .
