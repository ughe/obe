 1311.  SAMUEL ASHTON was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of April , 2 books, value 3s., the goods of John Chidley.
 JOHN CHIDLEY, I am a  bookseller, and live in Goswell-street. On the 11th of April, between one and two o'clock, I was in the parlour, I heard a footstep, and saw the prisoner going out of the shop; I went and overtook him, putting these two books into his bosom.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner pleaded distress.
 GUILTY Aged 38. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Three Weeks.
 1312.  HANNAH BARBER was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of May , 1 watch, value 3l., and 2 keys, value 1l., the goods of William Thompson , from his person.
 WILLIAM THOMPSON. I am a  seaman, and have been on shore about a fortnight. On the 29th of May, about eleven o'clock at night, I met the prisoner in Ratcliff-highway. She asked me home - I went into a house with her. I took out my watch and put it into my pocket. I went to bed with her; in about three minutes, a person came to the door, she called out to that person, then jumped out of bed and ran down-stairs as fast as she could - there was no light - I had not pulled off my clothes; the watch was in my pocket. I got up and missed it - went down and called the watchman. I have not found it; there was a light when I went there, but she put it out before we went to bed.
   JOHN FOLKER. I am a watchman. I saw Thompson, about eleven o'clock at night; he said he had been robbed, and shewed me the house - I found nobody at home, but a candle was burning; he described the girl, and gave me his address - I found the prisoner and took her to him; she has lived in that house about two months - Thompson was perfectly sober.
     GUILTY. Aged 35. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1313.  WALTER CREAK was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of June , 5 shillings, and 4 sixpences, the monies of Elizabeth Reid , his mistress.
 ELIZABETH REID. I am a  baker. The prisoner was in my employ. On Sunday night Green marked some money for me, and put it into the till, by my desire - the prisoner had no business at the till; on Monday morning five marked shillings and four sixpences were found on him by the officer - I had locked the till.
 Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. How long has he lived with you? A. Twelve months; his character was good; he had 10s. a week and his board.
   CHARLES GREEN. I marked 15s. by Reid's desire; the next morning I went to the officer, and had the prisoner taken; this silver is what I marked.
   WILLIAM CLARKE. I am an officer. I was fetched, and found this money on the prisoner, with a key which opens the till.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 1314.  WILLIAM PAFFEY were indicted for stealing, on the 21st of April , 1 jacket, value 18s., and 1 pair of trousers, value 12s, the goods of Thomas Sharp.
   EDWARD GINGER. I am servant to Thomas Sharp. I was informed the prisoner had stolen a jacket and trousers - I ran out, and caught him with them; he fell down with them on his arms.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was crossing Whitechapel; these things laid under the shop-window - I picked them up; a man said Stop thief! I stopped directly.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. Transported for Seven Years.
 1315.  JAMES BARKER was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of April , 1 cake of tallow greaves, value 4s., the goods of John King.
 JOHN KING. I am a  tallow-chandler. and live at Islington; a person told me this cake of greaves was stolen from the door; the prisoner was brought back with it.
   JOHN GIRDLER. I saw the prisoner and two other bigger lads - I turned my head and saw the other two lads placing the greaves on the prisoner's head; they told him to make haste - I stopped him with it - I heard them say, "You bl - dy fool, take it on your head. and go down Compton-street, and we will meet you there."
 Prisoner's Defence. A boy said he would give me a penny, to carry it to Compton-street.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Whipped and Discharged.
 SEVENTH DAY. THURSDAY, JUNE 7.
 Third Middlesex Jury - before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1316.  JAMES LOWE was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of June , 1 goose, value 5s., the goods of Joseph Mazoyer.
 JOSEPH MAZOYER. I am a  poulterer, and live in St. Giles'. On the 4th of June I was in my parlour, and saw the prisoner snatch a goose from the board - I followed. and took him about fifty yards off, with it.
   WILLIAM KEAN. I received him in charge; he said Copley, who had got away, took one goose, and told him to take this one.
     GUILTY. Aged 13. Whipped and Discharged.
