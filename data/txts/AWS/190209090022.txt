596. SAMUEL HOPE (20). Robbery with violence upon James Dearman
and stealing 10s., his monies.
Mr. Moses Prosecuted.
JAMES DEARMAN. I am a brick-maker, of 37, Ordnance Road, Hounslow
-on August 9th, the Coronation day, about 8 a.m., I was crossing the
Militia Fields, Hounslow-I had been with the prisoner till seven o'elock
in a coffee-shop, where he saw I had money-when I met him at eight
o'clock he knocked me down, not saying a word-he then said, "I am
mad give me your money or I will stab you "he had this elasp knife
in his hand-the blade was open-he took 10s. out of my pocket in four
2s. pieces and two shillings-before he let me go he stabbed me in the
chest through my two shirts, and eut my skin-I went for assistance,
and returned with my brother to the prisonerhe struck my brother with
the knife-my brother hit him, and he ran away-I had seen the prisoner
the night before-he had only a penny in his hand-we were good friends
till he attacked me.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. You had not shown me E14 in gold
and 22s. in silver at 2.30 on August 8th-I did not ask you what you were
doing with E13, nor gamble with you-I never took 11s. 6d. out of your
hand-my brother struck you on your neek with a piece of wood from
a
fenee-then you ran home-I had a silver watch and chain on.
GEORGE DEARMAN. I am the prosecutor's brother-on August 9th,
about 8.5 a.m., he called me-his vest was open-in consequence of what
he told me I went to the prisoner and asked him what he had stabbed
my brother for, and for the money he had taken from him in the Reereation
Ground, that is the Militia Fields-h said nothing-he tried to stab
me with this knife-I pulled a piece of wood out of the fence and struck
him on the left side of his neck-I had seen silver in my brother's possession
that morning in a coffee shop.
Cross-examined. I had not seen you and my brother gambling-I
did not see you after 8.15.
