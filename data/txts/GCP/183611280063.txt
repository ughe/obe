shillings into a little box, which was in a large one-neither of the boxes
were locked-on Monday the 7th, the prisoner came to my house, as I was
going to supper, and said, "I am going to work for your master to-morrow"
I said, " It is time you went home "-I was going to bed I live alone
- he said, “I will come early in the morning"-in the morning the came

little longer "-I gave him a cup of tea, and he cut some bread, and
buttered it-on Tuesday night I was going to supper, and missed some
cheese, and half a pound of sugar--I looked into my box on Wednesday,

morning, the 9th of November, I saw the prisoner washing himself at my
house-- he had slept there the night before he left the house for four or
five hours, and then returned, and asked me to take care of that box, giv-
ing me a small box-I asked him what was in it?--he said, Two sove-
reigns and a shilling"-he left it with me-he said he should return the
same evening, but did not, as he was taken into custody I kept the
'box till next morning, and delivered it to the constable.
