JAMES SHOWELL. I am in the service of Mr. Spink, of Gracechurch
street, a pawnbroker. I took in this pearl ring on the 18th of October,
1841, and advanced 58. on it-this is the duplicate I gave with it to the
best of my belief the prisoner is the man who pawned it-I could not
swear positively to him, from the lapse of time, and not having seen the
person before.
Alice WOODLEY. I live with my aunt, at No. 9, Newton-terrace, Ken-
nington-green. The prisoner took two rooms in my aunt's house in Oc-
tober last-he left about the 3rd of December he gave a notice of his
intention to leave, but he went off without our knowing it-in conse-
quence of some suspicion, I examined a pocket-book that was in his bed-
room, I think about a fortnight before he left, I cannot be sure I took a
copy of some duplicates that were in it-this is a copy of one of them
(reads)—"D. E. Spink, 2, Gracechurch-street, 18th of October, 1841;
1 ring, 5s. Williams, Kennington-green."
