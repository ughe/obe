THOMAS OSBORNE. I am a workman at Mr. Smith's. Powell world
there-I believe he is a relation of Mr. Smith's-1 have known him the
years and five months, and from what I have known of his character. I
not believe him on his oath, if he was on his dying bed- I have heard
talk very little of Chartism-I was not in the shop much lately-about tw
months ago be told me there was to be a Chartist rising, no one else
present-be asked me to join three or four months ago, it might be the
weeks after the Kennington-common meeting on 10th April, not more.
Cross-examined by MR. ATTORNEY-GENERAL. Q. Did you go to the
Kennington-common meeting? A. No, I was at Bishop Booner's-field
but at no meeting-I was there on the Sunday when people were al
lected, not during the evening-I was there on the day the police were
attacked-I was there when the church windows were broken, but
not see them I did not bear Ernest Jones speak- I do not know him
- I was at a meeting at Birdcage-walk-I cannot say if it was on 22nd
April-Powell pointed out a blacksmith to make a pike if I wanted on
- he asked whether I had joined I said, “Yes"- that was a lie, but1!
would not believe him on his oath, because I have found him out in
many-I heard him examined here on Tuesday-I was here to speak the
truth I was subpoenaed- I did not hear the witnesses ordered to leave the
Court, but as soon as I heard there were orders, I left--that is the only oct
sion I have heard him examined-he came in one day as if he was frightened
and said Lord John Russell had committed suicide-two or three days before
he gave information, he was sharpening a saw, and broke one of the wings of
the nut which it was fixed with, he said there was no man at that end of the
shop but me, I must get another-I said, "You can get one for 1d.," and gor
him one-be went out, I said to one of my shopmates, " Depend upon it be
will say it cost 2d.," and accordingly he did so, but a man in the counting
house said he gave it to bim-I did not deny breaking it-none of the othen
believed him, and it was the same with me, I did not attend Chartist meet
ings before 10th April—I attended a meeting at Birdcage-walk, it was quite
accidental- I went two or three times, on two or three Sunday mornings
was in some ruins where houses had been built-several persons spoke--Me
was one-no one went with me, I did not bear there was going to ben
meeting on 16th Aug, not till next morning-I was never taken up-1dd
not take Powell's advice and get a pike-I am not at regular work-if Mr.
Smith wants me, be employs me- I sometimes earn more than 30s. 8 week,
sometimes less I went to the meeting because it was proposed that as
many carpenters were out of employ, and their tools were all popped, and I
they would subscribe something to get them out of pawn, they could go to
work, and be able to be a great help to the cause I did not subscribe, I went
to oppose him-I spoke-May bad put the proposition at a former meeting
he was a perfect stranger to me-I did not go the next Sunday-if it w
brought forward, I should have told the parties he was doing it for his om
self-I do not think I went four times I have done jobs with Pennell-
there was a little difference between us-I am not aware that he charged to
with taking some wood-he never did to my face, and I never heard of it-
worked at Bishop's Stortford, in Hertfordshire--I left there about a lore
affair, which my parents were against me in-I brought her to London-1
married her first-I never heard Powell examined on oath.
