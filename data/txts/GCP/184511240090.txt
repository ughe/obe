84. ROBERT WHITE was indicted for stealing 5 ounces of rhubarb,
value 8d.; the goods of the London Dock Company, his masters.
Mr. BALDWIN conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES HARRIS. I am gate-keeper at the London Dock. I saw the
prisoner on the 11th of Nov. going out of the London Dock gate - I found
in his hat these three pieces of rhubarb-he said nothing then.
FREDERICK CRESSWELL. I am foreman of the drug warehouse at the
London Dock--the prisoner was working there on the 11th of Nov.-we
were working some rhubarb that day-the prisoner was amongst it as well
as other men---this rhubarb partakes very much of the same nature as what
we were working--the prisoner would have an opportunity of taking these
pieces-it was in charge of the London Dock Company.
Prisoner. I picked the pieces up in the passage of the water-closet, and
put them into my hat, thinking them nearly valueless.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 44.- Recommended to mercy by the Jury.--Confined Six
Months.
85. MARY COTTERELL was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of Oct.,
3 shawls, value 31.; and 1 gown, 168.; the goods of Charles Andrews, her
master :-also on the 14th of Sept., 2 petticoats, value 38.; 2 night-gowns,
48. ; 3 caps, 78.; 3 shirts, 38.; and 1 frock, 38.; the goods of Frederick
Crouch, her master; to both which she pleaded
Guilty.t Aged 17.-- Transported for Seven Years.
86. MARGARET M'DONALD was indicted for stealing 1 towel, value
1s. ; 2 yards of calico, 18. ; 1 purse, 18. ; 1 pair of gloves, 1s. ; 1 sovereign,
15 shillings, 3 sixpences, and 5 groats; the property of Joseph Rowe, her
master.
SARAH Rowe. I am the wife of Joseph Rowe, who keeps the Pembroke
Arms, at Kensington. We missed money from the till - I sent for an officer,
and secured the prisoner's box-she was in my service-I found this towel
and calico, and other things in it-this money was found in my own purse,
which was found on her person after she was taken.
MOSES STANBROOK. I am servant at the Pembroke Arms. I know one
of these sixpences- I took it-I can swear to it by a black mark round the
rim.
John CLARKE (police-constable T 98.) I searched the box-I asked the
prisoner if it was her's, and she said, "Yes," - I found the cloth and calico
-the purse was found on her person.
Prisoner. I had had some money when I went there; I got this money
on the Sunday ; I found this purse as I was cleaning the bed-room, and put
my own money into it.
SARAH Rowe re-examined. The prisoner borrowed 1s. of me the week
after she came-she was only with me six weeks-she had not, as she said,
1s. to buy her a clean cap for Sunday-the pot-boy lent her one also--sbe
received no money of us-she took 2s. out of the till-I accused her of it,
and she brought me them back again.
GUILTY. Aged 22. ---Confined Four Months.
