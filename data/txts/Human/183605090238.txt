 1359. ANN HOWELL was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of April , 1 frock, value 3 s  ., the goods of James Woolridge ; and that she had been before convicted of felony. MARY ANN WOOLRIDGE. I am the wife of James Woolridge, a master builder, and live in Kent-street, Borough. I first knew the prisoner six weeks ago, when she came to char for me—on the 23rd of April I missed a frock and several articles out of my bed-room drawer—she had been at work there that day—I have since found them at the pawnbroker's. ROSETTA HAWTHORN. I am married. I recommended the prisoner to Mrs. Woolridge, thinking her an honest woman—hearing of the robbers, I went to the Clink to the prisoner, and got this duplicate from her, which I gave to the officer. PERCIVAL SMALL. I am a policeman. I received the duplicate from Hawthorn, and apprehended the prisoner. WILLIAM FRAAS. I am shopman to Mr. Rosier, a pawnbroker, in Tooley-street. I produce a frock—I do not know who pawned it—this is the duplicate I gave the person. JOHN STOREY. I am an officer of the Borough. I have a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I obtained from Mr. Clark ( read )—I was the officer on the trial—the prisoner is the person.
   Prisoner . I did it through distress—I was left a widow with two small children. GUILTY.* Aged 41.— Transported for Fourteen Years.
   Before Mr. Justice Coleridge .
