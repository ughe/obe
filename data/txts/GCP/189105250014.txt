463. HENRY ZALILA (29), Unlawfully obtaining 4s. 6d. from
Hester Whitfield by false pretences.
MR. LAWLESS Prosecuted.
HESTER WHITFIELD. I live at Wimbledon Park—on 16th April,
between eleven and twelve o'clock, the prisoner called on me and said
that he came from Guildford for a situation, and the schoolmaster was
bankrupt; that he had applied to Mr. Turle, who recommended him to
go to Copenhagen, his native place, and advised him to go to ladies and
try and collect his fare back-I knew Mrs. Turle, whose husband is
head-master of a large school at Wimbledon-I think he said he wanted
£3 18s., and asked me to help him; I gave him 4s. 6d., thinking he was
telling the truth.
