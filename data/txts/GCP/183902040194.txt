JosEPH PEMBERTON (a prisoner.) I have been an errand-boy, and
lodged with my mother, in East-street, Manchester-square. I have known
Broad a little while-I bave only seen Holton twice or three times at most
-he lives in Tom's-court, Thomas-street, Oxford-street-I know a per-
son named Elwood. On Wednesday week, the 30th of January, between
six and seven o'clock, Broad and Elwood came to my house, and wanted
me to come to Gilbert-street, Oxford-street, to get some lead-Mrs. Parry's
stables are at the back of Gilbert-street-we went to the stable in Cock-
yard-Elwood and Broad lifted me up on the wall-I had no knife-I
pulled the lead off with my hands, and chucked it down, and gave it
to Elwood and Broad - there were two pieces-Elwood took the lead
into the mews, left it there, and went and borrowed a sack of Holton
-I got down from the wall into Gilbert-street, and went with Elwood
into Holton's shop, with a piece of lead, for which he received 48.
- he was to receive 48. 3d., but the 3d. was not paid-Holton put the
lead underneath the counter-this was between six and seven o'clock-I
afterwards met Broad in Duke-street, and he told me he had got 38. 6d.
for the other piece of lead-Elwood gave me half-a-crown-I did not see
Mott at all that night-we went again the following night, and that night I
saw him in Tom's-court-Broad was with me-Mott saw us together-
nothing was said when we took the lead into Holton's the night before
Broad produced the lead-Holton said nothing, but put it into the scale
and weighed it-he did not say what it weighed, but said it came to 78. 3d.
the two pieces-both the pieces were there-Broad took one, and Elwood
the other, into the shop when I went in with Elwood, his piece came to
48, 3d.-Holton paid him 48.- I was not there when Broad took in the
second piece- I did not hear Holton say it came to 78, 3d., but that was
what it did come to-he asked no questions where it came from, but put
it into the scale.
Cross-examined. Q. Why did you say at first he put it under the
counter, instead of into the scales ? A. So he did put it under the coun-
ter-he weighed it I did not think of that at first-I had no notion that
they wanted to steal the lead till I got there they would not let me go
home-they hoisted me on the wall-I went up, not quite willingly-I
could not help going up-he gave me the 2s.6d.- he said I had better
have my share of it-he pressed me to take it-I have just come out of
Newgate now- I have been in Newgate before, that is about six months
ago- I have been in this Court before, for stealing two straw bonnets-
I was in Newgate a week after that, and then at the House of Correction
for three months.
SAMUEL LOFTING. I am employed at Mr. Mivart's stables, in Cock-
yard, Davis-street. I took Pemberton into custody there on the landing-
place-I know nothing of this transaction.
EDWARD PIGGOTT. I received charge of Pemberton in Gilbert-street,
on the 1st of February, between 11 and 12 o'clock, and in consequence of
information, I went to the Three Compasses in Silver-street, and found
Broad-I told him I took him for stealing lead in company with Pember-
ton, in Cock-yard-he said it was not him, but Pemberton-1 went next
day to Holton's shop, in Tom's-court, but found no lead.
