LADY AMELIA SHAW. I reside at Kensington Gore. I drew this cheque
for 51, on Tuesday, 13th Oct., before I left Brighton, intending to change it
if I wanted it, but I did not it was in my porte-monnaie, where I had a
half sovereign and some silver on the Wednesday I went to Messrs. Foare's,
as I was going out of town next day, and got change for 201; there were
two 51. notes and 10%. in gold-I put them, in their house, into a small
Worsted purse, and 21. in my porte-monnais--the cheque was then safe, for
I took it in my fingers-I also had a penny piece, and I put them all into
niy pocket together, at a little before 1 o'clock-I walked up Chancery Lane
to a friend's house in Bedford Place, and have no impression of anything
touching me during that walk-I went in the evening to a meeting about
the Indian sufferers, and when I came home I missed my money.
