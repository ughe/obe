GEORGE Goodwin Turley. I lent Mrs. Lord 21., about the 17th of
Nov.-I am not quite positive as to the day-the prisoner was not present,
but I had seen him a moment previously-he stated that 21. was required to
be paid that very night, otherwise her husband would be drafted off, and it
would cost 301, instead of 201. to buy him off.
Prisoner. Q. Where did this conversation take place ? 2. In the bar of
the Salmon and Ball, about six o'clock in the evening-I saw Mrs. Lord in
the bar-parlour-you was in the parlour-you could not know or see, from
where you were sitting, what took place with me and Mrs. Lord, but you
understood I was going to lend her the money, and told me you were
gomg out with her to pay it, and after that I missed you-you told us all
you were going up to pay the money.

- the sum of 21. could not be paid for any such purpose as delaying a man's
