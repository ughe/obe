 269. JAMES ROOKE , stealing 1 saw, value 3 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of William James Ferrer . PETER BARLOW. I am in the employ of Mr. William James Ferrer, of Perrivale—I was at work for him on 14th Dec.—I had my master's saw to take care of—I laid it beside the hedge of the turnip-field—I saw two chaps  come to the place—I took no notice of them, but when I came to the place I could not find the saw—I told the policeman about it. THOMAS CRANE( policeman, T  166). I got this saw at the prisoner's house—he was not there—his father gave it me. NOT GUILTY.
