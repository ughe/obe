Prisoner's Defence. When I first went into this person's house I gave
him a sovereign; he put it into a glass, in which I think there were six or
seven sovereigns; my friend said he would pay, and the landlord went to
the glass, and put his hand in, and took out & sovereign; but he does not
know that it was the one; I gave him the sovereign afterwards, and he said,
“This is a bad one;" I said, "It is the one you gave me;" he said, “No; I
shall give you in charge;" I went out, and said I was unfit to be given in
charge; I was just out of a fever; he took me to the station, there was
nothing found about me; they all have access to this glass, his mother and
the others; he never looked in the glass, but put his hand in, and gave me
one out.
