WILLIAM GEORGE ERASMUS BAGG. I live at Richmond—I am an archi tect and surveyor, and have offices at 21, Whitehall Place—on 10th of last August I took my portmanteau with me up to the office—my sister was with me—I put the portmanteau in the outer room—that was 10.30 or 11 o'clock—I did not notice it during the day, but when I went to take it away, about 6.30 in the evening, it was gone—the outer office has a door with a spring lock, opening on to the staircase—there was no one in the office all day—these cuffs are mine—they have my name on them, which I wrote myself—there was also something in the bag belonging to my mother, Selina Bagg. SELINA DALE BAGG. I live at Richmond—this black silk jacket belongs to my daughter—I made it—I packed it in my son's bag the morning we went to the office. FRANCIS SPICE. I am assistant to Mr. Hollington, pawnbroker, 25 l ., Wal worth Road—this jacket was pledged on 10th August in the name of John Edwards, New Street, for 7 s .—I can't speak to the person who pledged it. WILLIAM PUTTOCK( Re-examined ). The duplicate relating to this jacket was found at the prisoner's lodgings.
   Prisoner's Defence . They were things I bought in the name of Wilson and some I pledged—I bought them of a dealer in Devonshire Square—this bag does not belong to me—I have only got one, and that has my name on it. GUILTY.

   MR. COOPER conducted the Prosecution . ARTHUR PAWSON. I am a barrister, and have chambers at 4, Elm Court, Temple—I identify these three pins and a scarf ring, which lost, with other things worth about 20 l . or 30 l .—they were safe on 17th February last, at my chambers—I missed them at 6 o'clock that evening—I have not seen the prisoner before. GEORGE PHILLIPS. I am assistant to George Attenborough, 18, Newing ton Causeway, pawnbroker—I produce a pin pledged on 18th February for 10 s . in the name of Edwards, by the prisoner. FREDERICK OHLSON.
