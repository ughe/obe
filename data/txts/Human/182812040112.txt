I knew the prisoner, but not before be married Rebecca Martin; the prisoner gave me instructions to prepare a deed of separation between himself and his wife - she was named Rebecca, the wife of James Hall - her maiden name did not appear; this was in October last - he was living near Goswell-road; he never shewed me a certificate of his marriage.(Certificate read.)
 Marriage solemnized in the parish church of St. Pancras, Middlesex, 1822 - John Hall and Rebecca Martin were married by banus, the 1st of July. By me, W. H. CHARLTON.
   WILLIAM MERRY. I took the prisoner at the Wellington public-house in Goswell-street-road - I heckoned him out - he trembled exceedingly, and said what was the matter; I charged him with felony, and when I got him out, I said it was for marrying two wives; he said,"Can't it be settled?" I said "No; you must go before a Magistrate;" he went on about two hundred yards, and said, "I will give you 10l. a piece to let me go;" and a few yards farther he said, "i will make it 5l. more;" he said, "What can be done?" I said."I have the documents in my pocket;" "Then (said he) I am done, I shall be transported."
 Cross-examined by MR. BRODRICK. Q.Who was with you? A.Sanders; (who I did not then know was an officer) - the money was not offered that we might take him to the watch-house, and not to prison.
   RICHARD SANDERS. I am a constable. I was present when the prisoner was taken - he wanted to settle it, and to take us to some house; we told him we could not settle it, we must take him to Worship-street - about two hundred yards further he offered us 10l. a piece; we said we could not take it - he then offered 5l. more.
   DANIEL DOBELL. I am a boot-maker, and live at No. 42, St. John-square - I was at the Wellington public-house when the officer came; I followed the prisoner out and heard the conversation pass - he said he would give them 10l. if they would allow him to be locked up in the watch-house, and not take him to the New-prison Clerkenwell; they said they could not.
 Prisoner's Defence. I offered them 10l. to treat me kindly, and not put hand-cuffs on me - I knew well I could not get away from two officers; I have no wife in existence but this lady, (Mrs. Denney) - I never left my home.
     GUILTY. Aged 37. Transported for Seven Years.

 225.  THOMAS CONDON was indicted for embezzlement.
   WALTER DOUGLASS. I am a stone-mason. The priprisoner and I were both employed by one master - we were at work at the new church, Chelsea; I employed him to receive money for me - I used to keep the men's time, and send an account of it to him; on Saturday, the 8th of November, I wrote out the bill of time of all the men that were there, and gave it to him - he was to take it to Ann Sharpe's yard - her executors carry on the business; my time was six days, which came to 30s. - he was to go to the foreman and get the money - he never paid it to me; I never saw him again till that day week, when he was brought back from Bristol - I believe Mr. Stukes has the bill I sent; he left the men in a miserable state, they had no money to go on.
   JOHN STUKES. I was foreman to Mrs. Sharpe. On the 8th of November the prisoner came to me with this bill, and I gave him 4l. 12s. 6d. for the wages of the several men, including his own money - he ought to have given it them within an hour; their names are, Douglass, Wright, Bishop, Dawson, Coleman, Hughes, Daley, Collins, Cunningham, and Owen Hughes; I gave the prisoner this piece of paper, specifying the sum due to each man - it was found on him.
 - WRIGHT. I was employed with the prisoner, and was to receive 30s. on the 8th of November; I never saw him till the Saturday following.
   JOHN BISHOP. I worked at the same place - my wages were 1l. 10s. - the prisoner was to have received it and paid me; I never saw him till the 17th.
   JOHN DAWSON. I was employed at the same place - I was to have 30s.; I never saw the prisoner till the 17th.
 - COLEMAN. I was employed in the same way -I was to have received 18s. - the prisoner never paid it me; he had paid me once before.
 - HUGHES. I was employed there, and was to have received 18s. - the prisoner never brought it to me.
   OWEN DALEY. I was employed there, and was to have 18s., but I did not.
   DAVID COLLINS. I was employed there, and was to receive 3s., but he never brought it to me - that Saturday was my first day; I saw Douglass give him the paper to go for the money - he was to have brought it to the Crown public-house, close by the Westminster-hospital, but did not come.
   TIMOTHY CUNNINGHAM. I am a labourer. I was to receive 18s. - the prisoner did not come to the public-house.
   OWEN HUGHES. I was to receive my money at the Crown, but the prisoner did not come to the house.
   DANIEL DAWKINS. I am an officer. I took the prisoner at Bristol, on Friday, the 14th of November - I told him what for; he said he got drunk, got into the Park, lost half of the money, and was ashamed to come back; I found this paper and 4s. 5 1/2d. on him.
 GUILTY. Aged 32.
     Judgment Respited.
