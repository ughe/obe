1710. JULIA O'LEARY was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, valde
6d. ; 1 pepper-box, 2d.; and 4 drinking-glasses, 2s. ; the goods of Thomas
Elwood, her master.
SARAH ELWOOD. I am the wife of Thomas Elwood-we keep the Foun-
tain, in the Broadway, Deptford-the prisoner lived in my service-she came
to me in Feb. and left on the 18th of April-while she was with me!
missed a pepper-box, a handkerchief, and four glasses- I discharged the pri-
soner in consequence- this is the pepper-box and the glasses-they are my
husband's—this bandkerchief is mine.
Cross-examined by Mr. Horry. Q. You once had the prisoner and her
sister in your service ? A. Yes--this pepper-box has our private mark on
the bottom--we were in business, and sold these sort of things--I have one
more of these boxes-this one has been in use twelve months-I missed
this on the 18th of April - I had seen it within a month before that, in the
kitchen-this is a common handkerchief- I cannot say to a day when I had
seen it, but it was within two months—I had had it about three months
these are ordinary drinking-glasses-one of them has been ground down-1
am sure about this one.
Court. Q. Is there any mark on the handkerchief ? A. No, but in
locking my drawer I shut one corner of the handkerchief in, and it is cut-
here is where it was cut.
John Evans (police-constable R 190.) I went to a house in Mill-lane-
I found this pepper-box and two glasses- I apprehended the prisoner at the
same house--I told her she was charged with stealing a quantity of glasses,
and other property of Mrs. Elwood-she said she knew nothing about them
--she said she bought the glasses and pepper-box, and Mrs. Elwood gave her
the handkerchief long ago-Mrs. Elwood denied it.
Cross-examined. Q. She speaks Irish very strongly, does she not? 4.
Yes-- she did not say that they were Mrs. Allen's, and Mrs. Allen bouge
them.
