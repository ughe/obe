WILLIAM HOLLAND. I am a lather, and live at 9, Cumberland Place,
Camberwell-between 1 and 1.30 on Sunday morning, 9th November, me
and Michael Collins were together, and Timothy Shea and Mary Kelly were
standing at the corner by the Lord Clyde; Shea called Michael across the
road to him, and said "Where does your brother Daniel live ?”—I said
“Will you have a drop of beer out of this bottle, which I had under my
arm-he said “No, I don't want your beer" -Michael said “What do you
want my brother Dan for?" he said "I want to fight him."--Michael said.
" What do you want to fight him for ?”—he said "Him and me had a bit
of a row this afternoon, and I want to finish it out, will you go and fetch your
brother Dan out ?”—he said "No, I will not " ---Shea then took off his
coat, and said to Michael “I will fight you," taking his coat partly off-
Michael said “No, I shan't fight you; I don't get my living by fighting"
Shea said “Will you go and fetch your brother Dan " he said "No," and
walker away, and I saw him ten minutes or a quarter of an hour afterwards
with Daniel Collins---while he was gone I stood with a bottle of beer under
my arm talking to a friend named Mills- I had no conversation with Shes
or Kelly in the interval—when the two prisoners returned Shea pulled off
his coat, waistcoat, and hat-he had been drinking-Daniel said "What do
you want to fight me for, Tate ? "---Daniel did not seem excited or anxious
to fight; he seemed to me to try to make peace as much as he could-Shes
said “You and me had a bit of a row this afternoon, and I want to finish
it out "-Shea and Daniel then shook hands, and Daniel took off his gost
and Shea said "God strike me dead, if I cannot finish you in two rounds I
will never put my hands up any more," and with that they sparred, and
Shea struck Daniel in the stomach, and he fell in the road that was the
end of the first round-they sparred again, and Daniel said "Well, that is
one for you, Tate," meaning that he had knocked him down the first blow
- Chapple then said "Give him that again and you will do it”--they
sparred again, and both struck out together, and Collins being longer in his
reach then Shea, struck him, and he fell, and he and Mary Kelly and Allen
helped him up, and Mary Collins took him home-Chapple seemed greatly
interested in it; he was dancing about as if he should like to see a fight
with one of them, and said "I hope to Ch i f he comes down you will
kill him," and I heard him say that he could kill him-neither Chapple o
Shea seemed sober—I do not say anything about Allen, but he was second-
ing Shea, backing him up, and saying go on, and holding his coat, and
Michael held Shea's hat and waistcoat-after Daniel helped Shea up he was
led away, and Chapple threw the coat across his shoulders, and a strange
man came up, and he and Mary Kelly assisted him home--he walked be
tween the two of them.
