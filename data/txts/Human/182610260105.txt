   EDWARD KING. I am a carman. I went to Burr-street to remove some goods, as I was going by the toll-gate near the London-docks; I saw Morris talking to the toll-man - he asked if I was going to Burr-street to load some furniture; I said, Yes - he asked if they would want any body to help - I said, I did not know; he said he had nothing to do, and had a great mind to go and see if he could get a job; he went in the van, and assisted to load the goods - this trunk came out last, and I was surprised at it, because I had given orders for the trunks to be placed in the bottom of the van - when we went to Crombie's-row Morris went with us, and he took this trunk out, and placed it on the ground; I asked him why he did not take it into the house; he said, the house was not ready; he then moved it to the side of the cart next the road; I asked him what he wanted with it; he said, To stand on it to reach a rope;" there was a rope that he might have wanted it for; and in a minute or two it was gone.
   SARAH TURNER. I live in Albion-street. I saw Mary Boden bring this trunk with James Spencer into Elizabeth Gascowen's room, No. 30, Albion-street, on the evening of the 29th of September - it appeared loaded and heavy; I went into the room, and saw Elizabeth Gascowen light a candle - I went home, and left them there; in about twenty minutes Gascowan and Boden came to me, and Gascowen said there was a box in the alley, and told me to go and take it which I did - this is it; I am sure it is the same that I saw them take into Gascowen's room - when I went out, the lock was off, but it was lying on the top of it; I sold it the next day to Mr. Dunbar - on the Sunday evening, Gascowan offered to give me two napkins, but I would not have them.
   JAMES DUNBAR. I keep a broker's shop. On the 30th of September I went to Albion-street, and bought this trunk of Sarah Turner.
   HESTER SOLOMAN. Mary Boden came to my house, and asked me if I knew any body who wanted to buy half a dozen of table-cloths; I cannot say when it was.
   JAMES LEE. I am an officer of Lambeth-street. I heard of the robbery; I went to Skinner's room, and found a table-cloth and towel - he and Gascowen were in bed together; I took Morris a few days after.
 MORRIS' Defence. I went there to get a job - they agreed to give me 2s. 6d. to assist; I did not hear him tell me not to unload; I took the trunk to stand on, and while we were getting a chest of drawers off, the box was taken.
 GASCOWEN's Defence. Spencer came up to me, and Boden, with the box, and said, "May I take this into your room till my landlord comes in?" he said it belonged to a sailor - he went out with a bundle; I have not seen him since; I found the table-cloth and napkin on the stairs.
 BODEN's Defence. Turner said she would burn the name out, as she knew what Spencer was, and she thought it was not got honestly.
   HANNAH BAKER. I live in Henry-street. Turner came to me, and said, if I would give her a gown and apron, she would not come here.
   SARAH TURNER. It is not true.
     NOT GUILTY.
 2018.  JOSEPH MOORE was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of October , 12 dwts. of gold, value 34s. 6d., the goods of Francis Carpenter , his master.
 FRANCIS CARPENTER. I am a  goldsmith, and live in Clerkenwell- the prisoner was my apprentice. On the 5th of October I gave him some pieces of platted gold to work up into a chain - he went out that evening, but returned at night, and the next day I received information and sent for an officer; the prisoner was searched in my presence, and I saw a lot of gold found, which weighed about twelve grains; I knew it to be part of what I had given to him - here are two pieces in the same form as I gave them to him.
 Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Have you not trusted him with much property? A. Yes; other persons have the same sort of gold - I know one piece in particular by the pattern.
   JOHN DAVIS. I am an officer. I searched the prisoner - he asked where my warrant was - I found this gold in paper in his fob; he said he hoped his master would forgive him, as it was but the second time.
