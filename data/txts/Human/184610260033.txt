 2028. MICHAEL BURNS was indicted for stealing 30lbs. weight of leaden bullets, value 7 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of William Bailey and another. WILLIAM WELLS. I am foreman to Mr. William Bailey and another; the prisoner had been in their employ about six years. On a Wednesday in Sept. I missed some shot—I told the prisoner, and the other men who were at work, of it—the prisoner said he knew nothing about it—I told him I missed some musket-ball shot—on the Friday afterwards I went to Mr. White, a gun-maker, at No. 3, Worcester-street, Gravel-lane, and saw a bag of musket balls on his table—I examined it—I am quite sure it is my master's—this is it.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you ever know me to rob you of a penny? A.  I never did—I never recollect accusing you and another man of taking money which I had in my own pocket. GEORGE POTTER. I am a marine-store dealer, and live in High-street, Wapping. About the 10th of Oct. the prisoner came and said, "Do you buy old lead?"—I said yes, we did—he said, "What do you give for it?"—I said, "A penny a pound"—he said, "I have some leaden bullets a nephew of mine took to the East Indies, and brought them back; I want to sell them"—I said I would buy them—he went out, returned in ten minutes, and brought 30lbs. of bullets—he gave me the name of John Stevens, 29, Charles-street, Wapping—this is what he sold me—they are musket-bullets. EDWARD WHITE. Mr. Potter sold me some musket-balls—I have every reason to believe these are the same—I gave them to the officer. GEORGE CARR( police-constable K  166.) I took the prisoner—I went to  Mr. White—he gave me this lead. GUILTY. Aged 52.— Confined Four Months .
 2029. GEORGE FISHER was indicted for stealing 1 pair of shoes, value 1 s  .; 1 handkerchief, 1 s  .; and 6 shillings; the property of George Hare . GEORGE HARE. I am a labourer, and live at Beaconsfield On the 20th of Sept. I slept at Uxbridge with the prisoner—he went away in the morning, and I missed my shoes, handkerchief, and money—these are my shoes. WILLIAM BEECHEY. I am an officer. I took the prisoner at Uxbridge—I found these shoes on him—he said, if Hare would swear to the shoes, he was done.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I bought the shoes. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months .
