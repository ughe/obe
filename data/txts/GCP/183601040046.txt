374. JOSEPH CARNEY was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of
December, 1 dead goose, value 5s6d., the goods of James Hall.
JAMES HALL. I keep a poulterer's shop in St. John-street, Clerken
well. I had a dead goose on my stall-board on the 29th of December
missed it in five minutes-I saw it again at the station-house---this is the
foot of it.
JOHN JAMES BERNARD (police-constable G 110.) I was on duty about
two o'clock, in St. John-street, and saw the prisoner run down Aylesbur-
street-I thought he had stolen something, and pursued him-he fell dowi.
and dropped the goose-I took him and the goose to the station-house, and
the prosecutor identified it.
GUILTY. Aged 12.-Confined Three Months.

376. CHARLES HALL was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of !
nuary, 1 pair of half-boots, value 58. 6d., the goods of Alexander Cowan.
ALEXANDER Cowan. I am a salesman, and live in Aylesbury-street
On the 5th of January I was in the parlour-Coleman came in, and said
a man, in a brown coat, had taken a pair of boots- I went out, and a
the prisoner-he ran down two or three streets when I came near hin,
he dropped the boots- I took them up, and called, "Stop thief" some
gentleman stopped him-these are my boots--they were inside my door,
twisted round a nail.
JANE COLEMAN. I was passing by, between eleven and twelve o'clock,

(George Squires, an eating-house keeper, gave the prisoner a good
character.)

377. FREDERICK CASTELL was indicted for stealing, on the 28th
of December, 1 jacket, value 58.; and i handkerchief, value 4d. the
goods of Samuel Parsons.
WILLIAM BAKER. I live at Wilsden. I saw the prisoner, and
lesser boy, on the 28th of December, in one of the fields in my father's
farm, picking up bones ; and when Parsons came in, he said he had lost
bis jacket-1 went out, and saw the prisoner and a little boy-the pri-
soner had a bag, in which was the jacket-it had been on a bank in the
field.
SAMUEL PARSONS. I was at work on the farm-I took off my jacket,
and missed it about ten minutes before twelve o'clock-I had seen the pri-
soner in the field just before-this is my jacket--this handkerchief is
another man's, but was in my pocket.
RICHARD DULLANTY. I am an officer, and took the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up under the hedge, by the side of the
road.
GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Three Months.
