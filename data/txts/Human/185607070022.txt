JOHN FREDERICK LANGFIELD. I am manager to Mr. Binns, a chenist. The prosecutor was brought there, bleeding very copiously from a wound on the nose—he lost very nearly a quart of blood—I bound it up, and sent him to the hospital.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I do not recollect doing anything; the blood on my apron was from a blow he struck me on my nose when we were on the floor; I have not the least recollection of anything afterwards; I have as recollection of seeing the razor; I have not a friend in the world.
