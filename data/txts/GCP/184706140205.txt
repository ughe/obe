1533. MARY ANN MARSHALL was indicted for stealing alb. 2oz.
weight of bacon, value 1s., the goods of William Musselwhite; and that she
had been before convicted of felony.
38
HENRY SHIELDS. I am servant to William Musselwhite, a cheesemonger,
in Lower-marsh, Lambeth-at eleven o'clock in the morning of the 3rd of Jane
I saw the prisoner take a piece of bacon, and put it under her arm-she in-
quired the price of several other pieces-I told her the price, and she put
them down again-she still held this piece under her arm-she walked away
-I stopped her, and told her I wanted the piece of bacon under her arm-
she took it from under her arm, and said she was only having a lark, she was
only playing-it was my master's bacon.
Prisoner's Defence. I thought I would have half a pound of it; I asked
the price of another piece ; that was 10d., this was only 9d.; he asked which
I wanted; I said I wanted the best piece; the wind blew my shawl over it,
and he said "I will give you in charge, I think you want to steal it;" I have
four orphans; I have been in a bad state of health for ten years; I made
away with the last thing I had in the world.
WILLIAM Pocock (police-sergeant P 14.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction at this Court, by the name of Mary Ann Ryan-
(read-Convicted 2nd Peb., 1846, and confined one year)-the prisoner is the
person-she had been convicted before that.
GUILTY. Aged 46.-Confined Eighteen Months.
