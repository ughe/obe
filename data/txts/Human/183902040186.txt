 750. THOMAS MORTIMER was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of February , 4 brushes, value 1 l  ., the goods of William Partridge Crate . WILLIAM PARTRIDGE CRATE. I live in East-street, Mary-le-bone, and am an oilman. On the 2nd of February the prisoner walked deliberately into my shop, unhooked these brushes from a nail, and walked out—I followed him about forty yards, and called "Stop thief"—I did not lose sight of him till he was stopped—he dropped them.
   Prisoner.  I throw myself on the mercy of the Court—I was in want, or I should not have come here.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Six Weeks.
 751. ELIZABETH EDWARDS and ELIZA EDWARDS were in dicted for stealing, on the   25th of January , 15 yards of ribbon, value 20 s  ., the goods of Thomas Bird and another. JOSEPH JEFFERSON EDWARDS. I am shopman to Thomas Bird and an other, haberdashers, in Commercial-road. On the 25th of January, between two and three o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoners came in together, and asked to look at some bonnet-ribbon—I showed them the drawer of rib bons—Elizabeth proposed to buy them—while she was looking at them, I saw Eliza concealing something under her cloak—they shortly after said we had no ribbon to suit them, and went out—I immediately missed the ribbon produced—they had but just got outside the door—I brought them back, and said they had taken a piece of green satin ribbon—Eliza said, "Search me," and began pulling off her cloak, and in the mean time this piece of ribbon fell—she said it fell from the counter, but I saw it fall from her person.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Where were you standing when you brought them into the shop? A.  By the side of them—I have al ways been confident that I saw the ribbon fall from under Eliza's cloak. WILLIAM SAWYER( police-constable K  24.) I was sent for, and took the prisoners to the station-house—while there Eliza pointed to her finger, and said, "I will give you this ring if you will endeavour to impress on the mind of the young man that the ribbon fell from the counter." ELIZA EDWARDS  — GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Six Months. ELIZABETH EDWARDS  — NOT GUILTY.
