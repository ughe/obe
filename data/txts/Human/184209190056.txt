 2588. PATRICK NOONAN and JOHN LOCKLAN were indicted for stealing, on the   10th of September , 6 brushes, value 4 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of John Anthony Winsland . JOHN ANTHONY WINSLAND. I am an oil and colourman, and lire in Great Pulteney-street. On Saturday evening, the 10th of September, I hung these six brushes out of my shop door—they were safe at seven o'clock—I did not miss them till the policeman brought them, about half-past nine. WILLIAM RUSSELL( police-constable C  113.) About a quarter-past nine o'clock I saw the prisoners in company in Walker's-court—I followed them to the corner of Great Pulteney-street—I saw Locklan take the brushes down and give them to Noonan—I took him—the other was out of my sight a few minutes, but was brought back by another officer.
   Noonan's Defence.  I saw some boy running with the brushes; he drop ped them, and I picked them up.
   Locklan's Defence.  I know nothing about it, (Noonan received a good character.)
   NOONAN *— GUILTY. Aged 12.
   LOCKLAN  — GUILTY. Aged 17. Confined Three Months, the last Week Solitary.

HENRY NICHOLLS. I am a confectioner, living in Piccadilly.
