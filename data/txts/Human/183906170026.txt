 1745. GEORGE MARCH was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of May , 8oz. weight of tobacco, value 2 s  .; and 4 shillings; the goods and monies of Robert John King , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined One Month.
 (The prisoner received a good character.)
 1746. PHOEBE MORRIS was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of May , 1/2 peck of potatoes, value 6 d  ., the goods of Joseph Reynolds , her master. JOSEPH REYNOLDS. I am a farmer, and live at Harmondsworth. The prisoner was in my employ to sort potatoes in the warehouse—she lives with a man named Charles Morris—in consequence of suspicion, I had her watched, and she was found with forty potatoes in her pocket—she had a large pocket in front to put them in, and two pockets by her side. ANN FRAZIER. I am the wife of William Frazier, a labourer. I saw the prisoner searched—I took the potatoes out of her pocket—she had one pocket down in front, and two on her side—the one before was larger than usual, and was made of everlasting—she was going home when she was searched. HANNAH REYNOLDS. I am the prosecutor's daughter. I watched the prisoner, and saw her put potatoes in her round frock pocket—I gave information, she was taken as she was going home, and the potatoes found. SARAH TAPPING. I am the wife of a labourer. I was present when the potatoes were taken out of the prisoner's pocket. WILLIAM THOMPSON. I am a horse-patrol. I received the prisoner on the 1st of June.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I had never taken any before, and then I should not if I had not wanted them. GUILTY. Aged 31.— Confined one Week.
 1747. JOHN BYRNE was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of June , 1 piece of handkerchiefs, value 6 s  ., the goods of William Wickham , his master.
 The prosecutor stating his name William of Wickham, the prisoner was ACQUITTED.
 1748. JOHN BYRNE was again  indicted for stealing, on the   5th of June , 1 pencil-case, value 2 s  ., the goods of Frederick Lipscombe ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Six Weeks.
