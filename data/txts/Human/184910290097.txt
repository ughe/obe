 1958. CHARLES EDWARD WILLIAMS was again  indicted for stealing 1 pair of boots, value 3 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Joseph Addis . JOSEPH ADDIS, Junior.  I am nine years old. One afternoon, about the 10th of last May, I was playing on Clapham-common—the prisoner came up to me and asked me to run—I said, "Yes"—he said, "Pull off your boots," and said he would mind them—Hughes, who was with me, was to run also—we were to start at Clapham Church, go to the pound and back again, and then the last was to have two pence, a knife, and a pair of German silver studs, and the other one six pence—he showed them to us, and gave Hughes the knife to put in his boots, and me the pair of studs to put in my boots, and I left my boots in his charge—we went to the place and ran back again, and when we got back he was gone and the boots too. WILLIAM HUGHES. I am eight years old. I was playing on Clapham-common with Addis; the prisoner came and asked us to play with him—we were to walk from the Church to the place where the sheep go, and run back, and we were to take our boots off, and be would mind them—the one who lost was to have two pence, a knife, and a pair of German silver studs; and the one who won was to have three pence or six pence—we walked together to the pound, and ran back, and when we got back he was gone and the boots too. JOSEPH ADDIS, Senior.  The first witness is my son—he went out on this day with boots on, which I supplied him—I was not at home when he came back—I afterwards saw him the same day without any boots. GUILTY. Aged 17. — Confined Six Months .
