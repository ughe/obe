783. JOHN WEST was indicted for stealing. on the 18th of February,
1 bottle, value 3d.; and 1} pint of wine, value 4'. Cd. ; the goods of
Richard Payne Hutchings.
RICHARD PAYNE HUTCHINGS. I keep the Coach ind Horses public-
house, in Welbeek-street. On the afternoon •of the 18th of February the
prisoner came to my bar and asked for half a pint of on credit—I
would not serve him, and just previous to that I had put two bottles ot
wine on a shelf by the bar—I turned to the bar-parlour, by the fire, and
returned in about five minutes, and missed a bottle of wine, the pri-
goner was gone from the house—about twenty nnnutes after, I saw him at
the corner of the mews, where my house is, and accused him of it—he de-
nied it, and abused me very much—I went to a stable just by, where
knew he was in the habit of going, and in the second stall of the stable
found part of my bottle of wine—I got a witness, and went into the
ness-room to see if anybody came to take it, and in about twenty minutes
the prisoner came to the stall and took the wine—I seized him, and said,
"Then I have got you at last ? "—he said, was red wine in the
bottle, the same as I had lost—the flavour was the same as mine, and the
mark on the bottle was the same as the other—nobody but him could
have taken it, as it was gone momentarily.
WILLIAM CARTER (police-constable D 110.) I apprehended the prisoner
—he said he was sorry for it, but he had been drinking o' he should net
have done it.
Prisoner. I did not say I had taken it at all. Witness. He said he
had been drinking or he should not have done it.
Prisoner's Defence. I had been in the house on Sunday moming at
serviee time, and drank more than I should ; and between the morning and
afterioon services, I went out to assist the groom, and went to lay down there
—the groom pushed me out of the stable. and if I had bad a bottle he would
have broken it—I then went to another stable where many people go—the
door was always open—I went to lie down there in the stall, and the wine
laid there—I laid down on it I did not know whether it was wine or beer—
I was in the act of drawing out the cork, but I did not do it.
GU ILTY. Aged 50.—Confined One Month.
