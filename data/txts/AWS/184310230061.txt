Q. As far as you could judge, is not British commerce to the coast of Africa
exceedingly serviceable to the natives A. That depends on which way it
is to be taken-if British commerce is to be employed in the slave-trade, I
doubt its being serviceable to the natives--my station extended from Portan-
dicque down to Madagascar-undoubtedly British commerce must be of great
benefit to Africa.
