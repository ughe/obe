1384. SARAH DUPROSS was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of
March, 3 brushes, value 58. 1 shawl, value 2s. ; 1 flat-iron, value 6d.;
2 table-cloths, value 38.; 1 apron, value 4d. ; 2 pillows, value 6s.;
sheets, value 28. 60.; 1 glass bottle, value 6d. ; 2 shells, value 18. 6d.;
2 cups, value 6d. ; 1 pewter pot, value 2s, 6d. ; and 1 pillow-case, value
61.; the goods of Edward Jones, and that she had been before convicted
of felony.
HE
HANNAH JONES. I am the wife of Edward Jones--we lived in Mount
Etna-place, Mile End-road. The prisoner occupied a furnished room at
my house-I missed these articles now produced—this pot has my hus-
band's initials on it,
EDWARD EDWARDS. I am a pawnbroker. I produce all the articles
mentioned, with the exception of the flat-iron--two of these artieles I took
in myself, of the prisoner, and I was present when she pawned the others.
John BARKER (police-constable K 103.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office (read)-I was
present at the time--the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 35.- Transported for Seven Years.

1385. WILLIAM YOUNG was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
April, 1 coat, value 11. 10s., the goods of Samuel Gibson.
Samuel Gibson. I live in Garnault-place, Clerkenwell. On the 27th
of April my nephew brought me a note- I went down into the passage,
and missed my coat-this now produced is it.
John EDWARD CLINE. I live with my uncle. On the 27th of April a
knock came to the door-- I went, and the prisoner was there-he gave me
a note, which I took to my uncle-when I returned the prisoner and the
coat were gone—the note was fictitious.
William MARTIN. I am in the service of Mr. Boyce, a pawnbroker.
I produce the coat, which I took in of the prisoner, on the 27th of April,
about nine o'clock in the evening.
GUILTY. Aged 23.- Transported for Seven Years.
