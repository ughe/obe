Q. Did it not occur to you that your distant relation might have put
some of these tools there? A. No, I did not ask him I have no reason
to know whether he did or not--the prisoner's workshop is not above half
a mile from my place.
Court. Q. When was the last application on your part, when you told
the prisoner you thought him bound to make good the time he had lost
during his apprenticeship? A. I cannot say whether it was just before he
was out of his time, or after the account is 25l. 4s. 3d. it was about the
22nd, I think-that was the day I got the warrant-I do not think that I
ever heard of any tools of mine supposed to be on the prisoner's premises
till about the 22nd-it was the same day-I do not remember that I had
heard it before-Clark did not give me any information the prisoner said
it was a hard thing for him to work to make that up, and I said it was a
hard thing for me to lose it I said to his father, “I shall give him up bis
indentures"-I left it to himself—I gave him a chance of working it out if
he chose he did not choose-I never demanded the money of the father
-I had the account made out to show his father the time he had lost, and
to see whether it was not right that he should make me some remuneration
for it--I thought it good to let him know, and his father also, the time he
had lost in my service, not taking into account any holidays or illness, but
just working time-Clark made out this account-I certainly knew the
prisoner had been absent when he first demanded his indentures--I have
been losing tools for three years, but till the 22nd of May, I never took
any steps to see whether he had them I had very great reason to suspect
him.
Q. Do you mean that you took any step against the prisoner till you
were foiled in this application? A. I could not prove it before-he had
been suspected a long time it was about the 22nd of May that I was told
I should find tools at his lodging-1 did not set up any claim against him
till he had asked for his indentures.

of these other tools.
