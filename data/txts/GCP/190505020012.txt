servitude for a coinage offence.

OLD COURT.-Wednesday, May 3rd, 1905.
Before Mr. Justice Channell.
357. FREDERICK CHARLES JOYCE (23), Feloniously wounding
Mabel Martin with intent to murder her, or with intent to do her grievous
bodily harm.
MR. PARTRIDGE Prosecuted..

prisoner for a year last Easter Monday-I have been going out with
him, and we were engaged to be married—I do not know how long we
were engaged; I should think since about April 2nd I was going away

tell exactly when that was; it was about last autumn-I did not tell him
