HUME, George (40, tailor), was indicted for and charged on
Coroner's inquisition with the wilful murder of Bertha Hume.
Mr. Travers Humphreys and Mr. Oddie prosecuted,
Prisoner, on being called upon to plead, made no reply. On the
question whether he was mute of malice" or Was unable to plead,
Dr. Seott, medical officer of Brixton Gaol, was about to be called,
when prisoner pleaded Not guilty."
