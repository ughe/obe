341. ELIZA WHITE was indicted for stealing 2 dust-pans, value 2 s  ?. the goods of Philip Keeble ; and that she had been before convicted of felony. JAKE KEEBLE. I am the wife of Philip Keeble—we live at Lambeth. On the 1st of Dec. we missed some dust-pans—these are them I am sure. MART IRVIN. I live in Lansdown-place, Sooth Lambeth. Between eleven and twelve o'clock on the 1st of Dec. I saw the prisoner go into Mr. Keeble's shop, and take two dust-pans, put them under her apron, and come out—I sent the servant over to tell Mrs. Keeble. WILLIAM LUCAS. I went after the prisoner—I saw her with the dust-pans—she saw me, and began to run—there was a hedge just by—she threw the dust-pans into the hedge, and ran on—a man stopped her, and I brought her back to my master's—she said if she begged the gentleman's pardon, would he let her go—these are the dust-pans—they were picked up in my sight. JOHN BROWN. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, at the Newington Session—( read—Convicted  3 rd Feb. , 1845, and confined four  teen days)—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 13.— Transported for Seven Years.—Recommended to Penitentiary.
 342. WILLIAM SMITH and HENRY COLLINS were indicted for steal ing 2 loaves of bread, value 4 d  ., the goods of Joseph Plumridge ; to which
   SMITH  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined One Month . MARY PLUMRIDGE. I am the wife of Joseph Plumridge, a baker—we live at Wandsworth. On the 11th of Dec, about half-past three o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoners came into our shop—Collins asked for a loaf—I gave him one, and asked for the money—Smith took a loaf, and ran away—I said to Collins, "Give the loaf to me"—I took it from his hand, and he ran off—I screamed for my husband. EDWARD GILBERT. I heard a cry of "Stop thief!" and saw the prisoners running—Smith had a loaf, and Collins was running with him—I took them—they divided the loaf, and ate it—they had no money.
   Collins's Defence.  I asked what the bread was a loaf; she said these loaves were 2 d .; Smith said, "Let me have one; "she gave him one; he ran away with it; she was going to give me one, and took it back again; she hallooed out; I ran after Smith; he broke the loaf in two, and gave me half of it.
   COLLINS  — GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined One Month .
 343. ROBERT SAWYER was indicted for stealing 10 sovereigns, the monies of Thomas James Robertson , and ano ther, his master and mistress. THOMAS JAMES ROBERTSON. I am a pawnbroker, and live in New Park-street, Southwark—I am in partnership with my mother—the prisoner was in our employ—on the 21st of Nov. I gave him ten sovereigns to get silver for—he ought to have come back immediately—he did not come—the next time I found him was on the 3rd Dec, at the end of Blackfriars-bridge—I took him—he said as soon as I took him that he had spent all the money, and had only 1 s . 6 d . left. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Nine Months .
