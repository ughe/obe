Prisoner. On the 28th of Sept. I went to Greenwich I had agreed to
meet a man and woman therein I did not find the party; I had a pint or two
of beer it was time to shutup I came out, and coming towards Deptford,
I met a marine, dressed the same as myself, in a white jacket and a frock;
he was running he said he was going to desert, and asked me to go with
him ; I said I should do no such thing I left him to go his way, and I went
mine I have since understood that man has deserted I went on to the bar-
raeks as fast as I could, and I saw a party standing near the barrack-gate I
thought they might be officers I thought, I know I am guilty, I have
broken my confinement but I will go straight in I was going to the gate,
and the policeman accused me of this I am as innocent as a child.

JURY. Q. Was the man who is away anything similar to this person ?

to be the prisoner's father.
