   MR. HONOUR re-examined . This is my watch, and the tame I gave to Hinds to repair.
   Prisoner's Defence . I had been bird-catching, and had the pistol in my hand when I met the policeman; I knew him, and showed him the pistol; I had done very well at it, and after selling my birds, was re turning to my father and mother; on the Saturday night I bad been to Barnet, and this key my mother bought for 4 d ., at a pawnbroker's—the watch I bought; I was not at Tring on the day of the robbery; I was at Watford, at Mr. Green's, behind the market-house.
