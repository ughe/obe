THOMAS ELDRIDGE and GEORGE THAKES were indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of August , 1 jacket, value 5 s  ., the goods of Edmund Frederick Wright , in a certain vessel, in a certain port of entry and discharge. HENRY MOORE. I am a labourer in the West India Docks. On the 23rd of August, I was at work in No. 1. warehouse—I saw the two prisoners coming round the corner, and stopped between two piles of wood, and I saw Thakes give Eldridge a jacket—he put it on-Thakes then said, "That will do, come on"—I met Mr. Stevenson, and told him. ROBERT STEVENSON. I am a wharfinger in the Docks. Moore pointed out the two prisoners as they were coming towards the gate which leads out-Eldridge had the jacket on his back—they separated at the gate of the Export dock, then Eldridge took off the jacket, came towards me with it on his arm, and I took him. JAMES COX. The two prisoners were given into my custody—I asked Eldridge if the jacket belonged to him—he said no, it was given to him by a man, but he did not know his name—I asked Thakes if he knew any thing of it—he said it was his, that he brought it to the Docks with him that morning. EDMUND FREDERICK WRIGHT. I work on board a ship in the Docks. I left this jacket, which is mine, in the forecastle—I do not know the prisoners.
