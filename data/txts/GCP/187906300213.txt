convicted with Workman at the Surey Sessions for housebreaking ou

ville-I know George Fossitt as a lodger of Workman's, and Patterson as
Workman's brother-in-law--I have had conversations with Fossitt about a
burglary in Westminster Bridge Road, but he did not mention Maudsley's
- at the end of January, 1878, he showed me a blank key, and told me
that he and Workman had a good job on-I have heard Fossitt and Work
man talking the matter over together--they said the key was made at
Workman's house and took two days to make, and it fitted the safe it was
intended to open-Fossitt told me that there was about 2,000). in the safe
I told him I should not let that die a natural death, and he said, “I should
think not; it's something worth attending to "-I called on him frequently
