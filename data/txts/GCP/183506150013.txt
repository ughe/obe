1375. JOHN MAHONEY was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of
June, 1 square, value 18. 6d.; I saw, value 4d.; and 1 Rouge, value 2d.;
the goods of William Ash plant: 1 apron, value id. : and 1 handkerchief,
value 3d.; the goods of William Walker.
WILLIAM ASHPLANT. I am a carpenter. On the 1st of June, I was
working at a building in Castle-street, Holborn-I left my tools on the
second floor-there was a hoard ten feet high round the building in the
morning when I came to work I missed them-abont balf-past ten o'clock
the same morning the prisoner was coming down Castle-street very much
intoxicated—he fell down by the building, and the square fell out of his
pocket--the policeman was lifting him up, and I found my saw in his coat
pocket-he had my partner's apron on, and he threw a gouge out of his
pocket-a handkerchief was found in his hat-all these tools are mine
the prisoner is a perfect stranger.
WILLIAM WALKER. I am a carpenter-I was at work at the building
--I lost an apron and handkerchief these are them.
Prisoner's Defence. I was at Thompson and Fearon's, in Holborn-
man came in there and offered the apron for sale for 3d. I tied it round
my waist-he said if I would put another penny towards it, he would
spend the 4d., which I did -he pulled out the handkerchief, and I bought
it of him for 24.-he put the other things down on the pitching block, and
told me to mind them till be returned-as he did not come, I took them up
and walked away with them.

1876. JANE JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of May,
1 iron pot, value 58. ; 1 pinafore, value 2d. il apron, value Sd; 1 brush,
value 1d.; and 1 towel, value 1d. ; the goods of John Carr.
ANN CARR. I am the wife of John Carr, and lodge in Ratcliffe-row,John's
row. On the 25th of May I left home at about half-past six oclock in the
evening, leaving the street door open-I left this property in the kitchen-
the kitchen door was open-I returned at about half-past eight, and found
the prisoner in custody with these things, which are mine.
Mary Hype. I live opposite the prosecutor. On Monday, the 25th
of May, I saw the prisoner loitering about the door-I opened the window,
and watched her-I saw her go into the passage on tip-toe with a baby in
her arms-she returned in ten minutes with the iron pot under the baby-
I called to Taylor, who lives in the house, and he brought her back.
WILLIAM TAYLOR. I stopped the prisoner, who was just putting the
things down-the pinafore and other articles were in the pot.
The prisoner pleaded poverty.
GUILTY. Aged 23. Confined Six Weeks.
