 128. GEORGE WILLIAMS  (23) , Stealing 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  ., the property of Thomas Burton , from his person; to which he PLEADED GUILTY;** Edward Hancock, City-policeman, stated that he had been six times summarily convicted, and that he was one of the most desperate thieves in London, and had twice threatened his (Hancock's) life. Four Years' Penal Servitude.
 129. JAMES MONK  (38) , Stealing 30 skins of leather, value 2 l  ., the property of Morris Sichel ; to which he PLEADED GUILTY  ; Mr. Eick, a carman, of George-yard, City; Thomas Harman, a carman, of Albion-place, London Wall; and Mr. Partridge, gaoler of the Mansion-house, gave him a good character. Judgment Respited.
 130. FREDERICK CLAYTON CLIFT  (25), Feloniously being at large before the expiration of the period to which he had been sentenced; to which he PLEADED GUILTY . It was stated by Dennis Howard, warder of Milbank, that the prisoner, (with two others) had broken out of that prison, and had stated that he got out by bribing one of the warders, which was untrue. Fifteen Years' Penal Servitude.
   There was another indictment against the prisoner for forgery.
