1411. JOHN LLOYD and BRIDGET LLOYD were indicted for
stealing 16 counterpanes, value 61. 8s., the goods of Sally Cann, the mistress
of John Lloyd.
EDMUND CANN, I live with my mother, Sally Cann, at Woolwich -
she washes for the Ordnance department-we have missed a number of
counterpanes which were entrusted to her to wash - John Lloyd was in her
employ-on the 24th of May, when I missed the counterpanes, I was
Looking for them, with the assistance of John Lloyd, and several others;
and while so doing, I saw John Lloyd with this remnant of one of the coun-
terpanes in his hand-he was trying to conceal it from my sight, and then
threw it down-- I took it up immediately it has the mark of the Board of
Ordnance upon it, I found three other pieces, with the same mark, con-
cealed in a bag containing cloth, which was in the store-roona, where the
counterpanes are before they are washed-John Lloyd had access to that
room, and to the counterpanes-he had no particular charge of them I
can speak positively to having lost twenty-six counterpanes.
