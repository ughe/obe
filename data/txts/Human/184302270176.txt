990. JAMES LYON was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of Feb ., 1 plane, value 4 s  .; the goods of Henry Gumm . HENRY GUMM. I am a carpenter, and live in St. John's Wood-terrace. I had a plane safe on Friday evening the 10th of Feb., about half-past five—I found it at the station on the 13th—this is it—it had been in the house keeper's room in an unfinished house. ALEXANDER REID( police constable D  124.) I stopped the prisoner with this plane, on the 13th of Feb.—I asked what he was going to do with it—he said it was not his, and he was carrying it for a man at the West-end of the town.
   Prisoner.  A boy asked me to carry it while he tied up his braces—I showed the boy to the policeman. Witness.  No he did not. GUILTY. * Aged 17.— Confined Two Months .
   Sixth Jury, before Edward Bullock , Esq.

   MR. PRENDERGAST conducted the Prosecution. JOHN JORDAN. I live in Fox-street, Limehouse, and deal in potatoes.
