768. JOSEPH SEALEY, for a like offence.
Messrs. BODKIN and BALDWIN conducted the Prosecution.
May Cox. My husband keeps a grocer's shop in Horseferry-road. On
Thursday, 17th Feb., the prisoner came and asked for half a quartern of
sugar--it came to three farthings-he gave me sixpence-I examined it, bent
it, and told him it was bad- he said he could not take the sugar, and I said he
should not take the sixpence either-he said a gentleman gave it him for
holding his horse-I kept it, and said if he did not go away I would send for
a policeman-he went away, and I placed the sixpence on the parlour mantel-
piece-I afterwards gave it to Cousins, the officer-I am sure it was the same
I bad from the prisoner.
Richard Cousins (policeman, B 30.) On 17th Feb. Mrs. Cox described
the prisoner to me-I took her to the station-she saw the prisoner there
and identified him- produce the sixpence I received from Mrs. Cox.
John Robert ALEXANDER FIELD. I live with Mr. Gold, an oilman, in
York-street, Westminster. About eight o'clock at night, on 17th Feb., the
prisoner came to the shop for a halfpenny candle-be put down a shilling-
I examined it, bit it, and called my master he asked the prisoner where he
got it-he said from a gentleman for holding a borse for about three hours-
the shilling was bad-my master kept it and let the prisoner go-I followed
him-I saw a policeman and told him—the prisoner ran up Snow's-rents-I
ran after him and caught him.
THOMAS Gold. The prisoner came to my shop for a halfpenny candle
- he gave a shilling which sounded bad- I walked round to my boy--he had
bent the shilling and held it in his hand-I asked the prisoner how he got it-
he said a gentleman gave it him for holding his horse-I said the gentleman
paid him very liberally, but had made a bad job of it, and he had better be
off-he went-I told my boy to follow him-he went and brought him back
-I gave the officer the same shilling
Charles TAPPIN (policeman, B 48.) On the night of 17th Feb. Field came

Gold's, and received this shilling from him.
CALEB EDWARD POWELL. These are both counterfeit.
Prisoner's Defence. I had the shilling given me; I did not know it was
