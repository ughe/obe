435. MARTHA WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 14th
of December, 2 printed books, value 158., the goods of John Watson;
and that she had been before convicted of felony.
de
JOHN Watson. I am a stationer, and live at No. 369, Oxford-street.
On the 14th of December the prisoner came to my shop, and wished me
to purchase several trifling song books of her-I refused to have any thing
to do with them—there were several cases of books on my counter-I ob-
served her make a roll against the counter, and give her hand a swing-1
went round, felt down her side, but felt nothing–1 let her go, and thought
nothing further of it till a policeman and a woman came in with this case,
containing this prayer-book and church lessons, which had been safe on
my counter, and had never been sold.
Ann FULLER, I am single--the prisoner is my sister-her friends
have tried all in their power to reclaim her, but all in vain. On the 14th
of December she sent for me to take these books-I asked where she got
them—she said she had taken them from Mr. Watson's shop---this is the
third time of her being tried here and at the Westminster Sessions.
WILLIAM CHADWICK (police-sergeant T 2.) I went with Fuller to the
prosecutor's, and showed him the books, which he said were bis-I took
the prisoner.
