108. The case of the Queen V. Maria Selina Elizabeth Scott (Lady
Scott), John Cockerton, Frederick Kast and William Aylott, adjourned
from December 7th, 1896, owing to the illness of the Defendant Kast, was
resumed on Monday, January 4th, 1897, when it was proved that Kast had
died in prison in the interval. At the close of the onse for the Prosecution,
after MR. LAWSON WALTON and MR. MARSHALL HALL had opened the case for
the Defendants, MR. Justice HAWKINS ruled that Kast's statements could
not be used as evidence. Counsel for the Defendants, upon that ruling,
stated that, as they could not prove all the allegations in the plea of justif-
cation, owing to the exclusion of Kast's evidence, the Defendants would with
draw the justifioation, and plead guilty to publication. SiR FRANK LOOK-
WOOD accepted a suggestion of MR. JUSTICE HAWKINS that the plea of
justification should be amended by striking out all the allegations with
regard to Kast, but Counsel for the Defendants did not consent to that
course, as the Counts relating to Kast would still remain, and as, in
their opinion, Kast's evidence contained strong corroboration of the case of
the other Defendants. The Jury thereupon returned a verdict of Guilty,
and the Defendants were sontenced to Eight Months' Imprisonment each.
