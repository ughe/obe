MR. SERGEANT BOMPAS. QAnd in that statement which you signed
and swore to, it was stated that when you returned from Messrs. Bult
you saw Mrs. Abrahams? A. It was- I asked the officer to correct
that statement, and he heard me-it appears he did not do so, but I did

statement.
Q. Did you sleep in the house of Mr. Solomon, in the Strand ?
A. I did I was present when the house was searched the first time
-I did not go up stairs--I have no rooom-I slept in the shop under
the counter-I had two boxes-one on the third floor, in which I kept
my clothes, which was broken open, while I was away, and another
in the top of the house, where I kept some clothes which belonged
to my deceased mother-it was up four pairs of stairs in an attic, which
we used entirely for lumber boxes and all kind of lumber-I was
not present when the officers searched that room--my other box was
broken open during my absence, or I would have opened it for them-I
was present the first time when they searched the lower part of the pre-
mises, but I did not go up stairs-Roe went up to search the gold was
in one of the boxes- I would have opened that box for them if I had been
up stairs-it was on the fourth floor where the gold was--I received a
message from Mr. Solomon to give it up-Mr. Abrahams gave me that
message--I had no communication with Mr. Abrahams from the time Mr.
Solomon was taken up, till he came with my deposition I received a
message from him in the presence of Lea the officer through Mr.
Abrahams to give up the bars to Lea-Mr. Abrahams, Mr. Solo-
omon's solicitor, delivered the message to me - Mr. Abrahamsson
came to the lodging, and told me his father wanted to speak to me, and
when I met him in the Strand he was with Lea the officer-he told me I
was to give Lea the five bars in my possession, and I accordingly did so-
I was out of the way at the time-I went down to the Strand from where
I was concealed, leaving the two Solomons at the place it was in the
day-time, about eleven o'clock-I considered I was safe then, as I was
told Mr. Abrahams wanted to speak to me I was staying at the
house of a Mr. Mosely, No. 32, Haymarket-he is no relation of So-
lomon.
Cross-examined by Mr. ADOLPHUS. Q. How long have you been in
the employ of Henry Solomon. A. Between eight and nine years I am
a Christian- I was never bound to Mr. Solomon by any article or indenture
of any kind-when I saw the gold dust come in such large quantities I
had very strong suspicions it was the gold dust stolen--I had some
doubt certainly.
