2343. RICHARD PARSONS was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of
September, 1 boot, value 6s., the goods of William Berrall.
WILLIAM BERRALL. I am a bootmaker, and live in Marylebone-lane.
About nine o'clock in the evening of the 1st of September, I received infor-
mation which induced me to go out-I missed a boot from the middle of the
shop-I ran into an adjoining square-I could not meet with the person
- I returned to my shop—the prisoner came and took another boot from
the hook-I said, "What do you want?"-I saw a boot behind him under
his jacket-I took it from him, it was mine-I said, “You have taken my
boot"-he said, “You have taken no boot from me"-my man took him
to the station.
Prisoner. I was in liquor, I did not know what I was about.
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Confined One Year.de
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)

value 6d.; the goods of William Nettleton Boyce : 6 printed-books, va-
Tue 8s. 4d. ; 1 pair of stockings, value 9d.; and 6 pairs of gloves, value
21.; the goods of Caroline Catherine Walker: 1 purse, value 3d., and 1
ring, value 1d., the goods of Mary M'Kensie.
CAROLINE CATHERINE WALKER. My mother keeps the King's Head
tavern in Pudding-lane; the prisoner was our barmaid. On the 2nd of
August my mother lost her wedding ring-in going up stairs to make the
veds I found the prisoner's keys under her pillow-I saw this ring and some
Suver in a purse in the prisoner's box-the ring is my sister's, Mrs.
Boyce-I put the purse in again, and locked the box-my sister went for &
policeman, who found in her box some gloves, books, a pair of mittens,
and a pair of stockings-the gloves, books, and mittens are mine the
other things are my sister's.
