I have attended the sales at Barbican on Tuesdays and Fridays for the last twenty-six years—I remember this transaction because I showed a brown mare to Cooper on the Monday, he bid £12 10s.—I told him I could not afford to sell it, and put it in my trap to drive from Barbican, then we adjourned to the Black Horse and transacted our business, and I saw the prisoner come in and another man follow him—there were other transactions which I did not notice—it was after Barnet Fair, which lasts three days—I have known the prisoner six or seven years.
