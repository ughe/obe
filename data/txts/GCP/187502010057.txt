WILLIAM HENRY ALLSOPP. I am a member of the firm of Morgau, Allsopp
& Co., commission agents—1 bave seen McCorkell several times—he said
"I want to dispose of some chrome ore which is at Pesth" I said "How
are you going to get it here "She gave me an analysis of it, which I think
was made by a man in Dublin, and left a small sample, which I returned to
him-he valued it at from 6l. to 81. per ton—it was not crushed, and there
was a considerable difficulty about selling it-my partner said that an
engineer should go to Hungary to examine the property and verify the
statement before any advance could be made, which would cost 1001.--that
was at the end of May or the beginning of June-I last saw him on the
subject at the end of October-I never made him believe that I would lend
him any money, it is not our business--if the matter had turned out pro-
perly and there was a market for the ore I should have thought twice about
advancing any money-we had two page of Professor Hull's report copied
and sent it to a friend, and I believe the analysis was satisfactory, it was a
large analysis and it appeared to be bond fide-a proposal was made to bring
out a company to sell the ore, but I declined that as the company had been
brought out before-it was Mr. Boddington who introduced MeCorkell—I
did not see Mr. Rock at all the matter came into my office about the end
of May, and at the end of October I finally declined to have anything to do
with it-I advanced no money-no money was forthcoming to go to Hun-
gary with I was not asked to supply it-I did not endeavour to raise
10,0001. at 2,0001. interest-it was a commission-this is the letter and this
is our reply. This was dated June 27th, 1874, proposing payment of a lump
sum of 300l. in lieu of commission, and 11. per cent. if an agency was after-
wards required.) Boddington wanted 3001. for introducing the business, but
we cannot read those letters from our letter-book, they are Boddington's
no terms were mentioned for a loan, it never went so far as offering terms-

him in my office-I saw him last about the month of October
Cross-examined by MR. METOALFE. He was then carrying on business in
