6152. MARY JONES was indicted for stealing 1 shawl, value 9d. ; and 1
pinafore, 3d.; the goods of Charles Dutton, from the person of Charles Dut-
ton the younger.
ELIZABETH DUTTON. I am the wife of Charles Dutton, who lives in the
Colonade, Brunswick-square-this child in my arms is Charles Dutton the
younger- he is three years and a half old-I bad a shawl-I divided it into
two parts, and put one part on the child, and when he was brought back be
had lost the shawl and his pinafore.
ANN BEALE. I saw the prisoner on the 10th of Nov., from twelve to
one o'clock--she was leading this child by the hand-he had part of a
shawl on and a pinafore-I saw the prisoner passing my house next morn-
ing, in the direction to Mrs. Dutton's, and some one said, in her hearing,
“That is the person that had hold of Dutton's child yesterday"--the prisoner
turned and went back.
MARIA EAGLES, of No. 40, Collonade. Between twelve and one o'clock
on the 10th of Nov., I saw the prisoner with the child, which had a shawl
and pinafore on-I told Mrs. Beale, next morning, that I had seen her with
the child-the prisoner turned and walked away.
