witness in June and July, and attended to Smith's room on the ground

grate-I saw white splashes on the grate like whiting-I saw a packet of
something like whiting in the cupboard-there was white sediment in the

-I am almost sure Fisher is the man-he came once in the afternoon

WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. I am Inspector of Coin to His Majesty's
Mint-these are counterfeit florins-three are from one mould and one
from a different mould-these are fragments of moulds—I find impressions
which the enlarged photographs represent- I went through them yester-
day-by dipping & coin in this solution of cyanide of potassium it becomes
silvered without the aid of a battery, producing the effect described by
Arrow on a bronze halfpenny—these small pieces of metal, kook shaped
are called coin ladders and are used to hold the coin in the solution-
the other things produced are capable of being used in making bad coin.
