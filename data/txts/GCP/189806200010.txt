DAVIES, Mayor.
ELIZABETH ADA RICHARDS. I am the wife of John Richards, and
assist him in the management of the Victoria Hotel, Charterhouse Street
-on June 4th I was in the bar, and Simmonds came in for a pony
of bitter, and gave me a florin-I said, “This is bad," and went to the
head barman-when I came back Simmonds was gone-I saw him at the
Police-court half an hour afterwards he did not wait for his change, but
he drank the beer.
ALICE POYNTER. I am barmaid at the Farringdon Hotel-on June 4th,
in the afternoon, I served Simmonds with a pint of ale and a twopenny
cigar-- he gave me a florin-I gave him the change
WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. I am Inspector of Coin to H.M. Mint-
these two florins are bad and from different moulds.
The Prisoners' statements before the Magistrate.-Davis says : "I was

me for being with a man, and I don't know him at all"-Simmonds says:

WILLIAM HENRY WHITE. I live at 138, Pester Road, West Ham, and
am landlord of 47, West Street, Bromley, which I let out in furnished
apartments about two months ago the prisoner hired my top back room
at 68. a week, and gave the name of Brown, a tailor-he lived there with
a woman-I have been in the habit of calling there for my rent-he left
on Monday, May 16th-the woman had left on the previous Saturday,
she is indicted with him—she was locked up, and I told him he had better
leave—as soon as he left I put a padlock on the door, but afterwards gave
the key to Mrs. Smith to go in and clear the room-no one else had access
to it till the police came- I then gave the key to the inspector.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I was present on May 15th when you
assaulted Margaret Sullivan-when she lived next door it was not with
the witness Ashworth, but with another man, not the man I let the rooms
to, but he was connected with you—when I went up on the Monday after
noon you were asleep, and Margaret Sullivan woke you up.
Re-examined. I have four houses-the prisoner was in No. 47, and
Margaret Sullivan in No. 49.
