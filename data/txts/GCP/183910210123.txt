2899. BENJAMIN JENKS was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of
October, 28lbs. weight of lead, value 58., the goods of Frederick Chitten-
den and others, his masters.
JOHN EDWARDS (police-sergeant B 14.) On the afternoon of the 22nd
of October I saw the prisoner leave the back of the house, No. 10, Bel-
grave-square, where there were some repairs doing-I followed, and stop-
ped him in Knightsbridge, and asked what he had got-he said, “Nothing"
- I found one piece of lead in his trowsers, and three other pieces in his
waistcoat and jacket pockets.
PREDERICK CHITTENDEN. I am a plumber. I have two partners the
prisoner was in our employ, and was at work as our journeyman, at No. 10,
Belgrave-square-I have no doubt this lead is ours he was working with
such lead.
Prisoner's Defence. A person in the trade gave me the lead.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 41.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined One Month.
2900. MARY FORD was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of October,
1 jacket, value 11. 10s. ; 1 waistcoat, value 58.; and 1 handkerchief, value
58.; the goods of Charles Powell.
CHARLES POWELL. I am a sailor, and live in a court in New Gravel-lane.
On the 22nd of October, about nine o'clock at night, I met the prisoner,
in the Black Horse public-house, Tower-bill-I went with her to the
King of Prussia public-house, in Blue Anchor-yard--it was then close
upon twelve o'clock-I after that went to a house in Rose-court, and went
to bed with her-I was a little the worse for liquor-I took off my jacket,
Waistcoat, and handkerchief, and put them on a chair-I awoke about
even o'clock in the morning, and missed them the prisoner said she
knew nothing about them, and that I had not any on when she brought
me to the house, but I know that I had put them on the chair I said if
she would let me have my things I did not wish to hurt her-but she
de fun, and laughed at me I said I would give her some money if she
would give me them back-I lost some money. I think I had about 6s.
