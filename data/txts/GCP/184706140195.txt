1522. JAMES LEE JONES was indicted for stealing 2 half-crowns,
3 shillings, and one 11d. piece, the monies of Job Charlton, from his person.
Mr. Wilde conducted the Prosecution.
JOB CHARLTON. I am a beer-seller, and live in Hog-lane, Greenwich.
On Monday, the 7th of June, the prisoner came to my house between five
and six o'clock-he and I afterwards had some conversation together-I am
clear that I had at that time 8s. and a lid silver coin in my pocket-I did not
give away or put away from my pocket that money-no one but the prisoner
sufficiently near me to have taken it from me--the prisoner went away about
seven o'clock-in about a quarter of an hour after, I found my money was
gone-I went after the prisoner-I found him, and charged him with having
my money-he said to the police officer, “I have some money, but what
money I have, that man gave me for adulterating his beer"-the mouey was
produced, and it was mine.
