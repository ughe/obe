HARRY WINTERBOURNE. The prisoner was in our employ to workhe
used to come to my premises generally every day-on the 7th of June I went
to his room with the officer, and saw a duplicate of a hearth-rug found-this

entries in it--1 have no doubt it is his handwriting.
Prisoner. Q. Is there not an entry of the rug in the book about five
months back A. I have traced my books for ten months back, and can find
no such entries.

-I found a duplicate of this rug, and this account-book.
