WILLIAM MULLER. I live at Oxford Road, Kilburn, and am divisional surgeon to the X division of police—I saw the prosecutor on 8th January at 8 p.m.—he had two severe wounds over the parietal bone on the left side of his head, very deep, but not entirely down to the bone—a blunt instrument had caused them—I saw that jemmy  on that night—the two wounds were likely to have been caused by it—he is seriously ill now. GUILTY on the third count. — Ten Years' Penal Servitude.
