Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant .
 2355. JOHN BROOKES was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of August , 1 pair of trowsers, value 125.; and 2 waistcoats, value 10 s  .; the goods of William Lidstone , his master; and that he had been before con victed of felony: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 25.— Transported for Ten Years.
 (There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
 2356. GEORGE SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of August , 1 pair of scales, value 5 S  ., the goods of John Barnard , and that be had been before convicted; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 23.— Transported for Seven Years.
 2357. JAMES POTTS was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of Au gust , 1 knife, value 2 d  ., the goods of Charles Thomas ; and 1 pair of boots, value 4 s  .; the goods of Henry Smith , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months.
 2358. GEORGE ROBERTS was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of August , 1 brush, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of James Munting ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined Ten Days, and Whipped.
 2359. SARAH GOBBY was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of Au gust , 1 pewter pot, value 6 d  ., the goods of William Pardy :— also, on the   22nd of August , 1 pair of shoes, value 1 s  ., the goods of George Frederick Lambert ; to both which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Six Months.
 2360. MARY BRESTON was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of August , 3lbs. weight of feathers, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Henry Aitken ; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Three Months.
 2361. JOHN MACK was indicted for stealing, on the   28th of July , 1 bridle, value 20 s  .; and 1 brush, value 1 s  .; the goods of Charles Henry Bloxan ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 40.— Confined Three Months.
 2362. CORNELIUS KNILL was indicted for embezzlement.
   MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution . WILLIAM BOURCHIER. I am a fur-manufacturer, and live in William-street, West Strand. The prisoner was my errand-boy—I sent him in June, to Mr. Shipley, of Regent-street, for 18 s .—when he returned, I asked him for it—he said Mr. Shipley would call and pay—he has never paid it to me—on the 23rd of July I sent him to Mr. Johnson for 2 l .—he has never paid me that—I believe the answer he brought back, was, that he would call. JOHN SHIPLEY. This bill—( looking at one )—is one my father owed to the prosecutor—I paid it to the prisoner on the 21st of June—he signed this receipt to it for 18 s .
