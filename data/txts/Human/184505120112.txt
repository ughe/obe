NEW COURT .— Friday, May  16 th , 1845.
   Fifth Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1122. HENRY JOHNSON was indicted for stealing 3lbs. weight of bacon, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of William Gunston ; and that he had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months .
 1123. HENRY WILKINS was indicted for stealing 1 sovereign, 2 half-crowns, 11 shillings, and 2 sixpences, the monies of John Emly , his roaster; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Three Months .
 1124. ANN RAYNER was indicted for stealing 5 spoons, value 3 l  ., the goods of Henry Hayes , her master; to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 45.
 1125. SAMUEL ISAIAH SOLOMON was indicted for stealing 4 neckerchiefs, value 18 s  .; and 11 waistcoats, 6 l  . 2 s  .; the goods of Elias Moses and another, his masters; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Six Months .
 1126. WILLIAM SHARP was indicted for stealing 6 pairs of boots, value 18 s  .; and 18 pairs of shoes, 10 s  .:—also, 22 pairs of boots, 3 l  . 13 s  .; and 34 paira of shoes, 4 l  . 9 s  .:—also, 3 pairs of shoes, 12 s  .; and 6 pairs of boots, 12 s  .: also, 4 pairs of boots, 10 s  .:—also, 3 pairs of shoes, 8 s  . 9 d  .; and 3 pairs of boots, 6 s  .; the goods of Daniel Depass and another, his masters; to all which indictments he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Twelve Months .
 1127. JOHANNA SMITH was indicted for stealing 1 pencil-case, value 6 d  .; 1 shilling, and 5 pence; the property of Octavius Rainsford , her master; to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined One Month .
 1128. JOHN RIDDETT was indicted for embezzling 2 l  . 19 s  ., which he received on account of Ann Segrott, his mistress; and that he had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 43.— Confined One Year .
 1129. EDWIN CLARKE was indicted for embezzling 5 l  . 1 s  . 4 d  ., which he received for James Withers , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined Nine Months .
 1130. JAMES GOUGH was indicted for stealing 1 copper, value 16 s  . 6 d  .; and 28lbs. weight of lead, 3 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Edward Dalby Chaston , and fixed to a building; against the Statute, &c.; and that he had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined One Year .
 1131. MARY PRITCHARD was indicted for feloniously forging and uttering an acquittance and receipt for 16 s  ., with intent to defraud Thomas Marston, well knowing it to be forged.
