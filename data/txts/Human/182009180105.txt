 1197.  WILLIAM WILLIAMS and ANN WILLIAMS were indicted for that they, on the 21st of July , in a public street and highway, in and upon Mary Busby , wilfully, maliciously, and feloniously did make an assault, with intent to spoil, burn, and deface her garments; and did wilfully, maliciously, and feloniously spoil, burn, and deface one shawl, value 10 s.; one gown, value 10 s.; one petticoat, value 3 s., and one pair of stockings, value 1 s., her property, and part of her garments on her person in wear, to her great damage.
   MARY BUSBY. I live in Herod's-place, Well-street, Well-close-square. On the 21st of July, about half-past eleven o'clock at night, I was coming from a dancing-house next door to Mrs. Williams, in Dock-street; I had lived with her about four months before; we had quarelled before this happened. As I came out I saw her at her door, but did not see her son; she moved her hands towards me; I did not see anything; nobody but her was near me. I immediately felt a great pain at my ancle like burning; I went home took my stocking off, and found three or four holes burnt in it; my shawl was all over with the same sort of stuff, and had holes in it; my gown was the same, and my petticoat. In the morning I went to Lambeth-street and took out a warrant against her; the other prisoner came to me and said he would serve me ten times worse, and said he was behind her at the time.
   FRANCIS FREEMAN. I am an officer of Lambeth-street. The prosecutrix came to me for a warrant and gave me her clothes, which I produce; the stockings appeared to be burnt with vitriol; all her clothes were burnt. I apprehended Mrs. Williams - she declared her innocence.
   ANN WILLIAMS'S Defence. I was washing very hard all that day, and never saw her. All this is done out of spite; she was a common girl.
   SARAH SEABROOK. I live in Dock-street. Busby complained to me of being hurt, and shewed me her clothes; her ancle was burnt just like a pea; there was a great hole all down her petticoat.
   WILLIAM WILLIAMS-   NOT GUILTY.
   ANN WILLIAMS-   GUILTY. Aged 44. Confined Six Months.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1198.  WILLIAM MARSHALL was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of September , one pair of boots, value 2 s., and four pair of upper-leathers, value 2 s., the goods of William Waters; and JOHN BAYLIS was indicted for feloniously receiving the same, knowing them to be stolen.
   WILLIAM WATERS. I am a boot and shoemaker, and live in Whitecross-street; Marshall was my journeyman. On the 1st of September I missed the boots, I went with Prince to Marshall's lodgings, told him he had been robbing me, and found the boots there. He said he took them off the stand as I sat at work. I went to Baylis's, and found four pair of upper-leathers there.
   JOSEPH PRINCE. I am an officer. I found the boots at the prisoner's lodgings.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 MARSHALL -    GUILTY. Aged 46. Confined Six Months, and Whipped.
 BAYLIS -    NOT GUILTY.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1199.  DANIEL GILBERT was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of September , one gown, value 8 s., the goods of Eliza Parratt.
   ELIZA PARRATT. I am servant to Mr. Long, who keeps a public-house in Charles-street, Hatton-garden. On the 2d of September the prisoner came in and asked to go backwards, he was told some one was there. He had half a pint of beer and then went. I saw him come into the kitchen and turn back. I went on the cellar-stairs, saw him go into the kitchen, open the dresser-drawer, and take my gown out - he was stopped in the passage with it.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 28. Confined Three Months, and Publicly Whipped.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1200.  WILLIAM DANGTRY was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of July , one spoon, value 3 s.; one pocket-book. value 3 d., and one 1 l. Bank note, the property of William Buckera.
   WILLIAM BUCKERA. I am a weaver, and live at Mile End; the prisoner was my servant, he left on the morning of the 19th of July without notice, and I missed my pocket-book. On the Sunday following I had him apprehended.
   JOHN CUSS, I am a pawnbroker. The prisoner pledged the spoon with me for 2 s.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I had only 6 s. a week.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Whipped and Discharged.
