-Godwin called on me early in February ; I had known him four or five
years-he asked if I could dispose of some machines for a young fellow
whose name he did not mention-a day or two afterwards a Kangaroo
bieycle arrived, but I do not know who brought it, I was out-Godwin
called a day or two afterwards and said that he wished a few alterations
made in it as soon as possible, as he intended keeping it for himself if
possible-while I was making them he called again and said, “I shall
not be able to keep that machine. I must get you to sell it"-I gave him
this form to fill up, which he took away and brought back signed,

retaining my expenses-he called again on February 3rd, and said he was
