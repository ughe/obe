SARAH MARY COY. I am the wife of James Coy. The prisoner lived in my service. On the 15th of July, as she was going away, I found a great many things of mine in her box—these are them—they wen all io her box, with the exception of one table-cloth, which she had thrown into a fire place.
   Prisoners Defence.  My mistress was very angry, and the books I put into ny pocket; if I put some things into the box it was at the time we moved; the holland is not my mistress's. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Four Months .

 2200. JANE ROBINSON and JANE SUSANNAH DAYLEY were in dicted for stealing 1 coal-scuttle, value 10 s  ., the goods of Mary Huffam ; to Which
   ROBINSON  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 40. MARY HUFFAM. I am a widow, and live in Russell-street, Brixton. I lost this coal-scuttle—I cannot say when—this is it. JOHN BARNETT NEALE. I am a pawnbroker. This coal-scuttle was pledged by Dayley on the 29th of June, in the name of Ann Day.
   Dayley.  It was lent me by Jane Robinson to pledge.
   DAYLEY  — NOT GUILTY
 2201. MARY ROBINSON was indicted for stealing 2 sheets, value 12 s  ., the goods of Mary Huffam . MARY HUFFAM. Amongst other things I lost some sheets—these are two of them—my name is on them. JOHN BARNETT NEALE. I took in these sheets of the prisoner, in the name of Robinson—she is the sister of Jane Robinson, but I was not aware of it—she pawned very few things, but Jane a great many.
   Prisoner.  I got them of my sister. NOT GUILTY
