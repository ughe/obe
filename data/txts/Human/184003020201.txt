Brown. Q.  Was not I on the path when you took me? A.  Half on and half off, and stooping over the prosecutor, I pulled him off—after I took him, the prosecutor got up and ran along the road, calling "Police."—I am sure that was after I pulled him off, not before—I was quite sober. THOMAS RILEY. I am a policeman. I was on duty on the morning of the 25th of February, in St. George's-road, and saw the prosecutor coming in a slanting direction across the road towards where I stood—he was the worse for liquor, but capable of going home—he walked straight, but at the station-house I saw he was the worse for liquor—I saw five per sons coming after him, six or seven yards from him—they could not see me where I was, which was within twenty yards of them—I saw Tanner come from the five, and trip the prosecutor up, and fall on him—Brown came up, and threw himself on the prosecutor directly he fell—he was about four yards behind him—I saw Lee come along the footway the moment the prosecutor was tripped up—I saw Tanner make an attempt at the prosecutor's pocket—I ran towards them—I saw Lee take hold of Brown.—I got up at the same time, and took Tanner, who was stooping over the prosecutor, and upon him, but on seeing me coming he straightened up, and tried to run away—I collared him—he tried all he could to get from me, by throwing himself about—they were both secured, and taken to the station-house—I am sure they were both sober.
   Tanner. Q.  Did you see me with the other men? A.  He was about four yards in advance of them—it was all done in a minute—they were all walking in the same direction.
   Brown. Q.  When Lee took hold of me, where was the prosecutor? A.  Laying on the ground, but when he got up he was confused, and ran up the road a few paces, calling "Police," not seeing that we had got them.
   Tanner's Defence.  I was going home entirely by myself—I was in liquor, and do not recollect knocking the man down—if I did, it was not to rob him.
   Brown's Defence.  When I heard the man call out, I ran to the corner of the street, and saw him lying down—I did not touch him—he never said at the station-house that I bit him—I was on the path when he took me.
   TANNER  — GUILTY. Aged 23.
   BROWN  — GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Twelve Months.
