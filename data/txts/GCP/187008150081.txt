came to nurse my mistress, who was ill-she nursed her for a fortnight-

dress which Selfe asked me to give her for the purpose of laying Mrs.
Simmons out-when Selfe came on Tuesday, I asked her where the night
dress was-she said “It is somewhere about;" but never mentioned it as
having been given to her.
