Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. What time did he come on the
28th ? A. About nine o'clock in the evening-we had the gas lighted
I went out directly, and saw the policeman mark the shilling-he came at
much the same time on the 29th.
JAMES BROok (police-constable L 118.) I recollect going into the
shop on the 28th-1 received a shilling from Mr. Hoad--this is it.
SARAH ANN KNIGHT. I live in Garden-row. On the 28th of Sep-
tember, about nine o'clock in the evening, I was playing at hoop in Garden-
row--the prisoner came and asked me to go and get a pennyworth of
apples-he told me to go up the street-there is a green-grocer's there
--- he gave me a shilling, and said he would give me a halfpenny if I would
make haste, and he would walk up and down while I went with it-Mrs.
Beaumont keeps that shop-I gave the shilling to her-I had no other in
consequence of something Mrs. Beaumont said, I went after the prisoner,
and saw him standing still by the court-I afterwards saw him at the top
of the street- I looked at him, and he ran away he did not wait for the
change or the apples--I am sure he is the man.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you ever seen him before ? A. No, not
till he gave me the shilling-be was not far off when I saw him at the
end of the street-about a week after I saw him before the Magistrate
the policeman asked whether I saw anybody like him-I said, “ Yes, I
think that is him."
Mr. Payne to ANN HOAD. Q. Did you see the man put the shilling
into his mouth ? A. Yes I was behind the counter-my husband was
just coming through the passage I said, “Wait a bit," and then I called
my husband-when he saw my husband coming I saw him put it into
his mouth-the parlour window is at the end of the shop-I tapped
there for my husband-the policeman found a good shilling on him.
Alice ANN BEAUMONT. I recollect Sarah Ann Knight coming to me
on the evening of the 28th of September, at nine o'clock-she gave me a
shilling- I gave it to my husband-he gave it me again, and I gave it to
Robett Penning.
HARMSTON BEAUMONT. I am the husband of Alice Ann Beaumont.
On the 28th of September I was sitting in a room adjoining my shop my
wife gave me a shilling- I went down the street-1 bent the shilling first,
and gave it to my wife again- I am sure it was the shilling she gave me
-I saw her give it to Penning.
Robert PENNING. Mrs. Beaumont gave me a shilling on the 28th of
September-I kept it about half an hour I gave it to Goff the officer-1
marked it at the time.
Cross-examined. Q. Did any other boy have it? A. No-we did not
play with it—we were going to play.
CHARLES BURGESS Gory (police-constable L 31.) On the 28th of Sep-
tember I went to the house where Penning lived-he gave me this shilling
---he marked it before he gave it me.
