HAROLD JENNINGs WHITE. I am a solicitor of 8, Whitehall Place, and
a collector of postage stamps-] know Creeke-on February 12th he
called on me and offered me these stamps for sale-they are two each of
I.R. Official King's Head 5s.. 10s. and f1-he asked f42 for them, which I
agreed to pay-I paid him by this cheque payable to bearer-he asked me
to give him an open cheque as he wanted to get it cashed-I don't think
he asked me to draw it 44 Pay Cash or Bearer," he simply asked for an open
cheque-my cheques are printed 46 or Order.'
Cross-ezamined by Mr. REED. I keep my counterfoils, it has simply
Cash I.R. Official on it-that would call to my mind to whom it Was
paid- had no difficulty in arriving at the fact that I paid the f42 to
Creeke-I had no entry in any book about itI have known Creeke for
some years-] am a member of the Philatelie Society, and I have known him
in connection with that Society-1 have always heard that he is a gentle
man of integrity-142 is a good price for the stamps, but I think it is good
value for stamps of that denomination-I have bought many rare stamps-
I bought some from him three or four years ago-I buy chiefly from dealers,
but I have bought some from collectors-I have bought other stamps
since I bought these from Creeke-] bought a 10d. stamp of the Office
of Works for which I gave f10-my experience shows me that there is
nothing unusual about official stamps being bought by keen collectors-
I have seen unused official stamps in the collections of other collectors-
I have seen them advertised at auctions, and have been present when they
have been sold-I have a great number of them myself.
Cross-ezamined by Mr. WILLIams. I first began to collect in 1893-
I do not know if I first saw an official stamp then.
Re-examined. By some means official stamps get out-it has been
a matter of common knowledge for some years that Creeke has had the
entree to Somerset House.
