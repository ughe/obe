John ANGLER (policeman, 144 L). On 2nd Aug. I was called to the
Railway beer shop-I took the prisoners into custody-I found on James
Griggs a good sixpence and 4£d. in coppers-Mr. Barham gave me this half
crown, and this shilling (produced).
James Griggs. I was very tipsy. Witness. No, he was not.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These are both counterfeit.
John Griggs's Defence. On that afternoon I was in the Red House, play-
ing at skittles; I saw this man there, and we had some play, and money
passed backwards and forwards; I came with him a little way home, and we
called in to have this beer.
JOHN GRIGGS-Guilty. Aged 25.
Confined Nine Months.
JAMES GRIGGS-Guilty. Aged 30.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 18TH, 1854.
