 820. HORATIO WILLIAM MAY , JOSEPH ENSELL MAY, and OCTAVIUS HENRY MAY, were again  indicted for stealing two gets of harness, the goods of William David Ramsay .
   MR. PURCELL Prosecuted. JOHN SMITH and WILLIAM DAVID RAMSAY  repeated their former evidence.
   RAMSAY stated that on the night of the  18 th he locked up his coach house, His harness was all safe the next morning. About  5 o'clock he found the doors wide open, but no marks of violence. He missed the greater part of two sets of harness, and he afterwards identified the harness as his property. The prisoners repeated their defence. GUILTY. JOSEPH ENSELL MAY then PLEADED GUILTY to a con  viction of felony in December, 1879, at Clerkenwell .— Two Years' Hard Labour. HORATIO MAY  — Twelve Months' Hard Labour. OCTAVIUS HENRY MAY  — Nine Months' Hard Labour .
