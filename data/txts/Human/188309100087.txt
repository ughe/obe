the amount was under 2 s . 6 d .—Cantle kept this grocery cash-book ( produced )—here is an entry of 17th October, where the total is overcast by 20 s .—that is Mears's book in red ink—the average refer to it—in addition to entering his other bills and amounts, Mears would have to check the amount put down by the customer, with six, seven, or eight persons waiting to be attended upon—he enters
