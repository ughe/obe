MUSGROVE, Mayor.
WILLIAM NEWCOMBE. I am assistant to a pawnbroker, 79, Snow-hitt.
I produce these articles; they were pawned by the two prisoners, but
Donovan offered them in pawn on 20th March in the forenoon.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you know the prisoners before? A. Yes
they were in the habit of pawning there.
Driscoll. I had been there twenty minutes before Donovan came in:
was pawning a shawl there. Witness. I do not recollect that-Donovan
had left the crape at home, and they both seemed anxious about it; she
went to fetch it, and Driscoll staid there till she came back.
CHARLOTTE ROUE re-examined. This habit-shirt and table-cloth are
mine.
Cross-examined. Q. Is there any mark on them? A. No, only my
own needlework.
DONOVAN. Aged 19.2
DRISCOLL. Aged 20.
Guilty of Receiving.-Confined Nine Months.
(There was another indictment against the prisoners.)
THIRD COURT.-Wednesday, April 9th, 1851.
PRESENT—Mr. Ald. Challis; Mr. Ald. Carter; and Mr. COMMON
SERJEANT.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant and the Seventh Jury.
861. THOMAS CHISLETT, embezzling 25l., and 131. 188. 9d.; the
moneys of Thomas Fricker and another, his masters : also, stealing 1
watch, value 91.; the goods of William Strickland: also, unlawfully
obtaining by false pretences, 1 watch, value 31.; the goods of William
Strickland : to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Transported for Seven Years.
862. THOMAS WATKINS, and HENRY JOHN WILLIAMS,
stealing 2 purses, and 6 shirt-studs, value 38. 6.; the goods of James
Pegg: Watkins having been before convicted: to which
WATKINS pleaded GUILTY. Aged 15.-Confined Eight Months.
WILLIAMS pleaded GUILTY. Aged 15.-Confined Four Months.
863. JOHN TAYLOR, stealing 8 half-crowns, 140 shillings, 36 six-
pences, and 6 groats; the moneys of Elizabeth Ware and another, his
mistresses : to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 19.--Confined Twelve Months.
864. THOMAS WILLIAMS, stealing 1 looking-glass and stand, value
98.; the goods of William Kirby: to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 22.-Confined Four Months.
865. OLIVER JACKSON, embezzling 3 shillings: also, stealing 7
shillings; the moneys of John Cornish, his master: to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Six Months.
866. JAMES SNELLING and JAMES PELLIN, stealing 1 iron pot,
and 2 scales, value 78.; the goods of Thomas Ellis, the master of Snelling:
Pellin baving been before convicted.
