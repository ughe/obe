 151. THOMAS M'PHERSON and CHARLES HOPKINS , unlawfully conspiring to cheat Edward Varley of his goods and moneys.
   MESSRS. BODKIN and POLAND conducted the Prosecution. JAMES KING. I am an engine-driver, on the Dover Railway. I resided, in Oct. last, in the Old Kent-road—in Aug., 1850, I gave an order for one or two pairs of trowsers, I forget which, and a blue beaver overcoat, of M'Pher son, which were delivered to me about six weeks afterwards—this is the bill I had with them ( produced )—with respect to paying the money, sometimes he would come to me when I was on the engine, and I would give him a sovereign—I have got the receipts—here is a letter which I received, demanding payment—I knew nothing of Mr. Varley—I thought I was dealing with M'Pherson.
   Cross-examined by MR. WOOLLETT . Q.  You have been an old customer of his? A.  I have had no other transactions but what are on these bills—I did not know him before—he was recommended to me by a customer—I did not pay him all the amounts at one time—sometimes he would come to me when I was not prepared.
