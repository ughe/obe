HORATIO THOMAS WATERWORTH. I am assistant to Mr. Bloxham-he
directed me to dispense some medicines for Mrs. Dore's child-I had a book
before me, in which he had written the prescription-I put two grains of
quicksilver, an ounce of castor-oil, and a saline mixture, composed of a scruple
of sesqui-carbonate of soda and about fifteen grains of citric acid-I wrote
the directions on them-these are the two bottles (produced)--the powder
is kept ready mixed in a bottle with a stopper, not put into papers.
Cross-examined. Q. I believe Mr. Bloxham does not keep arsenie in
powder in his shop? 4. No-we keep the liquor arsenicalis, but not in the
dispensary, it is locked up, for more abundant caution-we do not keep the
powder at all.
