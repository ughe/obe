some person locked the door.
John HESLOp. I am a licensed victualler, of Lisson-grove. I was one of

reference to the house, No. 60, Upper Norton-street, because I cannot reool-
lect it, it is so long ago I think I heard him say who was the oocupier or

COURT. Q. You had to try whose property it was A. Yes, but I

MR. PARRY. Q. Do you recollect anything about a bill of sale? A. Yes
-I think Sturt is the man whom Mr. Spokes lent the money to I am sure
I cannot tell you what he said-he signed the bill of sale for Mr. Spokes
I do not remember anything that he said, or the substance of it-I remem-
ber that there was an execution of Margetson's—the bill of sale was from

Spokes a bill of sale for the furniture,
COURT. . Did he say whom the furniture belonged to ! 4. He said

Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. What was the issue upon which
