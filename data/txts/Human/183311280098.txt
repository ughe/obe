 129.  CAROLINE MARRIOTT was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of October , 1 counterpane, value 5s.; 1 bolster, 4s.; 1 blanket, value 2s., and 1 towel, value 6d., the goods of Sophia Louisa Lewis.
   SOPHIA LOUISA LEWIS. I am a widow. The prisoner lodged in my house in King-street, for seven weeks; a young man came first and took the lodging for himself and his wife; the prisoner came the same evening - they lodged there for seven weeks; they were to pay me 7s. a week, but they only paid for one week - the young man had been to sea, and he said he was looking for a situation - the property stated is part of what was let to them with the room - I went into the room in consequence of what I heard - I said to the young man, "I understand I am to be robbed; let me see what is gone" - I then missed these articles; they told me they were gone, and the duplicates were in the room; I had never permitted them to pawn them.
 Cross-examined by MR. HEATON. Q. What did you say to the prisoner? A. I said, "I have been a mother to you, you will steal my bed next" - she said, "I should not stand nice about it" - I lent the prisoner my son's jacket to pawn, because she cried, and said she was in the family way and wanted to be married; I lent it her to get asked in church: I did not then know that she was married to another man, but I find she is - it was about a fortnight before they left me, that I lent her the jacket; she said she did not know how she should get married, and I said I would lend her my ring as she had not got one - I never lent her the things mentioned in this indictment, and she never told me she had pawned them till I went into the room - while I went for a policeman, I am told the man got out at the window, and the prisoner went out at the door - when I returned with the officer, I found the duplicates in the room; the prisoner came back and said, "I am the person who robbed you; I have come to give myself up."
   HENRY JOHN BRITTAIN. I am a pawnbroker. I have a bolster, but I don't know who pawned it.
   REGINALD CHARLES OTTER. I am shopman to a pawnbroker. I have a counterpane pawned by the prisoner; I knew her well.
   WILLIAM COOK(police-constable N 96). I was on duty on the 16th of October; the prosecutrix came to me and said she had been robbed, and she gave me these duplicates - I was about a week looking for the prisoner, but could not find her; she then came and gave herself up.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did she say she was ordered to pawn them by the young man? A. No.
 Prisoner's Defence. The young man ordered me to pawn these things; and when she missed them she said if I would get them back it would do. I pawned them in the name of Marriott, which is my name.
 GUILTY. Aged 17. - Recommended to Mercy by the Jury. Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 130.  PHILLIS SKINNER was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of October , 1 coal-scuttle, value 8s., the goods of James Decasne.
   JANE NASH. I live at No. 3, Steven-street. James Decasne has a house at No. 12, Homer-street, and I had the key of it to show the apartments to any person who called - on the 10th of October the prisoner called on me at ten o'clock in the morning, and asked me if there was a room to let there; I said there was. and I showed it her: she said she had been a servant, but had been married three weeks to a forman-sawyer; she referred me to her mistress, and wanted to come in that night - she came again about six o'clock in the evening; I went up to the room and missed a milk-pot and a basin from the mantle-piece; I thought she might have taken them off to put some images on - this coal-scuttle was let to her as a part of the furniture of the room; I gave it her to fetch some coals, and she went away with it; it was found at a broker's in the neighbourhood.
   MICHALE BROWN(police-constable I 22). I took the prisoner, and have the scuttle.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
