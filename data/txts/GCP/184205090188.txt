GEORGE LEWIS. I am owner of the Pathfinder. The prisoner made
an engagement with me for the voyage of seven persons to Port Philip, at
1251.-he paid me 271. deposit on the 14th or 15th of October—the vessel
left London about the 18th-731. was to be paid at Plymouth-I have
never received it, and know nothing of any such payment being made-
in the ordinary course I should have received it from the agent there.
Prisoner. Q. In making that agreement with you, I believe I made a
great many quibbles about the amount ? A. A great many, as to the
amount being larger than you liked to agree for-you agreed to take seven
persons in a space large enough for six-I agreed to make a difference on
that account-I knew nothing of any party but you-I engaged with you
for that number of persons, and unless the money was forthcoming, the
captain's orders were to turn the persons out of the ship-I do not intend
to refund the 271, of course I did not know you before-257. was to be
paid at Port Philip-you tried to make the most economical bargain you
could.
