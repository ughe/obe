I accompanied Mrs. Swan to No. 28, Crown-street, St. Giles's—she pointed out the prisoner to me, and charged her with having taken her child—I told her she must go with me—she said she would not go, the child was her own, and she would rather die than lose her child, she would not give it up for any one—after waiting half an hour I sent Mrs. Swan for another constable, and we got the prisoner to the station—we then went back, and found this bed-gown, shirt, flannel roller, and cotton roller.
