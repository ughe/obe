MAGNAY, Mayor.
ELIZABETH KENT. I am the wife of Edmund Henry Kent-he is land.
lord of the Prince of Wales, at Kensington-Louisa Harding lived in my
service since last May-I lost these spoons-I had them in my possession
on the lst of Aug.-they were then in the kitchen, where they are not usually
kept-they were brought there to be used, but they were not used-)
supposed that I put them away with the remainder of the spoons in a drawet,
in a closet on the second floor-when I missed them I spoke to Louis
Harding about them, but Mary Harding was not present-I have seen Mary
Harding at my house in the passage and the lobby when she has come to see
her daughter, but I have not seen her up stairs-these are the spoons-this
plate and saucer, and other things, are toys of the children-they are all
my husband's.
WILLIAM GEoRGE STEWART. I am a pawnbroker, and live in High-street,
Notting-hill. On the 17th of Aug. Mary Harding came and pawned these
three salt-spoons in the name of Harding, New-street"- am sure she is
the person.
Mary Harding. You cannot say that I pawned them. Witness. They
were pawned by you-this is the duplicate I gave.
SAMUEL PUDDEFOOT (police-constable T 127.) On the 20th of Aug. I
went to Town Malling, in Kent, and apprehended Mary Harding in a hop field
-I told her the eharge-she denied all knowledge of it-I then took Louin
Harding, and she said on the way to the court, 44 O mother, if it had not
been for you this would not have happened "--Mary Harding said, 44 Never
mind, child, don't ery, you can't help it "-at the station Mary Harding said
she pawned them, and that the girl found the spoons opposite the Prince of
Wales, three months ago-I searched Mary Harding's lodging, and found
number of duplicates, and amongst them this which refers to the spoons-she
directed me to her lodging, and I found her husband there.
Mary Harding. You said you told me the charge, and you did not; and
you would not let me speak one word to my child, you well know.
Mary Harding's Defence. During the time the child brought in the spoons
I was at work the children had several little toys, and these spoons, at play,
and I asked where they got them they said perhaps their sister Louisa
brought them in. I then went to Town Malling, and when I was asked
about them, I said I knew they were in the house, but I knew no more of
them. I have been thirty years in the neighbourhood, and worked for the
first of families.
M. HARDING-GUILTY. Aged 45.-Confined Six Months.
