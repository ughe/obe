The COURT expressed a doubt upon Mr. RoBINsoN's opening, whether
the evidence would sustain the charge for stealing from the person, but having
consulted Mr. JUSTICE CROWDER and MR BARON CHANNELL held, that,
though the cash-boa was not taken from the prosecutor's person, yet, it being in
the room in which he was sitting, he being aware of it, it 10a8 virtually
under the protection of his person, and, as to the second count, if the Jury
thought that the prisoners did grievous bodily harm, to disable the prosecutor
from protecting his property, their final intent being to rob; that count would
be good.
