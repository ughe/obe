Q. Did you say a word about the captain till you had been discharged ?
A. I never said it then-I did not go before the Police-court-I have not

the evening Mr. Cutting came round and also asked me to go next morning
-he knew where to find me, because I had left my address on board the

Court to ABRAHAM THOMAS CUTTING. . Have you any of the slips of

MR. METCALFE,Q. You say that you burnt them a week before this
matter turned up? A. Yes, about a week before I saw Mr. Roberts I will
swear I did not burn them afterwards; the reason was that I found I was
not going back to my ship—when I took them I put the money in one
pocket, and the slips in the other, and afterwards I put them in the fire, as I was
no longer belonging to the ship they would have shown the exact amount.
