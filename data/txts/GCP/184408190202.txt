Cross-examined. Q. Concussion may be occasioned by other means than
repeated blows? A. It may-a fall would produce concussion, excitement
alone would not a person being drunk would be more likely to have the
blood become turgid - the blow on the forehead was a severe one--I should
say that could not cause the appearances about the brain, because the vessels
were equally full all over the brain-I attribute it to concussion generally,
and turgidity-one blow joined to the excessive excitement might have pro-
duced the concussion, and caused the death.
