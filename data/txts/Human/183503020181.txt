 871. JOHN BARTON was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of Feb ruary , 1 looking-glass and stand, value 10 s  ., the goods of, John Goldspring , and that he had been before convicted of felony. JOHN GOLDSPRING. I keep a shop in Blackfnars-road, opposite the Surrey Theatre. I had this looking-glass outside my shop window—I lost it on the 4th of February, about eight o'clock in the evening—I can swear it is mine—it was within ten feet of the other goods—the window is railed round. GEORGE RUTLAND. I was going by the prosecutor's shop about eight o'clock that evening—I saw the prisoner take this glass and stand—I fol lowed, and took him back to the shop. RICHARD ARNOLD. I was then in the police. I took the prisoner and this glass, when he was brought back. JOHN KINSEY. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former convic tion ( read )—I know he is the person. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Transported for Seven Years.
 872. THOMAS GURNEY was indicted for embezzlement. JAMES MILLER. I am a baker, and live at Putney. The prisoner was in my service—he had to deliver bread and flour, and to receive money, if the customers plaid him—he has paid me money which he had received of Mrs. Chilman, but I cannot tell how much—he did not pay me 2 s . 9 3/4 d ., as received of her on the 26th of January.
   Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN . Q.  Do you keep any book? A.  Yes—I settled with him every night, by my book, but it is not here—I only know that this has not been paid, by my book—he has been a good ser vant I will take him again. NOT GUILTY.
