This case therefore rules yours; from that case it most clearly appears that yours was not a case of larceny but a breach of trust; your case in every respect is like that case that was not a larceny but a breach of trust. I am directed to say by the judges that the conviction against you was right. Confined Six Months in the House of Correction, and fined 1 s.
