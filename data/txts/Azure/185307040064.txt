Cross-examined by MR. GIFFOBD. Q. This was between 9 and 10 0'clock
at night? A. Yes ; the conversation that took place was not above a minute
or two—I was shown the prisoner at the police station—a person brought

get more wages—it is about five minutes' walk from where the prisoner was
standing to the shop I went to; it is out of sight—if I had been disposed I
might have gone away.
