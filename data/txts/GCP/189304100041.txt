Before Mr. Common Serjeant,
424. WILLIAM LAWSON (31) GEORGE HENRY RICHARDS(40)
ANDREW EDWARD MANN (47), and JAMES WILSON (54), Unlaw-
fully obtaining goods from divers persons by false pretences, with intent
to defraud.
MR. BODKIN, with Mr. TRAVERS HUMPHREYS and MR. HEWITT, Pro-
secuted; Mr. PIGGOTT appeared for Richards; Mr. R. GiLL and Mr.
GREEN for Mann; and MR. WARDE for Wilson.

metal merchants, of 143, Cannon Street, and early last June Richards
called and said he was a builder and zinc worker, of 46, Stalybridge Road,
South Tottenham, and wanted some zinc and solder-I asked for
references-he gave me a man named Catlin, of Carfax Square, Clapham,
to whom I wrote and received this reply (From F. W. Catlin, stating
vhat he had known George Richards six years, and done business with him
up to £50, and always found him just in his payments.) I then advised
