I took three dozen and a half of short boots, and one dozen and a half of slippers to the prisoner's house, and knocked at the door—I asked a little boy if Mr. Whale lived there—he said, "Yes"—the prisoner opened the parlour door, and said, "Bring them in"—he opened them, and found there were four pairs short, and he found fault with four pain; he said to me, "Take these back, and bring four pairs of others"—I said I could not leave the goods without the money—he said," You can't take these back; take these four pairs back"—he insisted on my going back with them, and said, "Can you come back this evening?"—I said, "I don't think I can"—he said, "Come in the morning by eleven o'clock, I will wait at home for you"—I went to my master and told him—he gave me four pairs of others for what I brought back, and the other four which were short—I went back, and saw the prisoner; he said, "I did not think you would come back to night"—I said I did not think I could—I said I must have the money or the goods—he said, "I can't give you the Others back, I have taken the trouble to size them and brush them, to send in the morning"—he took those that I brought, and took the invoice from me—he said he could not give me the goods back—I said I could not leave them—he brought me out a chair to sit down—he said, "You can sit there as long as you like: I shan't turn you out"—he said he should not give me the goods back, nor the money, if I called at eleven in the morning be would pay me—I stopped an hour—I asked him for the invoice to take back with me—he said he should not give it me back, and I was obliged to leave the house without anything—I went to my master's, and told him—my father was there; he wanted to know why I stopped so long—I asked my father to go with me—my father, my master and I, all went together—my father knocked at the door—the prisoner's wife came, and said, "Who is there?"—my father said, "Me"—the prisoner came down, and opened the door—we all went in—the goods were lying on the banisters—my master said he wanted the goods or the money—the prisoner said he would not pay to-night, he would call in the morning and pay.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did not I say you might take the goods back that night if you liked? A.  No.
