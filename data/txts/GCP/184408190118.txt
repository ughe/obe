2006. WILLIAM CRIPPS was indicted for stealing 1 waistcoat, value
38.; 1 knife, 6d. ; the goods of Edward Goodlake ; also 1 waistcoat, 38.;
and 1 knife, 60.; the goods of Richard Baker.
EDWARD GOODLAKE. I work for Mr. Smith, at Staines. On the 29th of
June, between eight and nine o'clock I put my waistcoat, with my knife in
it, under the hedge-I missed them both these are them.
RICHARD BAKER. I was working with Goodlake-I put my knife and
waistcoat under the hedge-I missed them these are them.
JOSEPH SMITH. I was working there-I saw the prisoner, and suspecting
him I watched him, I afterwards went into two pawnbrokers' shops-IS
Goodlake's waistcoat there- I went to the policeman, and took the prisoner-
he was searched, and Goodlake's knife found on him.
CHARLES BROADHURST. These things were given to me to bring here.
Prisoner. I am very sorry, I was not aware of the consequences.
GUILTY. Aged 40.-Confined One Month.
2007. ELLEN HAYES was indicted for stealing 1 shawl, value 78.; the
goods of Francis Cotton.
WILLIAM GROOM. I am assistant to Francis Cotton. On the 6th of July,
I missed a shawl-I had seen the prisoner at the shop a quarter of an hour
before.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Where was the shawl? A. Inside the
shop-he had been in the babit of pawning at our shop.
John Rose. I am shopman to Mr. Attenborough-this shawl was pawbed
by the prisoner on the 6th of July.
Cross-examined. Q. How far is your shop from Mr. Cotton's? A. About
ten minutes' walk.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 38.--Recommended to mercy.-Confined One Month.
