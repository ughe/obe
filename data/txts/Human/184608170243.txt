I went to the police-court, and came back to my son's-Mrs. Hart came near my son's house—she did not touch me, only used insulting words-my son went for the policemen—I had a stick in my hand I did not strike her with it—I was not struck by anybody; I was merely touched slightly on the cheek by Fiuo, but nothing that I wish to name, nothing to do any injury. GEORGE KIDNEY( police-constable L  179.) I came up with Robert Lar kin, jun.—I saw Finn and John Hart come up, and both of them pitched into  Robert Larkin, jun., as I was standing by his side—Finn struck him first—I laid hold of Finn, and he caught hold of Robert Larkin, jun., and threw him down—I pulled him off him—Elizabeth Hart came up, and scratched nay face and bit my arm while I had hold of Finn. WILLIAM HENRY GILBERT PARSONS. I was present—Finn first struck me—he knocked me down, and fell on the top of me, dragging the policeman—the policeman did not fall down—John Hart and his wife both attacked me—I saw Mary Truebody calling on the others to assist in rescuing her brother-in-law—after that I had the policeman's staff to protect myself—I was kicked down by Finn again, and then John Hart came on me, and hit wife too—John Hart put his hand on my left eye, and forced it in, and I fainted—I was acting at the request of Kidney—my eye bled.
   Finn. Q.  While I was in the constable's hand you struck me; did I not take the staff out of your hand, and give it to the policeman?. A.  I cannot say—it was at the time I was fainting.
