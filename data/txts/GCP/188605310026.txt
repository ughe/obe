WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. These coins ara bad-one of those picked

The Prisoners' Statements before the Magistrate. Murphy says: “I was
not aware it was any bad money; I was simply drinking with Swain."
Swain says: "This man has nothing to do with the money. I changed
the money. I did not know it was bad. We knew each other in the
Militia. The man did not know anything about the money more than I."
Murphy's defence was that he met Swain. sohom ho had known in the Militia

BARTHOLOMEW SWAIN (The Prisoner). I live at 4, Langley Court, Long

constable and gave him into custody-he was charged before the Magis-
