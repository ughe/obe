755. FREDERICK TIEDIMAN and CHARLES WHEELER were
again indicted for stealing 10 paving stones, value 58.; 10 other stones, 58.;
10 flags, 58.; 10 feet of stone, 38.; the goods of the Commissioners for carry:
ing into execution certain acts of Parliament.
WILLIAM Welch. I am a goldsmith and live in Baker's-row. The pave-
ment was being laid down near my shop, and my attention was attracted to it
-on the evening of Monday, the 22nd of Jan., after it was dark, I saw Tiedi-
man there-(he had been engaged there as a labourer in putting down the new
pavement)-I saw a truck there-Tiediman filled it with paving stones--I did
not see Wheeler at the time to know him-I saw the truck go away-I do not
know who took it.
ARTHUR KEEFE. I was engaged in repairing Baker's-row. On the 22nd
of Jan. I saw Wheeler and another young man bring a truck-Wheeler and
the other labourers put some of the stones into it--the other mason told me
to assist in taking the truck, and I went with it to the top of Baker's-row.
(Charles Waugh and George Andrews deposed as in the former case.) 24
WILLIAM FREDERICK EAST. I am surveyor to the Commissioners under
these Local acts--I spoke to Tiedimap on the 24th of Jan. I said the stones
did not appear to increase, and requested him to relay them immediately I
meant that they appeared to decrease-Tiediman felt inclined to be impudent,
and asked if I supposed he had taken them.

Not GUILTY..
