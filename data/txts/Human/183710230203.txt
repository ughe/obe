 2408. THOMAS LANE was indicted for a misdemeanor. THOMAS FLETCHER. I live at Stratford, in the parish of West Ham, and am an ironmonger. I did not know Mr. Young personally, but the pri soner had been several times for goods in the name of Young—on the 11th of April he said be came from Mr. Young for two coppers, the largest copper tea-kettle we had, and an iron boiler—he brought a string for the size of the coppers—we had none of the size, but he took an iron boiler—I gave him the boiler, and said I would go to Mr. Young, and speak about the coppers, intending to do so, but was prevented by some customers coming in—I believed the story he told me—I gave it him, conceiving that Mr. Young would be my debtor for it—I received informa tion that he was selling the boiler, and went and took him.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did not I pay 1 s . on the article? A.  No. MATTHEW YOUNG. I have seen the prisoner—I never sent him to Mr. Fletcher, nor did I authorize any one to get these things.
   Prisoner.  I offered to pay for the things, if he would look over it
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY  . Aged 31 ,—Recommended to mercy. — Confined Three Months.

 2409. GEORGE SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of October , 1 handkerchief, value 6 d  ., the goods of James Smith , from his person. JAMES SMITH. I live in George-place, Greenwich. On the afternoon of the 19th of October I was in Charlton-fair, between two and three o'clock, looking at a show—an officer came up and spoke to me—I put my hand to my coat, and missed my pocket-handkerchief—I told him so, and the prisoner was seized instantly and searched—a crowd gathered round, and a man produced the handkerchief to us—this is it—( looking at it. ) JAMES ADAMS. I am Superintendent of the Lewisham and Lee police. I was at Charlton-fair on the 19th of October, and saw the prisoner stand ing close behind Mr. Smith—I saw him put his left hand into Mr. Smith's right-hand coat-pocket, and take a handkerchief out—then put it into his right hand, and attempt to shuffle it up his coat—I took hold him immediately—I tapped Mr. Smith on the shoulder, and asked if he had lost any thing—and while I was searching them prisoner the handkerchief was produced—I am positive the prisoner is the person who took it—Mr. Smith moved a little forward while he was taking it, and I saw the handkerchief—it was the same colour as the one produced.
   Prisoners Defense.  I was looking at one of the shows, and the policeman collared me—I had no knowledge of the handkerchief—he searched me, but found nothing on me—and when we moved about a hundred yards, a Person brought the handkerchief up, and asked if he had lost any thing.
