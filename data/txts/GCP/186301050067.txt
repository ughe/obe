BLACKBURN; Mr. Ald. CHALLIS; Mr. Ald. FINNIS; Mr. Ald. HALE;

Before Mr. Justice Blackburn.
234. GEORGE BUNCHER (40), WILLIAM BURNETT (31%,
RICHARD BREWER (34), and JAMES GRIFFITHS (10) were in
dicted for feloniously forging and uttering a 51. note, purporting to be a
note of the Governor and Company of the Bank of England, with intent to
defraud. BURNETT-PLEADED GUILTY generally,
GRIFFITHS PLEADED GUILTY to the forgery, but NOT GUILTY to the
ettering. BURNETT and GRIFFITHS had previously PLEADED GUILTY to
other indictments charging like offences in other forms.
Sir FitzRoY KELLY, Q.C., MR. BOVILL, Q.C., MR. GIFFARD, and MR.
MATTHEWS, conducted the Prosecution. MR. SERJEANT BALLANTINE, with
MESSR. D. D. KEANE and F. H. Lewis, appeared for Brewer, and MIL PATER
for Buncher.
