 2. WILLIAM HOWELL , stealing 1 inkstand, value 1 s  .; the goods of the Trustees of the British Museum : having been before convicted.
   MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution. JOSEPH SHELVER. I live in Chapel-place, Brompton, and am in the employ of the British Museum.
