1951. HARRIET SMITH was indicted for feloniously receiving, on
the 13th of June, 1 weighing machine, value 11., the goods of Henry
Sheldon.
HENRY SHELDON. I live in Aylesbury-street, and am a baker. On the
13th of June I went down stairs about half-past seven o'clock, and whed
I came up again I missed the weighing machine off the counter-this is it
- I found it again the next week.
HENRY LANGDON. The prisoner brought this machine to my shop,
saying that an acquaintance of hers had it to sell—I bought it for 70. 6d.,
and put it out at the door-the next week the officers came.
WILLIAM BRADLEY (police-constable G 165.) I took the prisoner, and
asked her if she remembered selling a weighing machine-she said she did
not, but in going along she recollected that she did sell it.
NOT GUILTY.
