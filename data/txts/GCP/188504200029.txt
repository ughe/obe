JAMES GRIERSON. I am the general manager of the Great Western
Railway, and live at 4, Holland Villas Road-- Alice Grierson is my
daughter-ray attention was first called to the prisoner about October
1877, when my daughter made a complaint to her mother, and she
repeated it to me in her presence-the first I saw of the prisoner was at
the seaside at the end of July or beginning of August; I didn't know of
his existence before then-after that I saw him frequently about the
house–I did not arrive at Dinard until after the attack upon my daugh-
ter–I did not open any of the letters that have been produced here
