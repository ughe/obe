Cross-examined. Q. Is there any one lodging at your house ? A. Yes ;
a foreign gentleman, a professor of music, he has been lodging there about

Mr. COOPER. Q. I suppose if any one came and said "I want one of

WILLIAM SAVAGE. I am a clerk, in the bouse of Messrs. Grindlay and

with us—if this check had been presented it would not have been paid.

1. Assistant book-keeper and deputy cashier—there is a cashier above me;

Court. Q. Is there another house of Messrs. Grindlay and Co. ?
4. No-we are East India agents we carry on business at No. 124,

WILLIAM GODFREY ( policeman, C5). On 19th Feb. I took the prisoner

papers from Mr. Imrie.
