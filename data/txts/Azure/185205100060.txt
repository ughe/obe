April, I was going home, just about 9 0'clock, and when I got to Mr. Heiron's
I heard a female's voice cry something which I did not distinguish, and I saw
a man come out of Mr. Heiron's—l was about five yards off—the man came
out rather quickly, not running very fast, and ran across the road towards

JOHN ENWRIGHT (City-policeman, 647). I examined the prosecutor's pre-

and several pairs of trowsers thrown about just at the doorway—I afterwards
took these keys which I received from the inspector. and tried them on the
street door, neither of them would open it, but one would lock it, and one
of the smaller skeleton keys opened the shop door.
