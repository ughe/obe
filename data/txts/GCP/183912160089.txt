GEORGE Cheney, I am a beadle of Cheap ward, City. I know the
prisoner by the name of Thomas Jackson. On the 1st of March, 1837, he
was in my custody, charged with a burglary in the house of Heath and Co.,
No. 31, Old Jury he was remanded twice by the Lord Mayor, and at last
committed to Newgate for trial-I was present at his trial in this Court,
before Mr. Justice Littledale-he was convicted, and transported for life-
I have a certificate of his conviction, which I obtained from Mr. Clark,
whom I saw sign it-(read)--I have not a doubt of his being the man I
know him by his walk-when he was remanded I had to take him back-
wards and forwards three or four times, and he had a limp in his walk,
having had an accident, and I know his features--I received him in custody
from Meagle. hodi n
