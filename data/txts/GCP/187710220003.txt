751. ROBERT JONES (26), was indicted for unlawfully uttering
counterfeit coin.
MR. LLOYD and MR, AUSTIN METCALFE conducted the Proseoution.
HENRY SANSAM(Policeman 8 289). On the afternoon of 7th September
I was at the Bull and Butcher public-house, Whetstone, in plain clothes,
except a pair of blue trousers-Jones came in, and called for a glass of
ale, and tendered a florin--the barmaid brought it back bent-and I
went after the prisoner, and saw him one hundred yards from the house
talking to the prisoner Beattie-(see next case) on seeing me he went off ;
I followed, and said "Have you had a glass of ale ?"-he said that he went
in-I said "The florin is bad; you had better come back and see about it"
--Beattie then stopped-I said “You had better come back as well "--he
said “Me"-I said “Yes, you"--going the police-station Jones said
"Let my mate go, he has nothing to do with it”--the prisoner was
taken to the station and charged-Mr. Goodwin, the landlord of the
public-house, gave me a bag oontaining eight counterfeit florins besides
the one that was passed I searched Jones, and found on him two good
half-crowns, an albert, a locket, 1s. 10d., and three pawn tickets-I found
8 half-sovereign on Beattie.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. The coin was put into the till in my
presence-I waited to see the coin tried, and then followed you.
