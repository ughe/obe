Cross-examined. I did not examine the prisoner-my opinion before
the post-mortem was that the wound was caused by a blunt instrument,
but now my opinion is that it was a sharp instrument, because the cuts
in the cartilages were clean—the laceration might have been done by
the hilt of the knife, supposing it to have been well driven home-the
laceration was in the skin wound-the track of the wound was three
inches long, obliquely downwards, and to the left-I have had much
experience in wounds-I do not think it possible that the wound could
have been caused by a blunt instrument.
