JOHN SALMON. I am a furniture dealer of 115, High Holborn- I have
seen the prisoners together-Alcock brought me a gun in October-I
advanced 171. 108. on it-I received from him this paper with it. (Agres-
ing to realise if not redeemed within ton days.) I pawned the gun with
Best and Boyce.
Cross-examined by MR. HODsox. I gave Alcock the money in sove-
reigns, half-sovereigns, and silver during the day-I made no entry in
my books about it-I may have given him a cheque for about 41. 108.-I
understood that Alcock was Lord Hinton's business agent-I have known
them three or four years, and Wright fifteen-I saw no monogram on
the gun-I looked for that especially-I saw Horsley's name, no
initials-the gun was not redeemed-it was stopped when I went for it
two days afterwards--I have supplied furniture to the amount of about
501. to Lord Hinton through Alcock-I do not expect to be paid till Lord
Hinton comes into possession of his estates-1 supplied him on those
conditions- I looked at Burke's and another Peerage and found his
history.
Cross-examined by MR. JARVIS. Wright was agent for & Bill for &
Railway from Malton to Halsey Bay, in Suffolk, in 1882, and other Bills
- he was in the habit of taking shootings.
Re-examined. I did not know Lord Hinton had been a clown in a circus
-I sent the furniture to Lewisbam.
