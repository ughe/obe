335. LOUISA WEST and ELIZA CRANSTONE were again indicted
for stealing and receiving 1 pair of trowsers, value 98.; the goods of Thomas
Walters.
MR. RYLAND conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS WALTERS. I keep a clothes shop, at Richmond. On 31st Jan.,
I missed a pair of trowsers from my premises, between 5 and 6 o'clock in
the evening--they were in the passage, inside the front door-I saw them
safe about 5 o'clock, and missed them about 6 o'clock-I have seen them
since these are them.
