2094. HENRY FRIEND, JAMES RILEY, and JOHN BAVER.
STOCK were indicted for stealing, on the 4th of July, 1 fish-knife, value
12s., the goods of Charles James Morris, and another.
JOSEPH TANSLEY. I am in the service of Charles James Morris and
another, ironmongers, at the corner of Parliament-street-on the 4th of
July I was in the shop serving a boy named Hogan-Friend and Baver-
stock came in-they were not in the shop two minutes, and when they
left I missed a fish-knife-I ran out, and they had then overtaken Riley,
who was about two hundred yards from the house-I overtook them-
Riley started off, and ran-I left Friend and Baverstock in charge of
Hooker-I pursued and caught Riley-this fish-knife was found in Friend's
pocket when he was brought back.
GEORGE HOOKER. I was at my door-Mr. Tansley came up-he took
Friend and Baverstock--I took them from him-Riley went away-Mr.
Tansley went and brought him back.
John Drake (police-constable 101 A.) I took the prisoners, and have
the knife.
FRIEND-GUILTY. Aged 12.
RILEY-GUILTY. Aged 12. Confined Three Months.
BAVERSTOCK-GUILTY. Aged 13.
2095. MARY WALLER was indicted for stealing, on the 6th July,
2 gowns, value 15s. ; 1 brooch, value 10s. ; 1 veil, value 48.; I cape,
value 5s. ; 1 handkerchief, value 1s. ; 2 caps, value ls. ; 1 waistband,
value 1s. ; 1 night-gown, value 28.; and 1 towel, value 1s. ; the goods of
Elizabeth Ann Davis.
ELIZABETH ANN Davis. I am single, and live in Bateman-buildings,
Soho. The prisoner lodged and slept with me for one week-on Saturday
evening, the 6th of July, she went away between nine and ten o'clock, and
said she was going to spend the following day with her aunt-when I went
up stairs to bed I missed the articles stated-I expected her to return on
the Monday, but she did not come till she was brought to me by one of
the lodgers- I asked her how she came to take the things from me-she
said she had not taken any, but afterwards said she had taken them, and
sold one of the dresses in James-street, Covent-garden, and the rest were
in her box-we found the dress in James-street, but it is all picked to
pieces.
BENJAMIN RICHARD MURRAY. I keep a shop in James-street. I pur-
chased a cape and this gown of the prisoner on Saturday night, the 6th of
July, about ten o'clock-the cape I sold again---the gown I picked to
pieces, as it was very old-I gave it to the policeman.
JESSE PICTON (police-constable T 98.) I took the prisoner to the sta-
tion-house-she said she had sold one gown and one cape in James-street
-I went to where she had taken her box, and found the other articles
in it.
GUILTY.-Confined Three Months.
