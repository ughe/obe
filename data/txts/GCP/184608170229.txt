1744. HENRY WELCH was indicted for stealing 1 waistcoat, value 2s.,
the goods of William Henry Hancock --2nd COUNT, stating it to be the
goods of Charles Hancock.
WY
2
WILLIAM HENRY HANCOCK. I am twelve years old-I am at school at.
Mr. Ward's, at Clapham-I was playing at cricket on Clapham-common on
Thursday evening, the 30th of July-I put my waistcoat amongst some other
clothes on the grass, and while I was playing it was gone this is it-my fa-
ther's name is Charles Hancock-he pays for my clothes I did not see the
prisoner there.
GEORGE WALLIS (police-constable V 234.) I took the prisoner into custody.
in Clapham-road the same evening I said I wanted him for a waistcoat he
had taken to the marine store dealer's to sell—he said he took it off the cricket-
ground on Clapham-common-I went and got the waistcoat-this is it.
SI Guilty. Aged 10.-Confined Ten Days and Whipped.
1745. MICHAEL HYDE was indicted for stealing 1 sovereign, 2 half-
sovereigns, 7 shillings, and 1 51. Bank note; the monies of Charles Beer
Simmonds, from his person. 19
CHARLES BEER SIMMONDS. I am a tanner--and I live in Long-lane, Ber-
mondsey—I have known the prisoner four or five years-on Friday morning,
the 24th of July, I met bim in Long-lane, between twelve and one o'clock-
I was a little the worse for liquor, but knew perfectly what I was about-it
was between twelve and one that I met the prisoner-be wished me to give
him a drop of something to drink I was perfectly agreeable, and we went
together to the Kentish Drover's public-house, in the Kent-road--we went to
no other house--we went in there about four o'clock in the morning-I had
been walking about with him from between twelve and one till about four
o'clock-We had had nothing to drink---We tried at some public-bouses, and
they were shut-he took me a considerable distance, and I would not go any
further--we came back to the Kentish Drovers and ordered some coffee I

myself--the prisoner held the saucer sometimes-he wished me to drink more,
