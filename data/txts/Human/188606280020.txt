OLD COURT .— Wednesday, June  30 th , 1886.
   Before Mr. Justice Day.
 716. HENRY KEAT  (16) PLEADED GUILTY to carnally knowing Florence Alice Berg , a girl under the age of 13.— Eighteen Months' Hard Labour.
 717. JOHN PIKE  (30), JOHN RICHARDS  (22), and JAMES WAKELAND  (25), were indicted for a rape on Emily Orr .
   MR. POLAND Prosecuted. GUILTY.— PIKE  — Fifteen Years' Penal Servitude. RICHARDS and WAKELAND  — Ten Years' Penal Servitude each.
 718. CHARLES BRAND  (34) , Feloniously carnally knowing Alice Miller , a girl under the age of 13.
   MR. RIBTON Prosecuted ; MER. BURNIE Defended. GUILTY  . of the attempt. — Two Years' Hard Labour.
 719. HENRY ARTHUR STEEL  (29) , Feloniously carnally knowing Emily Jacks , a girl under the age of 13.
   MR. ISAACSON Prosecuted. GUILTY.— Ten Years' Penal Servitude.
 720. JOHN ORMSBY  (23) for the wilful murder of John Ormsby the elder .
   MESSRS. POLAND'S and CHARLES MATHEWS Prosecuted ; MR. RAVEN Defended.
   After MR. POLAND'S opening , MR. JUSTICE DAY , having read the depositions, considered that the death was the result of accident, upon which MR. POLAND offered no evidence. NOT GUILTY.
 721. MARY MULCOCK  (30) was indicted for the manslaughter of Mary Elizabeth Mulcock .
   MESSRS. POLAND and CHARLES MATHEW Prosecuted. SARAH JANE EVANS. I am midwife at St. Luke's Workhouse, Chelsea—on 21st May the prisoner was confined there in my ward of a female child—she neglected feeding it properly, I called her attention to it two or three times, she simply smiled—I had also complaints made to me by other people as to her treatment of the child—she left the workhouse on 11th June, taking the child with her—it was dressed in the same clothes as it had on when found—I saw it dead on 15th June, at St. George's Hospital—I recognized it by a mark just below the navel, and also by the clothes; it was the prisoner's child—the clothes had been sent to me by a patient, and I had them in my room for some days. RUTH PEARCE.
