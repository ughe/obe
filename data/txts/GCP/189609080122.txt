WILLIAM MARTIN (489 V). On September 2nd, in the early morning,
I was on duty in May Lane, Barnes, and saw two men-I turned my light
on them, and saw two policemen-they ran along the fence, and I saw the
prisoner run away from the horse, which was near the gate, with a halter
on his head.
CHARLES PATRICK I am coachman to Mr. Stanley Wood Keen; he
has a field in May Lane-I left a horse, worth £50, there on the evening
of September 1st, he had on the head of a halter, but no rope--this rope
is not my master's property ; I cannot account for how it got on the horse's
head-Pritchard called me up; I went into the meadow, and found the
horse, with the halter perfectly dry.
