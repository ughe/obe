Cross-examined by Toler. I was the first gentleman engaged when Mr.
Birks came he was set to do the new index, involving considerable time and
labour-it could not be done properly by three persons in two days--young
Birks would not work, and I reported him to you I told you I should not
retain him but for his uncle-you were very kind to Mr. Pugh, giving him
the use of your office for two or three months—Mr. Vosper was to have been
a partner of yours, but was taken out of your office to Holloway Prison after
being there five or six months and never paying you one penny-you were
very kind to him-he had a lot of money, no doubt, because I saw the
cheques drawn-I bought the ledger, account, and day book at Horton's in
Paternoster Row.
