1728. MARGARET EVANS was again indicted for stealing 1 1 half-crown, 1 sixpence, 3 pence, and 2 halfpence, the monies of James Bowler , her master. HELEN LEIGHTON BOWLER. I recollect the 11th of Feb.—I have it down in my book—( looking at it )—on that day I gave the prisoner 8 s . 4 d . to pay Mr. Bunce—I cannot recollect the exact coins—I cannot swear that I gave her a 5 s . piece—as far as my recollection goes, I gave her half-a-sovereign—she had to get something besides—she was to pay Mr. Bunce out of the half sovereign. NOT GUILTY.
 1729. MARGARET EVANS was again  indicted for stealing 8 half-crowns, 1 shilling, and 1 sixpence, the monies of James Bowler , her master. HELEN LEIGHTON BOWLER. I have here a memorandum of the 5th of Feb., of the sum of 9 s ., for the same account, to the same person—on that occasion I gave the prisoner two half-crowns and some shillings—she was to pay Mr. Bunce with the 9 s . I gave her.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  Tell me what sum you gate her? A.  Two half crowns, and four shillings, at least two shillings, and some sixpences—I took particular notice of the money I gave her that day (because I doubted if the bill was a true one on account of the sum—I expected the milk bill would have been more—she went out to pay it, came back, and brought me the receipt—I can swear I did not give her money to pay that and other things together—I did not give her a sovereign—I know it wa a half-crown, because I took it out of my purse, and gave it her. WILLIAM BUNCE. I am a cow-keeper, at Brixton, and supply Mrs. Bowler with milk. On the 5th of Feb. I had an account with her—it was 9 s . up to the 1st of Feb.—I never received that money from the prisoner, and new gave her a receipt for it.
   Cross-examined. Q.  When did she last pay you any money? A.  She has never paid me any, from the last week but one in Dec. WILLIAM JESSE( police-constable P  138.)
