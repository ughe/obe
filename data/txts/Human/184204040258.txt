   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  I thought you were a velvet-weaver? A.  I consider I am—I am also a Greenwich pensioner, and support three orphan children, besides my wife and family—I had come up to London on business that day—I had had three half-pints of beer in the morning, and 4 d,  of rum and water in the afternoon—that was all I had drank until this occurred—I was not ten minutes in the public-house by London-bridge—I did not sit down there—I called for a pint of beer, as a shipmate asked me to have a glass of ale with him, but I gave it away to anybody that would drink it—I do not know the exact place where the women got into the cab  —I swear I was sober at the time, and collected in every respect.
   Q.  When the women behaved so indecently, why not call out to the cabman to stop? A.  I knew it was no use, for they might have got out and taken away the money altogether—I thought it better to let them be till I caught sight of somebody else—I did not know for certain that I had been robbed till I got out of the cab  at the Rose public-house, New-cross—I went into the Rose, but was not there a minute scarcely—if the women went in I never asked them, and knew nothing about it.
   Q.  Did not you all four go into the Rose public-house after getting out of the cab? A.  Like enough—they were no company of mine—I do not know whether they were inside or outside the door—I was not positive I had lost my money till I got into the house—I did not give any money to either of the women in the cab  —I am certain of that—I had never seen them before they got into the cab  —I might have passed them on the road and not have known them—I never had anything to say or do with them—they might have been in the same public-house where I was, but I never saw them to my knowledge—I have been married thirty-two years, and was married at Hackney church—my wife was in the Court at Greenwich when the examination was going on—I did not say then that I did not like to give any particulars about what passed with the women in the cab,  because my wife was there—I said nothing like it—I told the Magistrate that they behaved very indecently—I did not mind my wife hearing it.
