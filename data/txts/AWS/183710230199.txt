ROBERT REEves (police-sergeant G 19.) On Manday, the 18th of
September, I was attending these sessions, and Mr. Battle, an ale-brewer,
came to me, and said in Davis's hearing, Do you know a man named
Vincent "-I said, 64 No "-he said, 41 I want to give him in charge for
felony, for obtaining some ale, and afterwards selling it 14. a barrel less
than he gave for it -Davis then same up and said, 44 Do you know that
party "-I said, 44 I cannot say I do know him "-he said, If you should
see him, take him into custody, he is one of the witnesses against me he
is one of the parties who has been riding about with your beautiful in+
spector, Mr. M'Craw"_ -I mentioned this before the MagistrateVincent
was taken before the Magistrate-Battle did not come forward, and Vincent
was discharged.
