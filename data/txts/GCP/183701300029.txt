504. JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of January,
5 handkerchiefs, value 18. 9d., the goods of George Blaylock; 2nd
COUNT stating it to be 4 yards of silk.
ENNA MARIA BEARE. I am in the employ of George Blaylock, a
linen-draper, in Fore-street. About six o'clock on the evening of the 5th
of January, the prisoner came to the shop, and asked me to match him a
since of gauze ribbon-he had a pattern in his hand- I told him I had not
pot it-he then asked me to match a piece of thread edging, and as I
tumed to get the box, I saw him slip the casement of the window back,
and take five silk handkerchiefs in one piece out, and put them in his coat-
Bucket-I sent a person up to Mr. Blaylock, and went round the counter
and held the shop door-a gentleman in the shop took the hankerchiefs
out of the prisoner's pocket, and laid them on the counter-a policeman
was sent for, who took him and the handkerchiefs.
MARY JOYNER. I am in the employ of Mr. Blaylock, and was in the
shop-I saw the gentleman take the handkerchiefs out of the prisoner's
pocket--they were given to the policeman.
GEORGE BLAYLOCK. I was called down the handkerchiefs were lying
on the counter-I went for a policeman-these handkerchiefs have my
mark on them.

505. WILLIAM LIGHTWOOD and JAMES HUGHES were in-
dicted for stealing, on the 3d of January, 1 purse, value 6d.; 7 sovereigns,
1 half-sovereign, and 5 half-crowns; the goods and monies of Thomas
Reid, from his person.
Thomas Reid. I live in Uxbridge-street, Notting-hill. On Tuesday
evening, the 3rd of January, I left London, and was going home I was
stopped after being at the Churchill Arms, Kensington Gravel-pits I was
going in there, and suppose these two men were at the door-I do not
recollect that, as I was so drunk-I found myself in custody next morning,
and all my money was gone. I recollect having it in my pocket when I
left London, between nine and ten o'clock I cannot tell when I became
insensible-I was not drunk when I started it was as much the effect of
being in company where they were smoking, as drink-I do not recollect
much after leaving Piccadilly.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. How much money had you
received ? A. 91. 16s., and I had 1s. 10d. in my pocket besides-I found
myself at Kensington in the morning.
JAMES AUGUSTUS REID. On the night of the 3rd of January, it being
late, my mother sent me out to look for my father-I saw him in Silver
street, in the Gravel-pits, near the Duke of York public-house-Lightwood
Muud hold of his right arm, and asked me what I wanted - I told him it
was my father-Lightwood let him go, and dropped something-he stooped
down to pick it up, and went across to Peel-street, near where he and
Hughes were working-Hughes was present, and he stopped, and used
treatening language-he threatened to knock my father- over head
- do not know what for-he got hold of my father's arm, and said be-
fore he would let go, my father should break his arm-he did not say why
be held his arm-- I called, "Police," and I think three came up.
