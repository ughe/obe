WILLIAM NAsH. On the 27th of July, I saw an omnibus, and the
horses had blue ribbons-I cannot tell the number of the omnibus, nor
who drove it-I did not see either of the defendants there, to the best of
my knowledge-I know Mr. Cowderoy-I do not recollect that I have
drank with him-I saw him at the Feathers that day, I believe-I do not
recollect seeing him on the box of the omnibus when the horses had blue
ribbons-] cannot recollect who I saw-I am in the habit of standing at
my gate, and seeing them go past.
