1329. CHARLES SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of April,
36 pence, 186 halfpence, and 11 farthings, the monies of Fergus Ferguson
M'Gregor.
JOHN WILLIAMS. I live in Brownlow-street, and am an undertaker-about
half-past eight o'clock in the morning of the 1st of April I went into Mr.
M'Gregor's shop, in Broad-street, St. Giles's, to ask for a loaf- the prisoner
caught my eye as I turned round-I had asked for the loaf before I got tho-
roughly in the prisoner said, “They will be here directly"-he appeared to
me to be patting the drawer in with his hand-he then came round the
counter, and called down the passage, " Jem, you are wanted”-he turned
round and went out-as soon as he got out I imagined he had been commit-
ting a felony-I followed and apprehended him-I asked what he had got in
his cap-he said something belonging to his father-I said he must come
back with me-he said he would not-Mr. M'Oregor came we took him
back, turned out his cap on the counter, and these coppers came out.
Prisoner. Q. Can you swear I shut the till ? A. 1 saw the action as if
you were shutting it-you were inside the counter, and had your cap down.
Ferous FERGUSON MGREGOR. I am a baker, and live in Broad-street,
St. Giles's-on the morning of the 1st of April I left my shop for a little
while-when I came back I noticed the prisoner coming out, bolding his cap
in his hand, with something heavy in it-I took him back and emptied his
cap on the counter, and there was the money stated in the indictment-
looked in my till immediately, and missed 16s. or 18s. in copper, which I had
seen about five minutes previously-he begged forgiveness.
Prisoner. Q. Can you swear to this money by any particular mark!
4. No; I had such money in my till.
Joun, Rose. I am a policeman. I was called in--I saw this copper on the
counter with the cap-the prisoner said it was not Mr. M'Gregor's.
Prisoner's Defence. It was mine-I am a costermonger, and was going to
market--I went in there for a penny loaf-nobody was there, and I chucked
a penny on the counter-it rolled off, and I went round to look for it--this
man came in- I went out-became and said, “What have you got " I
said, "Some halfpence"-I can assure you it is mine.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.
