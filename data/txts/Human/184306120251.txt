2036. PETER COOTE was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of June , 1 breast-pin, value 4 s  .; the goods of Elizabeth Catherine Watson , from her person. ELIZABETH CATHERINE WATSON. I live at the White Horse public-house at Charlton, near Woolwich. On the 12th of June I was at Deptford fair—the prisoner pressed by my left side, put his hand over my right shoulder, and took the pin out of my breast—I caught hold of his arm before he got his hand off my shoulder—the pin was in his hand—I am sure it was quite out of my clothes—he got his hand from me, and I caught hold of the skirt of his coat—my brother called a policeman—I saw the prisoner drop the pin—a little boy picked it up, and gave it to me—I gave it to the officer. GEORGE WATSON. I was with my sister—the prisoner pressed against her, and she said, "George, this man has got my pin"—I laid hold of his collar directly, and the policeman being close at hand came up directly—the prisoner dropped the pin by his leg, and a boy picked it up. CHARLES DAVIS. I was at the fair. I saw the prisoner put his hand round the prosecutrix's neck, and take the pin out.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Six Months .

 2039. ANDREW MAHONEY was indicted for breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Charles Wynn Davis , on the   15th of May , at Lambeth , with intent to steal, and stealing therein, 1 reel of cotton, value 1 d  .; 1 fork, 5 s  .; 1 parasol, 6 s  .; and 2 yards of cord, 2 d  .; the goods of Susan Coe . SUSAN COE. I am servant to Charles Wynn Davis, of Stamford-street, Lambeth. On the 15th of May I went to bed at eleven o'clock—I left the kitchen all secure—I am quite sure the window was down—I got up next morning at twenty minutes after seven, found the kitchen window open, and a square of glass broken—the plate drawer in the kitchen was partly open—nothing was taken out of it—there was six forks in it, but they were left—I missed the articles stated—this thimble and cotton are mine.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  The plate drawer was open, but nothing taken out? A.  No, there was a great deal of property there—these things are all trifling—the parasol belongs to a friend of mine—the things were all lying together the night before—I heard no noise in the night. THOMAS AKERS. I live in Thomas's-street, Stamford-street, about thirty yards from the prosecutor. About four o'clock on Tuesday morning I heard a noise, looked out of my window, and saw the prisoner running away—I ran down stairs, ran after him, and caught him—he had been drunk over night—it was evident he was under the influence of drink, but knew what he was doing—he spoke rationally enough at the police station.
