JOHN BRANN. I am a policeman of Maidstone. The prisoner, whom I
had seen once before, came to me on the 22nd of July, and said he wished to
give himself up for horse stealing, he and another had stolen a horse.
Cross-eramined. Q. Was he drunk? A. No, sober-he said, “I want
to give myself into your custody"-I said, "For what?"-- he said, "Horse
stealing"--I said, " From whom did you steal the horse ?"-he said, " From
Mr. Holmes, at Walworth" - he said there was a previous charge, and I should
find that on him-I took him to the station, and found a bill on him that he
was out on bail-- he did not mention the name of the other man I cau,
tioned him, and asked him what reason he had for giving himself up-he
said through the men that were bail for him.
