373. FREDERICK CLARKE was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd
of January, 1 pair of trowsers, value 58., the goods of John Ivimey.
MARY ANN IVIMEY. I am the wife of John Ivimey, we live at Shad-
well. On the 2nd of January these trowsers hung at the door, inside-I
missed them about five o'clock-they have the shop-ticket on them now.
JOHN MURRAY (police constable K 178.) I saw the prisoner, and two
other boys, in High-street, Shadwell—at twenty minutes past five o'clock,
looking in at the prosecutor's shop-window-the prisoner then took these
trowsers from a hook inside the door-he gave them a shake, and was
about to hand them to another lad, when Caroline Cook came by, be then
put them down under the window, and Cook took them up, I went and
took the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. There was a bill in a corn-chandler's window-I
thought it was a bill for an errand-boy- I went past, saw these trowsers
hanging at the door, and the legs were two inches outside-the officer told
the inspector that they were inside the window.
