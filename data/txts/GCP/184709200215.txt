2249. JOHN WRIGHT was indicted for stealing 120lbs, weight of
lead, value 11.; the goods of the South Eastern Railway Company
MR. BODKIn conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM Shaw. I am in the employ of the South Eastern Railway
Company. I attend to the goods department at the Bricklayer's Arms
station, on the night duty--the goods at this time of year chiefly come up at
night--we have two trains come in, one at twelve o'clock, and the Dover
train at half-past three o'clock in the morning--they bring market goods ;
Truit, fish, and meat-the prisoner was in the employ of Mrs. Stansbury,
Who carries goods from the station in her own carts--they come about two
o'clock every morning, and take fruit, fish, and meat--the cart the prisoner
was attached to was driven by Neale, the prisoner was guard to it--on the
morning of the 2nd of Sept., 1 particularly noticed two pieces of lead, which
had been brought up from Ashford, and were about to be put in the stores-
they were the property of the Company-I saw it safe about two o'clock that
morning-I afterwards saw the same lead at Aldermanbary--the prisoner and
Neale had been at the station before two o'clock that morning-their employ-
ment would give them opportunity to take that lead, if they were disposed
adoit--they had their fruit in what we term the dock-after I had been
