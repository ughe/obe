 1492.  LOUIS FRANCISCO was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of July , a watch, value 18 s., the goods of Thomas Farrar.
   EDGAR HISCOCK. I am an apprentice to Mr. Thomas Farrar salesman, High-street, Shadwell. On the 17th of July the prisoner came into our shop with a woman between five and six o'clock in the evening, he went behind the counter, where some watches were hanging. I was in the parlour, but could see what was done in the shop; he said he wanted a silver watch - I showed him one, and he did not like it. I showed him three others - he at length fixed upon one for 3 l.; he offered me a farthing for a sovereign, and then said he would go to his shipmates to give him money to purchase it. I did not miss the watch till about two hours after he was gone - it had been on the counter with some others for him to look at - they were then hung on a nail behind the counter as they were before. I have seen it since at the office, and am certain it was one of those I showed him - there was no person behind the counter - we had not one customer in the shop from the time the prisoner left till the watch was missed; I saw him about ten minutes after at a tobacco shop opposite us, but he did not come to our shop again.
 FREDERICK NICHOLSON. I am a pawnbroker - I have a watch which was pawned by the prisoner about five o'clock in the afternoon of the 17th of July, for 10 s.
   CHARLES TICKETT. I am a constable. I apprehended the prisoner about nine o'clock on Saturday evening, the 17th of September, in a pawnbroker's shop; I searched him, and then let him go. Mr. Farrar came up and said,
 "Have you got that man in charge." I then took him again, and on searching him I found a silk purse and a farthing, and a row of cornelian beads. He was a little in liquor, but not much; he knew perfectly well what he was about.
     GUILTY. Aged 25. Transported for Seven Years.
 1493.  JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of September , a jacket, value 14 s.; and a handkerchief, value 5 s., the goods of William Pickett.
   WILLIAM PICKETT. I am a carpenter, and live in Eastbourne-terrace. I had a jacket stolen from me the day before yesterday. I was at work under a gateway in Sydney-street, Stepney. I had gone out to an adjoining house, and on my return my jacket was gone. My handkerchief was in my hat, which was lying by the jacket. I received some information, and laid hold of the prisoner in an adjoining field; I charged him with stealing the jacket and handkerchief - he said he had done no such thing. I took him to the watchhouse. As soon as he saw me in the field, he dropped the articles, and went on. When I saw him first, he was rolling them up, and then ran away. I am quite sure he is the person. The field is about a quarter of a mile from the place where I was at work.
   SARAH EVERETT. I live at Stepney. I saw the prisoner come out from the gateway with the jacket under his arm. I went to Pickett, and asked if he had lost a jacket. He came and said it was gone, and then pursued him.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was coming from one gang of bricklayers, and was going to another - this man was running, and he took hold of me, saying, I was the man. I said,
 "What do you want; I will go with you whereever you wish."
     GUILTY. Aged 42. Confined Three Months.

 1494.  JAMES STEVENS was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of September , ten shillings, the money of Samuel Gould, his master.
   SAMUEL GOULD. I am a calico-printer. The prisoner has been in my employ from a child. In the course of the last three or four weeks my counting-house was entered by a key, and on Sunday night last I deposited 10 s. there, in a particular situation, having previously marked them; they were put into a desk, which was not locked.
   GEORGE GOULD. I am the son of the prosecutor. I sat up to watch the counting-house in an adjoining room, and about six o'clock on Monday morning I heard somebody walking to the door, which was opened with a key. I looked through the key-hole, and saw the prisoner go into the counting-house; he remained there five minutes, and when he came out I stopped him, and found 10 s. on him, which I gave to the constable the same morning. He had had a good character previous to this.
   WILLIAM STILWARD. I am an officer. Gould gave me ten shillings, which I have here. I took the prisoner into custody.
 GUILTY. Aged 19. Recommended to Mercy by the Jury. - Confined 1 Year.
