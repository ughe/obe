COTTON, Mayor.
THOMAS CAPON. I am the son of Mr. and Mrs. Capon and live with them
--I was let in at 11 o'clock on Saturday night, the 13th-I slammed the
door and went up to bed.
Prisoner. It was 2.45 in the afternoon when I entered the house, and
when I came back at 12.45 a.m. on the 14th the door was half-way open.
I shut the door and bolted it. When the door was examined the next
morning there was a bolt on it. It would be impossible for me to open the
door with a bolt without leaving any marks behind.
Mrs. Capon (re-called). I do not know whether either of the doors were
bolted in the morning-the policeman had not been in before or anybody-
I opened the front door to let the policeman in-that was not bolted and
these tools I found on the slab.
Guilty. Prisoner PLEADED GUILTY to a conviction for burglary at this
Court on 1st February, 1875, and another was proved against him-Seven
Years' Penal Servitude.
46. JAMES BROWN (16), Robbery with violence on John Pearson,
and stealing from him a tobacoo box, and 78 6d.
MR. DENNISON conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN PEARSON. I am a baker at Mr. Drew's, Shadwell, and live at 11,
Haswell Street, Bermondsey-on Satnrday night, 6th November, I was in
George Street, Shadwell, at about 1.30, when I was tripped up by the pri-
soner and other person not in custody-the prisoner put nis hand in my
pocket-I sung out “Police !" and a policeman captured him and brought
him back-I missed my money and my tobacco box-I am sure he is one of
the two persons.
HENRY SIMMONS (Policeman H 217). I saw the prisoner with another
man not in custody, loitering under the Dock Wall about ten
minutes afterwards I saw the prosecutor, and the prisoner and another
man took hold of him and threw him-the prosecutor shouted “Police," and
ran away-I followed, and when he got to the corner of Glasshouse Street he
threw something down-I caught him in Glasshouse Street-then he dropped
something else down-I asked him what it was-he said “Nothing"-I
looked down and found this tobacoo box (produced)-I then took him back
and looked and found a 28. piece, and prosecutor then came up and identi
fied him as one of those who had robbed him when the charge was read
over to him he said he and his mate had been to work and received their
money, 148. each-when he was searched a penny was found on him.
JOHN PEARSON (re-examined.) I identify the box -I cannot identify the
florin.
GUILTY-Nine Months' Imprisonment.
