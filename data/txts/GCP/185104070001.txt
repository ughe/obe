811. CHARLES YART, stealing 1 mare, value 101.; the property of
William Baldry.
MESSRS. ROBINSON and THOMPSON conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM BALDRY. I live at Twickenham-I know the prisoner. On
17th Feb. he came to me and said he knew a gentleman who wanted to
buy a cob---I sbowed him one, and let him have it to show to the gentle-
man-he brought it back, and said he had not seen the gentleman-I saw
him on 18th Feb.--I had a little pony--the prisoner asked me if I would
sell it-he said he thought the cob was too big for the gentleman-I told
him the pony was not for sale-nothing else passed respecting the pony
-I gave him no authority whatever to take it away-I saw him again on
the Thursday-he said, "If you would sell this pony I could make a
good bargain "--I said, "Yart, it is not for sale, don't you take it away"
-I was then absent till the Friday-I then ascertained that the pony was
gone I went to a house the prisoner was keeping for a gentleman, and
saw his wife-I could not get information where he was-I valued the
pony at 101.
