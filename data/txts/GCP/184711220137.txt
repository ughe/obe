ELISABETH SIMONS. I am the prisoner's sister-upon her being given
into custody on 19th Oct. I went to her, and she gave me directions to go
to Mr. Hickman' I knew Mr. Hickman before she did not tell me what
I was to go for went there between seven and eight in the evening
found him at home I told him my sister was in custody, that she had
boen robbing Mr. Marshall, and asked if he had anything there the immo
diately went into the bed-room to look into some drawers, and found some
things of hers there he took them from the drawers and tied them up in a
bandlo-he went into the bed-room by bimself I was not with him-he
brought the things into the sitting-room and tied them up there--he said he
would go to Mr. Marshall with them in the morning, and see what he could
do for my sister-that was all that passed, that I recolleet-he gave me a
sovereign to take to my sister-I came away in a cab, leaving the bundle
there there was only one bundle when I left-it was a large one, and con-
tained a great many things I do not remember whether be forced open the
dnswers from which he took the things, or whether they were open- I did
not hear any poise like the forcing of drawers-I could not see them.

grening-I have met them two or three times I met them in the neighbours

see the parcel opened-1 saw a letter a week after, and read it-I have
never seen Mr. Stinton write I saw the signature to the letter.
