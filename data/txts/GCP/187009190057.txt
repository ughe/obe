told came from Mrs. Hughes--I forget how I received it, or whether I re-
ceived it from the defendant, or his wife when I said I received it from
Pearse, I meant through him-I kept that money-I put my signature to
the document the day I received the money-I think I received 202. from
the defendant just prior to his bankruptcy in Lewes- I was not attorney
for the plaintiff, I believe Mr. Harrison was-I gave an undertaking to
sppear and consent to the judgment the day the writ was issued, and the

vent Court was in existence, in 1861, that was an every day, ocourrence--this
