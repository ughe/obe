902. GEORGE SMITH, (17), Burglary in the dwelling-house of
John Osment, and stealing a watch and chain and other articles, his
property.
MR. THORNE COLE Prosecuted.
JOHN OSMENT. I am a licensed victualler, of 338, South Lambeth
Road-on 8th May, about 10.45 p.m., my house was all locked up-I was
the last person up-my drawing-room is on the ground floor--the
shutters were not closed-next morning about 4.30 I heard someone run
downstairs- I went down and found the house in disorder, the back doors
open, and property packed up to be taken away value £60 or £70, and
among them this pair of boots which I had worn that day Sunday.
HENRY DENMAN (Detective W. I examined these premises with Inspector
Barrett; an entry had been made by the drawing-room window-they had
to climb up six feet and put the cateh back-on May 24th I received
this pair of boots from chief warder Hollingdale- I took the prisoner
outside Wandsworth Prison, and took him to Clapham station--when he
was charged he said, “Several of them have been on the game; I was
pinched, and I kept the boots."
FREDA LAWSON. I am a general servant-I was in Mr. Osment's
service-on May 9th I awoke about 3.30; I did not hear any noise-
I went to sleep a second time, and was awoke and saw a man in my
room-I cannot swear to the prisoner, but he is very much like him
-I gave an alarm.
HENRY BARRETT (Police Inspector W). I went with Denman on 9th
May to the prosecutor's premises; he handed me this pair of old
boots -I tried them on the prisoner, and they exactly fitted him-1
asked him if they were his-he said, “No”-I took the charge--he
said, “There were others in it as well as me.”
ALFRED H. HOLLINGDALE. I am chief warder of Wandsworth Goal-
I saw these boots taken off the prisoner's feet on 27th May. I handed
them to Sergeant Kay.
John OSMENT (Re-examined). I found this pair of old boots in the
garden on the morning of the burglary.
Prisoner. I was in Camberwell at the time the robbery was done. I
had some breakfast with two labourers, and one took a pair of boots off
his feet and asked me to pawn them, but they would not take them in at
the pawn-shop. Next day they took the boots away, and in the afternoon
they gave me some more things. I had the boots on when I was caught.
Nor GUILTY.
