811. JOHN JONES, WILLIAM WESTCOMB, and ABRAHAM
WAFFINGTON, were indicted for stealing, on the 5th of February, 60lbs.
weight of rope, value 38., the goods of the London Dock Company.
MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution. A
TIMOTHY CRAWLEY. I live in Angel-garden, Shadwell, and am a
labourer in the London Docks occasionally. On the 5th of February I saw
Waffington, who I had known five or six years, on the Eas!-quay- he asked
me to go and get some old junk, pointing to a shed close bym he said
it would fetch 88. a cwt., and he would make a pass himself to get it out-
I said I would not do such a thing for him, or any body else, as I had once
been in trouble for beating a policeman-he said I was not game--I then
left him, and stood behind a crane, and watched-in about two hours I saw
him come out of the shed, which he had pointed out to me he had a piece
of rope with him, which he put behind a pile of wood be then went back.
and got some more rope--the other two prisoners then came out, with each
of them some rope, and chucked it down in the same place behind the
wood-I saw them all talking together-I told the dock-officers of it-it
was between half past twelve and one o'clock.
