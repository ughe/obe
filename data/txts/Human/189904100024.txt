I am the wife of Frederick William Satchell, land-lord of the Apollo public-house, at the corner of East Street and Paddington Street, Marylebone—the prisoner and a man named Haynes were in the bar on Easter Monday afternoon—the deceased and his brother George White came in—they began to jangle , and I went round and told White and his brother to leave the house—they went into another bar—I told them to leave that—James was making the most noise—the two Whites went out—I said to the others that I was sur prised that Haynes, with a horse and cab, should want to quarrel—none of them appeared the worse for drink.
