JANE HOLLINGE. I am servant to Mr. Stephen Sabin, the prosecutor; I
know Thomas Sabin, and all the rest. On Tuesday night, 7th May, about
eleven o'elock, I saw Thomas Sabin and the elder Fetherston at my
master's gate, with a light, and heard one of them say they would be in the
house in the morning-the next morning I saw Reeves break the lock of the
gate-the two Fetherstons were waiting outside, and came in-Fetherston
then came to the front door, which was locked, and demanded admittance-
I was inside-he said unless the door was opened he would break it open in
less than ten minutes-they then went round to the side-door, and broke it
open with a crow-bar- cannot tell who broke it open-the two Fether-
stons, Reeves, Tubby, and Gould, then came in-an inner door, leading
from the wash-house into the kitchen, was also broken with violence-I had
fastened the doors the night before, and they had not been undone that
-Fetherston ordered the men to open the front-door and the
front-parlour shutters, which was done, and some of them went up-stairs
my mistress went into hysterics-m master was not at home.
