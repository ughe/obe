JAMES PRIESTLY. I am a pawnbroker. I have a cruetframe pawned in the name of Ann Conolly - the duplicate I gave for it has been produced.
   HENRY HAMPSTEAD. I am a pawnbroker. I have a gown, two bed-gowns, a shift, a shawl, and towel, which I believe to have been pawned by Ellen Sulivan, but I am not positive - they were pawned on Monday morning, the 28th of July - here are the duplicates I gave her.
 WILLIAM DREW re-examined. I took these duplicates from Ellen Sulivan, whom Thomas passed them to.
   CHARLES WALLER. I am an officer. I have four duplicates which I found in the pocket of Mary Ann Sulivan - they are for two curtains pawned for 4d., in the name of Ann Conolly, a cruet-frame in the name of Ann Conolly, a petticoat, bed-gown, and shift for 1s. 6d., in the name of Ann Conolly - the pawnbrokers have produced the property - I searched Sulivan's lodging - they both lodged together - I found the box which was lost partly burnt - the day after the prisoners were committed I had information, and went to the cellar, and found six wine-glasses and six shells.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Thomas's Defence. On Monday, the 28th of July, I got from my bed between six and seven o'clock - as I was going up Holborn I met a gentleman who asked me to take care of the property for him - he took me into a public-house for a glass of ale, and said he would not be long before he came back; and he accordingly came back, but during his absence, he sent a man to me to give him half a dozen of spoons, so I did - he then looked and took a quantity of things out and got them pawned, and then gave me the duplicates - after a short time he said the things belonged to his wife, who had taken away his best clothes, and a great deal of money, and as these things belonged to his wife, he should make away with them - he pawned every thing, and brought me the duplicates - I saw Ellen Sulivan about twelve o'clock, and asked her to have something to drink - she asked what property I had there - I told her a gentleman left them in my care, who said he would not be long before he came back, and he wanted them pawned - I asked her to stop into that public-house, while I went to look for the gentleman - I came back and did not see him - I gave her the duplicates in that cap to take care of for me - she cannot read, and she took them home to her sister Mary Ann, who took them - it was her sister's cap - she put it on her head - I did not pawn the spoons, and do not know who did - the man gave me the duplicates after pawning them.
 Ellen Sulivan's Defence. I met Thomas, as he has stated - he gave me the duplicates to mind - not being able to read, I did not know what they were - I put them on the mantel-piece - my sister Mary Ann took them up, and put the cap on her head.
 Mary Ann Sulivan's Defence. The policeman asked if I had a pocket on - I said, "Yes" - he then asked what I had in it - I said, "Duplicates;" and that some of them belonged to my sister, and some to myself.
 ELIZABETH GAHAGAN. I live in this house, and am the landlady - other persons lodge with me - It was alarmed by the sound of a foot coming up stairs, about a quarter past four o'clock - it appeared to stop in the passage - I had no reason to think they had gone into the room, not hearing a door open - I was alarmed - it was light then - I heard a man's foot going down stairs - I got the key of the prosecutor's room door, from where it hung, and saw a man going down with a bundle and a box - I ran after him - he much resembled the male prisoner - it was like him by his back appearance.
 HENRY BOSTON re-examined. The property was taken from the first floor front room - I do not know at what time it was opened - the value of the property lost is about 15l.
   JEMIMA BOSTON. I went to bed after my husband - I locked the room door, and the key hung in the landlady's room, which is on the same floor - we were awoke between four and five o'clock - it was light then.
 THOMAS - GUILTY of Housebreaking, not of Burglary.
 Aged 23. - Transported for Life.
 MARY ANN SULIVAN -    GUILTY. Aged 16.
 ELLEN SULIVAN    GUILTY. - Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.

 1402.  SAMUEL JOHN WILKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 27th June , 1 watch, value 13l.; the goods of Charles Millingen.
 MR. PHILLIPS conducted the Prosecution.
 CHARLES MILLINGEN. On the 27th of June, I was at the  Bank Coffee-house, in the City, with my father - the prisoner was in the coffee-house, talking to my father on business - he told my father his wife's aunt's executor had behaved very well to him, and he should like to make him a present of a gold watch; and asked my father if he had one by him - my father said he had not one by him - he asked what the price of one like mine would be - I had had it out several times - my father said, "About 13l., or 14l." - he said,"Would you allow me to show your son's watch to the executor? and, if he likes it, I will have one like it: if not, I will return it" - I gave him the watch, expecting him to return it in a day or two - he said he would return it in a day or two; that Mr. Alexander lived at Maidstone, and he should see him in a day or two - he has never returned it - I gave it to him to show to the executor, to see if he would have one made like it - it was worth 13l. or 14l. - it was my watch.
