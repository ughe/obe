JOHN WHITLAMB (police-conatable M 89.) I Was with West,
with this bundle of iron hoopsI followed him, till he laid them down at the
door of Mr. Gutteridge's cooperage--] then saw him go to the door of Mr.
Gutteridge's residence-the door was opened--he went in, and came out,
picked up the iron, and I followed him to the door and saw Mr. Gutteridge
standing with a light--I afterwards went there with West, and found a
buindle of iron in the back yard-to the best of my belief it was the same I had
seen before-I was at the station when the prisoners were there together-
heard Gutteridge say, he believed Robinson was the man that wore the smock-
frock that he had the iron of a week ago-Robinson had a smock-frock on.
