 976.  ANN McINTYRE was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of February , 1 pair of sheets, value 8s.; 1 blanket, value 3s.; 1 bolster, value 3s.; 1 pillow, value 2s.; 1 looking-glass, value 2s., and 1 candlestick, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Samuel Howard.
 SAMUEL HOWARD. I live in  Leg-alley, Long-acre. On the 3d of February, the prisoner took my second floor front room, at 5s. a week; she left me in ten days without notice, and I missed this property - I found her on the 25th; she said she had pawned my things and intended to redeem them, when she got the money - she told us where they were pawned; we went, and found they had been redeemed - I have not recovered them.
   WILLIAM OXLEY. I am a constable. On the 25th of February, I took the prisoner, and found on her three duplicates, not relating to this property; she told me where she had pawned the things, but they had been taken out.
   PHILIP EATON. I am a pawnbroker, and live in High Holborn. The prisoner pawned a sheet on the 5th of February, it was redeemed on the 15th.
   CHARLES WILLIAMS. I am a pawnbroker. The prisoner pawned a pillow on the 5th of February, and redeemed it by an affidavit, on the 20th.
 The prisoner put in a written defence, pleading distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. Transported for Seven Years.
 977.  GEORGE TURNER was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of April , 1 rule, value 1s., and 1 saw, value 3s., the goods of Edward Hill.
 EDWARD HILL. I am a  carpenter. On the 2d of April, I was at work in the North-road, Islington; I went to dinner at twelve o'clock, leaving my tools in the front parlour; the house was not finished - when I returned I missed these tools; the prisoner is a stranger.
   THOMAS KIRKWOOD. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Brick-lane, Old-street. I have a saw, pawned on the 2d of April, by the prisoner. I believe; but am not quite certain - this is the duplicate I gave for it.
   JAMES TAYLOR. I am a constable, and took the prisoner about four o'clock, on the 2d of April, and found this duplicate of the saw on him.(Saw produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I met a young man, who asked me to pawn the saw.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined Three Months.
 978.  JOHN TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of February , 1 round frock, value 5s.; 2 pairs of stockings, value 2s., and 1 shirt, value 2s., the goods of Elijah Ventham.
 ELIJAH VENTHAM. I live at  Hounslow. I lost these things on the 26th of February, out of my box, which was in the loft, at Mr. Woodwards, where I live; it was about seven o'clock - I was feeding a team of horses at the door, and heard a noise in the left; I went and looked up, and saw the prisoner coming down; I asked what he did there, he said he went there to lay down - I asked what he had got under his frock; he said, his shirt - I took it out, and it was this property of mine; he was a stranger - he struggled, but I called for help, and he was secured.
   WILLIAM KEEN. I live at Hounslow. Ventham called for assistance; I went up and took the prisoner, and saw these articles taken from him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I had come out of the country; a man stood by the horse-trough - I asked him where I could lay down, as I was in distress; he took me up to the loft, gave me the things out of the box, and said if I took them to Hounslow, they would tell me where to find him - and he would give me 1s.
     GUILTY. Aged 29. Transported for Seven Years.
 979.  MARY WELCH was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of March , 2 dishes, value 1s.; 1 plate, value 1d.; 3 knives, value 6d., and 3 forks, value 5d., the goods of William Bacon.
 WILLIAM BACON. I live in  Portpool-lane. On Sunday evening, the 23d of March, between seven and eight o'clock, I had been serving in my shop; I went out of the shop door to the private door, and met the prisoner coming out of the passage; I took hold of her, and asked what she had got; she dropped a plate which broke, and said, "It is from real hunger" - I took her back, and found these other articles in her pocket; they had been taken from my washhouse - she threatened to destroy herself.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 59. Confined Two Years.
 970.  GEORGE RANDALL and GEORGE TERRY were indicted for stealing, on the 14th of March , 28 bottles of port wine, value 3l., and 2 gallons of gin, value 1l., the goods of George Mills; and WILLIAM WARD HOBBS was indicted for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing them to have been stolen.
 There being no evidence but that of an accomplice, which was unconfirmed in any particular, the case was not proceeded in.
     NOT GUILTY.
