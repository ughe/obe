 43. JAMES HORTON was indicted for stealing, on the   4th. of No vember , 6 wooden boards, value 8 s  ., the goods of Joshua Ramsay , his master; and CHARLES WOOLFE for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing them to have been stolen; against the Statute, &c.
   JOSEPH CHILD . I, am a labourer. On the 4th of November I saw Horton driving a one-horse cart—I believe it was in Ann's-place—I sup pose a hundred: or two hundred yards from Mr. Woolfe's, and a good distance from the prosecutor's—he had some boards—I suspected all was not right—I die not know with whom Horton worked—he stopped at a distance from Woolfe's house, took the boards off the cart, and took them to Woolfe's, then came and drove the cart on—I went to Mr. Joshua Ramsay, No 36, Walbrook, whose name was on the cart.
