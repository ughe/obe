2770. FANNY JACKSON was indicted for feloniously assaulting
Fanny Kemble, on the 15th of September, and cutting and wounding her
in and upon her right arm, with intent to do her some grievous bodily
barm.
FANNY KEMBLE. I am an unfortunate woman, and live in St. An.
drew's-terrace, Waterloo-road. On the 16th of September I was washing

what you have got to say about me, say to my face, not behind my back"
—I lifted the window up—Mrs. Taylor pulled it down again—I then
knocked at the door—Mrs Taylor opened it—the prisoper said, If you

took a knife off the drawers within the door, and stabbed at my face—I

to the hospital, and was there a fortnight.
Prisoner. She struck me first, twice. Witness I did not, nor did I
attempt to strike her before she cut me.
JANE TAYLOR. The prisoner lodged with me. On the 16th of Septem-
ber she was in the same room with me, very drunk—the prosecutrix over-
beard her telling me of a depredation which the prosecutrix had committed
in my house during my absence, and she knocked at my door to come in,
and fight her—I locked the door against her-—she then attempted to come
through the parlour-window, and put her foot in, but I would not let her—
she then came back to the parlour-door, and battered till I thought she
would break the panel—I then opened the door, and asked what she was
going to do—she struck the prisoner twice over my shoulder, and the third
time she attempted to strike her, the prisoner said, If you strike me again
1 will mark you"—l had been cutting some bread and butter, and as soon
as the prosecutrix attempted to strike the prisoner again, she caught up the
knife and struck her on the arm—it was a common table-knife—it was
afterwards given to the policeman.
WILLIAM HENRY FREEMAN. I am a surgeon. The prosecutrix was
brought to the hospital with an incised wound in the fore-arm, about half-
an-inch deep, or rather more, and about an inch and a half long—it was
such a wound as might be inflicted with a knife—the wound is well now,
or nearly so.
THOMAs BLACK. I am a policeman. I was called in, and took the
prisoner into custody—a knife was given me by one of the inmates of the
house.
Priscner. I am very sorry it has occurred.
GUILTY of an Assault. Aged 22.—Confined Two Months.
