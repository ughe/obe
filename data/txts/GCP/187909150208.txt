875. GEORGE WEBB (25), Burglary in the dwelling-house of John
Lawton Haddon, with intent to steal.
MR. WILMOT Prosecuted.
JOHN HADDON. I am a civil engineer, at 21, Lansdowne Road,
Clapham-on 17th August, about 9.30, I retired to bed, leaving some of
my family up; and about 3 a.m. my wife woke me-I saw the outline of
the prisoner standing at the foot of the bed—I caught him by the throat
before he had time to move-his hands were on the mantelpiece, within s
foot of my watch and ring-We have since missed & silver top of a cruet
from the room he entered-I asked him how he got in ; and he said,
“Through the window, it was open"-I examined the premises and found
foot-marks leading to the window-it looks out on to a veranda.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. We had not found out the loss of the
cruet top when before the Magistrates.
ELLEN HORSFOLD. I am servant to Mr. Haddon-I saw the windows
closed about 9.45, and was the last person up.
GEORGE HAZEL (Police Sergeant W 18). I examined the premises at
21, Lansdowe Road, and found foot-marks of two different persons in the
back garden, and traced them to the balcony and found mud on the window
curtains
PETER BERRY (Policeman W 369). I took the prisoner in custody at
the door of the house, and searched him but found nothing on him.
GUILTY. He also PLEADED GUILTY to having been before convicted of
felony at this Court.-Five Years' Penal Servitude.
