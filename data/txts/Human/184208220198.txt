   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  How long had you been drinking? A.  I had drank nothing before that—I saw Rosen Brock pour out the gin—she took it out of the same glass that I did.
   COURT . Q.  Was there time for any body to put anything into the glass? A.  Yes—I have taken gin before—it never had this effect on me. JOHN TAYLOR FOSTER, Jun. I remember my brother bringing in the gin—I was sent on another errand, and Rosen Brock told me to make haste, and they would stop till I came back—I returned in a quarter of an hour—the prisoners were then gone, and my mother was stupefied.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How long had they been in the house? A.  From half an hour to three quarters—there was a good deal of conversation—Mr. Lazarus sent to my mother for some shirts, which ought to have gone in that morning. HENRY HATTON( police-constable H  43.) I took Cantillow—Rosen-brock was on the other side of the road, and was quite drunk—Cantillow was quite sober—she had six pennyworth of copper on her. ROBERT COLLINS. The little boy came to me for change fore sovereign, and I sent back five shillings in copper. NOT GUILTY
 2474. SARAH PETO was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of August , 1 shawl, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; 5 shillings, 2 sixpences, 2 pence, and 1 halfpenny; the property of Exsuperious Turner .—2nd COUNT  , stating it to be the property of Ann Turner . ANN TURNER. I am the wife of Exsuperious Turner, and live in Ironmonger-row, St. Luke. On Tuesday morning, the 16th of August, I met the prisoner in Old-street—I asked her home with me—she went, and I fell asleep on the bed—I left the shawl and money on the mantel-piece—when I awoke she was gone—this is my shawl—no one else was there. ELIZABETH BELLAMY. Mrs. Turner sent me to get a shawl out of pawn—when I brought it back, the prisoner and Mrs. Turner were asleep—I gave the shawl to the prisoner and twopence-halfpenny. CHARLES UNDERHILL. I am assistant to James Thomas Hawes, a pawnbroker, in Old-street—this shawl was pawned, in the name of Andrews, on the 16th of August. NOT GUILTY.
 2475. CHRISTIAN SWANSEA was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of August , 1 pewter pot, value 1 s  .; the goods of James Buckmaster . JOHN PRIOR. I am waiter in the service of James Buck master, who keeps the Swan Tavern, Hunger ford-market—the prisoner came there for a pint of porter and a short pipe, between six and seven o'clock, on the 18th of August—I suspected, and followed him out of the market—he came back, and as he was taking the pot from his pocket, I took it from his hand—it is my master's.
