Ro-examined. I wrote out my statement in the prison in consequence
of the advice given me the day after my sentence by the Governor of
Newgate-I sent it to Mr. Freshfield, after which a gentleman from his
office came to see me.
ALFRED JOHN BOYCE. I am a cashier at Messrs. Barnett, Hoare, and
Co.'s, of Lombard Street, who are agents for Stephens, Blandy, and Co.,
bankers, at Reading—on 12th January a person who I believe to be Max.
well gave me 2001. in gold and filled up a credit form in favour of Edward
Hunt-I advised Stephens, Blandy, and Co. of that credit-the man was
a stranger-he gave his name as 8. Soames-about 20th January some
man brought me four 51. Reading notes to change, and I changed them
for gold over the counter.

name of Edward Hunt, and spoke to me about 2001.-he showed me a
letter purporting to be from Mr. Soames--I opened an account for him
