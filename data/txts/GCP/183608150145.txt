ISABELLA FINDLEY. I am the wife of Lewis Findley, and occupy a
single room in Miss Jolland's house in Old-street, St. Luke's. I had a
pocket-book, with three £5 notes, a sovereign, and two half-sovereigns
missed it in the afternoon of the 22nd of June.I had seen it in the morning
of the 21st-the prisoners veeupied the second floor back room in the house
-my room is the third floor front-we found out the notes by the numbers
at the Bank.
