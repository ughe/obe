John PARKS. I am carter to Mr. Maskin, of Maidstone-- I was going up
White Cross-street on the afternoon of the oth of March, and saw the prisoner
near the corner of White Cross-street-she stooped and hurled a pair of black
gloves away-she was going towards Mr. Clark's shop-he was walking in
front of her-I got out of my cart and pick-d up the gloves at the side of Mr.
Hammond's, the ironmonger's, window just by the grating-his shop is the
corner, and Mr. Clark's is the next door-some one came after me, and
brought me back to Mr. Clark's shop-I gave the gloves to him.
Cross-examined. Q. How far had you got with the gloves ? A. About
three or four rods- I thought as the lady was dressed in a respectable way,
she did not want them; in fact, I did not know what they were, till I got up
into the van again- I did not know who was the owner, and whether the lady
wanted them or not-I was driving off down White Cross-street-I must
have gone by the pork-shop-I do not know whether there is a grating under
the pork-shop window-it was two or three minutes after I took the gloves
that I was brought back-there were some persons in the street.
