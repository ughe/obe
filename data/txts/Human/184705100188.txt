1284. ABRAHAM BERLYN was indicted for stealing 15 pairs of shoes value 1 l  . 8 s  . 9 d  .; the goods of Abraham Solomon , his master. ABRAHAM SOLOMON( through an interpreter. ) The prisoner was in my ✗ vice. On the 18th of March he took out fifteen pairs of shoes—he said he sold them to Mr. Jones—he did not give me the money—he made me enter them✗ in the book to Mr. Jones, fifteen pairs of shoes, at 15 s . a-dozen, 1 l . 3 s . 9 d .—he had leave to sell shoes.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  How long have you been in Eng land? A.  Eighteen months—I have been in business about twelve months in Goulston-square, Whitechapel—I am a native of France—I know Ments—I went honourably, there and came away honourably—my wife did not come to this country with me—she lived in France till I had lived here about eight months, when I wrote to her to come—I did not leave her there in any business—I gave up business when I left—I know nothing about her being put into prison for concealing my effects—I was oncei n partnership with a man named Altham—I have not been to Mr. Hart, the beadle of the Great Synagogue, and asked him for 9 l . to keep away from this prosecution—Mr. Hart has been to my house and inquired what the prisoner had dose, and I told him all the circumstances about it—I can swear I did not ask for 9 l . to keep away from this prosecution—I did not go to the Rev. Dr. Alder and ask him for money—he sent his servant for me; I went, and heard what he wanted of me—the prisoner told me he had been a wholesale shoe maker on his own account—I did not agree with him that he was to bring me his connexion—he was to find a connexion—we were not to divide the profits—he had 25 s . a-week—I have his receipts for that—he was not to receive 1 l . a-week and one quarter of the profits—he was not to have any✗ portion of the profits—he has complained to me about the way in which I keep my books. JAMES JONES. I sometimes purchase things of the prisoner—I did not purchase any shoes of him on the 18th of March.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You have purchased things of the prisoner? A.  Yes Abel Solomon was known to me, through the prisoner—the prisoner was the person I dealt with, and I paid him on delivery.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor  — Confined Six Months .
   ESSEX CASES .
