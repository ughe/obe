took her back to the shop when I saw the prisoner, she said if I would
go to Mrs. Prebble's shop, she would come to me I said, “No, I have lost
two days, I shall lose no more ; where you go, I shall go" she went and
spoke to & water cress man, and they went into a public house ; they kept
