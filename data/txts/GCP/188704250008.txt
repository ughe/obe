searched, and 6 half-crowns, 10 florins, 5 shillings, and 4 sixpences were
found on him-he said that he did not know Jones, who was there.
Cross-examined. It was getting dark, the lamps were alight and a
stream of people were passing, but I kept him in sight all the time-I
saw the money taken from his pockets, but saw no coppers.
