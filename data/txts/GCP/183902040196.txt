784. WILLIAM ABRAY was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of Feb-
ruary, 2 gallons of gin, value 1l. 18., and I pint of rum, value 28. 6d., the
goods of Richard Moss, his master.
RICHARD Moss. I keep the Adam and Eve, in St. John-street-road
-the prisoner lived with me about nine days. On the 7th of February,
after I was gone to bed, I heard a noise in the house-I went down, and
called out “Hollo"-he made no answer at last he said, “It's me"-
I said, "Who is me?"-he said, “William"-he said he was ill-he
was in the scullery-I kicked my foot against a bottle of gin, and asked
what it was-he said water-I asked what he was going to do with it-he
said he did not know-I found both the windows of the bar unfastened,
which I had fastened before I went to bed-he had got in at one window
just under a door, and opened the door-I brought the gin away, called
in a policeman, and he shewed us the tub he took the gin out of-I
found a bottle of rum in the kitchen, and a funnel wrapped up in one of
the children's dresses-he had broken the window by falling down, and
that disturbed me.
FREDERICK CHAPPELL (police-constable G 221.) I apprehended the
prisoner, and have the gin.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
