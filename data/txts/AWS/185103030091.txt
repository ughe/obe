770. KARL WERTH, stealing 1 knife, 1 pair of trowsers, and
1 waisteoat, value 8s. 3d. the goods of John Houston, in a vessel, in
a port.
JOHN HOUSTON. I am an apprentice to the William Lotsford, lying in
the West India Dock. On 15th Feb. I was away from the ship, and
when I returned E missed a waisteoat, a pair of trowsers, anda knife, from
my chest- had seen them safe an hour before-this is the knife (produced)
-it is mine-] never saw the prisoner before-be had no business on
the ship.
Prisoner. It is my knife. Witness. It is mine-I swear to it by a
little piece broken off thelarge blade-1 have had it two years.
MARK ANTONY LIDDLE. I am a constable of the East and West India
Dock Company. The prisoner was given into my charge on the 19th,
on another charge- found two knives and a key in his trowsers-pocket,
and another key in the waistband of his trowsers-it fell down his leg on
unbuttoning his trowsers- find that one will open the cabins of several
ships; it is a master key.
