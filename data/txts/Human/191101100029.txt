 Police-constable  ALFRED EDWARDS , 411 A. On December 22, about 4.15 p.m., I went to Bartrum and Harvey's warehouse, when I saw prisoner at the front door about to go away with this parcel under his arm. The manager, in his presence, said to me, "This man has pre sented this order for 6 1/2 yards of cloth," and he showed me Exhibit 5. He then said to prisoner, "Are you employed by this firm?" mean ing Curtis and Brooks. Prisoner said, "A man asked me to bring the order here, but I do not know him and I cannot describe him." He was given into my custody. At the station on being charged he made no reply, but later he said, "A man promised me sixpence when I brought this cloth back." I know nothing about the invoice.
 ----  BROOKS , of Curtis and Brooks, tailors, 88, Fleet Street, E.C.
