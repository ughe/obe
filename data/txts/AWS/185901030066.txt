194. JOSEPH ASHTON (43) WILLIAM IRWIN (43), and SAMUEL
TUTTLEBEE, Stealing 1 metal bell, value 311. of Thomas Quested Finnis,
Esq., and fixed in a garden belonging to him.
MR. COoPER conducted the Prosecution.
EDWARD RICHARD BENMORE. I am gardener to Thomas Quested Finnis,
who resides at Wanstead, in Essex-I knew a metal bell, which had been
hanging on a yew-tree on the lawn in front of the mansion-] saw it safe
on 28th November, about half-past four o'clock-I missed it on Tuesday,
30th November, about half-past seven o'clock in the morning, and I gaye
information to the police at Wanstead-I saw the bell on Ist December at
the Stepney police-station, and knew it-I looked well to see how it had
been taken away-they had rolled it under the fence-] have here a repre
sentation of the bell, and the inscription that was on it (produced) it came
from India-it was a Burmese bell.
WILLIam CoPPING (Policeman, K 379). On 1st December, from instrue-
tions I received, I made inquiry about a bell-I went to Mr. West's, in
Baek-church-lane-I found the bell there-I got some information from Mr.
West, and in consequence of what he told me, I went to Mr. Day, in the
West-India-road, Poplar, and, from what he told me, I went to the Vic-
toria Docks-I was there shown the prisoner Tuttlebee-I asked him
whether he knew anything about a bell-he said, said Are you
sure of that said, 46 Yes "Mr. Day then said to him, 4 You know
that bell that you brought to me, and took up to London yesterday "-he
said, at Yes" said to him, 66 Where did you get that bell -he said, A
man by the name of Joseph Ashton, called me up in the morning, about 7
o'clock, and asked me if I would sell it for him "-he said he lived two or
three doors down the street, and he shewed me the house-he said, 44 I will
give you all the information I can a man of the name of Irwin lives across
the Marsh, and he took it to London in his part detained Tuttlebee-
saw Irwin at 4 o'clock in the North-Woolwich-road-] said to him, 4 Where
did you get the bell from, that you took to London yesterday he said,
A short, full-faced man employed me "-when we got through the docks, he
showed me the street where he lived, but he said he did not know his name--]
took him to Poplar Station, and came back, and, from what he told me, I
took Ashton-he was in a beer-house-he went out the back way, and ran
away before he knew what we wanted but I caught him, and took him to
Poplar Station, and Irwin said, 44 That is the man that employed
Ashton made no reply-I asked him how the bell got into his cart-he
said he did not know.
