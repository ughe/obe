 2213. JAMES FRENCH was indicted for stealing 3 bushels of onions; the goods of Elizabeth Frances Ireland , and others: having been before convicted of felony. RICHARD JOSEPH IRELAND. I am in partnership with my mother, Eliza beth Frances Ireland, and my brother—we are gardeners, at Plaistow—we missed some onions from a field adjoining the Barking-road—I saw them safe between five and six o'clock in the afternoon, on the 9th of Sept.—I have seen some of them again—I can swear to them. WILLIAM EASTWICK. I am in the service of Mrs. Ireland—I have seen the onions which have been found—I believe them to be what I bunched—they are a good deal like them. MARY PARKER. I wash for the prisoner—I washed this handkerchief for him—I know it is his.
   Prisoner.  What can you swear to it by; I could have bought twenty like it where I bought it—mine had a mark on the corner of it? Witness  I never examined that, but I know it is your handkerchief. GEORGE HARRISON( police-sergeant, K  15.) I was on duty in the Barking-road, on the morning of the 10th of Sept.—I saw the prisoner come from gate in Mr. Ireland's field—I turned my light on and saw he had a suck—he left the sack and crossed to the Green-gate public-house—I found it was full of onions, and the month of if tied with this handkerchief—I went to him he laid down on his belly—I shook him, he pretended to be drunk—I dis covered he had lost the handkerchief from his neck—I asked what he had done with it—he said he had lost it—I said I had found one, and showed it him—he said it was not his—I took one of his shoes off, and found a great number of marks corresponding with it in the field where the onions were.
