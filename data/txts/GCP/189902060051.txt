-on December 25th, about 6.10 p.m., I was crossing Weston Street into
Elam Street-I heard a scuffle, and saw a soldier and a civilian walking
towards me—I walked on-in a few minutes the soldier came alongside
and struck me in the eye with a stick like this (produced), and called me
