FREDERICK Cook (police sergeant, D 1). On Tuesday night, 1st April
in consequence of information from Fudge, about a quarter past 10 o'clock,
I went to Whitechapel, and met M-Kenley in Chapel-street, coming
from the direction of the Rose and Crown, where the robbery had been com-
mitted—I followed him, Gibbs came up and said, “That is one of the men"
-I then went up to him, and said, “You must go to the station with me"
--he said, "What for?"I said, “For oommitting a robbery on a person
at the Burn's Arms skittle ground"-I thought that was the name of the
house at that time-he said, “Who told you I was one of them "--I said,
“ You will see that when you get to the station"-I took him there-he was
the worse for liquor-the prosecutor had been drinking, but he was perfectly
aware of what he was stating, he appeared to be like a man who had been
asleep for two or three hours, stupified-on the Wednesday, I went to Na
59, Seymour-place-I found Rands there, in a shoemaker's shop, with his
name over the door, he was at work behind the counter-I said, “You
must go to the station with me for being concerned in a robbery at the
Burn's Arms"-- he said, “That is a very unpleasant affair, for I wanted to
get these boots home to-night”-on the road to the station he said, " Are
they going to make me a witness or a prisoner?"-I said, "I have already
made you a prisoner"-I then took him to the station, and fetched the
prosecutor and Gibbs, who identified him as one of the party.
Cross-examined. Q. You found Rands working at his slop ? A. Yes;
it is a small shop—there were some children there; he seemed surprised.
SILVESTER THRUSH (policeman, D 190). On the Monday after the rob-
bery, I went in company with sergeant Cook to No. 17, Sadler's-place,
London-wall-I saw Oakenfull there, and his brother, standing at the foot
of the stairs-Cook told him he must consider himself in custody for being
concerned with three others in robbing a man at the Burn's Arms-he said,
"That is what I expected"-he was taken to the station and identified by
the witnesses.
