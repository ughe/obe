   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Was this before four o'clock? A little before four o'clock—it was darkish—I could not say whether the prisoners saw each other—I did not have a character when I went to the prosecutor's service—we never do. BENJAMIN HAMPSON. I am servant to Mr. Sinnott. I was watching and saw Carter come over the fence—Bowley came from the house, and I heard him knock at the wharfinger's door for him to get up—he walked away under the cart—in about two minutes Carter got over the fence, about four yards from Bowley—he passed by him within about three feet—I cannot tell which way Bowley's face was—Carter passed the stable-door I and took a ladder, chucked  it over the stable-door, and then he clambered up, and remained in about ten minutes—Bowley was watching Carter suppose—I cannot say which way he was looking—I saw Carter come out of the stable—he put this bag on his back, and walked past Bowley—he then heard my foot-steps, and he chucked  the sack over the fence—I had moved—he then came back and spoke to Bowley.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  You have heard the evi dence of the other man? A.  Yes—there is no door to the loft, it is all open—I was four or five yards from the doorway—I put my head out, it was rather dark, but not quite dark—I cannot say if there was any moon—we had no light, it was twilight—we had watched about a fort night at nights, and worked in the day—we never went to sleep in the loft, we only slept on Sundays—I nodded sometimes.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Had the other man any sleep? A.  No—I was awake a whole fortnight—I was five or sixyards off Car ter when he heard my shoes—I could see quite plain—I have been with Mr. Sinnott four years.
   COURT to GEORGE WHITE . Q.  Did you see Bowley go up to the whar finger's door? A.  I do not know—I heard a knock, whether it was at the wharfinger's or not, I do not know—I was nearly close to Hampson. JOSEPH KENT( police-constable L  164.) I was called this morning, and went on the premises—Bowley was given into my custody—I asked what he was doing there—he gave a reason for it—I went back and ex amined the place—I saw nothing there to give me reason to believe any thing of the kind had occurred—I produce the sack.
   MR. SINNOTT re-examined.  This is my sack, it has my name at full length on it, and it contains what I believe to be mine.
 (The prisoner received a good character.)
   BOWLEY— GUILTY.
   CARTER— GUILTY. Confined Six Months .
