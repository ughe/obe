This waistcoat was pledged on 27th of March, in the name of Ann Carty, I believe by the prisoner, but I cannot swear to her—this is the duplicate I gave. MARY ANN BRIDGE. I live at the station house in Marylebone-lane—I searched the prisoner—she asked if I thought she should get off about the waistcoat—I said, "Why, did you take the waistcoat?"—she said, "Yes, I did"—I am sure I said, "Did you take it?"—I did not say,"Did you pledge it"—( looking at her deposition )—this is my name—( The deposition being ready stated  —"She asked me if I thought she should get off for pledging the waistcoat—I said, 'Did you pledge it?' and she said 'Yes'")—I cannot say exactly whether I said "take it" or "pledge it"—I think I said, "Did you take it?" now I come to recollect.
   Prisoner.  I never said that I took it or pledged it. JOHN LORDING( policeman. ) I took the prisoner into custody on the 27th of March, at the prosecutor's—she said to him, "There is my shawl you can take and pledge; that will get 3 s . and get the waistcoat."
   Prisoner's Defence.  I did not say so; I never took the waistcoat. NOT GUILTY.
 955. CATHERINE MURPHY was again  indicted for stealing, on the   26th of March , 1 sovereign, of Mark Benjamin , her master. MARK BENJAMIN. About a fortnight or three weeks previous to the 26th of March, I lost from my pocket the key of a drawer in my bed-room where I kept cash—on the 21st I put some money in the drawer, and counted it—there were ten sovereigns—I locked the drawer with another key—on Wednesday I opened the drawer again, and missed a sovereign—I asked the prisoner that same morning whether she knew or had seen anything about it—she said, "How could I see anything about a sovereign when your drawer is locked?"—this was after I had lost the waistcoat—I gave her in charge on Thursday night. MARY ANN BRIDGE. I searched the prisoner, and found 19 s . in her shoe, tied up in a purse—she screamed dreadfully, and said, "Oh, don't say anything about it, for if you do, they will send me across the water."
   Prisoner.  I never said any such thing.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY>  . Aged 19. Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor. — Confined Six Months .
