Mr. CLARKSON called
David Woodcock. I am clerk to Messrs. Pickfords. I remember
Elizabeth Smith coming to the wharf on the evening in question- she
told me her brother had paid 278. instead of 20s., by mistake, and she
wished to see the prisoner, in order that she might arrange it with him-I
said he was not there then, and told her at what time he would be there
-she said, " I suppose I must depend on the man' honesty and recollec-
tion for the purpose of repaying the money"-1 said, “No; if we are
satisfied he has received more than he ought, I will take care the matter
shall be set right”—I did not in any way endeavour to secrete the prisoner,
or get him out of the way-the firm would not sanction any thing of the
kind-he has been three years and a half in their employ, and is a man of
honesty and merit, and has given satisfaction in every way.
NOT GUILTY.
2021. JAMES HAYES and EDWARD SCOTT were indicted for
stealing, on the 2nd of June, 1 loaf of bread, value 4d.; 1lb, weight of
butter, value 1s. ; and Alb. weight of bacon, value 4d. ; the goods of
William John Ward.
WILLIAM MARTIN. I am a lamp-lighter, and live in Spring-grove,
Lambeth. At half past three o'clock in the morning of the 2nd of June I
saw the two prisoners at the end of Devonshire-road, Larkhall-lane,
lurking about with two others-presently I saw Scott get over a fence,
dose by Mr. Ward's house-I gave information to the police—they
went round one way, and I the other the policeman got there first-I saw
him take Scott, and I took Hayes, who had a bundle with him-he threw
it down, I picked it up, and gave it to the policeman-Hayes was taken
a quarter of a mile from where he threw down the bundle-I chased him,
and lost sight of him about half a minute, in turning round a wall-there
was no one else there, except the other two-I saw Scott throw a parcel
over Mr. Field's garden.
