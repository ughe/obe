possession, with intent to utter it.

in Union Street, Borough, about 5.45-we were in plain clothes--I saw
the prisoner standing under a lamp rubbing something between his
finger and thumb-he went into the Albion public-house, and came out
and looked round-I went under the same lamp-he then leaned against
a fence at the corner of Bath Terrace-we went across to him, and I said,
"What is that you had rubbing in your fingers and thumb just now ?"
