Witnesses for the Defence.
SABAH GOODSHIP. I lived at Stevenage, in Hertfordshire, but now live
at Finsbury Park-on 5th July, I was in Mrs. Seeley's service-I remember
the removal from Hornsey Park Road, to the Euston Road-when we got
there Mrs. Seeley said "I think it is very unkind of Mrs. Charlton not
coming to put the things straight, as I gave her a wash-stand, a chest of
drawers, and a bedstead "-I had seen a paliasse, and bedstead taken into
Mrs. Charlton's house in the Hornsey Park Road-I helped to move the
palliasse ; Mrs. Seeley saw Mrs. Charlton, and me move it out of the room
the day before the removal --Mrs. Seeley said " Mrs. Charlton may have the
palliasse into her house "-Mr. Seeley had been away three weeks before the
removal, she had been drinking on the previous Tuesday and for about a
week-she used to send me for the drink, and Mrs. Seeley and Mrs. Charlton
became very friendly together-Mrs. Charlton only came in when Mrs.
Seeley sent for her; Mrs. Charlton came about 3 o'clock on the Thursday
morning-I did not hear any arrangement made as to her coming ; I did not
Bee the drawers go over the wall—the wall is not very high, I could look
over it, if I was not close to it, into the street without getting on anything
- before we got to Euston Road, Mrs. Charlton left the van and came back
to the Hornsey Rond-the goods were being put into the other van and
Mrs. Seeley was up and downstairs seeing to the goods—she gave Clayton
half a sovereign on the road to get changed-in the evening between 6 and
7 o'clock, she gave Clayton 78. or 78. 60.--I never heard Mrs. Seeley ask
Mrs. Charlton to drink with her, but Mrs. Seeley sent me to knock at the
scullery door for Mrs. Charlton to come in; I cannot say what for-they
drank together; that occurred once or twice a day-I came as & willing
witness-when I came up I was out of a situation-Mr. and Mrs. Charlton
gave me accommodation for one night--Mrs. Seeley said she gave Mrs.
Charlton the things for moving--that was in Euston Road on the day of
the removal _I looked surprised but did not say anything.
