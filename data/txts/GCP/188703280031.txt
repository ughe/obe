--they pulled up in Wade Street, Poplar, and Leech got down and
walked some distance, where he met Beer--they walked into High Street
and went into the Black Horse public-house--Wilcox followed with the

Beer in conversation-they remained about half an hour-Denning,
another officer, heard the conversation-Leech then came out, leaving
