Siath Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
100. PHOEBE ANDERSON was indicted for a misdemeanor.
Mr. ELLIs conducted the Prosecution.
ANN BEAUMONT. I keep a dairy in Rathbone-place, At half-past ten
o'clock, on the 1st of November, the prisoner eame to my shop for a pint
of milk and four eggs, which came to 6d.-she offered a half-crown-I
gave her one shilling and two sixpences--I put the half-crown in the til
-there was no other silver there-in ten minutes or a quarter of an hour
I went to take it out, and saw it was bad-] Ilocked it up pin a glass case, and
it never went out-she came again about seven o'clock, in the evening of
the 14th-my niece, Ellen Swindell, was in the shop-th prisoner asked
for a pint of milk-my niece called me-I found the prisoner there--my
niece wanted change for a half-crown-i was a bad one-I recognised the
prisoner, and said she had paid me a visit shortly before, and asked if she
had any more half-crowns-she said she had never been there before--
am positive she was the person-I could swear to her among a thousand-
I sent for a policeman, and gave him the two half-crowns.
ELLEN SWINDELL. I am the prosecutrix's niece. The prisoner came
on the 14th of November, about seven or half-past seven o'clock in the
evening, for a pint of milk-I served her-she gave me a half-crown-I
gave it to my aunt-it was the one I took from the prisoner.
MARK BRBAKSPEAR (police-constable E 30.) I took the prisoner, and
received two half-crowns from Ann Beaumont-the prisoner said she came
from Westminster, which is three miles from the prosecutrix's.
Mr. JOHN FIELD. I am Inspector of coin to the Royal Mint. These
half-crowns are both counterfeit in all respeets.
GUILTY. Aged 6.-Confined Six Months.
