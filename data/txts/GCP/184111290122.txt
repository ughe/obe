137. JOHN TIPSON was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of Novem-
ber, 24dwts. of silver, value 6s., the goods of John Joseph Dyer, his
master.
John Joseph DYER. I am a watch-case maker, living in Nicholl's-
street, Shoreditch. The prisoner was in my service--it was his duty to
make watch-cases. About half-past nine o'clock in the evening of the
9th of November, he was brought into my parlour, and accused of taking
some silver-when I came home I asked if he had got any-he said no,
he had not-I sent for a policeman, and said I would give him into cus-
tody-he then gave one piece of silver up, and said he had no more about
him—the policeman found nothing on him, but on the way he threw se-
veral more pieces away, and some turnings-they weigh 24dwts. together
- they are worth about 68.-I am confident they are mine.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. How long has be been in your
employ? A. Nearly two years I have fourteen or fifteen persons in
my employ- I did not tell his son on Tuesday that I would recommend
him to mercy.
