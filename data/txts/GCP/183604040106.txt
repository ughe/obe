WILLIAM BAKER Ashton (police-sergeant G 11.) I apprehended the
male prisoner on the 12th of March, at Richmond-street, St. Luke's-
we went to the station-house, he said he knew he had done wrong by
bringing his sister to Mrs. Betts, which was all he did I said, "You
had better hold your tongue, you will have some other charges brought
against you, and particularly one of taking out a basket of goods from
Mrs. Goulding's"-he said, “Unfortunately I did, but it was by the
advice of my sister."
