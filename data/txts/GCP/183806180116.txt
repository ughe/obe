1561. HUGH JOHN WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 18
of May, 1 tea-kettle, value 4s., the goods of John Beeson.
JOHN BEEson. I keep the White Horse public-house, Saffron-hill 1
saw my copper tea-kettle between six and seven o'clock on the evening
the 18th of May-I missed it a few minutes after seven o'clock-the pe-
soner had been in the tap-room-this is it-(looking at it)— I know it by
this mark.
Cross-examined by Mr. Phillips. Q. Was the prisoner tipsy? 4. He
was not-he had not the appearance of being tipsy-eight or nine person
were in my house that night-we have singing, but no music-we had not
any singing that evening- I do not take money from people who come in for
concerts—there is a young man that has my room up stairs-he takes the
money, and finds the music-I do not receive the money for the room-
the music brings the customers in-the kettle was in the tap-room.
JAMES STANLEY. I am shopman to a pawnbroker. This kettlemu
pawned by the prisoner.
Cross-examined. Q. What time was it pledged ? A. After eight
o'clock on the 18th of May-it is in the name of James Smith, No. 98
Saffron-hill-there were eight or nine customers in the shop-I am quite
certain of him-I recollect he was in the last box of our shop.
Prisoner. The pawnbroker said, "at the office;" it was from half-past
eight o'clock to just upon nine.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Three Months.

of Francis Duncan, in a certain vessel in a port of entry and discharge.

India Docks--the prisoner was in the Isabella Ann, on the south side of
the docks. On the 13th of June, I asked him to help me, and he came on
board my vessel about ten o'clock in the morning to do so-he stopped till
half-past eleven o'clock-I had thirteen boxes, and he took ten-there were
ear-rings in them-I had three pairs of trowsers, and a pair of boots--
ear-rings belonged to Walter Scott, the captain--he gave them me to mind
- the others were mine.
