3098. RICHARD TRENT, CAROLINE MALLABONE, and JAMES
KING, were indicted for feloniously assaulting James White, on the 11th of
Oct., putting him in fear, and taking from his person, and against his will, 1
pocket-book, value 1s. ; 2 sovereigns, and 1 59 note; his property; and
feloniously beating, striking, and using other personal violence to him.
JANES WHITE. I am a carpenter, and live in Russell-row, Locks-fields.
On Tuesday, the 11th of Oct., as I was coming up the Walworth-road, about
a quarter past one o'clock at night, going home, some parties came bebind
me, and gave me a push on the neck, which threw me down--as soon as I
was down, Trent's hands were in my right hand trowsers' pocket, and across
my breast-I cried out, “Keep your hands out of my pocket" - had scarcely
spoken the words before a policeman came up, and made a grab at Trent,
who instantly ran away, the policeman pursued him, sprung his rattle, and
he was stopped by a constable by the Elephant and Castle I found that I
had lost my pocket-book, a 51. note, and two sovereigns-the note was in
the pocket-book, in my breast pocket, and the sovereigns in my right hand
trowsers' pocket-I had felt the book safe about two minutes before I only
saw Trent-it happened close by a gas-light.
Cross-examined by Mr. CHARNOCK. Q. Has anything of this kind hap
pened to you before? A. Yes-I have been robbed three times, some years
ago-I was the worse for liquor, but was perfectly capable of taking care of
myself-I had been out in the day on business, and had refreshment at two
places--the last place was the Alfred's Head-I had not been there more than
a quarter of an hour I had felt my pocket-book safe two minutes before this
occurred, by putting my hand outside my coat pocket, and about three
minutes before that I doubled down the top part of the paper, beonuse it was
too long for the depth of the pocket, to keep it out of sight-1 had four sove-
reigns in my left hand pocket, which were not taken1 did not fall down
from being tipsy-I was pushed down from behind - I saw no one but Trent
- he was not assisting to raise me up in consequence of the helpless state I
was in--I swear he did not try to raise me up at all I had been to the
Alfred's Head with a man who sells mahogany- there might be some women
there-1 did not see any girls of the towo-I.was not treating or drinking
with any-I saw the prisoners searched nothing was found on them,
