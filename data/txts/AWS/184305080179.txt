1670. THOMAS HOCKODAY was indicted for stealing on the 21st of
April, 11 penkives, value 168. 6d., the goods of James Cross.
HENRY GLAISER. I am apprentice to Mr. James Cross, a stationer,
at Greenwich. On Friday morning, the 21st of April, I came into the shop,
and saw the prisoner there with a card of knives in his hand-he put them
down, and asked how much they were each-I saw another card of knives
under his coat, and asked what he had got there-he said, 14 Nothing"
said 1 You have"ihe put the knives down, and ran out of the shop-there
was no one else in the shop-I called Mr. Cross, who ran after bim-1 fol-
lowed shortly after, overtook Mr. Cross, and spoke to a policeman, who ran
after him-he got away in the grove am sure he is the person-these are
the knives he had under his coat--they are Mr. Cross's.
DANIEL MOYNAHAN (police-constable R 104.) 1 was on duty at Croom's.
hill, Greenwich, on Friday, the 21st of April, In consequence of information,
I pursued the prisoner- lost sight of bim in Blackheath-grove-] only saw
his back.
JOSEPH GREBN. I am a policeman. I apprehended the prisoner in the
Broadway, Deptford, on the 21st of April, in consequence of information.
Prisoner's Defence. I was returning home from my work, the police-
man stopped me, and said one of his men wanted me; the lad at first said
he could not swear to me, and next day he said I was the man.
