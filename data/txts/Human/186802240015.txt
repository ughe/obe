JOHN HAWKINS( Policeman N  249). I took Newman at the Rochester Castle and told her the charge—she merely nodded her head—I took her to the station, and received from the searcher a paper containing a half-crown, a florin, a sixpence, and fivepence farthing in copper—When I put the prisoners into the dock at the station, Godwin said to Newman "Hold your noise and say nothing"—I received these two bad half-crowns from Mr. Serjeant and this other from Mrs. Thorpe. ELIZABETH WEBB. I am female searcher at Stoke Newington police-station—I searched Newman, and found on her 6 s . 5¼ d ., and on Godwin a string of beads, and this doll, a large key, and 3 s . 4 d . in good money.
   JAMES SERJEANT ( Re-examined .) These beads are what I supplied to Newman. WILLIAM WEBSTER. These coins are all bad, and two of them are from the same mould.
   Newman's Defence . None of them were marked till they were mixed, and it is impossible to say it is the same piece.
   Godwin's Defence . She only knows me by my voice—two persons' voices may be alike—she does not positively swear to me.
   NEWMAN  — GUILTY. She was further charged with having been convicted of a like offence in  1865, to which she PLEADED GUILTY .—** Five Years Penal Servitude. GODWIN  — GUILTY. She was further charged with having been convicted of a like offence at the same time . CHARLES BUSAIN( Policeman N  26). I produce a certificate of both prisoners' conviction—Godwin then gave the name of Newman, but she is the same person. CHARLES EASON( Policeman N  342). I was present at the two prisoners' former trial—Godwin was convicted in the name of Elizabeth Newman the younger; they are mother and daughter. GODWIN  — GUILTY. Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
