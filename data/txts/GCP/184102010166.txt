will forgive me" I said, "I can't forgive you, I have been robbed 80

property you have given your father of mine, and I shall give you in charge,
and go and search your father's house" - I had him taken, and then went
to his father's.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. How long had Francis been in
your employ? A. Three months and two or three days I have been a
prosecutor here twice before--I never heard it said that I was a little
cracked-I have been sixteen years in business-the fender is not in this
indictment-Francis did not mention any thing about the articles stated in
this indictment- I have a sale-book-none of these things are entered
there.
NOT GUILTY.
