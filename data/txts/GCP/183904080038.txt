1212. JAMES WHITE was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of March,
1 coat, value 58. 6d. ; 1 pair of trowsers, value 58, 6d.; and 1 handker-
chief, value 6d.; the goods of Henry Barton.
MARY TORPEY. I live in Norfolk-street, Middlesex Hospital. Henry
Barton lodged in my front attic, and the prisoner lodged in the same room-
on the 12th of March, at nine o'clock in the morning, the prisoner came in,
went ap stairs, brought down a bundle, and went out.
WILLIAM BIDDLE. I am a pawnbroker. I have a coat, pair of trowsers
and a handkerchief, which were pawned by the prisoner, in the name of
James Florence, Union-street.
WILLIAM WINSBURY. I took the prisoner-he said he took the things to
get some money to go into the country, but if his cousin had given him some
money, as he had promised, he should not have done it.
Henry BARTON. I lodge in Mrs. Torpey's house. These things are
mine permitted the prisoner to pawn one or two things, but not these-
I have known him seven or eight years I think he did this, supposing!
should take no notice of it, and to get a few shillings to go home.
NOT GUILTY.
1213. WILLIAM COOPER and JOHN JONES were indicted for
stealing, on the 30th of March, 1 handkerchief, value 58., the goods of
Thomas Young, from his person.
Thomas Young. I am a solicitor. On the 30th of March I was in the
waiting-room, at Somerset House, on business at the Stamp Office-I saw
the prisoner Jones at my back, and Cooper on my right-I felt my pocket
touched three or four times--I got my stamps, turned to the table, and
missed my handkerchief-I looked at the prisoners, and saw Jones reach
over something to Cooper, which he put under his apron-I said to him,
"Let me see what you have got under your apron"-he avoided me, and
turned off-I saw the handkerchief drop from under his apron, took it up
and gave them both in charge.
Jacob WOODWARD. I am a policeman. I took the prisoners into cus-
tody.
Cooper's Defence. I was waiting there for my master's property-1
took this lad in to show him the place, and this gentleman accused me, but
I am innocent-I think he found the handkerchief under a gentleman's
feet.
