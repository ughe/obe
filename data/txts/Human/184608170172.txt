 1682. WILLIAM JOHNSON was indicted for stealing 1 printed book, value 1 s  ., the goods of William Russel Langher , in a vessel in a port of entry and discharge; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 61.— Confined Two Months .
 1683. GEORGE BILBROUGH was indicted for embezzlement.
   MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution. EDWARD BAKER GREEN. I am the son of Edward Green, a coal-merchant He has two wharfs—one is the Rutland-wharf, at the City-road, which was under my control—the prisoner was in my father's service as a clerk—it was his duty to collect debts, and to account to me on his return—here is a book in which he had to enter the sums he received; he never accounted to me for 3 l . 14. received from Mr. Frost, on the 1st of April; nor for 1 l . 10. 91, on the 3rd of May; nor for 4 l . 1 s . 6 d . f on the 7th of May—there are no such entries—his wages were 35 s . a week, and a house to live in, and coals—he was not put to any expense in collecting, except he chanced to be too late, and then he had a fund at his disposal, to pay for a cab  or omnibni.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Do you know what Derby clubs are 1 A.  Yes—I never went with the prisoner to one—I never belonged to one in my life. EDWARD GREEN. The prisoner was in my service—he never accounted to me for these sums. WILLIAM FROST. I am a retail dealer, and live in James-street, Shore ditch. I was supplied by Mr. Green with coals—on the 1st of April, I paid the prisoner, on account of Mr. Green, 3 l . 14 s .; on the 3rd of April, 1 l . 10 s . 9 d .; and on the 7th of May, 4 l . 1 s . 6 d .—he wrote receipts to the bills. JAMES BRANNAN( police-sergeant G  20.) I took the prisoner into cus tody on the 4th of July, at Rutland-wharf—I told him it was for embezzling various sums of money—he said, "Yes, it is no use to deny it; I shall say nothing about it; I will take the worst. " GUILTY. Aged 30.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor who stated that the amount of his loss was about  200 l .— Confined Two Years .
 (There were two other indictments against the prisoner.)
