ALFRED WHIYFIN. I live at Lea Bridge, Walthamstow-I was playing

WILLIAM MARMOYS. I live at Pearson Street, Kingsland Road, and

stacks were then on fire--the prisoners came off the grass on to the
footpath, and stood with some parties there looking- I left them on
