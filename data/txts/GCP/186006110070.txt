examined at the Police-court-I did not say when I saw Besborough that

I also got some pieces of leather-I did not say, when before the Police

not bought the tools from him but from another man-When Deeble, the

goods in my possession-I was charged with receiving the goods, but I am

I ever appeared in a Court of Justice except when I was an apprentice and
summoned my master for wages--I never was convicted- I have not had
penal servitude ; it is quite nntrue.
