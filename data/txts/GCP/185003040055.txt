591. MARTHA DIAMOND NEEDHAM, stealing 1 spoon, and
1 brush, value 48.; the goods of John Hurstwaite Leete, her master.
John HURSTWAITE LEETE. I live in Sloane-street. The prisoner was in
my service, and was discharged on 10th Feb.-immediately she was gone I
missed a silver teaspoon-this is it (produced)—it is mine-after she was in
custody, I saw a clothes-brush, which is my property, found in her box.
Cross-examined by Mr. COOPER. Q. How long did she live with you !
4. Since last Sept.-she left for concealing a female in a cupboard-I swear
the brush is mine, having seen it every morning for twelve months-there
was a great deal of more valuable property about the house than this spoon-
it is worth 2s. or 3s.
JAMES RATCLIFFE CHESTER. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a teaspoon,
pledged by the prisoner on the 9th Feb.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you ever seen her before! 4. Frequently—she
gave the name of Williams-it is a common plan for parties pawning to give
a false name-my master's is not more than fifty doors from the prosecutor's
-I lent 2s. on the spoon-we buy things of that kind if offered for sale.
GEORGE T&wsLEY (policeman, B 181). I apprehended the prisoner-she
gave me a pocket-book, with eleven duplicates in it, one of which relates to
the spoon.
