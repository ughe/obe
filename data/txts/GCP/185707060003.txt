twice before that charge, but never for stealing or felony; it was for
assaulting I was tried in 1862, and was in Newgate for breaking a man's
leg by accident-I was sentenced to twelve months, but for all that I was
not guilty—there were two of us in the case, the other man that broke the
leg got acquitted, and I was sentenced to twelve months—I suffered about
seven months—the second time I was one month in the House of Correc-
tion for an assault-I never was charged with stealing a penny-I ran after
you, and caught you by the collar-you punched and kicked me-I knocked
