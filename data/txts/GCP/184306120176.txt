John THOMAS CRESWELL. I am shopman to Mr. Wood, a pawnbroker, in
St. John-street, Clerkenwell. On the 19th of May, about half-past eleven
o'clock, the prisoner came and produced these razors to pledge--I had re-
ceived information, and asked him if they were his own-he said, "Yes," and
asked me to advance 5s. on them I detained him under frivolous pretences,
and said I would send to ascertain the value of them, while I sent for a po-
liceman--when the policeman came, I asked the prisoner again if they were
his own-he said no, they were given to him by a man with whom he had
been playing at skittles at the Sun Dials—he went away with the policeman.
Cross-examined. Q. He did not offer to go away? A. No.
MR. RYLAND. Q. Would you have let him go if he had tried ? A. I
think not-there were two young men in the shop.
