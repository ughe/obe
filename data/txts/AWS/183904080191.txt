Fifth Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
1396. WILLIAM HODGES was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of
Mareh, 1 saddle, value 58., the goods of Moses Jacobs, his master.
MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
STEPHEN SLAUGHTER. I am in the employ of Moses Jacobs, a glass-
merehant, in Berwick-street, Oxford-street. The prisoner was occasionally
employed as a labourer-my master rents some premises in Rupert-street,
where the horse and cart are usually put upon the 16th of Mareh, the
prisoner was employed to move the cart and some harness from one stable
to another, in Rupert-street, HaymarketI saw a gig saddle and other
things safe in the stable, between six and seven o'clock, when I went to do
up my horse-I went there again the next morning, and they were gone
on the 22nd I came through Smithfield, and saw a lad with my master's
saddle for sale- knew it, and gave the lad into eustody-this is the saddle
-I never authorised any body to take it.
Cross-examined by Mr. CHAMBERS. Q. What name is over Mr. Jacobs
door? A. Jacobs only-his son's name David Jacobs, it is on the cart,
but it is his father's business.
DANIEL SUTHERWOOD. I am a costermonger. I bought this saddle of
the prisoner on Saturday night, three or four weeks back, for 1s. 6d.-I
sent it to Smithfield on the Friday following, to be sold by a boy--it
would not fit my donkey-i has been a pony-saddle--he said he had a
collar and harness to sell.
Cross-examined. Q. Is the boy here A. No--I know it is the saddle
I bought-1 gave it to a boy named Johnny-he works for me.
