JACOB LEVY, 41, Fashion Street. Prisoner works for my brother.
On the day before prisoner was locked up I saw him. My brother
was then away for holiday. Prisoner asked me to give him 25s.
on account of his wages, and I gave him a sovereign and a 58. piece.
When I heard he had been looked up I went to the station and told
them that I had given the 5s. piece to prisoner.
Cross-examined. I know where I got the 5s, from; it was got with
£20 in silver.
The Common Serjeant said that he did not think it was necessary
to call the witnesses to character.
Verdict, Not guilty.

ANNIE RODER, 35, Woolwich Road, Greenwich. On April 8,
prisoner came into our shop between 5.30 and 6, and asked for a
packet of Player's cigarettes (24d.), for which he tendered a 5s. piece,
which was rather smooth and similar to the one produced, but not
quite so dark. I gave it to my husband, who took it into Mr. Higgs
next door to get change. He brought it back and gave it to prisoner
saying, "Do you know what you have got hero1 It is a bad one."
Prisoner looked at it, and said, "Bad" and put it back in his
pocket. He then gave me half sovereign, which I changed.
Cross-examined. I gave prisoner 98.9fd. After tendering the
coin he waited for some time. I could not swear to this being the
same coin. My husband was absent with the coin for about four
minutes; he was also away for about that time when he went to
change the half-sovereign.
