MR. CLARKSON, for the Crown, did not desire to dispute the proposition, that
under the former indictment the prisoner might have been convicted of an
attempt to murder, but that attempt would have amounted only to a misdemeanour,

dently intended to provide for the non-escape of persons charged with felony,

wording of the 11th section, which would have been altogether unnecessary had
