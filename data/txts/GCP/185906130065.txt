617. JAMES SCANES (58), was indicted for feloniously setting fire
to certain buildings in his possession, with intent to defraud the Phoenix
Fire Insurance Company. Other counts, for attempting to set fire to the
said buildings with the like intent.
Mr. TINDAL ATKINSON conducted the Prosecution.
