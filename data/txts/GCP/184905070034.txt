1112. CHARLES HILDITCH was indicted for embezzlement.
Ann North. I paid the prisoner on 2nd April 6s. 6d. for Mr. Hayes,
his master.
CHARLES JAMES Hayes. The prisoner was in my service-it was his duty
to receive money if I sent him for it, and to pay it me in the evening-he has
not paid me this 6s, 6d.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. May he have lost it? 4. When

1113. WILLIAM RADFORD, stealing 2 bunches of artificial flowers,
value 9s. 3d. ; the goods of John Foster and others, his masters.
MR. SLEIGH conducted the Prosecution.
DAVID FORSYTH. I am clerk to John Foster and others, artificial flower-
makers, of Wigmore-street. The prisoner had been porter there some time-
it was part of his duty to dust the boxes-on 25th April, about eight o'clock
in the morning, I saw him meddle with the boxes on the counter-he went
out a short time afterwards-I found in his coat, which was lying on the
counter, these two bunches of artificial flowers ; I sent for Mr. Duncan.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. "Q. What became of the coat? 4.
I left it there; he has it on now.
CHARLES DUNCAN. I am one of the partners of John Foster and Com-
pany. Porter called me—the prisoner returned shortly afterwards-I said to
him, “Is it not too bad, after you have been away three days from your
work, that the first thing you do, you should rob us?"-he said, it was-I
saw the flowers; I identified them.
GUILTY. Aged 30.-Confined Six Months.
