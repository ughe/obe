ARABELLA BUBNELL. I am single, and keep a ready-made linen shop, in Guildford-place, Clerkenwell—the prisoner worked for me about two months—I told her repeatedly to leave, but she would not—she came every day, early in the morning, and opened the shop—I did not like to turn her away—she came in the morning, on Whit Tuesday, the 6th of June, pulled off her shawl, and was still in the shop—I told her I would not have her work for me any longer—about an hour after, while I was serving a customer, she made an excuse to go into the back kitchen, and I followed her—she came back into the shop, and I perceived something under her shawl—I found it was 2 1/4 yards of new calico, which I had seen before on the end of the counter—it is mine—she said it was her sister's—I asked which sister—the said the one in Whiskin-street, that she had it to make a petticoat, and the would go to her sister—I said she should not, and gave her in charge—I know this calico, by a dirty mark or fold that is on it, and can swear to it—there were two duplicates found in her pocket, which led me to three or four pieces of Orleans cloth, which are mine. WILLIAM BURTON. I am a police-inspector. The prisoner was put into my custody. MARY ANN REDMAN. I searched the prisoner at the station—I found on her three duplicates, two of them were for calico and Orleans cloth. WILLIAM CLARK.  1970. WILLIAM YOUNG was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of June , 10lbs. weight of bacon, value 5 s  ., the goods of John Hutley . JOHN JAMES RESTIEAUX( police-constable E  49.) I saw the prisoner in George-street, St. Giles, on Saturday night, the 10th of June, about half-past eight o'clock, with this bacon under his coat—I asked how he came by it—he threw it down, and said he bought it of a strange man in Holborn—I asked him how much it weighed—he said nine pounds—I took him to the station, and found it weighed ten pounds—he was not drunk. BENJAMIN DUNT. I am in the service of John Hutley, a cheesemonger, in Broad-street, St. Giles. On Saturday night I saw the prisoner take hold of this piece of bacon—I asked him if he would buy it—he made no answer—I was busy with several people, and did not miss it till a quarter of an hour after, when the policeman came.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A man offered to sell it me cheap; he said there was nine pounds of it, and he wanted 4 d . a pound for it; he said he had got it from a chandler's-shop; I gave him 3 s . for it, and when the policeman came I dropped it accidentally—I was intoxicated. GUILTY. Aged 37.— Confined One Month .
