April, 1908.
SURREY CASES.
BEFORE THE RECORDER.
Prison
(Wednesday, April 29.)
HARBIGE, Frederick (23, carman), pleaded guilty of breaking and
entering the Wimbledon Steam Laundry and stealing therein one
cheque value £3 and 12 penny stamps, their property.
Sentence, Nine months' hard labour.
FRY, George Morton (37, no occupation), pleaded guilty of obtain-
ing by false pretenees from Ralph Joseph Chapman the sum of £1,
with intent to defraud; on March 9, 1908, obtaining from Albert
Walter Gamage the sum of £4 12s. 4d., with intent to defraud; on
March 10, 1908, obtaining from the said Albert Walter Gamage one
hat, with intent to defraud; on January 8, 1908, obtaining by false
pretences from Arthur Albert Stephens the sum of £5 10s. 5d., the
moneys of Messrs. Ainslie Bros., with intent to defraud.
The statement of counsel disclosed an extensive system of cheque.
frauds. Prisoner was stated to be the son of a gentleman who had
occupied a very high position. In 1889 he joined a dragoon regiment
as lieutenant, but resigned in the following year through debts in-
curred by betting. Subsequently he served with eredit in the Canadian
Mounted Police and was superintendent of police in Northern Nigeria.
He was also stated to be a member of the Geological Society and a
man of considerable attainments, who had been employed in connee-
tion with various mining propositions in Russia and Siberia.
Sentence, Twelve months' hard labour.
