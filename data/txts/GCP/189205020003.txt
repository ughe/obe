461. ALERED JOHN PROUD (47) was indicted for embezzling the
sums of £4 7s. 4d. and £1 18s., received on account of Alexander Malcolm,
his master.RIE DAD
O
A
ME. TRAVERS HUMPHREYS Prosecuted.
ALEXANDER MALCOLA. I am a publisher, and have an office
at 165, Finsbury Pavement, and also at Glasgow-in 1890 the prisoner
was in my employ as manager of the London office his duty was to
appoint and supervise the travellers, and to collect the accounts and send
them on to me at Glasgow, not later than a week-it an account was paid
by cheque it was his duty to send it to Glasgow-he had no authority
to endorse cheques-is was his duty to enter in a cash-book all suns
that he received, and to send each week to Glasgow a sheet copied from
the cash-book-I have here the cash-book for 1890-turning to
21st November, there is no entry of £1 188., received of Mr. E. Lovell on
13th November--there is no entry of £4 78, 4d., received from G. Greenberg
---there is no entry of those two sums anywhere in the cash-book,
nor have I ever received them-I first discovered these defalestions about
September or October last year the balance sheet of 22nd November,
marked "A,"is in the prisoner's handwriting--there is no entry in that of
the $1 188,—that is the sheet sent to me by the prisoner ; exhibit "B" is
& receipt from Lovell's--that is the prisoner's writing, signed by his
initials A. J. P., dated; it is a London account-exhibit "E" is &
cheque for £4 78. 4d. ; the endorsement on that is the prisoner's writing
-exhibit “F” is & receipt given to Mr. Greenberg; that is in the
prisoner's handwriting-he has never accounted for either of these sur
--I have the balance-sheet of 13th November--the $4 78. 4d. has beer
taken out.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. No one is joined with me in this
prosecution—I had a policy with the Guarantee Society; all I recovered
under that policy was about half of what you took ; I claimed under two
heriffo.
