EDWARD GILBERT HIGHTON. I am a barrister, of Bedford Square-on
16th June, in the afternoon, I rode down to the office of "Bell's Weekly
Messenger"-I went into Exeter Street, and gave a man, who I believe to
be the prisoner, my horse to hold, and told him not to take it out of that
part of the street - I was at "Bell's" office ten minutes, and then came out
and found the prisoner in the street, and told him not to take the horse
away, and not to walk him about, as the weather was so warm-I went to
the "Globe" office, and was there ten minutes or a quarter of an hour-
when I came back the prisoner and the horse had disappeared-I after
wards identified the horse ; he was worth fifty guineas-I recovered saddle
and bridle and all.
