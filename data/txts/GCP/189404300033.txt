ALBERT BROCK. I am foreman to Mr. Cohen--on 30th March I was
in the office and saw the prisoner there-I saw him pay a half-crown to
Mr. Cohen; he got it from the other prisoner-Tafterwards saw the
prisoner in conversation with Mr. Smith, the manager, and I heard him
arrange to call for something on a Sunday.
Cross-examined. I had no idea what the thing was that he was to call
for-I formed no idea of its purpose-I saw it, but did not form any idea
of its purpose, until it was all completed.
Re-examined, I suspected then, of course, what it was for ; that it was
for a bomb.
