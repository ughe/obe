JANE YATES, I am the wife of the last witness- I was living at 24,

Cross-examined. Q. How long did this roll of carpet remain in your
house? 4. From the Saturday night to the next morning-it was a good
while ago ; I cannot say the month-it was in April, May, or June, as near
as I can guess I think it was last year-I did not live at 24, Wheeler

have only been to me to bring me summonses- I have not seen them besides
- I have certainly spoken to them- dozen policemen have not been talk
ing to me--I bad a summons from Lambert, and another from Baldwin-

whether I knew Everett when they took bim-they have not asked me what
my evidence was-nobody examined me before I went before the Magistrate
---Ro policemen asked me what I could say before I went up-I was sitting
