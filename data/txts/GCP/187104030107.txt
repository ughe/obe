her she would have to accompany me to the station-she went towards the
window, and took something from her bosom- I took it from her hand it
was a purse, a sovereign, a half-sovereign, and sixpence-I asked what she
meant by this; she made no answer—at the station she said " There was a
fight in the room, and the money dropped out, and I picked it up and kept
it " I searched the place, but did not find any of the clothes--they have
not been traced-I have frequently seen the prisoners together they had
lived at this house for six weeks or two months - the female was not in her
own room when I took her, but in another room.
