286 EMMERSON MATHEWS (56), Unlawfully obtaining from
John Fraser Donald, an order for E35 with intent to defraud.
Mr. ATTENBOROUGH Prosecuted and MR. O'CONNOR Defended.
JOHN FRABER DONALD. Iam a surveyor, in partnership at Twickenham
in April, 1901, the prisoner used to come to my office and to my house
at Wimbledon-he said that he was a surveyor in the service of the
Great Western Railway employed by them in buying land, and that he
could get me a situation of E580 odd-the showed me some documents in
Vuly-these (Produced) are some of them-I did not read them-he said
that they represented 38,000 stock saw big figures on them, some
thousands of pounds-he asked me to take care of them for found
him an envelope and he put them in and wrote on them in blue pencil
Mathews, Bank of England" -these application forms are in his
writing-they are filled up by him to the best of my belief-on August
3rd we met at Twickenham and walked to the King' Head at Richmond
where he said that he wanted E35 to pay the men-I gave him this open
cheque (Produced) for E35-he did not endorse it in my presence, but
this endorsement is his signature-it was afterwards paid by my bankers
-on August 9th he came to me again and wanted another cheque for
$67 for the same purposeI went to a bank in Richmond which cashed
it, and it was afterwards debited to my account at my bank-on December
2nd I went with him to Putney, add gave him this cheque for E100 on
the London and South Western Bank, Putney, and he cashed it in my
presence-o September 5th I met him at Wandsworth, and he wanted
three separate cheques for the same purpose gave him these three
cheques (Produced -they bear his endorsement, and my bankers paid
them on October 15th-he ealled to see me at Wandsworth, he said that
he was going to Reading and had not quite sufficient money, and asked if
I would send some if he wired for it-I said Yes," and got a telegram,
and sent him this money order for E5-he said that he wanted the
money, as the Company would not settle with him till September 26thI
also gave him other money-I believed his statement about his being a
surveyor paid by the railway, I would not have advanced the money if I
had not-he came to me and wanted the documents back--I do not
know the date-I was to be paid out of them-I gave him back the
security-| have never been paid he kept putting it off from day to day.
