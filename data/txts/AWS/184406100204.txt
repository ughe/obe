WILLIAM Burns. I am clerk to Joshua Walker and others, lead-merchants

best of my knowledge.
THoMAs WATKINs (policeman.) I got this paper from Mr. Middleditch.
CHARLES MIDDLEDITCH. I got this paper from the head managing man at

WILLIAM Burns re-examined. I took it off the file, and gave it to clerk,
who handed it to Middlediteh, in my presence- could not swear that re-
ceived that paper from the prisoner-- have such a number of wagons, carts,

no recollection of putting that particular order on the file-I saw a cart in the

verbal order to the warebouseman to deliver the goods to the parties.
THOMAs SHILLAM. I am warehouseman in the white-lead department at
Messrs. Walker's. On the 11th of May, I received a verbal order from Burns
to pack two firkins of lead-I packed it-and noticed a cart backed up to
the warehouse gates-the prisoner had charge of it-I asked bim whether he
came for pipe or paint-he said for paint-white lead means paint in the
trade- asked him who it was for --he said for Mr. Middleditch-] saw the
two firkins craned into the cart- weighed them, and gave the weight out on
the slate-I am sure the prisoner is the person-there was nobody else with
the cart-he received the goods.
Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. You had never seen him be-
