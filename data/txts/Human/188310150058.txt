( This contained the statement, "I have had 140 l . of your money, which I owe you")—I went to the bank on the 29th and was shown these three deposit notes—the signature on this first for 150 l ., "Otto Stoeckius," is not in my writing—I did not authorise the prisoner or any one else to sign it for me, and I had no knowledge that he had done so—I did not write the signatures on these others for 100 l . or 10 l ., nor authorise them to be written—with his letter there was returned to me by the prisoner this deposit note for 10 l .—140 l . had been taken out of the bank and 10 l . left.
   Cross-examined.
