SAMUEL HERAPATH. I live on Holborn-hill. I saw the prisoner in
conversation with some women passing Mr. Thornhill's shop, on the 19th
of November--the women did not go into the shop the prisoner
was in conversation with about three or four females, and as they went
on they turned up Plumbtree-court-the prisoner immediately went to
the prosecutor's door, looked to see if he was watched, and took three
brushes from the door, and ran off with them—he ran from the door
up the court-I ran out against him, and he dropped the brushes im-
mediately-I ran after him, calling, “Stop thief!" he ran ap the court,
and then across the street.
JOA
John BURDEN. I am shopman to Benjamin Thornhill of Holborn-hill.

door--they were brought back the same evening.
FREDERICK PRINCE. I am a policeman. I produce the brushes.
Prisoner's Defence. I was intoxicated, and did it in a frolic--they
hung outside the door-I just gave them a chuck up-the witness called
“Stop thief" - I ran away.
