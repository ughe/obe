   Cross-examined by MR. LANGFORD . Q.  What did you say when you first went into Clements' house? A.  Inspector Bond told him that he understood he had some stolen property there—Bond was in the house before me; he went in at the front-door and admitted me at the back-door. JOHN BOND( Police inspector, B ).
