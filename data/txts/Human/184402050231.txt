 809. DANIEL HENDERSON and JAMES TURNER were indicted for stealing 180lbs. weight of lead, value 1 l  .; 3 s  ., the goods of Augustns Ray mond , in a certain   wharf, adjacent to the Thames . MR. CUR WOOD conducted the Prosecution. HAMOND JOBN GRIFFIN( Thames police-constable, No.  33.) On the 13th of Jan. a man came and spoke to me, and in consequence of what he said I went into the shop of Bourne, a marine-store dealer, and found the prisoners with some lead—v told Henderson I wanted him—he said, "What for?"—I said, "The pig of lead"—they were standing before the counter, apparently in the act of rolling it—the dealer was in the act of looking at it—I called my brother officer in, and gave them into custody, and I carried the lead—they said they picked the lead up on Church-stairs shore.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  Who gave you the informa tion? A.  I do not know—it was a man in Wapping Dock—he did not tell me where the men got the lead—I was going by, and he spoke to me. GEOROE BOURNE, marine-store dealer, Green Bank, Wapping.  Between ten and eleven o'clock at night I was called down, and told somebody wanted to sell some lead—my shop was shut—when I got down I saw two persons in the shop—I cannot say whether they were the prisoners—Idirectly after wards saw two Thames police-officers—the two persons were taken—there was some lead in a scale on my counter—I cannot say who put it in—my wife let the men into the shop, and called me down—I cannot swear whether it was such a pig of lead as this that was in the scale—I would not swear to it.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Was your wife in the shop? A.  No, she came down when the knock came, and she directly called me—I had no sooner got down than two police-officers came into the shop—I expect my wife did not put this lead into the scale—I saw nobody put it into the scale—this was be tween ten and eleven o'clock on Saturday night. JOHN ATWELLS. In Jan. I was in the employ of Mr. Mead Terry Ray mond; his brother's name is Augustus. My master's is the Thames Tunnel Wharf, Rotherhithe—I have the charge of the property on that wharf—on the night of the 13th of Jan. there were between 700 and 800 pigs of lead on the wharf, with one mark on them—I believe this pig produced to be one of them—I missed one on the Sunday morning which I know was there on the Sa turday night—I found a small door open at the end of the shed, but I did not miss the pig till the policeman came—Henderson is a lighterman—I have known him some years—he knows our premises—Turner was employed in watermen's boats, and I have employed him myself for a short time—he knew our premises.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How long have you known Henderson? A.  Four or five years—he was always a hard-working man, and honest, as far as I know.
