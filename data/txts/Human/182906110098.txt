 1223.  THOMAS JEFFERYS was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of April , 24lbs. weight of beef, value 6s., the goods of Thomas Mendy.
   THOMAS MENDY. I am a butcher, and live in Brick-lane. On the 24th of April I saw the prisoner take this beef from my counter, as I was in the adjoining room to my shop - he threw a handkerchief over it, and ran away with it; I pursued and overtook him - when he saw me, he threw it down and ran away.
   JOSEPH DYSON. I stopped the prisoner about threehundred yards from the spot; a young man came up and said he had stolen a piece of beef - I took him back to Mr. Mendy.
 Three witnesses gave the prisoner a good character, and one of them engaged to employ him. GUILTY. Aged 20.
 Recommended to Mercy by the Jury. Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 1224.  PETER JORDAN and CHARLES BESSANT were indicted for stealing, on the 1st of June , 1 carpet bag, value 8s., the goods of Thomas Lane and Allen Billing.
 GEORGE JUDGE. On the 1st of June, about eight o'clock in the evening, I was standing near Waterloo-bridge - I saw the two prisoners follow a gentleman, and then they passed on, and went to the prosecutors' shop; Jordan made an attempt to take a trunk, which he could not do - they then turned back and went to the shop again; I lost sight of them for about two minutes - we then crossed the way and went on to Somerset-house, looked down a turning, and saw them tying up something in a handkerchief; they came up, and I took Jordan, who had this bag under his left arm - Bessant ran off, and Fogg took him.
 Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. You did not see them take the bag? A. No; I saw them attempting a gentleman's pocket before - they then went and tried another gentleman's pocket; I was on the other-side of the way.
 THOMAS FOGG. I was with Judge in the Strand. I saw the prisoners together for about an hour; I know the prosecutors' shop - I saw Jordan stand against it, and Bessant was opposite; I then saw them going towards the Strand - Jordan had something under his arm; they went down a turning which leads to the Adelphi - we came up to them, and Judge took this bag from Bessant.
   JOHN BRODERICK. I am shopman to Thomas Lane and Allen Billing, trunk-makers. This is their bag; I did not miss it till the next morning - I had seen it safe at eight o'clock in the evening; I left at nine o'clock - the officer came after I was gone; we had only one of this pattern.
 Cross-examined. Q. Is there a private mark on it? A. No; I know it by hanging it up so often - it is not an uncommon pattern; the workmanship makes me positive it is the same - they are made by women; the handle of this is worn away by hanging it up so many times - I was thinking I should clean it up; it is what we call a halfyard bag, the next size would be a half-ell - our shop is very long.
 JORDAN'S Defence. I was passing the Strand on the 1st of June, and met a person I knew, who had this bundle - we went and had a pint of beer; he asked me to hold the bundle while he went on one side, and the officer came and took me; I never saw this prisoner before.
 JORDAN -    GUILTY. Aged 24.
 BESSANT -    GUILTY. Aged 23. Transported for Seven Years.
 1225,  ROBERT MURRAY was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of May , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Richard Richardson.
 RICHARD RICHARDSON. On the 7th of May I lost a handkerchief, as I was at the back of the Islington stage; it had been in my inside coat pocket - I did not miss it till I got home; I know I had it when I was at the Royal Exchange, at four o'clock - I afterwards saw it in the possession of the officer.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   GEORGE ANTHONY. On the afternoon of the 7th of May, I saw the Islington stage go by - the prisoner was behind it; he put his hand into the gentleman's pocket and took out this handkerchief - I went and took it from him and gave it to Mr. Powell, who put it into his pocket, and went to get the officer; I know this to be the handkerchief.
   THOMAS COPE. I took the prisoner. This handkerchief was given me by Mr. Powell - the prisoner told me he had been drawing the handkerchief, and this other handkerchief was bound round his arm; he said it was a hard case that he should be taken alone, for Charley Purley was with him - and as we were going along the fields he said" There's Charley Purley;" I went and took him, but he was discharge.   GUILTY. Aged 14. Transported for Seven Years.
 1226.  CORNELIUS MAHONEY was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of May , 4 half-crowns, 2 shillings, and 8 sixpences, the monies of Luke Brunton.
 LUKE BRUNTON. I am a  shoemaker, and live in York-street, Saffron-hill. On the morning of the 11th of May I went to settle with the prisoner and another of my men - there was only 1s. coming to him - I then went to my clothes in my box, and went away leaving the prisoner in the room - I had left my money in the box on the Saturday night, and when I went out that day, I left the key of the box on the bed - before I returned, word was sent after me that the money was gone; I came home and missed four half-crowns, some shillings, and sixpences - the prisoner had seen the box - it was where I kept my clothes - I was advised to go to Mr. Spooner, who told me to go in quest of the prisoner - I found him in a public-house intoxicated: I sent for an officer, who took him - I then went to Mr. Phillips, and then to a baker's where he had left part of the money - he had left his own old hat at Mr. Phillips; this is it.
