time to see as to the connexion of Cohen, and he subsequently called and
expressed his satisfaction; the deed was then executed in duplicate-some
short time after, Benjamin called upon me alone and represented that the
connexion of Cohen was perfectly satisfactory to him and that they were
doing very well indeed, that, in fact, they had scarcely touched the capital,
having been enabled by the discounting of Cohen's customer's bills to con-
tinue their trading-by the sixth paragraph of the deed a capital of 20001.
was to be provided-Benjamin was to bring in 10001., and the stock-in-trade,

marked A-I asked for it at the time of the execution of the deed, and I
was told by both that it had been left at their warehouse.
