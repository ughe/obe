FRANCIS LAMBURN GREENLEY re-examined.  All the things found in the trunk are my property. GEORGE MILLARD. I am apprentice to Mr. Greenley. I saw Mar garet Lawrence at my master's sale-room a fortnight or three weeks before she was taken into custody—she came and bought some bed-furniture—I did not miss anything after she was gone—she came three or four times in the course of the day after she had the bed-furniture—that amounted to 17 s . 6 d .
   Cross-examined. Q.  Can you undertake to swear that it was not before she brought the bed-furniture that she came two or three times? A.  That I cannot say—I swear I saw her after she brought the bed-furniture.

 632. MARGARET LAWRENCE was again  indicted for stealing 1 um brella, value 3 s  .; 1 petticoat, 6 d  .; and 1 waistcoat, 3 s  .; the goods of Francis Lamburn Greenley . FRANCIS LAMBURN GREENLEY. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Broom field-place, Deptford. On the 21st of Jan., about a quarter past five, my apprentice came to me in my private house and I went into my sale room—I heard the prisoner talking to the man about buying a petticoat—after some time I asked her where the petticoat was that she had stolen—she said she had not stolen a petticoat—I told her she had, and it was secreted under the child's clothes that she had in her arms, and I should give her into custody—I then saw the handle of an umbrella—I asked her where she got that—she said she had had it three or four years—I sent for an officer, and before he came she dropped a waistcoat—I gave her into custody—the things have all my mark on them, and they have never been sold. GEORGE HAY( police-constable R  91.) I took the prisoner and produce these articles. GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Six Months .
