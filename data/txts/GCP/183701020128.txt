449. PHILIP FULCHER was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of
December, 1 spoke shave-iron, value 9d.; and 1 saw set, value 18.; the
goods of Thomas Gudgeon, his master; and one spoke-shave, value It,
the goods of Joseph Gudgeon.
THOMAS GUDGEON. I am a wheelwright. I lost some tools about the
31st of December--the prisoner had been my journeyman for two week
- this iron and saw-set are mine-I had used this iron within ten
days.
SARAH BENNETT. I live at Lower Edmonton. The prisoner lodged
with me-these things were found in the room he slept in- I did not see
him bring them to the house.
RICHARD CARTER. I went to Mrs. Bennett's and found these things
in the prisoner's room.
JOSEPH GUDGEON. I lost this spoke-shave from the shop in the latter
part of the week.
Prisoner's Defence. They are my own-I have had them ever since I
was at the lodging,
