MR. SLEIGH conducted the Prosecution, and MR. Coopgr the Defence.
FREDERIOK BENJAMIN. I am a gentleman living on my private fortune,
at 34, Gloucester-gardens, Hyde-park-I received a letter, about the 14th
of May, by post-this is it (produced)-in consequence of the receipt of
that letter, wbich requested me to send a 5l note to a certain place, I
placed myself in communication with the police within half an hour of the
receipt of it-in consequence of what they said, I put a bl note in an
envelope, and directed it to "A. A., 5, Connaught-terrace"-I know nothing
of the prisoner-I discharged a female servant rather more than twelve
months ago there is not one iota of truth in the vile imputation contained
in this letter.
Cross-examined. There was a young woman in your service A. -
Yes--I have a wife-my wife gave her notice to leave, and she left at the
end of the monthshe was dismissed by me for some improper conduct on
her part that is the reason I had my suspicions. I have nothing to show
that this man knew her, or she this man I know nothing of the prisoner,
except that he is an old cab-driver.
