HERMAN DEMSOAL. I am hall-porter at the Dramatic Club, 133, Upper
Street, Islington--I have seen the prisoner at the club three or
four times-on Friday, 25th, he left the club between one and two R.m.
with a young woman who had called for him.
PAUL FRANZ. I am the proprietor of the Dramatic Club-the prisoner
hrs been a member since September 4th-I remember his wife coming on
the night of September 25th-I asked her inside to see if her friend was
there-when I went with her into the club the prisoner was there-he
did not see me; he was sitting with his back to the door-she left after
being there about a minute.
Cross-ecamined. Members of the club pay half-u-crown a quarter
have no licence to sell spirits and wines which I sell to members--there
are 500 members--no women, but women come in with members--we keep
open as late as three or four a.m.- very seldom tili five a.m. singing and
dancing take place amongst the members --- women come in if respectable
---there is a committee-women are admitted without being with
members--the police have not visited the club openly-the Excise have
visited it-I have been convicted four times—women of the town do not
call and ask for men.
