Cross-examined. I am a broker. I am not in any employment.
I am an agent for different merchants in Antwerp. I know of Klein;
he is a diamond merchant. I do not know of any Klein who is a

polishers. He has two or three people who take their goods from
him. I pay in Antwerp the price charged for the stones which I
am entrusted with. I pay with my own cheques and sometimes give
customers' cheques. I have authority to sell property in this country
and to give an invoice to the purchaser. I had to give an invoice
in my name. I very seldom effect sales on credit. I do, by commis-
sion, with the seller. I have authority to effect sales on credit if I
think it is right. I come over every Tuesday and return on Satur-
