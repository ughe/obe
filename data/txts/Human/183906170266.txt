GEORGE POWELL I saw the prisoner in the barrack-room after the Queen's birth-day, and while he was on parade I saw his cartridge, between the rugs of his bed—he was not present when I saw them—I do not. know whether they leave their ball cartridges behind when they go to fire blank cartridge, but he had left his behind—there were five in each packet, complete and regular as made up—he had left them between the rug, of his bed—there were two parcels, five in each, perfect and regularly made up.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How was it you were not on parade? A.  I was not a duty man—the men sleep in separate beds—I was cooking, and cleaning up the room, and saw this projecting out, and I found these package, one on the top of the other—I look the top one off, and put it down by the side, that it should not project so much. Q.  Did you count the cartridges? A.  I felt them with my finger—his pouch was not there—there was nothing at all remarkable in the appear ance of the cartriage—I could feel them with my finger, and the other package was the same size—I caught them up in my hand to pull the top one off—all I did was to put them out of the position they were in, that the bed might be put right, but I could see there were five in each—I did not open the packages, but I counted them outside—I could see them—they presented the usual appearance-nothing induced me to notice them—I did not see any other man's cartridge in his bed—I did not untie the packages, but I saw there were five in each—there was nothing outside the package to show the number inside, that I know of—( the package was here produced. )
   COURT . Q.  is that the common appearance of the cartridge? A.  Yes—they laid one upon the other, and I took the top one off, and put it by MR. ADOLPHUS . Q.
