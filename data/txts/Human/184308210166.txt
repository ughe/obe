 2427. JOHN INCH was again  indicted for stealing, on the   5th of August , 1 coat, value 10 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of John Wells . WILLIAM KIBBLE. I am shopman to John Wells, a pawnbroker, in Broad-street, Bioomsbury. On the 5th of August I missed a coat, which I had seen safe about an hour before—I saw the prisoner scuffling with a man about two hundred yards from my master's—I went up, took the coat from bim, and gave him in charge—when he got about fifty yards, he slipped out of his coat, and got away from the policeman, and was not taken till a week after—this is the coat—it is my master's.
   Prisoner.  I was not the person. Witness.  I took it from him—I have not the least doubt of him, as a week before he was in our shop redeeming some articles. GUILTY. † Aged 18.— Transported for Seven Years .
 2428. ANDREW MARTIN was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of August , 1 glazed window sash, value 6 s  . the goods of Thomas M'Carthy , and fixed to a certain building; against the Statute, &c. THOMAS GRIFFITH. I am in the service of Thomas M'Car thy, and live in Jubilee-place, Chelsea. He bought some green-houses, and I was employed to pull them down—I missed a frame and glass—I found a policeman, and went with him to the prisoner's house, in Simmonds-street—I found the prisoner on the works, in the same ground where the frame was taken from—I found the frame at the head of the prisoner's bed—he went there with us.
   Cross rexamined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Have you any doubt it was the sash? A.  None—he said if I would forgive him he would give up the sash—I then went to his house and got it.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY  . Aged 46.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined One Month .
 2429. WILLIAM WALKER was indicted for stealing, on the   28th of July , 2 boots, value 6 s  ., the goods of Joseph Hobbs . JOSEPH HOBBS. About eight o'clock in the evening of the 28th of July I was in my shop, and saw the prisoner looking in at the window—I watched, and saw him come to the door—I heard two boots drop—I ran after him, and I found two odd boots on him—he left the other two in the shop where he cut them down.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was coming along Shad well about nine o'clock at night, and saw a man chuck  them into the road; I picked them up. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Four Months .
