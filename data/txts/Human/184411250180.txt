No, there were other persons lodged there—a woman lived in the room with the prisoner—she has been since then sent to Maid stone—the prisoner was not there at the time of the search—I had seen him there about a week before. ELIZABETH CURTIS. The prisoner and Margaret Ford lodged with me in June last as man and wife—I was present when these waistcoats were found in the prisoner's bed-room—when the officer came, he insisted on the prisoner's room door being opened, and I got a key and opened it—the prisoner had slept there the night before, but I did not see him after nine o'clock that morning.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What room did the prisoner and Ford live in ✗ your house? A.  They lived in the room at the top of the house first, and then I removed my own things down—I did not remove his things at all—he came in late sometimes—Ford was taken into custody on the 17th of July, and when she was taken the prisoner went away—he was not there when the waistcoats were found—I had seen the waistcoats, and lifted them out from under the bed the day before.
   COURT . Q.  The prisoner came in late, did he? A.  Yes—my health was bad, I removed him down to the lower part of the house, and he was satisfied. JOSEPH GREEN( police-sergeant R  315.) I took the prisoner at New cross—I told him ✗ wanted him for a robbery at Mr. Sharp's—he said, "Very well, I will go with you quietly, and have what I am to have, and have done with it." NOT GUILTY.
 163. THOMAS BAULSOM was again  indicted for stealing, on the   16th of July , 2 waistcoats, value 7 s  ., the goods of Joseph Sharp . JOHN EVERDEN. I am in the employ of Joseph Sharp; here are two waistcoats, which he lost in the early part of June. On the 16th of July, Margaret Ford brought them to our pawn-shop—our sale-shop is divided from the pawn-shop, and they had been stolen from the sale-shop—we let Ford go, and then I went out and saw the prisoner on the opposite side of the road—I asked him whether these waistcoats belonged to him—he said, "Yes," and he bought them at a clothier's in Deptford, whom he denominated Cheap Jack  —these are my master's waistcoats.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  There was the private mark on one of. them, was there not? A.  Yes, and the mark had been cut out of the other—our two shops adjoin each other. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Three Months.
 164. ROBERT ENDSOR was indicted for stealing 1 work-box, value 3 s  .; the goods of Everard Banks . EVERARD BANKS. I keep a shop in Nelson-street, Greenwich. On the 12th of Nov. I had a work-box—I saw it safe between three and seven o'clock—I heard some one cry out, and I missed the box—I cannot swear to this box, but I believe it to be mine, and I had one exactly like it on the top of the pile.
