 1271. JOSEPH BARNARD LAFLEUR was indicted for stealing 1 watch, value 20 l  .; 1 watch-chain, 20 s  .; and 1 seal, 5 s  .; the goods of Jules Joseph Perrot .
 (The prisoner being a foreigner had the evidence interpreted to him.) JULES JOSEPH PARROT( through an interpreter. ) I live in Lower James-street, Golden-square—the prisoner has been in the habit of coming to me to beg for money—about a quarter before two o'clock on the 1st of April I had a gold watch, a chain, and key on the chimney-piece in the room—the prisoner came and begged for a pair of trowsers and a waistcoat—I went to my bed-room to fetch them, and left him is the parlour alone—I came back in about half a minute and found him close to the chimney—I gave him the trowsers and the waistcoat, and he went away—when I dressed I went to the chimney-piece and my watch was gone—there had been no one else there, and I saw the watch safe about five minutes before he came.
   Prisoner.  He could see in the second room where I was standing. Witness.  No, I had to go in a closet to get the clothes—I am a ballet master.
   Prisoner's Defence.  The policeman said Mr. Perrot wanted me; I said I would call on him to-morrow; I went with the policeman to the house, and not finding him at home I said I would return at ten o'clock to-morrow; the policeman followed me and arrested me. NOT GUILTY.

 1273. JAMES MARKS was indicted for stealing 2 firkins, value 3 s  .; and 561b. weight of fat, 3 s  .; the goods of James Ray Elliott , his master.
   MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution. JAMES RAT ELLIOTT. oilman, Edgware-road.  The prisoner was in my em ploy for about six months—on the 16th of March, from information from my apprentice, I sent for the officer and gave the prisoner in custody for robbing me—he said, "Me rob you; this is all through you, Dusty  meaning Mead, another servant—I went with the officer and Mead to No. 6, Little Earl-street—Mead pointed out the closet in the front room—I there found two firkins and a cloth, and in the cloth was a small piece of fat—the large firkin I knew to be mine, and the other I believe to be mine.
