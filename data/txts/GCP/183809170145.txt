2252. MARY ESOUM and SARAH GALLAWAY were indicted
for stealing, on the 30th of August, 8 sheets, value 21. ; 1 petticoat, value
26. 6d.; and 3 stocks, value 68.; the goods of William Cormack.
Bye Hackney. I am servant to William Cormack, nurseryman, New
Cross, Deptford-here are eight sheets, & petticoat, and three stocks-
they belong to my master-I had placed them on the grass-plat to bleach,
opposite the front of the house, about eleven o'clock on the morning of the
30th of August-they were gone between five and six.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Could this lawn be seen from the
Windows? 4. Yes-Mr. Cormack was not at home-I know them by the
marks.
