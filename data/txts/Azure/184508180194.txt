you call my wife a w— again, I will knock you word occurred
again, and then the prisoner raised bis arm, and the man fell—I assisted

he said, I hope I have said, You bad better run fora doctor,"

Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Might not the deceased have
stepped back to avoid the blow, and have stumbled? A. I cannot say—
they were tipsy—I saw the prisoner's arm raised—whether it was a push
or a blow I cannot tell.
