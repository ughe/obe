 511.  ABRAHAM ABERCROMBY was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of February , 1 ring, value 11s., the goods of James Sodo , from his person.
 JAMES SODO. I am a  hat-manufacturer, and live in Bethnal-green - on the 14th of February, about ten minutes past eleven at night, this happened - we had had part of two glasses of gin and water among six of us - I was coming across Hare-street Fields, and saw the prisoner talking to two females - he said, "How do you do, Sir?" - I said, "How do you do?" and put out my hand to shake hands with him- I had a ring on my finger, and he slipped it off - I told him not to take it off - he said he had got no ring, but he handed it to a female - I seized him, and handed him to the officer.
 Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. Did not you take him to be a friend? A. I thought at first he was one of my customers - I could not keep my ring on, for he is stronger than I am.
   JOSHUA HARCOURT(police-constable H 51). I was called, and the prosecutor had the prisoner by the collar - he said the prisoner had taken a ring from his finger - there were two women near him - the prosecutor had been drinking, but knew what he was about.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you see the prosecutor collar the prisoner? A. No; he said afterwards, that the prisoner gave the ring to one of the women; it has not been found.
   ELEANOR FLACK. On the night of this robbery, I met the prosecutor at Three Coldt-corner - he greatly insulted me, and followed to a house where I was going - I asked him what he meant, and he put his hands in my face- I told him he took great liberties - I could not get into the house, where I was going to sleep, and the prisoner came up, and the prosecutor gave charge of him - he had no ring on when he put his hands in my face.
 COURT. Q. Was any body with you? A. No - I am not married - I am in the workhouse - the prosecutor was very tipsy.    NOT GUILTY.

 512.  BENJAMIN WINCH was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of January , 1 saddle-tree, value 3s. 6d., the goods of John Asbury.
 JOHN NORMAN. I am a sadler, and live in Milton-street - on the 18th of January, the prisoner brought me a saddle-tree for sale, and asked 1s. 6d. for it - I asked him how he came by it, as I knew he was Mr. Reynolds's, the undertaker's servant - he said he had lived with his master a long time - I bought the saddle of him, and afterwards sent for his master, and found out the owner.
   JOSEPH ASBURY. This is my saddle-tree - it was taken out of my shop, in White-street, Little Moorfields- I am a sadler- I knew the prisoner, as his master is a customer of mine - on the 18th of January, he came to my shop with some harness to be repaired, and went away; and about an hour and a half after he was gone, a person came and asked if I had lost anything, and I found the saddle-tree at Norman's.
 Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. When did you see it last? A. The day it was stolen - my shop-window was not open - it did not hang outside the shop.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Confined Six Weeks.
 513.  ELIZA DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of February , 1 handkerchief, value 3s.; the goods of William George Robinson.
   HENRY GRIFFIN. I am a linen and woollen draper - on the morning of Wednesday, the 19th of February, I was on Ludgate-hill, returning from the theatre, about two o'clock in the morning - the gas lamps were burning - it was a very light night - I was walking with the prosecutor, who had been to the theatre with me - as we returned home we saw the prisoner; she made up to us, and took hold of the prosecutor's arm - I pushed her away - she came a second time - I pushed her away again - she came a third time - I threatened to give her in charge - the prosecutor felt and missed his handkerchief, and charged her with it - she said he might search her - he felt her bosom, and as she turned round I saw the handkerchief drop from her - he recognised it as his property - it dropped from her right hand.
 WILLIAM GEORGE ROBINSON. I am a  farmer- I was with Griffin - the prisoner accosted us two or three times and took hold of my arm - I had my handkerchief in my left hand pocket - I felt her take it out - I turned round and charged her with it - she denied it, and said something about searching her - I saw her drop it, at least I saw it before it got on the ground, close to her.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. It is false - the gentleman put his hand into his pocket on my leaving him, and his friend said,"Have you all your property safe?" - he said, "I do not know" - he looked down, and his friend said, "You have got his handkerchief" - I said "Search me," and then he said, "Oh there it is" - his friend had three or four handkerchiefs in his pocket, and in pulling them out I suppose he must have dropped it.
   WILLIAM GEORGE ROBINSON re-examined. I had two or three handkerchiefs in my pocket, but very different ones to this; they were not all in the same pocket - there was nobody near us - I had had a glass or two of liquor, but knew what I was about.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. - Transported for Seven Years.
