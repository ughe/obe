The indictment containing a variety of Counts charging a conspiracy with reference to two fires, one in June and one in November,  MR. BARON MARTIN  , after hearing MR. BOVILL  and MR. GIFFARD  , ruled that the prosecution must elect upon which conspiracy they would proceed, as he would not receive evidence having reference to more than one. The Prosecution, therefore, elected to proceed upon those counts relating to the fire in November. FRANCIS PERRY.
