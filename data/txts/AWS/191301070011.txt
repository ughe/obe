IRONS, William (30, carman), was oharged on Coroner's inquisition
with the manslaughter of William Trevatt.
Mr. D. J. Lewis prosecuted Mr. Warburton defended.
The Grand Jury had ignored a Bill for manslaughter and the prose
cution now offered no evidence on the inquisition. The death of Trevatt
arose fr m a fall in the course of a fight between him and prisoner, in
which Trevatt was the aggressor, and on the evidence there had been
nothing unfair on prisoner's part.
Verdict, Not guilty.
