 Police-constable CHARLES PELL, 266 C. On the morning of Novem ber 19 last I made a plan of the scene of the accident. The motor bus was still in Park Lane, opposite Dudley House. The railings between Hyde Park and Park Lane were broken down to the extent of about 11 ft. 6 in. I could not say what damage the motor-bus had sustained as I did not examine it. The front of the motor-bus was right up against the coping of the railings. The width of the road there is 41 ft. JOHN WILLIAM DOVE, four-wheeled cab-driver, badge number 14941.
