JOHN FIDDES (Glasgow Policeman D 54). On Wednesday, 7th Sep-

WILLIAM SIMPSON. I am a labourer at Glasgow-on 12th September
I was in the police-station at Glasgow charged with an assault-I was
neither drunk nor sober-I was put in a cell; the prisoner was in the
cell the worse for drink-we two were in the cell all night; we slept
ourselves sober-he had no shirt on-I asked him what he had done with
his shirt-he said "Coming down in the train I had a fight with a man
and he tore my shirt; I had two shirts on and I took them off and threw
them out of the carriage window because they were torn”-he said he had
come from Sydney as a stowaway, and that his father lived at Hamilton
-on 1st October I read in a newspaper an account of the death of John
King- I then went to the police- I was discharged by the Magistrate
next morning.
Cross-examined. He told me that when he got to Glasgow he did not
know whether he had any money or not.
