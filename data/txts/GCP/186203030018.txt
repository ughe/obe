Prisoner's Defence. I am innocent of stealing the watoh. I received it
from Mr. Benjamin in the coffee-shop in Eagle-street. At the time the
gentleman came for the watches Mr. Benjamin was busy, and said, “I cannot
come over myself, I will send some one over." I was in there taking some
coffee, and he asked me if I would go over with it. I said, “Yes." The first
watch I took over was a large gold one, as he told me, and he told me to ask
71. for it-it did not suit the gentlemen, and he gave me another-that did
not suit, and I took another, which I was to ask 31. for; the gentlemen said
he would give 50s. I took it back and told Mr. Benjamin about it, and he
said, "Go back, and take the 50s.," which I did, and I immediately returned
to the coffee-shop, and gave it to Mr. Benjamin, and he gave me half-a-crowa
for my trouble.
