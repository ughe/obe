Joan VALE(City police-constable, No. 65.) I was called, and took the
prisoner---Mrs. Dalman said if she would return the change she would not

she said, " They have got the half-crown, and I have got the change!
should be a fool to give good money for bad-it is only one piece, and they
cannot hurt me for that."
