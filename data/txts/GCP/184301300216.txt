Q. When had you any suspicion of anything wrong ?-Was it when you
heard something read out in your presence by Mr. Birch ? A. Yes; my
suspicions were first attracted by that circumstance I cannot fix the date of
that-it was after the 5th of Nov. ; I think the beginning of Dec.; I cannot
say exactly-it was on a Friday, either the first or second Friday in Dec.-I
had a bill against the executors of Mr. Constable for my attendance on him,
I think it was about 301.--that has not been paid--I have requested pay.
ment of it, I have requested payment from Miss Constable herself, as execu-
trix under the will-I first requested payment from her a few weeks, perhaps
a month, after her father's death, when she was getting together the bills that
were due-I afterwards applied to the prisoner, before I knew of the exist-
ence of this deed-it was about the 6th or 7th of Nov. that I first applied to
him-I have seen him once or twice-I have applied to bim verbally and
otherwise, perhaps, three or four times I wrote to him after I heard the
affidavit read of the existence of this deed.
MR. CLARKSON. Q. Did you make any application to him after you knew
of this deed, except in writing? 1. No.
MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Had you and he any personal dispute about it?
A. None at all-when I applied to him in writing I did not get my money,
or any answer-it was not then for the first time that I mentioned my sus-
picions to anybody-I had mentioned them to Mr. Hale before-that was
after my endeavours to get the bill from the prisoner--when I first applied
to the prisoner I did not know of the existence of this deed -I knew of Stan-
ley's death, and had read of Stanley's will, in Doctors' Commons, leaving his
property to his nephews and pieces--I had no dispute with the prisoner about
a picture, I requested him to give me one, which Mr. Constable had presented
me with in his lifetime.
Q. What made you apply to the prisoner for Mr. Constable's picture ? A.
