25. 9d., the goods of George Young.

John Burns. I am a fruit salesman in Covent-garden. The prisoner

was with me from fifteen to sixteen years, off and on-he received 11. 78. 6d.
of me, to pay Mrs. Almond, which he did not do.

him again till he was in custody.
