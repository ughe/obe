The JURY , after having retired for a quarter of an hour, desired to desired to return verdict of
   "GUILTY of killing his wife, but without premeditation."
   MR. JUSTICE BYLES informed them that he could not receive a verdict in that form. After further retiring for seven minutes they found the prisoner GUILTY  .— Recommended to mercy by the Jury on the ground that they did not think he went home with malice to commit the deed, but that something must have arisen when he was there, to cause it.
