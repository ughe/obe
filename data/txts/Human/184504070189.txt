Cutting lead into small pieces to stow it tway—it comes to us in large pieces—he was going to breakfast when he was charged with this—he said at once he had a piece of lead—he had worked for my master about two years, and up to this time we had every confidence in him—he said he had broken his knife in cutting the lead, and took this lead to repay him.  973. JANE STANNARD was indicted for embezzling 1 s  ., the monies of David Simmonds , her master.—2nd COUNT  , for stealing 1 saucepan, value 1 s  ., the goods of >David Simmonds, her master. DAVID SIMMONDS. I live in Sharpe's-buildings, Tower-hill, and am a tin-plate worker—the prisoner was in my service—she had been with me five days on the 17th of March—she was never authorized to reotive money for me. CHARLOTTE SULLIVAN. I live in Goodman's-yard. On the 17th of March, I went to the prosecutor's shop, the prisoner was the only penon there—I asked the price of a saucepan—she told me 1 s . 9 d .—I told her I would give her 1 s .—she said it would not do—I was going out, she called me back, and said I should have it—I paid her It. for it—I went afterwards for another saucepan, and told the prosecutor what had happened. DAVID SIMMONDS re-examined.  The prisoner had no authority to tell anything for me—if a customer came she was to ring the bell for my mother to come down—she never paid me the 1 s . for this saucepan—all the things in the shop were mine—I could not miss a saucepan. LYDIA SIMMONDS. I am the prosecutor's mother—the prisoner never paid me any money for goods sold—on the 17th of March my son was out—the prisoner did not tell me she had sold anything.
   Prisoner.  I never sold the saucepan. NOT GUILTY.
