NOTTAGE, Mayor.
TUN
I believed the firm was a genuine one from the appearance of the place
-on 27th March Randall called on me and asked the price of dogs'
biscuits-I told him 128. per hundredweight, and he said "Send me five
hundredweight"-he gave me this card headed "Elm Park Storey, 74.
Elm Park, Brixton, Mr. J. Smart, proprietor"-I said "We don't know
these people, do they pay cash?” he said “Yes, and if there is no one
there of course you will leave them and the cash will be forwarded on"
-I said "No, if they want credit they must give satisfactory references,"
and I heard nothing more from them.
Cross-oxamined by Young. When you paid me for the first lot of
goods I did not give you terms for the others, I said nothing about
terms--you told me that my traveller had agreed to give you a month's
credit, and I said “ Well then I will not call for a month."
Cross-examined by Randall. You told me that Mr. Smart had sent
you to ask for the price of the biscuits, and I told you that cash for
the first order and after that on terms; I said “Unless we hear from
you we shall not excute it”-I did not say "Shall I send 5 cwt, on
approbation?”
CHARLES KING. I am carman to Mr. Garnham-I delivered 5 ewt. of
biscuits at Melbourne Grove.
Cross-examined by Young. The goods were received by A. Young,
not by you, I think.
WILLIAM BYFORD. I am an oilman, of Maxtead Road, Peckham-in
March last Young called and ordered some goods, and offered me 2 ewt.
of biscuits, which I agreed to take at 108. 6d. per cwt.--I had been deal:
ing with Mr. Garnbam, and that was 18. per ewt. less than Mr. Garnham
-Young called on me again and asked me if I wanted to buy more
goods, I refused-Randall came to my place in February for oil-I knew
from a man I bought varnishes of that he was coming-Randall said the
oil was for Mr. Young, of Melbourne Grove.
Cross-examined by Randall. You paid for the oil.

on 25th March I again delivered goods, this is the delivery note signed
