JAMES WESTABY. I am a collar dresser, of 59, Red Cross Street-on
17th January I went to Moor Lane Station and met Mr. Corke-I produced
a sample of certain undressed collars and cuffs which were brought to me
in a sack with "D. R. & Co." on it in red letters, by a young man; and
two or three days afterwards I received this memorandum, after which
dressed thirty-six dozen of them-in consequence of certain enquiries I
produced a sample of them to Mr. Corke who identified them as being
stolen from him when I received the goods in the sack I did not know
where they came from-I had not had any transactions with the London
Wood Street Warehouse Company for some years—the message was “They
are from the Wood Street Warehouse Company, but do not touch them
till you hear from them "--I did not do anything to them till I received
the memorandum.
