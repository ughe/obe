1638. JOHN PITMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of May,
I jacket, value 48. ; and 2 handkerchiefs, value 28. ; the goods of Henry
Duncan.
HENRY DUNCAN. I am servant to the captain of the ship Madagascar
from Calcutta. On the 5th of May the ship was in the East India
Docks—I removed my chest, hammock, and other things to No. 8, Crisp.
East India-road—I covered my jacket overa bird-cage to keep the
bird warm—there were two silk handkerchiefs in the pocket—I stopped the
eart at the dock gates to take in another chest—the prisoner was about the
cart then—I went on to my lodgings—the cart came shortly afterwards—
another man wag driving it—I then missed my jacket and two silk hand.
kerchiefs, which are here.
Prisoner. I was never in the East rndia Docks the *hole day. Witness.
I am sure I saw him at the gate outside about two o'clock—I have not
doubt of his person.
Tuouas SHEEN. I am a policeman. I received information from
Duncan, and from the man who drove the eart—in consequence of which
I went to the Red Lion, High-street, Poplar, and saw the prisoner—I
asked him what he had done With that jacket—he said, I pledged it
Mr. Holly'e, took him to the station, and found 4d. on
him—I asked him where the duplicate of the jacket was—he said he gave
it to the person who sent him to pledge them—I asked if he knew the
person—he said be did not, they called him pussey—next morning he told
me tbe person's name was Hughes.
GEORGE HANCE. I am shopman to Holly, a pawnbroker, in High'
Street, Poplar. This jacket and two handkerchiefs were pawned by the
prisoner, about three o'clock in the afternoon, in the name of John Smith
—l asked him whose property it was—be said it was his own—be said
nothing about pussey or Hughes.
Prisoner's Defence. I was sitting in a public-house two sailors came
in, and one of them had a jacket, the Other had none; be asked me to go
and pawn his jacket; I went, and brought him the duplicate, and money,
and he gave me a drop of beer.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 20.—Strongly recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor
and Jury.—Confined Three Months.
