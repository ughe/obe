1804, JOHN SPENCE was indicted for stealing 3 shillings, 8 sixpences,
and 1 groat; the monies of Richard Reynolds, from his person.
RICHARD REYNOLDS. I live in the Commercial-road. On the 10th of
April I was employed as waiter at the Prince of Wales public-house at Stepney
fair-the prisoner and two or three others came around me, and pushed me
about-the prisoner put his band into my right waistcoat-pocket, and took
48. 10d. in silver, which I had counted not five minutes before I saw his
hand come out of my pocket, and be passed it to somebody else I put my
hand to my pocket, and the money was gone-I immediately caught hold of
him, and said, "You have robbed me" - I held him till the policeman came.
Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q. Were there not a number of persons
scuffling about? A. Not all of them there were some pushing about the
prisoner had been drinking--I am certain I was sober--the prisoner came and
spoke some nonsense first, and then he and two or three others pushed me
about-there were forty or fifty persons present, but not more than three or
four standing up about me.
WILLIAM MARCHING (police-constable K 879.) I took the prisoner-
found nothing on him but a knife.
Guilty. Aged 26.- Recommended to merou.-Confined Three Months.
