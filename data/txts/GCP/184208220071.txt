entering the dwelling-house of Thomas Burn, about three o'clock in the

value 31. 108.; 11 bed-gowns, value 168. ; 12 shifts, value 11. 48. ; 7
petticoats, value 108.; 2 aprons, value 28. ; 3 table-cloths, value 128. ; 8
