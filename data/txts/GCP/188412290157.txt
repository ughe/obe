CAROLINE GOLDSTONE. I am Thomas Goldstone's wife of 48, Lang-
thorne Road on the 22nd December, about 12, William Keeble came to
me and said "If you please, my mother says will you be good enough to
take this for her?"--I said "What is it Bill, the watch?"-he said, No,
Mrs. Goldstone, a ring ”-I said “Let's look"-I took it out of his
band and undid the paper, and said to my neighbour, who was sitting in
my house at the time, "Oh, this is a fine one, this isn't your mother's” —
he said "Well, my mother has borrowed it, she cannot send father's
watch till after Christmas" I said “Run on, Cocky, and I will meet you
at the top ".--he said "Mother says don't leave it for less than 11., it
came out of Mr. Bressay's for 24s." -Bressay is another pawnbroker-I
went to Mr. Withers with it, this is the ring-I pawned it about 12, and
got 11. and a ticket which I gave to William Keeble against Mr.
Withers' door-I have pawned a watch for Mrs. Keeble for months,
Monday after Monday-I am a poor woman and go round among families
in the neighbourhood-I pawn things.
Cross-examined by Sarah Keeble. Sometimes a dozen children come to
