ISAAC LovERID0E. I live in Price-street, in the parish of Christ Church,

MARY ANN GROVES. I am the wife of James Grove, a gas-lighter, in

the prosecutor's house—the window was up as far as it would go, and
body was in as far as his waist—I stepped on the pavement, and the pri-
soner jumped down, and went down Goodwin-court—I turned round and

but for that.
