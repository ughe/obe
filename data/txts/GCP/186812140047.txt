Lambeth-I asked him if he had seen anything of Geeson--he said, “Yes;

when we had tea, we went up stairs; I took out the cheque-book and said
to Crane, “Will you put this in your trunk for me t"-his brother was up
in the room with us --Crane said he would burn the book, and he put it in
the front of his drawer till he came back-I believe it was afterwards burnt
- I left it there, and have not seen it since I heard that Geeson was in
custody on the Tuesday evening-my father spoke to me on Wednesday
morning, and I went to Mr. Mullens and gave myself up-Crane was taken
soon after; he was standing outside the Mansion House.
