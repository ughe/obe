MESSRS. RYLAND and GURNEY conducted the Prosecution. JOSEPH HEADINGTON( City police-constable, No.  218.) On the night of the 20th of Feb. I was on duty in Holborn, near Brook-street—I saw Thorogood stripped, and in the act of fighting with some one—I went up, and desired him to put his things on and go home—he immediately struck me a violent blow in the breast, which caused me to stagger against the shutters—I laid hold of him by his neckcloth and one of his hands, to prevent his striking me again—Bailey and Read came up and laid hold of me at the same time—they both commenced striking and kicking me, with others that are not in custody—I was knocked down by either Bailey or Read, I cannot say which—they were both behind me at that time—there was a general scuffle—I was repeatedly kicked about the body by Thorogood, Bailey, and Read—I never let go my hold—Jackman came up to my assistance. HENRY THOMAS JACKMAN( police-constable G  192.) On the night of the 21st of Feb., between twelve and one o'clock, I heard a rattle, and went in the direction of the sound of it to Holborn—I saw Headington on the ground, with one hand hold of Thorogood's neckcloth and his hand, and the other two prisoners were kicking and striking him—I went up, and said that would not do, and Thorogood immediately kicked me in the groin—I was knocked down, but I immediately regained my feet and took hold of him, and the others caught hold of me—I was struck repeatedly in the face by Bailey—we succeeded in taking them all into custody. WILLIAM ARNOLD( Citypolice-constable, No.  204.) On hearing the rattle spring, I went up, and saw the prisoners striking and kicking the officers—I dragged Bailey away.
   Read's Defence.  I never struck the policeman at all.
   Bailey's Defence.  I had a few words with my husband that night, and went out, and got a little to drink. My brother got into a row , and when I went to see what was the matter, the policeman had got hold of him, and they said I was one; but I never struck the officer.
   Thorogood's Defence.  One of them caught hold of me by the handkerchief, and said, "You young b----, I will take you away."
   READ  — GUILTY. Aged 20.
   BAILEY  — GUILTY. Aged 27.
   THOROGOOD  — GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Six Month .
 1026. PAUL GARNSTON was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of Sept., 3 desks, value 4 l  . 10 s  ., the goods of John Gillett , his master. JOHN GILLETT. I am a cabinet-maker, and live in Fetter-lane, the pri soner worked for me. About the 22nd of Sept. he had some desks to work on in his own room—I met him on the 21st of February, and asked what he had done with them—he said he sold them to Mr. Willis for 10 s .—I said I must give him into custody—he did not give me any money for them.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did I receive the whole of the materials of you? A.  No—all the woodwork, but not the locks and hinges—they would not have been complete without them. BENJAMIN WILLIS. In October the prisoner called on me to ask for employ—I said I had none—he called two days after, and said he had three desks to dispose of, he had not the means of furnishing them, and he wished to sell them—I bought three of them.
   Prisoner. Q.  Were they completed? A.  No, in the rough, they were not half finished.
   Prisoner.  I admit parting with them, but they were not desks. Witness.
