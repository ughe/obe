489. SOLOMON BARMASH (37), WILLIAM BARMASH (16),
WILLIAM KOSMAN (18), and MAURICE ROBINOWITZ (29)
Feloniously forging and uttering an order for £593, with intent to
defraud.
MR. O. F. GILL and MR. A. GILL Prosecuted; Mr. PAUL TAYLOR appeared
for Solomon Barmash; and Mr. Biron for William Barmash.
Louis SECKEL. I am an export merchant, of Lauderdale Buildings,
Aldersgate Street-William Barmash was my office boy for twelve
months up to November 12th, 1892, and had the opportunity of knowing
who our customers were-he saw documents about the office, and copied
letters into the letter-book, and then posted them-I occasionally sent
him to the bank to pay in cheques - I did business with Adam Pearson and
Co., who have from time to time paid me, for goods received, cheques,
which the younger Barmash saw-some of the letters in this order for &
cheque-book from Adam Pearson and Co. are in William Barmash's
writing, and the endorsement on this slip and the address I should say are
his—this book is mine (Pound on William Barmash)I never gave anya
body authority to take it from my place—it contains the names and
addresses of manufacturers--the name of Adam Pearson and Co. is written
in it, and also “Bank of Scotland," first in pencil and then in ink. A
Cross-examined by Mr. BIRON. I had other clerks, but it was William
Barmash's duty to copy letters, not theirs, and oocasionally to clear out the
office-I did not send him to pay cash into the bank, only cheques-I
have seen him write more than half a dozen times-he made press copies
of letters, and addressed envelopes in a good commercial and I can
positively swear to these "p's” and to the whole words "Yours truly”
this is rather a peculiar á ¢ "_" John Jacobs " is written on the back
of this-I notice that the "p's" are quite different, and that the "1"
has a loop to it-I looked at different letters and picked those ont which
I thought were like his writing- I was away a considerable part of the
time William Barmash was there. I wrote this: William Barmash has
been in our employ twelve months; we always found him steady," etc.
ISRAEL LEWENSTEIN. I am a printer, of 38, Church Lane, Commercial
Road, in partnership with Solomon Kerstein-on November 25th young
Barmash came and wanted me to print something like this, showing me
something, and said he could get me lots of orders—I did not know him
before-he went out and came back, and gave me the order--this is a
proof of the document I printed and supplied to him.
