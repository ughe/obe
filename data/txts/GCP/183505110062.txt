WINCHESTER, Mayor.
John WILKINSON. I am a policeman. The prosecutor brought the
prisoner and this stool to the station-house.
Prisoner's Defence. A man I did not know told me to carry it to
Charing-cross.
George WIRKSTEAD. He was going in the contrary direction.

1156. JOHN WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
March, 1 pair of boots, value 16s., the goods of John Goddard Tyler.
MARY KENDALL. I am servant to Mr. Tyler, of Arbour-square, Com-
mercial-road. On the 27th of March the prisoner came in the afternoon
and asked for the last pair of new boots that Mr. Tyler had made for him
-I had never seen him before-I told him I had no orders to give any
boots, he had better call in the evening; he said no, he was to have them
then-he said Mr. Jones in the Road had sent him-I brought him down
one pair-he said those were not right-I took them up again, and drew the
boot-rack into the passage; he called up, “I know them when I see
them "-I said, " Then if you know them come and look for them;" he
came up and drew out a new pair, the bottoms were not soiled-he said he
would return them in the evening-I never saw him again.
JOHN GODDARD TYLER. These boots were mine-I had not sent the
prisoner for them.
JOHN DOUGAL SMART. I am foreman to Mr. Jones, of Whitechapel-road
-- he is a boot-maker-the prisoner was not in his employ- I did not
direct bim to go for any boots-we had nothing to do with Mr. Tyler.
RICHARD JONES. I live in the Commercial-road, and am a boot-maker
-I know nothing of the prisoner-I never directed him to go to the pro-
secutor.
GUILTY. Aged 21.
1167. JOHN WILSON was again indicted for stealing, on the 31st of
March, 1 pair of boots, value 30s. ; the goods of William Christopher.
WILLIAM CHRISTOPHER. I lodge at Mrs. Pearson's. She gave my
boots to the prisoner-I did not send him for them I lost a pair.
SARAH PEARSON. I keep a lodging-house in Turner-square, Commer-
cial-road. The prosecutor lodged there-the prisoner came on the 31st
of March, and said he came from Mr. Jones, in Whitechapel-road, for a pair
of boots of Mr. Christopher's, to be stretched-I said I had no orders to
give them, but I brought down two pair, and he took one-he said he
would bring them back in the evening, but I never saw him again till he
was in custody.
John Dougal Smart. I am foreman to Mr. Jones in Whitechapel-
road-I did not send the prisoner for any boots.
GUILTY. Aged 21.
