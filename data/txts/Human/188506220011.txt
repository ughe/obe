   Cross-examined by MR. ROLLAND . My dealings were with Bloomfield and Co.—I ordered goods from time to time; they were supplied to me—so far as I knew they came from Falkirk to Bloomfield and Co., and from them to me—the orders I gave to Bloomfield and Co. amounted to several thousands—I sometimes gave bills in respect of the goods—I never signed bills in respect of goods I did not receive; every bill I signed I had goods for—Forbes was never present at any conversation I had with Gardner about some houses, and was no party to any of the arrange ments I entered into with Bloomfield and Co. or Gardner.
