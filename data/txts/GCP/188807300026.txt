Prisoner's Defence. I lived at Mansion House Street eight months, and

MRS. JArco (Ro-examined). He had photographs come to him in

The Prisoner. The gun was sent to me on approbation-I wrote to
say I would keep it, but the gentleman took out a warrant before he
sent the invoice-I gave every information I could I gave the banker's
reference, because my father had a large account there for many years;
I had no account there myself, but I thought if I gave his name he
would give me credit.
