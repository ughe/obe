 830. DANIEL LEONARD and DANIEL TRACEY were indicted for stealing, on the   7th of Jan ., 37 yards of printed cotton, value 21 s  .; the goods of William Barnett . HARRIETT BARNETT. I am the wife of William Barnett, a linen-draper, in Cherry-garden-street, Bermondsey. About seven o'clock on Saturday night, the 7th of January, I saw Tracey take a piece of print from the door, inside the shop—Leonard was with him—I ran out, but could not catch him—I heard Tracey say, " Jack , there is a nice piece"—I am sure they are the two boys.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Where were you when you heard Tracey say this? A.  Close by the print in the shop—I know this print by the pattern—there is no mark on it. MARY ANN WHITE. I am the wife of David White, a lighterman. About half-past seven o'clock that night, two boys arrived at my house—it was dark—I do not know either of them—this is my mark to this deposition, which I made before the Magistrate.
   Q.  Did you tell the Magistrate, "The prisoner and two other lads came to my house on Saturday night?" A.  There were only two in company—I do not know which of the prisoners came, I think it was one of them—I think Tracey was one—I had not known him a long time—I had not had many things before from them—they had never been there before—I will not swear they had not—I think Tracey was one that came that night, and I think they brought this piece of cotton—I have heard of persons being in dicted for receiving stolen goods. GEORGE KEMP( police-constable M  205.) I went to White's house, and found the cotton—I apprehended Leonard, I saw him coming out of her house, between six and seven o'clock the same night. WILLIAM TERRY. I saw Mrs. Barnett following the two prisoners—they had a bundle, and were running from towards her house to Mrs. White's, round the back way of the houses.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What are you? A.  A labourer—I work for Mr. Denny, a corn-lighterman—I have never been in any trouble myself—I ran down the City-gardens to see which way they went, and heard Tracey say to Leonard, "Take it down to Mrs. Hurley's, as plain as I could understand, but Mrs. White's they must have said.
   Tracey's Defence.  I know nothing about it.
   LEONARD  — GUILTY.
   TRACEY  — GUILTY. Confined Three Months , and Whipped.
