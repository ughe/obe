I have known the prisoner a great many years—on the 3rd of January, I had occasion to go to Hammersmith for my master, and met the prisoner at the West-end—he said he was out of service, and asked if I could tell him of a place—he went with me to Hammersmith, and at last I invited him home, and we had some refreshment—he staid with me a quarter of an hour—soon after he was gone I missed an Indian-rubber cloak of my master's from the harness-room—my room adjoins that—I saw him the next day in Union-street, Whitechapel—when he saw me, he ran away—I called "Stop thief", and overtook him in the Commercial-road—I found the cloak on his back, and gave him into custody—this is it.
 (The prisoner put in a written defence stating that the prosecutor had made him intoxicated, and he was unconscious of having the cloak till the next morning.) WILLIAM BRAND re-examined . I did not lend him the cloak—there is not a word of truth in that—he did not tell me he was going to take it—he was not drunk—we had some half-and-half and biscuits, but nothing else. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Three Months.

EDWARD MAGIN was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of February , 1 handkerchief, value 4 d  .; 2 aprons, value 1 s  .; 1 petticoat, value 2 s  .; 1 shift, value 2 s  .; and 2 caps, value 6 d  .; the goods of Sarah Clarke . SARAH CLARKE. I am a single. I sent some clothes by Michael Collins to be washed, on the 4th of February—I have known the prisoner some times as a coal-whipper—he visited at my house.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  I believe you are servant at the George? A.  Yes—they were done up in a handkerchief. MICHAEL COLLINS. Clarke left a bundle in my charge in the tap-room, and in about twenty minutes I missed it—I went out for five or ten minutes, leaving the bundle safe—the prisoner was there—I put it on the side of the seat, alongside of my tin—when I came back he was gone and the bundle also—I went outside, and took it from him.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Was he sober? A.  I do not know—he was there about half an hour—I did not see him drink—I was not in his company, and did not exchange a word with him—he came in and spoke to a few of his work-mates—there were five persons in the tap-room—I did not see any bundle of the prisoner's there. THOMAS GILBERT. I live at Shadwell. I was talking to the prisoner on the pavement outside the public-house, when Collins came out and said, "You have got a bundle that does not belong to you?"—I said, "Have you, Magin, got a bundle?"—he said, "No; it is my own property"—Collins said it was not—I helped Collins while he took the bundle from under the prisoner's jacket—"Now," I said, "we have missed a great many things lately; if it is the servant's property, come in and clear your own character"—I walked in, and said, "Sarah, is this your property?"—she said, "It is, and I give him in charge"—he said it was a lark , but at the station-house he said he had bought a flannel jacket that afternoon for 4 s . 6 d . and he had lost it, and that he took this in retaliation for his loss—I have known him some time, and he bore a good character—if he was discharged, I would give him employment.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What are you? A.  A foreman to the coal whippers.
