 577.  MATTHEW HORRIGAN was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of March , one shirt, value 2 s., the goods of George Barford.
   CHARLOTTE BARFORD. I live in Gloucester-buildings, Whitecross-street. I lost the shirt out of the yard on the 7th of March - it is a court-yard, and has no thoroughfare; I saw the prisoner in the yard, but never saw him before. I saw him break the line, take the shirt, and run away with it. I opened the door, ran after him, and saw him going into a coffee-shop with it under his arm. I got an officer, and found the shirt on the floor of the coffee-shop.
   GEORGE MOORE. I am a pipemaker. I was in Chequer-alley, by the coffee-shop, and saw the prisoner run into it with something under his jacket. Mrs. Barford overtook him going into the coffee-shop, and charged him with stealing the shirt.
   JAMES HADLEY. I was desired by Mrs. Barford to apprehend the prisoner - there were about twelve others in the room. When I went in I told all the persons to stand up, and she pointed out the prisoner as the lad. I found the shirt just under him, it was wet.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. The woman and the constable came in, and pointed me out as the person without a hat; there were several others in the same room without hats.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Whipped and Discharged.
 Second Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 578.  MARY HILL was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of April , one coat, value 2 l., and one waistcoat, value 12 s., the goods of Frederick Schutt and John Hollings.
   JOHN HOLLINGS. I am a tailor. On the 13th of April I left my house a little before six o'clock - the coat and waistcoat were safe then in the cutting room, which is the front parlour. The prisoner is a stranger. I was sent for home about seven o'clock.
   MARY ANN SCHUTT. I am the daughter of Frederick Schutt. On the 13th of April I was in the garden adjoining the house, which is in Ridinghouse-lane. Between six and seven o'clock I heard some one walking in the passage, and perceived the prisoner coming out - the street-door is open for the accommodation of lodgers; she had these things under her shawl. I pursued her some distance, and brought her back with them.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 35. Confined Four Months.
 Second Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 579.  JOSEPH HARDING was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of February , one watch, value 4 l., and four seals, value 2 l., the goods of James Davey , from his person.
   JAMES DAVEY. I live in Harley-street, and am coachman to Mr. Mitchell. On the 4th of February, about half-past six o'clock, I was coming from Tottenham-court-road; at the corner of Portland-place and Devonshire-street I saw two men coming, one was about five yards behind the other - one caught hold of my arm, and the other pulled my watch out instantly and ran away. I called out Stop thief! the other came, took hold of my arm, and asked what he had done? I told him he had taken my watch, and broke from him. He said
 "Come along, I can run faster than you, and I will catch him." He ran a few yards, and then tripped up my heels - I am sure he is one of the two - the other came up the instant I had lost the watch. I never lost sight of the prisoner till he was stopped at the corner of Weymouth-street.
   JOHN MAUD. I am a gentleman's servant, and live in Portland-place, between Devonshire-street and Weymouth-street. I found the watch in the area on a Thursday, at the latter end of February, about nine o'clock in the evening - it had been thrown there.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   JOHN MARTIN. I was officer of the night; the prisoner was brought to the watch-house by Oram, about half-past seven o'clock, Davey came down with him, and charged him with robbing him - he told the same story which he has now, and was quite sure the prisoner was one of them.
 Prisoner's Defence. The man has sworn falsely.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Life.
 First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Serjeant.
 580.  PHILIP HYDE was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of April , one bason, value 1 s., and one ewer, value 1 s., the goods of Francis Tadman.
   FRANCIS TADMAN. I live in Old-street. On the 12th of April, between nine and ten o'clock in the evening, I stood on the opposite side of the way, the prisoner and another came to the window and took the bason and ewer; I pursued and took him. He said,
 "Have pity, for I am in distress."
   THOMAS HOBBS. I was standing nearly opposite the prosecutor's house, I heard the cry of Stop thief! and saw the prisoner with the things, the other got away.
 Prisoner. I saw them on the second step of the door, and being in distress I took them.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. Confined Two Months and Whipped.
 First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Serjeant.
