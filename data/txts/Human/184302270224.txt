1063. SOLOMON MARKS was indicted for obtaining goods by false pretences. WILLIAM HENRY MORGAN. I am warehouseman to Benjamin Wilmott Gabriel, of Watling-street. On the 21st of Jan. the prisoner called there—he said, "How is Mr. Gabriel?"—I said, "Pretty well"—He then said, "I want two dozen pairs of stockings for Mr. Moses Nathan"—I gave them to him—I should not hare given them to him if he had not said he came for them for Mr. Moses Nathan—I gave him a receipt, which he signed—I have it here. MOSES NATHAN. I am a tailor, and live in Ratcliffe-highway. The pri soner was in my service about ten months ago, but not in Jan.—I did not send him on the 21st of Jan. to Mr. Gabriel's for any stockings—I did not authorize him to take any from there, or to get any either then or at any other time. WILLIAM SAUNDERS. I am a hatter, and live in Shadwell. Between eight and nine o'clock on the 3rd of Feb. the prisoner came to my shop—he said he came for a hat or two for Mr. Lee, naming the size, and saying it was for the mate of a ship—in consequence of his stating he came from Mr. Lee, I gave him two hats—Lee was a customer of mine, and believing the prisoner came from him, I gave him the hats, which I would not have done otherwise. JOHN LEE. I am a tailor and outfitter, and live in High-street, Shadwell. I did not send the prisoner on the 3rd of Feb. to Mr. Saunders for hats—he was not in my service at that time, nor did I receive any. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Six Months .
 1064. JOHN BAILEY was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of Feb ., 1 copper, value 1 l  ., the goods of Stephen Goundry , and fixed to a building; and that he had been before convicted of felony. ELIZABETH MADON. I am the wife of Thomas Madon. I resided in a house of Mr. Stephen Goundry's at Hillingdon—I left it on the 20th of Feb., the copper was then safe, and fixed in the house—this is it—I am quite sure it is Mr. Stephen Groundry's. JAMES DARVILL. On Monday evening, the 27th of Feb., I was going to my house on Uxbridge-moor, which is about 100 yards from Mr. Goundry's house, where Mrs. Madon had lived—I met the prisoner and another man—they were both carrying the copper—as I came round the corner of a wall they dropped the copper and ran away—I am sure the prisoner was one of them—this is the copper they were carrying. JOHN ROLLE. I am a labourer. On the evening of the 27th I was going home from my work—I met the prisoner and the other man on Uxbridge-moor—they had the copper with them—I said to the prisoner, "Halloo, my lad, where are you going?"—I am sure the prisoner was the man, and there was another with him—they dropped the copper.
   Prisoner. Q.  Had not Mr. Darvill got the copper when you came up? A.  No, you dropped it.
   Prisoner.  He said Mr. Darvill had it when he came up; I am innocent of it RICHARD ROADNIGHT( police-sergeant T  11.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got at Mr. Clark's office ( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 27.— Transported for Seven Years .
