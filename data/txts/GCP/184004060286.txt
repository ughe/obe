1311. JOHN FITZGERALD was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of
April, 1 hearth-rug, value 108.; the goods of William John Dowding and
another, his masters.
Klopp
WILLIAM JOHN DOWDING. I am a heurth-rug manufacturer, and live
in Peter-street, Southwark-1 have one partner--the prisoner was in our
service. On Sunday, the 4th of April, about seven o'clock in the evening,
I was going into my own house, and observed a hearth-rug on the grating
before the window, where it had no business to be I pulled the bell to go
in, and saw the hearth-rug slide off the grating, and go down underneath
into my premises again- I went in, and met the prisoner at the top of the
cellar-steps- I went to where I had seen the hearth-rug fall to-I saw
nothing there I came up again- I went down with a light, and found the
hearth-rug under the cellar-steps, where it had no business to be.
WILLIAM TAYLOR (police-sergeant M 14.) I know Mr. Jeretnys the

warehouse into the cellar, and putting it on the grating to take away it is
my first offence."
GUILTY.-Confined Three Months.
