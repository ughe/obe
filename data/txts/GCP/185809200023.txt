Cross-examined. Have you seen him write? A. Yes; a good many
times-(Letter read: “Wolgast, June 26th, 1858. In answer to your
favour of 12th, 1 have made up my mind to consign to you the 8320
Sheffield wheat, shipped by the vessel Anna, Captain Kell; for which I
annex a duplicate bill of lading. At the same time I take the liberty to
make use of your kindness, and to draw 2,4007. for your account on the
