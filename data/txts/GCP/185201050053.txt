JAMES LANGTRY, JUN. I am the son of the last witness. I took a bag of
coffee in a truck on 30th Dec, to the prisoner-I went home with the truck,
and went back to the prisoner-I recollect my father leaving me there-- the
prisoner said to me, “Will you come in and have some pudding?" said,
"No, Sir, I have got plenty of pudding at home, I don't like it"-he took
me by the collar and led me back into the back parlour-he took me in to
have some pudding--the prisoner went out in the yard-I then went in the
yard to give some to the chickens, and he said, "Here is your coffee" I was
in the back room about ten minutes I could not, from the parlour, see what
he was doing with the coffee-when I went back, he said, “Here is your
coffee" -I took it, it burnt my back, I put it down-I at last took it home
-my father saw that coffee.
