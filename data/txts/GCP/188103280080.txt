EMMA ATTENBOROUGH, I am the daughter of Mr. and Mrs. Pether.
Cross-examined by MR. GRAIN. On 8th November I was assisting my
mother serving in the bar--I said at the police-court, "I did not see the
four-gallon jar brought in either by my father or Oarter on that day;"
that is true-I also said, “I was in the house assisting in the bar in the
afternoon and until the house was closed ”—I did not notice a four-gallon
jar there either in the bar parlour or behind the bar.
Re-examined. I was there when the stone jar was found, after my
father was in custody- I don't recolleet my mother doing anything with
it I did not smell it.
FREDERICK ATTENBOROUGH, I am the husband of the last witness-I
remember the Wellington being taken-I pawned some watches, rings,
and other articles for Pether, for the purpose of raising some of the
money to take the house ; I can't say what the amount was ; I gave him
the money; these are the tickets (Those amounted to 281. 126.—I was
outside the Old Bailey when Pether was being tried - I saw Holt; he
said that he had given Mr. Leggatt 31. for Mrs. Pether to assist her.
JOHN MARCHMONT MILES. I live at Emanuel Villas, Romford Road,
Forest Gate- I was formerly agent for the London and Lancashire Fire
Office, and as such I was acquainted with Holt-on or about 2nd Sep-
tember Carter, whom I did not know before, called on me with this
letter, in consequence of which I sent Mr. Donald to make an inventory
of the house-I gave him a form to fill up; he filled up the items from
Carter's dictation-I was not present; on Donald's return I saw the
paper; this is it (produced)-the writing in ink I did myself-upon that
the policy was drawn up-I afterwards received a letter from Carter,
before the policy was delivered—I destroyed that letter; it merely asked
why the policy was not sent-I wrote the letter of 13th September in
answer-I subsequently sent a letter with the policy, and received in
answer this letter containing a post-office order for 11. 48. 3d. from Carter
-the memorandum of the property to be insured was destroyed after the
last trial-I received it from Carter at the time he wrote the letter; to
the best of my belief it was in the same handwriting as the letter-1
never saw Holt write.
LAIRD DONALD. I live at Leyton Green, Essex-I was clerk to Mr.
Miles-on 3rd September by his direction I went to the Duke of Wel-
lington, Brook Street, Ratcliff-I there saw Pether-I asked for Mr.
Carter-I had the form with me, in which the entries in ink were
subsequently made by Mr. Miles-I put questions to Pether, and put
down his answers in pencil- I did not take any inventory.
