2019. THOMAS BERRY was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of
August, 5 pairs of shoes, value 11. 58., the goods of Edward Wallis.
EDWARD Wallis. I am a boot and shoemaker, and live in Lambeth-
marsh. I lost some shoes on the 15th of August--they were safe that day
at twelve o'clock, and missed at two-these are them.
JAMES WILTSHIRE. I am an apprentice to the prosecutor. Between
two and three o'clock that day I saw the prisoner take these shoes from
the door-I ran after him-he threw them down and ran away-I am sure
the prisoner was the man-there was another with him, he was secured.
Prisoner. I never had them in my possession at all-when I was taken
I was walking along.

2020. WILLIAM BOUTE was indicted for stealing, on the 8th
of August, 1 hone, value 6s., and 1 razor, value 28.; the goods of Charles
Atkinson.
CHARLES ATKINSON. I keep a shop at Lambeth. The prisoner came in
on the 8th of August-I left my shop while he was there I missed a razor
and hone the next day.
Prisoner. Q. By what mark do you know this razor and hone, are there
not fifty or a hundred by the same maker? A. I know them by the look
of them and the maker's name I have used them a long while.
Joun THORNTON. The prisoner came to me, and told me he had bought
a bargain, and showed me this hone and razor--this was Tuesday evening,
the 8th of August-I kept them till the next day, and gave them to the
officer-he did not offer to sell them-I believe he left them by mistake.
Prisoner. I was passing Whitechapel, and had a glass of half-and-half,
and a man offered me these for sale- I gave him 1s. 9d. for the hone, and
Bd. for the razor, which was 2s. 3d., all I had, and he took that for them-
the prosecutor has lodgers in the house, and there is free admission for any
one to take these things.
Charles Atkinson. We have lodgers, and they can have access to the
shop--this is one that I had used it was not for sale.
(Robert Downing, a hair-dresser, of No. 81, Snow-hill, gave the prisoner
a good character, and offered to take him into his employ.)
GUILTY.-Aged 19.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prosecutor.
-Confined One Month; One Week Solitary.
