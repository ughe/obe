Frederick Sadler was wanted for stealing a watch and scarf-pin from the
person--I had no knowledge of whom the larceny was from-about five
p.m. on Saturday, 23rd December, I went into a public-house in Oxford
Street, where I saw the prisoner, whom I knew previously-I said, “Sadler,
I am going to take you into custody for stealing a watch and pearl scarf
