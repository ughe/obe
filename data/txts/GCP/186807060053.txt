630. HENRY WILLIAMS (36), Stealing a watch of Frederick John
Crisp, from his person.
Mr. METCALFE conducted the Prosecution; and Mr. MONTAGU WILLIAMS the
Defence.
lona
FREDERICK JOHN CRISP. I am a grocer, in the Commercial Road-on
14th June, about 9.30, I was at the booking office at the Lea Bridge Station
-there were several other persons there I was going towards the window
to get my ticket-Us I got just by the barrier, in front of the ticket office,
the prisoner pushed against me, and I saw his hand leave my coat-I seized
him-he pushed in from the barrier; pushed me on one side, so as to get
between me and the barrier, in front of me-I had a waistcoat on with no
fob to it, and my watch was in the pocket, and the coat buttoned-1 had
seen it safe the moment before, I saw the prisoner's hand come from under
my coat, looked down, and saw the chain hanging, and the watch gone 110
one but the prisoner was near enough to have taken it; he was the only
man before me I immediately took hold of him, and gave him to the
station master--there were other persons near enough to him to take the
watch from him-he refused his address at the station-he said he travelled
in Manchester and Birmingham--he said, sooner than I should prosecute
him, he would pay me double the value of the watch it was a gold hunter,
worth twelve guineas--a ring was picked up, which corresponds with the
one on my watch.
