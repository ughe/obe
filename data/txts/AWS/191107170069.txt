Mackenzie knew this. In 1909 he offered to buy the residue of my
interest in the Novelty Van Syndicate for E300. I knew nothing of
his past history until this charge was brought against me. In April
or March, 1908, I met Mackenzie and toid him I had been to a meet-
ing of the Budget Protest League He offered to take over the work,
and as I had had two responses to one circular he asked me to hand
all the papers over to him and he would look after the business of it.
He assisted me in my political work from that time. All subscrip-
tions received were handed to him, he acknowledged them and dealt
with the correspondence. On August 5, 1909, I attended a meeting
of the Budget Protest League at Husbands Bosworth and another at
Marston. I was not at Eastbourne. On August 7 went to Brighton
in connection with the executorship of the late Madame Arditi, who
died on July 28 leaving me sole executor. I arrived there at 1 p.m.
and met Madame Arditi's daughter and son-in-law, Mr. and Mrs.
Romayne Walker, at Madame Arditi's flat. I returned on the follow-
ing Tuesday to Husbands Bosworth; Mr. Tucket, of Tucket and Sons,
agent of the Lafargue estate, on which was my house, was with me
on the 11th until he left at 3 p.m. On August 12 saw Mr Witham,
solicitor, of Witham, Roskell, Munster and Weld. Mackenzie had
come up from Margate and I met him at the Grosvenor Hotel. He
then owed me $500, E250 therbalance of the E300 for the residue
of my interest and E250 for shares he had taken in the Novelty Van
Syndicate. He paid me 2350 in banknotes and gold. I handed him
the signed transfer for the shares and a receipt for E100. I paid 8255
into my account at Williams Deacon's and Company. Mackenzie
was with me. We then went together to Messrs. Lumley and Co., my
solicitors, and I paid them 290. Two paying-in slips of the Credit
Lyonnais and the Banque Nationale in the name of J. W. Smith are
not in my writing. I knew J. W. Smith in 1907. I was giving up my
house in Husbands Bosworth, and I asked him if he knew of 8 piece
of grass land of 50 or 60 acres. He afterwards informed me of a piece
of land near Hove, which was let at E90. This was on July 30, 1909.
The letter was handed to Mackenzie to inquire into it and find out if
the owner (Mr. Champion) would do certain repairs on the property.
On August 7 Smith drove me over in his motor-car to see the land. He
left a card (produced) which I put in my tin box and which was found
amongst my papers by the police. In 1909 I got about E350 from my.
Greenbooth property, 2350 from my house at Husbands Bosworth,
2200 for some shares in a motor company. I got altogether 22,000
that year, but I was still rather pressed for money. On July 22 I
called at 22, Carlton House Terrace to see Mr. Guy Paget or Mr.
Norton. Neither of them was in. I asked the manservant to allow
me to write a letter, which I left with him. Letter (produced) to
