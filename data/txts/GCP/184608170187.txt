been paid into the Bank since-Pink gave me back my watch and chain,
which was also gone, and also my purse--the notes had not been in the purse
nor in the same pocket-1 had been up all night before, and had met some
philanthropic friends-I swear I had the notes, because I unfolded the
paper in the street about a quarter of an hour before--I did not count them,
but am quite satisfied I had my whole number-I am satisfied I had more
than three-in the course of the afternoon, Lloyd and Pink were taken into
custody-I stopped the notes at the Bank the same morning-I had received
them from the Bank.
Cross-examined by Mr. HORRY. Q. You had known Pink some time?
4. I had seen her twice-I never lost anything in her company before-I
have been in her company with money and with my watch and chain-she
induced me to take some spirits-she was in bed with me when I went to sleep
-she has occasionally put my chain round her neck, and returned it to me
I have given her money to do certain things with, and found it all right.
Court. Q. You trusted no notes to the prisoner? A. No-I had re-
ceived ten notes-I only paid one away, and I saw that one afterwards-I
did not make a mistake about the number of one of the notes at the Bank-
had paid one away-the clerk said, "You had better go back and get the num
ber of that note-I said it was either the first or last of the roll, because in
opening the packet of notes I should take either the top or bottom one-I went
to the person I paid it to, saw it, and it was the very first of the ten-I then
returned to the Bank-I traced one note to the Bank last Tuesday or
Wednesday--the other five have not been heard of-I have paid away the
other three to the party paid the first to he gave me cash for them-I did
it on purpose that one person might have the four.
Mr. HORRY. Q. Do you recollect any of the places you went to on that
night? A. I peeped in at the Caledonian coffee-house, but did not sit down
there-I had been in the neighbourhood of Hackney-I was in a public-house
in the neighbourhood of Brunswick-street for soine hours--I might have been a
little the worse for what I had before I left there I was going to Vinegar-
ground to see Pink-I had a lodging where I could have left my money, but
being late I did not like to disturb my landlord-it was certainly not at four
o'clock in the morning, instead of eight that I went to the Vinegar-ground-
did not go to sleep down stairs before Pink came- I did not go to bed before
she came-she induced me to send for some drink-I was not in the house a
quarter of an hour before Pink came- I was with Carroll principally she
went over for Pink-she was not gone two minutes--the moment Pink came
we went to bed to rether, but I never intended to have gone to bed at that
early hour of the morning-I only just went there to see her-my purse was
in my pocket with some gold lost about seven sovereigns-Pink returned
me two of them-she handed me my watch and chain and purse I had put
my watch on the side-table over night-my purse was in my pocket folded up
with my trowsers- I did not put my chain round her neck when I got up in
the morning-she was up and dressed it was between ten and eleven o'clock
--my watch-glass was broken, through their taking it out of the house
Will swear I was not toying with her, talking about not having a ring to fit
her, and wanting the landlady to get a cab to get a license to marry ber-I
was perfectly sober when I awoke, but was greatly excited-I had been on
London-bridge smoking a cigar before I went there - I did not drink.
