   MR. GEOGHEGAN submitted that, as the prisoner was only committed for obtaining one sum of £2, and the second  £2 had been added to the Indictment without leave of the COURT , that on the authority of Reg.  v. Sewage the case should go to the JURY on one charge only. MR. BESLEY contended that the objection should have been taken when the prisoner was called upon to plead, and that the Statute warranted any number of misdemeanours being put into the Indictment, without leave of the COURT. MR. GEOGHEGAN further contended that the Second Count was not disclosed in the depositions, and, if the Prosecution filed a faulty Indictment, it was not for him to object before the time came. THE RECORDER quashed the Second Count. GUILTY on the First Count only. He received a good character.— Four Months' Hard Labour.
