797. THOMAS SPARKES was again indicted for stealing, on the 8th of
Oct., 1 watch, value 31., and 1 guard-chain, value 10s. ; the property of
William John Bayfield, his master.
BENJAMIN LOCKWOOD. On the 8th of Oct., the prisoner was in Mr. Bay-
field's employ-two lads were also in the service-we had a pledge made on
the 1st of Dec., 1842, of a watch and guard, in the name of William Pinkney,
of Grove-place—that watch was afterwards applied for and missed the
prisoner left on the 14th of Oct.
John JAMES PINKNEY, general dealer, Grove-place, Lambeth. This watch
and guard is mine-I pledged it at Mr. Bayfield's on the 1st of Dec., 1842,
in the name of Pinkney, of Grove-place, for 11. 68.-I applied to take it out
at the latter end of November last year-I am quite sure this is the watch.
Prisoner. Q. How do you know it? 4. By the number 20,229 and the
maker's name, Duchesne.
MARY CHRISTINA Goodwin. I know the prisoner- I pledged a watch at
his desire at Mr. Jones's for 11., on the 8th of Oct. in the name of Clyne,
and on the 11th got 108. more on it-I sent the ticket to the prisoner in a
letter--this watch is a great deal like the one, and I believe it to be the same.
Prisoner. Q. Did you pledge it at my request ? A. Yes, you gave it to
me cannot say that I ever saw you with it before I pledged it-I cannot
swear this is the watch-is is a great deal like it.
CHARLES CRIPPs. I am in the service of Thomas Jones, a pawnbroker, in
St. George's-circus. On the 9th of Oct, a watch without a glass, and a
guard, were pledged in the name of Clyne-it was afterwards redeemed by the
prisoner-he employed me to get a glass to it-he pledged it again himself-
it was again redeemed on the morning of the 23rd of Jan. but not by him
-some other property of his was redeemed at the same time to the amount
of 31. 58., and was paid for all in silver.
