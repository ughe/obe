BEFORE MR. JUSTICE BIGHAM.
(Friday, January 10.)
HOLLIS, William John; manslaughter of William George Bel-
ham, Frank William Brunning, and James Smith. The like on
coroner's inquisition.
Mr. R. D. Muir, and Mr. Arthur Gill, prosecuted; Mr. Huntly
Jenkins and Mr. H. D. Roome defended; Mr. Charles Doughty held
a watching brief.
ELIZABETH MARY BELHAN said that her husband, one of the de
ceased, travelled daily to his work from Baker Street to West Hamp-
stead by the Metropolitan Railway.
EDWARD ROBERTS FULLERTON, medical man, said he was called to
West Hampstead Station on October 26, finding William Belham there
with a fractured skull; he was dead.
ELIJAH BRUNNING said that his son Frank William left home on
October 26 to go to work at Willesden Green, and travelled by the
Metropolitan Railway from Baker Street.
ISABELLA SMITH said she identified her brother, James Smith, on
October 28 at the Coroner's Court.
