   MR. CHARNOCK . Q.  Whatever you did, was it by the advice of those to whom you confided your interest? A.  I did—Mr. Barrett advised me, and he took me to the Magistrate. CATHERINE HEALEY. I am the wife of Dennis Healey, we live in Brownlow-street, Drury-lane. On the 20th of Dec. I saw Catherine Healey in Queen-street, Seven-dials—she was alone—she said, "Come on, I want you"—I said, "What do you want me for?"—she said, "My husband is in a dying state, and I want somebody to buy some bills"—she went with me to Mr. Silver's place, and gave me these bills—she staid outside—I asked if there was a man there who bought bills—I sold them to Mr. Silver for 21 l . 1 s .—I came out, and gave the money to her—she gave me 1 s . and we parted—I gave Mr. Silver these eleven bonds. JOSEPH SILVER. I am an agent for the purchase of these securities. On the day stated the last witness came to me, and brought these—I gave her 21 l . 1 s .—she asked 14 s . 10 d . in the pound—I sent word I was engaged, I should give no more than 14 s . 9 d .—she sent word I might have them—I said, I was engaged, she must call again—I returned in the evening—she came again, and said, "Here are the titulos, I will take your price"—I paid her 21 l . 1 s .—I saw the male prisoner that evening, I supposed they came from him—I called on him, and said I had bought some titulos, did they come from him?—he said, "No, they did not"—I said, "I thought you were short of cash, and wanted to put yourself in a little money, to prevent selling a quantity that I was buying of you."
   Cross-examined. Q.  You have got the receipt from Mr. Green for the 21 l .? A.  Yes, it is dated 6th of January. WILLIAM RUSSELL( police-constable C  13.) I got these titulos at the Police-court from Mr. Silver.

 742. JOHN HARRISON and GEORGE LEWIS were indicted for stealing, on the   15th of Jan ., 2 tame rabbits, price 6 s  .; the property of James Tillier . JAMES TILLIER. On the 5th of Jan. I had two rabbits safe in the shed—I missed them the same evening—I went after the prisoners towards Houns low, and met a policeman—I have seen the two rabbits again—I saw the prisoners going from my mother's cottage on the 5th. CHARLES BAKER. I bought these two rabbits from the prisoners, between four and five o'clock, on the 5th of Jan.—one was alive and one dead, but warm.
   Harrison's Defence.  I met a man close by a row of trees; he asked if I knew any body likely to buy two rabbits; he wanted 4 s . I said I bad no money, I would give him a cod-fish and nine fresh herrings, and he took them.
