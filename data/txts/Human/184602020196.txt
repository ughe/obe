   Cross-examined. Q.  You cannot say whether it was the prisoner or not? A.  I cannot. THOMAS JENKINS DUNNELL. I am the prosecutor's son-in-law, and occa sionally look after his business at Peckham—the prisoner has the sole charge of the gold delivered at that workshop—in Jan. I examined the gold deli vered there, and found a deficiency of 3oz. 13dwts., which is worth about 15 l .—I heard Mr. Seymour ask him if his work was up—he said, "No, not quite—I am a beating and a half deficient—I have done wrong—if you will look over it, I will work it out"—I went and fetched a policeman.
   Cross-examined. Q.  I believe Mr. Seymour said, "He means he will work it further into me?" A.  Yes—at that time we had not ascertained any deficiency, only what he stated himself—the work was given to other parties to finish. JOHN TAPLIN. I finished this work—it weighed loz. only—it ought to have weighed 4oz. 14dwts. SAMUEL WRIGHT( policeman. ) I took the prisoner into custody. GUILTY.— Confined Twelve Months .
