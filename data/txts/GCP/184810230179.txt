2416. JOHN DRAKE, stealing 2 bottles and 6 quarts of gin, value 168.;
the goods of Edwin Stokes, his master.
Edwin STOKES. I keep the White Hart, Woodford-the prisoner was in
my employ, to attend the yard and clean knives, and so on. On 10th Oct.
I went with the policeman to the coach-house, and found the prisoner and
another man, a stone bottle, and a glass one, and about six quarts of gin-
they had no business there—I saw the stone bottle last in the loft-the pri-
soner was not sober.
THOMAS DE LABERTOUCHE ( policeman, R 146). I found the prisoner in the
coach-house-he said the bottle was Mr. Stokes', and the gin came from
London-- on the way to the station he said he was tipsy, and took it from
Mr. Stokes' cellar.
Prisoner. I had been drinking all day; I saw the bottle, and took it.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Six Months.
