594. EDMUND MOORE, stealing 1 coat, value ll. ; the goodsof Thomas
Mills.
FRANCIS LIVERMORE (City policeman, 631). On 8th June, about a quarter
past 9 0'clock in the morning, I Was on duty in Duke-street, Aldgate—l saw
the prisoner coming in a direction from Mr. Mills'—l knew him before—he
Was carrying something under a large blue pilot coat, which be had on—I
followed him to Duke's-place, from there to St. Mary Axe, and then to
Camomile-street—l asked him what he had got—he said he had got nothing
—l undid his coat, and found this coat in front of him—I asked how be
came by it—be said if I would go back, he would take me to the shop where
he bought it—we were close to the station, and I said we would go there
first—he then said he bought it of a man in the lane for 188.—1 asked if
he knew the man—he said, No ' '—he said he was hawking them about the
lane, and he had them on bis arm.
