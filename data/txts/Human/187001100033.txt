 167. DANIEL WILLIAM BRIGHT  (38) , Feloniously shooting at Daniel William Bright the younger, with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm.
   MR. LEWIS conducted the Prosecution; and MR. COLLINS the Defence. DANIEL PIPER. On Sunday evening, 26th December, the prisoner was at my father's house—he left about 2.15 in the morning with his son, Daniel, and me—when we got into the street (Lever Street) the prisoner said, "You will hear a report," and after we had gone about twenty yards I heard a report of a pistol, and saw a flash, and two or three minutes after wards I saw the boy Daniel on the ground—he had been standing in the middle of the road, about a yard from the prisoner—I had been with the prisoner the whole evening; but had not seen a pistol in his possession, nor did I hear him say anything about one.
