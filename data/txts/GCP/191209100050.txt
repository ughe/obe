(Defence.)
THOMAS JONES (prisoner, on oath). I was never employed by Evans
and Sons. I was knocking about on June 17 and 18; things were too
rough to get work.
Cross-examined. I never saw Smith in my life. He does not know
me. My real name is William Gilbey; I have gone in the names of
Jones and Marshall. The writing on this document is not mine. I
do not know where I was on June 18. At the identification Smith
did not exactly come to me. The inspector said, “Is this the man
that had the load!”; he says, "I don't know, all I have come to see
is a bigger man who drove the van." I have never driven a horse and
van or handled a rein. I have been working at several places in the
name of Jones. The insurance card handed to me is mine. I cut
the name out of it. It was a mad thought. I cannot give a reason.
I say the witnesses are all telling falsehoods and swearing my life
away.
Verdict: Not guilty.
