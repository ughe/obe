EZRA HALLIWELL. I am a stuff manufacturer at Bradford, and am a
creditor of Michell for the balance due on a dishonoured cheque on the
St. James's Bank in favour of Keighley and Brother—that was in respect
of a transaction in July, 1878--I had had previous transactions with
Keighley, but not since-nothing was due in July when he ordered the
goods; they were 50 pieces of Russell cord, in two parcels, amounting to
1637. 148.--there were two delivered; one was to be paid for in September,
and the other in October-I saw Michell in my office in the middle of
November with Keighley, who introduced him as his future partner, and
said he was likely to come into between 3,0001. and 4,0001.- I asked him
how long he thought it would be before they got the money-Michell said
about three weeks or a month-I pressed them for the money; I wanted
1031. then-Keighley had paid me 607. out of 1637., and I was pressing
for the 1031.-he said that Michell had some property to be sold before they
could get the money-I fancy I afterwards received this letter of 20th
November. (Mr. Keighley here stated that the letter was in his own writing,
after a conversatiou with Michell. The COURT considered that the letter toas
not evidence.) I afterwards received this cheque (Dated December 10, 1878,
on the St. James's Bank, in favour of J. Keighley or order, drawn by F.
Michell and marked N. 8.)—that was dishonoured—I then wrote, and
Keighley sent me down two 201. notes on the 12th or 13th September
--that 401. came off the 1037., leaving 631., and I received a 202. note on
23rd December, and a balance remains of 431.
Cross-examined. I still hold the dishonoured cheque.
