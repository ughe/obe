Cross-eramined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. There was a great crowd at
that time? A. Yes, when we were coming out of the Gardens--the first
thing I saw was Church on the ground, and Baker on him-I did not see
Bentliff.
GEORGE BAKER. I am a carpenter. I was standing outside the Zoolo-
gical-gardens-there were a great many people, and Radcliffe said there
were three thieves going down-I then saw the prisoners following a gen-
teman, and Church took the handkerchief from the gentleman's pocket-
Rentliff was behind Church, and he beld the tails of his own coat up-
laid hold of Church, with the handkerchief in his hand, and we both fell
down I called to the gentleman that he had been robbed-I lost sight of
Bentliff then, but I am sure he was with Church, and had the tails of his
own coat up.
Cross-examined. Q. Has your name always been George Baker? 4.
Yes. I had been standing outside the gardens about three quarters of an
hour-the company began to come out about half past nine-I had been
at work that day for my brother, in Nevill's-court-he is a printer's joiner
-I was a policeman about six years, and discharged about eight months
ago-1 decline to say what I was discharged for-I never was here but once
while I was a policeman, but I was a patrol before the police came on, and
I attended here then a great many times-since I was in the police I have
been here once, about a boy who stole a pin out of somebody's handker-
chief-Radcliffe is an officer belonging to the Society for the Prevention of
Cruelty-I met him that night by the Elephant and Castle--I had known
him for some time-I had a job of work to go to next day-I was not look-
ing out for a job.
Q. Did you receive 2s. 6d. when you were in the police ? A. I was
accused of it by a reputed thief-I did not receive it from him-the Com-
missioners did not make inquiries into it-I was charged before a magis-
trate with taking this 28. 6d. to suppress evidence, the result was I was
discharged from the police--the witness against me has been in prison
since this, which I shall now be able to prove.
Q. When was it after the covering with the coat tails that you saw
Bentliff again? A. On the 12th of the next month, when the Queen went
to the house I was then standing there by myself-Radcliffe was a wit-
ness with me in the case for stealing the pin.
THOMAS RADCLIFTE. I am an officer of the Animals' Friend Society. I
was at the Zoological Gardens and noticed the two prisoners and another
person-I spoke to Baker-I noticed Church following the gentleman, and
the other two persons covering him, holding up his coat tails--I saw
Church put his hand into the gentleman's pocket, but I could not see what
he took out-Baker took hold of Church, and I took Bentlift, but seeing
Baker and Church fall, I went to them.
