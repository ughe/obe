CHARLES EAST. I live at 36, Brook Street, Chorlton-on-Medlock, Manchester, and am a hairdresser—on 25th September I shaved the prisoner at Manchester at a quarter to nine—after I shaved him I had about two minutes' conversation with him—he said he had had a bit of a bother with his wife and was going to London for a fortnight or three weeks till it blew over—I said, "It would do you more good if you put the fare (15s. 5 1/2 d.) on Cataract for the Great Eastern Handicap"—I backed it, it won, and I was paid.
