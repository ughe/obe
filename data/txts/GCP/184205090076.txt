1536. GRACE FAIRMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
April, 3 balfpence, the monies of John Whitehouse, her master.
John WHITEHOUSE. I am a linendraper, and live in Oxford-street.
The prisoner was my shopwoman-I gave ninepence in marked money to
Mr. Wilkes to be given to his servant-it was to be sent to my shop to
buy three yards of ribbon-I have since seen fourpence of the marked
money-it was taken from the prisoner's pocket within five minutes of the
time the money was given.
Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. What were the coins that you
saw which the officer took from the prisoner? A. Four penny pieces-I
had a character with the prisoner from her sister, with whom it was stated
she lived fifteen years, but this circumstance throws much doubt upon it-
the money I first gave was a sixpence, & penny, and four halfpence-I
made a private mark on the coins-I took particular notice of them in
order to be correct, my intentions being to set a trap-I gave tenpence
halfpenny in the second instance, but I cannot speak positively to the
coins--my impression is that it was a sixpence, four penny pieces, and a
halfpenny, but I cannot say-I gave that to Mr. Taylor to give to his
servant-he is not here, nor is Mr. Wilkes--the three halfpence now
charged applies to the money I gave Mr. Wilkes.
