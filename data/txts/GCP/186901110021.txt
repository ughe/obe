178. CHARLES HIND (34), Stealing a coat, of Timothy Sullivan.
MR. BRINDLEY conducted the Prosecution.
CATHERINE SULLIVAN, I am the wife of Timothy Sullivan, of 2, North-
Eust Passage, Wellelose Square-about 12 o'clock on Christmas night, my
husband brought the prisoner home, and asked me to get him a cup of tea,
as he was very ill-we made him as comfortable as we could, and he laid
down in our room, covered with my husband's coat-about 5 o'clock I went
down stairs, leaving him in the room, and as I was returning up the stairs
I met him on the lower landing I said, “What made you come down,
won't you have some tea ?" he looked on one side and passed out, and as
he went out, I saw that he had my husband's coat under his arm; it was
the coat that had been covered over him, it was worth 128. when I went
up into the room again I missed it.
GEORGE BROWN (Policeman H R 24). I took the prisoner into custody-
I told him he was charged with stealing a coat on Christmas morning-he
said he did not steal the coat; he slept in the house, but he was no thief.
Prisoner's Defence. I acknowledge sleeping in the house, but I know
nothing about the coat.
Nor GUILTY.
179. CHARLES HIND (34), was again indicted for stealing a quilt, of
Henry Adolph Hasselbrook.
MR. BRINDLEY conducted the Prosecution
Henry ADOLPH HASSELBROOK. I keep the Brunswick Arms, Hooper
Square, Whitechapel-on 28th December the prisoner took a bed at my
house-he said he was a seafaring man, and had come from the South Coast
of Africa when I got up, a little after 8 o'clock in the morning, he was
gone.
MARY MANNING. I am servant to last witness-on 28th December, the
prisoner had a bed at our house-I was up at 6 o'clock in the morning-the
prisoner came down about 8.40, and went out about half an hour after-
wards I went up stairs to make the bed, and the quilt was gone off the
bed-no one but the prisoner occupied the room.
Prisoner. Q. Did not I speak to you when I went out 1 A. You asked
me what was to pay--I said I did not know, I would go up to the master;
but you did not not give me time, you walked out and were gone before I
returned-you might bave concealed the quilt about your person.
Prisoner's Defence. I am not guilty. She must have seen something
about me if I had got it.
Guilty-Six Months' Imprisonment.
