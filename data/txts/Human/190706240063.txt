   BEFORE THE RECORDER
 (Thursday, June 27.) RYDER  , Madine (19, lady's maid), pleaded guilty to stealing a watch and chain, the property of Edith May Richards and felo niously receiving same. Stealing a coat and other articles, the pro perty of Valiquet Roma Loyd , and feloniously receiving same; steal ing a bicycle, the property of Edward Thompson Morris and another and feloniously receiving same; obtaining by false pretences from Paul Stefain divers boxes of chocolate and moneys of the value of 30s., with intent to defraud. She was released on joint recognizance of herself and Mr. Scott-France, the court missionary, in £25 each, on her undertaking to go to a home for 12 months, and to come up for judgment if called upon.
