 STEPHEN RENEW. I am a watchman. About half-past eleven o'clock I was two doors from the King and Queen, and heard a call of Watch! and Murder! - I called Williams over, and we both went in; I put my back against the door, and saw the prosecutor with his breeches down and his hat off - he said he was robbed, and gave Allen in charge - he staid at the bar.
 WILLIAM WILLIAMS. I am a watchman. I went into the house and saw the prosecutor - he said he was robbed; Allen stood against the bar - Renew sent me for the inspector, and he asked Fairburn if he could point out any body; he pointed out Allen - he was capable of answering me - he at first said he had two sovereigns, but at the watch-house he said two and a half; he said he had lost his watch, but on pulling his breeches up he found that - he was a little in liquor, but gave the name of the watch-maker.
 Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Was he drunk or sober? A. I cannot say he was particularly drunk - he appeared a little intoxicated and flurried; I saw him push my partner from the door - I do not know why.
   EDWARD WICKS. I am an inspector of the watch. I was called in - the prosecutor was in front of the bar, with his clothes in a disordered state; I saw his watch handed over to the landlord - the prosecutor and Allen were having an altercation; Allen was saying, "Did I rob you?" or, "Was it I robbed you?" I then said,"Did this man rob you?" he said, "If he did not, he knows who did;" he was throwing his arms about and stamping - I cannot say he was sober; I searched Allen, and found on him two pieces of chain, and he handed over another piece - the two I found proved to be a whole chain, and had one brass key attached to it; I found a sixpence and a few halfpence on him - as I took the chain from him, the prosecutor said it was his, but on the Monday morning he disowned it, and it was restored to Allen.
 Cross-examined. Q.You have heard him say to-day it was not the same chain? A. It was the same as I found on the prisoner - I thought the prosecutor confused, and a little in liquor.
 THOMAS NICHOLS. I am constable of the night. Allen was brought to the watch-house - part of a chain was found on him, which the prosecutor claimed, and afterwards said it was not his; I considered him very much in liquor - he stated what he had lost.
 Cross-examined. Q. He seemed very much intoxicated? A. Yes; he at first said he had lost two sovereigns - then said two sovereigns and a half, and a black leather purse.
 HENRIETTA PARRINGTON. My father keeps the King and Queen; the prosecutor was not sober when he came in - when he came out of the tap-room he complained of losing his purse and chain; Allen said, "If you suspect me take me to the watch-house and search me."
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you see him strike your father? A. Yes; he also struck the watchman - when he first came into the house he stood at the bar, put a sovereign on the counter, and wanted to bet about a patent for blowing the gas out; no wager was laid - he appeared to have drank a great deal before he came in.
 COURT. Q.Did he charge any body besides Allen with robbing him? A. Yes, the watchman that he struck, and he also charged my father with it - I am sure my father had no candle when he came into the tap-room.

 452.  MARY MURRAY was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of December , 1 desk, value 20s.; 2 watches, value 7l.; 4 seals, value 20s.; 2 rings, value 6s.; 1 pencil-case, value 2s., and 1 book, value 2s. the goods of Joseph Gardiner , in the dwelling-house of the Reverend Sir John Robinson , Bart.
 JOSEPH GARDINER. I am now out of a situation. On the 20th of December I was dining in the servant's hall, at the Reverend Sir John Robinson's,  Albemarle-street; I heard a knock in the area, looked up, and saw the prisoner going up the steps with something under her shawl - I did not know her before; she had slipped into the area - I went into the pantry, and missed my desk, which I had seen there a quarter of an hour before; the area leads to the pantry - the desk contained the articles stated in the indictment; the watches were silver - I gave 10l. for one ten years ago, and five guineas for the other four years ago - I value them at 7l. 10s.; I value all the property at 8l. 11s.
 JOHN POULTON. I am servant to Sir John Robinson, and was dining in the servant's hall. I heard a noise in the area steps, and saw the prisoner going up; I ran out and saw her crossing the road, with this desk under her arm - I followed and took her with it.
 Prisoner. Q. Did I not give it to you and tell you I had seen a person drop it? No; I saw her going up the steps with it - she said it was a box of linen; I said No, it was a desk.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was very much in liquor; I saw a person drop the desk down the area - I ran down and slipped; I took it up, and directly the man came up I gave it to him. GUILTY (of stealing to the value of 99s. only.) Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
 First London Jury - Before Mr. Recorder.
