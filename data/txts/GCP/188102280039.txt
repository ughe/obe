MESSRS. HORACE AVORY and MEAD Proseouted; MESSRS. GEOGHEGAN and

ABRAHAM BROMET. I am a wholesale jeweller, in partnership with
Emmanuel Levy, of 38, Jewin Street, City-I deal in cheap jewellery-
Hutchinson was a watchman in my service he droye a traveller's trap
on the 8th of September last he was out with it; when he returned
he reported the loss of a bag of jewellery of the value of about 401.-on
the 7th of October he returned home and reported the loss of another
bag of jewellery of the value of 301.-believing his story, I still con-
tinued him in my service on the 26th October he returned, reporting
the loss of the whole things, horse, trap, jewellery, and everything, in-
cluding a rug and a waterproof coat-the contents of the trap were worth
1207.; the horse and trap were worth about 801,- he gave me a descrip
tion of the way he lost it-I discharged him I placed the matter in the
hands of the police -I was taken to various places, where I identified my
property, some, produced by Mr. Motcham, of the wholesale value of
281. 178. 4d. ; by Mr. Hayes, 101. 188. 88.; by Mr. Wortowitsch,
87. 108. 10d. ; Mr. Muggeridge, 21. 118. 30.; Mr. G. Wortowitsch

bach, 211 168. 4.; Mr. Schworer, 11. 108. 4d.; Mr. Knight, 198.
Mr. Sullivan, 41. 13. 3d. ; Mr. Kesler, 68. 3d.; Mr. Warren, 28., and
Mr. Wallace, 31. 78. 100.-I have also seen my property found on the
prisoner's premisès, of the value of 297. 168. 9d.- the total value of pro-
perty stolen is about 1901.-I saw the rug at the police-station-the horse
and trap were returned the day after the loss, after my partner went to
see it at Walthamstow.
