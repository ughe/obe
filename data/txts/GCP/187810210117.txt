David's Defence. It has been hinted that no money passed between me
and Hollis. Where is the witness to prove that? Whenever I went to his
shop with the glue I always found him and gave him the receipt and he
gave me the money; on no occasion has he failed in giving me the amount:
in fact, I took over and above the amount on the receipts. Mr. Hollis, being
no scholar, did not deduct the 5. per cent. I always had the money for
every ounce of glue that was taken, even to the last. We went into two
other public houses and joined friends, and I met a third man and gave
him 51., and that accounts for no more than 168. or 176. being found
on me.
STEPHEN MARONY (Re-examined). I did not stop them when they came
out of the first public-house--they went inside two other public houses--I
did not go in, but I watched them and never lost sight of them.
