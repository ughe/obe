CLARISSA MATILDA MORNE. I keep a Berlin-wool shop, at Thomas-street,
Woolwich. On the 7th of July, about three o'clock, the prisoners came
together to my shop-Wild said, “Will you oblige me with pen, ink, and
paper, as I have lost two notes, and do not like to go back for a third"-she
asked me to write for her- I wrote the note produced-Wild told me what to
write--I then said, "It is to be signed"-she said, “Yes, J. Edwards"-
wrote " J. Edwards"-I inquired if it was to be sealed-she said it was of no
consequence-I gave it to Wild and she gave it to Lawson-1 had never
seen Lawson before-Wild had been a customer of mine-she said she was
tired, and told Lawson to take the note-she went away with it, and Wild
remained at my shop about twenty minutes-Lawson came back and said,
“Mrs. Sonnex will send it; here is your half-crown," and gave her half-4-
crown-she had other money in her hand, I did not notice what it was
Wild had purchased goods in my shop, and paid me 38. 6d. for them--I had
to give her 2d. change, which she refused to take-she tied some money in
the corner of her handkerchief, and they went away together.
Cross-examined by MR. BALDWIN. Q. Have you seen Wild often? .
Yes, more than once-she is a cripple-I was not surprised at her being tired
and sitting down-I never heard anything against her character-she said,
have lost two notes-Lawson was there at the time.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Did not Wild, in addition to what
you have told us, say, "Be careful, and do not lose the note ?" 4. Yes.
JAMES EDWARDS. I am a smith, and live at Woolwich, and supply the
town with water-I know the two prisoners-I never authorized them to go
to Mrs. Sonnex, to get money for me.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Your name is not James only? 4.
No, James Thomas Edwards.
WILLIAM GLADWIN (police-constable.) On the 11th of July I took both
the prisoners into custody- I took Wild first-I told her she was charged
with obtaining 21. from Mrs. Sonnex-she said Lawson told her to go and do
it-Lawson was not present-I took Lawson-she said Wild tempted her to
do it, or she would never have done such a thing.
(The prisoners received good characters.)
WILD-Guilty. Recommended to mercy by Prosecutor
