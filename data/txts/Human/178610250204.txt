 875.  JOHN CLOUD was indicted for feloniously stealing, on the 16th day of October last, thirty guineas, value 31 l. 10 s. and two half guineas, value 1 l. 1 s. the property of James Kensington , in the dwelling house of William Cato.
 (The case opened by Mr. Silvester.)
 (The witnesses examined apart, at the desire of Mr. Garrow.)
 Mrs. KENSINGTON sworn.
 I am wife of  James Kensington, No. 16, Weymouth-street, Mary-le-bone; I occupy the kitchen.
 Whose is the house? - It is the house of  William Cato; he lives in the two pair of stairs floor; the prisoner worked at Mrs. May's, turning a mangle; he was very poor and low, and had been a great while, till since we have lost our money; I saw the money on the 11th of this month, early in the morning; I kept it in a little drawer, at the top of the chest of drawers locked; I wrapped it very curious in a pair of black gloves; the gloves were left open, and the money was gone; I did not miss it till the Monday following; when I missed it, I found something had been put between the drawers and the lock, and bad wrenched it down; I missed it going to pay our rent; he came down into the cellar the back way with a kettle, and came up again, and stood at the street door; the next day he never came to his work; on Friday he came to work, but was in liquor; on Saturday he was in a great hurry to fetch his things out of pawn; on Sunday evening I saw him so much in liquor, that he could not unlock his room door; which was a stone's throw from our house; he was a dressed like a gentleman; when I found the money was all gone, it struck into my head it must be Mr. Cloud, and so I thought by all these appearances; we held our tongues all that day; in the evening Mr. Connelley and him came in very much in liquor; and as they were going out, we asked him to come to work the next morning; he said he would; but he never came; we took him to the Magistrate's.
 Was you present when he was brought? - Yes; he first said, that his cousin gave him the money; then he said, he worked for it; it appeared he had lain out in that week, in one way or the other, upwards of twenty pounds, with what he got out of pawn.
 Mr. Garrow, Prisoner's Counsel. How long have you known him? - A great while; he has worked for me ever since the beginning of April; I have heard say his wife and him have lived very creditable; but very much reduced.
