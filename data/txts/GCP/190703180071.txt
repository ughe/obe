and went to Bewdley. After being in different employments at Leeds
and Birmingham I came up to London, and was for some months
private secretary to Sir Henry Burdett. In 1900 I became assistant
secretary of the Frederick Hotels, Limited; I stayed there three and
s half years. I then borrowed some money from my wife's aunt and
started business in the City as a commission agent. Late in 1905 I
again went to Russia, and returned early last year. I could obtain no
regular employment, and my financial posiuon gradually got worse and
worse. I found that the condition of affairs made me rather erratio
and in a low state of health; I was frequently without food.

time when she was having some quarrels with Mr. Rayner--that if ever
