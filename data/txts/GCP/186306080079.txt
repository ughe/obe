828. JAMES SNOAD LAWES (44), Unlawfully obtaining within three
months of his bankruptcy a quantity of port wine from Alfred Thaokor,
under the false colour and pretence of carrying on his business in the
ordinary course, with intent to defraud. Other counts varying the mode of
charge. MessR8. HUDDLESTON, Q.C., SARGOOD, TALFOURD and Sarker,
conducted the Prosecution.
MR. GUEPARD applied, upon the authority of Young and Others v. the King
in Error, to have some of the counts quashed, or that the prosecution should elect
upon which set of counts they would proceed; the counts relating to different
transactions, and embarrassing the prisoner's defence. Mr. HUDDLESTON com
tended they were part of the same transaction THE COURT (after consulting
MR. BARON BRAMWELL) was of opinion it would have been better if there had
been two indictments, inasmuch as the transactions appeared to be distinct, but
in the absence of any more recent authority than Young and Othere v. the King
in Error, the Court could not quash the indictment.
