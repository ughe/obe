HENRY CHARLES BARKER police-inspector, 8). On 29th May, Dowden
was brought to the station at half-past five o'clock-about a quarter to nine,
Brown was brought-Dowden called him Jones- I heard a conversation

Jones said, “For a crack"-Dowden said, “Have they found anything on
you ?” “No ;" "Well they will find a swag on me; you are all right; I
shall be settled and dead lagged."
Dowden's Defence. If I had plundered the house, I should have had

the robbery.
