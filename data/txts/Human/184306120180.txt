 1935. JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of June , three shillings, the monies of Thomas Lloyd ; and THOMAS RABONE as an accessory.
   MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution. ELIZABETH LLOYD. I am the wife of Thomas Lloyd, a gun-maker, in Henry-street, Stepney. About half-past seven o'clock on the 7th of June, Smith came to me and said, "Mr. Lloyd has sent me for 3 s ."—I said, "Where is Mr. Lloyd's own lad?"—he said, "He is out"—I asked his name and where he lived—he said, "In Berner's-street, Commercial-road"—I asked what Mr. Lloyd wanted the money for—he said, "To buy some tools"—I believed him, and gave him three shillings—my husband came soon after, and I told him.
