Edward Groves, his master.
MR. THOMPSON conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY CLEMOW. I am in the service of Edward Groves, a clothier,
Lower-marsb, Lambeth-the prisoner was in his service as salesman
March, 1858, till August, 1861–this coat (prodaced) is the property
Edward Groves-our mark is in the sleeve-the value of it is 122 6d.
Cross-examined by MR. LILLEY. Q. Has Mr. Groves two establishmental
4. Yes; one in the Edgeware-road, and one in the New-cut-I think then
are about seven persons in the New-cut who sell articles of clothing; and
five, six, or seven in the Edgeware-road—that is the mark, in ink, on the
sleeve--two clerks keep the books of our establishment--the cashier taku
the money--he has to enter every sale--I have not searched the books-the
cashier is not here I have been in Mr. Groves' employ turned six years

MR. THOMPSON. Q. You can swear to its having been marked within
three years! A. It is my own mark; I marked it myself-all the articles

is what I should describe as a new coat.
CAMPBELL POOLE I am in the service of Mr. Robinson, a pawnbroker,
