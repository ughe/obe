represents where the knife entered, between the sixth and seventh ribs :

the external wound would be as near as possible at the lower border of the
sixth rib-I did not take this photograph-I tried the experiments myself,

prisoner-I think the attorney for the prosecution mentioned to me in

a photograph of the parts involved, the ribs-he suggested that I should
