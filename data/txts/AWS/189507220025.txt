HANNAH PENFOLD. I am married-I work at Mr. Whiteley's jam
factory at Hanworth-or July 5th I was passing through 35 about seven
in the morning-] saw the prisoner there, he was picking currants-he
said he wanted two lamps-I went to the lamp shed and brought him
two lamps, I lit one of them he said he did not require the other at
present] left it there, and the matches to light the otherI left the
lighted lamp where he was working, it was not on a bench, I think it
must have been a table, I forget, it was some kind of structure-I don't
think it was on the sacks of straw-I left a few minutes after.
