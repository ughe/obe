ELIZABETH HUTCHINSON. I am the wife of John Hutchinson, a carpenter,
of 43, Ponsonby-place, Pimlico-the prisoner lodged in my honse for a
month, from 27th April, to 24th May she occupied a furnished room with
a man, and went by the name of Gifford—they brought two boxes and a
carpet bag with them-the large box was taken away on the Saturday night
before they left, and the rest on the Sunday—I afterwards saw those things
at Stones-end station-on Satarday, 2d May, the prisoner borrowed 6d. of
me-she did not say she had no money, she said, would I lend her 6d. till
her husband came home-on 6th May, I saw the prisoner wearing the
brooch produced--they left on the 7th, and did not return till the 19th.
MARY PRITCHARD. I live at 15, Lambeth-square, and am the wife of
Thomas Pritchard, an engineer-on 24th May, the prisoner came to my
house-I had never seen her before-4 boxes had been brought that day by
a man, and given in charge to a lodger who has left-the prisoner asked me
if any boxes were left there--I said "Yes," and showed her down stairs-they
remained in my possession till the next day, when they were taken possession.
of by the police I have not seen them since.
ROBERT Dixon. I am a sawyer, and reside at 11, Waterloo-street, 8,
Philip's, Bristol-I know the prisoner she was living at Bristol until 17th
April-she lived there with a man named Oram, as his wife-I know she
was married to him-I was present at the marriage, at Calne, in Wiltshire-
about March, 1854-it was at a chapel, not a Church of England-I don't
know the name of the chapel -I married a sister of the prisoner's the
prisoner was living with her husband at Bristol, part of the time from
1854–I can't answer for all the time, because I was not living with them.
GUILTY on the second count.-Judgment respited.
