DIMSDALE, Mayor.
72. JOHN CARROLL (36), Stealing six cheques, the property of
Newton, Chambers & Co.
MR. RANDOLPH Prosecuted, and MR. PURCELL Defended.
JAMB CHALLEN MILLER. I am manager to Newton, Chambers & Co.
of Gray's Inn Road—their remises have a letter box opening on to the
street—on November 18th certain letters which I knew to have
come to hand, and on the 19th I examined the letter box and found a
sticky, adhesive substance on the flap and also inside—I commuuicated
with the police—I drew this cheque for (Produced), and sent it to a
man named Morris—I never got it back through the post office—this
other cheque for C7 78. 6d. is in favour of my company from Dredge—
it did not come in the ordinary course—this cheque for C4 48. by Tollett
& Wentworth did not arrive, nor did the letter relating to it ; nor did this
cheque for C3 128., with a memorandum and statement of account—nor
did this account of J. S. Percival ever reach me.
Cross-eaamind. This cheque in favour of Mr. Morris was originally
crossed—that has been obliterated very cleverly, but you can see it if you
look for it—a cashier would probably pass it.
ISABEL DREDGE. I am the wife of Frederick Dredge, the proprietor of
the Saracen's Head, Snow Hill on 15th I gave the "Boots
this cheque for $7 78. 6d. to post—it never came back—I afterwards
received an intimation from Newton, Chambers & Co.
MARE WESTMORE. On November 15th I was "Boots at the Saracen's
Head, and Mrs. Dredge gave me some letters to post, including one to
Messrs. Chambers—I posted them.
HARRY FREETH. I am clerk to Messrs. Tollett & Wentworth, solicitors
—this cheque for C4 48. is drawn by them, and this is the statement of
account—I placed them in an envelope on November 15th, addressed to
Newton & Chambers and posted them—I never had them back.
HENRY HASWELL. I am clerk to Garsford, Gray & Co., merchanta of
Lndon Wall—this cheque for C3 128. was drawn by them to Newton,
Chambers & enclosed it on November 16th with this statement Of
account in an envelope and posted it—it did not come back.
MARIA LOUISA FOSKE. I am in the employ of the General Post omee,
and have to deal with letters that are re-directed—on November 15th I
dealt With a letter to Parolles Of Upper Holloway—I have seen this cheque
for $1 before but it is different, it was crossed with two lines and " & Co."
—I enclosed it in a cover, and addressed it to Messrs. Newton—I have
never had it back.

ROBERT Scom (200 C). On November 23rd the prisoner vas in my

—he tried to put his right hand in his left jacket pocket—he got a small
