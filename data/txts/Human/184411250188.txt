HENRY SCOTT( police-constable P  241.) I took the prisoner into cus-tody on the prosecutor's charge—I went up with him, and saw the two pieces of ribbon—he took them out of the box, and handed them to me—the box was unlocked—the prisoner was not present—she was not in the house—I received the shawl from the prosecutor also.
   Prisoner's Defence . I took the shawl out of the closet in the shop boy's bedroom, where there were some old things; I did not think it would be missed; the two pieces of ribbon I took out of the show-room, when I was cleaning it in the morning. JOHN BUCKLAND re-examined . The shawl was taken from the upper room—it was not thrown by, as not to be used again—the value of the ribbon is about 6 s ., as near as I can form an idea, and the shawl 3 s .—I have inquired as to her former character, and believe her to be led away by the vanity of dress—there was much more valuable property within her reach. GUILTY  . Aged 19.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prosecutor  .— Judgment Respited  .
