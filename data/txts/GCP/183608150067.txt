of the house opposite, the prisoner walked out of the shop with a cont
under his arm-I had watched him go into the shop-I went after him,
and brought him back with the coat on his arm--the other man ran off as
soon as he saw me cross over.
WILLIAM Henry Boswell. I am a tailor, and live in King William-

looking round I missed a coat which had been on a figure at the door--I

the shop, and I left nobody in the shop.
