HOLLINGs the Defence.
THOMAs COLLIsoN. I am potman at Hiok's Tavern, King-street, Soho-
on the 1st of February the prisoner came there and tendered me 8 florin

it, and told him it was the second time he had been there-I told my
master, who gave him in charge with the coin.
Cross-examined. I am the only potman-the barmaid was there-I had
