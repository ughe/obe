2065. HENRY WILLIAM HIDE was again indicted for stealing, on
the 28th of May, 1 yard of velvet, value 145.; and 1 table cloth, 58.; the
goods of Harry Winterbourne and another, his masters.
Mr. Doane conducted the Prosecution.
HARRY WINTERBOURNE. The prisoner was in our service upwards of three
years-on his being taken into custody bis lodging was searched in my pre-
sence-I saw a duplicate of a waistcoat found this is the waistcoat-it 18
made of velvet such as I had in my warehouse on the 28th of May-I missed
a yard on that day, and this waistcoat corresponds with it I have matched
and compared it, and have no doubt of it--it would make up my deficiency-
this table cloth is mine-it has our cost mark and selling price on it--I have
strictly searched all my books and find no entry of it having been sold to
Hide.
