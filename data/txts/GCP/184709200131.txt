2193. JAMES CASEY was indicted for feloniously cutting and wounding
Charles Wrench on the neck and throat, with intent to murder bim :--2nd
Count, stating his intent to be to do him some grievous bodily harm.
MR. RYLAND conducted the Prosecution.

other inmates-the prisoner was near me he left, and then came back
again shortly, caught hold of the back part of my head by the hair and shook
me I had not said anything to him-I went a little way further on in the
