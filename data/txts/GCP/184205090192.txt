Cross-examined by MR, BALLANTINE. Q. Have you ever paid for any
of these suits of clothes that you have ordered? A. No-I am indebted
to the tailor for them-I paid for two prior suits-I am involved in debt
--that is not the reason I have indicted the prisoner-I am involved
through the prisoner-1 bave been drawn out of a fine business, and to-
tally ruined- I married the daughter of his second wife, in March, 1840,
about nine months after this pretended suit was entered into-she is
eighteen years of age now-I do not think she is under eighteen-I cannot
speak precisely to her age-I understood she was turned sixteen when I
married her-I had known the prisoner nearly twelve months before I
married her- I had no acquaintance with Wall until the prisoner intro-
duced me to him at the time I married I was in a flourishing business,
in Queen-street, Cheapside--at the time of my first acquaintance with the
prisoner, I slept at Clapton-I had no dwelling-house of my own-at the
time I married I was lodging in Tredegar-square, Mile-end-road-I took
my wife home there after our marriage-we resided there for about a
month, and then removed to Herman-hill-I was in furnished lodgings in
Tredegar-square-we occupied the ground floor for part of the time, and
the latter part of the time we had the first floor.
