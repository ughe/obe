I am an officer of the fire brigade stationed in Highgate Road—about 6 o'clock on Saturday, 23rd November, I was called to a fire at Sutherland House—I found the back-room first-floor well on fire—it had been partly extinguished by the Highgate Volunteer Fire Brigade—the room and its contents had been severely damaged by fire and water—the boards were severely charred or burnt. JAMES WARD. I am a grocer, and live in High Street, Highgate—on Saturday, 24th November, I delivered some goods at Mrs. Campbell's—the prisoner took them in—I had heard of the fire—I asked her if the fire was extinguished—she did not answer—I then said "Is the fire burning now?"—she said "No, but it will break out again."
   The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate . "I did not do it." In her defence she denied suggesting to her mistress the purchase of the oil, and asserted that the, statement she made to the officer was obtained from her by threats.
   She received a good character from a former master, who expressed his willingness to take her again into his service. NOT GUILTY.
