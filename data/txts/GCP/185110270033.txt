watch (produced ); he wanted me to change it for a gold one; he bought a
gold watch, and a mosaic brooch, for which he had to pay me 91. and the
Geneva watch-he gave me the Bank post-bill marked "G"--I saw him
write this " John Tomlin" on the back of it -- I gave him 411. 10s. change-
this gold watch and brooch produced) are what I sold to him.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you seen the party before with whom you made
