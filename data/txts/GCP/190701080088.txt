THIRD COURT; Tuesday, January 15..
(Before Judge Lumley Smith.)
BOOKER, Charles Henry (38, draper's assistant); carnally know-
ing his daughter, Ellen Elizabeth Booker, a girl above the age of 13
years, and under the age of 16 years.
Mr. G. L. Hardy prosecuted; Mr. Charles Doughty defended.
Verdiót, Not guilty.
It appearing that prisoner had been arrested upon the girl's mere
statement, without a warrant, and without information being laid,
Judge Lumley Smith directed the inspector in charge of the case to
inquire of his superiors whether it was right to arrest a man on
charge of this kind, on what the girl had said and the result of the
doctor's examination, and whether there should not be some prelimi-
nary steps taken.
