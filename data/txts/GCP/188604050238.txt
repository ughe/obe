GEORGE SMITH. I live at 26, Maryland Street, Stratford-I am
employed by the Great Eastern Railway Company-about 12.45 on
21st March I went into the Serpent beer-house-I saw the prisoner
making his escape over the wall-I followed him with Thread gold, and
was with Threadgold when the hat was found.
HANNAS SCHLOBSER. I am the wife of Henry Schlosser, the proprietor
of the Serpent beer-house, 33, High Street, Deptford—on 21st March I
closed the premises about 11 o'clock-every door and window was
fastened properly—by the back door there is a window which cannot be
opened-after the burglary I found one window opened-the bolts of the
door were pushed back-the bolts can be reached from the window by &
stick-a broomstick was found near the window-I missed 58. in
coppers, and about 18 or 20 half ounces of tobacco-it was all safe before
I left the house we were going back there to sleep.
THOMAS GAMES (Policeman K 372). Between 2 and 3 p.m. on 21st
March, in consequence of what I was told I went to Meeson's Wharf,
High Street, Stratford-I saw the prisoner; I told him I should take
him into custody for breaking into Mr. Schlosser's beer-house about a
quarter to 12-he said “What, me? I was in bed at a quarter to 12 at
night " I took him to the police station-I searched him-I found 11d.
on him in bronze-he was wearing a hat, the same make as this one
produced.
THOMAS WELLS TAYLOR. I keep the Bow Chambers lodging-house
at Bow-the prisoner lodges there—he came in on Sunday morning,
21st March, about 1.40-I was visiting my dormitory to see who was in
and who was out-I met the prisoner entering the hall, he bid me
"Good night” and went to bed.
The Prisoner's Defonce. I was wearing the same hat on the wharf
when they took me-it is all false- I was not near the premises.
