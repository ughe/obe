389. JAMES HOY was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of December,
1 basket, value 9d.; 2 smoothing planes, value 4s. 6.; 1 axe, value
13. 60.; 8 chisels, value 8s.; 1 square, value Is. 60. ; 2 gouges, value 28. ;
1 bevil, value 1s. 6d.; 1 screw-driver, value 18. 6d. ; 1 pair of pincers,
value 1s. ; 1 mallet, value 3d. ; 4 gimlets, value 6d.; 3 brad-awls, value
6d.; and 1 hand-saw, value 58.; the goods of William Barton: and that
he had been before convicted of felony.
WILLIAM BARTON. I live at Camberwell, and am a carpenter. I had
the basket and the tools stated-1 left them in a building at New-cross,
Deptford, at six o'clock in the evening, on Wednesday the 4th of Decem-
ber-I missed them the next morning at seven o'clock - these are them.
BENJAMIN HASELDINE. I am in the service of Mr. Greenly, a pawn-
broker in Broomfields. The prisoner pledged these things on the morning
of the 5th of December, for Is. 6d.
Prisoner. At the time you say they were pledged, I was at home in
bed-you said you were not certain of me, and the Magistrate told you
you should swear to me. Witness. I have no doubt he is the man-I
told the Magistrate I was almost positive-I have no doubt of him.
JAMES HAIRSINE, (police-constable R 37.) I went after the prisoner-
I saw him, and called after him by name-he said, "My name is George
Williams"-I said he must go with me to Mr. Greenly's-he said he did
not know him-I said, "Don't you know him?"--he said, "He is a
pawnbroker, but I never was in his shop"—I took him to the shop, and the
witness said he was the man who brought the tools.
• GEORGE DORSETT (police-constable R 94.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from the vestry clerk's office,
of Surrey-(read)-the prisoner is the man.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Transported for Seven Years.
