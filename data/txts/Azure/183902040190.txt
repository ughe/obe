JOHN WARD. lam a publican, and live in Gray's Inn-lane. On the
morning of the 8th of February I was in Mr. Stock's shop, and heard a
noise, as if glass was breaking—I waited, and ten minutes after I saw
a dirty hand come through the window, and take out a metal cock, through
the broken pane—I rushed out and caught the three prisoners in my arms,
standing as near together as possible—I looked over them, and saw the
cock in one of their hands, I do not know which—Downs bobbed his
head, and got a short distance from me, and I saw him throw the cock
down—I let the others go, and followed Downs—I saw Mr. Stock coming
towards me—I then ceased to follow Downs, and followed the other two,
and secured theri both, without losing sight of them hardly a moment—I am
certain they are the three—I had watched them for two hours previous to
the robbery, Mr. Stock having lost two cocks that morning—and I had
seen the three prisoners about his window six times that morning—Mr.
Stock lives next door to me.
