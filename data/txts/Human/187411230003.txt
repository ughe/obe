   Before Mr. Recorder.
 1. RICHARD ARTHUR CARDEN  (42), and CHARLES CLAVER  HOUSE MURRAY (18) , Were charged upon several indictments for forging and uttering three orders for 2 l  . with intent to defraud; also  for unlawfully obtaining nineteen cheques from the Cheque Bank by false pretences.
   CARDEN PLEADED GUILTY**— Seven Years' Penal Servitude . No evidence was offered against MURRAY . NOT GUILTY
 2. FREDERICK KINGWELL  (27) , Unlawfully obtaining by false pretences, from Thomas Hussey , 25 l  . with intent to defraud.
