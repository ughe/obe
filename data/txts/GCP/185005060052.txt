937. MARIA HANLEY, stealing 1 shirt, value 1s.; the goods of George
Lovett; having been before convicted.
ELIZABETH Lovett. I am the wife of George Lovett, of Perkins'-rents,
Peter-street, Westminster. On 19th April, I had a shirt hanging out to dry
in the yard-I saw it safe at half past seven o'clock in the morning, and
missed it in half an hour-the prisoner lodged with me at the top of the
house-after I missed the shirt, I went up, and accused her of taking it, and
she denied it-I told her nobody had been in the yard but her-there were
four or five young men in the room with her, and in consequence of what
one of them said, I went to Mr. Williams's pawn-shop in Great Smith-street,
and found the shirt there-I went back, and told the prisoner I had found
the shirt pawned for 6d., and if she gave me the ticket I would get it, and
she would have no more bother about it-she refused, and I gave her in
charge--this is the shirt produced)-it is my husband's.
