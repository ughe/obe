 862.  WILLIAM FIELD was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of April , 1 brass stand, value 1s. 6d., and 1 pair of tongs, value 1s. 6d., the goods of John James Rippon.
   ROBERT CARRON. I am shopman to Mr. John James Rippon, ironmonger, Castle-street, Marylebone. On the evening of the 10th of April I was in the shop, and saw the prisoner take the stand and tongs from the window - he went a little way down the street; I followed him, and took him with them - he said he was very sorry, but he had done it from want.
   WILLIAM WALSH(Police-constable E 54). On the 10th of April I took the prisoner with these things. GUILTY. Aged 26.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Confined One Month.
 863.  MARY LINCH was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of March , 1 coat, value 18s., the goods of William Forster.
   WILLIAM FORSTER. I live in Bethnal-green. On the 28th of March I went to a house in Church-lane, St. Giles'- the prisoner and another woman were there; I took off my coat, and laid it in the room - the prisoner took it, and ran off with it.
 Prisoner. I was not there at all.
   FREDERICK MILSON MILNER(Police-constable F 63). I produce the coat which I received of a pawnbroker, but he is not here.
     NOT GUILTY.
 864.  JOHN YOUNG was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of March , 4 keys, value 2s., and 1 stove, value 7s., the goods of Robert Eckett , and fixed to a building of his.
   JAMES MARTIN. I live in Noble-street, and am a carpenter; I have the letting of a house in William-street, Clerkenwell. I lost a stove from there on the 30th of March; I had seen it safe and fixed about five o'clock the same day - there were four keys in different locks in that house at that time - I afterwards saw the stove and the keys in the hands of the Policeman; they were the property of Mr. Robert Eckett.
   LEWIS PRICE(Police-constable G 48). I live at No. 11, John-street. Wilmington-square. On the morning of the 30th of March I stopped the prisoner in Bagnigge-wells-road, carrying this stove; he said he was taking it to Ray-street, for Mr. Hibbert - I took him to the station, and found these keys on him, which fitted the doors of the house, and the stove fitted the place; he was about two hundred yards from the house.
 Prisoner's Defence. I saw two men in the street, who asked me if I would do a job; they took me to Chapel-street, and gave me the stove and the keys - they had each of them a parcel; they told me to go to Ray-street.
     GUILTY. Aged 34. - Confined Six Months.
 865.  CATHERINE HAVIS was indicted for feloniously receiving, on the 25th of March , 6 pairs of stockings, value 1l. 5s. the goods of Mary Ann Johnston , well knowing the same to have been stolen.
   GEORGE HORNE. I am shopman to Mary Ann Johnston, a hosier, who lives in Tottenham-court-road; the prisoner was servant in the house. We lost five pairs of silk stockings, which had been near the window; they were wrapped in this paper, which has my writing on it.
 EDMUND DAVIS (Police-constable E 86). On the 25th of March I saw the prisoner near Mrs. Johnston's shop; she went in and came out again, and waited some time - her son then came out, and spoke to her; she went in again, and then came out, and I saw her with this bundle; I took her.
     NOT GUILTY.
 866.  MARY HOLLIDAY was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of March , 2 pairs of bracelets, value 1l.; 1 thimble, value 1s. 6d.; 1 thimble-case, value 1s.; 1 knife, value 4s.; 1 handkerchief, value 1s.; 2 yards of linen, value 2s.; 2 purses, value 5s.; 1 spoon, value 15s.; 2 rings, value 1l.; 2 seals, value 30s.; and 2 watches, value 6l., the goods of Thomas Walter Morris , in his dwelling-house; and that she had been before convicted of felony.
   MARGARET FIELDWICK. I am sister of Thomas Walter Morris; he lives at Bromley, in Middlesex- the prisoner was his servant. On the 1st of March I was present when a box was searched, which was said to belong to her - Mrs. Morris has died lately; I saw found in the box two pairs of bracelets, two purses, a silver knife, a silver thimble, and an ivory case - the other articles were not found: the prisoner said they were given her by her mistress, who was then dead - the articles had been missed, and by her mistress' desire her boxes were detained to be searched; the prisoner was then in the hospital. where my brother had paid a guinea for her admission - I found in her box a pocket-handkerchief, which I know had been my sister's, though my sister's mark has been erased, and the prisoner's put instead of it; the bracelets are worth 1l. - I had been staying there for several weeks; when the prisoner came out of the hospital, she came for her boxes, and I was desired to open them - the articles which were found were in this small box - she owned to the handkerchief being my sister's.
   JOSEPH CADRY. I am a constable. I went with the prisoner to the fever hospital; she desired her sister to bring this little box to her there, but they would not let it come in; I took it back to the prosecutor.
   GEORGE COLLIER(Police-constable N 94.) I have a key which I got at Mr. Morris'; I applied it to a drawer in which this jewellery had been kept, and it was gone - the prisoner sat there crying, and said she knew nothing of it.
   ELIZABETH BARNETT. The prisoner succeeded me as servant at Mr. Morris'; I know the property now produced had been in a drawer in Mrs. Morris' bed-room - my master came for me to go there and I saw this key found in the prisoner's box; she owned she had robbed her master, but hoped he would not prosecute her.
