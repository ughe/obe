away I spoke to Mr. Powell about something that was out of time, and he

- 30th, Clarkson came to the shop again-Mr. Shoolbred and Inspector

mation that the damask was pledged with us--Clarkson came to stop the
ticket of the damask, having lost the one that had been given him he was
