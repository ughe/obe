some comments in my paper about the Harney Peak Mine, in which Mr.

capital of 22,000,000 to hunt for tin-it was an American mine, of
which Mr. Grenfell was a director-among the people who brought
actions against me was the defendant; his action lasted for nearly four

which I have never had-I won the verdict-the conclusion of the action
was on 12th June-on the 13th there appeared in the Financial News an
article on the ease-after that there was no editorial allusion to it, but
I continued for some days to print the comments of provincial and
