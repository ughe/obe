These are my spoons I have brought the fellows to them—there is "S R" on the handle—there is the fellow to each of them—and this handkerchief I am sure is mine, it has a cut in it—I told the policeman one of the handkerchiefs we lost had a cut in it, near the border, which this has—I missed a pack of cards, and these are like them—the combs I believe are mine, there are teeth broken out of them—I have missed this hand-vice from my other tools—it had been in the washhouse—they made use of my own tools—I had seen the spoons the morning before the robbery and the combs most likely the day before.
   Brown's Defence.
