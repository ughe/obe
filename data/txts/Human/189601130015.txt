 128. GEORGE CHATTAWAY  (20) , Burglary in the dwelling-house of Thomas Wasmuth , with intent to steal, having been convicted at the Thames Police-court on April 6th, 1885.
   He PLEADED GUILTY to the burglary, but NOT GUILTY to the previous conviction. WILLIAM SMITH(452 H ). I produce a certificate of the conviction of George Chattaway at the Thames Police-court on 6th April for stealing oranges at St. Catherine's Docks—he was in my custody, and I was present at his conviction—the prisoner is the man—he was fined 20s. or seven days. JOHN NEWMAN( Police Inspector ). I took charge of the prisoner on April 4th at the Thames Police-court. I am sure he is the same man—on the night he was brought in for the burglary he recognised me, and said, smiling, "This is not for oranges."
   GUILTY .— Six Weeks' Hard Labour.
