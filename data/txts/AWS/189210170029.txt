941. JOHN LONG (36) and GEORGE SEALS (40), Robbery with
violence, with another person unknown, on Andrew Rognaas, and
stealing from his person a bag and contents and E2 in money, his
property.
MR. DRAKE Proseouted; Mr. K. FRITH appeared for Long, and Mr. SANDs
for Seals.
ANDREW ROGNAAS. I am boatswain of the 88. Midlothian-on Sep-
tember 27th I arrived in London, and was paid off at the shipping office
-I received 27, and went to the Seandinavian Home-I left E5 there,
and then returned to the shipping office and met an old man who they
call Hughes, and engaged him to take my bag from the ship to the
Home-we took a boat and went on board the ship, got the bag and a
chest, and took it ashore--he set it outside a public-house, and
we went in--I did not notice the prisoners there-I understand
English pretty well-I then went to another house with Hughes
-he got a barrow, and said something about his son that
referred to Long, who said, This old man is my father, and
I will take the barrow and luggage to the Home Home"-the old man
went away with the barrow-he took me to a house which I have
pointed out to the police, where I saw Long, who put my luggage inside
the house, and said he would drive it up to the Seandinavian Home for
nothing-the other prisoner fetehed the change, and there was a thirdn man
who is not ere-Seals brought a yan about seven p.m., and the prisoners
and I got into it-when we drove away one of them put his hand over
my mouth-Seals struck me on my face, and they put their hands in my
poekets, and took all I had-I was kicked many times on my faee, and
both eyes and my mouth were injured-I lost about E2-I would not ery
outI have not seen my luggage since-I do not know how I came out
of the wagon-I spoke to a constable-] picked out both the prisoners at
the station.
