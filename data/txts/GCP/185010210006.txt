Neale's Defence. I was not there above a quarter of an hour; I left work at
half-past five o'clock, washed myself, and when I got down there I saw
Pope; he said, "Are you coming home?" I said, “Directly"--we saw a lot
of policemen and a mob; one of the policemen came up and said, "You have
stolen a handkerchief;" I said, “I have done no such thing;" I was in my
working clothes, all over whitewash: he searched us both, but could not find
anything: he picked up the handkerchief about twenty yards off, and then
took us to the station.
GEORGE SCOTT re-examined. After Neale took the handkerchief from
Pope he stood still, and was in the act of putting it into his trowsers, and as
soon as I laid hold of him be threw it down.

at the office--I believe I have taken pledges in of him-I produce a cont
