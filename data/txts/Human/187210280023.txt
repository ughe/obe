EDWARD RICHARD GREEN. I am a tobacconist, and live at 84, Bishop's Road—I was present at a Vestry at Paddington when some letter was read—I never saw the letter. FRANK DETHERIDGE. I am clerk to the Vestry of Paddington—amongst the papers belonging to the Vestry I have a letter addressed to the Vestry of Paddington, purporting to be signed by one Richard Thomas—I do not produce it—I have not had any communication with Mr. Thomas upon the subject.
   COURT . Q.  Are you the custodian of the documents belonging to the Vestry? A.  Yes—the letter in question is in the strong room, amongst the documents belonging to the Vestry—I hold the key of the strong room—the Vestry passed a resolution declining to furnish a copy of the letter, or to produce it.
