THOMAS MOINTYRE. I am the cashier, I received this invoice for
71. 98. 3d., for the first lot of goods—the prisoner came up and said, "Oh?
by the bye I have not got sufficient money in my pocket, I must give you
this, and a 51. cheque," and he gave me 27. 58., and this bl. cheque (pro-
duced), this was 1.45 or 1,50 in the afternoon-he took away the goods and
the receipt-Monday being a Bank holiday, the cheque would not be paid
in till Easter Tuesday, I did not see him on 7th April but this cheque for
81. 88. came into my possession on that day-it is Page's writing-- it covers
this invoice—there is discount for cash-I should not have parted with
the goods if I had not thought the cheque was good.
Cross-examined. I do not know who received this eight guinea cheque-I
