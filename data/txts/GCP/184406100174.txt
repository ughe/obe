1763. MARY BRAY was indicted for stealing 9 yards of printed cotton,
value 6s. 6d., the goods of Robert Linsey.-2nd Count, stating it to be the
goods of James Forsaith.
James Forsaith. I travel with goods. I stop at the George public-house,
in Ruislip, regularly once a fortnight-I put my pack into the parlour and when
I got to Pinner, on the 2nd of May, I missed a cotton dress—I said nothing
till that day fortnight, and then the landlady at Ruislip gave me information
-I spoke to the policemen, and they found the gown-piece in the prisoner's
possession-it is mine.
Francis Gough (police-sergeant F 24.) I went to the prisoner's father's
house, with an officer, and asked for the dress she had on the Sunday previous
- she produced three dresses--I searched, and found this dress under some
carpeting under the stairs-she went on her knees, begged for mercy, and
said she had taken it out of the prosecutor's pack, and she would pay him for
it if he required.
Cross-examined by Mr. Doane. Q. Was anything said about her telling
the truth? A. No.
Stephen FOUNDRY (policeman.) I accompanied Gough, and I found the
gown--the prisoner caught my arm, and said, "For God's sake, let it abide
where it is ; don't take it.”
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Two Months.
1764. WILLIAM COLLIER was indicted for stealing 19 yards of printed
cotton, value 11s., the goods of Henry Lainson.
Alfred CLARKE. I am in the employ of Henry Lainson, a linen-draper,
at Greenwich. On the 21st of May we had a piece of printed cotton safe
inside the door at two o'clock-I missed it at half-past-this is it-it is my
master's—I know it by the private mark.
John Bovis (police-constable R 139.) I stopped the prisoner about half-
past four o'clock, on the 21st of May-I asked what he had got in his bundle
- he said, “ Clothes”– I asked what sort-he said, “Working clothes"—he
bad this cotton.
Prisoner. I picked it up.
Guilty. Aged 19.- Confined Three Months.
