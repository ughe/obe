(Defence.)
HENRY MACK (prisoner, on oath). I am 28 years of age and am
tenant of No. 5, Goring Street, where I have lived with my Wife for
six years. I am a lifelong total abstainer and do not know the taste
of drink. On certain occasions I and my wife have separated through
her drinking. On October 27 or 28, 1909, such a separation took
place. I met Bostock at a coffee stall, where I was asking a friend
