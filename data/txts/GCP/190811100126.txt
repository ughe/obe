CECIL AUSTIN (prisoner, on oath). Me and the man that is not
in custody, Arthur Williams, went to Mr. Paul's premises on Sunday
evening about seven o'clock along the roofs. The men from the fire
brigade station could see us. The man that was with me took the
slates off. When the hole was made in the roof he said, “Give me the
lamp." I gave him the lamp, but he could not light it, so I said
"Give it to me," and I took the lamp away and slid the bottom off
the lamp. I then held the light for him; he came back with a lot
of jewellery in a small wooden box; he says, “Get hold of these;
put them in your pocket." I put them in my pocket, and he went
back again into the shop, came out and says, " Come along; let us
get away now." We got over Mr. Paul's roof, and as we were coming
along he dropped one of those jemmies that is produced here on to
the roof. It made a noise, and Mr. Homer, who was in his garden,
