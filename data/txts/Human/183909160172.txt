 2680. JAMES CRUSE was indicted for embezzlement. RICHARD HOWARD. I live in Herbert's-buildings, Southwark. The prisoner was in my employ—if he received money from any of my custom ers it was his duty to pay it to me as soon as he returned—I did not send him to Mr. Ashton on the 26th of August to receive money—Mr. Ashton owed me 1 l . 19 s . 10 d ., if he paid that to the prisoner it was his duty to pay it to me—he did not pay me, and he did not return—he had paid me money he had received from Mr. Ashton before—he worked for me be tween six and seven years. HENRY MOSES ASHTON. On the 26th of August I owed the prosecutor 1 l . 19 s . 10 d ., which I paid to the prisoner. GEORGE GRIFFITHS( police-constable M  126.) I took the prisoner, and told him what it was for—he said, "I am sorry it was not 15 l ."
   Prisoner.  I said no such thing—I went to Mr. Ashton's in the morn ing, and got the money—I met a young man, and went to Bermondsey to see his father—when I had got a glass of two more I staid away from work—I had no thoughts of spending it—I spent some of the money—I went as far as Woolwich with him, as he was going to sea—I sat down and went to sleep, and lost a sovereign and a half. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Three Month.
 2681. JOHN POPE was indicted for stealing, on the   28th of August , 1 iron bolt, value 1 s  .; and 10lbs. weight of iron, value, 1 s  .; the goods of Andrew Spottiswoode and others, his masters. HENRY WOSTER. I am foreman to Andrew Spottiswoode and others, of the Gas Light Company. I have missed a great deal of property lately, suspicion was excited by the prisoner appearing very bulky, and told the gate-keeper to stop him—he was searched in my presence, and these two pieces of iron found on him, which I believe to be my master's property—this bolt is a part of an old building. EDWARD FLETCHER. I am a gate-keeper. I searched the prisoner, and found these pieces of iron—he took the bolt out of his trowsers at the station-house—the iron is laid on one side, what is useful is used again, and the rest is sold.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was removing some rubbish—I threw this out and thought it was of no value. GUILTY. Aged 31.— Confined One Month.
