ELIZA AUGUSTA ROACH. I am an apprentice to the prosecutrix. I was
present when she opened the prisoner's box in the prisoner's presence, and
saw the things found.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you hear the arrangement between them!
A. I did not-Miss Barwick is the young person whose box was broken
open-she was not an apprentice--she had left about three months--the
prisoner's box was locked.
John Piper (police-constable E 14.) On Tuesday, the 6th of May, 1
was called to Fisher-street, Red Lion-square, to take the prisoner-I after
wards received this property from Mrs. Hobson.
Mrs. HOBSON re-examined. These are my things I made the prisoner
no promise before I found the property--she acknowledged it was mine-
I would rather have forgiven her and let her go, than been had away from
my business-it was suspecting her of another transaction, and missing
more things afterwards, which made me have her taken up.
Mr. Jones. Q. Was the young lady you cannot find any relation of
yours? A. No-she did not run away-I do not know where her
friends live-she had been in my house about three months-she was not
a lodger-she came to London till she got a situation-I had known her
about a year and a half-she came from Stamford-I have written to 1
lady there who she lived with, but I cannot recollect her name at the mo-
ment-1 have not seen her since she left, nor ascertained where she is-
she was not a guest at my house, nor a servant, nor a friend-she came to
London to get a situation as a milliner- I do not know where she went to,
but I believe she has got one-she had answered an advertisement of mine,
and was to remain with me till she got a situation.
