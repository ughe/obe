CHARLES WALLER. I am a City police-inspector. The prosecutor
complained to me of his loss, and I accompanied him to Chapel-court,
Finsbury-square, where the prisoner lodged, about eleven or twelve o'clock
-I opened the street-door, and knocked at the side parlour door, which
was opened by the prisoner's wife - I asked if George was at home-she
said, "Yes"-I walked in-he was sitting by the fire-side I asked if his
name was George-he said, “Yes” I asked if he was down on the Croy-
don-road that evening-he said, “No”_1 asked if he was along with his
brother-in-law as far as the Elephant and Castle-he said, "Yes" I said,
"He charges you with robbing him of a bag of money" he said, "He is
a foolish man, I know nothing about it"-I then called Gaston in, and

51. Bank of England note, and 16. 2d.-Gaston said, “How could you
