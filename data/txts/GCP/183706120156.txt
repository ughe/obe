1597. THOMAS JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
May, two pairs of shoes, value 68., the goods of James Oliver.
THOMAS BAYLEY. On the 27th of May I was passing over Deptford-
bridge, and saw the prisoner trying to pull the shoes down from James
Oliver, the prosecutor's door-post-he took out a knife and cut the string,
and put them under his jacket-I ran in and informed the young man, who
pursued him, calling "Stop thief," and overtook him-he took them from
under his jacket, and threw them into the road.
JOHN AUSTIN. I am in the employ of Mr. Oliver. I went in pursuit
of the prisoner-he threw the shoes down-I took them up-these are
them.
GUILTY. Aged 26.-Confined Six Weeks.
