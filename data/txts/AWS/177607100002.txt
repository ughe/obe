S A W B R I D G E, MAYOR.
At a Common Council holden in the Chamber of the Guildhall of
the City of London on Friday the I7th of November I775,
A MoTIon was made and QUESTION put, That the whole Proceedings
on the King's Commiffion of the Peace, Oyer and Terminer, and
Gaol Delivery for the City of London, and alfo the Gaol Delivery for the
County of Middlefex, held at Juftice Hall in the 1dfailey, be regularly,
as foon as poffible after every Seffion, published by the Recorder, and:
authenticated with his Name: The fame was resolved in the Affirmative.
R I X.
