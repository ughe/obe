 "Sir—In reply to your advertisement relative to a situation, in the  Chron  icle of this morning, I beg to inform you that if you will favour me with a call to-morrow, between two and four o'clock, I make no doubt of being enabled, in a personal interview, to furnish you with what you require, through my connexion with influential parties. G. S. MUTCH ."
 "To B., 27, Bryanstone-street."
