GEORGE WALKER( Policeman K  612). On 12th September I placed my cape in the reserve room, about 2 p.m.—I then left till 6 o'clock, when I returned, and remained till 9.30—I then took my cape, and found this half-a-crown in it—I did not put it in my cape—no one came in between 6 and 9.30. JESSE CLARK( Policeman K  650). The prisoners were brought in about 4 o'clock—four men were also brought in for the purpose of identification—only the prisoners sat near the cape. WILLIAM WEBSTER. These two half-crowns are bad, and of the same mould—one is covered with teeth marks.
   Smith's Defence.  I never saw Roome before I went to have a pint of beer—I have been in prison two months.
   Roome's Defence. I  met Smith who said he had 6 d ., and we went to have some beer—we were given in custody—I did not know anything about it. GUILTY — Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
 754. HENRY CLARK  (62) , Unlawfully having in his possession counterfeit coin, with intent to utter it.
   MR. LLOYD and MR. AUSTIN METCALFE conducted the Prosecution. ALICE WELKINS . I am barmaid to MR. GARNON , of 414, Oxford Street—on 19th September the prisoner came in with three women and asked for a quartern of gin and a glass of bitter beer, and tendered a shilling—the liquor came to 7 d .—Mr. Garnon spoke to the prisoner, who left the house. Thomas Garnon. I am a licensed victualler, of 414, Oxford Street—the prisoner and two women came in on 19th September—the last witness gave me a shilling and said it was bad—I spoke to the prisoner, who ran out—the women also ran out on the other side—I ran after him, and saw him throw some packets down a grating—I gave him in custody—I went to the house where the grating was, and a young man gave me a parcel which contained 19 s .—I gave the bad shilling and the parcel to the constable. WILLIAM PRIEST. I live at 12, High Street, Bloomsbury, and am a porter—I was in the kitchen at Mr. Beaumont's—I heard something drop in the area, and found some shillings wrapped up—I gave the parcel to Mr. Beaumont. JOHN HENRY BEAUMONT. I live at 412, Oxford Street—I received from Priest a parcel containing bad coin—Mr. Garnon afterwards came, and the parcel was given to the policeman. PATRICK HACKET( Policeman E  436). The prisoner was given into my custody—he said nothing—these are the coins. WILLIAM WEBSTER. These two shillings are bad—here are also ten bad shillings, five being of the same mould as the one passed; and four bad florins.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I met the women and gave them some drink—they asked me to have some more; and when we went into the prosecutor's house they ran away—I know nothing about the transaction. GUILTY — Judgment Respited.
