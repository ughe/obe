403. ROBERT BUTCHER (18) and ELIZABETH CLARKE (35)
Robbery with violence on Emma Cann and stealing from her person 48. 10d.
and one handkerchief, her property.
MR. HARRIS conducted the Prosecution; and MR. FRIH defended Butcher
Exona Cann. I am single and live at 18, Goldington Street- about 12
o'clock on the night of 27th August I was in the George public house near
the Euston station I saw the female prisoner id the next compartment
I had kuown her before she came round to me and we had a quarteru of
gin, for which I paid I had about 58. in my pooket- she saw me take the
money out of my cloak pocket to pay for it and put it back again-I then
came out as they were closing-I proceeded a little way down Seymour
Street, and left her when I saw her talking to the male prisoner at the
corner of Seymour Street - I did not know him crossed over by St.
Panoras Church further down on the other side of the way, a little below
the church and the male prisoner came across the road I stood still for
minute or two and he gave me a blow on the chest I was in the set of
getting up again and was on the kerb, when he made another hit at me, and
forced his hand into my pocket this mud on my cloak was caused by my
falling--my right pooket was hanging out as I ran to the constable to give
him in charge-he also made a kick at me- as I was getting up, the female
prisoner was at his side and said “Have you got it 1"-I picked up my
bonnet, and by that time they had got away across the road and down Sey
mour Street I looked for my money and my pocket handkerchief did
not lose sight of them-Butcher said he would knock my brains out if I
followed him-I followed them to Ossulston Street and gave them into
custody.

not an unfortunate woman I have been under the protection of some one
for the last five years until lately--my occupation lately has been making
bonnets--I never go out in the way you mean I do not keep appointments
with particular gentlemen-I do not go to publie-houses for materials for
making bonnets after the person I went out with had met her husband I
went into a public-house and had a glass of ale, and then Clarke came

with my friend_Clarke did not appear to have been drinking -other people
