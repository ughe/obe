 554. THOMAS EDWARDS  (26) was indicted for wilful and corrupt perjury, committed by him in the Divorce Court, in the suit of " Partington v.  Partington." MR. FITZJAMES STEPHEN, Q. C ., with MESSRS. POLAND and BEASLEY , conducted the Prosecution; and MR. SERJEANT BALLANTINE the Defence. GUILTY — He received a good character— Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
 555. WILLIAM GARNER  (54), HENRY POORE  (33), and JOHN GARNER  (45), were indicted (with William Hart , not in custody) for unlawfully conspiring to defraud Frederick Henry Vivian of 330 tons of coal.
   MESSRS. METCALFE and GOODMAN conducted the Prosecution; and MR. STRAIGHT defended John Garner.
 —  INMAN . I am a clerk to Messrs. Vivian—on 6th July I served notices on the three prisoners to produce certain documents—this is a copy ( produced ). FREDERICK EDWARD VIVIAN.
