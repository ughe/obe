used to come about once in three months. As to the statement of
Mr. Cash that £12,000 came into the business, it passed through the
banking account, but that is not money coming into the business. I
consider the fact that Smith only had 4s. a day quite consistent with
his controlling a business of this magnitude. With regard to my
own position towards the business, I simply dealt through Herbert
Smith. I was his client. I opened stocks for myself on the Stock
Exchange, and they were debited to Smith's account, and the son
tracts went to Herbert Smith, in the name of Herbert Smith. Herbert
Smith also opened stocks in his own name for his own proteotion. I
met the brokers at Smith's office, and the stocks were put down to
him with the knowledge of the brokers. I never gave orders to stock
brokers to cover for Herbert Smith. The volume of business Smith
did gave him credit. I attended on pay-day, and picked out the
stocks that were mine. Without a technical knowledge of the neces
