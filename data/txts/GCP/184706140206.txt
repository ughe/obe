1537. JAMES ATKINS was indicted for stealing 1 cart, value 6l, the
property of Charles Norville.
FRANCES NORVILLE. I am the wife of Charles Norville—we live at Brixton
-I know the prisoner-in May I had a cart for sale-I put it in front of the
house-the prisoner pressed me to send it to an auction-room, and said he
would take it-I said I would speak to my husband about it-I got bis
leave, and sent my son up with it-it remained unsold till last Feb., and
then the prisoner went to Mr. Lloyd and gave him instructions for it to be
sold- I went to Mr. Lloyd, and he had sold the cart by private contract-I
have had no money for it-I did not authorize any one to sell it.
CHARLES NORVILLE. I am the husband of Frances Norville-I did not
authorize the prisoner to sell the cart-I have not received any money
for it.
WILLIAM HENRY LLOYD. I am an auctioneer--the cart was brought to
me by the prisoner and a ladit was deposited with me to be sold at my
auction, which was every Thursday-it was not to go under 41.-the pri
soner said he had been doing business for the party whose cart it was, and
he should not like it to go under 41.--I sold it and gave the money to the
prisoner.
Prisoner. I received the cart on the 19th of May--Mr. Norville's bill
amounted to 61. 158. 6d., and now his bill amounts to 201. 148. 6d.
CHARLES NORVILLE re-examined. I do not owe him a farthing I did not
authorize him to sell the cart.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not put the horse in the cart ? A. No-I did not
