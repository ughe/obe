ALFRED CRUTCHETT( Sergeant R. ) I have examined the prisoner, having the document of November 16th, 1904, before me, with Principal Warder Polling—I found upon him the whole of the marks recorded on the file; they are "V.H.E.M." tattooed on the front of his left forearm, a flower on his left forearm, a half bracelet at the back of his left wrist, a blue dot between the thumb and finger of his left hand, "J.R." on the back of his light forearm, "T.H." on the inner side of his left calf, all tattooed, a scar on his chin, and one on his left knee—he also had some additional marks, and when at the station he told me he had only had them a few days.
