I went to the prisoner's room, at Mr. Forster's to mark the things—I marked some stockings, and some other things with the letters "M P."
   MRS. FORSTER . I had some fringe of a rose colour—I had it dyed black—it did not take very well—I believe this fringe on this scarf to be part of that fringe—I missed a silk hat-band of my husband's, and this scarf has been a hat-band, I have no doubt—it is a peculiar kind of silk, and here are the remains of "C F" on these napkins, and "M P" is on them now.
   Cross-examined. Q.  I suppose you scarcely speak with certainty to this fringe? A.  Yes, I do—I saw it not many days before I left Lincoln shire—it is worth 4 s . or 5 s .—it was packing up when I was going from Lincolnshire—on the 6th of August the prisoner came to me, and asked me to search her boxes, and they were searched.
   MR. DOANE . Q.  Do you find the remains of the rose-colour on this identical fringe? A.  I do—I did not authorise her to take any of these things. GUILTY  . Aged 28.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury.— Confined Twelve Months.

MARY LUNN was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of August , 2 gowns, value 5 s  .; and 1 petticoat, value 4 s  .; the goods of Eliza Har per ; and 1 gown, value 3 s  ., the goods of Louisa Matthews . CATHERINE RAWLEY. There was a fire at Deptford on the 9th of Au gust, and I saw the prisoner take a bundle from the premises of Mrs. Gill—I set Edward Smith to watch her.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  What time of day was it? A.  About eleven o'clock in the morning—I have known the prisoner by sight—her husband is dead, and she has a family—she took these things out of a garden, where they were thrown for safety. EDWARD SMITH. I watched the prisoner—she took the bundle to a shop in Church-street. RUTH HARPER. I keep a shop in Church-street. The prisoner came and asked me to let her leave this bundle there—I gave it to the officer.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Had you known her? A.  Yes, for ten years. WILLIAM SHUTTLE WORTH( police-constable R  133.) I produce the bundle, which contains these articles, which I got from Mrs. Harper. LOUISA MATTHEWS. This gown is mine—it was at Mrs. Gill's to be mangled.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Do you know this petticoat? A.  Yes—it is my mistress's sister's. SARAH HARE. I found the petticoat on the prisoner's person.
