   Cross-examined.  I was sent to the sales to look on, to see how sales were conducted—I learnt nothing but what I knew before—one sale was at Lancaster Gate, a private house—I received 3 l . for salary for the week I was there—he did not complain to me of larking with the other clerks, and breaking the furniture—I heard him complaining; I did not break any furniture; I don't know what others did—I only saw one book kept, a register of businesses—we had to cut out advertisements from the papers of properties to be sold—I heard that the prisoner had another business at Clapham—I am 24 years of age; I had been a clerk in the Railway Clearing-house—I saw no correspondence—I did not write or post any letters; I saw no letter-book or copying-press. WALTER GEORGE BOND.
