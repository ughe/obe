WINSLOW, James (39, merchant), CARRIER, Frederick (37,
accountant), and HEPDEN, Frank Horace (31, merchant), pleaded
guilty of conspiring and agreeing together by false pretences to acquire
from such of His Majesty's liege subjects as might be induced to
forward goods to Darton and Co. and Frank Hampton certain of their
goods with intent to defraud.
Mr. Bodkin prosecuted ; Mr. Curtis Bennett appeared for Winslow;
Mr. Eustace Fulton for Carrier; Mr. Purcell for Hepburn.
Sentences (each prisoner): Ten months' imprisonment, second divi.
sion.
MCGRATH, Percy Wainman (38, advertising agent), unlawfully
exposing his person to the view of divers of the liege subjects of Our
Lord the King in a certain highway and public place,
Mr. H. W. Rowsell prosecuted; Mr. Curtis Bennett defended.
Verdict, Not guilty.
