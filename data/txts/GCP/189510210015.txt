770. GIOVANNI CHILOSI, Feloniously wounding John Sullivan,
with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm.
Mr. GEOGHEGAN Prosecuted, and Mr. Elliot Defended.
The prisoner, in the hearing of the Jury, stated that he was guilty of
unlawfully wounding, and the Jury thereupon found him GUILTY of"
Unlawfully Wounding.—Ile received an excellent character.-Discharged
on recognisances.
771. ARTHUR EDWARD LEE (23) PLEADED GUILTY to forging
and uttering a request for the payment of £10.
ARTHUR EDWARD LEE was further indicted for forging and
uttering a receipt for the payment of £10.
MR. RICHARDS Prosecuted.

room as the prisoner-in July I missed my Post-office book, which I
kept in an iron box in my room-I last saw it on July 31st-I communi-
cated with the Post Office—this notice of withdrawal and this receipt.
are not in my writing.

notice of withdrawal for £10 was handed to me on August 2nd by the
prisoner to the best of my belief-I telegraphed to the Savings Bank.
Department for instruction, and prepared this receipt-later in the day
the same person called again and signed this receip-I am sure that the
same person who signed the notice of withdrawal signed the receipt-the
receipt was signed in the wrong place, and I requested a fresh signature
-I then handed the book and £10 in gold to the prisoner-I am sure I
handed the money to the man who brought the withdrawal notice.
