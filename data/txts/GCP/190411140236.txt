JOHN FREDERICK Dickson. I am the secretary of the Dublin Dis
tillers' Company--they have their offices at Thomas Street, Dublin-
that company was incorporated in 1889 with a capital of about
£1,100,000-as regards the ordinary shares of that company they
are £5 shares fully paid; no dividend has been paid on them
since September 30th, 1893; the preference shares have not paid
any dividend since June 30th, 1900; they paid 3 per cent. for the six
months--I have got here the register of transfers of the company in
November, 1900; Mrs. Hooley had as registered owner in the company
1,200 shares ; on November 20th she had 1,200 ordinary shares standing
to her credit ; she afterwards acquired others—there were no transfers
in the office in November ; we did not receive a transfer until December
17th ; I think that was the date; as a matter of fact, on November 24th
there was a transfer for 300 shares sent from Mr. James Arnold Bradshaw,
and it was received by me on the 26th-the date on the transfer was No-
vember 14th-there were shares subsequently registered in her name,
which brought the total up to 2,520—I have not made out what is the con-
sideration in the transfers for that number; she bought them at various
prices; the amount she received for them would be £1,026 ; she sold them
at various dates ; she commenced selling about October, 1900, and she
cleared them off by selling to a Mr. Francis Redfern, on March 12th,
1903—I do not find that any of her shares were transferred into the name
of Lawson or Ormerod; the transfer is registered with us on Decem-
ber 17th from Ross and others to Mrs. Hooley, the consideration for that
was £225 for 300 ordinary shares; that would be 15s. apiece—there is
another, on November 20th to McCormack, of Donegal; those were 50
ordinary shares for £37 108.—there is another of the same date: Henry
Richard Glynn, of Kilrush, to Mrs. Hooley, 100 shares for £75.
