NEW COURT Saturday, February 29, 1868.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
277. WILLIAM MUCH (27), and CHARLES BROWN (28), were
again indicted see page 335] for unlawfully obtaining by false pretences
16. from Peter Henry Barquest, with intent to defraud.
MR. BEsLEY conducted the Prosecution, Mr. STRAIGHT the Defence.
PETER HENRY BABQUEST. I am a boarding-house keeper, of 3, Neptune
Street-on Saturday 1st February, Much came to me between one and two
o'clock and told me that Brown was coming with two sailors from the Com-
mereial Docks, who had been seven weeks on board the Ann of Cardif, and
that they wanted board and lodging-he asked me if I would advance the
money and take them in to board-he said that the expenses would be 7e.
each, as they had a cab-Brown brought two men in about half an hour,
dressed in sailors' elothes--there were two bags outside the cabthey got
out of the cab and came into my houseBrown Said he had brought two
sailors from the Commercial Docks, and asked me if I would advance them
the money for their expenses, and then he wanted 3a. each for their trouble
-I gave the money to Brown-I asked them whether they intended to pay
me when they were paid off, and they said " Yes"in Yes" consequence of what
they told me, I paid Brown 1-one of the sailors went away the same
evening, the other one stopped till 4th February, when he went away-he
owed me for board and lodging-I have never been paid-they left the bags
in the house, and I found they only contained rubbish-Watkins was not
one of the men.
