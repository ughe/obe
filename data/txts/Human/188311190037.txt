I am a cashier in the drapery department of the Stores—on 6th October the prisoner came to me and signed this deposit slip D for 1 l . 3 s . 11 d . on the deposit account of Mary Mackenzie—deposit slips are not countersigned by the clerk at the Stores when handed in—each customer has a book which contains counterfoils to these cheques—it is exactly like a banker's book in its arrangement—the "B.S." on it means "Bill sent"—I fill in the amount; the customer only signs her name—this was written by me from the customer's slip.
