336. JAMES WALLIS was again indicted for stealing, on the 17th
of December, 1 spoon, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Pisher.
BARBARA CALLAM. I reside with Mr. Thomas Fisher, in Park-street,
Islington. The prisoner used to bring potatoes to the house the spoon
was missed on Thursday, the 17th of December-I had seen it in the cup-
board the day before--it was not likely to be thrown into the ashes I do
not know whether the prisoner had any opportunity of stealing it.
RICHARD INGLEDEW (police-constable N 228.) I took the prisoner,
and found this spoon in his side coat pocket, on the 17th of December.
Prisoner's Defence. I went to Mr. Fisher's with half a cwt. of potatoes
-the witness went down before me, and showed me where to put them-
I walked up-stairs—she followed, and shut the door-1 was coming
across the road, with the truck-I had potatoes in another basket--there
were some cinder ashes-I saw something shine in them, and picked it up-
it was this spoon-I came home, and showed it to Mrs. Walker; and said,
"See, what I have found"-my young mistress came in, and I showed it
to her; and she said it would get some of the poor servants into a scrape
-she rubbed it, and made ont the letters; and thought it was Mr. Fisher's
-she told me to take it there the next morning, when I went for orders.
SARAH WALKER. He showed it to my mother, and said he found it
when I came home I saw it, and told him what he has stated.
BARBARA CALLAM re-examined. We do not throw our ashes out in
this place—they are thrown into a dust-hole, underneath the pavement.
Prisoner. I found it across the street, opposite the house.

336. JAMES MAYNE was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of
December, 1 mortar, value 28. 9d.; and 1 pestle, value 18.; the goods
of George Ansell and another.
RICHARD Tomkins. I am shopman to Messrs. George and William
Ansell, fixture-dealers, Great Queen-street. On the 30th of December,
I saw the prisoner put his foot on the threshold of the door, reach round,
and take this pestle and mortar-he then ran away-I pursued, and saw
him throw it down-the policeman took him, and brought him back,
within five minutes I did not lose sight of him.
Prisoner. When I was up at Bow-street, he said he did not see me
throw it down. Witness. Yes, I said I did.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Recommended to meroy by the Prosecutor.
Confined One Month.
