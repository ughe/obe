MR. SLEIGH to MR. MONTBITH. Q. Did you show this memorandum to
the prisoner? A. Yes, and he stated that he had paid 21. 5o. 2d.; he did
not make any observation about this paper, whether it was correct or not-I
showed it him-I cannot say whether he ever read it-this is a duplicate of
the account which had been previously left at his house-he bad been dealing
with the Company some few years.
MR. SLEIGH to MR. KITCHEN. Q. You made a call on the prisoner after
7th May, and made a further demand on him? 4. Yes, for about 5s, or 6s.
--he did not say he had paid the Company all that was due.

MR. METCALPE. Q. What was the usual amount of each quarter; did it
ever amount to 21. 58. 2d? 1. Not to my knowledge.
Court. Q. Just collect your thoughts; was it on 7th May you received
11:58. 2d.? 4. Yes, and afterwards there was something due for gas on the
old meter-I applied for that the latter end of May, or the beginning of June :
that was before he brought the receipt to the office when I made that appli-
cation. I told him I should call for the remainder, and I think he called about

pany, but robbing me of a sovereign-that was on 9th June,
Q. That was not the first thing he said ? A. When he first came I asked

21. 58. 2d. - I told him he had not; the receipt had been altered-he said it
was not; and on that I said, “You are not robbing the Company, but
