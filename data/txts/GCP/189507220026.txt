factory- June last year I engaged the prisoner as foreman of the
packing and finishing-rooms-I had known him beforeon 5th July I
was at the factory during the day, until one o'clock I was about the
premises as far as I know the prisoner had no business in the gallery-
it was no part of his business to be picking or strigging currants in 35

from one o'clock till the next morning-on arriving next day I went to
the gallery and the packing-room, and I made a communication to the
police-in company with Mr. Morgan, an inspector in the service of Mr.
Whiteley, I made an examination of the drawer in the packing-room-I
pulled out the drawer, and found this lid of a jam tin under a hole that
was burnt- there was a little dust from the brown paper lying on the
top of the lid which had been burnt through this produced is
one of the pieces - I think this card-board would burn more
