710. JAMES DELLAR, stealing 38., the moneys of George Lambert, his
MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE LAMBERT. I am a farmer, and live at Old Park-farm, Enfield
the prisoner was in my service. On 22nd Feb. I gave him 11. 13s. 70. to
fetch thirty-nine bushel sieves-the sum to pay for them was 11. 13s. 4d.,
and the 3d. was for the turnpike-gate-he was to bring only thirty-nine
sieves, because the man had made a mistake, and sent me twenty-one instead
of twenty the time before-on my return from town the prisoner was there to
take my horse-I said, "Have you got the baskets ?"-he said yes, he had
paid 11. 138. 4d., which was for one more than he had brought, because the
man sent one over the last time---some time afterwards a man called on me
about the baskets-I had in the meantime discharged the prisoner-he had
lived with me, on and off, for three, or four, or five years--I think it is about
five years since he first came-he was then a very little boy.
Prisoner. He only gave me 1l. 10s. 3d. Witness, No, I counted down
the money to him, Il. 13s. 7d.--I have not the least doubt of giving it to him.
JAMES UNDERWOOD. I am apprentice to Mr. Cripps, a basket-maker, at
Enfield-highway. The prisoner came to my master's for thirty-nine baskets
-he asked how much they came to-I said 11. 13s. 4d.-he told me before
he pulled the money out of his pocket that he bad not got enough by 38.-he
pulled the purse out of his pocket, emptied the money into his hand, and
counted it-there was 11. 103. 4d.--I received that of him.
Prisoner. He said two or three times it was right. Witness. No, I did
not -- I called my master, and he counted it-there was 11. 108. 4d.
LOUISA KEMPTON. I am servant to Mr. Lambert. I remember the
prisoner coming home with the thirty-nine baskets-Mr. Lambert asked him
how many he had brought--he said thirty-nine, and he had given 11. 138. 4d.
for them.
Prisoner. You said you knew nothing about it. Witness. No, it is
quite false.
Robert HANNANT (policeman, N 325). I took the prisoner on 20th March
- he had then left his master's—I told him he was charged with embezzling
31. of his master's-he said he had never had it; all the money he gave him
was 11. 10s. and a 3d.-piece.
(The prisoner received a good character).
Guilty. Aged 16.--Recommended to mercy by the Jury.-Confined
