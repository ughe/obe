 1631.  JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of August , 2 pewter pots, value 2s., the goods of Henry Herbert.
   MARY JACOBS. My brother, Henry Herbert, keeps a public-house in  Bunhill-row. On the 31st of August I was in the passage, and saw the prisoner with something under his apron; he asked for some water, and I told him to go into the wash-house, and he would find some; he took the tea-kettle, and walked, stooping, to the water cock, and stooped to fill the kettle; I saw him lay a pint pot on the dung-hill, and put a quart-pot into his hat; I left him in care of my brother, while I fetched an officer.
 HENRY HERBERT. I went into the yard on the 31st of August, and saw the prisoner, who, my sister said, had put the pot on the dung-bill - he said nothing.
   ROBERT LOCK. I took the prisoner; he said, "I was going to get a kettle of water," but afterwards, just as he got to the watch-house, he said it was through distress.
 Prisoner's Defence. I never said a word of the sort; I went to get a kettle of water, and there was none in the butt; I saw a pail of water there, and I took the quart-pot, and filled the kettle; as to the pint-pot on the dunghill, I know nothing of it.
 MARY JACOBS re-examined. He drew the water from the cock into the kettle.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined One Month.
 1632.  JOSEPH WINSALL was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of August , 1 basket, value 2s., and 1 bushel of pears, value 5s., the goods of Thomas Tittman.
 THOMAS TITTMAN. I live in the Strand, and  keep a stall in  Covent-garden-market. On the 4th of August I missed a bushel of pears, and a basket, which I had seen safe the day before - I saw them again the day afterwards; the basket has my name on it.
   WILLIAM THOMAS PARSONS. On the 4th of August I saw the prisoner running up Durham-street, about half-past eleven o'clock at night, with a basket of pears; I followed him - he threw it off his shoulder, and ran down William-street; I took him to the watch-house, and took the basket of pears.(Basket produced and identified.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I have been out of employ for four months. GUILTY. Aged 47.
 Recommended to Mercy by the Jury. Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 1633.  ANN WEBBER was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of September , 2 shillings, the monies of Edward Evans , from his person.
 EDWARD EVANS. I was at the Adam and Eve, public-house, Westminster, on the 5th of September - I went from home about twelve o'clock, and remained at the public-house the rest of the day; I was not very much in liquor, and I left my friend who I had been with, at half-past seven o'clock in the evening; I had some money on my person - I had never seen the prisoner before I was at Queen-square-office; I do not know how I got into her company.
 Prisoner. You had 2s. 6d. in the office the next morning, and you told the gentleman you had lost no money.
 Q. Then you do not know that you had more money then than you had the next morning at the office? A. Yes; my landlord brought me half-a-crown that morning.
   FRANCIS NEWSTEAD. On the 5th of September, I saw the prisoner and the prosecutor; he appeared to be in liquor, but appeared to have been extremely ill-used; he was quite stupified from the effects of the usage; the prisoner was accompanied by three young men and a boy about ten years of age - she placed the prosecutor against a door, and supported his head with her left hand, while she put her right hand into his breeches pocket, and passed something to the boy, who made off; she then put her hand again into his left hand pocket; I watched her; she changed her position, and put her right hand before his eyes, and put her left hand into his breeches pocket; I saw her put something in her bosom; I went and asked her if she was his wife; she said No - I went and told a grocer, who came out, and said it was a pity there was not a street-keeper about - I went on to Shetton-ground, and when I returned I saw three youths ill-treating the prosecutor in a most barbarous manner; the prisoner had his hat in her hand, partly concealed by her apron; I went up and remonstrated with them - I was afterwards desired to give my address, which I did; the prisoner ran off as soon as she knew there were officers coming.
   WILLIAM RAYNER. I am the officer. I came up and took the prisoner, who was running away; I found 2s. in her bosom; she said she had some money which she brought out to buy something.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went into the house where the prosecutor was drinking, and my friend sat down by him; he gave us some beer to drink; he had half-a-crown, and said he had pawned his coat for 6s., and had no more left- he then got up to go and he fell into the sink; I raised him up and set him against the wall, and told her to mind him while I went to get him some water; when I got back his hat was off, and I put it on his head.
 COURT to EVANS. Q. Did you pawn your coat that morning? A. Yes, for 6s.; I had but 1s. 11d. in the morning, and thought that not enough; I was sober then, and I went to the Adam and Eve with my friend, and we drank several pints of beer together; I then left him to go home; I was not so tipsy till I was knocked down and ill-treated.
     GUILTY. Aged 25. Transported for Fourteen Years.
