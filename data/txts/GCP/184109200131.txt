LOUISA SICKEL. I am the wife of Charles Frederick Sickel, and occupy
an attic at No. 59, High-street, Lambeth. The prisoner occupied the one
pair back room--he is a baker-I was in the habit of calling him of an
evening to go to his work; generally about a quarter to eleven o'clock-on
Monday evening, the 9th of August, I was in my room, in which I can
hear what is going on in the prisoner's room--about a quarter before nine
I heard him come up stairs, and heard some talking in his room--I did
not hear what was said--the talking did not continue more than two or
three minutes-it was the butcher-boy and the prisoner—the butcher's
boy went down stairs, after asking the prisoner what time he should call
him--the prisoner said, when he shut up shop, about nine or half-past
nine-about ten minutes before ten I called him and told him it was ten
-- he said, “I know it is"-I then returned to my own room about ten I
heard him-run down the stairs I heard him speak to some one when he
got to the bottom of the stairs, but I do not know who it was-I remained
in any room-I am quite sure the person who ran down stairs came out of
his room- then smelt as if there was wood burning somewhere, and went
down stairs-about two minutes before the prisoner went out, I had heard
the cracking of wood, which I thought might be by the striking of lucifers
on the wall-it was after that that I smelt fire-when I went down stairs
and came to the prisoner's door, I found the flames coming from under-
neath the door, and at the side of the door-the door was shut and locked,
and the key was in the door, outside I had four children up stairs, three
were in bed and asleep I got the children out of the house, after giving
an alarm in the house--the butcher's boy ran out naked the furniture in
hiy room is my husband's property,
Cross-examined. Q. Did you know the prisoner's cousin when he used
to be there? A. Yes I was lodging in the house then-be was Mr.
Hancock's tenant-I was not there all the time he was there--I had no-
thing done to my room when I went into it, that I know of we only
cleaned it-there are bugs in the house.
