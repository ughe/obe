   Before Mr. Recorder.
 833. RICHARD RAVENSCROFT was indicted for embezzlement.
   MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution. HANNALL CLARK. I live in Surrey-street, Strand. I had, on the 9th of Jan., purchased goods at Mr. Harvey's, in the Westminster-road, to the amount of 3 l . 18 s . 6 1/2 d .—on the 10th of Jan. they were brought home to me by the porter, but the prisoner is the person who saw the carpet mea sured, and I paid him 3 l . 18 s . 6 d . for the articles which I had purchased—I took a receipt from him for the amount—I told him I was going to the shop to purchase some drapery, and there were some little things which I had purchased at the shop, which I would pay for in a few days.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  What day was this? A.  On Tuesday, the 10th of Jan.—there was some sheeting and other things left, which he did not know the cost of—I paid 8 s . 1 d . for them on Monday the 16th—I paid the 3 l . 18 s . 6 d . at five o'clock in the afternoon. JAMES NICOL. I am clerk to Mr. Joseph Harvey. The prisoner was in his service on the 10th of Jan. as a salesman—he had been there from two to three months—Mrs. Clark was a customer of ours—it was the prisoner's duty to receive sums of money, and to pay them directly to the cashier, and then to tell me of it, that it might be placed paid in the book—he did not tell me, on the 10th of Jan., that he had received 3 l . 18 s . 6 d . from Mrs. Clark—on the 16th he came to me, and said, "I have made a mistake, I wish you would put it to rights for me; I received the money from the customer in Surrey-street, Strand; and though I ought to have made it paid in the book, I omitted to do so; I wish you would do it for me now"—I said, as I had no doubt he had paid the money to the cashier, I would do so, and asked the amount—he said 3 l . 18 s . 10 d ., but he had only received 3 l . 18 s . 6 d .; that he had received it on the 11th, and paid it to the cashier on the 12th—he did not say which cashier—I then made inquiries of the cashier—the prisoner keeps a book in which he writes the various amounts he receives during the day—I referred to it on the morning of the 16th, after he had spoken to me, and found that no such sum had been entered—in the afternoon of the same day I found it had been entered—it had been interlined betwixt the two days.
