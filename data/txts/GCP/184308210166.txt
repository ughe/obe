2427. JOHN INCH was again indicted for stealing, on the 5th of
August, 1 coat, value 10s. 6d., the goods of John Wells.
WILLIAM KIBBLE. I am shopman to John Wells, a pawnbroker, in Broad.
street, Bloomsbury. On the 5th of August I missed a coat, which I had seen
safe about an hour before-I saw the prisoner scuffling with a man about two
hundred yards from my master's- I went up, took the coat from him, and
gave him in charge when he got about fifty yards, he slipped out of his
coat, and got away from the policeman, and was not taken till a week after-
this is the coat-it is my master's.
Prisoner. I was not the person. Witness. I took it from him-I have
not the least doubt of him, as a week before he was in our shop redeeming
some articles.
Guilty.t Aged 18.- Transported for Seven Years.
2428. ANDREW MARTIN was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
August, 1 glazed window sash, value 6s., the goods of Tbomas M'Carthy,
and fixed to a certain building; against the Statute, &c.
Thomas GRIFFITH. I am in the service of Thomas M'Carthy, and live in
Jubilee-place, Chelsea. He bought some green-houses, and I was employed to
pull them down-I missed a frame and glass- I found a policeman, and went
with him to the prisoner's house, in Simmonds-street-I found the prisoner
on the works, in the same ground where the frame was taken from-I found
the frame at the head of the prisoner's bed-he went there with us.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. Have you any doubt it was the sash!
A. None- he said if I would forgive him he would give up the sash-I then
went to his house and got it.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 46.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined One Month.
2429. WILLIAM WALKER was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of
July, 2 boots, value 6s., the goods of Joseph Hobbs.
JOSEPH Hobbs. About eight o'clock in the evening of the 28th of July!
was in my shop, and saw the prisoner looking in at the window - I watched,
and saw him come to the door-I heard two boots drop-I ran after him, and
found two odd boots on him-he left the other two in the shop where he cut
them down.
Prisoner's Defence. I was coming along Shadwell about nine o'clock at
night, and saw a man chuck them into the road; I picked them up.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Four Months.
