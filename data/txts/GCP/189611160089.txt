MATHews Defended Parke.

High Road, Peckham-at the end of May Parke owed Mr. Dibden £22
odd for goods supplied to her at Cleveland House, Clapham Park, which
is a private asylum or home-Miss Parke told me she was the proprietor
in consequence of a letter received from her on June 1st, I called to see
her, but did not see her I was referred to her solicitor, Mr. Evershed, of
Moira Chambers, Ironmonger Lane-the next morning I saw a letter
from Mr. Evershed, addressed to Mr. Dibden—the letter is destroyed, but
was the same wording as the letter “A” (produced), and headed : "E. J.
Evershed, Solicitor, 17, Ironmonger Lane, Cheapside. Dear Sir,-My
client, Miss Parke, of Cleveland House, Clapham Park, has handed me
your account to attend to on her behalf. I shall be obliged it
you will let this matter stand over for a short time, as I
am endeavouring to arrange & sale of certain investments
of hers which should realise a large sum, when I will send
you a cheque for the amount owing to you. Kindly let me hear whether
you agree to this proposal.-Yours faithfully, EDWIN J. EVERSHED"_
