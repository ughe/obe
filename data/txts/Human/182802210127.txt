HENRY ROW. I am shopman to Mr. Morris, pawnbroker, High-street, Mary-le-bone. I took this shawl in of the prisoner - I had seen her before; it was on the 6th of February - I advanced 5s. on it in the name of Maria Wheatley, of William-street, by which name we had done business with her.

 717.  DIANA HARDING was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of February , 3 sheets, value 6s; 1 petticoat, value 6d.; 1 handkerchief, value 6d, and 1 shirt, value 1s., the goods of Henry Collard.
   SUSANNAH COLLARD. I am the wife of Henry Collard, a shoemaker, of Red Lion-market. The prisoner and I had been drinking together on the evening this matter happened; we had been drinking in my house, and in a public-house - we were all the worse for liquor; I do not know that the prisoner took the property - they were all separated about the room; it was my servant's fault: the sheets were at the fire airing - the shirt was in the drawers- she bid me a good night; I do not know where she lives - I know her well; I think it was about half-past eleven o'clock; she took nothing that I saw - nor do I know who took them; I saw the things the next day at Mr. Barnes's house.
   JOSEPH GRAY. I am superintendent of the Houseless Poor in Grub-street. The prisoner came there with two others drunk - they were Jane Moss and Ann Evans; the prisoner had this bundle - they were all intoxicated, but not so bad but they could walk; they came about half-past twelve or one o'clock on the 13th instant; I questioned them, and finding they had a bundle I sent them to the watch-house: Harding said she had bought the things in the bundle in Petticoat-lane - I saw Collard the next morning.
   JOHN BARNES. I was officer of the night. The prisoners were brought in, and said that they lodged in Playhouse-yard; I went there the next morning - Mrs. Collard came up and asked for the same three girls; I asked her what she wanted, and she said they had robbed her; the prisoner told me she bought the things in Rosemary-lane - they were wet.
   SUSANNAH COLLARD. I went to Playhouse-yard to see if they could give me any intelligence of the things I had lost - the things I saw at the watch-house were mine; I do not know how I lost them - there is no mark on the sheets; I think they are like mine - there were six on the horse; the handkerchief looks like mine - it is the same pattern - the shirt is my little boy's.
 Prisoner's Defence. A man stood at Mrs. Collard's door, and asked her to come out; she said, "Come in, girls, and have something to drink" - we went in, she quarrelled with her servant, and turned her out: there was a man there - he went out - Mrs. Collard gave us something to drink; the bundle was at the door, and I took it up, and as it was too late to go home we went to the Refuge.
 MRS. COLLARD re-examined. My husband was out - there might be a man there.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Confined One Month.
 718.  SAMUEL HENRY HIVALL was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of January , 4 wine glasses, value 1s. 4d, the goods of Barnet Abrahams.
 BARNET ABRAHAMS. I live in  Great Peter-street, Westminster, and am a broker. On the 16th of January I lost four wine glasses - they were down an area about a foot and a half off; I saw them about half an hour before - they are worth about 16d; I have seen the prisoner about Westminster.
   MARIA MORRIS. I am servant to Mr. Abrahams. I was minding the shop - the glasses were on a table down in the area; I did not see the prisoner before he took the glasses from the table - it was not far from the ground; the table was higher than the ground - persons passing could not take them without reaching over; it was between six and seven o'clock in the evening - I think on the 16th of January; the lamps were lighted; he came to the table; I told him to go away, or I would call my master - he used a bad expression, and before I could call my master, the glasses and the prisoner were gone: I saw him afterwards in custody of the officer - there was no other person near the table; I saw the officer before the prisoner was taken, and had described him both to the officer and to my master.
   GEORGE JAMES JENNINGS. I am an officer. On the evening of the 16th I learnt that the glasses were taken; a few minutes after they were taken, the prosecutor came to me, and said young Hivall had taken them - that he knew that it was him by the description of the girl; I knew the prisoner before - I took him the next evening; the glasses have not been found.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years.
