674. HENRY JONES, alias Derbyshire (15), Stealing 1 handkerchief,
value 48 of George Swallow, from his person.
GEORGE HART. I am a butcher, of Long-alley, Shoreditch-on 13th June,
between 11 and 12, I was in Barbican, and saw the prisoner and 2 others
following the prosecutor-the prisoner took the prosecutor's handkerchief
from bis right pocket behind, gave it to one of the others, and they all went
up a court-1 told an officer immediately, who went after them-I went to
the prosecutor-the officer caught the prisoner-I am sure he is the boy.
GEORGE SWALLOW. I was going up Barbican-Hart told me something
-I felt in my pocket, and missed my handkerchief, which had been safe
two or three moments before I have not seen it since.
THOMAS MORTIMER EATON (Policeman). I took the prisoner-he said it
was not him.
GUILTY.-He was further charged with having been before convicted.
