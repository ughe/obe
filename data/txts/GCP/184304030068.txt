OLD COURT.--- Saturday, April 8th, 1843.
Second Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
1156. WILLIAM CONEY was indicted for unlawfully obtaining by false
pretences llb. weight of isinglass, value 138.; to which he pleaded
Guilty.-Confined Two Months.
1157. JOHN THOMAS THOMPSON, alias Edward White, was
dicted for feloniously forging and uttering, on the 21st of Jan., an acquir-
tance and receipt for the sum of 61. 10s. ; also on the 13th of Jan., one other
acquittance and receipt for 21. 43. 6d., with intent to defraud Albert Dumm-
ler and others; also for embezzling 331., which he had received on account
of Albert Dummler and others, his masters; to which he pleaded
GUILTY.-Confined Two Years.
