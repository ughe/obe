PERCY JOHN CLARK. I am assistant divisional surgeon of police-on
20th September I examined Lottie Jones at the police-station-I found
her suffering from three contused wounds at the back of the head, two
on the left side and one on the right, and one incised wound of the right
eye down to the bone-they were all serious wounds-considerable
violence must have been used to inflict them; they might have been
caused by the head of this chopper-I saw it at the Police-court; there
were bloodstains on it.
The Prisoner, in his statement before the Magistrato, and in his defence,

sat down, and remembered nothing else till he was in the Police-station.
