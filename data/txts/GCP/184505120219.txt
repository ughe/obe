WILLIAM HENRY STEVENS. I am a grocer and cheesemonger, and
live at Barking, in Essex. About the 11th of April I bought this half-
hogshead of vinegar of Mr. Sandifer for 30s., in a regular way of business
- it was found at my place.
Cross-examined. Q. Was it worth more than 30s. ? A. A regular
vinegar-house traveller called on me, and I told him what I gave for this,
and I bought a 20 vinegar better than this was- I am not a judge, but
according to my judgment, I did not consider it anything like the quality
--there was a name on the cask.
THOMAS PRITCHARD (police-constable P 22.) I was looking out for the
prisoner at the latter end of April—he was taken on Sunday, the 4th of

of vinegar from Collingridge and Davis-he said he could explain the
matter, he had received an order from Mr. East, of Goldsmith-row, when
it was sent there Mr. East did not want it, he had then bought it of Mr.
East, and sold it to Mr. Sandifer.

1231. JOHN KING was indicted for unlawfully obtaining 36 yards of
linen cloth, value 21. 11s. 8d., the goods of Richard Breman, by false
pretences.
RICHARD BREMAN. I am a linen-draper, and live at Wandsworth. On
the 28th of March the prisoner came to me, and said be had been sent by
Mrs. Rote the butcher for twenty-eight yards of linen-cloth-I believed he
came from her, and let him have twenty-eight yards of linen-cloth, which
came to 21. 118. 8d.
ANN Rote. I am a butcher, and live in Larkhall-lane-I did not send
the prisoner for any goods to Mr. Brenam, nor did I receive the pro-
perty--I did not know the prisoner.
GUILTY.--Confined Four Months.
