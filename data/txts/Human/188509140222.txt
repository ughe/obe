 904. WILLIAM BARBER  (26), Breaking and entering the dwelling-house of George Hudson , and stealing a watch, a pair of ear-rings, and other articles, his property.
 MR. DUKE  Prosecuted; MR. PURCELL Defended. GEORGE MITSON. I am a shipping agent, of 7, Adelphi Villas, East Dulwich—on 17th June, about 8.30 a.m., I left my house locked up safely—I returned about 6 o'clock and found the front door forced open, some drawers and boxes broken open, and I missed a gold watch, a meerschaum pipe, some ear-rings, and other things, worth 10 l . or 15 l .—I have not seen them since. MARIA GILLARD. I live at 6, Adelphi Villas—on 17th June, about 1 o'clock, I saw two men passing—they stared at the house next door very much; they were a very short distance from me—about 5 o'clock I saw the same two men come out of Mr. Mitson's house and walk down the pavement—the prisoner is one of the me; I saw his full face—he had a bandage on his hand as if it was hurt—he had a bowler  hat and a loose coat—I saw the prisoner a week after with five other men; I knew him directly—I said "He is the third man from the bottom" without hesitating, and that was the prisoner.
   Cross-examined.  I was looking out at the window, which is level with the ground—I had to look across the garden; there were no plants to obstruct the view—the window was shut—I saw their full faces as well as their side faces—my evidence was read to me before the Magistrate, and I believe I said he was the man—I won't swear I did not say "I can't swear he is the man"—I have not talked about it to the police-man. AMY GILLARD. I lire at 6, Adelphi Villas, East Dulwich—on 17th June I was at the window and saw the prisoner knocking at a house—half an hour after, he passed with a man and stared at me—he had one hand bound up—he had a felt hat and, I believe, a blue coat loose—I saw him with four other men three weeks after, and identified him at once.
   Cross-examined.  No one was with me—Maria Gillard is my mother—she went to the station when I did; she went in first—I don't think she spoke to me when I came out. FREDERICK GREY.
