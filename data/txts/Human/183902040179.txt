 734. SAMUEL ALEXANDER was indicted for stealing, on the   29th of January , 2 pairs of boots, value 9 s  .; and 1 pair of shoes, value 3 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Samuel Janes . WILLIAM UDELL. I live in North-street, Maida-hill, opposite Mr. Janes, a shoemaker. At eight o'clock on the evening of the 29th of January, I was looking through my shop window, and saw the prisoner behind the counter, in Mr. Janes' shop, and near the window—I ran over, and found him at the further end of the counter with a pair of shorn in his hand—he attempted to remove some boots, but I prevented him, and gave him in charge—the policeman took two pairs of boots out of his pockets. JOHN ROCKET( police-constable D  33.) I was sent for, and found on him two pairs of boots. SAMUEL JANES. These are my property.
   Prisoner.  I hope you will be merciful. GUILTY.* Aged 16.— Confined Three Months, and Whipped.
 735. WILLIAM GRIFFITHS was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of January , 1 handkerchief, value 5 s  ., the goods of David Laing , from his person. DAVID LAING. I live in Lark Hall-lane, Clapham, and am an attorney. On the 22nd of January, I was in St. Martin's-lane—I felt a tug at my pocket—I turned instantly, and saw the prisoner about a foot behind me, with both his hands behind him, and my handkerchief in his hand—I said, "How came you by that handkerchief?"—he said, "What handkerchief?" I said, "The one you took out of my pocket"—he threw it down—I took it up, and placed my arm round his neck—he struggled, and nearly threw me down in the mud, but I put out my right hand, and a friend assisted me—I got up again, and put my arm round his neck again—he struck me about the head and body, and called to some companions to help him, but I held him till the officer came and took him. THOMAS CROSS. I am a constable. I was coming down St. Martin's-lane—there was a crowd, and the prosecutor was calling "Police"—I ran up, and collared the prisoner—he said, "I will go with you, but I am not going to be lugged about for nothing"—here is the handkerchief, it was not soiled at all—he was struggling very hard with the prosecutor. WILLIAM HENRY WYATT.
