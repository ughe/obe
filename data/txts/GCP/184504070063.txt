824. JOHN MILLER was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of March,
I pair of trowsers, value II.; 3 stockings, 3s. ; 2 shirts, 28. ; and 1 bag,
21.; the goods of Francis Monk.
FRANCIS Monk. I belong to the Unity fishing-smack, of Rochester,
lying at Billingsgate. On Tuesday evening, the 11th of March, I went
ashore at eight o'clock - I left the cabin door locked, and the articles stated
safe in the cabin- came back at eleven, and found the lock of the cabin
lying on the deck, and the bolt alongside of it-I went into the cabin, and
missed the things-these now produced are them.
PIERCE Driscol (police-constable H 24.) About one o'clock, on Wed-
nesday morning, I stopped the prisoner in Rosemary-lane, with this bag
on his shoulder, and this shirt in his hand, offering it for sale | asked
what he bad-he said, “A shirt" -I asked what he had in the
bag-he said, "Some stockings"-I asked what he was-he said, A
sailor"-seeing no appearance of a sailor about him, I searched the bag,
and found it contained the articles stated-he said the stockings were his
OWO-I asked his name-he would not give it-I asked him the marks
about the shirts-he said there was no mark the letters F M are on it.
John Hughes. I live in Leman-row, White Lion-street, Whitechapel.
On Tuesday or Wednesday evening & man asked me to buy this shirt-the
prisoner is very like the man-to the best of my opinion, it was him, but
I had never seen him before I gave him 18. for it-1 gave it up to the
constable when he came for it.
(The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that he bought the things of
two men, for 2s. 9d.)
Not Guilty.
Before Mr. Baron Platt.
