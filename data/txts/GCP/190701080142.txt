customers. I engaged Turner as a roundsman; he did not know the

say the business was cheap for £250; Blay would not take £200 for
it now. With regard to the Cressida Road place, my brother thought
of going into the dairy business; he said he would take a place if I
would lend him £50 on the same terms as his previous loan to me,
and in addition that I should stay with him for a time at £2 a week
and make a round for him, and that when it showed a profit of £3
1 week he would give me a bonus of £50; I was to buy the necessary
horses and cart, and pay stabling, etc. I agreed to this, and the Cres
sida Road shop was taken. I worked up the round; the last week I was
there I sold 684 barn gallons on the round, 72 lb. of butter, and 400
eggs. I settled with my brother, and left the place three weeks before
Davies took it. I can refer to 20 of the largest companies in London
who sell their milk at 3d. & quart in the summer, and they pay divi-
dends of 10 per cent. ; at 4d. a quart milk shows a profit of 100 per
cent. I deny that there was any conspiracy between me and my
brother to sell fraudulent businesses.

think he is a truthful man, but he misunderstood what I told him. I

When I asked Bull to remove our furniture in the middle of the

banking account was my share of the fraud. The three businesses
referred to were all genuine.
