Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL Q. How much drink did they have at
your house? A. To my knowledge only one pint of ale between the two—
about three minutes before they left, Mr. Cole came and said something to me,
in consequence of which I went into the room to see if the groom was in a fit
state to take care of himself—I thought he was—when they left I went out
to see in which direction they went, and saw them going to fetch their horses
from the mews at the back of my house—that was the last I saw of them—
the groom walked Very well—it was about half-past two that the receipt was
signed—the ale then stood on the table, but they had not drunk any of it.
