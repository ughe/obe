BEFORE JUDGE RENTOUL.
(Monday, October 17.)
LILLEY, Sidney (40, contractor), and WRIGHT, William Her.
bert (55, contractor). Both conspiring together and with Edward
Alfred Miller and other persons unknown to defraud Robert Batten.
by of 26 bullocks ; obtaining by false pretences and with intent to
defraud 16 bullocks from the said Robert Battersby. (These two pH.
soners were tried last Sessions on another indictment. See page 552.)
Mr. Bodkin and Mr. Graham-Campbell prosecuted ; Mr. Ernest
Wild defended Lilley ; Mr. Curtis Bennett defended Wright.
After Mr. Bodkin's opening speech, Mr. Curtis Bennett submitted
that he had opened no case in law against Wright.
Judge Rentoul. I do not think there is any case. I do not
how the jury could possibly convict him if the evidence was no stronger
than has been opened.
Mr. Bodkin said after that expression of opinion he would offer no
evidence against Wright.
By direction, the jury returned a verdict of Not guilty.
The case against Lilley was then proceeded with.
