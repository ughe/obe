RICHARD PEAKE. I am manager of the Oxford branch of the
London and County Bank-the prisoner opened an account there on 18th
October, 1878-we at first credited him with several hundred pounds to
commence with-the account went on, and the last transaction was a
discount charge of 97. 98. in July last, which left a balance of 128. 5d. to
his account on 30th September 51. was paid in and something drawn out,
leaving a balance of 28. 7d. on the wrong side of the account-after that
items were paid in to meet cheques—I returned cheques on September
21st, and subsequently, there being only a few shillings to meet them-
these cheques for 31. 108. and 71. were afterwards presented on 28th
November a small sum was paid in, and then there was 28. 7d. against
bim.
Cross-examined. We did not close the account; the 26. 7d. was repaid
-when I was written to I wrote to Mr. Whileway saying that I
could not give any information as to Mr. Brown's pecuniary position-
this is a copy of my letter-I did not mention the state of his account
we retain title deeds of his, on which we have advanced 5601., which I con-
sider is the mortgage value of the property-I have a valuation of the
adjoining building land, which sold at a very high price--the land which
we have the title deeds of is 43 yards frontage and 53 yards in depth ;
that comes to close upon 1,2001.--I told the prisoner that I would not
advance any more upon that seeurity-several cheques were returned
after these two-the prisoner's property has not been valued, only the
adjoining property-it is freehold-I allow 25 per cent. margin for the
safety of the bank-at a forced sale it would realise about 7001.--We
renewed the promissory note several times after serving the valuation.
Re-escamined. Nine cheques have been returned-we took some pro-
perty conveyed to him by his father--notice was given to him that
nine guineas was charged against him for charges, and he paid it in
cash.
THOMAS HALL. I live at the Red Lion, Newton, North Bletchley, just
opposite Newton Manor-I have had constant opportunities of seeing
Mr. Brown-he had no horses there--I served him with meat.
Cross-examined. I received one cheque which was paid-I then received
another for øl. 178., but was written to not to present it.
ROLAND WOLSEY. I am clerk to the House Property Investment
Company-I let the prisoner a house in Dashville Road, Shepherd's
Bush-he paid the first half year's rent, and left the second unpaid.
Cross-ecamined. He left between Midsummer and Michaelmas.
MR. Dixon contended that there was no case to go to the Jury, as all the
cheques but the last had been paid. The Court overruled the objection, but
would if necessary reserve the point.
GUILTY on all the counts but the sixth and seventh. -Judgment Respited.
