On Monday morning, the 17th of June, I was at the Marsh-gate, Lambeth, about twenty-five mi nutes before six o'clock—I saw the prisoner and the deceased quarrelling at the corner of the Upper Marsh-gate, in the road—I cannot say what it was about—I heard the prisoners threatening—they were both excited by liquor—they had come out of a public-house—I had seen the deceased come from the back door—the waterman was taking him home—the pri soner came out at the front door, and they saw each other again.
