MR. MONTAGUE CHAMBERS. Q. Who did you see as the leading people at
the Vestry, when the complaint was made? 4. Mr. Sheppard, Armfield,
Jennings, Bannister, and a great many others.
COURT. Q. Do you mean that when you were at Sheppard's, he con-
plained of smelling the bones? A. Yes, and I could not smell it coming
from anywhere, but that is more than a year ago, and the premises were not
finished.
MR. SERJEANT PARBY called
-BANNISTER. I have never seen Mrs. Cannon-I have never offered
persons money not to give their evidence against Mr, Burgess
Cross-examined by MR. MONTAGUE CAMBERS. Did you happen, yesterday
morning, to be in Perseverance Court, or Place A. NO-I have not taken
part in this prosecution-I am not one of the persons who applied to the
Vestry-I am the freeholder and landlord of the John Bull-I am not the
person who makes the manure.
MR. SERJEANT PARRY. Q. Did you offer anybody 58, for giving evidence!
A. No, and I never left home yesterday morning till 9 o'clock, and I have
witnesses to prove it-my mistress was in bed, and I was not down till
9 o'clock.
