THIRD COURT.-Saturday, September 26th, 1868.
Before Robert Malcolm Kerr, Esq.
841. CECILIA HARRIS (17), and EMMA HOGWOOD (18), Stealing a
purse and 138. 6fd., the property of Emma Randall, from the person of
Louisa Randall.
MR. Wood conducted the Prosecution.
MARY ANN SORIVING. I live at 5, Glover's Hall Court, Beech Street-on
Friday, 14th August, between 4 and 5 o'clock, I saw the two prisoners in
Aldermanbury-I saw Harris take a purse like this one from the the prose-
cutrix-she went away—I waited on the spot till I saw a policeman-I told
him of it, and he took them into custody.
Thomas MELLEPONT (City Detective 155). I received information from the
last witness, and took the two prisoners I said to Harris, "One of you has
stolen a half-sovereign"—they both said they knew nothing about it-I took
them to the station while they were in the dook, and I was stating the
charge, I saw Harris with her hand down by her dress, and I took out of
her hand this purse, containing a half-sovereign, a florin, a shilling, and 6d.
EMMA RANDALL. I live at 34, Waterloo Street, St. Luke's sent my
daughter Louisa to the warehouse of Messrs. Paterson & Co.-there was
13. 62. owing to me-I work for them she never brought the money back-
this purse is mine-there was a halfpenny in it, which one of my lodgers had
given her.
WILLIAM BURRILL. I am a clerk to Messrs. Paterson & Co., warehousemen
-Mrs. Randall works for them on the 14th August I paid her daughter
138. 62.there was a half sovereign and some silver.
