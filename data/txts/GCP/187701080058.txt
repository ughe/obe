Thomas WALTON. I keep the Victoria, King Street, Snow Hill-I wanted
a barmaid, answered an advertisement, and a person named Annie Harrison
called on me--in consequence of what she said I went to the White Hart,
Long Acre, on 23rd October, and saw the prisoner-I told him that she had
stated that sho lived with him nine months and was leaving on her own
account as he was not paying her sufficient wages-he said that it was
correct, she was an excellent barmaid, but he could not afford to give her
more--she came to me on 26th October, and on Sunday, 5th November,
which was her day out, she went out and never returned on the Friday
after she left, Wood called and asked me where the young person was, and
from his manner I could see that he knew perfectly well-I said that she
had left; he said "What for?"-I said "I do not think it necessary that I
should explain to you," and that was all that passed-she left a box behind
her with scarcely anything in it; she applied for it afterwards and I allowed
her to take it away.
