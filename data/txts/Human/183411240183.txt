SAMUEL DELVES re-examined. Q.  You were in many public-houses with these men drinking? A.  So I believe—I do not know how long I was in their company—the name of the last house is the Red Lion—on coming out of the Red Lion I had hold of Bush's arm—Clarke was on be fore—he sung  out, with some seafaring phrases, "Come on, why do you stay so long?"—Bush gave me a blow, and said, "I have done for you"—I am certain the man who sung  out was Clarke.
   COURT . Q.  Is it true or false that your father gave you 4 l . 10 s . the day before (the 16th of November,) to buy a watch with? A.  He did not give it me to buy a watch—he has given me money, 5 l . 10 s .—he did not on the 16th of November—he gave me money to buy a watch a good bit ago, more than a year ago—I had no sovereigns about me, to my knowledge—I do not know—I do not think I had—I had some silver—I was not earn ing more than 10 s . a week at the time—I did not give 4 l . 10 s . for a watch the day before—I was hardly in a state to give evidence when I was before the Magistrate, I was so bloody—I have not lived at Mount Zion, Tun bridge-Wells, for some time—I should think it is nearly two years—I now live with my father—I have been to sea since that—I was going to Wool wich, to go on board a government steam-boat. NOT GUILTY.
