JURY. Q. Which shop did he go to first, to Robertson's or Morgan's 2
A. At Middle-row-Robertson lives higher up, towards the West-end, in
Holborn-the prisoner had a blue frock on, and working trowsers-he was
rather shabbily dressed, more like me, than he is now-I could not account
for his being in a position to buy these things-we did not go to any other
shops-he did not always pay with sovereigns--he got change out of some
things- cannot say how many sovereigns he paid at Morgan's, or at Robert-
do not know whether he carried the sovereigns in his hat or his
pocket.
