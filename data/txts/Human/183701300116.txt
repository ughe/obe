 603. DAVID BURFOOT was indicted for stealing, on the   5th of January , 50lbs. weight of fodder, value 2 s  ., the goods of William Peters Price .
   MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution . HENRY GOMME. I live at Hanwell, and am a shepherd. I was ordered by Price to watch the fodder several evenings, and on the 5th of January, about six o'clock, I observed the prisoner come into the field and walk along the hedge to the stack, and take up one truss of fodder—he brought it back again to where I saw him come in—I said, "Good night—he stopped—I got over and spoke to him—he begged my pardon, and said this was the first time he had done it—I asked him how he came to do so—he said, he did not know, but he would put it back again, which he did.
