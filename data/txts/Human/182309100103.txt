 1246.  WILLIAM SMITH and JOHN WATERS were indicted for stealing, on the 12th of August , a looking-glass, value 5 s.; a tea-tray, value 2 s., and a trunk, value 2 s., the goods of Thomas Horning.
   JOHN MURRAY. I am a watchman of Ratcliff. On the 29th of August, at four o'clock in the morning, I stopped the prisoners in company together in Rose-lane: Waters had the tea-tray and looking-glass, and Smith a trunk; Smith said, they got them from on board of ship; Waters did not contradict him. I secured them, opened the box, and found out the prosecutor who claimed it; he lived at Limehouse.
   THOMAS HORNING. I am a publican. This property is mine; I left it in care of Mr. Trist.
   WILLIAM TRIST. I live in Gunn-lane, Lime-house. This property was left in my care; I put them in my loft, and missed them on the 12th, and found a window open, which had been shut at night.
 SMITH - GUILTY. Aged 16.
 WATERS - GUILTY. Aged 17. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Two Months.
 1247.  STEPHEN ABBOTT and JOSEPH M'NAIR were indicted for stealing, on the 2d of September , a box, value 3 s., the goods of Jacob Temple ; and fourteen books, value 20 s.; two table-cloths, value 3 s., and eight napkins, value 2 s., the goods of Thomas Richard Barker.
   THOMAS HEARMAND. I am inspector of the watch, and live in Fashion-street, Spital-fields. On the 2d of September, between three and four o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoners passed my window; Abbott had a box, and M'Nair was behind him, in his company. I followed them about a hundred yards; M'Nair took a cap off Abbott's head, and took the box from him; I stopped them, and asked whose box it was - M'Nair said it was his own - Abbott heard it, and said nothing. I took them both into custody. It contained books and linen.
   SAMUEL GREEN. I am a watchman - I was with Hearmand - his account is correct.
   JACOB TEMPLE. I am an upholsterer, and live in Bedford-place, Commercial Road. On the 2d of September Thomas Barker sent me these books to pack up, which I did, and put them outside the door - and missed them at half-past two o'clock. Fashion-street is half a mile off.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 M'NAIR'S Defence. A sailor gave me the box to carry from Ratcliffe High-way to Finsbury-square. I met Abbott and asked him to give me a lift.
 ABBOTT -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Two Months.
 M'NAIR -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 1248.  THOMAS DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of August , seven saws, value 20 s.; two planes, value 4 s.; five chisels, value 3 s.; a rule, value 2 s.; a bevil, value 2 s., and a pair of compasses, value 1 s., the goods of John Lloyd.
   WILLIAM FULLBROOK. I am a patrol of Hackney. On the 8th of August, at four o'clock in the morning, I was in Hackney Marsh; I saw the prisoner walking backward and forward by the river, with something on his back - I went to him and asked what it was - he said his own tools - that he came from High Wycombe, and would answer no more questions. I found a basket on his back containing these tools.
   JOHN GARVA. The prisoner was brought to the watch-house with the tools; he could give no account of himself.
   THOMAS FURLEY. I am apprentice to Mr. John Lloyd of Hommerton. I know these tools very well. They were taken from the work-shop on the 8th of August. The tiles were taken off the roof, and the laths cut away.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. Confined One Year.
