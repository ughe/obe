2159. JAMES DIGNUM was indicted for embezzlement. 1
JAMES MOUNT. I am servant to Mr. Pickering, of Clapham. On
the 18th of June I paid the prisoner 88. for Mrs. Wilson, his mistress. 6!
John Hart. On the 2nd of April I saw a Mr. Loat pay the prisoner
108, for his mistress.
Cross-examined by Mr. Doane. Q. Was this in consequence of a
fire at your house? A. Yes; the chimney was on fire, and Mrs. Wilson
was sent for-she carries on the business of a sweep the prisoner brought
the bill for it on the Saturday, and I saw him paid.
SARAH Wilson. The prisoner is my servant; it is his duty to pay
me money the same day as he receives it. On the 18th of June I sent
him to Mr. Pickering with a bill-he only paid me 48. of it it was his
duty to give me 88., if he received it-on the 2nd of April I sent him to
Hart for 108., but he only paid me 8s. of it-he ought to have paid me
in full.
