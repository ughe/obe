FITZGERALD, Edward (43, clerk), being a servant to Marshalls,
Limited, and to Eli Marshall, did steal divers of their moneys; being
a clerk in the employment of Marshalls, Limited, and of Eli Marshall,
unlawfully with intent to defraud did falsify a cash book belonging to
his said employers ; feloniously forging the endorsements on divers
bankers' cheques with intent to defraud.
Prisoner pleaded guilty of falsification of accounts.
Sentence was postponed to next Sessions,
