FREDERICK GREEN. I am a solicitor, and live at No. 13, King's Bench
walk, Temple. I send my linen to a laundress at Camberwell-I used to put

chambers, and left it at the porter's lodge-on the morning of the 8th of May,

I have not seen any of my property since.
