me as partner with him. A man came to me at the end of June.
I explained the circumstances to him, and he said, “We must bus
your brother's interest out or start in opposition." We agreed to
pay my brother £10, and he was going to another firm, but before
paying him we suspected he was going to introduce the vinegar to his
firm, so we agreed to pay him 10s. a week. The ledger produced has
not been made up from the day book in my time. The London busi-
ness was chiefly cash, and the country credit. The house ageney

had very little to do with it. I was as a rule outside looking after
