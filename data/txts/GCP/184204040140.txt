1298. JOSEPH WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of
March, 13lbs. weight of candles, value 68., the goods of Robert Armstrong
his master.
John WATSON (police-constable G 25.) On the 31st of March I wa
in Jerusalem-passage, Clerkenwell, about four o'clock in the morning, and
saw the prisoner pass- I followed him to Albemarle-street-he had to
bundle under his arm-I asked him what he had-he said it was his clothes
-I examined, and then found it was candles-he then said he had bought
them of Mr. Armstrong, of Gray's-inn-lane, and that Michael had served
him with them about ten that night, before the shop was shut-he said
had come from there, and was going home-I took him into custody.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. What direction was he going in!
A. Down Jerusalem-passage-I stopped him in Albemarle-street, going in
the direction of Sutton-street.
WILLIAM GRAY. I am a policeman. I was on duty about four
o'clock in the morning of the 31st of March-I saw the prisoner past
ing-I sent Watson after him-after he was stopped I asked him
where he brought the candles from-he said he had got them at Mr. Am-
strong's, he had just left work, and was going home with them
I asked him who gave them to him-he said. "Michael"-I asked if Mr
Armstrong was aware of it-he said he was not-I asked him if he had
paid for them he said no, he had not, he should pay for them when Mr.
Armstrong settled with him, as he had done before ; that Mr. Armstrong
owed him some money-I took him and the candles to the station-
found two packets of candles wrapped up in his pockets, some in cloths
and some in paper-altogether there was about 13lbs. weight of candles,
Cross-examined. Q. Did not he say he worked for his master, and
owed him money? A. Yes---candle-makers do work in the night-il
nothing unusual to find him out at four o'clock in the morning.

night, on the 30th of March, in the workshop-he was employed there
a candle-maker-I left him there at eleven-on the 31st some can
