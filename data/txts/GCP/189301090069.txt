THIRD COURT.-Friday, 13th January, and NEW COURT, January
14th, 16th, and 17th, 1893.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
209. GEORGE BENJAMIN, Unlawfully obtaining by false pre-
tences from Lewis Solomon fifteen bear skins and 1,794 musquois
skins. Other Counts, for offences under the Debtors' Act.
MR. HORACE AVORY Prosecuted, and MR. GEOGHEGAN and
MR. CARRINGTON Defended.
The prisoner, in the hearing of the Jury, ploaded guilty to the counts
alleging offences under the Debtors' Act, on which they found a verdict of
GUILTY.-Judgment respited.
