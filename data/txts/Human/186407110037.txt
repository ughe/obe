 692. ARTHUR ARNOW  (40) , Stealing 45 pairs of uppers for boots, the property of Abraham Flatau and another, his masters.
   MR. GRESHAM conducted the Prosecution, and MR. KEMP the defence. HENRY GEORGE PRIESTLEY. I am in the employ of Messrs. Flatau, of Leadenhall-street, boot and shoe manufacturers—I am a clicker,  which is a cutter of boot tops—the prisoner was in their employment for some years, up to May last, when he was discharged—on Monday, 20th June, I was in the neighbourhood of Victoria-park, and passed a boot shop, kept by Henry Nathan—I recognised some boots in the window, by the cut of them, as belonging to Messrs. Flatau, with whom I communicated, and on the Wed nesday following Mr. Jacob Flatau and I went to the shop, and asked to see some men's boots—I looked at the marks on the uppers, and knew that they were my own cutting—I had an officer waiting outside, and Mr. Flatow went to the door and beckoned him in—twenty-nine pairs of boots were then produced, the uppers of which I recognised by my own mark of the size.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Do I understand you that your employers manufac ture the uppers? A.  They manufacture the boots and shoes—I have been in their employment two years, and the prisoner has been there all the time—I do not know whether he was discharged or left of his own accord—Nathan was given in custody, and afterwards discharged.
   MR. GRESHAM . Q.  Were these pegged or riveted boots? A.  Pegged—Messrs. Flatau do not manufacture pegged boots, only riveted and sewn ones. JACOB FLATAU.
