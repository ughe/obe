say, Come with me to my club "-when Brummagem Bill got out of
the cab he had hold of the prisoner, whose coat was open; he seemed to

if he was talking to him-I said before, 66 Brummagem Bill was elutehing

sure Brummagem Bill ran away; he did not run very far, because people

smoke from the revolver-I could not see what became of him after he
ran-I did not see him fall-I did not stop to see him picked up and
taken to the hospital-I did not stop to see anybody fall-there were a
lot of men round there, and they appeared to be pushing one another
about-I did not see the prisoner at all before they tried to get into the
eab-I knew Brummagem Bill by sight before this, only to speak to once;
I did not know his real name, or any of his real names; I have only
heard him called Brummagem BillI knew Ellen Johnson, but never

would rather not tell their names, I mean I dare nottwo shots were
