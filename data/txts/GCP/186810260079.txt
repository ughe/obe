Cross-eramined. Q. Have you known John Bennett some time 4. About
eight years--I know some of his family, and have been at his house he

some considerable income by letting it in lodging his wife makes all the
expenses of the house and something over during the eight years I have
known him, his character has been most excellent he has been an upright

say he made 301. or 401. a-year by that.
