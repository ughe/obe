obtaining, by false pretences, of Matilda Cole, 21. ; the moneys of Richard
Alfred Cole.

chandler's shop at Hounslow—on 27th April, M'Cartby came and asked if
my husband was at home—I said he was not—he then said be had brought
some tobacco down by the train for a gentleman at the large shop above,

cheap—I asked what tobacco it was ; he said, was 27 lbs. of it,
