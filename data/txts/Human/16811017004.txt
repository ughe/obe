   Alexander Younger was Indicted for Robbing one John De Bule , a French-man living in St. Martins in the fields , of some Buttons, Silver and other things to a considerable value, but upon the examination of the Evidence, it appearing that he had been formerly a Custom-house-wayter, and at that time came to Aid and Assist one that seised uncustomed goods in the possession of the said De Bule, he was   aquitted.
   Elizabeth Warner was Arraigned for that she on the 24th of September last Robbed one Thomas Toply her Master of several sums of money the which she had confessed when she was carryed before a Justice of Peace, but having learned he lesson in Newgate, again denyed the same but the Evidence being plain against her, she was found   guilty.    Ruth Carter a Charwoman who had been acquainted in the house of the   Lord Brunkard was indicted for Fellonously entring the said house by takeing a pain of Glass out of the Window and opening the Cagement and entring, conveyed thence Lenin and such like to the value of 30 pounds, which being found in places where she had disposed of them, she confessed the fellony, but would by no means confess the breaking the Glass, yet it being evident she was found   guilty.    George Mathews a Souldier belonging to the Tower was Tryed for killing one Penner being his fellow Souldier , by knocking him down with his Musqnet, but a quarrel and sufficient provocations being proved, he was found guilty of Manslaughter only.
