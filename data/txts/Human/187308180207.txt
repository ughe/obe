I am an oil and colourman, 170, Lambeth Walk—in September last young Witham came and offered me some yellow wax for sale—he brought a sample first, and then a bag, about three-quarters of a cwt.—he asked 1 s . per lb. for it—he said he had bought it at a rummage sale of the South Western Railway—I asked him to show me a catalogue of the sale—he did not do so—I would not buy it, and he took it away.
   Cross-examined by MR. LYON . It was what was known in the trade as Jamaica beeswax; it was not all yellow, but light and dark—the market value at that time was about eight guineas a cwt. WILLIAM PEACOCK( Police Inspector L ) I was on duty at Tower Street station on the morning of 7th June—at that time Witham was in custody in one of the cells—T visited him during the night and morning, and asked if he was all right—he said "Yes," and he wanted to make a statement to me about the matter he was in the cell for, and requested that it might be detailed to the Magistrate—he said "I know all about it; I had some of the wax given to me to sell; I offered it for sale to a foreman of Mr. Hall's, Lambeth Road; they refused to purchase it. I left a sample; that was returned to me. I was employed by Mr. George Castle, of Union Street, Lambeth Walk; in June last a man drove up in Union Street, with a horse and cart, in front of a paper shop, near Castle's house, with three lots of wax. I weighed one of them; it weighed three-quarters of a cwt I don't remember what the others weighed. Mr. Castle was present at the time. SOLOMON DALES( Policeman L  34), At 10 o'clock on the night of 1st May, I took Harriett Scott into custody—I told her she would be charged with others in stealing wax from Messrs. Field's, and that Ralph was at the station—she said Ralph was a lodger of hers; if he knew anything about the wax she did not. WILLIAM RUMBLE( re-examined ). I have sworn that I was not taken to the Police Court by two policemen; that was what I said, and that was what I meant—I was taken by one man—I was not charged at all; no charge was made; I was taken to the station and let go—I was summoned by a man named Enowles for an assault—it was all put on to me by the Castles—no charge was entered on the sheet; I can swear that.
   Howe and the two Castles received good characters.
   RALPH  — GUILTY of stealing.
   SCOTT, M. A. CASTLE, HOWE, GEORGE CASTLE , and WITHAM  — GUILTY of receiving. Judgment Respited.
