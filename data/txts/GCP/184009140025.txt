2250. WILLIAM SMITH was again indicted for stealing, on the 20th
of August, 6 brushes, value 135. 6d., the goods of Harriet Frances
Batchelor.
HARRIET FRANCES BATCHELOR. I am single, and live in Quebee-street,
New-road. On the 20th of August the prisoner came to my shop, and
asked to look at a set of shoe-brushes--I showed him some he said he
wanted larger ones--I showed him two other sets-he looked at them, and
said he did not know which the parties would like, if I had any body to
send with him to No. 15, in the Grove-a little girl was standing by my
side-she said she knew where he said they were to go, and she would go
-I gave two sets of brushes to the little girl, and have not seen them
since, nor the prisoner, till he was in custody.

tea of the same description as mine the remains of the box corresponded
with the lid of the box I had at home.
