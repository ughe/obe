I was with Whitlam—I saw Reynolds go into Aitkin's shop—I followed him, inside—Baines had just passed—I could not see him when I was inside—I saw Reynolds take a 4 d . piece from his pocket, and put it on the counter—I told Mrs. Aitkin I thought it was had—she said she thought it was good—I said I was an officer, and I asked Reynolds if it was a bad one—Mrs. Aitkin took it and marked it, and gave it to me—Reynolds asked if there was no more money in the till—she took it out, and showed him there was no more—this is the 4 d . piece.  2177. JANE JURY was indicted for stealing 3 3/4 yards of linen cloth, value 9 s  .; 5 handkerchiefs, 3 s  .; 3 blankets, 15 s  .; and 76 yards of calico, 1 l  . 6 s  .; the goods of Thomas Shaw , her master.
   MR. HUDDLESTON conducted the Prosecution. RICHARD ROGERS. I am shopman to Thomas Shaw, linen-draper, High-street, Southwark—the prisoner was his cook. On the 18th of Oct., about ten o'clock at night, I went to the room occupied by myself—there is a spare bed in that room—as I went up stairs, I heard a rustling noise—I went into my room—there was no candle there, but I had one in my hand—I looked under the spare bed and saw the prisoner, and under the other bed I saw two parcels—I asked the prisoner who the first parcel belonged to—she said, to herself, and the other to Mrs. Cornelius—I opened the first parcel, and in it were three blankets, the property of my employer—I separated one of them from the other—it was one of a pair, of which I had sold one the day previous—in the other bundle I found fifty-three yards of calico, which was my employer's, and had his mark on—I then, with the officer, searched the prisoner's box, and found five cambric handkerchiefs, a length of lawn, and a piece or two of calico, which were my employer's, and had our private mark on them—these articles had not been sold—the prisoner was to have left on the Monday following.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  Who is Mrs. Cornelius? A.  A friend of the prisoner's, who came to the house occasionally. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined One Year .
