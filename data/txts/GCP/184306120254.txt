MARK WILLIAM GREEN (police-constable L 2.) I went into the Coburg
Arms on the morning of the 22nd of March, about a quarter to two o'clock-
there were from fifty to sixty persons outside—I saw Luxton bleeding from
the leg—Moody took him to Mr. Brooks, a surgeon-I went shortly after,
and found him there-the surgeon endeavoured to stop the blood-nothing
was done but tying a handkerchief round his leg—I searched for the prisoner,
but could not find him-I knew he lived in Holland-street- I went there
twice, and could not find him.
Cross-examined. Q. What sort of a house is the Coburg Arms? A. I
think it is the worst house in our division, frequented by girls of the town and
thieves.
WILLIAM Moody (police-constable S 8.) I took the prisoner on the 18th
of April, near Chalk-farm-) told him I had a charge against him for stabbing
two men near the Victoria Theatre some time ago-he said, "I know nothing
about it.”
HARRY BENJAMIN LAWRENCE BROOKS. I am a house-surgeon at West-
minster Hospital. I examined Luxton--he was bleeding a great deal from a
wound in the leg, near the ankle, inflicted apparently by a sharp instrument-
I stopped the bleeding, and took him into the hospital-he remained there six
or seven weeks-Bennett is there still.
Cross-examined. Q. Was it such a wound as a dinner-knife would pro-
duce ? A. Yes—it was a large knife-he was kept there for that wound
during that time.
Not Guilty.
2045. WILLIAM MONAHAN was again indicted for feloniously as-
saulting Samuel Bennett, on the 22nd of March, and stabbing and wounding
him on his left leg, with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm.
Mr. Payne conducted the Prosecution.
ALFRED COLLINS. I was at the Coburg Arms on the morning of the 22nd
of March, and saw the prisoner there--I saw him and Luxton fighting for
some minutes, from one end of the bar to the other—I went to the bar, hear-
ing the noise, and Bennett and I were together, within two or three paces of
the prisoner and Luxton-Bennett went to part them—they were standing
fighting, but when Bennett got to them through the crowd, the prisoner
