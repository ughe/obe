MART WINCH. I am the wife of Edward Winch, who keeps a beer-shop at Trafalgar-row, Greenwich. On 9th Sept. the female prisoner came and asked for a bottle of ginger-beer—I served her—she laid down a shilling and asked if I had a fourpenny-piece—I said I had not, and gave sixpence and fourpence—she said she wanted penny beer—I said I did not tell it—I missed the shilling, and gave her another, supposing I must have takenit—she said she would have a glass of ale—I gave her a glass and tenpenee—she gave me a shilling—I afterwards found I was a shilling short.
   Cross-examined. Q.  That was when the policeman came? A.  Before that—I was telling my husband as the policeman came in. JOHN WHITE( policeman, R  180). I took William, and said he was charged with receiving money of the woman—he said he had a right to receive what he liked from his wife—I found on him two sovereigns, sixteen shillings., twenty sixpences, thirty-two fourpenny-pieces, fourpence-halfpenny, and two ginger-beer bottles. NOT GUILTY.

PHCEBK BEAUMONT. I am daughter of Charles Beaumont, who keep a toy-shop. On 9th Sept., about six at night, the female came and asked for a. ball of white cotton—she put a shilling down—I did not take it up because she asked me the price of so many things—the cotton came to a halfpenny—I gave it her with two fourpenny-pieces, one penny, and five halfpence—I exchanged the halfpenny ball for two farthing ones—I turned round and the shilling was gone—I asked if she had taken it up—she said, "No"—no one else was there—she left the shop with the change and cotton.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You never took up the shilling? A.  No; the cotton was sold to her—I did not mean her to have it on trust—there was two shil lings in the till before she came, and the same after she left. JOHN CARPENTER( policeman ,) I saw Sarah go into the shop—the male prisoners waited thirty or forty yards off—she came out to them—I took her, and told her what it was for—she said people were great fools to give her change without having the money. JOHN WHITE( policeman, R  180). I took William, and told him the charge—he said he had a right to receive what he liked from his wife—I searched him and found a sovereign and other money. ANN GANDT. I searched Sarah, and found two balls and one real of cotton, and sixpence three-farthings.
