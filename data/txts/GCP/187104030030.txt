DAKIN, Mayor.
THIRD COURT.-Tuesday, April 4th, 1871.
Before Robert Malcolm Kerr, Esq.
289. THOMAS JUDD (28), Stealing 3 cwt. 3 qrs. 17 lbs. weight of sugar,
the property of Charles Woolley, his master.
Mr. Moody conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM BRYANT. I am manager to Charles Woolley, of Aldgate, a
oarman and contractor-the prisoner was in his employment about three
weeks-on Friday, 14th February, he was sent to the London Dooks for
three hogsheads of sugar, one of which he was to deliver in Covent Garden.
another in Marlborough Street, and the other in the Queen's Road, Bays-
water-he ought to have been back with bis van about 3 o'clock-he came
between 7 and 8 o'clock, the worse for liquor-his wages were 218. a week.
JAMES CORDIAL. I was delivery-foreman at the East Quay, London
Docks on the morning of the 14th February I delivered three hogsheads
of sugar to the prisoner-I produce the weight-book-the hogsheads were
numbered “14,5 « 16," and a 20;" No."14" weighed 18 cwt. 2 qrs. 2 lbs.;
No. "16" weighed 20 cwt. 1 qr. 6 lbs.; and No. "20" 19 cwt. 3 qrs. 13 lbs.
--he applied for the goods at 9.6, and the delivery was finished af 9.30.
CHARLES Ase. I am a cooper, employed at the London Docks—the three
hogsheads which were delivered on the 14th were delivered in good condition
these are the nails we use to fasten the tins on with, and the nails on this
tin are clout nails—we don't use them.
FREDERICK Srow. I was warehouseman to Mr. Mills, of Queen's Road,
Bayswater-on 14th February I received a hogshead of sugar, "No. 14,"
from the prisoner, about 4.30—when I received it, it weighed 16 owt. 3 grs.
26 lbs.- it ought to have weighed 18 owt. 2 qrs. 2 lbs. -- these boards are
in the same condition now as when I received them I had nothing to

ANTHONY WILSON MONGER (City Detective). I took the prisoner on
Saturday night, 25th February, at Mr. Woolley's place—I told him I should
apprehend him for stealing 3 cwt. 2 qrs. weight of sugar-he said "I know
nothing at all about it; you can do as you like with me”-I said it bad
been stolen from two casks, one on the transit from the London Dooks to
Bayswater, and the other to Marlborough Street-I found nothing on him
- he gave a correct address.
The Prisoner's statement before the Magistrate : "As I had the sugar so I
delivered it."
The Prisoner's Defence. As I had the hogsheads, so I delivered them, one
after the other, at three different shops.
GUILTY.
