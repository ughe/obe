   Cross-examined by MR. SERGEANT BOMPAS . Q.  Do you still carry on your father's business in Coventry-street? A.  I do—I was not in the Strand on Monday, the 2nd of April, to my recollection, nor on Tuesday, to my recollection—I always sleep there—I go there between nine and ten o'clock at night—I have nothing at all to do with the business there—I have no share in the business there or in Coventry-street—I took the two bars to Messrs. Bult's on the Wednesday—my father gave them to me—he did not tell me that he suspected or believed they were stolen—he told me they were gold dust—he gave me no reason to suppose he believed they were stolen at that time—I have no recollection of any thing of the kind—I do not recollect his intimating that they were stolen—I recollect his stating to me that these bars contained gold dust.
   Q.  Did you at once believe that it was the gold dust stolen? A.  I had my suspicions, not that my father believed it to be stolen, but I had my suspicions—I do not know whether my father suspected it or not—I did not ask him any questions at all about it.
   Q.  You believed they were the stolen gold dust when your father said they contained gold dust? A.  I had my suspicions—I cannot say I be lieved it—I had my suspicions, that is the short of the matter—my father merely said they contained gold dust—I immediately suspected it was the stolen gold dust—I had no such suspicion before my father said they were gold dust—no further conversation took place between me and my father about it, that I recollect, at any time—I do not recollect that he told me he had disposed of a bar or two before—I think I heard something of the affair of a bar being melted from my father—I do not know whether he told me so—I did not say I heard it from my father—I did not hear it from my father—I heard something about it in the house—I am not sure whether it was my father, my brother, or John Dean that told me about this other bar—I have not sworn it was not my father who told me—I am not certain who it was I heard it from—it might have been from my father—that was all that I heard from any body, that there had been a bar of gold sold—I heard nothing further—it was on Wednesday that I took the bars to Messrs.
