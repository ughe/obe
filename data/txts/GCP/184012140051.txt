306. JAMES SMITH was indicted for a misdemeanor.
JAMES BRANNAN police-sergeant G 20.) On the 27th of November, 1
was on duty in Goswell-street, in plain clothes. About half-past six o'clock
in the evening - I saw the prisoner and another coming along, and their hands
met as if something passed between them--I saw the prisoner put his left!
haud into his outside coat-pocket-I seized him-he forced his hand from
his pocket, and threw something across which glittered and sounded on the
stones like money-he resisted very much, and Cole, who was with me,
was forced to come to my assistance-I got him to a public-house-I then
went back and searched where I heard the money fall-I found nothing,
but I saw Cole with two shillings in his hand, and in the act of picking up
a third-I found on the prisoner 78, 10d. in silver, and 13d. in copper, and
some cheese, and tobacco in little parcels.
