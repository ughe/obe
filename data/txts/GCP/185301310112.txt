Prisoner's Defence. I can only state that I had been in the Royal Artillery,
nine or ten years, my only fault being drink; I got my discharge; I was
never a thief, or any other blackguard, but the drink has caused me to be
unemployed; I never was guilty of stealing anything; God above knows
that Blake gave me these things not to expose him; he said he would give
me anything ; I said, being hard up, if he had anything to give me I would
receive it thankfully, but nothing could induce me to keep his company
any more.
