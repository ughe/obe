John JACKSON. I am a chair-maker, at Fashion-court, Spitalfields. I
work for Mr. Isaacs--I went to Mr. Sadgrove with the prisoner, with a
chair-I saw Mr. Thompson pay him sl. 4s. for it-he returned to my
master's door with me he then gave me the slip.
Jows THOMPSON. In Sept., 1846, I paid 31. 4s. for the chair-I cannot
swear it was to the prisoner--I believe he is the man- I do not know him or
Jackson - I got a check changed for Mr. Sadgrove, put down the money, and
the person took it up-his shopmate was with him-I received a ticket from
