JOSEPH RAILTON. I carry on business in Regent-street, and am a silk mercer—I am in partnership with Mr. Peascod. The prisoner was in my employ, for upwards of two years, as porter—we have lately missed pro perty from the stock—here is one piece I missed myself—it has my mark on it—I have looked at them all—I found the roller belonging to these forty yards of silk, and here is some sky-satin I can identify—here is my number on one end of it, and the same number was found the same evening. his under the counter—they are all ours, I have no doubt—the value in 40 l . and upwards.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Had you any reason to sus pect him? A.  None—I have no reason to know that he has been led away, or that there was a woman concerned, till the officer came to our house—the officer found two keys on him, which led to some inquiries—his parents an poor, but I believe very honest and respectable—our establishment is very large—property does not generally leave the shop with the private mark on it—it ought never to do so. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Transported for Fourteen Years.
