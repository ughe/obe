MR. GEOGHEGAN to Hillier. I said before the Magistrate, In the
month of June a man came into the office with Indian stamps; I was
alone, and could not buy them; the man stopped in the office; I told
Humphries I had offered 8d. per rupee for them; he said he should risk
the selling of them, if he did not somebody else would "-before he
bought them I said, "If I were in your place I would risk buying them."
By MR. GILL. When the offer of 8. per rupee was made, Humphries
was to complete the transaction-he paid 169. or 178., and after the man
had gone he said it was a bargain, and asked me if I thought the stamps
were forgeries.
