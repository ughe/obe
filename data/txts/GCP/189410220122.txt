bitter-Mr. Crabb, in the policeman's presence, picked up Mr. Cherry's
pot and smelt it, and wrote something down in his book-he asked him
for my name, and Mr. Cash said, “Take Mr. Cherry's, as it is he who
ordered the bitter"-Mr. Crabb then went out--there were one or two

ginger-beer, with a dash of bitter, in the saloon bar. (The witness here

different bars, the public, private, and saloon bars of the house.)
