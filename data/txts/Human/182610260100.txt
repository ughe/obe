   SOPHIA FORDHAM re-examined. Q. How were they dressed? A. Norris had corded breeches and a corded jacket, of a dark colour - Edward had a kind of sailor's shirt, a light coloured jacket, and a cap - my father and Harrison took the prisoners - I was close to them; I swear positively they are the men, they were close together.
   JAMES MASON. I am a publician. I changed a 5l. note on the 23d of October, but it was not to either of the prisoners.
   JAMES FORDHAM. I am an officer. I apprehended Norris that evening from my daughter's information - she described him - I have known him for years. I took Edwards about twelve o'clock next day; he had a blue coat on. Norris was in a corderoy jacket and trowsers; we took him to the watch-house; he was talking to some woman there, and said there had been a to-do about a 5l. note at the bottom of Golden-lane. I know they frequented the Golden Anchor public-house all day long, and have moved them from there a hundred times - Edwards had changed his jacket for a coat.
   THOMAS HARRISON. I was with Fordham, and took Norris - Edwards was taken next morning; he had a blue coat on; I had seen Norris at the Golden Anchor scores of times; Edwards had changed his light coat for a blue one with a man named Bone.
 NORRIS' Defence. I was at the house having a pint of beer; Fordham came in; I said to Mrs. Jones, "What does he say?" he said there was a 5l. note lost; "Is there?" said I, but no one took me; as I returned from the play, Harrison dragged me into the watch-house.
 EDWARD'S Defence. I was at the corner of my own court; one Bone came and said, "Will you sell me your coat;" I said, No - he said, "Let me try it one;" he did, and then went into a public-house to light his pipe - he came out with a 5l. note in his hand, and said, "I have a 5l. note;" he ran against me, and ran away, leaving me his coat to put on.
 EDWARDS -    GUILTY. Aged 17.
 NORRIS -    GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.
 2004.  CHRISTOPHER GUDGEON was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of September , 1 pair of oars, value 6s., the goods of George Leonard.
 GEORGE LEONARD. I am a  boat-builder, and live at Poplar. On the 26th of September I lost a pair of oars out of my shed, which was locked; I had placed them there about one o'clock, and, at a half-past seven, when I left work. I saw the prisoner and another about two hundred yards off, with them on their shoulders; I looked at the mark - they threw them down, and ran away; I pursued, and took the prisoner - they must have got over my wall.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. A man asked if I could row - I said, Yes, and he fetched the oars, but where from I do not know.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Transported for Seven Years.

RICHARD JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of September , 2 planes, value 7s., and 1 saw, value 2s., the goods of George Kitchener ; 2 saws, value 14s.; 2 planes, value 5s.; 1 axe, value 3s.; 1 square, value 1s.; 1 bevil, value 1s.; 2 gimblets, value 8d.; 1 basket, value 1s.; 2 centre-bits, value 1s., and 1 chalkline, value 1s., the goods of Thomas Newberry ; 1 saw, value 4s.; 1 plane, value 3s., and 1 square, value 1s., the goods of James Barnett.
 GEORGE KITCHENER. I am a  carpenter and joiner. I left these tools safe, at half-past five o'clock on the night of the 13th of September, in a hoard by the Duke of York's premises, in the Green-park, and missed them next morning - I believe the shed was locked. The prisoner was a stranger.
   EDWARD HEDDINGTON. I am a constable. On the 14th of September I saw Kitchener in Lawrence's shop, New-cut, Lambeth, where he identified a plane; I went into the back parlour, and found a basket containing the tools in the indictment, among others. I found the prisoner in custody of a patrol. I returned to the shop with a warrant, and found some more property.
   THOMAS NEWBERRY. I am a carpenter. Some of these tools are mine.
   THOMAS TRIBE. I am a carpenter, and work at the Duke of York's house. On the morning of the 14th of September I went to the Rose and Crown public-house; Kitchener said he had lost his tools; I went to the Newcut with him - he found some of his tools at Lawrence's; I saw Redman with some tools in his hand, offering them for sale; Kitchener's name was on them; he took me to the prisoner, at the Three Compasses public-house; McKear, the officer, was fetched, and found thirteen duplicates of tools on him.
   JAMES McKEAR. I am an officer. I took the prisoner, and found thirteen duplicates on him - one of which relates to this property - I found a small tool on him; I asked his name - he said, they called him anything, and he lived anywhere.
   WILLIAM BENJAMIN JONES. I live with Mr. Wells, apawnbroker, Broad-street, Bloomsbury - I have two saws, which I saw the prisoner pawn at our shop, on the 13th of September, between six and seven o'clock - I am positive he is the man.
