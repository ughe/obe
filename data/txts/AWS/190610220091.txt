FLORENCE LUDLow. I am unmiarried and am staying with my
brother. I have been a nurse in domestio service. I had
sum of E115 in the Post Office Savings Bank at Knightabridge
on October 4 last. On that day, about 6.30 p.m. I was walking
along the Edgware Road when the prisoner spoke to me. L
had not seen him before. We got into conversation, and then
walked through Hyde Park towards Knightsbridge. He
tour me his name was Alfred James. He asked me
my name, and I told him. He asked me if I Was single.
He told me he had a tobacconist's shop at Hampstead. We
arranged to meet the next day at three O'cloek at Knightabridge
by the Park gates. We did so, and had a walk together, and
then took a bus to Hammersmith. During our conversation he
proposed that we should get married. He said he had some
money in the Bank of England; he did not say how much, but
that I should draw out my money and he would make it up to
a thousand. I had told him that I had money in the Savings
Bank, but did not say how much Hesaid I should have the inte.
rest on the thousand, that it would be for my benefit. I met
him again the next day, October 6, and I then accepted his
proposal to marry me. He asked me to withdraw my money
from the Bank and to put it in the Bank of England. I agreed
to do so, and gaye notice of withdrawal. He went with me to
the Post Office, but stayed outside. I had by then told him
the amount of money I had. On October 9 I went with him
to draw the money. I then received the 2115 all in notes,
which I wrapped up in a parcel with the Bank book. We then
got on a City 'bus. The prisoner then took the paroel from me,
undid it, took the notes and counted them, and then put them in
his pooket. I thought we were going to the Bank of England.
When we got to Newgate we got off the 'bus, as he suggested
we should go to St. Paul's Oathedral to get a special license
from the Bishop of London. We went there, and he told me
to wait a few minutes. He went inside. I waited half an hour
but saw no more of him that day or of the speeial license or
amy notes. I went straightway to the Knightsbridge Post Office
and told them I had had my money taken away, I then went
to the City Police at the Old Jewry, and gave a deseription
of the prisoner. On the 19th or thereabouts I was taken to
the poliee-station and shown a number of men and identifed
the prisoner. There is not the slightest doubt he is the man.
Each day I saw him I spent most of the day with him.
