Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. The prisoner Dever frequented
your room at all? 4. No--Nicholls made my bagatelle-board for me-I
knew Grimaldi two or three years ago, by coming to a house which I for-
merly had-I bundled him out of my house in the Old 'Change six or eight
months ago, and he had never been there again to my knowledge, till the day
the balls were taken-I do not know whether be was a friend of Nicholls'--1
have heard Nicholls say he knew him well—I have never seen them together.
Mr. RYLAND.Q. Do you know where Grimaldi is now? 4. I believe
he was sentenced last Session to six months' imprisonment, for stealing these
balls.
Mr. PRENDERGAST to JOHN NICHOLLS. Q. It was in Nov. that you
bought the balls ? A. Yes, I think it was on 27th–I gave information of
it to Healey in Feb.--I gave 16s. for the twelve balls-I bought them ready
made-I sent them to Mr. Healey, ten minutes after. I got them from the
prisoner-I did not tell him how I got them I took 9s. of Mr. Healey-he
gave 108., but Is. my young man spent-I scarcely knew Grimaldi at that time
--I had heard of him-I did not know him well, Mr. Healey is wrong there--
I never had any acquaintance with him till about that time-he was pointed
out to me by several persons, the witness Hammond for one, in Farringdon-
street-Hammond is a relation of mine that was the first time I knew bim
-I have not met bim frequently at the Sussex beer-shop-I may have seen him
there, but very rarely- I never saw him there but once to my recollection-
I may have spoken to him, but have never been in his company five minutes
at a time I have no recollection of being in bis company but twice when I
saw him in Farringdon-street my young man, who was with me, said it was
Joey Grimaldi-that was before I had heard of his carrying off the balls-it
was after this matter occurred that I saw him at the Sussex beer-shop-I did
not then allude to the balls-he told me he had got a door to sell, and asked
me if I would buy it-he said that Boxall had shut the shop up, and was
gone-be took me to the back parlour of a house in Field-lane, and there I
