WILLIAM COOK was again  indicted for stealing, on the of April, 1 watch, value 3 l  .; 1 watch guard, value 15 s  .; 3 half-crown 2 shillings, and 1 sixpence; the goods and monies of James Hayhoe . JAMES HAYHOE. I am a carpenter, and live in Robert-street, Brixton The prisoner came to me on the 8th of April—I left him in my room for about five minutes—when I returned he was gone, and I misted my watch, which I had left there—this is it—( looking at it .) THOMAS MUNTON. I am a pawnbroker in the Westminster-road, on the morning of the 9th of April the prisoner pawned this watch at on house for 25 s . JOHN HUGH SWEETING. I am a police-sergeant. I found the duplicate of this watch on him, on board the steam-boat. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Transported for Seven Years.
