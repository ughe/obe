A true NARRATIVE Of the Proceedings at the Sessions-house IN THE OLD-BAYLY,
 At a Sessions of Peace there held;
 Which began on Wednesday the 11th of this Instant October, and ended on Fryday the 13th, 1676.
 Setting forth the several Facts, Tryals, and Condemnation Of Sixteen persons sentenced to Die.
 Amongst whom was one for killing a man neer Islington almost two years ago.
 With an Account how many are Burn'd in the Hand and to be Whipt.
 With Allowance.
 LONDON: Printed for D.M. 1676.
