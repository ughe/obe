land, her mistress.
Ann IRELAND. I am a laundress, and live in Garden-walk, Clerken-
well. I employed the prisoner as a laundress-I missed, on the 30th of
June, two shirts and a shawl-these are them.
Thomas Wells. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Rosoman-street,
I have a shirt and a shawl, which I took in from the prisoner on the 29th
and 80th of June.
GEORGE DAVIS. I am in the employ of a pawnbroker. I took in this
shirt from the prisoner.

2 handkerchiefs, value 3s. ; 3 half-crowns, and 11 shillings; the goods
and monies of Thomas Girling.

Lawrence-street, St. Giles. We went to bed between ten and eleven

counted my money, put it into my fob, and put my trowsers under my pil-

when she let her in-she laid down by the side of my wife when I awoke
