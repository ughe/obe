I saw the prisoner coming down Hanover-street with the copper—he passed me—Mr. Jack was running after him—he said, "That man has stolen my copper"—the prisoner looked, and saw Jack point at him, and he threw down the copper and ran away—I pursued, but could not take him—I gave a description of him, and he was taken the next day—I am quite certain he is the person. JOHN BRADDICK( police-sergeant F  10.) I took the prisoner next morn ing, from the description that had been given of him.
   Prisoner's Defence . I am quite innocent. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Weeks.
 2912. JOHN PRICE was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of October , I box, value 4 d  .; and 37 cigars, value 3 s  .; the goods of Elizabeth Roberts . ELIZABETH ROBERTS. I am a widow, and keep a tobacconist's shop, in Paul-street, Finsbury. On the evening of the 21st of October a box of cigars was taken from my counter, but I did not see it—this is my box and my cigars—( looking at them .) JAMES CLIFFORD. On the 21st of October, a little before eight o'clock, in the evening, I was passing Mrs. Roberts's shop, and saw the prisoner standing by the counter, looking towards the parlour-door—I watched him two or three minutes and saw him take a box of cigars off the counter, and pot it into his apron—he came out—I let him get about two yards, and then took him. GUILTY.*** Aged 16.— Transported for Seven Years.—Convict Ship.
 2913. JOHN BERRYMAN was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of September , 2 table-cloths, value 10 s  .; and 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  .; the goods of Samuel Charles , his master. ELIZA CHARLES. I am the wife of Samuel Charles. I am a laundress. We employed the prisoner to carry the linen and to turn the mangle—on the 10th of September he went to Mr. Wooler's, in Margaret-street, Cavendish-square, and brought the linen—I counted it, and it was all right—the next morning I missed two table-cloths and a handkerchief—I allowed him to lodge in my house, as he was in distress—these are the table-cloths and the handkerchief—( looking at them .) JAMES ALDRIDGE. I am a cab -driver. I was standing by my horse's head one day—the prisoner came, and asked me if I would buy a ticket of a pocket-handkerchief—I said I did not want it, but he said he had no breakfast, and I bought it of him for 6 d .—I got the handkerchief—this is it. ROBERT ECCLES. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into custody. WILLIAM TRANAH. I took this table-cloth in pledge on the 19th of September, in the name of John Dixon—I do not know who from. WILLIAM HOWSE. I am a pawnbroker. I have a table-cloth, pawned with me on the 17th of September—I do not know who I took it of. GUILTY  of stealing the handkerchief. Aged 23.
