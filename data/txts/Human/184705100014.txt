 1098. JOHN DONOGHUE was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 5 s  .; the goods of Weston Wood ; from his person. WESTION WOOD. I live at Mark-lane. One the 30th of April, shortly before one o'clock, I was in Lombard-street, and felt somebody at my pocket—I put my hand into my pocket and missed my handkerchief—the prisoner was pointed out to me by a gentleman—he was half way across the street, going away from me—I went up to him—he immediately pulled the hand kerchief out of his trowsers'-pocket and gave it to me, and was walking away but I detained him—he went on his knees and begged me to let him go.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  Did you notice any other boys about? A.  No. GEORGE SCOTT, ( City police-constable, No.  560.) I was in Lombard-street, and saw the prosecutor holding the prisoner—I went up and took him in charge.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months .
