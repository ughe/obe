HOUGH pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Eight Months.
Jour LoveLock. I live in Vauxhall-road. On Saturday night, before I

go near Mr. Thompson's shop, and Hill picked up this caddy from the board
outside, and put it under his coat-he then walked up and down the road-I
told a policeman.
