606. JOHN HUMPHRIES was indicted for a like offence.
Messrs. Ellis and BOPKIN conducted the Prosecution.
AMELIA CLIFFORD. I am the wife of Thomas John Clifford, who kees
the Marquis of Granby, in Little Marylebone-street. On 31st May, about
10 minutes before 2 o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoner came for a quartern
of gin-it came to 4d.-he offered me a half crown-I gave him 28. 2d.,
and immediately found the half crown was bad- I gave it to my husband-
the prisoner was then gone-he came again about 10 minutes before 4 for
some rum--the barmaid served him-I saw him-be offered me in payment
a 58.-piece-I took it, and found it was bad- I gave it to my husband-I
am quite sure the prisoner is the same man who came on each occasion-I
have not any doubt of it.
Prisoner. You said you were almost positive it was me when your husband
came in. Witness. Yes, and I said I was quite sure it was the same bottle
that you brought before.
THOMAS JOHN CLIFFORD. I am the husband of the last witness. I got
a half crown from her on 31st May, about 10 minutes before 2 o'clock, and a
crown about 4, or 10 minutes before 4-I went round to the prisoner, and
said, “You are the person that was here this morning for a quartern of gin."
-he said, “No, sir"-I said, "Yes, you are "-he said, "I will go and get
a good shilling to pay for the rum "-I said, “No, you will go to the station
with me”-he went a short distance, and then turned round and ran away-
we had a long chase after him--the postman stopped him-I kept the crown
and the half crown till the prisoner was committed, and I gave them to the
officer-the prisoner said his master gave him the crown-I said he could
send for his master to the station.
James SURRY (policeman, D 305). I saw the prisoner on that day he was
stopped in Beaumont-street-I received this crown and half crown from Mr.
Clifford.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These are both counterfeit, made of Britannia metal,
