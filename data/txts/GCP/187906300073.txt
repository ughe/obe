631. EDWARD CRADDOCK (41), Being a trustee of three several
legacies for the benefit of Beatrice Ann Wilcox, did unlawfully convert
6561. ls. 6d. to his own use. MR. A. B. KELLY and Mr. RAVEN Prosecuted,
