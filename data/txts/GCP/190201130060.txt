bought of the Worcestershire Furnishing Company-Annie Clayton
went with me to Broadwater, near Worthing, and we lived there from the
spring of 1900 till I was arrested in April, 1901-I spent the notes I got
from Brook in the neighbourhood where I was living—I endorsed them
with any name I thought of; George Martin was one-I never used the
name of John, but I have been known as Johnson-Lucy Belcher is
