2031. THOMAS SMITH , breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Peter Cornish , and stealing 1 pocket-book, value 4 d  ., his property. PETER CORNISH. I live at 5, John-street, in the parish of St. Olave, Southwark, and am a tailor. On 14th Oct. I left home about nine o'clock in the morning—I left my room door locked; I returned a little after eleven; my room door was still locked—I unlocked it and went in, and saw the prisoner lying in the bed with his clothes on—I asked him what he did there—he said he came there to sleep—I asked how he got in, but that question he would not answer—I found a little drawer was open, and two shillings and a pocket-book had been taken away—the pocket-book was found in the bed, and one shilling laid on the table. JAMES BURTON( policeman, M  277). I was called in—I found the prisoner standing by the side of the bed—I saw a female take up the pocket-book between the beds where the prisoner had been lying—the prisoner said he came there to sleep; and he said at the station that he did not steal anything—he was quite sober.
   Prisoner's Defence.  On that Sunday morning I went to see a party there who lived in the bottom floor; I went in that room and laid down for half an hour. GUILTY —Aged 21.— Confined One Year .
