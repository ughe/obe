MICHAEL DUFFY (policeman, D 279). I know the prisoner. On
Sunday night, 9th March, I saw the prisoner with three men in the
Harrow-road-I told her I wanted her she said, “What do you want of
met"--I said, "For robbing a young man who was living with your
father"-she said, "Oh very well, I know nothing about him; I will go
with you "-I took her to the station, where Rolton identified this
bandkerchief, which was round her neck-she said, “It is not your
bandkerchief: I can bring witnesses to prove where I got it when we
vere in the outer court of the police-court she said she wanted to speak
to her mother, who was there ; I said she must not, unless in the presence
of a policeman-her mother said, “ Mary, tell me what you have done
with the things " -she said, “I am not a flat nor a fool; I am not going
to open my mouth in a public court to fill everybody else's; if I get off
I will tell him where the things are."

Prisoner. Q. What grounds had you for telling the Magistrate I was
a prostitute? 4. By seeing you with different men in coffee shops at
different hours of the night; I have no recollection of asking you in the
cob whether you had been out of town--you spoke to me and said,
"Where is Relton now, that young'ape' or 'fool?'"-I said I supposed
he would be at the police-court.
WILLIAM BENHAM. I live with Mr. Luxmore, & pawnbroker, of
8. Martin's-lane. I produce these clothes pawned by the prisoner on
8th March, about twelve o'clock, for 11. 128., in the name of William
Relton, who she said was her husband, who wanted the money for a loan
society-she pawned the coats and shirts at the same time--this is the
ticket I gave.
Tronas COOPER. I lodge at the prisoner's father's house. I had some
clothes locked up in a box in the room where Relton sleeps- I missed a
pair of boots and a shirt on Saturday night, 8th March. I found the box
unlocked, but not forced ; I am sure I had locked it I know these boots by
this strap being undone; this sbirt is like mine; there is no mark on it.
Prisoner. Q. Is this the first conviction of felony that has been laid to
my charge on your account? A. Yes; I never robbed your parents of
two handkerchiefs; they were not found in my coat-pocket-I do not
recollect your being suspected of taking them I never robbed my master,
Mr. Dark- I have been with him eight years--the Magistrate asked him
if he had anything to say against me, and he said no-I was charged
with stealing Relton's things and my own, but Mr. Broughton said he
never heard such a charge- I have never quarrelled with Relton; we may
have had a word if anything occurred.
MICHAEL DUFFY re-examined. Relton charged Cooper with being
concerned with the prisoner, as he had moved the things out of the room
into a loft two or three days previous to the robbery-the boots and shirt
were identified before the Magistrate as his, and he was discharged-there
was a handkerchief left at the station; I do not know who by-the
constable who went to the station is not here.
