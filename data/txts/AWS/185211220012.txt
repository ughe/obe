CHALLIS, Mayor.
Mary Ann Cronin- saw her married to the prisoner at Sborediteh Church
on 21st June, 1846-] knew both her and the prisoner- saw her in Sept.
last at Marlborough-street office that was the last time I saw her-I know
that they lived together after they were married-I cannot tell how long-
she came to me with a little baby.
Cross-examined by Mr. COCKLE. Q. Do you happen to know that she
left him very shortly after the marriage A. No; I saw them together after
they were married-I do not know that she left him, and went to live with
somebody else-] knew the prisoner before they were married-he came to
my house to see her while he was courting.
SARAH ANN WILson. I know the prisoner, I was married to him on
20th July, 1851- lived with him up to the time of his apprehension.
Cross-examined. Q. Where were you married A. At Christ Church,
Peckham I lived with him some time afterwards- did not know at the
time I married him that he had been married before-I did not live with him
before I married him-] had been living in London six or seven months
before- was ill part of that time-I was attended by a Mr. Flaharty- had
worked with the prisoner before I married him, but I did not live with him
-I did not represent myself as his wife, nor did he represent that I was-I
was not attended by any other medical man-I cannot exactly tell when I
first heard that the prisoner had a wife-I was not considerably in debt
previous to my marriage-tbe prisoner did not pay some of my liabilities-
this bill (looking at one) is for the prisoner's illness; I saw Mr. Flaharty
hand it in.
WILLIaM GODFREY (police-serjeant, C 5). I apprehended the prisoner.

station-he asked if it was for her maintenance-] told him no-I obtained
these two certificates from the churches-] compared them with the registers;
they are correct copies.
