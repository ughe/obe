OLD COURT-Tuesday, August 14th, 1866.
Before Mr. Recorder.
673. MARGARET DINAN (30) was indicted for feloniously wound-
ing Marian Strank, with intent to do grievous bodily harm.
MARIAN STRANK. I am the wife of William Thomas Strank, a greengrocer,
of Moor Lane-we had apartments in the same house where the prisoner
lodged-about twelve months ago I lent her some things to pledge to raise
some money upon-I had applied to her for the money or the things back
-on the night of the I7th July I was sitting in my parlour reading the
newspaper, with the door open-the prisoner same in, and hauled me down
off my chair by the hair of my head-she then took me up and smashed me
down again, and also kicked me-I have the wound, but it has been sewn
up-she went upstairs, came back, and did it again-she went upstairs a
second time, and same back and wounded me again-what she did it with
I do not know-no one came to my rescue-she cursed and swore all the
time she was doing this-she was quite sober-I had not spoken to her at
all.
Prisoner. When she gave me the things to pawn I gave her the 38. I
got for them, and she told me to put away the ticket, that her husband
should not see it.
Witness. It is false-I lent her the things to get bread for her children
-I did not tell her little boy that I would buy him some sweets if he
would say she took 68. off my mantelpiece.
HENRY FowELL (City Policeman 149). I took the prisoner into eustody
on the evening of the 17th July-I told her it was for assaulting Mrs.
Strank-she said, 46 I gave her no more than she deserved, and she must
summon me for it "-at the station she was charged with violently assault-
ing Mrs. Strank, and asked what she had to say to it-ghe said she had
not done it that time, but if ever she lived to come out again she would do
it the next.
Prisoner. It was my husband said that, not me.
