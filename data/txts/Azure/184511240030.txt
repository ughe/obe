OLD COURT.—Wednesday, November 26th.
Third Jury, before Edgard Bullock, Esq.
20. JOHN GOMM was indicted for embezzling 81. 18. Which be
had received on account of Thomas Willison, his master.
THOMAS WILLISON. I live in the parish of Hendon. On the 26th of Oct.
I employed the prisoner to take a cow to Mr. Gurney's—he was to deliver it
to him to sell, and to bring the money back to me—he did not return—I saw
nothing more of him till he was taken into custody—he has not paid me any
of the money—he had worked for me at different times, but had never
riven anything for me before.
DANIEL GURNEY. I live at Northall, Middlesex, and am a salesman at
Smithfield. On the morning of the 27th of Oct. the prisoner came to me in
the market with a cow for me to sell—he told me he brought it from Mr.
Wilshire, who I have known for yeare, and who I have sold a good many
beasts for—he told me he was to take the money home with him—I sold the
cow for 31. 58., and gave the prisoner an order on Mr. Tisdal for the money,
a bill of sale—I was in Mr. Tisdal's banking-house at the time, and told him
to present the bill—I did not see him receive the money.
THOMAS GEORGE TISDAL. The prisoner came on the 27th of Oct. and
dueed a paper, which I have not got—he should have taken it to his master—
in consequence of his producing tbe paper I paid him 31. Is. 6d.
Prisoner's Defenee. I was going across Smithfield-market ; I kicked against
a stone; I am a cripple; I had my hands in my pocket, and lost my money
in that way ; I promised my master to work it out, and pay him 58. a week.
Mg. WILLISON re-examined. When he was going to the station he said,
if I would forgive him he would work it out—I always found him honest
before.
G UILTY. Aged 55.—Confned Three Months.
