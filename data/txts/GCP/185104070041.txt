897. WILLIAM NORTON, stealing 3 handkerchiefs, value 8s. 6d.;
the goods of Henry Dove and another.
John BAKER (City-policeman, 255). On 8th March, about half-past
four o'clock in the afternoon, I saw the prisoner running in King Edward-
street, and heard a cry of “Stop thief I"-I saw the prisoner stopped,
took him into custody, searched bim, and found five silk handkerchiefs
and a pair of gloves in his hat – these are three of the handker-
chiefs (produced).
Cross-examined by Mr. Rigtos. Q. Where are the others? 4. I
was ordered to give them up to him; they were new ones I do not
know how many people were running behind me-the prisoner was first
in the race; I did not lose sight of him.

brother Henry. On 10th March, Baker brought these handkerchiefs, and
I missed them they were in the shop before I know them by the
