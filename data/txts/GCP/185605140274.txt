there are thousands; I can prove that I do not get my living by thieving;
I have been at work for Mr. Henry Hartrey, of Bermondsey, the last six
months, and I am not more than seven months from Ireland; I can look
any policeman in the face, and bid defiance to any one to say he knows me.

539. PETER FRANCIS MOGAN, stealing, whilst employed in the
Post Office, & post letter, containing a sovereign and 2 cards; the property
of Her Majesty's Postmaster-General.
Messrs. CLARKSON and POLAND conducted the Prosecution.
MATTHEW PEAK. I am a police constable attached to the Post Office in
St. Martin's-le-Grand on the morning of the 15th April I saw Blackburn,
the plumber, clear out a water closet on the basement floor of the General
Post Office-it was the second one on the left, coming out of the London
District Office-I believe it is one of the closets appropriated to the letter
carriers-Blackburn cleared out the pan, and stopped it, by putting in some
brown paper-after he had done this, I watched that closet - I went to the
back of it, to what is called an air shaft, and looked through a grating-
there is a gas light in the closet, which is burning all day; it was burning
then I watched there from about half-past 6 o'clock till about half-past 6-
