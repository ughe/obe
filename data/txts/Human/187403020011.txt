I was in Hoxton Square on 8th February—I saw the prisoner run out of No. 2, followed by a woman calling "Stop thief!"—I followed the prisoner along the Square, through King Street, and while entering the Prince of Wales public-house, I caught him and brought him out—as I was bringing him back, two others caught hold of him, and tried to get him away from me, but I succeeded in getting him into the house where I saw him run from—I only lost sight of him for a second.
