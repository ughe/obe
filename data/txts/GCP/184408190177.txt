2135. GEORGE EDWARDS was indicted for burglariously breaking and
antering the dwelling-house of William Gilbart, about ten o'clock in the night
of the ilth of August, at St. Marylebone, with intent to steal; and that he
had been before convicted of felony.
Mr. O'Brien conducted the Prosecution. bosaga
MICAIAH READ (police-constable E 30.) At a quarter past eleven o'clock,
on the night of the 11th of August, I was on duty on the prosecutor's
premises, sitting behind a screen in the shop, with some coats hanging over it
I saw the prisoner and another man come to the back of the folding-doors
--the other had a light-he tried to open the doors with keys for a quarter of
an hour-he broke them open, and they came in-the prisoner said, “It is all
right"-they came past me, and went to the door that leads to Oxford-street
his companion turned, and saw me I got up, and seized his companion
the prisoner seized me by -- and pinched, and struck me-I let go of the
other, and seized the prisoner-the other then went back to the other door.
and was going out-the prisoner called out, “Come back, there is only one
of them"-I felt something drawn across my hand, and found afterwards that
my little finger was cut-I got to the door in Oxford-street, got a policeman.
and took the prisoner-We found on him a bundle of skeleton-keys, and
two of them open the door in Turk's Head-yard, which leads to the back of
the premises--I had examined the doors at nine o'clock, when I went there,
and they were all right.

2136. MICHAEL FLEMING was indicted for feloniously cutting and
wounding Bridget Fleming, with intent to do her some grievous bodily harm.
Mr. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution. w
GEORGE YEOMAN HEATH. I am surgeon at University College Hospital.
Between one and two o'clock in the morning of the 20th of Aug. I saw
Bridget Fleming at the hospital, with a wound behind her left ear, about
three-fourths of an inch in length-it did not reach the bone, but appeared as
though some instrument had been pushed under the skin-I thought that a
common dinner-knife, which was shown to me, was the most likely instru-
ment to have effected it-I have seen her since, and some bruises appeared
near the wound, which would make it less likely than I at first supposed
--the wound was somewhat of a crescent shape, and there was a slight
wound on the margin of the ear, which seemed a continuation of the other
wound, as if the ear had been pressed against the head at the time the wound
Was inflicted-the wounds were not dangerous - I only saw her once after-
wards.
