196. BRIDGET DALTON was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of
November, 1 shift, value 2s., the goods of Mary Ann Ladbury.
(The prosecutor did not appear.)
NOT GUILTY.
197. FRANCIS ASSIOTTI was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of
June, 2 sheets, value 2s., the goods of James Sands.
MARGARET SANDS. I am the wife of James Sands, of Bloomsbury, a
lodging-house keeper. I let the prisoner a room-he lived in it a month
and then left-I missed the sheets off the bed, and every thing from the
room that he could make sixpence of-these are my sheets-(looking at
them)--they were pawned at Mr. Newberry's, who produced them before
the prisoner, and the prisoner said he had pawned them-he was not
authorised by me to pawn them.
WILLIAM HAYDON (police-constable F 54.) I took the prisoner and
have the sheets, which the pawnbroker gave up.
Prisoner's Defence. It was from poverty I did it, but with intent of re-
turning them.
GUILTY.-Confined Three Months.

198. FREDERICK PEARSE was indicted for stealing, on the 15th
of November, 1 clock, value 51. 58., the goods of Henry Hudson.
GEORGE WILLIS. On the afternoon of the 15th of November, about
four o'clock, the prisoner came up-stairs in my master's coffee-house, and
ordered a cup of tea, and toast, and bread and butter-the clock was then
safe, but it did not go-in half-an-hour it was gone-I have not seen it
since-I saw the prisoner go-he had his coat thrown over his arm, and
something bulky under it- he had asked me before for the paper, and when
he came down he said, “Never mind the paper now, I shall be back in an
hour," and when I went up stairs there was some dirt on the table, and the
works of the clock were all taken away-only the bare case hung up-
there was no one else there-there were two ladies and a gentleman there
half-an-hour before--the clock was there then.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Is the clock-case here ? A.
Yes, this is it.
NOT GUILTY.
