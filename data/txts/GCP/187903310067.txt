HENRY DUKE (City Police Sergeant 71). On the morning in question,

of the constables in the neighbourhood where the herrings were left, and

the information-I know the prisoner by sight, and that he frequents the

Cross-examined. I did not see young Mr. Nash or the prisoner in the

Nash junior, did not come out before the prisoner was given in custody-I
did not see the prisoner come out-I was nearly opposite on the footway,
when I took him-young Mr. Nash was in the shop and came out afterwards
- he did not come and join in the conversation or go to the station-Mr.
