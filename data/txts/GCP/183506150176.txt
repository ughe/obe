WILLIAM THOMAS. I am a police-inspector. I had some conversation
with Dunton on Sunday, when she was in confinement in the cage-she

did not know they were stolen.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. Was there any one present but
yourself? A. There was a man on duty, but he is not here.
