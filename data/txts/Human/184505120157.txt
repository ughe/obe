   Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON . Q.  How many beasts are slaugh tered at your slaughter-house in a week? A.  About forty—it is the duty of the men to take the fat to the owners immediately the beast, is killed—no fat has been demanded of me, but hides have—I should not have missed this precise fat unless it had been seized by the officer—the prisoner was under Kibot, and had to obey his orders—the men have the bladders for perquisites, but have no right to any fat whatever—we have on the average not more than one beast with yellow fat killed in a week.
