HENRY Perks. I am a carpenter. On the 3rd of August, I left my
tools at Mr. Berkley's shop, in Gray's-inn-lane, at half-past seven o'clock
in the evening, under the bench against the window, in the front shop.
THOMAS BANFIELD. I live in that house, and am a tailor. About ten
minutes past eight o'clock I was in the back place, and saw the prisoner
through the slit of the door, he made a dart into the shop, and fetched
out a basket of tools as light as he could-I made after him, and took him
in Liquorpond-street-I said, " Is this your property !"-he said, “No;"
and dropped it, and took to his heels I caught him and brought him back
to the basket-I did not know him before.
