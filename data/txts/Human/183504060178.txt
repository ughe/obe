1075. JOSEPH SINCLAIR was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   HON. MR. SCARLETT conducted the Prosecution . ANN HUME. I am the wife of Thomas Hume. I was living at Mr. Bromwich's, who keeps the Red Lion, at Shadwell. On the 20th of March, the prisoner came for half a pint of ale—I served him at the bar—he gave me a shilling, and I gave him 11 d . change—I put the shilling into the till—on the Monday following, the prisoner came again—he had half a pint of ale, and gave me a shilling—I put it into the till, but not with the other money—I laid it by itself, because I had some suspicion that it was a bad one—I recollected the prisoner again—I gave him the change, and as soon as he was gone, I took the shilling out of the till again, and gave it to Mrs. Bromwich—she said it was a bad one, and put it into a bit of paper—this was on the 23rd of March—on the 30th, the prisoner came again, and asked for three-halfpenny-worth of gin—I served him, and he paid me half-a-crown—I took it to Mrs. Bromwich, and as she was coming out of the parlour, the prisoner made off without his change—I sent after him, and he was brought back—I know the shilling was the one he gave me. MARIA BROMWICH. I received the shilling from Ann Hume, on the 23rd of March—I put it into a piece of paper, and kept it by itself till I gave it to my husband—on the 30th of March I received the half-crown from Hume, which I gave to my husband—I found a bad shilling in the till on the 20th of March. PHILIP BROMWICH. I received the shilling and the half-crown from my wife—I gave them to the policeman. JAMES RAYMENT. I pursued the prisoner, and took him—he said, "For God's sake, do not go to hurt me"—I said I would not, but he must go back and make some apology for what he had done—I waited till the officer came. JAMES ROOKE( police-constable K  245.) I took the prisoner, and have the half-crown and shilling. JOHN FIELD. I am Inspector of counterfeit-coin to the Mint. These are both counterfeit.
   Prisoner's Defence.  When I was taken back to the house, there was a shilling produced, but I had not been in the house on the 23rd—the wit ness said she shut the till, and then opened it again, and gave the shilling to her sister. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined One Year.
