for Pardon ; and wilh'd all Young-men might take Warning by him,
and be more dutiful to GOD and their parents, than himſelf had been :
and fo avoid ſuch a fad and untimely End, as this he was now come to.
When Yeſterday the Death-Warrant was come down, and he found by

The then (upon my exhorting him to make a full Confeſſion of his Sins.

within theſe 4 or 5 Months he had committed ſeveral (but no great) Rob-
beries on the Highway, fometimes about Paddington, and at other times

Courſe of Life is now a great trouble to his Soul, who heartily wiſh'd he
