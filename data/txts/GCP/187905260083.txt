ELIZABETE, Deeks. I am the wife of William Deeks, 12, North Street,
Manchester Square on 25th March, between 3 and 4.p.m., I was walking
in the Seven Dials, and had my daughter with me 15 years of age-I went into
Ja bird-seed shop, leaving my purse with my daughter, who was juist handing
it back to me as I came out, when two men came between us, and snatchel
it out of my hand, and ran away together into a court in Porter Street -I
followed them, but a crowd got between us, and I left them before they
took the purse they had followed us for some distance, and I saw the prisoner
80 as to recognise him, and pick him out amongst several others at the police-
station-I saw nothing more of him from the description I gave of him I
was taken to identify him at the police station - I have no doubt he is one
of the men who took my purse and 78. 11d. and a box-key-I knew him
agrin directly I saw him—the key has not been recovered-he had been
following 118; I noticed them before I went into the shop.
Cross-examined. The purse was between us, and I had just got hold of it
as it was snatched from my hand-my daughter had my parse, and was
waiting for me, and looking into the window, and I had just come out of
the shop-- I did not look in at the window-there were several people pass-
ing-I do not know much about the Dials-I can swear the prisoner is one
of the two youths who took my purse-I followed them into a court in
Porter Street, and they went into some house and banged the door, and a
lot of people were there and said, “You can't find them "-I identified the
prisoner about a fortnight ago at Marlborough Street-he was with about
15 or 20 others in a long row—there were several like the prisoner-they
looked about his age and size.
Re-examined. I had noticed the prisoner and somebody else loitering
behind us, and had noticed them sufficiently to identify the prisoner.
