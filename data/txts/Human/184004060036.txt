 1063. HENRY BROCKING was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of March , 1 basket, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 27 lbs. weight of butter, value 1 l  . 10 s  ., the goods of Henry Ilden Tilby . HENRY ILDEN TILBY. I bought a flat, containing 27 lbs. weight of butter, in Newgate-street, on the 6th of March, and put it into my cart—I went into the market, and when I came back the basket and butter were gone. GEORGE BOLTER. I am in the employ of Mr. Waite, of Butcher Hall-lane. I was at the top of Butcher Hall-lane and saw the prisoner take a basket out of the prosecutor's cart and run across the road with it—I gave information—we ran and took him with the butter. JAMES RAYNER. I live in Nelson-street, Shored itch. I was minding this cart, and went after the prisoner—I overtook him with this butter flat, which was the one put into the cart—he had got half a mile from the cart, and was looking over the butter, to see what he had got.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A man told me to stand by this, which I did, and I asked the officer to wait till the man came up, but he would not.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined Three Months.
