299. WILLIAM GOODBODY was indicted for stealing, on the 31s
of August, six oxen, price 1001., the property of Robert Tabrun Moseley,
MR. GUNNING conducted the Prosecution.
ROBERT TABRUN MOSELEY. I am a farmer and grazier at Somershan,
in Huntingdonshire. I have known the prisoner several years he is
drover, and I had employed him once or twice-on Monday, the 27th
August, I had eight beasts at St. Ives market-they were not sold there,
and I put them into the hands of the prisoner, to drive to London-IT
directions to him were, if he could sell them on the road he might, and those
he did not sell on the road he was to take to Mr. Pollett, my salesman
in Smithfield, for him to sell for me there-I have never seen any of those
beasts from that day to this, nor have I received payment for any of them
- they were Galloway Scots--the colours of two or three were varied, the
others were black-I saw the prisoner again on the 30th or 31st of 0
tober, at Huntingdon, in custody- I had been in the habit of seeing him
frequently before August-I saw him on most Mondays at St. Ives market
-I was there, I think, every Monday between August and October, and
never saw him-I came up to London on the 6th of September, and sa
Mr. Pollett on the 7th-I made inquiry about the beasts, and offered 1
reward.
Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. How long had you known the
prisoner? A. Some years—his family are highly respectable-I do not
know whether he drové other cattle as well as mine on this occasion-be
was at liberty to do so if he chose--there is a regular charge for drovers,
so much per head-so much for cattle driven, and so much for cattle
sold-I have been offered the money for two which were sold, on indem-
nifying the person who bought them.
MR. GUNNING. Q. Where does that person live ? A. At Ware, in
Hertfordshire that is on the road to London.
John BINGHAM. I am assistant to Mr. Earl, a beast-salesman at sl
Ives. On Monday, the 27th of August, I saw the prisoner with eight of
Mr. Moseley's beasts, driving them through St. Ives market, in a direction
for London.
Cross-examined. Q. What time in the day was it? A. About eleven
o'clock-I knew the beasts--they had two clips on the off-tit- I did
not see any letter "M" on them at that time.
