1369. RICHARD HORRILL, was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of April , 1 marble slab, value 10 s  ., the goods of Joseph Browne .—2nd COUNT  , calling it 1 piece of marble. WILLIAM LOVELL. I am in the employ of Joseph Browne, of University-street, St. Pancras. On the 7th of April I saw the prisoner coming out of our front entrance with this marble slab—I went into the show-room, where I had seen it safe, and missed it—I ran out and took him with it, about thirty yards off—I saw nobody else there. EDMUND NASH( police-constable E  70.) I produce this slab, which I got at Mr. Browne's.
   Prisoner.  I saw a man standing just in the entrance, and he asked me to take this for him. GUILTY. Aged 48.— Confined Three Months .
 1370. FREDERICK STEVENS, and GEORGE WOOD, were indicted for stealing, on the   7th of April , 5lbs. weight of bacon, value 2 s  ., the goods of Robert Edward Wiltshire . ROBERT EDWARD WILTSHIRE. I live in Cross-street, Wenlock-road, and am a cheesemonger. A little after twelve o'clock, on the 7th of April, a young man gave me information, and I missed three pieces of bacon—I ran out, and saw the prisoners turning the corner—I sent my boy after them—the police man, in the mean time, brought the prisoners and two pieces of the bacon, which were safe ten minutes before. JAMES DORMAN( police-constable N  205.) I saw the prisoners running, and each of them had something under his apron—I caught them, and saw Stevens throw down a piece of bacon, and Wood dropped another piece—I took them back to the shop, and one of them dropped another piece—I found a knife on Wood.
   Wood's Defence.  I picked np the bacon as I came out of the bath door. STEVENS *,— GUILTY. Aged 13.— Confined Two Months, & Twice Whipped .
   WOOD ,— GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Ten Days , and Whipped.
