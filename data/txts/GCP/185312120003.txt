Court. 6. Did you make a memorandum at the time? A. I did ; I
wrote it in a little account book ; I have not got that here, but I have a
paper that I copied from it-(Mr. RIBron did not object to the witness refer-
ring to the paper)—I see the money I paid for the 1,000Z bill was 6701.;
that was besides the 3001. bill—there was a deduction of 30% for discount.
Q. What induced you to advance that money ; what induced you to
discount that bill A. Because I was told it would be very profitable for
me that I should do it ; and I was told that I should certainly have back
the money in Oct. from Wright-I believed the statements that were made
to me by Crutchley and Morton, respecting Wright; it was on the faith of
those representations that I parted with the money-I had asked to see Mr.
Wright, and Crutchley told me that Mr. Wright did not like to have any busi-
ness with ladies—the 1,0007. bill was paid in May, it was on 13th May that
I went to Childs' to get the money out about June, Crutchley came to me
respecting a bill for 2,0001. ; I perfectly recollect all about that, because I have
got it written down on a piece of paper-he said that I had better change
the 1,0002. into 2,0001., which I did Col. Bulkeley's name was to that bill
-I said I knew something of Col. Bulkeley, that I believed he was a gentle-
manlike man, but that I never heard he was a man of fortune ; upon which
Crutchley said, "Oh, yes! he is a man of very good fortune –Wright's
name was also on that bill; I believe Mr. Wright was the drawer, and Col.
Bulkeley the acceptor.
Q. What was said about Wright by Crutchley on this occasion ? A. Only
the same as was said before ; I had received satisfactory information about
Mr. Wright from his friend and neighbour in Northumberland; it was that
induced me to take the 2,0001. bill—I advanced 7001. more on that 2,0001.
bill; that was in cash, and I gave up the 1,0007. bill about the end of Aug.
Crutchley came to me again, and proposed that I should have all these bills
back, and that they should be converted into a bond he said it was much
more secure because it would be upon Mr. Wright's landed property, this
large property in Northumberland—the amount of the bond was to be
2,6001.; the 6001. was to be added, because I was to advance 6007. more.
COURT. Q. You were to retain 3001. for discount, then 4. Yes; I have
