ROBERT HUMPHRIES, M.R.O.S. I practise in Hoxton StreetI know
Williams-on Sunday, 7th February, I was at Mr. Barnes's, 71, Hoxton
Street--Boyes came in after me there-in the presence of Barnes and
Williams and Orgar, Boyes made a statement-next day they went to
swear an information, on which Boyes was arrested-the statement Boyes
made was that he had made this spurious scent and sold it to Williams
as a genuine article, for which he got a legitimate price-he also stated
he heard Williams was in trouble over the matter, and he would not
allow him to suffer for his misdeeds, and he was going to speak up and
not let him suffer like the culprit.
