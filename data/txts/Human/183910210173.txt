BARTHOLOMEW HERRING. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into custody on another charge on the 5th of October—I found four hall-door keys on him, one of which fitted the prosecutor's shop-door, as well as if it was made for it.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Are these common keys? A.  Yes, not skeleton—the prosecutor's shop is in the parish of St. Mary, Newington—a card case was given to me by Freaker, who is not here, containing three dupli cates of  clocks, and these two among them—the case was produced in the pretence of the prisoner, who denied it being his. GEORGE SMITH. I am a policeman. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction—( read )—I saw him tried at the Newington Sessions last May, for stealing a violin—he was convicted. GUILTY.* Aged 21.— Transported for Life.

RALPH SIMMONDS was again indicted for stealing, on the   5th of October , 1 sheet, value 2 s  .; 1 pillow-case, value 6 d  .; 1 table-cloth, value 2 s  .; 1 pinafore, value 4 d  .; 2 towels, value 6 d  . 1 petticoat, value 4 d  .; 2 yards of printed cotton, value 1 s  .; 1 brush, value 6 d  .; 2 shirts, value 6 d  .; 1 pair of stays, value 6 d  .; and 1 handkerchief, value 6 d  .; the goods of Thomas Smith Thwaites : 1 shirt, value 4 s  ., the goods of Joseph Dickey : and 1 shirt, value 6 s  ., the goods of Edward Henry Thwaites . EDWARD HENRY THWAITES. I am a tailor and live in Trafalgar-street, Walworth, at my brother's. On the 5th of October I lost some shirts and other property from there, between one and two o'clock in the afternoon—I had seen them safe on the sofa in the parlour about half an hour before—I received information, ran out, and caught the prisoner at the bottom of the street with a bag—I asked what he had there—he said, "Nothing particular"—I asked if he would allow me to see—he said, "Yes"—I took the bag, and he ran off—I ran after him and got a policeman, who took him into custody—the bag belongs to the prisoner—he was seen to pull it out of his pocket at the door before he went in, and he placed the property in it—some belongs to me, some to my brother, and some to the apprentice. BARTHOLOMEW HERRING. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner, and the prosecutor gave me the bag.
 (  Property produced and sworn to .) GUILTY. Aged 21.
