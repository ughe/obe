1562. ANN LYNCH was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of May, 1
inkstand, value 11., the goods of Jonathan Pereira.
MARY JENNINGS. I am a widow, living as cook in the family of Dr.
Jonathan Pereira, in Finsbury-square. On the 8th of May I lost the ink-
stand, now produced, from the slab in the hall-it was safe at half-past
seven o'clock-I had occasion to leave the steps of the door for about two
minutes, and when I came back it was gone-I know nothing of the pri-
soner.
William Henry BAYFIELD. I am shopman to Mr. James Peachey, a
pawnbroker. The inkstand was pledged with me by the prisoner on the
9th of May.
Prisoner's Defence. I pledged it, but I did not steal it; it was brought
to me by a boy, who said it was his father's inkstand, and asked me if I
knew any one who would purchase it; I said I did not; I believed it
was his father's; I have not seen the boy since.
GUILTY. Aged 42.-Confined Three Months.
