October, 4 handkerchiefs, value 48. 6d. 1 brooch, value 48. ; 1 bed-gown,
value 10. ; 1 box, value id. ; 1 silver penny, and 1 penny: the property
of Fairfax West.
Ann West. I am the wife of Fairfax West, and live on Plumpstead

the 4th of October, part in my bed-room, and part in a down-stairs room
-I went out, and returned about half-past five o'clock-next morning I.
missed them--this handkerchief is mine.
ISABELLA M'TAGGART. I live on Plumpated Common. Between three
and four o'clock, on the 4th of October, I saw the prisoner go towards the
prosecutor's house-she returned in half an hour.
EDWARD NORTH. I am shopman to Mr, Dunn, á pawnbroker, in
Queen's-end, Woolwich. This handkerchief was pawned by the prisoner on
the 4th of October, in the name of Ann Ellis, Plumpsted.
