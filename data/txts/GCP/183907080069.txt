2109. JOHN BAYLIS was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of July,
I watch, value 21. ; 1 watch chain, value 2s. ; 1 watch key, value 2d. 1
coat, value 16.; 2 waistcoats, value 18s.; 1 pair of boots, value 85.; and
1 pair of trowsers, value 4s. ; the goods of William Wentworth.779
WILLIAM WENTWORTH. I live in the parish of St. George-the-Martyr.
Between four and five o'clock yesterday morning, my wife awoke me, and
I heard some one going down stairs out of the room-I had left the articles
mentioned in the room-I got up, ran down, and found the clothes on the
stairs--I looked about, but could not find any person-in half an hour, I
looked up the kitchen chimney, and found the prisoner there without his shoes
-I pulled him down I did not find any thing on him-the clothes were
on the stair-case, with a pair of shoes, which I believe belonged to the
prisoner-his hat I found under my bed.
JANE WENTWORTH. I saw a man making out of my room-I could
not say who it was.
JAMES M'CORMACK (police-constable E 59.) I was called, and took the
prisoner--this watch was found in the potato cellar by Mr. Wentworth.
Prisoner's Defence. I was ill and distressed I have not been long out
of the London Hospital.
GUILTY.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Three Months.
