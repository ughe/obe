3446. JOHN WOOLVETT was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of
October, 6 tame pigeons, value 88., the property of George Browne.
GEORGE BROWNE. I am a pupil at the school of Mr. Orlando Balls, in
Greenwich-road. I keep pigeons-I cut the feathers of one wing of three
of them, to prevent their flying--I had them safe last Sunday week, the
1st of October, and missed six on Monday-I saw them again after
wards-three of them had their wings cut, and three had not.

not mine-two persons named Collins and Hammond were also taken up,
