JOHN WILKIN. I am twelve years old, and live with my parents in

WILLIAM WEBSTER. This half-crown tendered is bad, and these six
others are also bad, and from the same mould as the one uttered.
Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing of the females ; I never saw them
till the day in question. I was going down St. Swithin's Lane, and they
were in front of me. The pavement is narrow ; I tried to pass, and one of
them said, “Where are you shoving to?” I made an apology, and asked
her to have a glass of ale. One of them put this parcel into my hand, and
Jesse seized hold of me. If the policeman had seen anything in my hand he
would have tried to secure it. He did not attempt to search me till the boy
came in. I think the man who offered the boy threepence gave him the parcel.
