 668. JOHN WILLIAMS , stealing 40 yards of cloth; the goods of William Derecourt , in a vessel in a port. JOHN FRIDY I am mate of the Munis.  On 25th Feb. the prisoner asked me to let him sleep on board—I allowed him—in the morning I found the bales in the fore-hold broken open—they contained cloth—I believe this pro duced to be part of it—William Derecourt is the master of the ship. JAMES HUTCHINS I packed this bale a fortnight ago for Messrs. Collard, of Coleman street—this cloth is part of it—I have a sample of the same. SAMUIL EGERTON( policeman, H  193). I stopped the prisoner coming out of the London-docks with six yards and a half of this cloth wrapped round his body—he said he bought it at Trinidad. CHARLKS FRASER( Thames-policeman , 73). I found twenty-one yards of cloth stowed away in the coal-hole—the prisoner said he had given it to another party, and not knowing where he was, he put it there. GUILTY Aged 29.— Confined Four Months .
 669. JOHN CLEVERLY , stealing 134lbs. of string, value 5 l  .; the goods of William Leschallas . MATTHEW EDWIN BISHOP My master's name is William Leschallas—we lost a bag of string on 31st Jan.—I saw it at the station—this is it ( pro  duced)—it was behind the door—the prisoner must have gone inside. JAMES SMITH I let out trucks—on 31 s ., Jan. a roan named George Goodey came and had a truck—it was not brought back—I found it at the station. FREDERICK HILLIARD On 31st Jan., about six in the evening, I saw the prisoner in Budge-row, near Mr. Leschallas, with a truck—I watched for about three quarters of an hour, and saw the prisoner and a man who is not in custody throw this bale of string into the truck—I watched them to Ham boro-wharf, Tower-street, and asked the prisoner if he was going down the wharf—he said no, he was waiting for a man who had hired him—I took him back to Mr. Leschallas—he then said he hired the truck to get a job, as he was out of work. JOSEPH COMBER KNIOHT( City-policeman , 437). I took the prisoner.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A man hired me to take it. FREDERICK HILLIARD re-examined.  On the evening before, I saw the prisoner lurking about Budge-row with another man—I followed them to Hambro'-wharf then. GUILTY Aged 33.— Confined Nine Months .
