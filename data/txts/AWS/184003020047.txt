849. THOMAS NEWMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of
February, 1 purse, value 1d. 1 half-crown, 13 shillings, 19 sixpences,
2 groats, 2 pence, and 2 halfpence; the property of James Leather,
from the person of Mary Leather.
MARY LEATHER. I live at Cranford, and am the wife of James Leather.
The prisoner and his mother came to or house, on the 17th of February,
and had a pint of ale--they were there about they were there
I had occasion to give change for sixpence-m pulled out my purse, and put
the sixpence in it returned the purse into my pocket had a half-crown
in my purse, and some shillings and sixpences, upwards of 258. in all-the
prisoner had first sat on my left-hand, and then he removed to my right-hand
side, which brought him next to my pocket-I had a child in my lap, and
noticed the prisoner several times to lay his head against my pocketain
he and his mother afterwards went away, and when they had been gone
half-an-hour I missed my purseoI went to the prisoner's house, and
asked if he had seen my purse and moneyathe said he had not--his father
made him get out of bed and bring his clothes down, and they were
searched-he declared be had never seen the pursemy son, who was
with me, heard some money rattle as the prisoner was getting out of bed,

the purse and money between the mattress and the bed, on which the
