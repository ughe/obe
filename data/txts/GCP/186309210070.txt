Prisoner's Defence. About half-past 12 I went with a girl to the house
where the soldier was, three of them were beating him; I told the sergeant

my business; he had a cane in his hand. I had a knife which I opened,
but I did no harm with it. I went out and came in again, and the mistress
of the house gave me in charge. I never saw it. I do not even know
whether this is the man or not.

1147. CATHE Mc EVOY (49), Stealing a basket of silver plate, the
property of George Robarts Smalley,'in his dwelling-house.
MESSRS. THOMPSON and Daley conducted the Prosecution.
MARY SMEATON. I was cook to George Robarts Smalley, who resided at
22, Victoria-road, Charlton-on 13th August, about a quarter to 2 o'clock,
I was sitting in the front kitchen, and heard & noise like a jerk in the pantry,
and then like one spoon sliding upon another-I looked through the windor
and saw a woman standing at the back door-I gave a dreadful scream, and
ran through the kitchen into the pantry and missed the plate basket, which
I had seen safe a quarter of an hour before I ran and called the house-
maid- I waited a minute on the stairs, and went into the pantry & second
time to be sure—I then opened the library door, and found the basket on
a table right by an open window-I had seen the prisoner before that day
looking in at the window-I only saw her back when I screamed, but I
saw her face afterwards, and said what business have you got there she
was then in the house-she went outside, and then said, "You have made
a mistake, I was not there"-as I went up stairs she said, “Come, and
look in my basket, I have taken nothing. what have you lost?"- this w
before I had seen the plate in the library; it was when I ran out on
hearing the noise—the library and the pantry are all on one floor-the
library is under the drawing-room at the back of the house ; you go up
stairs to it-the prisoner was standing inside the house at the library door
--there is a window which I saw through, and I saw the shoulder of
person, and then screamed--the library door was shut, but the window
open, and the plate could be put in there in a minute person in the

went down stairs again to make myself sure before I spoke to the woman
