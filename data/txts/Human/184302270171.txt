   Prisoner's Defence.  This woman persuaded me to leave my friends, and go to live with her; she is only a brother keeper. ANN JOHNSON re-examined.  I never kept such a house in my life—I keep a respectable house—the prisoner came to me and said she had been keeping her father's house, and had left him—she begged I would give her a night's lodging—I never suffer men and women to come to my house.
   Witness for the Defence. ANN CHANDLER. I am an unfortunate girl, the same as the prisoner, and Mrs. Johnson as she calls herself; she is married, but is living with a man who is not her husband; she said in my presence, that the lent the prisoner the sheets, and we treated her with a quartern of gin. NOT GUILTY.
 978. WILLIAM FOLEY was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of Feb ., 1 5 l  . Bank-note, the monies of John Innan . JOHN INNAN. I am in the service of the Marquis of Headfort. On the 9th of Feb., about half-past twelve o'clock, I was in a public-house with Daniel Rutherford, to whom I gave a 5 l . note to pay for a pint of ale—the prisoner was the boy at the house—I saw him receive the note from Ruther ford—he then left the room, and did not return—I never saw him again till he was at Queen-square. WILLIAM FREEMAN. I live in Pond-street, Chelsea. The prisoner was desired to come to me for change and he never came. JAMES BENNETT( police-constable R  243.) I took the prisoner in Church-street, Deptford, on the morning of the 10th of Feb.—he was drunk—the inspector taxed him with this—he denied it—he was asked if he lived at such a place in London—he denied that he did, but he was brought to London—he then said he received the note, and when he came out of the door he took a better thought, and thought he would keep it. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Nine Months .
 979. JAMES ALEXANDER was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of Feb ., 15lbs. weight of pork, value 8 s  . 9 d  ., the goods of John Clisby ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. ALEXANDER BLACKETT. I am journeyman to Mr. John Clisby, of Corn wall-terrace, Camden-town, a pork-butcher. On the 11th of Feb. he had four hands and springs of pork outside the window—I saw them safe at five o'clock, and missed two of them at ten at night—I can swear that the pieces which are here are part of them. GEORGE WILSON( police-constable S  134.) I met the prisoner about half-past six on the 11th of Feb.—he had a bag on his shoulder with this pork in it. JOHN EATON( police-constable S  193.) I produce the certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined One Year .
