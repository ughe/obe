Messrs. Godson and PSTERSDORF conducted the Prosecuction.
Charles FREDERICK Fielp. I am inspector of the police at Woolwich Dock-
yard—the prisoner worked there as a carpenter, and, I understand, for seven
or eight years. On the 28th of Sept., as he was hanging up his keys, I per-
ceived something move under his coat, and I said, "Mr. Ellis, I think you
are wrong to-night"-I found on him a mahogany bracket-on the next
morning he came to work at seven o'clock-I said to him, “You are my
prisoner; I must send a man to search your house"-1 sent Fisher at seven
o'clock, and finding he did not return I sent Wootton at eleven-I went
