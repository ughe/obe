1311. GEORGE WHITE and GEORGE WILLIAMS were indicted
for stealing 68lbs. weight of lead, value 85. 60.; the goods of Edward
Herring, their master.
DU
MR. Doanz conducted the Prosecution.
JOBHUA GOLDER (police-constable M 87.) On the evening of the 27th of
April I received information, and watched the premises of Mr. Herring, in
Trinity-street, Southwark-about half-past seven o'clock I saw the workmen
leaving, and saw the prisoners coming out with the others—they went down
Trinity-street-I followed them down several streets to the Waterloo-road-1
noticed White had something bulky about his person-be had a jacket on, and
walked heavily-I called Brown-I stopped White, and saw something fall
from Williams- I took White to the station, and found this lead on his per-
son, inside his trowsers, suspended to a belt round his loins, and there were
two nails to fix it to the belt-I found on him a knife, some money, and
a key.

HENRY DAVISON. I live in May's-buildings, St. Martin's-lane, and am a
plumber--the prisoners were in my employ at these chemical works in
Southwark--this lead is Mr. Herring's-it was on the premises there, I have
no doubt-the prisoner, and all the men there, had access to it.
Cross-examined. Q. How many partners has Mr. Herring? 4. I do
not know-I believe none I never said it belonged to him and his partners
- this is my signature to this deposition.
Q. Here it says, “It belonged to Mr. Herring and his partners ?" A. I
was not aware that I swore he had partners-it was not my lead I have
seen lead on the premises--I will not swear that I have seen this lead there
- have seen lead there of the same quality, but not rolled up like this.
EDWARD HERRING. I do not know whether the prisoners were in my ser-
