1111. JOHN ROLFE was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of February,
70lbs. weight of lead, value 12s., the goods of George Stevens, his master,
GEORGE STEVENS. I am a plumber, and live in Bermondsey-street-
the prisoner was in my service for about fifteen months-on the 9th of
February, early in the morning, I received information, I got up and say
a pig of lead standing in the passage by the side of my house-I waited
and saw the prisoner come and take it up, and go off with it-be had no
business with it-he was sent with another to clean the windows of the
Deaf and Dumb Asylum-I followed, and when I came up to him
put it down and ran away--I secured, and took him to the shop, and be
begged for mercy.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. How far had he carried it? 4
About twenty yards-he asked me to forgive him for the sake of a
family-he has a wife and eight children -- he ran sixty or seventy yarus
--I had bought this pig of lead-1 had twenty-nine in the shop.
CAROLINE HAWES. I saw the prisoner take away this lead.
GUILTY. Aged 43.-Confined Six Months.
