HENRY WILLIAM LOCKYER. I am clerk to Mr. Besley, Mr. Besley
was retained for the prosecution in the Queen v. Lytton by May, and 1
received 51. 158. Od. fees--that is all I have received from May, I had no
other consultations from him.
Cross-examined. I have known you some time, and this is not the first
brief you have handed me--you paid me the 57 158 Od. on the adjourn-
ment at the Court, and I presume yon knew there would be an adjournment
and further fees to pay I hardly think you have paid similar fees before
you have paid larger fees it was 3 guineas and a consultation-I saw you
afterwards with Mr. Hambro outside Mr. Besley's chambers, but do not
know whether you saw him I believe you did, and had a conversation
--I have the fees, but the other clerk books the attendances--the fees booked
were inclusive of the 51. 158. 0d, -the other consultations were booked to
Mr. Fullagar-I believe you had only the consultation on the brief; the
others were by Mr. Fullagar and Mr. Hambro--the special consultation
you had was subsequent to the adjournment,
By MR. WILLIAMS. He never paid me more than the one consultation
marked on the brief.
By the Court. I entered it as “May's matter" as though he were the
Solicitor, and afterwards in Mr. Fullagar's name—they were entered in the
day-book when I returned to the office as “Mr. W. May paid Mr. Besley's
fee " so and so, and then afterwards "Mr. Fullagar."
RICHARD NORTH. I am Mr. Metcalfe's clerk-on the 9th April at
the Middlesex Sessions, May handed me a brief-I asked him what he
was going to mark - he said three guineas--I said, “ You mean three and
one," and he said, " Mr. Fullagar said I was not to give you more than
three"-I said, “Then, being two counsel in the case, we must have consulta
tion," and he said “ Put down two and one "-he paid me, and I initialled the
brief and dated it-I had no other fee at all.
