Mr. OVEREND conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM TAYLOR PRITCHARD. I am in partnership with Mr. William
Mardon—we are solicitors, in Newgate-street. The prisoner has been in our
service more than once-the last occasion was between Nov. and 13th April,
when he left-his first duty was settling some accounts which were in arrear,
and after that he was employed as out-of-door common-law clerk-he was to
go to the public offices to issue writs and serve subpanas, and do the me-
chanical duty which was not done in chambers—we had a suit in which it
was necessary to serve Mr. Glass with a subpæna-I have the prisoner's
day-book, in his own writing-I settled with him on Saturday his account
for the week preceding--here is an entry, " Copy of subpæna served om
Mr. Glass, 11. 1s. Od." - I settled this account with the prisoner on 22nd
Dec.--this guinea was included in that account--the balance of it was money
due to us-he carried forward 188., but on casting up the book I find it was
only 178, due to us—that was carried on to the next week's account.
