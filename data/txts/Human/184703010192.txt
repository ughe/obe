   CHARLES BRYAN . I live in Rotherhithe—I know Mr. O'Connor. On Friday, 19th Feb., I was in his company part of the evening—I did not think he was the worse for what he had taken—he was able to walk and talk—I offered to see him home—I was coming home with him, and he said, "Bryan you are coming too far"—I was coming home with him because I knew he had money and a watch about him—I saw the chain on his neck—after leaving him, I watched him—I kept him in sight, and saw him going along the road—I saw to females and a man, at the corner of Neckinger-road, near Lilliput-hall—he passed them quietly—I was crossing the road, and I beard one woman say to the other woman," There is Connor from the Borough," and the other woman left and went after him—I turned my face and saw the woman who said the words, and I knew her face—I saw the woman who went after Connor take hold of his arm, or put her hand under his arm, I could not see which—they crossed to go under the arches, I there lost sight of them—I did not know whether they went under the railroad, or alongside of it—I walked along, looking for them, and I heard Connor sing  out for a policeman—I knew his voice—I ran towards him and asked what was the matter—he said he had been robbed of 20 l .—he had not his hat on—I looked after his hat, and I saw the prisoner Williams come up to him—I had known Williams before by sight—he put his hand in a rough manner on Connor's breast, and said, "Have you been robbed of 20 l ."—Williams's back was towards me, and I heard Connor say, "You villain, you want to draw my watch"—Connor hit him—I went up and said, "Do not hit him"—Connor said, "He wanted to rob me of my watch," and I laid hold of him—he said, "Let me go, let me go; what business have you with me?"—I said, "Stand till the policeman comes"—he stopped down, and his coat was coming over his head—I thought he would get away—I made a second grab  at him, he slipped away and ran off—I ran after him singing "Police!"—I came against a post, and it hit me so that I felt the blow for a week afterwards—I knew Williams before, and to the best of my belief he was the person who was with the two women.
