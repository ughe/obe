MARY COULSON. My father's name is Francis Coulson—he keeps a
beer-shop in Sion-lane, Islewortb. On Monday, the 18th of April, about

—Russell asked for arpot of table-beer, and offered me 6d. in payment, but

was gone, just as I got to the top of the stairs, I beard a noise—I thought
all was not right—I did notstop to get change, but ran down stairs—as soon
as I got down I missed my father' watch, which had been hanging under
the mantel-piece—I immediately ran to the door—the prisoners were all
gone, but had left the 6d. on the chest of drawers in the same room, and

I sent information to the station, and saw them again at the station in
charge the same morning—I am quite positive they are the three—I have
not the least doubt about either—this is my father's watch.

CHARLES HIGGINS. I am a shoemaker. About eleven o'clock on the
