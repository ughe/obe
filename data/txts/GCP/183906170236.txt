of the furnaces, is between eighty and ninety feet- I was in the workshop
when I saw Moses-it was my business to attend to the crucibles, but I
was not looking at them at the time-if I had been attending to them, my
back would have been to the front shop--I think there were some other
people in the front shop at the time, but I did not see any one distinctly-
I could not see all over the shop-had Mr. Moses stood against the
counter, I could not have seen him, but he stood against the wall and
looked over the counter-1 believe there was somebody else in the shop,
but I would not undertake to swear it-I cannot swear I did see any body
distinctly-I cannot say positively that I saw any body else, I do not
recollect seeing any body there--I cannot swear there was any body there
- I have no recollection of seeing any body else there besides Mr. Moses
-I have no recollection whether I saw any one else there or not.
