MICHAEL GARVEY. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner in Bath place, Wellington-street, Woolwich—he fired a pistol just at the time I came up to take him and said something—I found a pistol in his band, and took one from his pocket—they were both discharged—I had seen him discharge one of them up the street—the other was not loaded—I could not see whether it bad been recently fired, for I did not examine it—I went at first on hearing the report of fire-arms, and as I went I saw him fire another—as I took him to the station he said, "I have done it, and it would not have happened only I was drunk"—I looked at the door of the house and saw a hole through it four feet four inches from the bottom, and nine inches and three-quarters from the jamb—it might be nine or ten inches from the handle, but I did not take notice of that—it was about eighteen inches from the key-hole—it is the street-door and opens into the passage, which is of the width of the door. ANN CHERRY re-examined.  I was inside the parlour when I heard the pistol fired—the parlour door opens into the passage, and is about two feet from the street-door—two persons can pass in the passage—I could hear distinctly any thing that passes—there was no light in the passage. NOT GUILTY.

 1679. JOHN DALEY , JAMES ELLIS, and WILLIAM STEVENS were indicted for feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Lawrence Rusland , on the   13th of May , at   St. Paul, Deptford , and stealing therein, 2 hinges, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., his goods. THOMAS RAFFERTY. I am a wood-chopper, and live in Lower-road, Deptford. On the 13th of May, in the day-time, I saw the three pri soners together near Black-Horse-bridge—I saw Stevens pull up a plank and knock down part of a brick wall of Mr. Rutland's premises—then he went down into the cellar, pulled up the iron hinges, wrapped them in a coarse apron, and put them on the brick wall—the other two prisoners were standing looking at him, not many yards off—he gave the hinges to Ellis—they all three walked away together—they had not got many yards before Stevens handed the hinges to Ellis—I afterwards saw them all three together. THOMAS MOORS( police-constable M  231.) Rafferty pointed the pri soners out to me in Rotherhithe—I apprehended them—I asked what they had done with the hinges—Stevens said the witness was telling lies, that they had done nothing with the hinges—John Eyres gave me two hinges. JOHN EYRES. At a quarter after two o'clock on Thursday, the 13th of May, I met the prisoners in the Lower-road, Rotherhithe—Daley was down at the water edge in the ditch—from information I received I went to the spot, and found two hinges lying in the ditch, under the water—I gave them to the officer.
