 644. JEREMIAH GROGAN was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of December , 1 pair of boots, value 5 s  ., the goods of John Franklin ; and that he had before been convicted of felony. JAMES FRANKLIN. I live in the Walworth-road, in the parish of St. Mary, Newington, in Surrey. About five or ten minutes before eight o'clock, on the morning of the 20th of December, the prisoner came and asked if we had any worsted shoe-laces—my father is a shoe maker, and his Christian name is John—I told him we had no worsted ones, we had silk and leather—he asked to see a pair of silk—I went to the top of the shop to get them, and as I returned, I saw him putting his hat on—he asked me what the laces came to—I said 2 d .—he said a half-penny was enough for them—I said I could not take it—he went out of the shop—I went out and watched him—I saw him join another person, about five doors from the shop—I followed, and when he got to the turn ing, he left the other, took his hat off, and ran with his hat in front of him—I followed, calling "Stop thief!"—he ran down a court, and came up with his hat on, and threatened to knock me down if I ran after him, but I followed him into the Kennington-road, met a policeman, and gave him in charge—I returned to the shop, and missed a pair of boots off a nail—he had run down Prospect-place—a man named Clark lives there—after being at the shop, we returned to the court, and met Clark's boy, who asked if we had lost any thing, and his father showed us the boots—there is a school-room in Williams'-court, which joins the houses in Prospect-place—I find my father's mark on the boots—they had been brought into the shop the night before.
