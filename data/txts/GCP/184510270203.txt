Louisa GODFREY. I am the wife of George Godfrey, a tobacconist, in
High-street, Camberwell. On the 29th of Sept., Berry came to the shop
for a pennyworth of tobacco—he put down a counterfeit shilling-I took
it up, bit it, and said it was not good, and he went away without the
tobacco-he said he took the shilling on the Green-this is it I bit it.
