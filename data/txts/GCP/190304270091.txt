Re-examined. I have no wish to press the charge-I had an agreement
with Mr. De Brunet, dated in November last, that his money should stay

question was asked me at the Mansion House about my conspiring with
the prisoner to deceive Mr. De Brunet by drawing up a false statement
with regard to Norman and Stacey's account-it has been put to me for.
the first time to-day.
