1017. THOMAS M'BRIDE was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of August , eight ounces of tea, value 4 s., the goods of Anthony Weatherhead.
   WILLIAM COLLIER. I live with Mr. Anthony Weatherhead, who is a grocer, and lives in Coventry-street. On the 25th of August, about the hour of three in the afternoon, several customers were in the shop; I saw the prisoner come, take a packet of tea, and run out. I followed, and stopped him with it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I took it up to smell, and laid it down again.
     GUILTY. Aged. 50. Confined One Year.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1018.  HARRIET MURRAY was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of August , one watch, value 3 l.; two seals, value 2 l., and one key, value 15 s., the goods of David Harrison.
   DAVID HARRISON. On the 19th of August, about nine o'clock at night, I met the prisoner in St. James-street, and went with her to the Almonery. She left me before the morning, and as she had a handkerchief of mine I asked her to return, which she did at five o'clock in the afternoon. I then took out my watch; she said
 "What are you afraid of?" I said,
 "I am not afraid of you." She took it out of my hand, and went out with it. I slept there three nights, expecting her to return with it, but have never seen it since.
   EDWARD HANDLEY. I am an officer. I apprehended the prisoner about a week before the robbery.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 FIFTEENTH DAY. FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 29.
 1019.  FRANCIS ANDREWS was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of August , one coat, value 4 l., and one waistcoat, value 10 s., the goods of John Banks , from the person of William Clutterham.
   WILLIAM CLUTTERHAM. I am servant to Mr. John Banks, a tailor, who lives in Bentinck-street. On the 12th of August, about eight o'clock at night, I was taking this coat and waistcoat to Mr. Winford, Searle's Coffee-house, at the corner of Carey-street; I saw the prisoner and another man; I asked them the way to Searle's Coffee-house, they said lower down. I went on and asked a gentleman who stood at a door. They came up and said they were going there, and would show me. At the corner of Hemlock-court, the prisoner touched his companion's arm, then snatched my bundle, and ran down the court. I hallooed Stop thief! and the prisoner was taken in three minutes with it.
   ANDREW ROGERS. I live in Hemlock-court. I heard the alarm - the prisoner ran by - I jostled against him, and he dropped the bundle; I picked it up - he was stopped in a moment.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Transported for Life.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1020.  JOHN PHILLIPS was indicted for stealing, on the 22d of August , one watch, value 3 l.; one ribbon, value 2 d., and one key, value 10 s., the goods of James Stone , from his person.
   JAMES STONE. I am a tailor, and live in Skinner-street. On the 22d of August, about five o'clock in the afternoon, I was in King's-street, St. James-square- I stood clear of the mob. About ten young men rushed from the crowd, and snatched my watch from my fob; I believe the prisoner to be one of them. I saw it in about ten days, at Bow-street, through an advertizement.
   THOMAS CLONEY. I am a wine-cooper. I was sitting at home at tea, in Great Windmill-street, between four and five o'clock in the afternoon, on the 2d of September, I took the prisoner on the charge of Mr. Sawyer. He produced a watch at the watch-house, and offered it to Mr. Sawyer, in place of his own. The prosecutor claimed it.
   HENRY MONTAGUE. I am a footman. On the 2d of September, I assisted in taking the prisoner in St. James-square; on a charge of felony, and saw the watch found upon him.
   WILLIAM SMITH. I am a patrol. I took charge of the prisoner at the watch-house. He produced the watch.
 (Property produced, and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. A person who is gone to sea, pledged it in my name, and I redeemed it; the officer has a memorandum of it.
   WILLIAM SMITH re-examined. No papers relating to it was found upon him. I took him to the pawnbroker's where he said he redeemed it. They indentified him as the man, who pledged and redeemed it.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. Transported for Life.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1121.  SARAH LYON was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of July , ten handkerchiefs, value 10 s.; three silver coins, value 2 s. 6 d., and two copper coins, value 1 d., the goods of Samuel Cohen.
 MR. ANDREWS conducted the prosecution.
   PRISCILLA COHEN. I am the wife of Samuel Cohen, who is a linen-draper, and lives at No. 168, Shoreditch. The prisoner was seven weeks in our service. I asked her one day for a scarf of mine, which was washed at home. I had told her to put it under my bed to press - she said she put it under my bed - she seemed flurried, and I went to look and found a parcel containing five handkerchiefs, under her own bed. (the scarf was under mine) I had seen them in the shop the night before. I told her of it, and turned her away. She was apprehended two days after.
 Cross-examined by MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. How many of your daughters slept with the prisoner - A. One who is about fifteen years old, and a niece seventeen years old. I sent the prisoner away on Friday.
