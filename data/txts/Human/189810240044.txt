HELEN OSTRACHINI( Re-examined ). My husband did not assault me with a knife—the prisoners did not come to my rescue—I had no quarrel with my husband—I saw no knife used—I did not throw a glass at him—Cronin threw the glass and the mark is on the wall—my husband moved his head and escaped it. JOSEPH WYMAN( lie-examined ). I did not hear of the Italian using the knife till the next morning at the Court: not a word on the night—ten minutes previously I had passed the spot and there was no sign of a quarrel; all was quiet.
