   Laurence, in his defence on oath, said that he met Humphries while looking for work, who told him he was trying to dispose of his boots, which he had just got out of his bag, being a seafaring man; that a little further on he said to Humphries , "I may as well carry the pared for  you," which he handed to him, and that he never went into a shop with the parcel. In the dock he stated that' he was a respectable man and had worked honestly for the last three years, where upon Mr. Metcalfe called the following evidence: GEORGE SCRIMSCHAW( Detective Sergeant ). I know the prisoner Law rence—he bears a dishonest character.
