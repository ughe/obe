HARRY WORSYOLD (Detective H.). On the morning of October 15th
I was with Sergeant Lee keeping observation on No. 687, Commercial,
Road-at 11.40 a.m. I saw Wade and a woman enter the house, and
at 12.20 a.m. the woman came to the door, looked up and down the
road, and beckoned to somebody in the passage-Wade then came to
the door, and she made motions to him to go back-he went back,
and she followed him and shut the door & short time after that she
came to the door again, and again beckoned to Wade, who came to the
door, ran across the footway, and jumped on a passing tramoar going
in the direction of Limehouse-Lee followed on another car I was told
off to watch the woman, and I followed her to No. 83, Grosvenor Street,
where she entered.
Cross-examined. I believe No. 587. Commercial Road is his mother's
