JURY . Q.  Did you see the prisoner come on shore? A.  Yes, in a fishing smack, with his wife—the girl came up the passage after him, as it were pur suing him—she kept up with him—she came to the same place where we landed—directly he came ashore, I said, "That is not the man who knocked the man overboard"—she heard us all say so—she was not accusing him of it—she followed him. and gave him in charge.
   COURT . Q.  How was the man dressed? A.  In a blue smock-frock or shirt, and straw hat—the prisoner had on, I think, a sleeve waistcoat and white trowsers—I did not see William go ashore, but I looked round, and he was gone; and just as we got ashore, the prisoner came ashore with his wife—I saw no one else—that is the man who did it—( pointing to William Green. ) MARY ANN DOHERTY re-examined.  That is not the man who struck my brother. MICHAEL SCULLY re-examined.  I do not think that man was on the barge—I swear it was not him, but the prisoner who struck the deceased. WILLIAM GREEN. The deceased came on board and challenged my brother to fight—I was sitting alongside of him, and got up and stopped the blow.
 (  The Court having cautioned the witness that he was not bound to state any  thing that might criminate himself, the examination was not pursued.) NOT GUILTY.
