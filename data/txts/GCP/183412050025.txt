of oil, value 238.; and 3lbs. weight of paint, called Vandyke brown,
value 308.; the goods of Charles James Jones and others.
John SHOOTER. I am in the service of Charles James Jones and his
partners, who are oil and colourmen, living in Leadenhall Buildings, Grace-
church-street. On the 3rd of December I had some goods to take to va-
rious customers in a waggon-I had two boxes, containing thirty glass
bottles, and some oil and paint, directed to Mr. Hitehcock, Taunton--this
is the box-I was in Fore-street between five and six o'clock, and left my
waggon for about five minutes, at the outside I returned and saw this box
in the middle of Fore-street-I had left it safe in the waggon-I saw the
prisoner in custody immediately afterwards.
