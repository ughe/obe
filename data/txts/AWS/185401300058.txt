Mr. BODKIN. Q. When did you go to sea A. When I was about
sixteen that was after both those stealings of the goods-I went as boy on
board a man-of-war, and served till 1851-I fell from aloft on board the
Cleopatra, and broke my thigh-I left the navy in consequence of that acci-
dent, and I have received a pension ever since.
COURT. Q. How did you get on board a man-of-war A. I went into
the Marine School I got recommended there from a gentleman in the City
I got there as soon as I came out of prison.
