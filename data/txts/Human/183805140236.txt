   Prisoner. Q.  Was it in a fit state for work? A.  Decidedly not—I asked several questions who you knew about Clapham—you satisfied me about that—the mare could not have done a day's work if put to it—was blind of one eye.
   Prisoner's Defence . On the Friday before Good Friday I went to Smithfield-market, and bought the mare, in the presence of a man not here, but I could produce him on Friday afternoon—it was certainly not fit work—I kept it a week on Clapham-common—I gave 25 s . for her—the Thursday following I went to the common to see her, and she was lying down—I could not get her up—I left her there, and on Friday I went again—on Saturday I found it not fit for work, and I took it to Wallace—the who sold it to me said it belonged to a widow woman, living on Black heath. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Transported for Ten Years.
 1395. ELIZA MURPHY was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of December , 1 pair of stockings, value 2 s  .; and 3 handkerchiefs, value 7 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Benjamin Bridges , her master. BENJAMIN BRIDGES. The prisoner was my servant—she came on the 1st of October, and left on the 5th of February—I received information, and, on examining, I missed a pair of stockings and three handkerchief, when I heard she was in custody—the handkerchiefs and stockings were produced, and are mine—( looking at them .)
   Prisoner . I did not put the handkerchief in my box myself—If do not know how they came there—I cannot read or write—I did not know who they belonged to. Witness . When she was apprehended I went to her lodgings with the officer, and found them there—she told the Magistrate her address. ELIZA STOFFLE. I am the wife of John Stoffle, a policeman. On Mon day night, the 9th of April, I searched the prisoner at the station-house, and found on her these two pocket-handkerchiefs. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Six Months.
 1396. JOHN JONES and THOMAS WILLIAMS were indicted for stealing, on the   11th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  ., the goods of Peter Saynes , from his person. WILLIAM POUND. I am an officer of the Surrey Theatre. On the evening of the 11th of April I observed the two prisoners, in company with another, and watched—I saw Jones pick a gentleman's pocket, and hand the handkerchief to Williams, who put it under his jacket, and ran away very fast—I followed, and took him about fifty yards off, and found the handkerchief under his jacket.
