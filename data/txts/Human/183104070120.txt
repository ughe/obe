 965.  JOHN HADISTON and PETER GARRATY were indicted for stealing, on the 27th of March , 1 grafting-tool, value 4s., the goods of James Layton.
   PETER DANIELS. I and the prisoners are navigators- they worked in the same field as me, but not at the same spot. Layton left this tool with me about seven o'clock on Saturday evening, and I put it under the stairs at the Falcon public-house, Stoke Newington, where I sleep - I missed it about half-past six on Sunday evening - the prisoners were drinking in the tap-room; I went to them and said, "It is very strange you should go into the place and take the tool; it must be somebody who is in the house;" Hadiston followed me outside the door and said, "If you say we stole the tool I will smash you," (holding up his clenched fist) - I said I did not say it was them, but it must be somebody in the house.
   DANIEL DAWSON. I keep a retail beer-shop in Stoke Newington. The two prisoner came into the passage leading to my tap-room, in company - I asked what they wanted, they said the boy I employed to take out the beer- I said he was gone out, and asked what they wanted of him - one of them (I don't know which) said, to leave this tool till Monday morning - I said I would take care of it, as they used my house.
   JOSEPH MARSHALL. I am a Police-constable. I apprehended the prisoners on the 28th of March; they said nothing to me.
   EDWARD FREEMAN. I got the tool from the beer-shop- I have had it ever since.
   JAMES LAYTON. This is my tool; I left it in Daniel's care.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. How do you know it? A. It is marked with a piece of wire at each end - I never asked for 30s. not to prosecute -  Thomas McBride came to me and offered me 30s., but I never asked him for it - I do not know whether any body else was present. but nobody can say I asked him for the money.
 Hadiston's Defence. I am innocent.
   THOMAS McBRIDE. I offered to settle this with Layton - he asked me 5l. at first - he told me if I would not give that before he went before the Magistrate, he would prosecute the men - there had been a subscription raised among their friends.
 COURT. Q. Did Daniels or Layton want to make it up? - A.Daniels wanted 7s. for two days' work, and Layton wanted 30s. - they had been before the Magistrate once - the prisoners knew themselves innocent, and did not wish the money to be given, but our mates subscribed 30s. - we could not afford to raise 5l. - he afterwards said he would take 30s., which we put into the hands of Mr. Hudson - he said he would keep away from the Magistrate for 3s. 6d. a day.
 JURY to  PETER DANIELS. Q. Did you find the tool at a different house to where you lost it from? A. Yes, I found it at a beer-shop one hundred and fifty yards off.
 HADISTON -    GUILTY. Aged 29. Confined Three Weeks.
 GARRATY -    GUILTY. Aged 30. Confined Seven Days.
 966.  JOSEPH JORDAN was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of February , 1 coat, value 10s., and 1 pair of boots, value 10s., the goods of Robert Proctor.
 SARAH FOX. I am servant to Mr. Proctor, of  Perceval-street. On the 22nd of February we were having wine in, between six and seven o'clock in the evening - my master desired me to stand at the street-door, which was open, while it was brought in, and as I went up, I saw a man going out of the passage with a coat and pair of shoes, which were taken out of the hall - I gave an alarm; my master went out, and took him.
 Cross-examined by MR. BARRY. Q.How far off was he taken? A. About twenty yards - I did not lose sight of him; the boots were not found, but I saw him with them as well as the coat.
 ROBERT PROCTOR. I was coming out of my dining-room, and the servant said, "There is a man run away with your boots;" she pointed out the prisoner, who was running about twenty yards off - I ran, took him into a shop, and said, "Where are my boots?" he said, "I have no boots of your's;" he was very uneasy, and shuffling a coat off his back; I found he had my coat on over his own; it was rather dusky - he kept shaking his arms as he ran, and I suppose he was then putting the coat on - the boots have not been found.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. - Transported for Seven Years.
