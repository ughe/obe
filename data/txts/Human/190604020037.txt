 By the Court. Prisoner was very steady and punctual. LIZZIE HIRST, 285, Green Lanes, spinster. Prisoner was in my father's employ, and about the end of January he told me he wanted to speak to my father, to give notice to leave. I asked him why he wanted to leave. For some time he would not tell me, but at length he said he had been keeping company with Louisa Hutson, and Mrs. Munro, her mistress, had put a stop to the acquaintance. He assured me he had done nothing to cause her to do so. I told him it was a very silly tiling to give up his situation, and that, if he waited till July, in all probability, if the girl was sincere towards him, he would be able to have her without anybody interfering at all.
