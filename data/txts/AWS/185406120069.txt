772. JOSEPH WEATHERS and MATILDA SPEARING stealing
1 counterpane and other goods, value 100 the goods of Ephraim Grant.
Messrs. SLEIGH and GEARY conducted the Prosecution.
EPHRAIM GRANT. I am a convict keeper on board the Warrior hulk.
I did live in Charles-street, WoolwichI left there the latter end of April
during the time I occupied that house, the two prisoners had a furrnished room
in it-they occupied it about three weeks-I lived there when they took
the apartment, and then I removed to No. 5, Albion-road-wher I left the
house in Charles-street, I still continued to hold it am absent from home
myself sometimes for two days and three nightsabout a week or a fort-
night after I removed, I received information that the prisoners were gone
-I went, and everything was taken out of the room that belonged to me,
except three chairs-the house was left empty and looked up, but they had
not shut the front window-the prisoners had agreed to take four empty
rooms of me, and they removed the furniture from the upper room into the
lower room--besides the furniture which was in the room they occupied, we
missed a great many things that were not let to them ; a bed, a counterpane,
three blankets, four pillows, two saucepans, some flat irons, fire irons, a table
cover, some pillow-cases, and other things-my wife will tell you better than
I can- discovered where the prisoners were living in White-street, in the
Borough went there with Mr. Keefe, and took a constable with me-I
found some of my property in the room where the prisoners were-these
things my wife can speak to better than I can-here is a towel marked with
ink, and a pillow case marked with my own name on it-1 had not given
the prisoners any authority to take away the things.
