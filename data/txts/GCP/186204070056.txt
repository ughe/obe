433. OWEN LEE (28), and ELIAS BOSWELL (30), Stealing 1 mare
the property of Hamond Watts.
MR. DICKIE conducted the Prosecution.
HAMOND WATTS. I am a corn-dealer of Wick-road, Hackney-1 was the
possessor of a bright bay maroon 14th March, I turned her out on Hack
ney-common, a little before 9 in the morning, and missed her in the evening
about 6-I have seen her since in the possession of the police-I do not
know the prisoner-I value her at 151.--I bad not sold her-her tail bu
been shortened very much, but I am sure she is mine-I know her by
brand on the foot.
Lee. I bought the pony on 14th March, so I could not steal it on the
15th.
