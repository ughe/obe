FOURTH COURT.-Tuesday, September 14th, 1880,
Before Robert Malcolm Kerr, Esq.
478. WILLIAM JOHN BUSH (22) and CHARLES HILL (22),
Feloniously forging and uttering & receipt for 21. 48. 6d. with intent to
defraud, to which HILL PLEADED GUILTY!
Messrs. Cowie and BAGGALLAY Prosecuted ; MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS
defended Bush.
JACOB Nicholson. I am a traveller, of 13, Mulberry Street, Commercial
Road in July last I was at Stow-in-the-Wold-I applied for a post-office
order there for 21. 48. 6d., and this order (produced) was issued to me
payable to J. Webber, at the Post-office, 280, Whitechapel Road-I placed
I with a letter in an envelope addressed to J. Webber, 26, Leman Street,
Whitechapel, and posted it myself.
MARY LOUISA WOOLSCROFT. I am an assistant at the post-office at
Stow-in-the-Wold—this advice note (produced) relates to an order for
9. 46.6d, which I issued to Mr. Webber from Nicholson-it would be dis-
patched about 11 a.m. on 29th July, and would reach St. Martin's-le-Grand
the same evening.
Isaac WEBBER. I live at 26, Leman Street, and know Mr. Nicholson-
I did not receive any letter from him on or about 30th July containing a
post office order—this signature, "I. Webber," is not my writing, nor written
by my authority.
JOHN Clegg. I am inspector of letter-carriers at the E. C. office,
& Martin's-le-Grand-the two prisoners were employed there, and
were on duty on the evening of 29th July, Hill from 4 to 12 o'clock and
Dah from 4 to 8 p.m.- letter posted at Stow-in-the-Wold at 11 o'clock
would arrive at the E. C. office at 5.30 or 6 o'clock-Bush was
norting that evening, and assisting to dispateh letters for the Northern
District Post-office- letter from Stow-in-the-Wold might have been put
before him for sorting—he would also have access to letters of advice from
the same place-Hill was opening bays—they were both paid their wages
on Saturdays.
Crans-examined. There were eight sorters at that table besides Bush-
atters from Stow-in-the-Wold would go to that particular table.

department, General Post-office-the prisoners are letter-carriers at the
Central District office in consequence of complaints I instructed Cohen on
