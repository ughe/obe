JAMES POSFORD. I am a Policeman. On the 6th of September, at five o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner coming down Little Drummond-street, about two hundred yards from the prosecutor's yard, with this sack rolled up under his arm - I asked if he had any thing there; he said, "You see what it is as well as I do;" I asked if he had any thing else there - he said No, but I found the four weights in the sack; he immediately knocked at his own door, which he was close by - somebody opened it; he immediately plunged in, but I went in and secured him - he made great resistance.
 Cross-examined. Q. He told you he had found them? A. Yes - he did not say where; he was not going towards the prosecutor's house.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was coming towards this man's place - the Policeman asked what I had got; I said a sack and four weights which I had picked up in the field.
     GUILTY. - Aged 22. Confined Six Weeks.
 1921.  MARY CHILD was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of August , 1 bonnet, value 1s. 6d.; 1 apron, value 2d.; 1 handkerchief, value 1d., and 12 pence, the property of Thomas Evelyn.
   JANE EVELYN. I am the wife of Thomas Evelyn, of Lower Chapman-street, Commercial-road; my husband is at sea, and I sell nuts about. I was coming home about eleven o'clock, on the 23rd of August; the prisoner said she was without a lodging, and did not know where to go; I took her home with me - she went to bed in my room - I awoke about four o'clock, and missed her and all my things; I went with a Policeman, and found her drinking at the White Hart about five o'clock; she had my bonnet on, and all my things - the money was not found on her.
   JAMES SMITH. I am a Policeman. I found the prisoner with the bonnet, apron, and handkerchief on - she said she merely did it in a joke, and denied having had the money.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was drinking with her on Monday, and pawned my clothes for drink, and on Tuesday she got this up against me.
   JAMES SMITH. When I took her, she had nothing but a gown and black petticoat.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 33 - Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 1922.  EDWARD CRANE was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of July , 1 carpenter's plough, value 5s.; 1 bit, value 6d, and 1 stock, value 4s., the goods of Thomas Rhodes ; and 1 saw, value 5s., the goods of William Worledge.
   THOMAS RHODES. I am a carpenter. On the 12th of July, about eleven o'clock in the morning, I went with Worledge to get some beer - we left our tools in a new building at Stepney, and left the prisoner there; we returned in about ten minutes, found the door shut, and the things gone - nobody could come there without his knowledge - he was still there - we had shut our tools up in the house we worked in - we found them in pawn in about half an hour, in the Commercial-road; from what the pawnbroker said, we went over to the prisoner, and I said"You have been pawning my tools, give me the duplicate;" he denied having the duplicate, or knowing any thing of the tools - he went into one of the houses to get his coat to go away - I detained him, and when the constable took him, he took the duplicates from his pocket, and threw them on the ground - the bit, which I had left in my stock, we found in his pocket.
   WILLIAM WORLEDGE. I was at work at these buildings, and left with Rhodes; I lost my saw, which I found at the pawnbroker's.
   WILLIAM EDWARD RUMSEY. I am a pawnbroker, in the Commercial-road. On the 11th of July I took in a plough; and on the 12th, a tenant-saw and a stock - they were all pawned by the prisoner; he pawned the last thing about an hour before he was taken.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   JAMES ANDREWS. I am an officer. I found this bit in the prisoner's pocket.
     GUILTY. Aged 40 - Confined Six Months.
 1923.  DANIEL COWLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of July , 1 handkerchief, value 5s., the goods of Daniel Kinsey , from his person.
   DANIEL KINSEY. I am a saddler, and live in Regent-street. On Sunday, the 3rd of July, I was at the Parkgate, on Constitution-hill, and felt something at my coat pocket - I saw the prisoner putting my handkerchief up under his coat behind; part of it hung down - he seemed to be alone; I collared him, and gave him in charge.
   WILLIAM SHEPHERD. I am a Policeman. I was in the Green-park; the people were following the soldiers from the Palace - I saw the prisoner in the crowd; Kinsey gave him in charge with this handkerchief, and I found another between his skin and his shirt; he is in the Park almos every morning.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 22. - Transported for Life.
