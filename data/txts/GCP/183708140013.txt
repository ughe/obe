WILLIAM VANN. I am a Smithfield drover. I have known the prisoner a
good while-he employed me twice to kill pigs for him-six, I think, on the
first occasion, and seven or nine on the second-he paid me 1s. a head for
killing them they were killed in Brick-lane, Old-street-road-Mr. Holes
was present at the time--the prisoner told me the pigs were going to New
gate-market to be sold I did not take them there.
