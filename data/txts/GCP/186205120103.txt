the latter end of April-he had no authority to order these goods from
Thompson and Co.-I knew nothing about the first parcel of goods that he
ordered until 1st May-1 knew nothing about the goods that went to New
Brentford, or those taken to Tottenham-court-road-he had authority to

he had no authority to pledge cur credit at all to Thompson and Co.-I have
seen these orders-he had no authority to give any of them.
