498. THOMAS CHEESEMAN (20), Robbery with violence on Dennis
Bryan, and stealing from his person 98. his monies.
W ORK
MR. LILLEY conducted the Prosecution.
DENNIS BRYAN, the prosecutor, was called on his recognisance, but did not
appear.
ANN JONES. I live at 17, William-street, New Kent-road-I know
Dennis Bryan ; he used to live at the same place; he did on Sunday, 29th
April-on that day, about 2 o'clock in the afternoon, I saw him at the door
of the house standing in the passage ; some females came up; one of them
tied a stone in her handkerchief and struck Bryan on the head-I saw the
prisoner about ten minutes after--he rushed into the passage, knocked
Bryan down, and kicked him violently while down; he then got up and ran
out, and the prisoner ran after him-I saw nothing more Bryan left that
house on the following Thursday; he had been to the police-court on Wed-
nesday the day before I was present when he was examined before the
Magistrate the prisoner was at that time charged.
Prisoner. Q. What time was it when Bryan was standing at his door }
A. Just about 2 o'clock-I live down stairs; he told me he had been
robbed about half-an-hour after-he did not tell me he was coming round to
your house with a policeman—the disturbance began by Harriet Cross
coming round and challenging the other young woman-Bryan pushed her
away from the door-they had not been drinking in our house-I did not
see you take anything.
