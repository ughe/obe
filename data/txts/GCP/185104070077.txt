occurred (reading the deposition), "At the station-house I heard Cogan
say when he went into the coffee-shop, there was a bustling in putting
the bottles away ; I apprehended Cogan at the station, he having come
there with the prosecutor?" 4. Yes; I said it all to the Magistrate, and
have a witness to prove I said so.

the police were after him-I did not know anything of the robbery till
three weeks after it was committed - I took Wooderson on another charge.

938. SARAH BURCHELL, MARY FULLER, MARY DONOVAN,
SARAH DUNLEVY, and MARY COLLINS, were indicted for a riot.
2nd Count, assaulting Stephen Parry Powles in the execution of his
duty. Messrs. HUDDLESTON and RUSSELL conducted the Prosecution,
STEPHEN PARRY POWLES. I am master of Dunning's Alley Work-
house, which belongs to the East London Union, and is appropriated for
the reception of female paupers in that Union--the paupers dine together
in the hall, at twelve o'clock-the prisoners were inmates-on 1st April
Collins, Faller, Donovan, and Dunlevy were sitting at one table, and
Burcbell was employed in handing the others the meat after it had been
weighed-in the course of the dinner, Faller said she would be master of
the house - I do not recollect now what the others said-Burchell took a
potato off the plate of a girl named Sparkes, and I think she touched her
nose in so doing-I requested her to sit down, and as she was proceeding
to the end of the hall, she turned, and I saw her throw the potato-it
passed near my head- I suppose it was thrown at me I called on the
porter, who was present, to do his duty-a girl named Margaret Conner
attempted to put Barchell out of the hall-she resisted, and the porter
put her out-as soon as the door was shut, Fuller threw the contents of a
pannakin of beer in my face, and Collins the contents of the salt-box-
Donovan and Dunlevy each threw a plate, one of which hit me in the face,
and the other one my head, of which I have the mark now-after that
plates came rapidly, and pannakins from the prisoners, and I saw knives
and forks falling about me--there was a great disturbance, tumult, and
riot--I tried to clear the room, and several kicked me behind, but I can-
not say who it was-my face was streaming with blood at the time-I got
into the yard-Fuller, Donovan, Danlevy, and I believe Collins, followed
ne--Fuller got hold of me by the neck-handkerchief, and tried to
strangle me--the others also had hold of me I was afterwards rescued by
some of the other inmates-blood was then running off my nose and chin
-I am suffering from the blows now; these plaisters are from the effects
of them-I am still under medical treatment-after I was rescued, they
rushed up-stairs, and destroyed 250 panes of glass-there were also five
door-plates broken, and the sashes of the kitchen-window were broken
by Donovan.
