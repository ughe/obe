1 HENRY JAMES JAMIESON. I am a surveyor, living at East Brixton on the
9th of August, I reached the Greyhound Inn, on my way from Croydon
Assizes, and from something which I heard, I went up stairs, and saw Mr.
Estall on the landing of the first-floor-he explained to me the position in
which he stood, and pushed at a bedroom door, and opened it--the prisoner
was inside, and Mr. Estall introduced me to him. I told him that Mr.
Estall had informed me that he was trustee under the bankruptcy, and that
as he (the prisoner) would have to leave his house sooner or later, I hoped
he would take my advice, and go quietly, for then Messrs. Carter, Wood &
Co., might get him a smaller house, and place bim in business again I told
him also that Mr. Estall had offered to give him 101., and that I would use
my persuasive powers to get him another 51.--the prisoner said that he
wanted to perform an act of nature, and that he was suffering from piles.--
