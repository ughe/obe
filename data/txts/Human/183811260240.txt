 257. WILLIAM DADY and JOHN ROBINSON were indicted for stealing, on the   7th of November , 12 glass bottles, value 1 s  .; 2 jugs, value 1 d  .; and 1lb. weight of grease, value 2 d  .; the goods of Jeremiah Michael Evans , the master of Dady. JEREMIAH MICHAEL EVANS. I live in Clapham-rise—Dady was in my employ as footman. I missed some property, and charged Dady with having stolen certain empty bottles—he acknowledged his guilt, and begged for forgiveness, it being his first offence—I said it was my duty to give him in charge—I procured a search warrant, and went with the officer—I found at Robinson's house a great quantity of bottles, among which were the two which I now produce. ANN WENDON. I am housemaid to the prosecutor. On Wednesday, the 7th of October, I saw Dady take some glass bottles out of the stable and deliver them to Robinson, who was in the yard on my master's per mises—I think he took about a dozen—he put them into a hamper, took them out of the yard, placed them on a barrow, and wheeled it away—I asked Robinson where he got them from—he said he brought them from Balham-hill—he came back in about ten minutes, and then he had a bundle and a pitcher with kitchen grease in it—it came from Mr. Evans's house—I asked him what business he had there—he said he had nothing more than what he paid for.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  How many bottles did you first see in the hamper? A.  Two or three—I never issued invitations to fourteen persons to come to my master's house—I never had but one person to see me—three people called to see the cook one afternoon—they staid a very short time—I do not remember that there was a party there on Sunday, the 4th of November—Mary Barton, the late servant, called on Sunday, and my master was in town—there were two broken jugs, an old cullander, and lib. of grease, value 3 d . missing. JAMES GRACE( police-constable V  24.) I took the prisoner in charge.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Produce the two broken jugs and the cullander, A.  Here they are—Robinson lives not above three or four hundred yards off—I found the jugs in the yard adjoining his house, where there were a good many things kept—the cullander was in a yard, thrown out in a heap all lying together with other such property. NOT GUILTY.
