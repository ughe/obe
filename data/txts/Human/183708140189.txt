 2013. HENRY MAY  , alias    Harris, was indicted for feloniously killing and slaying James Edward Herepath .
   MR. JONES conducted the Prosecution. JOHN CLARK. I am a cabinet-maker. I saw a fight take place between the deceased and the prisoner, at Lambeth—they fought about two rounds—the first blow was struck by the prisoner—at the commencement of the second round I saw the prisoner strike the deceased on the left side of his head—the deceased continued to fight after that blow—after it was over I observed him looking for his hat—he was standing by me, and I thought he was coming towards me, but he tumbled and fell, and pitched into the kennel on his knee—he did not speak after that, but merely gave a deep sigh—his head did not come in contact with the ground when he fell then—they had been down together in the scuffle—he had been down twice, but was not knocked down; it was struggling in the fight—they were both down together—no blow was struck on either side after the fight had ceased.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Did it appear to you that a push of which the deceased complained before they fought was not intentionally done? A.  It did not appear to me that he meant to insult him—it was that the deceased was offended at, and then the prisoner offered to fight—the deceased said "That is what I mean," and prepared to fight—the deceased was sober—I consider the prisoner was in liquor—the deceased remained for about ten minutes after the fight without any thing happening to him.
