treasurer informed the members from time to time--£50 was put into the
Post Office Savings Bank-the last meeting but one of last year was
December 19th, and that was the sharing-out night-the prisoner was there
- his son was not-the prisoner said, “You are all aware my son, the
secretary, has absconded with the money. None of his family, or anybody
at Forest Gate have seen or heard of him since "-he was questioned
whether he had gone abroad, and he said he did not know, and after
some more questions the matter was left to an investigation committee
--the prisoner produced this balance sheet-it shows at the bottom
of it that the prisoner is responsible for £269 Bs. 9d.-he also produced in
cash £24 6s. ld., which where the takings of the last week-I was ap-
pointed on the committee of investigation, which met the next day-
the prisoner attended and said he had another £2 38. 3d., which he said
belonged to some member who had not come for his money the week!
before-he said he had forgotten the name-he was asked, among other
questions, did he know the duties of treasurer ? was he a paid treasurer ?
and was he not in debt on the sharing-out night the Christmas before
to some considerable amount ? and he said, “Yes”-that meant Christ-
mas, 1904—he did not say how much he was then in debt-he said he had
borrowed that money from a friend to pay it back-we asked him if he
had seen the bank book-he said, "Yes, in August, 1905, on my son's
table," but that he never took the precaution of opening it-he was asked
how his son had got it out of the bank, but he made no answer-he did
not say in what name the money had been banked in 1904, but we found
out that it had been banked in Walter Symons' name--we asked him
how much money he had borrowed in 1904, and he said about £70-
we asked him how it was paid back, and he said it was paid out of Walter
Symons' purse-Walter Symons was an insurance collector-we also
asked him if he knew that Walter Symons' furniture had been removed
from his address, and when he saw him last, and he said he saw him on
the Thursday before the Saturday of the last paying-in night, December
