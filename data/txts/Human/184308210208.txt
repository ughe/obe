I take goods from France—I have no office at clerks now—I had an office in Falcon-square—I paid 6 l . a quater for it—I lived there six months—I went from there to Oxford-court—I was there six years—in 1839 I was ruined, and took the benefit of the act—I then gave my name as James Henry Albert—I did not take my oath to the proceedings as Jew—I am a Protestant—I did not give my name as Jacob—when I went away I gave authority to the prisoner to carry on my business in Oxford-court, and left my office in his occupation—I paid him no money except the two weeks' salsry in advance—he was subject to expences on my behalf—I was in constant communication with him when I was at Paris—I know a person named Scalinger—Ihave seen him before with the prisoner—I have not been with him and the prisoner playing at cards—I have not been in friendly communication with the pri soner since I found this out—I know a Mr. Hill, Mr. Hobler's clerk—I have quarrel with the prisoner—I had a private quarrel at the Eight Bells—it was not about this business.
