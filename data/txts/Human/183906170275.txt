I went with her on the Tuesday evening, but I did not lend her my clothes—she came on a visit to her mother, and I let her sleep in my bed, as my husband was out. JANE CONNOR. I am the wife of James Connor—I keep the house—my husband is a police-officer in the R division—Myers lodged with me—I lost a bonnet, and a gown, and a ticket of a shawl, on Wednesday, the 22nd of May, out of Myers's room—this is the bonnet—it was new when the prisoner took it—I lent her a bonnet on the Monday—I lent her none of Myers's things.
   Cross-examined. Q.  When did you see her with the gown on? A.  About half-past twelve o'clock—I said, "What are you doing with it on?" she said, "Mrs. Myers gave me leave to put it on till the evening"—I did not see her again till the 6th of June. BENJAMIN LOVELL( police-sergeant R 15. )The prisoner was given into my custody on the 6th of June—she said she took the things without the intention of stealing them, she meant to return them, but when she got to Dover she was robbed—I asked her of what, she said three sove reigns, which a gentleman gave her—she was sent up by the Magis trate of Dover. MART WALKER. I am female searcher at the Greenwich station-house—on the 6th of June the prisoner was brought there—I found on her the gown, petticoat, shoes, and bonnet. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Nine Months.
