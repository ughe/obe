THOMAS ENSUM. I am a policeman,. I was on duty Salmon-lane, Limehouse, on Friday night last—Mr. Tomlin told me something, and I saw Williamson and Burton at the end of Salmon-lane, several hundred yards from Mr. Tomlin's house—another officer was with me—I took Williamson, searched him, and found these two pairs of spectacles and a silver thimble on him—he was afterwards at the station-house, and I found this coat concealed under his own clothes. EDWARD DRURY.
