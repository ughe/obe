I live at 12, Oxford Street, Southampton—I have known the prisoner since November, 1900—on March 5th, 1902, I went through a form of marriage with him—I thought then he was a single man—he led me to believe so—I have one child—I have lived continuously with him as his wife—the prisoner treated me very kindly lad we have lived very happily together.
   Cross-examined by the prisoner.  If it is legal, I am prepared to live with you after this is over. JOHN RICHARDS( Re-examined).  I have copies here of entries of a marriage between Henry Manser Weller and Bertha Eden—I have com pared them with the register at Somerset House—they are correct.
