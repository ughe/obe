CHARLES GREENFIELD. I am a painter and decorator, of 64, Noyna

the business—I do not know of any Charles Greenfield, a butcher, there
these signatures, “C. Greenfield," are not in my writing- I did not
authorise them--I have a sister named Clara—I have not seen her writing

left a little property at Worthing, which will be divided, it is not settled

Cross-examined. Her share will be over £100.
