1186 DIANA BLAKE was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of April,
2 sheets, value 20s. ; and 1 table-cloth, value 108.; the goods of Luke
Goddard.
JANE GODDARD. I am the wife of Luke Goddard, and live in Arthur-
street, Chelsea. On Saturday night, the 3rd of April, between ten and
eleven o'clock, I went to Mr. Hyams, a pawnbroker's, to redeem two sheets
and a table-cloth-I missed them while paying the money-the prisoner
was there at the time--these are them.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Have you any mark on them?
A. Yes--they are my own things, which I had pawned--the prisoner was
also redeeming things. A
JOSEPH BAKER. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner, and found
the sheets and table-cloth on her.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 44.- Recommended to mercy.-Confined Three Months.
