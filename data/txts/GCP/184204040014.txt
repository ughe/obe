1140. WILLIAM HATCH was indicted for stealing, on the 17
February, a gelding, price 31., the property of William Weaving.
WILLIAM WEAVING. I live at Hanworth, Middlesex-I deal:
horses as a knacker. On the 17th of February I had a brown zeldine
grass in Lawrence's field, at the Red Lion public-house, Hampton
took it there on Tuesday, and missed it about six o'clock on Friday
morning the 18th-I found it in possession of Winterflood, of Kensins
ton Gravel-pits.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. How long had you had the horse!
- I bought it on the Monday to work, not to kill-it was very poor because
the owner had starved it, but I knew it to be a good one-I gave 5l.fr
that and a cart-I had known it above twelve months, and am quite certsit
the horse found is the same-I had seen it very often-I have known the
prisoner two years, and believe he bore a good character-the gate of the
field was not locked-I should say the horse was worth from 31. to 51-
I did not see it in the field myself.
Thomas WINTERFLOOD. I live in Rabit-row, Notting-hill, and collet
food for pigs. On the evening of the 17th of February I was in a house in
Turnham-green, and saw the prisoner with a horse, which he offered for
sale for 258.-I gave him 22s.6d. for it-he gave me this bit of paper
(read-Thomas Winterflood bought of William Book a horse ; 11. 21.-1
described him to Weaving, and he was apprehended.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you ever seen the prisoner before? A. Not
to my recollection-I am certain of him-I could swear to him among
a hundred-when I saw him I knew him again- I took particular
notice of bim-he was with me from balf-past five o'clock till eight-Iw
obliged to rest the horse and give him food to get him home-I could not
lead him, I was obliged to drag him home-- I live ten or eleven mila
from the Red Lion public-house at Hampton-Redman was with me
the time.
JOHN REDMAN. I am a costermonger, and live in Sutton-streely
Kensington. On the 17th of February I was at Turnham-green wil
Winterflood, and saw the prisoner with the gelding-he asked 285. for
and sold it for 228. 60.-I sold the prisoner a small square for a pint a
beer after he had sold the horse.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you ever seen him before? A. Never-1
believe this square produced is what I sold him I found it among some
old iron I was sorting a few days before-I deal in iron.
Thomas Long (police-constable V 222.) I apprehended the prisoner in
Hanworth parish-he denied knowing anything about this.

Cross-examined. Q. How far is your house from Hampton
