Eleazer Boor. I live in Great George-street, Westminster. On

turned, and saw my handkerchief in the officer's hand-this is my hand-
kerchief-(looking at it.)
RICHARD BRADSHAW (police-constable D 102.) At a quarter past one
o'clock, on the 30th of June, I was in Hyde-park, opposite Clark's booth

him forward, and M'Henry took this handkerchief from the prosecutor's
pocket, put it into his bosom, and ran off.
