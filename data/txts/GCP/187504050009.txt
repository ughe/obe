243. HENRY HOWARD (28), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
Messrs. COLERIDGE and De MICHELE conducted the Prosecution
ANNIE BUCHANAN. I am barmaid at the King's Arms, Long Acre-
on the afternoou of 24th March I served the prisoner with half a pint of ale,
which came to ld.-he gave me a half-crown-I did not put it into the
till-while I was examining it he called for a screw of tobacco -I tried it,
found it was bad, and gave it to the landlord, who gave him in charge.
Prisoner. I called for the tobacco at the same time that I called for the
beer. Witness No-it was not till I examined the coin
WILLIAM FORREY. I keep the King's Arms-the last witness gave me
the bad half-crown-I did not hear him call for the tobacco-ho said that
he was not aware it was bad-I gave him in custody with the coin.
JAMES DAVID MANNING (Policeman E 317). I was in this publio house
and Mr. Forrey gave the prisoner into my custody-I asked his dame, he
said "Henry Howard _I found that that was false, and he told me his
right name afterwards and said that the half-crown was given to him by
a gentleman for carrying a box-he had no pipe or lucifers on him. ba
WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am Inspector of Coin to Her Majesty's Mint- this
half-crown is bad.
Prisoner's Defence, I am entirely innocent- gentleman asked me to
carry a box and gave me the half-crown.
