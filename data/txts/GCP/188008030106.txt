ing 18 chairs and some music-books of Richard Soloman Blair.
MESSRS. POLAND, MONTAGU WILLIAMS and PURCELL. Prosecuted.
RICHARD SOLOMAN BLAIR. I live at 3, Bath Street, Poplar, and am 8
Primitive Methodist Minister--on 13th July I conducted the service-I
examined the offertory-box; it had been forced and broken open-I missed
18 chairs from the chapel and have seen them since at the West Ham
Police-station-I also missed four lamps and some music-books-an entry
had been effected through the chapel window.
JOHN LLOYD (Detective Officer). On 14th July, about 6 o'clock, I 889
the prisoner coming out of No. 104, Cable Street, carrying a chair-he went
to 100, Cable Street, a barber's shop-I went in and took höld of him--he
struggled to get away-in the yard I found seven more chairs-he said,
“ You have made a mistake"-I said, “I have not "-he said, "I can prove
where I got them from, I brought them from a man at Forest Gate"-I
went to his lodgings and found a great bulk of property which the
prosecutor identified-I found some more chairs at Mrs. Doglow's and Mr.
Gates's-I found three other lamps and some tea-urns-they were sold by
the prisoner to a man in Leman Street.
SAMUEL SOLOMONS. I keep a tobaceo manufactory, 104, Cable Street,
St. George's-in-the-East-I know the prisoner-I saw the chairs at my
place on the evening of 13th July-I have bought various kinds of things
from the prisoner.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I did not buy six chairs from you.

offered me 10 chairs for 11.-I afterwards gave them up to the police.
