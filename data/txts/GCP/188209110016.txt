THOMAS FLANAGAN. I live at 2, Orchard Street, Ball's Pond-on
Saturday night, the 29th of July, I saw Henry Godden at the Duke of
York public-house, Kingsland—he was drunk and leaning on the table
when I went into the bar about 11 p.m.-I stopped with him to the
concert, and helped him home-it was about 12.5 when I took him
upstairs—the other chap who helped me stopped at the top of Mildmay
Avenue—when I put him on the bed he was drunk and insensible-I
came away, and talked to a neighbour about 10 minutes-I saw & crowd
I stopped with Henry Godden about 5 minutes I did not take his
handkerchief off.
