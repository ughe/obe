   MR. RODERICK . Prosecuted, and MR. HAWTIN . Defended. JOSEPH RAYMOND. Pooley, weigh-bridge manufacturers—he told me he had been to their Fleet Street branch, and had seen Mr. Pooley and had got the order from him—the signature is more like "Pollin" than "Pooley," and I told him so—he replied, "Whether it is 'Pollin' or 'Pooley,' Mr. A. Pooley acknowledging the order, and by return of post received a letter from them repudiating it—I then sent a telegram to their head office at Birmingham and received a letter from there confirming their other letter—I wrote to the prisoner about it and the letter was returned marked "House empty."
