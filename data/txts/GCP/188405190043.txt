584. FREDERICK SOHOFIELD (30) and HENRY WALKER (32).
Feloniously breaking and entering the warehouse of Henry Manser, and
stealing five bags of stick lac. Second Count, receiving.
Mr. ERNEST BEARD Prosecuted; Mr. KEITH FRITH defended Schofiold, and
MR. PURCELL defended Walker
JOHN ROGERS (City Policeman 824). On Saturday, April 12, about
8 o'clock p.m., I was on duty in Little George Street, Minories--my atten-
tion was drawn to a van being led past No. 1 by Schofield, who was in the
act of turning the horse round when I took hold of the reins and stopped
the van, and told him I wished to speak to him-he got over the tail of
the van, ran up Little George Street, and then returned into the
Minories, where he made his escape-I looked in the van and saw the
five bags there, and got my mates to take charge of the place while I
took the horse and van to the station-it contained these five bags, a
jommy, a coat, and a pooket-handkerchief-I next saw Schofield on
Monday morning, the 14th, in the station, where I was taken for the
purpose of identifying the person whom I saw in the van-I picked him
out from several others.
Cross-examined by Mr. FRITH. I did not see him in charge of a de-
tective who was bringing him to the station-he was placed with people
of all sorts, a lot of Oity sewer men were there in slops and trousers, not
with badges- I don't doubt there were people there generally resembling
Schofield in height, age, and appearance-I looked him right in the
face when I went to take the horse and van, and did not want to look
particularly at him when I identified him-I should put the time down
at 8.15-I made it 8.15 in my report-I will not swear it was not only 8.3.
Cross-examined by MR. PURCELL. I only saw one man at the van.
Re-examined. I left the premises in charge of other constables, and
did not examine them.
DENIS KENNEDY. I live at 9, Jewry Street, City-on 12th April,
about 8 o'clock, I was in Little George Street, Minories, and saw four
men leading & van, one of whom was Schofield-I was taken to the
station on Wednesday, the 16th April, and there picked him out from
among others.
