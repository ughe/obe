(Defence.)
ARCHIBALD ANDREWS (prisoner, on oath). Early in March of this
year I started business and tried to work up a connection. My object
was to canvass for orders and get them executed by the trade.
I
soon found that to open up a city office required more capital than I
had. When I had had an office for about a month I found myself in
difficulties. In some cases I executed the orders, and in other cases
I returned the deposits. About two weeks before I was arrested I felf
I would never be able to make the business pay, and that the more
orders I took the more trouble it would be. With this idea I started
to clear the deposits up, and I wrote to every customer stating that
I should be calling on him on the following Wednesday to return his
deposit. Quiek had one of those eards. I am quite willing to refund
