   OLD COURT .— Monday, October  23 rd , 1854.
   PRESENT  —The Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; Sir CHAPMAN MARSHALL, Knt, Ald.; Mr. Ald. HUNTER ; Mr. RECORDER ; and Mr. Ald. ROSE .
   Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury.
 1069. WILLIAM GARLICK was indicted for embezzling the sums of 6 l  ., 5 l  ., and 1 s  .; also  , the sum of 10 s  .; the moneys of George William Umpleby , his master: to which he PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Twelve Months .
 1070. FRANCIS SWIFT , embezzling 86 l  . 12 s  . 6 d  .; the moneys of James Yates and others, his masters: to which he PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 54.— Confined Eight Months .
 1071. GEORGE WICKING , stealing 6 gold seals, 4 gold pencil cases, and other articles, value 2 l  . 14 s  . 10 d  .; the goods of Joseph Smith and another: to which he PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Four Months .
 1072. JAMES JONES , stealing a saw, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of William Gibbs ; a plane and hammer, value 6 s  .; the goods of James Elder ; and a plane, value 4 s  .; the goods of William Howes .
   MR. COOPER conducted the Prosecution. JAMES ELDER. I am a carpenter, and live at Arlington.
