MR. BESLEY objected to the indictment on the ground that the 27th Sec. of
24 and 25 Vic. c. 98, required that a forged document, or record of a Court,
should be used, or intended to be used in a Court, and that there was no
evidence in this case to show that the affidavit was used, or intended to be used
in the Court of Bankruptcy, there being no proceedings or suit pending,
Tue Court considered that there was evidence to prove that the affidavit was
intended to be used in the Court of Bankruptcy.
