   THOMAS FARRANT. I am a Policeman. I took the prisoner into custody at Mr. Phillips' shop, and asked where he lodged; he said at No. 17, Newman-street, Edgware-road, in the second floor back room; I went there, and found all these articles; the prisoner afterwards asked me if I thought Mr. Phillips would come and prosecute him - I said that no doubt he would; he said he was very sorry for what he had done, that he did it through distress.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Had you made him any threat or promise? A. I had not.(Property produced and sworn to.)

 2049.  THOMAS PUGH was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of September , 14 yards of ribbon, value 10s., the goods of Thomas Winstead Green.
   THOMAS HUTCHINS PINDER. I am in the service of Thomas Winstead Green, linen-draper, Judd-street. I saw the prisoner in our shop with a female, and while she was looking at some ribbon, I saw him take a roll of ribbon from the drawer, and put it down, in the act, as I supposed, of giving it to the female who stood by him; I immediately caught hold of his arm, asked what he was going to do with it, and said, "We must keep a sharp eye on him;" he made no answer - he left the shop very shortly after; the female bought nothing - I told Mr. Green what I had seen; he followed, and took him.
   THOMAS WINSTEAD GREEN. The prisoner came to my shop on the 1st of September; I saw him go out - Pinder gave me information; I followed him thirty or forty yards till I met a Policeman, I then gave him in charge - the ribbon had been taken from him in the shop; nothing was found on him, neither money nor any thing.
   THOMAS HUTCHINS PINDER. I saw him take the ribbon from the drawer, and put it down by the side of him; I immediately caught hold of his arm, and took it from him.
     NOT GUILTY.
 2050.  JOHN MAY was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of August , 1 truck, value 30s., and 1 waistcoat, value 5s., the goods of Joseph Maynard.
   JOSEPH MAYNARD. I am a rag-merchant, and live at No. 47, Brick-lane, Old-street. I left this waistcoat hanging up in my shop, and the truck safe in the warehouse, about eight o'clock on Friday night the 17th of August; next morning I found it broken open, and the truck and waistcoat gone.
   JAMES FORD. I live in Fleur-de-lis-court, Spitalfields. I have a truck stands in the next yard to mine; when I opened my door in the morning, between eight and nine o'clock, the prisoner was there breaking up a truck - he did not live there; I asked what he was breaking it up for; he said he would sell it to me for 15s. - I said I would have nothing to do with it, and asked why he was breaking it up - he said it was too small for him, and he was going to build a larger one; the board, with the prosecutor's name on it, was thrown into my yard - I did not see it come off the truck, but when the officer came and asked for it I found it; I saw the same truck at the station that morning.
   WILLIAM SHEEN. On Saturday morning, the 18th of August, between six and seven o'clock, the prisoner came to my house, and offered me a truck; I said I would have nothing to do with it, and asked where he got it - he said it was all right; he had not got it with him - I asked where it was; he said in Essex-street, which is in Whitechapel - I accompanied him there, and saw the truck; I thought it was not right, and would not buy it; I asked what he wanted for it - he said 12s.; it was worth 25s. or 30s. - I walked home; he followed me, and offered it for 5s., then 4s. 6d., and then walked away.
   HENRY COTTON. I am a Policeman. I saw the prisoner in the yard with the truck in Flower and Dean-street, Spitalfields - I took him to the station with it.
   JAMES HENRY WILLIAMSON. I am servant to a pawnbroker. I have a jacket, pawned by the prisoner on the 18th of August, about eight o'clock in the morning.
 MR. MAYNARD. I saw the truck at the station-house; it is mine - the prisoner had been in my employ, and could read; my name and address was on it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going to market between three and four o'clock in the morning, and met a man who I knew by sight - he asked if I would sell the truck for him; he took off the jacket, and gave it me with the truck - I was to meet him in Whitechapel that morning, which I did, and told him I could not sell the truck; he told me to break it up and sell it - I was to have 2s. 6d. for it.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Confined Six Months.
