   Q.  When did you first obtain information about any of these parcels? A.  On the night of Monday, the 22nd of January, about half-past eleven o'clock—that relates to the first six—I had not heard of the fire at that time—I read an account of the fire in the newspaper, while I was waiting for the person I supposed would call for the parcels at the coffee-shop—I think that was on the Tuesday, the following day—in consequence of sus picions we had of the person who had left the parcels, I and Dyer were on the look-out for him several days—we were there till about two o'clock on the Wednesday, and then, as nobody came, were determined to open them.
