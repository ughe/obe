426. PIERRE COLLETTE (28), Forging and uttering a promissory
note for the payment of money, with intent to defraud.
Mr. Drake and Mr. HARRISON Prosecuted,
BEATRICE MARY CUNNINGTON I am single, and keep a boarding
school for ladies at Southgate House, Devizes - my mother lives there
with me the prisoner came to me as French master on September 28th
he produced several certificates -I have not seen them since he paid me
great attention, and at the end of October 'we became engaged to be
married, subject to the consent of my brother-it was to be private, at
the prisoner's request, till Christmas be at first refused to take
any salary, but I would not accept that he was to give about
theee lessons a week, and I was to pay bim £6 in three months, and I also
undertook to procure him other pupils not connected with the Rehool
which I did -- he bad Jodgings in Devizes-he did not speak English
very much one of his reasons for coming was to learn English - he was
very well received in the neighbourhood he told me he bad a great
many influential friends, and that his father was a large landed farmer,
beetroot grower—that on his marriage he would come into an in
heritance of £18,000 from his mother that he was a bachelor and 28
years old-he wanted to purchase a house in the neighbourhood, and
ultimately he wanted to take Fair View, which I believe on our marriage
he intended to settle on me it was not arranged whether we should give
up my school -Fair View is about half a mile from where we live-he
was going to purchase it for £3,000-he returned to France about the
end of November, he said to arrange his money matters-while he was away
my French classes were taken by an English governess who had previously
taken them the prisoner invited my brother and I to go over and visit
his people after Christmas, and we went over to France on December 31st
-We went to Marlo les Bains near Dunkirk we were next day in.

school at St. Marlo, but I have since found out that he is the postmaster
there-we saw very little of him: we saw him at the station, and again in
the evening at the post office, where we saw his wife; she presided there
