2210. DANIEL MORRIS, feloniously cutting and wounding Jane Ann
Morris, with intent to do her some grievous bodily harm.
JANE ANN MORRIS. The prisoner is my husband-I now live at 5, Eden-
street, Holborn. About twelve weeks ago, in May, I came home to where we
lived, in Webber-street, between nine and ten o'clock at night my husband
was sitting by the fire-be asked the little boy, who is twelve years old, what
the time was-he said, ten minutes to ten, I thipk-my husband spoke
about the impropriety of my not being at home before him, and asked me for
money-he accused me of being drunk as usual-I denied it-I was not-he
sisked for some supper--I said it was out of my power to give it aggravation
took place between us to the extreme-I accused him of being drunk-he
had begun to drink at six o'clock in the morning-he asked if I could not
furnish him with a sovereign next day-I said it was out of my power-he
accused me of many things, family matters-he bad the poker in the fire,
clearing it-I found him in the act to strike me--be made after me, and
struck me on the arm-I believe I put my arm up-I do not know where he
struck at-I may have told the Magistrate I put my arm up to guard my head,
and got the blow on my arm-I heard a noise, as if a basket was broken-
there was no basket there-I afterwards found my arm was broken-I fell-I
do not know what made me fall-I was then struck again on my legs with a
poker, which my husband had in his hand- I was struck four or five times on
the arm-1 pulled a chair near me I cannot say whether that saved me-my
husband broke it with the poker---some one came in I was taken to the
hospital, and have been there ever since.

ALEXANDER FREEMANTLE. I am clerk to a merchant, at 64, Webber-
street--the prisoner and his wife lived at 65. On 31st May, between ten and
eleven o'clock, I was in my back parlour, and heard very loud blows in rapid
succession, and screams of " Murder !" and "Save meľ-I went, burst the
door open, and found Mrs. Morris weltering in blood, and the prisoner stand-
ing over her with part of a broken poker-I said, "For God's sake, you are
not going to kill the woman!"-he said, "She deserves it"-I saw that her
arm was broken, and the elbow dislocated -- there was blood on the ground-
the police came in, and I gave him in charge-he seemed very cool and col-
lected-he put his fingers in his waistcoat-pockets, be gave one of his chil-
dren half-a-crown to pay for something, and took his snuff-box from the shelf
and put it in his pocket, as if nothing bad occurred.
