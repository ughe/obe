WILLIAM ROE ORCHARD. I am the son of Nicholas Orchard, an eating-house keeper, in London-street, Greenwich. The prisoner was in it my father's employ—he used to help in the kitchen—on the 11th of April, I heard my father discharge him as I was cleaning myself—the prisoner went into the bed-room to take his things about half-past two o'clock, and directly after he was gone I missed a shirt of my father's, which had been on the bed—I saw it again a fortnight or three weeks after, at the office with the prisoner—this is the shirt.
   Prisoner . It was five o'clock when I left the house, and my master saw what clothes I had—he searched me, and it was not about me. JOHN DAWSON. I am a pawnbroker at Greenwich. I took in this shirt of the prisoner on the 11th of April, in the name of John Harris.
   Prisoner . I was not in his house—I do not know where he lives. Witness . I am sure he is the person. GUILTY. Aged 20.
 1384. JOSEPH WHEELER was again  indicted for stealing, on the   19th of April , 1 coat, value 10 s  .; the goods of John Davis . JOHN DAVIS. I am a servant at a public-house at Greenwich. I lost my coat from an outbuilding in the yard, where we clean the boots and knives, on Friday the 19th of April—this is it. JOSEPH ABATHY. I am a pawnbroker at Blackheath-hill. I took in this coat of the prisoner on the 19th of April, in the name of Joseph Wheeler, for 5 s .
   Prisoner's Defence . I bought the coat in the fair for 1 s . 6 d . GUILTY. Aged 20.— Transported for Seven Years.

 1385. JOHN WILKINS and JAMES PAYNE were indicted for stealing, on the   16th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 5 s  .; the goods of Joseph Groves , from his person. JOSEPH GROVES. On the 16th of April I was at Greenwich-fair. I had a handkerchief in my coat-pocket—I did not miss it till the policeman told me of it—I then followed, and saw him take it from the breeches of one of the prisoners—this is it. BENJAMIN SAMUEL BLABEN( police-constable A  56.) About half-past five o'clock on the 16th of April, I saw the two prisoners in Greenwich-fair, trying several persons' pockets—they were together, and sometimes arm in-arm in front of Richardson's show—they were lifting up pockets and putting their hands in—when they got to the prosecutor, Payne took a silk handkerchief from his pocket, and passed it to Wilkins, who was covering him as he did it—I told the prosecutor and took Payne—Brooks took Wilkins. JAMES BROOKS( police-constable L  118.) I took the handkerchief from Wilkins' breeches—what Blaben has said is true.
   Wilkins' Defence . I was standing in front of the show when some person, I do not know who, thrust a handkerchief into my hand, and I thrust it into my button-hole, having no pockets.
 (  The prisoners received excellent characters .)
   WILKINS— GUILTY. Aged 15.
   PAYNE— GUILTY. Aged 15.
   Recommended to mercy— Confined Three Months.
