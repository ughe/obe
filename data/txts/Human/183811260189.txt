   Cross-examined. Q.  Have you not had some of the warrants back again? A.  No, not any, I believe the officer has three warrants for hogsheads, but they form a very small part of the whole, which was twenty hogsheads—some bills of exchange were sent under an envelope, during my absence, to my counting-house, but they have never been received as payment—some of them are on a Mr. Bragg of Bristol—Mr. Bragg has not given me up any warrants—Mr. Bragg waited on me some time ago, and made an offer to give up part of the wines he had, on my cancelling the bills against him, which he stated had been obtained by Mr. Trevethen under false pretences, but I have since discovered that Mr. Bragg has got an ad vance on those wines, and they are no longer in his power to de liver to me—I understand Mr. Bragg is a man of no responsibility at all—he told me he could not pay 6 d . towards the bills—he did not tell me his wife was a person of property—he said she had no property—the prisoner told me that Mr. Bragg's wife was a person, of property—that he had got a bond on her property—I believe he said so when we first talked about the parties to whom the wine was going—when I found that the money was not forthcoming, I called on the prisoner to know what he had done with the wines, and he gave a kind of history of what he had done or was going to do with them—he mentioned Mr. Bragg as a person to whom he had parted with some—he did not name him as a person whose bills he would give me—decidedly not—there was no talk about bills at all—he men tioned Mr. Bragg as a highly respectable tradesman of Bristol-now I happened to know Bristol very well—I referred to the name of Bragg, and asked who he was—he said he had been for a long time with a house he named, which an intimate friend of mine had lately established—that he was going to be one of his greatest friends and correspondents, and that he had got security from Mr. Bragg to the extent of 4 or 500 l . in a bond, I understood him to say, of some property of his wife's or family's—the bills on Mr. Bragg were one of 81 l . and one of 70 l .—the wine he had was worth 51 l .—the prisoner was apprehended on the 29th of October—I delivered him the warrant on the 11th of September.
