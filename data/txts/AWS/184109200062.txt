14th of September, 2 printed books, value 108., the goods of James By-
water Humphreys.
WILLIAM JAMEs ELLIOTT. I am in the service of Mr. James Bywater
Humphreys, who keeps the Crown coffee-house in Holborn. On the 14h
Sept. about a quarter to six o'clock, the waitress informed me she had some
suspicion-I went down the coffee-room, and she directed my attention to
the prisoner-I touched him on the shoulder, and asked him to step
into the bar-he followed me, and I told him I suspected he had some of
our books about his person- said he wondered at that-I said did
and I must see, and between his waistcoat and his trowsers, I found thes
directories, cut in pieces-they are my master's.

your uncle A. Yes-he is not here.
