MATTHEW REARDON. I am a policeman. A little before nine o'clock that evening I was in Devonshire-street, Lisson-grove—I saw the prisoner with something bulky under his coat—I stopped him and said, "Harper, what have you got here?"—he said, "Nothing but clothes, belonging to my sister"—I pulled this print from under his coat, and said, "You must come with me"—he said, "Let me go; I will walk along with you"—I did not let him go—he walked a few yards and then struck me in the face, kicked me violently, called for assistance, and got from me—I sprung my rattle—it was a very foggy evening, and he got away. THOMAS HAZELDINE. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner after wards on the 23rd of March—he said he knew nothing about it. GUILTY. * Aged 19.— Confined Twelve Months .
 1308. JOHN ROBERTSON, was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of March , 1 time-piece and case, value 3 l  .; the goods of William Marshall . WILLIAM MARSHALL. I am a watch and clock maker, and live in the Hackney-road—about a quarter before nine o'clock in the morning of the 18th of March I left my shop, leaving a time-piece on the table—the door was open—I came back about eleven o'clock, and it was gone—I saw it at Marylebone office—this is it.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  What do you know it by? A.  The name and the number—it had not been sold. WILLIAM POWELL. I am a shoemaker—on the morning of the 18th of March I was in Bell-street, Marylebone, with Langley—I saw the prisoner with something under his arm, covered with an apron—I went after him—he ran away—I ran and stopped him, and asked what he had got—he did not say; but said it had been given him—I took him, and found it was this time-piece.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Are you in training for the police? A.  No; I have been in the police—I went after the prisoner at Langley's request. WILLIAM EADE. I am a policeman. I was in Great James-street—Powell brought me the prisoner and the time-piece, covered over with an apron—I asked the prisoner what he had got—he said he did not know—I asked where he got it—he said a little boy gave it him round the corner—I made inquiries—the prosecutor came and owned it. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Twelve Months .
 1309. HELEN CONWAY, was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of March , 1 coat, value 15 s  .; the goods of John Cheeseman ; and that she had been before convicted of felony. JOHN CHEESEMAN. I live in Devonshire-street, Lisson-grove—on the 24th of March I had this coat hanging on a line near my parlour-door—in the afternoon, about three o'clock, I was in my parlour, and saw the prisoner go out of the shop-door with this coat in a shawl—I followed her next door and stopped her—I took it from her and brought her into the shop, laid it on the counter, and sent for a policeman—the prisoner went on her knees for me to pardon her—she said she was a transgressor, and took it "promiscuously"—she was intoxicated. HILL BECK. I am a policeman. I was called, and took the prisoner and this coat—I said, "You hear what you are charged with"—she said, "I don't care a d----n, you can only transport me"—she was drunk.
