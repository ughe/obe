THOMAS GEORGE ALLEN. I am a leather dresser, of No. 6, East Street,
Southwark Bridge Road. I know Peach - I went to Horsemonger Lane Gaol
several times to see her she asked me if I knew a woman named Richardson

not-she said, "I wish you did ; I have a message I want you to take to her

went back and told her that I had found Richardson's address she said,
I wish you would go to her, and tell her that I am here, in the place where
she ought to be ; and if she will not give me money to maintain me during
the time I am in this prison, and likewise provide money for Counsel for me,
I shall not be quiet ; she knows she had a share of the property, and can do
it if she likes I went to Richardson, who was at a chandler's shop, in
Lant Street, in the Borough, and delivered the message to her she said,
" If I did have a share of the property, I made good use of it; I have not
been such a fool with it as other people ; (I shall not give anything a day
or two after that, I went to the gaol, and told Peach that she would do nothing
for her she told me to go and ask her again, and I went the next day, or
the day after, and told her she must give something for Counsel for her, and
