WILLIAM BEAGLEY (Policemun N 94). About 2.45 I received information,
and went to Mr. Jordan's tap room window-I lifted it up, pulled the blind
back, and saw Williams and Pulley in the passage I asked them what they
were doing there—Pulley said that he was going to have something to eat
-he afterwards said that he was going to have his supper--they rushed to
the back, and I got in at the window, went through, and followed them
over several walls—I only lost sight of them when they dropped over the
walls-I eventually lost sight of Pulley, but kept Williams, in sight till
found him in custody of 102 N-I had seen Hillier at 1.40, close to the
spot, with the two male prisoners—there was no light in the room when I
looked in- I used my lantern.
Crow-examined by Mr. M. WILLIAMS. Q. Do you know a man called

Cross-examined by MR. RIBTON. Q. Do you mean to say that you saw
Williams in the house 4. Yes, in the passage-he was not lying down,
nor did he appear to be waking up-I am sure he is the man.

CHRISTOPHER MARTIN (Policeman N 473). On 28th April, a little before
2 a.m., I saw Hillier loitering about with two men, close by Mr. Jordan's
they went away till 2.40, and when the alarm was given she was standing
near the spot-I lost sight of her till after the alarm was given-I did not
see her in the interval, and then I saw her six doors off, and asked her if she
was the same woman I SAW with two men previously she said, “You saw
me with one man, not with two; I was with my husband"-I said, “Where
is your husband " she said, "He has gone round to the back, and
clambered over the railings to apprehend the thieves" she afterwards said
that she did not know where he was gone-she was on the spot at the
time of the alarm, and gave her address, Monyer Street, but it is false.
HENRY BILLINGS (Policeman N 36). At 1.45 on this morning, I saw the

herdess Walk-I saw Williams and Hillier at the station, at 4 o'clock.
