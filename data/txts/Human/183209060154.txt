 2099.  DANIEL LAMBERT was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of July , 1 coat, value 3l.; 3 waistcoats, value 16s.; 1 pair of trousers, value 18s.; 1 handkerchief, value 3s.; 1 shaving-box, value 2s., and 1 rule, value 1s., the goods of William Thompson.
 WILLIAM THOMPSON. I live in  Hartshorn-court, Golden-lane; the prisoner lodged in the same room with me. I missed these things from different drawers, on the 31st of July - I had not seen them after that; I saw the prisoner in about six hours, and told him I suspected him; he said he had pawned them, and told me where, but I have not found the clothes - the things altogether were worth 5l.; I never authorized him to pawn them.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did you never authorize him to pledge any thing? A. Never; I never agreed not to prosecute if he would pay me 6s. a week - I am porter to Messrs. Hale and Co., oilmen.
   HENRY PRINCE. I am foreman to Mr. Galloway at times. On the 31st of July, about two o'clock in the afternoon, the prosecutor, having missed his things, told me to search for the prisoner, and about half-past four o'clock, I saw him in the room, and asked what he had done with Bill's things - he cried, and said he had taken and pawned them at the corner of Worship-street; I went there next day, but the pawnbroker had left; the prisoner gave me the duplicate, which I gave the Policeman.
 Cross-examined. Q. You asked where he pawned them? A. Yes; he said the house was shut up, but the goods were to be delivered in Newgate-street; but there was a board on the house referring to Mr. Joyce of the New-road; I have known the prisoner fourteen years; I never said the prosecutor had agreed to take 6s. a week not to prosecute.
   SAMUEL KNAPP. My father is a pawnbroker in Golden-lane. I have a shaving-box and rule, pawned on the 31st of July, for 8d., in the name of Thomas Smith- I cannot say who by.
   RICHARD COTTER. I am a Policeman. I took the prisoner; he cried, and said he had given the duplicates to Prince, who gave them to me; one of them has the name of Pickford on it.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner delivered in a petition, expressing his contrition.
   EDWARD HANDS. I am an officer of St. Luke's. Prince told me the prosecutor had agreed that the prisoner should pay down 9s., and 5s. a week, and he would not prosecute him; this was about four o'clock in the afternoon - the prisoner was taken that night.
 GUILTY. Aged 19.
     Recommended to Mercy- Confined Fourteen Days.
 2100.  JOHN WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of August , 1 watch, value 5l.; 1 seal, value 10s., and 1 key, value 5s., the goods of William Scott , from his person.
   WILLIAM SCOTT. I am a lighterman, and live at Rotherhithe. On the 20th of August, about nine o'clock in the evening, I was coming out of the Minories, down by St. Katharine's-dock wall- I was sober; the prisoner and three more were coming towards me; they separated, two on each side of me; the prisoner was close to me - he caught hold of my ribbon, and snatched my watch with great violence - he dragged the ribbon right through the loop-hole; the two on the left went into the road - the other, who was with him, went on the right side; I ran after the prisoner, calling Stop him! he was taken in my sight, with my watch in his hand.
 Prisoner. It was done in a very dark place. Witness. Quite the reverse, it is lighted with gas all the way.
   JOHN DEMPSEY. I am a Policeman. I was opposite the Mint, and heard a cry - I stopped the prisoner, and found the watch in his hand; Scott came up and charged him with the robbery.
 Prisoner's Defence. I heard a cry of Stop thief! and saw a man throw something down; I took up the watch, and gave it to the Policeman.
   WILLIAM SCOTT. I remarked his face, and am certain he is the man who took it.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Transported for Life.
 2101.  ALFRED COOK was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of August , 1 spoon, value 12s., the goods of Thomas Rugless.
   ELIZA RUGLESS. I am the daughter of Thomas Rugless, who keeps a confectioner's-shop in Picadilly. On Friday, the 24th of August, the prisoner came into the shop for a basin of mock-turtle soup; I took it into the soup-room with a spoon, and in a very few minutes he called me into the room, and gave me half a crown - I gave him 1s. 6d.; he had only eat half the soup; he left in a very short time, and as he went out, I went into the room, and missed the spoon - my father went after him, and he was taken in a pawnbroker's shop; I saw the spoon at the office the same evening, and am quite sure it is my father's - I have cleaned it for many years; there are no initials on it.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. How do you know it? A. By the pattern; nobody came in or went out of the room before I missed it - we had no other customer.
   NATHANIEL BARNES. I am a pawnbroker. On the 24th of July, between four and five o'clock, the prisoner came to pawn this table-spoon; Mr. Rugless came in and took him before I advanced the money.
 Prisoner. Q. If you wanted a quantity of these spoons, could you not obtain them very readily? A. Certainly.
   CHARLES BAKER. I am a serjeant of the Police. Mr. Rugless brought the prisoner to the station-house, charged with stealing the spoon, which Barnes delivered to me; the prisoner gave no account of it.
 ELIZA RUGLESS. I am quite certain of this spoon; the prisoner never claimed it as his own - I know it from a mark on it; a flaw - my father followed him immediately.
 Prisoner's Defence. I purchased it of a dealer, which is customary in the trade I followed.
