did not she very often tell you that it was the annuity Mr. Harvey was allow
ing you! 4. No; I do not suppose the girl knew-on 7th March, 1851, I
went down to Chiswick, where the prisoner was residing; I did not ask for
21. on my annuity-I went down on my landlady's account; I did not get
21. ; I got 18d, and a bottle of wine to give to my dying landlady. A
Court. Q. Did you see the prisoner when you went to Chiswick ?
4. I did ; it was on a Sunday I asked him to come and settle with my
landlady, who was dying, and raving about the money--he said he could not
do it on the Monday, but as sure as God made him, he would settle my
whole affairs on the Tuesday, and I could settle with Mrs. Griffith as I liked.
