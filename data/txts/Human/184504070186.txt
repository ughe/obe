NEW COURT .— Wednesday, April  16 th , 1845.
   Third Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
 968. WILLIAM HENRY WHITE was indicted for stealing 3 bottles, value 3 d  .; 1 pint of wine, 2 s  .; 1 pint of ale, 4 d  .; 1 quart of stout, 8 d  .; 3 half-crowns, 13 shillings, and 7 sixpences; the property of Gustavus Dunning , his master.—Two other COUNTS  , stating it to be the property of the Sheriff of Middlesex.—And another, stating it to be the property of Laing Johnson .
   MR. O'BRIEN conducted the Prosecution. LAING JOHNSON. I was a wine-merchant, carrying on business at Hammersmith. I had an execution put into my house on Wednesday, the 19th of Feb.—the prisoner was put in possession of my premises by the Sheriff's officer that evening—I found him there the next morning—on the evening he was put in possession there was 17 s . 6 d . in the till—I examined the till on the Sunday, and there was nothing in it—I did not give him authority to remove wine, or ale, or beer. JAMES JENNINGS. I was in the service of Mr. Dunning. The prisoner was in possession of his premises—on Thursday, the 20th of Feb., I recol lect seeing a woman there who had come to him—the prisoner asked me for a light, and went down into the cellar—I saw him take a bottle of ale, a bottle of wine, and a battle of stout, and give them to her. GUSTAVUS DUNNING. I put the prisoner in possession of the premises on the 19th of Feb.—he was to take the money that was received—I did not give him authority to remove anything.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  It would be his duty to render you an account at the end of the time, of money received, and goods he might happen to sell? A.  Yes—he would have to sell, and to account to me for the produce—there was 1 l . 5 s . due to him—if he had entered the ale and wine against his account it would have been allowed in that manner—I believe he has had no opportunity of rendering his account—I have a little book of his, in which these articles are entered—I do not know when the entries were made. GEORGE LOW( police-constable T  50.) I took the prisoner—I found 1 1/2 d . on him. NOT GUILTY.
