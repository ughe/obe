CHARLES WILLIAM GOODHALL. I am house-surgeon, at Middlesex Hospital. On 26th Dec. a man named Dennis M'Carthy was brought there, with a semicircular wound over the right temple, about two inches long, extending to the covering of the bone; and another, about an inch long, at the back of the head, dividing the scalp only; and another, about four inches long, on the left shoulder—this knife would be likely to pro duce such wounds—there was nothing to cause immediate danger—we took him into the house for a month or five weeks.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I never saw the man before, and had no reason to do him an injury; he is quite a lad to what I am, and I would not do such a cowardly act; I had been drinking gin and beer for three hours and a half, and was not conscious of doing it.
