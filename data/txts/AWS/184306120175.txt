1927. WILLIAM SHARP was indicted for breaking and entering the
dwelling-house of John Graham Loughy on the 17th of May, at St. Marylen
bone, and stealing therein 1 breast-pin, value 11. 58.; 1 razor-case, 6d. ; and
7 razors, 21. 9s.; his property.
Mr. RYLAND conducted the Prosecution.
