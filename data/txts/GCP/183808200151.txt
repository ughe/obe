1939. CAROLINE HALE was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of
August, 2 sheets, value 6s., the goods of Griffith Thomas and another, her
masters.
GRIFFITH THOMAS. I live in Oxford-street, and am a linen-draper, in
partnership with Mr. Brooks-the prisoner was our servant-at half-past
ten or eleven o'clock on the 7th of August I heard there was a strange
Woman in the kitchen, Waters came up, and I stopped her with a pair of
sheets of mine-she said my servant gave her them to pawn-these are
ours-(looking at them.)
ELLEN WATERS. These sheets were found on me when I went into the
kitchen, the prisoner told me to pawn them for 2s. to give the milkman,
to get the bill to go up to her master.
Prisoner. I gave her them to carry up stairs-when she went to the
Oce, she gave a wrong name and address-she said she lived in Little
indmill-street. Witness. I do not live there at present-I went to the
use for some caps to wash for her.
