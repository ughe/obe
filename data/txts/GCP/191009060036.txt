HARDING, James (24, gardener), pleaded guilty of breaking and
entering the dwelling house of James Badenoch, with intent to steal
therein.
Prisoner confessed to a conviction of felony at this court on Septem-
ber 10, 1907, in the name of James Carlton, when he was sentenced to
three years' penal servitude. Previous convictions were proved against
him, including one of eight months at North London Sessions on
November 10, 1903, for burglary.
WILLIAM CRAWFORD, estate agent, 34, Crown Road, St. Margaret's-
on-Thames, stated that prisoner was in his employ from August,
1904, to March, 1907, and that he had always found him honest.
Sentence, 12 months' hard labour.
