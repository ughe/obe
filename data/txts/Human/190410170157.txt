I followed—Adams and the woman were taken into custody—the prosecutor said, "I wish you had been here sooner, we would have caught the other man who hit me on the head. the prosecutor had hold of my arm"—I took the female to the station—I saw the other man disappearing round the corner of Brook Street—the prosecutor was not drunk, but he was dazed and frightened.
