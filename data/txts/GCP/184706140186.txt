Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. On the Saturday morning, the
money was all safe ? A. Yes I thought the prisoner a person of great re-
spectability, the little time I had known her I do not know whether she
came recommended to my mother-she had been staying at the Royal-hotel,
in Chatham-place, but I do not know how long I did not know that she
had relations at Bristol-1 was away from home for two hours, or two hours
and a half, while we went round the parks-we are occupying apartments-I
did not secure my room door before I came away on that occasion-I never
do 80-I partook of the wine with the prisonershe did not come by my in-
vitation on the Friday-she did on the Saturday-I do not know whether
she is a person of a good Polish family or not-we had not agreed that we
would take a glass of wine together before she sent for it-she told me she
was in trouble about having lost her keys—when I went to her room it was
late at night-she was in bed-the officer was with me-I do not know
whether she was very much alarmed-there was no one left in my apart-
ments that I know of, during the two hours and a half that I was riding round
the park-it was not until my husband came home that we discovered the
box had been disturbed.
