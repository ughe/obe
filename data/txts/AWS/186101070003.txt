116. SCHMIDT SCHOTTE (29), was indicted for stealing a ring, value
12s. the property of George Fitch.
The prisoner being a foreigner, had the evidence explained to him by an
interpreter.
GEoRGe FITOH. I am a watchmaker and jeweller, of 5, Barbican-or
Saturday afternoon, 15th December, the prisoner came into my shop did
not know him before-he wanted to look at some signet rings he said he
wanted three of a sort-I showed him a tray-he said there were none
there to suit him-I showed him four more by themselves-he selected one
from them-he then said he thought there were some more in the window
that he could select from-I took out four more for him, and there was
another he thought would suit himhe then got me to look out another
from the window, and while my back was turned to get the ring, be abstracted
one of the others-I did not see him do it, buat when I came back from the
window, I looked at the rings that had been placed before him, and one was
missing- immediately asked him for ithe said he knew nothing at all
about it, that he was a respeotable man, and I was quite mistaken- said I
knew 1 was not mistaken, I was as positive that he had got one as possible,
for there was one missing, and unless I knew more of him than I did, I
should give him into custody for stealing it, which I did; but during the
interim when I went to the door to look for a policeman, he tried to throw
me down and get away, but I held him tight till a policeman ame-there
was a lady in the shop at the time, who had come in before the prisoner
she was waiting for a watch, I was altering the regulator for ber, but I left
her to serve him-after the prisoner was taken away, I searched the shop
and in a corner I found these two brass rings, imitation wedding-rings, and
these pieces of paper all bitten and torn up-I put them together, and it is
a bill of some goods to the amount of 381.-the missing ring was not found
