came over to our house, bringing the key of the box with him-he went up
into the room where the box was; it was there, lying open-he might be
there two or three hours, not in the room but down stairs, because he had
some refreshment-when we first broke open the box it was full of old news-
papers and different papers, and there were some old buckles, and a variety
