After that I received instructions to take proceedings—I got the Attorney General's fiat dated 22nd November, 1883—I got a warrant at Bow Street, and received information about the prisoner, and sent the inspector to Australia in December—the prisoner was brought back to England, and I gave evidence at Bow Street, and he was committed for trial.
