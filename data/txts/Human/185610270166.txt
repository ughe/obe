   Cross-examined by MR. METCALFE . Q.  Is Hughes's shop a little distance from the butcher's? A.  Just across the road, a very little slanting—there were a good many people in the butcher's; it was Saturday night, at 7 or 8 o'clock—she was at the side of me; that was the reason that I accused her—she followed close behind me from Mr. Hughes's—I was ten minutes in the butcher's shop, during which time people came in and out, but they had not to pass where I stood—I saw Rebecca Cooper directly I went out, and I saw the prisoner then; I could see her all the time—nobody would help me—I asked Mr. Bland, but he said that he could not leave his busi ness—the money was four half crowns, six shillings, two sixpences, and a farthing—the prisoner heard what I said to Mr. Bland, and ran out directly—I charged her with robbing me before she ran. REBECCA COOPER. I am the wife of William Cooper, of Queen-street, Woolwich. I was standing outside Mr. Hughes's door, waiting for my laughter, and saw the prisoner come across the road in a hurried manner, as if she had been doing something wrong—she did not run, but she hurried—she went to the middle of Mr. Hughes's shop, with a shawl over her arm—Mrs. Wane came in, and said, "You have robbed me of all my money, 17 s . and a farthing"—the prisoner said nothing—I said, "Hold her fast; do not let her go"—she turned round and said, "I am not afraid; I can stand to my tack "—I afterwards saw her in custody.
   Cross-examined. Q.  When she was first charged, did not she say, "Go along with you, I have not robbed you"? A.  Yes—it is a very busy place, like a market place. WILLIAM WILKINSON. ( policeman, R  237). On Saturday evening, 11th Oct., the prisoner was given into my custody by Mrs. Wane—she denied the charge—I asked her to produce her pocket, and found in it 6 1/2 d . in copper, and a yellow return ticket to London from Woolwich, of that date, Oct. 11th—she was searched at the station, and nothing was found upon her—I asked her what she came to Woolwich for—she said, "To see a friend"—I asked her if she could find him—she said that she did not know where—I asked where he worked—she said, "At some stonemason's yard"—she did not give his name—Woolwich is much more crowded than London streets on Saturday nights—she gave her address in Leman-court, Leman-street, Whitechapel—I went there, and she had lived there last May.
   JURY . to REBECCA COOPER . Q.  How long elapsed from the time of its being taken and the prisoner being given in charge? A.  It was more than ten minutes before we could get a policeman—we held her all the time. NOT GUILTY.

   MR. O'CONNELL . conducted the Prosecution. CHARLES PENTON. I live at Sandy-hill, Plumstead.
