 979.  ANN BUNKER was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of April , 2 pairs of leather soles, value 2s., and 3 pairs of half-soles, value 2s. 6d., the goods of William Thomas Binckes.
   WILLIAM THOMAS BINCKES. I am a leather-seller, and live in Great Newport-street, Soho. On the night of the 5th of April the prisoner came into my shop, a little after eight o'clock, and asked to look at some grained leather, which I put on the counter; I then left the shop, and went into a private passage, from whence I could watch her - I saw her put four pieces of leather into her pocket; I sent my girl for an officer to take her as she went from the shop - he took her; she dropped two pieces of leather, and then another piece; he then searched her, and found three pairs of soles on her - I have known her four or five years; her husband is always laid up sick, and she has three children.
   GREGORY COLE DUDLEY. I am a Police-officer. On the night of the 5th of April I took the prisoner about six yards from the prosecutor's door - she dropped two pieces of leather from under her cloak; the prosecutor took them up - I took her farther, and she dropped another piece of sole leather - I then took her into a door-way, and examined her; I found these three pieces of leather wrapped up in her gown.
 Prisoner. I took it in a mistake.
     GUILTY. Aged 42. - Confined Three Months.

 981.  JAMES ASHTON was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of March , 1 bonnet, value 12s., the goods of Thomas Cox.
   SARAH COX. I am the wife of Thomas Cox, a labourer- we live in Golden-lane. I had a bonnet in a cupboard in one of my rooms on the 21st of March - it was safe about eleven o'clock, and I missed it between eleven and twelve o'clock - this is it; I saw Elizabeth Bulmer with it in her hand last Monday week.
   ELIZABETH BULMER. I live in Milton-street, and am the wife of John Bulmer, a bricklayer. On the 21st of March I was in Chapel-street, and saw the prisoner there, between twelve and one o'clock - it is a good step from the prosecutor's; he asked me to buy a bonnet - I said I did not want one; he asked where I lived - I showed him over the way; he came to my house, and brought this bonnet - he asked me 4s. for it; I agreed to give him 3s. - I paid him 1s. 6d. then, and 1s. 6d. the next day; this is the bonnet - I gave it to the officer.
   JOHN COHEN. I am Bulmer's brother-in-law. I saw this bargaining about the bonnet when I went home to dinner - the prisoner was sitting in a chair in my room, with it in his hand; and while I was eating my dinner, in came my sister, and bought it of him for 3s.
   WILLIAM BALLARD. I am a Police-officer. I received the bonnet from Bulmer - she had it on her head, and the prosecutor claimed it.
   JOHN SMITH. I am an officer. I took the prisoner, in Golden-lane, where his father keeps a shop - he mentioned the name of some person who he bought it of, but we could not find him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I met a young man I knew that day, and he asked me if I knew any lady who wanted to buy a bonnet - I sold it for him.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Confined Six Months.
 982.  MARIA NEEDHAM was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of March , 8 shirts, value 30s.; 2 sheets, value 4s.; 2 table-cloths, value 5s., and 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of James Peckett ; and 1 gown, value 2s.; 1 shift, value 1s. 6d., and 1 night-gown, value 1s., the goods of Mary Jenkins.
   THOMAS HOBBS KING. I am a Police-officer. On the night of the 29th of March I stopped the prisoner in Maiden-lane, Islington, at half-past nine o'clock - I saw her before I stopped her, in company with a young man, who had this bundle under his arm; he gave it to the prisoner, and they separated - she came on, and he staid in the road; they had not seen me or Harris, who was with me - the prisoner then came on, and as soon as she got sight of me, she dropped the bundle; the young man ran across the fields - this is the bundle, it contains the articles stated; they were wet when I took them.
   ALFRED HARRIS. I am a Police-constable. I was with King, and took up the bundle - the prisoner said she knew nothing of it, and that she was one hundred yards from it, but she was not more than five yards from it - she said she knew nothing of the young man; but the next morning she said she was in company with him, and had some conversation with him about some money for some shoes - he goes by the name of Daddy Lee - that was the name she expressed.
   JAMES PECKETT. I live in White Conduit-grove, Islington. This bundle contains eight shirts belonging to me, two table-cloths, and the other articles stated - they are all mine, except what are my servants', Mary Jenkins; here is a gown, a shift, and night-gown of hers - they were all hanging in the yard behind my house on the evening of the 29th of March, and were missed at a quarter before nine o'clock - I know nothing of the prisoner.
 T. H. KING re-examined. Q. How far from the prosecutor's did you stop the prisoner? A.About three quarters of a mile - it was about half-past nine o'clock.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was coming down Maiden-lane, and a young man asked me to hold a bundle - directly I took it he ran away.
 T. H. KING. No, they were talking together, and she walked ten or fifteen yards before she caught sight of us; there were three more she impeached - the man has absconded ever since.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
