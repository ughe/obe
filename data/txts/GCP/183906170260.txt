HENRY LEOPARD. I live at Eltham. I was going there from Wolwich-
common, on the night of the 6th of June last year-I remember seeing Mr.
Watson pass me- I was then about three hundred yards from the Dover-
road, but I cannot say exactly-I crossed the Dover-road and entered Well-
hall-lane-after I entered the lane a little distance, I met the witness Nor-
ton-he was in uniform-I have known him from his infancy-he was
going in the direction of Woolwich-he was walking at a steady pace
- he was alone-he said, "Good night," and I bid him the same-We
called each other by name- I had got four or five hundred yards or more
up the lane when I met him-I described to the constable the place where
I met him, and he had the ground measured-Norton would not have an
opportunity of seeing me coming some time before we met-as the road is
zig-zag, winding-when I got further up the lane I got to Mr. Watson-I
had not seen any other person in the interval-I found him hurt very much
and assisted him to Eltham-I spoke of having seen Norton-his father
and all his relations live at Eltham-I had left Eltham that day, and was
returning home.
JAMES WATTs. I am high constable of Woolwich. The witnesses pointed
out the places to me which they speak of-I measured the distances, and
they are accurately stated in this plan produced)-I first saw Norton the
morning after the robbery-he made a communication to me on the subject
-in consequence of which the prisoner was taken into custody a few days
afterwards-he was not in the barracks that day-I was present before Sir
Thomas Matthew Wilson and Sir John Webb at the examination--he made
a statement before them, which was taken down in writing-I saw him put
his mark to it-looking at it-this is the hand-writing of the two Magis-
trates-he called a witness named Thomas Baker, who also made a state-
ment-this is it-I cannot swear whether it was made in Perceval's pre-
sence or not, but he was called as a witness for Perceval-I cannot
charge my memory whether he was present, because at one period the wit-
nesses were out of the room.
Cross-examined. Q. Were nut other witnesses called by Perceval be-
sides Baker? A. There were witnesses called, but whether by Perceval
I cannot say I know several were called, and examined one by one out
of the room-I do not recollect that Perceval called other witnesses besides
Baker-I was present in December last when Baker was convicted of this
offence he was the man who was examined at the office.
CHARLES STEWART WARDEN. I was present when Baker was called
as a witness before the Magistrate when Perceval was charged with this
offence-he was examined in Perceval's presence.
