 1802.  WILLIAM COLSON was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of August , 1 tin box, value 3d.; I sovereign, and 1 half-crown, the property of Joseph Dorrell , from his person.
   JOSEPH DORRELL. I am a waggoner. I had a tin box with one sovereign and one half-crown in it, in my left hand pocket, on the 25th of August; I was coming down Chiswell-street; the prisoner said to me,
 "Joe, do you drive the Chesham waggon?" I said Yes - he asked if I was going down to it; I said Yes - he said he was going that way, and as we were going along I lost the box and money from my pocket; I did not see it taken, but the officer asked me if I had lost any thing - I said "Yes, a box;" I suppose the prisoner walked half a mile with me- I had it safe while he was with me: I had been to Mr. Chapple's, and paid him four sovereigns about a quarter of an hour before - the officer showed me the box; I said it was mine - the prisoner was close to me and another boy; the officer took them both.
   JOSEPH NEWSOM. I am an officer. I saw the prisoner and another boy, one on each side the prosecutor, leading him along - I saw the prisoner put his hand into his pocket several times; I followed them to Barbican - they separated from him; I went, and took them both - the prisoner dropped this box at my feet; there was some tobacco in it, but nothing else - the prosecutor was drunk- he said he had lost his box, and at the office he said there was a sovereign and a half-crown in it.
 (Box produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. The officer came and took me, and said I dropped the box, which I did not.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1803.  WILLIAM LEWIS was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 5s., the goods of Robert Howson , from his person.
   ROBERT HOWSON. On the 9th of August, about nine o'clock in the evening, I was at the top of Cow-lane with a female; I felt my handkerchief go from my coat pocket - I turned, and saw the prisoner give it to another; I followed them. but the other got off - I followed the prisoner to Fleet-market - he began to run; I called Stop thief! and he was secured - I have no doubt he saw me; he stopped himself when I cried Stop thief! I am sure he gave the handkerchief to the other - I was quite sober.
 Prisoner. Q. Where did you take me into custody? A. In Fleet-market; I did not take the other because I wished to secure you both if possible; you attempted to get away, but could not.
   JAMES SLATE. I am a constable. The prisoner was brought into the watch-house a little after nine o'clock - he had nothing on him but a little iron screw-driver and a handkerchief, which he said was his own.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in  Cow-lane, and saw this gentleman take notice of me - I went on my way down to Fleet-market; I heard some person cry Stop thief! or Stop him! I stopped, and went back towards the prosecutor - I took out my own handkerchief while I was passing the gentleman, and wiped my face with it.
   ROBERT HOWSON. My handkerchief was a red one, and this is green - I did not see the prisoner take it out, but I saw him with it; he gave it to the other, who turned off to the right - when I got to the turning, the other was out of sight; I am sure it was my property - I had used it a few minutes before; it was a fine night, and just twilight.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined Six Months.
 1804.  GEORGE DASH was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of August , 1 pair of trousers, value 17s. 6d., the goods of Edward Philips Doudney and another.
   GEORGE DAVIS DOUDNEY. I am the son of Edward Philips Doudney; he is in partnership with my brother, and they are tailor s. On the 24th of August I was in the shop, and heard a noise - I went to the door, and saw the prisoner running up the court, tucking up something before him; I called Stop thief! and pursued - I lost sight of him when he turned, but when I turned I saw the same person running; I saw him throw the trousers down and stooped to get them, but I could not - I called Stop thief! and he ran into the officer's arms; the trousers were picked up and brought to my father's shop - I saw them in the shop not two minutes before; I have known the prisoner from a child, and know his mother.
   JOHN BARNLEY. I am an officer. I stopped the prisoner going into the George and Vulture in George-yard- he was running, and people after him crying Stop thief!(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. It rained very fast, and I was running - I never had the trousers.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Confined Two Months.
