2088. WILLIAM HARRINGTON was indicted for stealing, on the
19th of August, 1 shawl, value 18.1 and 1 apron, value bd.; the goods of
Jonathan Diaper.--2nd Count, stating them to be the goods of Ann Diaper.
Ann DIAPER. I am the wife of Jonathan Diaper, and live in Little
Ormond-yard. I am a tailoress-- I lost this property on Saturday, out of
a box up three pair of stairs -- the prisoner lived with his parents next door
to us the street door was kept open,
Joux Boyce. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Theobald's-road. I
produce a shawl and apron, which were pawned by Pigeon, on Friday,
the 19th of August.
Joseph Pigeon. I have known the prisoner six or seven years-
pawned this shawl and apron for him, on the day he was taken, at Mr.
Boyce's, and gave him the money.
EDWARD RAMSHIRE. I am a policeman. I apprehended the prisoner
on this charge, and he said he did it to get something to drink,
(Property produced and sworn to.)
Prisoner's Defence. I was very much intoxicated that day I went to
the privy and saw the shawl there-I brought it to Pigeon, and asked him
to go and pawn it--he spent the money in drink-I did not receive a half-
penny of it.
