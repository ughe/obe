THE
 WHOLE PROCEEDINGS
 On the Queen's Commission of
 OYER AND TERMINER AND GAOL DELIVERY
 FOR
 The City of London,
 AND GAOL DELIVERY FOR THE
 COUNTY OF MIDDLESEX, AND THE PARTS OF THE COUNTIES OF ESSEX,KENT, AND SURREY WITHIN THE JURISDICTION
 OF THE
 CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT,

   BEFORE THE RIGHT HON. WILLIAM FERNELEY ALLEN , LORD MAYOR of the City of London; Sir WILLIAM SHEE, Knt., one of the Justices of Her Majesty's Court of Queen's Bench; SIR EDWARD MONTAGU SMITH, Knt., one of the Justices of Her Majesty's Court of Common Pleas; THOMAS SIDNEY, Esq., Sir ROBERT WALTER CARDEN, Knt., WARREN STORMES HALE, Esq., Aldermen of the said City; the Right Hon. DAVID HENRY STONE, Esq., Alderman WILLIAM MCARTHUR, Esq.
