834. MICHAEL PINE was indicted for feloniously breaking and enter-
ing the dwelling-house of Thomas Atkins, on the 22nd of January, at
Lambeth, and stealing therein 1 coat, value 30s. ; 1 sheet, value 38.; 1
blanket, value 38.; 2 pillow-cases, value 4s. ; 1 counterpane, value 38.;
and 1 petticoat, value 2s. ; his property.
CATHERINE Atkins. I am the wife of Thomas Atkins, a lighterman,
and live in John-street, Commercial-road, in the parish of Lambeth-we
occupy the whole house. On Saturday, the 22nd of January, I went out
about half-past five o'clock in the afternoon, leaving my little girl, twelve
years old, down in the kitchen-I locked the parlour-door, and left the
key with her in the kitchen-I shut the street-door, and put the shutters
to, but did not fasten them-I came back about six, and found the shut-
ters open, and the bottom sash of the window thrown right up, so as to
enable any one to get in-it was shut down when I had gone out-1
knocked, and my little girl came to the door-I went in and missed the
articles stated-I saw them again about nine the same evening, at Tower-
street station--they are worth about 21. 58.
JASPER MALLION (police-constable L 21.) On the evening of the 22nd
of January, about a quarter to six o'clock, I saw the prisoner in Cornwall-
road Lambeth, about a hundred yards from the prosecutor's house,
and coming in a direction from it-he had a bundle with him I was about
crossing over to him-he saw I was making up to him, and dropped the
bundle, and walked on-I took him and the bundle-it contained this
blanket, sheet, and counterpane, which I produce.
Prisoner. I had not got them--he took me about a hundred yards
away from the things, which were lying on the ground. Witness. No; not
above twelve yards-there was a female standing near him by a gate, but
I saw him for seven or eight yards before I crossed over- I was on the
opposite side of the way, and suspecting him, made towards him, and he
dropped the bundle by his side, close by the gateway of a doctor's shop
he did not attempt to run away - I went and took him, and then returned
and got the bundle.
Prisoner. He asked the woman if I had left them there, and she said I
did not. Witness. I said to the woman, “You saw him drop these, of
course," but she made no answer-I distinctly saw the bundle drop from
the prisoner.
