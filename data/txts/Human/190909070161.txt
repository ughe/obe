Mr. Travers Humphreys submitted that there was no case to go to the Jury. There was-no evidence before the Court as to the cir  cumstances of the sale, except that of the defendant himself. No case had been made out of conspiracy between the transferor and trans feree, such as would bring it within the statute 13 Eliz., c. 5, s. 2, nor was there the evidence of intent to defraud, which was necessary in an offence under the Debtors Act, 1869, Section 13.
 After hearing Mr. Bigham, in reply,
 The Recorder said that, though there were circumstances of some option, there was really no evidence of intent to defraud, and he accordingly directed the Jury to return a verdict of Not guilty.
