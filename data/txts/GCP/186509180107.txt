RICHARD STEVENS (Policeman, M 66). On Saturday morning, 26th or 27th

which appeared to come from Counter-street-before I got there, I was met
by a woman named Larson, who was afterwards taken in custody- I spoke to
ber-she said something and pointed towards London Bridge, which was an
opposite direction-I then heard groans, and saw a man in a light coat and
the two prisoners run out of Counter-street-a man in a dark coat ran into

Blackfriars-road-he ran past the boarding, where the cap was afterwards
