MICHAEL PEISER. I am a Prussian-I live in Little Alie Street,
Goodman's Fields I have been in England about twenty years, keeping
an eating-house-I have known the prisoner about twelve years—about
April last I called at his house in Leman Street; there was a young man
lodging with the prisoner, and he told me that this young man had brought
over 20,000 guilders to buy goods- I asked him what goods-he said, "I
will tell you bye and bye what sort of goods, I will show you "-he took
me into a little room, and said, “Come in, I will show you what goods the
gentleman wants"-he took me into the little room privately, and brought
me in a twenty-five rouble piece-I said, “What do you mean by that?" -
he said, “These are the goods that the gentleman wants," and that he
brought the money to buy "-I asked him how much was one like that,
how much did he charge for it-he said, “Half a crown a rouble"-
rouble is about 28.9d. in English money– I said, "Is that the value of it?"
- he said, "Never mind the price ; if you only get a good customer we
shall make a bargain about it"-I said I would see about it.
