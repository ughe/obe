He had induced his brother-in-Law, Mr. Black, to advance close upon £5, 000 upon various securities which prisoner had forged, and, under the pretence that the money was invested on Mr. Rose's authority, prisoner representing that his brother-in-law was a client of the firm. By this means Mr. Black (a clergyman) had lost his entire fortune.
 Sentence, Five years' penal servitude.
