I was at the Three Cups, and saw the prisoner standing in the bar—I walked by him—Mr. Yates came in—the policeman came in, and he gave the prisoner in charge for passing bad money—the prisoner wrung his hands, and said so help hit God he had no bad money about him—Pickering searched him in my pre sence—I stood close to his elbow, and as I was going home, I found a bad shilling in my right-hand jacket pocket—it was not in my pocket when I left home in the morning—it was a white drayman's jacket—I had only 1 d . about me, and that I had spent—the shilling was not out of my sight till it was given to Seaman, the constable.
   Cross-examined. Q.  you went into the White Bear? A.  Yes—I did not give that shilling to the landlord—I pulled it out—my mate took it in his hand, and showed it to the landlord, but it was never out of my sight—my mate is not here—nor is the landlord—the landlord gave it to Seaman in my presence, and I marked it.
