   MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Was Shillitoe an acquaintance of Mr. Ireland's? A.  I do not know—I was taken to Marylebone-office, and then to Bow-street—the prosecutor there said that I and the prisoner went away together—Mr. Minshull said, "When you were here before, you sware he went away alone, and now you say they went away together;" and he discharged me—I have staid late at the prosecutor's house before I played there.
   COURT . Q.  Then on this night, whether October, November, or Septem ber, there was no quarrel or dispute, or any going away of the prisoner? A.  No—he went away about two o'clock—I had been in the habit of going to the house—the amount of the stake was generally 10 s . on every odd trick—there was a bet of 10 l . between the prisoner and the prose cutor—that was the first bet—then was no other of 10 l .—I did not bet—I stuck to my 10 s  —the prisoner is a common carrier; he keeps a cart and horse—it was about half-past ten or eleven o'clock—the bet was made, and they played two or three hours after that; I should think, eight or ten games—the prisoner sometimes betted a sovereign with the prosecutor.
   JURY . Q.  You had no precise sum playing for? A.  No—there was a pot of ale a game—there were ten pots of ale drank, or mostly so—the prisoner went out a little before me—I lost about 2 l ., but I betted on the trick—Ireland plays at cards, and all games. JOSEPH SHACKLE. I am an officer of Bow-street. I was present when Ireland was first examined on this charge, and I was there on the second examination—on that occasion the Magistrate remonstrated with the prosecutor on bringing him (Grinham) forward, and swearing that he and the prisoner went away together; when, on the first examination, he swore he went away alone; and he discharged Grinham.
   MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Was the prisoner asked what he had to say to the charge? A.  He was; and he said they were playing a game at whist and he won the money of the man.
 (Thomas Egleton, a tailor, of Lawrence-street, Chelsea; Edward Col  ton, a cow-keeper, of Sloane-square; James Edward White, a publican, of London-wall; William Bignal, a coach-master, Tothill-street; and Luke Thomas Flood, Esq., a Magistrate of Middlesex; gave the prisoner a good character.) GUILTY  . Aged 34.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury. Confined Six Months.
     ADJOURNED TILL THE FIFTEENTH OF DECEMBER.
