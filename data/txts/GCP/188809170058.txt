GEORGE BASLEMAN. I am a wholesale provision merchant at Great
Tower Street-I have known Shuttleworth some years; he has pur.

of this year I should say I sold him 200 cases of eggs at from 68. to 12.
---some were damaged and cracked and a good many good-I should

depends on the time of year and how much they are damaged-some-
times he looked at them, and sometimes took them on my word, and I
always found him straightforward-I do not think it would be unusual
for damaged eggs to be taken in a cart to be sold.
CHARLES Bown. I represent Messrs. Barker and Co., of Mark Lane
- it is not unusual to find men going round in carts to sell damaged eggs
- a guinea for a half case of slightly damaged eggs is very fair value-
I could not tell unless I saw the eggs.

trade is in damaged eggs, I have sold him other goods—I should sell
him damaged eggs at 68. and 108., it depends on conditions—108. a half
case would be fair value, taking the average of the eggs he dealt in-
if their condition was very bad they would not be worth so much.
