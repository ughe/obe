   Smith's Defence.  Do you think it is likely if a gentleman lost his watch and chain he would not go and give information to the police?
   Brookes Defence.  The two women stated that it was a gentleman in a light dress-suit; then why did they direct the constable to a gentleman in a different suit? GUILTY.
