ARTHUR, Charles (31, labourer), was indicted for (1) feloniously
shooting at George Haytread with intent to murder him or to do him
some grievous bodily harm; (2) feloniously attempting to discharge
a loaded revolver at Haytread, with intent to murder him or to do
him some grievous bodily harm, or with intent to disable him, or with
intent to prevent lawful apprehension; (3) attempting to burglariously
break and enter the dwelling house of William Sumpton and to steal
his goods in the same dwelling house ; (4) similar offence,“ being
armed with a dangerous offensive weapon”; (5) being found by night
having in his possession without lawful excuse certain implements of
housebreaking ; (6) assaulting George Haytread.
Prisoner pleaded guilty to (3) and (5), not guilty on the other
charges. He was tried on the first indictment.
Mr. Bodkin, Mr. Leycester, and Mr. Montague Shearman, junior,
prosecuted.
