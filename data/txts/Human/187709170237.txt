   Witnesses for the Defence. ARTHUR JOHNSON. I live at 14, Treherne Road, Brixton, and am the defendant's grandson; I live with her—I remember her being ill about the beginning of August—I was called at the police-court and gave evidence that she was ill on Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday—Sunday was the 5th, so Friday would be 10th—she was confined to her bed; I was in and out of the room during the day—she did not get up—our place of abode then was 1, Orchard Row, Camberwell New Road—14, Treherne Road is five minutes' walk from Orchard Row—she was very ill.
   Cross-examined.  I am twelve years old—I have always been quite sure that Sunday was 5th August—I did not say at the Lambeth police-court my grandmother was taken ill about Tuesday, 5th August—my grandmother visited 14, Treherne Road, before she went to live there—I saw Miss Watson write once before for my grandmother to a Mr. Sharland, our broker—I remember the Sunday, Monday, Tuesday Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday, because we moved into Orchard Row on Saturday, the 4th—I have had no conversation with my grandmother as to the evidence I should give—she never reminded me that she was ill at that time—we have talked about her being locked up—she told me to come up.
   Re-examined.  We were at Orchard Row about three weeks, and moved in on the Saturday—the day after my grandmother was taken ill, and she remained there up to Friday in the following week—I gave this evidence at the police-court—my grandmother told me It was the 5th August.
   By THE COURT . I go to school between 9 o'clock and 9.30—I did not go to school on the days in question, because we had just moved in and we could not find a school. MATILDA HARBISON. I am a married women and live at 1, Orchard Row—I remember going to the defendant on 4th. August; she had just moved in to 1, Orchard Row—I did not see her when she was ill in bed. ADELAIDE SILCOCK. I live at Victoria Road, Peckham, the defendant is my aunt—I remember visiting her at 1, Orchard Row, on 8th and 9th August—I found her bad a-bed, she appeared to be very ill and unable to get up—she said she had been in great pain since Sunday night—I do not know anything of Mary Ann Glyun—I have never written letters, for my aunt; I can write—Arthur Johnson is a good scholar.
   Cross-examined.  I was not with my aunt all day on 8th August, but I was on the 9 th—I remember the days so well because bank-holiday was on the 6th—it was a slack week, that makes me remember it.
