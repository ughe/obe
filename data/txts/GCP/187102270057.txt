get up stairs ? A. The house is double ; the front door leads into a pas-

could get from the bar to the passage ; but they had no business to do so
--they had no right up stairs.
Mn. STARLING. Q. Were they in the billiard room A. I do not know,
I was in the bar.
Susan RUMBALL. I am a widow, and keep this house, 200, Walworth
Road-on 6th February I left Mrs. Soyer in charge of the house, about
7.20—I locked my bedroom, and took the key in my pocket-I returned
about 11 o'clock, and found my door open-I cannot tell whether it had
been picked; but I can look it now the same as before.
Cross-examined. I know the prisoners as customers, and have employed
them to do a job-I had not employed them that day-I saw John at
the station-I did not observe that he had been drinking-I never go out
without locking my door.
John DOLLING (Policeman P R 13). On 6th February John Tossell

was not up to much good."
