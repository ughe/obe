 1752. MARGARET M'CARTHY was indicted for stealing 9 yards of printed cotton, value 4 s  ., the goods of Joseph Lawrence . FREDERICK MOSS. I am shopman to Joseph Lawrence, a linen-draper, and live in Crosby-row, Walworth. On the morning of the 4th of Aug., about eleven o'clock, I saw the prisoner look through the shop-window—I went to the door two minutes afterwards, and missed some cotton from off the box in the lobby—I had seen it safe five minutes before—I went after the prisoner, who was going towards the Elephant and Castle, walking fast—I overtook her about eight yards off—I asked her to let me look under her shawl—I there found this piece of printed cotton—it is Mr. Lawrence's—this ticket was pinned on it—she said, "Let me go, I have a young child at home"
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Where was this print? A.  On a box in the lobby—a person must have trod into the shop to have taken it RICHARD DAVIS( police-constable P  55.) I took the prisoner—I told her she was charged with stealing a piece of print—she said she had nothing to say to it
   Cross-examined. Q.  What did you find on her? A.  A pair of scissors and a book, nothing of value—she was very poor.
 (The prisoner received a good character and, her mother engaged to pro  vide for her.) GUILTY. Aged 17.— Recommended to mercy.— Confined Fourteen Day .
   Before Mr. Recorder.
