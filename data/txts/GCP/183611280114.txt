93. JOHN M'CORMACK was indicted for embezzlement."
GEORGE WILLOUGHBY. I live in Red Lion-street, Spitalfields. On Wed-
nesday, the 23rd of November, the prisoner came and offered himself as a
servant, and I took him into my service to carry out coals when ordered
between twelve and one o'clock on the same day I sent him with a half
cwt. of coals to Mrs. Matthews, in Grey Eagle-street- there was a lad
waiting for him-I desired him to bring 9 d. back, but I never saw him
again till he was brought to me in custody-he had only come to me that
morning-he was brought to me by a servant of mine-I asked what be
had done with the money for the coals ?-he said he had spent it, and had
thrown the coal-bag into the railing of the brewery--I gave him into cus-
tody- have never received the 9/d.
GEORGE WILLIAM MATTHEWs. I am twelve years old. The prisoner
came to me, on the 23rd of November, with the coals-he asked my mother
for the money, and she paid him 6d. and 3d. in my presence-he said
there was another halfpenny, and she gave it him he went away with it.
ROBERT BRITHOUSE. I am a policeman. I received him in charge,
and asked him how he came to do this ?--he said he did not know, but
he had spent the money.
GUILTY. Aged 14.--Transported for Seven Years.
