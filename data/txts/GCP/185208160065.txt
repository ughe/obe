CALEB GROVES (City-policeman, 248). I took the prisoner on Holborn-
hill on 10th Aug.- I took him to the station and searched him-I found on
him some earrings, which a person claimed at the Mansion House who bad
given them to him to mend - I asked the prisoner what he was-he said he
was a working goldsmith - I told him the charge he said there were some
mistakes about it.
WILLIAM Booth. I am in the service of Mr. Smily, and was so when the

put on the work-he came up out of his shop into my shop and asked for a
