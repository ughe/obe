 402. ROBERT ANDREWS was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of December , 1 coat, value 6 s  .; 1 waistcoat, value 4 s  .; and 1 pair of trow sers, value 8 s  .; the goods of James Bateman . JAMES BATEMAN. I live at the King's Arms, Elephant-street, Rother hithe; the prisoner lodged in the same house. On Saturday morning I missed my waistcoat and trowsers—I spoke to the prisoner about it on Saturday evening—he said he had taken them, and pawned them—I never permitted him to do so—he said he would return them at some future time—I found these clothes at the pawnbroker's—they are mine.
   Prisoner. Q.  If you thought I did not mean to return them, why did you not give me in charge when the coat went four or five weeks before? A.  There was a blue coat taken before, which I forgave him for.
   JURY . Q.  Have you been on intimate terms with him? A.  Yes—we have not associated together—we did not sleep together. JOHN WILLIAM COPE. I am a pawnbroker—I produce the coat, waist-coat, and trowsers, which were pawned by the prisoner.
   Prisoner's Defence . I told the prosecutor I intended to bring them back in a quarter of an hour. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined One Month.

 403. THOMAS NICHOLSON was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of December , 1 waistcoat, value 5 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Edward Groves . JAMES JONES. I am salesman to Edward Groves, in the New Cut, Lam beth. On the 12th of December this waistcoat was outside the shop between eleven and twelve o'clock—I watched the prisoner the whole day—I saw him take it from the front, and put it into his hat—he came to wards my shop—he saw me looking at him, and turned back—I collared him—he took off his hat, and threw the waistcoat down an area. JOSEPH TAYLOR( police-constable L  143.) I saw Jones with the prisoner on some steps which joined the area where this waistcoat was.
   Prisoner's Defence . Jones never saw me at all—I had not got this waistcoat. GUILTY .* Aged 21.— Transported for Seven Years.
 404. THOMAS JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of December , 1 waistcoat, value 4 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Edward Groves . JAMES JONES. I am shopman to Edward Groves. About nine o'clock on the evening of the 14th of December I missed a waistcoat from the front of my shop—it is not here—the other thief got away with it. RICHARD STAFFORD ROLFE. I am salesman to Charles Padden, of Lambeth-walk. On the 14th I was at my door—I saw the prisoner take a waistcoat from the front of Mr. Groves's shop, and pass it to another young man, who made his escape—he had made two attempts at my shop—I told him to go away, and he put his band up to his nose—I am sure he is the person.
   Prisoner's Defence . I did not take it. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Four Months.
