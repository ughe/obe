ALEXANDER SARTY. I am a clothes-salesman, and live in Monmouth-street. My son Henry is turned four years old—I saw these clothes safe on the child about half-past one o'clock, on the 31st of August—I went into Evans's shop, and found the child being dressed in the clothes produced—they are mine. ROBERT EVANS. From what my wife told me, at half-past twelve o'clock on the 31st of August, I went to the door, and saw the child naked—my wife pointed out the prisoner as the person who dropped the cloths—I went and took her into a passage. SARAH EVANS. I am the wife of Robert Evans. I was standing at the door, and saw the prisoner had stripped the child in a water-closet—I saw the witness take the clothes partly from her lap, and the others she dropped—I called my husband—he took her, and kept her till we gave her into custody. JOHN GREEN. I saw the prisoner come to the little boy—she said, "I will take you up to the station"—she said, "He don't know his aunt; come with me, I will give you a halfpenny." CHARLES CONNOR. I saw the prisoner come out of the water-closet with something in her apron—the child came out naked, crying after her—I followed her—she dropped part of the clothes into my hand.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was coming down the street; there were two women; one of them dropped the bundle; I called her to take the things. Connor came and accused me of taking them. When I came back the child was dressed in its things. I did not know whose they were. GUILTY. Aged 26.— Confined Six Months.
