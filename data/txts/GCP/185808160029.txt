JAMES CRAMP (Policeman, G 240). On that day, the prisoner and another
man were given into my custody, charged with uttering & counterfeit half-
crown—the prisoner said he neither knew the prosecutor nor yet the house.
-I searched him at the station and found on him a good half-crown, one
shilling, two sixpences, and 3d. in ooppers-the half-crown was not bent at
all. I took the two men before the magistrate they were remanded till
Monday, and discharged, this being the only case against them. The prisoner
gave the name of Charles Elliott.
