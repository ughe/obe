PIRIE, Mayor.
Cross-examined. Q. What day was this? A. Thursday, the 7th of
April, at ten o'clock in the morning-I saw the female at Bow-street.
Court. Q. Did she pass by any name at Bow-street ? A. Yes, Mary
Good-it was the woman now in custody by that name-(looking at her).
GEORGE BLAND. I drive a cab, No. 430. On Thursday, the 7th of
April I saw the prisoner at the Spotted Dog, Strand- I drove him from
there down to No. 4, Flower and Dean-street, Spitalfields-he had a bed.
a box, and one or two bundles inside the cab-Molly Good was with him
- they went into the house.
WILLIAM PARDOE BIDDLE. I am assistant to Mr. Soames, a pawnbro-
ker. I saw the prisoner first on Thursday, the 7th of April-he came to
my shop first alone, and afterwards accompanied by a woman by the name of
Mary Good-I have known her by that name some time-he came into the
shop, inquired if we would take in some bed and bedding and a trunk a
pledges--we objected to it not knowing any thing of him--he went away
and returned with Mrs. Good- I had known her during the time I had
been with Mr. Soames, eleven months-she said in his presence, "It is all
right, Mr. Soames, you need not be afraid, it is my husband just come from
sea"-in about an hour after he brought a trunk, went away, and returned in
about ten minutes with the bedI went away to dinner, and returned in half-
an-hour-he was still there, and the trunk open with the things displayed
on the counter-he got 21. 108. on them-he said he had just come by rail-
way, and wanted to go into the country, which was the reason he wanted
to raise money, and that the goods belonged to a passenger who died on
his way home from India, and gave him all these things-he returned in
about ten minutes with a bundle of women's wearing apparel, and some
odd china--there was two mantillas, a white scarf, shawl, a black figured
shawl, and some odd cups and saucers—the police have some of the appa-
rel and I the rest, which I produce.
STEPHEN Ludlow. I am special constable of Tunbridge, Kent. On
the 16th of April, in consequence of information from a man named Rose, I
went to where I found the prisoner, and identified him I then went into the
town to the inspector, communicated with him, and went with Humphreys to
where the prisoner was—the inspector brought him away, I found a watch
on him and some money, and a woman's jackonet apron-there are spots of
blood on it he was taken into custody by Humphrey-he was dressed in a
pair of trowsers and a short fustian jacket, as a bricklayer-I did not see
him with a hod.
GUILTY. Aged 44.-DEATH.
