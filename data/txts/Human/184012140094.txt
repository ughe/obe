I apprehended the prisoner on board the ship Argo,  in Limehouse-hole—it is a Russian ship belonging to Warsaw—a man named Yanstron was on board who was also taken on this charge, and discharged—I got this knife, which I produce, out of the prisoner's chest on board the ship. MARTIN MEGGISON BULL. I am a student at the London Hospital—I was there on the 1st of December, when Brian was brought in—I ex amined his person and found an incised wound on the right side of his chest near the arm-pit, about two inches deep, and such as might be inflicted by a knife—I did not consider it very dangerous—it was deep enough to cut into the cavity of the chest—he is still a patient there, but is almost re covered—he is now out of danger—the knife produced would have inflicted the wound. GUILTY  , of an Assault only. Confined Six Months.
