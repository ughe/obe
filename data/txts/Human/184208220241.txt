2513. JOHN HUBBARD was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of August , 90lbs. weight of stone, value 1 s  ., the goods of Thomas Cubitt . JAMES JEFFREY. I live at Hendon-wharf, Pimlico, and am in the employ of Mr. Thomas Cubitt. About half-past eight o'clock in the morning of the 20th of August, I went into a vault where there was a great quantity of stones belonging to Mr. Cubitt—I saw the prisoner watching about—he went into the vault and took out this lump of hearth stone—he had it under his arm, and when he saw me he dropped it, and ran off.
   Prisoner's Defence . I went down; there was a lot of rubbish; I was kicking it over, and this man called, "Stop thief;" I was taken, and this stone was brought; I had not touched it. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months.
 2514. CHARLES CROCKILL was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of August , 2 razors, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Thomas Clater . THOMAS CLATER. I live at Chelsea; the prisoner was in the habit of coming to my shop. On the 10th of August I lost two razors in a red case—they had ivory handles, and the name "T. Clater" on them. JOHN WILD. I am a hair-dresser, and live in George-street, Chelsea. The prisoner brought two razors to me for sale—they had white handles, and "T. Clater" on them—he said he saw a gentleman drop them out of his pocket, and he took them up—I did not buy them—they have not been found. NOT GUILTY.
 2515. AMELIA CATES was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of August , 91/2 yards of mouselin de laine, value 14 s  ., the goods of Samuel Hughes and another. SAMUEL HUGHES. I am in partnership with James Dean; we are linen drapers, and live in White chapel-road; the prisoner was in the habit of coming to our shop. On the 18th of August I counted some pieces of mouselin de laine, and placed them on the counter, as I rather expected the prisoner would come—she came in about a quarter of an hour, and bought half a yard of calico—I then missed some mouselin de laine—I followed and brought her back—she dropped this piece on the floor, in the middle of the shop, from tinder her shawl.
   Prisoner . I did not have it at all. Witness . You had got out of the shop, two doors off—I brought you back myself, and saw you drop it.
   Prisoner's Defence . I said I had nothing; he opened my shawl, and there was nothing there; he said he had missed a gown-piece; it was be hind the counter, and he said I took it. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Six Months.
