   By the COURT . I think he said that somebody had told him that a strange man went to the place and bought them—nothing was said before that I think that brought up the subject—they had been found there—he said that somebody had told him that a strange man had been and fetched the fire lighters from the oil-shop, and I said "You fetched them"—I know that because Mrs. Locke had told me she was going to send for them—that was a week before the fire.
