1923. SARAH BATES was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of July,
llb. of bacon, value 6d.; and 6lb. of pork, value 38. 6d.; the goods of John
Hutley.
DET 2012
WILLIAM CHAMANT. I am in the service of John Hatley, a cheese-
monger, in Broad-street, Bloomsbury. On Saturday night, the 10th of
July, about ten o'clock, I saw the prisoner take a piece of bacon-I was
going to take her, when my fellow-servant saw her take two pieces of
pork, which she dropped at her feet, and we were obliged to push her
away to get them--we took her in the shop, and found the bacon in her
apron.
Prisoner. This piece of bacon my daughter bought, and gave it to me
in my apron. Witness. Her daughter came to my master, and said it
was hers, but I saw her take it.
CHARLES PULLING. I was outside the shop--my master had such a
piece of bacon as this -- I believe it to be his-1 saw the prisoner take the
pork, and she stood over it-I did not see her take the bacon.
GUILTY. Aged 48.Confined Three Months.N ACIO
