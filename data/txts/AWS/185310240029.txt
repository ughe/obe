COURT. Q. What sort of place is this where the prisoners were A. There
are six cells; three on each side-I was in the middle one of the three, be+
tween the cells in which the prisoners were-they are divided by a brick
partition, about ten feet high the brick partition extends up to the ceiling,
but not in front, there is a grating for the air to come through at the top of
the doors-outside there is one common passage to them all the officers are
going to and fro in that passage at times-I was about three quarters of an
hour in the cell between them--I was there for the purpose of bearing any
conversation.
Q. Is that the usual course at the Rochester-row police station A. I

get into a cell between them, and hear what I could-it was not the sugges.
