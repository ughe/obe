NEW COURT.- Wednesday, February 18, 1865.
Before Mr. Recorder.
255. WILLIAM LEWER (33), Stealing 1 piece of paper and 51. in
money, the property of John Waller.
JOHN WALTER. I live at Southampton-on 14th January I was in London
--the prisoner was fore-cabin steward on board the boat I came from Haar-
lem in--he promised to go with me to show me the way to the house of a
man who had taken my bag the day before-I went with him to Dulwich,
and afterwards came to London, and went in a cab with him to Thompson's
boarding-house-I had very foolishly given him my purse, through having
taken too much drink, and when I got to the boarding-house I found a 51
note was missing-I accused him of it-he denied it, and Mr. Thompson
sent for a policeman who searched him, and found it on him.
Prisoner. You gave me the purse and the papers to take care of; I did
not know there was a bl. note-when I gave him the purse he said, "Where
is the bl. note ? "-I said, “It is either in the purse or on my person." Wit-
ness. That is not true.
FREDERICK THOMPSON. I keep a boarding-house on Tower-bill-Walter
and the prisoner came to my house, and after they got into the bed-room
Walter accused the prisoner of having a 5l. note- the prisoner denied having
it-he said that if there had been a note in the purse it would be there then
-Walter still said that he had lost it—the cabman had to be paid, and a 58.
piece was tendered to him to pay it-be returned 18. 6d. - when I got down
stairs I saw the prisoner with 12s. or 148, a bandful of coin, which he took
out of his pocket-I saw the 51, note found, and heard the cabman remark
that he had made a fine day of it—the prisoner said, “Yes, he is a fore-cabin
passenger, and goes away to-morrow."
ADAM CARR (Policeman, H 40). I was called to take the prisoner-I
asked him if he knew anything about the note-he denied all knowledge
of it-I asked if he would have any objection to my searching himbe
said, “No”-I put my hand in his pocket and found a 51. note, and the
receipt for a registered letter, which the gentleman identified.
Prisoner. Those were the papers the gentleman gave me to take charge of.
The prisoner produced a written defence, stating that he accompanied the
Prosecutor in a cab to Dulwich, that he asked him to take charge of his money and
papers, that he did not know that there was a 5l, note there, and was given
in charge without having time to search for it: but that he said, “ If you gave
me a 57. note, it is either in the purse or on my person."
Court to JOHN WALTER. &Where was the 51, note? A. In the purse,
placed in the inner compartment, and although I was decidedly tipsy at the
time I gave him the purse, I was perfectly able to look after myself when
I asked him for the purse I found it was not there the receipt for the
registered letter was in my purse with the 5l. note. Guilty.
Recommended to mercy by the Jury on account of the temptation.-Confined
Three Months.
