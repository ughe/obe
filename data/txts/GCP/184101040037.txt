458, CAROLINE HUGHES was indicted for a misdemeanor.
PR MR. Doane conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM WHITE. I am shopman to Thomas Baldwin, a cheesemonger,
in New-street, Dorset-square. On the 19th of November, the prisoner
came for some pork chops-she gave me a half-crown, and after she was
gone I found it was bad-it was burnt-on the 28th she came again for
some cheese, which came to 5d.-she gave me a half-crown--I told her it
was bad-she said, it was not-I said, “You gave me one about a week
ago"-I gave the half-crown to Mr. Balding, and went for a policeman,
and she was taken to the station-Mr. Balding's father gave me the half-
crown back again I gave it to Sergeant Pitt.
Thomas BALDING. I received, on the 28th of November, a half-crown
from White-I gave it to my father.
Thomas BALDING, sen. I received the half-crown from my son, and
gave it to White.
John BIRD ( police-constable D 142.) I received charge of the pri-
soner on the 28th-I saw White deliver a half-crown to Pitt, which she
gave me -it did not go out of my sight.
JOHN SWETMAN. I am apprenticed to Mr. Dalton, a stationer, in
Cockspur-street. On the 15th of December, the prisoner came for two
sheets of foolscap paper, which came to 2d.-she gave me a half-crown-I
looked at it, and told her it was bad-she said she was not aware of it,
she took it in a shop in Oxford-street, but she could not say what shop-I
gave it to Mr. Dalton, and went for a constable.
WILLIAM HENRY DALTON. I received a half-crown from Swetman-
I did not lose sight of it till I gave it to Hollington.
Charles HOLLINGTON (police-constable 4 134.) I was fetched and
took the prisoner-I have the half-crown, which I got from Dalton—the
prisoner said she took it at a haberdasher's shop in Regent-street.
JOHN FIELD. These are both counterfeit, and both cast in one monld.
Prisoner's Defence. I took it in a shop in Oxford-street.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Six Months. soos
