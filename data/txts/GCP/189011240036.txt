describe the difference in the star in the genuine credits the “O's” are
always in a straight line-this is not exactly like the "0"in the original,
it appears thinner at the top, where it is divided—the genuine letters of
credit are printed in New York-no letter of credit would be paid if the
list of correspondents became detached.
Re-examined. I have examined the signatures carefully, and I say
they are forgeries.
