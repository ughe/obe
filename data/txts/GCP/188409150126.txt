woman was lying on the floor- I saw that her throat was cut-I lifted
her up-she said, "My husband has cut my throat"-he said, “She did
it herself”--the wife said then, “ You did it" - Dr. Slater afterwards
came and attended to ber-I searched the room, and under the table

doctor came, and the woman was taken to the German Hospital -- I took
