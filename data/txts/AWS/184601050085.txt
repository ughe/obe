418. JEREMIAH GRAHAM was indicted for stealing 2 carpets, value
21., the goods of Robert Dennison and that he had been before convicted of
felony.
ROBERT DENNISON. I am sub-steward of Gray's Inn-on the 29th of
Dec., about two o'clock, some person had the keys of a set of chambers there,
which are mine, and were to be let-about five 'clock that day I went to my
office, and there I ascertained there had been a robbery- went to the station
and saw these carpets which had been taken from my chambers.
WILLIAM HOLDBB (police-constable E 100.) At half-past four o'clock on
the afternoon of the 29th of Dec., I saw the prisoner coming down the stairs
of the prosecutor's chambers, which are No. 6, South-square, Gray's Inn, with
these carpets- asked what he was about to do with the carpets-he mut-
tered something, but I did not understand him-h ran up stairs, and said to
some person up stairs, 44 The policeman is below, say all right"- stood at
the foot of the stairs, looking up, and the prisoner came down and two other
persons followed him-I secured bim-the others got away, but I held him
-he dropped these carpets in the passage, and E took them-these are them
-he wanted to know what I meant by holding him there, and wanted me
to let him go-I said I should not let him goI asked what he was going
to do with the carpet, and I believe he said he was sent to carry it-I stated
at the office that I asked him by whom he was sent, and where he was going
to take it.
Prisoner's Defence. Two gentlemen touched me on the shoulder, and said
