GUSTAVUS GIDLEY (Policeman K 468). On the morning of the 18th
August, about one o'clock, I was in Salmon's Lane, and noticed that the shutters
and window of the “Prince Regent” public-house were down, and a pane
of glass broken-I listened, and heard alucifer-match struck, and immediately
afterwards saw a light in the parlour-I called to a man and woman who
were passing by for their assistance-I then saw the prisoner Collins look
over the window and the shutter-he then jumped out of the window, and
I caught hold of him—I had a struggle with him—he got away, and was
shortly afterwards stopped-I saw Thomas also look out at the window,
and I tried to get in, and he pushed the shutter up inside I was then
admitted by the landlady at the door-I burst open the parlour door
Thomas then jumped out at the window, and was stopped by another
constableI gave my rattle to some one to spring—I am sure the prisoners
are the men.
