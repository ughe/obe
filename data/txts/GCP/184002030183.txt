781. CHARLES WARE and NATHAN NATHAN were indicted
for stealing, on the 28th of January, 2 hats, value 10s., the goods of
Charles Saunders, the master of the said Charles Ware.
CHARLES SAUNDERS. I am a hatter, and live in Blackman-street,
Southwark. Ware was in my service these two hats are mine, and are
worth 108.- on the morning of the 28th of January Willy came to my
shop, and from what he said I asked Ware if He had sold any hat or hats
that morning he said no-I repeated the question, and he still denied it
-Willy took Ware into custody.

4. One is beaver and one is silk-I have not put too high a price on
them--some silk hats are sold very cheap I have not known them sold at
188. a dozen-I would not sell these for less than 58. a-piece they are
finished and fit for sale--my bills are headed, "Salter and Co.,” which is
