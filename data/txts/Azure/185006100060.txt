MR. COOPER conducted the Prosecution.
ASHER BARNETT. I am a baker, of Middlesex-street—I know the
prisoner. On 28th Dec. I let him a house and bakehouse at ll. 15'. a week
—there was no agreement in writing—the stand, the chimney-glass,
other things stated, were all in the house and the bakehouse—he was to
them, and if he left the house to leave them as he found them—I neversold
him them, nor gave him permission to sell them—be paid me the rent up to
10th May—on 24th May I was awakened out of my bed—I went to
house and found the scale-stand, the chimney-glass, and other things gone—

cost me 31. 18s.—this stand, and scales and weights cost about 71.—1 found
them at Jacobs'.—l paid 41. to get these back—the prisoner had left the
premises entirely—he was not found for nearly a fortnight afterwards.
