1465. ELIZABETH SHERBURN, MARY ANN SOMERVILLE.
and ELIZABETH BURTON, stealing 51 yards of ribbon, value 15s., the
goods of Joseph Willis and another; Burton having been before convicted.
JOSEPH WILLIS. I am in partnership with Thomas Willis, at 2, Frederick
place, Lewisham. On the 11th of June, about half-past one o'clock, I found
the three prisoners in my shop - they wanted some French ribbon-I placed
a box of ribbons before them, which Somerville and Burton looked at-Sher-
burn in the meantime asked for a cap- I turned on one side to get it-she
said it was too dear-the two otbers bought two yards of ribbon and some
thing else, and they went out-a constable came in- I looked at the box and
missed two pieces of ribbon-these are them (produced).
WILLIAM NOAKES (policeman). I saw the prisoners together at Mill-
pond Bridge, Bermondsey, and followed them down to Greenwich. They
went into seven or eight drapers' shops-Somerville and Burton went into
the prosecutor's shop, and Sherburn went in about a minute afterwards-
Sherburn came out in four or five minutes, and waited about thirty yards
from the shop-the others then came out, joined her, and they went away
together-I left another constable to follow them, went in, and told the prose-
cutor what I suspected-I then went after the prisoners and found them
together-Somerville directly said, pointing to Sherburn, “That person is
not with us; we know nothing about her "- I took them to the station, and
the prosecutor identified them.
CHARLOTTE SARGENT. The prisoners were brought to the station-I
searched them, and found the ribbon on Sherburn.
Sherburn's Defence. We all three live together in Shoreditch; we went
to see Lee races, and coming along, a woman asked me to carry a small
parcel for her, and said she would give me a shilling.
WILLIAM GRADY (policeman, 206). I produce a certificate (read-
Elizabeth Johnson, convicted Jan. 1848, having been before convicted, and con-
fined six months) Burton is the person.
SHERBURN-GUILTY. Aged 20. le
SOMERVILLE-Guilty. Aged 20. Con
d 20. Confined One Month.
BURTON-GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined One Year.
1466. JOHN BROWN, stealing one watch and chain, value 11., the
goods of Thomas Laycock, from his person.
Thomas LAYCOCK. I am a labourer. On 20th June, about eleven o'clock,
I went to a public-house at Shooter's-hill and played at skittles with the pri-
soner-I drank and fell asleep-I then had my watch safe-I missed it soon
afterwards--this is it (produced)--I got it from a pawnbroker's.
Prisoner. He gave me the watch to pawn on Tuesday: I stopped away
and spent the money, and next day he gave me in charge. Witness. I did
not give him the watch-I have the guard which was round my neck, and
I found it cut when I awoke.
