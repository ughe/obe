57. WILLIAM WILLIAMS was indicted for a like offence.
MR. GIFFARD conducted the Prosecution.
CAROLINE AMELIA JANNAWAY. I am the wife of William Jannaway, a beer-
shop keeper, of Exeter street, Chelsea. On 26th Oct. the prisoner came for
half a pint of beer- I served him, and he gave me a half crown; I gave him
28. 5d. change, and he left-I directly took out the half crown, and found it
was bad ; there was no other in the till-I kept it till Mr. Jannaway came,
and it was then placed by itself on the back shelf-next morning I gave it
to Daborn.
• WILLIAM DABORN policeman). I produce a half crown which I received
from Mrs. Jannaway on the morning of 27th Oct.
HELEN EMERY.On Wednesday, 26th Oct., the prisoner came to my shop,
about a quarter to 8 o'clock in the evening, and asked for two eggs, which
came to 2d.—he gave me 1s., which I passed to my brother for change.
FRANK EMERY. My sister gave me a shilling when the prisoner was in
the shop; I tried it; it was bad-I told the prisoner he was aware it was
bad by his manner-he said he was not, and that he had more money-I
gave him in charge, and gave the shilling to the policeman.
JOHN WISE (policeman). I took the prisoner; he refused to give his
address-I received this shilling (produced) from Mr. Emery.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These two shillings are counterfeit, and so is this
half crown; it is from the same mould as one of those uttered in Osborne's
case.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Twelve Months.
