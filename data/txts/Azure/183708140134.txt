not—I was sure she had, and I found them under her shawl—these are
men's shoes—they asked for women's shoes.
Prisoner. I am a dealer in Petticoat-lane, and I went with this young

she went in, and I with her ; and in coming out he stopped me, and said I

for a policeman, and took me.
JURY to CHARLES SMITH. Q. Did you take them from her person?
