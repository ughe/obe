LATRD DONALD. I am Mr. Miles's clerk- I went by his direction on
or about September 3rd to the Duke of Wellington, and saw a man who
gave his name William Carter-I believe it was Pether, but having seen
them both in the business I do not recognise which it was-I took down
these particulars in his presence.
HENRY Hour. I am an auctioneer and public-house broker-I have
known Pether since 1875—he introduced me to Carter last July relative to
the purchase of the Duke of Wellington, but I had seen Carter before-I
told Carter to go down to Messrs. Hoare's brewery-I saw them after
they had seen Messrs. Hoare-they told me that if the reference suited
Carter would take the house, and he did take it-Mr. Davis was the
former landlord, and 1371. was paid to him for the goodwill and fixtures,
101. of which was for the stock-I provided them with 141. 58. 5d., as
they were short of the amount.
