ROWLAND DANDO. I live at No. 46, Aldermanbury, and am a wholesale
stationer and bag maker. I pointed out to Mr. Robinson a quantity of bugs
and paper, I think it was on 24th Aug.--I had that paper from Mr. Edis
of Aylesbury-street-my suspicion was not excited by the lowness of the
price—the bags were sold to me at about my cost price of them I examined
the paper, and saw something which induced me to go to Mr. Robinson-I
paid 121. 188. for that paper, and gave Mr. Edis a cheque for it.
EDWARD Epis. I am a tea dealer and grocer, at No. 22, Aylesbury-street,

buying bags of him-in the latter end of Aug., I sold Mr. Dando a number
of bags and paper, for which he paid me 121. 188.-I cannot tell how long !
have been in the habit of buying goods of Hurley-I have an invoice dated

me several invoices here is one on 27th Aug. 198. 60. for 28 lbs. of com
