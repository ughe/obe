 1891. THOMAS GLENMAN, and WILLIAM M'QUEEN , stealing 1 shawl, value 1 l  .; the goods of Thomas Jackson : Glenman having been before convicted. THOMAS JACKSON. I live at Hedge-row, Islington—on 18th July I saw a shawl safe on my door-post—I missed it in about a quarter of an hour—this is it ( produced ). WILLIAM HIGGINS. I live at Pleasant-place, Lower-road—I saw the prisoners loitering about Mr. Jackson's shop—they sat down on the step of the next door—I next saw Glenman pass this shawl to M'Queen—they went different ways. THOMAS MORRIS HARVEY. I am a pawnbroker—I took the shawl in on the next day. PETER TERRITT( policeman ). I produce a certificate—( read—Thomas Glenning, convicted Sept . 1846, and confined six months )—Glenman is the man.
   GLENMAN  — GUILTY. ** Aged 17.— Transported for Seven Years .
   M'QUEEN  — GUILTY. ** Aged 17.— Confined One Year .
 1892. JAMES BRYANT, was indicted for embezzling 12 s  .: 2nd COUNT  , for stealing 800 pantiles, value 1 l  . 12 s  .; the goods of " William Harding , his master. WILLIAM HARDING. The prisoner was my servant fifteen years. On 15th July, I told him to find me a purchaser for some tiles—I found he had sold them—I gave him no authority to do so—I could have sold them for four times the money—I asked him for the money—he said he had spent it. WILLIAM BUCKNELL( policeman, B  77). I took the prisoner, and told him the charge—he said he had sold them and spent the money.
   Prisoner . I did not know what I said. GUILTY  . Aged 50.— Strongly recommended to mercy  . — Confined One Month .
 1893. JOHN FISH , stealing 5 sovereigns, and 1 half-sovereign, the moneys of Jane Fish , in her dwelling-house. JANE FISH. I am a widow, of Frederick-place, St. Pancras—the prisoner is my son—on 26th July I was going out to market, the prisoner recom mended me not to take my money with me—I left a bag containing five sovereigns and a half in a box in the kitchen—I missed it next morning, and the prisoner also. JOHN PORTER( policeman ). I took the prisoner, and found 2 l . 4 s . 2 d . on him. GEORGE JOLIFFE. On 26th I took the prisoner's clothes from him in gaol—he said he had concealed a sovereign in the back of his coat—I said I had not seen it—he said he would rather lose it than say anything about it—I searched his coat and found it—a new suit of clothes were taken off him which his mother knew nothing of. GUILTY. * Aged 23.— Transported for Ten Years .
 (  The prosecutrix stated that the prisoner had repeatedly robbed her. )
