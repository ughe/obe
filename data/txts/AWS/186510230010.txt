Mr. POLAND conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. METOALFE the Defence
JoHN PEARCE. I am in the employment of the Citizen Steam Boat
Company, as assistant money-taker at the Old Shades Pier-I was there on
Tuesday, 22d August-it was my duty to keep the gangway elear-about
1 o'elock on that day, the prisoner came there with a basket of walnuts-I
did not know him before he-put his basket down underneath the window
where the people pay-it was a basket with a pile of walnuts on the top of
it-I asked him to move the basket, and he would not, and told me to mind
my own business- said if he did not move it I should have to move it
myself, beeause it caused an obstruction-he did not moveit and I put my
foot against the bottom of it, and pushed it on one side, and some of the
nuts fell off-the prisoner directly flew at me and said, 4 Pay me for my
said I had nothing to do with them, and told him to take them
away, and not cause an obstruetion-he then struek me in the mouth,
caught me by the collar, threw me, and fell on me-I fell on the ground on
my left side-we had a little bit of a struggle, and I got away from him
and got up-he then attacked me again a second time, aud threw me again,
and when I was on the ground, he laid hold of me by the collar and knooked
my head on the ground five times, I think-h dashed my head on beflag
stones-I got up, and went to try and find a policeman, but could not find
one, and living close by in Miles-lane, I ran home-my mouth was bleeding
from the effects of the blow-I went to bed, and the next morning Mr.
Humphreys, the doctor, saw me-I have been confined to the house a very
long time-two or three days afterwards, the Lord Mayor same and took
my deposition-on the Thursday the prisoner came to see me-I was then
very ill-a constable was sent for, and he was given into custody-he said
he was very sorry about it, and he was going to give his name and address.
