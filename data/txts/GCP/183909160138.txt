2614. JAMES BOWYER was again indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
August, 1 coat, value 16s., the goods of Robert Lester.
ROBERT LESTER (police-sergeant E 10.) I was stationed at George
street station-house--I had a great-coat hanging on a peg in the passage
leading to the kitchen-I missed it about the 9th of August-I saw
again on the 4th of September-this is it-it is my own private coat-
the time I lost it the prisoner was living at the George-street station-house,
MATTHEW KILLINGWORTH. I am a shopman to Mr. Perkins, a paw-
broker, at Chelsea. On the 9th of August I took in this coat of a person
in police clothes--the prisoner is the person, to the best of my recollection,
though the difference of dress makes a great deal of difference-to the best
of my belief, the prisoner is the man.
ALLEN HORATIO GARMAN. I took the prisoner. I searched his BOL
and found some letters in it-in one, which was addressed to James
Bowyer, I found the duplicate of this great-coat, pawned on the sun
August, in the name of John Britton, Battersea--the prisoner's father
at Battersea, and the pawnbroker's is in the road from the station-hous
to his father's house.
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Transported for Seven Years longer.
