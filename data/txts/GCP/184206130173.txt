Q. At the time of the destruction of this mortgage deed, did you know
the prisoner was a bankrupt ? A. No, Sir, I did not-I heard that they
were going to strike the docket that very night, after he destroyed the deed
--this was on the 11th of May-I met him at the office afterwards--I do
not believe I saw bim anywhere else before I was there I saw him the
same night, as he was to sell nine cows, and I was to have part of the
money, but, to the best of my knowledge, I did not see him afterwards--I
saw him that night in George-street.
