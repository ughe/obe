MARY ANN MOTT. I am married. I went to the King William pub lic-house to get my husband's supper beer—the prisoner came out at the door, and talked to two females—after that I saw Pearson without a cap—she said, "B—— Cumberland Poll,  I am ready for you"—Thomas asked the prisoner for the key, and she gave it to her—I did not see her doing any thing with it—Thomas said, "Now I have got the key, we will make it up with browns  to-morrow"—I then saw the prosecutrix run towards the prisoner to bit her, but she was so drunk she fell down and cut her lip—she got up—Perkins said, "Mary Ann, your mouth is bleeding"—she said, "I don't mind a little blood"—she went to strike the prisoner again, but fell against Mr. Talbot's door, and fell down backwards.
   COURT . Q.  Did you see how she cut her lip? A.  Yes, by the curb, and she split her nose up against the door—I only saw the prisoner in one dress—that was a lightish one.
   MR. HORRY . Q.  Do you know Talbot's door? A.  Yes—it has a sharp edge—I saw it next morning, and there is all the blood on it now—as soon as the blow was struck it spirted out. MARIA WHITE.
