WILLIAM MORRELL. I am assistant to Mr. Carley, chemist, 27, Berners-
street. I have known Edwards about four years and a half-he resided in
Brompton-square-I remember calling there four or five days after Christmas-
day- he was in the kitchen-I took refreshment there-Mrs. Edwards was
there it was about half-past eight-while I was there the lady in the parlour
came-she was introduced to me as Mrs. or Miss Charsleley-I saw her again
at's ball at Circus-street, Paddington--her name was not mentioned then.
CHARLOTTE BAKER. I am the niece of Mrs. Edwards. I was residing with
her-I knew the lady in the parlour-I can scarcely say when it was that
she first came there, but I think it was since Christmas-she went by the
name of Miss Underwood-she changed her name to Charsley-I cannot tell
how long that was after she came there, and from that time she was called
by that name-I knew her by that name-my aunt and cousin called her by
that name.
