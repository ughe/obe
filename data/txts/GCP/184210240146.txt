2939. EDWARD BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of
October, 1 dead fowl, value 3s., the goods of Robert Raggett.
WILLIAM BEECHER. I am shopman to Robert Raggett, a poulterer, in
Upper King-street, Bloomsbury. On the 6th of October, about a quarter
before ten o'clock in the morning, a young man came and told me I had
lost a fowl-I counted, and one was missing-I went with him and found
the prisoner in King-street, looking into a shop, with the fowl under his
arm.
Prisoner. I was going on an errand, a boy gave me the fowl to hold till
he came back. Witness. He did not say how he came by it-he said he
did not take it, and he knew nothing about it-it was wrapped in a hand-
kerchief.
WILLIAM BLOOMFIELD (police-constable E 114.) I took the prisoner
on the 6th of October.
