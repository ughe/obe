Burch said he was a very good customer, and though the bills were small, they were paid in money, which appeared as if it had come over the counter, which gave me the impression that they were genuine trade bills—I believed the balance-sheet of June, 1862, to be true—I had a loan from the Unity Bank, in 1859, of 1,000 l .,—that was in con sequence of the death of my partner, and it was afterwards reduced to 800 l ., or 750 l .,—I had had accommodation from the Unity from time to time—my whole transactions at the Unity amounted to 57, 714 l ., and my firm had transactions with it to the amount of 307,000 l .,—the accommodation I had at the Unity was given on the ordinary terms, and the loan was secured—I get equally good or better terms at my present bank—in con sequence of the death of my partner, I was about to transfer my account to the Unity Bank.
