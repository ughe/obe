HENRY WILLIAMSON. I live in Perceval-street, Clerkenwell. I had a boy in my employ—I charged him with stealing some gold from me—in con sequence of what he said, I went to the prisoner's house, in Fox-place, Clerkenwell, where the boy lived—I showed the prisoner the gold I lost, and the said she had picked up a piece like that in Clerkenwell-close, and sold it to Mr. Hearn for 10 s . RICHARD LIDGETT. I lived with Mr. Williamson. He charged me with stealing gold—I took three bits, at three different times, home to the prisoner, and she told me she sold them—the first morning I took it she gave me 6 d .—I took a bit on the Saturday morning, and she gave me 1 s .—I told her I had got it away from my place. CHARLES SAUNDERS. I am shopman to Mr. Hearn. I bought a piece of gold, but I cannot say of whom—I buy pieces of gold of poor women like the prisoner without knowing who they are.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was going up Clerkenwell-close, and picked up a piece of blue paper, and in it was a thin piece of gold; I did not know what it was; I took it to this young man, and asked him; he said it was gold; when Lidgett came home I told him, and he said, "You ought to give me part of it."
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 53.— Confined Six Months .

 3043. JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of Oct ., 1 truck, value 8 l  .; 7 1/2 lbs. weight of tea, 1 l  . 17 s  .; 71bs. weight of coffee, 12 s  . 10 d  .; 69lbs. weight of tea, 2 l  .; 10lbs. weight of candles, 4 s  . 6 d  .; 1lb. weight of rice, 5 d  .; and 3/4 lb. weight of cocoa, 7 d  .; the goods of Richard Sexton and another; and that he had been before convicted of felony. RICHARD SEXTON. I am a grocer, and live in High-street, Newington. I had a truck on the 12th of Oct. at my house—it contained 7 1/2 lbs. of tea, and the other things stated—I sent the whole of them in the truck, by Edward Copas, to take to my different customers—I saw the truck the next day at the station, with the different articles.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Did you put the articles up? A.  I put up some, and my man put up the others—I superintended it—I have one partner. EDWARD COPAS. I went to Mr. Huntley's, in Pump-row, Old-street, and began to deliver some of these things, and, while I was inside, a man took the truck, and the property—this is it.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Had you delivered any of the order? A.  Yes, three parcels—I left the truck for about ten minutes, and nobody with it—it was about five o'clock.
