 2004. JOSEPH OAKLEY was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of August , 1 bottle, value 2 d  .; and 1 1/2 pint of wine, value 3 s  .; the goods of Charles Bennett , his master. CHARLES BENNETT. I live in Bedford-row, Walworth-road. The pri soner was in my employ, and carried out wine—the constable brought him back to me. JOHN HAYNES( police-constable P  129.) I took the prisoner, and found this bottle of wine on him—I did not say that it would be better for him to confess.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  What did you say to him? A.  I stopped him in the street and took the bottle from him—I said, "Where did you get this bottle of wine from?"—I took it from under his coat—he said, "For God's sake don't choke me, I will go quietly with you"—he was perfectly sober—I took him to his master. CHARLES BENNETT. The prisoner was brought back—I believe the bottle to be mine—it was not sealed—as he was going to the station-house he admitted that he stole it—that he had robbed me, and begged I would not be hard with him.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did he use that word? A.  I think he did—I cannot take upon myself to swear he did—I had cut the letter "S" in the cork—he had been between five and six months in my service—he had a good character—I should say he was a little elevated. GUILTY. Aged 19— Recommended to mercy  — Confined Two Months.
