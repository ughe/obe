 1218.  JOHN JULIER was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of July , one watch, value 10 l.; two seals, value 4 l.; two keys, value 1 s.; one ring, value 3 s., and one piece of ribbon, value 1 d., the goods of Nicholas Dechemant , from his person.
   NICHOLAS DECHEMANT. I live in Frith-street, Soho. On the 3d of July I was in a pastrycook's-shop in Oxford-street. The City procession was returning from presenting an address to the Queen. I remained in the shop near the door; some people came up, pushed me, and snatched my watch. I have not recovered it.
   WILLIAM MILES. I am a carpenter. I stopped by the pastrycook's window as the procession passed. The prisoner was running with four or five behind him. He stopped directly he came to the prosecutor, looked round, and they all stopped in the centre of the door, made a rush, and I saw the prisoner snatch the prosecutor's watch. I am certain of his person. I told the prosecutor of it. Jones and I ran, caught the prisoner in a public-house in Oxford-street, and gave him in charge.
 Cross-examined by MR. BARRY. Q. Who do you work for - A. Mr. Ward, of Pimlico. I am going to the East Indies. I live at Peckham. I lost sight of him, but I am certain he is the man.
 Prisoner's Defence. He said at the office that he never lost sight of me. He said he would transport me to get his expences.
     GUILTY. Aged 25. Transported for Life.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1219.  MARY WEST was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of August , one necklace, value 5 s., the goods of Philip Hubie , from the person of Mary Hubie.
   FRANCES HUBIE. I am the wife of Philip Hubie. I sent Clark out with my daughter, Mary; she had a necklace on, but returned without one. I live in Brick-lane.
   ELLEN CLARK. I took the child out between three and four o'clock; the prisoner and another girl came behind us in Brick-lane, and then came before us; I am sure of her person; the prisoner then leant over my shoulder; I saw her hand go away from the child's neck, and missed the necklace; I charged her with stealing it; the other girl ran away; a gentleman seized the prisoner.
   HENRY EDLIN. I am a butcher. I heard the girl scream out that the prisoner had took the necklace; I saw the prisoner give something to the other girl, who ran away immediately. I secured West and gave her in charge.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was on the other side of the way.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Confined Six Months.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1220.  MARIA HARVEY was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of July , one purse, value 1 d. and 13 s. in monies numbered, the property of Peter Croiley , from his person.
   PETER CROILEY. I am a book-keeper, and live in Duke-street, Portman-square. On the 29th of July, about eleven o'clock at night, I was met in Oxford-street by a party of Irishmen, and was knocked down; my pockets were rifled of a silk handkerchief and other trifles; I received some severe blows; on recovering myself I found the prisoner by my side, and accompanied her to a house in Orchard-street, up three pair of stairs; I was in the same room with her and another woman; I went there for no improper purpose. I began to suspect all was not right, and pulled my purse out; the prisoner immediately snatched it from me, the candles were put out, and I was told to get down stairs or they would break my neck; I got down with some difficulty, and immediately pointed the house out to the watchman; we went in and I pointed the prisoner out; she was secured, and the purse found in the house, in the room I was robbed in; I described it before I saw it.
   THOMAS ALLSOP. I was constable of the night. I was at the corner of Orchard-street; the prosecutor fetched me; I got two watchmen and found the prisoner and two other women in the three pair room; he pointed the prisoner out, and gave this account. I found the purse under the bed between the sacking and mattress, empty; he described it before he saw it.
 (Purse produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I never saw him.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. Transported for Life.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1221.  JOHN FITZGERALD was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of August , one handkerchief, value 4 s., the goods of a certain man unknown, from his person.
   CHARLES READ. I am a constable. On the 5th of August, about half-past eleven o'clock at night, as the people came out of the English Opera-house, I saw the prisoner, in company with another, try several gentlemen's pockets; they followed two gentlemen; I saw the prisoner take the handkerchief from one of their pockets, and put his hand behind to give it to the other; I seized him and took it from his hand; a scuffle ensued but I kept him; the gentleman came to the watch-house, but I do not know where he is, or his name, or where he lives; he is an officer, and was going to France.
   THOMAS HUNT. I was with Read, and saw him take the handkerchief from the prisoner's hand; the gentleman said his name was Prince or Price, I do not know which; he gave no Christian name.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. Transported for Life.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
