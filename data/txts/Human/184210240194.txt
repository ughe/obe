 3041. JAMES GOSBELL , EDMUND SMITH, and FREDERICK SHACKLEFORD were indicted for a misdemeanor.
   MESSRS. CLAUKSON and DOANE conducted the Prosecution. DONALD CAMERON. I am in the service of Robert Milbourn, a silk warehouseman, in Newgate-street—he has no partner—on the 16th of May, Shackleford came, and asked whether we served country dealers—I said we did—he said he was commencing business at Maidstone, and wished to open an account at our house, he would pay part in cash, and give respectable references for the balance—he asked to be shown some silks—I showed him some—he selected a parcel, amounting to 81 l . 0 s . 8 d .—he produced this card, and was about writing the names of the referes cm the back of it, when I took him into the counting-house, to the count ing-house clerk—when he got into the counting-house he wrote these names of the referees—( read )—Bachelard, George-street, Mansion-house; Ingram, Addle-street; and Earp, Fleet-street—on the other side of the card is "Water loo-house, William Beaumont, Week-street, Maidstone"—Mr. Wells was in the counting-house—I said, "Mr. Beaumont, of Maidstone"—I left the card in the counting-house—Mr. Milbourn was not in at the time—Shackleford left instructions that the goods should be sent at six o'clock, to the Swan with-two-Necks, Lad-lane—that was all that passed that day—the goods consisted often yards of black satin, sixteen of white satin, and other things—the porter took them that evening—Mr. Wells accompanied him, and received some money—on the next day, the 17th, Shackleford came again, Mr. Milbourn was there then, and sold the goods—he selected number of goods, which came to 77 l . 5 s . 7 d .—he left instructions that they were to be left at the Swan-with-two-Necks, Lad-lane, at six o'clock—he left an order for a box of artificial flowers—on the 23rd be came again, and bought some more goods, amounting to 14 l . 16 s . and the flowers were sent with them—I addressed him several times as Mr. Beanmont—Mr. Milbourn, in my hearing, addressed him as Mr. Beaumont—he came again on the 31st—he bought some more goods that day, consisting of hand kerchiefs, shawls, scarfs, crapes, and satinet, in two parcels, which amount ed to 71 l . 13 s . 3 d ., and 14 l . 12 s . 6 d ., making 86 l . 5 s . 9 d .—the 14 l . 12 s . 6 d . was to be paid in the middle of the next month—they were to be sent as before, to the Swan-with-two-Necks—there was a direction written on them, "R.
