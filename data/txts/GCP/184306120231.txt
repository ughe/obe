2000. MARY ELLIS and ELIZA JONES were indicted for stealing,
on the 16th of June, I watch, value 31. ; 1 hat, 68. ; 1 neckerchief, 58.;-2
scount-books, 38.; and 1 half-sovereign; the property of Edward Thomas,
from his person.
EDWARD THOMAS. I am a draper, and live in High-street, Whitechapel.
I came home in a cab, about two o'clock, in the night of the 16th of June-1
got out in Whitechapel, at the Horse and Leaping Bar, opposite my house-1
had a glass of brandy and water--when I came out I was accosted by the
two prisoners and others--they took me to a house in Duke-street-I was in
liquor-1 had a watch, a hat, a neck handkerchief, and two account-books-
when the policeman came he found me on the floor, and my property was all
gone-I have not seen the hat and handkerchief since-these are the other
things.

Matilda BALDWIN. Mrs. Morris, who keeps a lodging-house, came to me
for a candle to light her to bed-she said, " I wish you would go and see if
my place is all right”—I went, and saw the prosecutor lying across the floor,
without a coat, hat, or cravat-two girls, I believe to be the prisoners, were
with him—Ellis said they had got a bloak, and he had got no money, that he
bad been knocked down in Whitechapel.
MICHAEL COLEMAN (police-constable H 177.) I was called by Baldwin
--the prosecutor's coat, bat, and cravat were all gone—the prisoners were
with him-Ellis was just rising up from him as he laid on the floor-she took
off the floor these books, and gave me I found the watch in her shawl, which
I found between the bed and the wall, after I had taken them to the
station.
Ellis's Defence. He was lying down by a court ; I spoke to him ; he
went down the court, and agreed to give me 3s. for the whole night; he had
got no money ; his two books and collar of his shirt were lying down by his
side; I picked them up and gave them to him. I endeavoured to pick him
up, bat was not able. When Baldwin came in, she said, “What is the mat-
ter?" I said, " I have a tipsy man, and he has no money;" they called in
the policeman; he came, and could find nothing ; then they took me.
Jones's Defence. We saw him lying down ; we went up; he got up, and
agreed to go with Ellis. He had got no money. The landlady came in, and
said, “Oh, what are you doing with the man ?" and she called the police.mod
