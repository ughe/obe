I saw the prisoners at the King's Head, where the man fell asleep—I was in the room before they came into it—he put his head down and fell asleep—the women were on the left side of him—I saw Pead put her hand into his pocket, and take out his tobacco-box—the other was sitting by the side of her at the time—I saw her open the box and count the money on the table-four shillings and seven sixpences—they then took the box and went out together—I did not wake the man, because Pead said, when she counted the money, that she considered that he ought to have more, and I thought she was his wife.
   Pead. Q.  What did he say to you when you said he was robbed; did he not tell you it was all right? A.  Yes he did—I told him directly after they were gone—he did not miss the box before I told him, and then he said it was all right—I awoke him directly after the women went out, about half-past five o'clock. WILLIAM MURRAY. I am a policeman. I took Jones into custody on Tuesday morning last at the Oxford Arms-Pead was taken by Marshall on Tuesday evening—I found 2 d . and a duplicate on Jones, for two pairs of pattens.
   Pead . I am unfortunate, it is true, but I am not guilty of this—I had been at the poor-house at Deptford, and meant to go to the Refuge.
   PEAD— GUILTY. Aged 18.
   JONES— GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Six Months.

 2063. THOMAS ENDERWICK was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of August , 14 yards of woollen cloth, value 13 l  . the goods of William Henry Whiteman , his master. WILLIAM HENRY WHITEMAN. I am a draper and tailor, and live at Woolwich. The prisoner was my apprentice—on the 20th of August, in the evening, when I returned from London I found the prisoner in cus tody—I afterwards saw these three pieces of cloth—I heard that it was gone and then I missed it—this is it—it is my property. MARY WHITEMAN. I am the prosecutor's wife. On the 20th of August, I was in the prisoner's bed-room between three and four o'clock in the after noon—the prisoner came down from the workshop into that room, which was very unusual—he saw me and he quitted the room very shortly after—I waited till he was gone, and looked into his box—it was without a key—I found in it a piece of cloth tied in a handkerchief—I took it into the cutting-room to one of our young men, and gave it to him—he went down and found in the prisoner's box another piece of cloth.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  How many young men had you? A.  About a dozen—I found this piece of cloth in the box just after he left the room—I did not see him do any thing to the box, but I saw him move another box.
