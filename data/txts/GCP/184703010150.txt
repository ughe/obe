CARROLL, Mayor.
Before Mr. Justice Coltman.
812. JOHN BURNS was indicted for a robbery on Thomas Evans.
and putting him in fear, and stealing from his person 1 half-sovereign.
2 half-crowns, 3 shillings, 4 pence, and beating, and striking, and using other
personal violence to him.
MR. PARNELL conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS EVANS. I am a chimney-sweeper, and live at Leytonstone, On
13th Jan. I sold 60 bushels of soot, which came to 1l. 108., to Mr. Bell, of
Woodford-I loaded it in a cart--the prisoner assisted me I was to give him
18.-Bull paid me about three o'clock-he gave me a sovereign and a half-
I went from Bull's to the Castle, kept by Mr. Green, about ten minutes"
walk from Woodford, and changed a sovereign to pay the prisoner-Green
gave me a half-sovereign and silver-1 afterwards changed the half-sovereign
to pay what little debts I owed, but I still had half a sovereign-I paid away
38., and gave the prisoner and Bull's man, who had helped me to load the
Boot, 38. for drink-that made 68.--I paid away the rest at Mr. Fuller's shop,
just by the Castle, except half a sovereign, two half-crowns, 38., and four-
penny worth of halfpence-the prisoner was in the tap-room when I got the
change-he went out with me after I paid Bull's man-he followed me out,
and asked me for some money-when I first went into the public-house I
paid him his shilling, and gave him some drink-when we came out, he said,
“I ought to have more money"-I said I could not afford to give him more
- he said, "I am d d if I don't have more," and knocked me down with his
fist-I fell to the ground, and was insensible for a minute or two-he struck
me in the eye--my eye was cut open-it was all in a gore of blood-this was
not above 100 yards from the Castle-it was about four o'clock, or a quarter
after-I had my money in my jacket-pocket outside, and had a smock-frock
over that-when I came to myself, the prisoner was gone, and every farthing
of my money--my wife gave information to the police.
COURT. Q. Had you had any liquor? A. I drank out of two quarts of
beer, but no more I was no more intoxicated than I am now the money
was wrapped up in a piece of paper in my jacket-pocket-Mr. Green wrapped
it up for me when he gave me the change--I had been to a shop and paid
some money, but wrapped the rest up in the same paper-I wrapped it up
safe not more than five minutes before I went away.
Prisoner. Q. Did not you tell me to come up to the Castle, and you
would pay me 1s. ? A. No-I told you to come to my house between five
and six o'clock in the evening, and I would pay you.
ROBERT GREEN. I am a victualler, and keep the Castle, at Woodford. On
13th Jan., about four o'clock, Evans came to my house-the prisoner was
there-I gave Evans change for a sovereign, half-a-sovereign, and 106. in
silver, I do not recollect what-in about half an hour I changed half-8-sove-
reign for him-I gave him 3 half-crowns, 2s., and 14d., deducting 444
for a pot of porter-I did not wrap it up for him-I did not notice when be
went out of the house.
JOSEPH HEMMINGS. I keep a beer-shop at Woodford. On 18th Jan.
about six o'clock in the evening, the prisoner came to my house there were
three more there who came with him, or soon after-they had something to
drink, which the prisoner paid for with 2 half-crowns and 28.--the first
payment was half-a-crown---the shillings may have been change out of that
half-crown--the second payment was 28., and the third, another half-crown.
