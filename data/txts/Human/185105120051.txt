No; I live with my father, mother, brother, and sister—Mrs. Blanchfield drank some of the gin—Mrs. Jewell was at the fire when I brought Mrs. Blanchfield in—Mrs. Connell came to my house about two o'clock on the Sunday morning—it was there the was found in bed with two men; they were my father and brother—I picked up Mrs. Connell's cap in the morning; it had hair in it—I showed it her, and she told me to burn it—it was much lighter than her own hair—Mrs. Jewell's hair was light—I burnt it.
