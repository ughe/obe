HARRIETT GABALLY. I am a widow, and live at Brooker's-buildings,
Pittfield-street, Hoxton-I know the prisoner-I was present at his marriage
with my daughter, at Shoreditch Church, on Christmas-day, five years ago
my daughter is alive, and has been here to-day.
MARY BARRY. I live at 30, Essex-street, Strand- I know the prisonet.
On the 29th of last Dec. I was married to him, at Bloomsbury Church, in
the name of Mary Barry- I did not know that he was a married man-
Edward Anderson was present.
EDWARD ANDERSON. I was one of the witnesses at the last marriage, and
saw Mary Barry and Dennis Dailey married.
Prisoner. Q. Was I sober the night previous to the marriage? 4. Yes,
certainly.
