Q. Do you mean that he did not take money, and account when the Board
met? A. To my knowledge he had not such authority-I cannot swear that that
did not occur, I was not at the Board every meeting the prisoner was not a
director, he was a shareholder-there is one marble covered cash book, another
green, and another red this marble covered book is the only rough cash book
that I know of there was a journal connected with his office; no other book
--this marble cash book is Mr. Rowley's writing-no one had anything to do
with this book but him.
