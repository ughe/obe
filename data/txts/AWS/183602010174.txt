555. JOHN LATHAM was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of January,
3 gowns, value 9s. 26 yards of silk, value 21. 128. 1 night-cap, value
2s. and 1 pillow-case value 1s. the goods of Barnett Harris.
BARNETT HARRIs. I am a tailor, and live in Drury-lane. The prisoner
lodged at my house with his mother-on the 16th of January, I missed
these articles from the drawer-I had seen them three weeks previously-
I did not see the prisoner till past eleven o'clock on Saturday night-he
then confessed that he had taken them, and spent the money.
JoHN ROLLs. I am in the service of Mr. Bassett, a pawnbroker, of
Great Queen-street. I took in this silk for 10s., I believe of the prisoner
-he said he brought it from his mother, and was fifteen years of age.
WILLIAM ROBERTS. I am shopman to Messrs. Walmsley, pawnbrokers,
in Drury-lane. I have some silk pawned for 128., and a gown for 6s., by
the prisoner-he said his mother sent him with it-his mother is in the
habit of pledging at our shop-she sends things by her servant.
JAMEs SCoTT. I am shopman to Moat and Appleton, of Little Warners
street, Clerkenwell. I produce a brown silk gown, which I took in of the
prisoner-] lent 18d. on it-it is worth about half-a-crown-he said he
was fifteen years of age, and came from his mother-he gave the name of
Keefe.
Prisoner. I did not pawn it, another boy did, who was not half my
size. Witness. I believe it to be the prisoner.
Prisoner. Mr. Harris told my mother, if I would confess every thing,
he would try to do something to get me employment.
BARNETT HARRIs re-examined. I did not-I never saw him from the
time I missed them till he was at the station nor did I see his mother
-she occupies my third floor, and is very industrious.
LATHAM. I am the prisoner's mother. Mr. Harris told me that
he suspected him, and nobody else and if he would acknowledge where
the property was he would do nothing to him-I have a husband who can
neither see nor hear-] have never received a farthing of this money.
GUILTY."
Aged 14.
Confined One Month, the first and last weeks to be solitary.
