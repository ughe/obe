(Friday, June 3.)
ROBINSON, Edward (23, coachman), pleaded guilty of carnally
knowing Alice Cade, a girl under the age of 16 years.
Sentence, Three years' penal servitude.
ISAACS, Abraham, rape upon Elizabeth May Maggs.
Mr. Thatcher prosecuted; Mr. C. F. Gill, K.c., and Mr. Clarke
Hall defended.
At the of the evidence for the prosecution Mr. Gill submitted
that there was no case to go to the jury. Mr. Justice Grantham
