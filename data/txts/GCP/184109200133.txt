do not smell of turpentine.
MR. BALLANTINE. Q. When you said there was a smell of turpentine,
did you refer particularly to the shavings? A. The policeman took them
up and smelt them-I smelt turpentine among the shavings underneath the
floor, to the best of my judgment-I have been in the service of the Com-
pany four years in October.
