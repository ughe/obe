CHARLES ULLMAN. I am a clerk in the office of Mr. Bretton, solicitor
-I served a notice, of which this is a copy, according to instructions I
received from the office- served it on a servant at Mr. Cook's office,
18, Warwick Street, Golden Square (This was a notice to produce the deed
and certain cheques and letters).
Cross-examined by MR. SERJBANT BALLANTINE. Q. You say the notice was
served from the office of Bretton & Company! A. From Mr. Bretton's;
he is a solicitor-the notice is signed "E. Lewis & Co."-I have seen it
before--it was not read over to me-I did not read it-it was given to me
by one of Mr. Bretton's clerks-I did not see the contents-I know it was
a notice, because I took it, and the one I served and the others were read
over together by the two clerks--Mr. E. Lewis is with Mr. Bretton; he is a
partner - I was in the room when the two clerks read it over ; but I did
not take notice of it.
