27. JOHN RAYCRAFT, alias John North, stealing 34lbs. weight of
lead, value 58.; the goods of James Collins, having been before convicted.
JAMES COLLINS. I am a builder, and have some premises in King-street,
Camden-town-I had some lead there-I missed somethis which is pro-
duced is it-I have compared it-it is mine.
JAMES Melvis. On 15th Oct., at half-past six o'clock in the morning, I
was on the top of one of the prosecutor's buildings--I saw the prisoner
removing this lead-I called to him and said, “I have caught you, have I?"
- he was about fifty yards from the building, carrying this lead before him.
Prisoner. He said, “That is the lead I saw on the top ;" I said, “There
it is," throwing it on one side; it was not off the premises; the other boy
employed me to take it.
THOMAS CAMPION policeman, A 358). I produce & certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction at Clerkenwell, by the name of John North-
(read, " Convicted January, 1847, confined three months")- he is the person.
