 2180. JOHN JONES was again  indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of May , 1 hat, value 15 s  .; 1 pair of gloves, value 4 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 handker chief, value 3 s  .; the goods of William Jones . WILLIAM JONES. On the 23rd of May I was at the Castle public-house, in Whitechapel—I hung up my hat, which had a pair of gloves and a handkerchief in it—when I came to look for them, they were gone, and an old hat left in the place of mine—I lost my hat altogether—I did not observe the prisoner there. JOHN TAPSCOTT. I keep the Castle public-house. On the 23rd of May I was sitting drinking a glass of grog with a friend—the prisoner came in, and called for a glass of ale—I served him—he then went out backwards, and returned—before he went he hung up his hat—when he came back he took the handkerchief out of the prosecutor's hat, and wiped bis face with it—he then doubled up the handkerchief, put it on his forehead, put the hat on, and went out—I looked up soon after, and saw an old hat in the room of it—I am certain he is the person—the prosecutor went out, but could not find him.
   Prisoner. I  never was in the house in my life. GUILTY. Aged 28.— Transported for Seven Years.
 2181. MARY ANN MATTHEWSON was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of July , 1 candlestick, value 2 s  .; 1 pillow, value 2 s  .; 1 tea-pot, value 6 s  .; 1 blanket, value 5 s  .; and 1 sheet, value 2 s  .; the goods of Edward Vale . ANN VALE. I am the wife of Edward Vale; we live in Cable-street, Wellclose-square—the prisoner lived in our first-floor back-room—she was there six weeks, and paid for a fortnight—she went away without notice, and left two children behind—I went into her room, and missed the arti cles stated—they have been found at the pawnbroker's, and are mine. JOHN FREDERICK SIMONS. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a blanket, pillow, and sheet, pawned by the prisoner. THOMAS JOHN LATIMER. I am a pawnbroker. I have a candlestick and tea-pot, pawned by the prisoner.
   Prisoner.  I did it from want, not with a view to rob her. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Six Months.
