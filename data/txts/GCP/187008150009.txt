ELIZABETH SABILE. My father keeps & stationer's shop at 132,
Boundary Road in July the prisoner came in, and asked for & sheet of
paper, an envelope, and a stamp, which came to 14d.-she gave me a bad
half-crown-I told her it was bad, and she took it from me; as she did so
a policeman came in- I told him she had bad money-she said she would
take it back where she had it from the policeman said he would follow
her-she went out and the policeman after her she afterwards came back
with the policeman and asked for a sheet of note paper, an envelope, and a
stamp, and gave me a 3d. piece, and I gave her 1jd. change-she came
back afterwards with another constable, and I charged her with passing
bad money.
