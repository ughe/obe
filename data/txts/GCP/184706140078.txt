FREDERICK RUSSELL (City police-constable No. 69.) On 24th of May,
about a quarter after eleven o'clock, in consequence of information, I took a
prisoner in charge in the City-road-I told him it was for assaulting Pert
Doyle, who was very ill, and not expected to get over it—he said he was very
sorry for it, it was his own fault, for he struck him (the prisoner) first-
said he should serve him so again, and any one else who struck him.
Cross-examined. Q. Was he near his own house? A. No, at Islington
about a mile off.
