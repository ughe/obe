HERBERT WILLIAN UMNEY, recalled. It is a very common practice
for knives imported from Germany to have the maker's named filed out
in order to conceal the fact that they come from abroad. I cannot tell
you if this is a foreign knife.
Verdict, Guilty of wilful murder.
Prisoner being called upon said, I still say I am not guilty. I re-
collect nothing barring the revolver. I recollect a noise, and that
must have been the firing of the revolver. A dastardly assassination
is absolutely foreign to my nature, as my past action will show. I
have taken a kife, but how many have I saved! If you go back two
years, on May 29, 1906, I saved two lives and was instrumental in
saving a third at the risk of my own, and yet knowing the dangers I
committed that dastardly assassination. Can you reconcile that I
am not afraid of death. There it is. From my heart I recollect
nothing barring the noise of the firing. It is not myself I feel sorry
for. I am only sorry for the friends that have befriended me.
