1008. WILLIAM TAYLOR, embezzling 21. 58.; the moneys of John
Stephen Wolveridge, his master; having been before convicted.
John STEPHEN WOLVERIDGE. I am a licensed victualler at Deptford-
the prisoner was in my employ in March and April to clean pots-I paid bim
18. a day and his keep-on 19th April I sent him to Mr. Soames, the
shipbuilder, with a sailor's advance-note for 21. 58.-he was to bring back
the money-he did not come back-I next saw him on 30th in custody.
SAMUEL FRANCIS SOAMES. I am brother to Mr. Soames, a shipowner of
Black wall. On 19th April this advance-note (produced) was brought to me
I cannot say who by- I paid 21. 5s. to the person who brought it-it has
been in my possession ever since.
JOHN STEPHEN WOLVERIDGE re-examined. This is the note I gave the
prisoner-there is a mark on it-I cannot say whether it is the prisoner's
- he can neither read or write.
John WHITE (policeman, R 180.) I took the prisoner on 30th April in a
lodging-house at Woolwich I told him he was charged with stealing 21. 55.
of his master-when the charge was read over to him, he said he had received
the money and lost it, and then he said that he was no servant.
Prisoner's Defence. I drew the money and lost it. The
Joun CARPENTER policeman, R 38). I produce a certificate-(read-
William Taylor, Convicted April, 1849, and confined four months)--the pri-
soner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Twelve Months.
