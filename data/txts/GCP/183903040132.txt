1076. EMMA HALL was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of March.
7lbs. weight of bacon, value 48., the goods of James Rogers.
JAMES Rogers. I keep a cheesemonger's shop at Paddington. On the
4th of March, about a quarter before three o'clock, I saw the prisoner o
out of my shop, I had been in an adjoining room-I missed a gammon of
bacon, and followed her-she had got two or three hundred yards--I took
her, and took the bacon from under her shawl-she said she was very
sorry, and hoped I would take no notice of it.
GUILTY. Aged 15.- Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor and Jury.
Confined Eight Days, and delivered to her father.
