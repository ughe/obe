   Jane Burn.  I was standing at the table, peeling potatoes, but I was not concealing the tub—I was not near it. WILLIAM PRICE( police-sergeant L  11.) I received information that this girl had been robbed—I made inquiry, and ascertained it had been taken to a place in Carlisle-lane—we went there, and found Richard Burn busily engaged in roasting a piece of beef—MR. Cole claimed it, and asked where his salt beef was—the male prisoner said, "You will find no salt beef here"—Jane Burn went from where she was to the end of the table, where there was a tub, which she partly covered—the male prisoner said he bought the beef in the New-cut the night before—Jane Burn said she had not been out that morning—after that she said she had been out, but not with the girl; and after that she said she had been out with the girl, and had lost her.
   Elizabeth Burn's Defence.  I met this girl—she asked me to tell her where she could get some tops, and I went with her—she then said she was going for some potatoes, and told me to hold her basket—I stopped above half-an-hour, went up and down, and asked a good many people if they had seen her—I took it home, and my father, not knowing who it belonged to, put one piece down to the fire—my mother had not been in two minutes before the policemen came.
   Richard Burn's Defence.  My child was seat out in the morning, about a quarter before eleven o'clock, to get two penny-worth of turnip-tops—she met this girl, and they got to play together, and then my girl came home, and brought the basket—I was ill in bed—I sent my eldest daughter out to see if she could find who it belonged to—my wife had not been in two minutes before the officer came. WILLIAM PRICE re-examined.  The woman said, if any body was punished, it ought to be the man, as she herself was innocent—she said the wished it to be sent back again, and he would not.
   ELIZABETH BURN  — GUILTY  . Aged 14.— Judgment Respited.
   RICHARD BURN  — GUILTY. Aged 43.— Confined One Year.
   JANE BURN  — NOT GUILTY.
