EDMUND HANBOROUGH JOYNON. I carry on business as William
Joynson & Sons, paper-makers, at St. Mary Cray-I have done business.
with Messrs. A. Joseph & Co.-it has been personally conducted by Mr.
Emanuel Joseph-my last invoice from the Josephs is dated February
28th, 1900---since June, 1899, I have purchased canvas to the amount
of 23 tons and some hundredweights at £13 58. per ton at Blackfriars
when this prosecution was started I still had 12 tons 7 cwt, of it in my
possession-I remember Mr. Skinner from the Post Office visiting my
premises, but I will not be sure of the date--I showed him the canvas I
had remaining, and he examined the contents of two bags in my presence
-subsequently the 12 tons were removed from my premises by Inspector
Walker; the rest of the 23 tons had been pulped-I have purchased Post
Ooffice canvas from Mr. Nash, a Post Office contractor-all that is not in
stock that I have purchased from him has been pulped.
