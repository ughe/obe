WILLIAM Ross (police-constable D 157.) On that morning, I heard
a cry-I ran down the mews, and saw the prisoner running towards
me- I took him back to the prosecutor's--he said he had done nothing-
I said, “What did you run for ?"-he said, "Has not a man a right to
run if he likes"-I went to the house and got the cloak--I found two
duplicates on him, of two coats--he said, "I hope you will bring
nothing else against me."

NEW COURT.-Friday, June 17th, 1842.
Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
1855. HENRY JONES way indicted for stealing, on the 29th of
May, 2lbs. of lamb, value 1s. ; 8 ounces of veal, value 3d.; and 8 ounces
of mutton, value 3d. ; the goods of Georgiana Upton, his mistress: to
which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
1856. JOHN MEEK was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of May,
1 dog-collar, value 9d., the goods of Frederick Elliott; to which be
pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
1857. CHARLES WILSON and HENRY WILSON were indicted
for stealing, on the 12th of January, 3 watches, value 177. 10s.; 3 necke
chains, value 111. 58.: the goods of Samuel Sharwood :-also on the 9th of
April, 130 yards of silk, value 311.; and 14 yards of velvet, value 6l.; the
goods of Thomas Howell :- also on the 2nd of May, 11 watches, value
441. ; the goods of Richard Stephen Waylett: also on the 5th of March,
56 yards of linen-cloth, value 41. 148.: 200 yards of silk, value 286;
and 4 wooden rollers, value 18.; the goods of Thomas Gardiner, and
another; to which
CHARLES WILSON pleaded GUILTY. Aged 20.
HENRY WILSON pleaded GUILTY. Aged 22.
Transported for Seven Years on the first indictment, and Seven Years
more on the second indictment.

James Smith, his master :-also, on the 1st of March, 4 handkerchiefs, value
148., the goods of George Monser, his master; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Nine Months.
