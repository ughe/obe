• Edward KEMP. I live at No. 14, Union-street, Kingsland-road. On
Tuesday morning, the 19th of July, between twelve and one o'clock, I
was standing at my street door-I heard a rattle, and a cry of "Stop
thief!"- I looked up the street, and saw two men cross from my side of
the street, and run up the turning opposite, which is Union-buildings-
I did not see where they came from I had no opportunity of seeing their
faces--they were some distance ahead of me I saw the policeman after
that I was in the middle of the road then I did not run after them till
the policeman came up to me- I then ascertained what was the matter,
and I ran with him after them they were thirty yards ahead of me before
I ran-it was dark-after I turned the corner at the top of the buildings,
there were two men close to me, coming in the opposite direction-I could
not form any judgment whether they were the same men or not-I did
not notice them-I saw the policeman lay hold of one-he walked to the
bottom of the buildings with him very quietly-he then asked what he was
taken into custody for, and said he would not go-a scuffle ensued, he
slipped out of his coat, and ran away-I did not observe his face.
