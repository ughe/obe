WILLIAM TAYLOR( police-sergeant M  14.) On the 16th of September, about a quarter before nine o'clock, I was passing a marine-store shop in Kent-street—I saw the prisoner in the shop, and a female there was weighing these pieces of lead—I then saw the prisoner take this brass-rail from his pocket and put it into the scale—I went in, and asked where he got it—he said it was his own property, he had bought it, and he was in the habit of attending sales, and was a jobbing master for him self—I said he must go to the station-house—in going along we met a female, who said to him, "Halloo, George, where are you going?"—he made her some answer which I did not hear—these are the articles.
   MR. PUGH . These are our property. GUILTY. Aged 28.— Confined Six Months.
 (There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
