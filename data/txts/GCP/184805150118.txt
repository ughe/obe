the moneys of William Fisber, from the person of Sarah Fisher.
MERS. RYLAND and LAURIE conducted the Prosecution.
SARAH Fisher. I am the wife of William Fisher, and live at Poplar. On
Monday afternoon, 3rd April, I got into an omnibus at Blackwall, for the
purpose of going to Lambeth-1 sat on the left-hand side as I got in, and
there was about room for two or three persons between me and the door
person sat between me and the door-- there was room for one or more between
me and that person-I had about 178. in silver, wrapped up in a piece of
paper, in my pocket op my right-hand side, which would be nearest the door
- it was a pocket made in the dress--the prisoner got into the omnibus in
St. Paul's churchyard—I had ascertained that my money was safe just
before-I had occasion to use my handkerchief, which was in the same
pocket, and I felt my money safe- I am quite sure of that-the prisoners
on my right, between me and the party next the door-I am quite certain
that she got in at St. Paul's-churchyard—there were more persons in the
omnibus, but not on my right side-they were sitting opposite, and at the
other end-it was nearly full--the prisoner sat quite close to me-the omni-
bus went straight on to the end of Bridge-street or Farringdon-street-the
prisoner got out there - I went to use my handkerchief again, when she got
out, and found it hanging to my pocket, and my money was gone-I named
it to a person in the omnibus, and she advised me to tell the conductor to
stop—the omnibus was stopped, and I got out-I had not observed which
way the prisoner went the conductor told me, and I went in that direction
- it was towards the bridge I met the prisoner coming back from the bridge
she was about as far up the street as Bride-court-I told her that she bad
taken the money from me-she said she had not, that she had plenty of
money-she showed me her purse, and wished me to go bome with her to see
ber respectability-I declined doing so, and gave her into custody-there wa
one half-crown among my money, and shillings and sixpences-I am quite
sure the prisoner is the only person that sat close to me, and she threw her
dress over me several times-- she had a plaid silk dress with flounces, and
visette, which she has on now-she threw her dress over my knees two or
three times, spreading it out.
