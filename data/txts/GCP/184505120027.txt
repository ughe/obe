1047. WILLIAM BEDFORD was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of
May, 1 lamb, price 11. 178., the property of John Weath and another.
WILLIAM ROGERS (policeman.) About four o'clock in the morning of
the 2nd of May, I saw the prisoner loitering about Oxford-street, and
watched him a long time at last I saw him go and untie a lamb, which
had been left a few minutes previous by a drover, who could not get it
along with the rest--he untied it, tied a handkerchief to it, and by force
drove it nearly 400 yards I followed, and asked what he was going to
do with it he said, in a confused manner, he had just found it, and was
going to take it home for a short time, till he could take it to Smithfield.
