HENRY HACKER. I am clerk of the works in charge of the Crown
land at Aldershot-I was shown a place by Tapner on Purbright Common
where a fire had taken place; it was on Crown land-about 76 feet of
gorse was burnt, it was about 10 to 12 feet wide, in some places I think a
little more-the gorse in some places exceeds five feet in height- a path-
way runs across the common; the fire was on the side of the pathway-I
only noticed it on one side particularly, it was only on one side that there
was a perceptible burning.
