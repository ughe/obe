Flactman gave me some keys, with which I went to 35, Smith Street,
Chelsea-in the front room, on the second floor, I searched the cheffioner
--in the left-hand drawer I found the six-ebambered revolver (Produced),

The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate : "On Saturday after-
noon one of my colleagues ; he is a reservist going out to the front ; came
to bid us all good bye, five of us. He said to me, Ted' that is the nick
name they call me, Wait for me; I am going to Chelsea ; we will go
together. When we got out at Sloane Square Station he said, "We will
have one more drink; I may never come back. We went and had it. Ho
made me late for my dinner. That was the cause of my wife and me
having words. I never fired the revolver, but fell against the door, and
it discharged in my hand. You have got all the rest, except that the
witness knocked me down."
