914. JAMES SHAW was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of March , two boxes, value 20s., the goods of James Powell , and that he had been previously convicted of felony.
 JAMES POWELL. I live in  Prince's-street, Westminser. On the 30th of March I heard the latch of my shop-door go, as I was in my parlour; I looked into the shop and saw the prisoner walk in - my little girl went into the shop, and said "Some one will come directly," and went to ring the bell; the prisoner at that time put something in his righthand pocket, which I thought was a shell; he then said he would call again; I saw him stoop and put something into his left-hand pocket - I said Halloo, stop! he made a rush out and broke a pane of glass; I said to Mrs. Powell,"A man has run out and taken two shells in his pocket, run and call stop thief!" - in three minutes be was brought back to the shop; he said he had broken the window - I went and put my hand into his pocket, and partly pulled out a box; the officer then took him; these are my boxes - I had nothing on but my shirt and stockings at the time.
   GEORGE CLIFFORD. I am the officer. I took the prisoner, and have the property. GUILTY.
   ROBERT TURNER. I am an officer of St. George's, Hanover-square. I have a copy of the record of the prisoner's conviction, on the 10th of September, in the tenth year of his present Majesty's reign - I was a witness, and know he is the man. (Record read.)
     GUILTY. Aged 32. - Transported for Life.
 915.  JAMES OSBORN was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of March , 3 trowels, value 5s., the goods of Joshua Faint ; and 1 trowel, value 1s., the goods of William Lewis ; and that on the 18th of February last he was convicted of felony by the name of James Morell.
 JAMES MANNING. I am a bricklayer's labourer. I was working at No. 6,  Taylor-street, Carnaby-market, on the 15th of March, which was undergoing repair - I saw the prisoner there five minutes after six o'clock; Faint had left at three because it was wet - I was going out when the bricklayer told me to go up for his jacket; I went up and saw the prisoner in the two-pair front room on his knees, with his hat beside him - he was taking the tools out of the box and putting them into his handkerchief; I stood behind him and asked what he was doing- he said he was coming to finish this job; I said,"There are some men here besides you" - he said, "I am come to do it;" - he took a trowel and began to beat some mortar - I took him in charge; he had not been at work there.
 Prisoner. You asked me what I was doing, and I said I came from Mr. Croxon, the builder; I was on my hands and knees mixing up the mortar, and you struck me. Witness. I did not.
 JOSEPH FAINT. I am a  journeyman bricklayer. I left at three o'clock, on account of the rain, and had put my tools away; these three trowels are mine.
 WILLIAM LEWIS. I am a journeyman bricklayer. This trowel is mine. I had left it in the same box; the prisoner had no right there.
 Prisoner. Faint knows me and my father and mother.
   JOSEPH FAINT. Yes, I know all the family; I came from the same village in Hertfordshire. GUILTY.
   JOHN LANDON. I am an officer. I got this certificate of the prisoner's former conviction from Mr. Clark's office; on the 18th of February last the prisoner was convicted of felony, by the name of James Morell- I was a witness and know he is the man - (certificate read.)
     GUILTY. Aged 35. - Transported for Life.
 916.  ALFRED GAZELEY and JOHN MUNN were indicted for stealing, on the 9th of March , 130 lbs. of pork, value 3l., the goods of John Ambler ; and that, on the 16th of July, the said Alfred Gazeley , was convicted of felony.
   BENJAMIN AMBLER. I am the son of John Ambler, of Ball's-pond. On the 8th of March my father killed a pig, and hung it in the front yard; I and my father sat up that night in the shed, and three other persons sat up in the stable, as we had lost three pigs before; at two o'clock in the morning the two prisoners came together - they got over two high sences into the back yard, and I saw no more of them till they were bringing this dead pig back, which had been in a new shed in the front yard; they were both carrying it - it weighed eighteen stone - we took them, and this time we saved the pig; I have seen Gazeley before.
 WILLIAM SHEPPARD. I am a groom. I was in the front yard, and saw the prisoners come to the pork; Munn untied the rope, and Gazeley took it on his back - they then both carried it.
   JOSEPH TODD. I was watching at the place, and took the prisoners.
 Gazeley's Defence. We leave it to the mercy of the Court.
 MUNN -    GUILTY. Aged 19.
     Transported for Seven Years.
   THOMAS COPE. I am an officer of Islington. I have a certificate of the former conviction of Gazeley - I took him, and was at the trial; I know he is the man.
 GAZELEY -    GUILTY. Aged 19.
     Transported for Life.

   JAMES SWAIN. I am errand-boy to Mr. Woollard.    ELIZABETH ALLEN. I lodge at Mr. Thomas Goode's, in Old Street-road.
