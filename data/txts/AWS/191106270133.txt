MAHMOUD, Omar Beni (35, labourer), robbery with violence upon
Emily Cooper and stealing from her the sum of 5s., her moneys.
Mr. Pasmore prosecuted.
Police-constable GEoRGe PROSSER, 226 G. On June 15 I was on
duty in plain clothes in Oxford Street at 1.30 a.m. I saw prosecutrix
and prisoner there. I heard the woman scream and looked in the direc-
tion. Prisoner got hold of her by the arms and swung her round.
She fell to the ground. As she was on the ground prisoner kicked her
in the back with the flat of his foot and knelt down by her side. She
was screaming all the time. I ran up as he was running away and
caught hold of him. He commenced to struggle. I twisted his arm
round his back and some money fell from his hand on the pavement:
There were no people about at the time. There were 20 or 30 there
after I got assistance. I picked up 41d. in coppers. I did not see
anybody else pick up anything. I only saw him kick her once. Before
the assault I saw them speaking for two or three minutes.
I did
not think it necessary.t watch them. He was perfectly sober.
