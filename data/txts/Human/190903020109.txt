I produce the bankruptcy file of Herbert George Gustavus Pallash and Alfred Leopold Pallash. They are described as carrying on business at 144 and 144A, Falkland Road, Tottenham, under the style of the Falkland Manufacturing Company and lately carrying on business at 39A, Langham Road, Tottenham, general dealers. It was their own petition, and the receiving order was made on the same day, March 18, 1908, and they were both adjudicated bankrupt on that day. Their preliminary examination was taken next day and is signed by both of the debtors. I produce the state ment of affairs and transcript of debtors' public examination. The statement of affairs shows liabilities over £900 and assets nil. On May 11 an order for a goods and cash account for the three years prior to the receiving order was made by the registrar. I produce those accounts. An order to prosecute under the Debtors Act of 1869 was made on December 4 against the two bankrupts and the other two defendants for aiding and abetting.
