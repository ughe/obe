PRISCILLA HERD. I am servant to Mr. James Haldane, a tailor in Bond-
street-Mrs. Sleaman has his linen to wash-I received from her a basket of
linen it was brought by the prisoner on the 28th of August--there were only
seven shirts in it instead of nine, which I had sent to be washed-these are
the two shirts, they are my master's.
Ang SLEAMAN. I am the wife of Robert Sleaman--I wash for Mr. Hal-
dade-I know these two shirts well-I gave them to the prisoner on the
28th of Aug. to take to Mr. Haldane.
John DRYDEN. I am assistant to a pawnbroker in Tothill-street-these
two shirts were pawned on the 30th of Aug., in the name of John Aldhouse
- this is the duplicate that was given of them.
WILLIAM MILLERMAN (policeman, B 95.) I took the prisoner-I told
him it was for stealing some linen-he said he knew nothing of it I received
the duplicate from Mrs. Sleaman.
