1645. ELIZABETH WEARING was indicted for stealing, on the 7th
of May, 1 pair of half-boots, value 23. 6d., the goods of John Vorley.
JOHN VORLEY. I am a boot-maker-I live in ohn's-court, Cavendish-
square-on the 7th of May, a policeman brought these boots to me-
I had seen them safe on the Saturday before, on the stand outside
my windowthey had been mended that day.
SOPHTA NOBLR. I searched the prisoner at the station-hotse- found
two duplicates on her-one was for these boots.
WILLIAM CUMMING (police-seryeant D 3.) I went with the duplicate,
and found the half-boots at the pawnbroker's.
SAMUBL HENRY BEZANT. I am a pawnbroker. The prisoner pawned
these half-boots on the 7th of May.
Prisoner's Defence. I bought the boots, and the shoes of a woman I
used to buy slippers of, when I was in service.
GUILTY. Aged .-Confined Six Months.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)

1647. GEORGE EARLE was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of
May, 12lbs. weight of iron, value 1s., the goods of George Baillie 5 and
that he had been before convicted of felony.
Joan ARMSWORTH. I am in the employ of George Bailey, of Hanwell.
I took the iron-work off an old cart on the 14th of May, and placed it in
the cart-shed-] received information, and missed it-this is it (looking
at it.)
PHILLIS HUGHEs. I live at Brentford-the prisoner brought this iron
and some bones to me-I gave him 5d. for it.
Prisoner. This is not the iron I sold her-it was some old hoops
which I gave 2d. for. Witness. I bought no other iron of him but this.
