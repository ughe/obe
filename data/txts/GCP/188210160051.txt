-I also found a tear on the shoulder of the coat-I cannot tell what that

get away-I held him till the policeman came, and he was secured-Mr.
Munday had taken one pistol from him; the other was found in the hall
-I took them both to the station, and handed them to the police I was
under the impression that I heard seven shots.
