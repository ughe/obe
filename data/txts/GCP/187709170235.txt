749. ROSETTA SKINNER (60), was indicted for a libel on Elizabeth
Mary Ann Cleveland.
Mr. BURNie conducted the Prosecutior; and Mr. Frith the Defence.
THOMAS JOHN DAVIS (Postman 478 S.W.) I deliver letters at the house
of Mr. Cleveland, 78, Lothian Road-on 9th August this post-card (produced)
was given to me by another postman, it was marked by bim “No answer"
on the same evening-I took it to the house where they bad tried to deliver
it the next morning at about 8.40 and delivered it to Miss Cleveland, it
bears the mark of the Stockwell office; it would be collected from that part
and taken to the Stockwell office.
ELIZABETH MARY ANN CLEVELAND. I live at 78, Lothian Road, and am
the prosecutrix's daughter-on 10th August I received this post-card and
handed it to my father.
Cross-examined. I have known the defendant since Easter Monday-I
have been on pretty good terms with her; we bave not been friendly lately
when we have met ; I had a little disagreement with her-I have not a very
good feeling towards her-we had no very serious quarrel -I know Jeannette
Watson - I do not know a young man named Sindy Scott-I am not engaged
-the first time I saw Jeannette Watson was at Lambeth police-court; I
know nothing about her.
Re-examined. The defendant was staying in our house, and my father
gave her notice to leave.
ALFRED CLEVELAND. I live at 78, Lothian Road, Camberwell-on 10th
Angust my daughter gave me this post-card-I do not know the writing-
made inquiries.

card-the post-card produced I wrote at her dictation; she took it to the
post-I did not write it out of my own head-I do not know anything about
Miss Cleveland.
