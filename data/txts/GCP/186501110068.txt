friars-on the morning of 12th, I was going on horseback from there to
London-bridge—I had no cart with me-it was about ten minutes before 7
--when I got into Thames-street I noticed two men rupying along towards
Blackfriars - I did not take particular notice of the first one, but I took
particular notice of the other-I caunot say whether the first one had a slop
on, or whether it was bis shirt it was something white the prisoner is the
second one-I took particular notice of bim-I am quite positive he is the
man-I then turned up Queen-street, and saw Mr. Franks' warehouse door
open, and a man standing there--the horse and cart came afterwards; I was
told that a robbery had been committed directly, and I gave a description
of the men I had seen running.
Cross-examined. l. Do you think you could identify the first man you
saw! 4. I could not; I had never seen him before; be was running as
fast as he conld, and the second man too-my horse was walking- I thought
that there was a fire, or that they harl done something wrong I thought
there was a fire, and I looked round to see where it was, after the men were
gone by-there were a few other people in the street, not many; I had never
seen the prisoner before-he bad a dark coat on; he was in the road, and
so was the whole thing did not take long; I looked at him running after
the other before I came up to him-I can't identify the first man at all.
Mr. Wood..Q. What kind of hat had the second man on A. I can't
say whether it was round or oval; it was not a high hat or a caphe had a
little hair on his chin.
COURT. Q. Did the man actually pass you! 4. Yes; close by me;
