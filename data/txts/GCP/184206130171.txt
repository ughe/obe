1894. GEORGE RICHARDSON was indicted for embezzlement.
JAMES Frith. I am a wine and spirit-merchant, in Bishopsgate-street.
The prisoner was in my service about the middle of last March--I took
him as a commercial traveller-I afterwards employed him to receive
money on my account-I received this letter from him on the 29th of
May-1 know it is his handwriting—be sent it on Sunday- I gave direc-
tions to have him apprehended the next day-there was an account due
to me from Farr and from Furnival—the prisoner has never accounted to
me for the sums due to me from them.
Prisoner. You agreed to give me 21. a week as salary, from my saying
that 158. a week was as little as I could do to travel on, and 11. 58. to live
upon ; you did this when I had been a fortnight or three weeks in your
service, in presence of your wife ; you applied to me to apply to the Gua-
rantee Society for security for 1002., but my two referrees being out of
town, I declined it, but I offered to procure you security for 100l. from
friends; your answer was, “Never mind, there is no occasion for it."
Witness. I wanted security, and they would not be security for him after
making inquiries--I only agreed to give him 11. 58. a week, and said in a
month or two I would raise bim 5s. a week, till it came to three guineas.
(Letter read) —"Dolphin, Long-alley, 29th May. Sir, with feelings
of true repentance, I make another appeal to you. I, on the other side,
give a faithful account of what I am due to you, from a consciousness that
I have acted wrong. I hope you will this time forgive me. I have a
friend who will be my security for the amount, if it be repaid, by instal-
ments; if you will give me another trial, all shall be right for the future.
I shall not absent myself, for the sake of my family. I hope this time
you will be lenient. I shall be at the warehouse to-morrow morning, at
nine o'clock. With all humility, and hopes of forgiveness, I remain, &c.
Garratt, 51. 88.; Gale, 178.; Rout, 198.; Farr, 198.; Buoyard, 16. 183
Furnival, 198.; Payne, 11. 128.- total, 111. 158."
Prisoner. You were about discharging your man Thomas, and you
said to me, “Has he said any thing to you about going away?"
said he had, and you said, "Don't you think you bad better get into con-
Versation with him, and say you have a salary of two guineas and a half a
week." Witness. Nothing of the kind.
