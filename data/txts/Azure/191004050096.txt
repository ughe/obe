specialist. Since 1906 1 have been in business for myself, at first
in Piccadill\. In April, 1907, I became chairman and managing direc-
tor of the Carlton Publicity Company, Limited, I had previously been
trading, as the Carlton Publicity Company. It was an advertising
agency and had nothing to do with the publishing Of magazines.
Felvus was employed by me as an artist a few months before I turned
the business into a limited company. I then had partner, Mr.
Aubrey Hopwood, and we sold our business to the limited company.

through making a bad debt of 22,500, the company went Into volun-
tary liquidation. I was also managing director of George Pite and
