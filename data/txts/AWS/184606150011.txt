1220. WILLIAM PITT was indicted for stealing 36 pairs of shoes, value 3L.
48.; and 32 pairs of boots, 4l. 168. ; egoods of Abraham Brandon, his master.
ABRAHAM BRANDON. I am a boot and shoemaker, and live in Hounds-
ditch the prisoner was in my service. On the 3Oth of June I gave him a
bamper with twenty-four pairs of shoes, thirty-two pairs of women's boots,
and, twelve pairs of children's boots-I told him to deliver the greater portion
of them to Mr. Joseph Johnson, in Tottenham-court-road and the remainder
to Mr. Pritchard--he never came back to me.
JOsEPH JOHNSON. I am a customer of Mr. Brandon. I ordered a quan-
tity of boots and shoes of him, and they came up as they got them finished-
the prisoner did not bring me any on the 3oth of June last yearI received
some afterwards, but not by the prisoner-] did not see him afterwards.
JOHN BULLEN police-constable H 222.) I took the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. I got as far as Chiswell-street-] had a pint of beer,
and left my goods outside ; when I came out they were gone, and I did not
like to return without them.
