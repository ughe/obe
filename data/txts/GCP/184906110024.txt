GBORGINA BOURGOM. I reside in King-street, Golden-square. I put this
letter into the post, in Oxford-street, at ten minutes past two o'clock, last
Wednesday-a friend wrote it for me, I saw 2s. put into it.
MATTHEW PEAK. After the prisoner was given into my custody, I took
the letter to the person to whom it was addressed-he opened it in my pre-
sence, and it contained 2s.
(The prisoner received a good character.) Guilty. Aged 18.-
