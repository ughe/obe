 262.  JAMES WATSON was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of November , 1 cap, value 1s. 8d., the goods of Samuel Moses.
   SAMUEL MOSES. I am a slop-seller, and live in High-street, Shadwell. I saw the prisoner on the 19th of November walk into my shop, take a cap down, and walk out; I followed, and took him three houses off, with it.
 Prisoner's Defence. It was laying at the door; I did not go into the shop.
     GUILTY. Aged 35. - Confined One Month.
 263.  THOMAS SHEARMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of November , 1 wooden frame, value 2s., an 133 lbs. of soap, value 16s., the goods of George Blakeway.
   THOMAS MORGAN. I live in Great Russell-street. I saw the prisoner on the 29th of November, take this soap and frame from Mr. Blakeway's shop; he either went round the corner, or in at the door of the shop - he carried it across the street, and then put it down on the step of a door and ran away - he was pursued and taken; I know he is the man.
   JOHN RYLES. I am a Police-constable. I heard the alarm, and met the prisoner running; I took him six or seven hundred yards from the prosecutor's.
   GEORGE BLAKEWAY. I live in Great Russell-street. The soap and frame are mine, and were in my shop; I found them on the opposite side.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had nothing to do with it. GUILTY. - Aged 25.
 Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor. Confined Three Months.
 264.  WILLIAM SINGLE was indicted for stealing' on the 30th of November , 1 window sash, value 10s., the goods of Thomas Rushton , fixed to a building, &c., against the Statute.
 2nd COUNT, stating it to be the goods of  Richard Kent Hart and William Dodd.
 3rd COUNT, stating it to be the goods of  Rowland Wilks.
   AMBROSE TIDMAN. I live in Hackney-road. I had the care of an empty house; the different persons it belonged to, (who are named in the indictment,) agreed to put me in possession of it, as the ownership was disputed- on the 30th of November I met the prisoner coming out of the house with two sashes in his hand, which I had seen secure the day before, and fixed; I asked what he was going to do with them - he said to get them repaired; the door had been wrenched from the hinges, and taken away, but he had not business there.
   CHARLES HOWELL. I had seen the prisoner at the house several times, and on that day I gave information; I saw him getting the sash out of the frame - I told Tidman, and we went and took him.

 265.  WILLIAM RANDALL was indicted for stealing on the 24th of November , 6 spoons, value 3l. 3s., the goods of Bezzett Doveton.
   JEMIMA BENTLEY. I am in the service of Bezzett Doveton, Esq., of Dorset-place. On the 24th of November I was coming from the drawing-room; I stopped in a little room, where the lady's maid and the nursery-maid sat at work - I heard a noise down stairs; I ran down, and at the butler's pantry, where the plate is kept, I saw the prisoner, who was a stranger; I asked what business he had there - I callede him a thief, and said, "You are after the plate;" he said, "I am not after the plate - I came to see whether the chimneys want sweeping or dusting;" I said, "Why did not you knock at the door?" he said he got his bread in an honester way than thieving - he took off his jacket, and shook it; I was simple enough to let him go - when he was gone I counted the plate, and missed all these soons - I ran up the steps, and saw him turn into Park-street; I went and got the officer to follow him - he brought him back, but the spoons are lost; I am certain he is the man - the spoons were all safe when he came to our house.
   SUSAN BLADWIN. I am nursery-maid at the house - I saw the prisoner take off his jacket, and shake it; we then counted the plate, and the spoons were missing.
   RICHARD AMEY. I am a Police-officer. Our serjeant gave me a description of the prisoner, and I took him; he said he was not the man, and asked me to let him call his brother - he called his brother, who was not at all like him, and the prisoner got away.
   THOMAS ARNOLD. I am a Police-officer. I took the prisoner on Saturday morning at his brother's house, in William-street; when I inquired for him a woman said he was not there, but I searched the house, and found him there asleep - he denied all knowledge of the plate.
   CHARLES LAUGHTON. I went with Arnold, and took the prisoner at his brother's; what he has stated is correct.
 Prisoner's Defence. On the 24th of November I was loading same soot away, and was in doors from half-past two o'clock till half-past four; I then went home, and was at home all night - I went out next morning as usual; I was not out of the way.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. - Transported for Seven Years.
