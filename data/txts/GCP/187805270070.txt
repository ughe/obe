EDWARD THOMAS BIGGs. I am a pawnbroker and jeweller, of Maiden-

into and 5001. worth of property taken-these ear-rings and chain are a
portion of the property, and both these rings, which latter have my
private mark scratched on them inside.
Cross-examined by Ms. METCALFE. There is no private mark on the
ear-rings.
Re-examined. I have no doubt they are mine.
HENRY MOCRORY (Re-examined). I found the chain in Mrs. Neil's
room, and the pawn-tickets of the ear-rings.

occupied by the prisoner Neil and the man who escaped.
Cross-examined by Mr. FRITH. Mrs. Fox was let out on her own reoog-
nisances last night.
