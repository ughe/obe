1899. JAMES BAYLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of
June, 1 mare, price 15l., the goods of John Arnold.
STEPHEN FLEMING. I am constable of Hornsey. The prisoner was
given into my custody on Saturday, the 6th of June, by Mr. Arnold, upon
the charge of stealing his mare-I searched him, and found a duplicate and
a Smithfield horse-ticket, in a bag containing some linen-I took charge
of the mare for the prosecutor.
Joan ARNOLD. I live at Old Weston, in Huntingdonshire, seventy
miles from London, and am a miller-I lost a mare on the 8th of June
had seen her safe on Friday, as late as nine o'clock-she was turned into a
little paddock--the gate was shut; she could not stray missed her
about six o'clock in the morning-I came off immediately to London, on
the Boston coach at the Wellington-bar I saw the mare-I knew her
immediately she was grazing, and the prisoner was lying on the grase
she seemed to have sweat a great deal -- I took hold of the mare, and be
asked what I was going to do I said if he would come with me I would
tell him-he followed me a few steps, then turned into Mr. Johnson's
fields-he then ran about a furlong, and was stopped by a person I
accused him of stealing the mare-- he said he hoped I would forgive him
it was accidentally that I found her-I did not know the prisoner I
gave the mare up to the officer.
