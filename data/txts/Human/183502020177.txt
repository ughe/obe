JOSEPH CLARK. I live with my father in Prospect-place, Newington. He is a turner—on the morning of the 20th of December, I went into the yard of our house, and found a pair of boots—there is a school-room be tween our yard and Williams'-court—it is a low building—a person in Williams'-court could throw the boots over the school-room into our yard, easily—I took the boots in to my father—somebody told me they were in the yard, and I found them—my father delivered them to the constable, in my presence—I am quite sure they were the same.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Do you mean to say they never went out of your sight till you gave them to your father? A.  Why, yes, I suppose for about five minutes—the wall of the school is in Williams'-court—there is no outer yard in Williams'-court—our yard is on the other side of the school-room from Williams'-court—they must have been thrown over the school-room—I found them about four feet from the school-room, which is about six feet broad—they were thrown about the middle of our yard—I could throw them over the school-room myself—a boy next door told me of it—he was in his master's garden. WILLIAM SMITH( police-constable L  4.) On the morning of the 20th of December, I was called to take the prisoner into custody—I met Joseph Clark—I went to his father's, and received a pair of boots from him, in his son's presence. JAMES FRANKLIN re-examined . I know these boots to be my father's—they have his mark on them.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What is the mark? A. "F. 5."—we know the size—my father marks his boots in that way—I know them to be the boots which hung on the nail, by that mark—there were others marked the same, but there was not another pair hanging over the rail, of that size—we had sold no 5's that week, and these had only come in overnight. PETER MCKEAND( police-constable M  153.) I have a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—I was present at the Town-hall, Borough, when the prisoner was tried and con victed—he is the person mentioned in the certificate ( read. )
   Cross-examined. Q.  How long have you known him? A.  I never saw him before he was taken on that charge—I cannot say whether I have seen him since—I know his brother. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Transported for Seven Years.
