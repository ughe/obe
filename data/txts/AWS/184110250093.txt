CHARLES Dawes. I keep a receiving-house for letters-the prisoner
was in my service-it was part of his duty at certain times of the day
when the letters were heavy, to assist in getting them out of the box-he
has done so repeatedly-I have seen him write-I believe the name
"Thos. Smith, 307, Oxford-street," on the face of this note, to be his
hand writing-I cannot swear to it.
Cross-eramined. Q. How long has he been with you T A. Nearly
seven years, four of which he lived in my house-it was King's duty more
immediately to attend to the letters-towards four or five o'clock, when
the letters got heavy, the prisoner assisted King-when I was present, I
did not require it-I had the highest opinion of the prisoner until this
charge was made-I had a good character with him when be came to
me.
GUILTY. Aged 25.-Transported for Twelve Years.
