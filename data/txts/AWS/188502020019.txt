252. HENRY ALLPRESS (40), Stealing a wateh of James Freeman
from his person.
MR. MUIR Prosecuted; Mr. PURORLL Defended.
JAMEs FREEMAN. I am a bookbinder, of 2, Playhouse Yard-on 30th
August, about 3 p.m., I was at Snow Hill railway-station with my son-
s train came in, I opened a carriage-door, and my son went in first-I
was about to follow him, but was hustled by one man on my right, one
on my left, and one behind-I looked behind and found a person I did
not know had got hold of a man named Florence, who has been convicted
-I found my guard dangling and missed my gold wateh, worth 151.--I
never saw the prisoner till I was at Guildhall.
Cross-ecamined. This was at the darkest part of a very dark station-
there was not a great crowd.
JAMEs HENRY RAOKLEY (City Deteetive). On August 30th, at 3 p.m.,
I was with Cross on Snow Hill, and saw Mr. Freeman and his son followed
by four men who I know by sight well-the prisoner is one of them-
they went on to the platform, and two men named Florenee and Holt
were there the prisoner pushed Florence against Mr. Freeman and
Florence took Mr. Freeman's wateh chain, and with his other hand took
the wateh off the chain-I seized him, and Mr. Freeman said That man
has got my wateh "-I was struggling with Florence and the other man
when the prisoner, who was behind Florence, ran and jumped into a
second-class carriage-the other man got away from me and jumped in
front of the engine and ran up the tunnel-Holt went up the stairs and
Cross took him-Florence and Holt were charged, convicted, and sentenced
to seven years' penal servitude.
Cross-eramined. The prisoner was with the others on Snow Hill, who
were all within four feet of each other; he was behind Florence-I after-
wards saw him sitting in the station and said to the inspector 44 What is
that man here for I want im"-he was by himself-I had not the
slightest idea he was there.
Re-etainined. The prisoner and Florence were talking together-the
prisoner's description was cireulated and he answers to it-they all
turned round once or twice.
