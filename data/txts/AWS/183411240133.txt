126. WILLIAM SINGLETON was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of
November, 1 pair of half-boots, value 68. 6d., the goods of Mary Dickens.
WILLIAM BEDPORD. I am a policeman. On the 14th of November, I
went to a coffee-shop in Church-street, Bethnal-green saw the prisoner
there, in company with six others, sitting in a box-I looked round, and
went away, and got another officer-we went and searched the persons
there-I found nothing on them, but under the seat I found an old pair of
shoes-I then looked at the prisoner's feet, and he had this new pait of
half-boots on, and he owned the old shoes-he said he had bought these
new boots in Petticoat-lane, and had saved the money by a peuny or two+
pence at a time.
