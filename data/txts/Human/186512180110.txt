Q.  Are you in the family way by him? ( The witness did not answer. ) A.  I have not told Mickle john that I never gave him any towel at all—I believe I said I had not, till I remembered—I had forgotten it at first, but I afterwards told Micklejohn that if he found an old cloth marked "Stable," that I had lent it to him. GUILTY.
   He PLEADED GUILTY ** to a former conviction of felony in the name of   John Snellgrove , in November, 1862, Sentence, Confined Eighteen Months .—Seven Year's Penal Servitude .
   There were two other indictments against the prisoner for larceny.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, JANUARY  8, 1866.
