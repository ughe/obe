   NEW COURT .— Thursday, August  1 st , 1889.
   Before Mr. Recorder.
 632. MARY COLLINS  (26) , Feloniously uttering a forged Bank of England note, with intent to defraud.
   MR. WOODFALL Prosecuted. JESSIE RUSCONI. I am the wife of Arthur Rusconi, who keeps the Black Swan, Ryder Street, Leicester Square—I know the prisoner as a customer—on 28th June, about 11. 50 p. m., the prisoner came in and handed me this note, and asked if I would oblige her with change for a £6 note—I handed it to my husband in a room at the back, he gave me £3 10s. in gold, and told me to take 30s. from the silver in the till—I left the note with him, and gave the money to the prisoner—while she was counting it my husband came and said to me, "Who gave you this note? "Isaid, "This person here"—he said to the prisoner, "I don't think this is good"—she said, "I am not used to paper money, and I did not know but what it was good; my mistress gave it to me to get change; she lives in Panton Street, Haymarket"—she gave back the money to my husband, and he called a constable.
