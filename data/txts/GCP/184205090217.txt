1684. JAMES CUMMINGS was indicted for bigamy.
SUSAN COLLIVER. I live in Church-street, Greenwich. On the 10th
of January last I was married to the prisoner by license at Stoke Damerel
Church, Devonshire. I lived with my mother up to that time at Stoke
Damerel-she is a pensioner's wife-I lived with the prisoner a week, and
then some other woroan came and claimed him I had no money-he con
ducted himself well-it is I that prosecute.
Cross-examined by Mr. Payne. Q. I believe you or your friends paid
the fees for the marriage ? A. Yes--the prisoner was in the Sappers and
Miners--I never heard that his other wife had been guilty of felony and
imprisoned, and that led him to enlist in the Sappers and MinersI did
not send word that if he would pay the money and the wedding expenses
there would not be anything done to him-I sent to him to say I heard
he was married we lived together one week-he left me because the com-
pany came to Woolwich-he was obliged to go-I intended to follow
the month after-he did not leave me the first wife came to me herself
I did not learn from her how long she was absent from him I bad known
the prisoner seven weeks before I married him-he came down to that
place with his regiment.
