 1138.  JOSEPH BRYAN was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of August , one handkerchief, value 3 s., the goods of a man unknown, from his person.
   JAMES WAYLING. I am a constable. I was in St. James's-square, and saw the prisoner in company with a gang of about twenty, going along by the side of the Queen's carriage. I saw him clap his right hand quickly into his bosom. I immediately seized him, and took the handkerchief out of his bosom. Furzeman collared him also, and brought him away.
   JOHN FURZEMAN. I am a constable. I saw twenty or thirty thieves with the prisoner. I saw him follow a gentleman in the crowd, pull his handkerchief out, and put it into his bosom. I lost the gentleman in the crowd, there was so many of them, that it was difficult to get the prisoner out. He said he picked it up; that was impossible, he could not pick it up in the crowd.
 Prisoner's Defence. The handkerchief was thrown on my arm, two or three people wished to take it off - I did take it off.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. Transported for Life.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1139.  JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 22d of August , one watch value 3 l.; one piece of ribbon, value 2 d., and one key, value 2 d., the goods of John Napier , from his person.
   JOHN NAPIER. I live in Grand Junction-street, Whitefriars. On the 22d of August, at half-past four o'clock, I was in St. James's-square, and felt my watch going from my pocket, a great many people were about. I looked down and saw the prisoner pulling it from my pocket, and saw it in his hand. I seized his hand with it, he stooped down and dropped it. Butcher picked it up. I gave him in charge.
   JOHN BUTCHER. I am a carpenter. I was in the square facing Mr. Napier, and saw him seize the prisoner round the waist. I saw the watch at his heels. I forced myself down and got it.
   WILLIAM HURST. I live in Richards's-buildings, Shirelane. I was with Mr. Napier, he missed his watch, we both seized the prisoner. I saw him drop it.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in the square, a parcel of people shoved me into the prosecutor's hands, and said I had robbed him.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Transported for Life.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1140.  ELIZA WHITTINGHAM was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of August , one shawl, value 9 s. 6 d., the goods of James Perry.
   JANE PERRY. I am the wife of James Perry, we live in Chequer-alley. About three o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoner came to speak to me. I missed the shawl immediately she left. She came again in the evening and I detained her.
   TIMOTHY PERRY. I am an apprentice to Mr. Cassels, a pawnbroker, Old-street; the prisoner pledged the shawl with me.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 30. Fined One Shilling, and Discharged.

 1142.  EDWARD DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of September , nine pieces of timber, value 5 s., the goods of William Woods.
   WILLIAM POWELL. I am servant to Mr. William Woods, a carpenter, who lives at Hampstead. This timber was in a house next to the prisoner's. About ten o'clock at night, I saw him go in with a candle, and bring some wood out. I went in and missed nine peices.
   GEORGE FURNAN. I went to the prisoner's house, and found seven peices of timber.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner pleaded distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. Fined One Shilling, and Discharged.
