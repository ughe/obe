THIRD COURT.-Wednesday, January, 17th, 1906.
Before J. A. Rentoul, Esq., K.O.
186. HENRY PAINTER (31), Stealing a gelding and set of harness
belonging to Charles Alfred Lawrence.
Mr. Jones, for the Prosecution, offered no evidence.
Nor GUILTY. (See next oase.)
187. RICHARD DAVID BULLIMORE (40), ROBERT ROBERTS
(60), WALTER SMITH (31), THOMAS WALSH, and HENRY PAINTER
(31), Stealing a gelding, a cart, and set of harness belonging to William
Barnard. ROBERTS PLEADED GUILTY.
No evidence was offered against PAINTER. Not Guilty.

MR. WING Defended Bullimore.
