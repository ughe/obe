1324. JAMES HENNESSEY , JAMES FAGAN , PATRICK FA GAN, and ABRAHAM ATTRIL were indicted for stealing on the   7th of April , 26lbs. value 3 s  ., belonging to John Sherrott and others and fixed to a building; against the Statute &c. JOHN SHERROTT. I live in Lower John-street, Commercial-road, and am one of the trustees of my father—he has a house at the corner of stone-stairs, Ratcliff. On the 7th of April I received information, and went to that house on the 8th and nearly all the lead had been stripped off to that house and the fiat—one piece of lead has been shown to me, which weighs 26lbs.—I compared it with the roof, and to the best of my belief, it came from that building.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  When had you seen it safe? A.  I do not think I had been on the house before. JOHN EDWARD KIMBER. I am in the employ of Mr. Jeffereys, a butcher in Broad-street, Ratcliff-highway. When I came home froth carrying out my meat, I saw Hennessey standing with his back up against a brick wall, and looking down Stone's stairs—I called another boy, and we agreed to watch Hennessey—I saw him come up Stone-stairs by the corner of Mr. Sherrott's house, with something under his frock—we both turned out, ran Beross the road, and caught him—just as we got to him, he dropped this lead—I picked it up, and put it under the block in out shop. ROBERT WITTLETON. I am a policeman, On the 7th of April I found the prisoner Hennessey and this lead in the butcher's shop—I went to Mr. Sherrott's house, and the lead fitted it exactly. WILLIAM BASLY. I am a policeman, I was at the station when Hen nessey was brought in—I asked him what he was crying for—he said he was brought there for some lead stolen from stone-stairs; that he did not cut the lead, but some one else did. JOHN SHERROTT re-examined , I have a fifth share of the house at the corner of Stone-stairs, Ratcliffit had been unoccupied for the last five months. THOMAS MANNING. I live in Crown-street, St. George's On the Wednesday in Easter week I saw Attril and another boy go to a marine store shop, on Stepney-causeway, with some lead in a red nightcap.
   Hennessey . I saw the lead, and went down and took it up.
   HENNESSEY— GUILTY. Aged 13, Recommended to mercy by the Jury. Confined One Month.
 J. and P.  FAGAN , and ATTRIL— NOT GUILTY.
