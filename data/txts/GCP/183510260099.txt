MR. ELLIS conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN MATTHEWS. I am servant to Mr. James Lee, a butcher. The
prisoner came to his shop on Saturday, the 3rd of October-be wanted a
bit of beef-I offered to take 7d. for it-he cut it into slices, and gave
my master a half-crown-I saw it-the prisoner came to the shop on the
Saturday following, and asked for another bit of beef, which my master
sold him I do not know what it came to, but I saw him tender a half-
crown to my master, and he was taken in the shop-I am sure he is the
same person who came before.
TEVE
JAMES LEE. On Saturday, the 3d of October, I was having my dinner,
and my young man asked if I would take any less than 6d. a pound for
this beef-I saw it sold, and the prisoner gave me a bad half-crown-I
put it into a drawer, in the desk, where there was no other silver, and af-
terwards gave it to the beadle—the prisoner came again on the 10th-I
sold him a piece of beef, which came to 6d.-he gave me another bad half-
crown-I said that it was bad, and asked if he had any more he said he
had, and was about tendering me another, when he was taken-he said he
had never been in the shop before.
Prisoner. I never was in his shop till the day I was taken.

- in taking him from Guildball, at the corner of Goldsmith's-hall,
he slipped his hand from the handcuffs, and ran off, he was stopped by an
officer.
