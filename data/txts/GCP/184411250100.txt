59. JOHN ANDRÉWS was indicted for stealing 1 breast-pin, value
248., the goods of William Spear, from his person.
ROBERT HODGMAN. I am a glass-cutter. On the 28th of Oct. I was
in Fleet-street, and, from some circumstance, I thought it necessary to
take the prisoner into custody about half-past three o'clock.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. Did you take him before Goff
camel A. Yes--I cut glass for Mr. Green, in Gravel-lane- I worked
for him twenty years ago, and have been with him now about three months
-I was once taken into custody as I was going to work, twenty-one or
twenty-two years ago I was supposed to be in company with a pick-
pocket, but I was discharged instantly that was the first time I ever was
in trouble-I never was charged with passing anything in my life, nor
ever in Newgate, any other time than the time I told you of, and never
was in any other prison in my life.
Jury. Q. What induced you to take the prisoner ? 1. I saw him
take another pin from another gentleman-I took him about ten yards from
Bride-lane--the prosecutor lost his pin in the Poultry.

said, “I have no pin about me, I have lost my own pin" - I took him
to the station, and found inside his coat these three pins; and in his out-
side pocket, before I got to the station I found this other pin, which is
partly broken-I found this silver card-ease in his trowsers pocket.
