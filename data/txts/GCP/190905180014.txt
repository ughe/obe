WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER, officer of the Mint, said he had examined
the three coins, but owing to the way they had been dealt with as
exhibits, paper being pasted over a great part of them, he could not
say whether they were all from the same mould.
Verdict, Guilty. A number of convictions were proved.
Sentence, Nine months' imprisonment for each of the three offences,
the sentences to run concurrently.
BEFORE MR. JUSTICE DARLING.
(Wednesday, May 19.)
DOVE, James (24, carman), and BAKER, Charles (23, carman);
both feloniously and violently assaulting Lilian Raymond, and
against her will feloniously ravishing and carnally knowing her.
Mr. Warburton prosecuted; Mr. Huntly Jenkins defended Dove;
Mr. Lawrie (at the request of the Court) defended Baker.
Verdict, Dove, Not guilty; Baker, Guilty.
Sentence, Baker, Five years' penal servitude.
