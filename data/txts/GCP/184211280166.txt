Before Mr. Recorder.
244. JOHN BAKER was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of Dec., 1
shawl, value 16. 58., the goods of James Cook.
MARIA Coox. I am the wife of James Cook, and live at Deptford. On
the 2nd of Dec. I left home about ten minutes before eleven o'clock, leaving
my shawl on a sofa in my room-I returned in about ten minutes, and it was
gone.
HARRIET AUSTINS. I am the wife of Jessee Austins, and live next door
to Mrs. Cook. On the 2nd of Dec. I saw the prisoner watching my window
- he then went away, and returned again-having been robbed the day before,
I watched him-he kept watching about for some minutes, then left my win-
dow, and went past the door- I went to the door to look after him, but lost
sight of him for a moment I then saw him coming out of the prosecutor's
house with something concealed under his jacket-I caught hold of him, and
found the scarf on him.
Francis Baker (police-constable R 11.) I took him into custody, and
• received the shawl.
Mrs. Cook re-examined. This is my shawl.
Guilty. Aged 13.-Judgment Respited.
