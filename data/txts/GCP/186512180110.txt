PHILLIPS, Mayor.
Q. Are you in the family way by him? (The witness did not answer.)
A. I have not told Micklejohn that I never gave him any towel at all
believe I said I had not, till I remembered- I had forgotten it at first, but
I afterwards told Micklejohn that if he found an old cloth marked "Stable,"
that I had lent it to him.
AGUILTY.
He PLEADED GUILTY ** to a former conviction of felony in the name of John
Snellgrove, in November, 1862, Sentence, Confined Eighteen Months. Seren
Years' Penal Servitude.
There were two other indictments against the prisoner for larceny.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, JANUARY 8, 1866.
