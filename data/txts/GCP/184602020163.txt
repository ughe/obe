626. CHARLOTTE FIELDER was indicted for stealing 2 breast-pins
and chain, value 158.; the goods of James Hone, from his person.
JAMES Hone. I am a mariner-I live in Crosstree-lane, Deptford--on the
night of the 16th of Jan. I was walking in High-street-I saw the prisoner-I
knew her before-she introduced her arm through mine and we walked a short
distance-she gave me a chuck under the chin, and shortly after she repeated
it-I put my hand up, and missed a union-pin which I had in my scarf-I
know I had it about three minutes before I missed it-I laid hold of the pri-
soner, and called for the police--the policeman came after I had held the pri-
soner about a quarter of an hour-I had only removed a short distance from
where I laid hold of the prisoner-I searched for the pin with a light after the
policeman came-I found it down on the pavement near where we were I
knew her before-this is my signature to this deposition--there was some-
thing read over to me-I knew this was a chargeI thought I was going to
sign what I had stated--it is here said, "At balf-past eleven last night I was
walking in High-street; the prisoner, of whom I knew nothing whatever, came
and put her arm through mine."
Prisoner. He did not charge me with stealing the pin. Witness. I do not
know what it was that was read over-I did not charge her with stealing the
pin-sbe gave me a chuck under the chin-I felt the scarf draw up she was
in liquor--the pin might fall out.
JOSEPH WHITE (police-constable R 330.) I heard a cry, and came up-
the prosecutor had got hold of the prisoner by the sbawl-be charged her with
stealing his pin-he said she chucked him twice under the chin, and the last
time he felt the scarf draw up-I sent him for a light, and found the pin
about a yard from the place.
Nor GUILTY.
