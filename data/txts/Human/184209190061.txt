   Prisoner's Defence.  I had not the least intention of taking any thing, or I should have gone off before; I had been asleep in the room for three-quaiters of an hour. LEWIS WILLIAMS. He pretended to be drunk, but in my opinion he was quite sober. ROBERT SUTTON re-examined.  He was quite sober. GUILTY. Aged 38.— Transported for Ten Years.
