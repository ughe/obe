THIRD COURT.-Wednesday, May 31st, 1899.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
425. BONI JOSEPHS (25), Feloniously forging an endorsement on an
order for payment of £25, with intent to defraud.
MR. ROOTH Prosecuted, and MR. SANDERSON Defended.
CHARLES BARTLETT. I am a boy messenger, of 56, Ranelagh Road,
Pimlico—on May 1st I was called to the Gaiety Buffet, where I saw the
prisoner and another man--one of them said, “Take this to 29, Fins-
bury Circus, and wait for an answer," handing me a letter-I went and
BAW Mr. Weedon, and gave him the letter-he gave me a letter, which
took back to the Buffet, and handed to one of the men-I told them that
the gentleman said he could not give me the money, as the signature was
not on the back of the cheque—he paid me, and I went away.
Cross-examined. I said before the Magistrate that the other man gave
me the envelope, and then later on I said it was the prisoner.
