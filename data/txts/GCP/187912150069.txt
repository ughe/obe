117. JOHN AIREY (45) and WILLIAM KELLY (30). Unlawfully
obtaining by false pretences from Maurice Lindsay 108. 6d., with intent to
defraud. Also for conspiracy, and obtaining various sums from 22 other
persons by false pretences
Messrs. Gorst, Q.C., POLAND, MONTAGU WILLIAMS, and AUSTIN METOALYE
Prosecuted; Messrs. WILDRY WRIGHT and BroXTON Hioks defended
Airey, and Mr. T. C. Jarvis defended Kelly.
MAURICE LINDSAY. I am a merchant of 26, Milk Street-in March last
I wanted a small loan and saw an advertisement in a daily paper, in
consequence of which I wrote to a person named Buer, of London Bridge
Approach, in answer to which I received a form in an envelope-before I
filled it up I took it to Mr. Buer's to ask him if all the questions were to be
filled up the name of Buer was up, and I saw Kelly there--he said they
need not all be filled up, so I took it away and filled it up and sent it to
the office-I received no answer, and in about a week after I went to the office
again and saw Kelly-I asked him why the money had not been granted,
and he said he had not received the form back from the surety on the first
visit I had paid a fee of 108. 6d. at Kelly's request before I paid it he said
*I shall require a security," and I gave him the name of Mr. Rogers, 6,
Mount Villas, Upper Sydenham-he gave me no receipt for the 108, 6d. -
said my surety had not received any letter-Kelly and he could not under
stand it, and said a letter had been written, and he showed me a letter
copied in copying ink in a book and he said he would write Again- little
more than a week elapsed, during which I saw Rogers-I saw Kelly again
and I believe he said he would write again about it -- I took no further notice
and never had the loan or my 108. 6d. back.
Cross-examined by MR. WRIGHT. At that time I was a clerk in my
father's office receiving 801, or 907. a year, that was not my whole income
-the defendants asked me my whole income and I told them it was about
1907, per annum-it wanted time to calculate ; my income changes they
said they should require a surety-I believe they said he must be a responsible
man-I said Rogers was a responsible man ; & householder-I did not tell
them he was a gardener at weekly wages I put it in the forme eked
out his income by letting apartments, and I was a lodger in his house
swear I was not in his debt in my lodging account-Rogers I believe is
not here I was not told at a subsequent interview with the defendants
that Rogers had absolutely declined to be my surety-I last saw Rogers
about three or four months ago-he has now moved, not far from where
he lived.
