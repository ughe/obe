place, Hampstead-road. It is my dwelling-house, and is in the parish of
St. Pancras-on Wednesday, 20th March, I went to bed about half-past ta
o'clock--I was not the last up-I have a son who comes in after me-I was
awoke about four in the morning by a noise-I sat up in bed, and called,
"Who's there?"-I got no answer-I was going to lie down again, and saw
a man in the room, between me and the window I called out," Tom
thinking it might be my son-he made no answer, but walked out at the
door, on to the staircase-I called out, “Thieves !" struck a light, and
followed him—when I got into the shop, I found the shop-window pushed
down, leaving room enough for a person to get in- I had left it secure when
I went to bed-I fastened the shutters myself.
Prisoner. Q. Can you swear that I am the person that was in your
room? A. No- I did not fasten the shop-window, but I am sure it was
shut, and the shutters were fastened.
