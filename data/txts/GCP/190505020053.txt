SAMUEL CROOKER (Detective, City). About 6 p.m. on April 5th I went
to 29, Cowley Road, Brixton, Mr. Savage's stable-about 10.15, as I was
standing at the gate with Mr. and Mrs. Savage, Smith came to the bottom
of the road-Mrs. Savage said, “This is the man who called this after
noon”—I let him come by the garden gate--he went up to the stable
door, he examined the padlock, and jumped up and looked over the top-
he then walked away-I went up to him and told him I was a police
officer-I asked him what he was doing at that door-he said, "Nothing"
-I took him to the station, where he was charged-he said, "I am
innocent."

being wanted at Grimsby on a judgment warrant--the next day he
