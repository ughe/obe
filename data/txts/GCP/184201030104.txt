OLD COURT.—- Friday, January 7th, 1842.
Third Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
534. WILLIAM TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of
January, 1 pewter pot, value 18., the goods of James Howard ; to which
he pleaded
GUILTY.-Confined Two Months.
535. SUSAN ALLUM and JAMES IRON were indicted for steal.
ing, on the 4th of January, 20 lbs. weight of coals, value 3d., the goods
of George Knight, the master of Allum; to which
ALLUM pleaded GUILTY.-Confined Three Months.
JOHN JAMES ALLEN (police-constable E 159.) On the morning of the
4th of January I was on duty in Regent-square, St. Pancras ; about a
quarter before eight o'clock, the prisoner Iron came with an empty basket
past No. 46, Regent-square, he looked down the area, went further on,
returned, and passed several times-he went down a court in Sidmouth-
street, returned again about twenty-five minutes after eight, and went to
the area gate, he again looked down, and stepped back-he went to the gate
again-it appeared to be opened by somebody-he went down the area,
and came up in about seven minutes, with the basket full of coals- I went
up to him, and told him he must go to the station-he asked what for-1
said, “On suspicion of stealing the coals"-on the way to the station, he
said if I would let him go he would never do it again- I took the female
prisoner into custody at Mr. Knight's house, No. 46.
GEORGE KNIGHT. I live at No. 46, Regent-square. The prisoner
Allum was my cook-in consequence of suspicion, the policeman was de-
sired to watch-after the male prisoner was taken, I told the female I had
a charge to make against her-she asked what for- I said, “For stealing
property "-she said she had not taken any thing, but afterwards she said
she had been in the habit of having her futher down to clean the knives
in the morning, and occasionally gave him broken victuals-I said she had
given him other things besides-she said she had not-1 accused her of
the coals, and she said she had done it more than once-I have another
servant, who was ill at the time, and had not got up.
