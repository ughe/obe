1218. THOMAS WARD and JOHN SKELTON , stealing 4 bushels of oats, value 10 s  .; the goods of John Edwards Bennett , their master; and WILLIAM RAYMENT, for feloniously receiving the same.
   MR. PARRY conducted the Prosecution. JOHN EDWARDS BENNETT. I am a hay and corn salesman, at King's Arms Wharf, Belvedere-road, Lambeth—I contract to supply hay and corn to the Government—Ward has been in my employ for eight or ten years as carman, and Skelton from twelve to fifteen years—he was granary-man—it was the carman's duty to draw oats to the barracks—I have a very slight knowledge of Rayment—it was Skelton's duty, as granary-man, to load Ward's cart—Ward takes out, in the course of a day, twenty or thirty quar ters—I was in the habit of supplying the Regent's-park Barracks—I sent there every morning twelve quarters of oats, in twenty-four sacks—Rayment has never bought corn of me—I was not on the 3rd April to supply him with a sack of oats—Skelton and Ward had no authority from me to supply him with a sack of oats that day—I had employed Romain and Garforth to watch my premises at six o'clock that morning.
   Cross-examined by MR. HUDDLESTON? Q.  Were you ever in partnership? A.  Yes; that has been terminated from two to three years—I never was a bankrupt—no trustees have been concerned for me or for the benefit of my creditors—I have fourteen or fifteen carmen besides Ward—I have no other regular granary-man but Skelton—there is a man employed occasionally, if an extra man is wanted—I send out about thirty-six quarters of oats in a day, and hay and straw—no man takes corn but Ward, except on particular occasions—he always takes the corn to the barracks.
   Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL . Q.  Did you give this particular order to Ward that day? A.  There was no particular order, because it was his regular duty—suppose another order was to be taken out the same day, it would not be his duty to take it at that time—it was his duty to go to the barracks, and no other man—if he had oats to take elsewhere the same day, it would not be his duty to take them together—he had the twenty-four sacks for the barracks, and I never allowed him to take any other—I do not pretend to speak to this sack or these oats—my foreman is not here. WILLIAM ROMAIN( police-sergeant, L  38).
