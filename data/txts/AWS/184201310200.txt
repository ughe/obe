854. JAMES ELLIOTT was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of Ja.
nuary, 7lbs. weight of suet, value 38., the goods of John Vooght, his
master: and SARAH SAUNDERS, for feloniously receiving the same,
well knowing it to have been stolen against the Statute, &c.
ALFRED CAMP. I am apprenticed to John Vooght, a butcher, in Lark
hall-lane, Clapham. About half-past eight o'clock, on the 26th of Ja.
nuary, I went into a timber-yard adjoining our premises, and saw a bundle
of fat tied up in a handkerchief-.I took it in, and my mistress opened it-|
took it back, and put it into the same place- believe it to be my master's
fat-on my afterwards going to the spot it was goneI fetched my master
-he told me to watch in one place while he watched in another, and at a
quarter-past nine he called to me-he said he had got the prisonerI
had seen the prisoner wearing such a handkerchief as the fat was in, round
his neck that morning.
Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. You did not see him do any
thing with A. No.
JOHN VOOGHT. The prisoner Elliott was in my service. On the night
of the 26th of January I was outamy apprentice Camp came to me--in
consequence of what he said I told him to watch-I saw the prisoner coming
home-I asked him where he had been-he told me--I asked him what be
had taken there-he said, 66 Nothing"--this is mutton suet.
