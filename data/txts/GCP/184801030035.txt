MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
MARY FITZSIMMONS. On 17th Dec. I was in the service of Major-Gene-
ral Fleming, who was lodging in the house of Mr. Bright, in Baker-street--
my mistress sent me out about seven o'clock that evening, I returned about
ten minutes to eight-when I came up the stairs as far as the drawing-room
door, I lighted my candle from one on the slab outside the drawing-room door
- I smelt a great smell of lucifer matches coming from my mistress's bedroom
up stairs- I went up, the door was wide open- I looked through the hinge-part
of the door, and saw one man, I heard two speak the man I saw was at the
table at the foot of Mrs. Fleming's bed-there were two dressing-cases and a
Bible on the table—the dressing-cases were kept apart from each other, but
when I looked through the door one was on the top of the other--I had seen
them about six o'clock-the man was settling one on the top of the other-I
did not see him lift them, but he was fixing one on the other-I took a good
view of the man-it was the prisoner Phillipson-I have no doubt whatever
about his being the person-I shut the door, took hold of the handle, and
screamed "Murder! General Fleming, there are robbers in your room"--I made
as much noise as I could- I held the door outside, and they pulled inside-
they got it open, rushed out, knocked me down, and kicked me-I did not see
them-it was dark—if Mrs. Fleming came out of the drawing-room she would
have an opportunity of seeing the persons who came down the stairs
Phillipson was brought back to the house in a few minutes I knew him

MARTHA GARDINER. I am housemaid to Mr. Bright, of No. 11, Baker-
street. On the evening of 17th Dec., I let in Mary Fitzsimmons-about
three minutes afterwards I beard her screaming up stairs--I thought she
was in a fit-I ran up the kitchen stairs, and saw two men running down
from the drawing-room stairs-I saw the face of Phillipson-tho men ran
round George-street-I saw Phillipson stopped-I was near him when he
was stopped—I am sure he was the same person that had passed me in the
house.

John RAGSDALE. I am an ironmonger, in Croydon-street About eight
o'clock, on the night of 17th Dec., I was near the corner of George-street,
and Manchester street, and saw two persons running - Phillipson was one I
stopped him-Martha Gardiner was within about ten yards of him at the
timehe said he was not the person, it was the man running on the other
side-I could not see the other so as to recognize him again.
John COOPER policeman, D 84.) On the night of 17th Dec, I heard a
cry of “Stop thief I" and saw Ragsdale stop Phillipson-he said, " I am
not the man"I took him back to the prosecutor's house, searched him
there, and found a latch-key and 4d., a pair of gloves, and a handkerchief-I
afterwards found on him at the station this black bag, which I produce it
would hold a large quantity of things-1 then returned to the prosecutor's
house, and this lucifer-box was given me containing matches, and a piece of
wax taper-the latch key found on him will not open the door of the house
in Baker-street.
