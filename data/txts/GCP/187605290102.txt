own responsibility-Francis also introduced Mr. May (See last case), who had

ledger--I have not been paid for any of those things-the total amount
supplied to Turner and Francis was 441. odd—I presented Dixon's bill at
the South-Western Bank, where it was made payable, but there was no
account there and never had been, and it was dishonoured-I then went to
Dixon's place, which was worth about 251. a year, and a shoemaker was at
work in the back parlour.
Cross-examined. Francis introduced May about 6th October-Turner
