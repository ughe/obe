 1056.  JOHN ROACH was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of March , 1 watch, value 30s., and 1 watch-chain, value 1s., the goods of Edward Grubb , from his person.
 EDWARD GRUBB. I am a  labourer; I was walking with my wife in Lower Berner-street, Commercial-road, between eight and nine o'clock, in the evening of the 25th of March - just as I was turning a corner the prisoner came across the way, and snatched my watch; he ran off- I pursued, and did not lose sight of him till he was taken with it.
   JOHN JEFFORD. I am an officer. I saw the prisoner running - he met me, and I stopped him; I tripped my foot against a scraper, and as I arose I saw him throw something from his hand; the prosecutor came up, and said he had taken his watch.
   GEORGE ROBINS. I took up the watch.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was playing, and heard the cry of Stop thief! I ran, as other boys did - the officer stopped me, and the prosecutor said he had lost his watch, but he did not believe it was me, as there was a corner turned.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1057.  STEPHEN SIBLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of March , 2 shoes, value 5s.; 2 shirts, value 3s., and 1 duck frock, value 1s., the goods of William Wilson.
 WILLIAM WILSON. I am a  mariner. I lost the articles stated out of the cabin of my vessel, which lays in the river; the prisoner was on board - I told him I had missed them, but I have never seen them since.
   JOHN REED. I am mate on board the ship. The prisoner was an apprentice on board- I know there was nothing lost out of the shop till he came on board; the prosecutor lost a pair of shoes, two shirts and a frock on the 22nd of March; I questioned the prisoner about them; he gave me no answer - I asked where he slept; he said at home; I said I would send to his father, which I did - he then absconded from the ship, and was not taken for same case; I then asked him what he had sold the shoes for, and he said half a crown.
   JOHN LITTLE. I am a Thames Police-officer. I took the prisoner - I said he was charged with stealing Wilson's clothes; he said he had sold them to different Jews in Houndsditch - I went with him there, but he could not point them out.
 Prisoner. This is the first time I ever stood at a bar; I hope you will have mercy.
 GUILTY. - Aged 16. -    Judgment Respited.
 1058.  JAMES STAMFORD was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of March , 1 wheelbarrow, value 5s., the goods of Charles Evans.
   JAMES POSFORD. I am a Police-constable. On the evening of the 30th of March I saw the prisoner and another man - the prisoner was wheeling the barrow down Tunbridge-street; I crossed the New-road, towards them; the prisoner let go the handles of the barrow, and ran away towards Battle-bridge - I knew him very well; I went after him, saw him come out of a penny show, and stopped him.
   CHARLES EVANS. This barrow is mine, and was taken from my shop window at St. Pancras.
 The prisoner received an excellent character.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Confined Three Months.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1059.  MARY STEVENS was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of February , 1 shirt, value 5s.; 1 blanket, value 3s.; 1 pillow, value 2s., and 1 pillow-case, value 6d., the goods of Ann Shaw.
   ANN SHAW. I am a widow, and live in Laystall-street, Holborn. The prisoner was my lodger, and a very good lodger; she came as a married woman - I could not get my rent of them; I put a padlock on the door - Hoskins, a man she lived with, brought the key in an hour afterwards - I missed these articles stated, and found the duplicates of them in a corner of the room.
   JOSEPH HUETSON. I was in the service of a pawnbroker in Gray's Inn-lane. I have a blanket, pawned by the prisoner on the 3rd of November.
   JAMES STANLEY. I am servant to a pawnbroker, in North-place, Gray's Inn. I have a shirt, a pillow, and a pillow-case, which were pawned by the prisoner.
     NOT GUILTY.

   JOHN WEBSTER. I live in Turner-square, Hoxton- the prisoner lodged twice with me; the last time was up to the 2nd of April, 1826.
