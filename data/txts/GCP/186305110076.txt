OLD COURT.—Thursday, May 14th, 1863.
Before Mr. Justice Williams.
714. SARAH WIGLEY (25), was indicted for, and charged on the Coroner's
Inquisition with the wilful murder of her new-born child.
Mr. POLAND conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. Cooper the Defence,
Guilty of endeavouring to conceal the birth. ---Confined Six Months.
715. STEPHEN THOMAS PIERCE (18),PLEADED GUILTY to indecently
assaulting Ellen West. He received a good character.-Confined Six Months
He was also charged with a rape upon the said Ellen West, upon which MR.
SLEIGH, for the prosecution, offered no evidence.

of James Goodliffe. MR. TAYLOR conducted the Prosecution.

children occupied the second floor of that house—the youngest child was
named James, and was 8 or 9 months old- I saw the child after it was
picked up-it was not then dead-it died about 6 that night-I had seen
the prisoner three times that day—the first time was about 9 in the
morning-they were going on very noisily, and I went up and saw her
