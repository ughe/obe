   Cross-examined by MR. MARSHALL . I work for my living at 15, Kenmure Road—I have been a cabinet maker on and off for fourteen or fifteen years—I have regular wages according to the work I do—I renovate and keep antique furniture—I do not fake it—I put lines where they are wanted, but not marqueterie lines—I worked for Mr. Waatt—I have been in the public-house trade, and in one place for eight years.
