MESSRS. BODKIN and POLAND conducted the Prosecution.
John AlDry. I am a builder and live at Charlton, my wife keeps a
hosier's shop. On 24th Sept., about 7 o'clock in the evening, the prisoner
came for half an ounce of black thread-I served him-he threw down
a half crown, which rung well-my attention being taken off for a few
moments my wife gave him change, and he went out-I found the half
crown was bad, I went to the door-I did not see the prisoner, but I saw
the policeman-I showed him the half crown, and told him what had hap-
pened-I kept the half crown till 3rd Oct., and gave it to the policeman.
WILLIAM HARDY. My father keeps the Earl of Warwick, at Woolwich.
On 25th Sept., the prisoner came there about 8 o'clock, I served him half
a pint of beer-he put down a shilling, I picked it up, and sounded it on
the counter--it sounded very well-I tried it with my mouth I found it
bad, and called my father who was in the kitchen-he came in, and I gave
him the shilling the prisoner was still there-my father told him it was
bad-and he said, "Give me that shilling, I will pay you for the beer"-
my father kept the shilling.
