810. WILLIAM ALLEN, stealing 2 sacks, value 2s. ; and 8 bushels of
oats, 30s. ; the goods of Andew Inderwick and others, his masters.
ROBERT BLOWER. I am manager to the London Conveyance Company,
Mr. Andrew Inderwick is the chairman- I have missed oats-the prisoner
was carman-on 27th Jan., he had carried oats to the stable at Trongate
wharf, and he took in the horses of the regular horse-keeper-he had no
business to take the oats away from there.
Cross-examined by Mr. HORRY. Q. Who paid for these oats? 4.1
should imagine Mr. Inderwick-I am under his orders- I am not a pro
prietor.
Thomas Powell. I am horse-keeper to the London Conveyance Com-
pany. On Thursday night, 27th Jan.. I fed the horses at Irongate-wharf-
asked the prisoner to take in my horses at the last journey, and feed them-
I left two sacks of oats in the stable-there were four bushels in each sack-
I left him at eleven o'clock at night-when I came next morning the two
sacks of oats were gone—the horses could not have eaten eight bushels.
Cross-examined. Q. It was your duty to feed the horses when they came
from the omnibusses ? A. Yes I was on my way home from the stable
when I met the prisoner-I had locked the stable, and I gave him the key-
I went home and went to bed-I bad not been in a public-house with the
prisoner-he has been in the Company's employ several months, and I nearly
five years.
John Crort (police-sergeant, D 4.) At half-past twelve o'clock at night,
on 27th Jan., I met the prisoner at Irongate-wharf-he had this sack, cob-
taining four bushels of oats, on his shoulder-I asked what he had ther-
he said, “Eh !"-I asked him the second time-be then said he was going to
carry it for a wager-I said, "Where ?"-he said, "To the Great Westen
Railway" I said, “I am going that way, come along"-he then threw the
sack down-I sprung my rattle, my brother offlcer came up, and he found
another sack.
GEORGE GOUNDRY (policeman, D 170.) I heard a rattle, and went and
found this sack which contained four busbels of oats.
ROBERT BLOWER re-examined. The two sacks are the property of the
London Conveyance Company.
GUILTY.-Aged 27.-Confined Six Months.
