 1097.  EMMA MARIA SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of March , 12 yards of ribbon, value 5s., the goods of Thomas Prall.
   CHARLES GURR. I am shopman to Mr. Thomas Prall, a linen-draper, in Tottenham-court-road. On the morning of the 30th of March the prisoner came to the shop, with another young woman; I served them with three-quarters of a yard of ribbon; I then saw the prisoner put her hand to her bosom - I missed a roll of ribbon; I had counted the rolls before I showed them to them; I followed them out of the shop; they turned a street, and began to run; I called them back, and the prisoner produced this roll of ribbon, and a remnant; there were twelve yards of ribbon, worth 5s.; I gave them both in charge, but the other was discharged.
 Prisoner. You did not see me put it into my bosom. Witness. I saw your hand in your bosom as I came from the window.
   JOHN LANDON(Police-constable E 150.) I took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. The other girl took it, and put it into my bosom, under my shawl - I walked round the corner; I gave the ribbon out of my hand to the witness.
   JOHN LANGHAN. I went last night to her mistress, who keeps a respectable public-house; she is not able to attend, but stated that the prisoner had lived with her three times, and she will take her again.
 GUILTY. Aged 17. Recommended to Mercy- Confined Fourteen Days.
 1098.  JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of March , 3 breasts of mutton, value 2s., the goods of Edward Kimber.
   GEORGE FORD(Police-constable F 50.) Mr. Kimber, who is a butcher, and lives in Bell-yard, Temple-bar, applied to me to watch his shop, on Saturday night, the 11th of March, and about half-past three o'clock on the Sunday morning, I saw the prisoner at the bottom of Bellyard - he crossed, and went into Apollo-court; I went and knocked at the prosecutor's door, and they let me in - I had been in about ten minutes or a quarter of an hour; the prisoner then came; he got on the ledge outside, and put his arm into the shop - in a few minutes a match broke out on fire, and he could see every thing that was in the shop; I went towards the front door, on my knees - he had then got down, and got up again with a stick and a hook at the end - he fixed the hook into a breast of mutton, and drew it towards him, but it came off, and dropped into the shop- he then took another, but that also dropped in the shop; he then took a third, and that he got out - I opened the door, and pursued him with the stick and mutton in his hand; he ran up Apollo-court - I lost sight of him for a moment, but two City watchmen came up; I went into a house into which the prisoner had gone, and met him coming down stairs - the stick and the meat he had put on the stairs.
 Prisoner. Q. How far up the stairs did you find the meat? A. About four stairs up the second flight, where you returned to meet me; I had not lost sight of you half a minute - I did not say I did not know you without taking you to the light; the two watchmen had lights - I had not.
   EDWARD KIMBER. I am a butcher. I employed the officer to watch my shop; I saw a man put his arm through into my shop, and a match burst out into a light - he got down, and got up again; I saw the stick and the book stuck in the meat - I did not see his face - I was watching in the adjoining room; I have been repeatedly robbed.
 Prisoner's Defence. I never was on the stairs - I was out of doors when he took me.
     GUILTY. Aged 30. - Transported for Seven Years.

 1100.  THOMAS TOOMEY was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of March , 1 handkerchief, value 2s. 6d., the goods of John Kiser.
   MARGARET KISER. I am the wife of John Kiser - we live in George-street, Commercial-road- I am a laundress. On the 10th of March the prisoner came to my house, and while he was there a woman brought in some things, which she had been mangling; she put them in a chair, and the prisoner sat down on them - he rustled about a good deal, and I wanted to know what he was about; I put him out of the chair, and when he was gone I missed this handkerchief, which should have been among the things, but I had not seen it in the chair - I went to the prisoner's father, and told him; the prisoner came to my house, and said he knew nothing at all about it, but he afterwards brought the duplicate - I went and got it out of pawn; this is it - I got it by means of the duplicate he gave me; it was one I had washed, and my husband is answerable for it.
 Prisoner. She said she would give me 6d. if I went and got the duplicate. Witness. He asked me 6d., and I gave it him.
