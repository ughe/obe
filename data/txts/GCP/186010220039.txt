ALFRED Bond WACKET. I am a letter-carrier in the General Post-Office

prisoner was there, setting up his letters for delivery- I was sitting opposite

I was on the one side of the partition, and he on the other-he left his seat, and
was absent two or three minutes-when he returned I saw him scratching and

sovereign-he was rubbing white paper which was round it, firmly adhering

prisoner left the office before me.
