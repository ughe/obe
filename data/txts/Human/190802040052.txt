 Re-examined. Exhibits 4 and 5 are a letter and envelope which appear to be in the same handwriting, which were produced by Cripps at the police court. "Tombs, New York, December 16, 1907. Dear Mr. Cripps,—I wired you yesterday as regards the car. Now will you be kind enough to send it back to Mr. Rendall, 4, Great Win  chester Street.
