shillings, 60 sixpences, and 30 groats, the property of Jonathan Brazefield,

1 pair of shoes, value 4s., the goods of Joseph Ward, and that she had been
before convicted of felony
SARAH WARD. I am the wife of Joseph Ward-be is waiter at a tavern
I keep a shop at Greenwich I lost a pair of shoes from one of the shelves

not out for this month the shoes were safe, at eleven o'clock last Mon-
day when I went out, and when I returned, about a quarter past eleven o'clock,
they were gone-these are them-l never saw the prisoner till about ten that
morning, when she came to ask for a ready-furnished apartment at my
house.
