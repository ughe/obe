927. WILLIAM FLOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of April, 1
purse, value 6d. ; I half-sovereign, 3 half-crowns, 18 shillings, 8 sixpences,
and 3 halfpence; the goods and monies of William Ridley, from the person
of Mary Ridley.
MARY RIDLEY. I am the wife of William Ridley, and live in Castle-
street, Shoreditch. On the 2d of April, I was on the dickey of the Clap-
ham coach-I had about 21. 158. in my pocket, there was half-a-sovereign
in my purse, and the rest was half-crowns and shillings loose in my pocket-I
saw a person near the coach-side taken into custody-he had been hanging
on behind the coach-the moment he was seized I found my pocket had
been cut, and some money, which remained, fell about the dickey when I
got up- I have recovered altogether 21. 14s. 6d.-my pocket was on my
side.
JAMES MALLARD. I am a coachman. I was not driving the coach, but
sitting on the roof, behind the box-I saw by the shadow of the gas-light
there was somebody hanging behind the coach, close to the prosecutrix-I
looked behind, and saw somebody putting his hand towards her petticoats
behind-I told the driver to go on steadily-I jumped off the coach, pulled
the prisoner down, and said, You bave been cutting this woman's pocket"
-the prosecutrix got up, and four half-crowns, 38., and a half-penny fell
out of her pocket-I held the prisoner, called a policeman, and gave bim
in charge--some money was found on him-I do not know how much.
