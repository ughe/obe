544. HENRY ALFRED MESNER was indicted for stealing, on the
24th of December, 1 watch, value 31. ; key, value Is.; and 5 sove-
reigns ; the property of Thomas Winmill, from his person.
MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS WINMILL I keep a chandler's-shop. On the 24th of De-
cember I went to a public-house with the prisoner—I had known him six
months—we drank together—I saw Rosell, who I knew, by coming to m
house with the prisoner—I know his person well—before I fell asleep
had a watch safe and five sovereigns, besides some silver—the prisoner
and Rosell were with me when I fell asleep—when I awoke I missed my
watch and money—Rosell was just leaving the room—the prisoner was
pretending to be asleep, but I am perfectly satisfied he was not—I shook
him, and he rose up—I said I had been robbed—he said he knew nothing
about it, and he bad been robbed himself—he lost 38. 6d. out of his
pocket.
NOT GUILTY.
