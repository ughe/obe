sheep, which part of them were he said that he knew some of them
were very poor, but he thought they were short of keep, meaning that
there was a scarcity of grass- I asked him what he did with them he
said that he sent thirty carcases to Mr. Bucknall, of Newgate-market,
twelve to Messrs. Dean and Hatton, of Newgate-market, for sale, and four
he retained for his own shop-I asked him what he did with the skins
he said he sent thirty to Mr. Williams, of the Borough-market, for sale,
and sixteen to Mr. Kelly I should mention that the thirty skins, when
I saw them, were all daybed over with red ochre, to disguise them, but it

premises it appeared to have been a dwelling room at some former time,

corner, showing that sheep had been there very recently, there was no con-
