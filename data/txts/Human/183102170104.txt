 594.  WILLIAM FERGUSSON was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   THOMAS BEVAN. I am a surgeon, and live in Queen-street, Cheapside. I know Job Grimshaw, of Staples Inn-buildings, a bookbinder - I cannot identify the prisoner's person; but this paper was brought to me, and I delivered to the person who brought it, three sovereigns, for Mr. Grimshaw, on the 27th of December, believing that the paper came from Grimshaw, who had bound books for me-I saw the prisoner at the Mansion-house about three weeks after, charged with this offence - (paper read.)
 27th of December, 1830.
 SIR, - I take the liberty of writing to you to ask the favor of the loan of 3l. for a few days; if you can oblige I shall be glad.
 Yours, J. GRIMSHAW.
   JOB GRIMSHAW. I live at No. 6, Staples Inn-buildings, and am acquainted with Thomas Bevan. The prisoner was in my service about nine months, and had left about eighteen months when this occurred; I believe while he was with me I sent him to Mr. Bevan's on business - I did not send him on the 27th of December, or any body, to request the loan of 3l. from Mr. Bevan; this paper is not written by me, nor by my authority - the prisoner never brought me the 3l.; I did not know he was going on such an errand.
   HENRY CLEMENTS. I am assistant to Messrs. Hooper and Bevan, of Queen-street. On the 27th of December I recollect the prisoner calling on Mr. Bevan with a note - it was not sealed, and I opened it; it requested the loan of 3l.; when Mr. Bevan came in he read it, went up stairs, and brought the prisoner down a small brown paper parcel - I am certain of his person, but not positive of the date; it was about that time - I saw him in custody at the Mansion-house about three weeks after, and was quite certain of him.
 JURY. Q. Did you ask the boy where he came from? A. The moment he brought it he said he came from Job Grimshaw, and it requested an answer; he did not say where Grimshaw lived.
 MR. BEVAN. I gave him the sovereigns in brown paper.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Transported for Seven Years.

 595.  BENJAMIN MILLS was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of January , 1 cloak, value 5s., the goods of Joseph Markham.
   JOSEPH MARKHAM. I live in Williams'-mews, Hampstead-road, and am driver of the cab, No. 194. On the 18th of January I went into a chemist's-shop, No. 260, Tottenham-court-road, and left my cab at the door, with a cloak on the seat; I saw the prisoner pass, and take the cloak - I ran out; he turned into Russell-street, and threw it down - I took it up, and followed him; I lost sight of him for about a minute; when I saw him again he was still running - I have no doubt that he is the man; I took him - the Police-constable came and took him; he said he was very sorry for it.
 Cross-examined by MR. BARRY. Q. Was any body with you when he made this statement? A. The Policeman was; it was not a very dark night - it was between ten and eleven o'clock; he was taken within one hundred yards of the spot.
   JOSEPH STANNARD. I am a Police-constable. I heard a cry of Stop thief! I ran, saw the prisoner running very fast, and a number of people after him, up Russell-street, Tavistock-street, and round into Tottenham-court-road - he told me he had had no victuals the whole of the day, and he would starve before he would do any thing of the kind again.
 Cross-examined. Q. He seemed very contrite? A. Yes - he was quite cut up about it.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Confined One Month.
 596.  HENRY JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of February , 1 pair of trousers, value 7s., the goods of Jonas Benjamin.
   MICHAEL GABRIEL. I live in Homer-street, Marylebone, and am a dealer in clothes. I was at my door between ten and eleven o'clock on the 16th of February, and saw the prisoner handling some trousers at Jonas Benjamin's window - I thought he handled them rather longer than he ought, and I saw him unloop a pair, put them under his arm, and walk down the street: I followed, and as he turned the corner of Crawford-street, I tapped him on the shoulder - I took him back; a mob gathered round - he endeavored to get away, but I gave him in charge.
   THOMAS HENRY THOMPSON. I am a Police-constable. I took the prisoner outside Mr. Benjamin's shop: I asked how he came in possession of the trousers - he said he had picked them up.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going down the street, and there were several Jews going by, with loads of clothes - I saw the trousers on the pavement; I took them up, and walked quietly down the street - the witness said he would keep me; a number of people came up, said he was no officer, and had no right to hold me, so I tried to put his hands off.
 JURY to  MICHAEL GABRIEL. Q. Did he bring the same trousers back which you saw him take from the shop? A. Yes, these are them - they have the mark on them.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. - Confined Six Months.
