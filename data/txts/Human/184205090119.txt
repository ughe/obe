1577. WILLIAM DYSON was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of April , 13lbs. weight of beef, value 3 s  ., the goods of James Hayward . JAMES HAYWARD. On the 20th of April I missed 38lbs. of beef—in consequence of what I heard, I ran out, and overtook the prisoner, with my beef on him.
   Prisoner.  He did not overtake me with the beef, I had no beef with me. Witness.  I saw him with it, called, "Stop thief," and he dropped it. THOMAS HARRIS. I was in St. John's-street, and saw the prisoner go to the prosecutor's shop, take a piece of beef, and walk away with it—I told the prosecutor, and he ran after him.
   Prisoner. Q.  Where did I take it from? A.  From the lid of the pickle-tub. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Three Months.
 1578. THOMAS BRYAN was indicted for embezzlement. SAMUEL RICHARD NEWTON. I am a grocer, living in Upper Charlton-street. The prisoner was my porter—it was his duty to receive money for me, and to pay me the same night—if he received 2 s . 5 d . from Mrs. Archer, on the 19th of March, and 14 s . 11 d . on the 19th of April, from Mrs. Salmon, he has not paid me; I asked him what he had done with it—he said he had bought clothes, and spent it—that is all he said about it—he is a defaulter to the amount of 12 l .
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  He told you directly, did not he? A.  I had been round to make inquiry, and questioned him when I returned in the evening, and he said he had received it, and spent it. FANNY TIERNEY. I am the daughter of Mrs. Salmon. On the 19th of April I paid the prisoner 14 s . 11 d . for her—I gave him a sovereign—he gave me two half-crowns and a penny in change. ANN JONES. I paid the prisoner on the 19th of March 2 s . 5 d . for Mrs. Archer.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Six Months.
 1579. WILLIAM RAVENSHAW was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of April , 1 coat, value 2 s  ., the goods of Edward Hall . EDWARD HALL. I am gardener to Mr. Pitt, of Kensington. I left my coat in the tool-house on the morning of the 21st of April, and it was gone—this now produced is it. JOHN GROVES. I am in Mr. Pitt's employ. Between three and four o'clock I was riding in the lane adjoining my master's house—I saw the prisoner come over the garden paling, with this coat under his arm—I se cured him. GUILTY.* Aged 17.— Transported for Seven Years.
 1580. FREDERICK BRANDON was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of April , 1 brush, value 9 d  ., the goods of John Stephens . HENRY COVE. I am apprentice to Mr. John Stephens, a brash-maker, in Upper North-place, Gray's Inn-road. On the 11th of April I saw the prisoner take something—I followed him—he ran away—I overtook him, and he threw this brush from him—it is my master's.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY.* Aged 14.— Confined Three Months.
