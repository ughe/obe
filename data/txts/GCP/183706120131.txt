1556. JOHN DIXX was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of June, 1
bolster, value 6s. ; and 2 sheets, value 4s. ; the goods of Richard Eleon;
and that he had been before convicted of felony.
ELIZABETH ELEON. I am the wife of Richard Eleon, who keeps the
White Horse, in Little Britain. On the 9th of June, at half-past ten o'clock
at night the prisoner came and asked for a lodging-he paid eightpence for
it and went to bed-the next morning he came down at half-past eight
o'clock with a large bundle—that caused my suspicions, and I sent after him
--these sheets and bolster are mine—they were on the bed that night.
EDWARD GADSBY. I live in Jewin-street. I stopped the prisoner in
Falcon-square on the 9th of June-he dropped this bundle I gave him
in charge with it.
