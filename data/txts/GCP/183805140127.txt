1316. THOMAS JELPH was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of
April, 1 shovel, value 18d.; and 1 spade, value 28.; the goods of John
Cordell: 1 shovel, value 2s. ; 1 spade, value 18d.; and 1 spit-iron, value
6.; the goods of Jesse Mason: I shovel, value 18d., the goods of Samuel
Dearman : and I shovel, value 18d.; and 1 spade, value 28.; the goods of
John Bright.
JOHN CORDELL. I live in Three Fingers-buildings, Kingsland. On the
24th of April I was at work with some other labourers-between one and
two o'clock we buried our tools--the prisoner was with us, and helped to
do it-he went out of the field with us this is my shovel and spade.
JESSE MASON. These tools are mine.
SAMUEL DEARMAN. I am a labourer. I was present when these tools
were buried--this shovel is mine.
