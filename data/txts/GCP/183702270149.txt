DANIEL DEARLOVE. I am shopman to Thomas Lloyd a grocer, of
Aldersgate-street. The prisoner came to our shop from ten to eleven
o'clock on the 8th of February, and asked if I could tell him where to buy
the best Olasso juice, that is Italian juice-I asked what quantity be
wanted-he said, "A chest or two"-I then said Bedwell and Yates, or
Williains would serve him as cheap any one I did not write this order nor
any order-it is not written by my master or any one in the shop We
Dave no one of the name of Smith in our employe did not see that the
prisoner was drunk.
RICHARD TAYLOR (police-constable G 140.) I received the prisoner
into custody- he told me he had been made the dupe of a party, and one of
them was waiting outside-he was not very drunk.
THOMAS LLOYD. I keep the shop in Aldersgate-street. This order is
written by me nor any one in my employ-I do not know the prisoner
at all-I know nothing about this order.
