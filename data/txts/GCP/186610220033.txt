MR. GIFFARD contended that that enactment had nothing to do with the rule
of law. A document could always be put into a witness's hand to refresh his
memory. Mr. Denman's Act, though it had altered the rules of evidence, had
not altered the law, and the practice was not to allow the question to be put
without putting the deposition into the witness's hands.
