JAMES NUTT  (38), was indicted for feloniously omitting to surrender himself to the Court of Bankruptcy, after having been adjudged a bankrupt; also,  feloniously removing, concealing, and embezzling part of his personal estate, with intent to defraud his creditors; also,  unlawfully obtaining goods on credit within three months of his bankruptcy, under the colour and pretence of carrying on business in the ordinary course of trade; to all of which he PLEADED GUILTY — Three Years' Penal Servitude.
