First Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
1810. PHILIP EDMUND DOVER, WILLIAM DOVER, JOHN
KENDRICK, HUGH DOYLE, MICHAEL DOYLE, HENRY
LAMB, WILLIAM BARRETT, and ROBERT PAGE, were indicted
for a misdemeanor.
(This case arose out of an endeavour forcibly to take possession of a
house and stock of furniture, under a claim of partnership. A verdict of
GUILTY was recorded by consent, and the defendants entered into sure-
ties to appear for judgment when called upon.)
1811. ELIZA MURRAY was indicted for stealing, on the 28th
of April, 1 kettle, value 148.; 1 bag, value 28.; and 1 set of bed-
furniture, value 21.; the goods of John Westerdale; to which she
pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Three Months.
1812. CHARLES JAMES SYMS was indicted for stealing, on the
24th of June, 1 spoon, value 30s., the goods of Richard Aldolphus
Hooper; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Confined Three Months.
1813. PETER DONNELLY was indicted for stealing, on the
24th of June, 2 spoons, value 1l. 38., the goods of Robert Reynolds, his
master.
ROBERT REYNOLDS. I live in Fleet-street. The prisoner was in my
employ-I missed two spoons on the 24th of June, and discharged my
maid servant in consequence.
JOHN JAMES SIMons. On the 24th of June the prisoner brought this
spoon to my shop for sale it is in two pieces-I asked him where he got
it from-he said it was his-I requested him to tell me where his master
lived-he would not, and I took him to the police-office.
