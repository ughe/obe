SAMUEL SEARLE. I keep the Salisbury Arms. Mr. Davies has a betting establishment there—I issue tickets for him—the one produced is not issued from my place—it purports to be signed by "E. Searle," a niece of mine—it is not her writing. ELIZA SEARLE. This is not my signature to this ticket—I used to sign tickets, but have not done so for some time. WILLIAM EDMUND DAVIES. I am a sporting character, and have an establishment at Mr. Searle's. I sometimes keep a book there—when I make a bet I give a ticket—the ticket produced is not mine—allowing it to be a genuine one, if "Clincher" Had won, the holder of the ticket would have won 102 l .—it is possible I may have given the prisoner a ticket at some time.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I took 30 l . to 3 l . the morning after the Chester cup was run, in Ox ford-street, and I gave 3 l . for this ticket—I did not know it was bad. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Six Months .
