2254. ROBERT BAGGOTT and JAMES PULLEN were indicted
for stealing, on the 11th of July, 10 lbs. weight of ham, value 108., the
goods of John Smith.
Joun Smith. I keep the Victoria tea-gardens at Bayswater. About
twenty minutes past eleven o'clock at night, on the 11th of July, I missed
a ham-1 saw it safe just before—this is the knuckle of it.
Cross-examined by Mr. Horry. Q. Is not your name Jones? A. No,
I went by that name for thirty years--it was the name of my nurse who
brought me up-- I did not know my parents.
JOHN KIMBREY (police-constable D 102.) On the night of the 11th of
July I stopped Baggott with a ham under his jacket-Pullen was walking
with him towards the Edgware-road, down Cambridge-street-I asked
Baggott what he had got-they walked on, and I spoke to them again-
Pullen fell back, and said it was a ham they had picked up down by the
Crown, at Bayswater-I saw there was no dirt upon it-I said, "You
never picked this ham up there"--they said they were coming from Ham-
mersmith, and picked it up between the Crown and the Victoria gate-the
ham was warm.

PULLEN - GUILTY. Aged 28.5

person; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
ROBERT ROBSON. I live in Long Acre. About nine o'clock, on the
8th of August, I was going down Snow-hill-I missed my handkerchief
I turned, and saw the prisoner running--this is my handkerchief-no one
was near but the prisoner.
CHARLES GRANT. I live in Alpha-grove, Holloway. I was in Far-
ringdon-market, and heard a cry of “Stop thief" --the prisoner passed me
-I followed him to Shoe-lane-he went into a house-he threw this hand-
kerchief behind him-I took it up, and gave it to Mr. Robson.
FREDERICK RUSSELL (City police-constable, No. 503.) I produce a
certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's
office-(read)--the prisoner is the person.
