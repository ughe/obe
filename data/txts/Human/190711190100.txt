That was while he was staying in my house. While he was staying with me he was to pay 4s. for bed and breakfast, and as a matter of fact he never did pay anything. While he was staying with me I wanted some explanation over Watson. I wanted to know if he could tell me anything about it; in fact, I was beginning to get a bit uneasy just about that time.
