MARY MERRYFIELD. I am the wife of Joseph Daniel Merryfield.
These girls came to my place to lodge, three weeks ago to-day-I said I
could not make it convenient, but they said they did not care what shift they
made, so as I could but lodge them-I lodged them that night, and they
were to pay me 8d. a night-they lodged there that night, and the next,
and the third night one came to my place, the other did not- she came in
in the night, and went to bedI heard her fasten the door-in the morning
I awoke about seven o'clock-I missed the girl and my things I went to
the station-house, and stated my loss--these are the things I can swear
to them—this is my baby's frock--this is the basket I lost that night-
this bonnet was laid on the top, wrapped up in a bit of patchwork-my
husband has left me these two years.WORTAT TARYTOS
