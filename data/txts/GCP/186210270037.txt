him-I did not see Catherine Bryan at that time-David Bryan went into
the house and was very noisy-the person in the bar refused to serve him-
Casey was the only one in the bar with him. she was trying to persuade
him to go out, but did not suoceed-he continued making such a noise that
it was impossible to carry on the business, and we tried to eject him-

Bryan said that he should not go out for him, or for anybody, until he was

one of the doors-he caught hold of my arm and wrenched it round. I have
felt pain ever since--we did not use more violence than was necessary. We
