THOMAS TILLEY. I am a grocer, at Avebury, Wilts—I know the prisoner as a traveller to Cross and Gordon—on 15th January, I paid him for them 33 l . 13 s . 11 d . by this cheque, and he gave me this receipt—the cheque has been returned to me through my bankers as paid.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How long have you known the prisoner? A.  Between two and three years—I never knew anything against him.
