   Cross-examined. Q.  Do you hear that he met with an accident two years before, and was subject to giddiness; might that have been so great as to cause him to fall? A.  I examined that place, it seemed to be an old injury, and round it there were no symptoms of congestion—undoubtedly a man subject to giddiness might be so excited by drink, as to cause him to fall down—I think in this case the rupture must have been caused by the fall. GUILTY of Manslaughter. — Confined One Week
