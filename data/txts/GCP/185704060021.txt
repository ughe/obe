JOHN PALMER. I am landlord of the Windsor Castle, at Kensington.
On Saturday, 14th March, the prisoner came about 4 o'clock-he asked for
a pint of 6d. ale-I served him, and he gave me a bad half crown; I bent
it, and threw it on the counter-I said, "This won't do "-he did not drink
any of the ale, but went out immediately, and took the half crown away-
I am quite certain it was bad.
