DAVID  , Mary (28, waitress), was indicted for maliciously publish ing certain false and defamatory libels of and concerning George Florentino ; also attempting to kill and murder herself. A jury was sworn to try whether the was competent to plead. GEORGE GRIFFITHS, medical officer at Holloway Prison. I have had prisoner under my observation and care since September 24 last and previously. I have formed the opinion that she does not realist the position in which she stands, and that she is not competent to instruct anyone to represent her. She has attempted to commit suicide in prison. Her condition is neurotic. She has constantly threatened to commit suicide and says she will do so on the first opportunity.
 Verdict, That prisoner is unfit to plead. Ordered to be detained during His Majesty's pleasure.
