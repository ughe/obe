BESLEY, Mayor.
ROBERT BELL. I am a detective-I saw the prisoner on Saturday, the 17th
of September-I said "I shall apprehend you for stealing two calves,
the property of Mr. Munro-he snid "I did not steal any calves"-on
the way to the station he said he had borrowed the money from Mr.
Munro, and had the calves, and that he had sold them to a butcher in
Bermondsey-before Sir Robert Carden he said he had sold these calves
to a person named Wrangham.
Thomas WRANGHAM. I am a meat salesman in the Metropolitan
Market-I bought two calves of prisoner for 6l. 158.-he came to me,
and said he had got two calves that he had bought in the country, and
he wis certain I would buy them, and I said “When can I see them" --
he said he had to go in the country to get them-this was in the
morning.
Prisoner's Defence. I did not mean to rob the man of the money or the
calves; I had not time to get the money together to pay Mr. Munro,
or I should have done so.
Nor GUILTY.
