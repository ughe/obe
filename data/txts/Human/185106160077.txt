To the best of my recollection, you spoke to me; you made a great many observa tions to me—I believe I declined giving you an answer—I think Hawkes's name was mentioned—I did not say where I was, or what I was going to do—I did not say I was in bed—the officer found me at my own house—I was up-stairs, but was not in bed—I did 'not say that I had been playing at cards the night before in a public-house, or drinking—I did not say I had been as, drunk as a cove  overnight—I called at the Torrington Arms, but did not have any gin—my object in calling was to inquire what you bad been there for—I swear I did not call for a quart of porter;
