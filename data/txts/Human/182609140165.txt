WILLIAM STAPLETON. I am a journeyman carpenter. On the 14th of August I was in Fleet-street, and heard the cry of Stop thief! - I saw the prisoner running as fast as he could; I pursued, and took him in Poppin's-court, with the umbrella.
   WILLIAM SPRIGGENS. I am the prosecutor's son. I saw the prisoner come in and take the umbrella off the rail, and run off with - he was brought back by the witness.
   DANIEL TURNER. I am the officer, and took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I had been two days without food, and the officer, seeing my distressed situation, gave me something to eat. GUILTY. Aged 20.
 Recommended to Mercy by the Jury. Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 1750.  EDWARD ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 5s., the goods of James Barlow , from his person.
 JAMES BARLOW. I live in Fore-street. On the 20th of August I was walking with my wife, about half-past eleven o'clock at night - she told me I had lost my handkerchief; I felt, and it was gone; I turned round and seized the prisoner close at my elbow - I did not see the handkerchief.
   HARRIET IMMYNS BARLOW. I was walking with my husband, and saw the prisoner take up his coat-pocket, and draw the handkerchief from it; I saw it in his hand - he threw it on the ground - one of his companions took it up and ran off with it; I told my husband, who turned round and seized him.
 Prisoner's Defence. At the corner of  Long-lane the gentleman turned round and seized me - the lady said I had taken the handkerchief.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Whipped and Discharged.
 1751.  JAMES JENNINGS was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of July , 1 basket value 8s.; and 18 loaves of bread, value 9s. 6d., the goods of Josiah Kinsey.
   WILLIAM WITTY. I am a baker, and live in the Borough. On the 3d of July I was in Holland-street, Blackfriar's-road, and saw the prisoner with a basket of bread on his shoulder - I followed him to the top of Holland-street, and on to Blackfriar's-bridge; the master of the bread came up, and asked me if I had seen a man with a basket of bread - I said, Yes; and we went on to the bridge - he came up to the prisoner first, and took the basket from him - the prisoner struggled to get away - he said he did it for want.
   JOHN HARPER. I am the officer, and took charge of the prisoner on the 3d of July - I took the bread and the basket - here is the basket.
 COURT to Mr. WITTY. Q. Did you see the officer receive the same basket that Kinsey took from the prisoner? A. Yes; I lived with Josiah Kinsey's master, and had carried this basket, which I had made for myself - it holds just twenty four loaves; I left four months ago - I know the basket.
 Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. Why is not Kinsey here? A. I do not know why; I do not know whether he has any other name.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 1752.  JAMES READ was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of August, 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of John Shedden Scrimgeour , from his person.
 JOHN SHEDDEN SCRimgEOUR. On the 25th of August I felt something at my pocket, I turned round and collared a man, but I cannot say it was the prisoner - the officer came and took him - the officer took up my handkerchief, which was safe an hour before.
   HENRY HUGHES. I am the watchman; I saw the prisoner and two others following the prosecutor about fifteen yards; I saw the prisoner put his hand into the pocket, and then the other two went away - I crossed over, and saw the handkerchief on the ground - I seized the prisoner, and took up the handkerchief.
 Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. Were there not a great many people about? A. Yes; there was a dreadful storm at the time - the lightning did not frighten me.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 1753.  JAMES BAKER was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of August , 1 silver snuff-box, value 11s.; 1 marlin-spike, value 3s., and 1 pepper-castor, value 1s., the goods of Moses Joseph Cashmore , his master.
 MOSES JOSEPH CASHMORE. I am a  jeweller, silversmith, and merchant, and live in Bevis-marks. The prisoner was in my service upwards of a year and a half - on the 30th of August I missed a coat and pair of trousers, and afterwards a marlin-spike was gone; I had missed the snuff-box six days before, and had sent him to different coffee-houses to inquire if I had not left it there; I told him and the servant girl that it would be prudent for me to go to their houses to see if any thing was there; I pretended to go, but went to Worship-street; I then went home with the officer, and told the prisoner he might sleep in the house that night, and go before the Lord Mayor in the morning, but after two o'clock he got up, and went out, pulling the door after him; I got up, and spoke to the officer, who was on duty at my door.
 Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. What had the prisoner a week in your service? A. Six shillings, and sometimes breakfast and tea, besides presents from friends, who come to my house - he was very honest - I have entrusted him with thousands.
   JAMES HARDEN. I am an officer. On the morning of the 31st of August the prosecutor desired me to go with him after the prisoner, whom we found in Jamaica-street, Commercial-road, at his sister's house - he was coming out with the marlin-spike in his hand - I found the pepper-castor in his hat, and a duplicate for a silver box - he said he was going to destroy them, and return to his master.
