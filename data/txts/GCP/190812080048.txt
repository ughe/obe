To prisoner. Since the committal for trial I have not talked to

Police-constable WALIAX ALLEN, G division. On the light of

him to the divisional surgeon.
To prisoner. Prosecutor was not drunk; he had been drinking.
Police-constable WILLIAK HARRIS, G division. At 2.30 am. on
November 22 I went to prisoner's address and found him in bed.
On my telling him I had come to arrest him for malicious wounding
he said, "All right, Mr. bleeding Runacre, the tike; he did this to
me three weeks ago."--pointing to a scar on the side of his face-
"and I meant to do it I said, "Did you?" He said, "No. If
be gets over it and crosses me, the boys will do him in. Did he
come the copper on me!”
To prisoner. It is doubtful whether you knew that I knew your
address.
Verdiet, Guilty.
WILLIAN REYNOLDS, warder, Brixton Prison. Prisoner has been
sentenced to 21 days, 9 months, 18 months, 3 months (twice), 12
months (twice), and has against him several summary convictions.
Detective-sergeant THOMAS Cook. When out of gaol prisoner is
very quarrelsome, and the people of Hoxton are afraid of him. When
he is in liquor he will go into & shop or a public-house and demand
food or drink. Tradespeople have complained.
Sentence, Three years' penal servitude.
