soner-I found we could not get our money of Joseph, and I asked the
prisoner what we were to do-he said he found that Joseph conld not open
the house, and we had better take our fixtures back again, and we said,
Very well, we would sooner do that than lose the whole"-William said

mentioned-it might be-I then took away all the fixtures.
