232. LYDIA MORTIMER, unlawfully endeavouring to conceal the
birth of her female child. Guilty. Aged 19.-Confined Three Months.
233. ANN SCANLAN, stealing 5 handkerchiefs, value 21. 88.; the
goods of Thomas Bosberry: having been before convicted.
MR. PLATT conducted the Prosecution.
ROBERT STOREY. I live at 36, Gracechurch-street. In July last, I
was living with Mr. Thomas Bosberry, a draper, of Regent-street. Ona
Saturday evening, at the latter end of July, the prisoner came to the shop
and asked to look at hosiery of different descriptions which I showed her
-she asked to be showed some black silk handkerchiefs, and I did so-1
observed her looking round towards the door, and then saw her shuffling
ber dress on the right side--the parcel of silk bandkerchiefs was then in
front of her, near the edge of the counter-I removed them from her, went
round the counter, and asked her if she had anything on her that did not
belong to her-she expressed surprise that a person of her respectability
should be charged with such a thing- I asked her to move from the cous-
ter a short distance, which she did, and I distinctly saw five handkerchiefs
fall from the right side of her person on the floor-I picked them up-I
showed them to her, and charged her with them-she said she knew
nothing of it-Mr. Bosberry sent for an officer, and she was taken into
custody-the handkerchiefs were his property, and worth 21.-she had
directed the things she had selected to be sent to the Hon. Spencer Cow-
per's, in Bruton-street, and she gave her own name, but I forget it-I
went and inquired there, but did not take the goods-she was taken before
the Magistrate-she appeared in a very bad state of health, and in conse-
quence we withdrew the prosecution, but before that, when she was taken
before Mr. Bingham, she said she was very sorry for what she had done.

before the Magistrate ? A. Not exactly-it was previous to entering the
