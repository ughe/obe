- he is not one of the persons I have mentioned to-day-the alteration-
book was also kept in the shop; that did not affect the stock, but I had
to keep an account of all alterations—the cheque-book and the paying-in
book were kept in a small office on the ground floor at the back of the
shop, which Mr. Kino used, and the clerk who addressed wrappers for
the country used it the safe was in that room-Irving was ordinarily
employed with me on the second floor-he assisted me with the books
Mr Belton, the accountant, kept the private ledger-he came once
week-the stock-book was in my charge-when a length was out of
piece of cloth, it was entered in a small book, and was supposed to be
entered in the stock-cutting book-it was my duty to enter from the
small book the length which was cut off-the stock-cutting book con-
tained the original lengths and prices, and if that had been properly kept
it would have enabled Mr. Kino at any moment to ascertain the
amount of stock, but at the time of the fire it might have been a month

cut off a piece they were supposed to enter it into that book, and I

I had made entries in it for a month before, but sometimes we were
month behind-the small book was in the shop when the fire occurred;
