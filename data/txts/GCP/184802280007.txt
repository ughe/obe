THOMAS MOORE. I am a carrier at the New Inn, Old Change. These
printed tickets are such as are issued from my office, as far as the printing
is concerned; I cannot say they are mine—they are not filled up by me-I
gave no one authority to receive any money on them-mine have “The
Talbot" on them, as well as “New Inn"--my brother and I are connected
with both places- this is a ticket I should issue-this ticket does not
indicate where the barrels are sent from, it might be either place I and
my brother issue these tickets in common-I generally keep them in my
desk.
Cross-examined. Q. What character does Bishop bear? 4. I never
heard anything against him-he was not in my employ.
ROBERT IREBON (policeman, M 113.) I had charge of the prisoners at the
station-these tickets were there-Bishop said he had signed the book, but
did not fill up the duplicate, that he did not do the writing- I believe he
said he had received the money-(The tickets were here read, they were all
signed "Henry Bishop.")
