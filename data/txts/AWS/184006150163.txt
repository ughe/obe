WILLIAM WHEELER. I am a labourer, and live in Fox-street, Beth-
nal-green. On Tuesday the 26th of May, I had been to my club, at the
White Swan public-house, Whitechapel, and received 121. 4s. 4d. in
sovereigns and half-sovereigns, and a good many half-crowns-] was going
along Brick-ane at twelve 'clock at night-I was a little the worse for

-I never saw them before-they said they would see me home asked

comner of Gibraltar Walk-I do not know whether I fell down or was

was down when the policeman came up was hurt-I got a kick in the
