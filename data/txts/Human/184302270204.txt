 1037. SAMUEL JONES was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of Feb ., 1 shilling, the money of Sarah Hanson . SARAH HANSON. I am servant at Canonbury-terrace, Islington. The pri soner came to sweep the chimney in the morning of the 27th of Feb.—I had 3 s . 1 1/2 d . in my purse—I took one shilling out and put back the 2 s . 1 1/2 d .—I turned my back to the back room—about nine o'clock I missed a shilling—no one bad been in the room but the prisoner from the time of my leaving 2 s . 1 1/2 d . in the purse, and my missing a shilling. WARD STANWELL. I am street-keeper. I went after the prisoner about eleven o'clock—I told him it was for stealing a shilling from No. 15, Canon bury-terrace—he said he had not stolen anything—I took him to the house—he then put his band into his pocket, and said, "Here is the shilling, I did take it."
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Ten Days .
 1038. JOHN OLDBURY BURNS and WILLIAM WHEATLEY were indicted for stealing, on the   24th of Feb ., 1 saddle, value 1 l  . 5 s  .; 2 bridles, 18 s  .; 1 collar, 5 s  .; 1 pair of harness, 8 s  .; 1 crupper, 7 s  .; 1 breeching, 12 s  .; 1 belly-band, 2 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 pair of reins, 2 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of William Ben nett Bailey . JOHN LYNCH. I am carman to William Bennett Bailey—the prisoners have been employed by him. Between seven and eight o'clock on the 24th of Feb., I hung up my harness—about ten o'clock I was sent for, and the door of the place where the harness was was open—(it was locked when I left it)—the harness was all gone—this is it—it is my master's—they must have used a false key to get in. THOMAS MALIN( police-constable H  74.) On the evening of the 24th of Feb. I saw Burns with this harness in a sack, about a quarter before nine—Wheatley was in his company—I had seen Wheatley give Burns a lift up with it on his shoulder—when I detected Burns, Wheatley passed by.
   Burns.  I am a porter; I was employed to carry this.
 (Burns received a good character.)
   BURNS  — GUILTY. Aged 36.— Confined Three Months .
   WHEATLEY  — GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Nine Months .
