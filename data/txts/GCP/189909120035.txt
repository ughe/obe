MESSRS. CHARLES MATHEWS and A. GILL Prosecuted, and Mr. DRAKE
Defended.
CHARLES HENRY RAINER. I keep the White Swan, Fleet Street I
have had an account with Messrs. Ehrman Bros., and from time to
time I paid the prisoner sums of money in discharge of that account the
last sum I paid was £2 9s. 4d. in cash on May 25th-I took a receipt in
the ordinary way from the prisoner-about the second or third week in
July the prisoner came and said that he had had some dispute with the
cashier at the firm, and asked me if I would oblige him by giving him
back the receipt for the money which I paid on May 25th, and he would
return me the cash, which I did he said he was going to leave London
and work for the firm in Birmingham-I gave him the receipt and took
back the money.
