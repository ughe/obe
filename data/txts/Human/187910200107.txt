EDWARD KNOWLES. I am a groom to Mr. Cockell—I shut the stable gates about 7 p.m. on the 7th—I missed my coat ( produced ) next morning, and found it in the garden while going round with the constable—I also found these boots ( produced ) in the garden—the shoes produced are Mr. Cockell's, I have cleaned them. JAMES ARMSTRONG( Police Sergeant ). The prisoner had these shoes on at the Worship Street Police-court on the 8th—I asked him to take them off, and Mr. Cockell identified them as his property—I was with Knowles the night before in the garden, and he gave me the boots, and the coat the prisoner now wears—I took the boots to the police-court, and asked the prisoner if he knew anything about them, and he said, "Yes, they are mine"—I said, "Take off those you have and put these on," and he took off Mr. Cockell's shoes and put on the boots.
   The Prisoner's defence before the Magistrate ami in Court was that he had received the things from a former servant of the prosecutor's, and did not know they were stolen. GUILTY.— Twelve Months' Imprisonment.
