SAMUEL IND. I live in Devonshire-terrace, Devonshire-street, St. Dunstan's. On the 28th of April I was crossing Greenwich-park towards Blackheath—the prisoner came up to me and asked the time—I said, "Twenty-five minutes to one"—I had a watch with a ribbon and key—I took it out to look, and I put it into my fob—he turned round twice—I suspected he meant no good, and I turned to the road—he turned and said again, "What time is it?"—I told him again, and he snatched at my guard—it broke, and then he snatched at my ribbon, and took it with the key, and two rings—he went towards the gate of the park—I followed him and had him taken, and they were found on him. AARON OUTRED( police-constable R  128.) I heard the cry of "Stop thief"—I went up and took the prisoner—I found a ribbon, a key, and two rings in his pocket, which the prosecutor claimed. GUILTY. Aged 40.— Transported for Ten Years.

 1381. RICHARD FOWLER was indicted for stealing, on the   30th of April , 3 razors, value 5 s  .; and 1 front of hair, value 1 s  .; the goods of William Moore . WILLIAM MOORE. I am a hair-dresser. On the 30th of April the prisoner came to me, with another man, who wanted his hair cut, and while I was doing it the prisoner was very busy about the shop, looking about—the other man got up, with his hair half cut, and said, "I won't have any more of this;" and they both went out without paying me—about three minutes after they were gone I missed three razors and a hair front—these are them—( looking at them .)
   Prisoner. Q.  Which man went out first? A.  You went out first—I asked you to sit down, but you would not—you went to the glass and combed your hair out—you have come into my shop before you washed. GEORGE HOGG. Between five and six o'clock, in the evening of the 30th of April, I was in the tap-room of the King's Arms, and bought these two razors of the prisoner.
   Prisoner. Q.  Was there not another man with me? A.  There was not. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Three Months.
