 804. EDWARD MAY was indicted for stealing 9 1/2 lbs. weight of cake, value 8 s  ., the goods of Samuel Frazer Wooller Tomkinson . SAMUEL FRAZER WOOLLER TOMKINSON. I am a pastrycook, and live in Deptford-row, Newington. On the 8th Jan. I lost part of a cake from a stall at my door—it was brought in to my shop—I knew it by two pins which were stuck into it where the paper had been pinned on. JOHN ROBERT MOSLEY. I was crossing East-lane, Walworth, on the night of the 8th Jan.—I observed a crowd opposite the prosecutor's shop—I saw the prisoner in the crowd, and three other boys round him—the prisoner took up the cake and ran across the road—I stopped him—he dropped it, and I took it up—he dropped down and began to kick me violently. GUILTY. Aged 14.— Confined Eight Days , and Whipped.
   Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 805. ELIZABETH REYNOLDS was indicted for stealing 1 shawl, value 1 l  .; 1 pillow, 5 s  .; 2 sheets, 15 s  .; 1 blanket, 5 s  .; 1 tablecloth, 6 d  .; and 1 petticoat, 3 s  ., the goods of John James Bafry . MARTHA BARRY. I am the wife of John James Barry, of Paradise-build ings, Knightsbridge. The prisoner came to lodge at my house, and left on the 13th of Dec.—I missed a shawl and other things—these are them—I lost fife pocket handkerchiefs and a habit shirt from my private apartment. JOHN ARCHIBALD, pawnbroker.  I produce these things—I took them in of the prisoner on the 15th and 17th of Nov., the 18th, 25th, and 2nd of Dec.—they are all identified by the prosecutrix.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was driven to the utmost extremity, having buried three of my children previously; she had lent me one shawl and one petti coat, she sent me to pledge for herself; she also leat me a hand kerchief, and then she threatened to give me in charge. I had two of these things in my possession. I gave up the duplicates, and said I would return them as soon as I could; she threatened me with a policeman, and I left the house with her shawl on. MARTHA BARRY re-xamined.  I did not send her to pledge anything—she said she had been ill, and was very poorly—I waked on her for the first five weeks, and then she said she was much better, and would not allow me to make her bed or do anything. GUILTY. Aged 42.— confined six months .
