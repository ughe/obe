MARY ADAMS, dressmaker, Butler's-buildings, East Smithfield.  Between six and seven o'clock in the evening of the 2nd of May I was in Aldgate—I saw the prisoner's hand drawing a purse from my pocket—it contained one sovereign, two half-crowns, thirteen shillings, and two groats—he ran off, and I lost sight of him—I was kicked by a person—I saw the prisoner go as far as the cabs  near Aldgate church. ANNE BAIGENT. I live with my parents. I was in Aldgate that day, and saw the prisoner run and put something into his pocket—he ran towards the Minories, and I lost sight of him. MICHAEL CONDEY( police-constable H  138.) I was coming up the Mino ries, and saw a mob round the prosecutrix—I went to her, and from her description I went to the prisoner's house, in Gower's-walk, Whitechapel about half an hour afterwards, I found him with two others—he was in the act of shifting his clothes—he was taking his coat off—he sometimes goes out in a jacket—I would not let him put on another coat. ROBERT THORP. I saw the prisoner at half-past six o'clock that night opposite Whitechapel church with two others.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I had no other clothes to put on; I had not been out all day. ABRAHAM MULCAHAY( police-constable N  436.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 14.— Transported for Ten Year .
