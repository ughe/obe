property of John Winter, from his person.
JOHN WINTER. I live in Spur-street, Leicester-square. I was walking

spoke to me—I looked, and found my purse which had been in my coat-
pocket, lying in the road—there were three sovereigns, one half-sovereign,
and a half-crown in it—I had seen it safe within ten minutes.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. There were other persons about?
A. Yes—it was about two o'clock.
