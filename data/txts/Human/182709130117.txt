 1831.  THOMAS DELAY was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of September , 1 watch chain, value 4l.; 2 seals, value 20s., and 1 watch key, value 5s., the goods of John Johnson , from his person.
 MR. CLARKSON conducted the prosecution.
 JOHN JOHNSON. I live in Holywell-street, Westminster, and am a  pavior. On the 13th of September I was at the Haymarket Theatre, and left about half-past eleven o'clock- I was going up St. James-street, and the prisoner came in front of me, and siezed my chain and seals. I put my hand down, and caught the watch; the chain and seals were broken from it - I pursued him behind the coaches, but I never lost sight of him - I caught hold of his coat, the flap tore off - I seized him again; he threw me down, and we both fell into the mud, but I never lost sight of him.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you see me give any thing to any body? A. No; I had taken rather too much wine; my watch was safe at the corner of St. James-street.
   WILLIAM DARDNER. I am a watchman of the Hay-market. I saw the prosecutor and prisoner laying one on other, in the road, in the Haymarket - I seized the prisoner, and took him to the watch-house - I found nothing on him.
   SAMUEL LACK. I assisted in taking him to the watch-house. I looked down, about ten yards from him, and found a watch, with the spring broken; it was running down when I took it up; he was taken to the watch-house - that watch was claimed by another gentleman.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you not say it was found one hundred yards from me? A. No - ten yards.
   JAMES STAPLES. I am shopman to Mr. Gilbert, of Cockspur-street. I have some broken pieces of chain which came into our possession yesterday.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was returning from a friend's house, this gentleman took hold of me, and thinking him rather in liquor, I endeavoured to get from his grasp; he seized me again, and we fell down; if he never lost sight of me how could I part with it?
     GUILTY. Aged 23. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1832.  MARY EDWARDS was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of July , 1 pair of boots, value 5s., the goods of Eliza Amphlet , widow , and 1 pair of boots, value 6s., the goods of William Dudley.
 WILLIAM DUDLEY. I am  servant to Eliza Amphlet. On the 19th of July, at five o'clock in the morning, the watchman alarmed us, and I missed a pair of boots, shoes and brushes from the boot-hole, by the kitchen door; they were safe at ten o'clock the night before - I know nothing of the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   JAMES BOUND. I am a farrier, and saw the prisoner on the 20th, in Bell-street, selling these boots to a Jew - I said nothing to them - Dudley came to me next day, and I told her of it - I know the prisoner and the Jew by sight. On the Monday I saw the Jew, and told him of it - I had seen the shoes and boots before - Dudley had shown them to me - the Jew gave up the property on receiving 4s.
 WILLIAM DUDLEY re-examined. Bound pointed out a Jew to me, and I got the shoes from him - I got the boots from a pawnbroker's, which he took me to; he would not pay the 4s. to get them out, and I paid it.
   FRANCIS BRADBURY. I am a constable. I took up the prisoner - I went to the Jew's house, but he has not been found since.
 Prisoner's Defence. I slept at Porto Bello farm that night - I was coming to Paddington to buy some victuals, and met a women, who sold me these boots and shoes for 4s. - I saw the Jew, and sold them to him for 6s. 6d.
     GUILTY. Aged 41. Transported for Seven Years.
 1833.  JOSEPH MARSHALL was indicted for stealing, on the 22d of July , 5 live tame rabbits, price 7s., the property of James Walker.
 JAMES WALKER. I live in  Hyde-place, Westminster. On the 22d of July, in the morning, I missed five live rabbits, which were safe the night before - I do not know the prisoner.
   ELEANOR CHICK. I live at the Crown and Sceptre public-house. On the 22d of July, at half-past six o'clock in the morning. I was at my window; as I was getting up, I saw the prisoner climb the wall; he returned with two rabbits, which he gave to another person - I went to call Ridley, and when I returned I saw him give the other man two more; he then went and got another, and gave that to him.
 Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. Why did you not give an alarm? A. I called Ridley as soon as I was dressed.
   THOMAS RIDLEY. Chick called me. I slipped on my trousers, and saw the prisoner over the wall with two rabbits, which he gave to a person on the other side; he got over, and carried one away in a bag; he took them from Walker's premises.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you say a word about this, till you heard the rabbits were stolen? A. I mentioned it to several people, and on Monday morning Mrs. Walker came to me about it; the wall is about five feet high; they also got over a fence.
   JOHN WARNER. I was on duty with Walker, who is a watchman; he gave me information, and I took up the prisoner; he said he was in Chapman-street, with his brother, looking at a fight at the time.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Confined Six Months.
