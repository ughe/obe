 265.  JANE CONNOR was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of November , one clothes-horse, value 3s. the goods of Martha Brown.
 MARTHA BROWN. I am a  widow and keep a clothes-shop in John-street, Edgware-road; I had a clothes-horse in my passage, on the 28th of November - I had seen it about seven o'clock in the evening, and about half-past seven a witness came and told me of it.
   SARAH SPINKS. I saw the prisoner in the passage, near the street-door, with her right elbow resting on the clothes-horse; I was going for a pot of beer - I returned in a few minutes, when the horse and the prisoner were gone.
   ANN KEYTON. On the 28th of November two women came to my shop, No. 19, Barrett's-court, and asked me to buy a horse; it might be about eight o'clock in the evening - I bought it for 4s.; I cannot be positive the prisoner was one.
   JOHN ARGUST. I took the prisoner, and found the horse at the house for sale; the prisoner always denied having had it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had nothing to do with it - I was washing in the house she lives in at night; I told the person I was washing for I would come in the morning and wash up the place; I went to the door and the witness was going out for beer; she said, "Don't shut the door till I come in;" I said, "I will not;" I put on my pattens and went home; I went the next morning and the woman sent for the officer to take me.
     NOT GUILTY.
