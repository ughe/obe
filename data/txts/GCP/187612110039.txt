GEORGE WRIGHT, B.M. I am surgeon to the Y division, and live at 408,

I went to the Caledonian Road police-station and saw Sergeant Lambert-

gasping for breath he was only able to speak in a whisper-his face was
grazed on the cheek bone; the skin was broken ; his eyes were blackened
