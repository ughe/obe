79. CATHERINE DONOGHUE, stealing 1 yard of damask, 1 yard of
chintz, 1 wrapper, and other articles, value 38. 2d.; the goods of Edward
William Brayley, her master.
EMILY FORD. I reside with my sister and her husband, Edward William
Brayley; the prisoner was in their service. On 14th Nov. I lost some
damask, chintz, calico, and a wrapper-I spoke to the prisoner, and she denied
knowing anything about them-her box was then searched in my presence,
and the things found there--they belong to Mr. Brayley.
Prisoner's Defence. I brought them from my own country; the wrapper
does not belong to Mr. Brayley.
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Confined Three Months.
