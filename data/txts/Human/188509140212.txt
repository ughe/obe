 889. ISAAC WEBB  (24), Stealing two watches, the goods of John Grindon .
   MR. GEOGHEGAN Prosecuted. JOHN GRINDON. I was a pianoforte, organ, and harmonium dealer and am now a watchmaker at 376, Barking Road, Canning Town—there is a glass case containing watches behind the counter, which you would have to go round to get at—at right angles to the counter is my work ing-board, which a person must pass to get at the watches in the case—on 15th June I was in my little room, which leads through a door into the shop; both the shop door and the door between it and my room were open—I have a canary hanging at the back of the shop which generally makes a loud noise when strangers approach—I heard it between 15 and 25 minutes past 5 making a louder noise than usual—I immediately went into the shop, where I saw the prisoner standing at the far end of my working-board, between a piano and the end of the board—the public had no right to go there—he said "Have you any silver wires?"—I said "I have not"—he then left the shop immediately—I had seen two gold watches safe in the case at 8 or 9 a.m. on the 15th—directly the prisoner had gone I went over to the watch case, and I missed one of the watches there; I did not notice the other, because it had been behind some others—I went at once and gave information at the police-station, and when I came back I missed the second one—the value of the watches was about 16 l ., one of them was a gold repeater—I did not see what became of the prisoner after he left the shop—on 30th June I saw him at Chelmsford and was sure of his identity—the watches have not been recovered—I had not seen the prisoner before to my knowledge.
   By the COURT . I knew him by his features—he seemed as I came into the shop as though he had just been in the act of turning round. JOHN ROBERT LOWE. I live at 430, Barking Road, Canning Town, and am a music teacher—on 15th June I went into the prosecutor's shop about two or three minutes after 5, I stayed about 10 minutes, and left at about 12 or 13 minutes past 5—I noticed the case of watches, to the best of my belief I should say there were 30 watches in it; I particularly noticed a gold repeater there, I had seen it on several occasions before and had spoken to Mr. Grindon about it—I did not notice it as I went out—I did not notice anything about the other one. JOHM GRINDON( Re-examined ). From the time Mr. Lowe left my shop to the time the prisoner entered I heard no one else come in—the canary would give me warning when the door was opened—I came in directly after Mr. Lowe left—my wife is generally in the shop—there were two ladies in the house at the time and frequently in the shop—I gave directions that the shop should be watched on that day—no one else was in the shop when I came in, the door was shut when I came in—there is a bell which rings when any one comes in, it is up six feet high.
