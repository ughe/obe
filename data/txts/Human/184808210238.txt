 2054. JAMES STEVENS , stealing a show-case and pictures, value 22 l  .; the goods of Antoine Claudet ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 38.— Transported for Seven Years .
 2055. HENRY MORTIMORE and JOHN LUC KEN , stealing 1 hand kerchief, value 1 s  .; the goods of John Ellis , from his person. JOHN ELLIS. On 6th Aug., about half-past six o'clock in the evening, I was in Battersea-fields—I felt a tug at my pocket, turned, and saw the prisoners close behind me—Mortimore was the closest to me—I missed my handkerchief, which I had seen safe a quarter of an hour before—the prisoners were taken—my handkerchief was found on Lucken—this is it. WILLIAM OLIVER. I was with Mr. Eliis, and saw the prisoners close behind him—one said to the other, "He has got his hand on it"—in about two minutes I heard of the loss—I went after the prisoners, and found them together—I found the handkerchief on Lucken. GEORGE MARQUARD( police-sergeant, V  21). I saw the two prisoners in company—I took them, and found this handkerchief and three others, on Lucken.
   Mortimore . One of them belongs to me.
