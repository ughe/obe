ENOCH Dawson Howitt. I am a surgeon, and live in Apollo-buildings,
Walworth-on Sunday, the 25th of Jan., I was called to the police station, and
saw the prisoner-he stripped before me-1 found two scratches, as if by finger-
nails, just below his right arm, a bruise at the back of the right arm, a bruise
and scratch on the same elbow, on the left arm two scratches and a slight one
below the elbow-the three first fingers on the left hand were cut, and the
cuts were evidently made with the broad part of a sharp instrument-thier
were cut across about one-eighth of an inch-had I heard nothing of the trans
action, I should have said a razor would have done it-his shoulders were
very much discoloured and bruised, and he was very much discoloured with
blood-he said he was in the habit of sleeping without his shirt, and then he
was stopped from saying anything more by the police, or he would have
stated more--I saw his wife before I saw him-I think about half-past five
in the afternoon- I did not see the wound in her throat-I saw the strapping
-I never saw the wound-I removed two strips of plaster to enable me to
form some idea of the extent of the wound-I could see there was not a
wound greater than the plaster-it extended very little beyond the wind
pipe-I could form a notion as to the position of the wound.
Court. Q. Could you tell that there might be a wound below that, and
if so that it could not have been inflicted by the party herself? A. It is im-
possible for me to give an opinion-I could give an opinion the same as Mr.
Otway has, if he is capable of forming an opinion whether the woman or the
man committed it, I can form no opinion of it myself. onc a
JOHANNA MUNTON examined by MR. BALLANTINE. I know the charge
made against my husband.
Q. On the night of this unfortunate occurrence did you cut your own
throat, or did he do it? A. Yes, I did it myself - I have never said from the
beginning to the end of the transaction that anybody did it but myself-my
husband came out to me while I had the razor in my hand-I do not know
whether he forced it out of my hand-I became insensible--I saw him come
to me-he came close up to me--whether he forced the razor out of my hand
1 do not know-my husband never desired me to take a false oath against
anybody.
Mr. Robinson. Q. There was no quarrel between you and him that
morning? A. None.
John COLLIER (policeman.) I went to the house and examined it I found
blood in different parts of the room-I searched the prisoner at the station
and saw marks of blood down his shirt and under his shirt, on his body
down to his feet-he was covered with blood-there were scratches on his
back-I called Mr. Howitt to examine him-the scratches appeared freso,
between his shoulders and on his arm.
