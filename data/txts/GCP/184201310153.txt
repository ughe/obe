· Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. Why his right shoulder? A.
Because it was nearest to it-if he got in head foremost he would take it
with his right shoulder-some ink had been spilt two months before the
penny-piece has a hole in it-it was found in Humphreys's poeket, amongst
ninepence in coppers-there was no mark about Humphreys it was the
house of Woolford's father we went to, and Humphreys is Woolford's
brother-in-law.
John JENKINSON (police-sergeant T 26.) I was called about one o'clock
to the mill by Mr. Harman-they had broken in at a window-I saw the
desk broken open, and some ink spilled in one corner-I went to the pri-
soner's residence-I found on Humphreys this penny produced, and some
marks of ink on Woolford's bands-when I took them to the station I found
the mark of flour on Woolford's shoulder-he would probably mark it in
getting in-I could not get into the house for some time-they were in
bed, but there was plenty of time for them to get rid of money—there was
ink on this penny, and on another penny and one halfpenny, found on
Humphreys, but the ink is worn off now.
THOMAS Coox police-constable T 45.) I saw the prisoners together
about nine o'clock that night, going in the direction of the mill-I have
compared Woolford's shoes with the marks in the ground, and they corres-
pond exactly.
Cross-examined. Q. The road to the mill is in a direction towards their
house, is it not? A. Yes-when I saw them together they were going in
a way which would lead them home the foot-marks were right underneath
where they got in-there were other foot-marks there—the marks were in
gravel, which was wet-I made the comparison in the morning, about
eight o'clock.
Court. Q. Were there more than the foot-marks of two persons ?
Yes, a great many-I compared Humphrey's shoes with the foot-marks-
I am sure there were not the marks of his feet.
HUMPHREYS-GUILTY. Aged 26.-Confined Four Months.
WOOLFORD-GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Six Months.

January, 1 pewter-pot, value 6d., the goods of Matthew Cockburn; and
that she had been before convicted of felony.

man--he stopped her, and took this pot now produced out of her basket-
