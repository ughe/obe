NEW COURT.-Monday, May 16th, 1836.
Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
1298. GEORGE SUTTON was indicted for a misdemeanor.
MR. ELLIS conducted the prosecution.
WILLIAM EVERETT. I am a pork butcher, and live in Chichester-place,
Gray's-inn-road. On Thursday, the 14th day of April, between six and
seven o'clock in the evening, the prisoner came into my shop for a penny
black-pudding-I served him, and gave him the change for a shilling--the
instant he was gone I found it was bad- it was the only one I had I went
and gave it him back-I had bitten it-I found him about forty yards off-
there was another one with him—they were eating the black-pudding, and
in conversation together-I asked where he got it-he said he borrowed itd
a baker; and if I came home with him he would give me the full amount-
I would not-I returned and saw the policeman-we went and found him
in Liverpool-street, just at the back of my house-they saw us and ran-
the officer caught the prisoner and the other they were taken to the sta
tion-house, and then we went back to the place where they were taken, and
found the shilling that was offered to me, in the road knew it by my
biting it that was after they were discharged, as nothing was found upon
them the instant they saw us they began to run, and on the spot I found
the shilling.
JOHN ARCHER (police-constable G 150.) I received information on the
14th of April, and saw two persons coming up Liverpool-street-they ra
away-I saw something seemed to be dropped by the prisoner-I ran and
caught him, and then gave him to the witness, and took the other they
were taken to the station-house and discharged, nothing being found
went back to the spot and found this bad shilling.
WILLIAM EVERETT. This is the shilling I received from the prisoner
and returned to him.

in and took it out of my hand I am sure it was the one the prisoner gave
me- Parkin asked where he came from--he said, "From a baker's shop
just by" he was detained and given in charge.
