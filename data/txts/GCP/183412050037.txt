228. ELIZABETH COLLINS was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of
December, 2 breast-pins, value 108. ; 1 purse, value 1d. ; 3 sovereigns ;
2 half-sovereigns; 8 half-crowns; 7 shillings; and 2 sixpences; the
goods and monies of William Thompson.
WILLIAM THOMPSON. On the 7th of December I was at Staines. I
met the prisoner about nine o'clock at night-we went down a bye place
by ourselves, and while there, she robbed me when I met her I had a
purse containing three sovereigns and two half-sovereigns and 158. 6d. in
silver in my pocket, and a box, which was not taken-I had two breast-
pins in my shirt, which were attached with a chain- I know one sovereign
was bent, owing to a coach wheel going over it-1 can swear to that one-
I had pulled my breecbes down-when the prisoner had left me for two or
three minutes I missed my purse-I pursued her immediately I had
given her 6d. which had been loose in my waistcoat pocket-the silver had
been loose in my pocket-when I overtook her, I told her she had robbed
me of my purse she said she had not robbed me, and had not got the
purse I called the constable these are the pins, I have no doubt about
them, and this sovereign I can swear to it was bent when the wheel
went over it-this is my purse I was sober.
Cross-examined by Mr. Phillips. Q. Was the 6d. given her by a
previous agreement ? A. Yes I have not tried to make money of the
transaction, but her brother came to me-- I took three sovereigns and 20s.,
but I sent it back in twenty minutes or half an hour I had not then been
before the magistrate I did not tell any one I had the money till I sent it
up the constable had come down to tell me to go before the magistrate
I took the money, thinking the prisoner would be discharged.
