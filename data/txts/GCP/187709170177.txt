hand; I do not exactly remember the words- I told Mr. Hall from what I

meantime Mr. Todd purchased one at Mr. Franklyn's to go on with-I do
not know whether they were supplied at the same price-prices varied
Bocording to the size I do not think complaints of overobarges were made
80 much after that.
