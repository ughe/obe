GEORGE HULL (Sergeant P R 1). On 28th March, in consequence of in-
formation, I went to 28, Content Street, Lock's Fields--I found Lewis there
in the back parlour-I told her I was a police-constable, and I suspected her

began crying, and said she knew nothing of it, she was at home at the time
-I took her and the man she was with to the station at Walworth, and

afterwards went with Larigan to Lewis's room--there were three baskets
there-Larigan brought one away. NATHA
