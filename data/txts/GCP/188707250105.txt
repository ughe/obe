EDMUND BOLTON. I live at 18, Earlsdale Street, and am manager to
Mr. Tann, safe manufacturer, at Newgate Street-on the 28th February
I was present when Mr. Tann paid the prisoner 11. 18. to pay to Mr.
Ourtice-I saw the receipt signed and handed.
WESTLY HENRY FLETCHER. I am an architect of 7, Prince's Street,
Cavendish Square-I paid the prisoner 12. 18.; I don't remember the
date, and he handed me this receipt-this is my cheque; it has been
paid.
Cross-examined. I don't know if he had called when I was not there
-I think the prisoner signed the receipt then and there.
Agnes HEWITT. I am lessee of the Olympic Theatre-on 1st June,
about 10.30 a.m., I paid the prisoner three guineas on account of Mr.
Ourtice, and he gave me this receipt.
Cross-examined. I paid it a fortnight or three weeks after the order
was given--the prisoner had called four or five times- I was there.
THOMAS ROXBUROH (Poliooman E 131). At a quarter to 6 on 24th
June I was called by the prosecutor's son to take the prisoner into
custody at a public-house in Holborn-in answer to the charge he said
he would come to the station-the prosecutor charged him there with
embezzlement.
Cross-examined. The son called me to give the prisoner into custody
for embezzling three guineas; he said it was his father's property-
nothing was said in my hearing about going to Mr. Ourtioe to explain.
