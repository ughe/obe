   COURT . Q.  You say this is the only forged note you ever saw, with the water-mark? A.  Yes, my Lord—I have seen no other forged Polish notes in England that those produced—the others are badly executed—this one is perfectly well executed—none of the others have any water mark—I never saw any Polish forged notes that had a water-mark, ex cept this.
