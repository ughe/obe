I keep a beer shop, at No. 20, Lower Whitecross-street On Friday afternoon, 4th July, Harding and Beckley came to my house—I knew Harding before—Beckley was a stranger—Harding asked me for change for a 10 l . note, this is the note—I had not sufficient change, and went out to get it—when I came back I told them it was a bad note, and a forgery, and they ought not to bring it in to me—upon that they consulted together, and I believe one of them went to the station house.
