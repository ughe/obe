276. SARAH ROACH and MARY BRYANT were indicted for
stealing, on the 18th of May, 18 yards of ribbon, value 138., the goods of
John Dennis and another. 19
CHARLES SIMPRINGHAM. I am shopman to John Dennis and Son,
of Tooley-street. On the 18th of May, between four and five o'clock, the
prisoners came into the shop, and the shopwoman showed them several
boxes of linen--as I went round the counter she said to me, "psq,"
which is a term we use when we see anybody in the shop doing wrong
--they left the shop--we afterwards missed a piece of ribbon, which
the policeman brought to the sbop about eight o'clock that evening-I
know the ribbon produced to be ours--I had it in my hands between
twelve and one o'clock that day, and cut the edges to trim it, and noticed
it was faded a short length in-how it got from the shop I do not know.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. When did you cut the edge ?
A. About nine o'clock that morning-I swear to it by the style of ribbon,
and it being faded- I went before the Grand Jury in Surrey about this,
and was in Court when the charge was read over to the prisoner-the
charge was not gone into there-I believe that was the fault of the clerk.
COURT. Q. If you preferred an indictment there, how came you here
on the same charge ? A. We knew the parties were frequently at our
shop-the policeman did not tell me to come here--my employers said
they knew their characters to be bad, and they deserved punishment-I
knew they had been imprisoned six months for another offence, from
the Surrey Sessions-I think we came before the Grand Jury here the next
Sessions.
