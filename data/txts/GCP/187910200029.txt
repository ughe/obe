MR. JUSTICE HAWKINS excpressed a doubt whether upon this evidence the
intent alleged could be supported; the intent must accompany the act; in this
case, the prosecutria, to prevent being injured, herself seized the knife, and no
caused the wound. Although it might amount to unlawfully wounding, the
intent could scarcely be inferred. As the point did not appear to have been
raised, he would, if necessary, reserve it.
