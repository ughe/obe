520. JOHN JAMIESON, stealing 1 spoon, value 38.; the goods of
Thomas Mason. .
GEORGE WRIGHT. I am waiter at the Jamaica coffee-house, St. Michael's-
alley : Mr. Mason is the proprietor. On 27th Jan., soon after six o'clock,
the prisoner entered the coffee-room, sat downl, and took a paper-he after-
wards went to a table where there was a glass; took a spoon out of it, and
was going out-I stopped him, and said, "You have stolen a spoon"-he
took it out of his pocket, and gave it me--I gave it to Mr. Mason-be gave
it to the constable.
Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON. Q. Has Mr. Mason any partner?
4. No-persons are not allowed to come there to read the newspapers-if a
subscriber wants to meet a person there, it is common to do so--the prisoner
did not appear in the least intoxicated.
THOMAS Mason. I saw the prisoner stopped, going out of my door-the
waiter gave me the spoon; I gave it to the officer--this it.
JOHN LATHAM TURNER (City-policeman, 531). I took the prisoner, and
produce the spoon.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 42.- Recommended to morey hy the Jury.- Confined One Month.
