WILLIAM RING. I have been in the employ of Mr. Thomas, of Old Bond
Street, 12 years—I produce their return approbation book-on 16th July.
1878. I returned to the defendant a lady's ruby and diamond half-hoop ring.
three brilliants and two rubies, value 407.
(The defendant's examination in bankruptcy was here put in.)
THOMAS FULLER CARTER. I am an accountant, of Gresham Buildings-I
was receiver under the proceedings against the defendant in August, 1878,
and prepared the statement of his affairs from the instructions of Mr. Spire,
his solicitor-Harris gave me a deficiency account; it is really a statement
taken down from his mouth on the morning of the meeting - I have no copy
of it I gave it to the solicitor; this is it (produced) I heard the defendant
questioned at the meeting, and he confirmed these items—I asked him to
sign it, and he refused-I objected to sign it or to be responsible for it-I
prepared it by his instructions by word of mouth, not from his books - I
moeived certain of Harris's stock, which I locked up in the safe, and on 3rd
February handed it to Mr. Garrett, the trustee, according to their inventory
this is his receipt for it.
Cross-examined. I did not object to this account to the defendant-I told
him before the meeting that most likely the creditors would require him to
give some particulars, and he gave me that I did not analyse any of the
statements, but I can tell you about some of the items after paying 10s. in:
the pound off the actual amount, 11,0001., the odd sum was allowed for my
costs-Mr. Garrett told us only to put down 1,4381. because the debtor told
me Mr. Garrett would not prove for the whole amount, only for 108. in the
pound-I paid Tuck and Godfrey their composition myself, excepting the
last bill-there is only 101. unpaid to them if they are in the present
account again, that is treating their debt as revived the defendant estimates
his household expenses for two and a half years at 2601. a year, but from
his examination at the Bankruptcy Court 3602. would be nearer the mark
that mistake is against himself-I prepared this amended deficiency account
(produced), which shows about 6301. unaccounted for. (Giving the items)--the
value of the stock handed over to me by the debtor was 1,3857. 148., and the
stock on approbation 1527. 98., making a total of 1,5327. 38. of stock available
for his creditors-his daughter was married during that period, which put
him to considerable expense.
Re-acamined. I did not prepare this amended account by testing the
books--I did not exercise my ingenuity at all; it is a plain statement of
Tacts-there is in it a difference in valuation of furniture in 1876 and 1878
- put that in as a credit on the deficiency account the amount is 751.
