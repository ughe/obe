SMITH, James (33, umbrella maker). Uttering counterfeit coin
twice on the same day.
Mr. Pickersgill, M.P., prosecuted.

Road, Notting Hill. About 6 p.m on June 25 prisoner came in
and asked for a pennyworth of shag, and gave me a florin. I handed
