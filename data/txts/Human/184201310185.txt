I have no reason to know that he said anything about the horse to my wife—he said something of this sort at Wandsworth—I had no idea that he had sold it—I never turn my horses out of a night—I took the prisoner out of charity, and paid him 18 d . a night.  837. ELLEN DALEY was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of Ja nuary , 3 pints of gin, value 3 s  .; and 2 bottles, value 4 d  .; the goods of John Watkins , her master. JOHN WATKINS. I keep the Goat's-head public-house in the Wands worth-road—the prisoner was my cook for about three months. On going into my cellar on the 11th of January, I discovered a quantity of gin on the floor under a vat, and found the key of that vat on the top of an ad joining vat, with the handle wet, as if just laid down by a wet hand—I went into the kitchen, and found two bottles of gin in the meat-screen—I placed them in the same situation in which I found them, and with Trim mings, my son-in-law, retired into a little dairy adjoining the kitchen, to watch—Trimmings got on a chair, so as to look over the top of the door, where there is a blind—in a short time the prisoner came into the kitchen, opened the door of the meat-screen, took out the two bottles of gin, placed them under her apron, and was about to take them out of the kitchen-door into the yard—she went across the kitchen to go out—I then came out of the dairy, stopped her at the door, and said, "Mary, these two bottles of gin belong to me"—I took them from her, sent for a policeman, and she was taken into custody—her room was searched, but nothing of mine found.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Is there a dresser in the kitchen? A.  Yes—I have a pot-boy—the prisoner and he have not had any quar rel to my knowledge—I never heard that the prisoner was called down into the kitchen, with directions that there was a goose to be roasted—she had no notice of her trunks being searched—I had as good a character with her as a girl need to have.
