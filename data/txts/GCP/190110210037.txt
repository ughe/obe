Before Mr. Justice Bigham.
756. RICHARD CORNELIUS ELLIOTT PLEADED Gurury to the
publication of a false and defamatory libel on Joseph Lawrence. Dis
charged on his own recognisances. And (757) EDWARD PALMER (31)
to the manslaughter of Henry Wilson. Three years' penal servitudo.
758. EDWARD PALMER was again indicted on the Coroner's in.
quisition with the wilful murder of Henry Wilson.
Mr. Muir, for the Prosecution, offered no evidence. Nor Guilty.
759. GEORGE PARSONS (30), Feloniously killing and slaying
Arthur Benjamin Cox.
Me. BEARD Prosecuted, and MR PURCELL. Defended.
Elste BEAUMONT. I live at 32, Vauxhall Foad, Lambeth-on Sunday,
September 29th, about 7 p.m., I was with my upele, the deceased, by
Vauxhall Cross, waiting for a tram car to go in the direction of Wands
worth the tram came up and was standing still in the road; we were close
to it-& short distance behind the tram there is a publie lavatory-my
uncle went towards the hind part of the tram on the left side I was just
going to put my foot on to the step of the tram- my uncle was standing
sideways-I saw & two-wheeled cart coming towards us in the same direc-
tion as the tram was pointing; it was going fast ; it knocked my uncle
down; it caught his right shoulder ; it nearly hit me-my uncle fell down
straight along by the tram-the man who was driving did not pull up
but whipped the horse and drove on quicker than he had been driving
before-I ran after the trap-I left my uncle in the road-I called out to
the man to stop, then I went back to my uncle-he was taken to St.
Th mas's Hospital—he died on the following Tuesday morning at 12.15.
Cross-examined. We were there before the tram came up-I did not
botice a lady trying to get into the tram-I saw the trap before it reached
us-I did not think it would come so close as it did I had not got hold
of the rail of the tram--when I looked to see where the trap was after my
unele was knocked down it was more than the length of the tram away;
it had passed the tram horses—I am quite sure the man whipped the horse
--I saw a woman in the cart-I did not see that she had a baby in her
arms, or that there was a little girl in the cart, and a boy kneeling in the
frunt- the man was holding the whip above his shoulder-when the trap
came back I did not notice that the whip was in the socket, with the strings
of a straw basket round it--I do not know that my uncle had a bruise on
his right hip-he had his head towards the tram horses when he was
knocked down-I should have noticed him step back if he had done so
before he was knocked down; he did not step back.
