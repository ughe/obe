1846. SIMON NEWMAN was indicted for feloniously and knowingly
uttering a forged request for the delivery of 27 yards of kersey mere, with
intent to defraud John Paunett Bull and others.
Joan Collis. I am assistant to Messrs. Bull and Way, of St. Martin's
lane, woollen-drapers. On Wednesday, the 28th of July, the prisoner presented
me with this order for kerseymere (produced), he said he had got it from
Mr. Holman-I took it to Mr. Way.
DEX
HENRY SAVORY WAY. I am a woollen-draper, and live at No. 52, St.
Martin's-lane-Collis brought me this order-I went to the prisoner and
asked if he brought it from Mr. Holman-he said “ Yes"-I said, “Did
Mr. Holman write it?"-he said, "Yes"-he afterwards said Mrs. Holman
gave it him, and afterwards that he found it gave him into custody,
SIMON HOLMAN. I am a tailor, and deal with Mr. Way. I sent no order
to him by the prisoner-I have known the prisoner fifteen years- he knew I
dealt there-this order is not my writing.
Prisoner's Defence. A butcher asked me to take the order to Mr. Bull;
I was not aware that I did wrong.
