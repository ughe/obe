VICTORIO PRETI (Interpreted). I come from Italy-I have only been in
England two years—I live at 10, Eyre Court—on the night of June 14 I
saw Podesta and a man named Maranconi standing together --I saw
nothing in Podesta's hand—I do not know Ramondo.
MARIA MELLA. I live in Eyre Court, Saffron Hill—Emilio Ramondo
lives in the same house he came home without a hat on the night of
June 14, and said that he had done his share-he went away a day or
two afterwards, and I saw him no more.
