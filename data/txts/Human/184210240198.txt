   MR. CLARKSON . Q.  DO you know of any written paper that was sent to Maidstone? A.  No—I do not know the fact at all—it was merely a matter of discussion between myself and some other persons—we wanted to possess ourselves of all the information we could, and it was considered desirable to have Gosbell, not as a witness, but in consequence of one of the creditors fancying that Gosbell was only a servant there, he was in vited to town, as he might give some information—I understood he was to be written to on that account—he came up, and returned to Maidstone, without our being able to elicit any thing from him—subsequently I saw him before the Magistrate—he was put on his defence, and I think he was required to give security for his appearance—he was bound in his own re cognizance of 40 l .—I think that was on the second or third meeting—I think that he was bound to appear—there might have been several meet ings in which he was not bound over, but I did not notice—I had no con trol over him—I do not think, after he was put on his defence, that he was discharged, except on his own recognizance—I left this to my attorney to manage—we were desirous of getting what information we could from those whom we believed to be innocently acquainted with the transaction.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRICE . Q.  Did Shackleford refer you to any other person besides Bachelard? A.  Yes, to Ingram—that reference was satisfactory as far as it went—he is a reputable tradesman.

WILLIAM WEST. I am warehouseman to William Thomas and John Smart, silk manufacturers, in Milk-street, Cheapside.
