   Cross-examined. Q.  When the cheque was dishonoured, did you detain goods which stood in his name? A.  I should have' done so, but after I got the cheque I let the trucks of bricks go—I think there were twelve—they went away on 8th May—there was some stone left at Richmond, part of that was detained—I knew he was building a large quantity of houses at Twickenham, some on Mr. Wheeler's estate, and some on Mr. Swanston's estate—the goods were carted by Pearse from the station on to both estates.
