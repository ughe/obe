 2475.  EDWARD MARTIN was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of October , 1 waistcoat, value 7s., and 2 handkerchiefs, value 3s., the goods of William Patrick.
 WILLIAM PATRICK. I lodge in  Lower Gun-alley. On the night of the 6th of October I went to bed, and left my waistcoat and two handkerchiefs in a chair by my bedside- the prisoner slept in the same room; I left him there when I got up next morning - I went up afterwards, and missed the things; the prisoner was then gone.
   MARY PETRIE. I am the wife of Robert Petrie; we live in Gun-alley. The prisoner came to lodge with us on the night of the 16th, and borrowed half a crown of me the next morning.
   JAMES WILLIAM BARGINALL. I am a waterman, and lodge in Gun-alley. The prisoner engaged me to go and fetch some things for him on the morning of the 17th; he gave me the slip, and I found him just getting into another man's boat - I pursued him, and took him to the Thames Police-office.
   JAMES WHITE. I am a Thames Police surveyor. I received the prisoner in charge, and found on him a waistcoat, two handkerchiefs, and 3s.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was out of the room an hour before he missed the things - I bought them of a man in the street.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Confined Three Months.
 2476.  JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of October , 2 jackets, value 1l. 18s.; 2 pairs of trousers, value 1l. 10s., and 4 shirts, value 6s., the goods of Henry Burton.
 HENRY BURTON. I am a  mariner, and lodge at the Golden Anchor, in Leadenhall-street. I fell in with the prisoner; he had been an old shipmate of mine - he said he was in distress; I told him to call on me, and I would give him some employment - he did so, and I gave him a bag, containing jackets, trousers, shirts, and a variety of other things, to take to the rendezvous house, on Towerhill - I afterwards went there, but he and the things were not there; I received information, and went to the Angel, in Back-lane - I found him there, and part of my things were on his back; all he had on, except his braces, were mine - there are still three shirts, some jackets, and trousers missing.
   JOHN EASTBROOK(Police-constable K 25). I took the prisoner; he had these things on - he at first said he had sold the rest to a Jew, in a house; I asked him to tell me where the house was - he then said he sold them to a Jew in the street: I found 6s. 5d. on him, which he said was part of the money he had got for them.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. - Confined Three Months.
 2477.  SAMUEL GEORGE was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of October , 1 jacket, value 10s., and 1 pair of trousers, value 10s., the goods of George Thomas Limington : 1 pair of trousers, value 3s., the goods of George Molland ; 1 pair of trousers, value 10s., and 1 jacket, value 7s., the goods of Joseph Clare.
   GEORGE THOMAS LIMINGTON. I live on board the Hyena. On the 8th of October the prisoner came on board with a jacket, which he asked me to buy; I said it was too small for me - he then showed it to Molland, who also refused it; he then tied it up again - he went on shore with me and Molland; after I had dined I returned to the ship with Molland, I missed my jacket and trousers from my berth - these are them.
   GEORGE SEAMAN(Police-constable H 150). I apprehended the prisoner, and asked what he had done with the bundle; he said he should not tell me, for if he had six months in the House of Correction it would only serve to pass away the winter - I afterwards found in a boat belonging to a Dutch vessel, this bundle, which contains the property.
   THOMAS WHITSITT. I belong to the Silesia barque, which laid alongside the Hyena. I saw the prosecutor, Molland, and the prisoner go on shore; I afterwards saw the prisoner go on board the Hyena, and then come on shore with a large bundle in his hand; I do not know what were in it.
     NOT GUILTY.
 2478.  ANN WASHINGTON was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of October , 1 sheet, value 18d., the goods of Simon Knee.
   ANN KNEE. I am the wife of Simon Knee. He let the prisoner a room in a house adjoining to ours, which we rent - she took it on the Monday evening, and we never saw her again till the Friday.
   GEORGE WILLIAMS. I am a pawnbroker. I have a sheet, which was pawned by the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   GEORGE SPOTTISWOOD PECK (Police-constable R 143.) I took the prisoner - she said she had pawned the sheet.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 29. - Confined Seven Days.
