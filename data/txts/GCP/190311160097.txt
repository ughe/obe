Cross-examined. I heard him say before the Magistrate that he did
not remember writing the letters, as he was drunk-he said, “All those
notes I sent off to Scotland Yard I sent under the influence of drink,"
or words to that effect-I have known instances where people have sent
statements which on inquiry have proved to be wholly unfounded-I
