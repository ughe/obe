CONYERS TOBIAS METCALPE. I am a waterman, and live in Black
smiths' Arms-place, Back-church-lane, in the parish of St. George, Best

me-my wife remained with me till eleven or twelve o'clock, and we went

house that night-when we went back to the house I asked my wife
give me the key to open the street door-I unlocked it, and found a
usual-I went up stairs to the middle room, on the first floor--that
about twelve o'clock, or near upon one-I found that door forced open-
it was the room I used to live in- I came down stairs again-I found the but
of the door was forced open, and a large screw on the bottom of the box
staple forced out the lock had been slipped back, as if it was unlocked-

ing, and went out-I came, in about half an hour, and went up stairs, and

a trunk which had been in the cupboard, put out into the room and broke
open-there was no lock on the closet-the trunk belonged to Stanbour
--it had been given to him by my daughter, and he had the key of it--the
prisoner was at the public-house when I went there he left shortly after
my wife came-I think that must have been after nine o'clock-the per

a ball--the public-house is not half a mile from mine-I went out next
morning, about half past ten o'clock, and looked for a policeman, having
suspicion of the prisoner-I told the policeman of the circumstance, and
