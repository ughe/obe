FRANCIS HARTLAND, Thames Police. On the morning of
August 14 I saw Jackson in Milner Street, Chelsea. I told
him I was a police officer inquiring about the forging of the
Belle steamer tickets. He said he had had some, but he and
his pal had sold them, getting 6d. or 1s. out of each ticket.
