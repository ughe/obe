294. MARGARET DONAHOO was indicted for stealing 2 window-cur
tains, value 5s.; the goods of Moses Benjamin, her master.
Moses BENJAMIN. I am a fishmonger, and live in Duke-street, Aldgate,
The prisoner was in my service-on the 13th of Dec., about one o'clock, I
found her stupidly drunk-I missed my window-curtains-these produced
are mine, and were left in a chest in the second pair of stairs room-] can-
not tell whether the chest was locked or open-the prisoner had no business
with them.
ROBINSON WEBB (policeman.) I took the prisoner, searched her, and
found the curtains in her apron. I asked where she got them from-she said
she brought them from Ireland-] asked where she had been living-she told
me to find out, it was no business of mine.
Prisoner. Q. Did not I come to the police-office of my own accord!
A. I do not know.
Prisoner's Defence. My mistress gave me the curtains.
TAMAR MARKs. I did not give the prisoner the curtains or let her have
them directly or indirectly.
Prisoner. You did, and you said the Lord strike you dead if you did not
make me leave your house that morning I went to the station, not as
drunkard, but to get my wages.
GUITy.-Aged -Confined Sia Months.
295. ANDREW FURNESS and HENRY HICKS were indicted for
stealing 2 loaves, value 4d. the goods of William Fogan and another.
DAVID FOGAN. I keep a baker's shop in Hatton-garden. I heard
a
smash of glass in the window, ran out, and a man told me something- over
took the prisoners together, and took them back-Hicks took a loaf out from
under his frock-Furness had another, and commenced eating it- believe
them to be my loaves, but cannot exactly swear to them- told Hicks he
had broken a square of glass and taken some bread-he said he had not,
that Furness had broken it, and he took the bread.
Hicks. I did not say I broke it; I found the bread on the step of the
door.
JAMEs CORNISH (policeman.) I took the prisoners into custody-]
asked what they meant by breaking the glass-they said they did it through
hunger.
Furness's Defence. I did not break the window.
Hicks's Defence. I found the bread at the door; I did not know the
window was broken.
Mr. FOGAN re-eramined. My loaves were not at the door-they could
not get there.
