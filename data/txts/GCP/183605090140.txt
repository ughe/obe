1269. WILLIAM EADON was indicted for feloniously receiving, on
the 5th of April, 4 bushels of oats, value 108., the goods of James Bonner,
which had been lately before stolen, well-knowing the same to have been
stolen ; against the Statute, &c.
HUGR BONNER. I am a cow-keeper, and live in Horse-ferry-road,
Westminster. On the morning of the 20th of April, I went down the
yard, and saw John Street, my father's horse-keeper-I afterwards say
Street come out of the stable, with a sack on his back, which he put on the
steps of an omnibus- I saw the prisoner come out of his stable, and take
the sack from the steps of the omnibus, and pitch it at the corner of the
stable - I told him they were my father's oats-he said they were not-1
had him taken.
NOT GUILTY.
