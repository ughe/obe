CARROLL, Mayor.
738. CHARLES GODFREY was indicted for stealing 1 spoon, value
2s. ; the goods of Guillaume Mauris Baumann, his master; and I buckle,
ll. ; the goods of Malvina Boquet de la Palme.
ANNE MARIE FRANCOISE BAUMANN. I am the wife of Guillaume Mauris
Baumann—he is a Belgian—we live at No. 15, Harley-street—the prisoner
did live in my service—he had left the night before be was found by the
policeman in the area.
PATRICK JENNINGS ( police-constable D 123.) On the morning of the 17th
Of Feb., between four and five o'clock, I was on duty in Harley-street—my
attention was attracted by a noise in the area of No. 15—1 asked if anybody
was there—I got no answer—I went over the area gate, and went down the
steps—I found the prisoner there, and the cellar or vault was full of smoke—
I asked the prisoner if he was servant there—he said he was—I said, Do
you live here now said, rang the kitchen bell—Mrs. Bau-
mann and two servants and a gentleman came down—the smoke was cop.
siderable—it did not blaze much—another constable threw some water into the
vault—I searched the prisoner, and found on him this spoon, this buckle,
some duplicates, and a box of matches—he said he picked the buckle up—
the spoon he acknowledged to taking—I enquired why the straw in the vault
was set on fire—he said he was cold—it smoked so that we could not enter
the place—we were obliged to get three or four pails of water—it was a
quantity of straw and hay burning.
Prisoner. I broke the spoon, and I did not like to give it up; I hoped
to restore it when I had money enough.
MRS. BAUMANN. The name is cut out of this spoon—I have got one similar
to it—this is our spoon.
MALVINA BOQVET DE LA PALME. I am single, and live at No. 15, Harley.
street, with Mrs. Baumann—this buckle belongs to me—I do not know where
I lost it from—I was in France when it was lost—I have a buckle here like
it—they are shoe buckles—they are imitation diamonds set in silver—I think
this buckle was in a desk in the drawing room.
Prisoner's Defenee. I found the buckle; I did not think it was of any
value.
GUILTY. Aged 16.—Confined Three Month'.
