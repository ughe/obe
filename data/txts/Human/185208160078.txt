 844. JAMES BRADSHAW was indicted for feloniously killing and slaying Alfred Lowman : he was also charged upon the Coroner's Inquisition with the like offence.
   MR. RYLAND conducted the Prosecution. GEORGE FAYRER. I am a surgeon, residing at Barking. On Sunday, 4th July, about 3 o'clock in the afternoon, I was called to see the deceased—he was in a house near where the accident happened—he was then alive, but he only lived a few minutes—he did not show any symptom of consciousness—I examined his body after death—the cause of his death was a very severe fracture on the side of the head—the brain appeared—it might be occasioned by a very severe blow—it might have been from a cart falling on him.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Did you know the prisoner before? A.  Yes; he lived within half a mile of Barking—he is married, and I believe has two children—he was not used to drive, and I may observe that I have heard that the horse was a difficult one to drive. ROBERT JAMES POWELL. I am a labouring man, and live at Barking.
