   Re-examined . The prisoner and I had been standing at the door about half a minute before the man reeled and fell—we did not think he was insensible, we thought he was drunk—I did not see any blood on his face. GEORGE DEAN. I am a bricklayer's labourer, and live at 9, Grey Street—on the evening of the 19th April I was coming down Grey Street, within about ten feet of where this happened—I saw the prisoner putting a man out at the door—there is a little bit of a step there, about three inches high, and when the man got to the edge of the step he slipped round with his feet and fell right across the pavement—the prisoner had got hold of one shoulder—there were only me and two ladies at the door at the time; after the man fell of course there was a great crowd.
   Cross-examined.
