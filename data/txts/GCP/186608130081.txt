ANTHONY WILSON MONGER. I am a detective officer of the City-I
took prisoner into custody on Thursday, the 26th July, in Mr. Seott's
house, in the Minories-before doing so I told him I should take him into
custody on a charge of forging and uttering a bill for 301.-I gave him a
caution--he said, "I have nothing to say ”_I put him in the cab, and
going to the station he said, “I know it's a forgery. I am very sorry Mr.
Scott has taken these steps in the matter. My cousin knows nothing
about it. Had Mr. Scott served my cousin with a copy of a writ he would
have paid the money, as he has done before."

take them up.
