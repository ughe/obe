I went back to examine some mining property and to settle up
my affairs. I have not heard that I am wanted in America by
the postal authorities. I had settled up all my affairs that had
any reference to the postal authorities. There is an offence
against the Ameriean eriminal law called using the United
States mails for the purpose of fraud, and the United States
postal authorities are entitled to apply to a Court of Justice and
get what is called a Fraud Order made against a person who
has used the mails for the purposes of fraud. I know a Fraud
Order was made against me in July, 1904, but that is a different
thing to being wanted by a Court of Justice. I believe the
Fraud Order made against me has not been rescinded. I have
been in America twice since, and was there for five months last
year. I do not know that the New York police have sent in.
formation to this country that they want me for that oflence.
