   BEFORE THE COMMON SERJEANT .
 (Wednesday, March 29.) LARKINS  , William Ashley (32, none) , unlawfully writing and publishing and causing to be published a certain false, malicious, scandalous, and defamatory libel of and concerning Elsie Piggott .
 Mr. Eustace Fulton prosecuted.
 The Jury were sworn to try whether prisoner was insane, so as to be unable to plead.
 Detective-inspector  THOMAS TAPPENDEN , F Division, produced and proved a printed document bearing the name "Larkins' Employment Scheme." SIDNEY REGINALD DYER, Medical Officer, Brixton Prison. I have had prisoner under my daily observation since he came to the prison on March 6 with a view to discovering his mental condition. I have seen this printed document headed "Memorial of William Ashley Larkins to the Lord High Chancellor of Great Britain." (This was read.
