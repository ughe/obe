FREDERICK George NETHEROLIFT. I have been an expert in handwriting
for twenty-five years. I assisted my father before, who had a long experience
in these matters--I entirely agree with Mr. Chabot that that letter, purport
ing to come from Mr. Hale, came from the same person who wrote the
others.
Cross-examined. Q. Have you and Mr. Chabot disagreed 4. Twice in
our lives-we did not disagree at Bow Street-Mr. Chabot declined to give
a decided opinion, and I gave one---my father and I disagreed once I give
my independent opinion I was not called at the Police Court.
