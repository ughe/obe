MURTY, Philip (17, bootmaker), CHAPMAN, Patrick (16,
printer), and ALLEN, Thomas (15, stickmaker), were charged on
the coroner's inquisition with the wilful murder of Garrett William
Dundon. Mr. Muir, for the prosecution, said the prisoner's were all
brought up before the magistrate charged with wilful murder. The
magistrate discharged them on that charge, and with his lordship's
permission he proposed to offer no evidence. A verdict of Not guilty
was entered.
