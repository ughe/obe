 1415. GEORGE HOLLOWAY was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of April , 1 shawl, value 5 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Michael Smith . MICHAEL SMITH. I am a linendraper, and live in Crawford-street, Marylebone. On the 24th of April, I had some shawls pinned to an iron rod at the door—I heard a noise at the door between nine and ten o'clock —I went to the door, and saw the prisoner going away—I heard a call of "Stop thief"—I ran after him, and overtook him, with one of the shawls in his possession—I called a policeman, and gave him in charge—he said he did not intend to steal it, he was only going to look at it—he was about twenty yards from the shop.
   Prisoner.  I never had the shawl at all. Witness.  I found it in his possession. JOHN GILBERT( police-constable D  73.) I heard the cry of "Stop thief," and secured the prisoner with the shawl under his arm—he said he was only going to look at it.
 (  Property produced and sworn to. ) GUILTY.* Aged 28.— Transported for Seven Years.
