   Cross-examined. Q.  Is it true that he refused to tell you where he got them? A.  Yes—he refused to give me a satisfactory account—I said so before the Magistrate, but do not see it in my depositions—it is not there. GEORGE HUNT. I am a livery-stable keeper, and live at Islington. I also have two places, one in John-street, and the other in Barnsbury-street, Islington—the prisoner had been in my service a fortnight when he left me—I never sent him to Mr. Watts for blacking or brushes, or authorized him to get any.
   Cross-examined. Q.  If you wanted brushes would there be a great crime in a man going and getting them? A.  Yes, without my orders—my men have not done so during the twelve years I have been in business—I never allow any one to get anything on credit, or in my name—Mr. Wood is in part nership with me—I call it my place, because I live there—my name is George.
