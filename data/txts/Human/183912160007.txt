   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT .— Monday, December  16 th,  1839.
   First Jury, before Mr. Recorder .
 276. WILLIAM EDWARDS was indicted for embezzlement and larceny.
   MR. BODKIN , on the part of the Prosecution, declined offering any evidence . NOT GUILTY.
 277. JAMES GRIFFITHS was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of December , 14lbs. weight of lead, value 2 s  . 3 d  ., the property of Francis Bramah and others, his masters; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined Three Months.
 (The prisoner received a good character.)
 278. GEORGE DOUGLAS was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of December , 1 pastile lamp, value 14 s  ., the goods of James Wheaton ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 14.— Confined Three Months.
 279. ELIZABETH LABURN was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of November , 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  ., the goods of James Stewart Robertson , her master.
   MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution . MAGDALENE ERSKINE ROBERTSON. I am the wife of James Stewart Robertson, who keeps an hotel, in Little Brook-street, Hanover-square. The prisoner was our house-maid—she left on the 2nd of December—in November, in consequence of something that passed in reference to an old lady named Williams, from Bath, who lodged at car house, a servant of ours, named Hudson, was taken into custody, and made some statement, in consequence of which the prisoner was taken on the 9th of December, and the officer Fraser produced a cambric pocket-handkerchief to me—( looking at one )—this is it—it was missed about the 27th of November—I had not given it to the prisoner, nor was I aware she had it—it must have been taken from my room—she had to clean the rooms—she left at a week's notice, which is not usual—she gave as notice on account of our leaving the hotel—she did not wish to live with our successors.
