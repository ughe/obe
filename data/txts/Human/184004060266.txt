 1292. HENRY HEIDEN was indicted for feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Oliver Fain , on the   31st of March , and stealing therein, 1 watch, value 3 l  .; 1 watch-chain, value 6 d  .; and 1 seal, value 6 d  .; the goods of William Burgess . WILLIAM BURGESS. I am foreman to Mr. Oliver Pain, of Broadway, Stratford, in the parish of West Ham. I had ft watch on the 31st of March, which I saw safe at eight o'clock at night, in the drawer in the bakehouse, which is part of the house—I came back at eleven o'clock—It was gone—I immediately informed the police, and told my suspicions—the prisoner was taken into custody on the following morning—this is my watch, chain, and seals—( looking at them )—the bakehouse-doors were shut when I left it—I shut them after me—when I returned, they were still shut—our people go in and out there. FRANCIS HEAPHY. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into cus tody, and told him Mr. Pain wanted to speak to him—he did not ask me what it was for, but accompanied me to Mr. Pain, who told him, in my presence, that a watch had been stolen out of the bakehouse, belonging to his man, and that he, the prisoner, was strongly suspected—he said the watch was stolen between nine and eleven o'clock—the prisoner re marked, "I was in-doors at a quarter to ten o'clock"—there were foot-marks in the garden—he afterwards said that he went into the parlour, stole the watch, and put it into his boot—he pulled off his boot, and the pro perty was found in it—he said he was sorry for it.
   Prisoner.  It is the first time I was in trouble; I throw myself on the mercy of the Court.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY  of Larceny only. Aged 23.— Recommended to mercy. Confined Three Months.
   Before Mr. Baron Alderson.
 1293. GEORGE HARVEY was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of April , 1 gelding, price 6 l  .; 1 set of harness, value 10 s  .; 1 cart, value 4 l  ., and 3 pigs, value 3 l  . 19 s  .; the property of John Regan . JOHN REGAN. I live at East Ham, in Essex, and deal in pigs, which 1 take about in a cart. On Monday, the 6th of April, I met the pri soner at the Duke's Head public-house, Walthamstow, about half-past twelve o'clock—he was generally about that house—I had three pigs in my cart, which had a pony to it, with harness on—I had to show some pigs to a lady, but I was taken very ill, and the prisoner said he would mind the cart, if I gave him a pint of beer—I asked him to take the horse and cart, and show the pigs to the lady—he was to have returned in a quarter of an hour, but I saw no more of him till he was in custody at Barnet—I never told him to sell the pigs, but to show them to the lady—the policeman afterwards showed me two of my pigs.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Suppose the lady took a fancy to them—the price of two was 28 s ., and the other, 24 s .? A.  Yes—he was to have a pint of beer when he returned.
