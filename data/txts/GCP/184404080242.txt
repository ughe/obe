1322. JOHN JACKSON and DANIEL GLANVILLE were indicted for
stealing 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of a certain woman, unknown,
from her person.
John WHITE (police-constable R 180.) On the 9th of April I was at
Greenwich-fair-I saw Jackson put his hand into a woman's pocket, take
something, and pass it to Glanville, I took them, and found this handker-
chief crumpled up in Glanville's pocket.
John SEAGER. I am a labourer, and live at Deptford. I have been in
the police, but am superannuated— I was with White, and saw the prisoners
go down the fair in company, about 150 yards—I saw Jackson take some-
thing from a woman's pocket, which appeared to be a handkerchief, and pass
it to Glanville-I seized Jackson-he tried to get from me I tried to get
the woman, but the crowd was so great I could not get at her-I have been
getting my living the last six months by attending at the police-court, and
going on messages for Mr. Finch or the Magistrate.
Jackson's Defence. A boy took the handkerchief from a young woman,
and threw it to me : Glanville was not there.
(Glanville received a good character.)
JACKSON-GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
GLANVILLE-Guilty. Aged 14.-Confined Ten Days, and Whipped.
