SARAH COOPER. I live at Lewisham. I was Mrs. Brundell's nurse during
her late confinement on the 27th of Aug. last, between two and three o'clock
in the day, nearly three o'clock, a knock came at the door-I answered it,
and Weston and Cooper were there they wished to speak to Mrs. Brundell
--Weston spoke I said I did not know that he could speak to her, but I
would go and ask her--he said he must speak to her, for he had some very
particular business- I went to ask her if she could speak to them, and they
walked in after me, and shut the door after them I spoke to Mrs. Brundell,
and before I had time to return Weston followed me down stairs into the
front kitchen, to Mrs. Brundell-she was not keeping her bed, but sitting in
the front kitchen, which was the sitting-room--I heard him say to Mrs.
Brundell that he was come on very particular business I went into the back
kitchen on hearing that I afterwards came into the front kitchen, and asked
Mrs. Brut:dell what was the matter-she said she could hardly tell me what

up stairs with them when they came down again they went into the back
