1836. JANE SMITH was indicted for stealing 1 pewter-pot, value 8d.
the goods of George Gilbert; and that she had been before convicted of
felony.
RICHARD COMPTON (police-constable D 87.) I was on duty at Padding-
ton-green on the morning of the 28th of Aug.-I saw the prisoner go through
the churchyard-she turned to the left and went to a comer, where this
pewter-pot was, in a gallon can-she stooped and pretended to pick up some-
thing-she took the pot and put it in her basket, and was coming awayI
took her-she said she did not mean to steal it-when I got her to the
station she said she only had three months for it the last time.
