623. THOMAS RAMSAY was indicted for embezzlement.
MR. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.
FRANCIS GEORGE MARCH DESANGES. The prisoner was my town travel-
ler up to 1846; it was his duty to receive money for me and account as
he received it he has never paid or accounted for 51. 8s. from Mr. Cox,
71. 53. from Mr. Salter, or 111. 58. from Mr. Kent-I asked him how they
came not to be paid over if he had received them--he said, " If I have re-
ceived them I must pay you"-I turned round and he was gone, and he
never came back-he has never paid them or accounted for them-he always
gave me the small accounts as he collected them-no memorandum was made
of his having paid them-1 had been for some months asking him about these
accounts, particularly Mr. Kent's-he said he had not got the money; and
Mr. Salter being dead, I sent him again for that, and he said they had not
paid, and I afterwards found they had paid months before-he was nearly
thirty years in my father's service and mine-be left me for eleven months
I took him back, and paid him higher wages, and then he began to rob me.
Cox. I am clerk to Le Bousfield and Company, silk-manu-
facturers. On 20th July, 1846, I paid the prisoner, on their account, 51. 88.,
for Mr. Desanges-this receipt (produced) is the prisoner's writing.
JAMES JOSHUAX WHITER. I am warehouseman to Mrs. Salter, a silk-
manufacturer-she was indebted to Mr. Desanges in July, 1846, 77.58.-Mr.
Salter, who is dead, must have paid the prisoner, because he alone paid his
accounts-I got this receipt off the file-it is in the prisoner's writing.
WILLIAM ABBOTT KENT. I live in Milk-street, Cheapside-I owed Mr.
Desanges 111. 48., and paid the prisoner by cheque-be asked me not to
cross it, and I did not-he wrote this receipt in my presence.
GEORGE TEAKLE (police-sergeant, H 8). I took the prisoner on 8th Feb.
-I said he was charged with embezzling various sums of Mr. Desanges-he
said he thought it was all dropped a long while I said I had been looking
after him at the time he absconded, but found he was gone into the country,
that the amount was very large, about 301.-be said, "No! I never had (or
received) above 241.- I have seen the prisoner four or five times and my
opinion was, that it was all dropped because I had not seen Mr. Desanges."
Guilty.-Confined Six Months.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
