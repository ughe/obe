FREDERICK HARNETT. I am a fruiterer living at Lower Edmonton-
am known as 46 Ginger Johnson"- November 22nd last I was at
Barnet Market and bought there a chestnut pony-on the way home I
saw the prisoner-the pony he was driving refused to go-I pulled up
and spoke to him-we went and had a drink together-we stayed in the
public-house for about thirty minutes-he asked me if I would buy the
lot-I asked him to change with the pony I had-he said no, he wanted to
sell the lot right out, he could do with the money-I asked what he wanted
the asked E7-I eventually bought it for E4 108.-he gave me this receipt
--on November 23rd I went to Spitalfielde Market with the samne pony
someone spoke to me there and I was given into custody and charged with

receipt.
