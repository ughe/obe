CHARLES GRAHAM GRANT I am police surgeon of division-I
examined the body of the deceased with the last witness-I have heard
his evidence-in all essential particulars I agree with itafter the injury
to the chest, in my opinion, it would have been possible for the woman to
have run across the road-the knife was handed to me by the police it
had been newly sharpened and was covered with oil-there was 2 moist,
red stain upon, it having the appearance of blood; on testing it I found
it to be so-the knife fitted the wounds and corresponded to the incisions
in the body.
Cross-eramined. It would be an unusual thing for a woman to be
able to run across the road after such a wound had been inflieted in her
ehest; if there had been no evidence of the time or method in which it
was inflieted, I should have expeeted it to have been inflicted after she
crossed the road and before she died.
Re-examined. In my opinion it was quite possible for her to have crossed
the road after the wound was inflieted
