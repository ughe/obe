Lodge, was indebted to me-I took steps to enforce a bill of sale that I
held; I was the first mortgagee; I paid about 2001. arrears of rent on the
house—the bank gave me notice that they claimed the property-I sold
on the 15th and 16th June, 1877, under my mortgage; a large quantity of
the property was bought in; we acted in conjunction with the Lombard
Bank- I put up the lease for them as well, they being mortgagees of the
lease—this is a copy of the account of the goods they bought, amounting
to 5421., with the addition of 1161. that I received from Dixon-I was paid
for the goods bought by the bank with cheques for 1791. 58., 2001., and
91. 58., and a 1001. bank note, and there was a cheque for 471. 108. of a
person who was indebted to the bank, for whom we were instructed to act,

bought in, making a total of 5421.—Dixon absconded some time in the
beginning of July; he had made false declarations that there was no
previous bill of sale I have received all these amounts on behalf of the
West Lodge estate.
