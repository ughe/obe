said, “We have some that were delivered to me yesterday, and I have not paid

Jane ELIZABETH ORTIEB. The prisoner Waite called at my house one
evening to solicit my custom, saying that Mr. Pickett would allow him a
commission on any person who was a good customer- I have dealt with him
in that way from that time--for the first three weeks a young man from Mr.
Pickett's shop brought the things, and from that time I received them of
Mrs. Symmonds.
Cross-examined. Q. Did he say that Mr. Pickett had given him permis-
sion to sell on commission? A. Yes-the three first bills were Mr. Pickett's
bills-he received about 48. a week of me.
ELEANOR BOAKES. I have been in the habit of buying things weekly of

-I was introduced to ber by an old widow-lady.
