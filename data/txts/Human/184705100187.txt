ELLEN MUNICAN and ELLEN SULLIVAN were indicted for stealing 6 blankets, value 3 l  .; 8 sheets, 1 l  . 1 rug, 12 s  .; and 1 pair of drawers, 6 s  .; the goods of William Brown , their master; and that Sullivan had been before convicted of felony. WILLIAM BROWN. I live in Stratton-terrace, Shadwell. The two pri soners were in my service—Munican left me on Saturday, the 10th April, and Sullivan left on Monday the 12th—when she had left, I missed the articles stated—I had Munican taken up—she said she took two blankets and pawned them in one shop, and one blanket at another shop, and had shared the money with Sullivan. MARY ANN SYRELL. I live servant with Mr. Brown. On Saturday, the 10th April, Sullivan took a blanket off the bed—she took it into the back room, folded it up, and gave it to Munican, at the bottom of the stairs—I saw her do it, and Munican directly afterwards took it out.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Where were you? A.  up stairs, in the sane room—it was taken before me—it is a lodging-house for sailors—there are not many persons coming in and out. SAMUEL SMITH. I am a pawnbroker. I have two duplicates of a blanket pawned for half-a-crown, in the name of Ann Brown—the person who pawned it had one duplicate and I had the other—it was pawned on Saturday the 10th of April, and taken out on the Monday—I cannot say it was by either of the prisoners. EBENEZER CLARK. I am assistant to Mr. Hall, a pawnbroker, in High-street—I have the duplicate of a pair of blankets pawned by a female, but redeemed again. WILLIAM CHARLES POTTER( police-constable K  212.) I went to Wapping workhouse, to apprehend Munican—I told her I wanted her, I wanted her, for robbing her master of some blankets and sheets—she said, "I am very sorry I did take the blankets and sheets"—I then went to Sullivan, I told her wanted her for taking a sheet and blanket—she said she did not, but she had received 1 s . 3 d ., part of half-a-crown which Munican pawned a blanket for. HENRY M'KAY( police constable K  54.) I produce a certificate of Sulli van's former conviction—( read—Convicted the 23rd March, 1847, and confined ten days, solitary )—she is the person.
   MUNICAN  — GUILTY. Aged 17.— Recommended to mercy by prosecutor.
   — Confined One Month .
   SULIVAN  — GUILTY. Aged 16.— Recommended to mercy by prosecutor
   — Confined Three Months .
