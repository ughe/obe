WILam STEPHEN. I am captain of the Satellite, a coaster-in October
she loaded a cargo of salt at Middlesborough-o October 7th I engaged

I have no better clothes than these these are all I have got "he had a

-we arrived in the Thames, and began to discharge cargo about October
