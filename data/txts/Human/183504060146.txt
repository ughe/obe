1054. JAMES GIBBON was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of March , 1 tame fowl, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the property of William Lathan . WILLIAM LATHAN. I Jive in Dean-street, Soho. I am a dairy-man, and keep fowls—they run in the street in the day-time. On the 26th of March I missed one—they had been all right the morning before—this is my hen—I can swear to every feather almost. JOHN INNS. I live in Little Windmill-street, St. James's. I bought this fowl of the prisoner. SAMUEL GODDARD( police-constable C  103.) I took the prisoner—he said he stole one fowl, which belonged to Lathan—the other he knew no. thing about.
   Prisoner . A man asked me to buy the fowls—I took him to this wit-ness to sell them. NOT GUILTY.
 1055. JAMES GIBBON was again  indicted for stealing, on the   27th of March , 1 live tame fowl, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the property of William Lew inton . WILLIAM LEWINTON. I live in Westminster. I lost four fowls, out on every morning, from the 26th to the 29th of March; this is one of them. JOHN INNS. I bought this fowl of the prisoner, on the day after the other.
   Prisoner . I met the same man again; he gave me 4 d . for selling this. GUILTY. † Aged 20.— Confined Three Months.
