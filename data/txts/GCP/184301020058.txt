7lbs. weight of bacon, value 3s. 10d., the goods of William Thomas
Goodge.
WILLIAM THOMAS Goodge. I live in Upper Lisson-street, Marylebone.
Between eight and nine o'clock in the evening of the 27th of Dec., I had a
piece of bacon outside my door-I missed it, when a person came and told
me-I ran after the prisoner, and found the bacon on him-he struggled to
get away.
HENRY Mune. I saw the prisoner take the bacon.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going along, some young man came and
dropped the bacon.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
