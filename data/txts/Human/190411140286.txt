We use Fox's till—the silver is kept at the side—it is registered if it is put in the till—we count the coppers occasionally, but we can tell about what amount there ought to be—we were very busy when the male prisoner was in the bar—about twenty people were there—the male prisoner called for drinks—he was near the counter—the female prisoner put the coppers into his hand from her closed fist—I was about two feet away—she took as many coppers as her hand would hold—I allowed them to repeat the operation four times without saying a word to her. JOHN FROST. I am a licensed victualler, and keep the Pigeons public house, Romford Road—my barmaid Jeffreys left me on Saturday, October 23 th—the female prisoner is not that barmaid, but she was a barmaid at my public house—I did not give her notice—she was discharged on Saturday, October 22nd—Jeffreys had served me three years in two terms—there is no suggestion against her character, but the very opposite—I received a communication as to the character of a barmaid calling herself Jeff revs, and thinking it was my late barmaid I gave her a character—in consequence of a further communication I received from Buckingham I sent for him and saw him.
