JAMES HILLS. I am cashier to Messrs. Clark, Morris, and Clark, attorneys,
in Coleman-street--I have got a rough cash-book here-Rawlins gave me
an amount on 23d October; the sum total was 1561. 118. 50.-there was a
1001. note, 37,625, May 19th, 1860; a 501. note, 50,679, of June 20th,
1860, and the rest in cash.
WILLIAM TINGEY. (The evidence of this witness, given on the former trial,
was read over to him, to which he assented.)
Cross-examined by MR. SLEIGH, Q. Were these bills to which you refer
Holcroft's trade bills? A. They were drawn by Holcroft on his customers,
and amount to between 2,0001. and 3,0001.we had discounted trade bills
of Holcroft's for four or five years they came to me from Mr. Ramsay, who
told me he received them from Durdin--when Ramsay called on me with
reference to procuring a loan on the security of a bill of sale, he was alone
-I never saw Durdin in company with him-Ramsay is the person with
whom I negotiated, and I returned the money to him on the negotiation not
being carried out.
MR. GIFFARD. Q. Is Mr. Ramsay here to-day! A. Yes this (produced)
is the bill of sale.
THOMAS VALENTINE HEUTSUN. I am a clerk at Messrs. Currie's bank,
29, Cornhill-this produced) is Holcroft's pass-book-he opened his account
on November 21st, 1850—I find a credit on 20 December for 1101., but
for that sum on the 2d there would not have been assets to meet a cheque
for 1501. on the 3d, not taking the whole transaction; but there would be
on the 3d, because there was money paid in to meet it-striking out the
1102. there would not have been enough assets to meet the 150%.
COURT. Q. If nothing had been paid in on the 3d, there would not
have been money enough to meet the drawings-out of the 3d 1 4. The
transactions of the 2d would not have been sufficient to pay the transactions
of the 3d; even with the 1101. we should not have had enough-it was the
payings-in of the 3d that enabled us to pay the payings-out; leaving a trifling
balance it is some time ago since I was at Messrs. Currie's-I can hardly
recollect what I was there my evidence is secondary, the party being dead.
MR. SLEIGI. Q. I want the book by which you can tell me of what that
1501. consisted ? A. May I step down and look for it--the books are here,
but some party has neglected to bring them up. (The witness went to search
for them.)
Thomas DYSON (re-examined). This entry of 1101. in this book (produced),
is my writing—it represents a cheque drawn by Durdin on the Commercial
Bank, and paid to Currie & Co. in a charge of 1761. 108. it was paid on
3d December, 1851.
COURT. Q. We have heard that Mr. Millard went to the bank, saw
Durdin, and got the money for it; that is not the way in which the Bank
discounts bills ! A. Certainly not; if he got money he would have &
cheque cashed at the counter ; but a bill would have to be submitted to the
committee and cashed the next day-Durdin had not authority to discount
a bill for Holcroft as a customer of the bank.
MR. GIFFARD. Q. I believe for some years the bank have not discounted
bills for Holcroft ? A. Not for five, six, or seven years.
MR. SLEIGH, Q. Was it that the bank declined to go on discounting?
4. I cannot say.
