Before Mr. Recorder.
1287. JAMES WHITE was indicted for stealing 30lbs. weight of iron,
Value 4s., the goods of the Eastern Counties Railway Company.
upany
"Mr. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.
Joseph Benton (police-constable K 381.) I am stationed at Stratford.
In Feb. I saw the prisoner and Henry Revel, coming up the Eastern Counties
Railway- I watched them-they came to a wall five or six feet high, and Revel
got over--White lifted a bag up, and Revel took it from him--they went into
Stratford, and carried the bag- I watched them they saw me, and tried to
make away-I ran and stopped Revel, and took the bag from him-White
made his escape--this is the bag-I am sure it is the same that White gave
him-it contains iron bolts and pins--the prisoner had no business on the
railway, nor to get over the wall they could go a different way
Prisoner. Q. Where was it you saw us? A. I was against a school,
at the end of Great North-street-I watched them come up the line
when Revel got over, and White handed the bag, I walked up to them they
saw me and cut up Stratford-I stopped Revel, who has been tried, and
White ran as hard as he could.
JAMES GALE. I am gate-keeper in the employ of the Eastern Counties
Railway Company. I can identify these bolts and nuts as the property of
the railway company-here is the Eastern Counties Railway mark on them
-I am in the babit of receiving them at my gate-there is such property on
the railway--they are using on the line daily-people watch the workmen away
to their meals, and take these things-these are as good as ever they were.
Prisoner's Defence. The officer did not see me with Revel; I did not
hand the bag; I was not on the line; Revel said he should like to do some-
thing to get a month.
Guilty.* Aged 19.-Confined Two Months.
