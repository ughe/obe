HENRY TRUMPSHAW  , alias    Drabshaw, was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of August , at   St. Pancras , 1 work-box, value 2 l  . 10 s  .; 1 ring, value 5 l  .; 1 measure, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; 1 penknife, value 4 s  . 6 d  .; 1 pair of scissors, value 4 s  . 6 d  .; 1 stiletto, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; 1 needle-box, vale 2 s  . 6 d  .; 12 reels, value 6 s  .; and 1 bodkin-case, value 3 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Henry Beckley Richardson , in his dwelling-house. HENRY BECKLEY RICHARDSON. I live at No. 1, Gothic Cottages, Park village East, Regent's-park. On the 21st of August, a little before three o'clock, Host a work-box, which I had seen that morning on the loo table in the drawing-room on the ground-floor—it contained the articles stated—the house is almost surrounded by a garden, and the street-door is kept open during the day—the garden-door was kept locked—I think I have some recollection of the prisoner—he has been a sweep, and I think has swept the chimneys in my house. WILLIAM SIMPSON. I am pot-boy at the Victoria, in Crescent-place, Hampstead-road. The Gothic Cottages are just across the road—I know the prisoner. On Tuesday, the 21st of August, about twenty minutes after two o'clock, I saw him under Mr. Richardson's palings, with another boy—I did not see the other boy get over, but I missed him, and then I saw him hand the box over to the prisoner, who walked away with it—they went away together till they got to Augusta-street, and then they parted—I ran on and followed the prisoner with the box—they walked right up the Albany-road, and turned down by the canal—I ran round to see if I could see the prisoner again, but could see no more of him—I then ran and asked Mr. Richardson if he had lost any thing—I and the police man went to look for him, but could not find him—I saw him in custody eight o'clock—the box seemed a dark one, with some white about it—it poured with rain at the time—I did not know whether I might take him, as I could not see a policeman.
