SAMUEL FRANKLYN (Policeman). About 10.45, on the night of 9th
January, I went round the prosecutor's premises--the ducks and pigs
were safe- I considered them in my custody-at 1.30 I saw the sow run-
ning about, very savage at her pigs being gone-she ought to have been in
the piggory-I watched, and saw the prisoner enter the shed.
ROBERT Eves. I live at Lewisham, and am & pork-butcher-I dressed

would be a likely instrument to do it with, or something else without
sharpe edge-anybody could see that these pigs were not properly killed
some butchers hit them on the head to save them from hallosing, but ant

Cross-examined. If I took hold of a young pig of 16 lbs. it would squeak,
so as to be heard a considerable distance off, but if they were knocked
on the head first, and then taken, they would not squeak.
