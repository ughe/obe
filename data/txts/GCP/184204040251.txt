MR. CLARKSON called
ELIZABETH REDGRAVE. I live at No. 6, Walcot-square, Lambeth.
My brother is an attorney-I live with him. I know the prisoner-I have
been on terms of intimacy with her for a year and a half, or better--and have
visited her for phat time-she came to my house on the 12th of February,
Saturday, about three o'clock in the afternoon—she came to take tea and
supper-I have referde to a book to ascertain that it was the 12th of February
-she did not leave my society till she left for good that night, which was
at ten o'clock-we went out shopping together-she did not go home be-
tween three and ten o'clock-she returned, and took tea and supper with me
-I have seen this purse two or three times before --(the one identified by
Mrs. Peppercorn)- I have not seen it since it was finished-I saw the
prisoner knitting it in the month of January--if it it not the same, it is
exactly like it-she has brought it to my house--she has worked at it
there--to the best of my belief this is the material on which she was work-
ing- I know the difference between working it round and working it the
other way.
MR. ATTORNEY GENERAL. Q. Do you know the meaning of a
purse being worked round ? A. No, unless it is what I have seen, worked
round in this way-I saw her at work on this in January-I never saw
the clasps, or tassels, or rings--I have seen it two or three times I know
nothing of this other purse-I did not see the tassels or rings put on that
-I have examined the first one, and she complained of the silk being fine at
the time she came to my house at three o'clock, on the 12th of February-I
remember the day very well—I find a memorandum in my book-I am come
forward to speak the truth-I always make a memorandum of every thing
I purchase-on that day I made several purchases - I can name the articles
-my attention was drawn to the fact on the evening of the day the pri-
soner was taken into custody- I was not there when she was taken-1
heard of it in the evening of the same day-I do not know that I named
to any body particularly that I could prove she was from the post-office
from three to ten-the attorney applied to me to give evidence yesterday
evening-I never communicated with him before that I told him what
evidence I should be able to give, but never before last night-1 had
named it to my brother, being with him, that I had put it down I re-

was with me that day-I shouid think the prisoner is about twenty-one
