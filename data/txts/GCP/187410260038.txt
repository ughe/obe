LUGIGI BRENTINI. I am & confectioner, of 84, High Holborn, on the
morning of October 7th, I served the prisoner with some ginger beer and

she got it from a gentleman she did not know-I broke it and gave it to
the policeman.
HENRY HARRIS (Policeman E 49.) Mr. Brentini gave the prisoner into
