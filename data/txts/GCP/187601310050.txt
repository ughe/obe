GUILTY.-Twenty Years Penal Servitude.
166. ELLEN EVANS (20), DAVID CLARK (19), and HARRY
SMITH (21), Feloniously cutting and wounding John Lodge, with intent to
do him some grievous bodily harm.
Mr. Rilton conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN LODGE. I am a stoker of 18, River Street, York Road-I know
the three prisoners by sight only on 28th December between 10 and 11
o'olock at night, I came out of a public-house in Pentonville Road; I did
not see the prisoners till Smith struck me on the left side of my face, I then
saw the other two prisoners- I said to Smith “What is that for ?" and
then Clark struck me on the right side of my face with his fist; Smith
then struck me again, and I fell, and he knelt on me, and bit me on my
face, and Clark kicked me on my side-I did not feel anyone stab me, but
when I got up I found I was stabbed just below my elbow-there were four
stabs on my arm, and five cuts in my coat-I did not see Evans do any.
thing, she was close by while I was on the ground, but I do not know
whether she interfered--I had never seen her before I was taken to the
hospital in a cab-the blade of the knife was broken in my arm.
Cross-examined by Evans. I did not ask you if you would come and
have something to drink-I did not ask you in the public-house if you
would come and live with me- I did ask you to go to America with me--
I did not strike you twice-I did not say I would make you come with me.
By THE COURT. I was in the public house, but not with Evans-it is
the Builder's Arms, kept by Mr. Odell - I think Evans was there, and I saw
Smith there.
