(Defence.)
UMBERTO ANTONIO ARTILIA (prisoner, on oath, examined through
an interpreter). I am porter and vegetable man at the Charing
Cross Hotel. I have been in England 28 months, and come from
Piedmont. On the night of July 22 I was in a club at 35, ON
Compton Street, with a couple of friends. I saw Rissone in the
club. He asked me about hope, and I answered him, "He who
hopes dies." I left the club at half past 12. My friends and myself
went out together, leaving Rissone in the club, and went dowa War
dour Street. I next saw Rissone in Wardour Street. He came after
me and insulted me. He said he would do for me, using an expres
sion which said the interpreter) it is impossible to translate. (Ti
arrangio per le feste.) It is not true that I said, “If any e comes
near me I will open him." He tried to quarrel with me. I never
saw him before this night. I said to him, “Go to bed if you are
drunk. We are not looking for you. We do not want you." It is
not true that I pushed him as he was wheeling his bicycle. I went
into St. Martin's Street with my friend. We wanted to go to the
club. Prosecutor came up to us with a policeman. I was lighting
my cigarette. He gave me a blow in the head and threw me dowo.
When I got up I saw a knife in his bands, and jumped at him and
threw him down. We both fell to the ground and I took hold of
the knife; then I heard him shout, and I ran away. I stabbed bim
with the knife I took from him. I never had any intention of doing
him any harm. My own hand was injured. I got wounded when I
took the knife from him.

"I hope to have a drink with you another time." I did not there
upon turn round to prosecutor, and say, "What is the good of
hope !" He addressed me; I did not address him." Prosecutor
did not say to me, "Hope is better than nothing." I answered him:
" Who hopes dies." I left the club before prosecutor. I will swear
I did not say: "Si qualamo mi viene vicino lo apro." Prisoner for
ther cross-examined gave a categorical denial of the whole incident.
