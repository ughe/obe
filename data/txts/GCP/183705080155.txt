REBECCA ANN NICHOLLS. The prisoner came to my baker's shop, at
Lewisham, in March, at ten minutes past six o'clock one Friday even-

Cross-examined by Mr. DOANE. Q. It was rather dark? A. It was
rather dusk, but I judged of it from the sound.

the prisoner came-I am certain of him-he asked for a penny roll—I gave

piece, a sixpence, and a halfpenny-I kept the shilling apart from all the
