1291. EDWARD BELL was indicted for feloniously uttering an order for
the payment of sl., well knowing it to be forged, with intent to defraud
David Charles Barker.
DAVID CHARLES BARKER. I live in Hampton-street, Somers-town, and
am a grocer. I have known the prisoner about eleven months--in Aug. last
he owed me 6s. 11d., which I had applied for several times-he came to my
shop on the 17th of Oct., about half-past five o'clock, and said he had brought
me the money at last-he gave me this check for 81.-he said, "I am too
late for the Bank this evening, and if you will take this I will pay your bill,
and I want more goods" -I said I could not change it-he said, “Do you
know anybody who can change itt-I gave him my card, and said, "Go to
Mr. Bentley in Chapel-street, he will change it for you"--he went away with
it, and returned in a quarter of an hour I said, “ Have you got change !"
he said, " Mr. Bentley will send it," and immediately Mr. Bentley's young
man came in and put eight sovereigns upon the counter towards me--the
prisoner took them up-he had goods amounting to 118. or 128. including the
6s. 11d. I did not see him afterwards till be was apprehended.
Cross-examined by Mr.MELLOR. Q. The check professes to be drawn by one
Tabor? A. Yes--I have inquired, but cannot find Tabor-I knew where the
prisoner resided-the check was not presented till the 30th of Oct.--that was
Mr. Bentley's neglect.
OLAY
THOMAS West. I am servant to Mr. Bentley. The prisoner came and
asked my master for change for a check-master said he could not give it
to a stranger, but sent me with eight sovereigns, which I put upon the counter.
HENRY FOTHEROILL. I am clerk to the London and Westminster Bank.
Princes-street. This check was presented in Oct. last, drawn by Robert
Tabor returned an answer, "No account."
Cross-examined. Q. How many customers are there? A. About 8000-
I do not remember them all I have not our books here- I will swear Tabor
had no account there, I referred to the books, and there was no such name
some of our accounts are closed soon after they are opened-I recollect
the check being presented.co
JOHN AYLIFr policeman.) The prisoner was given into my charge-he
said he should be able to prove where he got the cheek.
Guilty. Aged 29.-Confined Two Years.
