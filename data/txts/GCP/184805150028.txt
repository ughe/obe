Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Who was with you when you spoke
to Alexander ? A. Harvey and Neville-I think he was about six or seven
doors from the house--the outer door was closed- I was in George-street
when I first saw him-I do not think he could see me I was in a house,
and Neville and Harvey also--another policeman in private clothes was further
on in the street-it is very likely Alexander knew me by sight-when he
came out he turned to the left towards Marylebone-street, and I pursued him
the other constables were by when he said I had got him to rights - I had
received my instructions in writing from Mr. Powell--the other policemen
were under my directions—I should not have gone to the back attic unless
Alexander had told me that was his room-I should have gone to the house.
MR. BODKIN. Q. Did you lose sight of Alexander from the time he left

minutes after he came out of the house, and we were back in the house two
or three minutes after he came out-he did not communicate with anybody-
I did not give him an opportunity.
