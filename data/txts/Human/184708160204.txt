   Pheasant's Defence.  I was passing along Long-Acre one evening, and was taken into this brothel, and I was not only taken into one place, but into two, where they gave me something to drink drugged it, and took 18 s . or 19 s . out of my pocket; I was determined to indict the house, and I did so, and I determined to follow it up; this is the first time I have heard anything about any offers having been made; I know nothing whatever of that; if anything of that kind has been done, it has been without my sanc tion or knowledge.
   HUMPHERYS  — GUILTY . Aged 54.— Fined  50 l  ., Confined Eighteen Months , and to enter into his own Recognizance in 200 l  . for five Years.
   PHEASANT  — GUILTY . Aged 38.— Confined One Year , and to enter into his own Recognizance in  40 l. for Three Years.
