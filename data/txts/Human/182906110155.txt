 1348.  HENRY SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of May , 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of a certain person unknown, from his person.
   WILLIAM CRAIG. I am a Bow-street officer. On the 20th of May, about eleven o'clock in the day, I was in Barbican - I saw the prisoner, and a person shorter than him, following a gentleman; I saw the prisoner go up to the gentleman several times, and make attempts at his pocket - they left that gentleman, went on to Finsbury-square, and then followed another gentleman up Chiswell-street into Beech-street- I there saw the prisoner take a handkerchief from the gentleman's pocket; there was a cart going by, and I lost sight of the other, but I took the prisoner; I called to the gentleman - he said he had lost his handkerchief, but would have nothing further to do with it - I did not see it after it was taken from the pocket.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. So you neither have the handkerchief, nor the gentleman? A. No; I have never prosecuted for stealing from persons unknown before - I did not think I was justified in taking him for an attempt.
 CHARLES CLITHEROW. I am a City officer. I was crossing the street, and saw Craig with the prisoner; he spoke to a person who was with me, and asked if he was a City officer - he said No, and I took the prisoner.
 Cross-examined. Q.You saw no handkerchief? A. No; I searched the prisoner, but found nothing on him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was searched, but nothing was found.
 JURY to WILLIAM CRAIG. Q.Did you see the handkerchief? A. Yes; and he ran across the road - I did not see it afterwards.    GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined One Month, and Whipped.
 1349.  EDWARD CROW was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of April , 1 handkerchief, value 4s., the goods of Benjamin Soares Willis , from his person.
 BENJAMIN SOARES WILLIS. I am in the house of Smith, Payne, and Smith. I was on  Ludgate-hill, and lost my handkerchief from my coat pocket; I did not feel it, but was told of it - I had had it five minutes before; I turned round, and a friend pointed out the prisoner, who was about three yards from me, and had his hand in his right-side pocket - I ran up to him, pulled out his hand, and my handkerchief was in it.
 GEORGE HUSSEY. I was walking with Mr. Willis; I saw the prisoner close behind him - I watched, and saw him draw his hand from Mr. Willis' pocket and put it into his own - I asked Willis if he had lost a handkerchief - he said he had, and took it from the prisoner's hand.
   RICHARD AMBROSE. I am an officer. I was standing by and took the prisoner; I found two other handkerchiefs on him, all of different patterns.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that his handkerchief was thrown at him, and as nobody owned it he put it into his pocket.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
 1350.  MARY MOORE was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of June , 5lbs. of beef, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Patrick Clare.
 JOHN VICKERS. I am servant to Patrick Clare, a  butcher, of Fetter-lane. On the 13th of June, about ten o'clock in the evening, I was serving in the shop, and saw the prisoner come across the street, take a piece of beef, and run off - I ran and brought her back with it.
 The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that she was intoxicated, and unconcious of having taken the meat.
     GUILTY. Aged 48. Confined Fourteen Days.

 1352.  HENRY ROSETTA and JAMES GILMORE were indicted for stealing, on the 30th of May , 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of a certain person unknown, from his person.
 JOHN MURREY. I am a letter-cutter, and live at Westminster. On the 30th of May, at half-past ten o'clock at night, I was near  Furnival's-inn, Holborn; I saw the two prisoners together - Rosetta put his hand into a gentleman's pocket, and took out a silk handkerchief; Rosetta, Gilmore, and another person crossed over, and I saw them all looking at it - I went to Simons, the watchman; he asked me to assist him in taking them - I look Gilmore, and he took Rosetta - I said, "The other has the handkerchief," but he got away; the watchman gave me the two prisoners, and went after the other, but could not find him - I went up Holborn after the gentleman; he was with a friend - they were both intoxicated; I wanted him to come back, but he said, "D-n the handkerchief, I will take no trouble about it."
   CHARLES SIMONS. I am a watchman. Murrey gave me information - I saw three persons together; I took Rosetta, and the third ran away - I gave Rosetta to the witness, and followed him up Union-court, into George-alley, but he got away; in the mean time, the prisoners were taken to the watch-house; I was beset by twenty bad characters in George-alley, and I believe if I had not made my escape, I should have been killed.
 GILMORE'S Defence. I was coming home - I was taken hold of, and shoved into the watch-house; I was searched, but nothing found - I never saw this prisoner in my life.
 Two witnesses gave Rosetta a good character.
 ROSETTA -    GUILTY. Aged 13.
 GILMORE -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
