 1336.  HENRY WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of May , 14 yards of ribbon, value 8s., the goods of George Simpson. The prosecutor did not appear. NOT GUILTY.
 1337.  JANE GRAHAM was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of June , 2 caps, value 4s.; 1 gown, value 2s.; 1 shift, value 1s.; 2 yards of ribbon, value 2s., and 1 basket, value 6d., the goods of Charles Burrows.
 SARAH BURROWS. I am the wife of Charles Burrows - we live in  Eden-court, Regent-street; the prisoner slept at my house for ten nights. On Sunday night I went out nursing at Chelsea - on the Tuesday I returned and missed these articles.
   JOHN VARLEY. I am shopman to a pawnbroker in Lisson-grove. On the 9th of June the prisoner pawned this cap for 1s. 6d.
   RICHARD BULL. I took the prisoner. She said she did not care about going to the watch-house.
   SARAH BURROWS. This is my cap.
 Prisoner's Defence. I met the prosecutor's wife, she gave me something to drink, and then persuaded me to go and live with her; a broker came to seize the things - he gave me leave to take mine; she is no more than I am myself, and used to go out with me of an evening.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 1338.  JOHN POWELL was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Joseph Walker , from his person.
   JOSEPH WALKER. On the 19th of April, as I was going down Holborn, I wanted my handkerchief and missed it; I had not felt any thing done to me - I had it but a few minutes before; I saw the prisoner close behind me, and the watchman had the handkerchief.
 GEORGE FORDYCE. I am a watchman. At ten minutes past eleven o'clock the prosecutor walked by me with a lady, the prisoner was close behind him - I followed, and saw him draw this handkerchief out of his pocket - the prosecutor seemed to feel some one pulling at his pocket; the prisoner threw it behind him, and it fell on my foot; the prosecutor turned, and said,
 "He has got my handkerchief;" I said, "No; I have it."
 Prisoner's Defence. I had been drinking, and was going home - I passed this watchman, and saw him pick up the handkerchief; he laid hold of me, and said he saw me take it; I am a stranger in London, and am a native of Liverpool.    GUILTY. Aged 27. Transported for Seven Years.
 First London Jury - Before Mr. Common Sergeant.

 1340.  JOHN ALLFORD and WILLIAM MATTHEWS were indicted for stealing, on the 12th of May , 2 bookwriting-cases, value 20s.; 1 other writing-case, value 7s., and 1 work-box, value 2l. 10s., the goods of Benjamin Slee , from the person of William Pye.
   BENJAMIN SLEE. I am a writing and dressing-casemaker. On the 12th of May I packed up a parcel of articles, and among them were three writing-cases and a small work-box, covered with tortoiseshell: I gave it to William Pye, to take to Mr. Lund's, in Cornhill - he came back without it; I know nothing of the prisoner.
   WILLIAM PYE. I received this parcel; I was going down Fleet-street, and saw a man taking some gold fish out of a bowl - Matthews stood looking at him; he asked if I knew of a place - I said No; he then asked how far I was going; I said "To Mr. Lund's, in Cornhill" - he asked if I had a place; I said "Yes, at Mr. Slee's, in Silver-street;" he went down a street, and I went on; when I got half way down Cheapside. I saw Allford - he asked me if I did not come from Mr. Slee's; I said Yes - he said Mr. Lund, his master, had sent him to my master, and if he overtook me he was to take the parcel, and I was to go home, which I did.
 DANIEL FORRESTER. I was going up Fleet-street on Thursday, and saw the two prisoners together; I took them to Mr. Slee's, and the lad identified them - he expressed no doubt about their being the persons; it was from his description that I took them.
 ALLFORD's Defence. I was coming from over the water - I saw Matthews, and asked him if he knew of a place; the officer came and took us - the boy said at first, he knew me by my trousers, and then by the hat.
 MATTHEWS' Defence. I never saw the boy.
 JURY to PYE. Q. Was any one near enough to hear the conversation that took place? A. No; it was about a quarter of an hour afterwards that I saw Allford.
 ALLFORD -    GUILTY. Aged 19.
 MATTHEWS -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Life.
