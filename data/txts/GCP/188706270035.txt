THOMAS ROBERTS (Re-examined). At Bardocchi's I found 29 collars, and

cold chisel, and four keys in his pockets.
By Bardocchi. I found these things at 57, Northampton Road—I know
it was your room- Pretasio pointed out his box himself.
The Prisoners' Statements before the Magistrate. Bardocchi says: "I was
lodging with two other men at 57, Northampton Road, Clerkenwell-
one of the men left, and he left behind him the collars and the coats,
also a piece of iron and some keys-on 6th May I was at Tottenham and
not in London - I was at Tottenham for three days and seven hours, and
returned to London on the evening of 8th May.” Pretasio says : “The
things I am charged with stealing I bought in Eyre Street Hill-I
think it was on 13th or 14th May if I had known they had been stolen,
I would not have bought them."
Bardoochi in his defence stated that he went into a court for a cortain pur-
poss, and being the worse for drink took some blankets which were hanging
there to his bedroom; that Pretasio and a man named Garganotti were lodging
in the same room, but that the latter had gone when the policeman came to the
room.
Pretasie in his defencs said that he had bought the things which had been

THOMAS ROBERTS (Re-examined). Pretasio told me there was another
