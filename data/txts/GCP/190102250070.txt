HARRY SAUNDERS (126 R). I was with Cole at the Point-I saw the
prisoner coming towards us-he shouted, “Hands up," and pointed a
revolver with his right hand-I ducked and dealt him a blow with my
truncheon-when he was on the ground he said, "Is she dead?” I said,
"No"-he said, "I wish I had killed her."
Cross-examined. I heard the report of the revolver-I was not with
Cole then, but we ran together to the spot, and the prisoner was coming
towards us at a moderate pace-he held up the revolver; I could distinctly
see it--the spot where we met him was not lit-this was at 11 o'clock on
a snowy night in February-I could distinctly see the revolver; his hand
was uplifted at the moment I struck bim-Cole and I were then side by
side, and his sight is as good as mine--I did not ask for the revolver, but
somebody did -a man named Parrett, 21 or 22 years old, was also
present; I saw no one else-I struck the prisoner across his head, because
I feared he was going to fire the revolver-I only struck him once-his
head was towards me; I was a little on his left side; I sprang at him-going
to the station, I held his right arm with my left hand, and my truncheon
in my right-I was ready for any violence-he was sober, in my opinion;
I should not call him dazed.
