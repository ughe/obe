 102. (L.)  Thomas Rogers was indicted for obtaining by false pretences two lobsters of John Easton.~
     Acquitted.
 He was a second time indicted for obtaining a fowl by false pretences, the property of John Attwood.~
     Acquitted.
 103. (L.)  James Allen was indicted for that he, together with Samuel Brumage , did unlawfully and knowingly defraud Samuel Belamy of 96 lb. wt. of nails, val. 42 s. by false pretences.~
 See the trial of Brumage No. 58, in Last session's paper.
     Guilty. T.
 104. (L.)  Francis Leicester was indicted for obtaining two guineas and a half of Sarah Norman by false pretences.
 The prosecutrix did not appear. Acquitted.
 105. (L.)  William Richardson was indicted for obtaining of Peter Majour the sum of 10 l. by false pretences.
 The prosecutor did not appear. Acquitted.
