At the interview there was no bullying  by Mr. Lloyd, nothing of the kind—the prisoner admitted having received the goods—on 2nd July he said he had received them, and was very much pleased with them—he said he might not get his money on 7th, as the Board commenced paying on that day, and paid alphabetically—they sometimes began at A.  and sometimes began at Z ; but, he was sure to be paid by the 12th, his name being in the middle.
