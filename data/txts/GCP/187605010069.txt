HENRY DAVIs. I carry on business with my father and brother as fur
merchants at 4, Jewin Crescent-early in October we were the holders of
an acceptance of the defendant's for 301.-—I went with my father to his
residence- I had not the bill with me because it was not due ; it had two
or three days to run-he said that he was quite prepared to pay it, and
he gave me a 507. note from a japanned cash box in a drawer there were
several other notes with it ; I could not see what they were, but it was to
his advantage to show me that he had a lot of money because he wanted
me to give him credit for more goods-he was to have 201. returned, but
he bought more goods and said that I might keep it on account-I said that
if he would call at the warehouse I should be happy to do business with
him, and he came on 4th October and bought 902. worth of goods on scoount,
and gave me a bill for the balance; I allowed him 24 discount on the order
--the bill was never paid—my brother was present, he gave evidence at
Bow Street similar to mine--he is in Germany. VE S
Cross-examined. I did not tell Richard Dixon that I had seen 8001. in
the cash box-I saw him for the purpose of getting him to take as cash
5001. or 6001. more bills-I offered him Myers' bill for 697. 108., but he
said " This bill is not worth the paper it is written upon " I told him I
thought the man was trustworthy as he had paid me a bill a short time
before, but I never told him I had seen him take out his cash box with
8001.- I called on Myers in the way of business and not to get money,
although the bill was not due till the 8th I took 401, on the 4th-he gave
me a 501. note, but I did not offer him the change because I had not got it
with me--you must ask him why he gave it me before it was due-I told
him I would give him a cheque for the change when he called at my house
-he did call for the balance, I asked him to buy more goods, and he gave
me a 691. 9s. bill this (a small mahogany box) is not the cash box I saw-
I am still under the impression that it was a japanned cash box-I saw some
papers in it--- he took out several notes, but I cannot say whether the 201.
note was at the top-I know that they were notes because he said so, he
said "I have some more money besides," but whether they were 51. notes
or pieces of tissue paper I do not know I have not given him the 301. bill
back, he has not applied for it, and was at my bankers when he came it is
in my possession now-it has never been applied for; you can have it if
you want it_I believe Mr. Mason took the stamped receipt when he came.
