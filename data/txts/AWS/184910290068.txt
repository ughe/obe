LOUIsA WITTICH. I am the wife of Conrad Wittich, of 75, St. George's.
street. On 11th Sept., about nine o'clock in the evening, I went in my
shop and found the prisoner there--I heard her ask for a half-quartern loat,
and give my husband a 5s.-piece-I took it out of bis hand-it was bad-I
asked the prisoner where she got it from-she said from her father, who
brought it from work-I asked where she lived-she said at 15, Prince's.
square-I told her it was no such thing, for Mr. Hastings lived there-sbe
then said she lived in Wellelose-square-I told her it was no such thing, and
she knew the 5s.-piece was a bad one-sbe then went out of the shop and said
she would go and call her father-sbe wanted to have the 58.-piece back, but
I would not let her-I stood at the door till the officer Wilks, came by-I
gave it him, and gave him information.
Cross-eramined by MR. ROBINSON. Q. How near were you to her in the
first instance A. I was in the shop-I did not serve her-I think I have
seen her before-her face seems familiar to me-this is the 5s.-piece-I made
a mark on it.
CONRAD WITTICH. The 5s.-piece my wife took from my hand was the
same I had received from the prisoner.
