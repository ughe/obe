359. GEORGE ENDERSON (30), GEORGE GUBBY (21), and
THOMAS UPTON (23), Robbery on William Nation, and stealing from him
108, 6d. in money, bis property.
MESSRS. BAIRD and SNAGG conducted the Prosecution, MR. WARNER SLEIG
defended Enderson, MR. THOMPSON defended Gubby and Upton.
WILLIAM Nation. I am an engineer, and work at Messrs. Easton and
Amor's, of the Grove, Great Guildford-street, Bridge-road on Saturday, 4th
February, I left work at 1 o'clock-about half-past Il or a quarter to 12
that night I was in Mint-street-I was perfectly sober-Enderson pounced
upon me, and the other two rushed round me, threw me down, and picked
my pockets of 108. 6d. and a door key, and took a cap that was on my head
I have not the least doubt whatever as to the identity of these three men
-I swear to them--when they came and collared me, I said, “For God's
sake don't rob a poor man who has been hard at work all the week," and I
shouted “Murder 1” and “Police !"-I received an injury on my shoulder
when I was on the ground-I believe it was from a blow, and I fell on my
head also ; I was thrown down—when I shouted, the prisoners ran round the
corner of the street as hard as they could run-I lost sight of them, and
made away to find a policeman-I went to the station, gave information to
the inspector, and then went home about 3 o'clock, I was going to the
station again and met a policeman coming for me---six men were shown to
me then, and I picked the prisoners out from them they had been appre-
bended on that afternoon- I have now no doubt whatever of their identity,
