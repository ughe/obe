Court. Q. Was your quarrel with Gordon about the settling of your
secounts ? A. No; I was working with him at the time-I left off work
that day, and went back two days after to settle accounts-the quarrel was
about a diamond snap that was brought to the shop by a piper, named
Mackenzie-I made no demand of money of the prisoner-he electro-plated
some few things for me alter our quarrel, and that took up the amount of
money due, and left me in his debt 28. or 28. 6d. I bare not paid that I
have not bad it in my power or I would have done so I did not communi-

nication to be revenged on him-there were no other workmen assisting

from giving the order for the purse-I was outside the door, and came in as
Dr. Hailey came out-Johnson was there.
COURT. Q. Can you point out Dr. Halley? 4. I cannot, because I was
not in the shop when he was there I was going in, and when I saw the
gentleman in the shop, I went outside and remained there, because my busi-
Bess was private-I cannot tell whether he was a tall or a short man, he was
not particularly tall-I think he was rather stout-I cannot exactly recollect,
for I never saw him before.
MR. PARNELL. Q. Was there a conversation going on when you went
int A. Yes; between Mr. and Mrs. Gordon and Johnson-Dr. Halley's
name was mentioned-I heard Johnson say he would make the purse-top, and
get it hall-marked for 18.-I was at the Tavistock Arms on Friday, 26th
May-Johnson and Gordon were there-I saw Gordon pay Johnson 10s.
. Did you hear Gordon say, "This is for making Dr. Halley's purse,
and for Hailing it; and as you have bad 58. or 68. already, I shall settle the
balance to-morrow?" A. Yes.
COURT. Q. What was it that was said ? A. Johnson said, "I want some
money, Gordon"-be said, “What do you want?"-Johnson said, "What
can you let me have ?"-Gordon said, "I can let you have 108. ; you have
had some during the week; what have you had ? _Johnson said, "58."-
Gordon said, "Then I can let you have 10s., and if you will come to-morrow
might, I will let you have the remainder"--that was all.
