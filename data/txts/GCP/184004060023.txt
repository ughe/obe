1036. RICHARD RYAN was indicted for feloniously breaking and
entering the shop of Samuel Lands, on the 27th of March, and stealing
therein 24 pairs of boots, value 171.; and 9 pairs of shoes, value 31: his
goods.
SAMUEL LANDS, I am a boot-maker, and live in Fore-street. On the
night of the 27th of March I came home about a quarter before eleven
o'clock and found two men in my shop packing up my goods--the door
was locked against me-I had left it locked about half-past nine o'clock
--the lock had been picked apparently-1 lost a good many boots and
shoes-there were four parcels ready packed up in the shop-on my
opening the door one man came out on me, and the other bounced out
against my wife and knocked her down--the prisoner is one who came out
I pursued and took him without losing sight of bim--the value of the
property is 181. or 201.- the door was left on the catch lock, and as soon
as I put my key in it opened-I am quite certain I left it locked. ee
Prisoner. He said at the station-house it was so momentary he could
not swear to the man who came out. Witness. I am quite certain of him
-I never lost sight of him.

yards up Milton-street, and throw away something which jingled-1
afterwards received from a boy, named Fry, in the street, a bunch of pick-
lock keys-I saw the prisoner secured.
