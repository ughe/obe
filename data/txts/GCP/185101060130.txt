MUSGROVE, Mayor.
Cross-examined. Q. This was Christmas week? 4. The week before:
I had been drinking with the prisoner part of that day, and from seven to
nine the evening before--he was drunk two or three times that week.
WILLIAM NORMAN (policeman, P 79). I took the prisoner on 20th
Dec., and told him he was charged with stealing gold-he said, "Am I the
picked one?"-I searched him at the station, and in his right-hand
trowsers pocket I found this small quantity of gold, with some tobacco-
dust-I produce the box,
Not Guilty.
