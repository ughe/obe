JAMES WATTS (Policeman, F 82). I was on duty in the Strand on the
night of 4th April, about 20 minutes before 8 o'clock in the evening-I
saw & crowd ; I went up, and saw the last witness there, and the prisoner
-Burke had the prisoner by throat, and as soon as I came up the prisoner
gave him into custody for robbing him of a purse containing 48.- Burke
said he had not done so—he went quietly with me to the station, and there
the prisoner charged him with that offence, and it was entered on the
charge sheet -- I did not hear any charge stated by Burke.
