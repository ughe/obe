   Re-examined.  I tried to put the key into the latch lock, the smaller one, which you can open from the inside or outside—I believe the lamp was taken down and suspended in the basement on the Saturday by Colin—I was out when it happened—I could not tell you if it had been burning there—the oil for it was kept at the back of the shop—I saw it standing by the safe—when I was sent for oil, all the original gallon had been consumed; the can was empty—the lamp had just been filled up with the remains of the gallon—there was no necessity, for the purpose of testing the lamp, to send for new oil—we always burn gas in the base-ment; it is very dark there—I have been seven years next June with Mr. Wilson—I knew before the fire that Mr. Wilson was insured, but I did not know the particulars—when I extinguished the gas and blew out the lamp I took all the matches away in my pocket—I did not get a van because we did not want to work late on a Saturday afternoon; it was very near time to shut up—afterwards, when there was this talk about my having 18 s . a week, Mr. Wainwright was present and heard what was said—until I went to the police-court on Monday morning I had no knowledge that any one was to be employed to watch the case on my behalf—I did not pay a farthing.
