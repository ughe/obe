62. GEORGE WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of
November, 127lbs. weight of tobacco, value 481., the goods of Jacob
Nathaniel Barlin and another; and that he had been before convicted of
felony.
JACOB NATHANIEL BARLIN. I am a tobacco and snuff manufacturer.
I have lost 127lbs. weight of tobaceo, value 481. -I am in partnership
with my brother David - I have examined this tobacco-(looking at it) - it
is ours.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Who is M Mahon? 4. The per-
son employed in carting tobacco from the London Doeks-I am sure this
is mine it has a mark on it-it was stolen on its transit to our house--it
was a purchase made at a public office-I had not seen it before I know
it by the mark on the bale, and by the excise permit which accompanies it
from the Docks to our stock--there is no possibility of mistaking it.

Cross-examined. Q. Did you miss it by turning round to look for it?
A. Yes I was riding on the front-I was nearly at Broad-street-the
prisoner had got, I suppose, twenty yards past the watch-house, nearly
opposite to Houndsditch-I did not notice whether he was sober-ho
stumbled, and the bale fell on the ground---my master's name is Henry
Foster Mellin-he would be answerable for the bale if lost-my instructions
were to carry it to Mr. Barlin's-it weighed 127lbs.--the prisoner could
get that upon his back off the cart without assistance it was not
fastened in any way-it was quite at the tail of the cart-I felt no motion of
the cart when it went away-it could not have dropped it was right up
to the tail-ladder-it was pulled over the tail-ladder, which was chained up
as tight as I could chain it.
