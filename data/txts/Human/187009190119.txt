   MR. SERJEANT BALLANTINE . Q.  Will you state exactly what took place on the subject of this child having had diarrhoea? A.  Waters told me that it had suffered from diarrhoea, that it had been under the care of Dr. Harris, who had seen it the previous day, and she expected every minute he would call again to see it that day; she said she had not given it anything except what Dr. Harris had ordered; she said that on the Saturday morning, I am quite sure of that, because I pressed her very much, knowing that laudanum was on the table, and seeing that the child was suffering—that was her exact answer, that she had not given the child anything but what he had ordered, as far as medicine was concerned—she did not, at that or any other time, say that laudanum had been used in relation to the children, or Nothing syrups, or anything of the kind; I particularly asked that question; I mentioned soothing syrup and sleeping or composing medicines, or anything to make the child sleep; she told me so on two separate occasions, for I pressed her very much; I said "Are you quite Bure that you have not done so?"because I was equally sure that the child had had it administered.
