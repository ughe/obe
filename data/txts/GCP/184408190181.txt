2141. FREDERICK PRATT was indicted for stealing 2 handkerchiefs,
value 4s.; 4 shillings, 1 sixpence, and 1 farthing, the property of William
Ayton.
WILLIAM AYton. I am a seaman. I slept in the same room with the
prisoner, on Wednesday night, the 22nd of Aug.--there were some other
Todgers there-when I awoke in the morning the prisoner was gone--I missed
two handkerchiefs and 48, 67d., which I had placed under my pillow when I
went to bed—I have seen one of the handkerchiefs since this is it-I gave
the prisoner in charge the day following.
HENRY Paintop. I live in Feathers-court, Drury-lane. On the morning
of the 23rd of Aug., the prisoner came up to the room where I work, and
offered this handkerchief for sale.
THOMAS GARLAND (police-constable F 21.) I took the prisoner-he said
he had taken the handkerchief, and would get it back for the prosecutor on
the morrow.
Prisoner. I own to taking that handkerchief, but the other things I know
nothing about.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Three Months.
