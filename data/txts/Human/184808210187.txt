SARAH BENNETT. I am single, and live at Mrs. Price's—I know the prisoner Bascoville has called on Roderick three or four times at Mrs. Price's, and slept there twice with her. On 2nd Aug. Roderick was there with a friend—she told me a gentleman gave her this meershaum pipe—she had not seen Bascoville that night, I can be on my oath of it, nor did I. WILLIAM EDMONS( City-policeman , 528). I took Roderick in the Borough-road, and found a pair of Bascoville's shoes in the room—she said they were her brother's—Bascoville asked for them at the Mansion-house, and claimed them as his. WILLIAM ROMAINE re-examined.  This is the Lord Mayor's signature to this deposition—( read )—"Bascoville says, I have bought the pipe, at least I bought the ticket of it, and took it out of pawn at the pawnbroker's in the Surrey-circus, one day last week."
   Bascoville's Defence.  The pipe cannot be identified; the father used the pipe for a fortnight, and the colour alters by smoking; I have smoked it myself; Roderick told me she slept with a gentleman, who gave her the pipe, as he had no money; she gave me the ticket.
   Roderick's Defence.  A gentleman gave me the pipe, as he had not enough money; I pawned it nest day, and gave Bascoville the ticket. NOT GUILTY.
