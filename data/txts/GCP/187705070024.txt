433. GEORGE HURST (17), Feloniously forging and uttering an order
for the payment of ll. 108., with intent to defraud.
MR. ATTENBOROUGH conducted the Prosecution,
JANE PALMER. My husband is a purveyor of cat's meat-on Saturday,
April 14th, my daughter brought me this note in an envelope, and I went
to the door and saw the prisoner; I asked him if my husband gave him the
note-he said “Yes." (Read: "Please pay the bearer for me 11. 108.,
T. L. Palmer"-I then gave the prisoner 8 sovereign and a half-sovereign-
he asked me for a pen and ink and wrote “Paid" on it, in my presence-
I only know him by sight.
John PALMER. I am the husband of the last witness-the note produced
is not my writing I did not authorise the prisoner to take it to my wife.
JAMES WILLIAMS. I have seen the prisoner write—this document (a
book) is in his writing.
WILLIAM BLAKE (Detective Officer). The prisoner appeared to a summons
for obtaining money by false pretences and was ordered into my oustody by
the Magistrate.
Prisoner's Defence. The book produced by Mr. Williams is not in my
writing, though it is like it.
Guilty-Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
