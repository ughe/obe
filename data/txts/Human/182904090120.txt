CHARLES WALLER. I am a Bow-street patrol. I went to the house, and found this pipe and cock; the lead is fresh cut, and part of it was lying near the main-pipe in the kitchen.
   JOHN CRICK. I live next door to the house where the lead was cut. This spoon, which was found on the prisoner, is my property - it was in my back premises; they got over the wall to cut the pipe off; the key was left at No. 20, and the pipe was cut from there and No. 19.
 Prisoner's Defence (written.) I was returning home, and passing by Wellington-street, I heard the cry of Stop thief! I went down to see what was the matter, and when I had got about half way down the street, I kicked something before me; I took it up, and found it was a spoon - I put it into my pocket, and followed after them; when I had got to the top of the street, on turning round the corner, I slipped down - the witness turned round, and laid hold of me.

 942.  EDWARD BUTLER was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of April , 6 half-crowns, 7 shillings 3 sixpences, and 9d. in copper, the monies of Francis Macnamara , from his person.
   WILLIAM LEWIS. I was at the White Lion public-house, in St. Giles', on the evening of the 2d of April; the prisoner and prosecutor came in together - the prosecutor was very tipsy; I saw the prisoner put his hand into the prosecutor's left-hand pocket, and take out the money- it was in a bit of paper; he gave out 1s. to pay for the beer - he kept the money in his hand some time, and then put it into his own pocket; there were five or six people in the same box; there was no concealment - he got up, went into the yard, and came back; I told him if he did not give him the money I would fetch an officer - he told me not to trouble my head about it; I went for the officer, and the money was found on him: he was not so drunk as the prosecutor - he had been drinking.
   FRANCIS MACNAMARA. I am a Chelsea pensioner. I had been with the prisoner, and we had each got our pensious; I got drunk, and do not know what happened.
     NOT GUILTY.
 943.  ROBERT BURN was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of April , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of John Parton , from his person.
   JOHN PARTON. I am an attorney. I was in Charlotte-street, Fitzroy-square, on the 3d of April, and was informed the prisoner had picked my pocket; I pursued him to a court in Goodge-street, and found my handkerchief on him - I took him to the office, and he was committed.
     GUILTY. Aged 14. Transported for Seven Years.
 944.  JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of March , 1 wooden till, value 1s.; 1 shilling, 1 sixpence, 17 penny-pieces, and 28 halfpence, the property of Adam Mott.
   HANNAH MOTT. I am sister of Adam Mott, who lives in George-street, Euston-square. About four o'clock in the afternoon of the 16th of March, I was in the parlour, adjoining the shop, which is a grocer's; I heard what I thought was a knocking on the counter, but it proved to be the unlocking of the till; I looked, the till was gone, and I saw a person going out of the shop with it, under his arm - I believe the prisoner to be the man; I ran to the door, but he was out of sight; I saw him again in about three hours, at Marlborough-street; this is the till - there was about 8s. 3 1/2d, in it; a person could reach over the counter, and get it.
   WILLIAM HAWKINS. I am an officer. On the 16th of March I was standing at my own house; the prisoner ran by with this till, and the money rattling in it; it was just after four o'clock; he was about four hundred yards from the prosecutor's - I pursued him down a mews; he threw the till into an area, at the corner of Euston-square and Drummond-street, and most of the money went into another area; I took him about one hundred yards further, without losing sight of him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was never inside the shop, and know nothing of it; I was going to a gentleman of the name of Clarkson, second mate of the Repulse; a man came behind me, and threw this till into the area - there was a cry of Stop thief! I ran after the man, and this gentleman took me.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 945.  ROBERT CARTER was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of April , 1 goat, price 10s., the property of Randall Edward Brewin.
   DANIEL GILES. I am a bird-fancier. This goat was on Mr. Chapman's premises, at the back of Barnsbury-park. Islington, on the 11th of April; there is a bank round the field; the prisoner ran, took a strap from his pocket, and led the goat away - he took it into Maiden-lane, and the officer took him; I had not known him before.
   WILLIAM BERNARD. I am an officer. I saw the prisoner leading the goat along; I asked him where he was going; he said, home; I asked him where that was; he said somewhere about Holborn; I took the goat to the officer, and gave information.
   WILLIAM DOOBER. I am servant to Mr. Randall Edward Brewin. The officer showed me a goat, which I knew was my master's - I know nothing of the prisoner.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went after a situation at a varnish-maker's, in Liverpool-road; I was to go on Saturday for an answer - I thought I would go and cut two turfs; a man in the field asked who the goat belonged to; some men said they did not know; he then asked me if I would earn sixpence or a shilling, and take the goat with him - he called the goat, and it came; he took hold of it, put a string round it, and told me to lead it - he walked on, and went away.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years.
