1827. CHARLES HERMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of
June, 2 breast pins and a chain, value 31., the goods of Frederick William
Seckerson Wood, from his person.
FREDERICK WILLIAM SECKERSON WOOD. I live in Liverpool-street,
King's Cross. On the 29th of June I was in King-street, Cheapside, at
the Election there was a crowd I had a pin in the breast of my shirt,
and lost it-I charged the prisoner with taking it be denied it, and of
fered to be searched-a person near me accused him of it-the pin has not
been found 1 felt it drawn out of my shirt.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. There was a great crowd? A.
Yes-he said he was willing to be searched-it is what I call a union pin,
which is two pins united by a chain.
