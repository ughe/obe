 548. JAMES WILLIAM HONNER , embezzling and stealing 5 s  . and 10 s  .; the moneys of Joseph Conquest Hartwell , his master: to which he PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Four Months .( There were two other indictments against the prisoner. )
 549. JAMES GRAWLEY , stealing 16lbs. weight of leaden pipe, value 10 s  .; the goods of Frederick Corfield .
   MR. SLEIGH . conducted the Prosecution. MARY JONES. I am the wife of John Jones; I live in Gurney's-road, West Ham. On Thursday, 8th May, about 12 or 1 o'clock, I saw the pri soner in a back yard, near to some unfinished houses, near to my house—I saw him pick up some leaden pipe, and I believe he had two pieces in his hand—there was another lad with him—I called to the prisoner, and asked what he was doing there—he threw the pieces of lead on the ground, and ran away—I picked them up, and gave them to the policeman—these are them—I have known the prisoner some years—I have no doubt he is the boy. JOSEPH BENTON. ( policeman, K  381). I took the prisoner into custody—I told him it was on suspicion of stealing some lead from some new houses—he said he knew nothing about it, he was not there. FREDERICK CORFIELD. I am the owner of those houses—I believe this lead pipe to be mine—I was about to enclose the yards—they are not yet enclosed. NOT GUILTY.
 550. ANN WINCHESTER , stealing 2 sovereigns; the moneys of James Kettle , her master. HARRIET KETTLE. I am the wife of James Kettle. The prisoner came into my service on 4th April—on. 11th April I had 50 l . in a glove on the mantelpiece—I had tied it up two days before—I saw it safe at 7 o'clock, at a quarter past 7 I saw it had been untied—I counted it, and found two sovereigns were missing; one was marked—I had the rheumatism, and could not dress myself, and I asked her to assist me, and I sent her to fetch the remainder of my clothes from my sleeping room, where the glove was. JOHN PADDICK. ( policeman, N  301). On the morning of 11th April I was sent for to the prosecutrix's house—Mrs. Kettle said she would give the prisoner in charge for stealing two sovereigns—I said to the prisoner, "You must go along with me"—she went into the back room to put on, her bonnet and shawl, and she took up this basket ( producing it )—I said to her, "Does this belong to you?"—she said, "Yes"—I looked into it, and found these two sovereigns—the prisoner said she did not know how they came there—before I found them she said she would give Mrs. Kettle her watch, and stop in her service till such time as the money was paid. HARRIET KETTLE. These are the two sovereigns, one is marked.
   JURY . Q.  How came you to leave the money there? A.  I meant to have taken it down, but I forgot it—I generally carry it in my bosom, but laid it on the mantelpiece at night.
   Prisoner.  I know nothing about it; there was another servant who swept the room a quarter before 7 o'clock. Witness.  I have a girl to nurse the children, but she had not been up stairs. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Six Months .
