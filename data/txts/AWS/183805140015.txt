1157. JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of
April, 1 apron, value 3d., 1 collar, value 3d. S dusters, value 2d. the
the goods of Matilda Richardson: and 1 bag, value 2d.: 3 towels, value
6d. and 1 gown, value 28.: the goods of Hannah Foster.
CHARLES HANKINSON. I live in Tottenham-road, Kingsland, and am
a gardener. On the evening of the TOth of April I was about leaving a
piece of garden-ground at the back of the houses in Maberley-place, and
saw the prisoner crossing the grounds, and proceed to the premises-he
went over the wall-he had no basket or any thing with him-I lost him
for about two minutes, and then saw him return-he ran to leap over a
ditch, but missed his leap, and fell in-1 seized him by the collar and
called for the policehe did not resisth fumbled in his pockets, but I
did not see him drop any thingth policeman searched him, and found s
quantity of keys on him-he had a basket when I stopped him, which F
gave to the policeman.
HUGH HAGGERTY (police-constable N 174.) I was on duty in Totten-
ham-road, and heard a noise from Maberley-place went forward, and
Mr. Hankinson had the prisoner by the collarhe gave him into may cus-
tody with this basket of linen-I was proceeding to the station-house with
him, and he put his hand in his right-hand coat-pocket, and took from it a
key which I took from his hand, as he was going to throw it away-I then
took three large skeleton-keys and a screw-driver, three small trunk keys,
and one door-key from him-the linen appeared rather damp, as if it bad
not been long washed-it was rough dried--I afterwards went back to the
ditch and found a screw-driver.
MATILDA RICHARDSON. I am a widow, and live in Maberley place, in
the service of Mrs. Hannah Foster. On Tuesday, the 10th, I went into our
back garden, adjoining Mr. Hankinson's man called to me, and asked
if I missed any thing, and I missed these things-they all belong to me,
except two towels and a duster, which belong to my mistress- things
were hanging out to dry.
Prisoner. I picked the keys up-I had nothing to eat the whole day,
and not a farthing in my pocket did it through distress.
GUILTY. Aged 50.-Confined Six Months.
