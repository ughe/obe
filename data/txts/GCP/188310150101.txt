HARVEY WILMOT. I am district goods manager at the Great Eastern
Railway, Bishopsgate-Parker was depôt clerk there Moore was goods
agent or clerk in charge at the Royal Albert Dock-about 28th August
I received information from Mr. Jones about 10 bags of coffee sweepings;
I made inquiries, and on 31st August saw Moore at Bishopsgate-.I told
him I wanted to know how it was he had anything to do with this coffee,
and who the sender was; he said that the sender was a man who had
bought it from a rummage sale, and had given “181." or "161 for it!
-I said that there was something very suspicious about it, and it looked
very much as it it was stolen, and he must produce the sender to me,
otherwise I must suspend him-I am rather confused, as there were 80
many interviews, and it is difficult to fix dates; I have some pencil
memoranda here--I had another interview with him next day, when he
produced the sender, and said that what he had said before, was untrue,
that Coker was the man who told him the coffee sweepings had been
ordered away by the Customs, and he had sent them to London to a
friend of his named Parker to dispose of for usOoker came in then, I
think, and said “They were given to me by the mate of the steamer"
(the Clyde I think he said); “I saw Moore and asked him to dispose of
them "--Moore was present, and said that he was to have part of the pro-
ceeds—I think he said that they were to pay Parker a small portion for

wards he added that they were to be divided between Moore and himself,

he said "I know nothing about it, I had the coffee and sold it, but I
