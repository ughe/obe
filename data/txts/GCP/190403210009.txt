NEW COURT.-Tuesday, March 22nd, 1904.
Before Mr. Recorder.
300. GEORGE RUTTER (27) PLEADED Guilty to three indictments
for forging and uttering orders for the payment of £80, £80 108., and £70.
with intent to defraud. Eighteen months' hard labour.
301. GEORGE MASON (28) and FLORENCE LANGAVIER, Three
indictments for burglary in the dwelling houses of Rose Debelin, William
Howes, and John Grave.
Mr. FITCH, for the prosecution, offered no evidence against Langavier.
Nor GUILTY.
302. The said GEORGE MASON PLEADED GULTY to breaking and
entering the dwelling house of John Grave and stealing an overcoat, his
goods, also to a burglary in the dwelling house of William Howes and
stealing an overcoat and other articles, his property, having been con
victed of felony at Clerkenwell Sessions in October, 1901, in the name of
George James. Eighteen months' hard labour.
303. ABRAHAM LEVY (20), Robbery with violence, with two other
persons unknown, upon Alfred Mason, and stealing his watch, chain, and
48s. his moneys from his person and against his will.
Mr. RODERICK Prosecuted.
The Jury being unable to agree, were discharged without a verdict. (See
page 447.)
