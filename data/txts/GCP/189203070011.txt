The prisoner, in the course of a long defence, said that a company was formed
to carry on the paper; that Cawthorn, with others, applied for the secretary-
ship; that he never told Carothorn that all the advertisoments were genuine;
nor that he was a surveyor to the School Board, although he told him that he
had valued for the Board ; that he never told Cawthorn that ho owned the
Greenhithe estate, but that he was going to purchase it by means of a mortgage,
for which solicitors were going to find the money; that he made no specific
statement as to the issue of the paper being 2,000 copios a week ; that Caw-
thorn's £100 was paid for the purchase of 200 shares in the newspaper com-
pany, and that he told Cawthorn that after a month's notice ho could have his
£100 back in exchange for the 200 shares; and he denied that he had any
intention to defraud.
