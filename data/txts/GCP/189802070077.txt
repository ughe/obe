WALTER FREEMAN. I am a journalist employed on the reporting staff

immocently of a crime of shich the writer was guilty, having imitated the
writing, and stating the circumstances given in the evidence-it was signed,
"C. S. Hawkins.”)
The prisoner's statement before the Magistrate (rohere he was also asked
if he called any witness): "I am not going to. All I have got to say
is, they could have arrested me at my house. I told them they were
mistaken. I know nothing about it. I can prove where I have been.
They have got an innocent man. I have got a witness, but I shall reserve
him for the Old Bailey."
