Bermondsey—I delivered the second parcel of goods from Messrs. Anderson to Young—the delivery note was signed by a female. MARIA LOUISA WALTERS. I am the wife of George Walters, of Church Street, Camberwell, a pianoforte manufacturer, he is ill—on 19th March our traveller Beard brought the prisoner Young and another man, he introduced them to me as the two Youngs, they selected a black and gold piano at the price of thirty-six guineas—they gave their address at Melbourne Grove, Champion Hill; they said they kept an oil shop—I agreed to let the piano out on the hire system, and this document ( produced ) was signed by the prisoner Young and the other person who represented to be his brother, as F. Young, 3, Dulwich Terrace, Champion Hill, and Arthur Young, name address—the piano was sent—the prisoner signed "Frederick Young" and the other one "Arthur Young"—we never got our piano back nor yet any money for it—my husband went to that address—he is at home ill—he has not been able to come out since last week—I was at the police-court when he was examined—I saw him sworn and heard him give his evidence, and saw him sign his deposition.
   Cross-examined by Young.  It was arranged that Mr. Walters should call before we sent the piano, but he did not call; he sent the piano.
 (  The deposition of George Walters was here put in and read, stating that he sent the piano by Smythe, the carman; that he afterwards called, but found the shop closed. ) HENRY SMYTHE. I am carman to Mr. Walters—on 19th March I delivered a piano at Champion Hill to the prisoner Young—I gave this agreement to him, and he signed it, and his brother signed it.
   Cross-examined by Young.  I swear positively you signed "Frederick Young." ALFRED KNIGHT. I live at 90, Villiers Street, Walworth, and am a grocer—I bought a piano of Young after the March quarter—I saw it at his shop at Champion Hill—the name of Young was over the shop—I gave him 12 l . for it—he showed me a piece of paper, and said the brokers had sued for rent—I understood the piano was not on hire from what he said to me—it was taken to my premises, and has been claimed by Mr. Walters—I have made some arrangement with him.
   Cross-examined by Young.  You called at my place on 26th March—I showed you that the broker had distrained on the piano for rent—I don't recollect you first asking me to advance you 12 l .; you might have done so—I saw a man in possession there—I brought the 12 l . with me, and saw the piano—you paid me the 12 l ., and I sent you the receipt in the morning. JAMES FOX. I am clerk to Henry R. Condy, of Battersea, a white lead manufacturer—we had a travelling man named Toby—we supplied through him two quarterns of white lead, value 4 l . 13 s ., to Frederick Young, of Champion Hill, and we received this receipt, signed "A. Young:" I received this postcard, signed "Frederick Young," request ing the lead without fail on Monday—on 7th April I received this other order for five cwt. of lead, value 4 l .—on the 10th a man came and fetched it, and gave this receipt, signed "A. Young."
   Cross-examined by Young.  We received this order through Toby, out traveller—he is not here; we are in search of him—this is not the only order of the kind that we received through him.
