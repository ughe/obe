 1091. JANE JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of March , 1 Bible, value 2 s  .; 1 umbrella, value 1 s  .; 1 table-cloth, value 2 s  .; 2 napkins, value 1 s  .; 2 pair of stockings, value 3 s  .; 1 petticoat, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 shift, value 2 s  .; the goods of Ann Parrott . ANN PARROTT. I live in Ship and Billet-row, Greenwich. The pri soner and her mother came to lodge at my house on the 18th of December—on the 17th of March, I went out at half-past nine in the morning, to go to Bexley—I left Elizabeth M'Kenzie and the prisoner in the house—the prisoner's mother had gone away on the Monday before—I had a box in my house, containing the greater part of the property stolen—I found on the 18th of March, that it had been opened, and the articles gone; and a pair of stockings from another box up stairs. MARY DUNFORD. I am the wife of Henry Dunford, and live at Green wich. On the 17th of March, the prisoner came to our house, and brought an umbrella, a Bible, a table-cloth, and two napkins—she asked me to purchase them—I asked if they were her own—she said, "No," but they belonged to the person who sent her with them—I gave her 1 s . 4 d . for the Bible and umbrella, and 1 s . for the table-cloth and napkin.
   Prisoner's Defence.  M'Kenzie gave me the things—I did not know they were stolen. ANN PARROTT. M'Kenzie went away the same evening, and she did not return—the prisoner went out, but I went and found her—I could not find M'Kenzie. NOT GUILTY.
   Second Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant .
 1092. JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the   28th of March , 3 glass cruets, value 4 s  ., the goods of John Roberts . JOHN ROBERTS. I live in High-street, Deptford, and deal in earthen ware. On the 28th of March, I heard a noise in my shop—I went into the shop and saw the prisoner—he asked me if a gentleman at Blackheath, named Foster, had ordered some decanters—I said, "No"—he said, a gentleman of that name was coming, and he just called to put me on my guard—he then went away, and I missed these cruets from a stand on the chair—I followed, and took him with them in his pocket.
 (The prisoner pleaded poverty.) GUILTY  . Aged 34.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor and
   Jury.  — Confined Five Days.
