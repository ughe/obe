passing that would be the Saturday morning about 11th November, I
think; every person was told to have a six-chambered revolver with him
-I had three to earry that morning, one belonging to Brady, and
another belonging to James Mullett; I think they were afraid of being
arrested with them on them; they gave them to me the night before to
earry -I met Molloy that morning at the Queen's Quay, Brunswick
Street, going there; he was the first I met-I complained to Molloy at
having to earry the revolvers without having the price of a ear-he said
my brother had got plenty of funds for that, and he should supply me
with it-I know that Molloy had a revolver; I saw the mark of it on his
coat, he had a short light coat-it was one of the revolvers which James
Boland brought-Molloy showed it to me I did not see the
pattern of it before that; it was a different pattern to what we
had-we went to Merrion Square South, and more went to Fitz-
william Square and Fitzwilliam Street-Molloy showed me the revolver
