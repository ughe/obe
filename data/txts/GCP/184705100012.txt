Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. How do you know this is the paper

so to him-the clerk is not here-I have not sworn to-day, and altered my
evidence in order to make it not necessary that the clerk should be here-
there is not a person in Mr. Dudley's employ who is very much like the

this on the Saturday-Mr. Dudley came to me, and asked if I had served any-

Dudley's presence—Mr. Dudley knew it, because he sent in some work, and
this iron was set off against it, I heard something about an account in which
31. 98. was paid by mistake--when the prisoner came for this iron, there was
no one in the yard but me- I suppose our clerk gave him the invoice-I did
not see him—there are seven or eight persons at our place no one is called
to identify the prisoner but me--the others were at breakfast I saw the
prisoner again on the Sunday afternoon, at the station-I had seen him come
to our place before-I do not know the writing on this paper-I can sweat
it is the order the prisoner gave me.
Court. Q. What did you do with the paper ? A. It was given in the
shop, and put on the file- I looked at it before, and saw Mr. Dudley's name
on it-I am quite positive it is the same paper-I had seen the prisoner
frequently before-I am positive about him.
CHARLES DUDLEY. I am partner with George Dudley, my brother-we
are whitesmiths, and live in St. Luke's. The prisoner had been in our
employ, but he had left one day before this transaction-I do not know this
note-it is not my writing, por my brother's, or any one authorized to write
it-it is not written by any one on our establishment-I have sent the pri-
soner for goods of this description- I did not send him for the goods men-
tioned in this paper, and they never came to my premises.
Cross-examined. Q. How many persons bave you in your employ? A.
Ten-I have no managing man-my brother is not here I have no other
partner-the writing of this paper is not like my writing-it is somewhat
like my brother's I know that some goods were sent to Mr. Farmer,
amounting to 21. 98., and a draft for 31. 98. was paid in mistake--I never

anything of the kind, that I swear positively-I have not a person in my
