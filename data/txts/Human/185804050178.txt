   Q.  Were not these the words you used before I went over, that you had employed two separate men to get the money from her, but they were "duffers," and would not stick to her? A.  No, I did not; I told you I had spoken to one man, who was a debt collector, but I had not received any answer, and you said you could soon get the money, but it was no use to put it into a debt collector's hands—I did not say I intended to serve her out, and if you would stick to her, I would give you 30 s ., whether you got it or not—I did not call at your house the next morning, and urge you to go again and see her—I called upon you, because you asked me to advance you 15 s ., and I came to tell you I would advance you 15 s . towards the pro ceedings—Imet Wells as I was coming down to your house—I did not ask him to come with me to your house—I met him at New Cross; he was going towards London; I did not ask him to turn back and come, he proposed to come—I asked you what the expenses would be, and you said you would guarantee they would not be more than 30 s .—you did not tell me you would not act unless under the advice of a solicitor, and that it would be necessary to have a solicitor—I did not urge you then to undertake the matter, and say I would give you 30 s . for your trouble—I did not say, "If you will come up to my house I will give you 15 s . now"—I advanced you 15 s .—Wellswas there—that was not as a part of the payment you were to receive; it was to take proceedings in the Proctor's Court—I did not tell you that the collector told me I must administer before I could go into the County Court—I did not say before the Magistrate at Greenwich, that I knew you were not a solicitor—after you first called on Mrs. Spurway, you told me she said she could pay the money, but would not pay without giving me further trouble—I did not tell you that she said she had been told she was not liable, as she had not administered—she told me herself she had not administered—I know a man named Fogo, who attends the Greenwich Police Court—he did not advise me to take these proceedings against you—Iwas sent to him by the Police to ascertain if you were a solicitor—I never heard his name mentioned other than by you—he told me since that you were to have been articled to him, but that you were not a solicitor—Wells never called on me on the Friday the money was received;
