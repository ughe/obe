May, 6 yards of drugget, value 28., the goods of James Martin.
JAMES MAHER (police-constable R 18.) On the 20th of May, about a
quarter-past twelve o'clock in the night, I saw the prisoner at Deptford,
carrying this drugget under his right arm I asked him where he got it
he said at the Fleece public-house, which was just by—I turned to go to
inquire if what he said was true, and he instantly threw it down in the
road, and ran as hard as he could I overtook him, and with the assistance
of another took him into custody-he resisted violently. r o
JAMES MARTIN. I live at Deptford, and am a dealer in marine stores.
This drugget is mine- it was covered over a load of salt, and was taken off
the top of my cart, with which I was going off the next morning--my cart
stood in my yard, which is walled in and has gates to it, but the gates were
not shut, I believe.
