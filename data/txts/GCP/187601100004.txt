in the day, a boy brought me a message, in consequence of which I went
to the gate of the factory and there saw Colleman; I had never seen him

with me, and asked if I could appoint a place to meet him at 1 o'clock,
which I did at the Anchor and Hope, Millwall I went there at 1 o'clock

asked me if Mr. Ellis had a patent for the manufacture of ammonia-I

men, and asked me if it was sold in a powder or liquid I said in a powder
- he said he would pay me for the information if I gave it to him-I said

are, and if I answer them I shall get myself into great trouble"-he asked
me if I remembered a foreigner named Alexander being at the works about
two years ago -I said I did not, as I had not been there so long-when1
