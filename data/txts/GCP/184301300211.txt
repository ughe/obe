costs due from Mr. Constable.

Q. The application about that deed to the Judge occurred on the 1st of
Dec., 1841 ? A. Then the deed must have gone out of our hands a few days
after that, I was not present when the deed was handed to the party-I had
examined it before it left my office, to see how it was executed at the time I
parted with it to the prisoner it had the same attestation and execution to it
as it has now-it purported to be signed by Richard Constable, Elizabeth
Hollis Constable, and Joseph Stanley-at the time I introduced the prisouer
to Miss Constable I had paid no attention to the mode in which the deed was
attested.
