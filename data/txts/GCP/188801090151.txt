yards of Orosby Hill Lodge-I have known the prisoner and the de-
ceased Hannah Bowles since last spring, and the boy Edward Bowles I
only knew a short time-I used to go into the grounds sometimes with
Mr. Bowles and see him there-Mrs. Bowles was a very nioe kind of
person-I never saw any signs of drinking about her-I have never been
there at meals-on Friday morning, 23rd September, I heard of her
death-I went to the lodge _I was going in search of Bowles at the big
