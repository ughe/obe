Miss Standen was brought to me about 2 a.m. on September 17. She

A long letter was handed up to the Judge by prisoner, which was
read. It was apparently from his brother in Canada and, referring
to the act charged, hinted that prisoner was probably mad and
should be dealt with as such.
Dr. Scort, recalled. (To the Judge.) Prisoner has been quiet and
morose, but I cannot find any evidence of actual melancholia or
other form of insanity. He has been amenable to the prison dieci
pline and has always been able to hear and answer questions and
talk coherently. I found no insano delusions and he did not vary in
his statements.
