1895. JAMES MEAGAN, stealing 2lbs of bread, value 3d.; the property
of Elizabeth Coutts, and another; having been before convicted.
HENRY DONIMORE. I live with Mr. Bailey, a grocer. On 24th July I
kaw the prisoner take a loaf from a baker's barrow in the street.
ROBERT WILLIAM Esau. I examined the loaf that was found-it is
Elizabeth and Ann Coutts's property.
CHARLES AUSTIN. I saw the prisoner throw something away, and run
away-the loaf was picked up at that spot,
ALFRED LINDNEY. I was present at the prisoner's trial, and produce a
certificate-(read-Convicted Jan. 1847, and confined three months.)
GUILTY.** Aged 16.-Confined Six Months.
