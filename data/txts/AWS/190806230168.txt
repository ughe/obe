present for your kindness to me." I meant kindness in voting for

After I sold the governess pony and eart to Hirst I may have bought
horse and four-wheeler; that was about 18 months, perhaps, after
wards. I gave E38 for the four-wheeler and E25 for the cob. The
gold penknife was a very small one, as far as I remember. I do not
know that I should know the knife again. I think I got it at Mr.
Greaves's. I do not remember what the repair was to the knife which
Hirst gave me to have repaired. Mr. Hirst first suggested I should
build houses at Herne Bay. I-was buying houses where they were
cheap-in London, not Herne Bay, Mr. Hirst indueed me to buy
6ix plots; then a considerable time afterwards he suggested that he
should build two houses for me on the six plots of ground; that be
could build the houses with 44 this man (Budd) cheap. My wife used
to dissuade me. Some time after I said that I would have the two
houses built on my plots. A little while later I was round at The
Crowns one night and Mr. Hirst said, I have been thinking, Cal-
butt, your plots are a little too far away from my farmhouse; you had
better buy three of my plots." Finally, he said he could let me have
the three for E55. I do not know that my six plots were spoken of
as the site for the building. My plots were not down a side lane. I
know the Blaekburn Road; I suppose you would all it a road; it is
laid out, but not made up. My land was in Coldwell Road, I think
turning out of Blaekburn Road. I think Mr. Hirst's site was only
slightly more valuable than mine. It would not have been for me to
have told Mr. Hirst that I did not like building on his site. I might
have said before the magistrate that I said to Hirst, after I had made
