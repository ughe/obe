1540. JAMES HOWARD was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
June, 1 pair of half-boots, value 4s. 6d., the goods of Jonathan Tilley,
JONATHAN TILLEY. I am a boot and shoe-maker, and live in Ratcliffe
highway. On the 18th of June I heard my apprentice cry out “Thief"
I ran from my shop to the next door and caught the prisoner--I asked
him whether he had got any thing--he said, "No" - I turned my head,
and somebody said, “What is that on the ground ?" and I saw this pair
of half-boots, which are mine.

Christopher Marlow. I am apprentice to the prosecutor. I saw the
prisoner at the door cutting these half-boots down, from the nail on which
they hung-he ran away with them-I ran after him-I saw the shoes on
the ground, and my master picked them up—there was another man at the
corner of Bluegate-fields, and when I caught the prisoner the other man
went away.
Prisoner. I never cut them- I was talking to a shipmate, at the corner
of Bluegate-fields--I saw the man throw the shoes into the road-the boy
said there was a knife about me, but the officer could find no knife-this
boy took hold of me, and it was a minute and a half before his master
came up.
CHRISTOPHER MARLOW. He dragged me along a few steps, but I held
him tight till my master came.
