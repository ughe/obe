REUBEN ROSENTHAL, licensee, the "Britannia, "Berwick Street, W. I know prisoner as a customer; I have heard him speak of his wife and family.  To prisoner. You did not say, "Yes, I could not help myself, as I got the girl in trouble." MAUD EMILY DETTMAR, 67, Rattray Road, Brixton.
