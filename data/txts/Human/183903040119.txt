   Prisoner.  I took it out of the stable to sharpen my razor with. Witness.  He had no right to take my things—it used to be kept in my missed it—I asked him for the key of the stable to get it one Sunday, and it—I mentioned it to him, and he denied all knowledge of it, and accused boy, named Webster, of taking things away—he advised me to lock after the boy, that he was a suspicious character, and no doubt had,, got my hone—I never quarrelled with the prisoner—the chest the tools were taken from was locked, and was broken open.

GEORGE STEPHENSON. I work for Mr. Pollard, of Tottenham Court-road. I lost a plane from his premises, on or about the 28th of November—this is it—(looking at it. )
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDEROAST . Q.  When had you seen it? A.  The day before it was taken—I went to High-street Office the witness Newman was taken up for offering it in pawn—the prisoner was not there the first or second time—I think Newman was the second time, and then the prisoner was taken—I did not hear remanded say that the prisoner was not the woman who gave her the plane. WILLIAM SMITH. I live in Hanway-street, and am in Mr. Pollard's employ—I missed a plane off a bench, on the morning of the 28th of November—I had seen it safe on the 27th—this is it.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did not Newman state that she received it from a with a black bonnet and black trimmings? A.  No, a white. bonnet, black trimmings, and a black dress—she said there was another son present—she did not give that person's name and address, but she, it herself—that woman was taken through mistake, and was discharged.
