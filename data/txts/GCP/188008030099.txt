Re-examined. I applied for the rent myself-I generally saw a man
named Shultz, and after that Townsend came in to see me one or twice
respecting it-I knew him by sight in the City—they had previously been
in Noble Street-he said that place did not suit them; they wanted ground
floor premises-he referred to Seal and Co., and I made inquiries and found
they had paid their rent; that was all I wanted to know.
ARTHUR PEROY JACKSON. I am articled to my father, who is solicitor
to the Cordwainers' Company--they are the freeholders of 81, Wood Street
--Townsend had an agreement with the company with references to offices
there-I produce the agreement we had two references, and we made
inquiries of them through Messrs. Debenham, Tewson, and Farmer-I
produce the replies; one was from Z. Martin Seal and Co., 30, Hemsell
Street, Falcon Square, and the other from A. Grant, New Bond Street, and
upon those we accepted Townsend as tenant-he remained till October,
1879, leaving a quarter and a half rent due, 451. - he paid nothing--we put
in a broker-the goods realized 111. 18. 6d; but the broker's charges were
81. 176. 7d., so we received 21. 38. 11d., we heard nothing more of him.
Cross-examined by Mr. OVEREND. Townsend complained of his stoek
being spoiled through some fat leaking through the ceiling from a kitchen
above-we did our best to stop it--we did not make him any allowance-
we did not waive the half-quarter's rent in consideration of the damage.
ARTHUR HENRY PLUMMER DALE. I am manager of the Peckham branch
of the London and South-Western - Bank-a person named Seal had an
account there; I believe it to be the prisoner-it was opened in the name
of Sidney Seal-it is our custom to require two referenees-he referred to
Mr. Hilton and Mr. W. Townsend, of 81, Wood Street--they were applied
to, and were satisfactory--the account was opened with 2001., and at its
dose, about June or July last, was overdrawn about 21.----there never was
& balance of anything like 1,0001.-the overdrawn balance of 21. was paid
by Mr. O'Brien, the prisoner's solicitor.

showed it to Mr. O'Brien when he called I put the name of Zephaniah
Martin Seal under the signature, in pencil - I don't remember communi-

name-he gave his address 3, Friern Place, Peckham Rye-Mr. O'Brien said
that the account was not Z. Martin Seal's--I did not tell Mr. O'Brien that
I did not know the man by sight who opened the account I had a very
