THOMAs NANKEVILLE. I reside at St. Agnes, Cornwall--I have lately
returned from Australia-] knew a person there of the name of Richard
James Hawk Read-I know his father quite well ; his name is John Read
-he formerly lived at Helston-ir August last I was at Sandhurst-I saw
Richard Read there-I was with him at the bank of Australasia the day
previous to his leaving-he got some bills there-I saw him enter a receipt
of money previously paid- can't be positive what the amount of the bill
was-he left Sandhurst next morning, and sailed in the Royal Charter-I
received a letter from him the same day stating that he was going on board,
and that he had engaged a passage in the second cabin-the bill he received
at Sandhurst was like the one produced-I came home in the Swiftsure, one
of Green's ships, which followed the Royal Charter.
SAMUEL GRENFELL. I live at St. Ives, in Cornwall-I was in Australia,
