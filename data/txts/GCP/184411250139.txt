121. JOHN SHEA was indicted for unlawfully uttering counterfeit

Mr. Ellis conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM SWAINE. I am shopman to Mr. Potter, a tallow-chandler in
Crawford-street--on the 14th of Nov., about six o'clock, I saw the pri-
soner at my master's counter-he asked for a penny candle-I put the
candle down-he offered me a counterfeit sixpence-I noticed directly that
it was counterfeit, and I bent it and gave it to my master-he collared
the prisoner and took him to the station, which is about ten minutes
walk-I went with him-as we went along I heard money jingle on the
stones-the prisoner was close by the spot-we stopped instantly, and I
saw a boy pick up two pieces, which he gave to some person who I
thought was a policeman, but found it was Mr. Potter.
