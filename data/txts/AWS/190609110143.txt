am a German, and in the month of January last was looking
for some employment. I came to this country at the beginning
of November in last year to pick up business habits and the
language. I am in receipt of an allowance from my relatives in
Germany. In January I saw the advertisement produced in
the Daily Telegraph" and in a German papery wherein pri-
soner advertised from Chichester House, Chancery Lane, as
Krause and Co., that he gave legal advice and obtained loans.

in Germany. He asked a preliminary fee of 2s. 6d. for making
inquiries. I called several times, but did not get any money.
