Cross-examined by Mr. PricE. Q. And you do not know him by any
other name now? A. I suppose his name to be Shackleford-I do not
think his name is Jenkins, because a plate was found on his person when
he was taken, with the name of Shackleford on it, and also some cards-I
was examined in Mr. Mumford's case last Sessions against Shackleforil,
and related the same story-I do not recollect which name was given in
first, Ingram or BachelardI do not know how I came to go to Mr.
Ingram first-I did not know anything of him, or of Mr. Bachelard-
Shackleford obtained from us about 551. worth of goods altogether the
last parcel was not sent.
JOHN MUMFORD. I am warehouseman in Milk-street, Cheapside. On
the 11th or 12th of July, I came into the warehouse, and saw Shackleford
there, purchasing goods-Wagstaff was the salesman-he introduced Shack-
leford to me-be said, “Mr. Mumford, Mr. Beaumont"-some goods had
been arranged to be purchased-I said to Shackleford, "Your name is Beau-
mont?"-he said, “It is, my name is Beaumont"-I said, "Are yon buying
goods on your own account?"-he said he was-he then continued the pur-
chase--when he was about to leave, I said, "Where will you bave these
goods sent to?" he asked if they could be ready by six o'clock-I said they
could-he said, "I wish them to be sent to me at the Railway Tavern"-I
then asked him for his name, that it might be entered correctly in the
books--I then wrote his name as he dictated-I showed it to him after
I had written it, and he said it was right-he said if the goods were sent
there at the time, he would pay 151.- this is the paper I wrote, and
showed him-(read)—"Robert William Beaumont, Pool's Railway
Tavern, at six o'clock; and send a receipt for 157." -I directed the goods
to be sent-the porter went, and I went with him when we got to Pool's
Railway Tavern I did not find him I ordered the goods to be taken home
- afterwards took him in a tobacconist's shop, in Fenchurch-street-be
was about making a purchase of cigars.
Cross-examined by MR. PRICE. Q. Have you given us the same ac-
count as you did last Sessions ? A. Yes-1 believe he did not pay for the
cigars- I have no means of knowing his name, but by what he gave
I have seen it written.
