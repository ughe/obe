Crawford, with intent to defraud; forging the endorsement on a certain order for the payment of money, to wit, a banker's cheque for £112 4s. 6d., with intent to defraud; forging and uttering, knowing the same to be forged, a certain authority for the delivery and transfer of goods, to wit, deeds relating to certain premises, with intent to defraud.
 Prisoner confessed to a previous conviction of felony at the Warwick Sessions on October 20, 1908. He was stated to be an extremely clever and ingenious forger and to be a danger to the public. Dr. Dyer stated that he was feeble physically.
 Sentence: Twelve months' imprisonment, second division.
