Q. Did Charles Lavey then write something on the back of the note, and
did you take the pen and make a mark on it? 4. I never saw the note
when I got the 2s. I said I was going down to the ship-I never heard him
say he would send down my things--two days after, some things were sent on
board to me as the vessel was just quitting the dock, by Eels-be did not
give me the bundle-I would not have it-he offered it to me-he went on
board the vessel to Gravesend--the captain came on board about six or seven
in the evening I do not know the name of the waterman-I never saw him
before or since, till I saw him here--I did not select any clothes in his pre-
sence--he never came to me as I came out of the Golden Eagle, and said, "I
want my 5s."-I did not say, “Stop a bit, I shall get the money for my note"
- I did not go into Lavey's counting-house, or back room, with Murgatroyd,
the waterman--I was never in it-Murgatroyd did not say, on my putting my
mark on the note, “Why, you signed your name to the ship's articles, why
don't you sign your name to the note ?"-I did not say I could not be
bothered to write then, and that my mark would do as well, nor any
thing to that effect-Charles Lavey did not pay Murgatroyd 58., on my
account, in my presence -- I do not recollect Murgatroyd saying, when I
first saw him that day, “ Yates, where have you sprung from 1"- I did not
reply, “From the straw-house" - I had come from my lodging in Rosemary-
lane that day-I do not recollect the party's name-it is a barber's shop,
opposite Dock-street--I paid fourpence a night-l'had got work in the Lon-
don Dock, now and then, sufficient to pay my lodging- I did not tell Murgat-
royd I had come from the straw-house, that I was ready to go anywhere, and
would give him 10s. if he could get me off-this note is for more than half a
month's advance-half a month's advance would be 11. 28. 60.-I know
Henry Gardiner I do not know whether he is the man that took me and the
other sailors on board at the Commercial Dock-I had a bundle when I went
on board there, containing a shirt and pair of trowsers- I lodge with Gardi-
Der now I do not recollect seeing Gardiner on board did not receive &
bundle from him on board-I never said to Gardiner, in speaking of this
charge, that I had been persuaded to do this; that I would go through with
it, as I had begun, as I had an idea of making a good thing of it-I never
said, "If I do anything, I shall not take less than 101."Gardiner wanted
me to take 101. and go away to Southampton; and I was taken by the arm
yesterday, and told a lady wanted to see me I had nothing to drink on the
day it is said I signed the note- I was not inside a public-house that day—I
paid for one pot of beer for some persons that befriended me, but I did not
go after it; they brought it me, and that was all I had to drink--I came ashore
trom my voyage last Sunday fortnight I hurt my back getting out of the
ship, and did not make any complaint about this before the Magistrate till
this day week-I then went to the station.
