No—I think I should have known his step if he had been one of the passers by—I spoke of the same smell about twelve months ago, and the prisoner said I need not be afraid, he wat much obliged to me, for I had a mind to mind his property and my own too—I do not know that tar is used in the prisoner's business—I was never on his premises further than the coun ter—twelve months ago he carried on his business there—his wife lived in the corner house then—I cannot say when his wife died—she was with me about three weeks before she died, or it might have been a month or six weeki—I think the last time I saw her was about six months before the fire—I did not see her within six or seven weeks of the fire—I cannot say as to two months. JULIA MARIA ALLEN. I am a school-mistress, and live in Beech-street.
