John BARR. At the time of the robbery I lived at Catherine Cottage,
Lewisham-about a fortnight before the 26th of last month I left home-
our house was fastened up securely-there was no one in charge--I and
my wife went away-we returned home on the 25th-we found the kitchen
window broken open-inside the house I found everything in confusion-I
have had that pistol (produced) for twenty years-these razors were in the
back kitchen drawer-my wife will speak to the other artioles they were
all safe when we left.
Mary Ann Barr. I am the wife of last witness--we left our house
about a fortnight before the 25th-when we returned we found the house
broken into, and everything in confusion---I identify those sheets, that
towel, those throe night dresses are mine, and those table-cloths--these were
safe in the house before I left—that counterpane was on the bed-I identify
that silk dress, those six sheets, that ivory thimble case, and those earrings
are mine-those other articles were taken from our house there was somo
fringe that I know, and that jucket also.
JOHN EDGAR (re-examined). These the female prisoner was wearing.
JOHN JONES. I was assistant to Mr. Folkard, pawnbroker, 67, Gray's

GEORGTINSLEY, I am assistant to Mr. George Pockett, 16, Liquor

ESTHER INGWOOD. I am the female searcher at the Bishopsgate police
