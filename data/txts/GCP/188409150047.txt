EuZA ANN SHEPHERD. I live at 3, Ashwin Street, Dalston-at the
corner of Ashwin Street and Beech Street there is a Baptist Chapel; the
front part is in Ashwin Street, and between my house and the chapel
there are two houses- I keep the refreshmeat rooms at Dalston station
on Friday night, 1st December, 1882, about a quarter-past 10, I left my
home to go to the Dalston station; as I came out of my door I saw two
men struggling in the road close to the pavement, right opposite my
house--I could not see their faces, but I saw one was & policeman in
uniform--as I was closing the door I heard a shot fired, and immediately
afterwards I heard a second shot fired-I was partly down the steps then
I was go frightened that I rang the bell and went back into my
house-I only heard two shots-I saw that the other man had & wide-
awake hat on his head-after I heard the first shot fired I heard the
policeman cry out “Help”-I was in my house about twenty minutes,
and I then went to my place of business as quick as I could-it was a
dark wideawake hat.
