DANIEL CHaPMAN. I am station-master at the Overton station, on the
South Western Railway-on a Sunday in July last year, I remember Henry
Brown coming to the Overton station by the Basingstoke train-1 can't
say for certain what Sunday in July it was, but I believe it was the 13th;
it was in July-he had with him an oil-case black bag and a great coat,
which he was carrying over his arm-he was the worse for liquor; that
attracted my attention, and also that of several boys in the yard they
laughed at him-I remember a porter, named Dodd, coming to the Overton
station-he arrived on Friday, 4th July-that could not have been on the
first Sunday after Dodd came, because he was not on duty then it must
have been the Sunday after, for I saw Dodd on that occasion, and he walked
out of the yard with Brown-th train, by which Brown came, was due
Overton at 7.38 in the evening.
