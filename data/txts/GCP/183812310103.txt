526. WILLIAM PEARCE was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
December, 2 cushions, value 11.; and 1 rug, value 2s.6d.; the goods of
Sarah Wayland.
dow
SAMUEL WAYLAND. I am assistant to my mother, Sarah Wayland, who
is a widow, and is a post-horse-keeper at Richmond. On the 27th of
December, about seven o'clock, I saw two cushions, and the rug safe in the
gig under the gateway on my mother's premises at Cumberland-yard,
Richmond. I had been to take a party to a dinner, and left the gig and
this property quite safe--my sister called me about nine o'clock, and I
found the carriage door open-the cushions and rug were gone these are
the articles-(looking at them)-I am quite sure they are my mother's.
John PATTERSON (police-constable 1 68.) I was on duty on the even-
ing of the 27th of December, at Putney. About ten minutes before
twelve o'clock, I was coming past the Eight Bells, and saw the prisoner
offering these articles for sale - I asked where he brought them from-
he would not tell me--I asked where he was taking them-he would not
tell me-I said, I must lock him up-he would not tell the Inspector his
name, nor any thing else.
