JAMES BUTT was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of May , 1 handkerchief, value 4 s  ., the goods of John Robson , from his person.—2nd COUNT  stating it to be the goods of a man unknown, from his person. FREDERICK BLASTON. I am a corporal in the Royal Marines. I was at Greenwich fair on the 21st of May, between five and six o'clock in the afternoon—I observed the prisoner put his hand into a gentleman's pocket and take a blue handkerchief out—I touched the gentleman, and told him—a constable by my side took the prisoner—he dropped it from under his coat—the gentleman gave his name, John Robson, in the presence of the prisoner—I am sure this is the handkerchief—I put two bits of string on it, to know it again—I read the gentleman's card—he was a hat-finisher, and lodged in Grange-walk, Bermondsey.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  Was there a crowd? A.  Yes—I have never seen the gentleman since—he went off after he had been be fore the Magistrate. JOHN JAMES BARNARD( police-constable G  110.) I was there, and saw Blaston touch Robson—the prisoner made off from a man, and I took him—he dropped this handkerchief from under his coat on the ground—the man, in presence of the prisoner, said his name was John Robson, and this was his handkerchief—I found four other handkerchiefs on him. NOT GUILTY.
