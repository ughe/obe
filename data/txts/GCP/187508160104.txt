CHARLES HEMMING (Dover Police). I found the prisoner at Dover, on
10th May, and banded him over to a Woolwich officer.
GEORGE BROWN. I am a clerk in the Friendly Societies' Registration
Office-I produce the registered rules of the Sir Walter Raleigh Lodge of
the Independent Order of Odd Fellows, Manchester Unity, held at Woolwich,
signed by Thomas Danvers, also the appointment of trustees and of the secre-
tary, and other papers which have been deposited—the three trustees are
Alexander Walker, Alfred How, and W. Langham, the resolutions appoint-
ing them are signed by them, and are sent to us as I now produce them-
they are signed by the prisoner as secretary.
The Prisoner received a good character.
Guilty.
Recommended to mercy by the Jury-Five Years' Penal Servitude.
There were other indictments against the prisoner.
458. JOSEPH FORD (18) and HENRY DUNN (21), Robbery on
Francis Callaghan, and stealing from his person a cigar-case, a handkerchief,
and 11s., his property.
MR. Douglas conducted the Prosecution; and MR. GILL the Defence.
FRANOS CALLAGHAN. I am a quartermaster in the merchant service,
and was paid off last Wednesday fortnight-I was at Woolwich on Friday
night, 6th August at the Steam Packet public-house--I left when the house
closed at 12.30-I was making my way quietly along Beresford Street by
myself when someone knocked me down from behind; they rifled my
pockets and took 118., & silk handkerchief, and cigar-case-I may have
spoken to some one on my way before any one touched me-Ford knocked
me down from behind-I seized him as he struck me, and I held him several
seconds; that is how I know him-I am perfectly satisfied that Dunn was
one of the men; he assisted in rifling my pockets-after that they ran away,
except one, whom I kept in my grasp, but he afterwards got away-I then
gave information to the police- I called out “Police" when they attacked
me-I went across to the alley and stopped there ; I cannot say how long,
whether ten minutes or a quarter of an hour I then went away, found a
policeman, and gave him a description of Ford and Dunu-he then took
me to a house where I found Ford in bed with his mother-I recognised
him as the man-his mother said to the policeman "What are you doing
there!" and Ford, who was putting on his boots, said that if she didn't shut
up he would put the heels of his b- boots in her mouth-I afterwards
went to another house with the policeman and stood on the stairs, when
Dunn was brought down-I said he was the man.
Cross-examined. I had been to two or three houses during the evening
-I was not the last person to leave the Steam Packet; several went out
when they were closing--they were going to shut up when I came out; they
close at 12.30-I did not turn round at all when I was attacked, but seized
the man from behind--the opportunity I had of seeing him was two or
three seconds- I did not notice the men before I was struck, to my know-
ledge the whole affair took six or seven seconds- I had never seen any of
the men before; the night was cloudy-I had not had much to drink that
night-I may have spoken to a person by way of a compliment on my way
-I did not interfere in & quarrel -I am not aware of having been
spoken to.
Re-examined. The house was not cleared when I came out -I came out
with several others- I was not turned out any more than with everybody else.
