HENRY HAYDON. I am servant to Mr. Harding, a butcher, in Southamp-
ton-street, four doors from Mr. Grey's. I saw the mob at Mr. Grey's, and
Burden among them-he was right opposite my master's a young chap
came out of Mr. Grey's, and Burden and another went after him--Burden
took a paper parcel, about as large as the palm of my band, out of the hand
of the man who came out of Mr. Grey's, and put it into his pocket-he went
away, and I saw no more of him.
