I do not know—the men were on Mr. Rhodes's ground at first, and came on Mr. Bacon's field after us—it was within five minutes after we had shot their dogs that Kitley fired his gun—the gun I had was a common fowling-piece—the men had only one gun among them that I saw, and that gun was dis charged—they had no time to load their gun again before I fired at them—my gun was loaded with powder and shot, I believe No, 5, shot, which is very small—partridge shot—that would kill a partridge thirty or forty yards off—I cannot say whether my gun would kill a bird at fifty or sixty yards.
