WILLIAM SOUTHGATE (prisoner, on oath). Perjury has been com-
mitted in this case. Parsons, the other prisoner, said to me when

The Common Serjeant ruled that prisoner could not give evidence
of what Parsons said; he must be called a witneæ.
Examination continued. I am charged with Parsons in stealing
this copper wire which I am absolutely innocent of. My mother
knows that I was in bed at the time and had been in bed every night
that week before 11 p.m. When Parsons got caught he told Detective
Smith that I was the other man, which is absolutely false. Parsons
is dragging me into this out of revenge.
Cross-examined. I have known Parsons, he •has been frequently
with me. I was not with him on February 5 or 6. I have spoken
to Sillett once or twice. It is false that I went Eagle House at
11 p.m. on February 7. Parsons is called by the nick-name of Lippy.
I never lodged at Eagle House.
