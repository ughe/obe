1728. JOSEPH STALLARD was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of August , 6 live tame geese, price 24s., the property of William Nicholls.
   GEORGE NICHOLLS. I manage the business for my uncle, Mr. William Nicholls. I lost six geese on the 22nd of August; I had seen them on the day before - I have seen two geese since, but I cannot swear to them - they were white ones, and ours were all white - they correspond in size - I went to the watch-house, and found the prisoner there, with two geese that morning; he had been employed by my uncle - I have seen him in the stables, the premises are surrounded by a wall six feet high, with spikes at the top.
   DENNIS BEALY. I am in the prosecutor's employ. On the 21st of August, between seven and eight o'clock in the evening, the geese were all safe; there is no fastening to either of the doors of the kitchen where they were; I went out about five or half-past five o'clock in the morning; I found the pond had been dragged for fish, and I saw blood on four separate places at the end of the pond; I have seen the two white geese - one of them has a particular claw, which I had marked, as being one of the two small ones which I had kept back - I had twenty-five of them, and sent them up as they were wanted - they were dead when I saw them.
 Cross-examined. Q. What is the mark? A. A hole in the claw - the Magistrate asked how I knew them; I said one had a topping on it; I said so at the watch-house, that I could swear to one of them; I do not know that I was asked about the feet - I went to prove the property; I do not suppose the Magistrate asked me how I knew them - one of the gentlemen asked me some questions - I do not know that I was asked to swear to the geese - I will not swear to the colour.
 COURT. Q. What do you mean by saying you would not swear to the colour? A. They were mostly all white.
   WILLIAM MASSAM. I saw the prisoner near Paddington, at five o'clock on Sunday morning, with a basket; I asked what he had - he said a hock of bacon and a bit of bread; I collared him - he said he would go with me, and I let him go; he ran into the canal up to his waist, ran down two hundred yards, and then trampled the basket under his feet in the canal; my brother officer took him, and I got a boat - a person drew  the basket out with a stick; I took it to the watch-house, and found in it one small goose and one large one - one with the head off and one on; they were quite warm, and appeared lately killed- I afterwards saw the witness; I do not recollect that he gave me any description of the geese before he saw them - I produce the legs of the geese.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did he describe them after he saw them? A. I did not hear him say any thing about them - I asked the prisoner where he brought the basket from; he said from Stone-bridge.
 Prisoner. I was at large for six hours. Witness., My brother officer took him to the watch-house, and the inspector sent him back with the officer to find the property - he went into the water to find it, but could not; I went into the boat and got it, and the prisoner walked away - some of the men were to give him a glass of gin; he was taken the same morning.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did your brother officer make the slightest attempt to prevent his going? A. No.
 JURY. Q. Are you certain it was the same basket? A. Yes - I never left the spot till I found it.
     GUILTY. Aged 31. - Confined Six Months.
 1729.  WILLIAM THOROGOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of August , 1 tub, value 2s., the goods of James Beal.
   JAMES BEAL. On the 30th of August, about ten o'clock in the morning, I missed a tub from my shop; I had seen it a quarter of an hour before - a person came and told me of it; I went up the street, as I was directed, and found the prisoner fifty yards off with it.
   RICHARD MILLER. I took charge of the prisoner; the prosecutor took the tub back - the prisoner said he had no tub; at that time the man had it again.
 Prisoner's Defence. It was out at the door, and distress drove me to do it.
     GUILTY. Aged 35. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1730.  WILLIAM WHEELER and JOSEPH WARD were indicted for stealing, on the 8th of September , 1 pair of trousers, value 4s., the goods of William Mote and John Appleford.
   THOMAS HURLEY. I am a law-writer. I was walking by the shop of Mote and Appleford, and saw Ward walking away with these trousers - the two prisoners were together; Ward first put them under his coat, and then handed them to Wheeler - they were both near the door when Ward took them; they were hanging rather inside the door, and fastened by a string - I did not see them cut down; I told Mr. Pyatt of it, and the prisoners were stopped in Exmouth-street - Wheeler threw the trousers and a pair of scissars away from him; Ward was taken in ten minutes or a quarter of an hour - there was no cry of Stop thief! they ran when the officer was in pursuit.
 Wheeler. I was coming from Covent-garden, where I had been to buy some walnuts - he cannot say he saw me at the door. Witness. Yes; I saw him go, and ask the price of a handkerchief - I swear Ward gave you the trousers as you turned the corner.
 Ward. He never saw me near the place. Witness. Yes - I am sure he is the person I saw walk away from the shop with the trousers.
   MICHAEL RADFORD. I am an officer. On Wednesday, the 8th of September, a person told me he believed Wheeler had taken a pair of trousers; I crossed, and got within a few yards of him - he had them under his arm; he ran, and threw them down with a pair of scissars - I took him; he said he had picked them up - I asked why he ran; he said he was startled at the time - I took him towards the station, and my brother officer was called to take Ward.
   WILLIAM BRYMER PYATT. I am in the employ of William Mote and John Appleford; the witness told me a pair of trousers had been stolen - there is a mark on them; they were on the door-post.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   THOMAS MOOR. I am a constable. I took Ward; I saw Wheeler throw down the trousers - Ward was walking down Coppice-row; the witness said he was the man he saw at the door.
 Ward. I was going to Smithfield - I was never in his company at all.
