KATE ENGLISH, 2, Flower and Dean Street, Spitalfields. On a
Monday evening, I cannot remember the date, I met prisoner at the
top of the street, but he gave me a penny. I went into the 44 Three
Cranes public-house and had a drink. I went there again after-
wards and was talking to another woman when prisoner and another
man came in. He asked us to have a drink and we accepted. I did
not see what he tendered and I did not hear the barman say anything.
I heard an argument about some money but E did not hear what it
was as I was talking to my friend. When the barman said it was
bad prisoner said, You do not think I have come in here with bad
money. I did not hear him threaten the barman; I was talking
to my friend.
ALBERT CLEMENTS, barman, Weavers' Arms." On November
14 prisoner and another man came in and I refused to serve them

Verdict, Guilty.
Fourteen previous convictions were proved, dating from 1893, none
for coinage offences. Prisoner had a remanet to serve of 273 days
from a sentence of three years' penal servitude in 1908.
