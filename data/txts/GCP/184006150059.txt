1623. JOSEPH SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of May,
1 handkerchief, value 58., the goods of James Honey, from his person.
JAMES Honey. I am a woollen-draper. On the 25th of May, I was
in St. James's Park, about five minutes past one o'clock, as they were
firing the guns, and felt a tug at my pocket-I turned immediately round
--the prisoner had hold of my handkerchief, but it was not quite out of
my pocket-I seized hold of him with one hand, and my handkerchief
with the other-I called the police, and gave him in charge the corner of
the handkerchief was still in my pocket. KAOIBOYALE
