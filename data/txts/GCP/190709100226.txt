saw the prisoners, Friend and Riley, at Dorking Police Court, and

and harness, which has been found in your possession." Riley said.
"I never got in the trap. I do not know anything about it. Tony
went with Friend for å drive." Friend said, "I wish to make i
statement. I borrowed the horse from a man named J. Lovell,

Green, but I do not know where. Lovell had the cob which had the
set of harness on, which was on the animal when I was arrested
Dorking. He had a rug over the horse, and was carrying the saddle

Riley said his landlord would lend him one for a bit of silver. I say

Yes,' and took the cob down to Mr. Luck at Dorking, and sold i

To Mr. Thomas. When I first saw Head he said he did not lend
