1845. JAMES TAPPIN was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of July,
2 pairs of boots, value 108,, the goods of William Spencer.
WILLIAM SPENCER. I am a general salesman, and live in Whitechapel-
road. On the 4th of July, about a quarter past two o'clock in the after-
noon, I heard a noise at the door-I turned my head, an 1 saw the prisoner
running away with the boots in his hand, close to the door pursued
him-he threw them down-I followed, and took him without losing
sight of him -these are my boots(looking al them)-they had been on
a stall-board in front of the house.
JAMEs RYE. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into custody, and
found on him two boxes of cart greaseh said he was in the part grease
line, and they were samples to go round to get orders.
GUILTY. Aged 18.--Confined Six Weeks.
