THOMAS MOORE. I am a bombardier in the second battalion of the Royal
Artillery. After this occurrence had taken place I went into the room
No. 7, and the doctor came in soon after me-I examined the prisoner's
pouch about eleven o'clock, and found two bundles-one appeared smaller
than the other-it appeared to me that one cartridge was gone out of one
of the bundles, but I did not open them-I could tell by the feel and ap-
pearance it was tied in a different way to what it usually is I had not
the slightest doubt that one had been taken out--one could not be taken
out without my knowing it.
WILLIAM RICHARDSON. I am a surgeon and medical officer. I examined
the body of the deceased - I found a gun-shot wound passing through
the body-that was undoubtedly the cause of death.
Court. Q. Could you tell on which side it entered ? A. On the right
of the spine, between that and the lower angle of the blade bone, and
came out between the centre of the breast and the right nipple--it broke
one rib in front, and two ribs behind, and passed through the heart.
CHARLES JAMEISON. I am a gunner and driver in the second battalion.
I know the prisoner-I saw him on Saturday afternoon, the day before
this happened-he was speaking to me about his extra parade-he had to
parade with the night guard, and he told me he would make it all right in
the morning-he said nothing else to me that night-he said some time
before that the Sergeant-major had a spite against him-I do not recollect
how lately before the Sunday that was.

1968. JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of May, 1
handkerchief, value 3d., the goods of a man unknown, from his person.
JAMES WILD (police-constable D 124.) I was on duty, in plain clothes,
on the afternoon of the 20th of May, in Greenwich fair-I saw the pri-
soner in company with another man, feeling gentlemen's pockets at last I
saw them standing behind a man-the prisoner took this handkerchief out of
his pocket, and put it into his own flap-I seized them both, and told the
gentleman-he felt, and missed his handkerchief-I took it from the pri-
soner, and the gentleman claimed it-I told him to follow me to the sta-
tion-house, but the crowd was so great that he did not-I do not know his
name-I found on the prisoner six gambling dice and a pair of combs.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Transported for Ten Years.
