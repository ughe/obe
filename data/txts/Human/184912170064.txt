I am the wife of Charles Gardner. I have been help ing Mrs. Dashwood since last April—she is the wife of Thomas Dashwood—on 1 st  Dec, I saw her do up these two frocks and other articles, and give them to a little girl, Maria, to take to the mangle—she is three years and five months old—she is now very ill with the measles—she went out with the bundle—she came back in about half an hour with the policeman.
   Cross-examined by MR. O'BRIEN . Q.  How do you know Mr. Dash-wood's name? A.  I gave it at the registration of the child—the men where he works call him Tom  —his wife calls him Thomas.
