Thomas STREET. I am a chimney-sweeper, and live near Mr. Taylor,
On 1st Jan., about five o'clock, I saw the two prisoners a short distance from
Mr. Taylor's, I passed them, and went in doors - 1 afterwards came out, and
went to Mr. Taylor's shop-window-I then saw Gibbs come, on his hands and
toes, from round by the till-Cullinan was a short distance from the shop-
said to Gibbs, "Halloa l" and he made the same answer ran after him
and asked him to stop; he would not-1 came back, and as I put my foot on
the step of Mr. Taylor's door, Cullinan ran by, and said, "You -
I will give you something;" and he directly ran away in the same direction
as Gibbs-I gave information to Mrs. Taylor and the police-sergeant, and
went and called my brother out-I took Gibbs, and gave him in charge to
my brother--the officer took Cullinan.
Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q. How far was Cullinan from the shop
when you saw Gibbs come out ? A. Five yards-he was not looking in at any
shop-I could see him by the lamp of the window-I was inside the shop
when I saw Gibbs; he ran out, and I after him-I had seen Cullinan before,
in the afternoon, and took notice of him he was standing still, by the shop-
door, when he made the remark to me; he did not run against me, or stumble.
JOHN STREET. My brother called me out-I saw Gibbs put his hand
into his side-pocket and drop a half-crown.
