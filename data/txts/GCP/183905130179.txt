1709. MARY BAYNON was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of
April, 1 pewter pot, value 1s., the goods of John Thomas Percy; 1 pewter
pot, value 18., the goods of William Nelson; 2 pewter pots, value 2s., the
goods of Ann Willmot; 1 pewter pot, value 1s. 8d., the goods of. Thomas
Davey; and 1 pewter pot, value 18 8d., the goods of Joseph Towers : and
FRANCES BÂYNON, for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing
them to have been stolen ; against the Statute, &c.
MR. JERNINGHAM conducted the Prosecution.
John Thomas Percy. I am a publican, and live at Rotherhithe. Here
is a pewter pot of mine it has my name and address on it.
CHARLES HALLAN (police-sergeant R 3.) I went to the prisoner's
premises—I found Frances Baynon, the mother, up in a corner of the top-
room, and there were two pint pots by the side of her she was hustling
amongst shavings-she said she told her daughter not to bring them-one
of these pots is Mr. Percy's.
Frances Baynon. I was cleaning down stairs-he asked where I
lived, and I told him up stairs-he said, “Go up, and I will follow you."
Witness. That is wrong-I asked a little girl down stairs, and I found
you in the corner of the room-I then searched the shavings and found
these two quart and two pint pots besides.
Thomas Davey. I am a publican. One of these quart pots is mine,
it has my name and address on it.
WILLIAM Nelson. One of these pots is mine.
