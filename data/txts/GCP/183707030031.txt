1654. JOHN BARNETT was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of Junes
40 yards of printed muslin, value 51. 185.; 8 yards of cloth, called challi
value 11. 168. ; 48 yards of silk, called gossamer, value 61. 16s. and 15
stocks, value 41.; the goods of Thomas Fardell; and that he had been
before convicted of felony.—2nd Count, stating them to be the goods of
Thomas Ovington and another.
Jonx WARREN. I am a carman in the employ of Mr. Fardell. I drove
his cart on the 21st of June, with a parcel containing these articles, which
I brought from my master's office, No. 35, Leadenhall-street, and was
going to take to the Brunswick Wharf, Black wall-at half-past seven
o'clock I was going along the Commercial-road, and heard one of the pri-
soner's comrades whistle—I looked over my shoulder, and saw the prisoner
getting off the shafts with this parcel-he ran across the road, and dropped
it-I ran after him, and Smith took up the parcel-the prisoner ran three
quarters of a mile before I took him, but I was close behind him, and he
was taken in my presence.
Cross-examined by Mr. Doane. Q. Where were you ? 2. By the
side of my horses I was no great way from the shafts- it was quite light
I did not see any body till I heard the whistle-Smith was behind the
wagon-I chased the prisoner three quarters of a mile round streets and
courts, but I was close to himbe was never out of my sight there were
great number of persons about-he was quite a stranger to me, but I
can swear he is the man—I have got scolded for losing goods before, and
have had to pay for them.
COURT. Q. How far were you from the prisoner? 4. Two or three
yards.
GEORGE SMITH. I was with Warren-I was behind the wagon-I did
not see any one jump from the shafts, but I saw the prisoner the instant he
had jumped down-he had this parcelhe ran across and dropped it-I
took it up and went to the wagon, and kept it till the policeman came and
took it of me.
