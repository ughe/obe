1762. JOHN PRIOR was again indicted for embezzling, on 26th June
21. 12s.6d.; the moneys of William Shaw, his master.
Mr. Robinson conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM HOOD. On 26th June, I directed the prisoner to take half
load of bay, which came to 11. 178. 6d., and half a load of straw, 16... do
Mr. Parson's, at Fish-street-hill-he was to receive the money and bring it
back-be did not bring the money to me-I saw bim when he returned, but
had not time to speak to him.
Cross-examined by Mr. Parry. Q. You were very busy? A. He went
away before I came back out of the office-I asked him next morning for it
- he remained in our service two days after that, and was taken into custody
at Smithfield, where I attend.
MR. Robinson. Q. What did he say the next morning? 4. He told
me he had lost it.
WILLIAM Shaw. The prisoner has not paid me the 2h 12s.6d.
John Parson. I lived with John Connett, my son-in-law, a butcher of
Fish-street-hill. I purchased some hay and straw in Smithfield; it was
delivered at Dowgate-hill--the prisoner then came back to Fish-street-hil,
and I saw him paid the money, 21. 12s.6d.
Not Guilty.
