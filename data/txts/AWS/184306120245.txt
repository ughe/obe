2027. THOMAS SKILLIN, RICHARD SMITH, and SARAH
DAVIS were again indicted for stealing, on the 20th of May, 2 shillings,
2 sixpences, 30 pence, and 46 halfpence; the monies of Stephen Skillin.
STEPHEN SKILLIN. On the night of the 20th of May I had 7s. 21d. in
copper, and 2s. in silver, and two sixpences wrapped in a piece of paper,
which I intended to give my old woman in the morning when she was sober
the 2s. were black-I put it all in the cellar, behind the water-butt, in the
evening-I afterwards went down to look at the rabbits, and the money was
gone, but I was so intoxicated I did not know what I was about.
Skillin. Q. Did not I ask you for some money to get beer, and did not
you say if E went down in the cellar, and looked behind the water-butt, E
should find some there A. I do not remember, was so tipsy.
CAROLINE Mace. I heard the prosecutor say he should go and feed his
tabbits--he went into the cellar, came up, and went in doors-the old lady
fell down--| saw the three prisoners go down into the cellar, and come away
-I think the old man told them the money was there.
NOT GUITY.
