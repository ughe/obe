 252.  ELIZABETH CRATTY was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of November , 1 shawl, value 10s., the goods of Sarah Butler , her mistress; to which she pleaded. GUILTY. - Confined Three Months.
 253.  ISABELLA CLARK was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of December , 1 handkerchief, value 5s., the goods of Henry Woods.
 ELIZABETH WOODS. I am the wife of Henry Woods, and am a laundress. I took a handkerchief like this out of a basket in my own room - the prisoner was washing for me.
   GEORGE ADNAM. I am a pawnbroker. The prisoner pawned a handkerchief like this with me on the 2nd of December, and redeemed it on the 3rd.
     NOT GUILTY.
 254.  JOHN CHAMPHIN and CHARLES BARNET were indicted for stealing, on the 12th of November , 1 live tame rabbit, price 2s., the property of Anthony Rule.
   ANTHONY RULE. I live at Tottenham. I lost a rabbit on the 12th of November - I had seen it safe at seven o'clock that evening, near my stable door in the hutch, which was fixed against the stable wall - the hutch door was shut and buttoned; I came home about eight o'clock, and missed it - I saw it on the 13th, hanging at Mr. Garner's door. a grocer and cheesemonger; I knew it was mine - the prisoners are labourers, and live a little way from me.
   JOSEPH STACE. I live with Mr. Garner. About a quarter past eight o'clock, on the evening of the 12th of November, champhin came to his shop, and asked 20d. for this rabbit - I took it to Mr. Garner, who told me to offer him 1s. 4d.; he went out and came in again, and said he would take 1s. 6d., and we gave it him.
 Cross-examined by MR. CHURCHILL. Q. Did you know him? A. Yes; I did not see the other boy - Champhin lives at Tottenham; I have bought rabbits of him be-- I have been at his house; this was such a rabbit as I had not seen before.
   JOHN FOWLER. I am a constable. I saw Barnet on the 13th of November, up the road, with his father's cart- he ran across the road; I said, "You were going to run away;" he said, "No, I was going to speak to Anthony Rule about the rabbit that Champhin and I found last night, in the lane;" I found nine-pence on him, which he said was half the money they got for it.
   ANTHONY RULE. This is the skin of the rabbit - my stable is in the corner of my yard; there is a skittle-ground adjoining it; there is but a slight fence - if the rabbit got out of the hutch it might get out of the ground: I had had it five weeks.   NOT GUILTY.
 255.  JAMES EVES and WILLIAM GOUGH, were indicted for stealing, on the 5th of December , 1 watch, value 2l.; 1 watch-chain, value 1s.; 3 watch-keys, value 3s.; 1 handkerchief, value 1s.; 1 snuff-box, value 6d.; 1 brooch, value 10s.; 2 gold pins, value 6s.; 12 penny-pieces, and 10 halfpence, the property of Charles Loxton.
 CHARLES LOXTON. I am a  toll-collector. in Cannon-street-road. Eves had been with me as a toll-collector-Gough worked at the docks, and he knew the toll-house; on the night of the 5th of December I was in my toll-house; and fell asleep about eleven o'clock - I had my coat on, and the articles stated were about me; the brooch and the pins were in the snuff-box, and the money was over the fire-place - there is a back-door, which was latched but not bolted; I was awoke by the Policeman - I then missed the articles, and reported the circumstance to another officer, and as we were returning, we overtook the officer bringing the prisoners to the watch-house - they were searched, and a key was found on Eves; I did not hear Gough say a word - this is the key, but I do not know that I can swear positively to it; there are a great many alike.
 COURT. Q. Did you ever swear that "The key produced is the same pattern with that in my snuff-box, it had lost its ring, and I have no doubt it is the same?" A. Yes, I have no doubt, but I should not like to swear to it without I was positively certain - it has very much the appearance of it, but I would not swear it is the key.
   JOHN EAGLES. I am a Police-officer. About ten minutes before twelve o'clock, I was at the turnpick-gate, and saw the two prisoner come out of the toll-house - an officer asked me it it was all right; I said, yes - it was Eves' brother, and knowing him, I had no suspicion of him; he was very much in liquor, and could scarcely stand - I then went up my beat, returned, and saw the door open; I went and said to the prosecutor, "Charles, you will lose your shirt" - I saw the back door was open; he then looked and missed his property - we went to several places, and on coming back found the prisoners in custody; they were taken to the watch-house, and Eves threw this key, two or three little bits of paper, 2d. and a bad shilling on the table; he caught up the key again to put it into his pocket; the prosecutor said, "Put that down, it is mine."
 Cross-examined by MR. CHURCHILL. Q. Had the backdoor been fastened? A. I do not know; the front door was open eight or ten inches.
