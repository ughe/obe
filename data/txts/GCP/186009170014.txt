CARTER, Mayor.
MR. Lewis conducted the Prosecution.
JOSEPH PROBERT STRICKLAND. I am a draper of Gravesend-on Ilth
June I received some goods from the prosecutor - I was not to receive credit
particularly-I pay cash principally-this (produced) is the invoice-on 3rd
July I paid the prisoner the amount, 11. 08. 90.--this receipt is in his writing
9d. has been deducted--that was not discount, it was an allowance for two
boxes.
Prisoner. l. Would yop not have paid me the amount even on the day
after if I had called ? A. Yes.
WILLJAM HACKNEY. I am a hatter at Bow-lane, Derby, and a customer
of the prosecutor's I was indebted to him in a sum of 91. 68.-I had a
credit of four months to pay it-on the same day that I received the goods,
26th July, the prisoner called and asked for 21. on account-he said that his
samples were fast at his hotel and he could not get them away, and could not
get back to London-I paid him 21. and he gave me this receipt (produced)
--be allowed me 28. discount—this is his writing.
Prisoner. Q. In the event of your paying this amount to me should you
not have expected the discount that I allowed you on this part of the
account? A. Yes-it was not the day before that I received the goods, it
was the same morning, I think.
JOSEPH ROLE. I am a cap manufacturer of 130, London-wall the
prisoner was in my service at 258. å week and 24 commission and his
travelling expenses-his duties were to collect orders, to receive money, to
pay it to me when he was in London, or send it up to me by post-I have
not received 11. 08. 9d. of him from Mr. Stickland of Gravesend, or 21. from
Mr. Hackney on account of a larger sum ; I never expected it to be paid-
I had given a month's credit or three months without discount-he had no
right to receive the money because the goods were sold on credit.
COURT. Q. But if the customer chose to pay it, would he be authorized
to take it for you? A. Yes, allowing the proper discount.
MR. LEWIS. Q. Is there a person named Bidwell in your employment ?
A. Yes—he was not authorized to receive money in my absence or from the
prisoner as my clerk.
Prisoner. Q. Whose business was it to enter those amounts so collected
by me? A. Bidwell's principally-when you paid me money I put my
initials--no other party made entries but Bidwell ; you made your own
entries, and when you paid the money over to me I put my signature I
have entered amounts which I have received, but there are very few ac-
counts which I have received myself, except government contracts, which
you do not interfere with-when I receive money myself I do not affix my
siguature-I have no occasion ; I know my own writing-these are the
books (produced)—here is an entry of your's and another of mine which I
collected myself—this is my initial, "G.R."— I received a cheque from Captain
Jeakes for 751. on 26th or 27th June-here is the entry-I have never
placed my signature to five or six consecutive amounts, at once for four or
five days, signing over leaf and over leaf, except when they were booked
in the same day and the money was given to me it may bave occurred
once or twice when the sale has been effected two or three days before-this
(produced) is the ledger-the account of Wright of Hackney-road is not in
it; but it is your book-keeping, you could not keep your books—I am not
a very good scholar and you took advantage of me I remember going to
the Roebuck at Woodford, on Sunday 8th July, to dinner-I do not re-
member the day preceeding that that it was your duty to call on Wright
