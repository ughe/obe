619. BRIDGET CLINTON (27), Feloniously wounding William
Victory with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
MR. WARBURTON Prosecuted.
WILLIAM VICTORY. I am a dock labourer-on 15th July, about 10.30,
I was with Charles Maiden in Barking Road-we met a woman and asked
her to have a drink-I saw the prisoner there with & man she is con-
nected with ; I knew her before-a female (not the prisoner) asked my
friend to have a glass of beer, but he did not have it eventually, I was
struck by the prisoner's friend, and the prisoner hit me with an umbrella
and cut my face, and when I turned to go home, she said, “You b--y
f- cow, I will have your life this night”-no conversation had taken
place between us before that-she then stabbed me over the ear with a
butcher's knife half worn out, which she took from her sleeve-she gave
me two strokes, but only inflicted one wound—I was taken to the
hospital-I was perfectly sober.
Cro88-eramined by the Prisoner. I did not strike you twice and kick
once, but you struck me with an umbrella-I saw a knife in your hand;
I do not know what you did with it-there was no provocation on my
part.
CHARLES MAIDEN. I am a bricklayer, of Rathbone Street, Canning
Town-on 15th July I was with Victory in Barking Road, and saw the
prisoner stab him in the ear-hole-she struck at him twice-she was
not drunk; she had had one glass in my presence-she took the knife
out of her sleeve-I did not see what became of it—there was no con-
versation before that.
FRANK CORDER. I am house surgeon at Poplar Hospital-on 15th
July, about 11.50, Victory was brought there with an incised wound in
front of his left ear, about an inch long, and not very deep, from above,
downwards; it was done with a sharp edged instrument with force-
he was in no danger-he has not been at the hospital, he was attended
by his club doctor—the wound has healed.
GEORGE PALMER (Police Sergeant). On 16th July, about 2.30, I went
to 16, Carlton Road, and saw the prisoner I told her I should take her
in custody for stabbing a man named Victory-she said, “I am innocent;
I do not know the man"-she was sober.
Cross-examined. Victory did not come to the station till two o'clock to
give information-I searched the room, but could not find the knife or
umbrella.
