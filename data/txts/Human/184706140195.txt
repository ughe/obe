   Cross-examined. Q.  You live next door but one to Mr. Thorn? A.  Yes, at an eating-house—it was dark—Dyble was outside when I came up, somewhere between the kerb-stone and the door, not many yards off Glover and the policeman—he did not go to them till after they had struggled some time—I did not hear him say, "Come away, don't be a fool!"—they had been on the ground a few minutes before he came up—I was there when Wadlow appeared to lose his senses—I did not assist him to the station, or go there. THOMAS EDWARD DRINKWATER. I am a surgeon, and live at Woolwich. On the 9th of April Wadlow called at my surgery—he was considerably bruised about the forehead, and had two black eyes—his right hand was a good deal torn and lacerated—the skin had been rubbed of the knuckles—the thumb of his left hand was severely sprained, which was followed by a good deal of inflammation—he complained of pain in his chest—the injuries were such as to keep him from duty several days.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How long was he off duty? A.  About five days—the main cause of that was the sprained thumb—there was no serious in ternal injury, or any appearance on the arm or side which made it necessary for him to keep away from duty. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined Four Months .

 1522. JAMES LEE JONES was indicted for stealing 2 half-crowns, 3 shillings, and one 1 1/2 d  . piece, the monies of Job Charlton , from his person.
   MR. WILDE conducted the Prosecution. JOB CHARLTON. I am a beer-seller, and live in Hog-lane, Greenwich, On Monday, the 7th of June, the prisoner came to my house between five and six o'clock—he and I afterwards had some conversation together—I am clear that I had at that time 8 s . and a 1 1/2 d . silver coin in my pocket—I did not give away or put away from my pocket that money—no one but the prisoner sufficiently near me to have taken it from me—the prisoner went away about seven o'clock—in about a quarter of an hour after, I found my money was gone—I went after the prisoner—I found him; and charged him with having my money—he said to the police-officer, "I have some money, but what money I have, that man gave me for adulterating his beer"—the money was produced, and it was mine.
