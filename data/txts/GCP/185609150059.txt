GEORGE BINET, and STEPHEN MOYSE, were indicted for unlaw-
fully conspiring, by molesting certain workmen in the employment of
George Frederick Young and others, to force them to depart from the said
employment.-Other Counts, varying the manner of stating the charge.
MR. ATTORNEY-GENERAL, with Mr. SERJEANT BALLANTINE and Mr. HOLL,
conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE FREDERICK YOUNG. I am in partnersbip with Sidney Young and
Charles Magnay-I carry on with them the business of ship builders, and have
done so for forty years. In the early part of this year we had, I suppose,
about 300 or 400 men in our employment-we had regular times of work,
and for going off work in the summer time the men commenced work at
5 o'clock, and worked from 6 till 8, then from half-past 8 till 11, from 20
minutes past 11 till 1, from 2 to 4, and from half-past 4 to 6 o'clock, making
