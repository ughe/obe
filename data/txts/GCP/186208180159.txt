pony and trap-the next day they asked me to buy it the prisoner was
taking the pony away from my stable and I stopped it-he said he had got
no money to pry for the horse's keep-I said I should detain the pony and

Court. 0. What was the use of asking him whose property it was in

MR. GENT. Q. Did you advance him 108 ! A. Yes; on his promise to
