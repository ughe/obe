PIRIE, Mayor.
NEW COURT.-Tuesday, 30th November, 1841.
Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
18. GEORGE BOOTHBY was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of
October, 8 half-crowns, and 34 shillings, the monies of Gottlieb Irion
Schunter, his master; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
19. JAMES WEBBER was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of No-
vember, 6 knives, value 1s.; 3 forks, value 6d.; and 2 spoons, value 6d.;
the goods of Seth Worrall; to which he pleaded
GUILTY.. Aged 28.-Transported for Seven Years.
20. EDWARD JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of No-
vember, 1 pair of boots, value 48., the goods of John Such; to which he
pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
21. CHARLES MATTHEW CARTER was indicted for stealing, on
the 26th of November, 56 lbs. weight of iron, value 2s.6d., the goods of
William Thompson, Esq., and others; to which he pleaded
GUILTY Aced
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Six Months.
22. JOHN DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of Novem-
ber, 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of Edward Roach, from his
person.
EDWARD Roach. I live in Angel-street, Regent-street, Westminster.
Between five and six o'clock, in the evening of the 15th of November, I
was in Smithfield, some one gave me information, and I missed my hand-
kerchief-I ran after the prisoner, the constable took him, and took my
handkerchief from him—this now produced is it.
WILLIAM HARDING (City police-constable, No. 271.) I stopped the
prisoner, who was running-I found this handkerchief on him, under his
jacket.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going to the doctor, as my mistress was very
bad; I was going into John-street, and picked the handkerchief up at the
corner of Smithfield.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
23. ANN ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of No-
vember, 37 printed books, valued 71. the goods of Samuel Daniel Evans.
SAMUEL DUKE EVANS. I am a bookseller, and live in Cheapside. I
lost thirty-seven printed books about the 18th of November-Mr. Forrester
has them.
SARAN MAYBON. I am servant to the prosecutor. On the afternoon
of the 18th of November, about four o'clock, the prisoner came to the
shop, and asked to look at some Church Services--I showed her some
Bibles and Prayer-books-she looked them out to the amount of 71. 08. 6d.
-she said if I would send the lad with the books with her she would pay
the lad, she had not sufficient with her to pay me in consequence of that
I sent them by the lad- he was to bring back the money, which he did
not.
