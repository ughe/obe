184. GEORGE JOHNSON (30), Feloniously having counterfeit coin in
his possession, with intent to utter it.
Jeho
Messrs. O'CONNELL and Brown conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES BRANNAN, I am employed by the Solicitor to the Treasury, in
Mint cases--on 7th January I was with Fife, and other officers, in Black-
friars Road, and pointed the prisoner out to Fife—when he got under the
archway of Blackfriars Bridge, Fife seized him, and said that he had received
instructions to take him in custody, as he was suspected of having counter-
feit coin-he struggled very violently, and I saw him take his closed hands
from both pockets, and drop a packet containing ten half-crowns, wrapped
separately, with tissue paper between them--the officers took from his other
hand a packet, which fell on the ground-it contained nine bad half-crowns
similarly wrapped up-I said "Harry, what name are you going to give
now ? I need not tell you my name; I have received instructions from the
Treasury to look after you as dealing in counterfeit coin"--I asked where
he lived-he said that he had no fixed home-he first gave his name Chandler,
and afterwards Johnson.
Prisoner. . Did you find any bad coin at my lodging? A. You said
you did not lodge there-I went to room No. 13, and it appears you lodge
in No. 15—I did not meet Charles Somers in the City a fortnight previously,
and ask him where you lived, for I knew-I had you watched to 10, Pitt
Street, Old Kent Road, when you were released from Yarmouth Prison-
I did not say that I would not mind giving him 51. if he would put you
into my clutches you set up a similar defence before.
JOHN FIFe (Police Inspector). I was with Brannan--he has given &
correct account of what passed. po s it
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These two packets contain bad coin.
The prisoner, in his defence, stated that Charles Somers gave him the
packets to hold while he went to get measured for a waistooat. Guilty. He
was further charged with having been convicted at Yarmouth, in October. 1868.
of a like offence, to which he PLEADED GUILTY**--Ten Years' Penal Ser-
vitude.
