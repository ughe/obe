A. Yes ; when I took the form back on the certificate, the name is

Prisoner. Q. Did you not state to me that you had seen a letter from
Mr. Bailey to Thompson, saying that if he did not pay me he could not
go? A. I am certain I never did state that to you. I do not believe
that I ever did state so- I did to Mr. Bailey.

MR. ROBINSON. Q. Do I understand you went by the name of Green-
wood amongst your work people! A. Yes; but my certificate was in the
name of Hales-my wife's relations found out more than I did, and I was
compelled to be married in the name of Hales I have been married eight
years; but I have gone by the name of Greenwood I was bound appren-
tice in the name of Greenwood, and persons who worked with me knew me
by the name of Greenwood; but my wife went by the name of Hales.
Hindes and Learmond were workpeople they knew me by no other name
than Greenwood, nor did Thompson.
