put his hand into the prosecutor's pocket, take out this handkerchief, and drop
it on the ground-the prisoner picked it up, wiped his nose with it, and put
it into his pocket-he was close behind at the time the other took it-I went
and told the prosecutor, took him back to the prisoner, and said to him, "You
have got this gentleman's handkerchief" he said, “No, I have not" - At the
same time he put his hand into his trowsers pocket, and dropped it on the
