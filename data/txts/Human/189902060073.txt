I am a baker, of 85, Benchan Lane, West Croydon—on August 11th I was living with the last witness—I remember his coming home about five p.m. with Adamthwaite—Dowding paid Adamthwaite £14—they were there about forty-five minutes—I afterwards identified Adamthwaite at Southwark Police station.
   Cross-examined. MR. GEOGHEGAN . Before August 11th I had never seen Adamthwaite—I did not give a description to the police, Dowding did—I picked Adamthwaite out at once. EDGAR KIRBY.
