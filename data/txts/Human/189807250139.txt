   Cross-examined by  Mr. MARSHALL HALL . With regard to the issue of the 28th August, of which I have no copy, that would have been an issue in the middle of the week—an irregular issue—they are generally published on Saturday—the 28th would have been an extra issue—the 24th is Saturday and the 31st is Saturday—I believe there is an obligation on every paper to send every number to the British Museum, but I have no copy of the 28th—it never was sent to us.
