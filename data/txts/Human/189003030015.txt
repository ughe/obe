   Cross-examined by the Prisoner , Several other prisoners were there when you said that if I had the other man it would have been a good job—I did not tell the Magistrate that, because you were committed then. CHARLES DYER( Police Inspector ). After the remand on this charge I received a communication from the prisoner, and went to him on February 6th, and he made a statement to me respecting the man in whose company he was on 30th January, when he was arrested—he said, "He gave me the two coins to pass." "WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. I am Inspector of Coins to Her Majesty's Mint—these coins are both counterfeit, and from different moulds.
   The prisoner, in his defence, stated that he met a man who owed him 15s. who offered him some bread and cheese, and sent him into the shop with the florin; and he got the change and gave it to the man, who gave him a second coin, hut finding they were watched, said, "Throw it away, it is bad," which he did. He contended that if he had known they were bad he would not have been likely to carry them, with a convict's licence in his pocket. GUILTY **.— Twenty Months' Hard Labour.
