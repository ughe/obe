 2035.  JAMES VICKERY was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of October , 8lbs. weight of brass, value 5s., the goods of Daniel Mears , his master.
   MARY ANN MEARS. I am the wife of Daniel Mears, a brass finisher, who lives in High Holborn- the prisoner had been in our employ for one week - we missed a good deal of brass in the course of that week, but I did not suspect him - I went into the shop several times and spoke of it.
   BENJAMIN CLITHEROW. I am an apprentice to Mr. Mears. On Monday morning, when the prisoner came to work, I was in the yard, and heard the box opened - I went up-stairs, and missed some brass; and as the prisoner was going to breakfast, mistress told him, to leave what he had got behind him; he said he had nothing, but bread and butter - he went into the room, and after some time, pulled this metal from his hind coat-pocket - he was taken in charge, and owned that he had taken it.
 MRS. MEARS. Clitherow's statement is correct - the prisoner said, if I would go with him, he would take to me where I should find what was gone; he took me to Bevill, where we found 21/2lbs., and to Bassett, where there was 53/4lbs.
   THOMAS BEVILL. I am in the employ of Mr. Moss, a dealer in marine-stores, No. 7, Kingsgate-street, Holborn; I bought 11/2lbs. of brass of a person at 4d. a pound, but cannot say it was the prisoner - there was iron among it, or it would be worth 6d. - some of it was pure brass - I have no knowledge whether I bought it of a man or woman - it was some time the week before Mrs. Mears came - I have been in the shop a year and three-quarters - I cannot say whether I made any inquiry about it - I have no belief as to who I bought it of, whether it was man, woman, or child.
   JOSEPH CASTER. I live with Mr. Bassett, No. 19, Eagle-street, a dealer in marine-stores - I bought 53/4lb. of brass of the prisoner, at 5d. a pound - I did not know it was new.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.
 2036.  JOSEPH HULL was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of September , 7 shawls, value 36s., the goods of William Phillips and others, his masters.
 MESSRS. ANDREWS and LAW conducted the prosecution.
 WILLIAM PHILLIPS. I am a  lighterman, and have partners - the prisoner was my foreman for eight or nine years; I know Mr. McVicar, he is a mercantile agent - I received orders from him to ship goods for him, from the Galley-quay, on the 10th of July; and some more on the 10th of August; the prisoner was constantly employed at that quay as our foreman.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. How many persons have you had taken up? A. Four; two were discharged, and the bills against two were ignored - two of those worked under the prisoner; one of the witnesses has absconded since.
   JOHN McVICAR. I am agent to Messrs. Chippendall and Co., of Manchester, calico printers - I was directed by them to make a sale of some shawls in August - I consigned some of the same shawls and other handkerchiefs to Messrs, Gregson and Co. - I received them from Messrs. Chippendall; they were all to be shipped to Calcutta - Wilkinson, Fisher, and Co., are our packers; I sent these goods to them to be packed, and forwarded to Phillips, as the lighterman - the goods I received from Chippendall, answered to the invoice - they were unpacked in my warehouse, and I sent some at different periods to the packers' but not all; those I sold to Messrs. Gregson, also went to the packers' - I have got all the stock in my possession, except what I sent to the packers' - (looking at some shawls) - I cannot say these are part of what I sold to Messrs. Gregson, but they are the same pattern, size, and texture of cloth, and are Chippendall's make - such shawls as these would go into Phillips' hands.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Do you know the manufacturer? A. Yes - he is here; I have not sold a piece of this description since October or November last; I sold some of these patterns about that time - but here is one pattern which never was sold; I believe it was only printed eight or nine months ago. Previous to November last I may have sold three or four hundred pieces of some patterns - I will not positively swear none of this particular pattern have been sold.
 Mr. LAW. Q. Were any of the shawls at all sold for home consumption? A. None; the former ones all went to Phillips, except eight pieces, which were sent to Messrs. Goldsmidt, about two years ago; the barder of this one is a new pattern - I believe I never sold one of that pattern; the others have not been made more than two years and a half.
   JOHN CHIPPENDALL. I am in partnership with others. at Manchester. We manufactured these shawls - this one is of a particular pattern, which we have altered this year - we first sent some of them to town about six months ago; I had sent none to town in October last; the calico is made expressly for us, and is of a thicker texture than usual; we have sold two other parcels of this new pattern, for shipping, besides what we sent McVicar, and we have made presents of five or six single shawls, but those were all 8-4ths, and these are 6-4ths. McVicar is the only agent I employed for the sale of the 6-4ths.
