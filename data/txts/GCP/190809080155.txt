(Thursday, September 17.)
George DEARSON, Church Street, Plaistow, carman and contractor.
As the latter end of June Wood called upon me to see if I had re-
ceived two bales of South African fodder which his firm, Campbell,
Phillips, and Co., were sending me as a sample. I said I had not
received them. He told me the firm were sending out samples to
borsekeepers to try and sell the stuff. On July 2 he called again and
I told him I had received the two bales. On July 9 he called and
asked me if I could cart some fodder for Campbell and Phillips from
the wharf, which I agreed to do at 28. 6d. a ton. On July 5 or 6
Wood called with French (Mann) whom he introduced to me. French
said he had opened a place in St. Mary's Road, and there would be a
lot of carting for him at the same price as I was doing it for Campbell
and Phillips. On July 6 I carted 150 bales from St. Mary's Road by
my carman Pantling -I could not say whether this was from St.
Mary's Road or Leytonstone Road. On July 9 Wood and French
again called and ordered me to send five vans to Bond's Wharf, which
I did on the 10th and 11th. I received delivery order produced from
Wood. Two of Watkins's vans were sent. On the 13th I sent vans to
