Defended.

Company as foreman of the yard of the Windsor Castle, Hammersmith-

up to 11th June last--all our omnibuses are ear-marked in that way-
lith June I had a report from Mr. Hare and directions to suspend bim-
I showed Ayckbourne the report a few days after I received it--Mr. Hare
is the road manager of my district.
