Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. Were you the constant com-
panion of the unfortunate woman? A. I was, we were great friends that
was known to her husband--when I came in and found her in this state of
pain I was of course a good deal excited-I got a bottle of hot water to put
to her stomach-1 had a young baby with me, and I put it in bed by her
side, and she wished me to come to bed with her --I had been with her before
when she was poorly-she seemed very anxious for me to do everything I
could for her went into my own house and fetched the hot water for her
stomach.
