804. THOMAS BOND, stealing 1 watch, valge 158., and 5 shillings;
the property of Joseph King, his master.
JOSEPH KING. I am a turner, and live in Bartholomew-close. The
prisoner has been my errand-boy about eighteen months-in consequence of
something that had happened, I left five marked shillings on a window stool
on 11th Aug.-I watched the prisoner-I saw him come in the room; he
had to go to a sink we have in the back parlour, to draw a pail of water-
he looked round, and saw the five shillings by the side of the cash-box-he
set the water running-he then looked in the front shop-he then made a
grab, and took part of the money-he went and turned the water off, and
made a second catch at it-he then went and drew about a pint of water and
made another snatch at the money-I could not see what he took each time-
be then took the pail away, and closed the door- I went to the spot, and the
money was all gone-I went and spoke to the policeman-the prisoner did
wbat be bad to do, and went out to go home for the night--the policeman
took him, and found on him the five shillings, and my watch which I had
missed about a week before-this is it-I had named it in the shop.
Prisoner. It is the first time; I beg for mercy.
WILLIAM SHAW (City-policeman, 224). I took the prisoner to the station,
I found on him these five shillings-this watch was in the leg of his trowsers,
fastened to the bottom of the trowsers by this chain,
Guilty. Aged 15.-Confined Seven Days and Whipped.
