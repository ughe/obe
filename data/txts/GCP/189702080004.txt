THOMAS CHOPPING. I am assistant to my father, a furniture-dealer, at
64, Mare Strzet, Hackney-on December 31st, between 8.30 and nine,
the prisoner came by himself and purchased a chest of drawers, a carpet,
and a rug, which came to £4 158.-he offered in payment this cheque,
which was endorsed --he said his name was Burnari-I told him we
would give him the difference when the cheque was paid-I paid it into
my bank, and it was cleared in the ordinary way - next saw the prisoner
on Saturday, January 2nd, two days after-on that day we knew the
cheque was paid, and we delivered the goods at 224, Cambridge Road-I
sent the balance of money with the goods, but the prisoner was not in,
and our man brought the money back—shortly after the prisoner came,
and I gave him the difference.
Cross examined. The first day he came was Thursday-I paid the
cheque into my bank next morning.
Re examined. I did not see the prisoner on Friday, January 1st.
JAMES CHOPPING. I am the last witness's brother-I delivered the
furniture at 224, Cambridge Road, on January 2nd.
WILLIAM AUGUSTUS PEARCE. I live at 224, Cambridge Road, Bethnal
Green-on December 31st the prisoner called, and wanted to take two
rooms, as he had found employment in the City as a general warehouse-
man, and he wanted to bring his wife from the country to live there-I
had two rooms to let-he told me his name was Barnard--I let him the
rooms-on Saturday, January 2nd, some furniture was delivered there-
I saw the prisoner that day, before and after the furniture was delivered
the first time he asked if the furniture had arrived I said, "No"-he
said, "I will go and see about it ”_while he was away the furniture
arrived-when he came back I told him it had arrived, and he said, "I
will go out, and after a while come back and lay the carpet "--when he
came back the second time he said he did not require the rooms any
longer; he had taken two more that were more convenient, and if I did
not mind could he take the goods away-he paid me 6s. for the rooms-
my suspicious were aroused--the goods were taken away by a man with a
barrow, who was outside-I went to Mr. Chopping-I sent my son to
follow the barrow.
