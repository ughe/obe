   Cross-examined. Q.  Do you know what the "22" indicates on the cask of vinegar? A.  It indicates, if we have it from respectable houses, that it is 22 strong—the 22 is frequently marked on something under 22, that we may not show we are selling 16 vinegar we put 22 on it—I have it from respectable houses—I never buy of cheap houses—I spiled it and showed the vinegar to Mr. Stevens—I had nothing to test the strength of it. WILLIAM HENRY STEVENS. I am a grocer and cheesemonger, and live at Barking, in Essex. About the 11th of April I bought this half-hogshead of vinegar of Mr. Sandifer for 30 s ., in a regular way of business—it was found at my place.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Was it worth more than 30 s .? A.  A regular vinegar-house traveller called on me, and I told him what I gave for this, and I bought a 20 vinegar better than this was—I am not a judge, but according to my judgment, I did not consider it anything like the quality—there was a name on the cask. THOMAS PRITCHARD( police-constable P  22.) I was looking out for the prisoner at the latter end of April—he was taken on Sunday, the 4th of May, at his own house—I told him it was on a charge of stealing a cask of vinegar from Collingridge and Davis—he said he could explain the matter, he had received an order from Mr. East, of Goldsmith-row, when it was sent there Mr. East did not want it, he had then bought it of Mr. East, and sold it to Mr. Sandifer.
   COURT to LOUISA EAST . Q.  What name does your husband go by? A.  He is generally known by the name of Thomas. NOT GUILTY.
