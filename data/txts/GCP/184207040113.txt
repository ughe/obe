Prisoner's Defence. I owed 78. 6d., and the person said he would
summon me: I asked Mrs. Ross for 58., and the bed, and I pledged it for
88.; I told Mrs. Driscoll that my aunt had a bed in pawn for 8s. I did
not think she would ever release it; if she would give me 5s. for the
duplicate, she might get it back again; I said I would make an affidavit;
she said if I did, she would take the bed out; I got it, and she released
it; they have sworn they do not know me; Mrs. Driscoll has known me
