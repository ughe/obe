Carey, in his defence, stated that he was walking home with the prisoners,
and told them they could sleep at his house that night. Anderson said that
Carey said he might sleep on the floor at his house. Cronin said that
Carey said he might sleep in his house, and that, hearing a constable coming
upstairs, and saying he should charge him (Cronin) with attempted
burglary, he squeezed himself into the chest of drawers. Thompson said that
he went into Carey's house to sleep.
GUILTY, CAREY ** then PLEADED Guilty to a conviction of felony
at this Court in January, 1895, and ANDERSON*** to.a conviction
of felony in September, 1894.
Police officers stated that the prisoners were a terror to the neighbourhood.
that they did no work, but associated together, and with a terrible gang
of thieves, that Thompson had only latterly been associated with the
others, and three years ago bore a good character.
