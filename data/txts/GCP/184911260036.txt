26. MARTIN CONNOR, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 38. 6d.; the
goods of William Edward Saker, from his person : having been before
convicted.
JOSEPH HEDINGTON (City-policeman, 20). On 30th Oct. I was on Lon-
don-bridge-I saw the prisoner go behind Mr. Saker and take a hand-
kerchief from his pocket- I told him, and took the prisoner to the station-
I found another handkerchief between his legs.
WILLIAM EDWARD SAKER. I was on London-bridge-I had a handker-
chief in my pocket about an hour before this handkerchief has every
appearance of being mine-I have no mark on it.
MICHAEL HAYDON (City-policeman, 21). I saw the prisoner take the hand-
kerchief, and put it under his coat.
