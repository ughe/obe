2313. SUSAN EUSTACE was indicted for stealing, on the 26th
September, 1 smock-frock, value 28., the goods of David Hopkins.
SOPHIA Hopkins. I am the wife of David Hopkins, of Burn-street
Marylebone. The prisoner is my sister-in-law-we were removing, a
Monday the 26th of September, and she wished me to let her help me,
account of my giving her her breakfast, as sbe was so distressed-I missed

-I told her to give me the duplicate, and I would get it out-I did not

Prisoner. I did it through distress.
