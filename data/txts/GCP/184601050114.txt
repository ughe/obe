HENRY BRADLEY (police-constable R 250.) I went to Mr. Lovegrove's
house on the Sunday morning-I had seen him on the Saturday afternoon
I received this crown from his servant-I showed it to Mr. Lovegrove at the
station-he said it was the one he received-it has been in my possession ever
since.
PAMELA REBECCA BROWN. I am sister to Gregory Brown, who keeps
the Star public-house at Woolwich-on the 20th of Dec. I saw the prisoner
about five o'clock in the afternoon at my brother's house-he had a glass of
port, which came to 4d.-he paid me with a 58. piece-I gave him change, a
half-crown, two shillings, and two pence-he asked me if I would let him
have a bed - I told him I could, by his paying for it then--he looked nearly
the same as he does now-his face was not the same as it is now-he had
an imperial, and his coat was buttoned ap- I kept the crown in my hand, bit
it, and gave it to my brother-he took it in his hand, and went out of the
house--the prisoner was brought back in a short time by my brother--we then
sent for the policeman-the policeman handed me the crown-I again marked
it- this is it-here are the marks on it.
GREGORY BROWN. I received a crown from my sister on the 20th of Dec.
- I went after the prisoner and overtook him in a minute or two-I said the
erown was a bad one-he said he was very sorry and he would change it-I
said I should thank him to come back with me-he came back voluntarily
I asked him if he had come down to Woolwich on business, he said, "No"-
I asked him if he was known there-he said, "No"-1 sent for an officer and
gave him into custody- I gave the crown to the officer--my sister marked it-
it was then about five o'clock or after five-the distance from Greenwich to
Woolwich is hardly three miles.
JOHN WALKER (police-constable R 298.) I produce this crown piece which
I got from Miss Brown-I took the prisoner, I found on him one half-crown,
two shillings, and two pence, and there was a half-sovereign laid on the counter
which was pointed out as his money-I did not find it on his person.
GREGORY Browy re-examined. The prisoner produced the half-sovereign
to change the bad 58.-piece with
Mr. John Field. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint-these crowns
are both of them counterfeit, and both cast in the same mould.
Prisoner's Defence. With respect to the second charge, I plead guilty, but
of the other charge I solemnly declare I am innocent; for private reasons I
have not communicated with my friends.
Guilty. Aged 23.-Confined Twelve Months.
