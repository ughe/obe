2555. JOSEPH ELMES was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of July,
1 watch, value 21. ; I guard, 1s. ; and 1 key, 3d.; the goods of Thomas-
Cross.
Mary Ann Cross. I am the wife of Thomas Cross, of Woolwich-the
prisoner lodged with us, I saw this watch on the mantle-shelf on the evening,
of the 28th of July, and missed it at half-past seven o'clock next morning-
the prisoner had access to the room.
CHARLES SELLER. I am shopman to a pawnbroker, at Woolwich-on the
29th of July, in the morning, the prisoner pawned this watch in the name of
Grosvenor-I am quite sure of him-he came for a further advance on the
Monday, and I gave him in charge.
Thomas THOMPSON. I took the prisoner in charge-he said he had
bought the duplicate of another man.
Guilty. Aged 23.-Confined One Year.
