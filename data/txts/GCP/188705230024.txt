632. GEORGE DOWDEN was indicted for a libel on William
Hancock.
MR. SAUNDERS Prosecuted; MR. BAKER Defended.
The libel charged the prosecutor with seducing a girl of 14, who afterwards
became the prisoner's wife, and the RECORDER considered that as no plea of
justification was pleaded, there was no answer to the charne.
GUILTY. Recommended to mercy by the Jury. - To enter into his owon
recognisances in 501. to come up for judgment if called upon, and to keep the
peace for twelve months.
633. FANNY DOWDEN PLEADED GUILTY to a like offence.
To enter into recognisances in 201. to appear and receive judgment if callad
upon, and to keep the peace for twelve months.
