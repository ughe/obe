1340. JOHN NOREY was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of Mark.
2 pair of trowsers, value 8d., the goods of Bernard Hegan; and that he had
been before convicted of felony.
ELIZABETH HEGAN. I am the wife of Bernard Hegan, and keep
clothes shop at Holloway.--I had these trowsers safe inside the shop between
six and seven o'clock on the 28th of March.
John Wright (police-constable N 127.) I stopped the prisoner in the
Liverpool-road with the trowsers under his arm between eight and nite
o'clock on the 28th of March-he said a boy gave them to him.
Prisoner's Defence. A boy said he had them given to him, and asked me
to carry them.
John PAGAN (police-sergeant N 19.) I produce a certificate of the pi
soner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office-read)-the
prisoner is the person who was convicted.
Guilty. Aged 16.- Transported for Seven Years.
1341. WILLIAM HOLMES was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of
March, 1 coat, value 168., the goods of William Stevenson,
WILLIAM STEVENSON. I live in Brewer-street, Golden-square. On the
evening of the 11th of March I went into the Marquis of Granby publis
house, and I placed my great coat on the table-I missed it about ten minutes
after--the prisoner had been sitting near it--this is it--I never saw him
before.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. I believe there were a great many
other persons there? A. Not at the time it was taken-- I think only the
prisoner and another man.
CATHERINE KEEFE. I was in this public-house. I saw the coat folded
up on the table-there was a young man sitting on the side of the tablete
prisoner took up the coat, put it on his right arm, and walked out of the
back door.
Cross-examined. Q. Did he come in again soon after ? 4. Yes, and
given into custody.
JOHN HOWARD (police-constable P 85.) I took the prisoner--he said be
would leave 5s. in the landlord's hand and bring the coat back in five minun
-I said if he would allow me to go with him I would let him go, but be
would not.
RICHARD SAYERS. I produce the coat which was pawned on the 14th &
March by a man, but not the prisoner.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 21.- Confined Two Months.
