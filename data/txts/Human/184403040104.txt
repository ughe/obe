 991. CHARLES HAYES was indicted for stealing 1 shirt, value 5 s  ., the goods of William Wilkinson . ANN WILKINSON. I am the wife of William Wilkinson, of Hornsey-road. About three o'clock in the afternoon of the 29th of Feb. I missed this shirt from my garden—it is my husband's. JOHN MACKENZIE BRUCE, shopman to Mr. Briggs, pawnbroker, Chichester place.  The prisoner came to our shop and offered this shirt in pledge on the same day—I stopped it. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three months .
 992. SARAH YOUNG was indicted for stealing 1 cloak, value 6 s  .; 1 frock, 6 d  .; and 1 pair of stockings, 6 d  ., the goods of George Frederick Lam bert ; and that she had been before convicted of felony. GEORGE FREDERICK LAMBERT, pawnbroker, Old-street, St. Luke's.  I was in the shop on the 26th of Feb.—I received information, and saw the pri soner about twenty yards from the shop—I stopped her and asked her what she had got—she dropped this cloak, and two other articles.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  These things could be taken without coming into the shop? A.  They must have come to the door—there was another woman with her—I am sure the things dropped from the prisoners and the other woman had left her. TIMOTHY MC CARTHY( police-constable H  75.) I produce a certified of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark'soffice—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined one year
 993. ELIZABETH REILEY was indicated for stealing 2 gowns, value 5 s  .; 1 cloak, 9 s  .; 1 apron, 1 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 jug, 4 d  ., the goods to Thomas Doolen . MARY DOOLEN. I am the wife of Thomas Doolen, and live in Ogle-street. On the 9th of Feb. the prisoner come to nurse my husband, who was ill, and came into the room—I was absent ten minutes—when I came back I missed my dress off the door, and a lace dress—she took a jug to go for some beer, and did not return. THOMAS CROSSLEY, pawnbroker.  I produce two gowns, pawned by a female—the officer afterwards brought these duplicates, which I had given to the person who pledged the dresses.
