CHARLES BUTCHER (Detective Officer C). I had a warrant handed to me
in August, and I endeavoured to find the prisoner-I made every inquiry
at the West End, where he was well-known, and at the Horse Reposi-
tories, and Queen Square-I continued to endeavour to find him up to
November I took him into custody the early part of November-I told
bim it was in reference to a cheque for 31. 10., in Down Street-he said
Adams would settle the matter-I know Adams-the last time I saw him
was in Piccadilly-I have uot seen him since a fortnight before the prisoner
was committed--I have seen the prisoner in Adams' company.
Cross-examined. The prisoner also said “When I gave Adams the cheque
I expected the money would be paid in."
Guilty.
