live in Horse Ferry-road. On the afternoon of the 20th of November I
was with James Jones in St. James's Park-I saw the prosecutor enter the
passage which leads to New-street, followed by Watts, Richardson, and
Batchelor -Richardson was close to his back, and Watts on the side of him,
and Batchelor by the side of Richardson-as they were going up the steps
into New-street I saw Watts drop the three keys, which I now produce,
from his left-hand-I picked them up-he was then close by the prosecur
tor's side-when he lifted up his foot I took up the keys-the prisoners
turned-] was behind them, and they pushed me-I secured Watts, and
gave him to Jones then took Batchelor, and gave Richardson to a police-
