735. CAROLINE LUCAS (19) was indicted for a like offence.
MR. COLERIGE conducted the Prosecution.
DANIEL HUNTER. I keep the Exmouth Arms, Stepney-on 4th July
the prisoner came for a pint of half-and-half, and gave me a florin; I gave
her change, and she left-I put the florin in the till, there was no other
florin there-I afterwards gave it in change to a customer, who broke it
with his teeth and returned it-I did not keep the pieces-I am certain
the prisoner is the person-on 13th July she came again for a pint of
half-and-half, and gave me a florin, I broke it in two, and told her it was
the second time she had passed bad coin, and I should give her in charge
-she said that she was not aware it was bad, and had not been in the
house before the previous night.
Prisoner. I was never in your shop except on 12th and 13th July.
THOMas Cox (Policeman 187 K). I took the prisoner and received
this broken florin.
WILLIan WEBsTEr. This is a bad florin.
