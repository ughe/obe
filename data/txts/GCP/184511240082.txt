63. JANE HISCOT was again indicted for stealing 1 ring, value 11. 108.;
2 salt cellars and stands, 12s. ; 1 butter dish, 38.; and 2 egg cups, 1s. : the
goods of Caroline Mary M'George, her mistress.
MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
ELLEN Price. I found the duplicate of this ring in the prisoner's box-I
took it out of pawn at Mr. Thomson's, in Sloane-street.
WILLIAM CORNISH (police-constable B 35.) I found a box at Knights-
bridge barracks, in possession of Mrs. Etheridge-I found all these other
things in it, which I produce.
ELIZABETH ETHERIDGE. I am the wife of a corporal at Knightsbridge
barracks--I know the prisoner-she had been confined, and she asked me to
take the baby and this box which she should not want-these things were
in it.
CAROLINE MARY M'George. I am a widow, and live in Wilton-crescent
-I keep a school--the prisoner was in my service as cook-I discharged her
three years ago next January--this is my ring-it is an old family ring, with the
name of " James James" on it, and the date-I missed it-these are my salts
- they are very old fashioned-this is my basin-the stand and the top of it
are not here, but I lost them.
Prisoner. The ring was given to me by a lady at her school - I never saw
these glass articles in her house - I bought them.
GUILTY.

64. JANE HISCOT was again indicted for stealing 1 neck-chain, value
21.; and 1 fan, 1s. ; the goods of Emily Meikham.
Mr. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
EUPHRASIA Howarth. I live in Wilton-crescent. The prisoner lived
servant with me, and left me last May-this neck-chain and fan belong to
my niece, Emily Meikbam-she is eight or nine years old--they were given
to her by her mother, who is in the West Indies.
Prisoner. That chain never was in my box-the fan was given to me by
the coachman.
ELLEN PRICE. I found the duplicate of this chain in the prisoner's box-
it was in pawn for 15s.- I took it out of pawn-I had never been in Mrs.
Howarth's service, and had no opportunity of taking it.
Prisoner. I have to beg for mercy; I have been accused shamefully wrong.
Guilty. Aged 25.- Transported for Seren Years.
