Finsbury Pavement. He arrived at 2.15 p.m. I asked if his furni-

housekeeper had told me I asked him whether he was engaging any
body else 1 He said yes, there would be several 'juniors. He pro-

Not hearing from him I went to the offices, and there I met three
other young men in the same position as myself. I have not seen
the prisoner since, nor received any money or further instructions
