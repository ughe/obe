1239. EDWARD WEST was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of
May, 1 cask, value 88., the goods of Thomas Garrett.
Thomas GARRETT. I am a cooper, and live in St. Mary-at-Hill. I had
a brandy cask in the cellar, which I saw safe at half-past seven o'clock
I did not see it taken, but I missed it about a quarter of an hour after-
wards--I saw it afterwards at Mr. Voller's, on St. Martin's-hill-I can
swear to it, having coopered it mysell-my cellar doors were open.
HENRY HELLETT. The cask was brought to Mr. Voller's by the pri-
soner-he asked me to buy it-I said I could not do so without Mr. Yol-
ler's approbation, and he was absent-the officer then came in and took
him into custody.
Prisoner. I hope you will be merciful, I have a wife and two children.
Guilty. Aged 24.-Confined Three Months.
