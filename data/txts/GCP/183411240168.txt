WINCHESTER, Mayor.
WILLIAM BRIER. I was at the Ram, at Smithfield-I owed Mr. Wes-
ver some money-Storey came and brought this note (read)—"Mr. Bryer,
please to send the bill and the money by the bearer. Mr.Weaver, Lower-
street, Islington." I detained Storey, and went and took the prisoner.
THOMAS WEAVER. I live at Islington. This is not my writing.
GUILTY.-Confined Six Months.
