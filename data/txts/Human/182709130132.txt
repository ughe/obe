 1900.  JAMES LANGHAM was indicted for feloniously assaulting William Cormack , on the 22d of July , at St. James, Westminster, putting him in fear, and taking from his person, and against his will, 1 watch value 5l.; 1 seal, value 10s.; 1 key, value 7s., and 1 chain, value 3s., his property.
 WILLIAM CORMACK. I am a  coach-painter. I was with my wife on the 22d of July, at the Hampshire Hog, public-house, at the corner of Kent-court, Betwick-street, between ten and eleven o'clock at night; we had been there about a quarter of an hour - I went in with my wife and a friend - I was quite sober, and had my watch safe. I saw the prisoner there when I went in - I knew him before - I had no conversation with him; two other men were drinking there with him - the prisoner kicked up a row with my friend. I prevented their fighting, and he gave me a violent blow on the head, got me against the wall, got his head in my stomach, and at the same time drew my watch from me - I could not make resistance - I was down in the corner; my wife went to the door to call Watch!
 Q. Was he ill-using you at that time? A. Yes - he gave me a violent blow, in fact we were fighting - he went away. I have not found my watch - the officer did not know of it for about nine days.
 Prisoner. We were drinking there - he came and struck me first. Witness. I did not, he was quarrelling with Gillagan.
   ELIZABETH CORMACK. I am the prosecutor's wife - I was with him at the Hampshire Hog. I saw the prisoner knock my husband down. I went to the door to call Watch! and Murder! I saw the prisoner come out with the watch and chain - I could swear to the chain. I begged of him not to rob me; he gave me a push against the wall, and ran off - I cannot exactly say which way, as I was confused with the blow.
 Prisoner. Q. As I was going out, the watchman caught hold of me? A. I cannot say whether he had him then, as I was so much hurt with the blow.
   EDWARD GILLAGAN. I was at the Hampshire Hog, and saw the prosecutor and his wife there. I saw Cormack had his watch in his fob when he came in - I saw the chain and seals. I did not see the prisoner take it.
   THOMAS GOOK. I am an officer. I received a description of the prisoner, and took him twelve days after the robbery, on the Sunday fortnight. I told him it was for a piece of work in a public-house, and then said it was for robbing a man of his watch; he said he had not been in the house at all - I took him to the watch-house. I put him with a number of other prisoners, and took the prosecutor there - he clapped his hand on his shoulder, and said "This is the man;" and wanted to know where the property was - the prisoner said, "If I tell you, you will prosecute me all the same;" he then said, if I went to his father's, I should hear of the watch - I went there, but his father denied it; a man was charged with being with him, but there was nothing against him. The prosecutor told me of the robbery on the same night, but I could not find the prisoner.
   HENRY HOWLETT. I am a watchman. I was in Berwick-street, and was called; the prisoner was coming out at the door. I seized him, and asked if he was the man; the landlord came up to me, and said "Come and clear the house." I let him go to clear the house - the watch had not been named at that time. I saw Mrs. Cormack, but she did not tell me of it till I had let the prisoner go. I then cleared the house.
 ELIZABETH CORMACK. I was so confused with the blow, I could not mention the watch at first.
   ELIZABETH WAITE. I am landlady of this house. I saw all these people there that evening. I heard a great riot, and wanted the watchman to come to restore quietness.
     GUILTY- DEATH. Aged 19.
 1901.  ESTHER MURRELL was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of September , 2 coats, value 4l.; 1 waistcoat, value 1l.; 1 shirt, value 2s.; 2 handkerchiefs, value 1s.; and 1 snuff-box, value 4d., the goods of John Hammond , in his dwelling-house.
 JOHN HAMMOND. I live in  Old Burlington-mews, Regent-street, and am a groom; it is master's house - I live in it alone; it is provided for me. On the 13th of September, between eleven and twelve o'clock at night, I took the prisoner home to sleep with me - I awoke between two and three, and missed her and this property, which was safe overnight. I cannot swear to the prisoner being the woman - I gave her some gin; we went to several public-house - but she had her veil down. I did not swear to her before the Magistrate - I will not swear to her; I had no candle in my room.
   JEREMIAH RANDALL. I am a watchman of St. James'. On the morning in question I stopped the prisoner in Regent-street, with a bundle containing these articles - she said she got them from a man she had been sleeping with; and was going to take them to her brother's - that he gave them to her instead of money; I found the prosecutor at Marlborough-street, in the morning.
   JOHN VIRGO BUCKLAND. I received the bundle and the prisoner in charge, about two o'clock that morning.
   JOHN HAMMOND. These are my property - I had the coat and waistcoat that night; I did not authorize her to take them.
 Prisoner's Defence. If he is the man, he put the things on the table and told me to take them; he had given me a great deal of brandy.
 GUILTY. Aged 32.
 Of stealing only. - Transported for Seven Years.
