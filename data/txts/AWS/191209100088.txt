HAROLD HARDY, officer at the fire brigade station at Stamford Hill.

Place at 5.57. I found three separate fires: one in the first-floor front
room, another in the back room, and a third in the eupboard on the
landing; I should say they had been burning about a quarter of an
hour.
Cross-examined. Only the stuff in the rooms was burning, not the
place itself, but the fire had caught the paintwork of the wall in the
back room.
