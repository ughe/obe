Before Edward Bullock, Esq., and the Fourth Jury.
320. JAMES LAYTON, unlawfully obtaining goods by false pretences.
Hugu TULLOCK, I am in the service of Leaf, Coles, and Co., Mr.
Leaf's name is William, On 29th Nov, the prisoner came to the entering-
room, and asked for some French merino for James Coster, of Aldermanbury,
who dealt with Messrs. Leaf, and I let him have seven and a half yards, at
41.9d., on that representation, worth 11. 15s. 7d.
HENRY DAUGHTON. I am in the service of Coster and Co.--the prisoner
was in their service, and left on 5th Nov.--he bad no authority to go and
represent as he did no one could give him authority but me.
Prisoner. Q. How long have you knows me? 4. Seven or eight years
I never knew you to be guilty of anything wrong--you bore a good character.
WILLIAM TRECASKIS (City policeman, 493.) The prisoner came and asked
me if I was on duty-I said, "Yes"-he said he wished to give himself up
for two or three cases of felony-I told bim to be careful what he said,
having the appearance of a respectable man--be said he had done it, and it
might as well come out at once-- he said at the station he had been to
Leaf, Coles, and Co., and obtained a short length of merino, representing
himself as coming from Coster. it
Prisoner. I admit that I am guilty, I have no defence to offer.
Guilty. Aged 29.- Recommended to mercy by the Jury.
