   Prisoner.  This man wanted to take very indecent liberties with me, and a young person who was with me said, "Don't have anything to do with that man." I never asked him for any gin; I went home to a gen tleman's residence in Gos well-street, and he gave me 4 s;.  I then went to have a cap of coffee; a female there came out with me, and the prosecutor asked me to go and have a drop of gin at a place where he said he had been on the Sunday morning. HARRIET HAYWARD. I searched the prisoner at the station—I found a shilling and a halfpenny in her stocking, and 1 s . 4 d . in her hand—she said she would have given the old man his 2 s . if it had not been for that b—r of an inspector.
   Prisoner.  I said I would sooner give him 2 s . than I would be locked up till Monday—you know that was it. Witness.  I have spoken nothing but the truth—she was dreadfully drunk, and the language she made use of to the inspector, and every body else was dreadful. GUILTY. Aged 28.— Confined Three Months.
 1922. BIDDY MURPHY was indicted for stealing, on the   8th of June , 1 1/2 yard of woollen cloth, value 12 s  ., the goods of Thomas Maloney . THOMAS MALONEY. I lodge in Henry-street, Hampstead-road. I hung a piece of woollen cloth on a line in the front kitchen, on the 8th of June—I missed it in the afternoon—this is it.
 THOMAS MANSELL. I am a pawnbroker, in Hampstead-road. I took in this cloth of the prisoner on the 8th of June. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Ten Days.
   Sixth Jury, before Edward Buttock, Esq.
 1923. WILLIAM STOKES was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of June , 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  ., the goods of Frederick Harris .
 ,  MARIA HARRIS . I am the wife of Frederick Harris; we live in Lisson Grove.
