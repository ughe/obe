Cross-examined by Mr. PARRY. Q. How long have you been in England!
A. I arrived in June, but I had been here before in Oct. --my family were
in the West Indies, but I was educated in England I was in the West
Indies six years, up to May, 1846-I was educated at Brompton-I did not
go to college-1 thought at one time of studying medicine, but I did not-1
did not follow any profession-I was on a plantation in the West Indies,
what we call a manager, or a land-steward-the accounts passed through my
hands-it was an estate of about 600 acres-the negroes were a part of my
management-it belonged to Mr. Benjamin Green I was paid about 100k
a year-I had sometimes more, sometimes less-I remember once working
for 501.-it was in the Island of St. Christopher-I mixed amongst the
planters, and occasionally visited Government-house-I left the West Indies
in May, 1846, and went to America-I came here in Oct., and then again in
Jane, to prove my father's will-I had given up my engagement, and was
gentleman about town, amusing myself in the metropolis, going occasionally
to the theatre and public entertainments—I lived with my friends, but I had
a private lodging I never was in a gambling-bouse-I had been at Cremore-
gardens once before I went merely to amuse myself I had not been in the
