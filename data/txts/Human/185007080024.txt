 1253. ANN MARIA TOLFREY , unlawfully obtaining 35 l  .; the moneys of William Henry Richardson , by false pretences.
   MESSRS. HUDDLESTON and O'BRIEN conducted the Prosecution. WILLIAM HENRY RICHARDSON. I am a shoemaker, at 19, Sun-street, Bishopsgate.
