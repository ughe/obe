   Before Mr. Recorder, and the First Jury.
 533. GEORGE WILLIAM DRAKE , JAMES WILLIAM DRAKE, GEORGE HOOK, and JOSEPH ANDREW LEWIS , unlawfully con spiring together to defraud Thomas Mackey . NOT GUILTY.
 534. HENRY HARRISSON , unlawfully assaulting Rahmet Alli , on the high seas.
 (  The RECORDER informed the JURY that the witnesses had gone abroad, and therefore there was no evidence against the prisoner. ) NOT GUILTY.
 535. THOMAS GLAISTER SHARPE , unlawfully assaulting Loodie and Sumshere , on the high seas.
 (  The RECORDER , under the same circumstances as in the last case, directed a like verdict. ) NOT GUILTY.
