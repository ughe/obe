MARY WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of March , 1 snuff-box, value 1 l  .; 2 seals, value 16 s  .; 1 pair of bracelets, value 2 s  .; 1 clasp, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 2 snaps, value 1 s  .; 1 cross, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 2 pencil-cases, value 10 s  .; 1 pair of ear-rings, value 3 s  .; 2 brooches, value 12 s  .; 3 shirts, value 7 s  .; 1 handkerchief, value 5 s  .; 1 watch-guard, value 1 s  .; 1 buckle, value 2 s  .; 1 eye-glass, value 6 d  .; 1 watch-key, value 6 d  .; and 2 seals, value 6 d  .; the goods of Thomas Na thaniel M'Phail . THOMAS NATHANIEL M'PHAIL. The prisoner was servant where I lodged—I have lost a quantity of jewellery—this is mine—( examining some. )
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Has not the greater part of it been restored by the prisoner's parents? A.  Yes—I am not aware that there is any more missing. SAMUEL BROOKS. I am apprentice to Mr. Chadwick, a watchmaker. I received this box as a present from the prisoner about five weeks ago she said it was her brother's, add he gave it her.
   Cross-examined. Q.  And she lent you a volume of Shakspeare, I be lieve? A.  Yes. THOMAS SILVESTER( police-constable F  103.) I took he—she said she had done wrong, and deserved to be punished.
 (The prisoner received a good character, and a witness engaged to main  tain her till she got a situation.) GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Nine Days.
 1272. SUSAN HARRIS , WILLIAM JONES, and WILLIAM HARRIGAN were indicted for stealing, on the   8th of April , 6lbs. weight of bacon, value 4 s  ., the goods of Henry Coe . HENRY COE. I am a cheesemonger, and live in Fitzroy-square. On the 8th of April, about half-past eight o'clock, these two pieces of bacon were taken from my shop. GEORGE ANTHONY. I saw the three prisoners in Marylebone-street standing at a cheesemonger's door, about ten yards from yards from Mr. Coe's—they then walked on to Coe's shop—Jones looked in several times, then walked in, and brought something out—Harris stood opposite, and then he went in—I missed Harris, and then I saw Jones give something to Harrigan—they walked on, and Jones whistled—Harris crossed, and tied up the bacon in a handkerchief—I got an officer, who took them.
   Jones.  He says he saw me go into the shop; it is false. Witness.  I have every reason to believe that the boys are the dupes of the female pri soner—I have seen her about.
   Harris's Defence.  I did not know the boys before; they gave me the bacon, and asked me to carry it home for them.
   HARRIS  — GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Three Months.
   JONES  — GUILTY. Aged 15.
   HARRIGAN  — GUILTY. Aged 14. Confined Nine Days and Whipped.
