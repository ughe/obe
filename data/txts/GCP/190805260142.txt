Detective-sergeant MARSHALL stated that prisoner was convicted in
1903 and sentenced to 12 months' imprisonment for long firm frauds
carried on at Leyton. Considerable doubt had been expressed as to
whether Wormald should not have been charged with conspiracy is
this matter (several jurors : Hear, hear) and it was by no means
certain that that course would not be adopted. Prisoner after his release
was for eight or nine months out of employment, and then became
agent for a firm of Belgian matmakers, subsequently commencing
business in Pelly Road.
Sentence, 18 months' hard labour.
