was called to St. Andrew's-terrace, Waterloo-road, and saw Trussell outside
No. 5, in a chair, and bleeding very much from a wound on his head-he was
insensible--a surgeon was sent for, and after some time he came to bimself-I
brought some women from the house No. 5-the prisoner was not one of
them, she made her escape from the back of the house before I was let in
she was not produced before the prosecutor that day at all.
