Mr. Muir went on to describe how after the last alleged illegal
operation proseoutrix went to visit a Mrs. Mabel Lynch in Prospect
Road. Whilst there she fainted and prisoner fetched her home.
She told him she was unwell and went to see Dr. Mackintosh. When
she returned she told prisoner Dr. Mackintosh had told her she
ought to be in bed, and prisoner told her she would not go to bed
there. Prosecutrix afterwards went to Dr. Mackintosh and made a
statement, and he, seeing the serious nature of it, called in another
doctor and had the statement attested.
Mr. Justice Bucknill asked if it would be fair to rely on suoh corro-

Mr. Muir said he might say at once that Mr. Bodkin and himself

they came unanimously to the conclusion that there was not.
Mr. Justice Buoknill. That settles the matter. His Lordship in
