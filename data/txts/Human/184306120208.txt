On the 10th of May I received information, and went to the prisoner's place in Somer's Town—I said I was a policeman, he must consider himself in custody for stealing two 20 l . notes and seven sovereigns—he said, "Do you mean to charge me with stealing?"—I said, "Yes"—he said, "When?"—I said, "On the 2nd of May"—he said, "That is right; I can prove I took the note on the 29th, from a gentle man to whom I sold a pony-chaise."

 1966. WILLIAM McNESS was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of April , 6 spoons, value 3 l  ., the goods of Thomas Quarterman . GEORGE KILNER. I am waiter at the York hotel, Bridge-street, Black-friars, kept by Mr. Thomas Quarterman. The prisoner came there on the 24th of April, between three and five o'clock, and had a glass of wine and a biscuit—he took his seat at a corner table—the dinner table was laid, and these six large spoons were on it.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  What do you know these spoons by? A.  By the mark of "N" on them, the initial of the late landlord of the hotel—I do not know what his name was—I do not know them by any other mark—they are the spoons that were used in the house. CHARLES WILLIAM PARR. I am in the service of Mr. Brown, a pawn broker, in Fetter-lane—these spoons were pawned there on the 24th of April, between four and six o'clock, by the prisoner—I am sure he is the person.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you say before the Magistrates, that he was the man who pawned them? A.  Yes—( looking at his deposition )—it was not stated that the prisoner pawned them—I have not said since I have been here that if any body would give me 3 l . I would go away—I have mentioned about 3 l .—I did not mark the words I said. JAMES TIDMARSH( police-constable M  51.) I produce these six spoons marked with a cross on the back, and the letter "N" in front—I found them at Mr. Quarterman's hotels—four at one hotel and two at another. GEORGE TOMKINS( police-constable M  166.) I received charge of the prisoner, on the 26th of May, for stealing plate. NOT GUILTY.
   Jury half Foreigners.
