1848. JAMES SAMS was indicted for embezzlement. KA
ELIZABETH SADLER. I am the wife of George Sadler, of Queen'e-mewa,
Pimlico. On the 18th of March the prisoner came with some beef-I paid
him 6s. 50.--I do not know who for- I had never seen his master.
Cross-examined by Mr. WYLDE. Q. Have you a book in which the items
are marked down ? A. No-1 had a ticket, but no receipt.
James Cowelt. I am a butcher. The prisoner was in my service, and
it was his duty to pay me the same day as he received money-I sent him
with some meat for Mr. Sadler, on the 18th of March, which came to 6s. 5d.
- he did not bring back the money-it was his duty to do so..
Cross-examined. Q. Was not he in the habit of taking out meat to sell to
customers, which he himself only knew? 4. No-he ordered meat, and
booked it to Mrs. Sadler-1 did not know her then--he was not in the habit
of taking out meat, selling it, and giving part of the profits to me---my wife
received 21. odd from him on the 22nd of March-previous to this I had no
customers in the Palace till he got them--I knew that he had one there of
the name of Bloss-he used to receive the money from them, and account to
me for it.
JANE COWELL. I am the wife of James Cowell. I received 21. 58, from
the prisoner, for a bill he had taken in, but not from Mrs. Sadler,
Cross-examined. Q. What is that book in your hand ? 4. The book in
which I book the meat to my customers-here is the account of 21. 58.-- the
prisoner was not in the habit of taking out meat and selling it on our account
until he got the order-he did not bring us many customers-on the 18th
he said that he should leave us I had had no settlement with him after
the 18th-I asked him who was to pay the remainder of the money which
ne took out meat for-it amounted to 21. 138. 3d - he put it down to Mr.
Sadler, and others.
