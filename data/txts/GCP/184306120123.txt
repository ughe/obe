1832. JAMES BRISTOW was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of May,
41lbs. weight of sugar, value 11. 88., the goods of Richard Pollard ; and that
he had been before convicted of felony,
RICHARD POLLARD. I am a grocer, and live in Union-street, Spitalfields.
On the 13th of May I had a lump of sugar safe about seven minutes before I
missed it—this is it—I know it because the paper is torn at the top-I swear
to it-it weighs 41lbs. 2oz.
Cross-examined by Mr. Payne. Q. Did you ever weigh it? A. Yes,
before I lost it.
William Day DaviES (police-constable H 36.) I stopped the prisoner
with this sugar, in Dorset-street, about a hundred yards from the prosecutor's
-he threw it down, and ran about twenty yards-I ran after him.
Cross-examined. Q. Where did you stop him? A. In Fashion-street,
the next street to where I saw him-I am quite sure I saw him throw it down
-it was between eleven and twelve o'clock at night. A
