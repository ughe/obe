1173. LETITIA JAMES was indicted for stealing 1 pair of scales, value
48.; and 2 weights, 4d.; the goods of Robert Hann.
LYDIA HANN. I am the wife of Robert Hann; we live at Woolwich.
We had this pair of scales safe in the morning of the 20th of April - I missed
them in the afternoon-these are them.
GEORGE DIACOAID. I purchased these scales of the prisoner.
Cross-examined by MR. MELLOR. Q. Do you know she is a married
woman? A. I did not know it till after I purchased them I have seen her
husband since-I never bought anything of her before.
GUILTY. Aged 47.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Two Months.
1174. JAMES DOUGLASS was indicted for stealing 2 purses, value 78.
1 groat, 1 3d. piece, 1 2d. piece, and i 1d. piece, the property of Sarah
Long, from her person.
SARAH Long. I live at No. 22, Upper Thames-street-on Sunday, the
3rd of May, I was on the pier at Geeenwich-in consequence of information
from the policeman, I searched my pocket, and missed two purses--one of
them had four coins in it, a groat, a three-penny, a two-penny, and a penny.
piece-lhe other purse was empty-the prisoner gave up the steel purse when
he was charged-the officer then asked him for the other purse-he put his
left hand in his pocket and drew it out
Cross-examined by Mr. DOANE. Q. Are you married ? A. No- I know
these purses were safe two hours before-there was rather a crowd-the packet
had just come up, and the people were rushing to get on board-1 knew nothing
of it till the officer spoke to me-I was taking care of an invalid brother.
Joyn Evans (police-constable R 190.) I was on the floating-pier, af
Greenwich-I saw the prisoner in company with a boy--they were very busy
in the crowd-I watched them-I saw them close up behind the prosecutrix
and another female-they then went and sat on a seat at the further end of
the pier--I spoke to the prosecutrix, and then spoke to the prisoner- he gave
up one purse, and pulled the other out of his pocket-the coins have not
been found--I had seen the prisoner and the boy three-quarters of an hour
before in Greenwich College.
Guilty.* Aged 26.- Confined One Year.
