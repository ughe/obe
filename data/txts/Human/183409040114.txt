 1424.  GEORGE SMITH and GEORGE BREWER were indicted for stealing, on the 1st of August , 1 necklace, value 6s.; the goods of Henry John Northfield , from the person of Susannah Mary Northfield.
   WILLIAM BRITTLE. I am errand-boy to Mr. Northfield, in Kingsland-road. On the 1st of August, my master's - little girl was playing at the door - I saw the two prisoners Smith undid her necklace - I went to them - he then dropped it - Brewer picked it up, and put it on the child's hand.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1425.  GEORGE SMITH and GEORGE BREWER were again indicted for stealing, on the 1st of August , 1 shovel, value 2s.; the goods of Charles Campbell.
   WILLIAM BRITTLE. After these boys had taken the beads, I saw them cross the road to Mr. Campbell's oil shop - Brewer took the shovel from the private door - he gave it to Smith, and they walked away - I did not see them again till the next morning, when they went past - I told my master, and he gave them into custody.
   CHARLES CAMPBELL. I lost a shovel on the 1st of August - it has not been found - the prisoners were taken the next morning.
 SMITH -    GUILTY. Aged 15.
 BREWER -    GUILTY. Aged 14. Transported for Seven Years.
 1426.  MARY SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of August , 1 sovereign, and 1 watch, value 2l.; the goods and monies of Richard Doggett , from his person.
 RICHARD DOGGETT. I lodge in  James-street, Oxford-street. On the night of the 24th of August, I met the prisoner - she went with me to my lodging - we laid on the bed, and William Lawes, who is apprentice there, was in bed at the time - I undressed myself, and the prisoner was partly undressed - I then had a sovereign and a watch - I went to wind my watch up - I felt very sick - I put my watch into my pocket again - I then felt my sovereign there - the prisoner staid about twenty minutes - I then went to bed, and when I awoke in the morning, I missed my sovereign and watch - I went to Quebec-street, and found the prisoner - I gave her in charge.
 WILLIAM LAWES. I was in bed, and I got up and put on the prisoner's stockings - she was tipsy, and she let a sovereign fall - I asked her if it was hers - she said, "Yes," and I gave it her - I could not make the prosecutor understand any thing - when the prisoner was at the foot of the bed, she said, "I have got the young man's watch"- I cannot tell whether he gave her the sovereign.
   WILLIAM JOHNSON(police-constable D 62). I took the prisoner - she stated where she lived - I went there, but could not find the watch - the prisoner said she gave it to the landlord to keep - the landlord said he knew nothing of it, but he came to the office and brought his daughter, who gave the watch to the prosecutor.
 Prisoner's Defence. I do not know whether he gave me any thing - I was very much in liquor - I do not know what I did - I know one undressed me, and the other dressed me, and put me on the man's stockings.
   MARGARET GREGORY. The prisoner came to my house, and pulled out the watch - she said the man took her, and made her drunk, and she did not know what she did.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1427.  WILLIAM MURRELL was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of August , 1 coat, value 3l.; and 1 pair of trowsers, value 5s.; the goods of William Hurford , his master.
 WILLIAM HURFORD. I am a  tailor, and live in Bloomsbury- the prisoner worked for me - I missed a coat and trowsers on the 12th of August.
   WILLIAM COOMBES WATTS(police-constable S 170). I found this coat and trowsers on the prisoner, on the 12th of August, about five o'clock in the morning - I stopped him on suspicion - he said he was going to Norwich to get work, and he had bought the trowsers in Somers-town - I said I would go there, but in going he said it was no use keeping me in suspense, he would tell the truth - they were his master's.
 Prisoner. I was not in the habit of receiving my money every Saturday night, which forced me to do as I did. GUILTY. Aged 22. - Recommended to mercy. Confined Six Months.
 1428.  FREDERICK MICHAEL was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of August , 1 hat, value 7s., the goods of Simon Simmons.
 SIMON SIMMONS. I  deal in clothes, and live in Chapel-street, Edgware-road. On the 18th of August, I saw the prisoner walking about the whole morning, with a newspaper in his hand, and no hat on - about two in the day, when the shop was full, he came in - he pulled out a card, and said, "I am a merchant, and to-morrow morning I shall have a cargo of goods, come over, that will stock all Marylebone" - I said, "Do not bother me; what do you want?" - he said, "A white hat" - I showed him one - he said, "This will do; I will go and look in the glass" - I thought he was going into my room, but he ran off in a moment - my neighbour followed and took him.
   EDWARD ROBINS. I keep a public-house, next door to the prosecutor.
