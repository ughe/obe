52. MARK NICHOLAS POWLES (20), Feloniously aoknowledging
certain recognizance in the name of another person, he not privy or a
consenting thereto.
being
MR. SLEIGH conducted the Prosecution.
BARZILLAI WOoD. I am assistant clerk at the Worship Street Police
Court. On 18th July last there was a charge pending against two persons
named David Gould and Nathan Houltran for forgery-1 have not got the
charge sheet herethese (produced) are the original depositions in the case,
and were returned to this Court as such-I know it to be a fact, that those
persons were charged with a felony before Mr. Hammill, the presiding
Magistrate at Worship Street, on July 18th-that was the last examination
-they had been remanded from time to time-] believe an application was
made to admit them to bail-I was not present in the Court when any
person offered himself as bail-I Was in an adjoining room, where the
recognizances are always taken-nome one came into the office, and gave the
name of Thomas Powles, No. 6, Portland Terrace, Victoria Park Road, as
bail for David Gould and Nathan Houltman-I do not remember that
person-he described himself as a hosierI took the recognizances-the
bail was for 204. each prisoner-] stated the conditions of his recognizances
to that party in the usual mannerI first aaked him his name-he gave
me his name and address, which I entered in a bookI then told him that
he was bound in the sum of 201. on condition that David Gould, and also
the same sum of 201. that Nathan Houltman, should appear at the next
Session, at the Central Oriminal Court, to answer a charge of forgery-
I gave him at the same time a notice to that effect-he said he would
acknowledge himselfhe said he was content said he was to acknow-
ledge himself in the sum of 201. on condition that David Gould, and
a like sum of 201., that Nathan Houltman, should appear at the next Session,
at the Central Criminal Court, to answer a charge of forgery and then
he acknowledged himself-the recognizances were not made out at that time
-these (produced) are the recognizances that I returned to Mr. Clark at
this Court in that case, in the regular and ordinary way-they were made
out by me-they bear the signature of the presiding Magistrate, Mr.
Haumill, who heard the charge against Gould and Houltman.
