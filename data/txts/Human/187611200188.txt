 104. GEORGE PEARSON  (38), was indicted for feloniously killing and slaying Charles. Upsdell ; he was also charged on the Coroner's Inquisition with the like offence.
   MR. BESLEY and MR. HORACE AVOEY conducted the Prosecution; and MR. WILLOUGHBY the Defence . ALICE SPARSHOTT. I am the wife of Frederick Sparshott, and live at Hunston, in Sussex, next door to the deceased Charles Upsdell, his garden adjoining ours—it is separated by a high wall of Mr. Pearson's, and by a quick set hedge of Mr. Upsdell's, you can see through the hedge, I could not see into Pearson's garden—on Thursday afternoon, 26th October, I was in my garden and heard Pearson in Upsdell's garden—their gardens adjoined they run at right angles to mine, Upsdell's garden is on the right and Pearson's on the left—there is a high wall-between them—I heard both Pearson's and Upsdell's voices; I heard Pearson telling Mr. Upsdell "Your cows are in my garden "—Upsdell used bad language and said "I will do for you, and I will see it out Pearson "—I heard no more words, I heard blows, I could not see them, the wall separated us—the blows were in Pearson's garden—I saw them in Upsdell's garden and go towards Pearson's, and it was after that I heard the blows; I could not tell how many blows; there were more than two; they were heavy blows—I heard no voices at the time I heard the blows—I heard no more expressions after they left Upsdell's garden—the next thing I saw was Mr. Upsdell return to his own house by the hedge, the way he went; that was about a quarter of an hour or twenty minutes after—he appeared to walk very weak, and he was covered with blood—I did not notice his head, he had no hat or cap—he was not doing anything with his hands, only walking to his own door.
   Cross-examined.
