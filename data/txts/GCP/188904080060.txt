received any payment as payment from the Times ; what I did in taking
the proofs of witnesses was at the request of Mr. Soames, and I suppose
Mr. Soames does not intend to take my work for nothing-it
was not exactly in the expectation of a reward when Mr.
Soames asked me to do what I did; I did not care whether
I had money or not; I am expacting more than my money
