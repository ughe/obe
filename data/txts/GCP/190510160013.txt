NEW COURT, Tuesday and Thursday, October 17th and 19th, and
OLD COURT, Monday, October 23rd, 1906.
Before Mr. Recorder.
734. JOHN GEORGE ELLIS (50) PLEADED GUILTY to feloniously
forging and uttering an endorsement to a bill of exchange for £50, with
intent to defraud. It was stated that £35 would be recovered. Judgment
respited for that purpose. --And (735.) THOMAS CHAPMAN (25) to
feloniously marrying Elizabeth Ann Tapling, his wife being alive. It
appearing that in the event of his not being sentenced to imprisonment he
would rejoin the Army, and in consideration of Elizabeth Ann Tapling
not desiring him to be punished, the Court discharged him on his own
recognisances.
