SARAH JUDITH SUTTON. I am the niece of Edward Drury, who keeps the
Ship and Sun, in the Walworth-road. On 27th Dec., about nine o'clock in
the morning, the prisoners came together-Haws asked for a quartern of gin
I served her-she gave me a half-erown-I saw it was bad, cut it with a
knife, and took it to my aunt, who was in the parlour-when I came back
Brown was gone-Haws was taken into custody-the half-crown was given
to the officer by my aunt— I had noticed the prisoners in the shop, about eight
o'clock that morning.

ANN THOROGOOD. I searched Haws at the station, and found in her
pocket half-a-crown, 13. 4d. in silver, 4d. in copper, and a handkerchief
Brown was at the door-Haws bad not the handkerchief when she first came
in, but she said to Brown at the door, "Give me my pocket-handkerchief"
-Brown gave it her into her hand, and I found in it a bad half-crows-I
found on Brown a sixpence, and 3d. in copper,
CHARLES FURLIN. I was a policeman. On 27th Dec. Brown was given
into my custody she wanted to go to her attorney-I would not allow her
I took her to the station--Haws was there, and asked Brown to give her her
handkerchief-she did so on the way to the station Brown made several
attempts to drop it, but I would not allow her.
CALEB EDWARD POWELL. These two half-crowns are both counterfeit,
and cast in the same mould.
