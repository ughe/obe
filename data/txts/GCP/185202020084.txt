271. JOHN SMITH, for a like offence.
MESSRS, SCRIVEN and POLAND conducted the Prosecution.
SARAH SWINDLIS. I keep a chandler's shop, in Wellington-street, Black-
friars-road. On 15th Jan, the prisoner came for a quarter of an ounce of
tobacco- it came to 1d.-I served him, and he gave me a sixpence-1 gave
him 5d. in copper, and put the sixpence into the till -- there was no other
silver there-1 took it out in about half an hour, and found it was bad-
one had been near the till in the mean time I marked it, and put it back
again on 17th Jan, the prisoner came again- he looked through the window
-I knew him-he came in and asked for two eggs-he gave me a shilling-
I tried it by the detector-it was bad-I sent my little boy for a policeman,
and gave the prisoner in charge, with the sixpence and shilling- I had kept
the shilling in my hand the whole time.
JAMES BROWN (policeman, M 55). I took the prisoner on 17th Jan.-
Mrs. Swindlis gave me this shilling and sixpence.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These are both bad.
