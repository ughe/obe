Yes, as long as they were employed in the house, but not to carry work home—they had no authority to sell paper or prints, nor to carry them home—when I discharged Elizabeth Larkin she said I falsely accused her—that she was always very honest, and she and her husband called me all to pieces—I distinguish amongst this paper and prints some that the bad to work upon.

 1051. WILLIAM THOMAS PALMER was indicted for stealing 1 pair of boots, value 12 s  . 6 d  . the goods of Thomas Sanderson . THOMAS SANDERSON. I live in Barbican, and am a boot-maker. On the 21st of April the prisoner came to my shop—he did not buy any thing, but as soon as he was gone I missed a pair of blucher boots from the wall—these are them.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  There was something said about new footing some boots? A.  Yes, there was—he asked me for one of my cards, and while I was getting that I infer he took the boots—I am sure these are the boots. EDWARD PIGGOT( City police constable, No.  169.) I took the prisoner in Fore-street—I found one of these boots in each of his coat-pockets, and one of Mr. Sanderson's cards upon him.
