Cross-examined by MR. SERJRANT BALLANTINE. Q. Did you watch Mr.
Leach's house with Fearn A. No; I watched a man who was going to
his house, for perhaps twenty minutes—there were two parties who had told
Fearn they kuew nothing about it, and when they saw us they shifted and
went round another road; and I said, “We will see where those parties are
going;" one of them was Gutteridge-Fearu had told me what Gutteridge had
said-we waited twenty minutes; not longer--we waited so long, because they
would not go, and I wanted to see where they did go to; and when they
went, I went away.
