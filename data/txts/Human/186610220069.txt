   MR. POLAND . These returns are made with a particular object, which is to let the Stamp Office know the amount of duty to be paid. They accept this form year after year and accept these figures as correct, and receive payment upon their footing. The statute makes it necessary that the returns should be sent to the Stamp Office . MR. BARON PIGOTT. Yes, and the question is whether the statute is not a mode of requiring .
   MR. POLAND . It is not necessary there should be a requirement in writing . MR. BARON PIGOTT. There is no doubt that if they had said to the trustees or principals, "We shall not accept this without you bring your chief clerk," the chief clerk would have come in future .
   MR. SERJEANT BALLANTINE . It says, "sworn before me this/—day of/—Justice of the Peace for the County of  —." That is the requirement issued by the Stamp Office, and my friends are relying on it . MR. BARON PIGOTT. This is an old form, it is very common to strike out part .
   MR. SERJEANT BALLANTINE . This was struck out at the bank . MR. BARON PIGOTT . Because it was not done before a Justice of the Peace. I am not disposed to decide the matter myself, but as you are anxious to have it decided now, I will take an opportunity of consulting with my learned brother .
