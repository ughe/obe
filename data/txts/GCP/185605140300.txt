NOEL ANTONIE GAUTIER (through an interpreter). I am employed at the
Western Railway at Paris-it is my duty to place tickets on the luggage
that is registered to London. On 19th Jan, it was my duty to paste
tickets on the luggage, and I did so there was only me and the weigher to
do so I do not recollect pasting a ticket corresponding with this one (pro-
duced) I might possibly have pasted it, but I am not sure if this bag
(produced) had been brought, and registered for London, it would have
been my duty to have pasted a ticket on it-the remains of the ticket on
this bag correspond with the ticket I should have put on-I put a mark of
a red cross on registered luggage- I put the mark where it would show
most, on the front-here is the mark of the red cross on this bag - this is
the way in which I should make the cross-I cannot swear to this--this is
the way bill of that day, No. 13-I should enter the luggage that day-if
I were absent it would have been the weigher's duty to enter it I do not
know whether I was absent.
