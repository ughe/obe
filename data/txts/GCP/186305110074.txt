The prisoner Ramsey's statement before the Magistrate put in and read.
"I met Holden on Saturday morning between 7 and 8 o'clock; he told me
he wanted to buy some patent plate. I inquired what size; he said 131
by 84. I said, How many do you want l' he said, 'Two dozen or two dozen
and a half.' I then left him, and went into the warehouse, and cut twenty-
eight. I asked him if he was going to pay; he said, Oh yes, by all means.
I said it would come to 21. He said he had a 51. note, and would get it
changed, and believing him to be an honourable man, I cut the glass and put
it in a paper parcel, and delivered it to him with a promise that he would be
in in balf an hour, and pay for them. By that time our clerk would be
there I left him and went back to open our warehouse, and a police-
constable came and took me into custody. I bave nothing more to say."
