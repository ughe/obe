Cross-examined by MR. WILLIS. We got the order on 8th March-we
never went to any statement of claim-I should say the greater part of the
£70 costs was over the injunction, because the affidavits were made for
that—the motion for attachment was dismissed, and Hill had to pay the
costs, because he had stated that he had received £175 for the bill, and it
was afterwards ascertained that he had done nothing of the sort-about
£20 of the costs were for the application for attachment.
