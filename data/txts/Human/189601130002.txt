THE
 WHOLE PROCEEDINGS
 On the Queen's Commission of
 OYER AND TERMINER AND GAOL DELIVERY
 FOR
 The City of London,
 AND GAOL DELIVEEY FOR THE

 COUNCIL, PURSUANT TO WINTER ASSIZE ACT OF 1879,
