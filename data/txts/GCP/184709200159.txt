there sometimes--they said no; they had taken a lodging in Brompton.
Cross-examined. 0. Then you are not certain whether they lodged in

-the prisoner got his living as a baker-I have seen him in the neighbour-
hood for years--I have known him to live as a baker for years--we do some
times have a disturbance in the house, but we quiet it as soon as we can-it
does occur.
