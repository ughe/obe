Re-examined. It may have been two or three days before 26th July
that Mr. Furness asked me to go to the Court to listen to what happened;
it was not a fortnight-I have a list of the claims against Mr. Furness,
-Mr. Hardman's is among them.
WALTER FURNESS. I am a builder-up to a fortnight ago I lived at
136, London Road, Herne Hill-I know the defendant as manager to
Mr. Hardman, a builder-I used to occupy premises belonging to Mr.
Hardman in Atlantic Road, for which I paid $45 a year-I used to see
the defendent on the premises-I employ several men-I am learning
Jeffrey the business, and he attends to it when I am away-I have
some relatives at Hastings named Savage-I went to Hastings this year
with my wife and family and a servant girl, and stayed from July
9th to July 21st at Mrs. Churcher's, Worthing House, Church Street,
Hastings I did not come to town at all during that time-I was not at
my house in London Road on the morning of July 13, nor did I re-
ceive from the defendant that morning & judgment summons issued by
Hardman against me; I was in Hastings on the 13th, and had
breakfast with my wife and family, and Mrs. Churcher attended on us-
I saw my uncle that day at eleven o'clock-we walked on the Parade, and
then dined at a restaurant, then went on the pier and went to East-
bourne by boat, and came back without landing, and at half-past five
had tea at the Creamery-I was in Hastings that night and Tuesday
also I slept at Worthing House, and at 7.45 a.m. I was in bed-the
first I heard of the judgment summons was the notice of adjournment
which came by post-I think it was after that I saw Mr. Wilson and
asked him to do something for me.
Cro88-eramined. I am sure this document was the first intimation I had
of the judgment summons being granted-I had heard it mooted that
there was a judgment summons, returnable on 26th-I told him 80,
but I did not attend, as I had not had the summons--the County-cours
bailiff had not twenty judgment summonses against me in one year!
heard from one of my men that it was the 24th-the first I heard of 1
was the introducing the word official-I did not think it worth whileto go
or send to the County-court to know whether the summons was returnable
that day-I knew that personal service was required, and as I had not been
personally served my interest in the matter had not commenced-my
lodgings at Hastings cost £1 a week-I took a few pounds down
with me I was engaged in some building operations, and had about six
labourers working-I left money with Jeffrey, a lad of nineteen, to
whom I am teaching the business; it was not a cheque-he had a cheque
drawn by Mr. Smith, of Loughborough Road; that cheque was sent
down to me at Hastings, endorsed by me there, and paid into my uncles
account-Jeffrey had not got it when I left-he did not try to cash it,
to my knowledge-the weekly wages averaged about 358. each -Jeffrey
was responsible, in my absence, to see that the work went on-if I had
beon in London between the 9th and the 21st I should have gone round
to see how they were getting on-I got daily reports from Jeffrey-I have
not got his letters; it was no use keeping them-July 12th was Tuesday
will not swear that all this did not take place on Tuesday-if my servant
