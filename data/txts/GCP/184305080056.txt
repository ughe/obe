1468. CHARLES HERMITAGE was indicted for stealing, on the 8th
of May, at St. George, Hanover-square, 12 spoons, value 101., the goods of

1469. MARGARET TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
April, 24 yards of gambroon, value 168.; and 65 yards of printed cotton,
value 1s. 4d.; the goods of James Stephenson.
JAMES STEPHENSON. I am a linen-draper, and live in Sidney-place, Com-
mercial-road. On the night of the 28th of April I went to my shop door, and
observed the prisoner standing by the side of some remnants of print, rolling
her cloak up, and putting the remnants of print between the lining of it-I
watched her some time-she saw me looking at her, and pulled out one piece,
came and asked me to measure it, which I did, and said it came to 7%d.-she
paid me for it, and I afterwards saw her go and take three more remnants-
she then went to the window, threw her cloak over some goods which lay
there, and took a piece of gambroon-she could not see me, as I was behind
some goods-she went out, I followed, stopped her four or five doors off,
opened her cloak, and saw the gambroon-she dropped it on the pavement-
I took it up, took her back to the shop, and took the remnants of print out of
her cloak-she said she had not done it, and afterwards acknowledged it, and
begged forgiveness.
THOMAS SQUIRES. I took her in charge-she was sober.
00.
Prisoner's Defence. I was not sober.
Guilty. Aged 59.-Confined Eight Months.
