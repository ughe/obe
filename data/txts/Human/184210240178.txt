On the 10th of October the prisoner came to our shop to sell I silver fork—she said it was her own, that her name was Smith, and she lived at the first house over Blackfriars-bridge, on the right hand—I said that was a glass-manufactory—she said, "Yes"—I said I did not believe it was hers—she said she would go and fetch a person—she went away—I did not see her again till I was fetched to Mr. Worth's.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  HOW long was she in your shop? A.  Nearly half an hour—only one other person came in during that time. JOSEPH CLIFF( police-constable D  118.)
