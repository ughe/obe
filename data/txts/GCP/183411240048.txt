52. THOMAS JUSCAPE was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of
October, 2 sets of window blinds, value 123., the goods of Joseph Cooke.
JAMES SINGLETON. I am porter to Mr. Joseph Cooke, upholsterer,
New-street, Dorset-place. On the 25th of October, between nine and ten
o'clock in the morning, a person gave me information—I went out, and
saw a man running along the New-road with these blinds-I pursued-he
turned down Gloucester-place, and I lost sight of him-he threw dow
these window blinds, and I took them up they are my master's—the
prisoner was taken in a few minutes.
Cross-examined by Mr. PRILLIES. Q. Were they outside your door ?
As Yes I only saw the man's back.
