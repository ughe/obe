OLD COURT.--Monday, August 14th, 1871.
Before Mr. Recorder.
558. WILLIAM GODDARD (26), PLEADED GUILTY to stealing one
tankard, eighteen spoons, eighteen forks, and other articles, of Henrietta
Davidson, his mistress ; also to forging and uttering endorsements upon
two orders, one for 27., and one for 201., with intent to defraud-Six
Years' Penal Servitude. And 559. WILLIAM HENRY SMITH (17), to
feloniously forging and uttering a cheque for 101., with intent to defraud-
Twelve Months Imprisonment.
NEW COURT.-Monday, August 14th, 1871.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
560. CHARLES MARTIN (23), PLEADED Guilty to unlawfully having
counterfeit coin in his possession, with intent to utter it-Twelve Months
Imprisonment. And 561. JAMES GREEN (19), to burglariously breaking
and entering the dwelling-house of William Brooke, and stealing therein &
coat and other articles, his property-Nine Months' Imprisonment,
562. SARAH SMITH (46), Feloniously uttering counterfeit coin.
MR. COLERIDGE conducted the Prosecution ; and MR. KELLY the Defence.
ELIZABETH GRIFFITHS, I am servant to Mrs. Watt, who keeps a dairy
at 47, Lyle Street-on 27th July I seryed the prisoner with two eggs she
gave me a shilling—I took it to my mistress, said something to her, and
then went out for a constable—I had seen the prisoner in the shop six
weeks before, when she bought two eggs, and gave me a shilling, which was
put on one side of the till in & separate part-it was found to be bad not
many minutes after she left.
Cross-examined. The woman did not hear what I said to Mrs. Watt-
the policeman said to the prisoner "How long ago is it since you passed the
other shilling P"-she said "It was not me at all" - I said " It was you ; it
was four weeks ago since you came in," and she said "Oh no, it was not
me at all”-she denied it thronghout.
