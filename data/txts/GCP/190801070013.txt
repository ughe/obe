DEHRING, Elisa, having pleaded guilty on December 18, 1907 (p.
386), of conspiring with Elsa Herget and other persons to procure
the miscarriage of the said Elsa Herget, was brought up for sentence.
During the interval prisoner had made a long statement about the
matter, which differed from her original story and which she stated
was the real version. Her previous chantieter was found to be good,
and his Lordship thought, as she was not one of those persons who?
