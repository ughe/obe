out together-on 27th April, I was with Anderson-I saw the Somes go into

I had previously taken the two Somes in custody--we found White in bed,
told him to get up, and asked him his name, he said, “ Henry Watson "-I
told him I should take him in custody for being a deserter, also for being

uttering sixpences last Thursday-he said, “What?" I said, " Uuttering,
