Before Mr. Commissioner Kerr.

coin. Second Count, having counterfeit coin in his possession with intent to
utter it.

mother-about 6.30 p.m. on 12th December I was going to Mr. Taylor's,

told me to get a quartern of gin, because his wife was ill-he gave me
