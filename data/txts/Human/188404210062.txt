I should not like to swear it—I remember his answer was that he did give the document—there was a good deal of confusion at the Court, and it is a good while ago—I do not pledge my oath more than I have said—I believe the question was whether he had given that document to Mr. Bacon—I should not like to swear it—I was not a witness in the civil suit at the County Court between Mr. Bacon and the prisoner—I know nothing of for whom the verdict was given.
