529. CHARLES WHICHER was indicted for embezzlement.
MR. PRENDERGAST conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE DRAKE. I am clerk at a sugar refinery at Rateliff. I employed
Mr. Rutherford as my medical man-I owed him a bill of 51 146., which
I paid on the 21st of February, 1838, to the prisoner, in Mr. Rutherford's
shop-he wrote “Settled" on the bill.
Cross-examined by Mr. Clarkson. Q. Have you any recollection of
the transaction of paying him, except from the memorandum on the bill ?
A. Yes, I remember going into the shop, and paying him, and his writing
the memorandum.
Hugh Francis. I live in Ratcliff-highway. I was a patient of Mr.
Rutherford's, and owed him a bill of two guineas, in March, 1838-I paid
two pounds to the prisoner in Mr. Rutherford's shop, on the 2nd of
March, 1838, on Mr. Rutherford's account-the two shillings were not
asked forthe prisoner wrote this receipt on the bill in my presence
" Settled, Chas. Whicher."
SAMUEL RUTHERFORD. I am a surgeon and apothecary in Ratcliff-
highway. The prisoner was in my employment from June, 1836-he
occasionally received money on my account--in 1838, I asked him occa-
sionally if certain persons had paid, and he said No, but they would pay
at Christmas-on the 15th of November, 1838, I made some discovery,
and went over, with him, a list of patients who owed me money-I asked
him whether he had received Mr. Drakes accuum-ho wid No, he had
not-I asked him the same about Mr. Francis-he said No, he had not
- he was in my service at that time, but left that afternoon-he never
acknowledged having received these two sums on the contrary, he said
he had not received them.
