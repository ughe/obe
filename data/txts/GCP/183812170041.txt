the prisoner with a pie-she gave me the shilling-I laid it on the shelf, at

you bring the person you had it of"-she left the shop, and did not take
the pie nor the shilling—I showed the shilling to my husband—he laid it

berland-market. About twelve o'clock, on the 29th of November, the

I kept it till Monday evening, the 3rd of December, when he came again
for another pint of porter, and offered another half-erown-I recognised
