man-she asked 41. on it-I asked her what it was--she said it was a dia-

"Franklin, No. 18, Walworth-road"-I said, "I will accompany you, and
see that it is right"-she then said, " I don't live there, I live at Islington"
-I said, " What part of Islington?"-she said, “Vineyard " and was
about to make some addition, but caught the word up, and said, “Goswell-
terrace," (I think, No. 5,) and said, “The ring was given to me by a gen-
tleman who slept with me last night, and said that he lived at No. 18,
