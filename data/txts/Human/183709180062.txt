HINRICH BASS re-examined . I did not authorise this woman to pawn the jacket—I did not know that she had it—I had a man with me—I do not know his name—I did not give a young man in charge before the women—I asked a young man if he knew who these two girls were.

 2113. CORNELIUS DRISCOLL was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of August , 2 boots, value 12 s  ., the goods of Joseph Chipchase . JOSEPH CHIPCHASE. I am a shoemaker, and live in High-street, Shadwell. I had some boots, on the 18th of August, hanging outside the window, about eight o'clock, and I missed two at a quarter past eight o'clock—I received information, ran out, and overtook the prisoner at no great distance, with these two boots in his possession—they are mine—a police man was with me, and took him—he said, "I have got nothing," and dropped the boots at his feet.
   Prisoner . I know nothing at all about them—I had been drinking with a few friends. CHARLOTTE CROUCH. I saw the prisoner take the boots—he seemed sober. GUILTY. Aged 22.
 2114. CORNELIUS DRISCOLL was again  indicted for stealing, on the   7th of July , 2 pairs of boots, value 13 s  ., the goods of William Henry Thomas . WILLIAM HENRY THOMAS. I am a shoemaker, and keep a shop in Shadwell. On the 7th of July I had some boots—I lost two pairs that had been hanging outside my window—I had seen them safe in the morning. WILLIAM WILSHIRE( police-constable K  249.) I apprehended the prisoner, and found on him four duplicates in a box—one of them led me to these boots at Mr. Reynolds'. DAVID POWELL. I am servant to Messrs. Reynolds—these boots were pledged with me by a woman for the prisoner—it states on the duplicate by Ann Haley for John Driscoll.
   Prisoner . I picked up the box, and four duplicates in it as I was going to work, the very day I was taken. GUILTY.
 2115. CORNELIUS DRISCOLL was again  indicted for stealing, on the   5th of July , 1 waistcoat, value 4 s  ., the goods of Joseph Robert Minton . JAMES DARLING. I am in the employ of Joseph Robert Minton, a tailor, in Ratcliffe-highway. I missed a waistcoat from our door on the 5th of August—it was outside the window—this is it. WILLIAM WILSHIRE. I found a duplicate of this waistcoat on the prisoner.
