CHARLES KEAN. I am a warehouseman in the service of the Carton
Company-on 17th December Goodwin asked me to look at some ranges
and 24 register stoves for a person named Williams--I could only get 21
stoves and six ranges- I wrote the name of Williams on the boilers of the
ranges in chalk, and then told Goodwin, and he completed the order.
Cross-examined by Croxon. I had the order for 11 ranges and 24 stoves,
but I could only complete six ranges.
