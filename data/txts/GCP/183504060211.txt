of April, at St. George the Martyr, 1 half-crown, the money of Henry
Chester, his master; and GEORGE TYLER, for that he, before the
said felony was done and committed, to wit, on the same day and at the
same parish, did feloniously and maliciously incite, move, procure, counsel,
hire, and command the said William Bradbury to do and commit the said
felony.

HENRY CHESTER. I am a bread and biscuit baker, and live in St.
George's-circus, Southwark. Bradbury had been about six weeks in my
service-on Friday, the 3rd of April, I was absent from home, and, on re-
turning in the evening, Mrs. Chester gave me information. STORONT
ELIZA CHESTER. I am the prosecutor's wife. Bradbury came to me
for money to fetch 1 cwt. of salt-I gave him two half-crowns-he said
we wanted salt-when he came back he brought me la. 6d.when Mr
Chester came home I told him what I had sent for Tyler has lived with
us for about a year and a quarter, and was in our service at the time-rhe
was a trusty servant, as we thought, I thought the salt was wanted for the
baking- I supposed Bradbury had bought 1 cwt. of salt, as he gave me
1.. 6d. change-Tyler was in the bakehouse at the time. I do not know

WILLIAM MOORE. I live in the London-road. I sold Bradbury half a
cwt. of salt, for which he paid me 1s. 9d.-he gave me half-a-crown, and I
