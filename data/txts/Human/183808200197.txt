 2002. EDWARD DODMAN was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of August , 1 bridle, value 10 s  ., the goods of John Osborne . WILLIAM ARNOLD. ( police constable D  84.) On the 23rd of August, a little before nine o'clock, I met the prisoner in Portman-square, about a quarter of a mile from Mr. Osborne's—he said, "It is a fine morning"—I saw part of a bridle between his waistcoat and shirt—I ran after him, and said, "What have you got there?" he said, "A bridle"—I said, "Who does it belong to?"—he said, his friend, but would not tell me who—I took him to the station-house, and found this bridle, part under his jacket, and part in his hat—he then said some friend gave it him in Montague-square. WIILIAM SMITH. I am ostler to Mr. John Osborne. I know the prisoner—he had been in Mr. Osborne's employ, but had left him, and worked for a man in the Mews—this is Mr. Osborne's bridle—it was taken from the boxes where the horses were.
   Prisoner . I was not in the boxes that morning—I was only sweeping the yard.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 31.— Confined One Month.
