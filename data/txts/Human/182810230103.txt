MR. PRENDERGAST conducted the prosecution.
 WILLIAM HICKS. I live in  Old-street; the prisoner was my nurse. I missed property, and gave direction to Meadows; and on the 15th of October, the prisoner was apprehended at my house; I charged her with stealing spoons, table cloths, and a towel; she said she had not got the duplicates, but had lent them, or made away with them, but she could shew where the things were pawned; she took us to Burgess, who produced a table cloth, and towel; from there she took us to Mr. Smith's, where we found three spoons and a table cloth. I am independent - I took her from the workhouse.
   GEORGE MEADOWS. I am a constable. I took the prisoner, went to the pawnbroker's, and found the property.
   SIDNEY SMITH. I am a pawnbroker. Meadows and the prosecutor came to my house, and I produced the spoons and table cloth.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner received an excellent character.
     GUILTY. Aged 36.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Judgment Respited.
 2185.  JOSEPH HILLIER was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of September , 1 watch, value 1l.; 1 pair of boots, value 12s.; 9 handkerchiefs, value 20., and 2 shirts, value 5s., the goods of James Kemp.
 JAMES KEMP. I am  coachman to Mr. Stevens, of Brighton. I lost the articles stated in the indictment from Regency-square, Brighton, at the back of the mews, on the morning of the 28th of September, or the night before - some were in the stable, and part in my bed-room; I saw the prisoner in the room that morning - he had been working in the stables, and was going to assist me - he left that morning, about five o'clock, and about ten I missed my property; I could not find him.
 - HART. I am an officer of Upper Tooting. I apprehended the prisoner in Wardour-street; I had come to town with Pleasant. I found part of the property at his lodging, No. 32, Wardour-street; his wife was in the room - we were watching for him there from half-past eleven to half-past one o'clock, and then saw him coming up the street; Pleasant said, "This is him;" he turned down a passage - I ran, and collared him; he said, "Is that you, Pleasant?' I found at his lodging three white handkerchiefs and other things.
   JOHN VIRGO BUCKLAND. I am a constable of St. James'. I assisted in taking the prisoner, and on his person found this handkerchief and watch.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 40. Transported for Seven Years.
 2186.  JOSEPH HILLIER was again indicted for staling, on the 26th of September , 1 watch, value 30s.; 2 waistcoats, value 10s.; 1 shirt, value 5s.; 5 handkerchiefs, value 4s.; 1 neckcloth, value 1s.; 2 pairs of stockings, value 2s.; and 1 pair of boots, value 2s., the goods of Robert Pleasant.
 ROBERT PLEASANT. I am  coachman to a gentleman at Brighton. I lost these articles from my room at the mews the same morning; the prisoner assisted now and then at the stable; I came to town with the constable, and went to Wardour-street, where I found my property - he had my boots on; a watch and two handkerchiefs were found.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. He left them in my room.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. Transported for Seven Years, from the expiration of his former sentence.
 2187.  THOMAS BAYLIS was indicted for embezzlement.
   SAMUEL WESTCOTT TILKE. I live in High-street, Mary-le-bone, and am a baker. The prisoner was my apprentice, and employed to receive money; George Parker owed me 3s. 9d.
 GEORGE PARKER. I paid the prisoner 3s. 9d. on the  7th of October, on Mr. Tilke's account.
 MR. TILKE. He paid me 11d. on this account, on the 7th of October; here is the entry in his own writing - he settles with me every evening; he was taken on another charge.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Was it not for spoiling rolls that you took him? A. Yes, on the 7th of October, in the evening; but he had made up his book before he went; if there was any errors we correct them on Sunday - he once summoned me to the office: the Magistrate dismissed the charge, and said it was a mercy to horsewhip him, for kicking me; I said if he went on in that way he would soon be there again - he has been two years with me.
 Prisoner's Defence. The very evening I made this mistake I pointed out an error of five loaves.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Three Months.
 2188.  HENRY EDWARDS was indicted for embezzlement.
 MR. BODKIN conducted the prosecution.
   CATHERINE EDWARDS. I am the wife of Henry Edwards, who left me about eleven years ago; I have not heard of him for eight years. The prisoner is my eldest son; I am a baker- he managed my business for some years, and conducted himself well till lately. On the 13th of October Biggs owed me 13l.
   MARGARET BIGGS. I keep a chandler's-shop, and deal with the prosecutrix. On the 13th of October I owed her 13l. 0s. 1d.; the prisoner came that day, and said."I want some money - my mother has a bill to pay for flour;" I gave him ten sovereigns, and he gave me this receipt - (reads.)
 MRS. EDWARDS. He never returned to me after this; I have not had the money.
   JAMES HENRY BRYNE. I keep a chandler's-shop, and know the prosecutrix. I found the prisoner in Old-street; I took hold of him, and he said the money was all gone - I delivered him to an officer.
   HENRY SHELDRAKE. I am an officer. I took the prisoner, and found on him 17s. 4d.; he said that was all, but I found a bag with five sovereigns on him after that.
 Prisoner's Defence. I think it useless to deny my guilt - I hope judgement will be passed with mercy.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Transported for Fourteen Years.
