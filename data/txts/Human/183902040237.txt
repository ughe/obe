THOMAS DYSON and GEORGE MORRIS were indicted for stealing, on the   26th of December , 1 till, value 1 s  .: 2 weights, value 1 s  .; 10 shillings, 12 pence, 24 halfpence, and 240 farthings; the goods and monies of John Shove . JOHN SHOVE. I am a corn-dealer, and live at Lewisham. On the 26th of December, about five or six o'clock, I left my till safe in the shop, and went into the back parlour—my man-servant at the same time went out at the front door—I cannot say whether the till was in the shop at that precise moment, although I believe, at the time of the examination, I did recollect a circumstance which enabled me to say it was the case positively—a few minutes after leaving my shop I heard what I thought was the rattling of the scale against the desk—I went into the shop, but saw no body there—I went to the street door, and looked round, but could not per ceive any one—a customer came in in a few minutes, and I then missed my till—there had been about 5 s . in farthings, 2 s . in other copper money, and from 10 s . to 20 s . in silver, in it—the scale was directly over the till—I know the prisoner Morris by his coming to the shop for a person who was an occasional customer, and he says, and I believe it is correct, that I gave him 1 s . that afternoon as a Christmas-box—I might have given him that from the till, or from my pocket—the till is a drawer with divi sions—the farthings were by themselves, the other coppers by themselves, and the silver by itself.
   Cross-examined by MR. CHAMBERS . Q.  What day was it you lost your till? A.  The 26th, boxing-day—I recollect giving Morris 1 s . that day. WILLIAM BEVINSTOCK. I am a labourer, and live at Lewisham bridge. On the evening of the 26th of December, as I was going through the village, I saw the prisoner Dyson inside Mr. Shove's shop, leaning over the counter, and Morris looking in at the window—there was no body else—I went on to Mr. Lindsay's, and on returning saw Dyson come outside with something under his arm—he called Morris by the name of George, and said, "Come on"—they walked together till they got into the road, and then went faster—I afterwards went to the Fox and Hounds, and saw both the prisoners down stairs in that house—Dyson asked me to fetch him a pot of ale, which I did, and he gave me twenty-four far things to pay for it.
   Dyson.  I did not give him any money to fetch ale—I had some ale, and he drank with me. Witness.  He asked me to drink, but I did not—I told him I had some up stairs.
