EDWIN KARSLAKE (Re-examined). He had not another florin to hand

HENRY JAMES LAURENCE (Re-examined). The prisoner was not searched
before Karslake searched him—when Walker brought him back I had
the bad coin in my hand, and he tendered a florin to my wife, and got the
change—he had two, one good and one bad, and when he was fetched
back he tendered a good one in substitution for the bad one-he had
already got his change.
GUILTY of the first uttering.-Six Months' Hard Labour.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, DECEMBER 16TH, 1889.
