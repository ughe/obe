and keep fowls--they run in the street in the day-time. On the 26th
March I missed one--they had been all right the morning before-thisi
my hen--I can swear to every feather almost.
JOHN INNS. I live in Little Windmill-street, St. James's. I bought
this fowl of the prisoner.
SAMUEL GODDARD (police-constable C 103.) I took the prisoner-be
said he stole one fowl, which belonged to Lathan--the other he knew -
thing about.

ness to sell them.
