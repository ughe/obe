Yes, I was married at a church against Book Road, Stoke Newington
neither the first nor second wife complained"t second wife was con-

EDWARD LEIGHTON. I am chapel keeper at the Wesleyan Chapel,

JOHN KENNARD (Re:eramined by MR. GEOGHEGAN). My daughter and
