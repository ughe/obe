1093. WILLIAM HOPKINS was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief,
value 2s., the goods of Nathaniel Marchant, from his person.
NATHANIEL MARCHANT. I amn a boot-maker, and live in Collingwood
street, Shoreditch. About eleven o clock at night, on the 4th of April, I was
walking in Shoreditch-I felt a tug at my coat pocket behind--] turned
round, and saw the prisoner and another person walking away from me-I
put my hand into my pocket, and my handkerchief was gone walked after
them, and saw the prisoner pass a handkerchief like mine to his companion
-I laid hold of the prisoners he struggled, but I kept him till the officer
came-his companion ran away.
GUILTY. Aged 9.Confined Sia Months.
