THIRD COURT.-Saturday, March 10th, 1894.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
310. JAMES HORNE and CHARLES PRESCOTT (62), Feloniously
breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Charles William Wyatt, and
stealing a chronometer clock. Second Count, receiving the same.
MR. HEDDON Prosecuted; Mr. OVEREND Defended Horne ; and Mr.
WALLACE Defended Prescott.
ANNIE LEMON. I am servant to Mr. Walker, of 13, Pelham Place
South Kensington--on 9th January last I went to bed at a quarter past
ten, after locking up the house-about half-past seven in the morning, or
going into the dining-room, I missed the clock from the sideboard-this
(produced) is it-I noticed that the window was open; the catch had
been forced from the outside-nothing else was missed.
CHARLES WILLIAM WYATT. This is my clock-it is a chronometer by
Frodsham; it cost my father £60-—it was a presentation to him, together
with a lot of plate and other things, when he ceased to be agent to Lord
Penrhyn—there is no inscription on it.

merchant's office in the City, and have been so for twenty-eight years-on
Sunday afternoon, 21st January, between one and two, Horne came in-I
have known him twenty-five years I heard him openly, in the saloon
