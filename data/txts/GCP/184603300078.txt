902. WILLIAM GREEN was indicted for stealing 1 promissory note,
for 5l., the property of Charles Bailey, from his person.
MR. Doane conducted the Prosecution.
CHARLES BAILEY. I live at Great Berkhampstead, in Hertfordshire. I
went to the bank at Hemel Hempstead, on the 13th of Feb. and received
there some 101. and five 51. notes, Nos, from 1343 to 1347-I paid away
the 101., and three of the 51. potes- I came to town on the 24th of Feb. I
had then the two remaining 51. notes, Nos. 1345 and 1346, safe in a purse
in my right hand breeches pocket-I have been in the habit of putting up at
a public-house in Villier's-street, but the house was full-I went to the bar of
the house-I had my money safe then I was then going into the Strand,
and just as I got to the edge of the Strand, in three or four minutes after!
left the public-house, I missed my money-there were a great many people
passing, and some of whom might have got my money without my percely:
ing it-it was about half-pást ten at night I gave notice to the police, and
to Messrs. Currie, in Cornhill, who are the agents of the Hemel Hempstead
bank--some days afterwards I saw the Hue and Cry, and in it there was a
note, No. 1345, advertised as being found at Cambridge.
COURT. Q. Did you notice who were in the public-house? 4. There
were three men on my right hand side at the bar, as I was asking the land-
lord for a bed-nothing passed to draw the attention of the persons there
my conversation was to the landlord-I called for a small glass of gin and
water, and put my hand into my pocket, and paid for it-I put my hand on
my purse, and pushed it down further-I lost my purse, three sovereigos,
and a half-sovereign, and the two notes, all went together.
