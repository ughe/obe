 830.  ANN CALNAN was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of March , 2 half-crowns, the monies of John Thomas.
   SARAH THOMAS. I am the wife of John Thomas, a green-grocer- we live in Ratcliff-highway. On Sunday, the 26th of March, there were three half-crowns in my till, and a great deal more silver; I left the shop about ten o'clock, for about a minute, and when I returned I saw the prisoner in the shop - I had seen him on the Sunday before - the till was open; I said, "I believe you are the little girl who robbed me last week;" I put my hand on her shoulder, and drew out the till, and said, "You little hussey, you have robbed me again;" she had her hand clenched, which I opened, and found in it two half-crowns; I had taken half a crown a few minutes before.
   CHARLES TICKLE. I am an officer. I took the prisoner, and bring the money.
 GUILTY. Aged 10.
     Judgment Respited.
 831.  CHARLES CURRIE was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of April , 20 yards of stair carpet, value 35s., the goods of George Chowles.
 GEORGE CHOWLES. I live in North Audley-street, and am an  upholsterer. On the 1st of April, about eleven o'clock, I went to Bayswater, and while there I received information - I came to town, and missed these three pieces of carpet, which are mine.
   JOHN LEWIS. I am a Bow-street patrol. Between twelve and one o'clock, on the 1st of April, I saw the prisoner in Brook-street, about half a mile from Mr. Chowles'; he had the carpeting concealed - when I came up to him he dropped it and ran - there was another boy with him, who ran another way.
   RICHARD BOWDEN. I saw the officer throw down this carpet by my door.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went down the street - a young man came up to me, and said he would give me sixpence to take it to Bond-street - he rested it on the post, and the officer came and asked what I had got - I said I did not know - he said, "Who does it belong to?" I said, "That young man;" he ran away, and the officer after him - I then thought he had not come by it honestly, and I ran off.
 Mr. CROWLES re-examined. I had left three painters in the shop.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
 832.  JAMES CARTY was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of March , 1 coat, value 30s., the goods of George Biddlecomb.
 GEORGE BIDDLECOMB. I am a  butcher, and live at Twickenham. I drove my cart to Cavendish-square on the 30th of March, and left my coat on the seat of the cart, while I went into a gentleman's house - I returned in about three minutes and missed it - I drove on, and in St. Giles' I saw the prisoner walking with my coat on - I got out, took hold of him, and said it belonged to me; he said he picked it up in the street.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up.
     GUILTY. Aged 53. Confined Three Months.
 833.  ELIZABETH NICHOLLS was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of February , 1 bed, value 1l. 15s.; 2 blankets, value 5s.; 1 pillow, value 2s., and 1 pillow case, value 6d., the goods of Charles Jones , in a lodging room.
   JANE JONES. I am the wife of Charles Jones - we live in Little Union-street, St. George's, East. I let a furnished room to the prisoner at 5s. a week - she had not completed her week; I went out to work on the 2d of February, and came home about half-past eight o'clock at night - she was gone, and I missed this property; she never returned to pay the rent; I am quite sure of her person - I do not know how she gets her living.
 Prisoner. She was perfectly aware that I pawned the things, and I gave her part of the money. Witness. I knew nothing about her pawning the things, and never gave her leave to do it; she had been in the house with a lodger of mine, for a few weeks before she lived with me.
   JOHN FIELD. I am foreman to a pawnbroker, in High-street, Shadwell. I took in a bed of the prisoner, to the best of my recollection, on the 1st of February, on which there was 5s. more advanced the day after.(Bed produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I gave her part of the money towards the rent - another person took the room besides me; I had left the room, but the other person was there.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. Transported for Seven Years.
 834.  ELEANOR RYAN was indicted for stealing, on the 22d of March , 1 silver thimble, value 1s., and 1 earring value 1s., the goods of Amelia Mills, widow, her mistress.
 AMELIA MILLS. I am a widow, and live in  Titchfield-street. The prisoner was my servant- I had an officer to search her, and he found on her a thimble and an earring, which I had seen about a fortnight before in my kitchen-drawer - I had accused her of taking them - she said nothing.
   WILLIAM REED. I am an officer. I searched the prisoner, and found a thimble and a gold ear-ring, which the prosecutor said were her's; the prisoner said she brought them with her.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I found the thimble on the floor - I put it into my pocket and forgot it - my mistress said she had lost the ear-ring for three years.
 GUILTY. Aged 19. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Three Months.
