WILLIAM PICKMAN. I live in Albany-street, Regent's Park, and am a
artist and watchmaker. On the 12th of March, about one o'clock in the
day, Kiley came, and offered to sell me a piece of silver, which I have
here-I asked where he got it-be said he saw some policemen drivine
some boys along by the railroad, and after they were gone he picked up
this-I asked if he had any more, and he produced these other two piech
-he asked me if I would buy it-I said no, but I would stop it-I told
him to come the next morning, and I would tell him all about it-I went
to the station-house.
WILLIAM ECKETT (police-constable D. 54.) I was at the station-house
when Looney was brought in his friends came afterwards, and brought
Dilley, and in consequence of what they said, I went to Buckridge-street
and found Kiley in bed, with his clothes on- I went to Titcbfield-street,

John TEDMAN. I am a police inspector. I remember the prisoner
Welch being brought in- I told him he was charged with stealing two forko
-- he said he had met a person a few days previous, who asked him where
he lived, and he had told him and the person said, " Is there any thing in
the house you can steal, if there is we will divide the money between us:
there any plate in the house ?"- that he said, "I believe there is"- the other
said, “Try and get it" -and he took the forks, and gave them to him.
John PRYKE re-examined. This is part of my master's property-I had
seen it safe the day before I missed it.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Has it not the appearance of
having been in the fire ? A. It has.
WELCH-GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Five Days.
KILEY-GUILTY. Aged 17.-- Transported for Seven Years.
LOONEY-NOT GUILTY.
