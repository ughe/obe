Aldersgate Street, and saw the two prisoners at the corner of Mr. Woolmer's
shop - man was with them-Groom and the man were standing by the
side of a pile of goods, forming & sort of corner, Williams was behind
them. I saw her put her hand from behind them and take a piece of goods
-Groom and the man then walked arm-in-arm & short distance down
Aldersgate Street, Williams followed them. I spoke to Hancock, the
policeman, and saw him take the women into opstody.

as far as Jewin Street before I spoke to the policeman--when they got to
Jewin Street, the man and Groom separated, and Williams and Groom
spoke to each other. I saw nothing done with the print. To
