2530. JOHN HODDER was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of Aug.,
1 saw, value 5s, 6d.; the goods of Edward Hardy.
EDWARD Hardy. I live with my father, Edward Hardy. This saw was
safe about six o'clock last Tuesday evening, in Hoxton market-I missed it,
and ran after the prisoner, and took him with the saw on his arm, in Long
alley.
WILLIAM ANDREW FERGUSON (police-constable G 181.) A little before
seven o'clock I saw a crowd-I went up-the prisoner said, “You are not
wanted, it is only a lark”--the prosecutor gave him in charge-in going along
he said he bought it for 1s.
Prisoner's Defence. A lad said did I want to buy a saw? I said I had
only 1s. I bought it.
Guilty. Aged 27.-Confined Three Months.
2531. JAMES SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of Aug.,
1 watch, value 5l.; 1 seal, 10s.; and 1 key, 103.; the goods of Richard
Thomas Scott.
CHARLOTTE Scort. I am the wife of Richard Thomas Scott, and live in
High-street, Poplar. About a quarter to twelve o'clock, on the 18th of Aug,,
I left the parlour to speak to my husband-1 left a watch hanging on a nail
over the chimney-piece-I was away about two minutes-when I returned
the watch was gone-the prisoner was in the parlour-I asked what he
wanted-he said he had paper to sell -no one could have taken it but him--
he was taken in about five minutes-there were two boys with him.
Prisoner's Defence. I went with some paper; there was no one there, 86
I went out.
Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
2532, HARRIET EDMONDS was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of
Aug., 30 yards of merino, value 11. 4s. ; the goods of Richard Cole Baker
and another.
JAMES Ferguson. I am in the service of Richard Cole Baker and another,
linen-drapers, Leather-lane. I saw the prisoner and another come into the
shop--this merino was on a pile in the shop-the other one took it, and gavo
it to the prisoner, who put it under her shawl, and went off-I ran out,
and brought her back with it.
Prisoner's Defence. The young woman asked me to carry it, and she
would give me a trifle.
Guilty. Aged 16.-Confined Two Months.
2533. ANN SCOTT was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of July, 1 jacket,
value 1l.; 1 handkerchief, 2s. : 1 printed book, 6d.; 1 tobacco-box, 2d;
and I order for the payment of 41.; the property of William Thompson: and
JOHN JONES for feloniously receiving i jacket; and SUSAN MASON
for feloniously receiving 1 handkerchief, 1 printed book, 1 tobacco-box, and
an order for the payment of 41., part of the said property; well knowing the
same to have been stolen, against the Statute, &c.
The prosecutor did not appear.
