 2204. PHINEAS TWINE was indicted for embezzlement. WILLIAM HUBE. I am a newsvender, and live in Surrey-place, Wands worth. The prisoner was in my service about six months, to distribute newspapers, and receive money from customers occasionally, which he was to pay to me the same evening—on the 22nd of July he ought to have come at six o'clock—he did not come till two, and then I discharged him—I supply Mr. Feathers of Lansdown-road, and Mr. Hobson of the Quadrant, at Wands worth, with papers—I did not receive any money of the prisoner for Mr. Fea thers on the 26th of June—he always kept his money till it was finally set tled in this book, and then he paid it over to me—that his money might be safe I gave him this little box to keep it in—he did not bring me any money on the 3rd of July, or on the 10th of July, from Mr. Feathers—I asked if he had been paid, and he said no, Mr. Feathers was out of town—he did not pay me any money from Mr. Hobson on the 20th of July—I asked him about it expressly.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORNE . Q.  Are you in a large way of business? A.  I serve about two hundred Sunday papers, and I may serve seventy daily papers—Mr. Hobson's account was for the Saturday edition of the "Weekly Messenger," and of four days of the "Times"—sometimes he had the "Times" three or four days running, and sometimes only one day—he always paid on Saturday—the prisoner was the only boy who took out papers for me in the week days—on Sunday I have another boy who takes one district, and the prisoner the other—he received money from some customers on Sa turday, and from others on Monday and on other days—Mr. Feathers and Mr. Hobson were particularly regular in their payments. SUSAN SOUTER. I am in the service of Mr. Feathers, of Stockwell. He has newspapers of Mr. Hube—the prisoner brought the papers every morning—on the 26th of June I paid him 1 s . for Mr. Hube, for newspapers which he had supplied; and 1 s . on the 3rd of July, 1 s . on the 10th of July, and 1 s . on the 17th of July.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What makes you recollect these four particular days? A.  I always paid him on Wednesday. ANN FRANCIS. I am servant to Mr. Hobson, of Wandsworth. I paid the prisoner 1 s . 2 d . for his master—it was Mr. Hobson's money.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What day was it? A.  On the 20th of July, on a Saturday.
