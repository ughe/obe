   Prisoner's Defence.  When he came to take me we were both on the ground; the gentleman had me by the throat—he accused me of robbing him of 5 s .; I said, "No, it is false;" the policeman asked what I had in my hand; I said that was nothing to him, what I had I should give up to his betters; I am quite innocent; the prosecutor told me to follow him; I asked him to treat me to something to drink; he said he could not, for he was too near his home, and did not wish to be seen. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Transported for Ten Years .

 2159. ANN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of Oct , 1 shawl, value 12 s  ., the goods of John Cope Folkard . JAMES GEORGE POOLE. I am shopman to Mr. John Cope Folkard, a pawnbroker, in the London-road. On Friday evening, the 24th of Oct., about five o'clock, I was taking in the goods from the door—I saw the prisoner pulling different things about, and asking the prices—I went into the shop with some of the goods I was moving, and when I returned to the door a gown was partly pulled down—I told the prisoner to go on, as she did not want to buy anything—she went outside, and I saw her pull down a shawl, put it under her arm, and go away—I followed, and took the shawl from under her arm—this now produced is it—it is Mr. Folkard's property.
   Prisoner.  I was very much in liquor at the time, and did not remember the shawl till I was at the station. Witness.  I do not think the was; she pretended to be so. JOHN PAYNE( policeman. ) I took her into custody—she did not appear intoxicated. GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined Two Months .
