ing by the prisoner and prosecutor-I saw the prisoner strike him three times
-- he offered me 1s. or 6d. to go for a constable, but I was afraid -I intended

constable"- I went over the bridge, and turned up a lane, to go to Cook's,
the constable, but he was not at home-I cut across some fields into the

1267. JOSEPH ANDERSON was indicted for stealing, on the 6th
of May, 1 bolster, value 6s. ; 2 pillows, value 68. ; 1 sheet, value 4s; and
1 blanket, value 58.; the goods of William Jackson.
MARY JACKSON. I am the wife of William Jackson, a carpenter, in Wil-
mington-place, Clerkenwell. The prisoner lodged at our house for about
a month, and left without notice, last Saturday morning-be did not come
home at night I found in his room five duplicates for the articles in ques-
tion, which had been let with the room I knew nothing of their being
pawned- his wife lodged with him-he used to come home to meals, ex-
cept for the last week I had lent him some money.
