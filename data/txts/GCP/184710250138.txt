2428. JOSEPH DEVEREUX was indicted for stealing 1 soup kettle-lid,
value 3s. ; the goods of James Boyd and another, his masters.
GEORGE ATKINSON. I am foreman to Mr. James and Mr. David Boyd,
of Marylebone. The prisoner was in their service-on the evening of the
7th of Oct., I saw the prisoner come out of the shop-he appeared to have
something round his body, under his jacket-I followed him to a marine-store
shop, in Southmoulton-passage--I looked through the window, and saw the
marine-store shopman bend this lid, and throw it under the counter-it is
copper soup pot-cover-I went into the shop, and asked the prisoner to come
with me to Mr. Boyd's-he asked me to forgive him-I told him I could not,
he must come back to his master-the lid was given into the constable's
hands-this is it, it is my master's property, worth about 38.--I can swear
to it by the private mark-when the store shopman gave up the lid, the pri
soner ran away with it-1 ran and overtook him-I found an officer in
Oxford-street-the prisoner asked the officer and me to let him go-I gave
him to the officer, and told him to bring him back to Mr. Boyd's-hem
away a second time, and I caught him and gave him to the officer against the
time I saw the prisoner, I said to him, "What do you do here with this lidt
and he said, "O God forgive me, God forgive me," and then be said he
found the lid in the rubbish."
Cross-examined by Me. PAYNE. Q. Are you the only person here from
the firm ? A. Yes- I was shutting up the shop when I saw the prisoner
come out with something round his body-I left the shop, and went to the
marine-store keeper's-1 was not told to watch the prisoner- went of my
own head-I know this is a kettle-lid-it is termed so in the trade--the pol
soner was a bricklayer's labourer-there was some rubbish there-Mr. Boyd
has a son in the business—I do not know of any other partner.
JOSEPH GILBERT (policeman, D 177.) I saw the prisoner and Atkinso
together, in Oxford-street-Atkinson spoke to me, and the prisoners
“For God's sake let me go," and ran away-Atkinson overtook him,
took him to his master's shop the prisoner had this lid in his left hand, and
he gave it to me-he said he had found it in the rubbish in the cellar Where
was at work.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 28.-Confined One Month.
