JAMES GEORGE DANGAR, clerk to the Welsbach Incandescent
Company, Westminster. On October 24 last I received type-
written order produced from Bernard A. Shaw, value £2 28.
On November 9 I also received typewritten order produced for
goods value £10 12s.6d. They were despatched to address
given in both cases. On December 12 I received written order
produced from A. Irons, hardware merchant, Albert Road,
North Woolwich, ordering goods to the value of £2 2s. They
were despatched. I received a further order on January 1 and
also on January 19. They were despatched. I had no idea that
Irons and Bernard Shaw were connected. On March 30 I re-
ceived order produced from Revett Bros., The Arches, Station
Approach, Forest Gate, to the value of £4 4s. They were sup-
plied. I did not know that Revetts were connected with Irons
or Shaw. I have applied for payment in all three cases, but
have got no payment at all.
Cross-examined by Mr. Thompson. I do not know Albert.
I only saw him at the West Ham police court. I made in-
quiries about the three firms before supplying any goods. As
à result of those inquiries I sent the goods to the three addresses.
Re-examined. The total amount of goods sent to Prince Re-
gent's Lane was £13 2s.6d.; to Irons £22; and to Revett
Bros. £4 4s.
