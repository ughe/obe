PERCY TIPPER. I am sub-manager of the Shaftesbury Avenue branch of the City Bank—the prisoner Clifford had an account there which was opened on 18th November, 1890—she signed the signature book and had authority to draw cheques—Jane Money also had authority to draw cheques, she signed the book in my presence—the account is still open, but we told them two months ago we could receive no more money—this, is the authority ( produced )—I have got 22 cheques here which have been more recently presented—thirteen of. them were signed by Morley—I believe these two letters ( produced ) to be in Miss Clifford's writing.
