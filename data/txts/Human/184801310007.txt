 559. GEORGE ANDERSON , stealing 1 snuff-box, value 5 s  .; from the person of a man unknown; having been before convicted. WILLIAM HAMILTON. I am a warehouseman, and live at Artillery-lane, Bishopsgate. On 15th Jan., about half-past two o'clock, I saw the prisoner following a gentleman down Cornhill, he put his hand into his pocket, took a box out, and made off—I followed, he struck me in the face, I fell, got up again and pursued him—he fell down, and was taken by Hill—the box was found close by him—I have not been able to find the gentleman. WILLIAM HILL( City policeman , 405.) I heard a cry of "Stop theif!" and saw the prisoner and Hamilton running—the prisoner fell down by Mansion-house-place—I took him, and found this box by him—it has been advertised, but we cannot find the owner—Hamilton made the same state ment that he does now.
   Prisoner's Defence.  Two men asked me to buy the box; I gave them 2 s ., and a pot of beer for it. ROBINSON WEBB( policeman. ) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction—( read—Convicted Aug., 1847, and confined three months )—he is the man—he had had three months before that. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Transported for Seven Years .
 560. JOHN RAFTER , stealing 1 purse, value 6 d  .; 1 half-crown, 8 shil lings, and 9 pence; the property of Clara Paul , from her person. CLARA PAUL. I am single, and live at 25, Houndsditch. On Saturday evening, about a quarter past seven o'clock, I was in Houndsditch—I had a purse containing three half-crowns, and eight shillings—I missed the weight, turned, and saw a boy about the prisoner's height running—I followed—my purse has not been found—this is one of my shillings—( produced )—it has "L" on it. MICHAEL DEADY( City-policeman , 45.) I ran after the prisoner through several streets, and overtook him—he threw 10 s . 6 d . in silver into the street, among which was this marked shilling.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Two Months
