GRAFTON GOATLEY. I was in the publishing department of the
Morgan Newspaper Company-I was acting publisher-I went into the
office in November, 1896, about three months after Anecdotes was started

Roger's name was on the two papers as printer and publisher-in 1897
there were some proceedings at a Police-court, after which Morgan said
that he thought it better that my name should appear on the papers
instead of Rogers, and during December, 1897, it did appear as publisher
for three weeks-whilst it was so appearing Berkeley came down to my
office and asked me if I would siga some certificates-he was manager of
the advertising department then the certificates were as to the publi-
cation number of each issue-I said I would sign the certificates pro-
viding I could fill in the figures, which I knew I cannot remember
what Berkeley said to that, but he made some remark and left me-he
never asked me to sigu any more certificates—I did not sign any-I
remained as publisher about two weeks, when Mr. Morgan changed
my position-he did not give any reason - I think he said something
about as Mr. Rogers' name had been on the papers 80 long he
thought it should be replaced-no fault was found with me-
