well—I put an iron bar down, and found a great quantity of oil in the well-
should say 4 or 5 inches all over, I could not tell exaotly-t afterwards

there was any flooding in the cellar at that time-if there had been several
inches of water in the cellar I should have seen it at that time, I did not
believe Bolton's statement, and the prisoner went on with his duties as
