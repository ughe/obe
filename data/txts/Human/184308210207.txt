 2528. THOMAS CANEY was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of De cember , 7 printed books, value 14 s  ., the goods of Elizabeth Tomlin . ELIZABETH TOMLIN. I keep a circulating library in Merlin-place, Clerken well—the prisoner came to me on the 10th of Dec, and had seven volumes.
   Cross-examined by MR. JONES . Q.  Is Mrs. Scriven your sister? A.  Yes—they are my books, and the premises are hers—I am single—I never saw the prisoner before that day—persons pay according to the time they keep the books—there was no time mentioned to return them that I recollect.
   COURT . Q.  What is the usual time? A.  A week or a fortnight—we lend them for six or seven months, if they are going to take them into the country—he never told me they were for the country—they have not been returned. JANE SCRIVEN. The prisoner came for. the books on the 10th of Dec.—I am sure he is the man. ANN GUNN. I am the wife of Mr. Gunn—I remember seeing the prisoner that day—I have no doubt about of him.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Had you seen him before? A.  Yes, two or three times in Clerken well.
   JURY to ELIZABETH TOMLIN . In what name did he take the books? A.  Mr. Scott—there was no deposit paid for them.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY.— Confined Six Months .
