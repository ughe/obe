1688. WILLIAM STAFFORD, stealing 1 timepiece, value 25s. ; the
goods of Charles Walter Teeton.
CAROLINE TEEton. My husband's name is Charles Walter ; we live in
the Waterloo-road. I missed this timepiece produced, from the parlour man-
tel-shelf, on 15th March-I had gone up-stairs, leaving the shop door open.
JAMES REEKS. I took this timepiece in pledge of a man I do not know
- this is the duplicate I gave.
George WARPLE (City-policeman, 221). I took the prisoner, and found
this duplicate on her.
MR. BALLANTINE called
-- CHEESEWRIGHT. I keep a beer-house, at St. Pancras. The
prisoner is a customer of mine-- about the middle of May three men came
and had three pots of beer, and would not pay for it-one man left this
duplicate of a timepiece instead afterwards sold it to the prisoner for 1s. and
two pots of beer.
Not Guilty.
1689. ANN HANDON, stealing 2 gowns, 3 sheets, and other articles,
value 6s. : the goods of John Hudson, from the person of Sarab Hudson the
younger.
SARAH Hudson. My husband's name is Jobn; we live at 5, Adam-
place, Redcross-street. On 8th June I sent my little girl with a bundle of
mangling-she returned with two cakes, without the clothes, crying and say-
ing a female had stolen the bundle.
Francis MESSENGER. I saw the little girl in Redcross-street with the
prisoner, who put something into her hand, and she went into a cake-shop,
and the prisoner ran away with the bundle-I gave information, and identi-
fied her as she passed my house on Thursday-I am sure she is the person.
Prisoner. I took the pinafore and petticoat off the child, but I did no
inore.
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
