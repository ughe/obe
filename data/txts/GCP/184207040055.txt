ROBERT GILLETT. I am agent to David M'Intosh, who is erecting
some buildings in Albert-square, Commercial-road-on Monday, the 20th
of June, he had a barge load of bricks at Ashley's-wharf, and the prisoner's
father was employed as carman to draw them to Albert-square-I went
to Mr. Fitzgerald, in Green-street, adjoining York-square-I found
a load of bricks shot out, opposite Mr. Fitzgerald's premises-they
were of the same description as those in the barge at the wharf-I had
some coversation with Mr. Fitzgerald, and from what he told me I went
back to the wharf to find the prisoner-I found him at the works, at Al-
bert-square, about five o'clock in the afternoon—I said, “Clements, how
came you to shoot these bricks on Mr. Fitzgerald's premises "She said,
"I know nothing about it, I never shot a load of bricks at Mr. Fitzgerald's
in my life "-he was employed under his father to cart these brieks-it
is nearly a quarter of a mile from where the barge was to Albert-square
bave compared the bricks with those in the barge, and have no doubt they
are Mr. M'Intosh's.
