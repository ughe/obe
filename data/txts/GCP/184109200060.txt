2417. CHARLES POOLE was again indicted for stealing, on the 7th
of September, half-a-pint of beer, value 1d., the goods of James M‘Nair, his
master.
JAMES M`NAIR. I received information that the prisoner was robbing
me, and placed a policeman in the cellar, who caught him-he had no right
to go into my cellar at all on that occasion.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Were you at home? A. Yes
-1 used to send him down into the cellar, perhaps once or twice a week,
as I am lame.
RICHARD HILL (police-constable T 113.) I was sent down to watch,
on the 7th of September-I saw the prisoner come down with a pint-pot,
go to the butt, and draw some beer-he turned to go up, and I took him-
he said he hoped I would not take notice of it, as he should be ruined if
did, and he would put away the pot.
NOT GUILTY.
2418. WILLIAM CHIFNEY was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of
September, 1 101. Bank-note, the monies of Benjamin Elam, his master,
in his dwelling-house.
BENJAMIN ELAM. I am a chemist, and live in Oxford-street, in the
parish of St. Mary-le-bone--the prisoner was my errand-boy. On last
Friday I missed a 101. Bank of England note from my cash-box-I am
sure it was left in the casb-box, which I locked when I made up my cash
on Wednesday morning-I sent for an officer, who spoke to the prisoner-
he said he had picked it up in the back shop, under the desk, wbile sweep
ing it out, I cannot say that it was impossible I could have dropped it.
it may have been the case.
Jury. Q. Were there any sovereigns in the box? A. Three or four
sovereigns, but no silver.
Thomas Bryan. I know the prisoner-I met him on Thursday-
he told me he had a 101. noteI asked him where he got it-he said, from
his place— I told him he had better take it back-he said he could not-
he gave it to me, and I gave it to Dawes.
ROBERT DAWES. Brown gave me the note, and I gave it to Patterson
- was to change it, and have 108.
WILLIAM PATTERSON. I went to change the note at a linen-draper's in
Shoreditch-as I was coming along that way Dawes and Bryan offered me
10s. to change it.
JOHN CLARK. I produce the note-Patterson came for a shirt, and of
fered it--I stopped it, thinking it was a forged one.
GUILTY. Aged 13.-Transported for Ten Years-Parkhurst.
