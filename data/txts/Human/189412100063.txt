WILLIAM HERBERT HAYWARD. I am one of the firm of Henry Lewis, jewellers, of New Bond Street—I knew the prisoner by sight in years gone by—I have seen him in the shop with Mrs. Anderson—they have both been customers—the prisoner had, I believe, been there to buy jewellery on credit before August, 1894, but we did not let him have it—on August 8th this year he wanted to buy a diamond ring, price £75, and offered me as security a bill for £150, purporting to be accepted by Mrs. Anderson, and mentioned Harris and Cheetham, solicitors, as a reference—I knew that Mrs. Anderson was a lady of means—we parted with the ring on August 15th, and retained the bill. ( Dated July  26 th, at three months, for  £150, payable at the London Joint Stock Bank, Victoria Street ,)—I next saw him at Bow Street—I wrote this letter to him on October 22nd, a few days before the bill was due, to 36, Bedford Row.
