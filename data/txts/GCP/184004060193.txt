sovereigns, 2 half-sovereigns, 10 shillings, 1 watch-guard, value 28.; and
1 pair of boots, value 7. ; the property of John Swanson. t oday
John SWANSON. I am a seaman, and lodge in Gravel-lane. On the
25th of March, I went to Bluegate-fields with the prisoner-1 bad four
sovereigns, two half-sovereigns, ten or twelve shillings in silver, and a silver
watch-guard-- I took off my clothes, put them on a chair, and went to
bed-I sent the prisoner for some gin and porter, which she brought-I
sent her for a ligbt-she said she had no money-I told her to put her
hand into my pocket, to find some coppers among the silver, which she
did, she went out, and did not return-I got out of bed, overhauled my
things, and found all this property gone, and my shoes-no one else had
been in the room--nobody else could have taken them.
GEORGE WILLIAM GRAVES (police-sergeant K 11.) I went to Tothill-
fields Bridewell, and saw the prisoner, I told her she was charged with
stealing 5l. 108. and a pair of shoes, from the prosecutor she said he
must be a very false man, she had only taken 31. 108., and knew nothing
about the shoes.
