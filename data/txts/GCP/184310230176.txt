3044. CLARA LEWIS and JAMES GLENDON were indicted for steal.
ing, on the 18th of Oct., 1 watch, value 6l.; 1 watch chain, 158.; and 1 watch-
key, 58.; the goods of Samuel Irvine.
SAMUEL IRVINE. I am butler to Samuel Duckworth, Esq. A few minutes
before one o'clock, on the 18th of October, my watch, with a gold chain and
seal, was on a nail over the fire-place in the pantry, which is about five yards
from the area door-) returned in half an hour, and they were gone-this is
the watch-chain and seal.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. What time had you seen it
safe ? A. A few minutes before one-the bell rang, and the kitchen-maid
went up to answer it--the watch must have been taken about that period-I
do not believe that while the kitchen-maid went up, the party came and com-
mitted the robberythere was another person taken up, but not in conse
quence of what the kitchen-maid said-she could not identify that person.
WILLIAM EDWARD VINSON. I am shopman to a pawnbroker. This watch
was pawned on the 18th of Oct., before four o'clock, by the prisoner Lewis.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you know her? A. Yes; she frequently pledges
- I do not know that she keeps a bad house-I offered her 21. for the watch
--she then said I knew whose it was, but did not mention the name of any
person- do not recollect her saying that a gentleman in her house asked her
to pawn it.
JOSEPH KENT (policeman.) I took Lewis-I told her it was for pledging
this watch-she said, when at the station-house, that she had pledged it.
HENRY WYATT. I am shopman to a pawnbroker. This seal, chain, and
key, were pawned by Glendon on the 18th of Oct., about three o'clock.
