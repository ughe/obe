OLD COURT; Monday, February 25.
(Before the Recorder.)
COLLINS, Robert (35, boilermaker); breaking and entering the
shop of Charles E. McConnell and stealing therein two cigarette cases
and 11 packets of goods.
Thomas FRANCIS EALs, manager to C. E. McConnell, 164, Barking
Road. On February 11 I left our shop securely locked up at half-
past ten p.m. There is a large front window to the shop, with no
slutters. Early next morning I was called by the police, and found
that the window had been broken. I identify the cigarette case and
cigarettes produced as having been taken from the shop; their value
is about 6s.
WILLIAN HART, & scavenger, in the employ of the West Ham
Borough Council, said that about three a.m. on February 12 he was
walking along Barking Road when he heard a breaking of glass; he
$w prisoner standing outside MoConnell's shop. Prisoner came up

dow." Witness gave information to the police.
