 1378. JOHN KNOTT was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of April 1 cloak, value 18 s  .; and 1 shawl, value 2 s  .; the goods of William Smith : and 1 cloak, value 10 s  .; the goods of Mary Griffiths . WILLIAM SMITH. I was on a visit at Mr. Mason's, at Deptford. On the 25th of April an alarm was given by my wife's sister that the window was open—I ran out, and on going down Grove-street I saw two young met—I made towards them—to the best of my belief the prisoner was one of them—they divided—one came towards me without a bundle—the pri soner went on with the bundle—I walked after him—he walked fast, I began to run—he dropped the bundle and ran on—a man took him—I picked up the bundle, and it contained these two cloaks and the shawl—two of them are my wife's—I will not be positive the prisoner is the lad who dropped the bundle, but I believe he is. GEORGE GRIFFITHS. I live at Deptford. I saw the prosecutor running after the prisoner, crying "Stop thief,"—he dropped this bundle close by me—I am certain he is the person. FRANCIS WALL. I saw the prisoner running towards the Plough-bridge—he ran and concealed himself under the palings.
   Prisoner's Defence . He did not see me running. I did not have the bundle at all—the witness said he could not swear to me at all. GEORGE GRIFFITHS re-examined.  I stared in his face—I saw the pro secutor running—the prisoner walked gently past me till he got four or five yards, and then ran off directly. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Six Months.
 1379. SAMUEL SHORD was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of April , 1 pair of boots, value 8 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of James Gilham . JOHN PILGRIM. I am shopman to Mr. James Gilham, a boot and shoe maker at Greenwich. On the evening of the 11th of April I ran out of the shop, being alarmed, and saw the prisoner going round the street—I went up to him—he threw the boots down—these are them—they are my master's, and they had been in the shop just before. GUILTY. Aged 28.— Confined Three Months.
