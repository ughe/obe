CHARLOTTE SWEET. I keep a stall in Barking market—on the 19th of August, the prisoner came and asked the price of a looking-glass—I said, 6 d .—I then offered it her for 5 d .—she went away, and in a short time came back and asked the price of my knives and forks—I said 6 d .—she went away then came back and said, "Well, how about these knives and forks "—I looked her out two knives and two forks, and said she should have them for 10 d .—I then said 9 d .—she seemed to object—I said she should have them for 8 d .—while I was putting them up, I saw her putting something in her pocket—I turned, and said I had lost some knives—I felt them in her pocket—I told her to put them down—she would not—I tried to get them out, and in the struggle her pocket fell down—I was taking them out and throwing them on the stall, and the officer came up.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  While she was in your shop, did a man come in? A.  It was not in a house—there were several persons round the board—one man bought a knife—I believe no other person bought any thing—she had never mentioned a candlestick till I found this one on her—I saw her take the knives, and then I seized her. MARY ANN SWEET. I am the prosecutor's daughter—I was with my mother, and saw the prisoner taking the things off the stall which she did not buy, and put them into her pocket.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY  . Aged 19.— Recommended to mercy — Confined Two Months .

 2555. JOSEPH ELMES was indicted for stealing, on the   29th of July , 1 watch, value 2 l  .; 1 guard, 1 s  .; and 1 key, 3 d  .; the goods of Thomas Cross . MARY ANN CROSS. I am the wife of Thomas Cross, of Woolwich—the prisoner lodged with us—I saw this watch on the man tie-shelf on the evening of the 28th of July, and missed it at half-past seven o'clock next morning—the prisoner had access to the room. CHARLES SELLER. I am shopman to a pawnbroker, at Woolwich—on the 29th of July, in the morning, the prisoner pawned this watch in the name of Grosvenor—I am quite sure of him—he came for a further advance on the Monday, and I gave him in charge. THOMAS THOMPSON. I took the prisoner in charge—he said he had bought the duplicate of another man. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined One Year .
