1660. JOHN JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of May,
1 jacket, value 68.; 1 pair of trowsers, value 6s. ; 1 shirt, value, 2s. 6d. ;
1 handkerchief, value 18, 6d. ; 1 pair of braces, value 6d. ; 1 pair of stock-
ings, value 1s.; and 1 hat, value 6d.; the goods of Frederick Jones : and
1 hat, value 48., the goods of James William Jones.
FREDERICK Jones. I live with my mother, in Luton-street, Padding-
ton. On the 30th of May, about half-past nine o'clock in the evening, I
was bathing in the Serpentine - I left my clothes on the bank, and a hat
belonging to my brother, James William Jones- I was in the water about
ten minutes--when I came out, my clothes and the hat were gone-- those
now produced are them-I saw nothing of the prisoner till he was in cus-
tody.

DAVID WATTS. I am a clerk. I was crossing the Park about a quarter
of a mile from the Serpentine, and saw the prisoner running away with a
jacket hanging from under his own jacket-I ran up and asked what he
had-be made no reply-I took from under his jacket this jacket and pair
of trowsers--the rest of the things he threw down-he said, "Now you
have the toggery, you had better give me a milling, and let me go."
SHADRACK KIDDLE. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into cus-
tody-he had the hat in his hand-the clothes were handed to me by the
lodge keeper.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.
