WILSON, George (22, painter). Burglary in the dwelling house
William Albert Cox, with intent to steal therein.
Mr. Tully-Christie prosecuted.
WILLIAM ALBERT Cox, master tailor, 593, Barking Road. Between
11 and 12 p.m., on August 19, I bolted up the premises and left every.
thing all right. About 3.30 a.m. on August 20 my son heard a noise
downstairs. He told me and I went to the landing and listened, when
I heard scuffing downstairs. I went to the front window, opened it,
and blew my whistle. On the Other side I saw a policeman running.
About six minutes after he brought prisoner back I had not seen
him before. I found the back parlour window had been forced open,
the fern stand was pushed a little back, and there were marks of a
knife on the woodwork of the window.
