2301. DAVID HEALY was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of July,
1lb. of leaden pipe, value 1s. 6d., the goods of the Society of Licensed
Victuallers.--2nd Count, stating it to be the goods of James Frederick
Blake.- 3rd COUNT, stating it to be the goods of Robert Selmon
Mulley.
JAMES FREDERICK Blake. I produce the charter of incorporation of
the Society of Licensed Victuallers--the whole of the property of the
Morning Advertiser belongs to the Society-I reside on the premises-they
are all under my care,
George Bedford. I live in Gold-place, Clerkenwell. I saw the pri-
soner coming out of the Morning Advertiser office on the 18th of July
I saw a piece of lead projecting out of his pocket-I told of it.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. Do you know that he is a work-
man that had been employed there ? A. No, he was employed there the week
before- I was waiting outside-some work had been carried on there by
the prisoner's master-I saw him coming out the back way into Shoe-lane.
Joun WAYTE. I am employed in this office. I followed the prisoner
to the house of Mrs. Clark, a marine-store dealer.
MARY Clark. On the 18th of July, a man came to my house, and
brought a bit of lead to the best of my belief the prisoner is the man-
I gave the piece of lead to the boy, and then to the policeman-this piece
of lead is like it.
Cross-examined. Q. You will not swear that is the lead he brought ?
4. No, directly he left me two little boys came, and said, "What did that
man sell ?" and-Wayte took the lead.
John SPRING (City police-constable, No. 315.) I went to Clark's, and
received this lead there- I took the prisoner in Holborn, about three
quarters of an hour afterwards-he said, “I will go any where with you"
- he accompanied me down to the station, and there I told him the nature
of the charge-he denied it.
RICHARD CLOAK. I am stoker at the steam-engine, at the Morning
Advertiser office-I have lost fifteen feet of lead-I saw the prisoner going
to the vault on the morning of the 18th of July- told Wayte to follow
him--this is part of the lead I lost-Mr. Blake is the secretary of the
Licensed Victuallers' Society--Mr. Mulley had been master of the prisoner,
but he was discharged on the Saturday night before. 11
Cross-examined. Q. Did you take this piece of lead, and fit it to any
place? A. This is the remainder of the fifteen feet I lost-I cannot tell
how many workmen of Mulley's were there on the 18th-there were a
number of men at work there.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 36. Confined Four Months.
