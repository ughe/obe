   Cross-examined.  You did not put the half-crown in your teeth to try it at the station—I choked you for nearly two minutes before you would let it go out of your throat.
   Re-examined.  I went to the address the prisoner gave me at 5, St. Margaret's Court. EDWARD HAYES. I am a waterman at the Metropolitan Tavern, South-wark Street—I saw the prisoner with the constable—I did not see the constable leave the prisoner—I was drawing water at the time.
   Cross-examined by the Prisoner,  I saw you standing close by the constable. WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. I am assistant examiner of coins at the Queen's Mint—these four coins are all bad, and from two different moulds—the one found on the prisoner is from the same mould as one of the others.
   Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate. "I had three half-crowns. I won them and a shilling at tossing. I did not know that they were bad. I changed one at the Sun. I never put my hand to my mouth, and I had every opportunity of throwing it away had I known it was bad. "
   The prisoner, in his defence, repeated the statement. GUILTY.

 96. GEORGE ALEXANDER  (32) , Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
   MESSRS. POLAND and CHARLES MATHEWS Prosecuted. FLORENCE HOWE. I am assistant to Mr. John Howe, at Cleveland Road, North Cross Road, Dulwich—on 15th November the prisoner came, at 7.20. p.m., and asked for a bottle of Bass, which came to. 6 d .—he tendered a florin in payment, and I gave him 1 s .6 d . change and put the florin in the till—the prisoner left—soon after a woman came in, and while she was there a man came in and asked for a bottle of stout, which came to 6 d .—he gave me a two-shilling piece, and I gave him 1 s . 6 d . change, and placed it in the same till—I took both the florins to my father, and they were both bad—I went out and saw a constable, and gave him the bad coins and a description of the man who had tendered them—the same night I went to the police-station and saw the prisoner there—I charged him; he did not hear what I said. JOHN BARRELL( Policeman P  32). On 7th November, about a quarter
