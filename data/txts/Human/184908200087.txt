The figure the liquorish old Lombard cuts is highly amusing, and the teasing yarns, such as damsels of a certain class can alone spin, will be found instructive to aged gallants, whose passions are apt to o'erstep their prudence; if the exposure will make only one old sinner pause in his career, our object will be gained."
