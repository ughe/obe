2474. SARAH PETO was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of
August, 1 shawl, value 28. 6d.; 5 shillings, 2 sixpences, 2 pence, and
1 halfpenny; the property of Exsuperious Turner.-2nd Count, stating
it to be the property of Ann Turner.
ANN TURNER. I am the wife of Exsuperious Turner, and live in
Ironmonger-row, St. Luke. On Tuesday morning, the 16th of August,
I met the prisoner in Old-street-I asked her home with me-she went,
and I fell asleep on the bed - I left the shawl and money on the mantel-
piece--when I awoke she was gone-- this is my shawl-no one else was
there.
ELIZABETH BELLAMY. Mrs. Turner sent me to get a shawl out of pawn
--when I brought it back, the prisoner and Mrs. Turner were asleep-I
gave the shawl to the prisoner and twopence-halfpenny.
CHARLES UNDERHILL. I am assistant to James Thomas Hawes, &
pawnbroker, in Old-street-this shawl was pawned, in the name of
Andrews, on the 16th of August.
NOT GUILTY.
