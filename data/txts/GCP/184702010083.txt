THIRD COURT.- Friday, February 5th, 1847.
TUTO
- 5 rada
First Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
550. WILLIAM WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing 2 lbs. weight of
brass, value 1s. 3d., the goods of Thomas Callow, his master.
- ANTHONY CARTER. I am carman in the service of Thomas Callow, a
brass-founder. The prisoner was in his employment-on the 14th of Jan. I
was directed by my master to watch the prisoner-I was placed under a manger
in the second stall in the stable-I found some brass lying under the manger.
--the prisoner came in, took & candle from a candlestick, and went and re-
moved the brass, and put it under some straw he then went and cleaned the
pony-on the same day, before that, I found my chaff-bin had been moved,
and a parcel of brass concealed behind it.
Thomas Callow. I am a brass-founder, and live at Bow-common-
have no partner. On the 14th of Jan. I went to the harness-room for a head-
stall, and could not find it I ordered a search-Carter afterwards pointed
out a quantity of brass stove behind the chaff-bin, and I put him under the
manger to watch-he gave me information the same night and I went with the
policeman to the stable the policeman made a search, and found the prisoner
up in the loft I did not see him there, but I saw the policeman bring him
down-he had no business there—the brass was not kept in the loft
-the harness-room comes into the stable-I told the prisoner to go and
fetch the metal that was behind the chaff-bio-be said he never saw it-
said I could show him somebody who saw him put it there, or words to that
effect--I pulled a truss of clover on one side, and Anthony came out-when
ne saw Anthony he did not say a word, but was very much confused the
brass is mine-it is not valuable of itself, but the harness bad been cut to
pieces, and the brass taken out.
Prisoner. Q. You have otber men about your place ? A. Yes, but I never
missed anything until you came into my employment-I thought as you
were out of work, if I employed you you would know better than to rob me
-I did not much fancy the company you kept.
