contracts figures have also been inserted, as in Tyler and Co.'s con-

written by the same person. I find in the other letter books of
Fraser, Herbert Smith, and Chalmers and Cox that figures have
been inserted as in the other cases. "Herbert Smith " is amongst
these, and "Chalmers and Cox," but I could not speak as to whose
writing the figures are in. In my opinion, the handwriting on Ex.
hibit 50 is Miss Longs. That is the account of Miss Griffiths. I
find a similar handwriting in Tyler and Co.'s letter book. There is a
great similarity between "R" in Exhibit 50, in the name of "Alex.
ander R. Fraser," and in the words "Rio Tinto" in these accounts,
