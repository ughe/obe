1444. RICHARD FOSSEY was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of
April, 1 willow cutting gauge, value 8s., the goods of Robert Bathurst.
ROBERT BATHURST. I am a willow-cutter, and live in Bacon-street,
Bethpal-green. The prisoner worked for me for nearly two years-on
the morning of the 14th of April, about seven o'clock, I missed a guage,
and found it in pawn in the Hackney-road—I had seen it safe about eight
o'clock in a rack in the workshop-the prisoner had left me about ten
days at that time I sent for him he came to my house, and denied all
knowledge of it-I gave him into custody-this produced is it.
3 THOMAS ROWLEY. I am assistant to Mr. Cotton, a pawnbroker, in the
Hackney-road. On the 14th of April, the prisoner pawned this gauge, in
the name of John Davis-I am sure of him.
HENRY COTTON. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner in charge.
Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing about it.
NOT GUILTY.
