of linen safe in my cart when I paid the toll at Smithfield-Mrs. King called
my attention to the loss of it - went down Long-lane, and saw the prisoner
with it on his head--the policeman stopped him, he threw it down, and ran
away-I picked it up.
Cross-examined. Q. Were yon and Mrs. King both in the cart? A. Yes
- it was seven o'clock at night, and was in a dark place.

conviction-(read-William Matthews convicted Aug., 1845, and confined six

238. MARY ANN SIMPSON, stealing 1 pewter pot, value 1s.; the
goods of Andrew Alexander.
William Howe (policeman, H 168). On 8th Dec. I was called to the
Crooked Billet--the prisoner was given in my charge for stealing this pot-
(produced)- she said some one must have put it into ber basket-I found it
there.
WILLIAM SMITH. I am potman to Mr. Andrew Alexander, of the Crooked
Billet. On 8th Dec., between eight and nine o'clock at night, the prisoner
came and called for a pint of beer, and stood some time at the bar drinking it
-a woman told me something I asked the prisoner what she had in her
basket--she said, "Nothing"-I felt, found the pot, and told my master-the
policeman came and took it out-she said she knew nothing about it, some-
body must have put it there.
Prisoner. I was there all the afternoon, and was very tipsy. Witness.
She was not there above twenty minutes—the pot is worth 1s.-she may bave
been the worse for liquor.
Guilty. Aged 42.- Recommended to mercy.--Confined Seven Days.
