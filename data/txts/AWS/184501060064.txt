and that Payne had been before convicted of felony.
WILLIaM ENGLAND (police-constable T 170.) I was watching the
prosecutors' premises, on the 4th of Jan.- saw the two prisoners
very busy about a wagon which was going out, and going backwards and
forwards from the stable to the wagon-the officer, Mitchell, came up
his beat, and the prisoners came to the gate, and watched him away-
directly he was gone, Maslin led two horses out in front, and Payne drew
the wagon out with one horse-they then hitched the two horses on, and
were proceeding on- we stopped the wagon, and found Solbs. of clover hay

WILLIaM DANIEL MITCHELL (police-constable T 167.) I was with

JAMes TILYER. I am a farmer, and live at Harmondsworth-my bro.
