heard the evidence-I gave my statement to a solicitor-Brown is no

By the COURT. I heard Miss Smith swear that she had seen my
brother in her office when he was at Hertford Market-I did not say
anything because I did not like to disturb the Court, but I can take my
oath my brother was not there.
