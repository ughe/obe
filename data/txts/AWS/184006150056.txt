1615. ROBERT STEVENS was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
May, 12lbs. weight of beef, value 78. 6d., the goods of Henry Webb.
JOHN BRBTT. I am eleven years old, and live with Henry Webb, a
butcher in Church-street, Bethnal-green. On the 10th of May, about
half-past seven o'clock in the evening, the prisoner, who was a stranger,
came for a mutton-chop--he had a kind of a bag in his band-I cut the
chop, and while I was busy he went to the back of the shop-he gave me
6d., I gave him 21d. changehe was walking out with the bag under his
arm, and the policeman stopped him-there was 124lbs, weight of beef
found in the bag-it was my master's, and had been at the back of the
shop, and I then missed it.
