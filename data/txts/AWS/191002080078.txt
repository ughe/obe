With regard to counts 7 to 12. relating to the destruction and mutilation of books,
there was no evidence against the prisoner (assuming the books to have been
intentionally injured) further, there was no sufficient evidence that the books
had been injured other than by an accidental fire. On count 17, 18, and 19,
relating to the goods received from Hawkins, Roberts, and Brown and Co..
there was no evidence to show that the goods had been disposed of other than
in the ordinary way of the prisoner's trade.
