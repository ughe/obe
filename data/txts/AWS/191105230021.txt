LAWRENCE, Frederick (30, fireman), breaking and entering the
shop of Herbert Downs and stealing therein 760 lb.. in weight of tea
and other articles, his property.
Mr. Sydenham Jones prosecuted.
HERBERT DowNs, grocer, 141, Victoria Dock Road, E. On May 2
about 10 p.m. I locked up my shop. Returning next morning about
a.m. the first thing I noticed was that some bins we put tea in had
been removed from the place where they were put overnight. I also
noticed there was tea missing. I sent for 8 constable. On looking
round I found that the warehouse gate had been forced from the top
and side; the bolt was wrenched off. There were marks on the gate
which correspond with the jemmy produced. About 760 lb. tea were
missing, some bottles of Bovril and Oxo, and five dozen tins salmon.
The value would be about E45
CHARLES CRaNE, carman to W. R. White. On May 3 about 7.30
a.m. I was proceeding to 52, Eastfield Street. Before I went there
I see the van drive by me in Ashton Street, where I was inquiring
for a pair of ponies to do my day's work, and sent a man named
Nash for a constable while prisoner and another man were unloading
the tea at 52, Eastfield Street. I daw them carry in four to six bags
of tea. I was standing at the corner and did not want prisoner to see
me and did not see exactly the amount they took in.
