 1246. JOHN GOLIKER was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of April , 9 feet copper pipe, value 3 s  .; the goods of the Gaslight and Coke Company , his masters.
 There being no proof that the Company were entitled to sue by the name stated in the indictment, the prisoner was ACQUITTED.

GUILTY. BARNEY WILLIAM WOOD. I keep a public house in Whitecross-street, Cater was my servant, while she was with me I lost the articles stated—I knew that Butterfield was acquainted with her—she lived in a court a little way from my house—I spoke to Butterfield about this—she said she partook of the property that my servant had taken and brought her, and if I would let her go she would speak the truth—she gave me the duplicates of what she had pledged—she did not deny pledging them.
   Butterfield . I sent for you, and told you I had the tickets—you said you would forgive me, and not sent me to prison, Witness. No, I did not. CATHERINE HANNAH WOOD. I am the prosecutor's daughter. I know the property, but I do not know anything about Butterfield. THOMAS BROOK. I am a pawnbroker.
