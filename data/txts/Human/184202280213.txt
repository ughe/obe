Thursday, the 17th of February—I cannot tell the name of the street, my stable is in, but it is within twenty-three paces of the prisoner's house, for I paced it this morning on purpose to be sure—the prisoner kept the key of that stable for me and my master—I came to town that evening, and it was about half-past nine when I got to the stable—I called at the prisoner's mother's house, where he lives, and saw him—he told me his mother was ill—he gave me the key of the stable—I told him I had an order for him, and money with it—I had been constantly in the habit of coming up to him with money and orders, according to my master's direc  tions—he said he would go and get it the first thing in the morning, it being go late that night, and he had been for the doctor—I afterwards ap pointed to meet him that night at the beer-shop, where I always have my meals, at the top of the lane—I went to the stable, groomed my horse, and then went to the beer-shop to have my supper—the prisoner came to me there at or near upon eleven—I cannot say for a few minutes either way—he remained with me at the beer-shop till within a quarter or ten minutes of twelve—I gave him the money and the order there, and parted from him against my stable-gate, within twenty-three paces of his own house—he went straight to his own house from me. NOT GUILTY.

 1103. JOHN THOMAS was indicted for feloniously assaulting Daniel Bradley , on the   28th of February , with a felonious intent to rob him. DANIEL BRADLEY. I live at No. 4, Wild's-buildings, Borough, in the parish of St. Saviour, Southwark, and work at a hop warehouse. On the night of the 27th of February, I was returning home along Holland-street; a female addressed me and asked where I was going, I said I was going home; she said would I go home with her—I said not, and went on—I turned down the street, and she came down on the same side of the way, as far as a house near which I was attacked; she wanted me to go in there, I refused—she used some words—I said I did not care for her as tong as I behaved myself well—I went about five yards from the place, and three men attacked me, a few yards from the house; one of them came up the street, and struck me a blow in the face—I cannot say where they came from—they said nothing to me—one of them struck me in the face, and the other two attacked me severely—they struggled with me a few moments, till a woman and her husband was passing, and the husband interfered—I knocked the prisoner down, and kept him till the policeman came up—the men were stranger to me—I was sober—the first thing they did was to strike me; and the other two struck me about the body—they said nothing—no attempt was made to take anything from me—they could not overhear any conversation between me and the female.
   Prisoner.  I was coming from my aunt's, and went down Moor-street—I had drunk a little, and ran against him; he struck me in the eye—that made a disturbance between us; two men came and interfered because he kicked me in the gutter.
