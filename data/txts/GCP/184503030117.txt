HENRY SMYTHE. I lodge at Mr. Lynes's. On Thursday evening, the
27th of Feb., I received a 50l., and a 401. note, of Mr. Brown-I placed
them both in my tin cash-box, on the drawers in my bed-room--the box.
was locked, but I had not the key of it in my pocket-I cannot swear
where the key was--on the Friday I took out the 401. note, and left the
501. open in the box-I locked it, and left the key in some part of the
room - I went on Saturday to the box, and the 501. note was gone.
