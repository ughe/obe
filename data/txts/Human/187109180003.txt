 630. DANIEL FEILER  (49) , Unlawfully conspiring with others to obtain a quantity of bricks from the Cowley Brick Company , by means of false pretences.
   MESSRS. POLAND and MONTAGU WILLIAMS conducted the Prosecution; and MR. WARNER SLEIGH the Defence. HERBERT BARLEE. I am manager to the Cowley Brick Company, Limited, at Cowley, near Uxbridge—Mr. Edward Milliard and others are partners in the business—about 11th January I received this letter, marked "A:" ( Read: "11th January, 1871. 1, Beechey Street, Leyton Road, Stratford. Mr. Barlee. Sir. Please inform me price of stocks at Battersea, and do you deliver by barge or monkey boat, and oblige yours respectfully, William Hedges.")—I wrote this reply: "12th January, 1871. Mr. William Hedges. Sir. The price of our stock bricks, delivered at Battersea Wharf, is 28 s . per thousand; grizzels, 3 s . less per thousand. We deliver by barge, not by monkey boat. (Signed) H. Barlee."—On the 13th I received this letter
