HORACE YEARWORTH( Re-examined ). During the time I was in the employ of the firm at Aldersgate Street, Mr. William Saull, of Northampton, used to attend periodically, but the accounts were sent to him monthly—he attended after the partnership expired, and when I left in May or June he would have access to the books, but he had a monthly extract sent to him—I do not remember whether he had the monthly extract after the partnership expired, but he came there and had access to everything. GUILTY Strongly recommended to Mercy by the Jury.. —Six Monty Imprisonment.
