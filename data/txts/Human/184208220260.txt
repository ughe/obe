   MR. PAYNE . Q.  The conversation took place on the Monday, and the quarrel on the Saturday? A.  Yes. GEORGE GOOD( police-sergeant R  2.) The prisoner was given into my custody by Mrs. Burrows, at the coffee-shop, on Tuesday, the 9th of Au gust, for forging a receipt—I asked him to produce the receipt, that I might show it to her—he refused to do so—he went up stairs—I followed him, and Mrs. Burrows came immediately after me—I did not see her till I got into the room—I went in after the prisoner—I asked him to let me see the receipt, so that Mrs. Burrows might see it—he said he was advised not to show it, and would not show it any more—I had seen it before—he afterwards pulled it out, and laid it on the table—while it was on the table, Mrs. Burrows came in—she said that it was a forgery, it was not hers, she had never seen it before till she had seen it at Mr. Smith's office—the prisoner took it off the table and put it into his pocket—I took him into custody, found the receipt on him, took possession of it, and have produced it to-day—the prisoner called a man named Tuck, as a witness, before the Magistrate.
