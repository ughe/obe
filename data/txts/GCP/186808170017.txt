670. CATHERINE ALLEN (3), Feloniously forging and uttering an
order for the payment of 9027. 98. 3d., with intent to defraud.
MR. SERJEANT SLEIGH with MR. POLAND conducted the Prosecution, and
MR. WARNER Sleigh the Defence.
WALTER REED. I am a clerk in the London and Westminster Bank,
St. James's Square branch-Henry Allen opened an acoount there about
18th January, 1864-I cannot give the exact date-on opening the account
he would have to sign this book--I saw him sign it, and I filled up the ad.
dress for him as he said he could not write, that he was not a great scholar,
and would I write it for him -- the address is Latimer Road, Hammersmith
--that would be the ordinary signature on which the account would be
drawn out-I am accustomed to act upon handwriting at the bank-these
two cheques for 7051. 188. 6d. and 9021. 9.. 3d., I think, are not in Allen's
handwriting—in my judgment they are not-these deposit notes (produced
are genuine, as far as I can judge-- there are nine of them.
Cras-examined. Q. Are you perfectly certain of that i A. Yes, they are
genuine-I have no difficulty whatever in saying so they are not exactly
similar one with the other, but they resemble the signature in the signature-
book.

AUGUSTE DELVALL I am a cashier at the St. James's Square Branch of
the London and Westminster Bank-I know that a person of the name of
Henry Allen had a deposit account there I have been in the habit of acting

remember her bringing this deposit receipt for 7002 on the 18th January
she came with 2001. in notes, to be paid to her husband's account--that
