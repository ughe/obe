Q. Then what did you go to him on the second occasion for? TO
ask him if he knew a party named Lawrence—that was what I went to

Q. I ask you before Mr. Middleton, how much were you to get from
him if you succeeded in getting his property back ? A. Nothing at all—
he made me no promise—I did not expect any thing—nothing was said
about any little consideration.
Q. Then you went to this place twice without the expectation of any
thing more than your costs here? A. I left that to the generosity of the
prosecutor—I did not know whether I might get any thing or no.
