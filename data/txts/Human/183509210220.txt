   Q.  Do you know there is a Paddington committee? A.  I do not—there is what is erronecously called a committee, a meeting of the proprie tors of that road—they meet on Thursday nights at the White Lion—I have been at the meeting—It is held in the first floor front room—any one may walk in—there has been no secrecy when I was present—there is very seldom any thing done there at all—sometimes some of the pre prietors would come and tell another that his men had been kincking up a disturbance on the road—It was always settled between the person who made the complaint, and the person whose servants were complained of—I have known of drivers being discharged by the proprietors on account of—their misconduct—I cannot say who it was—the subscription that was paid was half-a-crown a week for each member—It was to pay the time-keepers—there is no other subscription among the proprietors that I know of—the half-crown was not paid there in general—one of the conductors pays for his three omnibuses down Oxford-street—there are several time-keepers—one is deputed to receive the subscriptions, and pay the others—I never knew of any subscrition between my father and the other pro prietors, to support any opposition while Mr. Bray was running his omni buses—I have the sole management of my father's omnibus business, and had it at that time—If there had been any subscription made to oppose Bray, it must have come under my knowledge.
   Cross-examined by MR. SERGEANT BUMPUS . Q.  This which is "er roneously called a committee" meets at the White Lion? A.  Yes—I do not know that I have ever omitted going for the last four months—some times there are only two or three of the proprietors there—It is called the Paddington Committee—I do not know of any jurisdiction that it has—the proprietors who formerly fixed the times go to that meeting—they all go occasionally there—I never knew of any secretary—my father has had an omnibus, called the "Tally Ho," on that road for some time—that was put on against a man's omnibus of the name of Lewis, and run him down last autumn—I know the name of George Craig—I never knew he was a pro prietor—I know Shillibeer personally—I do not remember his being run against—not for six months, I do not—I remember an omnibius with the name of Newman on it; that was run against, and run off, and he sold it to Bolton, I believe—Bolton set up an opposition against him, I believe.

I have seen it before it, and behind, and on the same line of road—It was one of Bolton's buses  —I do not remember the name of it, or who was the driver on conductor—Mrs. Turnbull was a proprictor up to the last nine months—I never remember seeing her name on a bus . nor her attending the commit tee—I did nto attend them rugularly till the last Christmas—I have bees there before—I do not remember seeing her there—I do not remember her having an omnibus run against her.
