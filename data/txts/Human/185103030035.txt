 710. JOHN CARDEN , stealing 1 handkerchief, 1 half-crown, 4 shillings, and 1 sixpence; the property of Thomas Gray , from the person of Ann Gray . MR. CAARTEEN conducted the Prosecution. ANN GRAY, I am the wife of Thomas Gray, a tailor, in Windmill-street. On Saturday night, 28th Dec, about half-past nine o'clock, I was standing at the corner of Wardour-street, looking at some persons quarrelling—I had a half-crown, a sixpence, and four shillings in a hand kerchief in my pocket—it was what I had earned myself—my husband is ill—as I was going home I missed my money and handkerchief.
   Cross-examined by MR. MEW . Q.  Do you live with your husband? A.  Yes; I had a cotton dress and a shawl on, on that occasion—I was not at the place above a quarter of an hour—my handkerchief was white—there were many persons about there. CATHARINE BRITTON.
