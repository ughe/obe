name of Green at Brewers'-quay-I believe the prisoner Green is the
man-I had no written order when I got the last logf the cart-note was
attached to it, underneath the string-it was exactly like this—I took it
back to Mr. Coles and he gave me one of his own cart-notes-- I took the
cart-note that was on the sugar, with the goods, to Ivermee-I cannot re-
collect whether he signed it or Mrs. Ivermee—I can read—to the best of my
recollection this is the note--I left the one I got from Brewers'-quay at Mr.
Ivermee's and the other one I took back to Cole.
