NEW COURT.-Monday, November 18th, 1872.
Before Robert Malcolm Kerr, Esq.
4. JOHN THOMAS (42), PLEADED GUILTY to unlawfully uttering coun-
terfeit coin.-Two Years' Imprisonment. 5. ROBERT WILLIAMS (23),
to unlawfully having counterfeit coin in his possession, with intent to utter
it, after a previous conviction.-Five Years' Penal Servitude. 6. HENRY
WEST (31), to unlawfully obtaining two velvet mantles of William Holloway,
by false pretences. Five Years' Penal Servitude. 7. EDMUND STURDIO
(32), to stealing one coat, the property of Frederick Walker.-Twelve
Months Imprisonment. And 8. EDMUND COOPER ** (60), to stealing
a coat of John Neat Dymond, after a previous conviction.-Seven Years
Penal Servitude.
9. JANE BURT (51), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
Mr. DE MICHELE conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES HILLS. I am shopman to George Nicholls, a draper, of Brixton
Road-on 16th July I served the prisoner with some buttons and pins,
which came to 3d.she gave me a bad florin-I gave it to Mr. Nicholls.
GEORGE NICHOLLS. The last witness brought me this coin-I found it
was bad-I asked the prisoner where she got it she said she was sent by
her mistress, Lady Montague, of Wynn Road, Brixton-I said that no such
person lived there she then said that she picked it up-I marked it, and
took her myself to the station, and gave the coin to the sergeant- this is it
(produced)here is my mark on it.
