THOMAS JAMES (prisoner, on oa:h). My friend and me went out at
seven in the evening to have a drink. We went into the Horse and
Gro m, Holloway Road, and stopped there till we could not get any
more beer. It was nearly turning-out time. We came out and went
up the road to the Swan, near the Highbury Road Picture Palace ;
then these two constables claimed me, for what I don't know. I
thought I was to be charged with being drunk. I was not two minutes'
walk from home.
ARTHUR JONES (prisoner, on oath). I have a letter here from the
managing director of the firm for whom I have been working and
be gives me a good character. I was never in trouble before of any
sort. I went with this chap to the Horse and Groom, and when we
came out I saw a constable get hold of him and then one came for
me. We had had a good deal to drink, but I have never done any-
thing dishonest. I did not touch prosecutor, and I did not see what
the other man did.
