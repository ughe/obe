THOMAS WILSON. I was a seaman on board the Old England-on the
Saturday night before we left her, I was at the pumps about 8 o'clock--they
sucked-after that I was at the wheel—I had no course given me, only to
keep company with the brig; it was fine weather, and there was not much
sea---we left the ship about 4 o'clock on Sunday--the water was then about
half way up to the white streak outside--she was deep in the water, and
seemed to be sinking--I cannot say the height of the white streak from the
water when she was in proper condition.
DANIEL Davis. I am a labourer-I coaled the ship with patent fuel,
which was tapered down towards the keel, fore and aftshe was not decked,
and they laid the fuel between the beams—there was a between-deck for.
ward and a little aft.
COURT. Q. Do you understand ships ! 4. Yes; I cannot tell you what

deck beams was 8 or 9 ft.-I never measured the upper-deck-I should
say that was 7 ft. more; it is generally a foot less to the between-deck
beams.

ABRAHAM POPHAM. I represent the West of England Assurance Company
-I produce a policy, dated May 17th, 1869, on the captain's effects, for 2001.,
for twelve months from the date of suiling from Cardiff-Mr. Hinson's clerk
afterwards came to me and said, "You must hold that policy over, we will do it
from London to Cardiff"--the premium paid was six guineas-that is an ordi-
nary amount for a captain's effects-I thought it rather large, and asked Mr.
Ellis, who said that the captain was part owner-I saw the captain after the loss
of the ship, and said I thought it was a very stupid affair on his part, and
a very bad job, and I said, "Worse than that, losing your log-book, captain;
what did you do with that "-be said it was all done without a thought,
and I think he said he gave it to the brig, and left it on board; I forget
exactly I had very little conversation with him-he said that he thought
a new log would look better than the old one, and I said I had rather have
seen the old one-I have been a captain myself-he then brought in a
statement of his effects, which he had saved, but he brought no log at all.
