481. GEORGE EDWARD NASH (24), was again indicted with
CHARLES CROSS (23) and FREDERICK EVERLEIGH (26) (see page 575),
for feloniously forging ang uttering a request for the payment of 21. with
intent to defraud.
MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS conducted the Prosecution, MR. DOUGLAS MSTOALYE
appeared for Cross, and Mr. CHARLES MATHEWS for Everleigh
EDWARD MONTAGUE GLADWIN. I am manager to Mr. Robert Ford, pro
prietor of the Forresters' Music Hall, Cambridge Road-on 19th August this
letter was handed to me by one of the servants in an envelope addressed to
Mr. Ford-I went into the lobby and there saw Cross -I asked him what
he was waiting for-he said for an answer to a letter-I said “Who from"
--he said "From Mr. Fayer"_I asked him what he wanted-he said he
had to take back either 21. or an I 0 U-I gave him 21. and then asked for
his name and address-he said " Charles Cross, 4, Bentley Terrace, Kings-
land," and I wrote that at the back of the letter-I have seen Nash in the
hall as a customer several times---Mr. Fayer was a singer at the hall at this
time.
Cross-examened by Nash. I can't say that I had seen you at the hall for
a month or two before, or while Mr. Fayer was singing there.
WILLIAM BURNHAM FAYER. I am a comedian, and live at 393, Kenning-
ton Road - on 19th August I was engaged at the Forrester's Masio Hall-I
know Everleigh by sight-only-I did not write this letter or IOU-I have
seen Cross; I did not authorise him to give my name or deliver this letter;
or Everleigh either, or to receive any money on my account-I never saw
Nash to my knowledge.
