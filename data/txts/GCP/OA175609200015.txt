WIL LI A M C A N N ICOTT,
Who was Executed at Tyburn, on Monday, September 20, 1756,
For the Murder of his Wife, on Tueſday the 20th of July, 1756.
Written by Himſelf, while Confined in Newgate.
In order to prevent the Publick from being impoſed on by falſe and Spurious Accounts,
both of me and the unfortunate Occaſion of my Suffering, as well as the Motives
that urged me on to commit the atrocious Crime, I bave delivered with my own Hana
Writing, a juſt Account thereof, in which I bave juſtly and truly informed the Pub-
lick of the whole Progreſs of that ſad and wicked Tranſaction, from the Beginning
to its final and fatal Catoſtrophe, which no other Perſon could do : And that, at
my own Requeſt, I defire it may be publiſhed the Morning ſucceeding my unbappy
End, by Mrs. Judith Walker, the Publiſher, at the Golden Key in Fleet-Lane,
declaring most ſolemnly, as I muſt ſoon anſwer the contrary, at the great Judgment
Seat of Almighty God, before whom I muſt soon appear, that I have delivered no
other Account to any Perſon whatever to be publiſhed.
Prefs-Yard, Newgate, Sept. 17, 1756.
WILLIAM CANNICOTT,
Witneſs, WILLIAM Voce.
