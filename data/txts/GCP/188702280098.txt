By MR. WILLIS. He did not ask me my name-I had given my name

-I was sent there by the Magistrate for not going to school-it was not

hopping.
JOSEPH CHAMP (The Prisoner). I am just turned 14-in the middle of
June I lived at 6, Trinity Street, Blackheath Hill-I was looking for
work, and met a boy named Evans and we went to 15, Park Place, Green-
wich, an empty house, belonging to the South-Eastern Railway, and took
& copper from there, and some lead from the lavatory-we took them to
the prisoner Robinson on the Friday before I was taken on Saturday-ho
gave us 38. for the copper and 6d. for the lead-he asked us if anyone
saw us bring it in-we said “No”-we shared the money-I was not
with the other boys on Thursday, when the lead was taken to Robinson's.
Cross-eramined. I did not take the lead from the house in Eastoombe
Terrace-I was not with them on the Thursday, it was Friday that I
went to Robinson's with the copper, and on Saturday I was taken-I had
not been to Robinson's on the Thursday.
HENRY DAVID SMART. I am station-master at Maze Hill Railway
Station--on 12th January I was called to 15, Park Place, and found that
& copper and some lead had been taken away-the copper was fitted to
the place.
Cross-examined. I have known Robinson a number of years, and as far
as I know he is a respectable man, but he has not come in contact
with me.
ROBINSON received a good character. GUILTY.-Six Months' Hard
Labour. NEVILLE, KILMAN, and CHAMP. -Four Months' Hard
Labour each.
