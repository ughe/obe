WILLIAM COOTE. I am a carpenter, and live at Bethnal-green. The
prisoner lodged a month with me-on the 25th of June I quarrelled with
my wife, and when I went home at eight o'clock that night, the prisoner
was gone, with my wife, and all the articles stated I went with the police
man to No. 1, Tarling-street-we went up stairs and knocked, and the
prisoner came to the door in his shirt-I saw the articles in the room-I
said, "These are my goods, and I will have them"-the prisoner said, "I
will see you d d first" I went in and found my wife undressed, and
my child was asleep on the bed.
