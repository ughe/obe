1539. MOSES MOSES was indicted for a misdemeanor.
NOT GUILTY.
1540. HENRY ROBERTS was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of
May, 1 handkerchief, value 1s. 6d., the goods of John Wills, from his
person.
John Wills. I am a hosier, and live in the Poultry. On the evening
of the 24th of May I was in Newgate-street--I felt a twitch at my pocka
- I turned round, and collared the prisoner-there was no one else ne
me within four or five yards-no one could have given me the twitch but
him-I picked up my handkerchief at his feet, and dragged him along to
the Post-office, where I gave him into custody- I did not see him drop
my handkerchief.
Prisoner. I did not take the handkerchief; there were two young me
who walked on forwards, and one of them had a smock-frock on; this
gentleman turned and took me; I said, “I have not picked your
pocket." Witness. I saw no one near me.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Three Months.
1541. JOHN WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of May,
3 spoons, value 308., the goods of John Reader.
EDWARD MURRAY. I am waiter at the Crown Tavern, Bow-lane. On
the evening of the 21st of May the prisoner came to the coffee-room-!
got him some brandy and water-he gave me a card to go to Mr. Sawyer
for him, which I did --I had collected my plate, and put it into a basket on
the table--I suspected I had lost some, and missed three spoons- I went
to my employer, and they were found in the prisoner's pocket-be
did not say any thing-he was not sober-these are the spoons.
Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. Had not one of the spoons
gravy in it? 4. Yes—he had put it in that state into his pocket-I should
say he scarcely knew what he was about, or he would not have put a
spoon with gravy in it in his pocket-I was gone on the errand for bin
about three or four minutes--there was nothing to have prevented his
going off with these things during my absence.
John READER. I was present when these spoons were found in the
prisoner's pocket--they are mine.
Cross-examined. Q. Have you heard your waiter's evidence? 4.
Yes--I believe the prisoner was inebriated -- he made no answer when be
was taken-I believe he hardly knew what he was about.
NOT GUILTY.
