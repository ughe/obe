Later COUNSEL informed the  court that the prisoner had signed a document which was satisfactory to the prosecution. The  court held that as there was no proof that the Married Women's Property Act ever took effect in Melbourne the presumption was that the Common Law took effect in that Colony, and there  fore in this case there was no legal defrauding of the wife.
