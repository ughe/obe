182. JAMES BROOKS (24), Robbery on Dennis Hickey, and stealing
138. 6d. his money, MR. LANGFORD conducted the Prosecution.
DENNIS HICKEY. I am a bricklayer's labourer, and live at 3, George-
street, St Giles-on Thursday night, 24th December, I was at a public-house,
at the corner of Pancras-street-I was by myself—the prisoner was there
I left the public-house about 11 or a quarter-past-I was not tipsy, and I
was not sober; I had a drop taken—I came out of the house by myself-
the prisoner followed me out, and followed me to the corner of Mortimer-
market, the next corner to the public-house—he then tripped me, and
knocked me down, and when I was down he kicked me in the hip, and my
hip has been very queer since he collared me, put his hand in my pocket,
and took 138 6d. from me ; there were two half-crowns, three two-shilling
pieces, two shillings, and one sixpence- constable came up and took him
into custody-the prisoner was drawing his hand out of my pocket when
the constable caught him-the money was safe in my pocket when I left the
public-house.
