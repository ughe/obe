Mr. RECORDER; Mr. Ald. FARNCOMB; and Mr. Ald. CHALLIS,

Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury.
435. HENRY SLAPE, Jun., was indicted for unlawfully obtaining
2 geldings, price 631, and I chaise, value 201. ; the property of Samuel
Lazarus Lawrence, by false pretences.
Nor Guilty.
436. WILLIAM CARTER, stealing 1 pewter pot, value 1s. ; the goods
of David Hughes: also 1 pewter pot, 1s. ; the goods of James Wyatt: also
1 pewter pot, 18. 6dr; the goods of William Parish: to all of which he
pleaded Guilty. Aged 25.-Confined Nine Months.
437. KENNETH SUTHERLAND, embezzling 9 sums of money,
amounting to 601. 185.; the moneys of Charles Fisher Wace, bis master : to
which be pleaded Guilty. Aged 36.-Confined Eighteen Months.
The prosecutor stated his loss to be 2001.
438. GEORGE GAMBLE, stealing 14 pieces of brass fittings, value 58.;
the goods of John Henry Machu, his master: to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Six Months.
439. JOHN NICHOLLS and CHARLES MOULDING, stealing 4lbs.
weight of cheese, value 2s. ; the goods of Thomas Pillow, in a barge on the
Thames: to which
NICHOLLS pleaded Guilty.* Aged 17.-Confined Four Months.
HENRY MILLER. I am a watchman, in the employ of Thomas Pillow.
On 27th Dec. I watched the barge Luke all night, and left her at the Wap-
ping entrance of the London Dock-I returned about twelve o'clock in the
day, and found the heads of two casks knocked in, and missed some cheese
from them, which was in Mr. Pillow's care.
JONAS WEBB. Between eleven and twelve o'clock in the day I was at
Wapping Old-stairs, and saw the prisoner and another on the barge-Nicholls
was cutting cheese out of one of the casks-Moulding jumped on sbore, and
put some cheese into his breast-I told an officer.
William Thomas BRIDGES (Thames-police-inspector). I took the prisoners
