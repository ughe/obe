a

might be an hour and a quarter, and then I came back again to Chureh's-
he was there when I went in, and when I left-I might have stayed there
half an hour, or a little more.
Oross-ecamined. On the Monday night I was not 80 bad from having too

JBAN BAPTISTE DUPUIs. I am a cabinet maker of 35, Manchester Street,
Latimer Road, Notting Hill-Thomas Munt married my sisteron 2nd
Mareh I went to the Rising Sun with him ; before that I went and had a
cup of tea with him-it was about ten minutes past six when we got to
the Rising Sun; I saw Porter and a female-Mrs. Porter and two ladies came
in afterwards-I don't know their names-Chureh was there opening the
door as we went in-I stopped there about two and a half hours-I then
left-Church was there the whole time and when I left; I know that
because I spoke to him and he to me.
Cross-eamined. Only my wife, her sister, and her husband, and
myself were at tea-Mr Web met us at the beershop-we went home
from Shepherd's Bush Station at half-past nine-I was first spoken to about
this about three weeks afterwards--Mrs. Church's brother asked my brother
if he recollected being at Chureh's on Sunday, 2nd Mareh, and he said yes;
and he asked where I lived and they came to me together to talk the
matter overhe asked me if I recolleeted being at tea at Munt's and being
at the Rising Sun, and seeing Chureh there-he did not ask me the time
I left-I knew at that time that Churalr was in custody-Mrs. Munt did
not say anything to me about itI have heard and read about the Richmond
murder-I don't remember Mrs. Munt speaking about it.
Re-examined. I believe it was between four and five on 30th March
that Mr. Chureh's brother and Munt same to me-I then remembered very
well that it was on 2nd Mareh that I had lieen to the Rising Sun.
