1808. WILLIAM BRYANT, stealing 1 purse, value 6d.; 1 sixpence,
1 penny, and 1 halfpenny; the property of Eliza Granville : from her
person.
Eliza GranviLLE. I am single. On 3rd Sept., I was landing from a
steamboat at London Bridge, and missed my purse, which I had safe in my
pocket a minute before I saw the prisoner before me in the crowd-he felt
down over a step, and I saw my purse between his fingers—I called out-
the captain caught him-he got away from him, and the policeman stopped
him--there was 6d., 1d., and d. in the purse--this is it (produced).
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. What boat was it ? A. The Anit,
halfpenny boat-it was very crowded--the people pass out over a board -
the prisoner was about two yards from me when I saw the purse in bis hand
-there were a great many people between me and him--when he got up
again be commenced running-I did not see the purse thrown away-I am a
vervant out of place-Esther Bull was with me.
ESTHER BULL. I was with Granville- I heard her call out, and saw the
purse in the prisoner's hand-he threw it down, and I picked it up, and
gave it to her again-this is it-the prisoner was caught.
Cross-examined. Q. How long have you known Granville ? A. Two
years, I am single--I am a servant, but have been out of place three weeks
-I was on the pier when the prisoner threw the purse I cannot say whe-
ther it was the captain, or any one else who caught the prisoner, because I
stooped to pick the purse up-I have no family- I was in St Giles' Work-
house last Christmas I had a child there, but it is dead-I have had two,
but they are both dead—I was three months in my last place I have not
been in many places.
ANDREW Smith. I am master of the Ant steamboat-I recollect the
prosecutrix being on board on 3rd Sept.--when we came to London Bridge
I heard a cry, saw the prisoner running, and caught him on the pier.
Cross-examined. o. It is rather difficult to run away from the Ant, is it
not? 4. No; he was very nimble-there may have been a step to go up
to London-bridge it is a moveable platform-it was not low-water time,
perhaps about balf up.
Guilty. Aged 18.
