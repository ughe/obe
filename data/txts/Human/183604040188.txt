 1039. JOHN CREED was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of April , 4 pairs of sheets, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Joseph Bark . JOSEPH CORPSE. I am servant to Joseph Bark of Shadwell. On the 24th of April, between four and five o'clock in the afternoon, an opposite neighbour gave me information that a boy had taken four pairs of shoes—I has been absent about five minutes—he brought the prisoner to me with the shoes—these are them—they had hung at the door post. ENNEIS M'ALLEN. I am a policeman. The prisoner was given into my charge with the shoes. CHARLES CRAVENER. I live directly opposite the prosecutor—I looked out at my door, and saw the prisoner receive these shoes in his pinafore from another boy opposite the shop—they both ran off—I pursued, and brought the prisoner back with them—the other boy was rather less than him. GUILTY. Aged 11— Whipped and Discharged.
 1040. WILLIAM HIGGINSON was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of April , 1lb. of bacon, value 7 d  ., the goods of Joseph Russell . JOSEPH RUSSELL. I am a cheesemonger, and live in High-street, Shad well. On the 7th of April, about ten o'clock in the day, the prisoner passed my window, and put his hand on the shopboard with his apron own it—a person said he bad taken a bit of bacon—I ran after him, and caught him with it in his hand, four or five yards off—I gave him in charge.
   Prisoner's Defence . A boy came running by me, and threw the bacon into the road, and ran off—Mr. Russell laid hold of me—a gentleman said, "It is not him, the boy is gone on"—he immediately said he would make me pay for it.
   MR. RUSSELL . It is not tune—he had the bacon is his hand when I collared him, and he threw it the road. GUILTY.* Aged 16— Transported for Seven Years.
