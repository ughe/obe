 165. MARIA SCOTT was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of Nov ., 5 oz. weight of pork, value 2½ d  ., the goods of William Gunston . WILLIAM GUNSTON. I keep a shop in Ex mouth-street. Between eleven and twelve o'clock on the 22nd of Nov. the prisoner came for a pork chop—she took two, and dropped one in her lap—she had one weighed, and then went out—I sent my boy after her, and this other one was found on her.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  You saw her take this one? A.  Yes—the other came to 3 d .—she said, when she came back, that she did not know what possessed her to take it. NOT GUILTY.
 166. THOMAS TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the   29th of Nov ., 6lbs, weight of beef, value 3 s  .; and part of a cooked hare, 2 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of James Belcher . JAMES BELCHER. I keep the Barclay Arms, at Cranford. On the morning of the 29th of Nov., about half-past eight o'clock, a coach-load of people and a van-full came to my house, they went into my kitchen, and pulled all the things about there, and others of them went up stairs—I called the servant, but those who were in the kitchen would not let her out, while others were plundering the larder—the prisoner was one who was there—there was a piece of beef, a cooked hare, (almost a whole one,) and three quartern loaves, taken out of the house—they paid for the liquor which they drank, but not for what they took out of the cupboard—they did not ask for the beef or the hare, which I missed—this is the beef—it is mine.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  Did you notice the prisoner there? A.  Yes, and served him with rum—there were thirty or forty persona al together—I understood they were going to a fight—they were going from Lon don—my maid-servant is not here—there was no money paid to her—she could not get out of the kitchen—the money that WM  paid for liquor was paid to me at the bar.
