 1046.  GEORGE HAWKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of March , 1 handkerchief, value 6d.; 1 jacket, value 2s.; 1 waistcoat, value 3d.; 1 petticoat, value 1s.; 4 yards of cotton, value 2s.; and 1 shirt, value 1s; the goods of Elizabeth Webster.
 ELIZABETH WEBSTER. I live at  Islington, and am a widow. I keep a mangle: the prisoner is my nephew - he is a painter, but was out of work: I have not seen him for some time - I missed these articles about the 31st of March; I spoke to the prisoner, though I had no suspicion of him; he owned it directly, and said he did it though hunger.
   JAMES TAYLOR. I am a constable. I took the prisoner at a coffee-shop; the person who keeps that shop produced this petticoat, which she said she had of the prisoner; he told me he sold this handkerchief for four-pence, in Steven-street; he took me to Mrs. Warley's, in the Harrow-road, where he said he had sold the jacket and waistcoat for one shilling.
   MARIA WHITE. I keep a coffee-shop, at No. 31, Great James-street, Lisson Grove; the prisoner sold me this petticoat, an old shirt, and a bit of calico; he came to me at eight o'clock in the morning, and said he was straving: I said I had no money - he said he wanted no money if I would let him have a shilling's worth of victuals, and let him have the rest at night; I bought them of him for two shillings - they were not worth the money - the calico was not worth five-pence a yard when new.
   CATHERINE WALLER. I live in Harrow-road, and keep a clothes shop, I bought an old jacket and a waistcoat of the prisoner for one shilling - the jacket was very old; I sold them in about three days for eighteen-pence.
   CHARLES COTTLE. I am a constable: I went with Taylor: I have part of the petticoat, which I got when I went to the coffee-shop the second time.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 GUILTY. Aged 18. Recommended to Mercy. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 1047.  JOHN CHENNER was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of April , 1 coat, value 30s.; 3 waistcoats, value 10s.; 2 handkerchiefs, value 10s.; two shirts, value 6s.: 1 pair of trousers, value 8s.: and 4 pairs of stockings, value 4s.; the goods of William Evans.
 WILLIAM EVANS. I am a  labourer; I had a bundle, containing all this property, and was going, on the 10th of April, to Cirencester, I was at Brentford trying to go by Tanner and Bailey's waggon - I gave the bundle to the prisoner, to carry it to the waggon - I had not known him before; I was to follow - but he ran faster than I could, to stop the waggon; it was about a half-past nine o'clock at night - I was following, and I met two men, who put me in the wrong road; I thought the prisoner went towards the waggon; I should have paid him if I could have found him.
   JESSE FULLER. I am constable. About a half-past eleven o'clock at night I met the waggon near Brentford Bridge; the prosecutor came and told me he had been robbed, and a young man told me he had seen the prisoner running away from the waggon; I enquired and found the prosecutor had been drinking with him - he was a little the worse for liquor; I went to Old Brentford and found the prisoner in bed - I saw the woman whom he lives with at the door; I went up to the prisoner, and asked for the bag which he took from a pensioner; he said, "What bag" - I said, "If you don't produce it I shall take you out of the house;" I pressed him and he fetched it and gave it me - he was quite sober; I have known him a long time.
   JOHN LAWLER. I am a gardener; about twenty minutes before eleven o'clock that night, I went into the Castle public-house, to have a pint of beer - I saw the prisoner, the prosecutor, and some others in the tap-room - they made some movement - the prisoner took the bundle, and ran out; the other man went to the prosecutor, and said, "Come, you must go, you can't lodge here tonight." They all rushed out, and the prisoner went quite in a contrary way to the waggon - I told the constable, and went with him to the prisoners.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. He put the bundle into my hand; that is all I have to say.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. Transported for Seven Years.
