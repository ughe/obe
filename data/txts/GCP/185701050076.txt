ESSEX CASE.
Before Mr. Recorder.
233. JAMES CRAWLEY was indicted for stealing 1 pair of trowsers,
value 88.; the goods of James Stephen Grove.
Mr. Doyle conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY GILES. I am assistant to James Stephen Grove, in High-street,
Stratford. On 18th Dec. I saw the prisoner pull down a pair of trowsers
which were hanging in the doorway, and go away with them under his arm
-I pursued him--he saw me, and threw them away behind an archway-
I caught him, and gave him into custody-these are the trowsers (produced).
Cross-examined by Mr. PLATT. 2. What time was this! . About 5
o'clock in the afternoon, it was not dark, we had the gas alight-I actually
saw the prisoner pull the trowsers down, I was near the doorway-the
prisoner was sober when I caught him, he afterwards pretended to be drunk,
and had to be taken on a stretcher to the station.
THOMAS ADDINGTON (policeman, K 189). The prisoner was given into
my charge by Giles- I believe he had been drinking a little, but he pre-
tended to be quite drunk and senseless, he threw himself down and refused
to move, and we got a stretcher and took him to the station, he was very
violent. GUILTY.** Aged 17.--Confined Twelve Months.
