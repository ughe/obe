MATILDA BAXTAB. I am single, and lodge in the front parlour, at 46,
Molyneaux-street, Marylebone. On 5th Jan, the prisoner came to lodge in
the baek parlour there on Wednesday evening, 12th Jan., about six o'clock,
I locked my door, took the key down into the kitchen, and put it on the
dresser-I left my things all safe in the room. I slept in the kitchen that
night with the landlady, Mrs, Derrett-I went into my room again, about
half-past eleven the next day I found the door locked as I had left it-I
missed & dress from behind the door, and a box containing a quantity of
books and linen, a Bible and Prayer-book, six large night-gowns, ten towels,
a quantity of night-caps and collars, a sovereign, a shilling, and a sixpence-
there is a door between the front and back parlour, which was shut and
bolted when I left the room--when I came back it stood half open-it had
been forced-the lock was forced back and the bolt broken I saw the prisoner
next Monday, about half-past seven-she had a pair of black silk stockings,
a chemise, marked "Matilde," and a pair of drawers on, all mine, all this
property (produced) belongs to me.
