I found nothing that it is at all necessary you should be acquainted with; I examined them with reference to arsenic—I did not examine them before—I examined the bottles containing the viscera—I first examined the uterus, I did not analyse it, but examined it; and I concur in all that Mr. Barwell has said respect ing it—it was in an impregnated state; I agree with what Mr. Barwell says, between the fifth and seventh week, as nearly as I could judge—I next examined the oesophagus or gullet;
