74. GEORGE SMITH, stealing 12 feet of wood, value 74,; the goods of
Henry Edward Wardell.
JOHN WALKER. On 15th Nov., about 6 o'clock in the eyening, I was at
New Charlton, in the Manor Way, acting as watchman to Mr. Smith's
premises he is a contractor, and there is timber and wood on his premises
--I saw the prisoner coming from the premises, with a plank on his
shoulder, and I was going towards them-I knew him before, and he knew
that I was the watchman-it was a foggy night, and he could not see me
before he came near me he could not see five yards before him-I said,
“Halloo, what have you got there !"--he said, “I am going to Mr. Fuller's,
the coal merchant's, and have got 6d. to take this there"-I followed bim
some distance he put it down, and said, "Let us go and have some beer;
it does not come from your place"-I refused, and detained it.
