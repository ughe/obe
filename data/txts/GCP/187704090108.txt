Letter 73 was from Bale to Mrs. Bale, from Rotterdam, enquiring after his
children, and stating : "No doubt you have heard the three of us were
apprehended here last Saturday morning, I do not expect to leave here for a
month or more, on account of the extradition treaty. Letter 74 was in
pencil said to be Benson's, dated : " Friday. The bearer has been confined
with your husband and myself in the prison in Rotterdam, will you please
send this on to Mrs. Alice, and ask her to let it reach Dr. Fuffer or Mrs
Twentyman immediately; when Dr. F. has read that note he had better
make arrangements for some one to meet the bearer, &c. Dr. Fuffer will
know this handwriting." Letter 74 sent from Rotterdam, was to Scrogger,
requesting him to send a few pounds on Fuffer's account by Post Office Order,
as for 71., they could live for a month on the debtor's side of the prison, where
they would have meat, dec., signed F. K. The Scotch telegrams were one from
Yongs, Bridge of Allan, January 10th, 1876, to A. Miller, Post Office, Burnt
