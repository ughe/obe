788. WILLIAM FRANCE (18), Stealing 36 rabbit skins of the goods
of Joseph Coulden and another his masters, and FREDERICK WILLIAM
CAIN (18) and ELIZABETH SHENSTONE receiving the same know-
ing them to be stolen. FRANCE and CAIN PLEADED GUILTY.
MESSRS. THORNE, COLE, and DOUGLAS METCALYE conduoted the Prosecution;
and MR. SERJEANT BALLANTINE and MR. CHARLES MATHEWS defended
SHENSTONE
GEORGE WESTWOOD (City Detective). On the 11th inst. I went, in
company with constables Osborne and Maroni and Mr. Coulden, the
prosecutor, to Shenstone's shop, 83, Goswell Road-I went in alone-I
should call it an ordinary manufacturing fur-shop-I saw Mrs. Shen-
stone there, and afterwards the others came in-I said to Cain, in the
presence of Shenstone, "Is this the person to whom you sold the
skins?"-he said "Yes"-I then said to Shenstone "Have you bought
any skins of this lad?”-she said “Yes”-I said “How many?"-
she said “It might be two or three dozen”-I said “How much did
you give for them ?”-she said "18. each "-she pointed to a shelf
in the shop, and a person in the shop fetched down a large quantity
of prepared skins, some of these produced)-she and her son sorted
out three dozen-she said “There they are; I don't know whether
they are all Mr. Coulden's"-I then said "Do you know this lad ?"
(pointing to Cain)-she said "No"-I afterwards said "Don't you
know what he is ?”- she said “No: a furrier. I thought." or "I think."
I won't be sure which I said "Have you bought any more?”- she
said "No"-I said "I want two dozen and nine more"-she said "I
have not bought any more"-I said “We are going to take these
away (Referring to the three dozon skins), and you will be charged with
receiving them knowing them to be stolen ”-she said “Oh, the vaga-
bond! I wish I had never seen them”-she said to Mr. Coulden "How
many are there, how much are they?”-he said "I cannot have any.
thing to do with it”-she then went to the back of the shop with
the other officer, Osborne-I afterwards searched the shop, and brought
away another ten dozen skins similar to these.
Cross-examined. I should think Shenstone went to the back of the
shop with Osborne to put her shawl and bonnet on, and to see that she
did not run away-they were out of my sight-I have no idea whether
they were alone together-it was she who mentioned Mr. Coulden's name
-I had not mentioned it, I am quite sure-Mr. Coulden was in the shop
all the time.
