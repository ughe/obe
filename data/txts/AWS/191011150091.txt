saW the prisoner with another man leave 9, Temple Street. I followed

thing bright from his vest pooket. He appeared to observe me, and
walked across the road; the other man ran away. I eanght hold of

in consequence of what he said I left prisoner and went into 39, St.

prisoner in, searched him, and found four counterfeit sixpences (dated
