1565. MICHAEL BRYAN, unlawfully assaulting police officers in the
execution of their duty.
Richard MARTIN (policeman, N 175.) On 10th June, about half-pest
twelve o'clock in the day, I was in High-street, Islington, and was called by
a female, who charged the prisoner with stealing three umbrellas, and a
him in charge-he swore he would not go to the station, and struck me
the mouth and chest-he got away and ran down George-yard - I did not
think it prudent to follow, but sent for assistance-two other men came.and
we all three went and found him at his door, with a poker--I rushed at him
-he hit me a sharp blow with it-my hat saved me-he slightly broke the
skin of my head-be struck me once or twice again-I closed with him-
other constables came, and after a desperate struggle we got him out, and
were then compelled to send to the station for the night duty men--he wa
drunk.
MATTHEW SHOESMITH (policeman, N 122.) I went to Martin's assist-
ance into George-yard, and saw the prisoner at his door, with a poker-he
struck Martin with it, and went in--we followed him-I tried to rescue
Martin from his grasp-he kicked me violently-another constable care
-we got bim up, and he kicked me again, knocked me down, and kicked
me under the right eye-other policemen came, and we got him to the
station.
Thomas GOODERHAN (policeman, N 433.) I saw the prisoner at his door,
with a poker-he struck Martin-- I went in and received several severe kicks
from the prisoner in different parts of my body, and have felt it ever since.
Prisoner's Defence. I said I knew nothing about the umbrellas, and ran
down a court; I was drunk, and resisted; they cut two wounds in my leg
and one in my shin; the doctor attended me twice; they tore my clothes so
much that they were obliged to give me these I have on. (The prisoner here
showed his leg, which was dreadfully wounded, and he had two wounds on his
head.)
RICHARD MARTIN re-examined. He received no blows before he used the
poker--we were obliged to strike him in our own defence.
GUILTY. Aged 29.-Connfied Four Months.
