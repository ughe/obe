WILLIAM GEORGE COLE, 76, Forest Street, Forest Gate, carman. I
have lived next door to prisoner with my parents twelve months: he was
there when we came. There is a wooden fence dividing the two
gardens. When I came home to my dinner on Sunday, August 15.
mother stood in the kitchen crying. I went into the back garden.
and standing on a chair told prisoner if he did not stop calling my
mother filthy names I should put a stop to it. He had a jug in
his hand and threw the contents into my face, saying, "Take that
you bastard." I was seen by a doctor and my father sent for the
police. I was not seriously injured.
