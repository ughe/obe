Emma Harvey. I live at 41, Ashdown Street, Kentish Town, and am eleven
years old--on Saturday evening, 9th April, I was sent on a message for my
mother-on my way I met the prisoner-he said “Will you go and get me
a half-quartern loaf, and I will give you a penny when you come baok"
Emily Scott was with me-the prisoner gave me a florin, and I went to Mr.
Philby's for the loaf, gave him the florin, and he spoke to me and went over
to the prisoner-I pointed him out-Mr. Philby asked the prisoner why he
gave it to me, and he denied giving it to me-I am quite sure he is the man
who gave it to me.
Cross-examined. Q. What time was this A. Between 7 and 8 o'clock-
1 had seen the prisoner before, up and down our street--the man had movod
when I came out of the shop, he had gone round the corner--there was no
woman there--he was alone--he called a woman up after, and she was taken
to the station, too.
