Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. When Carroll was crossing
the road to avoid the blows, he afterwards fell ? A. Yes I did not my.
self observe what made him fall at the time he fell the prisoner was a few
yards from him, and when I afterwards saw him bleeding the prisoner was
in custody.
