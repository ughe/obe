WILLIAM MARCHANT. I am a police-inspector. On the 10th of Jan. Mr. Warrener gave me five keys, which I gave to Burt—I saw Howell searched at the station, and some keys found on him—these are them—I locked them up at the station, and next morning, between ten and eleven o'clock, I tried one of them myself to the room No. 6—I got that key from the cupboard—I returned it to the cupboard again—I kept it till I gave it up to Burt.
   Cross-examined. Q.  When did you give it him? A.  On the Saturday, between ten and eleven o'clock, I think—I gave it to him at the Mansion-house, to produce before the Lord Mayor—I had seen Burt find them on Howell—I locked them up—the key of the cupboard was in the station—the station clerk had charge of the lock-up place as well as me.
