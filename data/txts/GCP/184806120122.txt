HENRY CARR. I am a turnkey in the Qneen's Prison, and superintendent
of the night-watch. On 17th May the prisoner was confined there as a Crow
prisoner, among the third class of debtors-in consequence of something
that occurred about half-past eight o'clock, I went with Dennis and Martin,
two other officers, to his room to search for spirits-Dennis said he had come
to search the room by order of the Governor-the prisoner said, "Search," or
4 Search away"-Dennis went to one corner of the room and I to another-
the prisoner said to Dennis, “ You have been annoying my family for some
time"-he directly laid hold of Dennis by the collar and struck him-I went
to his assistance, and be struck me also-he then made a rush to the oppo
site side of the room, where there was a cupboard, snatched up a knife in one
hand, and a knife and fork in the other-I followed close after him, supposing
it was his intention to destroy some spirits-when I saw the knife in one
hand, and the knife and fork in the other, I rather shrunk back, and be
rushed at Dennis--I ran towards him and attempted to lay hold of him, and
he stabbed me in the left wrist-I have not been able to use my arm since-
Major Hawkes and the prisoner's wife and daughter were in the room.
