JAMES HARTLEY. I live in John-street, Crutched Friars. I have more
than one partner-I am proprietor of the Iron-gate wharf-the steam ves-
sel called the City of Limerick belongs to us-it goes from Dublin to
London, calling at Falmouth-Messrs. Carne are our agents and corres-
pondents at Falmouth-I was in Dublin on the 25th March-I arrived in
London on the 28th-Lewin Caspar was in my service as confidential clerk,
and in my absence he was to superintend and manage my business-this
letter (No. 6) I produce from among my own papers- I see the words "Mr.
Bristow, Please attend to this, L. CASPAR"- that is Lewin Caspar's hand-
writing-(read)" Messrs. James Hartley and Oo., 16, John-street,
Crutched Friars. Falmouth, Oct. 8, 1838. Gentlement, We are in receipt
of your note of the 6th instant, and in reply we beg to say the turtle is for
Mt. Haggard, at the Bullion Office, Bank of England. The Duke of
Cambridge arrived at noon; all well. We are, gentlemen, your obedient
servants, W. and E. C. CARNE. Per George Hugoe: P.S. We wrote
you last night, per box, informing you we received the chest tra, which
shall be retumed. Mr. Bristow, Please attend to this, L. CASPAR."
HENRY Moss re-examined. Q. Is that the letter about the turtle,
which Lewin Caspar gave you to imitate 1. Yes.
Cross-examined by Mr. JONES. Q. Have you ever seen that letter be-
fore? look at it carefully. A. I never saw this letter, not with this writing
at the bottom-there is no doubt I have seen this letter before if this is
not the letter, it is precisely like the letter which was lent to me to the
best of my belief this is the very letter that was left with me more than
three months before this transaction-this is the letter, I do believe, that I
copied the hand-writing from.
Court. Q. You say so positively that is the letter you copied the hand-
writing from? 1. To the best of my belief that is the hand-writing, and
that was the contents of it.
Mr. JONES. Q. What made you say just now, not with that writing
at the bottom of it? 1. I do not recollect the writing at the bottom of
it I do not recollect to have noticed it-it may have been there
I have no recollection of saving just now that I had never seen the
letter before with that writing at the bottom.I will not swear I did
not say so, but what I mean is, I have no recollection of having seen this
writing at the bottom-it may have been there, but I have no recollection
of having noticed it-I have looked at the back of the letter-I said I
had seen it before, after looking at the direction and looking at it all over
- there is nothing particular at the back which enables me to identify it,
only the direction, because I was to imitate this sort of direction-I see a
figure 6 on the back-on my oath it was not that which enabled me to say
it was the letter- I do not remember seeing it I will swear I did not
notice the No. 6 at the back--I may have seen it, but I did not notice it
-I will not swear that I did see it-I looked all over the letter, but I was
looking at the direction I cannot swear that I did or did not see the figure 6
