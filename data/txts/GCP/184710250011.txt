JAMES CARTER. I am a carpenter, and live at No. 17. Devonshire-street
I know Mr. Gardiner-1 kept the key of the premises belonging to him in
Wellclose-square-I accompanied him and Rowe there, I think on the 17th
of Ang I took a hammer and a screw-driver with me and drew the staple.
because the key was mislaid- I went again to the premises on the 20th of
Aug, and found the doors open-I went in, and saw four men-I could not
swear that I saw either of the prisoners the men I saw were acting as
labourers, moving the bricks and materials-the rafters and purlins, and
gutter-boards were gone, and the lead too-I did not know either of the four
men I saw--I have not given the key to any one since the 17th of Aug.,
when I went to the premises with Rowe-I hold it in my possession now-
Rowe knew I had it I went to the premises again, after the 20th, and put
on a fresh lock.
Cross-examined by MR. HAWKINS. Q. You are a carpenter to Mr. Gardi-
ner! 4. Yes-be is a gentleman–I went to his premises-Rowe was there,
and there were other gentlemen whom I do not know-there was nothing said
about pulling down the building, to my knowledge--I stopped down, and
they went up stairs-I cannot tell wbether they went on the roof under-
stood the premises were condemned to be pulled down, but afterwards it was
altered – I heard that from the parish-Mr. Gardiner bad not said anything
about the building being condemned--wben we see a building shored up by
the parish we know it is condemned.
Did you not tell Ablett that you were to superintend the pulling down
a building? 4. If it was condemned, not without-I never told him that the
building was going to be pulled down, and that I was to superintend it
I did not say it was condemned - I considered it was, by the parish shoring
it up-I did not say that it was going to be pulled down, and that I was to
be foreman in pulling it down-I did not say that I was to be foreman to
Mr. Rowe-we had no engagement-Ablett did not say to me, "In case you
are not employed, come to me, and I will help you to a job "-I found the
key of the premises in my cupboard, where it was always kept--that was
after the lead was gone.
JOSEPH RICHARDS. I am a carpenter and joiner, and live in Victoria-place.

oridge-road-he told Rowe he was doing very wrong; how came he to break
the place open-Rowe said, what security bad be for his 151.?-Mr. Gar-
ainer said he would bave given him his sovereigns back : what right had be to

better than that.
