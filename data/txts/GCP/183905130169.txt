1701. SARAH ORGAR was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of
May, 1 loaf of bread, value 3d., the goods of Henry Wallace. S
Henry WALLACE. I am a baker, and live at Putney. The prisoner
came to my shop on the 4th of May, about eight o'clock in the morning,
took a loaf off the shelf, and concealed it under her shawl-I came
into the shop, and she asked me for a two-pound loaf-I weigbed it, she
paid for it, and walked out with both loaves-I followed her about ten
yards, she turned the corner, and I gave her in charge-both loaves were
found on her-I knew her before she lived down a lane close by my
shop-she has a husband and a large family.
