873. RICHARD HITCHCOCK was indicted for stealing, on the 7th
of February, 1 gelding, price 25l. ; 1 cart, value 81.; 1 set of harness,
value 21. ; 20 sacks, value 11. 10s. ; 4 horse-cloths, value 4s. ; 1 bag, value
18.; and 56 bushels of oats, value 91. 98.; the goods and property of
James Muggeridge.
JAMES WHOWALL (police-constable M 166.) I was in Tennis-court,
King-street, Southwark, on Thursday, the 7th of February, between seven
and eight o'clock in the evening-I saw a horse and cart standing still-
it was a very bye place, my curiosity was excited, and I hid myself where
I could see the cart, but nobody could see me--in about ten minutes I
saw the prisoner coming down a turning called Bowling-green-lane, and
he took the mare by the reins--there was another man with him—the pri-
soner then took the cart down Bowling-green-lane into King-street, and
on to Maze-pond-I followed them the other man saw me, and he went
away-when the prisoner got to Maze-pond, he looked round and saw me
-I then went and asked where he was going-he said, “Home”-
I said, “What, are you the carman?"-he said, "Yes"-I said, "Where
is your whip ?"-he said, “Up in the cart"-but there was no whip there
--I said I should take him to the station-house-he said it was very hard,
as he saw the horse and cart standing, and he was going to take it home;
but he was going in the wrong direction-l asked him what was the direc-
tion on the cart-he could not tell me, but I found on it the name of
Mr. Muggeridge, Tottenham Court-road-the cart was then three or
four miles from there, and the prisoner was going in a contrary direction
- I took the mare and cart, and my brother officer took the prisoner.
Prisoner. He asked me if I was the carman-- I told him "No, but I
saw the horse and cart coming down King-street," I knew the owner, and
was going to take it home. Witness. I am sure he said he was the carman.
JOHN ALLEN (police-constable M 87.) I was with Whowall-I took
the prisoner-there was another person with him, who got away-I asked
the prisoner where he found the cart-he said, “In the Borough,"
and shortly afterwards he said he was coming through Mermaid-court, and
saw it standing, and was going to take it home-Tennis-court leads into
Mermaid-court.

ment-I put the nose-bags on the horses--when I came out the horse and
