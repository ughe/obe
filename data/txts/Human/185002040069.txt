On the 31st Jan., about four o'clock in the afternoon, I received information about a pig; and about half-past seven the same day, two hind quarters and part of the ribs of pork were brought to me with the hair on—I could tell that it was part of a pig which I had lost, and which I had seen safe at seven that morning.
