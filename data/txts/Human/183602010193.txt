Yes; as to the removal of the goods—at noon-day—help me to put them into a van—I know nothing more than than seeing the glass on the lawn—I did not see the prisoner take it away—I did not make the bargain at the Fleet, but at the house, prepartory to my seeing him there.
   MR. PAYNE . Q.  Have you ever been in any other part of this Court? A.  Yes; that is not relevant to this question—I have been at Clerkenwell—I indicted a man named Hall, who robbed me of 5 l  —I was never in dicted for having sworn falsely.

I dare say they could have produced fifty, they were such a set of man—persons came forward to say, and he was acquitted certainly—I was never in Chelmsford gaol, nor any prison in Essex—the prisoner was not em ployed to take the glass home; but only to get the things into the van. CHARLES COOKE was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of January , 1 copper, value 15 s  ., the goods of Ann Nash , and fixed to a build ing against, the statute, &c; and that he had been before convicted of felony. MARIA NEWTON. I am servant to Miss Ann Nash, of Camden-tow, Kentish Town. She had a copper at her house, fixed to a building—it was lost on the 6th of January—this is it.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  How do you know it? A.  By the burn; there was water in it when it when it was stolen—I can swear to it by the burn at the side, and grease inside—I saw it between eleven and twelve o'clock in the wash-house—I did not miss it till the policeman came in the evening—the wash-house is at the bottom of the garden. ALEXANDER LYON( police-constable S  43.) I was son duty in Camden-town, and saw the prisoner with a copper on the evening of the 6th of January—he went to Mr. Bignell's—I heard him ask Mr. Bignell to buy the copper—I came in, and asked were be got it from—he said, "From Mr. Sherriff of Kentish-town"—I said I was not satisfied, and he must go with me there; and when we got outside the door, he said he had stolen it.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Had you said he had better tell you all about it? A.  No—I left the copper at the shop—I got back again about seven o'clock—I know it was the same I had left, by my initials, which I had put on it—the prisoner said he had stolen it when he came out of the shop—he did not tell me where from. GEORGE WILLIAM MURRAY. I am a policeman. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, from the office of the clerk of the Court ( read ) the prisoner is the man who was tried.
