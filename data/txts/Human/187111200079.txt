Before Mr. Baron Bramwell.
 67. ALBERT CHARLES GRIFFIN  (32), was indicted for feloniously carnally knowing and abusing Emily Mary Cassiday , a girl aged 8 years. MR. MORGAN THOMAS conducted the Prosecution; and MR. RIBTON the Defence. GUILTY — Eight Year's Penal Servitude.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY , 11 TH DECEMBER , 1872.
