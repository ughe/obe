Mary Ann BRADSHAW. I am the wife of John Bradshaw, and live at 17,
Star Lane, North End, Fulham-I am the prisoner's sister-on 27th March
the last witness was at my house, when her husband came in; she was
sitting in my husband's arm chair-as she was erossing to sit by my side
the prisoner pulled her back-I did not see him take the knife up, but I
saw it in his hand-I bad put it on the table—they both fell on the floor-
I was so frightened that I did not see what he did, I only saw the blood as
she laid on the floor-I said "Oh! he has done it," and ran out to get help
-I have heard the prisoner say if she did not live with him he would
certainly do for her that was after he came out of prison the last time-
he had three months-he came out in January-he asked me if I would
see what I could do to get them to come together again, and he said “If
she does not, I will do for her."
Johx BRADSHAW. I am the husband of the last witness-I came home

--he got her down on the floor, and knelt on her chest-some young man
