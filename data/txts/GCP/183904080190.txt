1394. MARY M'GILL was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of April,
10 handkerchiefs, value 21., the goods of William Dalton, her master.
WILLIAM DALTON. I live in Harper-street, Chelsea. The prisoner
worked for me on the 5th of April-I heard from Lady Ross and others
that their things were not right-I then went to the pawnbroker's, and
found four handkerchiefs-I got an officer-I went to the prisoner's house,
and gave her in charge--these handkerchiefs are mine-they belonged to
my customers- I had them to wash.
GEORGE BROOKS. I am a pawnbroker. I produce seven handkerchiefs
which I took in of a woman at different times, in the name of Ana
Clarkson-these are the duplicates I gave-I do not know the woman.
HENRY KIMBER (police-sergeant V 20.) I went to the prisoner-she
said she knew nothing about them-I said I was going to take her to the
station-house-she asked me to allow her to put on another gown-she
then said if the prosecutor would allow her time she would take them out.
WILLIAM DALTON re-examined. The man the prisoner lives with, said
in her presence, that her name was Clarkson, but she always worked for
me in the name of M'Gill.
Prisoner. I did not pledge all the handkerchiefs-I throw myself on the
mercy of the Court.
GUILTY. Aged 35.-Confined One Year.
