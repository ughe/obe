Prisoner's Defence.  I have been getting my living honestly. I have been living at Mr. Barton's for some months, and I left of my own accord; a friend of my father's when he went to Australia, left 10 l . between my brother and myself; my brother, who is ill, has had 5 l ., and I have had 2 l . 15 s . Mr. Rymer pays it to us; if the policeman had gene the proper way to work I should have got a good character; they seem to have pressed the case as much as they could against me. NOT GUILTY.
