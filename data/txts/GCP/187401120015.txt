109. MAHOMET WIGGETT (23, a black), Robbery on Emily Durndell,
and stealing 28. MR. PLATT conducted the Prosecution.
(The evidence was interpreted to the prisoner).
EMILY DURNDELL I am fifteen years of age, and live with my father, at
11, West India Dock Cottages on 13th December, about 12.30 in the day,
I was in the kitchen, counting some of my father's money; the front door
was open-the prisoner came in and said he wanted 2s., I told him I could
not give it him, it was father's (he had been there before at 7.30 that!
morning for a lodging)-he said he must have it-I told him again I
could not give it him-he swung his arms about in all manner of direc-
tions, and looked so that I was frightened, and I gave him the 28. and he
went away.
Prisoner. l. Did you give it of your own free will! 4. I gave it him
because I was frightened of him-he said he was hungry and wanted some-
thing to eat-I had 24s. counting; he was satisfied with my giving him
the 28.
Nor GUILTY.
110. JAMES EDWARDS (30), Feloniously wounding Susan Norman,
with intent to do grievous bodily harm.
MR. Guys conducted the Prosecution.
Hugs NEWLAND (Policeman B 26). At 12.15 on Monday morning,
29th December, I was with another officer on duty in Pimlico; I heard a
cry of “Police" and went to No. 58, Cambridge Street, & crowd was
collected outside the door; the prisoner was being held by a young man,
who said he had assaulted a female inside the house. I called & constable
and told him to detain the prisoner-I went into the house, and saw the
prosecutrix in the parlour, sitting behind the door, bleeding very profusely
from the head, which was out I took her to the hospital in a cab, and the
prisoner was taken to the station - I produoe & poker, which was given to
me by the servant.

excited than drunk, he was very exeited--the prosecutrix was very excited;
she was not insensible she did not charge him then, or at any time
she appeared before the Magistrate-she did not make any charge against
him - 1 charged him from what I had heard-I saw Miss Ralph after
wards; she was excited - the prosecutrix keeps a tobacoonist's shop; I
know nothing about the house.
