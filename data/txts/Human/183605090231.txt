 1351. MARY SAWYER  , alias    Frampton, was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of April , 1 watch, value 3 l  .; 1 chain, value 9 d  .; and 1 key, value 3 d  .; the goods of George Rudram . GEORGE RUDRAM. I am a gardener. I met the prisoner at Greenwich, on the 24th of April, at a public-house—we had some gin—she took me to a hour—I gave her 1 s . to pay for the room, and 2 s ., 6 d . for herself—I hug my clothes on another bed head in the room—she asked me for 1 1/2 d . to get some gin—she went to my clothes, and book my watch out, and ran out of the room—I gave the alarm on soon as I could—being a stranger, I did not know which way to go—I called the policeman—I am quite sure she is the person. BRIDGET SULLIVAN FITZGERALD. On Sunday night, the prosecutor and prisoner came to my house—I did not hear them go up—stairs—I was asleep, but after the prisoner was gone, the prosecutor came down, and he awoke me, and said the girl had robbed him—I did not see her in the house at all. JAMES WILLIAM ALLUM( police-constable R  89.) I received informa tion, and took the prisoner in Cut-throat-lane—I have never found the watch.
   Prisoner . He said at one time he gave me the watch, and then he said that I stole it. GEORGE RUDRAM. No—I did not. BRIDGET SULLIVAN FITZGERALD . The man told me that he gave her the watch to take care of—I told that to the Magistrate—the prosecutor told it to me, and Mr. Look wood was in the room—the prosecutor appeared sober—I was in bed, and did not see them come in—I was asleep till he came down and awoke me.
   Prisoner . He gave me the watch, and told me I might keep it—I put it down, and do not know what became of it. NOT GUILTY.
