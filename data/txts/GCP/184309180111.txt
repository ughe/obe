HANNAH NEWBOM. I am the wife of Samuel Frederick Newsom-the
prisoner was in the habit of coming to our house - the last time was on

Mr. Dexter's, the others at another place, and torn the tickets up.
THOMAS DRYLAND. I produce these earrings, which were pawned on the
9th of September.
