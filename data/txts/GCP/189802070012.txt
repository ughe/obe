NEW COURT.-Tuesday, February 8th, 1898.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
147. FITZHERBERT CECIL WILLIAMS (44) PLEADED GUILTY
to six indictments for stealing six railway bonds for 1,000 dollars each,
and other bonds, the property of Thomas Layton and another, his masters.
-Three Years' Penal Servitude. 148. GEORGE EAMES (20),
ARTHUR MILLER (19) and HENRY NEWMAN (20), to two
indictments for burglary and stealing 431 postage stamps, four dozen
pocket-knives and other articles, Miller having been convicted at
Clerkenwell on June 1st, 1896. EAMES and MILLER-Nine Months'
Hard Labour each ; NEWMAN-Six Months' Hard Labour. And
149. MINNIE GREY (36), to unlawfully obtaining £5, also postal
orders for 30s., £2, £2 10s. and other sums, by false pretences.-
Judgment Respited.
150. TEDDY DONOGHUE (16), Feloniously wounding Elizabeth
Hans, with intent to do her grievous bodily harm. The prisoner stated
that he was guilty of unlawfully wounding, upon which the Jury found
that verdict.-Discharged on Recognizances.

Occasioning him actual bodily harm.
