 2112. ELIZABETH BATCHELOR was indicted for stealing, on the   8th of September , 9 pewter pots, value 10 s  ., the goods of Abraham Shore . ABRAHAM SHORE I keep a public-house, and have lost nine pewter pots—these are mine—I lost them on the 8th of September, at about four or five minutes past three o'clock in the afternoon. THOMAS HODGE( police-constable G  125.) I found the prisoner between four and five o'clock in the afternoon, with these pots under her shawl.
   Prisoner.  I merely took them up to look at the name, and had no intention to take them. Witness , She was a quarter of a mile from the prosecutor's, in Northampton-street, but I think she had been drinking. ELIZA HILL. I am twelve years old. I saw the prisoner put the pots under her shawl, and told the officer—she carried them and put them down in a corner, in Northampton-street, and then she said, "Oh, by G—we won't leave them now we have got them, there is nobody here to see us"—there was another woman with her, but the prisoner had them, and dropped them, and then took them up again. GUILTY  . Aged 33.—Recommended to mercy. — confined Four days.
 2113. GEORGE HYDE was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of Sep tember , 1 headstall, value 2 s  ., 6 d  ., the goods of Richard Pratt . PATRICK LONG. I live in Tash-cour. About half-past ten o'clock on the 1st of September, I saw the prisoner going out of my yard—he had this headstall rolled up in his smock-frock, but I could not see what it was then—I asked him what he wanted, as I saw him come from the stable—he said he wanted some dung—I took this headstall from him when, he got into Portpool-lane—he took it out of his smock-frock. WALTER STANFORD. I live as horsekeeper to Mr. Richard Pratt—this is his headstall—I saw it safe at twelve o'clock, and at half-past three o'clock the prisoner was brought back with it.
   Prisoner.  I came up from Manchester—I called in at a public-house and a man came and offered it for sale—I gave him 1 s . for it, as it was all I had—I went down this yard to speak to a man, and was going on, I took this halter out, and threw it on my shoulder, and they came and took me. CHARLES LIZARS. ( police-constable G  220.) I took the prisoner, and offered to go the public-house where he said he had bought it, and he would not let me. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months.
