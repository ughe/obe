SABAH TABRAHAM. I was directed by Mr. Jordan to watch his premises
I was at my own door, which is opposite his shop-I saw Donovan go into
the shop; both she and Linge took the potatoes, and placed them in her apron
-she then went out-the potatoes had not been weighed, and there was no
money paid that I could see; I had a full opportunity of seeing, if any thing
of the sort had passed.

Cross-examined. Q. The prisoners were standing close together when the
potatoes were taken out, were they not? A. Yes--they were some distance
from me-I swear that no money passed between them-I was at the door,
and could see plainly-they were standing side by side, and both faced the
bin-Donovan stood nearest to me; her side face was towards me.
Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. You were stationed there for the express purpose
of watching? A. Yes; and I have done so on other occasions; I was parti-
cularly attentive to see whether money passed, and am positive it did not.
