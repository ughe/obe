1568. THOMAS M'CANNON was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of June, 1 cap, value 6 d  .; 1 pair of socks, value 9 d  .; 2 handkerchiefs, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; and 2 shillings; the goods and monies of Donald M'Kay . DONALD M'KAY. I am a baker, and live at Woolwich. I made a holi day on the 2nd of June, and went to Gravesend—I returned at ten or eleven o'clock, and got a lodging out—the prisoner slept in the same bed with me, but he was quite a stranger—he came to bed afterwards, while I was awake—I know he is the person—I had the money and the property stated—when I awoke in the morning I missed this property, and the prisoner was gone—I made inquiries, and found him at the Mason's Arme—I gave him into custody. SAMUEL NICHOLLS. I am a constable of Woolwich. The prisoner was given to me—I found this property on him, and 8 1/2 d . DONALD M'KAY. This is my property—these socks were in the same pocket with my two shillings—these are my handkerchiefs and cap—the prisoner is a marine.
   Prisoners Defence.  I have no recollection of being at the house—I was intoxicated. GUILTY. Aged 29.— Transported for Seven Years.
   Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant .
 1596. JAMES HURLEY was indicted for stealing, on the   15th June , 2 half crowns; 2 shillings; and 2 halfpence, the monies of Sarah Thri field , from her person. RICHARD TOZER( police constable P  149.) On the 15th June, I was at Deptford fair, in plain clothes—I saw the prisoner put his hand into seve ral females' pockets—he at last went to a lady, put his hand into her pocket, and took something out—I spoke to him, "and took his hand—he dropped a half-crown, two shillings, and some halfpence, and I found one half-crown in his hand—I spoke to the lady—she gave her name as Sarah Thrifield.
   Prisoner . I was looking at a show—I saw the money on the ground—I took it, and the officer took me. DANIEL RIORDAN( police-constable R  81.) I was at the fair, and saw the prisoner attempt several ladies' pockets—he then put his hand into Sarah Thrifield's, and dropped a half-crown, two shillings, and some halfpence. GUILTY. Aged 10.— Confined Eight Days.
 1570. JAMES PRICE was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of June , 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  ., the goods of Edward Henry Thompson , from his person. EDWARD HENRY THOMPSON. I am a mariner, and live at Greenwich. On the 16th of June, I was at Greenwich fair—I felt something—I turned, and saw the prisoner running with my handkerchief on him—this is it—I saw him throw it away.
   Prisoner . He said he saw me take it from him. Witness . No; I did not. THOMAS ENSUM( police-constable R  87.) I took the prisoner and the handkerchief—he said he had it, directly I took him. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Three Months.
