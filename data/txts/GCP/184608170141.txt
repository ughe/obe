1625. JAMES GILLETT was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value
2s., the goods of Charles Miller, from his person.
Charles MILLER. I am a gun-polisher, and live in Smith-street, Stepney.
On the 1st of Aug. I was standing in Cannon-street, and felt something touch
my pocket-my bandkerchief was gone-I turned, and saw the prisoner by
my side, with it in his hand I caught hold of him, and took it from him-be
used very bad language-- I turned round for a policeman, and he knocked
me down-I gave him into custody.
JOSEPH HEAD (police-constable.) I saw the prosecutor holding the pri-
soner-he had got up when I arrived.
Guilty.* Aged 21.-Confined Twelve Months.

1627. HANNAH M'CARTHY was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief,
value 6d. ; 1 half-sovereign, and 1 shilling; the property of Giovanni Rovillo,
from his person.
GIOVANNI RoviLLO (through an interpreter.) I live at No. 73, Leather-
lane. On the 20th of July I came out of the hospital, and three days after-
wards I was walking for air in the street, and met a woman-I think the pri-
soner is the person-she took my handkerchief from me, and went in-doors
with it-I entered the house, and with one hand she gave me the handker-
chief back, and put her other hand into my pocket, and took out a half-
sovereign and a shilling.
Prisoner. It is false, you went home with me, and I laid on the bed with
you ; and because I would not submit to your indecencies, you bit me. Wit-
ness. I had nothing to do with you; I did bite you to make you let go
of my money, and four or five women came in and beat me, and tore my
clothes.
John WooD policeman.) About ten o'clock in the morning I passed
Bluegate-fields, Shadwell, and saw the prosecutor and prisoner struggling-
she had him by the hair of his head-he was bleeding from the mouth-the
prisoner said he gave her 1s. to go home with her, and wanted the 1s. back,
and that caused the bother.
GEORGE ROGERS (policeman.) I brought the prisoner to King David-
Inne station on the road she said the prosecutor gave her 1s. to go home
with her, and that in the scuffle she took the half-sovereign and shilling out
of his pocket, and gave it to another girl--the house is a common brothel.
Prisoner's Defence. Instead of my taking anything from him, he took a
handkerchief from my neck; the blood on his mouth came from my finger.
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Confined Nine Months.
