Q. What was the false pretence which induced you to part with this?
1. He had talked to me about giving me security, and I was desirous of
having security-he said he had given Wrench fourteen days credit, and he
would give me an order upon Wrench and Co. to receive the money in
fourteen days-I understood I was to have the order at that time when he bad
the blue it was in consequence of that statement I gave him the blue
he stated, he had not got the bill in his possession, but he could get it in
twenty minutes : and as it was getting very late, it being then half-past
four, he would feel obliged if I would allow the man to take the cask to
Wrench and Co.'s, as he believed they were desirous of shipping it that
night-he had at first proposed that I should go to Liverpool to see his
uncle--I declined that, and he then said he would give me a bill for 187. on
