I paid the £1,000 to him on that day by two cheques, one for £940 5s. 3d.,

ou the Capital and Counties Bank, Oxford Street branch-it is endorsed

I did not see anything the matter. with him-I saw him occasionally after
that-- I remember seeing him on November 30th at my office, when hedid some
business with me-I executed an assignment—he appeared to be all right at
that time-I was also concerned in the sale of the White Hart, which was
