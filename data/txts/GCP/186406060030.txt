GEORGE MEAD (Police-sergeant, F 9). About half-past 12 on the morning of
3d April, I saw the prisoner and deceased at the corner of King-street,
Drury-lane--they were in the middle of the street, and had both got their
arms up in a fighting attitude-they were both laughing--I requested them
to go away-the prisoner said, “ All right, old fellow; good night," and they
both walked away down King-street- about four or five minutes afterwards,
I heard the cry of Police !" in King-street-I immediately went up, and saw
the deceased lying on the pavement, and the witness Crowley had got a cloth
over his face-he was bleeding-he was assisted to a surgeon's, and from
there to the Hospital-he was quite insensible.
