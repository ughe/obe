ELIZA GLANVILLE( Re-examined ). I saw no easy-chair in the drawing room—there was no table or toilet set or clock in the boy's bedroom—I suppose the boy did not wash—there were strips of carpet, but no chair or towel airer or ornaments on the mantelpiece—I do not remember any folding table in the dining-room; there was a piano there, but I do not remember a music cupboard—I think I must have seen them if they had been there—there was no servant in the house but me, I was sup posed to dust the rooms—I do not recollect a small occasional table in the dining-room—I saw one music-book—there was one easy-chair in the breakfast parlour; I do not remember two, nor any table napkins or rings, or fish knives and forks—I do not remember an easy-chair in the kitchen; I remember two trays, not five, and three bentwood chairs.
