 2144. JAMES BARKER was indicted for embezzlement. ELIZABETH STEVENS. I live at Lower Homerton, and am a widow, and a cow-keeper. The prisoner was in my employ—it was his business to receive money, and pay it to me—he has never paid me 10 s . 4 1/2 d . received from Mrs. Hawkins. CAROLINE HAWKINS. I am the wife of Henry Hawkins, of London-lane, Hackney. I had my milk of the prisoner, and paid him daily—I never paid him more than 8 d . at a time—it was his duty to give it to his mistress—I have paid 10 s . 4 1/2 d ., and more. ELIZABETH STEVENS re-examined.  He should account to me every day, but he let this lady's account stand till it amounted to 10 s ., 4 1/2 d ., and said it was not paid—he has not paid me 3 s .; 5 d . of Mr. Harley's. WILLIAM HARLEY. I deal with the prosecutrix. I paid him every day—we never let it run except it was for want of change—I have paid the prisoner 3 s . 5 d ., at once more than that. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Six Months.
