ens'-buildings-I then saw Craske and Mascall-in consequence of informa

BENJAMIN HOLLOWAY (a prisoner.) I am now in Newgate on a charge of
stealing two duplicates -I know Brockwell and Barker by sight-I went of
my own accord to see Barker's sister at a lodging-house in Keate's-court,
Keate-street, Spitalfields-I do not know the day of the month-I did not
then know of this robbery--saw his sister-she told me her two brothers
were taken-it was on a Tuesday, about eight weeks ago, as near as I can
recollect-I first saw Barker about five o'clock the same evening in Keate's
court, with Brockwell-I went there the next day I found them there then
--they were out when I first went-I went to see his sister, as I was a friend
of hers- I was there about an hour and a half-they were out at the time-I
went, altogether, three or four times to Keate's-court, and saw Barker and
Brockwell each time-they asked me if I would go down to their houses for
some bottles which contained some ketchup, and whether I would bring them
away-I said, Yes-both of them asked me to do it--they did not tell me
where I should find the bottles-they said a person down there would give
them to me when I got there they said they were in the habit of making a
lot of ketchup - I did not know their houses-Barker said his house was
No. 37, Tucker's-buildings-I do not know where Brockwell's house is
did not go down about the bottles-while I was there a person came in and

indeed ?"-Brockwell said, "We know who it is"-I do not know that person
-a good many persons lodged at that house the person said a good many
