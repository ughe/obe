649. DANIEL MURPHY, breaking and entering the shop of James
Knight, and stealing a pair of boots, value 3s., and other goods his property.
JAMss KNIGHT. I live in Church-street, Deptford, and am a shoemaker.
On Monday night, 11th April, I closed the shop myself about 11 o'clock, and
went to bed-on the following morning at 5 o'clock I was called up by the
police-I went down, and found the shutter pulled down and the window
broken-I am quite sure it was closed the night before-I missed two pairs
of new blucher boots, a pair of old ones, two odd ones, and the upper leather
-the pair of old ones are here-I will swear to them, they are my property
- know them by the repairing of them inside-I repaired the upper part
myself.

there."
