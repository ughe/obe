MR. CHAMBERS. Q. Is the chain solid gold? A. It is solid of its

WILLIAM BOOTH. I am in partnership with my brother, Robert Jacob
Booth. On the 1st of May I saw Burt at our shop-he produced a silver
watch, and chain attached, and asked 101. on it-I looked at them, and
asked him if the chain was gold-he said, "Yes"-I asked him if it was
fine gold-be said, "You can tell "-I then told him the chain was only
metal, and if he took it to any other house in town he might be taken into
custody- I looked at the watch it had the name of "M. Tobias, Mino-
ries," on it.
Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. What metal is the chain ? A. Brass
I expect-I tested it with aquafortis-1 presume it to be brass—it is not gold,
MR. CHAMBERS. Q. Did you test it while he was there ? A. Yes ---
it boiled up green-it was not gold.
MR. CLARKSON. Q. Was there no gold in it? A. I do not expect
there was.
