2018. THOMAS JONES was indieted for stealing, on the 8th of
September, 1 pair of half-boots, value 58., the goods of George Powell.
JOHN FRAZIER. I was walking past Mr. Powell's shop, on the 8th of
September, about half-past eleven o'elock, and saw the prisoner at the
door, looking at the shoes which hung on the nail he then took a pair
and ran off with them-I ran after him, and told the officer to stop him.
JOHN STokes (police-constable G 79.) I was called, and took the
prisoner with these boots on him.
GEOBGE
POWELL. I keep the shop, which is in Holborn. These boots
were hanging outside my door on the 8th of September-they have my
name on them.
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Transported for Seven Years.
