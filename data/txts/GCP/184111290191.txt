230. CHARLES DARBY was indicted for stealng, on the 30th of
November, 1 cloak, value 158., the goods of Gregory Dumanski.
GREGORY DUMANSKI. I was at the Palace Arms public-house, Pim-
lico, on the evening of the 30th of November, and lost my cloak-this
now produced is it.
John Penrose. I was at the Palace Arms, on the evening of the 30th,
of November, about half past nine o'clock-I saw the prosecutor and pri-
soner there.
John Curry. I am a corporal in the First Battalion of Coldstream
Guards. I met the prisoner at a quarter past ten o'clock that night-he
had a bundle with him which contained the cloak-I took him into
custody.
Prisoner's Defence. I went into the Horse Guards and had a pint of
beer; a gentleman asked me to drink with him, which I did ; 1 then went
out, and a man gave me the cloak to pawn.

231. WILLIAM CAPES was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of
November, 13lbs. weight of bacon, value 78., the goods of John Ayling;
and that he had been before convicted of felony.
John AYLING. I live in Barnard-street, Bethnal-green; I keep a
shop.--On the evening of the 25th of November, I was sitting in my par-
lour, and heard somebody come into the shop- I looked, and missed two
pieces of bacon-I went out and found the prisoner, who had one of them
under his arm I had seen them safe three or four minutes before.
WILLIAM THOMPSON. I saw the prisoner take the bacon from another
person, who came out of the shop with two pieces-he was three or four
yards from the shop.
Prisoner's Defence. A man asked me to buy it; be left one piece with
me, and ran off.
GEORGE Trew. I am a policeman. I produce a certificate of the pri-
soner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office-(read)-
the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY.--Confined One Year.
