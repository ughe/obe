another.
JAMES JOHN FOSTER. I am foreman to William and James Soames, of
Spitalfields, soap manufacturers. The prisoner was in their employ-be,
with two other men, were at work with me on the 16th of July– I received
information, and the prisoner asked me if he might go to breakfast - I said
“Yes, but I must search you first "-I found on him this bar of soap.
Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. Is your master here? Å, No.

fifteen years there-- when I said I must search him, he said, "I have
nothing about me;" but when I found it, he never uttered a word—it is
usual to leave a piece of soap for the men to wash with, but not bars of
soap-it was concealed in his frock.
