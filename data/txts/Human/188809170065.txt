JOHN SEALEY. I am carman to Charles Pollock, of Dowgate Dock—on March 29th I received a case of goods from Dowgate Dock—I delivered them at 39, Seething Lane, in accordance with the instructions produced: "With reference to one case received from Mr. Kœnig please send same to our office, 39, Seething Lane, E. C., Henry Goodwin and Co., and collect on Thursday"—we had instructions to call again after the case was repacked—the case was not given us. SIMON FERDINAND FELDMAN.
