 574. NORAH MAHONEY was indicted for stealing, on the   4th No ember , 1 bedstead, value 2 s  ., the goods of Richard Burn . RICHARD BURN. I am a fruiterer, and live in Little Shire-Lane. In October last the prisoner lodge in my house, and continued there till the 7th of December—I missed a bedstead, and charged her with having taken it—she said she did not expect I should take her up for so trifling a charge—I told her, after living rent-free for so many weeks, and committing such a number of depredations, I must—I went to Cornelius Mahoney, in Wild-court, Drury-lane, and there I saw the bedstead.
   Prisoner . I had it six weeks before I went to his house—it is my own and not his. Witness . It is mine I am sure. ALICE LILEY. I Live at Mr. Burn's I saw the bedstead in the collar in October, before the prisoner came to the house—I have seen it since—it is his, and was so before she came—it had a sacking on then. BRIDGET MAHONEY. I am the wife of Cornelius Mahoney, and live in Wild-court, Drury-lane. The prisoner gave me the bedstead that was found at my house nine weeks before Christmas, and said her brother gave it to her before she went to live there—she lived on the third floor, in Wild-court—she had it in the room—I am no relation to the prisoner—I always knew her by the name of Macarthy—she lodged in the room a bedstead in the room, and she did not want that, so she let me have it—there was no sacking to it then. JAMES NOCKALL, I am a policeman. I charged the prisoner with this—she said she did not think Mr. Burn would prosecute her for so sim ple a thing.
   Prisoner . I did not say any such thing; it is false; tell the truth. Witness . I swear that is the truth.
   Prisoner's Defence . I owed him 3 s . rent, and he said he should give me in charge because I ran away with his rent. RICHARD BURN re-examined . She does own rent, but I freely for gave her when she left the house—I have no object in this prosecution but justice, nor had I the least idea of punishing her—I should not have thought of finding this bedstead, only on 26th of January my bed-room door was broken open, and my drawers ransacked of every thing; I had reason to suspect the prisoner, and I had proofs of two instances, but I told her I would forgive her for that.
