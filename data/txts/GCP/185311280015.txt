(Upon MR. PARRY proposing to put in the book, MR. RIBTON objected, upon
the ground that the signature was a receipt, or in the nature of a receipt, and
was not stamped. Mr. PARRY did not tender it as a receipt for money, brit as

and reserve the point if it became necessary.-Cases referred to, Rex v. Starkie,
