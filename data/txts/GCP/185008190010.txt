JOSEPHINE LEBAS (through an interpreter). I know all these things to
be my property-. I saw them in my place before I left, about the month of
May--this shawl is mine-I have worn it-this card-rack came from Paris.
and this pair of scales.
Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL. Q. Have you any other name than
Josephine Lebas ? A. No; I am not married.
Cross-examined by MR. COCKLE. Q. How long have you been in England?
A. I returned on 23rd June-these reels of gold are for embroidering-these
are worth about 28. each-some are dearer- I cannot tell how many I had in
my house when I left-there might be eight or ten.
