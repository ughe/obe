3003. WILLIAM WOODMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of
Aug., 1 box, value 58.; 1 coat, 108.; 2 shirts, 78.; 3 aprons, 2. bd.;
1 handkerchief, 38. ; 1 cap, 4d. ; 1 pair of braces, 4d. ; and 1 oomb, 10d;
the goods of Thomas Woodward.
THOMAS WOODWARD. I am a baker, and live in Blackman-street, in the
Borough. The prisoner was in my employer's service--about the 15th
of Aug. I sent him to fetch a box, containing the articlesstated, from Mr.
Chinn's-he did not come back-I have not seen any of the things.
Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q. Did you say, if you received 30,
you would consider that a recompense for the loss of your trunk? 4. Ya
-a sovereign was not offered to me-the prisoner's mother spoke to me about
the 30s. on the Monday week after
Court. Q. When did you find him? A. On the 5th of Sept.- he said
he came to give himself up, and wanted the case settled.
CAROLINE CHinn. I live with my husband, in Shenston's livery stables.
The prosecutor left a box in my care at Christmas- I kept the key--the pri-
soner came for it-I said, “You are the young man who has come for Mr,
Woodward's box?"-I delivered it to him locked-I did not give him the
key-the prosecutor bad a key as well as me it contained the articles stated.
DANIEL HALEY (police-constable M 181.) I took the prisoner-I asked if
