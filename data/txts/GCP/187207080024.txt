ROBERT CARTHOYES. I am a packer in the employ of Pawson & Co., of
St. Paul's Churchyard-I packed this parcel (produced)—it has been opened
and a quantity of muslin abstracted- I put six pieces of goods of this class
in it, and there are only four now-I cannot say whether it contained these
goods produced.
Prisoner. Q. Look at this thread and say if that is not the same which
is used in your warehouse? A. It may be I will not swear that these two
pieces of muslin belonged to this packet.
HENRY LUKE. I am & van driver in the employ of the Great Western
Railway, I received this parcel from Pawson's, and delivered it in good
position.
Louis JOHN CROSS. I am a porter to the Great Western Railway
Company-I received this parcel in good condition--it had not the least
appearance of being tampered with–I delivered it to Spencer.
JAMES SPENCER. I am a porter in the employ of the Great Western
Railway-I received a parcel from Cross and put it outside the Reading
truck on the platform-it was in sound condition when I left it-it must
have been wilfully thrown off the bank, it could not have fallen off. 30
Prisoner. 2. What makes you swear that! A. I left it with other goods
which were 5 or 6 ft. from the bank- there were not so many goods there,
that there was no room for your foot.
HENRY ARMS. I am superintendent of the Smithfield goods station-it
was no part of the prisoner's duty to repair any parcel which came open-
proper officers are appointed for that duty-if the prisoner found an open
paroel, it was his duty to take it to the superintendent of the platform; it
was no part of his duty to repair it--he entered the service on 9th May, as
porter, and at his own request, he was transferred and made a policeman
on 19th June, he was appointed by me, subject to confirmation, and on the
day of this robbery his appointment had been confirmed in the morning
all I know of him is from his written testimonials.

Prisoner. Q. Was it not my duty, if I saw a parcel broken, to count
the contents ! A. Yes, and to make a report to Mr. Groves, the super-
intendent of the platform, not to take the repair into your own hands.
FRANCIS BURDETT NORBURY. I am warehouseman to Pawson & Co., of
St. Paul's Churchyard-I identify this parcel as having been there, and
these two pieces of muslin as having been in it-I tied them together, and
sent them to the packer.
WILLIAM ROWLAND (City Detective). The prisoner was given into my
custody at Smithfield station as he stood in the dock he said " This is a
trumped-up case, I don't know nothing about it; I merely kicked it with
my foot, and jumped down to pick it up, and as I was about to put it on
the bank, I was collared."
Prisoner's Defence. It was my duty, when I saw the parcel lying there
to pick it up, and put it in its proper place-I distinctly deny that I ever
mended it.
ROBERT CARTHOTES (re-examined.) The parcel is not mended with the
same material; one is cocoa-nut, and the other twine.
Guilty-Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
