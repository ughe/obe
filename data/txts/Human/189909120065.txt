I am a carman—I was not called at the Police-court—on Saturday evening, July 15th, I came out of the Black Lion at 10 minutes to 10, and saw Mr. Lovell and the prisoner—he was a stranger to me, but I had seen him with Stepney before—I went to the Eyre Arms, and did not see him again.
