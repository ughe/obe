Midland Railway Company - after the prisoner was taken into
custody on 25th February I went to his lodging, 81, Goodwin's Road,
Cattle Market-I searched the rooms, and among other things I
found this biscuit box-I afterwards showed it to him and asked him to
account for the possession of it-he said he had it made a present to him
at the time he was married.

hamper to go by rail to Mr. Trussell, of Brighton—I put in it 12 articles:
nine breakfast cruets, one butter cooler, and two biscuit boxes-this
produced is one of them-I produoe a similar one from our stock-it is
our special casting, and the engraving is ours--I have no doubt this is
one of those I packed-the total value of the parcel was 77. Os. 6d.
