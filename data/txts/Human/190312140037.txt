   Cross-examined.  I entirely agree with Dr. Griffith's evidence. JAMES SCOTT. I am the medical officer at Brixton Prison—I have had the prisoner under my observation there since December 2nd—at that time in my opinion he was of unsound mind and has so remained until to-day—I am of opinion that that unsoundness had existed at least from November 24th.
   Cross-examined.  I have seen certain letters purporting to come from him to hit family; several attacks of malaria are mentioned in them—if the statements are true the malaria and fever would affect his mind as well as the hardships he has undergone—I think he will recover but it will take some time. GUILTY  but insane at the time and not responsible for his actions. To be detained during His Majesty's pleasure.
