1145. GEORGE TAYLOR, stealing 43 biscuits, value, 25.; the goods
of Henry Armstrong, his master; and WILLIAM AUSTIN, for receiving
the same; to which
TAYLOR pleaded Guilty. Aged 21.-Confined Three Months.
HENRY ARMSTRONG. I am a biscuit-baker. Taylor was in my service-
I never authorized him to give any biscuits away-these produced are mine
-I know them by the machine they are made in- I lost such.
WILLIAM CHARLES POTTER (policeman, K 212.) I saw Austin coming
down St. George's-street, in a direction from Mr, Armstrong's-he looked
bulky-I asked him what he had he said, "Biscuits "-I asked how he
came by them-he said be bought them-I said "Where ?"-be said, "I
can't tell you "- I took him to the station-I found forty-three biscuits in
his trowsers and shirt-these are part of them- he said, on the way to the
station, he would tell the truth; that Taylor, who works at Mr. Armstrong's
gave him them.
Austin's Defence. Taylor gave them me; I asked if he thought I should
get into trouble; he said, “No; or else I would not give you them."
(Austin received a good character.)
AUSTIN-Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
