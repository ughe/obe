I fastened my sister's house on Sunday even ing, about ten minutes before six o'clock—I pushed up the front parlour shutters, pulled the street door after me, and tried it—it was quite fast—it opens by a large key—I was present when my sister tried it as she passed it to go to church—when we came back in the evening it was open about a foot—the lock was not injured—it was pushed back. EDWARD FUNNELL. I am a policeman. Between seven and eight o'clock on Sunday evening, the prisoner Johnstone ran past me on London-bridge, not very fast, from the Borough—in consequence of information, I followed and took him at the corner of King William-street—he said nothing then, but when we got about half-way over the bridge, he said he had just come from Rat cliff-high way, and bad been drinking there, and said, "I came up the steps, did you not see me?"—I said, "No, I saw you on the bridge"—I took him to a tobacco-shop, where the other prisoner was searched, and then to the station.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  Of course you have told us exactly what occurred? A.  Yes, I remember every word he said to me, and cannot be mistaken—he first passed me on the bridge, not a rod from the corner nearest to Adelaide-place—there are steps leading up from the water, and steps out of Thames-street.

 2774. JOHN RICHARDS was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of September , 1 knife, value 6 d  .; and 1 glass-rummer, value 1 s  . 10 d  .; the goods or Jonathan Hall . JONATHAN HALL. I keep the Ship and Whale public-house in Rother-hithe. On the 22nd of September I received some information from one of the customers—in consequence of which I ran out and saw my daughter alongside of the prisoner, bringing him back—I took this glass and knife from him—it was mine—he asked me to allow him to pay for the knife—he had no business with either of them—he had got about 200 yards.
   Prisoner.  I was not six yards from the house—I was very much in liquor. Witness.  He was not drunk. JOHN THOMAS( police-constable M  237.) I searched the prisoner, and found five knives on his person, and this glass.
   Prisoner.  These knives were given me on board the George William,  at the Commercial-dock—there was no one belonging to them—Mr. Hall, the ship's steward, said I might have them.
 (  The prisoner handed in a statement, partly in prose and partly in verse, representing that he had fought under the late Admiral Nelson in various actions, and begging for a lenient sentence. ) GUILTY. Aged 62.— Confined Seven Days.
