2311. WILLIAM PERRY was again indicted for stealing, on the 23rd
of July, 1 quilt, value 6d. ; 1 inkstand, 18.; 1 saccharometer and case, 10.;
2 locks, 18. ; 2 table-cloths, 18. ; 2 towels, 4d. ; 2 caps, 4d. ; 1 finger-glass,
6d. ; 2 printed books, 1s. ; 1 basket, 2d. ; 1 waiter, 2d.; the goods of Amelia
Davidson.
Amelia DAVIDSON. These goods are all mine. I have been continually
losing things- I could not say when I saw them safe-they were in different
parts of my house.
JOSEPH Higgs (police-sergeant S 30.) I found these articles concealed
under the prisoner's stairs, covered with a sack.
HENRY WILLIAMS (police-constable S 260.) I took this handkerchief off
the prisoner's neck.
JOHN KILBY. The prisoner took a room at my house on the 29th of July,
he said he wanted to put some goods in and to come and live in it-he after-
wards brought two bundles-the policeman afterwards took the same things
away.
Prisoner. The things were found at my house-I did not carry them
there, nor know that they were there-I never had an opportunity of moving
them from Mrs. Davidson, and I was never about her rooms.
Mrs. DAVIDSON. He was not, that I know of.
Nor GUILTY.
