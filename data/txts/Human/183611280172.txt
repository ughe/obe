SARAH BRADSHAW was indicted for stealing on the   21st of September , 1 yard of carpet, value 4 s  .; 1 tea-caddy, value 5 s  .; 2 pillow-cases, value 1 s  .; 2 bolsters, value 4 s  .; 1 bolster, value 5 s  .; 2 blankets, value 12 s  .; 1 table-cloth, value 1 s  .; 1 towel, value 1 s  .; 1 printed book, value 5 s  .; 2 tumblers, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 set of irons, value 5 s  .; the goods of Elizabeth Tilsed . ELIZABETH TILSED. I live in Baltic street. The prisoner lodged with me for about eight months—I missed these articles form the room I let to the prisoner. THOMAS KIRKWOOD. I live in Brick-lane, Old-street, and am a pawn broker. I took in these articles of the prisoner. THOMAS MALIN. ( police-constable C  156.) I took the prisoner at the pro secutrix's house—she gave up the duplicates of these things at the station-house.
   Prisoner's Defence.  My prosecutrix has sworn falsely—she knew of these things being gone about four months—when I was taken, to Worship-street the clerk told me that she had been there five weeks back—when I have talked to her, she said if I would let her have 10 s . or 12 s . I might let them be—what I did was through distress. ELIZABETH TILSED re-examined.  I did not give her in charge, because I could not catch her in-doors, to give her in charge—I did not authorize her to pledge these things. GUILTY —Aged 38. Confined Six Months; Three Weeks Solitary.
