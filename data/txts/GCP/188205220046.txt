is a stable at the rear of an adjoining house, and it was by passing
through that and the yard, and mounting a 7-feet wall, getting into the
area of the prosecutor's premises and in by the kitchen door-I noticed
several foot impressions in the loose mould where flowers were growing,

different footmarks, some were larger than others-the drawer in which
