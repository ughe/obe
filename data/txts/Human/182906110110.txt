 1265.  MARY ANN SUMMERS was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of June , 19 yards of ribbon, value 1s. 6d., the goods of John Savage.
   WILLIAM RUSSOR. I am in the employ of John Savage, haberdasher, of Crawford-street. The prisoner came there on the 8th of June; there was a drawer of ribbons standing before her - I was waiting on another lady - I saw the prisoner take a piece of ribbon, and put it under her shawl - I then saw her take two more pieces, and put under her shawl on the other side - I went to her, and she said, "What is the price of this?" - I said,"Three-halfpence" - she bought one yard of it; when she had gone out I fetched her back - she opened her shawl, and a young man saw her throw down the ribbon which she had taken.
 WILLIAM ROE. I was in the next shop - the prisoner was brought back into the shop, where I was - I saw her throw this piece of ribbon down in the middle of the shop under the table - I took it up - there are nineteen yards of it - it is worth eighteenpence.
 Prisoner. I did not drop any thing. Witness. Yes, you did, and I took it up.
 Prisoner. Q. You looked about the shop, and found this ribbon, and then you asked the females there if they had had that ribbon, and they said, No - then you said,"You must have bought it." A. No, I saw you throw it down - I fancied I saw you throwing something.
 Prisoner's Defence. I took nothing out of the shop but what I purchased - I said, if you will bring a female, I will strip entirely - this young lad looked about for some time, and then be asked the females if they had had it; they said, "No, not since Saturday" - then he said, "You must have had it."
     NOT GUILTY.
 1266.  SUSAN MORRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of April , 6 shirts, value 10s.; 2 shifts, value 10s.; 4 sprons, value 3s.; 12 collars, value 6s.; 9 pairs of stockings, value 9s.; 2 collars, value 4s.; 4 night-caps, value 2s.; 2 night-gowns, value 1s.; 1 handkerchief, value 3s.; 3 dresses, value 1l.; 4 petticoats, value 10s.; 2 habit-shirts value 3s.; 1 pair of sheets, value 10s.; 3 table-cloths, value 8s.; 3 napkins, value 3s.; 2 kitchen-cloths, value 1s.; 6 towels, value 4s.; 2 curtains, value 1s.; 1 pair of drawers, value 1s,; and 2 waistcoats, value 5s. the goods of John Todd.
 PHOEBE GREEN. I live with Mr. John Todd,  No. 18, Pavement, Moorfields. Jane Morris washes for the family - the prisoner is her grand-daughter. On the 22d of April, the prisoner came and asked me for the dirty linen, and said she came from Mrs. Morris - I had not seen her before - I had but just come into the family - I went and told my mistress, who desired me to give her the linen - I gave her the articles stated to be washed by her grandmother.
 JANE MORRIS. I wash for Mr. Todd - the prisoner is my grand-daughter - I did not send her on the 22d of April, nor know of her going - I had not seen her for eight weeks before.
   WILLIAM HALE. I am an officer. On Monday, the 27th of April, I received information from George Morris- I went with him to the Salmon and Ball public-house, in Bunhill-row, up stairs to the dancing-room, and took the prisoner, with part of the prosecutor's property on - I took her to the watch-house - I found a key on her, which unlocked a box, where the remainder of the property was, at a house in Rose-lane, Spitalfields.
 JANE MORRIS. I knew nothing of this - the prisoner had been missing from the family eight weeks - when I went for the linen, they said she had had it.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 16.
 1267.  SUSAN MORRIS was again indicted for stealing, on the 27th of April , 1 counterpane, value 8s.; 1 sheet, value 6s.; two table-cloths, value 10s.; 4 towels, value 2s.; 3 waistcoats, vlaue 1s. 6d.; 1 cap, value 3d.; 5 handkerchiefs, value 3s., and 1 pair of stockings, value 1s., the goods of George Appold.
   MARY PATMAN. I am servant to Mr. George Appold; of Finsbury-square- Morris washes for us. On the 27th of April the prisoner came and asked for the dirty linen for Jane Morris - I gave her the articles stated.
   JANE MORRIS. I wash for Mr. George Appold; I did not send the prisoner there on the 27th of April, and never received any part of the property.
 WILLIAM HALE. I am an officer. I took the prisoner; I found this part of the property in Rose-lane, on Tuesday, the 28th of April - I got it from the box, the key of which I took from the prisoner.
   MARY PATMAN. This is part of the property, the rest has not been found.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years for each offence.
