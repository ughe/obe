let out in apartments-I put in a distress, and was sent for by the lodger,
who said that the goods were his-I saw & woman, who referred me
to Sidney-I said “Who is Sidney?" she said "A man who lives in
the kitchen below " I sent for him to my office; he came; I asked him
what right he had to send me a reference of that description, and put
the letter before him-he said it would be all right; that Mr. Grey was
a man of means, and would pay the rent when it was due-I said "Grey
is in Holloway Gaol, and I am surprised to find that he has taken my
property, and that he has two other houses in the same state," and I
ordered him out of the place- I wrote to Grey, and after he got out of
gaol I saw him about the middle of May-I asked him what he meant
by taking the house and not occupying it-he said that his daughter
objected to go to the house-I told him that his references were false and
did not live there, and complained about his giving me the name of Sidney
- he gave no explanation of that; no rent was ever paid I distrained
in January for the three quarters all the goods were obtained by
lodgers-I did not get possession till 1885, and it has cost me about 1301.-I
believed that the referees were respectable people, xud I judged also by
Grey's appearance-I believed that he was a man of means, and wanted
the house for his own occupation; I would not have let the house to let
out in lodgings. (MR. GEOGHEGAN here stated that the two Isetta wished to

SARAH SMITH, I am the wife of Francis Smith, a packer-we are now
oooupying 8 Block, Peabody Buildings-on 3rd April I took the third
floor at 36, Essex Street, from Sidney-I paid my first week's rent
through Mrs. Wilson ; Sidney asked me for it-I told him that Mr. Grey
had given me notice; he said that he was the landlord, and he would
show me if I did not pay him, and next day he put in the brokers for
three weeks, and I paid him to save my things being taken-I applied
to a Magistrate Sidney occupied the back kitchen and a room between
the first and second floors-he sat down below and slept upstairs--there
were other lodgers there, Mrs. Wilson was one I have children; I had
the rooms upstairs, but did not occupy them all-I went with Mrs.
Wilson to 2, Bossborough Gardens, occupied by Grey-after going to
the police-court I left 36; I would not trust myself there any longer.
