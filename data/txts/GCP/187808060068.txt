Cross-examined. I joined this firm when Mr. Beaumont went away ill-

I took charge--I had been going there for a month before, looking over the

went to the premises about 20th April—I am not related to Mr. Beaumont

made myself thoroughly acquainted with the business—I told the prisoner it
was very hard that he should take 3001. odd after he was already 3007. in

paid 261.the accountant did not report to me that he said to the prisoner
If you pay 2001. down there will be no prosecution, but if you don't there

-he said that the prisoner had proposed a bill of sale, but he said that
would not be worth a rap-he said that he told the prisoner I was 80

day—I noticed by the accounts that the prisoner's sales had increased very
much, but that we had never got the money-although the business was
large it was not profitable.
