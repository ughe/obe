ERNEST CHARLES WILLIS. I am a bicycle manufacturer, of 152, Union
Road, Leytonstone-I make the “Clarence " bicycles-the name of the
maker and the number are put on the front tube, and the transfer
“Clarence" on the back tube-I gave information to the police, and on
August 14th I went to the station, and afterwards to 67, Vicarage Road,
Leyton, where I saw my machine in an out-shed-the transfer was taken
off, and our shield, but the machine was ours; the number could not be
taken out-it was worth about £7-the prisoner gave an address to my
manager, which was put in this book.
HARRY Rous (372 ). I received information about this bicycle on
August 7th, and on August 14th, about 9 p.m., I saw the prisoner in the
High Road, Leyton-I asked him what he had done with the bicycle he
had hired from Mr. Willis-he replied, “I never had one ; I do not know
where the place is "-I took him to the Police-station and charged him-
he said nothing in answer to the charge--he said he lived at The
Laburnums, 67, Vicarage Road. Guilty. --Three months' hard labour.

scarf-pin, and other articles, and 28s. in money.
MR. METCALFE Prosecuted.
JOHN RONALD JOHNSTONE. I live at 4, Friary Road, Custom House,
and am military expert for Simmons' Ordnance Syndicate -I am a
retired sergeant-major-between 9 and 9.30 p.m. on Friday, August 2nd,
1 was returning home through Victoria Dock Road—I was iguorant of
the whereabouts of the Tidal Basin Station, and asked the prisoners the
way-I recognise them—they said they would show me; they were going
that way-I walked with them along Victoria Dock- I wanted to thank
them for their kindness, and took them into a public-house and bought
them drink-I do not know the name of the public-house-after leaving
there they led me along by a school off Lansdown Road, and then
turned sharp to the right on to some empty ground, with hou-es on
one side-there was no pathway across it-when we got on the waste
land the first thing I knew was I was hit on the right ear, and rumbled
on the ground-I cannot say which prisoner hit me ; for the moment I was
stunned; no one but the prisoners was with me-while I was on the
ground one of them, I cannot say which, laid across me and caught me
by the throat, and strangled me—they rifled my pockets, and tore this
pocket out entirely; it contained a pipe-they took an 18-carat gold
watch, No. 1258, and chain, a solid block front pin set with a diamond,

pocket, and some in my right hand trousers pocket--they also
