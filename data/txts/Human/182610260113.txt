 2037.  DANIEL BROCKFORD was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of October , three 5l. Bank notes, the monies of William Cooper , from his person.
 WILLIAM COOPER. I am a  sailor. On the 9th of October, in the evening, I was at Poplar, and next morning, I missed three 5l. Bank notes from my jacket-pocket - I had come on shore and slept at the prisoner's house for a fortnight - he keeps a lodging-house. I had been paid on Monday, and told the prisoner I was paid - he took me to his wife, and I paid her 14s. which I owed - I went to several public-houses, and got home to the prisoner's house in the evening; neither he or his wife were at home - I went to bed and counted my money - I laid down across the bed, and in two hours one Valentine awoke me, and I undressed, but did not look for my money; I went to bed, and in the morning missed the notes. The prisoner and his wife were in the opposite room; I said,"Brockford, do you know any thing of my money?" he said, I was drunk last night; I said I was not - he came into the room in his shirt, and said I had better look about, which I did, but could not find it - there were other lodgers, and the door was open all night.
   HUGH LONCHARY. On this night I was playing at cards when the prosecutor came in - he sent for half a gallon of beer, and then went to bed. The prisoner came in afterwards, then went out, and came in with his wife, and said we had better go to bed, which we did; as I went up-stairs, the prisoner touched me on the shoulder and said, "I am going to put you into another bed;" I said,"I have no objection," and I went with Valentine - soon afterwards I got up to go down-stairs, and as I passed Cooper's room-door, I saw the prisoner putting his hand into his jacket pocket, and saw him take out three pieces of paper, which he separated - I did not go down-stairs, but returned to my room, and the prisoner came in directly and said to Valentine, "There are some men on the bed in their clothes, will you help me to get them to bed?" he did so, and came back. I am sure he took the paper from Cooper's jacket, which was on his back at the time.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did you accuse him of this till ten days after? A. I went to the officer next day - I was turned out of the house by the prisoner's wife before this happened - I met the prisoner on Thursday, and fought with him; he got two men to beat me - I did not say I would be revenged - the prisoner has surrendered.    NOT GUILTY.

 2040.  WILLIAM LACK was indicted for embezzlement.
   PHILIP LORTON. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Bishopsgate-street. The prisoner was in my employ, and entrusted to receive money, which he had to bring to account every night when he balanced the day-book; on the 5th of May he accounted for no money received from Mrs. Cole; he should have put it into the till with the duplicate - the duplicate she brought is not to be found, nor is it entered off - the prisoner should have taken it out of the till at night, and give it to the person who keeps the books to enter it off; he keeps a daily settling-book, and casts up the amounts of principal and interest, and hands them to the boy; I had him searched last Friday week, and we found on him this duplicate of Cole's, among several others; I accused him of having stolen the principal and interest, which he acknowledged and begged for mercy.
 Cross-examined by MR. BRODRICK. Q. Was your accusation respecting any particular duplicate, or the whole? A. I said, "I shall be able to bring this case home to you"- I did not say he might as well tell at once; he has authority to take one guinea a week from the till, debiting himself with it; there is nothing due to him; the person receiving money for duplicates writes on them, and this is his writing on it - it is 1d. - that proves he received interest and principal - I have two other shopmen.
   ELEANOR COLE. I pawned a remnant of stuff at Mr. Lorton's on the 15th of April; I believe the prisoner took it in - I am certain I redeemed it on the 5th of May, and paid him 5s. principal and interest.
 Cross-examined. Q. When was your attention called to this? A. Last Friday - I gave him 5s. - I recollect it well.
   RENTON NICHOLSON. I am shopman to Mr. Lorton. By his desire I have examined the account of the 5th of May - there is no entry of this transaction.
   RICHARD MORRIS. I apprehended the prisoner in the shop, and found twenty duplicates in his pocket - this was among them; Mr. Lorton said, "Have you not been robbing me of the principal and interest of these duplicates?" he said, "I have, and hope you will have mercy, considering my family;" I went to his house, and found some more duplicates.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. Confined Six Months.
