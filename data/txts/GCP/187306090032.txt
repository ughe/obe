Cross-examined. I have never been in trouble before I do not know
whether the other men are in the habit of taking books I made the

him something as security, nor did I then give him these books I did not
tell him that any of them were my perquisites--I did not tell him 1
was stealing them from my employers-none of them were given as
security for money lent me-I also sold some books to a party named Keel,
but not out of these twenty-three-I have not sold any to anybody else
the last book I took Plume I had 6d. for, with a promise that he would give
me more when I saw him again-I never pawned any Church Services
anywhere.
WILLIAM LARE. I am assistant to Mr. Rowley, a pawnbroker, of High
Street, Camden Town--I produce a Church Service, pawned on 8th May
by Plume for 58., it is worth 25.
Cross-examined. I would have advanced half & sovereign if he had
wanted it,& detective came to me and I went to Guildhall Police Court,
and saw Plume, and knew him-no one else was in the dock.
Re-examined. I have no doubt of his identity.
CHARLES OWEN ODELL. I am Assistant to Mr. Solomon, a pawnbroker, of
214, Gray's Inn Road-I produce two Church Services, one pawned on
1st March for 78., and the other on 3rd May for 108., by Plume-he told me
that the trade price of one was 258., and I lent him 108.-he gave his name
John White.
Cross-examined. I saw him at Guildhall, but I had described him before
I was taken there the books were wrapped up just as they are now.
Re-examined. I knew him before-I think he redeemed & coat and waist-
coat at the time be pledged the second book.
CHARLES SHEPPARD. I am assistant to Mr. Smith, pawnbroker, of 1,
Upper-street, Islington--I produce a Church Service pawned on 3rd May
for 58., it is worth about 108. 6d.-I am not positive who pawned it-Henry
White is the name on the ticket.
Cross-examined. I said at the Police Court that this was the man who
pawned it I do not know what has made me of a different opinion but I
thought it over-this was the only Church Service we could find I have
no recollection of seeing either of the prisoners before.
WILLIAM MOORE. I am assistant to J. F. Horne, a pawnbroker-I pro-
duce a Church Service pawned by Bick on 28th April for 58. in the name of
Henry White--it is worth a guinea.
