(Tuesday, July 18.)
RITTER, Annie (43, housekeeper). Using a certain instrument
upon Anna Trischer with intent to procure her miscarriage.
Mr. Muir, Mr. Symmons, and Mr. Travers Humphreys prosecuted;
Mr. George Elliott, K.O., and Mr. Cassels defended.
Verdict, Guilty.
Sentence, Five years' penal servitude.
HAYWARD, Charles (35, bricklayer), was indicted for and charged
on the coroner's inquisition with the manslaughter of Charles
Richardson.
Mr. Curtis Bennett prosecuted; Mr. C. A. H. Black defended.
Mr. Curtis Bennett, stating that it was clear that prisoner, who
was a man of good character, had acted in self-defence, offered no
evidence, and a verdict of Not guilty was returned.
ROBINSON, Thomas (43, glass beveller). Feloniously wounding
Eliza Ross with intent to kill and murder her.
Mr. E. L. Hadfield prosecuted.
ELLEN MARY PICKFORD, house physician, Royal Free Hospital,
Gray's Inn Road. On July 5 Eliza Ross was admitted; she was
suffering from eight cuts about the head and shoulders and one cut
on the right forearm; the longest one was an inch long; they were
only skin deep. This knife (produced) could have caused them.
ELLEN GENT, 62, Lever Street, St. Luke's. Ross lives in the same
house with me. On July 5 I was standing at the door with her, when
prisoner came from behind & van and stabbed her two or three times
with an open knife which I saw in his hand. I got a shovel and beat
him away. He walked straight away.
