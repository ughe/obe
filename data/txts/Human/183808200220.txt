ANN GRIMSEY was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of August , 1 gown, value 6 d  ., the goods of Mary M'Caul ; and 3 petticoats, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 candlestick, value 6 d  .; the goods of Mary Field Parkins . GEORGE COOK. I saw the prisoner between four and five o'clock on the 23rd of August—I watched her, and saw her go into the house No. 25, Greek-street, Soho—she was in there about a minute and a half, and came out with a bundle—I let her go two or three steps—I then asked what clothes she had got, and whether they were her own—she said they were—I gave her in charge—these are the things she had. MARY M'CAUL. I am servant to Mary Field Parkins. She lives opposite to Mr. Cook's, in Greek-street, Soho—I had some wearing apparel which I saw safe on the 23rd of August—I missed it—these things are mine—this candlestick is Miss Parkins's. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined One Month.
 2035. JANE HODGSON was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of August , 1 cup, value 1 s  .; 1 saucer, value 1 s  .; and 1 pair of clogs, value 1 s  .; the goods of Abraham Joseph , her master. ABRAHAM JOSEPH. I am a dealer in foreign china, and live in North Audley-street. The prisoner was my servant—I was not at home on the 15th of August, but this cup and saucer ( looking at them ) are mine. ELIZA FALCKE. I am the prosecutor's sister-in-law. The prisoner was going away on the 15th of August-her box was brought in and searched, and in it were this cup and saucer, and pair of clogs—the clogs are my sister's—I can swear to them—I spoke to the prisoner—she hesitated at first, but at last confessed they were ours. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Recommended to mercy .— Confined One Month.
 2036. JOHN HARRIS was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of August , 1 cloak, value 2 l  ., the goods of Sir James Clark, Bart. JAMES CARD. I am footman to Sir James Clark. I placed a cloak of his on the front seat of the carriage, on the 21st of August, at a quarter past three o'clock—it was an open carriage—this is his cloak—( looking at it .) THOMAS TOWN. I am coachman to the prosecutor. I was driving the carriage, and saw three chaps  about Hanover-square—the prisoner was one—they came down the pavement and turned back, but after that passed the carriage, and took the cloak off the front seat—I called to them—they all ran off, turned into Maddox-street, and I lost sight of them.
   Prisoner. Q.  How do you know I was one of the three? A.  Because I had noticed him more than any of the rest, in consequence of an impudent answer he made to some workmen.
