   NEW COURT .— Wednesday, April  6 th,  1842.
   Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1175. CHARLES NEARY was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of March , 21bs. weight of bread, value 4 d  ., the goods of William Clarke , from the person of Thomas Brooks ; and that he had been before convicted of felony: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months.
 1176. CHARLES DODIMEAD was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of January , 2 coats, value 1 l  . 5 s  .; and 2 pair of trowsers, value 10 s  .; the goods of Benjamin Briggs ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 33.— Confined Three Months.
 1177. CHARLES HORNE was indicted for embezzlement; to which he pleaded GUILTY  . Aged 12.— Recommended to mercy — Confined Eight Days and Whipped.
