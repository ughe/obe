 2427. JAMES JONES  , alias    Fury, was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of August , 1 coat, value 20 s  ., the goods of James Packer . JAMES PACKER. I live in Milton-street, Cripplegate. I missed a coat from my sit ting-room, on the 21st of August, at break fast-time—the prisoner slept in the same bed with me—I asked him if he had taken it—he said he had, and he did not know how he could have done it, for be was in toxicated the day before, and what he had done with the ticket he could not give me any account—he had pawned it, but be did not know where. SAMUEL SLOPE. I am servant to Mr. Russell, a pawnbroker, in Fore-street. I produce a coat pawned by the prisoner in the evening of the 20th of August, for 125. SAMUEL MALINGS( City police-constable, No,  457.) The prisoner was given into my custody.
 (  Property produced and sworn to. )
   Prisoner's Defence.  I certainly did take it, find told the prosecutor where it was, as near as I could recollect, under a promise that he would do nothing to me, and when I left the house with the policeman, I thought we were going to take it out. JAMES PACKER re-examined.  I told him if he would tell me where it was I would forego the prosecution, but I was in great confusion at the time.
 (The prisoner received an excellent character.) GUILTY  . Aged 26.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Ten Days.
