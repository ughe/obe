HENRY Dawson (Policeman, A 301). At half.past 10 o'clock, on 10th
January, I was in Crown-street, and saw Harrington leave No. 24-she
went down Crown-street and towards the Dials--I stopped her seeing she
had something under her large cloak, and asked what she, bad got-she
said, “My things "-I said, “What are you going to do with them !"-
she said, “To take them to a woman in the Dials; I do not know her dame,
if you come with me, I will show you "-I found nine sheets under her cloak
--going to the station she said that a young man gave them to her to carry,
and was going to give her a shilling, and he ran away just as she came out,
and she did not know his name.
Cross-examined by Mr. RIBTON. Q. Do you know Hart? 4. I have
Been him about, but never spoke to him before-I have never heard that
he is in the habit of giving information to the police.
Cross-examined by Mr. W. SLEIGH. Q. Did the woman give any descrip-
tion of the man who had given them to her! 4. No; there was no man
with her— I have seen her before.
WILLIAM AOKRILL (Police-sergeant, F 15). From information I received, I
took the two male prisoners at a public-house at the corner of Tower-street,
Seven Dials, on Thursday, the 12th-I told them I wanted them for a
robbery in Golden-square-Gavin said, “Who told you !"-I said, “Gordon
told me he wanted you "-he said, "All right, give me fair play"-I took
him to the station.
