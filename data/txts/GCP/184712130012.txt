SAMUEL Dean. I am ostler at the Swan, Golder's-green, Hendon. On
17th Nov., between ten and eleven o'clock, Kennedy came to the bar and
asked for half a pint of beer, for which he gave my mistress 1s.-she said it
was a bad one, and gave it him back-I asked him to let me look at it-he
did so, and bit it-it was bad.
GEORGE Wells. On 17th Nov., a little before two o'clock in the after-
noon, I was in Wise-lane, near Mill-hill, and saw the two prisoners there
together; they were talking, and in company-they were about three quarters
of a mile from the Angel-I saw them go up the hill together, and kept them
in sight till they got within a quarter of a mile from the Angel.

watching some cattle, and saw the prisoners coming up the road together
they were going towards the Angel, about 100 yards from it.
