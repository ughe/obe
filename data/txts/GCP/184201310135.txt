768. HENRY BERRY was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of Ja-
nuary, 3 dead bares, value 158.; and 3 hooks, value 6d. ; the goods of
William Tucker.
William James. I live in Liverpool-street, Walworth-I was passing
No. 287, Strand, on Wednesday, the 19th of January, and saw the prisoner
take three hares with the hooks, off the bar on which they hung ---he walked
across the Strand and down Norfolk-street-I went and inquired at the shop.
of Mr. Tucker-he sent his man out with me, and we took the prisoner at
a public-house near there, with the hares lying by his side.
WILLIAM TUCKER. In consequence of information, I sent my man
after the prisoner-I saw the hares brought back-they were nine, and
were worth 158.
