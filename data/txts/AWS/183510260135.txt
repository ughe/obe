2277. THOMAS WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 19th
of October, 1 pair of trowsers, value 208. 1 waistcoat, value 10s. 2
Jeremiah Keeble.
handkerchiefs, value 2s. ; and 1 pair of braces, value 2d.; the goods of
JEREMIAH KEEBLB. I am mate of a coasting vessel belonging to
Ipswich-she was lying at Barking Reach. The prisoner is quite a stranger
to me-on the morning of the 19th of October, the watchman called me,
and I found the prisoner by the side of my bed in the cabin, lying on
the floor-I turned him out of the cabin-I missed the property stated-I
got an officer, and came to London, and found the prisoner on Tower-hill
-he had my handkerchief and braces, on him.
GEoRGe WILLIAM RAINBow. I am an officer. I came to London, and
found the prisoner with this handkerchief round his neck, and these braces
inside his shirt-he was intoxicated.
Prisoner's Defence. I do not know whether they took the things from
me-I was too far in liquor-I served my time in Barking, and was
there all day on Sunday, speaking to the overseer, but they would not
relieve me.
GUILTY. Aged .-Confined T'wo Months.
