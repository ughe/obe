173. GEORGE JOHN PAGE was indicted for stealing 4 feet of brass tubing, value 4 s  . 6 d  .; 2 iron drills, 1 s  .; and 2 iron brackets, 2 s  .; the goods of the London and South Western Railway Company , his masters.
   MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution . GEORGE WILLIAMS( police-constable V  97.) In the morning of last Saturday week I went with Dalby and Ratcliff to No. 56, Wickham-street, Vauxhall—Ratcliff went up stairs and brought the prisoner down—I staid below—I then asked him to accompany me up stairs while I searched his bedroom—I found nothing there—the prisoner said, "It is no use looking here, you will find nothing; if you don't look into the box that is down in the back room I will give you a sovereign"—I went down stain where Dalby and Ratcliff were placed, and found a box there—it was locked—I asked the prisoner if that was his box—he said it was—I asked if he would be so good as to unlock it—he said he had not got the key—I said I should break it open—I did so, and found three large pieces of brass tubing, which I produce, and some drills and a screw-wrench were taken out of the box by Dalby—I then took the prisoner into custody.
   Cross-examined by MR. NEALE . Q.  What was the exact hour when you searched his bed-room? A.  Between seven and eight o'clock in the morning—when Ratcliff brought him down, I told him what I came for—I searched the lower part of the house—there were two rooms in the lower part—his wife was there, and two children, I believe—there was no lodger that I know of—I partly searched the, rooms—I had previously gone up stairs with the prisoner, and, searched his bedroom—I told him we had come to take, him into custody, for offering some brass tubing for sale, and we had come to search his house—he then said, "It is no use your searching here, you will not find anything; if you don't search the box that is down stairs I will give you a sovereign"—no one was present then but the prisoner and myself—the sovereign was not given to me—I had just done searching the room when he said those words—he was pre-sent when we searched below—I did not find any sovereign on his person, or in the house—the box was in the back room, which is a kitchen or workshop—I had been in the house about two minutes before I saw him coming down stairs.
   MR. CLARKSON . Q.  How long have you been an officer? A.  Eight years and a half. ROBERT RATCLIFF( police-constable V . 26.) I accompanied Williams and Dalby to the prisoner's house—I went into the bedroom, found the pri-soner in bed, and took him into custody—under a carpenter's bench in his workshop I found two iron brackets—when I was taking him to the sta-tion, he said some person must have put the, brass tubes into his pocket—he did not tell me how they got into his box. JASPER B. DALBY. I am superintendent of the South-Western Rail way police—I know the prisoner—he was not employed last Saturday week on the railway—I accompanied the other two officers to his house on the day in question, and saw the articles found—>as Ratcliff was lifting the brass tubing out of the box, he said, "Ah, there it is, there it is, but I can't help it; if money will settle it, you shall have it; but I will bring no one else in besides myself."
