NEW COURT .— Tuesday, March  9 th , 1841.
   Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 973. MARY DOYLE was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of Febru ary , 1 pair of stockings, value 1 s  . 4 d  .; and 11 shillings, the property of Joseph Johnson , from his person. JOSEPH JOHNSON. I live in Orange-street, Bethnal-green. On the morning of the 27th of February I went to the Black Swan public-house, in Schoolhouse-lane, Limehousc, I got drunk, and fell asleep—I had 15 s . in my left hand trowsers' pocket—when I awoke a little after four o'clock I had only 2 s .—I had spent 2 s ., and had lost 11 s .—I had seen the prisoner all day in the house—she was gone when I awoke—I also lost a pair of stockings from my coat pocket. WILLIAM BURN. I was at this public-house all day. I was going past the tap-room door, and saw the prisoner's hands in the prosecutor's coat pocket, and taking out a pair of stockings—I took them from her, and put them into his pocket again—I did not see her hand any where else. PHILIP HENRY. I was in the tap-room. I saw the prisoner put her hand into the prosecutor's coat pocket, and then into his trowsers—after I heard some money link , and she put her hand in her bosom. GUILTY  .— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Three Months.
 974. MICHAEL WALSH was indicted for a misdemeanor. GUILTY. Aged 35.— Confined One Year.
 975. MARY ANN MIDDLETON was indicted for stealing, on the   30th of January , 2 blankets, value 5 s  . 6 d  .; 2 pillows, value 4 s  . 6 d  .; 1 sheet, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; and I looking-glass and frame, value 6 d  .; the goods of John Smith : and that she had been before convicted of felony. JOHN SMITH. I keep part of a house in Vine-street, Saffron-hill. On the 27th of January the prisoner hired the first floor of me—after I heard something I went and missed the articles produced, from the room—they are mine. HENRY HALL. I am a pawnbroker in Baldwin's-gardens. These two blankets and the other articles I took in pledge from the prisoner.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I did it through distress. JOHN KENT. I am a policeman. I produce a certificate of the pri soner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )— the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 31.— Confined One Year.
