 1953.  ANN WEATHERALL was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of August , 9 shifts, value 90s.; 1 shirt, value 3s.; 1 child's coat, value 4s.; 1 yard of linen, value 3s., and 1 shawl, value 5s., the goods of Edward West, her master.
   FRANCIS SHERLEY. I am servant to a pawnbroker. I produce the articles stated in the indictment, which were pawned by the prisoner.
   EDWARD WEST. I am master of  St. Anne's workhouse, Westminster. The prisoner was a pauper in the house; I had employed her as a servant for three years - these are my property; the prisoner had access to them.
 Prisoner. I am sorry for my crime. GUILTY. Aged 57.
 Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor. Confined Six Months.
 1954.  JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of August , 30 yards of towelling, the goods of George Clarke.
   GEORGE CLARKE. I keep a linen-draper's shop, in Oxford-street. On the 15th of August, between seven and eight o'clock in the evening, a woman told me something was gone from the door - I looked, and missed a parcel; I went out, and took the prisoner four doors off, turning a corner - he had this bundle of towelling, which is mine; he said he picked it up.
 Cross-examined by MR. BALL. Q.Was it outside your shop? A. Yes; he was not running; I have heard he was in distress.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that he had found the property.
     GUILTY. Aged 31. - Confined Three Months.
 1955.  JOHN WHITE was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of September , 1 waistcoat, value 4s., and 1 brooch, value 1s., the goods of Thomas Garner.
 THOMAS GARNER. I am  groom to the secretary to the Portuguese Embassy, in Park-crescent. The prisoner was in the same service, and slept on the same floor; on Saturday week I missed this brooch from my writing - desk in the servant's hall, and this waistcoat from my trunk, which was locked, but the key was in it - the prisoner kept his linen in the table drawer; I inquired of the prisoner about these articles, and he saw me and the under-butler take every thing out of the room to find them - I then looked into the prisoner's drawer and found seventeen duplicates.
   THOMAS LUCAS. I am a pawnbroker. I have a waistcoat, a watch-guard and a brooch; I know the guard and brooch were pawned by a man in livery; the waistcoat is in the name of John White.
 Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN. Q.Are the duplicates of these articles here? A. Yes, the guard and brooch are in the name of Robinson.
   THOMAS GARNER. This brooch and waistcoat are mine.
 Cross-examined. Q.Is there not another Portuguess or Spanish family in the house? A. Yes, there are three men servants - the steward will not allow persons to visit the servants; the drawer in which the duplicates were found was not locked.
   PHILIP WEBSTER. I am an officer. I went to the house, and saw the prisoner - I saw the duplicates taken from the drawer; the prosecutor selected two of them - the prisoner said, "You were in great haste, I intended to return them, but I was in distress."
     GUILTY Aged 22. - Confined Three Months.
 1956.  MARY WOODHAM was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of September , 16s., the monies of Thomas Spearing , from his person.
   THOMAS SPEARING. I am a haberdasher. On the 12th of September I had dined in the City, and got rather fresh, but knew I was going the right way to Hammersmith, where I live - I met the prisoner as I was going into the Park; I felt a pressure, and my property was gone - I said, "You have robbed me, young woman, if you will give me back my money, you may go about your business;" she said she was innocent - I said, "You know better, I felt you take it;" the Policeman was just by - he took her, and found 16s. in her hand; I had had 18s. in my pocket, but two were left.
 JOSEPH HANSON. I was on duty between eleven and twelve o'clock on Sunday morning, and heard words between the prisoner and prosecutor; I got within three paces of them, and concealed myself behind a tree - I heard the prosecutor say, "You have robbed me of all I have got; if you will return the money, I will let you go;" I went up, and said, "What has she robbed you of?" he said 16s. or 18s. - I took her to a gas-lamp, and said, "What have you in your hand?" she said her own money - I opened it, and found 16s. 1d. in it; she said at the watch-house that a captain had given her 14s. -1s. 1d. was her own; the other 1s. she could not account for.
 Prisoner. Q. The prosecutor said I robbed him of two sovereigns? A.No, he said he had two sovereigns in his pocket, and so he had.
 Prisoner's Defence. He was very tipay.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
