2582. WILLIAM CO0MBS was indicted for stealing, on the lst of
September, 1 handkerchief, value 58., the goods of Thomas Bunting Lee,
from his person and that he had been before convicted of felony.
THOMAs BUNTINg LBE. I live at Daventry, in Northamptonshire. I
was in Smithfield, a little before eleven o'clock in the morning, on the
lst of September-] heard sometbing; I turned, and saw a gentleman in
pursuit of the prisoner-I looked, and my handkerchief was at my
feet--] took it up-I had used it in St. John-street-I saw the prisoner
caught-thi is my handkerchief.
CHARLES WOODHOUSE WOOLASTON. I was in West Smithfield on this
morning-I saw the prisoner lift up the prosecutor's pocket and take
o0t this handkerchief-I made towards him-be threw it down and ran
through the market-I pursued, and took him in Long-lane, without
losing sight of him.
STEPHEN SMITH (City police-constable, No. 254.) I took the prisoner,
and have the handkerchief.
Prisoner's Defence. I was twenty yards behind the man when the
gentleman laid hold of me.
