HENRY BATESON. J am a surgeon, living in the Waterloo-road. Early
on 14th March I was called to the Tower-street station to attend Wells—I
found him with a small wound on the upper and under part of the left side
of the head—one wound was bleeding ; it was such a wound as would
likely to be caused by a policeman's truncheon—the skin was separated, the
scalp was torn through—I have since dressed the wound—on the constitution
of a temperate man it would not be dangerous, but scalp wounds am
generally dangerous.
