   Prisoner's Defence.  A policeman stopped me, and asked me where the brushes were; I said, "What brushes? if you suspect me of stealing any thing, there is the police court over the way; take me, and have me searched;" I had not been near his premises. ROBERT MATTHEWS. re-examined.  His hand was behind him, but I saw no brushes. WILLIAM HENRY WILLIAMS. re-examined.  As he passed about twenty yards from the gateway, he placed the brushes under his coat; I saw them distinctly in his hand. GUILTY.
