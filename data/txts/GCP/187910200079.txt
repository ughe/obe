JOHN Law. I am a newsvendor, of 16, Gate Street, Lincoln's Inn
Fields--these two pink placards (produced, headed " Town Talk") were
sent to me; I received them with the weekly issue of Town Talk from the
office itself.
Cross-examined. I continued to sell the paper up to the week before
last.
