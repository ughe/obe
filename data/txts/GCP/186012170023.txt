Mr. CLERK. Q. Does the letter-carrier who has to deliver both the
letters and newspapers, take the letters from the newspaper table A. Yes;
I myself took the newspapers from the newspaper table on this morning
the boxes are divided on the table-after the letters are put into the Wardour-
street box, it is the Wardour-street letter-carrier who takes them out of
that box-the letter-carrier has to deliver both letters and newspapers, the
letters were being sorted at one table, and the newspapers at another-when
the letter-carrier has set np his letters, he fetches the newspapers which have
been sorted for his walk-the pocket in the prisoner's coat was at the side,
and went right to the bottom of the lining-I cannot tell whether his coat
was buttoned at the time.
