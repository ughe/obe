John Willis. I am a hosier, and live at No. 371, Oxford-street. I have

-previous to the omnibuses of Aaron Bray coming on the road, they were
conducted as orderly as it was possible for vehicles of that description
be; so much so, that I allowed them, two years ago, to change horses
my house-since Aaron Bray began running up the street, to my cost

- from what I have seen, Bray was decidedly the aggressor, because I con
