440. SAMUEL CLIFTON was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
December, 4 lbs. weight of pork, value 28. 9d., the goods of William
Clements, from the person of George Cole.
GEORGE COLE. I live with William Clements. On the 19th of De-
cember he sent me with a leg of pork in a basket to No. 8, Pearson-street
-I met the prisoner in Weymouth-terrace-he pointed to a door, and
asked me to look what number that was- I got upon the steps to look at
the number, and while doing so, he snatched the pork out of my basket,
and ran down Weymouth-terrace with it-I followed, crying "Stop thief"
- he ran back then, and up Brunswick-street, and the policeman got him
-I am sure he is the same person.
George Cushion. I was returning home, and just as I got down
Weymouth-terrace I heard a cry of “Stop thief"-I turned, and saw the
prisoner running towards me-- i pursued and took him in the Hackney.
road at the top of Brunswick-street--I did not see the pork till a gentle-
man handed it to me.
Thomas ELKINS. I was passing the end of Brunswick-street on the
evening of the 19th, and heard a cry of “Stop thief"-I saw the prisoner
running, and in crossing the path he stumbled-on looking round I saw a
hand of pork, which I took up and handed to Mr. Cushion.
JAMES MEAGHER. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner-the pork
was given to the owner, and he identified it.
Prisoner's Defence. I had just left off work, and hearing the cry of
"Stop thief" I ran, and they stopped me.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Two Months.
441. RICHARD DONOVAN was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of
December, 1 frock, value 38. 6d., the goods of William Nathan.
Robert Purser. I am a pawn broker, and live in Limehouse-cause-
way, near the prosecutor's. On the 24th of December the prisoner brought
this frock to pledge-I found a private mark on it, which I thought was
Mr. Nathan's; I detained him, sent for an officer, and sent the prisoner and
the frock to the prosecutor's.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. Had he had any thing to
drink? A. I think not.
EDWARD DRURY. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner and the frock
to Mr. Nathan's—I asked the prisoner how he came by it-he said another
boy picked it up, and gave it him, in Mr. Nathan's shop.
