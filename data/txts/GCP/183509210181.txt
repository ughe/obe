First Jury, before Mr. Baron Gurney.
2107. HENRY DEMPSEY was indicted, for that he, on the 2nd of Sep-
tember, at St. Mary's, Lambeth, feloniously did forge a certain receipt for
money, to wit, for the sum of 51., with intent to defraud Sarah Eliza Mas-
sey, against the Statute, &c.-2nd Count, for feloniously offering and ut-
tering the same, well knowing it to be forged, with a like intent.-3rd
Count, that he having in his custody and possession a certain receipt for
money, as follows, that is to say—“ Received of Mr. Dempsey the sum of
51., for one quarter's rent, due the 25th of March last, 1835.-S. MASSEY,"
feloniously did alter the same, by then and there altering the figure 5 be-
tween the figure 2 and the word of into the figure 4, and by striking out
and obliterating the word “March," and writing above the same the word
June, whereby it became, and is as follows that is to say,) "Received of
Mr. Dempsey the sum of 51., for one quarter's rent, due 24th of June last,
1835.-S. Massey"-with a like intent.--4th Count, for uttering the said
forged receipt, with a like intent.

MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
Sarak Eliza MASSEY. I am single, and live in Cornwall-road, Lam-
beth. The prisoner rents a house of me, at 201. a year, in Cornwall-road,
next to where I live-I gave his son a receipt for the quarter's rent, due in
March -- I cannot recollect the time-looking at a receipt) this is the receipt
I gave him, I believe; it now purports to be a receipt for a quarter's rent
due on the 24th of June last-the 4 having been altered from a 5, and
"March" struck out, and "June" written over it; but when I gave it to his
son, it had the words “25th of March," and not the 24th of June--this altera-
tion has been made without my consent or knowledge I never received a
farthing of the quarter's rent up to June- I wrote the whole of this receipt
except the alteration I have repeatedly applied to the prisoner since
that for the June quarter-he has put me off from time to time, saying I
should have it in a fortnight, a week, and then ten days-I at last em-
ployed Cox to distrain for the June quarter, about the 2nd of September
I never saw this receipt except in the condition I gave it to him.
