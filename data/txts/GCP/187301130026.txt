ORLAR
HIBAM FIRTH (Policeman E 47). Kay called me at about 7.45, and I
went with him to Granby Place, on the first floor, and found the deceased
lying by the side of the bed, and the prisoner standing by him-8 sheet was
wrapped round bis head; I pulled it off, and saw two large outs on the side of
his head, and blood flowing from them there was blood on the bed and on the
floor-I spoke to him, but he was not able to answer I asked the prisoner who
had done it, she said, "I did it”-I said, "What with?" she said that she
had struck him twice on the head with a chopper--I asked her where the chop
per was she said she did not know-I searched, but could not find it, but
when I returned to the house & second time I found it in the back room, at
the back of a porter's basket-I had not searched in that place on the first
occasion—there was blood and hair on it-I assisted the man down stairs;
he was put in a cab and taken to the hospital- the largest quantity of
blood was on the bed-the prisoner said on the way to the station, “I will
do it again when I come out."
Cross-examined. When I first went in she appeared very much excited
- she had been drinking-she was partly under the influence of drink-
do not know whether Moriarty was under the influence of drink; he was
stupefied by the blow-I did not investigate to see which bed the sheet
came off-the blood was on different parts of the bed, as if he had been
rolled over the bed the bed in the other room had not been laid on-there
was & bruise on each of the prisoner's cheeks I did not notice her arms or
her body.
GEORGE BEALES. I live at 9, Granby Place, and am manager of the
house and court where these people lived-I live two doors off on the
evening in question, about & quarter to 8 o'clock, the prisoner came into
my room very excited, and trembling so that she sat down, and then she
told me that she had hit Mr. Moriarty on the head with a chopper-I said
"Have you really hit him on the head with a chopper?" she said "Yes"
