JAMES SMITH. I am a butcher, and live at Cambenwell. My young man in going out with the cart lost a piece of beef, this is it—( looking at it )—it was found in an oven partly cooked—I know it is mine—it is my own cutting out of a loin. ABRAHAM PUTLEY. I live with the prosecutor. I was sent out with some meat on the 19th of January—I went to a house at Vauxhall to leave some, and then I missed this piece of beef—the prisoner was near ray cart at the time I left it—he said at Union Hall that he found it by the side of the cart—it could not have fallen out. JAMES HOOPER( police-constable P  83.) I was called on the 20th to take the prisoner's mother—she had this meat in her possession partly cooked—I do not know whether he lives in her house.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I found it all over  dirt—I washed it and took it to my mother. NOT GUILTY.
 864. THOMAS SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of January , 1 handkerchief, value 4 s  ., the goods of Thomas Peyton Ban bury , from his person. THOMAS PEYTON BANBURY. I am a clerk to a solicitor. On the 25th of January, between three and four o'clock in the afternoon, I was near the Town Hall, in High-street, Borough—I felt a tug at my pocket—I turned and saw the prisoner pulling my handkerchief from my pocket, and putting it down by his right side—I collared him and asked him for my handkerchief—he said he had not got it—he dropped it, and pulled me from one side of the street to the other—he struck at me and fined to get away, but I never let him go—the officer came and took him.
   Prisoner.  When you turned the handkerchief was on the ground, and you asked a gentleman if he saw me take it—he said be did not. Witness.  I saw the handkerchief in your hand—there was a gentleman who saw me struggling with the prisoner, and he said, "Hold on to him, young man," and when the prisoner was taken, I said to the gentleman, "Did you see him take it?"—he said, "No, I did not"—the prisoner stuck his nails into my hand. JAMES GIRLING( police-constable M  112.) I saw the prosecutor strug gling with the prisoner—the prosecutor's hand was bleeding—I took the prisoner, and a young woman took up the handkerchief. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Transported for Ten Years.
 865. EDWARD PARKER was indicted for forging and uttering, on the   28th of January , a request for the delivery of 2 bars of solder and a piece of lead, with intent to defraud Charles Edward Matthews and to other; to which he pleaded. GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Two Years.
