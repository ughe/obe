   Prisoner. Q.  Did I interfere with you before you did with me? A.  I spoke first—you did not ask which way I was going—I did not say I would not go for an hour, nor challenge you to come into the barge—after I was overboard, you did not say you were sorry for it, but it was as much my fault as yours.
   COURT . Q.  Where was the long staff?— A.  I left it there when I went over—I saw it not a minute before. WILLIAM STEVENSON. I am a labourer, and was in St. Katherine's Dock, taking a barge round the pier-head—I saw the passage was blocked—I could not get along—I stopped my barge within about twenty yards, and saw the prisoner jump off his own boat on to the deck of the prosecu tor's barge, and throw the prosecutor over between the lighter and my barge—I was not near enough to hear what passed—after he came up to the surface of the water, the prisoner went on board his own boat, and assisted to pull him up by the left hand—I could not render assistance from my barge, as it was loaded—I cannot recollect whether there was a long staff on the prosecutor's barge—I had got on the prisoner's boat, and assisted the prosecutor out of the water—I directly went to my own barge and did not hear any conversation—I heard the prisoner say it was as much the prosecutor's fault as his—I did not hear Lunt answer, as I turned to my own barge—I saw no struggling before he was thrown over—it was done momentarily—he went down head first.
