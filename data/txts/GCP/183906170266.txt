GEORGE POWELL re-examined. I saw the prisoner in the barrack-room
after the Queen's birth-day, and while he was on parade I saw his cartridges
between the rugs of his bed-he was not present when I saw them-I do not
know whether they leave their ball cartridges behind when they go to fire
blank cartridge, but he had left his behind-there were five in each packet,
complete and regular as made up-he had left them between the rugs of his
bed-there were two parcels, five in each, perfect and regularly made up.
Cross-examined. Q. How was it you were not on parade? 4. I was
not a duty man-the men sleep in separate beds- I was cooking, and
cleaning up the room, and saw this projecting out, and I found these
packages, one on the top of the other-I took the top one off, and put it
down by the side, that it should not project so much.
Q. Did you count the cartridges ? .. I felt them with my finger--his
pouch was not there-there was nothing at all remarkable in the appear.
ance of the cartridges- I could feel them with my finger, and the other
package was the same size-I caught them up in my hand to pull the top
one off--all I did was to put them out of the position they were in, that
the bed might be put right, but I could see there were five in each-I did
not open the packages, but I counted them outside- I could see them-
they presented the usual appearance-nothing induced me to notice them
- I did not see any other man's cartridge in his bed-I did not untie the
packages, but I saw there were five in each-there was nothing outside the
package to show the number inside, that I know of-(the package was here
produced.)
COURT. Q. Is that the common appearance of the cartridge ? A. Yes
- they laid one upon the other, and I took the top one off, and put it by
the side.
MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. If one cartridge was taken out of either of those
packages, must you not see it immediately? A. Yes, unless any thing
else was put in.

show himself with the guard- I said, “You have plenty of guards now"
