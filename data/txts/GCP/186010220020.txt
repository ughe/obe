Cross-examined by Mr. DICKIE (for Holly). Q. How long had you been
watching Holly? A. Six weeks; there were four of us engaged in watehing
him-I frequently saw him during that period, not duily, I think I saw
him fifteen or sixteen times I never searched him until this occasion;
used niy discretion about it, I thought it advisable not the first thing I
said to him was “ What have you got about you?"-he said he had nothing
--I handed bim over to Legg-he repeated that he had nothing about him,
and said “Let me come out," and he rather objected to the way in which
we handled him.
Cross-examined by Mr. LANGFORD (for Florance.). Q. Did you ascertain
