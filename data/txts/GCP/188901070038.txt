saw the prisoner except the twice-we were always paid on Saturday,
except once or twice, when it was Friday.
Re-examined. Bill Dodd was employed on this drainage work-he is

Mr. Prior's place the prisoner allowed the beer-my wages were always
21s.- I don't know whether this road was joint work.
