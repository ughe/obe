   Cross-examined by MR. CHARNOCK . Q.  What part of the house do you occupy? A.  The four upper rooms and one kitchen—the Connelly's occu pied two rooms under us and another kitchen—Brown was servant there at the time—I had only seen Buckley there once before, and that was on the Wednesday previous—I do not know whether she had any parcel there—she was with Brown one part of the time I saw her—I only saw her for a short time. JANE HARRISON. I live in Wellington-street, Strand. I have seen Buck ley once—a relative of hers, named Quin, lived servant with me—I have known Wigzell about a year—on the 29th of June she brought me a note to change—I took the number of it at the time—I scratched it with my finger nail on the back of a letter—I have it here—it is 61086—I sent Mary Quin to get it changed—I believe she is a relation of Wigzell's—while Quin was gone Wigzell said her husband had found it—she afterwards said, "If any thing is said about it, for God's sake say it was given to you by a gentleman"—I live at a cigar-shop.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  When was this second occa sion? A.  When the officers came after Quin, about a fortnight after, I think—I went to Wigzell's house on Quin's being taken for passing the note—the note was traced to me, and then I said it was Wigzell gave it me, and so it was—I am single—I do not keep the cigar-shop—I am in it sometimes, and have a sitting-room and bed-room up stairs—I live under the protection of a gentleman, who visits me there—other gentlemen may come sometimes—Harrison is my real name—I have gone by the name of Wilson, but by no other name. JOHN WILSON. I changed the note for Mary Quin, Mrs. Harrison's servant. She brought it to the bar to my sister, who asked me if I would change it—I said, "Yes," and my sister gave her the 5 l .;—I put it in a little drawer in the bar, and about an hour and a half after paid it to Mr. Summers, of Russell-street—I should not know it again. JAMES SUMMERS. I received a 5 l .; note from Mr. Wilson. I marked it at the time "John Wilson" on the back—this produced is it—I paid it into Coutts's Bank on the Monday following. JOHN VANDER KISTE. I am a clerk in the Bank of England. I produce this note—it was brought into the bank by Sutton, one of the out-tellers—I do not know whether it was paid in by a banker.
