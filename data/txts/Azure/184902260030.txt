ME. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
I am in the service of the London Dock Company. On
JOHN Mounts.
9th Feb., between ten and eleven o'clock, I was set to watch the second-floor
Of the West-quay-warehouse—after some time the prisoner went by the boxes
of tea he stooped down, took off his hat, and placed it on the floor—he took
some tea from one of the boxes, and put it into his hat—he came back to the
place where I was—I took him by the collar, and said, Holloa! we are
both met made no reply—I gave him into custody.
Prisoner. I saw him there, and thought he might be stealing tea; I took
it, to see what he would say. Witness. He did not see me at all—he was
not aware that I was there—he was quite in a convulsion when I laid hold
of him.
