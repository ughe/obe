ROBERT EDWARDS. On the 13th of April I was at Twickenham, standing
with some other boys, near Mr. Warner's the prisoner splashed me with
dirty water, and then struck me I threw a stone at him, which did not touch
him-he then threw one at me, larger than my double fist, which hit me on
the top of my head-I fell down on my face he then knelt on me, and beat
me on the back of my head with a flint-stone larger than my fist-my head
bled like a stuck pig- I was taken to a surgeon.
