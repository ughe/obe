CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT .
   MUSGROVE, MAYOR. FOURTH SESSION .
   A star (*) denotes that prisoners have been previously in custody—two stars (**) that they have been more than once in custody—An obelisk (†) that they are known to be the associates of bad characters.
   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT .— Monday, February  3 rd,  1851.
   PRESENT  —The Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; Sir WILLIAM MAGNAY , Bart., Ald.; Sir JAMES DUKE , Bart., Ald.; and Mr. RECORDER .
   Before Mr. Recorder, and the First Jury.
 440. CHARLES HAILING was indicted for wilful and corrupt perjury. NOT GUILTY.
 441. HENRY HAILING was indicted for a like offence. NOT GUILTY.
 442. REUBEN AMISS was indicted for a like offence.
 (  No evidence was offered. ) NOT GUILTY.
 443. JOHN BAKER , embezzling 1 l  . 10 s  ., and 14 s  . 6 d  ., the moneys of John Eyke , his master; also, obtaining 8 half-crowns, 10 shillings, and 1 sixpence, the moneys of Richard Matthewman , by false pretences: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 37.— Confined Four Months .
 444. WILLIAM PIPER , stealing 24 pots of bears' grease, and other articles, value 5 l  . 18 s  .; the goods of William Phillips Hughes , and another: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 40.— He received a good character.— Confined Six Months
 445. GEORGE FRANCIS , stealing 5 purses, and 1 bottle of oil, value 5 s  . 6 d  .; also, 2 purses, and 1 bottle of oil, value 5 s  .; the goods of Henry Trinder , his master: to both of which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Two Month .
 446. CHARLES BARTON , stealing 1 pair of boots, value 13 s  .; the goods of Thomas Steel : to which he pleaded GUILTY.* Aged 18.— Confined Three Months .
