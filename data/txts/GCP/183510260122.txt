NEW COURT.-Saturday, October 31st, 1835.
Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
2256. MARY ANN HODGSON was indicted for stealing, on the
24th of October, I shawl, value 3s., the goods of Elizabeth Walker; and
1 cloak, value 78., the goods of Eleanor Walker.
ELIZABETH WALKER. I live in Wood-street, Spitalfields. The po
soner came about the 24th of October, and wanted me to write a petition
for her, which I did, and when she was gone, I missed a shawl and cloak
-I went to the station-house, and saw her-I asked how she came to
take the shawl and cloak--she said she did not know that she was a very
criminated wretch ; I had been a very kind friend to her, and she had done
it through the means of a very wicked man, who had the duplicates this
is the cloak-We have not found the shawl.
Cross-examined by Ma. Doane. Q. Whose shawl is it? 4. My
sister Eleanor Walker's—the prisoner worked for me ahout two months
- her husband is a bricklayer, and had fallen off a ladder.
JOSEPH BLOG. I am a pawnbroker. I have this cloak, which w
brought on Saturday, the 24th-I believe, by the prisoner.
GUILTY. Aged 31.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prom
secutrix. --Confined Ten Days.
