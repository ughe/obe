 465. JOSEPH BARNETT was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of Dec ., I coat, value 10 s  .; the goods of Richard Reeks . JOHN STOREY( City police-constable, No.  414.) I was on duty, on the 6th of Dec., in plain clothes, in Farringdon-street, with David Storey—I saw the prisoner and two others cross the road—one took the coat out of the cart, and gave it to the prisoner—I pursued them all three, and stopped the prisoner with the coat in his possession—I took him to the station—this is the coat. DAVID STOREY. I saw this—I have heard what John Storey has said—it is true. RICHARD REEKS. I am a licensed victualler, and live at Roehampton, This is my coat—I left it in my cart in Farringdon-street that evening whilst I went into a shop.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A person stole the coat and threw it to me; he made his escape. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Recommended to mercy.— Confined Three Months .
 466. MARY ANN MALEARAM was indicted for stealing, on the   29th of Dec ., 2 blankets, value 2 s  .; 2 pillows, 5 s  . 6 d  .; 1 sheet, 2 s  .; and 1 coun terpane, 2 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of John Hoddy . CHARLOTTE HODDY. I am the wife of John Hoddy, a shoemaker, in Carr-square, Moor-lane. In consequence of information, on Thursday the 29th of Dec., I went up into one of my rooms, and missed two pillows, one sheet, two blankets, a counterpane, and a patch-work quilt off the bed—I went into Carr-square, and saw the policeman—I went with him to the sta tion, and saw the property there—this is it. BENJAMIN SEXTON( City police-constable, No.  161.) On the 29th of Dec., about five o'clock, I saw the prisoner coming out of Carr-square—she had this bundle in her arms—I apprehended her.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I met a woman; she asked me to carry the bundle for her to Union-street, and she would give me sixpence; the policeman stopped me, and asked where I got the things; I told him. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Six Months .
