     GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years.
 870.  WILLIAM DUGDALE was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of Robert Tate , from his person.
   ROBERT TATE. I am a goldsmith, and live in Regent-street. On the 8th of April, between eleven and twelve o'clock, I was in Conduit-street; I was followed by two suspicious looking lads - I felt for my handkerchief, and it was safe; they got behind me - I felt again, and it was gone; I turned and seized the prisoner, who was nearest to me, and took it from his breast - it was mine; I have not brought it here.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going up the street, and two boys let this handkerchief fall; I took it up, and put it in my breast.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined Three Months, and Whipped.
 871.  ALICE ALLISON was indicted for stealing. on the 10th of March , 1 blanket, value 5s., and 2 sheets, value 10s., the goods of William Winder.
   WILLIAM WINDER. I keep a public-house, in Gray's Inn-lane; the prisoner took a room there, on the 7th of March; on the 10th I was out for my pots, and saw her in the street; I followed her down Fox's-court, and saw a blanket hanging down - the street-keeper came by, and I gave charge of her.
   ANN WINDER. This is my husband's blanket; I know it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had just come out of the poor-house, and I did it to get a bit of muslin, to make some caps, which I had an order for, to enable me to pay my rent; I should have put it on the bed again in a night or two.
     GUILTY. Aged 60. Confined Eighteen Months.
 872.  JOSEPH HOWELL was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of March , 7 lbs. weight of hog's lard, value 4s., the goods of Thomas Moger.
   THOMAS MOGER. I am a cheesemonger, and live in Whitecross-street. I had seen the prisoner pass my shop several times, on the evening of the 4th of March: about nine o'clock, he took a bladder of lard, which was inside my shop, but the window was open - the watchman stopped him with it.
   SAMUEL YATES. On the night of the 4th, I saw this boy and another going along Whitecross-street; I watched them for a time, and then lost sight of them - I saw the prisoner with something in his hand; I asked what he had got - he said he did not know; I found this lard.
   MARY JACKMAN. I saw the prisoner take this lard from the window; another boy, in a brown coat, stood in the road to look while he took it.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Whipped and Discharged.
 873.  JOHN MCNAMARA was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of March , I shovel, value 2s., the goods of John Ivers ; I shovel, value 2s., the goods of William Ivers ; and 1 jacket, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Southcott.
   JOHN IVERS. I am a labourer. I lost a shovel on the 5th of March, from Battle-bridge, where I was at work for Mr. Southcott - this is it.
   GEORGE WADDINGTON. I am an officer. On the 5th of March I was in the New-road, and saw the prisoner with these two shovels and this jacket; I took him to the office - he asked to go backwards, and put this jacket down the privy.
   THOMAS SOUTHCOTT. This jacket is mine; I lost it from Mr. Jackson's yard, on the 5th of March - I never saw the prisoner.
 The prisoner pleaded poverty.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. Transported for Seven Years.
 874.  MARY ANN PEARCE was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of April , 1 pair of gloves, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Henry Simpson.
   SARAH ANN SIMPSON. I am the wife of Henry Simpson, a glover, of New-street, Covent-garden. The prisoner, (Lady Barrymore,) came to our shop last Tuesday morning, about half-past ten o'clock, and asked for a pair of gloves; I knew her, and was frightened at her, and told her we had not any that would suit her; she said she must have a pair - she was rather tipsy; she stood some minutes, and did not say any thing - she then said again she must have a pair; I told her she had better wait till Mr. Simpson came home, and perhaps he would give her a pair; she said she would not, but she would take these - she took a pair off the counter, and walked away; I had not dealt with her before, but she came to the shop during the very cold weather - I did not know where she lived; she had had a pair the night before, and paid for them.
   WILLIAM RILEY. I am an officer. I took the prisoner in James-street, Covent-garden, and found these gloves on her; she appeared very drunk, but she makes herself so sometimes, and you cannot tell whether she is or is not.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went in there the evening before, and purchased a pair of gloves; to the best of my recollection I paid 1s. 4d. for them, and he told me any time I wanted a pair, I was welcome to them - I do not know what made me do it; I am not in the habit of going in there - I have no recollection of being in the office till I was before the Magistrate.
     NOT GUILTY.
 875.  JAMES REEVES and CHARLES SMITH were indicted for stealing, on the 6th of April , 4 candlesticks, value 4s., the goods of George Kerr.
   EDWARD QUIN. I am a wine-cooper. Last Monday I saw Smith take a parcel from under the window of Mr. Kerr, a broker, at Poplar; I put down my truck, and took him with a pair of candlesticks; I asked what he had got - he said, "A pair of candlesticks," and I took him back; in about ten minutes I met the officer with the other prisoner.
   THOMAS GRAY. I am an officer. I saw Smith and Reeves in company at Poplar; they separated, and I went after Smith - I went and got these candlesticks of Mr. Kerr, and took Smith into custody.
