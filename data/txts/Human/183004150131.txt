 964.  JAMES FRY was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of January , 11 sovereigns, the monies of John Meredith , his master.
 JOHN MEREDITH. The prisoner was my  servant. On the 2nd of January my wife sent him with eleven sovereigns to my brother.
   WILLIAM MEREDITH. The prisoner came and brought me eleven sovereigns in a paper parcel; I saw the parcel, but not the money - I told him to take it back and to tell my brother I was not going in the City to-day, and he had better pay it himself.
 Cross-examined by MR. CRESWELL. Q. Then he delivered you a parcel, which you suppose contained the money, and as far as that went, completed his errand? A. Yes; I did not take it out of his hand - I went before the Magistrate; I heard what the prisoner said - I think it was taken down in writing; I did not say I would save his neck, but would transport him for life - I might say he deserved transporation.
   MARY ANN MEREDITH. I am the prosecutor's wife. I put eleven sovereigns into a silk purse, tied that in a brown paper, and sent the prisoner to William Meredith - I told him to say I was very unwell, or I would have brought it myself; I never saw him again till I saw him in a green-grocer's shop, in East-street, on the 26th of March, and had him taken.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you go to his mother's? A. Yes; he had been there, but was out - I have never seen the money.
 Prisoner. As I was returning I got into a crowd; my pocket was picked, and I was ashamed to return.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 965.  WILLIAM HILLIER PERRING was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of February , 2 quarts of wine, value 8s.; 1 quart of rum, value 3s., and 3 bottles, value 6d., the goods of Robert Brown.
 MR. ADOLPHUS conducted the prosecution.
   ROBERT BROWN. I keep the  Berwick Arms, at the corner of Castle-street, Oxford-street. About Christmas my cellar was under repair; I employed Godfrey, and he employed the prisoner, who I believe is his son-in-law - the door was taken off and repaired by the prisoner; in that cellar there was wine to a considerable amount - it is the inner cellar; it opens into the spirit cellar - I have examined the partition between the two cellars, and find a hole cut on one side of the post, for a bottle to pass and re-pass, and a mark where some one's finger had been cut and bled; I never allowed the prisoner to go to my cellar after the job was over - he never asked; on Saturday night, the 27th of February, my servant gave me information, and I had the prisoner taken on the 1st of March.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. When did you find the blood? A. On the Monday morning, when I found three bottles broken, and lying in the cellar; I do not know how long it had been there - the prisoner was taken on the same morning; I did not order his hands and wrists to be searched - the officer mentioned the blood to me.
   HANNAH HOARE. I am the prosecutor's servant, On the Saturday I saw the prisoner come from the cellar - I followed him up stairs into the tap-room, where he had been in the whole of the day; I do not think he had been at work for a day or two.
   WILLIAM TAYLOR. I am a porter and chairman. On the 27th of February I was with the prisoner at the prosecutor's house, waiting to be paid; my father and other persons were there - the prisoner beckoned me out, I went out of the room: he said, "Don't take any notice, but it is my birth-day; and if you will go with me I will give you a glass of wine;" I said, "We had better go back, and pay that one pot between us;" he said we should not be five minutes: I went with him up to a house, in Castle-court, where Lousia Lawrence lodges - when we got there, he took a bottle of wine out of his pocket, and we drank it; I think I had two glasses: he then pulled out a bottle of rum - Lousia's mother said, "This won't do:" the prisoner and I had a glass a piece; I do not think there was much drank - the bottle and the rest of it was left there: we then went to Mr. Brown's, and paid a pint a piece towards the pot we had - I thought I had been treated enough and did not expect to have a second edition of it; but when we were coming out: he said, "I have another bottle of wine at home, suppose we drink it at your lodging" - we went to his father-in-law's to get it: I did not go in, but he did - we went to Mr. Brown's to get a light: he then went to a fishmonger's-shop, and he served out the wine among a number of people there; we then went home and drank the rest.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you see him take any wine from Mr. Brown's? A. No; Godfrey is a carpenter - he bore a fair honest character.
 COURT. Q. How long have you known him? A. I suppose nine or ten years; he has borne a good character as a tradesman, as far as I know - there was one time I heard he had been here, but I was not in Court, and did not know much of him then: I have only heard of it since this business.
