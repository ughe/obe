THIRD COURT.-Tuesday, April 5th, 1870.
Before Robert Malcolm Kerr, Esq.
325. JOSEPH BOWERS (22), PLEADED GUILTY to stealing three coats,
the property of Philip Henry Burch.* ---Twelve Months' Imprisonment.
326. JOSEPH BUCK (19), and JAMES KINGSTON (20), Robbery, with
violence, together, on Archibald Barlow, and stealing a watch and chain, his
property.
MR. BRINDLEY conducted the Prosecution ; MR. RIBron defended Buck, and
MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS defended Kingston.
ARCHIBALD BARLOW. I am a weaver, and live at 99, Hope Street-on
22nd February, about 1.50 in the day, I was in Spicer Street, coming along
to Brick Lane-something seerned to pass my face and made me feel giddy
- I was then met by the prisoner and two others- I was pushed against
the wall, and I saw Kingston's hand go to my pocket-he took my watch
and chain--I was held down by the other two, and was nearly strangled-1
can't say that Buck was one of the two men that held me-my watch was
worth about 71.-my senses were nenrly gone, and how I received the cut
on my eye I can't say.
Cross-examined by Mr. M. WILLIAMS. Q. Where were you coming from
4. From the City of Canterbury public-bouse, Preston Street, not five
minutes' walk from where I was attacked-I had had two or three drops of
gin-I was a little the worse for drink, but I was not insensible.
Andrew Bort. I live at 2, Fleet Street-I saw the prisoner and two
other men-Buck had Mr. Burlow up against the wall, and his hands over
his mouth-Kingston made a snatch at Mr. Barlow's watch, and undid
his chain--they then stepped backwards into the road, and ran away-
afterwards saw Kingston at the station-he was placed with other men,
and I picked him out-Buck was afterwards placed with other men, and
I picked him out-he said if it was twenty years to come he would pay
me out.
