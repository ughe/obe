known the prisoner about five years-he came to me on Thursday, the

“Will you allow me to take this tweed, and I will leave this cloth the
while ?"--I said, “Certainly"-he said, "I will bring you the money on

day, and said, "I have not sold the tweed, but no doubt I shall be able to
sell it to-morrow, and I will then come and bring you the money, and take
