971. MARGARET M'CARTNEY ( ), Stealing 1 purse, and 18s. 83d, in
money, the property of James King, from the person of Sophia King.
MR. KEMP conducted the Prosecution.
Sophia KING. I am the wife of James King, a labourer in the Arsenal -
on the evening of 20th September, I was in the shop of a pawnbroker at
Woolwicb, and while there I put my band in my pocket and missed my
purse-I observed the prisoner, she had stood by me in the shop-she had
left previous to my missing my money-from what was said, I followed the
prisoner and overtook her-I said to her, “You have taken my purse and
money "-she used very bad language, and struck me in my face-I saw my
purse in her hand-it contained 188. 60, and one halfpenny with a hole in
it--this is the purse, and here is the halfpenny with the hole.
ELIZABETH COBB. My husband is a fisherman - on the evening of 20th
September I was in High-street, Woolwich I saw the prisoner and the
prosecutrix fighting-1 asked what was the matter, and the prosecutrix said
the prisoner had robbed her of her purse-I saw it in her hand, and she
said, “You are a bigger woman than I am, or I would smash you" and she
threw the purse at her-I took it up, and I said, “There is no money in it”
-I saw the money in the prisoner's hand-I took hold of her hand and
endeavoured to open it.
WILLIAM CROUCH (Policeman, R 332). The prisoner was given into my
charge-took her to the station, and found in her hand 108. 6d., 23d. and
one halfpenny with a hole in it-& half-crown was found by the female-
searcher-this purse was given to me by the last witness.
· Prisoner's Defence. I saw the purse and picked it up-all my clothes
were torn off by this woman.
Guilty.-Confined Nine Months.
972. PATRICK DOYLE (21), Stealing 1 watch, value 21. and 1 watch-
guard, value 158. the property of Patrick Carroll, from his person.
Mr. LANGFORD conducted the Prosecution.
PATRICK CARROLL. I am a labourer at Woolwich-on the night of
Saturday, 10th September, about half-past 11 o'clock, I went to a house in
Martha's-place-Susan King was with me,I went into a room with her-
when I had been in eight or nine minutes, the prisoner came and burst the
door open-seeing what was done, I walked out of the room, and the prisoner
hit me on the side of the head and knocked me down--I fell down outside
the door, and the prisoner took my watch out of my pocket, and the guard
from my neck—the watch has not been found-it was worth 21. 158.
and the chain 158.-Susan King and Elizabeth Nicholls were present-they
hallooed out, and called the prisoner by name.
ELIZABETH NICHOLLS. I am single-on Saturday night, 10th September,
I was in that house with the last witness-I live there-King and Carroll
came there, and Doyle came and burst the door open-I was in the room,
and Carroll and King went in the passage I did not see what was done,
but I heard Carroll say he had lost his watch-I had seen the chain on his
neck when he came into the room-I am sure Doyle was in the passage,
and I heard a scuffle.
Court. Q. Carroll came there with King? A. Yes; sbe and I were
living together-as Carroll came out I went in, and I saw Doyle in the
passage--- I did not know Doyle before.
