JAMES SOOTT. I am medical officer. at Brixton Prison--] have had
seven years' experience at Holloway and Brixton ; during that time I
have constantly had to observe prisoners with a view to judging of the
state of their minds--I have given evidence at this Court upon many
occasions-the prisoner came under my observation on August 6th
I saw him about 5.30 p.m., that was less than twenty-four hours after
the death of the children-I have kept him under observation since then
with a special view of judging as to the state of his mind-I have frequently
talked with him, and observed him, and had reports on his conduct
from the warders-he had a light all night and continual observation-
I have studied the depositions-I have Seen no evidence of insanity in
him-when I first saw him I thought he was sane-I have seen nothing
in the depositions or in my observation of him to make me believe that
he was insane on August 5th.
