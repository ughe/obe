784. NATHAN DANIELS was again  incicted for stealing, on the 2nd of Nov., 1 bottle-jack, value 7 s  ., the goods of Asher Prior , his master. JOHN MOORE. I am shopman to Asher Prior—we had missed a bottle-jack—I have examined this, and believe it to be my master's—the duplicate of it was found on the prisoner at the station.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Can you say when you lost it? A.  No. JOHN BAGULEY. I am a pawnbroker. I took in this bottle-jack, on the 2nd of Nov.—I have a corresponding duplicate—the jack appears quite new. GUILTY  . Aged 26.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor. — Confined Nine Days .
 785. GEORGE BROOKS and GEORGE MAYS were indicted for stealing, on the   26th of Jan ., 1 tea-pot, value 10 s  .; the goods of Henry Jones . HENRY JONES. I live in Rose-street, King-street, Covent-garden. About half-past six o'clock, on the 26th of Jan., I was standing at the door, and saw Brooks pass—I went into the counting-house, and heard a cry of "Stop thief"—I went and saw the prisoners in custody, with this tea-pot—I believe it to be mine.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Is there anything you know It by? A.  I have missed such an one. RICHARD JONES. I heard the cry of "Stop thief"—I ran out—the two persons passed me, from the staircase inside the house—I immediately fol lowed—I lost sight of Mays and followed Brooks—I am sure he was one—when Brooks got into New-street he stumbled, and this tea-pot fell from his hand—I took it up and gave it to the policeman—I believe Mays to be the other man—he resembles him in every respect—he had a frock-coat on—I did not see his face. SAMUEL SALDER. Between half-past six and seven o'clock, I was passing up New-street, and heard a cry of "Stop thief"—in a few minutes, the prisoners brushed past me—I stopped Mays—Brooks stumbled, and a blue bag fell from him—nobody but them were running—they were hatf-a-dozen yards from the prosecutor's house.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What are you? A.  A clerk out of employ. I lodge in Dorset-street, Salisbury-square. I have been out of employ three months—my last place was with a hosier, in Brewer-street—I lived there four years—I left because I had a quarrel, not on a charge of dishonesty—I do not recollect exactly what it was.
 (The prisoners received a good character.)
   MAYS  — GUILTY. Aged 19. BROOKS  — GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Six Months .
