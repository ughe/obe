 353. RICHARD BURGESS was indicted for stealing 5 yards of oil-cloth, value 5 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of James Vine . JAMES VINE, salesman, Gloucester-place, Islington.  About seven in the evening of the 11th of Dec. I had some oil-cloth outside my shop—I missed it two or three minutes after—this is it. CHARLES RANDALL( police-constable N  172.) I stopped the prisoner in Noel-street about a quarter past seven o'clock, on the 11th of Dec., with this oil-cloth—he said he brought it from his father.
   Prisoner.  My father told me to look after a place; some lads said, "Carry this just over the field; if you see a policeman say it is your father's." GUILTY. Aged 14.— Confined Three Months .
