1900. JOHN WAITE COLLINGWOOD was indicted for feloniously
uttering a forged endorsement to a promissory-note for E20, with intent to
defraud Solomon Cowan.
MR. HoRRY conducted the Prosecution.
SOLOMON Cowan. I live in Goswell-street-I know the prisoner. On the
20th of April he produced a promissory-note for 201., at two months after
date, dated 8th of April-he wanted me to discount it-I said I would rather
not do it, having had one or two transactions with him before, and had a good
deal of bother with him-he said if I would do it he could get his father's
name to it, and I returned it to him-when I first saw it it had only one
name to it, which was his own endorsement-he represented his father as
