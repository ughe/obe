Verdict, both Guilty.
Prisoners withdrew their plea of not guilty of obtaining £6 15s, by
false pretences from Henry Hardcastle and pleaded guilty.
Lucas confessed to a previous conviction of obtaining money by
false pretences at this Court on June 20, 1904, in the name of Edward
Smith, four other convictions were proved. It was stated that from
October, 1910, prisoners had been committing these frauds,
Sentences : Lucas, 18 months ; Cope, Nine months, each with hard
labour, upon each count, to run concurrently.
