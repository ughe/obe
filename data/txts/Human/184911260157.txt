 148. MARY ANN LAWES , stealing ¼ lb. weight of tea, 4 ounces of sugar, 8 yards of lace, 4 ounces of plate-powder, and two boxes, value 17 s  .; the goods of Thomas Whitaker , her master. JANE WHITAKER. I am the wife of Thomas Whitaker, of Park-terrace, Sydenham. The prisoner was in our service as cook for nine months, and left on 8th Oct.—I then missed some pieces of lace from my bed-room—I had five different patterns of lace—I have some lace here which I have compared with that produced by the officer—I also missed a box like this ( produced )—my husband has the Yorkshire Gazette  sent to him, and we bad the one dated 4th Aug., we missed a quantity of it while the prisoner was with us—the value of the lace varied from 4 s . to 5 s . a yard.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  Was it some time after she left that you missed the articles? A.  About three weeks after she left we missed some preserves, and that induced us to have her boxes searched—I could recognise the lace the moment I saw it, without the pieces which correspond—I had a great quantity of each pattern—it is not an uncommon sort of lace—she did not have to find her own tea—after the Yorkshire Gazette  was read, it was used about the house. HENRY PAYNE( policeman, R  6). I apprehended the prisoner on the 1st Nov., at Mr. Widdon's, on Sydenham-hill—I told her she was charged with stealing some preserves, and other articles—she said she knew nothing about them—I said she must go to the station—I sealed up her boxes at this House, and afterwards fetched them to the station—I then fetched Mrs. Whitaker, and the boxes were opened in the prisoner's presence, and Mrs. Whitaker identified these articles—there were four or five ounces of loaf-sugar wrapped in the Yorkshire Gazette  —she said the lace was given her by a former mistress, who was dead.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How much lace is there? A.  About three or four yards—these tin boxes are such as are used in confectioners' shops—I found this box of  plate-powder ( produced ) in her box. MKS. WHITAKIR re-examined.  I know that box—the lace was taken from me whole, and has been made up into collars and other things since.
