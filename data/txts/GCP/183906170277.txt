prisoner lived with me four years as a horse-keeper-he had left me in
October-I frequently missed pails and horsecloths, but I did not suspect
him-I believe he was out of employ-these things are mine-they have
my name on them.
"Prisoner's Defence. These two pails I used while I was with him in
carrying water home-I forgot to take them back, and the cloth I chucked
on my shoulder, because it rained-I forgot to take it back.

JOHN LAMBERT. I am a carver, and live in Pitt-street, Old Kent-road.
The prisoner was my journeyman for two or three months I missed this
property on the 10th of May, from a chest of drawers in the front parlour
--the prisoner went away that day, without giving me notice I paid bim
at the end of every week, and this was on Monday morning I had left
him at work-I found him at the station-house, with my coat and trowsers
on-I examined, and missed a sovereign and fifteen shillings from the top
drawer, and the clothes--he behaved very well while with me.
MARY LAMBERT. I am the prosecutor's wife. I put a sovereign and
fifteen shillings into the top drawer a few weeks ago, towards our rent-I
did not miss it till last Monday week.
