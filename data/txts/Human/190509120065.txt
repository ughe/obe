   Cross-examined by MR. THORNE . I was not in Milan in 1898; I was at Rome—I know that in 1898 there was a rising of the people in Milan—I do not know that the number of killed in that rising was more than 400—I heard that two members of the Italian Parliament, Turati and De Andreis, were tried by a military tribunal, and also a priest, Don Al bertario, who was Director of the Osservatore Cattolico, for inciting the people to rise—Turati and De Andreis were sentenced to fifteen years' penal servitude and Don Albertario to ten years—in Italy newspapers in free, but the first copy or proof must be given to the Public Prosecutor—I cannot testify that the words in this paper ( Produced ) are what Bresci said at his trial—when I put the interrogation the first time to him, he said, "I have not killed a man, I have killed a King"—( Paper read ): "I have attempted the life of the Chief of the State because, in my opinion, he is responsible for all the victims of the system that he represents to the defenders.
