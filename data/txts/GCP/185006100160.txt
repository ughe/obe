Cross-examined. Q. Do you know Bryant very well ? A. Yes: my
brother-in-law assists him in his business-1 do not know whether he assists

WILLIAM GLADWIN (policeman, G 122). I apprehended Brown on the
17th May--we went to Bryant's shop, and I asked Brown to point out the
man-he pointed out Bryant, and I asked him where the spirit-pump was-he
said, “I bought no spirit-pump"-I said, “Copper "-he said he only bought
two pounds-I asked to see his book-he showed it me, and I left it in his
possession--he showed me this copper, and said that was it-Brown said,
It was in three pieces, and you gave me 15d. for it" - Bryant said, no, he
did not, and stooped down, pointing to Mrs. Taylor, his housekeeper.
Cross-examined. Q. Did he say anything about Mrs. Taylor ? A. No;
he signified by pointing that she paid the money-the Magistrate admitted
him to bail, and he surrendered here to-day.

1202. JOHN BURLEIGH, WILLIAM BRYAN, JOHN OBIE,
MARY ANN STONE, and EMMA SMITH, stealing 1 purse, and 1 knife,
value 1s., 1 half-crown, ld., and 1 d.; the property of Mary Ann Stevens,
from her person : Burleigh having been before convicted: to which
BURLEIGH pleaded-GUILTY. Aged 10.-Confined Twelve Months.
John CARPENTER (policeman, R 38). On 22nd May I was on duty in
Deptford, between five and six o'clock, and saw the prisoners together, I did
not know them before; I watched them into a crowd ; they went to a lady, the
little boy Burleigh stood at her side, Bryan and Obie behind her, and the
females in front; Burleigh felt her pocket, and then they all went to another
part-they went to several persons, and then to Mrs. Stevens-Stone pushed
Burleigh near Mrs. Stevens, Bryant and Obie placed themselves near Bur-
leigh, looking down upon him to see what he was doing-Smith placed herself
so as to hide him. Burleigh got his hand in Mrs. Stevens's pocket and took
it out with nothing in it-Stone looked, and seeing he had not got any thing,
stopped till Burleigh put his hand in again, and it came out with a purse in
it was down on my knees, and got up as they were all going out of the
crowd, and with assistance took them all in charge-Burleigh threw down a
purse; he had his left-hand in his coat pocket-l put my hand in, pulled his
out, and took from it a half-crown, one penny, and one halfpenny--they all
resisted very much, and denied all knowledge of each other, except Bryant
and Obie, who said they came together.
MARY ANN STEVENS. I am a widow. On 27th May, I went to Dept-
ford fair-I had a half-crown, one penny, and one halfpenny in my pocket;
a purse with nothing in it, and a knife-I saw Burleigh look up in my face
the officer spoke to me; I missed my purse, and saw it in his band; my
money was all gone but a halfpenny-this is my purse and knife-produced.)
