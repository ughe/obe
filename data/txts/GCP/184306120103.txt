1796. MICHAEL GRAHAM was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of
June, 63lbs. weight of lead, value 8s., the goods of Thomas Parker, and
fixed to a building—2nd Count, for ripping and breaking it, with intent to
steal it.

WILLIAM PARKER. I live in Bainbridge-street, St. Giles's. On the night

of the 12th of June, about a quarter to eleven o'clock, I was in bed, and was
aroused by my mother-I went into her room, and heard the bricks rolling,
down the roof of the house - I went on the roof, and found the prisoner lying
in the gutter, the lead torn out from the gutter-board and chimney, and all
the nails drawn out-I took hold of him, and said, he should come along with
me-he said, “I must do something, for I am starving" -a policeman came
and took him—the lead is my father's, Thomas Parker's, property.
