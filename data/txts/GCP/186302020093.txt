EDWARD PANTHER. I live at 8, Graham-terrace, Macclesfield-street, City
road, and am & carman in the employ of Mrs. Hills, of Fetter-lane-on 23d
January I saw both the prisoners at a very few minutes after balf-past 8
they came into my mistress's shop- they did not speak to me then-I did
not hear them speak to my mistress -I was ordered by her to put the horse
in the cartCallan went and helped me, and asked me to make haste, as they
were in a hurry-I then went to the corner of Stonecutter-street, Shoe-lane,
which is some forty yards from Mr. Chapman's shop-Oallan rode in the cart
with me-he then left me, and in a very few minutes he came back with a
mok of potatoes on his back-I saw Goude whilst he was loading-he did
not speak to me-fourteen sacks were loaded into the eart-Goude was
walking round the cart, and in the vicinity, whilst the loading was going on
--when the fourteeu sacks were in the cart Callan said to him, "There are
fourteen sacks, will that be enough 1" -Goude said, "Yes," and he directed
me to go to Lamb's Conduit-street-I did not know his name at all at that
time-I went up King's-rond towards Lamb's Conduit-street, and when I was
going to turn to the right, Goude directed me to go to the left, to Red Lion-
street-he was walking behind the eart the whole way I went to Red Lion-
street, and stopped at Mr. Lomas' shop--he is a greengrocer and potato
dealer-Goude went and spoke to Mr. Lomas, and Mr. Lomas said the potatoes
were not large enough--I had directions then to go to Claro-market-I went
there--Goude rode with me I stopped at a publio-house, and he went
sway-while he was gone Callan and another man, who helped him carry
the sacks in, came up, and Callan asked me where he was I told him I did
not know--he then asked me if I had got any money to stand beer-I told
bim I had not; I had only enough to buy my breakfast with Goude came
buck while we were talking, and put the sample of potatoes that he had
taken back, into the back again-I then went from there to Skinner-street,
Somers-town-Goude went away for about a quarter of an hour, and when
he returned he said they were as far off as ever. 100
