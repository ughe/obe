1320. STEPHEN LITTLE and CHARLES MUNCEY were indicted
for embezzlement. Mr. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
JOSEPH BowTELL. I am in the employ of Messrs. Strutt and Co., of
Wood-street. They send goods in the country by the Eastern Counties Rail.
way-I paid the prisoner Little for the cartage of them on 14th Feb. I
paid him 18.; on 10th April, 10d.; and on 14th April, 28. 6d.
Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON. Q. Do you recollect seeing Little ?
A. Yes; here is his writing in the book on each of these occasions - we send
a good many packages by him-I make a separate entry of each of these
packages-I make a note for him.
WILLIAM SIER. I am in the service of the Eastern Counties Railway, at
the goods station in Brick-lane-Little was a collecting carman in the City
-it was his duty to go to houses and to receive goods, and payment for the
cartage of them he was to get it entered by Muncey, who was my junior
clerk, and to give the notes to Muncey to make the entry from-Munceyi
duty was to make up the sheet which would represent the whole amount
accounted for by Little, and then Little would pay the money to the cashier
-in Strutt's account of 14th Feb., here is 8d. entered in Muncey's writing-
on 10th April, 7d.; and on the 14th of April, 28.--I have here the memo-
randum of Little's of those dates—the sums received appear to be on 14th
Feb. 18.; on 10th April, 10d.; and on 14th April, 28. 6d.
ROBERT FARR. I am cashier to the Eastern Counties Railway Company.
It was Little's duty to pay to me the cartage he received-I have no check
on him-I rely on Muncey's entry in the book-on 14th Feb. Little paid
me 8d. from Messrs. Strutt's; on 10th April, 7d.; and on 14th April, 23.-
my initials are to these different payments.
Cross-examined. Q. This book is brought to you? 4. Yes; by Little
-I add up the entries according to Muncey's entry, and ask Little for the
gross amount, and what that is he pays me-I am not hurried-there are
about twenty different houses called on in a day, and five or six entries from
each house perhaps-he has never paid me more than there are my initials to
-I never had money to pay back to him to my recollection-he might bare
paid me money afterwards if he had paid me short, and I had made a mistake
-I never recollect his paying me any-it is always my duty to receive the
money, unless I get a day's absence-Little always brings me the money-
do not believe that his boy ever settled with me--this is Muncey's book-
is made by memorandums brought to him by Little from the City houses
the book is added up by me, and the amount is paid to me by Little.
