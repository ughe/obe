   MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  You mean you could not mistake one for the other? A.  No; I never saw the prisoner till he was at the office—I have no in teres of any kind in the result of this trial.
   JURY to SARAH FARROW . Q.  Are you quite clear the prisoner is the person you saw enter the door? A.  I am positive. GUILTY.* Aged 36.— Transported for Ten years.
