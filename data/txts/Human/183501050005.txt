THOMAS FRY was indicted for burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of George Gray , on the   30th of December , at   St. Bridget , alias   St. Bride, London , about the hour of seven in the night, and feloniously and burglariously stealing therein 1 guard chain, value 4 l  .; 1 watch chain, value 9 l  .; and 2 seals, value 6 l  .; the goods of the said George Gray ; to which he pleaded GUILTY — DEATH. EMMA PIMM. I live with my brother at No. 2, Hooper-street, Westminster-road, in the parish of St. Mary, Lambeth.
