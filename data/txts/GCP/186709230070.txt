Cross-examined by Mr. RIBTON. Q. Did you see the deceased about
half-past seven on that morning? X. Yes; she had been moved on to
the mattress—in laying her out on Thursday morning I tore her shift-
this is the shift that is covered with blood-she remained in it till the next
morning-I tore it to get it off her right arm-I rather think it was off the
other arm-I was forced to tear it because I could not raise the weight of
her body-it was made to open a little in front; there was & small place
to open, but whether there was a flap to it I do not know. I tore it down
the middle, nearly to the bottom; there was no other tear in it that I
noticed.
