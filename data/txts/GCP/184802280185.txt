970. EDWARD INGRAM, stealing 2 sovereigns; the moneys of Edward
Willis.
Edward WILLIS. I live at Clapham. The prisoner lived with his grand-
mother, and often came to my house --about a fortnight ago I missed the key
of a box, in which I had four sovereigns-on 17th Feb. I opened the box,
and there were but two sovereigns in it.
AlexandER RAPLEY. I left the prisoner in the room where the sovereigns
were, alone.

CHARLES BILLETT. I met the prisoner on Clapham-common-he pulled
. key out of his pocket, and said that was the key that would get two or three
couters out of Mr. Willis's box, and he meant to buy Hallet's little van, and
a donkey, and set up a duster.
Prisoner. I said it was a key I picked up.
Georgx CUTTINON. I saw the prisoner in Brunswick-buildings, Clapham
-he showed me two shillings-he, I, and some more boys, went to the play
-next morning I saw him with two shillings more.
MAURICE SHANNON. The prisoner went with me to buy some boots-I
saw him with three or four half-crowns.
HANNAH RULE. I sold the prisoner some bread—he gave me a half-
sovereign.
FREDERICK LANGTON (police-sergeant, N 19.) I took the prisoner-I
asked him what he had done with the key of the box-he said he had lost it
-I asked how he came by it-he said he had found it by the old man's door.
Prisoner. I did not have the money; I picked up a half-sovereign.
Guilty. Aged 15.-Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor.-
Confined Ten Days.
