HORATIO HASLEHAM. I am an auctioneer, and live in Jerusalem-passage.
Some time between the 1st and the 27th of June the prisoner came to me
and brought these curtains to me for sale.
Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q. Have you known him some years ?
A. Yes, five or six-I put these curtains up twice without selling them--I
had advanced 8s. on them--the prisoner is in the upholstery line, in the
employ of Mr. Walker, in Holborn-it is quite common for brokers to
buy articles at one auctioneer's and to take them to another-it was the third
time these curtains were put up that the prisoner's brother bought them-I
knocked them down to him as an ordinary customer-I do not know whether
he was aware that they were his brother's—the prisoner bad brought articles
to me for sale before.
