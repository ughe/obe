 1434. MARY HOUSELIP was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of May , 1 pair of trowsers, value 3 s  ., the goods of William Jeffery . JANE JEFFERY. I am the wife of William Jeffery. I work for a ware-house—the prisoner had been a lodger of mine four or five months—her husband is a sugar-baker. On the 21st of May, I made this pair of trow sers, all but the button-holes, and I took them into the prisoner's room, as she is a tailoress, to ask her where the buttons were to go on—she told me to leave them for a few minutes, and she would sew them on for me—I left them, and, next day, I asked her what had become of them—she said I had been into her room, and taken them—I said I had not—she then accused the girl, who denied it; the prisoner then knocked her down, and ran away—I pursued, and she was taken by the officer—she denied having taken them, but the duplicate was found on her of that and five other pain. JOHN WARRINGTON. I am in the employ of a pawnbroker. I produce the trowsers, which I took in, on the 22nd from May, from Ann Clements. ANN CLEMENTS. I pledged these trowsers by the prisoner's direction—I pawned them in my own name, which I always do. WILLIAM BUTLER( police-constable K  48.) I took the prisoner—she ran up stairs—I went after her, and I told her I took her for a pair of trowsers—she told me where they were pawned—these are them.
   Prisoner's Defence , I did it through distress—my husband has left me. GUILTY. Aged 35.— Confined Seven Days,
 1435. EDWARD PHILLIPS was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of May , 1 coat, value 30 s  ., the goods of Edward Phillips Doudney and another. EDWARD PHILLIPS DOUDNEY. I am a tailor, living in Lombard-street, and have one partner. On the 16th of May, I lost a coat from my shop—this is it. EDWARD DAVIS. I was up stairs when this coat was taken from the shop—I ran down, and took the prisoner in Leadenhall-market with it. JOHN M'DONALD. On Saturday night, I was walking up and down the shop, and saw the prisoner take the coat and run up Ball-alley—I sung out, "There's a man ran off with a coat"—Mr. Davis ran round Grace-church-street, and caught him.
   Prisoner's Defence.  No one saw me take it, and it was not found in my possession. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Three Months.
