JOHN WEATHERBY. I am a hosier, and live at 90, Lower Marsh, Lambeth.
On Saturday night, 11th May, I was fetched to the station-house-I missed
about nine pairs of stockings, or it might be a pair of two more they had
been on a shutter under my window-I found my stockings at the station
these are them produced).
THOMAS JAMES PAINTER. On 11th May, I saw the prisoner passing over
the road-I went up to him, and said, “Halloo ! what, thieving again -
I took hold of him-be turned, and began to kick and bite me--he dropped
these stockings-he struggled to get away- said, “I know you; you are a
regular thief; I had you before" - held him as long as I could he fought
me in a desperate manner, and had it not been for assistance, I should have
lost him-I gave the stockings to the policeman-the prisoner bit me through
the knuckle.
