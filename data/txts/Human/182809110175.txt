 1927.  EDWARD BROOKS and ELIZABETH SMITH were indicted for stealing, on the 17th of July , 1 bed, value 5s.; 2 sheets, value 2s.; 2 blankets, value 2s.; 1 coverlid, value 3d.; 1 bedstead, value 2s.; 1 broom, value 1s.; 1 pail, value 6d.; 1 water-butt, value 1s.; and 1 stove, value 2s., the goods of Sarah Thomas.
 SARAH THOMAS. I am a  widow and I live in Whitecross-street. The prisoner Smith took a room of me for herself, her husband, and child; when they had been there a week I found she did not live with her husband - she staid there about three weeks; I did not live in the house - I received information, went to the house, and found Smith was not there; I found a key to fit the door, I got in, and missed these articles - I once saw Brooks there, but not when the things were taken.
   MARY FLOWERS. I am a widow. I live in the house with the prosecutrix; I saw Brooks in a shed with the water-butt in his hand; I do not know when it was.
   ELIZABETH WEAVER. I am a widow. On the 17th of July, Smith came to me and said she was going into the country for ten weeks, and asked me to take care of a bed, a bolster, some chairs, and a little crockery, and she would give me 4d. a week - Brooks soon after brought a bed; I said, "You are not the person I agreed to take it of, without her I will have nothing to do with it;" she came directly after, and said it was all right - I took it in, but had nothing more.
   THOMAS ELMER. I am a broker. I bought a bedstead and stove of Brooks; he said he was going to harness-work, and wanted to sell them - I gave him 5s. and a pint of porter for them.
   THOMAS HARRISON. I am a constable. I found the prisoners at a house in Crown-court, Golden-lane, both intoxicated.
   JOHN TOWNLEY. I am a watchman. I assisted in taking Smith to the watch-house; on the road she said money would be no object, and I think mentioned a sovereign.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 BROOKS' Defence. I never knew but they were Smith's things - I carried them for her, as she said she wanted money.
 ELIZABETH SMITH's Defence. The man did not know but they were my own - I was in great distress.
 SMITH -    GUILTY. Aged 43. Confined Two Months.
 BROOKS -    NOT GUILTY.
 1928.  FRANCES ELLIS was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of August , 1 bolster, value 6s.; 1 pillow, value 3s.; 1 blanket, value 2s.; 1 quilt, value 1s. 6d.; and 2 sheets, value 2s., the goods of James Bye.
   ANN BYE. I am the wife of James Bye, and live in Hamilton-row, Clerkenwell. On the 20th of May the prisoner came to lodge with me, and staid till the 20th of August, when I suspected all was not right; I got a constable, and missed these things, which were let with the room.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did not her husband have them? A. Yes; she told me she pawned the things herself - not that her husband watched at the door.
   JOHN BUTTON. I am a pawnbroker. I have a blanket, one sheet, and a pillow, pawned by a female - I do not know who.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you look outside the door? A. No.
   JOHN ABETHELL. I am a pawnbroker, and have a bolster, sheet, and quilt pawned by the prisoner.
   SAMUEL MOUNTSTEVENS. I am an officer, and took the prisoner - she told me the things were pawned, and the duplicates were at her daughter's house - a little boy came in, and she sent him for them, and he brought them; her husband was present, and said they were pawned with his knowledge, and he said so at the office, but was discharged.
     GUILTY. Aged 48. Confined Six Weeks.
 1929.  THOMAS BULGER was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of August , 19 shillings, and 15d. in copper monies, the monies of Richard Tomlinson.
 RICHARD TOMLINSON. I am a  simpler for the chemists. On the 17th of August, between two and three o'clock in the afternoon, I went to a public-house, and had a pot of beer: I fell asleep, and had 19s. in silver, and about 2s. 6d. in copper, in my pocket - I had seen the prisoner before I went to sleep, and when I awoke my silver was gone from my left hand trousers pocket, and my copper from the right; the prisoner had left the place; he came to the same house in about a week, and instead of asking my pardon, he was rather saucy, and I had him taken.
   JOHN DICKS. I am a coal-porter. On the 17th of August I was in the Blue Boar public-house, Cock-lane; the prisoner went and sat by the side of Tomlinson, and soon after I saw his hand in his pocket; I said I would not see such a thing done where I was - he jumped up, and challenged me to fight; after some time he came and sat down, and took up a bag which fell out of the prosecutor's hat, which was on the table; I got up, and went to another part of the room; when the prosecutor awoke he was gone.
