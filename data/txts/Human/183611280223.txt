HAMMOND SMITH( police-constable R  75.) I was with Wild when the men came for the apples—I noticed the countenances of the men—the prisoner is one I am positive—I afterwards took a man at the top of a ladder and saw the prisoner underneath—I am positive he is the same person.
   Cross-examined.  I believe you had the misfortune to let your prisoner go? Yes—I had them both at the time the tall one made his escape—the other policeman was gone to the corner of the wood—I called to him, but the other got back to the house and got away. HENRY CLARK. I am coachman to Mr. Farquhar. I believe I have seen this basket—I would not be positive to it. JOSEPH HILL GREGORY re-examined. Q.  There were some walnuts lost? A.  Yes—there are some at the bottom of the sack among the apples—this is the gathering basket which I lost—I know it by three little marks on the that I made with my knife.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you make these marks since the apprehension of the prisoner? A.  No—they have been there ever since I have, about eleven months.
   MR. DOANE . called JAMES WATSON. I am the landlord of the White Horse, within half a mile of this place. On Sunday night the 27th of last month, I recollect seeing the prisoner at my house—he was there about three o'clock in the afternoon, and staid there till ten minutes before eleven o'clock—that was the time he went—I went into the tap-room between eight and nine o'clock, he was then asleep—when I went in at ten o'clock he was in company with one or two more, drinking some beer—at half-past ten o'clock I took him in a pot of beer—he said, "I shall not pay you for it, I did not get my money on Saturday night—I shall receive my money from Mr. Ledger, at Deptford"—at ten minutes to eleven o'clock he came to the bar to my wife, and asked what beer he had had—he said he would pay to-morrow—I went into my tap-room, and it was ten minutes to eleven o'clock—I shut up at a quarter before eleven o'clock—I am quite positive that between a quarter past ten o'clock and ten minutes before eleven o'clock the prisoner was on my premises.
   MR. BODKIN . Q.  How far is your house from the wood? A.
