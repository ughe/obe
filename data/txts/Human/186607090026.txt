   COURT . Q.  When was the bill brought to you? A.  Between the 12th and the 14th November, certainly.
   MR. METCALFE . Q.  Was it brought to you then by Mr. Potts? A.  By Mr. Potts—I at first declined to discount it—I declined it in toto  —I did not direct him to bring other bills, I simply declined it—after that he brought me other bills—on 24th November he brought back this bill of 200 l ., and one for 100 l .—that was the earliest in date, one dated 21st November—I gave him 100 l ., less the discount—I gave him 80 l .—that was in two cheques, one of 50 l . and the other of 30 l .—he brought the next day another 100 l . bill dated 24th, and I gave him a further advance of 100 l ., less the discount, 80 l . again—I gave him 80 l . and 5 l . for commission—I should say the bill was brought on the 25th, the next day—that 5 l . was commission for him as an agent, because the 200 l . bill was not drawn, and he produced a letter from Mr. Break spear—the 200 l . bill was not signed—it was accepted, but not signed.
   MR. CHAMBERS . Q.  You mean it had not the drawer's name? A.  The drawer's name.
   MR. METCALFE . Q.  The first bill became due on 10th February, I think? A.
