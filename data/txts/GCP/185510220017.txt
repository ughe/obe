HENRIETTA FRANCES SUTTON. I am the wife of Frederick Sutton, am a
schoolmistress, and live in St. Bride's place, Vauxhall Bridge-road; I know
Lawrence. On the Saturday morning before I went before the Magistrate,
she came to my place with this paper, and asked me to read the name on it
- I told her did not know whether it was Flight or Clight; I took it to
be a tallyman's bill, and told her so, and that it was of no use I had never
seen a cheque before; she then left-I went to her place about half an hour
afterwards the prisoners came in while I was there-Lawrence asked Riley
if she would take a walk with her as far as Charing-cross-she said, "Yes"
-Lawrence told me that she found the cheque in the Vauxhall-road, be-
tweeen St. Bride's place and the George the Fourth, on Friday evening,
between 6 and 7 o'clock-I did not see any other money in her possession
her husband is a mason, I believe-_I heard her say that her husband kept
her very short, and that she had pawned something that week.
