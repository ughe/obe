1592. HENRY JORDEN was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value
28.; the goods of John Gibbons, from his person.
JOHN GIBBONS. I live at 11, Rotherhitbe-wall. On the 30th of June,
about a quarter-past ten o'clock, at night I was passing London-bridge, and
felt a tug at my pocket-I turned round, and saw my handkerchief in the
prisoner's possession-I caught hold of him-he tried to throw the hand-
kerchief over the bridge-a policeman came up and I gave him in charge this
is my handkerchief produced)—I am quite sure he had it in his hand.
Prisoner. Q. Did you see me take it ? A. No; I felt it go ; and
when I turned round it was in your hand I did not pick it up off the
ground.
John NEEDHAM PEACOCK (City police-constable, No. 567.) The prisoner
was given into my custody by Gibbons.
Prisoner's Defence. The handkerchief was chucked down three yards from
me, and he picked it up; the policeman came about seven minutes afterwards ;
there were two more behind me; he would not take them ; it was not me
that took it.
