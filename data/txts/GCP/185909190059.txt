863. CHARLES HAYES, Stealing 24 lbs. of bacon of Thomas William
Mason.
Mr. HORRY conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS WILLIAM Mason. I am a grocer, of Plaistow-on Saturday
night, 20th August, the prisoner came for some bread and cheese-I sup-
plied him and he went out-he came in again about two minutes afterwards,
and I saw him go out again and take a piece of bacon from a board at the
door-he passed the window with it in his hand I could not get out quick
enough to take him-I saw him in company with some one else at the
Coach and Horses, about six yards from the shop door-1 followed him in
that direction-I am quite sure he is the person.
Cross-examined by MR. SLEIGH, Q. What time was it? A. About half-
past 10 o'clock—I do a middling trade-nobody else was in the shop but
myself—the prisoner's employers came and bailed him.

- I took him opposite the prosecutors window, which adjoins the Coach and
