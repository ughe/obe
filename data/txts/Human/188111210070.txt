   Gross-examined by Kunz.  I did not give you 11 l . 11 s . only, but the Whole 13 l . 13 s .—it was on the 5th that you asked me to change a cheque from Germany—you repaid me the 2 l . loan, and gave me 2 s . for the Christmas goose club.
   Re-examined.  I change from 20 to 25 cheques every week—I had lent Kunz 5 s . and 10 s . at a time, and the 2 l . belonged to me—I should not hate lent it to a man who was not a customer—it was not a betting transaction.
