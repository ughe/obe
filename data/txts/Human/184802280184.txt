   Cross-examined. Q.  You were examined before, and the Jury disbelieved you? A. "I do not know, they acquitted the two prisoners—I am not doing anything now—I left Mr. Pool's in Jan.—I left on my own account, without giving notice—I went away in the evening, and did not go back—I went after my character—I lived with Mr. Pool from July last, and I lived before that with Mr. Buckeridge, a publican, for six months—I was not brought up to any profession—I have been to sea—I was never engaged in any smug gling transaction—I lad off Deal for a week—I was assistant-steward of a convict ship for two years—I did not run  any goods at Deal—I and my uncle have not been engaged in smuggling—before I was lying off Deal, I had been lying at Deptford and Woolwich, taking in prisoners—I now have my victuals and my lodging at my mother's—I walk about in the day, look ing after a situation—I have heard of a situation this morning, to as servant in an engineer's office—I have done nothing since Jan, last—I live with my mother, and have not been out of Woolwich further than to Plum stead and London, backwards and forwards—when I come to London, my mother pays my passage—I have no other means of getthing money, without I earn it—mu mother is a laundress—my father is living in Woolwich—he is a baker my trade, but he is bedrideen.
   MR. BALLANTINE. Q.  You lived in one situation two years, in another eighteen months, and in the last from July? A.  Yes; and before I went to sea I lived with Mr. Dell, a wine-merchant, four years—I have a written character from my last place ( producing it )—it was given me by Mr. Pool. GEORGE RADFORD police-sergeant, M 19. ) I apprehended Carney, and began to look after the prisoner on 24th Dec., between nine and ten at night—I knew where he lived, but had not known him before—I was not able to find him at his premises—I made all the inquiries I could on the subject—I was not able to get him—I did not know Verrall before he was taken.
   Cross-examined. Q.  In the yard where the prisoner's premises are, are there not many people? A.  There are workshops and warehouses—the pri soner put in bail last Saturday fortnight—I have been looking for him before.
