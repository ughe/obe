the goods of Benjamin Green.be
EDWARD EAGAN. I am in the employ of Benjamin Green. On 19th
Aug. I went to Plaistow, and missed some rope, which I bad left in a basket
the week before--this is it (produced)-I made these knots--it is Benjamin
Green's.

RACHAEL RAYNER. I keep a marine-store shop, at Stratford. The pri-
soner and another brought some ropes in a bag--they weighed fowt. and 7lbs.
-I paid the prisoner 4s. for them-these are part of them-I knew the other
man, but had not seen the prisoner for twelve months.
GEORGE HARRISSON (police-sergeant K 15). On 23rd Aug. I went to
Mrs. Rayner's, and saw Mr. Green identify the rope.
JOSEPH BENTON. I took the prisoner-he said a man gave him 6d. to

Mr. Green's. GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
