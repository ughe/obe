 Three witnesses gave the prisoner a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 24. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Fourteen Days.
 822.  WILLIAM COOK was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of April , 1 plane, value 2s., and 1 jacket, value 2s., the goods of William Sharpe.
 WILLIAM SHARPE. I am a  carpenter, and live in Burton-street. I had these tools in Wilson-street, Gray's-inn-lane, on the 6th of April, when I went to breakfast; and when I returned they were missing.
   WILLIAM WARD. I am a bricklayer, and live next door to where William Sharpe was at work - Rogers came and told me to go into the house - I went, and met the prisoner coming over the back wall - I got over and held him till the officer came - I believe he was in great distress.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was quite in distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. Confined Fourteen Days.
 823.  WILLIAM BOWDLE was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of March , 1 coat, value 1l., the goods of John Ward.
 JOHN WARD. I am servant to Mr. Heath; I knew the prisoner and allowed him to sleep in the stable on the 8th of March; and on the 9th, I sent him to get some shears from Mr. Day - I gave him some money to pay for them; he brought me a message, in consequence of which I went to Mr. Day - when I returned he was gone and I missed the great coat off the bed.
 Prisoner. He gave me the coat to pawn and gave me the ticket of another; he told me to get that, and to pawn this. Witness. There was a duplicate of another coat which he took away - upon my oath I did not give him the coat to pawn.
   GEORGE KING. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Oxford-street. On the 9th of March the prisoner pawned this coat with me for 10s. - he then gave me a ticket of another coat, in the same name, John Ward - he took that one out and went away.
   JOHN PEARMAN. I met with the prisoner in a public-house, near Hyde-park-corner, on the 18th of March - he asked if I was out of service, and said he had two tickets, which he would sell for 5s. - they were of coats, which he bought at Ramsgate.
     GUILTY. Aged 25. Confined Six Months.
 824.  SAMUEL HEARN was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of April , 1 pair of breeches, value 9s., and 1 hat, value 6d., the goods of David Davis.
 DAVID DAVIS. I am in the service of Mr. Sparke's, a cow-keeper. I lost a pair of breeches and a hat on the 1st of April - I had seen them safe at half-past seven o'clock the evening before.
   WALTER OWEN HILLS. I am a pawnbroker. This pair of breeches were pawned on the 1st of April, about nine o'clock in the evening, in the name of John Bennett, by the prisoner.
   ELIZABETH JONES. I bought this hat of the prisoner on the 1st of April, between eight and nine o'clock in the evening.
   JAMES GRIBBLE. I apprehended the prisoner. He told me he had sold the hat and pawned the breeches - he told me the places - he is well-known in our neighbourhood.
   WILLIAM BARNET. The prisoner asked me to go down with him and take the things - he gave them to me - I did not know they were stolen - he went and pawned the breeches and sold the hat.(Property produced and sworn to).
 Prisoner. It was him who pawned the breeches - I took them and gave them to him.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.
 825.  WILLIAM BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of March , 1 book, value 3s., the goods of John Wilton and William Wilkins Wilton.
 WILLIAM WILKINS WILTON. I am a  bookseller, in partnership with my father, John Wilton, we live in High Holborn. We lost a book, called "Humphrey Clinker," between seven and eight o'clock in the evening of the 6th of March - I was in the parlour, and a witness came and brought the prisoner in we searched him but could not find it- we took a light and looked in the yard just by our door and found the book.
   JESSE PHILLIPS. On the 6th of March I was passing, this shop and saw the prisoner and another young man - the prisoner took a book off a small shelf and put it into his trousers' pocket; I collared him and pushed him into the shop - we afterwards took a candle and I found the book about two yards from the place.
 Prisoner. Q. You said there were three or four persons. A. No; you resisted, and turned to go up the gateway.
   RICHARD GARDNER. I was called to take the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I was standing near the window, with three or four other persons - as soon as the alarm was given they all ran away, and I stood still.
