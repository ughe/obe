748. MARTIN PFEIFFER (40), Stealing 50 Napoleons and other
coins, the property of Wilhelm Bergman.
Mr. Besley and MR. CROome conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. STRAIGHT
the Defence.
WILHELM BERGMAN (through an interpreter). I am a merchant and live
in Poland, I had been in England five months in May, or June, 1877-I do
not know whether that was the date of my going or of my coming back-I
do not know a word of English ; I corresponded with the prisoner before I
left here--I arrived in London again on Wednesday, 8th August, and put up
at the hotel Causier, 23, Broad Street, Bloomsbury, and telegraphed to the
prisoner announcing my arrival in London-he came to my hotel the same
night about 6 o'clock, and I had a conversation with him in Polish-he told
me be should be able to purchase some Chamois skins-he did not say who
from, only that he would call next day about them--he said that to-morrow
about noon we should go to the merchant, but did not give me the merchant's
address-about 11 o'clock next morning his wife called and I went with her
to 43, Clifton Crescent, Peckham, where they live-we went into the dining-
room where I saw the prisoner in bed, he said that he had caught cold and
had sent for the merchant who would be there in two hours--I remained
there, and at the end of that time Mrs. Pfeiffer came upstairs and said that
the merchant had arrived the prisoner put his clothes on and went down
with me to the merchant, whom I had never seen before--I have since
pointed him out at the Cafe Monico, in Piccadilly, to Mr. Woolf; his name
is David Von Hand-the would be merchant produced a little bag in which
there was one Chamois skin as a sample, and they conversed in English
which I did not uuderstand- examined one skin and made a bargain for
