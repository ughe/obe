1687. RICHARD WELHAM and WILLIAM CONNOR were indicted
for uttering counterfeit coin.
Messrs. BODKIN and ESPINASSE conducted the Prosecution.
James Howorth. I am in the Royal Marines, at Woolwich, and am em-
ployed in the barber's shop there. On the 31st of March, Welbam came to
be shaved-I asked him for the money which he owed me he said he had
not got any, only sham money-he produced two half-sovereigns to me out
of his purse, where I saw he had ten or a dozen more he gave me two, and
told me to keep them till he came again; not to part with them, or he would
never pay me what he owed me-he was immediately called away by the
corporal, and I put the half-sovereigns in a piece of paper into my pocket;
and when Suttle came I gave them to bim.
Cross-examined by Mr. Payne. Q. Where did you put them? A. Ina
cupboard, under lock and key-they remained there some days the polics.

ANN SONNEX. I am the wife of John Sonnex, who keeps the Duke on
Horseback, in High-street, Woolwich. On the 11th of April I saw my hus-
band clear the till of all the gold coin-there was none left in the till —Con-
nor was employed in waiting in the house as an extra hand during the fair,
Between four and five o'clock in the evening of the 13th of April, Welham
came in-he asked for half a pint of rum, which came to 18.-I put it on the
counter-Connor ordered it-Welbam came up, nnd said to Connor, "Billy
is this for me?" Connor said, "Yes, Dick"-Welbam said, “Here, take this
half-sovereign," and Connor took it from him, and gave it to me I gave 9s.
in change to Connor, and he gave it to Welham-I put the half-sovereign
into a bowl in the till there was no other half-sovereign there-when we
closed for the night, I took the coin out of the bowl and put it into a bag-
there was no other half-sovereign but that, and I took it up stairs-on the 14th
of April Welham came again in the evening—it was his general practice to
come of an evening-he was in and out a good deal that day, and treating
persons with liquor-he asked me for half-a-pint of gin-and-raspberry, which
came to 8d.-1 served him-he gave me a half-sovereign, and I gave him
98. 4d. change-Connor stood by, but had nothing to do with the change-in
the course of that night Connor came for a pot of beer, and gave me a half-
sovereign--he said it was for Dick, the marine-I gave him the change, and
