I am divisional surgeon of police, and on October 6th, about 10.45 p.m., I was called to 19, Myddelton Square—I saw Florence Purkiss, and stayed with her for some time—she was suffering from shock and fright—I did not see the deceased at the time; she had been removed from the house—later on I saw the prisoner at King's Cross Road police station—I remained watching him for half an hour; while doing so he was charged and searched—I heard what he said to Briggs—he was perfectly sober, rational, and, in my opinion, perfectly sane.
