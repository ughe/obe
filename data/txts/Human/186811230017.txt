 35. JEREMIAH DACEY  (20), and JAMES DRISCOLL  (17) , Robbery, with violence, on Caroline Elisabeth Evans , and stealing from her person a pocket and 3 d  ., her property.
   MR. BRINDLEY conducted the Prosecution ; MR. M. WILLIAMS appeared for
   Dacey ; and MR. W. SLEIGH for Driscoll . CAROLINE EVANS. I am single, and live at 44, Dudley Street, St Giles's—on Saturday flight, 14th November, I was walking through Seven Dials with two young men—the prisoners came up—Dacey struck my young man's friend, and Driscoll struck my young man—Dacey pushed me against the wall, while Driscoll tore my pocket out of the right side of my dress—there was 2 d . or 3 d . in it—I ran away, and did not know they were in custody till next morning.
   Cross-examined by MR. WILLIAMS . Q.  What time was it? A.  A few minutes before midnight—I was with James Orford; the other young man was Robert Smith—I had not long come from work—the young men I was with were not called before the Magistrate—I saw my young man last on Saturday night—he knew I was coming here to day—I had more than a 1 d .—I cannot tell whether it was in halfpence or pence.
