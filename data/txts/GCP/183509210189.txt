Thomas Price. I am certain the pony is the one I lost-the field

Henry Banks. Price came to me and asked if I knew St. George's
market - I put my pony into the cart, and drove him over to the market,
where I made inquiry, and found Bennett talking to the policeman–I said
I had come for the pony-he said, “ What sort of a one was it?" I said,
"A small one, a mare, nearly black, with grey hairs, and a big mane"
Taylor was alongside - I said, " I must give this gentleman into your cus-
tody-I asked Bennett to let me see the pony-he said, “ No, allow me
to go and get the person I bought it of"-he went and brought Showell and
another not in custody-Showell was detained.
Showell's Defence. I was at the Elephant and Castle man came to
me and said, “Have you any money ? here is a pony we can go and buy"
- I went round to the knacker's—the man asked 258. for it-I said, “ It
is not worth that" he said the knacker had bid 18s. for it--I at last
agreed to give 198. for it he gave me his direction-we went to a pub-
lic-house and had a pint of beer-I had to pledge to get the money, and
while I was gone he wrote the direction.
