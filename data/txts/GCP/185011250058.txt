71. THOMAS ELLEN, stealing 3 metal cocks, value 188.; the pro-
perty of George Clarke: having been before convicted.
GEORGE CLARKE. I live at Bennett's-court, Marylebone, and am a
smith. On 31st Oct., between six and seven o'clock in the evening, I
went out of my shop, I was away three minutes, and when I came back I
saw the prisoner and another young man coming from my shop-I went
into my shop, and missed three brass cocks which I had seen safe ten minutes
before-I then went after the prisoner and stopped him in David-street,
and he struck me very violently-I took this cock produced) out of his
pocket-it is mine, and one of those I missed.
Prisoner's Defence. I met a young man, who said he had bought two
brass taps, and I was going with him to sell them; I did not know they
were stolen.
MICHAEL Bright (policeman, D 106). I produce a certificate--(read
--Clerkenwell - Thomas Allen, convicted March, 1847, confined sit
months)- I was present at that trial-the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Twelve Months.
