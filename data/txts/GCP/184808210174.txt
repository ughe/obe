HOOPER, Mayor.
Cross-examined. Q. How old are you? A. Eighteen--I have given
cigars in the docks--I do not know the difference between stealing and
smuggling-I have not helped the men in the vessels to get cigars out of the
docka without their paying duty.
HENRY REDMAN. I am sergeant of the van that conveys the prisoners to
and from the Court. On the day the prisoner and Kippax were taken I was
stationed near the cells where they were locked up--they were in separate
cells, but near enough to speak to each other--the prisoner called out Joe"
two or three times-I got up from the seat, and went to the door, which was
open--the prisoner called out “ Joe, tell them that the mate of a Spanish
vessel gave them to you, and that they came out of a Spanish ship, and the
dock people can't swear to them"- Kippax said, "I shan't tell them that" -
the prisoner said, “What will you tell them ?"-he said, "Quite different to
that-I know whose doing it is-it is all Bill's doing."
Cross-examined. Q. Where were you? A. In the gaoler's room which
joins the cells.
JOHN ARNETT. I am foreman in the cigar-floor, in the St. Katherine's
Docks. On the day before this there were twenty cases of cigars--those cigars
produced are to the best of my belief the same as were in the cases.
Cross-examined. Q. I suppose there is nothing particular about them!
A. No--to the best of my belief they came by a Spanish vessel.

ALL, stealing 1 watch, value 31. 108.; 1 seal, 3d.; and 3 keys, 3d.; the
property of John Knight, in a barge on the Thames; Willis and Roberts hav-
ing been before convicted.
John KNIGHT. I am master of the brig Belus. On the morning of the
30th July, I was awoke about a quarter before five by a footstep, and saw
Whitall by my bedside, in the cabin-my watch was in an old shoe, and be
was in the act of grabbing it-I grabbed bim and he pulled me out of bed-I
caught his legs as he was going up the ladder-I went on the deck and saw
Roberts-he had no business there--I saw a boat along-side, and Willis in it
-I called out "Police !"-Whitall and Roberts got into the boat, and it
pushed off - Roberts left his cap on board—I am sure the prisoners are the
men-they were taken that morning when I went up to Whitall he swore
he would jump my b-y guts out--this is my watch produced.)
Cross-examined by MR. PARRY. Q. Was it not dark? A. No; the sun
was rising when I awoke-Whitall was by my pillow, hanging over me-I
was not a good deal excited, I was hurried-I should say this took about
two minutes.
Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. You do not mean to say it
was light? A. Yes it was this was in a large state-room, the door was
open, and there was a light came from the hatchway and the sky-light-
held Whitall about a quarter of a minute-I had never seen him before, and I
did not want to see him then-I took notice of him-I saw him again in
about ten minutes-I bad not hold of Roberts, but I can swear to him.
John Ellis GODFREY. I am an officer of the Customs. I saw a boat
under the bow of the prosecutor's vessel-I saw Whitall on the deck-I am
sure it was him-he went over the back quarter of the vessel, and got into
the boat--the other two prisoners were in it-I know them all three.
