Bankruptcy Court. The prisoner was formerly employed by Mr. Turner, of
the Bankruptcy Court, who is now dead-I am acquainted with the prisoner's
bandwriting, and have been so I should think four years--I have frequently
seen him write, almost daily-I believe the writing on this check to be the
prisoner's-no part of it is Mr. Cooper's writing-he was not in town on that
date ; he was at Penzance-I had a letter from him that morning-he had
been away for a fortnight previously, and he came back about a fortnight
afterwards.
