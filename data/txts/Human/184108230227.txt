2305. JOHN BRISTOW was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of November , 3 planes, value 5 s  .; 1 rule, value 1 s  .; 1 square, value 1 s  ,; 1 bevel, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 gauge, value 3 s  .; the goods of Robert Robin son , his master. ROBERT ROBINSON. I am a carpenter, and live in Magdalen-street, Southwark. The prisoner was my journeyman—he had no tools of his own, and I allowed him to use my tools on my premises, but not to take them away—I missed the planes and other things stated—some of them are here—the rest I cannot find.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Have you been in the habit of pawning things? A.  I have pawned many things on my own account, but I never suffered a man to do it—I never authorized the prisoner to pawn any thing, that I swear—I paid him 28 s . a week, and found him in tools, which is not usually done—I do not owe him any thing; on the con trary, he owes me money, and here is proof positive in his own handwrit ing—I never said, after he bad pawned tools for me, "Don't take any more tools; if you want money, ask me for it, and you shall have it;" nor any thing of the kind. EDWARD MATTHEWS( police-constable M  68.) I took the prisoner 09. the 14th of August—I told him it was for stealing Mr. Robinson's tools—he said he had them, and had made away with them—he gave me the duplicates, and I found these tools at the pawnbroker's—when the pri soner was in the station he fell on his knees, and begged forgiveness. NOT GUILTY.
 2306. GEORGE HURFORD was indicted for stealing, on the   30th of Jane , 1 wooden box, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 7 pints of currants, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 4 quarts of gooseberries, value 10 d  .; 5 cauliflowers, value 5 d  .; 7 cabbages, value 3 d  .; 5 candles, value 5 d  .; and 1/2 lb. weight of soap, value 1 d  . the goods of William Ludlam Barton , his master. WILLIAM LUDLAM BARTON. I am a tallow-chandler, and live at Barnes. The prisoner was in my service. REBECCA GATHINOTON. I am servant to Mr. Hull, of Barnes. On the 29th of June the prisoner brought a box to our house—he wanted it to go by the London Parcels Company—the box was given to the officer. JOHN BUSAIN. I am an inspector of police. I produce the box, which was found at Gathington's—I showed it to the prisoner, and he acknow ledged it was his—I saw the property in it produced, before him—he said there was no plate in the box, but the box and contents were his master's. JOHN CANNON( police-constable N  201.) I found the box—it was opened in my presence—it contained the currants and other things stated in the indictment—the prisoner said they were the property of his master.
   Prisoner . I did not say they were the property of my master. Wit  ness. Yes, you did—you said I should find no plate in the box, but a little fruit—after cautioning him as to what he might say, he said what the box contained was his master's—he said that in presence of three of us in Mr. Barton's kitchen.
   Prisoner. I bought the garden stuff. GUILTY. Aged 26.— Confined Three Months.
