OLD COURT.-Friday, September 18th, 1840.
Third Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
2361. THOMAS FREDERICK GUNN was indicted for stealing, on
the 16th of September, 26 spoons, value 6l. 10s. ; 2 pairs of sugar-tongs,
value 108.; 2 bags, value 2d. ; 2 shillings, 4 crowns, 7 half-crowns, and 40
shillings; the property of Ann Robinson, in her dwelling-house; to which
he pleaded
GUILTY.-Confined Nine Months.
(The prisoner received a good character, and was recommended to mercy
by the prosecutrix.)
