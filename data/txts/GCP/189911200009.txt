RICHARD WALTER Davis. I am an assistant in the Detective Depart-
ment of the General Post Office-on October 13th I was with James
keeping observation on the prisoner, who I knew well—he left at 5.15
p.m., walked to the Bank of England, and got into a tram car for Fins.
bury Park-I got in and sat on the same side, and James got on the top-
the prisoner got out at Seven Sisters Road-I got out immediately behind
him, and followed him to the post office, about 15 yards off, where he filted
in the name of the office and S. Steel-he was paid 28. - James was stand-
ing outside the shop, but near the counter--the prisoner left, and I spoke
to Mr. Miller, who gave me this order (Produced)-I reported the
matter.
Cross-examined. This was about 6.45—I had been watching the
prisoner three weeks, and had seen him several times leaving the Lang-
bourne, where he is a waiter-he lives close to the Cattle Market-I got
off the tram close to Hornsey Road, and about a mile from Hollon ay
Road Railway Station-he got out of the tram at 6.49-I looked at my
watch-I went into the post-office behind him, and saw what he was
writing ; 246, Hornsey Road, and the name of Seal-I did not follow him
home-I did not see him till next day at the Post-office, when he was
called up by Mr. Wynn.
Re-examined. I made my report the following day--I looked at my
watch after the order had been casbed, and it was 6.45–I did not speak
to Mr. Miller till after the prisoner left.
WALTER CHARLES JAMES. I am an assistant in the Detective Depart-
ment of the General Post Office-I accompanied Davis on October 13th
to the Langbourne Hotel, and saw the prisoner come out at 11 minutes to 6
-I got outside the tram, and went to Seven Sisters Road, and got off
and saw the prisoner go into the post-office-I waited outside, and saw
him signing something--I did not follow him after that I had known
him for four days.
Cro88-examined. I saw him leave the Langbourne between 5 and 6, and
go to the railway station, and take a ticket.
ROBERT PETERSON MILLER. I live at 246, Hornsey Road-I cashed
this order for 2s. on Friday, October 13th-I cannot say who cashed it,
but a detective drew my attention to it, and I gave it to him to endorse.
Cross-examined. I saw the man write the order, but his back was
towards ine-he was in the shop three or four minutes-Davis asked me
for some stamps, and I served him-I could not pick the map out-there
is a wire screen-I could see a man's face if he was standing up writing,
but not if he was stooping down.
