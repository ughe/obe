Re-examined. Calcutt used to call at the "Salisbury Arms" for
refreshments, and Trott also. I knew Loftus to be a relative of
Stammers. There was no difference between these men and any
other customers who came into the house,
Mrs. MARY ANN STAMMERS, wife of defendant Stammers. I went
to live at the "Salisbury Arms" in 1898. Mr. and Mrs. Osloyst
used to come to the house four or five times a week as customers;
in course of time we got on friendly terms with them and visited
each other. On one or two occasions I called at Calcutt's house in
Grafton Street. I remember the Calcutts going to Southend for a
change and Calcutt asked me to go there. It is not true to say
that I went uninvited, or that I went bec: use I quarrelled with my
husband. I went down on a Thursday and returned on the Satur-
day morning' with Mr. and Mrs. Calcutt. Mrs. Calcutt came up
every Saturday to pay the men. Calcutt also asked me if I would
let my boy go down and spend a few days with his boys. I was not
at great expense while I was there; I left half a sovereign on the
dining-room mantelpiece. I do not remember a mirror coming to
the house; I was at Clacton when it came, and some time after it
was in the clubroom done up ir paper. It was some time after I
found out it had come from Calcutt. With regard to the bath, it
was in our yard for quite twelve months. I spoke to Calcutt two
or three times about sending it away. The last time he was at the
saloon bar, and I was at the counter; he oame back to me and said,
"For God's sake, Mrs. Stammers, don't mention that bath to me
any more." He put his two fingers up like that (describing). At
last my husband and I arranged to have the club room altered, and
the bath put there. I remember hampers being made up for Calcutt
on two or three occasions. There is no foundation for the sugges-
tion that there has been any misconduct between Calcutt and I or
that he has spent money upon me.
Cross-examined. Mrs. Calcutt told us she had no convenience
for visitors at Southend. She gave up her own bedroom to me. My
son was there a few days. I have known Calcutt nine or 10 years;
he was very often in our house, but I have only been to their house
once or twice. Calcutt sometimes came to the "Salisbury Arms
with Guardians. We put up the bath because we thought we might
as well use it as let it rot.
