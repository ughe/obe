gether arm-in-arm-the other figure has a pitchfork in its hand, and home
on its head-I have been in the street on Sundays as well as other days,
it is my duty to go round every morning-I have seen the same exhibition
there they were not taken down during Divine service, to my knowledge
-I saw them up-I have seen them as I went to church, and in the midst
of church time, when it is my duty to go round, I have seen them
there as before I have seen an unusual number of people collect on week
days and Sundays, on both sides of the way-sometimes more and some
times less--sometimes twelve, or sixteen, or eighteen on each side, and
sometimes, I should think, nearly forty on each side of the way at a time
-several times persons have been compelled to go into the carriage-way,
instead of going along the footpath-I have continued to observe this up
to the present time--I was not able to attend on duty yesterday, but I
saw it on Sunday week, and the number of persons, and the exhibition of
the figures, were not the least altered. OV O
