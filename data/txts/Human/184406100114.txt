CHARLES COLE. I was employed at this house on the Saturday—the prisoner was employed there in painting the spout at the back, with white paint—none of the others were painting with white paint—on the Monday Bridges told me what had happened—on the Wednesday I took the prisoner at another house—I tied his hands behind him, a card be fore him, and painted his face—I sent for the prosecutrix, and asked what she charged him with—he said he did not deny it—I told him if he did not give back the sovereign I would give him in charge—he said he could not give it up, he would stop and work it out—I had a brush of paint in my hand, and did what I was sorry for—I put it in his face, and tied him to the rails. GEORGE GRAFFHAM. I heard the prisoner charged with stealing the sovereign—he denied it at first, and then said he was very sorry, he would work it out. NOT GUILTY.
   NEW COURT .— Thursday, June  13 th , 1844.
   Sixth Jury before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1643. WILLIAM MILLER was indicted for embezzling 2 s  . 2 d  ., the monies of James Townsend , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined One Month .
 1644. FRANCIS MULSHAW was indicted for embezzling 4 s  . 3 d  ., the monies of his master, John William Stupple ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months .
 1645. ANN MURPHY was indicted for stealing 2 sheets, value 4 s  .; and I looking-glass, 5 s  .; the goods of William Higgins ; and that she had been before convicted of felony; to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 40.— Confined One Year .
 1646. CHARLES SIMMONS was indicted for stealing 1 waistcoat, value 7 s  ., the goods of William Strange ; and that he had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 40.— Transported for Seven Years .
 1647. MARY GEARY was indicted for stealing 2 sheets value 5 s  .; 2 towels, 2 s  .; 1 table-cloth, 1 s  .; 1 frock, 1 s  .; and 1 sofa-cover, 1 s  .; the goods of Davis Clashofska , her master; to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined One Year .
 1648. WILLIAM HARLEY was indicted for embezzling 1 l  . 4 s  . 8 d  ., the monies of his master, William Lewis Clark ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined One Month .
 1649. RICHARD WIGGINS was indicted for embezzling 5 s  ., the monies of his master, James Trinder ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months .
 1650. ANN DAVIES was indicted for stealing 1 frock, value 1 s  . 3 d  .; and 1 pinafore, 9 d  .; the goods of George Curry , from the person of Henry Curry ; to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 33 .— Confined Six Months .
 1651. JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing 1 truck, value 3 l  ., the goods of James Calcott : also 1 truck, value 4 l  ., the goods of James Saul ; to both which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 26 .— Confined Six Months .
