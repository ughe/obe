1489. WILLIAM ARCHER was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of July , a pair of breeches, value 3 s., the goods James Henry Williamson.
   WILLIAM JOHN LONGER. I am shopman to James Henry Williamson, a pawnbroker, of Brick-lane. These breeches are his property; I had hung them up between eight and nine o'clock in the morning of the 26th of July, and missed them when the goods were taken down. I had attended the shop that day - they were not sold.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   WILLIAM HAYES. I am an apprentice to Mr. Attenborough, a pawnbroker, of Crown-street, Finsbury. - About half-past four or five o'clock on the evening of the 26th of July, the prisoner brought these breeches to pawn, which he said belonged to his brother, in Long-lane - I had my suspicions, and sent for an officer; he then said he saw a man running with them, and drop them.
   JAMES FITZPATRICK. I am an officer. I was sent for to take the prisoner - he said he saw a man running, who dropped these breeches; he picked them up, and thought no harm in it.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. Transported for Seven Years.
 1490.  JAMES REES was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of September , a reticule, value 2 s. 6 d.; a bunch of keys, with a ring, value 4 s.; a handkerchief, value 1 s., and a pair of gloves, value 2 d., the goods of George Shepherd , from the person of Ann, his wife.
   ANN SHEPHERD. I am the wife of George Shepherd. On the 15th of September, about half-past seven o'clock in the evening, I was in the New-road- the prisoner came suddenly by me, and snatched my reticule out of my hand, which contained a bunch of keys, a handkerchief, and a pair of gloves; he left the chain in my hand: I did not see his face till he was brought back by a man, but by his figure I knew him to be the same.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you see me running? A. I saw the man who took it - he took off his shoe, and threw it at me, which I gave him again.
   CHARLES COUSENS. The prisoner and these articles were brought to me at the watch-house, by a man, who said he saw him throw them away; the prisoner said he had done it, and he was sorry. He appeared to be in a great deal of want.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years.
 1491.  HENRY PAPE was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of August , a handkerchief, value 2 s. 6 d., the goods of Barnard William Moreland , from his person.
 BARNARD WILLIAM MORELAND. I am a  wax-chandler, and live in Oxford-street. On the 14th of August I was in Nassau-street - a person named Woodward came and asked if I had lost my handkerchief; I felt, and found it was gone - he then shewed it to me; I had had it not ten minutes before.
 Prisoner. The gentleman said at the office that it was not his property. Witness. No - I expressed no doubt about it.
   JOHN WOODWARD. I was in Mortimer-street- I saw the prisoner and another lad following this gentleman, for about one hundred and fifty yards - I then saw them separate suddenly; I took the prisoner, and found the handkerchief between his trousers and his jacket. When I said I would take him he threw himself down, and began to kick; I took him up, and carried him to Nassau-street, where I found the prosecutor.
 Prisoner. I had found it half an hour before.
 JOHN WOODWARD. I followed him all the way up Mortimer-street, and am quite sure he did not pick it up.
     GUILTY. Aged 12. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1492.  SAMUEL COWDEN and JOHN GRIFFIN were indicted for stealing, on the 2d July , a handkerchief, value 3 s., the goods of Charles Munns , from his person.
 CHARLES MUNNS. On the 28th of July, I was in Sydney's-alley. The officer came and asked me if I had had any thing taken from my pocket. I felt, and missed my handkerchief from my right-hand coat pocket. I had it about three minutes before - here it is; they had the prisoners in custody with it.
   WILLIAM COLTON. About half-past eight o'clock on the evening of the 28th of July, I saw the prisoners in the courts leading from Leicester-fields to St. Martin's-lane, following a gentleman; the prosecutor came by, and they pressed up against him. I saw Cowden do something, and then put something into his breeches and go away - they then took to the left hand, and the prosecutor the right. I sent Reardon to tell the prosecutor, and took the prisoners into a public-house. I then took off Griffin's hat and found the handkerchief.
 Prisoner GRIFFIN. Q. I was not in company with Cowden? A. Yes, you was for a quarter of an hour backwards and forwards through the courts. I had seen them in Long-acre before they saw the prosecutor.
   DANIEL REARDON. I was with Colton. I saw the prisoners first at the end of Long-acre; they went to the end of St. Martin's-court. I then saw the prosecutor, and the two prisoners close behind him. I did not see them do any thing; but I saw them leave him at the end of the alley, and they turned off to Bear-street. I then went and told the prosecutor.
 COWDEN'S Defence. I was coming from work. I saw something on the ground, and kicked it before me. I then took it up, and saw it was a handkerchief. I met this young man at the corner of Bear-street, talking to a woman - I shewed it to him, and he said he would give me a pot of beer for it.
 GRIFFIN'S Defence. I live in Stephen-street, Tottenham-court-road. I met a young woman, and stood talking to her at the corner of Bear-street; the prisoner came to me, and said he had found this handkerchief, which he would sell. I said I would give him a pot of beer for it, and we were going to have it, when the officers came up and took us.
 COWDEN -    GUILTY. Aged 19.
 GRIFFIN -    GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Fourteen Years.
