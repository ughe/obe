Walworth. On 22nd Sept., I saw the prisoner pass the window with some
cheese in a basket-I ran after him, he turned round, and when he saw me
he dropped the cheese and ran away-I ran after him and caught him-he
said, "Let me go, let me go, I did not mean to do it"-the cheese weighed
28lbs., and was worth 188.--I had seen it safe in our shop-window half an
hour before, partly in and partly out-I was serving customers in the shop,
and did not see what was going on outside any one outside could get hold
of the cheese and pull it under the window-I missed it before I left the
shop-I did not lose sight of the prisoner.
JOHN TRAFFORD (policeman, P 175). I received the prisoner in charge
from Brown-he said starvation would drive a man to anything.
Prisoner's Defence. It is my first offence.
WILLIAM ROMAIN (policeman, L 38). I produce a certificate (read-
Henry Francis, convicted March, 1849, and confined six months)— I was
present the prisoner is the person.
Guilty."* Aged 20.-Confined Twelve Months.
2054. WILLIAM ANDREWS and JOHN DOUGLAS, stealing 1
skewer, value 38.; the goods of Dame Isabella Cooper.
MR. Mew conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS HONEYBOND. I am a watch-maker and silversmith at Brentford.
Some time in Sept., Douglas came and offered me for sale a broken silver
skewer, which had a crest upon it-I said it looked suspicious, and asked bow
he came by it-he said he found it in the dust-he did not say where-he said
he worked for Mr. Weston, the dust-contractor- I told him he must leave it
and inquiry must be made, and he must call again in a few days-he said he
had no objection--I gave the skewer to the police-Douglas called again,
and I told him I had given it to the police, and my orders were to give him
into custody-he went out of the shop and spoke to a man outside, who
I believe was Andrews, but I cannot swear to him—these are the pieces of the
skewer (produced).
HENRY UPTON (policeman, V 44). I received information from Mr.
Honeybond, and he gave me this skewer, and a description of the parties-I
took the two prisoners into custody-Andrews said, "I had the skewer, but
I did not offer it for sale"- he said Douglas did.
HANNAH ELLIS. I am cook to Lady Isabella Cooper, of Isleworth-she
is a widow, and was so when I went to live with her six years ago—this broken
skewer is hers, and has her crest on-this one (producing another) is one
now in use-I have known Andrews about four mouths-he used to come to
Lady Cooper's for orders for Mr. Waterman, the poulterer-be used to come
to the kitchen where the skewers lay about.
Cross-examined by Mr. WOOLLETT. Q. What establishment bas Lady
Cooper ? A. About sixteen servants-I never saw Andrews come inside
the kitchen-door--a great many tradespeople come to the house.
GEORGE HIGGINS. I am butler to Lady Cooper. I know these parts of a
skewer-when the policeman came I missed such a one.
Douglas's Defence. Andrews told me he found it a fortnight before, stuck
in the ground.
(Andrews received a good character.)
Not Guilty.
