380. JOHN CURRAN and RÉBEOCA CURRAN, unlawfully receiv-
ing 241 lbs. weight of flour, and 15 lbs. weight of baking powder; the goods
of Samuel Wolfe, which had been obtained by false pretences.
Mr. RYLAND having opened the case, MR. RIBTON called the attention of
the Court to the omission of the word “knowingly," from the 2nd Corant of
the indictment, and contended that there must be an averment that the goods
toere unlarofully and knowingly obtained (see Reg. v. Henderson and Bars
low, 2nd Moody, Crown Casas, page 195.) MR. RYLAND contended, that as the
indictment stated "such goods having been so, as aforesaid, unlawfully
obtained," that was sufficient. The COURT (having consulted Mr. JUSTICE
Erte) stated, that as the indictment followed the words of the statute, and had
not been dernurred to, it was good.)
