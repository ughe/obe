men to The Rocks and The Rosary, Bedfont-we arrived at 9.8; the
buildings were well alight, there was a mass of flames from top to
hottom; a portion of the roof, I believe, was in-the two houses were
nlight all over-there was about forty minutes' delay in getting water,

- I came to the opinion, a day or two afterwards, that the fire was caused

11 small window-frame was lying under the front wall of the house on
a heap of bricks, on the right of the tree when facing the house-
I saw the sash and two sash weights, they were in a line
with the dining-room-Police-constable Petty showed me the well next
morning-when Mr. Rees came down we cleared the debris, and I formed

room; the hearth stone was shattered-the only indication was in the
