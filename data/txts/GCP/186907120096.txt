Thomas CHAPMAN. I live at 4, Charles Square, and am a newsvendor
I have lived there eleven years-I have known the prisoner twelve
months on the night of the fire I was in the London Depot beer-shop-
went there about 7 o'clock, the prisoner was there then I remained up to
the time of the alarm of fire, and the prisoner did not leave my presence
-I did not see Batts come in.
