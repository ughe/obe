OLD COURT.-Monday, January 5th, 1846.
First Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
361. JOSEPH HEAVISIDE was indicted for unlawfully assaulting John
Huggard on the high seas.
Not Guilty.
362. JOHN KLOBA was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of June,
3 watches, value 100l.; 1 seal, 5l. ; 1 watch-chain, 5l; I watch-key, 1l.;
and I watch-stock, il. ; the goods of Charles Edward Viner; to which he
pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 65.-Transported for Seven Years.
