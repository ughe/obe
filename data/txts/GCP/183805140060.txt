1224. HENRY READON was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of May,
177lbs. weight of cheese, value 11s., the goods of William Richard Jackson.
WILLIAM RICHARD JACKSON. I live in Orombie-row, Commercial-road,
and am a cheesemonger. On the night of Saturday, the 5th of May, 1
had a cheese on the stall-board-I missed it from the board, and saw the
prisoner under the gateway with it under his arm. 15
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. What time was this? A. Be-
tween ten and eleven o'clock at night I did not know the prisoner before
-a little boy brought the cheese back-I am certain it is mine--it was
outside on my board.
DANIEL KEATING (police-constable K 92.) I was on duty in Jubilee-
place, Commercial-road, and saw the prisoner running-he fell down
took him and brought him back to Mr. Jackson, who identified him.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you find any cheese on him? 4. No.
NOT GUILTY.
