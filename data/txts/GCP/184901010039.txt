413. GEORGE BULL, stealing 1 half-crown, 4 shillings, 2 sixpences,
1 penny, and I farthing; the moneys of Jeremiah Tavis, from his person.
JEREMIAH Tavis. I am a labourer, of 51, Union-street, Hoxton. On
Christmas-day, between two and three o'clock in the afternoon, I was at the
end of Plummer-street, City-road-I had a scuffle and fight with a man, and
fell on the ground; as I was getting up, several men fell on me the prisoner
was one-I felt his hand in my trowsers'-pocket, and held it there--I was
struck by some other man, and he withdrew his hand and ran across the
rond-I missed a half-crown, four shillings, two sixpences, and some copper,
which was safe just before.
Prisoner. I was not there. Witness. I have not the least doubt of him-
I recollect his features.
ISABELLA Tavis. I am the wife of the last witness. I was with him-
he fell out with a man and began fighting with him-he fell, and I saw the
prisoner take his band out of my husband's pocket and run across the road
I am sure it was the prisoner--my husband was kept down by several men-
some one attempted to stop the prisoner, and was knocked down in the
kennel by two men,
WILLIAM Bons. I am a plumber and glazier, of Union-street, Hoxton. I
saw Tavis tastling with the prisoner and several others the prisoner got
away-I struck him to prevent him-Tavis called out that he was robbed-
I ran after the prisoner and was tripped up by four or five of them, and the
prisoner escaped-I am quite certain he is the man.
JOHN JENKINSON (policeman). On the evening of 29th Dec, I met the pri-
soner in Bath-street, City-road, within two hundred yards of the spot-I
told him the charge-he said, "Why, I was not there, but, however, it is no
more than I expected"-I knew him well by sight.

was at my mother's, 12, Little Nelson-street, Bath-street, City-road-he is a
deputy son of my mother-my sister-in-law and my mother were there, four
of us altogetber--we dined at ball-past one; it was over about three or half-
past-we had half a shoulder of mutton-the prisoner was there till hall-
past ten, when I left, except when he went to fetch a pint of beer, and then
was not away above two or three minutes.
