SOLOMON BERON, 133, Jubilee Street, E. I have no occupation.
Deceased was my brother; he was 48 years old. He was livin at
133, Jubilee Street; I was not living there at the time. He was
in Russia; our family left there when he wag a year old, and
we lived in France for 30 years. We carne to this Country in 1894.
Since that time he lived in the East-End; he did no work. He had
nine small houses, bringing him in a clear income of 225 a year. He
had no banking account; he used to carry his money about with him.
At the time of the crime he had 212 on him. He always wore a gold
watch and chain, attached to which was a 25 piece, worth altogether
about 230. This is his pipe (Exhibit 39). I last saw him ahve
10.45 p.m. on Saturday, December 31, in Fieldgate Street. He
alone. I tried to speak to him, but he did not speak to me. On the
following Monday I identified his body.
Cross-examined. My brother David lives with me now. The week
preceding the crime I was living at a Rowton House at 7d. a night.
