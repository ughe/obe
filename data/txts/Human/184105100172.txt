 1524. THOMAS HARRIS was indicted for stealing, on the   10th April , 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  ., the goods of Joseph Butler Ross , from his person; and that he had been before convicted of felony. JOSEPH BUTLER ROSS. I live in Holland-street, Brixton-road. On Saturday evening, the 10th of April, about four o'clodk, I was in Red-cross-street—I missed my pocket-handkerchief—I saw the prisoner is custody some time after—this handkerchief now produced is the one I lost. GEORGE MERCER. I was in Red cross-street. I saw the prisoner take a handkerchief from Mr. Ross's pocket—I gave information to the police—I did not pursue—I saw him go down Fishmonger-alley. HENRY HART( police-constable M  80.) I received information, and took the prisoner out of a house in Fishmonger-alley—I saw him attempt to conceal the handkerchief in his breast, as he was going into the house, but when he was brought out the handkerchief was missing—he had chucked  it down among some grains in the passage. JOHN HARVEY( police-constable L  85.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from the Clerk of the Peace of Surrey—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Transported for Ten Years.
 1525. BENJAMIN MATTHEWS and THOMAS ROBERTS were indicted for stealing, on the   24th of April , 4 bundles of scaleboards, value 14 s  ., the goods of Jonas Hamling , their master; to which
   MATTHEWS  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 29.— Confined Six Months. JONAS HAMLING. I am a scaleboard-cutter, in Union-street, Lambeth-walk. The prisoners were my journeymen—on the morning of the 24th of April I was in my bed-room, and saw Roberts crossing my yard, coming from a shed, with the scaleboards on his shoulder—I ran down, and found him in the act of closing my yard-gate, and coming back without them—I ran out, and found-Matthews with the boards on his shoulder—I told him to come back, which he did, and threw them down—I gave them into custody.
   Roberts's Defence.  I went to work in the workshop; I saw the yard-gate open; as I came back from shutting the gate, my master ran part me; I had not the boards on my shoulder; he brought Matthews back.
   MR. HAMLING re-examined.  I saw Roberts with the boards on his shoulder
   ROBERTS— GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined Six Months.
 1526. ANTHONY ALDRED was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of March , 12 pieces of woollen cloth, value 7 s  ., and 28 pieces of kersey-mere, value 3 s  ., the goods of William Camp , his master. WILLIAM CAMP. I am a tailor, and live in Broad-wall, Christ Church. The prisoner was my apprentice—I missed these articles—I cut them out myself, and know them particularly well—I went to Mr. Cornaby's shop, and found them there—the prisoner had been with me four years in July—I received 29 l . with him—he bad three more years to serve—he was not authorized in any way to part with these.
