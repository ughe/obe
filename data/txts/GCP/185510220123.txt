ROBERT HAYWARD. I am a Custom-house agent. Some broad silks
which came for the prisoner by the Albion, in July, were consigned to me

twice-I think he came on the date of the arrival.
SOLOMON ABRAHAM HART. I live at No. 22, Bury-street, St. Mary Axe,
and have been a merchant and warehouseman for the last twelve years. I

been returned to me for being damaged my stock is large, I cannot exactly
tell-I had at the time ninety pieces, amounting to 10,000 yards-I have
had one length returned which is worth 51. or 10%.
