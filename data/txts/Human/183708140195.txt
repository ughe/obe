 2019. THOMAS BERRY was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of August , 5 pairs of shoes, value 1 l  . 5 s  ., the goods of Edward Wallis . EDWARD WALLIS. I am a boot and shoemaker, and live in Lambeth-marsh. I lost some shoes on the 15th of August—they were safe that day at twelve o'clock, and missed at two—these are them. JAMES WILTSHIRE. I am an apprentice to the prosecutor. Between two and three o'clock that day I saw the prisoner take these shoes from the door—I ran after him—he threw them down and ran away—I am sure the prisoner was the man—there was another with him, he was secured.
   Prisoner.  I never had them in my possession at all—when I was taken I was walking along. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months; Six Weeks Solitary.
 2020. WILLIAM BOUTE was indicted for stealing, on the   8th of August , 1 hone, value 6 s  ., and 1 razor, value 2 s  .; the goods of Charles Atkinson . CHARLES ATKINSON. I keep a shop at Lambeth. The prisoner came in on the 8th of August—I left my shop while he was there—I missed a razor and hone the next day.
   Prisoner. Q.  By what mark do you know this razor and hone, are there not fifty or a hundred by the same maker? A.  I know them by the look of them and the maker's name—I have used them a long while. JOHN THORNTON. The prisoner came to me, and told me he had bought a bargain, and showed me this hone and razor—this was Tuesday evening, the 8th of August—I kept them till the next day, and gave them to the officer—he did not offer to sell them—I believe he left them by mistake.
   Prisoner.  I was passing Whitechapel, and had a glass of half-and-half, and a man offered me these for sale—I gave him 1 s . 9 d . for the hone, and 6 d . for the razor, which was 2 s . 3 d ., all I had, and he took that for them—the prosecutor has lodgers in the house, and there is free admission for any one to take these things. CHARLES ATKINSON. We have lodgers, and they can have access to the shop—this is one that I had used—it was not for sale.
 (Robert Downing, a hair-dresser, of No. 81, Snow-hill, gave the prisoner a good character, and offered to take him into his employ.) GUILTY.—Aged 19.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prosecutor.  — Confined One Month; One Week Solitary.

HENRY AMBRIDGE was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of July , 16 yards of silk, value 2 l  . 18 s  .; 1 shawl, value 6 s  .; 3 handkerchiefs, value 15 s  .; 1 pair of stockings, value 5 s  .; 2 yards l-   8th of woollen cloth, value 1 l  . 11 s  .; and 2 1/4 yards of kerseymere, value 15 s  . 9 d  .; the goods of John Cuthbertson , his master. JOHN CUTHBERTSON. I am a linen-draper, and live in the Borough road, Southwark. The prisoner was in my service—I missed this silk, and shawl, and other things—I believe these to be mine—( looking at them )—the prisoner had an opportunity of taking them.
