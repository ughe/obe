of Her Majesty's Postmaster-General
Mr. BAGGALLY Prosecuted.

Twickenham Common-on 19th January, about 5.45 p.m., the prisoner
came and asked for one postal order for 10s., and two for 5s.-I stamped
them, and was holding them in my left hand, when he asked for a shill-
ings-worth of stamps, and with his left hand snatched the orders from me
and ran out-I followed him to the door, and Mrs. Billings, the assistant
post-mistress, ran out, and I telegraphed to the post-master at Twickenham
and the other offices-I next saw the prisoner at the Police-court-these
are two of the three stolen orders.
