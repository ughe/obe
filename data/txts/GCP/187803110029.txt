347. JAMES PURCELL (18), Stealing a watch and chain from the
person of Albert Bucktheil.
MR. RINGWOOD oonducted tho Proseoution.
ALBERT BUCKTHEIL (Through an Interpreter). I am a seaman, of
Perseverance Place, Shadwell-on Sunday, 3rd March, I was in Royal
Mint Street, and the prisoner ran up to me and tore my watuh from my
waistcoat pocket-this chain broke by the pull--my coat was open-he
ran into a by-street-I ran after him-a young man tripped him up and
he fell, but got up again and ran into a policeman's arms without my
losing sight of him-I am sure he is the man.
FRANCIS SOMONDS (Policeman # 50). I saw the prisoner running from
the prosecutor and caught him-I told him the charge; he made no reply
-he said at the station "I will pay for the watch if you will let me go "-I
found nothing on him.
Cross-examined. The prosecutor did not say that if he got 24s. he would
not come up to swear against you.
GUILTY.** -Nine Months' Imprisonment.
348. WALTER LONG (27), Burglariously breaking and entering the
dwelling-house of Charles Ewald Hencke, and stealing therein two coats
and other articles, his property.
MR. BRINDLEY conducted the Prosecution; and MR. GILL the Defence.
CHARLES EWALD HEROKE. I live at 66, Tollington Park on Friday,
1st February, I fastened all the doors and windows and went to bedI
was called about 6.30 next morning by the police, and found the drawing-
room window open and the door leading from the drawing room foroed-it
had been fastened, and there were marks on the wood-work-the window
opens into the garden—the iron bar of the shutters was taken away-I
missed four coate-I found a drawer in the breakfast room foroed open
from where I missed a gold chain-some umbrellas were taken from the
stand, but they were returned by the police—the value of the property
was about 201.-those are two of the coats produced.
