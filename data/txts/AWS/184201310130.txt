ANN MAxToN. I am a widow, and keep a baker's-shop in Spencer.

bread and to receive money-I sent him out on Wednesday, the 26th of
January, to my customers-h did not return-] sent for him about nine
o'clock the same night-he was found at his father's house-he said he
had received 11. 6s. 2d., and had lost the money-I gave him into custody
-I went with him to the station-he said he hoped I would not take
him up-I have customers named Johnson and Hill-Mr. Johnson owed
me 11. 0s. 4d., and Mr. Hill 3s. 4d.-the prisoner had bills to deliver to
the customers on the Monday previous-those sums were included in the

ELIZABETH HILL. I am the wife of Richard Hill, of White Conduit-

January, on account of his mistress-I have a bill which he put 44 paid" to
-(read.)
Mrs. MAXTON re-examined. The prisoner brought me some money on
the Monday and Tuesday-he did not tell me that he had received these
sums-he absconded on Wednesday-] did not ask him about them-he
ought to have paid me the same day as he got the money-he had no busi-
ness to retain it.
WILLIAM MARTIN (police-constable G 144.) I took the prisoner into
custody on Wednesday, and found no money on him-I took him to Mrs.
Maxton.
Prisoner's Defence. On Monday I went to Mrs. Hill's; she paid me
38. 4d. did not pay it in : on Tuesday Mr. Johnson paid me 11. 0s. 4d.,
and I lost the money on Tuesday I did not like to tell my mistress ; on
Wednesday night I sent my brother to her to say I had lost it.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 7.-Confined Six Months.
