copper, value 108.; the goods of Mary Davis, and fixed to a building.
JOHN WEST. I occupy a house at Hounslow, next door to Mrs. Man
Davis's house, from which the copper was taken. On the 19th of Dec., il
discovered that house was unfastened- I went to make it secure, and saw the
prisoner in the yard with a short ladder, which did not surprise me, as he wa
in the habit of going there at all times— I went into the kitchen, and there
was no copper there then.
REUBEN HALL (police-constable T 181.) I had missed a copper from

pair of stays, value 18. 6d., the goods of Elizabeth Byfield ; and that be
had been before convicted of felony.
ELIZABETH BYFIELD. I live in Great Smith-street, Westminster. On

get the buttons- I could swear to the handkerchief on his neck, and the mark
in his face.
