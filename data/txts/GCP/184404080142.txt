1166. EMMA PUMFREY was indicted for stealing 85% yards of flannel,
value Il. 158. 6d., the goods of Thomas Matthews.
JAMES THOMAS. I am apprentice to Thomas Matthews, linen-draper,
High-street, Aldgate. We had a roll of flannel on the 21st of March--I saw
it safe about an hour before we lost it-the policeman brought it back-it is
my master's-I can swear to it.
EDWARD BURGESS (police-constable H 198.) On the 21st of March I saw
the prisoner go to Mr. Matthews' door, and take away the flannel-I followed,
and took her with it.
Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
1167. JOSHUA SHORT was indicted for stealing 10lbs. weight of
bacon, value 5s., the goods of Richard Hathaway.
ELIZABETH HATHAWAY. I am the wife of Richard Hathaway; we live in
Euston-place. About balf-past eight o'clock on the night of the 12th of
March, I had this bacon safe-I missed it two minutes after-I am sure it is
mine.
CHRISTOPHER NORTH (police-constable N 17.) I stopped the prisoner on
the night of the 12th with the bacon.
Prisoner's Defence. A young man gave it me, and told me to go to some
name in Goswell-street, to meet him there.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Two Months.
Sixth Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
