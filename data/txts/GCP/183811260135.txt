WILLIAM ROWLANDSON. I sold this print to the prosecutor the day

WILLIAM DAY DAVIS (police-constable H 36.) On the 8th of November
I was in Middlesex-street, Christ Church-the prisoner passed me with
this print under his arm-I asked what he was going to do with it-he
said he got it from Hare-street Fields.
Prisoner's Defence. I bought it of a hawker-I was dismissed from
Worship-street for a week- I was to come again, and if there was no pro-
secutor I was to have the property, and knowing that I had bought it, I
went to own it, and was taken.
