Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON. Q. When you made bim this liberal
offer, he said, | shall not give it up, I will go to the station A. Yes—
I opened his box the day before with a key from a bunch of keys we have
in our possession—the first key I tried fitted the box—I had never looked
into his box before—my wife looked in after I had named the circumstance
to her—I looked in on the Friday that we marked the money, and I looked
in again the next morning, and 3s. Gd. more had been added from about
the middle of Friday—I had paid bim his week's wages on Saturday, the
22nd—when I looked into his box on the Friday I saw coppers amounting
to nearly 28., and I saw him with a respectable suit of clothes on— that
gave me suspicion, which was on my mind for a considerable time—there
was a deficiency of money on the Thursday, when my wife said she had
noticed him serve a great many customers—no other persons go into the
shop—no person goes behind the counter but my own children—I found
only one marked sixpence on the prisoner—there were two marked shil-
lings in the till that morning, and one shilling and a sixpence not
marked.
