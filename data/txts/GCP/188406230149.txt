WILLIAM ASHFORD (Policeman R 361). On 14th June I went with
Marsden and Weever to 88, Church Street, and called Simpson out-
Weever shook his head; we then went to 44, Bayland Street; I knocked
at the door, went into the room, and there saw the two prisoners;
I brought out Simpson first; Weever immediately identified him--I
handed him over to Marsden; I went back and fetched out Leighton
and the prosecutor identified him also I searched him; no money was
found on him-Weever was bleeding very much from his face and
head.
