 The prisoner called— GEORGE TEAKLE( police-sergeant H  8.) Q.  How long have yon known me? A.  Two years—I cannot say I have seen you in bad company—you have applied to me in distress, and I gave you two loaves—you asked me to get you into the police—I believed you were reformed—I found your place in a destitute state—there were some duplicates of weaxing apparel there.
 (  The prisoner put in a written defence stating that he was a Spitaifields weaver, and that his family were in the greatest distress. ) GUILTY. Aged 43.
