1986. LEONARD PROVO was indicted for stealing 18lbs. weight of mustard, value 6 s  .; and 2 casks, 1 s  .; the property of John Green . WILLIAM SWAISLAND. I am in the service of Mr. John Green, an oil and colourman, at Woolwich. On the 5th of Aug., the prisoner came to the shop—he wanted two casks of mustard, and two gallons of mixed pickles—I gave him them from under the counter—he afterwards said he wanted three bill-heads, which I gave him from off the file—he took one, and wrote on it Mr. Vine's name, what was under it I do not know—he said I was to go with him and receive the money—when I got to the Ordnance Arms, he took the mustard, and told me to go on with the pickles to Mr. Vine, at the Dover Castle—When I got there, the pickles were not ordered.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE Q.  Had you known the prisoner previously? A.  No—my master is not here—I have been shop-boy there four years—this happened twelve months ago—I do not know whether the prisoner had had dealings there before—I only lost the mustard—I am sure the prisoner is the man—the mustard was in casks, it was worth 2 s .—I was not to bring back 8 s . and the bottles—I did not hear that—I said I was going with the things—I let the prisoner have the mustard—my master was not at home—the prisoner said he came from the other house to get these things.
   COURT. Q.  Did you let him have them, because you were to go with him to get the money? A.  Yes.
   MR. BALLANTINE. Q.  Had you full authority at this shop to sell articles? A.  Yes—I had not to ask my master's permission—if there is nobody in the shop I am allowed to sell things—I am not allowed to trust people. JAMES VINE. I keep the Dover Castle public-house, at Woolwich. I did not order the prisoner to bring any mustard or casks—Swaisland brought two gallons of pickles—they were not ordered.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did your wife order any mustard? A.  No.—she was present when the pickles were brought, and the prisoner was left at the Ordnance Arms—I returned the pickles back. WILLIAM GLADWIN( policeman, R  122.) I have been searching for the prisoner, and have not been able to find him for nearly twelve months. NOT GUILTY .
   Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
