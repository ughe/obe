money to make up for two night's lodging. I came out of the

about it." I had a chance to get away. The police came up and I

There was a young fellow with Spencer; I expect it is Collins. I
was there when my brother was arrested. He was further up the town
looking in a baker's shop. Neither of us tried to get away.
