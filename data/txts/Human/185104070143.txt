JAMES MOORE. re-examined.  When I came in, my daughter told me the woman asked her for change for a half-sovereign; she said she only had 5 s ., and held the money out at the same time in her hand; that the prisoner said, "Give me that (at the same time taking it), and I will send my boy for change;" that she called the boy, but he would not come; that the boy disappeared, and the woman also—my brother was not there.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did not you draw me a glass of ale three days after? A.  No. ANGELINA MOORE. re-examined.  The prisoner said, "Give me the 5 s ., and I will get it changed"—she did not ask me to lend it her—I told my father she had taken it out of my hand, not that I had lent it her.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did I not come to the house five times afterwards, and say you lent it me? A.  No.
   JURY . Q.  Did you see the half-sovereign? A.  No; my cousin was with me—she is not here—she is fourteen years old—my uncle was not there—we were alone—the prisoner stayed about ten minutes after she got the 5 s .
   Prisoner.  I had no half-sovereign. WILLIAM IRWIN( policeman, R  811). I produce a certificate of the prisoner's conviction—( read—Central Criminal Court, Convicted Aug. , 1850, and confined three months)—  I was present—she is the person. GUILTY. Aged 38.— Confined Twelve Months .
 997. DANIEL NOLAN , stealing 2 pieces of carpet and 1 copper boiler, value 21 s  ., fixed to a building; the goods of Amelia Lambert . ELIZABETH HAZLEWOOD. I am the wife of James Hazlewood. On 29th March, about seven o'clock in the morning, I missed the copper from its place in our out-house, and two pieces of carpet from a box—I had seen it safe at seven o'clock on the evening of the 28th—this ( produced ) is the carpet—I did not like to swear to the copper—they are the pro perty of Amelia Lambert—I am a lodger in the house. JOHN WHITE. ( policeman, R  180). On 29th March, about seven in the morning, I saw the prisoner in Church-street, Deptford, with a copper on his shoulder wrapped up in these two pieces of carpet—I stopped him and asked where he brought the copper from—he said from his own house, 7, Wellington-street, East Greenwich, and he was going to sell it, it was his own—I went to his house, but he had not been there that night. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Six Months .
