   Re-examined.  When we are asked for a copy of an affidavit, or a copy of a will, we charge 1 s . for looking up the papers; that would be search, 1 s ."—if we are asked for a copy either of a will or affidavit we always charge 1 s . for looking at the original for the purpose of making le copy, and we charge 6 d . a folio for the copy; five folios would be a half crown—when we make a copy either of an affidavit or will we charge so much for looking at the original and comparing it, as well as for making the copy.
