COURT. Q. Then, according to your statement, the arrangement was
not perfect, wanting the security upon which you insisted before you ad-
vanced the money? A. Just so-although Mr. Watt had put his name to
it, he had not perfected the note in the shape upon which I insisted before
I advanced the money-when I got White's signature I did advance the
money.
