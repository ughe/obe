WILLIAM WEBSTER. These shillings are all counterfeit and all unfinished
except one-they are all from one mould.
Prisoner's Defence. I never saw the parcel till the policeman opened it; it
was brought into my place by the young man I was living with, who told
me not to open it. I work very hard to support myself and child.
MR. COLLINS, as amicus Curiæ, suggested that as the coins were unfinished
they did not resemble the current coin of the realm.
WILLIAM WEBSTER (re-examined). They did resemble it, but the GET was
not marked off.
Mr. COLLINS further suggested that a shilling has a milled edge, whereas the
edges of all the coins produced were rough. The Court having consulted the
REOORDER left it to the jury to say, whether the coins or any three of them
"resembled, or were intended to resemble a piece of the current silver coin of the
Realm called shillings."
