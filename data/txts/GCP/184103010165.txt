963. JAMES MOORE and EDWARD THOMPSON were indicted
for stealing, on the 2nd of March, 8 pieces of wood, value 9d., the goods
of Thomas Cubitt.
DUNCAN GRANT. I am private watchman to Thomas Cubitt, at Thames
Bank. About twelve o'clock, on the 2nd of March, I saw the two prisoners
going towards a pile of deals---Moore took up this piece of deal, and walked
away with it 200 yards-he then broke it into small pieces, and Thompson
assisted him and tied it up in a bundle-I went and took Moore-he re-
sisted-I went and called a policeman-when I returned they were making
away with the wood among the vacant places in the stones.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Do you know whether Moore's
father is employed on the premises ? A. No-Thompson was walking close
to Moore when he took the wood- I do not know whether Moore had been
taking his father's dinner-it was after Moore began to break this up that
Thompson assisted him--one of the masons employed on the place was
helping them to put the wood away-1 do not know his name Thompson
split up part himself-he did not attempt to get away-he said nothing at
the time.
(The prisoners received good characters.)
MOORE-GUILTY.-Confined Two Months.
THOMPSON-NOT GUILTY.

Thomas Geeves. I keep this public-house. On the 2nd of March I
