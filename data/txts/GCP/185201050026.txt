John HOWELL. I am foreman to Mr. Nash, a stove-maker, of Newgate
street. Oxley has worked for him five or six years-it is the same firm as
Wright and Nash - they have two places of business-Oxley was absent on
boxing-day-it was a general holiday-be is one of the best men I have, one
of the most quiet and sober.
Cross-examined by Mr. RYLAND. Q. What period did he work for you
in 1848? A. Not at all, nor in 1847-this was the first winter he has worked
for me for three or four years- I only employ him now and then in the winter,
but I have seen him occasionally selling his goods in the street, and have
bought baskets of bim.
OXLEY-Guilty. BURKE-GUILTY.
James Pole (City policeman) stated that both prisoners had been summarily
Convicted of felony seven years ago, and that Oxley was sentenced to three
months imprisonment in 1848 for violently assaulting the police, and attempting
to rescue his brother from custody.
OXLEY-GEILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Eighteen Months.
BURKE-GUILTY. Aged 23.-Confined Twelve Months.
