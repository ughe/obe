209. BRIDGET M'NALLY was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of Nov ., 5 brushes, value 7 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of John Tubbs . JOHN TUBBS. I am a brush-maker, and live in Berwick-street, St. James's. The prisoner was my charwoman-these are my brushes—I did not sell them to her. CATHERINE BRIANT. I bought these five brashes of the prisoner—I sold them to Mr. Roberts—I am sure they are the same.
   ROBERTS . I purchased these brushes of Briant
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 38.— Confined Six Months .
 (There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
 210. MARY GOLDSMITH was indicted for bigamy. SUSANNAH ABEL. I am the wife of John Abel, and live in Hope-street, Spitalfields. The prisoner is my sister—she was married to Robert Gold smith, at Hackney-church, on the 23rd of August, 1835—I was one of the subscribing witnesses—I was present at the same church, in Nov., 1837, when she was married to Daniel Sinfield—Goldsmiih has been dead three years and a half—when my sister was married last we considered him dead.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANX . Q.  Did Goldsmith quarrel with her for not getting her property on the day. he was married? A.  Yes—the mar riage was never consummated—Sinfield knaw that she had gone through the ceremony of marriage with Goldsmith—I told him that he was separated from my sister, and I considered he was dead—Sinfield had no property—my sister had a good house and furniture—he has ill-treated her. since, and she has had to go before the Magistrate nine times—he has held a threat of this charge to get her property from her, and he has got a portion of it. DAVID SINFIELD. I married the prisoner on the 16th of Nov., 1837—she never represented to me that she had been married before—she had on pro perty whatever.
   Cross-examined. Q.  After you were married, did you not go to live with her? A.  Yes—I did not take her to my house, because I had children—I had nothing of her—not a blanket or sheet to cover me at night—I have threatened to charge her with bigamy as soon as I could prove it. NOT GUILTY.
 211. ROBERT SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of Nov ., 1 musket, value 10 s  ., the goods of William Forman . WILLIAM FORMAN. On the 23rd of Nov., I lost a musket which was out side my shop window for sale—the one produced is mine. I am in the employ of William Forman, ef Paradise-place, Hackney. On Wednesday, the 33rd of Nov., I was standing in front of my master's premises—I saw the prisoner take the gun, and run away—he dropped it—a man took it up, and brought it back.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A man said he would give me a few halfpence to carry it; there was a call of, "Stop thief;" and he said, " Chuck  it down," which I did—the policeman came and caught me. GUILTY.* Aged 18.— Confined Four Months .
 212. MARY DACEY was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of Nov ., 1 shawl, value 4 s  . the goods of Adam Pratty . JULIA PRATTY. I am the wife of Adam Pratty. On the 24th of Nov. I lost my shawl—I got a policeman, and went after the prisoner—I found her, and found this shawl on her between her shift and petticoat, in a lodging-house. GUILTY. Aged 27.— C I Three Months.
