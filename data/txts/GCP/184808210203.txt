Eliza PERRIAN. My husband keeps the Joiner's Arms, beer-shop, at
Lewisham. On 20th July, about twelve o'clock in the day, the prisoner
Cooper came and asked for half a pint of porterit came to a pennybe
gave me a bad half-crown-I looked at it, threw it down, and told him it
was bad-he spatched it up instantly, and gave me a good one be went out
and I sent a boy to follow him-I saw Cooper join Leary and another
they went to Lee.
Cross-examined by Mr. Parne. Q. How far were the other persons from
you? 4. About 100 yards--I had never seen Cooper before-he was at
our place about two minutes.
