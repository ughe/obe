   Cross-examined by MR. SERJEANT PARRY . Q.  Is Mr. Tayloe your solicitor? A.  He is joint-solicitor with Mr. Clabon—Mr. Tayloe was solicitor to the Unity—he was not constantly present at the board-meetings; he was sometimes—instructions were given to him to sue for overdue accounts—I presume that he was acquainted with all the accounts that were overdue—I saw these weekly statements—I did not see the detailed balance-sheet, to my recol lection; the balance-sheet that was published I saw—I suppose there was a detailed balance-sheet agreeing therewith.
   COURT . Q.  Is it the usual course to have a detailed balance-sheet? A.  Yes—I have no doubt that a general balance-sheet, and a detailed balance-sheet were handed to the directors; I cannot charge my memory with this—we had the profit and loss balance-sheet, and the general balance sheet together, but as to the details I cannot undertake to say that the full detailed balance-sheet was there.
