o'clock in the evening, I received information from a Mr. Kelley, in conse-
quence of which I went into the Broadway, Westminster, to look for the

watched at the publio-house in Tothill Street-we then saw the prisoner
and two or three others leave the public-house--he came down Tothill
Street towards the Broadway, then passed above the Broadway, and then
turned back as I was walking towards himseeing he was going up Tothill
Street again, I directed the other constable to go up and stop him that way
- he immediately turned round and made a run at me, and said "You
