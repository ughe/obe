   T. BONO ( Re-examined by MR. DEMICHELE ). I have examined the con tents of the box which was found, and which was claimed by the female prisoner—I have found in it a number of articles, the subject of other burglaries in the neighbourhood of Leatherhead, and also a number of pawntickets—they were burglaries from places where the female prisoner has been employed, and the articles are identified by persons living in those houses—in the case relating to the breaking into a girls' school at Leatherhead there were two or three pairs of men's drawers and night-gowns, flannel petticoats unfinished, three chemises, and two children's pinafores—that is all from the school—they were stolen in May last—I also found a pair of boots on the female prisoner, which have been identified as the produce of a burglary at Leatherhead—on August 10th and on August 11th another burglary was committed at Leatherhead, and I found ten table knives, rolls of cloth and cuttings, the subject of a burglary at Mr. Shoolbred's, who identified them—when I charged the male prisoner, he said, "I found the things wrapped up in a cloth at the bottom of Mr. North's yard."
