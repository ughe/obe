1496. WILLIAM HARRIS and DAVID EVANS were indicted for
stealing 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Horn, the younger,
from his person.
THOMAS HORN, JUN. I live in Barbican. On the 23rd of June I was
at Billingsgate, and lost my handkerchief-I had seen it safe about half an
hour before-this is it.
George Scott (City police-constable, No. 560.) On the evening of the
23rd of June I was at Billingsgate market-1 saw the prisoners, and
watched them they attempted to pick seven or eight persons' pockets-
then saw Harris take this handkerchief from the prosecutor's pocket, and
pass it to Evans--I took them both, and the handkerchief-this is it.
HARRIS-GUILTY. Aged 15.-Confined Three Months.
EVANS-GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Six Months.
