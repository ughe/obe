 1178.  HENRY WILSON and AMELIA WILSON were indicted for stealing, on the 31st of August , a bed, value 2 l.; a bolster, value 3 s.; a pillow, value 3 s.; a blanket, value 10 s.; a quilt, value 10 s.; two sheets, value 4 s.; a trunk, value 4 s.; six pictures, value 6 s.; a set of fire-irons, value 3 s.; a looking-glass, value 3 s.; and four chairs, value 6 s., the goods of William Hannaford , in a lodging-room.
   ELIZABETH HANNAFORD. I am the wife of William Hannaford. The female prisoner took a lodging at my house for herself and husband. They both came and lived together for four months. The lodgings were furnished. On the 31st of August, the articles stated in the indictment were missed; they still remained in the lodging - we had them secured. The man said, he certainly knew they were gone, but did not say how they went.
   ELIZABETH BRITAIN. I bought some pictures and a jug of the female prisoner; also four chairs at four different times.
   THOMAS KEARNEY. I was fetched, and took the prisoner in charge. I went to Britain's, in Clement's-lane, and found two pictures., a trunk, and a jug; the woman said she would replace them if she would give time.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   AMELIA WILSON'S Defence. I intended to replace them - she knew the things were parted with three months before.
   ELIZABETH HANNAFORD. I did not know they were gone till Sunday.
 AMELIA WILSON -    GUILTY. Aged 29. Transported for Seven Years.
 HENRY WILSON -    NOT GUILTY.
 1179.  RACHAEL SIMMONDS was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of July , a bolster, value 5 s.; and two blankets, value 2 s., the goods of Sebrino Guzeer , in a lodging-room.
 SEBRINO GUZEER. I am a  seaman, and keep a house in Gray's-inn-lane. The prisoner took a furnished room of me on the 8th of July.
   SARAH GUZEER. I am the wife of the prosecutor. I let the prisoner a furnished lodging on the 8th of July. She remained for a week. I asked her for an iron I had lent her. She shut the door in my face, and said she had none. I asked her repeatedly for it - she would not open the door. I said if she did not, I would fetch a constable. While I went, she left. I found the door open, and these things missing.
   JOHN TYE. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a blanket and pillow pawned by the prisoner, one on the 15th of July and the other on the 18th, in the name of Summers.
   WILLIAM CREED. The prisoner pawned a blanket with me on the 16th of July, which I produce.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   GEORGE STRIPLING. I am an officer, she was given into my charge. I saw the duplicates of these things found on her, she is frequently out of her mind.
     GUILTY. Aged 66. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 1180.  JOHN GIBSON was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of July , a sheet, value 5 s., the goods of Henry Rogers , in a lodging-room.
   MARY ROGERS. I am the wife of Henry Rogers. I let the prisoner a furnished lodging more than a year ago; he is son-in-law to one Buckley, who had it before him. He was apprehended this day four weeks, and I then missed a sheet and blanket off another bed, which I had let to him with the room.
   HENRY JOEL. I am servant to Mr. Beauchamp, pawnbroker, Holborn. I produce a sheet pawned on the 14th of July, in the name of Gibson, I cannot say who by.
   WILLIAM MARSH. I produce the duplicate of this sheet. I apprehended the prisoner, and he gave me the ticket.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I pawned the sheet, nothing more, it was done through extreme distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. Confined One Month.
 1181.  RICHARD BUCKLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of August , a bed, value 1 l., the goods of Henry Rogers , in a lodging-room.
   MARY ROGERS. I let the room to the prisoner before Gibson - I missed nothing while he had it. I found this bed pawned on the 6th of August, 1822, at that time he was my lodger.
   WILLIAM MARSH. I am an officer. I apprehended the prisoner, and found the duplicate of the bed upon him.
   WILLIAM CREED. This bed was pawned with me by a woman.
 Prisoner's Defence. It was my full intention to redeem it, - I should have done it in a fortnight - I was distressed.
     GUILTY. Aged 63. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
