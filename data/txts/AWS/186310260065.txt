Cross-eramined by Mr. METCALFE. Q. Did you you ever see Mr. Dawson

that Mr. Dawson was a "society," or a secretary-Mr Turner, who I was
acting for, told me that he had been articled to a solicitor, or was a solicitor

-I received the instructions by letter, in the name of Turner-I think it

-I did not notice whether the post-mark was Bath, or London-I Baw him

him also in May and June--I knew on 31st July that there were criminal
proceedings against him, and did not see him after that till he was in custody
