Cross-examined by Mr. Methuen. I have paid at Fashion Street
£2 5s. a week for 10 years, which is more than the rent of Artillery

(To Mr. Thorne.) I did not sign an agreement for partnership-

nership. I did sign an agreement for letting the place to a man

man. (To the Jury.) I received no consideration for signing the
