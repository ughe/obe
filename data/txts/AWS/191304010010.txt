ELLEN KEMP, wife of last witness. On Wednesday, February 12, at
about noon, prisoner opened the door of our flat and walked in. I had
never seen him before. He had a brown paper parcel. He said, Are
you Mrs. Kemp!" I said, Yes." He said, It is all right, I have
seen Albert." I said, Are you sure1" He said, Yes." I left him
in the room sitting on the chair, while I went with my children to my
mother's to get some food. My husband had gone to look for work
at 4 a.m. that morning. I returned from my mother's at about 2 p.m.
Prisoner was putting his overeoat on; he said to me, 44 Tell Albert I
will see him between half past seven and eight to-night." He then
left. I afterwards noticed that he had left the brown paper parcel
on top of the cupboard. My husband came home at about 5. As the
result of what I told him he looked at the parcel and opened it. (The
witness corroborated her husband.)
