THOMAS RICHARD HOLDER. I am apprentice to John Moore, a butchery
in Nelson-street, Greenwich. On the evening of the 19th of August
was standing outside the shop, and saw the prisoner waiting as I thought
to buy some meat-I asked her what she would buy-she asked me the
price of a piece of veal - I did not know the price-she put it inside-our
young man was weighing some veal-she went io, took a piece of beef
put it under her shawl, and came out I took hold of her arm, and said,
"You have a piece of beef" --she then threw it on the step of the next door,
and said she had no beef, and had not seen it I gave her into a witness'
charge, and went down the step and picked up the beef.
