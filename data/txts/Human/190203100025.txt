Cross-examined.  During the night the deceased vomited once, and the vomit smelled of whisky.
   The prisoner in his defence, on oath, said, that he fought with the deceased because he said he had used bad language to the barmaid the previous night; that during the fight the deceased fell and struck his head on the kerb; that he was quite a stranger to him; that he did not want to fight, but that the deceased made him; that he did not want to hurt the man, and that he did not strike him behind the right ear from behind.
