1424. CHARLES LEDGER was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of
May, 1 can, value 148., the goods of Thomas Wilson; and that he had
been previously convicted of felony.
Thomas LARKINS. I am barman at the King's Arms, Back-hill, St.
George's. I received information, and followed the prisoner- I found him
three or four doors from the house, with this can and a bag under his arm
-I pursued, calling, “Stop thief"-he immediately began to run-I say
him drop it at the corner of Johnson-street- I took it up, and brought it
back-it belongs to my master, Thomas Wilson-I had seen it in the
bar three minutes before.
JAMES HICKLEY. I live at Mr. Wilson's- I was at the bar, and saw
the prisoner take the can off the cask, and take it into the tap-room-- I ran
and called Larkins.
THOMAS JONES. I am a Thames policeman. I saw the prisoner with
a bag in his hand-Larkins pursued him-he had a can under his arm-he
dropped it, and I took him.
JOSEPH Eady. I am a policeman-I produce a certificate of the pri-
soner's former conviction which I got from Mr. Clark's office I was present
at the trial-he is the person who was so convicted-he bad three months
(read.)
