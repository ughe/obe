I took the prisoner on Tooting common—he laid down on his back, and asked me what I wanted with him—I told him he knew—he said, "I know nothing but the parcel I took to Chapman"—I said, "That was all wrong"—he said, "I gave Miller the money."
 (Edmund Craven, a wheelwright, and Ellen Simpson, of Tooting, gave the prisoner a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months.
   ADJOURNED TO THE 16TH OF DECEMBER , 1844.
