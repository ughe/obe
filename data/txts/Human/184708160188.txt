I had coals of the prosecutor—on the 27th of July I paid the carman (Gilder) 1 l . 2 s .—this is the receipt. JOHN GILDER. I am the carman—I received the money from Mrs. Chignell; I gave it to the prisoner, who is the clerk.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Do you recollect giving him that 1 l . 2 s .? A.  Yes—I have received a little money from time to time—I received this money for Mr. Coomb—these were not Mr. Hedges' coals at all. WILLIAM BURTON. I paid 2 l . 5 s . 6 d . about the 24th of July to the prisoner for Mr. Hedge.
   MR. ROBINSON to MR. HEDGE. Q.  Have you any partner? A.  No—Mr. Coomb does his business at the wharf, and sends out coals—it was the duty of the prisoner to receive this money and hand it to me—I am answer able for it.
   COURT. Q.  You directed the prisoner, your servant, to receive it? A.  Yes—it was his duty to pay the money received of Mrs. Chignell to Mr. Coomb—the 2 l . 5 s . 6 d . paid by Mr. Burton to the prisoner was my money.

 1925. THOMAS KIRKMAN and JOHNWALKER were indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; 3 half-crowns; and 2 s  .; the pro perty of Arthur Burnell , from his person. ARTHUR BURNELL. I live in James'-terrace, Camden-town—on the evening of the 23rd of July, I was with some friends in the Commercial-road—I got intoxicated—I then came to Whitechapel-raod—I lost a silk handkerchief, and about 14 s . in money—some was in pocket behind, and some in my trowsers pocket—this is my handkerchief, when I got sober I identified it. THOMAS KELLY( policeman  119 H. ) I was in plain clothes in White-chapel-road that night, and saw the prisoners with Burnell—they brought him to the Brass Founder's Arms—he would not go in there, and they took him to Fieldgate-street, and then on to the Commercial-road—they got him into a public-house—he was there perhaps twenty minutes—they went from there to a cook's shop—I saw Burnell use his handkerchief before he went into the cook's shop—I staid till they came out—they went to another public-house, and were still dragging him along—they then separated—I took Walker on suspicion of robbing Burnell—on our way across the road, Walker tried to drop this handkerchief, but we caught it—Kirkman was standing a little further on.
