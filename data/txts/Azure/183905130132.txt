1638. WILLIAM JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 4th
May, 101bs. weight of beef, value 68., the goods of Alfred Robinson.
ALFRED ROBINSON. I am a butcher, and live in Seymour-place. I
bad a piece of boiled beef inside my window-board, on the 6th of May—
the prisoner was brought in with it—it is mine.
SAUVEL CARTWRIGHT. I live at Church-hill, St. Pancrag. I saw thi
prisoner take the beef off the board that night—I ran and took him.
Prisoner's Defence. A boy took it and chucked it down at my feet.
GUILTY. Aged 23.—Confined one Month.
