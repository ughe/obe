   MR. SOUTH ( re-examined ). There was some loose straw, but it was a distance away from the ricks, I should say five or six yards; there were no holes in the ricks, no pigs had been turned into the field; there was no signs of any person having been sleeping there—the ricks were about ten yards from the road—I last saw them before the fire between 12 and 1 o'clock that same day. WILLIAM ATTER( re-examined ). The prisoner did not give himself up to me—he could not have gone to the station to enquire for me—I heard nothing about it from my wife. NOT GUILTY.
