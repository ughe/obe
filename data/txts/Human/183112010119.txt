 282.  JAMES WOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of December , 1 half-crown, the money of William Williamson.
   WILLIAM WILLIAMSON. I live in Brown's-lane, Spitalfields, and am a trimming-maker. I have employed the prisoner between two and three years; he slept in the house on the 2nd of December - I had counted my money that night; I had one sovereign and five half-crowns in my watch-pocket, and one shilling and some halfpence in my trousers pocket, on a chair by the side of my bed - I awoke about half-past six o'clock; he was out of bed, and I heard the money rattle - I laid very still for a few minutes, and then I saw him go down on his knees, and replace the trousers on the chair; he left the room - I counted the money, and sent for a Policeman.
   WILLIAM DRABBLE. I am a Police-officer. I was sent for to the prosecutor's - I received the prisoner in charge, and found on him three 1d. pieces, a farthing, a knife, and a pair of scissors, but no half-crown.
   WILLIAM WILLIAMSON. I told him I had sent for an officer; he had about an hour to get rid of the money before he came - he was not in my sight all that time; I never found any thing wrong in him before. GUILTY. Aged 20.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Confined One Month.
 283.  DANIEL MARCHANT was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of December , 3 half-crowns, 5 shillings, 1 sixpence, and 11 1/2d. in copper monies, the monies of Joseph Allen , from his person.
   JOSEPH ALLEN. I am a gardener, and live in Lissongrove. On the afternoon of the 2nd of December, I was in the Champion public-house, in Earl-street, Lisson-grove; I had some bread and meat, which I laid down - I counted my money before I went in, as I had been taking up some trees, and did not know exactly what I had; I found I had 13s. in silver in my right-hand coat pocket, and 11 1/2d. in my left-hand coat pocket - I put three half-crowns in my breast-pocket, leaving the other, which was in sixpences and shillings, in my coat pocket; I went into the tap-room, had a pint of beer, and saw the prisoner sitting in another box by the fire - I was rather fresh with liquor; I fell into a dose - I was not exactly fast asleep; I felt the prisoner's hand in my pocket - I took hold of it; he ran across into the box where he came from; I charged him with picking my pocket, as I found I had not one farthing left, and told him I would serve him out for it - I went to the door, sent a man for a Policeman, and staid outside the door a quarter of an hour or twenty minutes, while he went for him.
 Cross-examined by MR. J. ALLEY. Q.When are you paid your wages? A. At uncertain times; I had then been at work for Mrs. Lewis, North-bank - I had taken 24s.; I was not very drunk: when I get a little liquor it takes my head, and I have been desired by publicans to stay away from their houses; I had another pint of porter at Maida-hill, and one at the Portman Arms, in the afternoon - I had had no ale nor spirits that day, and I had one pint at the Champion; I carried my silver in my coat pocket, because both my trousers pockets were torn - I went in behind the door of the Champion; I did not see any one but the prisoner - he sat between two women in a box in a corner; there might be other persons in the room; I sat by myself - there was no one in the next box- I did not want any man in the room to fight, that I will swear; I was not exactly asleep - I should have been asleep if I had not been disturbed; the prisoner's hand was in my pocket, and I caught hold of it - he twisted it from me, and went back to his seat; I told him I would serve him out - I went out in a moment for the Policeman; I do not know how many persons were there - there were several persons in the room when the Policeman came; I was very much agitated - I sent an Irish labourer for a Policeman; he was a stranger to me - I gave him a shilling yesterday morning - the prisoner did not attempt to run away, but staid in the room between the two women - I have charged several persons with robbing me; I never took any body up - I have been robbed there before.
 COURT. Q. How many persons were in the room when you went out for an officer? A. I did not take any notice - the prisoner and the women were there; there might be other men.
