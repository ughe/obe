JAMES FIERY. I live at 3, Elizabeth-place-I was standing outside the
door of No. 2, and saw Shea come up-he asked for Dalton-after that I
saw the female prisoner throw four or five stones among the crowd, but I
cannot tell to what individual-I saw Miles outside in bis shirt-sleeves, and
his hand up in the act of throwing- I cannot say what he had in his hand
-I saw Shea lying on the ground before that I saw four stones thrown at
Shen, but cannot say where they hit him--they were clinkers from the iron
founders--they were lying in the road—I saw Mrs. Mahoney before that, she
came out with a poker in ber band.
1 Cross-examined. Q. Were there a number of people throwing stones and
clinkers ? A. Yes—I saw Shea throw some stones- I do not know whether
Miles's hand had a stone in it wben I saw it up-I saw nothing leave his
hand-he was two yards from his own door-I saw Shea when he was on
the ground-I cannot say if any one struck him on the head with anything
- he was down about two minutes before I went away-I did not see him
bleeding—Miles was two or three yards from him when he was in the act
of throwing-his face was rather towards the left and Shea was on the right,
on the ground, but his face was inclined to the left-I cannot say who be
was aiming at.
Court.Q. Do you know these people! A. I know the prisoner and
these men--they are all Irish---Miles lived in Eyre's house--they were all
friends together-Mahoney and her husband live in the same house--I never
was in the house - I have heard Miles say that he comes from Clare-l do
not know whether Shea was a Kerry man--the two prisoners lived in the
house to which Shea went to resoue Dalton, I believe.
