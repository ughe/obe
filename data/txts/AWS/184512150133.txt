WILLIAM HORNER POPHAM. I am a surgeon, and live at No. 5, Queen's
Head-row, Newington. On the Thursday afternoon about five o'elock I was
near the Peacock public-house- heard an explosion, which appeared to be
of fire arms-in consequence of information I received, I went into the tap
of the Peacock public-house- saw the deceased there, supported by two
men-he was on the ground-he was very much exhausted-I found a
wound on the left side of his breast-he survived a very short time after I
entered- do not know that it was above a minute-it was a gun-shot
wound- made a post mortem examination on the Saturdayeol found the
bullet had passed through the heart and entered the lungit had gore come
pletely through to the other side--that was the cause of death.
