and stealing 1 wateh; value 308. his property.
AARON LEMON (through an interpreter). 1 amu printer. On 9th May I
was walking with a friend in the neighbourhood of Spitalfields, about noon-
I had a silver water in my right waistcoat-pocket, with a guard to it saw
the prisoner with five or six others-they came close to me-one of them
threw a potato, which went through a window-they took hold of my friend,
and it appeared as if they wanted him to pay for the broken windowithey
said he had broken it-I went towards him, and they let him go and took
hold of me, and the prisoner drew my wateh out of my pocket and ran away
with it-he was taken by a policeman and brought back in less than ten
minutes--this is my wateh (produced).
