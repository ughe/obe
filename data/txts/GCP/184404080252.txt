1333. WILLIAM GOODWIN and JOHN BASHFORD were indicted
for stealing 1 bushel of a mixture of chaff, oats, and beans, value 18d.; the
goods of Harvey Combe and others, the masters of Goodwin; to which
GOODWIN pleaded Guilty. Aged 32.-Confined One Month.
BASHFORD pleaded Guilty. Aged 63.-Confined Two Months.
1334. JOHN BRADLEY was indicted for burglariously breaking and
entering the dwelling-house of Richard Neate, in the night of the 30th of
March, at St. Mary, Newington, with intent to steal and stealing therein, 1
till, value 6d. ; 32 cheroots, 38. ; 1 box, 2d.; 1 bag, 2d.; 1 clock weight,
2d. ; 2 sixpences, and 12 pence, his property.
RICHARD NEATE. I am a licensed retailer of beer, and live in Villier's-
street, Walworth Common, in the parish of St. Mary, Newington. I keep
the house on Saturday, the 30th of March, I locked up my house-between
two and three in the morning I was aroused by the police, I came down, and
saw the prisoner in custody at the foot of the stairs near the bar-I found
the tap room window facing the street, wide open, and my till on the table,
which I had left underneath the bar counter over night I missed two six-
pences-a bag was taken from a cupboard in the bar, which was found in an
adjoining room-the house had been entered by the tap room window which
was shut down close over night, and the shutters were sbut, but not fastened
-had seen the prisoner in the tap room the evening before-he was not
in the tap room when I went to bed.
Prisoner. I was in the house playing at cards; I was intoxicated; I fell
asleep, and got under the seat, and was locked in ; I could not make any
body bear; I pulled down the shutters, and the policeman was standing out-
side. Witness. It is a very small tap room-after all the customers were
gone I went and took the glasses off the table--the gas was burning
strong, and if he had been in the room I must have seen him.
HENRY PRIEST (police-constable P 243.) I was on duty on Walworth
Common on the 30th of March, between two and three o'clock in the morning
- I noticed the prosecutor's tap-room window open-I got in, and found the
prisoner in the back kitchen, in one corner, without his shoes--they were in
the tap-room.
HENRY BURR (police-constable P 175.) I know the prisoner-on the
Saturday night, about a quarter or twenty minutes past twelve o'clock, I saw
him and two others take a cab in East-street, and go in the direction of
Villiers-street-about a quarter to two I met him again- I suspected, stop-
ped, and searched him, and found on him a halfpenny, a tobacco-box, a
lucifer-box, and a knife-he was not quite half a mile from Villiers-street-
he was sober.
Prisoner. It was on Friday night, the 30th, I was going down East-lane,
and met two young chaps and a cabman I knew; we asked him to give us a
ride: we all three got in, and rode a little way; we then came back, and had
some beer at the Bell; I saw the policeman about an hour and a half after,
and he told me to go about my business.
RICHARD NEate re-examined. A box of cheroots was taken from a cup-
board in the bar, and found on the floor : and the bag and money were all
removed from where I had left them.
Guilty.. Aged 18.-Confined One Year.
