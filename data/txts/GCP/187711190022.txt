dentist-Leach came to me under the name of Wood, on the 16th July,

received this letter from him marked L-Leach did not come to my house
after that-he said he took the office for an auctioneer's business-I
think he gave me a card I have not got it-seven barrels of chicory
and coffee were delivered at my house on the 27th July, but I would not
take them in, and they were sent back to the station.
