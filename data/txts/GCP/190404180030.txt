383. JOHN EDWARD TITCHMARSH (63) PLEADED GUILTY to a
libel on John Picard. Three months' imprisonment, at the expiration of
which time to find a surety for £50, and to enter into his own recognisances
of £100 to keep the peace for twelve months, or in default to be further im-
prisoned for three months.
