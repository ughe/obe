Robbery with violence on Hershel Goldform, and stealing £1 Is. 6d., his
money.
MR. MORTIMER Prosecuted.
HERSHEL GOLDFORM (Interpreted). I am a tailor's presser, of 112,
Cable Street--on August 29th I left a friend, Latinski, about midnight,
after leaving a public-house I sat on the kerb in Brick Lane-two women
approached, and sat near me-whilst one spoke the other began to search
my pockets-I protested, and a red-haired man gave me a blow from behind,
and searched iny pockets--I had 299. 6d.- the police found 7s. and
twopence and 6d. on the floor-I also lost a watch and chain, value 158.
--the prisoner is the man, but he was without a collar-I recognised his
face-I screamed, and a man looked from a window, and then joined
in chasing the prisoner.

you knocked me down-I looked at my watch at a few minutes past 12
I paid 99. 6d. and 159, 6d. for the watch and chain-the chain is
Englisb-made, but the watch came from Russia-I bought it in England
all was in the left trousers pocket-the watch was silver-there was no
key-I am positive the prisoner is the man.
