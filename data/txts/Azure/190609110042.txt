made to stop the bleeding. She died very shortly afterwards.
On August 27 a post-mortem examination was made.
The body was in quite a healthy condition. The wound was
one that might have been caused by the knife produced. The
wound was slightly downwards and backwards ; not much force
would necessarily have been employed in striking the blow. The
