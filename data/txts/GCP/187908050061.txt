Frank EATON LANDER I am cashier at the Southwark branch of the
London and Joint Stock Bank-Buckworth kept an account there, which
commenced some years ago I produce a copy of his account the balance
on 3rd June was 11. 88. 50.- from that date till about the middle of July
there were on the one side a number of cheques presented and paid to the
General Post-office, and on the other side a large number of post office orders,
which were the orders that have been produced they were presented at the
General Post-office by one of our clerks for payment--the balance is made
up in our books from S0th June, and shows a balance there of 21. 28. in
Buckworth's favour-I find a cheque drawn by him on 30th June for 1401.
upon our bank, payable to the Post-office, and presented on 1st July-there
would be nothing in the bank on 30th June to meet it, the balance that
morning being 27. 28.-I find that 1711. 178. 6d. cash was paid in on 1st
July-I cannot say if 597. 88. 6d. out of the 1711. was in post office orders;
they are not dated, and I know nothing about the perforated date there
are no orders here of the 1st July—there are five of them perforated July
2nd-they have passed through our bank, and are presented to the Post-office
for payment next day-these orders bear the perforated stamp, and come to
591. odd—without that 597. there would not have been sufficient in the bank
to meet the 1402. cheque on 1st July-I find a further cheque from the
Post-office for 1451. on 2nd July, the cash paid in on that day 1461. 98. 2d.,
out of which I find six orders perforated 3rd July, amounting to 597. 168. 8d.,
without which there would not have been sufficient on the 2nd July to meet
the 1451. cheque---3rd July cheque, 1401.; cash paid in, 1401. 78. 1d., out of
which are eight post-office orders perforated 4th June, amounting to 771.,
without which there would not have been sufficient to meet the 1401. cheque
- July 4th, cheque, 1301., and 1371. 78. paid in, of which four post-office
orders amount to 401., without which there would have been insufficient to
pay the cheque—5th, cheque 1401., cash 1501., including post-office orders,
371., without which there would not have been sufficient-7th, cheque 1301.
and 1504. paid in; six orders, 591. 108., without which there would not have
been sufficient-8th July, 1601 cheque; 1601. paid in, out of which five
orders, 491. 198., without which there would not have been sufficient-9th,
1101. cheque; 1212. paid in, out of which 391 198. by post-office orders,
