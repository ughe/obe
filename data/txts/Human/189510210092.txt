   Re-examined.  In dealing with bearer warrants it is not necessary to disclose the seller; there is nothing on the face of the document which reveals the seller—the Goldfields Company, through their own brokers, could put 50,000 bearer warrants on the markets, and no one would know whence they, came, although such a number might affect the price—I had previous reason to believe the statements conveyed' to me through Mr. Hart; I had inquired and believed Leach to be a person of respectability and credit.
