FREDERICK COLLINS. I was in the room when the check was handed to
Mr. Cuthbertson I cannot say whether I was in the same box-I do not
know the writing of any part of the check-I believe the check is given in
my favour-my name is F. Collins.
Cross-examined. Q. You have no doubt that the check was drawn in
your favour, have you? A. Well, I cannot say- I believe Smith called me
when the check was cashed; I was in another part of the room-while the
money way one the table, Smith took some of the money and I some, but
how much I cannot exactly say; it could not have been exactly half-I might
have had pretty near half of it-I took it because Smith owed me money,
and I supposed the check was obtained by honourable means I did not
know it was made payable to me, I had not seen it before I swear that-
I afterwards saw Lawes, and he gave me the money to pay the check, when
the case went before the Clerkenwell police-court, that was on a Saturday-
he did not give me the money before I appeared, because I went up there
first to ask if it was correct- I have the money now-I did not pay it over
--I was not allowed to do so-I was given to understand by somebody
about the Court, I cannot say exactly who, that it could not be settled,
as it had been once, before the Magistrate--the holder of the check never
applied to me for it, and so I could not have done it-it was not about this
check at all, it was about Mr. Mills' check, that was in the same way-I
mean to return the 77. 108.--I have not done it, because I have had no
application; it will be done- I have no wish to hold another man's money-
I was to pay it to Mr. Mills.
Q. Now, on your oath, were you not desired to pay 77. 108. to Mr.
Cuthbertson, I only want an answer, yes or no, and just be cautious?
A. Well, I cannot state which way it was now, the two transactions have
been so mixed up together-I swear 71. 108. is all the money I have
received for the purpose of paying the parties-that is not all the money
I have received of Lawes since 3rd Aug.- he did some private business for
me I do not know how much I have received I have no account-I have
not received more than 71. 10s. from him to pay checks- I have not receired
151. from him since 28th Aug.-I received the 77. 10s. on the Saturday, and I
do not remember that I have received any other sum of him since 28th Aug.
I may have received money on my private account; but not to my recol-
lection-I fancy it was previous to that-it was not 501., I should say it
was between 201. and 301. - I swear that was not for the purpose of paying
the two 77. 10s. checks- I did not undertake to pay the other 77. 10s.-
the private accounts between us were about some money Lawes had got for
me, for which I gave him a bill, which I shall have to take up at the end of this
month-I think it is for 301.--I suppose I received between 201. to 301.
from him ; but I cannot tell-besides that, I have received nothing else
except the 71. 10s.
COURT. Q. Was your name on Mills's check ? A. Yes: I saw it at the
police-court-1 did not see it before Mills had it.
