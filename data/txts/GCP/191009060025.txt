(Wednesday, September 7.)
STENNINGS, George (27, labourer), and PEARL, Lewis (20,
painter), both possessing counterfeit coin with intent to utter the
same.
Stennings pleaded guilty.
Mr. Pickersgill, M.P., prosecuted.
Detective FREDERICK BIGGS, H Division. On July 22 at 6.30 p.m.
I was in Commercial Road with Police-constable Chambers when I saw
the two prisoners and another man. I seized Pearl and said I was &
police officer and believed he had some base coin in his possession.
He dropped bad florin (produced) from his right hand. I picked it up
and said, “This is a bad one. Have you any more like this." He
said, "No. Anyone would think we had done & murder." I found
another bad florin wrapped in paper in his waistcoat pocket, also two
pieces of similar paper. He was taken to the station, charged, and
made no reply.
Detective GEORGE CHAMBERS, H Division. I accompanied the last
witness and arrested Stennings, on whom I found 10 counterfeit florins
