GEORGE FRASER. On the 30th of July, I was at the slaughter-house in
Newport-market-Anderson came and asked me, in Guildford-street, if I
would come in and have a ride-I went with him till we came to Newport-
market, and Nellis' cart was there; he called out to Peploe, and asked
him to lend him his tub; he said, "I shall not lend it to you, you broke
my tub before when I lent it you"-he afterwards brought it over to him
-I was sitting on the cart, and Anderson asked me to come and help him
out with them, and he brought out two half-tubs full, and then he fetched
out the third tub, and that had a great deal more in, than the other two, then
he said to me, “Oh, I have got more than I ought to have, half belongs
to this other man," and then he beckoned to Peploe, who came over and
took the guts off the curb-stone, and chucked them into Mr. Nellis' cart-
Anderson had no tub.
