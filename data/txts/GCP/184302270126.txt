Cross-examined. Q. Do you know whether it was all that was due to you?
4. Yes, it was, I am quite sure-I left it to him-he said it was all-I de

EDWARD WILLIAMS. I am a trimmer in the prosecutor's service. On
Saturday, the 18th of Feb., the prisoner paid me 2s. 73., it was two shillings,
a sixpence, and two halfpence.
Cross-examined. Q. I suppose you do not know how much was really
due to you? 4. Yes--I took an account of it that week, it being little
money-I do not think more was due to me-I will not swear it, there might
have been, I cannot tell - I should have kept whatever he gave me- I counted
it up as 2s. 70.-I expected that before he paid it to me--it was what I re-
koned it would be-I knew exactly how much was due to me that week.be
had told me what was due to me before he paid me--sometimes they give
better prices than at others-is was the same as I had at other times for the
work.
