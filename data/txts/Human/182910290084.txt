 2057.  JANE RIDLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of September , 1 blanket, value 5s., the goods of James Watt.
   JAMES WATT. The prisoner had a room on the same floor as me. On the 12th of September my wife was out nursing, and I had to make my own bed - I went out, and put the key under the mat in case my wife should come home; when I came home I saw the bed clothes pulled about, and missed the blanket - there is no mark on this, but I had a pair of them, and this is the same as the other.
   CHARLES WILLIAMSON. This blanket was pawned with me by the prisoner, on the 12th of September, about twelve or one o'clock.
 Prisoner's Defence. That man has sworn false - I came home, took my own blanket, and pawned it for 2s.; he has owed me a great animosity for some time, because I said he brought in women of a night.
   JAMES WATT re-examined. Q. Did she complain of you for bringing women to the house? A. No; I never had any one there but my wife; I have lived with Mr. Burgin eleven years - the man she lives with is not her husband; she came up to me for a light when they came there, and I said to my wife, "I don't like these people, for I heard him beat her, and she threatened to hang him;" I never spoke to her but once in anger, when she was coming up- I told her to keep her distance; I am sure my blanket was gone.
   CHARLES WILLIAMSON re-examined. Q. Did she say before the Magistrate it was her own? A. Yes; she has not often pawned blankets, but I have known her these twenty years, and never knew any thing wrong of her.
     NOT GUILTY.
 2068.  ANN RICHARDS was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of October , 1 bolster, value 6d.; 1 blanket, value 6d.; 1 sheet, value 1s., and 3 yards of carpet, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Elizabeth Clark.
   ELIZABETH CLARK. I am a widow. The prisoner lodged with me for three weeks, till the 18th of October, in Cow-lane, New Gravel-lane- it was a furnished room; I did not know she was going. On the 19th of October I took my child to school; when I returned there was no one in the house - I missed the articles stated.
   CHRISTOPHER GREEN. I took the prisoner on the 19th of October - I found 12s. 8d. on her, and a duplicate of a bed pawned for 14s.
   JAMES WAYLING. I am a Police-officer. The prisoner was brought to our office; I asked what had become of the things - she told me she had taken them to Bluegate-fields, and sold them; I went there, and found all but the bolster-case.
   EDWARD EDMOND CHILD. I am a pawnbroker, and live in High-street, Shadwell. I have a bed, but the person who took it in is out of town; I have seen the prisoner at my master's.
 Prisoner's Defence. I did not tell the officer I had sold them; I said I had left them for a few halfpence - she gave me leave to take any thing I liked, as she knew the distress I was in.
   ELIZABETH CLARK. How could I do so? I never did.
   JAMES WAYLING. She told me she left them at a house a Jew lives in, who I knew took in such things; I went and asked for them - they were delivered up.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 29. Confined Six Weeks.
 2059.  WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of September , 1 jacket, value 5s.; 1 shilling, and 1 halfpenny, the property of Diedirick Klinck.
 DIEDIRICK KLINCK. I am a German. On the 28th of September I was at the  King Harry, in Red Lion-street, Whitechapel, about ten o'clock at night; there was fiddling up stairs - I went up; a man put his hand into my pocket, and took my money - I caught hold of him, and several of them beat me; the landlord came up, and took me down - I said I would not go without that man; they came down, and said I should fight, and they would give me fair play - I pulled off my jacket, and the prisoner was taken with it.
   GEORGE HEARNE. I am a watchman. I produce the jacket which I took from the prisoner - this shilling and this halfpenny were in the pocket; I was going to cry ten o'clock, saw the row, and the prisoner was running from the crowd with it in his hand - he turned the corner and I caught him; I asked him how he came by it - he at first said it was his own, and then said it was the man's who was fighting, and he was going to take it home to his wife; I took him to the watch-house, then went to the crowd, and brought the prosecutor, who claimed it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I found the jacket half a dozen doors from where they were fighting.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years.
