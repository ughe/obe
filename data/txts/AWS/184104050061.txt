1057. JOHN DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of March,
56lbs. weight oflead, value 128., the goods of Richard Howlett.
MARY ANN RUTLAND. I am in the service of Mr. Howlett, a plumber
and glazier in Aldersgate-street. On the morning of the 25th of March.
about half-past eleven o'clock, I was in the kitchen, and heard a noise-I
went up and saw the prisoner] followed him to Charterhouse-square, and
lost sight of him-he had a bag with some lead on his shoulder, when he
was taken by the policeman.
WILLIAM PRYCE. I live in Aldersgate-buildings I was in Black
Horse-yard, and saw the prisoner run out of the prosecutor's shop with a
bundle on his shoulder-he went into Charterhouse-square, and was taken
by the policeman,
GEORGE RUSsBLL (City police-constable, No. 234.) I was on duty,
and took the prisoner with this bag full of lead.
RICHARD HOWLETT. I am a plumber. I have examined this lead,
and know it to be mine-i had been in my house an hour and a half
before I saw it at the station.
Prisoner's Defence. I came up Old-street-a man asked me whether I
would carry this for him to Mr. Lufkins's in Aldersgate-street-I came to
the prosecutor's shop, which was the first one I came to, and this girl
came to the door--1 asked her if Mr. Lufkin lived there, she said no-
went out, and asked several persons if they knew the name, they said, no
-I came back, passed the shop to go to the person who gave me the bag,
and the policeman stopped me.
MaRY ANN RUTLAND re-pxamined. He asked me who lived there, I said
I did not know, but I would go and ask my mistress-he took the bag up
and ran away, leaving the gate open-I heard a noise before I came to the
door.
GUILTY. Aged 20-Confined Six Months.
