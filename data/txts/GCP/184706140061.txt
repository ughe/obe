these mugs? A. I did I went afterwards to his lodging-house, No. 20,
Upper Wimpole-street-I saw him there-I said he must come to Mr. Hall's,
the pawnbroker's-I insisted on his so doing-be said, “You have come
about the plate ; what shall I do; I will do anything; I will get you the
money"-he went with me to Mr. Hall's, who said to him, “Is that plate
you pawned yours"-the prisoner said, "It is no use telling & story, it is
not"-he said, "Pray forgive me"-he said it was for a temporary purpose,
for rent; he was in arrears; he would go and get the money and redeem the
articles-he said that was the whole of it-Mr. Hall said, "How am I to
know whether that is the whole of the property; where are the tickets !"-he
said, " At home”-he left-I went to Wimpole-street, about two minutes
afterwards--the prisoner came out of the dining-room after a short time-he
said, "Oh good God! you are come to ruin me"-he said he would go and
get the money-he rushed out of the house at the front door-Mrs. Wilson
came out to me--the prisoner came to Mr. Hall's about eight o'clock in the
ovening-he laid down three sovereigns, and asked if there was a policeman
at Mrs. Wilson's-he said, “Let me take the plate that I may replace it".
Mr. Hall would not take the three sovereigns, and he took them away
with him I went with him back to Mrs. Wilson's, and he was given in
charge.
ELIZABETH MARY WILBON. I am a widow, and live at No. 20, Upper
Wimpole-street. These articles belong to me the prisoner bad no authority
to part with them he came into my service as a single man, on the 19th of
May,
Cross-examined. Q. From whose service did you receive him? A. From
Sir Arthur Brooks'-I know he lived some time at the Travellers' Clab before
he went there-I understood he had been out of employ some time after he
left there--I have since seen a person who calls herself his wife-I understood
his character was good up to the time I took him I have a large service of
plate-these are articles not in general use--I should not be so likely to miss
them as forks and spoons.
