Smith, a solicitor at Leominster, and that the next-of-kin were going to
take proceedings, and that if any of them came to me I was not to have
anything to say to them-I asked Thomas, I could not say when, what
he had done with the genuine will, and he said he had placed it in
a jar in the cellar for safe keeping, and had afterwards broken the jar
and burnt the will lest any one should find it that was some time
