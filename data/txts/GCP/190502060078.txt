ALBERT CLARK. I am an officer in the Hanwell Fire Brigade-m
father's stables are next to Mr. Wilson's-on January 8th, at 7.40 p.
I was at those stables--I looked round to see if everything was all right-
Mr. Wilson's stables were properly locked with a padlock on the door-
there were then no signs of fire-I received a call about 1.20 a.m.--
partly a volunteer brigade-during the day we fire a maroon to call the

bells-we do not use a maroon at night--no maroon was used on this night.
