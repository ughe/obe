   Cross-examined by LAWSON . You called as a client—I saw very little of you; I think not more than a dozen times—I have no idea of your deals with Hooley beyond that you had something to do with the Electric Con struction Company—I do not think there was any official quotation for any of those shares—I never saw any deal between you and Hooley.
   Re-cross-examined.
