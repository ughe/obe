   JAMES KING. I am a labourer. The two prisoners came up to the prosecutor in the Park, and went on to the first public-house; they staid there about half an hour, and then went on to the Bag of Nails - I did not go in at either of the houses.
   CHARLES BANKS. I was at the Bag of Nails, between three and four o'clock on the 7th of April: the two prisoners and the prosecutor came in; they called for half a pint of rum - Toomey passed as his wife, and the other as his sister; a man there wanted to shake hands with the prosecutor, and the prisoners fell aboard of him; Toomey took the bundle and went out; Allgood put her hand into his pocket, and they both ran off in different directions - the prosecutor was sitting still; he was intoxicated.
   JOHN WILLIAMS. I am a pawnbroker. The two prisoners pawned all this property at our house, on the 7th of April, between seven and eight o'clock in the evening; they were not tipsy.
   CHARLES AUGUSTUS DORRINGTON. The prisoner Allgood was given into my charge at near twelve o'clock at night - there was nothing found on her; she was intoxicated.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 ALLGOOD'S Defence. I met this man in St. James'-park - we were both very much intoxicated; he caught hold of us both, and took us to a public-house; he brought us all kinds of liquor; I do not know how I got home - but as to the property, I had none.
 TOOMEY's Defence. I do not know how I came by the bundle; I think he must have given it me - I had no notion of robbing him; we had been all drinking the whole of the day, and Bankes was like the rest; we were all intoxicated.
 ALLGOOD -    GUILTY. Aged 27. Confined Three Months.
 TOOMEY -    GUILTY. Aged 36. Transported for Seven Years.
 899.  JAMES BEDDOES was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of March , 4 caps, value 5s.; 3 petticoats, value 7s.: 1 pair of stays, value 3s.: 1 collar, value 1s., and 1 pocket, value 1s., the goods of John Rogers.
   ELIZABETH ROGERS. I am the wife of John Rogers. On the 23d of March I had packed up the articles stated in a band-box, to take to the owner; I was driving them in a cart, about a quarter to nine o'clock - I have seen the articles since; these are them.
   CHRISTIAN PODZUS. The watchman brought this property and the prisoner to the watch-house; I asked him what he had to say; he said Nothing.
   MICHAEL LYON. I am a watchman. A gentleman brought the prisoner into Hand-court, and gave him to me, with this property; the owner was not there - I took him to the watch-house; I saw the cart, but did not see him take any thing; but that gentleman did.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was crossing up Red Lion-street, and two lads were fighting in the road; one said,"I shan't carry this;" and another said, "I shan't;" I said, "What is in it;" and they said, "Will you have it;" I took it, and this gentleman took me.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.

 900.  ELEANOR COTTER was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of February , 1 gown, value 4s., and 1 apron, value 6d., the goods of Sarah Porton.
   MARY JACKSON. The prisoner came to me, on the 24th of February, about twelve o'clock, and asked for Louisa's things; I said I had not any of them - she then asked for Sally's blue gown and apron; I gave them to her, supposing Sarah Porton had sent her.
   SARAH PORTON. I had never seen the prisoner that day; she used to work in the same house as me - I did not send her for the things.
   BOYD SILVESTER. I am an officer. I took the prisoner on the 2d of March, she had this apron on - I asked where the gown was; she told me she went for some things, and got these in mistake; that Samuel Smith, who was outside, said "Keep them;" they took them to the Minories, and pawned the gown.
   EDWARD PERKINS. I am a pawnbroker. I took this gown in pawn from the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.
 901.  JANE HARVEY was indicted for stealing. on the 21st of March , 2 shirts, value 10s., the goods of Mary Whitingham.
   MARY WHITINGHAM. I live in Homer-street, and am a poor working woman. The prisoner, whom I had known before, came to see me on the 21st of March, and while she was there I brought some shirts into the room, in a basket; they belonged to my landlord; I had them to wash - she remained from nine o'clock in the morning till two or three; these are the shirts.
   WILLIAM PARKER. I am a pawnbroker. These shirts were pawned with me, on the evening of the 21st of March, to the best of my belief by the prisoner. I gave her this duplicate.
   ROBERT CRAIG. I took the prisoner, and found this duplicate on her.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. Transported for Seven Years.
