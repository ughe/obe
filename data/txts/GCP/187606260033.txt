Tower Street-I was in their employ in the early part of 1872-at that time
I knew Mr. Godfrey and Mr. Brown-I saw Mr. Brown at our office, but I
could not say which month—I don't recollect receiving any communication
previously—the conversation, to the best of my recollection, was that he
wished the goods that had been invoiced to Worship Street sold through
Keiling's—this invoice (produced) was received by our firm I do not know

James Parten. I am a carman in the neighbourhood of Worship Street
and Aldersgate Street-in 1872 I conveyed wines from different parts for
Mr. Brown-I brought some from Aldersgate Street--I never bought any
for any amount of Saul, Godfrey & Brown-I never authorised anybody to
invoice wines in my name--I never received any money in any shape or way
from Saul, Godfrey, or Brown for wines, only for work that I did.
