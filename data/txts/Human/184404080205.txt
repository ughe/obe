ELIZA PHILLIPS. I make use of Mr. Brett's copper—about half-past nine on Wednesday morning, the 20th of Feb., I was using it—I lighted the fire—I left about twelve to get dinner—when I came back after dinner I saw a small box broken up, and burning in the fire—it had just caught light—I shut the furnace-door, and went in to get some coals—the prisoner was there when I came in, and no one else but her and me—I did not put the box on the fire—when I came back with the coals the box was all in a blase—I put some coals in—the prisoner turned and looked at me—I did not speak to her as she was a loose character. MART ELDRIDGE. I use Mr. Brett's copper for washing. On Thursday, the 29th of Feb., in clearing the furnace to make my fire, I found this lock and two pieces of hinges—I laid them down at the corner of the kitchen, and Mr. Brett's little boy laid them on the copper—I did not see him do it—I heard the boy tell his father that he laid it there. HARRIET GIBBONS. I live next door to Mr. Shephard. About the middle of the day of the 5th of March I saw my litte boy pick up this lock by the dunghill—he gave it to me, and I gave it to Mr. Shephard. CHARLES JECKS( police-constable T  20.) I took the prisoner at Blackwater, in Berkshire. I told her I took her for stealing a box containing money from Mr. Shephard—she said she did not do anything of the kind, what money she had laid out for clothes she got by prostitution. NOT GUILTY.

 1279. MARY FURLEY was indicted for the wilful murder of George Furley .
   MR. PRENDERGAST conducted the Prosecution. JOHN FAIRFIELD. I am master of Bethnal-green workhouse The pri soner was an inmate of that workhouse—she had two children—one was about three years of age, and the other one year and ten months, whose name was George—we called it George Furley—both the children were with their mother in the house—she left the workhouse finally on the 20th of March—she left by her own consent—she applied to the guardians and received 6 s .—she asked for it and received it—I heard that she had other money in her possession, but I did not know it myself—she left on a Wednesday, and had the child George Furley with her—I saw her next on the following week after her leaving the house, 1 think on the Tuesday, at the time of the inquest—the surgeon was there—I then saw the child dead, whom I had known in the workhouse by the name of George Furley—during the time the prisoner was in the workhouse she was allowed to have the care of that child—it was always with her day and night.
