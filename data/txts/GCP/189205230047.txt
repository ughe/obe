HENRY JAMES Coore. I am clerk to Mr. Alfred L. Bayer, a solicitor
at Hastings-he is agent to the Sun Fire Office, of London-on 6th
March, 1870, a man giving the name of Richard Beecroft called at our
office as to the insurance of certain furniture, stock, and fixtures of a
coffee-house at 31, Pelham Street, Hastings-I took down the par
ticulars-he wanted to insure for £550, £850 on furniture, £150 on
fixtures, and £50 on stock and utensils—I went to 81, Pelham Street,
and looked round-it was a medium class of furniture-I suggested
some separate insurance; he declined that, and said he would rather
leave it as it was-I accepted the insurance for the £550—I believe he
paid half-a-crown, for which, I believe, I gave him a receipt-I filled up
an order-sheet for a policy to be made out, and sent it to the Sun Fire
Office, 63, Threadneedle Street ; that was about the 12th March, and on the
20th a policy came down from London, made out for £550 in the name of
Richard Beecroft ; this is the policy (produoed); a notice was sent to Richard
Beecroft, at 31, Pelham Street, stating that the polioy was awaiting him ;
it was never called for, it was never taken up, and it was returned by me
to London on the 14th April -I never saw him afterwards.
Heoror CHARLES HEALE. I am a clerk in the Sun Fire Office, London
-on 13th March I received an order for an insurance on the premises,
31, Pelham Street, Hastings-& policy was made out and sent to our
agent on the 19th-on the 14th April it was returned, stating that it had
not been taken up.
