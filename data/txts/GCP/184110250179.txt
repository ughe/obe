2784. JOHN BROWN and BENJAMIN MUNDAY were indicted for
stealing, on the 25th of September, 1 brass-cock, value 28., the goods of
Thomas Perry.
SARAH Nice. I am the wife of Thomas Nice, we live at Lambeth.
We missed a brass cock from our house it was the property of Mr. Perry
-I do not know his Christian name.
NOT GUILTY.
2785. JOHN BROWN and BENJAMIN MUNDAY were again in-
dicted for stealing, on the 25th of September, 1 brass cock, value 28., the
goods of Richard Palmer Roupell; and that Brown had been before con-
victed for felony.
MARIA WALKER. I am the wife of William Walker, of Roupel-street,
Lambeth. We missed a brass cock from our house on the 25th of Septem-
ber-it was the property of Mr. Richard Palmer Roupell—I had seen it
safe on the 24th.
HENRY LATCHFORD (police-constable L 87.) I was on duty in York,
road, on Saturday morning, the 25th of September-I saw the two pri-
soners coming in a direction from Roupell-street-they passed me, and I
followed-when they got to the corner of York-road, they ran-I followed
Brown-he dropped a cock from his hand, but not this one-I took him,
and found another new cock on him, and nine pieces of leaden pipe in his
pockets.
MARK WILLIAM GREEN (police-sergeant L 2.) I pursued Munday, and
found this brass cock of Mr. Roupells in the direction in which he ran-
it was about five o'clock in the morning, on the 25th of September.
Brown's Defence. I had been to the play the night before, and was too
late to get in doors—I found this property in Pedlar's-acre.
JOHN BISSETT (police-constable L 67.) I produce a certificate of
Brown's former conviction, which I got from the office of the clerk of the
peace of Surrey-(read)—he is the person.
MUNDAY-GUILTY. Aged 26.-Transported for Seven Years.
BROWN-GUILTY. Aged 25.
