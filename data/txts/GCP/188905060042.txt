WHITEHEAD, Mayor.
MR. HERBERT SMITH Prosecuted.
MARTHA MERCY RICH. I am a widow, and live at 5, President Street,
St. Luke's on 9th April I went to bed about 9, leaving everything
right in the house; I was not the last up; the door was left on the
latch-I was aroused between 11 and 12, and found the police there and
the room in disorder, but did not miss anything a crowbar and lantern
were found in the room.
ALFRED HEDGES. I lodge in the house-on 9th April I came home at
half-past 9-I was the last up-everything was right-the door was
on the latch-it could only be opened from the outside.
FREDERICK WEBSTER (Policeman G 205). On 9th April, about half-
past 11, I was on duty near this house; I found the door had been forced
open by some instrument-in the front parlonr I found the table drawer
open, and this jemmy in the corner; the back door was open-I went to
the rear of the premises, and in a back-garden abutting on No. 5 I found
the prisoner in the custody of G 184there were marks on the front door
corresponding exactly with the jommy-I examined the prisoner, he was
sober-I found dirt-marks on his hands and clothing, corresponding with
the dirt on the wall-several of the bricks were displaced, and there were
marks of a scramble- anybody dropping over the wall would drop into
the garden of 4, Rahere Street.
MATTHEW BLEE (Policeman G 184). On 9th April, at half-past 11,
in consequence of information, I went to No. 4, Rahere Street, and in the
water-closet in the back yard I found the prisoner he was sober-I
asked what he was doing there-he said, “Nothing"-he said he had
been fighting in the street, and had run in there to get out of the way
there was no entry into that garden except through the house while I
was there the last witness came over from 5, President Street-the
prisoner was afterwards charged with committing a burglary at 5,
President Street-he made no reply-I asked him for his address-he
gave it me--it was correct; 12, Rolestone Road, St. Luke's-that is
about 300 or 400 yards from President Street-the height of the wall
between the gardens is 7 or 8 feet-it is an old wall.
AMELIA ANN TENNANT. I am the wife of John Tennant, of 4, Rahere
Street-on 9th April, about 11 at night, I was in my bedroom-I heard a
noise, like a lot of men on the wall separating our place from President
Street--they seemed to be going in different directions one of them
came in our direction the noise seemed to cease then, and I heard foot-
steps coming up the yard, and try the yard door-it was bolted-I was
frightened, and sent for the police, and the last witness came-no one
could pass through the house to the garden unless the door was unbolted;
but it was bolted.
Ann KENNEDY. I live at 4, Rahere Street-on this night I went for
the police when they came I unbolted the back door, to let them into
the garden.
MRS. RICH (Re-examined). I do not occupy the whole house, only the
kitchen-I rent the house-the jemmy was found in the front room,
which is used as an office by the Charity Organisation Society—there was
money in that room-nothing was taken-I heard nothing in the house
till the policeman woke me.
