 2015.  JOHN GREEN was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of August , 24 lbs. of coffee, value 1l. 10s., the goods of John Ferris.
   JOHN BISHOP. I am an officer. On the 10th of August I was in the West India-docks- there was a waggon at the back of No. 5 warehouse; I received information, and went to it; I saw the prisoner drive it off just before I came up to it - I desired him to stop the horses, and asked if he was the carman; he said he was - I said I was come to examine it; he then said he was not the carman, but the carman was gone down the dock: I got into the waggon, and found in it a bag, containing 24 lbs. of coffee, concealed under the tilt - the prisoner then said he had picked it up, seeing it run out of a cask; I asked how he came by the bag; he said he had worn it as an apron - I was about to go up the dock with the waggon and horses, when the carman came, met me, and took charge of it; I asked if he knew the prisoner; he said No; I then took him up to the entrance, and reported it to Mr. Foy - he had this coffee weighed; the cask of coffee was taken out and reweighed, and the quantity in the bag just corresponded with that missed from the cask.
   JOHN FOY. I am superintendent of Police at the West-India-docks. On the 10th of August the prisoner was brought to me by Bishop; I examined the casks in the waggon, and one had been plundered - I had it weighed, and it wanted 24 lbs. of the original weight; this found in the bag is the same quantity and quality - I examined the carman and the prisoner, as to whether they knew one another; they said they did not, but it was proved they had loaded the waggon together - the carman went off after they had been to the office.
   JOHN FERRIS. I am the carman. It was my waggon, and I am responsible for it.
   GEORGE JONES. On the 10th of August I was at the West India-docks to receive some tierces - I saw this waggon drawn into a nook in the west wing; the waggoner went up for his pass, and the prisoner was left in care of the waggon.
   JOHN ROGERS. I am foreman in the docks. The property loaded in the waggon was what has been stated.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Seven Years.
 2016.  WILLIAM HUGHES was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of July , 1 pair of boots, value 3s., the goods of John Andrew Belsham.
 JOHN HAYWOOD. I live with  John Andrew Belsham, a shoemaker, in Clare-market. On the 21st of July the prisoner came, and said he wanted a pair of shoes - I told him to take a seat; I went to call Sheppard; as I came back I saw him take a pair of half-boots, and put them under his coat - Mr. Sheppard came up; the prisoner showed him a bit of string, and said he wanted a pair of shoes of that length; he said he had not any; he went out - I told what I had seen; I was sent after him - I found him one hundred yards off - I told him to come back; he did so, and was given in charge.
   WARD SHEPPARD. This witness called me, and the prisoner wanted a pair of shoes, which I had not got - he went out; the lad gave me the information; I sent him after him, and he brought him back - these are the shoes.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Confined Six Weeks.
 2017.  JAMES JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of August , 8 yards of Valentia, value 40s., the goods of John Bedward.
   THOMAS FARRANT(Police-constable C 42.) On the afternoon of the 10th of August I saw the prisoner in company with another; I went up, and followed them to Vine-street; the prisoner came back, stepped into the prosecutor's shop, and almost touched me as he came out; he stepped in again, and said, "Very well, Sir, I will tell him;" I thought I might be wrong in my suspicion - I looked into the shop, and there was no one there - I called to Stone, my brother officer, who took him with this valentia.
   WILLIAM NOAKES. I am in the employ of Mr. John Bedward, a woollen-draper. I did not know of this being taken till the officer brought the prisoner back; I know this is my master's.
 GUILTY. Aged 19. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Six Months.
 2018.  ANN KEEN was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of July , 6 spoons, value 25s., and 1 pair of sugartongs, value 5s., the goods of Francis Hart.
   MARY ANN HART. I am the wife of Francis Hart- we lodge on a first floor in Claremont-place; the prisoner occupied the kitchen, and so did I, for cooking and other purposes. I lost my silver spoons out of the kitchen - I taxed her with them; she denied it - she was a servant out of place.
   REBECCA WARREN. On the 16th or 17th July the prisoner came and asked my permission to leave a small box in my place - she left it there; I gave it to the prosecutor's mother.
   WILLIAM ALDRIDGE. The prisoner, on the 17th of July, pawned these sugar-tongs with me.
   JOHN NESBITT(Police-serjeant E 6). The prisoner was given to me on the 22nd of July; I gave her in charge of one of our men - she was taken before the Magistrate next day; I met Mrs. Hart's mother, and this property was given up to me.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. Between six and seven o'clock in the morning I got out of bed, and found the six spoons in my room, the duplicates, and 5s. 6d., and a half-pint bottle with some rum in it - I told the witness, and I said the owner should have them in time, but I should keep the money till she had it.
     GUILTY. Aged 45. - Transported for Seven Years.
