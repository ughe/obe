PEARCE  , Frederick John (23, painter), pleaded guilty of break ing and entering the dwelling-house of John Wm. Wilson , with intent to steal therein.
 Mr. Hart prosecuted.
 Prisoner had been known to the police for a considerable time as the associate of thieves, and had three previous convictions. Sen  tence, Six months' hard labour. WALLER  , William (59, coster), pleaded guilty of stealing from a waggonette one overcoat, the goods of John Heathfield . Ten previous convictions were proved.
 Sentence, Nine months' hard labour.
