I should have returned him the change, but I should have taken the legal interest, and not more—that is not my usual way of doing business—I may charge more, but I considered the captain a safe man—I have never given any other money to persons in names that I knew were not theirs—I have never lent a gentleman money, and taken his account in a name that I knew was not his—I might not find it out.
   COURT . Q.  Do you mean to say that you have not done so? A.  I have not—I have never lent money for the sake of usury.
