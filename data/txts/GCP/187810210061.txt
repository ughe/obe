881. FRANCIS LAFFER (28), Obtaining four springs, &c., by false
pretences, and WILLIAM COOK (42), Feloniously receiving the same.
MR. F. H. LEWIs conducted the Prosecution, MR. PURCELL appeared for
Laffar, and MR Futton for Cook
John LAMBERT. I am a coachmaker at 66, Great Queen Street - I have
been carrying on business with Messrs. Pfeil and Co. by means of a page
book--they are ironmongers-when goods were required we sent a man with
the book produced; he was to get what he wanted and have them entered in
the book-we received an invoice for them perhaps quarterly or monthly-
Laffar had been in my employment four or five years or longer--he was
aware of our custom with reference to the pass-book-he frequently had access
to it on the 6th September I received an invoice for some articles that are
not entered in that book, and found they were not for me I went to the
prosecutors, and took the invoice back to them and said it was not for me
they had not got the book; it was taken away by the man who fetched the
goods--I find no entry in this book of any of the goods mentioned in the
invoice--I find an entry of goods there which I never ordered, four cart
arms and three bundles of spring steel, nor did I give Laffer or any other
person authority to order them-on receiving the invoice I searched for the
pass-book, and it was not there-two or three days afterwards, when before
the Magistrate, I got back the book-it was left at Laffer's residence, I
believe.
'Cross-examined by MR. PURCELL We sent the book 'blank to Messrs.
Pfeil with a verbal order for the goods wanted; they enter the goods and
send them at the same time-Laffer may have been eight years in my service
-the way of dealing with the book is well known to everybody in the
establishment—the book is left about and sent by various men to Messrs.
Pfeil we have 30 or 40 men.
JOHN RENNIE. I ann porter to Messrs. Pfeil and Co., of Broad Street
on 6th September I was at work very early in the morning-I recollect
someone calling, I do not know who, and presenting this book--he had a
squint-he said he came from Lambert's-he produced the book and ordered
goods of me-I knew the custom; if the book came we were to allow
them to have anything they asked for-I delivered all these goods--there was
& van standing at the door--this was between 8.30 and 9 a.m.-the
carman was there, and the man who came to ask for the goods.
