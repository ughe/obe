OSCAR CLAUSBEON. I live at 2, Montague Place, Russell Square-the
prisoner lodged with me from 14th till 29th December, when he disappeared,
owing me £4 8s. 10d.-he left his wife and child-his wife paid something
for a subsequent period.
Cross-examined. Your wife did not say she had left you ample money
to pay the bill—she told me she was left without means I thought you
had run away-your wife stayed one week after you left-she paid for
the week she was there—I did not make things unpleasant for her-you
made no false representation to me.
The prisoner, in defence, produced a long written statement to the effect
that up to Saturday he had hoped to have been defended by Counsel; thal.
at the end of October he had £600 in cash and £400 with outside brokers,
and 1.0.U.'s for large amounts when he was arrested, which he called
upon Inspector Holmes to produce, and now he would not get a penny
of the thousands he had hoped to receive; that he had gambled on the
Stock Exchange and so had been unable to meet his liabilities, and had.
merely exaggerated his position with a view to obtain credit.
GUILTY.-Twelve Months' Hard Labour.
