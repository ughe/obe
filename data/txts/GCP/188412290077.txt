I attended at your office to get your signature to some transfers,

10 per cent. dividend on my shares up to the time you were in the bank
--we paid 10,0001. for the goodwill of the old Company, of which 5,0001.

Re-examined. The nominal value of each paid-up share was 108.-we
have not made any call owing to our losses; we have had to call up the
108.-it was 28. 6d. at a time before that, then 58., then 78-three calls
have been made since November, 1883, of half-a-crown each, bringing it
to 108.-Pryor paid 251. out of the 3001.; he has never paid a shilling
since.
