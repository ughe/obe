(Defence.)
Mr. Kingsbury said that it had been his intention to call the defendant
as a witness, but since this prosecution had been begun this family,
though they were not sufficiently afflicted before, had lost their eighth
child. Mr. Chisholm had two grown-up konsone an officer in the Mary
and the other an officer in the Army; but to-day he had only the naval
lad left. His son who was in the Army was out in South Africa and
taking part in the suppression of the Ferreira raid when he was attacked
with illness and died. In these circumstances he had not the heart to
call Mr. Chisholm.
Mr. Justice Grantham said that po reflection would be made on the
non-calling of Mr. Chisholm. They all sympathised with him very much
indeed.
