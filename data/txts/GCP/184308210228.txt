2563. JOHN WESTON and JOHN BROWN were indicted for stealing,
on the 19th of Aug., 1 purse, value 1d.; and 4 half-crowns and 1 sixpence;
the property of Stuart M'Garry, from the person of Maria M'Garry.
MR. PRENDERGAST conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM GLADWIN (police-constable R 122.) I was at Woolwich on
Saturday night, the 19th of Aug., at a quarter or twenty minutes before ten
o'clock. I saw the prisoners coming in a direction from Mr. Smith's shop,
about twenty or thirty yards from it, and knowing one of them I took them
to the station-I found two half-crowns, three shillings, and twopence on
Weston, and two half-crowns, five shillings, and threepence on Brown-they
both said that they were hawkers, that they had no home, and had come from
Tunbridge Wells races.
JAMES PARRY (police-sergeant R 8.) The prisoners were brought to the
station, and this money was delivered to me by Gladwin-the half-crown that
is recognised by the prosecutor was found on Weston.
MARIA M'GARRY. I am the wife of Stuart M'Garry. On Saturday night,
the 19th of Aug., I received seven half-crowns from my husband--I paid my
rent with some of it-I then went to the butcher's to buy something- I went
to pay for it, put my hand in my pocket, and my purse was gone-I had had
at that time four half-crowns and one sixpence in my purse-the rest I had
