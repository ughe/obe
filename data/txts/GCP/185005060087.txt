THOMAS GARRATT (policeman, Š 392). I took the prisoner into custody,
about a quarter before six o'clock that morning-when I first came in, he was
standing behind the bar-after I had taken him to the station, Mr. Shepherd
and I went into Brill-yard, between six and seven, and searched there I
found the street-door of an unoccupied house was open-I went down to the
cellar, and found in the area this purse, which could have been slipped through
the grating over the area. I went back to the Brill, and Mr. Shepherd pointed
out the marked money to me-he gave me the purse and its contents; I took
it to the station, and delivered it to the inspector-I received it back from
him--this is it.
