If this is Mr. Goddard's way it is the best thing for me, for I shall be in possession of a public-house in 12 months"—he was taken to the station, charged, and dis charged on 9th January—he said at the station that Mr. Goddard knew that he had the clock, and that it was for Spiers and Pond, and he told me on one of the remands that it was no good for me to go to Spiers and Pond as the clock was never intended for them.
