FANNY UPHAM (continued). I received a letter of which this is a copy.
(This was a request to forward the interest on the sum advanced.) Shortly
after I sent a post-office order in a letter to Mr. Brashier-I think it was
the 17th July-in July I was at Brighton--while there I received another
letter about this matter-I cannot find that letter-this is & copy of it
(looking at the letter-bool). (This letter, which Mr. Read stated to be in
Heath's writing, pressed for payment of the notes.) I think the letter I re-
ceived was signed by John Heath-in consequence of receiving that letter
I sent this post-office order for 21. 108. to Mr. Brashier ; that was for two
months' interest on the 251. bill—I afterwards sent this post-office order of
September 6th for 178., I think to Heath; it is payable to Brashier-on
the 8th October I sent this order for 21. 108., payable to Brashier-that
was the next instalment on the 251. billon 31st October I sent this one
for 178. 6d. to John Heath, and on December 15th this one for 258.,
half of the 21. 108., also to Heath ; and on the 15th January, 1867, this
one for 258., the balance of the interest, also to Heath-there was one
other in February ; I think that was for 178.--I made that payable to
Brashier personally at my own house at Hampstead I received this letter
of October 10th, after sending one of these orders. (This was an
acknowledgment of the order for 21. 108., and called attention to the bill for
81. 108. falling due on the 27th.) The 81. 10s. bill was a two months bill
-I paid 178. interest for that every two months-all the money orders I
have spoken of are in respect of the two bills for 251. and 81. 108.- I did
not borrow any money from Brashier or Heath after 20th July-I received
this letter. This letter, stated by Mr. Read to be in Heath's writing, was

which this was an answer-I think I received this letter about August or
