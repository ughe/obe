I received instructions from Mears in the last week in September—he

he could get no account how the affairs of the partnership were
on—I drafted a deed of dissolution of partnership, and had it eng—
but before that, on October 1st, I saw Lester as to the terms of the dis.
solution, and it was settled that he was to pay Mears 250, and takeover
