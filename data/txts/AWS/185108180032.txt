Cross-ezamined. Q. Could you hear what passed A. Yes; I was

in liquor.
MR. BODKIN. Q. Which room were you in ? A. The room underneath,
just at the bottom of the stairs-I heard the fall very distinctly : it shook
the whole place-the place was very quiet, and if a blow had been struck
which did not cause a fall, I should have been sure to have heard it.
