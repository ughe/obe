 2203. WILLIAM JOHNSON  , alias    Bailey, was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of August , 13 loaves of bread, value 5 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Jo seph Jackson ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. THOMAS MEADOWS. I am journeyman to Joseph Jackson, a baker, in Tooley-street. On the 18th of August I left my basket and bread at the corner of William-street, Hampstead-road—I went to Mornington-place to serve a customer—I returned in six or eight minutes, and missed twelve 2lb. loaves, and one 4lb. loaf—I went up William-street, and found the prisoner's basket standing at the door of a doctor's shop, and found it con tained my bread—I stopped there about two minutes—the prisoner was coming down the street with a man holding his collar, with the 4lb. loaf under his arm—I could swear to the bread—it was my make. PHILIP SMITH. I saw the prisoner take twelve 2lb. loaves, and one 4lb. loaf out of the prosecutor's basket, put them into a basket, and take it with him—I followed him till he put it down—he took one out, and I took him. JOHN ROUSE( police-constable E  24.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 13.— Transported for Seven Years—Convict Ship.
 2204. EMMA SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of Au gust , 1 dram-glass, value 10 d  ., the goods of James Etherington . JAMES HUTCHINGS. I am servant to James Etherington, who keeps the King's Arms' public-house, Tothill-street, Westminster. About half-past eight o'clock in the morning of the 25th of August, the prisoner came to our house—she called for a quartern of rum, which I gave her—she drank it, and took the glass off with her—I went after her about one hundred and fifty yards—I took her, and she had the glass under her left arm—she said she had not got it—it belongs to James Etherington.
   Prisoner.  I was very tipsy. Witness.  She was. GUILTY  . Aged 23.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Seven Days.
