Mr. FLOOD. Q. How long after you said “Good night” to Collins
were you knocked down ? A. Not above two minutes-I can't say
whether he was in sight when I was knocked down—I did not see
him, my back was to him-he went straight ahead up the Bridge Road
in a line with me and the deceased.

Hammersmith--on Saturday night, the 23rd November, at twelve o'clock,
I was near the Bridge Road; I saw three men come from the direction of
the Swan--they were Collins, Dear, and the deceased—I knew him, but I
did not know his name—they went against Mr. Squib's corner, and stood
there as the clock was striking twelve-they were talking together, seem-
