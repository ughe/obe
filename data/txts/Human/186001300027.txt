MARY ANN KING. I am assistant to Mr. Churchman, a stationer in Broadway, Hammersmith—on Saturday-evening, 21st January, Cadogan came into the shop and asked for half a quire of note paper—it came to 2 d .—he gave me a 2 s . piece—I gave him 1 s . 10 d . change, and he left—I put the 2 s . piece in the till—there was no other 2 s . piece there—some time after Robinson came in the shop, and I gave him that 2 s . piece.
