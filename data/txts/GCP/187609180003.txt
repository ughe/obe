4 dead fish of Moss Benjamin and others, their masters.
Mr. F. H. LEWIS conducted the Prosecution; MR. STRAIGHT appeared for
Hopkins, and Mr. WARNER SLBIGH for Stroud.

with Kenniston, another officer, watching the prosecutors' premises-about
1.15 I saw Hopkins standing at Mr. Benjamin's shop door---Stroud went up
and spoke to him--they both went into the warehouse for about two or three
minutes, when Hopkins returned to the door alone, he looked up and
down the street, then turned round sideways and Stroud passed him, and
came out with a salmon in his right hand; he was carrying it by the head,
openly exposed-Hopkins looked after him as he went up the street, and
then looked up and down-Stroud crossed the street, bought a mat basket
