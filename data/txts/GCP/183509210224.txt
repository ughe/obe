came from our third journey, we went to Mr. Trevett's stable, which i
very nearly a quarter of a mile out of the road, and they followed us to the
stable, huzzaing, and saying they would give the old cobbler a turn as they
passed by--they said they would melt his wax before they had done with
him-that was said so that any person who was standing on the ground
might hear it-it was hallooed out from the top of their omnibus-they were
hallooing out,“We will melt the old cobbler's wax before we have done with
him"--that was in Market-street, where our stables are we went there
to take the horses home--they had no reason to go, for their hond
stand in Holborn I went home to tea in Star-street, and when I came

corner of our yard, till we went out--when I got on the omnibus,
