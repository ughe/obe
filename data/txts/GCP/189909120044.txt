of them-I knew Jewe.l before, but had not had any words with him-
I saw his face, and saw him fire on the day before this, Sunday, the 13th
-he lifted a revolver at me.
Cross-examined by Jewell. I have been charged with assault once; it
was for hitting a lad with & belt-I cut his head open-. I got two
months-a witness named Underwood was called on my behalf; I do not
