116. MARY ANN TURNER was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
October, 1 work-box, value 58., the goods of Christiana Barnard.
CHRISTIANA BARNARD. I am single, and live in Nelson-court, Fashion
street. I had known the prisoner, by working with her for about six weeks,
she lodged with me only three nights—it is a house of my own hiring, and
she came to lodge there-on the 20th of Oetober she had to dress a doll, and
asked me to give her the key of the house, which I did, and I told her
to return it- she did not-I went, and missed the property.
John GAREY. I am a furniture broker. On the 20th of October the
prisoner came to me to sell a work-box and a glass- I asked if they were
her own she said they were-she sold them in the name of Jones, No. 4,
Fashion-street- I took down her address.
CHRISTIANA BARNARD re-examined. These are my property, and part of
what I lost- I worked in a room with the prisoner at making shirts.
Prisoner. I have not taken all the property.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Three Months.
