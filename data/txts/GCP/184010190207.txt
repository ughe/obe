2679. JAMES EMERY was indicted for feloniously receiving, on the
15th of September, 4 decanters, value 12. 78. 8d., the goods of Apsley
Pellatt; well knowing them to have been stolen ; against the Statute, &c.
Mr. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution. 20
Robert King. I am in the employ of Mr. Apsley Pellets us a stopperer.
These decanters (looking at them I stoppered-thesa quintet ones I marked

marked twenty-seven quart decanters, from N6s. 1 to 27-the numbers on
