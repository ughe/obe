of Charles Knight, at Rotherhithe, and stealing 1 pump box, 1 flanch, and
1 swivel, value 21. 38., his goods.
CHARLES KNIGHT. I am a plumber, at Rotherhithe-street. On 26th
Feb., about one o'clock, I left my shop-I returned in ten minutes and
found the window broken and a brass box, a patent box, and a swivel gone-
they were mine—it is my dwelling-house, in Rotherhithe parish.
THOMAS GREENSHIELD. On 26th Feb. I saw the prisoner with some
brass under his apron-he gave the box of a pump to another person, who
has been convicted, about three yards from the shop-I said I would go
and tell-Carter afterwards sent me into a public-house-I saw the prisoner
- he asked what I wanted - I went to tell Carter, and he got out at the
back door.
