1774. ELLEN WHITE, stealing 1 ring value 41. 48. the goods of
Emmeretta Whitley.
EMMA GEORGENA WHITLEY. I live with my mother, Emmeretta Whitley,
who is a widow-we lodged at Gloucester-place, Portland-square, where the
prisoner was servant, and moved from there on 11th September to Park-
place Regent's-park, on which day my mother left town-on 6th Sept. I
missed her ring from her finger-I had seen it there two days before.
JOSEPH PLATT. I live with my uncle, a pawnbroker, in Upper York-
street, Marylebone-abou six weeks ago the prisoner and another girl came
to our shop-one of them, I do not think it was this one, offered a diamond
ring in pledge-I asked her whose it was she said she had it from a private
friend in the country, and afterwards she said she found it--I said I must
detain it, and I sent it round by the police
SARAH AUBTIN. I live at 59, Gloucester-place-Mrs. Whitley and her
daughter lodged with me-I recollect the ring being lost, and searched the
house for it with the prisoner-th prisoner was told that it was lost and was
desired to look everywhere for it-her sister has also been charged with this.
WILLIAM IsoN (policeman, D 81.) On 19th September I went to 59,
Gloucester-place, and spoke to the prisoner about the ring-she said she
knew nothing of it-I asked her if she knew any one in Cireus-street; she
said she did not, she had not got a soul in London-I asked what her name
was; she said 44 White"-] said, 44 I understand your name is Connolly"
she said, 44 It is not; it is White"- told her she must consider herself in
custody, and I took her to the station.
JAMEs CLARK (policeman, D 268). I went to Cireus-street, and found
Connolly there, whom I took on this charge-she took her child, who is
between five and six, with her to the station, and the prisoner was produced
her-as soon as the child saw the prisoner, she said, 44 Here is aunt Ellen
-Connolly said to the prisoner, 40 Don't you say anything.'
MARY ANN BURDETT. I live at 27, Cireus-street. Sarah Connolly has

have heard Connolly speak of her in her presence as her sister.
