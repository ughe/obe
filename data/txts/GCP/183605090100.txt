1230. DENNIS MÓDONALD was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of
March, 13 yards of lawn, value 21. 9s. ; the goods of Elisabeth Kempton
and another.
John Burton. I am shopman to Elizabeth Kempton and another,
of Oxford-street, linen-drapers. In the month of March I missed a piece
of lawn, numbered 328, from their counter-I have since seen it in pos.
session of an officer-it was not sold, from its not being marked with
our private mark, which was not put on it, in consequence of its not being
there when the goods were marked.
Cross-examined by MR. Paillips. Q. How do you know this from
any other? A. It has our name on it-the seven pieces that we had were
all that were marked-we have the others now-the prisoner's wife was
not taken up for stealing this.
MATTHEW Peak (police-constable G 198.) On Sanday, the 27th of
March, I apprehended the prisoner in Rose and Crown-court, Long-alley
-I took him to the station-house, and told him I was going to search his
place-he said, “If you are going, take the keys of my boxes, or else I
dare say you will break them open"-he gave me three keys- I went to
the first door in Christopher-square, Long-alley-I applied the keys to
the boxes, and found the piece of lawn produced a person named Dell
pointed out the room--the prisoner said it was his property, and he had
brought it from Jersey.
