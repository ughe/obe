2978. JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of Octo-
ber, 1 handkerchief, value 38. 6d., the goods of Wilson Walker.
JANE ENTWISTLE. I am in the service of Wilson Walker, a hosier, in
High-street, Marylebone. On the 4th of October, the prisoner came into
the shop and asked for some needles-I gave him some to look at-there
was a blue handkerchief hanging up-he asked to look at it-I gave it
him-he said he would call the next day, which he did, and said he had
come for the bandkerchief-I put it on the counter-he took it to the
door-he brought back a blue cotton one, and took away a silk one--be
said he would call for the blue one in a short time, and I was to put it
in the name of Smith-he was afterwards brought to the shop, and I
charged him with having taken the silk bandkerchief-he said he had not
taken it- this is the handkerchief he took.
SPENCER LEE. I live with Mr. Walker, a pawnbroker, in High-street
This handkerchief was pawned at our shop by the prisoner.
GEORGE ROGERS (police-constable D 71.) I took the prisoner-be
said, so help him G-, he knew nothing about it-I found the duplicate
of the handkerchief on him.
GUILTY. Aged 33.---Confined Two Months.
