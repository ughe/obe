King's Cross-I worked at a fringer's, but have left to better myself-on
Sunday, July 6th, I was crossing Argyle Place, King's Cross, between

the Wellington public-house-they took the horses out, put them in
the stable, and pulled the cart into a yard with open wooden gates which

in the middle of the road in English-I erossed the road to speak to a
boy-Michaelo said to me, Sling your f-g hook, I know you
I said. 44 Perhaps you do "-I know him by seeing him-Gerrali same out

wards-the gates were shut-a little while after I saw Michaelo go up

Tory said he did not want to fight-gomeone said, One has got a knife
-ne of the English chaps pulled off a belt-when the Italians saw that
