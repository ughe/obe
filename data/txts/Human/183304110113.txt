 873.  JAMES BAILEY was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of March , 1 chaise, value 20l., and 1 set of harness, value 2l., the goods of James Hunter.
   JAMES HUNTER. On the 13th of March the prisoner came to my shop, and hired a chaise in the name of Mr. Bradfield, for about three hours; he took it away, and I saw no more of him till the 16th - Mr. Bradfield was not a customer of mine; I found the chaise at Putney, on the 15th of March - it was a black gig; I let him have it, believing it was for Mr. Bradfield - he said it was for Mr. Bradfield, of Ropemaker-street; I believed the story to be true, and I parted with it for three hours only - he had a set of harness also.
   CHARLES WINSLEY. I am stable-keeper at the White Lion, at Putney; the prisoner and another man came there on the 13th of March, in a chaise - the other man drove it, and said they wished to put it up there for the night; the other man left the same evening, but the prisoner remained till the 15th - he offered the chaise to me for sale, and to several other persons, to two in particular; and one gentleman had the horse out and tried it - when the prisoner left, he told me to sell it if I could get the price for it, which was 13l.; I asked him if the other person, whom he called his master, should come for the horse and chaise, whether I should let him have it - he said Yes, and asked if I should know him; I said I did not know that I should, for I scarcely caught sight of him - he observed, no one would come for it but the right owner, and I was to let him have it; the prosecutor came down the same night and owned it, and I brought it up to the office the next morning.
   WILLIAM ATFIELD. I am an officer of Worship-street. I took the prisoner on the 15th of March - he told me the horse and chaise were at the White Lion, at Putney, and he had had 14s. or 15s. on it.
   CHARLES BRADFIELD. I live in Ropemaker-street; I never desired the prisoner to get the chaise for me, nor gave him any authority to get it - he borrowed my horse, as he said, to go to Clapham; he went and got the chaise of the prosecutor - I never authorized him to sell my horse.
 Prisoner. Q. You lent the horse to Macklin? A. They were both together.
     GUILTY. Aged 39. - Transported for Seven Years.
 874  JOHN POUNSFORD was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of April , 4 gowns, value 16s.; 1 pelisse, value 2s.; 2 frocks, value 3s.; 1 table-cloth, value 6s.; 1 curtain and valance, value 1s., and 1 scarf, value 5s., the goods of James Knowles.
   SOPHIA KNOWLES. I am the wife of James Knowles, a law-stationer; we live in Old Boswell-court, Carey-street. On the 10th of April I was in my front room, and heard a step on the stairs; I looked out, and saw the prisoner going down the first pair of stairs, with a bundle under his arm - I directly ran to the front window, and saw the prisoner go out of the house and turn a corner; I and my daughter ran down and pursued him; I ran a short distance, and then returned - my daughter went on; he was soon after brought back by Kelly, with the bundle, which contained all these articles; the scarf had been taken from a line in the attic, and the other things were taken from a box, which had been locked, but which I found wide open, and the room door also, which had been locked; the street door is always open, on account of business.
