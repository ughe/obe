 2383. THOMAS RILEY was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of October , 2 tureens, value 3 s  .; 1 tureen-cover, value 6 d  .; 1 ladle, value 6 d  .; 3 dishes, value 4 s  .; and 1 salad-bowl, value 2 s  .; the goods of George Owen Ramsey . ELIZA OKELEY. I am servant to George Owen Ramsey, of No. 20, Brunswick-square. On the 20th of October the prisoner came from the dust-hole—I never saw him there before—I saw a bag on his back—this is my master's property. THOMAS CULLUM( police-constable E  140.) On this afternoon, at half past five o'clock, I was looking towards 20, Brunswick-square, and saw the prisoner, who is a sweep, come up the area, with a bag on his shoulder—he was going towards Great Coram-street—I stopped him—he dropped the bag before I stopped him—he is the man. JAMES BARNETT( police-constable G  53.) I took up the bag at the corner of Great Coram-street—I saw the prisoner drop it—it contained all this property.
   Prisoner,  I was out of employ. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months.
 2384. HENRY DEATH was indicted for embezzlement. HENRY PARKER. I am a baker. The prisoner lived with me as journeyman for three months—it was his business to receive money—if Ann Pearce has paid him 7 s . 6 d . on the 26th of February, it was his bu siness to account to me for it—he has not done so—he left me without notice on the 15th of March. ANN PEARCE. I paid the prisoner 7 s . 6 ¼ d . for his master, on the 26th of February—I have his receipt for it in my hand.
   Prisoner.  In going across the fields I met a man who asked me the way, I said I would go with him and show him—he asked me to have a drop of ale, which I did, and then I dropped asleep—and when I awoke, I had lost the money, and did not like to go back. GUILTY  . Aged 17.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor. — Confined One Month.
 2385. WILLIAM YOUNG was indicted , for embezzlement. JOHN DAVID STRANGWARD. I am a baker. The prisoner was my journeyman for three weeks or a month—on Friday last he went out three times with bread—it was his duty to bring me the money he received from customers—if Balls paid him 5 s . 10 1/2 d ., part of 8 s . 3 1/2 d ., he has not given it to me—he never made his appearance with bread or money after be went out the last time—I authorised him to pay 2 s . 5 d . for me at the Court Requests. CHARLES RAYNER BALLS. On the 13th of October I paid the prisons 8 s . 3 1/2 d . for his master.
