I recollect this row  —Punch was trying to make his escapeout of the door, bleeding very much; and Hagen had hold of the hair of hishead, beating him with his hand—the female rushed out with the poker, threwhim back on the ground, and while on the ground she beat him on the headwith the poker as violently as she could—my feelings were excited, and Icalled out, "Oh, you b—h, you bitter b—h, you want to murder the man! you murdering b—h, you do!"—she then went in, and shut the door, andcommenced eating her dinner—I went to the window, and said I would comeup against her; and she held up a potato, and asked me to have a piece.

JOHN WILSON. I am house-surgeon at Guy's Hospital.
