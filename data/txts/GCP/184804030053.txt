WILLIAM PEARCE (policeman, H 154.) The prisoner asked me at the
station if the knife was found in bis head or underneath the ear-I said I did
not know-she said she was very sorry, and said, if it had been under the
ear, she supposed it would have done for him quickly-I bad told her where
the wound was--she said she had known him four or five months, that he
had had connection with her, and that after a short time she told him she was
in the family way, and after that he proved quite different to her, and said
she must go and find another father for it, for be would not-she said he had
been out of work five or six weeks, and she had supported him a great portion
of that time, and that night, going to the lodging, she had only 183., and
she paid that for the bed- the next morning they * * *, that she had not a
farthing in her pocket, that he turned round at her with a sort of sneer, that
she did not know what to do, and therefore she inflicted the wound; that her
intention was to have done for him, as he was so unfeeling to ber that be
ill-used her ; that he had very often jeered her, and d-d, and he had said
