JAMES CHRISTOPHER Evans. I am one of the Thames-police Surveyors.
I know Mr. Lewis, the dock-master, of the Regent's Canal Company
got information from him that induced me and Fogg to go, on the 15th of
January, to the Regent's Canal Basin-about seven o'clock I concealed my
self with Fogg in a little box-there is a jetty there, and barges lying
under it-in about twenty minutes I saw a man go from the jetty to a barge
-in a few minutes the same man came from a barge to the jetty-I heard
him hauling something like a rope; and he went across the jetty he then
ran towards the gate--we ran out of the box towards him, and he threw the
rupe from his shoulder-I ran to the gate, and caught his foot, but he
kicked it out of my handI ogot ver, and pursued him to White Horse-
street, Stepney-I did not lose sight of him when I got there he ran into
a public-house-I was gaining on him-I ran in, ran past the tap-
room door to the bar, and was just asking the landlord where that man was,
When he ran out of the tap-room into the street-I ran, and took him
said, "I have got you at last"-he said, "Well, I won't run away"
