181. MARY ELTHON was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of No-
vember, 4 shirts, value 21. ; 7 pairs of stockings, value 38. 6d. ; 4 hand.
kerchiefs, value 4s.; and 2 cravats, value 2s. ; the goods of Edward Lam-
bert, Esq., her master.
EDWARD LAMBERT, Esq. The prisoner's sister was my laundress,
On Wednesday, the 24th of November, the prisoner came to supply
her place-I gave her on that or the following day, two shirts to get
washed, and some stockings and handkerchiefs she brought one shirt
back, and I gave her another on Saturday-I have ultimately missed four
shirts, and the other things stated-these articles are mine--they are
marked exactly as mine are.
THOMAS VORLEY. I am shopman to a pawnbroker. I produce some
shirts which were pledged by the prisoner on the 20th of November.
MR. LAMBERT Te-examined. The prisoner came to me on the 24th-
I never saw her before, but her sister had pawned half-a-dozen shirts pre-
vious--I attributed this crime to my servant, but I discovered that the
prisoner was the person who pawned them all-she lived with her sister
-she could have taken her sister's key and gone to my chambers.
WALTER Crow. I am a pawnbroker. I have one shirt pawned by the
prisoner on the 27th of November.
MR. LAMBERT. This shirt is mine.
Prisoner. I pawned this one to get some soap and other things—I know
nothing of the other articles.
GUILTY. Aged 28.-Confined One Year.
Before Mr. Justice Coltman.
