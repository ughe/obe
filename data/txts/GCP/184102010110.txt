731. WILLIAM HIDE was again indicted for stealing, on the 14th of
January, 110 yards of rope, value 31., the goods of John Balls.
John BALLS. I know this rope to be mine--it was stowed away in the
place about a month before it was stolen-I missed it on the 15th, on
examining the place, and saw it at the office on the 19th.
John Hill. I am in Mr. Balls's employ. On the Friday night I heard
of this rope being missed-I had seen it about ten weeks before I knew
it again when I saw it at the police office on the Tuesday following.
GODFREY LANGBELL. My evidence on the former trial is correct-
know nothing more.
William DRAKEFORD. I am foreman to Mr. Alcock, a builder. On
the Monday evening I went to the prisoner to know where he had bought
the rope-he said of Davis, in Hampstead-road--the prisoner brought the
rope to Mr. Alcock's premises about ten o'clock in the day, which is within
about a quarter of a mile of the institution.
WILLIAM CUMMING. I am a policeman. I found the rope at Drake-
ford's. I pointed it out to the prisoner, and asked if it was the rope be
had sold to Mr. Alcock-he said he did not sell it to Drakeford, but he had
paid him for it.
NOT GUILTY.
First Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
