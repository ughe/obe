Q. How many times has your conduct been questioned in courtsd
justice, for gross usury, extortion, and fraud ? A. Now I mean to say.
upon my oath, that such things may have been said, and counsel will tako
liberty to say such things, and most untruly sometimes, but nothing of
that kind ever occurred-I never was before a Magistrate on any charge,
not even a watch-house charge- I have never been questioned in any case
in courts of justice.
Q. Do you discount bills ? A. No-I do not make loans to people.
and then supply them with snuff-boxes-only in a way of business, like
other tradesmen-I never was a money-lender in my life--my name is
Hudson, of Oxford-street, a tobacconist-I take the liberty of saying,
that I am notorious for not being a money-lender.
JOHN COLE. I am a chemist and druggist, and live at No. 101, High
Holborn. I know Bray's omnibuses- I have observed other omnibuses
near them when they have been going along the road-I suppose a thousand
times—the others were driving continually before and behind them, and
trying to prevent passengers, alarming ladies, and driving so close behind
it as to make it dangerous for females to get in- I have never seen Bray's
do so to the other omnibuses.
RICHARD PRIESTLY. I live in High Holborn, and am a bookseller. I
have seen Mr. Bray's omnibuses, and other omnibuses with them, one
behind and one before, quite close-I have seen that frequently-I do not
know the names of the other omnibus-one was the "Emperor."
COURT. Q. Did it appear, from what you saw of the two omnibuses,
one before and one behind, that there was fair competition or unfair! 4.
Unfair; I think that means were made use of to prevent Mr. Bray carrying
on his business fairly.
