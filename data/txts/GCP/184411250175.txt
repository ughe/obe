155. LYDIA SCOTFORD was indicted for stealing 1 pair of stock-
ings, value 6d., the property of Maria Dent: 2 brushes, 23.; 2 pairs of
gloves, 18. 4d. ; 9 lbs. weight of soap, 5s. ; 11 sheets of sand paper, 5d.;
5 yards of linen sheeting, 2s. ; 1 towel, 6d.; 5 yards of ribbon, 1s.; 1
pair of gloves, 28. ; and 2 printed books, ls.; the goods of George
Scholey, her master.
MARIA DENT. I am needlewoman to Mr. George Scholey. The pri-
soner was his housemaid- I received a bundle from my mistress at the
kitchen door--the prisoner was then gone up stairs—I believe my mistress
got the bundle out of the carriage-I examined the bundle-it contained a
bed-quilt, a round towel, some pieces of soap, and other things-they were
my master's, Mr. George Scholey.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. What do you know this
counterpane by? 1. Two small holes in it-it is rather old-there is no
particular shape about these holes-I can identify this round towel-she
said she was very sorry.
COURT. Q. Was she charged with stealing these things ? A. Yes,
and she said she was very sorry, she could not tell what made her do it.
GUILTY. Aged 40.-Confined Three Months.
