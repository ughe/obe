MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did not you, at the police-office, insist upon the
hat, and cloak, and screen being put upon the man? 2. I did not--they
were put on him.
Court. Q. Did that increase or diminish your opinion that he was the
same man? 2. I was convinced he was, when I saw him dressed in the
same way as the man was who addressed me at the Hummums-I first re-
marked that his hat was bent behind when he walked arm in arm with me
-I am taller than him, and I noticed it; and I saw the same bend in the
hat when I saw him in St. James'-square-I cannot say that I made that
remark before the magistrate I might have said it.
MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Was what you said read over to you? A. Yes, I
signed it-I cannot call to mind whether I mentioned about the bend in
the hat.
