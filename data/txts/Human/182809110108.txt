   THOMAS AUSTIN. I am a fireman. I saw three persons running in Brunswick-square on the 5th of September, and Baker was pursuing them - he was quite exhausted; he did not call to me, but I pursued them about twenty yards, and when they got to the back of the Foundling I stopped Cox: I did not see Hilder stopped - when I took Cox he said he knew nothing of that person (meaning Hilder); I asked him what made him run- he said he heard the alarm, and he was running too; he said, "Don't go to hold me, I will go any where with you - I saw a card thrown over the rails of the Foundling by one of those who were running - I rather think it was Cox; I asked him what made him run, and throw the card over the railings - he said he knew nothing about it; I saw Bird pick it up - there were five rings on it; I rather expect it was Cox threw it.
 Cross-examined by MR. CHURCHILL. Q. Did you stop him immediately after it was thrown? A. No - I followed him just round the corner; I lost sight of the card for that time, but it was not picked up till I returned.
 Cross-examined by MR. CHAMBERS. Q. You were walking along the square and saw some persons running? A. Not more than three I believe; I ran, and Cox said he ran because he saw others run.
   JOHN BIRD. I live at No. 4, Artichoke-court, Cannon-street - I am a porter. I was coming across Brunswick-square on the 5th of September - I saw the two prisoners running, and Baker pursuing, and just before they got to the gate I saw Cox throw something over into the square; I think they were not more than two yards apart - I got over, and picked up this card, which I gave to Baker in going to the watch-house.
 Cross-examined by MR. CHURCHILL. Q. Were they running abreast? A. Yes - there were a good many people running after them, but none before them at the time they passed me.
 Cross-examined by MR. CHAMBERS. Q. Did you go in pursuit of them? A. No: I got over and took up the card - I did not lose sight of the card, till I gave it to Baker - there are trees and grass in the square, and a quickset hedge; the card was just on the other side of the hedge - I took up the first card I saw - there was no other that I saw.
   HENRY GRAMMER. I am a constable of the Foundling hospital estate. I searched the prisoners at the watch-house- I found on Cox 1l. 6s., and on Hilder 8s. 6d. and two thimbles; this is the card I received from Baker - it has been in my possession from the 5th of September.
   WILLIAM BAKER. This is the card I received from Bird - these rings which are on it are a part of what were taken from my uncle's.
   CHARLES JONES. I live at No. 2, Turnstile, at Mr. Oxley's. On the 5th of September I saw these cards in our shop, which is in the way from Mr. Newcomb's to Brunswick-square - they were about a yard within the shop; I gave them to Baker - there are ten rings on them.
   JOHN LIMBRICK. I have had the care of these two cards of rings ever since, and this piece of glass, which fits the window exactly.
 MR. CHURCHILL to  WILLIAM BAKER. Q. How is your window secured from being broken, is there no latticework outside? A. No - it might be broken by an elbow, if a person ran against it; I have lived there three years and never had a window broken accidentally.
   JOHN PRESTAGE. I was coming across Brunswick-square and saw Hilder in a fighting attitude, striking Baker; I went up and secured him - as we came to the watch-house he begged and prayed of me to let him go; as we came along, Bird was getting over the railings with the rings - Hilder turned round as he saw the rings put into Baker's hand, and said as the gentleman had got his property, I might as well let him go - we then went to the watch-house.
 COX's Defence. I am entirely innocent; I was running at the time the mob was, and was taken.
 HILDER'S Defence. I was coming from my own house in Princess-street, Whitechapel to Monmouth-street; coming across Brunswick-square, as I went into the square, the prosecutor came to me, and said "You have been to my shop and stole some rings:" I began to shove him, another man came up and took hold of me - I said "Let me go, I know nothing of the property stolen.
 COX -    GUILTY- DEATH. Aged 20.
 HILDER -    GUILTY- DEATH. Aged 25.
