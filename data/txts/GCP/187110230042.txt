HENRY HANOOOK. I am Vice-President of the Royal College of Surgeons
-I received a subpæna to attend the trial at Westminster; not at Kingston
-I was present two days—I did not receive anything for my expenses until

day before the investigation at Bow Street-I think that was 22nd August
-I received it from Dr. Tanner-it is not true that I attended three days,
nor that on 24th July I had received anything—there was no agreement
about the 251.--that is my usual charge.
Cross-examined. I think I was examined the second day; there was a

day, and gave evidence ; the verdict was given on the third day-after I had
given my evidence, I turned round to Serjeant Parry-I was sitting under
him, and he gave me leave to go-I had no personal communication with
Mr. Tanner on the subject -I think I said to Serjeant Parry "Now I may
go, I suppose ?" and he told me I might go-Mr. Tanner may not have
been aware of my absence- I received the cheque for twenty-five guineas
from Dr. Tanner, who called me into the case, where a medical man iden-
tifies himself with a case, and communicates with other medical gentlemen,

us in not to pay the money out of their own pocket, but to obtain the fees
- I looked to Dr. Tanner, and he did pay me a cheque before these in-
