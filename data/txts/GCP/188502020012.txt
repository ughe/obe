Before Mr. Recorder.
249. EDWARD JOHN MOODIE (35), was indicted for a common
assault on Alfred Wilkinson. Other County, for using violence to him and
intimidating him, in order to compel him not to work at his trade. Other
counts, for watching and besetting the house where he was employed, in
order to intimidate him and others.
MR. WILDEY WRIGHT Prosecuted; MR. HIOPWOOD Q.C., with Mr. Besley
Defended.
