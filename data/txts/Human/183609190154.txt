ROBERT HINE( police-constable B  140.) I was on duty at half-past nine o'clock on the night of the 6th of September, in Vincent-square—I heard a noise at No. 44—I went there, and observed the prisoner on his hands and knees behind the house—the garden gate was locked—I asked what he did there—he replied, to ease himself—he came over gate—I took him to the station, and, with the sergeant, went over the premises—we found a quantity of lead taken from the porch, and we found this lead there—it seemed to fit exactly—there was a knife found on the top of the porch where the lead had been cut from, and two pieces of iron hoops.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  Was it not dark? A.  It was rather dark—I could not have been mistaken in the prisoner's person, he was so near to me—I could see him on his hands and knees—I came up and looked through the paling, and saw him—I then went to the gate, and found it was locked—I called to him—the lead was about a yard from where I saw him. PHILIP PALMER. I have charge of this property under my father's executors—they are Mr. Abraham Harrison Dry and Mr. Stafford Dry.
   Cross-examined. Q.  The house was empty? A.  Yes.
   Prisoner.  My clothes were down—I was taken very bad—I got over to ease myself.
   Guilty.

 2184. CATHERINE WICKHART was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of September , 1 pewter pot, value 3 s  ., the goods of John Grinham . FREDERICK HART. I am servant to Mr. John Grinham, who keeps the Fox and French Horn public-house, Clerkenwell-green. I stopped the prisoner on the 19th of September, at half-past eleven o'clock, near my master's, and found under her shawl this quart pot, which is my master's—she had been in the house that evening.
   Prisoner.  I had been drinking there all the evening and tossing, and on the Saturday I had been treating every body in the house. Witness.  On the Saturday she was there, and brought some people in with her—she was tossing—she was sober when she came in on Monday—she got drunk on Saturday. ROBERT GOSWICK( police-constable G  205.) Hart gave the pot to me, and I took the prisoner.
   Prisoner.  It was standing on the stool, and I went to ask a woman to have some beer, supposing there was some beer or ale in the pot I had been drinking from, and I took this pot inside, but not to steal it. FREDERICK HART re-examined.  She had been to other public-houses on Monday as well as ours—she came tipsy on Monday evening—she had got two yards from the door when I took her. NOT GUILTY.
