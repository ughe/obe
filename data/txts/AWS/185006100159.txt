1201. THOMAS BRYANT, feloniously receiving 1 spirit-pump, value
148.; the goods of John Hammond, well knowing it to have been stolen.
Mr. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN HAMMOND. I keep the Star and Garter, Woolwich. A man named
Brown was in the habit of using my house-I had a copper pump in my
stable, worth 14s. or 158.-1 had not seen it for some time when Gladwin
came, and in consequence of what he said I found it was not there-Brown
was apprehended by my direction, and I went on I7th May with him and
Gladwin to the prisoner's shop-Gladwin asked Bryant if he had brought
any copper or pump-he said he had brought some copper-Gladwin asked
how much-he said, 2lbs. or 3lbs."-he produced this, and said, 40 That is
it' (produced)-that has nothing to do with my pump-this metal is much
thinner-my pump weighed 6lbs. or 7lbs., and had a sort of cylinder, or
basin, at the top.
Cross-examined by Mr. PARNELL. Q. What is Brown A. He is a
porter-the prisoner's book was produced-there was an entry corresponding
with this copper, but the date did not correspond-he pointed out an entry
referring to copper on the 13th-the weight to that was I think 3lbs.-there
Was no entry of copper on the 16th.
GBORgs Brown (a prisoner.) On 16th May I took a copper pump of
Mr. Hammond's, about three yards long, and broke it into three pieces-each
piece remained a portion of a pump-] took it to Mrs. Ford--she refused to
buy it, and I took it to the prisoner's, who is a marine-store dealer-I asked
whether he woald buy it-he said, Yes"-he weighed it, and said it came
to 15d.-I do not know what it weighed-h did not ask me any questions at
all-I was half tipsy-be went out of his shed to his house, got the money,
paid me, and I left-we had to go into the street to (get to his house, leaving
the copper in the shed.
Cross-examined. Q. What are you d. A labouring man--I was
employed that day to wash two carriages-I am not in constant employment
-I live in High-street, Woolwich-] was applied to to be a witness since
have been bere-I was not told it would be better for me-I hope I shall get
off-this is the first time I have been charged with anything-) was not
acquainted with Bryant-I have never been in Maidstone Gaol-the copper
produced is not part of what I sold the prisoner.
MARY FORD. I keep a marine-store shop, in Union-street, Woolwich.
On 16th May, Brown brought a big round piece of copper, like a spout--he
was tipsy, and I refused to have anything to do with it-I saw him go to
Bryant's shop, and come out again with him-h then had no parcel-they
went to Bryant's dwelling-house, and then Brown went away-he had nothing
with him then.
Cross-examined. Q. How long have you been a marine-store dealer?
A. Between five and six years-] live next door but one to Bryant-I am
on good terms with him- have lived in the same house twenty-seven
years- never kept a brothel- kept a lodging-house-the parties lodged
there a night, and away again- give 5d. for old copper.
