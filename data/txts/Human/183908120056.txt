 2227. MARY LAMBERT was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of June , 3 bottles, value 9 d  . 10 wine-glasses, value 13 s  . 1 sheet, value 3 s  . 6 d  . 1 spoon, value 6 d  . 1 printed book, value 1 s  . 1 brush, value 3 d  . and 1/2lb. weight of cake, value 1 s  . the goods of James Palmer , her master. JOHN PALMER. I keep the Queen Victoria public-house, in Skinner-street, Clerkenwell. I employed the prisoner as a char-woman, and missed some property—I went to her room, and there found this box, this brush, the water-bottles and glasses, and other things, which are mine.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Are you sure about these glasses? A.  I had such, and I lost such as these—I can swear to this book—I lost such things as the others—the whole may be worth 1 l .—I do not know that the prisoner was in distress—I found some money where she lived, and she had some money owing to her—she is a widow, and has one child. WILLIAM PENNY. I am a police-inspector. I took the prisoner—these things were found in her room, in Buckston-street, Clerkenwell.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Was she in distress? A.  No; there was pro perty in the room—she had no shift on, but her dress was comfortable for a working woman.
 (William Samuel Stevens, oil-merchant; John Colden, Bridgewater-gar  dens; Ann Lutter, a widow; and William Wix, gold watch-case-maker; gave the prisoner a good character.) GUILTY.—Aged 25.
