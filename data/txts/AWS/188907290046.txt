MARINUS ZIEGLAND. I am in the employment of William Oekhurst, a
horse dealer-I went in the S.S. Denmark from London to New York in
charge of some of his horses, sailing on 16th May-one night I slept
the horses, and next night another lad did-I was provided with a rug
by my master when Isailed; I slept on it-about three days before I got
to New York I missed the rug-I had last seen it in my cabin-the boy
used the rug as well as I-when we slept at night near the horses we slept
on the hay, and covered ourselves with the rug-I complained that the
rug could not be found-I did not see it again till this morning-this
is my rug-I have had it two or three years, I thinkI know it is mine
by the fat and dirty marks and hay seeds in it, and it is broken at the
corners, and there are holes in the corners like this-the stripes are the
same colour-we have many rugs at home.
Cro8s-eramined. I have had this rug three years or longer, and used it

cloth-I used it for myself sometimes-it would get these hay and straw
marks in ordinary use-we often put holes in the corner.
Re-eramined. When there is a draft we make holes in the corners, and
fasten up a rug to keep the horses out of the draft.
