lane. On Tuesday, the 30th of August, I received this note by the hand
of the prisoner Crispin-he said Mr. Adams had given it to him-(read
-"Dear Sir, In consequence of the misconduct of Messrs Everet's
man in calling and making such a disturbance in the street, I shall decide
opening your shop; therefore I send you the key"-Crispin said he had
let the shop to Adams; that he had taken it in the first instance for his
self, but he could not get goods as he thought he should ; and having been
at some expense, and the rent coming shortly due, he had let it to Adans
at 30s. a week; he had paid him one week's rent, and there was another
due-he said he had no reference at all with Adams, and he should be
very glad to find him, so as to get his rent-he did not offer to help me to
find him.
Cross-examined. Q. Did he not say he should be very happy to assist
