HARRY SMITH. I am manager of the Abbey Arms, Barking Road-
about 10.50 p.m. on Saturday, April 1st, the barmaid, Daisy Towers,
brought me a counterfeit shilling-I bit it and bent it-she then pointed
the prisoner to me as the man who had given it to her, and I said to him.
“What do you mean by handing this over the bar ?"--he said, “What!
is the matter with it?” and I said, “You must know it is bad "-he

the next night, when my attention was called to him again I went out and

EDWARD CHAPMAN. At 10.50 p.m. on April 1st I was in the public
bar of the Abbey Arms when I saw the prisoner, whom I know-he called
for two glasses of ale from the barmaid, which he got-he put a shilling
on the counter, which she picked up and took to Mr. Smith-he said to
her, “Which one gave it to you?"-she pointed to the prisoner, who then
put his hand in his pocket and brought out 2d. to pay for the two glasses
of ale-I was in the bar the next evening when I saw him arrested.
