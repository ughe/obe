   Witness for Carr's Defence. WILLIAM COST. "—I gave him 1 s ., and he was with me till 12.20—I did not see him again till the following Thursday—the Albert Arms is opposite the back entrance to Marlborough Street Po lice Court—two and a half miles from where this assault took place—it would take any one over half an hour to walk—I have known Carr nine years as a respectable working man—I never heard anything Against his character, and if the shops had not been so busy he would have had a great many witnesses here to speak to his character.
