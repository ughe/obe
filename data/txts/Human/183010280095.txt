   JOHN LIMBRICK. I took the prisoner, and have the watch and handkerchief.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence I met the prosecutor at the corner of Hatton-garden, and took him to this house - when we got to the room, he said he had but 1s.; I was then going to put on my shawl and bonnet to leave him - he then said he would give me his watch; when I was gone to sleep he wanted to take it from me, and I would not let him, as he gave it me. GUILTY of stealing only. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years.
 2067.  MARIA BOOTH was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of September , 2 spoons, value 9s.; part of one gown, value 6d.; 1 pair of pockets, value 6d.; 5 half-crowns; 1 shilling, and 1 sixpence, the property of Charles Culf.
 MARY CULF. I live in Brown's-court, and am the wife of  Charles Culf. On the 29th of September I was in Seymour-place, and met the prisoner, who had been a lodger of mine, but had left me about ten weeks - she said nothing to me, but ran and snatched my basket out of my hand; I was very much alarmed, and could not speak to her - she struck me, and I should have been down, but I caught a rail which supported me; she pulled me along very violently, broke the string of my pocket, and the pockets fell down - a young man said, "Have you lost your pockets?" I felt and said, "They are gone;" the prisoner had them in her hand - I went and said, "They are my pockets;" she said, "You d-d old fool, I know they are, but I will not give them to you" - when we got to the watch-house, the gentleman desired her to give them to me; there were a few little things in the basket, but in the pockets were the spoons and my money - she took up the pockets, and put them under her arm; she was in my debt.
   HENRY CARTWRIGHT. I was just coming home from work, and saw the prosecutrix and the prisoner - I stood a few minutes, and saw the prisoner pick up the pockets and try to conceal them to her breast; I went and asked the prosecutrix if she had lost her pockets - she said Yes; a Policeman came up and took her.
 Prisoner. Q. Were you there when the Policeman came up? A. Yes - you had the pockets, and he took you; he is not here.
 Prisoner's Defence. The prosecutrix has some things of mine - I met her in the street; there was another woman with me, and she said, "Did you ask Mrs. Culf to give up your things?" I said No - she said, "There can be no harm in asking her;" I pulled her gown, and said, "Do you mean to give up my things?" she said, "No, you good for nothing wretch, I will not" - she struck me twice; the Policeman came up while I had one hand on the basket; I let it go - she was walking off, and said, "I will give you a night's lodging in the watch-house" - I then felt something against my feet; I did not know what it was - I stooped and picked up the pair of pockets; I gave them into the Policeman's hand - he said, "There feels something like spoons in them;" whether she heard that or not I cannot tell, but she turned back, and said, "Give me the pockets" - he said, "I shall not deliver them to any one till I get to the watch-house;" when there she said I had insulted her in the street, and mentioned five half-crowns and two spoons, as being in the pockets - the inspector asked her if these were every thing in them; she said Yes, and was going away perfectly satisfied, and made no charge against me - he said, "You had better let the woman go about her business;" I went down, and then she made a second statement, said I seized her in a violent manner, and by some means untied her pocket.
 MARY CULF. I have some goods of her's, but her husband begged me to keep them and the receipts, and he would come and settle with me.
     NOT GUILTY.
 2068.  THOMAS JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of October , 2 sheets, value 5s.; 1 counterpane, value 9s., and 1 shawl, value 1s., the goods of George Culpitt.
 JOSEPH COOPER. I am a constable. On the 26th of October, I was standing behind a brick clump, and saw the prisoner come by with a bundle on his head - I asked what that was, and I found it was wet linen; he said he was going to a brick clump for a pair of trousers - I took him; he was about three quarters of a mile from the prosecutor's - it was about a quarter before eight o'clock in the evening.
   ANN CULPITT. I am the wife of George Culpitt - we live in Copenhagen-street. This property was hanging to dry at my house; here are two sheets, a counterpane, and a shawl - I hung them out at three o'clock in the afternoon, and missed them at seven; I never saw the prisoner.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was crossing by the back of the White Conduit-house - I picked up this bundle, and said I would take it to a Policeman, but I could not see one till I came to Liverpool-road, when I saw two Policemen going behind the clump with two girls; I went to get a pair of trousers, which had been given me by a lady, but they were not quite dry - I told the Policeman I found these things.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Transported for Seven Years.
