Mr. Child's stable on 18th Dec., about from 5 o'clock to half pastI saw
the bull there-when I first saw it, it was in Victoria Street-I afterwards
saw it in Mr. Child's stable, the long place; it was standing quiet-I am
accustomed to cattle-I did not see Mr. Child thereI did not see any thing
done to the bull-an elderly gentleman said the bull should not stop there-
I saw four or five men there; I cannot swear to seeing Mr. Child-I have
not been examined before-the bull was not doing any mischief at the time I
saw it there.
