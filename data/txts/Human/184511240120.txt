This cotton was brought to me one night while I was unloading coals in Worship-street, by Burton—he asked me half-a-crown for it—I told him I had got no money with me—he said it would suit me for my wife, and I should have it for 1 s . 9 d .—I gave him 4 1/2 d . which was all the money I had, and he was to call on me for the rest.

 139. CHARLES BUNN was again  indicted for stealing 3 shawls, value 21. 10 s  ., the goods of Henrietta Barker ; and 1 snuff box, 5 s  ., the goods of Matthew Charles Barker ; and MARY CRAWLEY for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing them to have been stolen.
   BUNX  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined One Year . MATTHEW CHARLES BARKER. I live in New-street, Bishopsgate. On the 7th and 8th of Oct. I employed Lawrence to move my goods—I saw them deposited in the van, and Bunn was assisting him—I missed the snuff-box and three shawls now produced. ERASMUS LAWRENCE. I am a carman. I employed Bunn to assist inmoving these goods—a short time afterwards he showed me a silver snuff-box—he said he found it in Barbican—he offered it me for 1 s .—I refused it, and returned it. THOMAS HENRY BROOK. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a black crape shawl, which was pawned by Baker, on the 20th of Oct. SAMUEL HUGOETT. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a shawl, which I took in of Baker, for 5 s ., on the 15th of Oct. ELIZABETH WILSON. I have a shawl—my grand-daughter went to her aunt's to dinner, and her aunt sent me the shawl to look at. ELIZABETH EMMA WILSON. I went to my aunt's, Mrs. Chandler, to dinner, and she gave me this shawl to bring to my grandmother. MARTHA CHANDLER. I gave her this shawl—my husband got it out of pawn, at Mr. Fowler's, in Old-street. WILLIAM CHANDLER. I bought the duplicate of this shawl of Crawley, for 1 s ., 6 d .—I cannot remember the day—I got the shawl out. HANNAH BAKER. I pawned the other two shawls for Crawley—I used to go of all her errands, and do what I could.
   Crawley.  I own to receiving these things; Bunn said he bought them; I did not know they were stolen. JAMES BRANNAN( police-sergeant G  20.) I went with Eagles, and found this snuff-box—Bunn told me I was wrong about the shawls—I found this shawl, the duplicate of which was purchased by Chandler—Bunn stated he got the whole of the things from Charles Booz. CHARLES BOOZ. It is not true—I was at the moving job with him, but I know nothing of these things.
   CRAWLEY  — NOT GUILTY.
