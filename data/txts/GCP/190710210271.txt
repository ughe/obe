circulars except under the guarantee I had given to Parkinson, and

to the date of the guarantee. I have examined the books of the
Argus, and find they did no work for any of them after September,
1904, the date of the guarantee. I think Parkinson said they did,
but I think he is muddled. The copy of the guarantee set out in
Parkinson's evidence is an exact copy of the guarantee I gave. The
Argus Company, through Messrs. Lewis and Lewis, sued me on my
guarantee in the month of December, 1904, before my guarantee had
become operative. I do not remember whether I put in any defence
to it. They got judgment; I think we agreed it. I was then served
with a bankruptey notice, which was set aside 'on the ground that it
did not tally with the description in the judgment. They put in the
bankruptoy notice “Trading as Herbert Smith." That was dismissed.

letters, draft circulars, and so on. The draft circular covering several
pages is in Hawkes's handwriting. What has been sworn to be my

words. Hawkes was a very proficient circular writer, and very
good newspaper writer of large experience. I should think he is over
70 years old; he may be 80. On page 12 of this there are the words,
just declared " in my writing. The explanation of that is this that
these are evidently drato circulars and draft letters of Hawkes's, and
if he had asked me (as no doubt he did) to make a grammatical alters
