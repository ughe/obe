Gardens-I saw Ernest Olive making his way up over a piece of rising
ground leading to the bridge; he had no hat; he staggered somewhat-I
went to him; he had then reached a stone wall, which he supported
himself on-Isav that something was the matter, and that he was

where his wounds were temporarily dressed-g constable and an am-
