Before M. Recorder
669. EDWARD BLAND (36), was indicted for unlawfully converting to
his own use certain securities entrusted to him as a broker. Land!
Messrs. POLAND and MEAD conducted the Prosecution, and MR. STRAIGHT
the Defence.
A-
JAMES SWIRE. I am a chemist of 131, Briok Lane, Bethnal Green-I
have known the prisoner for about eight years--I employed him as a
stockbroker to buy and sell stock for me from time to time-I changed my
investments as the market changed-1 did not consult him about it, I
only gave him orders- early in June, 1870 I had some Egyptian stook
which I desired to change for Peruvian stock; he was to buy 2,0001.
Peruvian stock for me--I saw him personally for that purpose. I desired
him to sell some stock, after the lapse of time, I could not say exaotly
what, and buy me Perus in their place-he was to pay for them out of the
proceeds of the other stock-I afterwards received from him by post, this
contract note -I know the prisoner's handwriting ; this is signed by him.
(This was dated June 6, 1870, and stated that 1,8101. worth of Peruvian
stock, less commission, was bought by the prisoner for the witness, of Mesare.
Baker do Sturdy). He had previously bought for me some Turkish Stock
--this is the contract note for it signed by him, it is for 4002., dated May
13, 1870; he had also pnrchased for me 6001. Peruvian stock - I ordered

-I have not got the contract note for that, I don't know what has become

a few days after the purchase of the last 2,0001, I went to bis office
did not see him, I never saw bim after, I saw his brother Harry-I called
