NEW COURT.-Monday, January 10th, 1870.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant. * **
146. JOHN WILSON (34), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MR. DUNns conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE ENDELL. I am barman at the Wellington public-house, 321,
Strand-on 31st December, about 7.50, I served the prisoner with a half-
pint of ale which came to ld, he gaye me a sixpence, I broke it with my teeth
and put it on the counter, he pushed it back, and said it was no use to him;
he put down ld. and walked away-I went out and saw him walking along
the Strand, counting something in his hand; I followed him to a passage,
and gave him in custody with the pieces-he did not speak.
Prisoner. Q. Did not I say, "Are you certain I am the man " 4. No..
HENRY BOURNE. I live at 35, Exeter Street-on 31st December, I was
in the Wellington public house, and saw the prisoner, he called for a half-
pint of porter, and tendered a bad sixpence, which the barman broke with
his teeth-the prisoner then paid for the ale, and swept the pieces over the
counter, saying that they were of no use to anybody else-he drank the ale
and left-the barman and I followed him as far as Burleigh Street-he
. appeared to be sorting some money-he crossed over to Carting Lane, and
stooped as if to put something in his boot or on the ground.
THOMAS Mo DONALD. I am an assistant jeweller, of Chelsea-on 31st
December, I was in the Strand, and saw the prisoner followed by Endell, he
appeared to be counting something in his hand-I saw him taken in custody
at the corner of Carting Lane, and after he had left two or three minutes, I
examined the ground and found a sixpence, which I took back and gave to
Foulkes over the bar.
HENRY FOULKES. I am barman at the Wellington - I received a bad six-
pence from McDonald and gave it to a potman who is not here—I did not
look at it.
John ROGERS (Police Sergeant 38). The prisoner was given into my
custody by Endell, and I received these pieces of a sixpence-I afterwards
received this sixpence (produced from the barman-there were three pieces,
but I have lost one-28. 94d. in copper, and a fourpenny piece were found
on him.
