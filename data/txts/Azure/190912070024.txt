HENRY SULLIVAN (prisoner, on oath). On November 21 as me
and Foulser went over to Hackney Marshes to have a game of football
I picked up a paper parcel and looked at it. Foulser said, ' Show
me." I gave them to him, and he put it in his pocket and took no
more notice. We could not get a game. On the way back Marl-
borough met and spoke to us, and a little while after I turned round
and found Foulser being held by a police offcer. I ran away. I did
not know he was a police officer, or I should have gone straight to him.
I ran away because once coming home from football I was set about
two or three fellows who went down my pockets, and took everything
had. I told Marlborough what we had found. He had not the coins
at all. I did not see him give anything to Fchilser. I know nothing
about him throwing anything into the water.
Cross-examined. I was arrested by Sergeant Edwards. I did not
