Richard Allcock between three and four o'clock near Highgate Archway, and saw the two prisoners, and a woman who crossed the road and went into a shop—the man went up the same road and sat on a barrow; the woman came out and went up Whitshall Park, and he followed her to a stationer's shop—he dawdled till she came out, and then she went to the arch, and they went on in company to Finchley—going round Church Road she gave him something which he put in his pocket—I saw her go into about five places, while he waited outside—they walked and talked together in the intervals. RICHARD ALLCOCK. I was with Dent, and saw the prisoners walking and talking together and saw them separate. SELINA DEAN. I am a tobacconist, of 9, Woodham Terrace—I served the prisoner Woodburn with a bottle of something, price three halfpence—she gave me a half-crown, and I gave her the change. WILLIAM OVERTON. I am a grocer, of Lansdown Terrace, Highgate—on 8th July Woodburn came in for a bottle of Kopp's ale and gave me a half-crown—I compared it with another, and then went after her, and asked her if she knew she had passed a bad half-crown—she said, "I know where I took it," and gave me a good one, and I returned her the bad one.
   Cross-examined by Woodburn.  You did not leave hurriedly—I do not think by your manner that you knew it was bad. CHARLES COATES. I am manager to Mr. Badlestone, of Finchley—on 13th July the prisoner bought one pound of worsted, price twopence, and gave me this coin—I found it was bad. DAVID SINCLAIR( Policeman ). On 15th July I was on duty with another constable—I had received information, and saw the prisoners—Ford had some conversation with me, and I took a half-crown from his waistcoat pocket; we struggled, and he took a package out of his trousers pocket containing these coins, with paper between each half-crown. ELIJAH COE(108 S ). I saw the two prisoners together coming out of Mr. Badlestone's shop—they turned in my direction—I afterwards took Woodburn; she denied all knowledge of the man.
   Cross-examined by Woodburn.  Ford said that he did not know you—nothing was found on you. ALFRED DIXON( Police Sergeant ). The prisoners were charged with passing bad coin—they made no answer to the charge—Woodburn was asked her address—she said, "I have none. WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. These half-crowns are bad, and from two different moulds—the one uttered is from the same mould as some of the ten found in Ford's pocket.
   Woodburn's Defence.  I went out for a walk, and went up to Ford, and asked him the way to Highgate Wood. We walked a little way together. I gave the half-crown at the shop believing it to be genuine. I then went to the other shop and paid with another half-crown, believing it to be good. I left him and went up the road; he came after me and asked me to have something to drink. I saw no more of him till after he was arrested. He gave me four half-crowns, and three of them turned out to be bad. GUILTY  .— The JURY stated that they believed her to be under the influence: of Ford.—Judgment Respited.
