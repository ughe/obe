EXMELINE BENTLEY. I am an assistant to Mr. Whiteley in the foreign
show-room-I have charge of the fitted dressing cases- I receive them

Cross-examined by MR. WILLIAMS. I know of no record being kept of
dressing-cases coming into my stock to sell-I speak from memory

FLORENOE LATHAM. I am an assistant in the foreiga show-room-I
keep all the writing-cases--I also sell dressing-cases - I do not remember

Cross-examined by Mr. WILLIAMS. I have no record of what stock
comes--we sell a good many dressing and writing-cases--we sell cases at
the prices mentioned--we sell more of the cheaper ones.
MARGARET SEAMAN. I am an assistant in Mr. Whiteley's perfumery
department-I am the only one whose duty it is to weigh combs when
they come to the show-room from the warehouse we have a great
number of cheaper ones, from 6fd. and 74d. up to 15. 111d.---looking at
the invoice of 20th July I have no recollection of these combs at 78. 6d.
and 108. 6d. each-if they had been tortoiseshell I should have weighed
them--some are described as tail combs at 78. 6d. and 9. 60.--I received
no tail combs.
Cross-examined by Mr. Grain. I could not swear that we had not had
them if it means dozens, but it says "each"-we sell a vast quantity of
the cheaper combs.
