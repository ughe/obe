750. WILLIAM ALDERSON was indicted for stealing 71bs. weight Of
bacon, value 38. 9d., the goods Of Daniel Arthur Sykes.
DANIEL ARTHUR SYKES. I live in New-end, Hampstead-lane. On the
7th of Feb. I saw the prisoner in my shop—be took a piece of bacon off the
counter, and threw it to a person at the door—I came into the shop—he asked
for a pennyworth of tobacco—I asked what he had done with the bacon
—he denied taking it.
GUILTY. Aged 18.—Confned Three Months.
