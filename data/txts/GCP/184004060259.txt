Cross-examined. Q. You state you are a physician, do you dispense
medicines ? A. I do not at present -I formerly did I did not at this pe-
riod the prisoner was very seldom in the habit of being at my house about
her mistress-she knew the house well.
Court. Q. She knew that you did not dispense medicines probably?
A. No, I think she did not-I was formerly a general practitioner, and she
did not know that I had changed—I have made use of my diploma lately-
I have had it many years I have put "Dr. Reid" on my door now-be-
fore it was Mr. Reid-I did that on the second of April the plate was off
on the Tuesday.

MR. BALLANTINE. Q. You have told us a remarkable expression, that if
she was hanged she could not support her child on her wages, were those the
exact words she used ? A. Yes-I feel some doubt as to the precise time
at which she made use of that expression-I had not told her it was a very
serious affair, or any thing of that kind.
