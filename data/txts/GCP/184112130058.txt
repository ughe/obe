378. WILLIAM QUARTERMAN was indicted for stealing, on the
13th of December, 1 pair of trowsers, value 8s., the goods of Richard
Hassell.
CHARLES NEWMAN. I live in Nelson-street, Stepney, with my father,
Samuel Newman. About twelve o'clock, on the 13th of December, I was
in my master's, Mr. Hassell's, shop-I saw the prisoner take a pair of
trowsers from just inside the shop-door-I told Mr. Clarke.
SAMUEL CLARKE. I am shopman to Mr. Richard Hassell. Newman
told me- I ran out, and took the prisoner with these trowsers-they are
my master's.
Prisoner. They were hanging outside.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
379. JOHN MILLER was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of October,
1 coal-hole plate, value 18., the goods of Charlotte Ashton.
WILLIAM SHADWELL. I am waiter at the King William the Fourth
public-house, in King William-street, Strand, kept by Mrs. Charlotte
Ashton. About ten minutes to nine o'clock, at night, on the 1st of Octo-
ber, I missed the plate that covered the coal-cellar, and the next morn-
ing, about eight, the prisoner came, and said my mistress was fineable if
she did not have the plate down, and he asked to be employed to put one
down-my mistress asked him what it would come to-he said, " About
38. or 3s. 3d."-she employed him to put it down-this one-(looking at it)
-is the same pattern, and the same size as the one we lost, and I be-
lieve it to be the same.
JOSEPH THOMPSON (police-constable F 39.) I saw the prisoner in cus-
tody last Thursday, I asked him if he had got any plates at home-he
said "No"- I went to his lodging, and in a cupboard I found nine cellar.
plates, one of which is this that has been identified.
Prisoner. The officer came to me in the Strand, he got hold of an old
man, and said he was cobabiting with me, he said he saw that man
taking up plates, and recommending me to do the jobs, and I had never
seen the man- I told this officer there were four or five plates in the cup
board.
GUILTY. Aged 64.-Confined One Year.
(There were three other indictments against the prisoner.)
