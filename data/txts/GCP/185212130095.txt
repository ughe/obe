150. MICHAEL CARROLL, unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
Ma. Eulis conducted the Prosecution,
ALFRED JAMES BEARSLY. I am potboy at the Horse and Groom beez
shop, in the Old Kent-road. On Wednesday evening, 10th Nov., about
half past 9 o'clock, the prisoner came there for a pint of 6d. ale-1 served him
- he offered in payment a half crown-I took it, and rung it on the table-I
found it was bad-I sent for my mistress, and I went to fetch a policeman.
Prisoner. Q. Was it me or the other man gave you the half crown ? A.
You ; 1 brought it out of the parlour-I gave my mistress the half crown,
the servant never had it in her hand—I do not know who paid for the pint
of ale; I went for a policeman.
Mr. Ellis. Q. Did the prisoner come with another man? 4. Yes;
the other man stayed while the prisoner was there they were both taken.
ELIZABETH BOXALL. I am the wife of Robert Boxall, who keeps the
Horse and Groom, the last witness is in my husband's service-I received.
from him a half crown on 10th Nov.-I was called down stairs, and he gave it
me-I took it in the parlour, and saw the prisoner and another man I told
the prisoner he could not have that half crown in his possession, without
knowing it was bad-be said he did not know I sent for a policeman, and
he took the men into custody-the prisoner said he was a cab driver, and
took it in the dark, and he had changed half a sovereign at Greenwich.
Prisoner. Q. Who gave you the half crown? 1. The last witness; he
did not give it to the servant-you did not say to me that if I would break it
in half, and give you half, you would endeavour to find the man you had it
of--you did not tell me you had saved a little money, and changed a half
sovereign at the White Hart at Greenwich.

ROBERT BURNHAM (policeman). I took charge of the prisoner and

HANNAH BARBER. My husband keeps an eating house near the Borough
Market. On 25th Nov, the prisoner came between 5 and 6 o'clock in the
afternoon for two ounces of beef, and a halfpenny worth of bread; he gave -
me a shilling in payment-I tried it, and bit a piece out of it: I called my
husband out of the parlour, and gave it him.
ROBERT HENRY BARBER. I am the husband of the last witness. I
received a counterfeit shilling from her-I saw the prisoner in the shop, I
asked him where he got it-be said at the Waterloo station-I sent for a
policeman, and gave it him.
