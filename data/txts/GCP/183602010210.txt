587. ALICE DANDY was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of January,
94 yards of printed cotton, value 88., the goods of Francis William Vant.
FRANCIS WILLIAM VANT. I am a linen-draper, at Woolwich. On the
8th of January, the prisoner came into the shop for a few patterns of print
--my young man attended to her-I went to speak to a customer, and
when I came back, I said to my young man, "That young woman, 1 am
convinced, has got something; follow her out" --she did not hear that-
she went out, and my apprentice followed her.
William RICHARDS. I am the prosecutor's apprentice--I recollect the
prisoner coming to the shop-I was not the person who served her-1 fol-
lowed her by my master's direction, to a pawnbroker's, who produced the
property to me.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Who served her? A. Jabes
Garrett-he is not here I cannot swear he did not sell her the cotton.
Thomas BURT. I am a pawnbroker, at Woolwich. The prisoner came
to my shop, and offered to pawn this print-I asked how many yards there
was of it-she said she could not tell—she said she brought it from Miss
Grief-while I was questioning her, Mr. Vant's apprentice came in, and
claimed it.
Francis WilliAM VANT re-examined. I am convinced it was not sold,
for I was standing by the prisoner when she left the shop, and she had no-
thing but a few patterns of prints.
Cross-examined. Q. If so, she had not the print? A. That was all
the young man gave her-I was not present all the time she was there,
but I saw the apprentice give her some patterns.
