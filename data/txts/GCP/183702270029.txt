John MORGAN. I am a clerk in the Post Office. I was on duty on
the morning of the 14th of January--the Fulham bag would be made up
about half-past nine or twenty minutes to ten o'clock-I that morning
received a money-letter directed to Elizabeth Foreman I have signed an
acknowledgment of having received it I should not bave done so unless I
had received it-I received it from Mr. Biller-it was entered in the Post
Office; bill and a receipt was put up with the bill to be signed by the party
to whom it was directed-bere is the Fulham bill—it is in Mr. Linwood's
handwriting--the money-letter was tied in front of the bundle, and put
into the bag-it was then tied up in the bag-when these letters are
marked off by Mr. Linwood, they are put into the Fulham box, and then
put into a parcel, tied and sealed by me--we send the receipt with it, which
is returned afterwards.

4. Yes, my name- compared the letters with the book before handing

named Langham, and he ties and seals it-I see him do it that is in the
general course of business-there is no signature of Langham's.
