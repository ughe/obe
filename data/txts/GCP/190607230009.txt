(Defence.)
MICHAEL HARNETT (prisoner, on oath). On June 22 I met
Baker, and went with him to bis mother's house at Hammer-
smith. We left there at half past nine and got to Aldgate
about 11. We went into one or two public-houses and
had drinks. I met an old shipmate named Montgomery, and
we also met two girls. When we got to this coffee stall Baker
proposed that we should have some tea; he called for five
cups. While we were at the stall prosecutor came up and
stood staring at me. I said, "Have a good look at me and
you will know me"; and I just caught him by the lappet of
