234. HANNAH SHEEN and MICHAEL SHEEN were indicted for
stealing, on the 23rd of November, 2 counterpanes, value 58.; and 2 blan-
kets, value 48., the goods of George Dawson and another, the masters of
the said Hannah Sheen.
DANIEL PAMPLET. I am a patrol of Bishopsgate. On the 23rd of No-
vember, I saw Michael Sheen go into Mr. Dawson's private door he had
nothing with him then he came out in about a quarter of an hour with a
bundle-I crossed the road, and asked what he had got-he said, " Things
to wash"-I took him to the watch-house, and found these things in the
bundle.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. I suppose they made a con-
siderable bulk? 4. Yes; they were tied in this apron.
JAMES MARCHANT. I was with Pamplet-I went to Mr. Dawson's,
where Hannah Sheen lived servant-I found some things in her box.
Cross-examined. Q. How do you know it was her box! A. Her
master said so in her presence, and she did not deny it-I then went to
Shoreditch, where Michael Sheen said his aunt lived I waited, but she did
not come.
