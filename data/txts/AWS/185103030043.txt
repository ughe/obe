722. JOHN BAILEY, embezaling 3l. 108. of Edward Wallace and
another, his masters.
Mr. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.
EDWARD WALLACE. I am in partnership with Join Doherty we are
artificial flower-makers, at Bridgewater-square The prisoner was in our
employ it was his duty to carry out goods, and receive the money for
them-on 28th Feb., I sent him to Messrs. Hitehcock, of St. Paul's
Cburchyard; he was to receive 31. 10s.-he did not come back again.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. What wages had he A. 108. 6d.
a week, and 3d. in the pound on what he sold-he went round to various
houses every morninghe has been in our service more than four years,
and has sometimes received 501. or is about 18 or 19 years old, and
was a most excellent lad, till since Christmas-I sent bim to Hitehcock's
shortly after three o'clock, and about half-past-seven his mother came to
me, and said she was very sorry John had come home, and said he had
lost all bis money-I saw him in custody the same evening, for which I
was very sorry-I was in hopes he would have made his escape.
EBENEZER HUNT. I am clerk to Messrs. Hitchcock and Co. On 28th
Feb., I paid the prisoner 3l. 108., in silver, on account of Messrs. Wallace
and Doherty he gave me this receipt (produced)-there was 21. of it in
sixpences.
