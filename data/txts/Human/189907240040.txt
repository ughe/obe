HUBERT DUCK( Detective Sergeant ). I was present at the Police-court when Cohen was charged—I heard Walter Lines Wiskar give evidence; both the prisoners cross-examined him—he is now dangerously ill, and cannot attend the Court—this is a certificate from the doctor—I saw the doctor write it—I saw Wiskar sign his deposition at the Police-station.
