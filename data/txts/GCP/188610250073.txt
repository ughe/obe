about Wednesday; my father had the clothes on—both Weiners were
there, and Palmer-Leon went up and looked at the clothes, and asked
him how they fitted him, pretending that he was a tailor, joking-they
stayed about two hours-I went out and fetched them some drink
and cigars-when I brought it father told me to go downstairs
--afterwards they all went out together-I had not seen Palmer
