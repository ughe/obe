Bird's Defence. I have served fifteen years in the Hussars, and have

Dicks Defence. I am a hawker, and am a teetotaller. This man asked
me to have a glass of ale, which I refused, knowing that I should have to
pay for a second so I stopped outside. Do you think it likely that if I
