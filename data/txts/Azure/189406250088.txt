MARY ANN CHARLOTTE FOSTER. I am the wife of William Foster, of
20, Pitt Street—I was in Mr. Fox's room one day before Christmas
and heard a very loud report, which appeared to come from the room

I heard three noises altogether—one Sunday afternoon I was asleep, and
heard Mr. Fox call down the stairs—I did not know that the prisoner
was going to leave till I saw his room empty in the morning—I had to
go downstairs for a bundle of wood at 6.30 a.m., and saw him and his
wife outside with a barrow.
