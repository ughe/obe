 335. GEORGE LARMAN. was indicted for a like offence.
   MESSRS. ELLIS and BODKIN conducted the Prosecution. SUSAN BLAKE. My husband keeps a beer-shop, in Colt-lane, Bethnal-green. On 22nd Dec. the prisoner came for half a pint of beer, about half-past two o'clock in the afternoon—he gave me a half-sovereign—I showed it to my husband—it was a bad one—he came and asked the prisoner if he was aware it was bad—he said, "No"—he then paid me with a penny. THOMAS BLAKE. My wife showed me the half-sovereign—I considered it bad—I said to the prisoner, "Did you give this half-sovereign?"—he said, "Yes"—I said, "Are you aware it is bad?"—he said, "No"—he said he took it of Mr. Burford, a man who goes about with greens and other things—I said, "Come with me to Mr. Burford's"—we went on, and in going along, I said I thought he had known it was bad, and a man who was with me went for a policeman—I asked the prisoner if he had any more about him—he put his band into his pocket, and pulled out a had sovereign—I gave him, and the sovereign and half-sovereign, to the officer. CHARLES WINTGEN( policeman, K  301). The prisoner was given to me, and this sovereign and half-sovereign. WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint. These are both counterfeit.
