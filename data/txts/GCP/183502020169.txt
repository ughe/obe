634. MARY ANN WHITE and JANE JONES was indicted for steal-
ing, on the 10th of January, at St. Paul, Deptford, Kent, 1 watch, value
31.10s.; 2 seals, value 21. ; 1 watch-key, value 105., 1 split ring, value 2d.;
and I watch-ribbon, value 4d. ; the goods of George Morgan, in his dwell-
ing-house ; and MARY MOOR was indicted for feloniously receiving the
same, well knowing them to have been stolen, against the Statute, &c.-
2nd Count. For receiving them of an evil-disposed person.
GEORGE MORGAN. I am a hair-dresser, and live at No. 6, Broom-fields,
in the parish of St. Paul, Deptford, Kent. I occupy the house entirely
myself. On the morning of the 10th of January, I met the prisoners
White and Jones, in High-street, Deptford-my brother was with me at
the time-- I took them bome to my house, and gave them some supperI
went into the shop with Jones, and while I was there my brother came into
the shop and said something to me-he wished me to give him some money
I refused-Jones and I returned to the parlour-White and my brother
were there--the women went away, and after they were gone I missed a
watch which had hung over the mantel-piece in the parlour-I gave infor-
mation to the police I have since seen it in the possession of the policeman
Collings--the prisoner Moor was never in the house, to my knowledge.
