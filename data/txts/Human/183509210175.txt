 2091. MARY ANN FINCH was indicted for a misdemeanour.
   MESSRS. SCARLETT and ELLIS conducted the Prosecution. GEORGE KING. I am a coach-driver. I took the prisoner and two others on the 25th of August from the Bricklayers' Arms to the Broad way, Deptford—the fare was 1 s . 6 d . each—the prisoner took a bag out of her bosom, and handed three sixpences to Ann Brown, who gave them to me—two out of the three were bad, the third was good—I found the two were bad by tendering them at the corn-chandler's—I am sure they are the same—I gave notice, and went with the policeman to find the prisoner and the other persons—they were nearly at Dentford Bridge—I saw them taken—they went to the station-house, and on the way, the pri soner threw herself on the pavement, near Greenwich turnpike—the offi cer took a bag out of her hand, which contained twenty-two sixpences.
   Prisoner.  I did not fall down at all. Witness.  Yes she did, at the turnpike. WILLIAM SMITH. I am a police-sergeant. I went with the last witness, and took the prisoner and two othes—the prisoner that I took, which was Brown, flung herself down—I found nothing on her but 2 s . 6 d ., which were good, and some halfpence—these are the two sixpences that the coachman offered at the corn-chandler's—this basket was found left in the coach—It contains a file and some dirt—the prisoner owned it the next day. GEORGE HARRIS. I am a policeman. I took this prisoner into cus tody—I saw something in her right hand—I said, "What have you got?"—she said money—there was 2 s . in sixpences good—In going over the brigdge she was very busy putting her hand into her bosom—she threw her self down—I seized her hand, and found in this rag twenty-two bad six pences—a good half-crown, and some halfpence, were loose in her pocket. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of counterfeit coin to the mint. These twenty-two are all bad, and these other two—they are all cast in the same mould. Prisoner's Defence.  I picked them up at the public-house door—I went to inquire about my husband, who was gone to Portsmouth—I was making the best of my way there with a woman—we went to a public-house to have a pint of beer—It poured with rain—a gentleman called my little dog out—I went to call him back, and saw this rag with the money in it—I put them among my own good money—they were all mixed toge ther—I did not know that they were bad—I then went on to Deptford, and said, as it rained, I could not go further that night. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Eighteen Months.
   Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
