 571. EMILY WATT , stealing 1 sugar-bason, 1 cream-jug, and 1 tum bler; the goods of Thomas Robert Evans . THOMAS ROBERT EVANS. I am a broker, at High-street, Woolwich. On the evening of 27th Jan., about six o'clock, I missed from inside my shop a tumbler, sugar-bason, and cream-jug—these are them—they are mine. MARY WILKINSON. I was passing and saw the prisoner at Mr. Evans's, and knowing her I watched, and saw her take something and put it under her shawl—I gave information. CHARLES MAT( policeman ). I received information, and went to the prisoner's house, and told her she was charged with stealing glass—she said she had it—I found it—it was identified. GUILTY.— Confined Six Months .

 573. WILLIAM ROBERTS was indicted for unlawfully obtaining goods by false pretences. CAROLINE RICHARDS. I am the wife of Joseph Richards, a victualler, of Woolwich. On 25th Jan., the prisoner came and asked for five pints of ale, and five pints of porter for Mr. Bull, who I knew—I asked if he had any order—he said no, Mr. Bull told him to give his name, and it would be all right—I let him have it.
   Prisoner.  I said it was Mr. Bull's foreman sent me. Witness.  I did not know he had a foreman—I said Mr. Bull usually sent an order, and you said as an excuse that he was unloading three large loads of bricks. HENRY WARRHAM. I am Mr. Richards's potman—I was present when the prisoner came the last time, and asked for a gallon of half-and-half—my mis tress told me to follow him—he went into the George and Dragon—I went in, and saw him and two or three more drinking it JAMES WILLIAM BULL. I live at Charlton—I have some labourers work ing for me—I never saw the prisoner before, and never gave him or any one any order for beer—I have no foreman. JOHN GRIFFIN( policeman ). I took the prisoner, and told him the charge—he said Mr. Bull's foreman sent him—I asked if he knew the man—he said, yes, if he were to see him. GUILTY. Aged 28.— Confined Three Months,
