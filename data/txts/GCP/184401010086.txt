489. THOMAS PRITCHARD was indicted for embezzlement.
JOSEPH SKINNER. The prisoner was my servant-I paid him 188. a week,
which was the sum Gunner, the last prisoner, had paid him I engaged him
-I told him on the Monday morning that Mr. Gunner had given the whole
affair to me, and from that time he would be my servant-he said, “Very
well," and every week he received my money.
Cross-examined by Mr. Doane. Q. Did you not find him in the service
of Mr. Gunner? A. I understood he was a journeyman-I never under
"stood he was his apprentice.
Not Guilty.

BEATRISS BRAY. I am single, and live in Denmark-street, St. George's.
On the 28th of August the prisoner came to work for me at trowsers making
at my house-she was working there on the 29th of August at seven o'clock
in the morning I went out at a quarter after eleven that morning, and camne
back at a quarter after twelve-I left her at work when I went out, and when
I came back she was gone-I missed a gown, a pair of boots, and a petticoat
from a chest in my own room- this is the gown and petticoat-I found them
in pawn two hours afterwards.

CHARLES JAMES MERCER. I am shopman to Mrs. Avila, pawnbroker,

SARAH TURNER. I am the wife of George Turner; I live in Denmark
