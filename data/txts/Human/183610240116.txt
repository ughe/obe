Yes, he did, and said if Mr. Pink did not fetch it away, he would send it to the boiler's—he said he would not pay any body till he saw Mr. Legg about the cow, that it was sent from Mr. Legg, and if it was his property, he should have it—I was bail for the prisoner's appearance—I have known him many years, he is as respectable a man as any I know.
