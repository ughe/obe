RIDLEY PORTER. I am a surgeon of the London Hospital. The deceased was brought there about half-past 2 o'clock in the morning—he died there about 10 minutes past 12 that night, from a wound on the lower part of his person, from which the bowels were protruding to the extent of four inches—there was hope of recovery, but it very seldom happens—it was a wound which might have been inflicted with a knife of this kind. ABEL SIMMS( policeman ). I had Antola in my custody—he cannot speak English at all—I do not believe he understands it—I tried to make him understand, but could not—I do not think he was shamming.
