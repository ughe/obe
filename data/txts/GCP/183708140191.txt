2014. ANN BROWN and ELIZABETH WHITE were indicted for
stealing, on the 26th of July, I sock, value id. ; 1 purse, value 8d.;
2 sovereigns, and 2 half-crowns; the goods and monies of John Drake
Darby.
Joan DRAKE DARBY. On Wednesday night, the 26th of July, I came
out of the Surrey Theatre, and went to the Alfred's Head, and met the two
prisoners-I made a proposition to have some supper, and then proposed
to go to bed with White--I laid down on the bed--the other prisoner was
in the room--this was about ten o'clock-I can swear I had 21. 53. and
more in my pocket-I put the two sovereigns into my watch fob, and then
took them out and put them into my sock, and put it under the pillow
Brown shuffled up and took it from the pillow- I was not aware that she
was coming to bed-she had laid down for about two minutes—I did not
like my company, I turned round and they both went away, and the sock
and money were gone.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Had you been drinking? 4. I
had-I had been at the Westminster election, and drank a little there
was at the Theatre before the performance began-I went in and out, and
drank--I sat in the Two Shilling Gallery-I do not know what I paid-I
forget now, I went in company with two young women, but neither of these
-no gentleman was with me- I cannot tell how much I drank- I drank
in small quantities--I was quite sober-I drank only once at the election
-it was part of a pot of half-and-half-I had a pint of half-and-half,
when I came out of the Theatre, with one of the women - I had one
glass of ale at the Alfred's Head I was in no employment at this time
I drank a little balf-and-half at supper-I did not go to sleep I was wide
awake, and saw them go out of the door-I got up, and as soon as I got
my clothes on, ran and called the policeman-I was quite sober at the
time I lost the money--they were taken the next day, and I was examined
on the 28th-I treated the other girls at the Theatre-I gave them no
money besides-I had been one day out of place there is no mark on
this purse, but every honest man knows his own purse-there was 5s. in
it-I did not see Brown take the money, but it was missing from her side
-I saw her scrambling at the pillow-I did not lay hold of her.
JOSEPH JAMES (police-constable M 67.) I received information, and
went after the prisoners, and found them-I found nothing on Brown-on
White I found two half-crowns, 3 d., and this purse.
MARY ANN CLARK. I know White. I met her the next morning-
she asked me to go and buy a frock with her she bought one-it cost
45. 6d.
