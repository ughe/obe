 2483. HANNAH MAHONEY was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of August , 1 jacket, value 5 s  ; the goods of William Crockford . WILLIAM CROCKFORD. I am apprentice, on board the British Monarch , lying in the West India Docks. I met the prisoner on the 20th of August in Ratcliffe-highway—I gave her 1 s . 6 d . to go with her—she took me to a house in Blue gate-fields—I put my jacket on the bed—when I awoke it was gone—I asked the prisoner where it was—she said she had pawned it for 2 s .—I told her to get it, and she began to abuse me—this now produced is it. THOMAS JAMES SHUTS. I am shopman to a pawnbroker. This jacket was pawned on the morning of the 20th of August, by a female.
   Prisoner . It was not me that pawned it. GEORGE SWADEN( police-constable K  92.) I took the prisoner—she said she had not pawned the jacket—this is my signature to this deposition—I cannot read it.
   COURT . Q.  Did you tell the Magistrate this, "I took the prisoner on this 'charge; she said she pawned the jacket, and he had given her no money?" A.  I did say those words, and what I said was correct—I did not think for the moment what I was saying just now. NOT GUILTY.
 2484. WILLIAM FREEMAN and JOHN WALKER were indicted for stealing, on the   20th of August , 1 shawl, value 5 s  .; the goods of Mary Jane Brown , from her person. MARY JANE BROWN. I am single, and live in Tottenham-place, Tot tenham Court-road. I am a servant, and was on my way home about two o'clock in the morning, on the 20th of August—I sat down, and went to sleep—the policeman awoke me—I saw an empty cab  standing in a gate way—I got into it, and went to sleep—I was awoke by Freeman pulling my shawl—he pulled it off my shoulder—Walker, who was with him, received the shawl, and put it under his smock-frock—I told Freeman, if he wanted sixpence for a bit of bread, I would willingly give it him, but not to distress me by taking my shawl—this is it. CORNELIUS SAVORY. I am a policeman. I saw the prosecutrix sitting on the steps of a public-house—I awoke her—I saw her again about four o'clock—she was crying, and said she had lost her shawl—(she had it on when I first saw her)—I afterwards saw the prisoners pass—I took them to the prosecutrix, and said, "Are these like the men?"—she said, "Yes, I believe these are the men"—I found this shawl on Walker.
   Freeman . I picked it up.
   Walker . We found it as we came from the lock.
   FREEMAN  — GUILTY. Aged 27.
   WALKER  — GUILTY. Aged 20. Confined One Year.
