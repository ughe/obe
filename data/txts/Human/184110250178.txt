 2782. JOHN HARDY was indicted for stealing, on the   30th of Sep tember , 1 pair of boots, value 16 s  .; and 1 pair of shoes, value 10 s  .; the goods of John Matthews , his master. JOHN MATTHEWS. I am a shoemaker, and live in Blackfriars-road. The prisoner was in my service. On the 30th of September I was out on bu siness, and when I came home I thought the prisoner walked about as if he had a pair of new boots on, but I did not like to speak to him, as he had not been in the house above twenty-two hours—my lad then told me something, and I told the prisoner that my lad thought he had a pair of our boots on—he said, yes, be had, and he meant to keep them, and he had told Dick  of it—I said, "What do you mean by that, why did you not tell me of it?"—I told him to sit down and pull them off—he pulled off one, I saw my mark on it, and sent for a policeman—he then began to shuffle about, and pulled a pair of shoes out of his pocket—he pulled another pair out of his pocket, and put them behind him.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Are you the man that comes here so often? A.  I think I have been here twice—I was robbed of thirty thousand pairs once—I never was here but once, the other case was at Horsemonger-lane—I am sorry I am here at all but while there are so many pawnbrokers about, it is no wonder I am robbed—I asked my boy if the prisoner had told him that day that he meant to take these boots, and pay for them, and he said he told him nothing of the kind—the shoes were red shoes—they were not lying on a board against which he was leaning—when he took off the boots I saw him put them in the wheels behind, and push them through, and he took a pair of boys nailed shoes out, but I could not swear to them—I did not see the shoes in his hand—I saw him put his hand into his pocket, and lift up his hand. RICHARD GYE. I am foreman to Mr. Matthews. I recollect the pri soner going to his tea about half-past six o'clock that evening—he went to a public-house at the corner of the bridge-foot, as he told me—then he came back and said he could not go there, there was no place to sit down—I sent him to Holland-street—I had noticed when he went out that his pockets were very full—I noticed that he had a pair of new boots on, and when my master came back I told him of it—he had riot asked me about taking them, or told me that he had taken them—my master came out and told him he had got a pair of his boots on—he said, "Yes, I have"—my master told him to take them off—I saw him. take a pair of red shoes out of his pocket and put them in the wheels.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you see him put the boots on? A.  No.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 29.— Confined Six Months.
