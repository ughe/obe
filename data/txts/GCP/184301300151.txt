WILLIAM TRUMPER. On the 30th of Dec. I bad five quarters of beans at
Mr. Austin's mill, for the purpose of being split--I sent five quarters more

these split beans that are here I can say nothing about, but these whole

the loss of splitting, there was a deficiency of upwards of 500lbs, in the ten
quarters-two sacks of split beans will weigh 474lbs - I will swear to those
beans that were taken from Lutman's cart.
