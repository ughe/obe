ESDWINS MILES. I am a carpenter, and live in Marlborough-street, Green wich. This gimlet and three centre-bits are mine—I saw them safe on Fri day night at No. 19, Ship and Billet-row, where I was working—I left them there locked up in a back room—I missed them on Saturday morning at half-past six o'clock, on going to work, and found the lock burst open—the pri soner had been working there a day or two previous—I missed three saws, four planes, and these things—I have never recovered any but these bits and the gimlet—I know these are all mine—I have a particular mark on one—I have been in the habit of using them daily. CHARLES PHYPERS. I am a carpenter, and live at Royal-hill, Greenwich, On the 15th of April I bought these three centre-bits and gimlet of the pri soner for 10 d .—I asked how he came by them—he said they were given to him by a gentleman, in East-lane, Greenwich, who was going abroad.
   Prisoner.  I never said so; I said I had found them in the rubbish. Wit  ness. He did not. EDWARD BUCKLEY. I am a policeman. I apprehended the prisoner—I found nothing in his possession.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I found the things in the rubbish; I did not think they were of any value, and put them into my pocket. GSUILTY. Aged 33.— Confined Two Months.

Yes—I turned round immediately on feeling the tug, and saw the handkerchief, the prisoner, and the constable—I cannot say whether the handkerchief was in my pocket or hanging out.
