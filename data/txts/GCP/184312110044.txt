353. RICHARD BURGESS was indicted for stealing 5 yards of oil-cloth,
value 5s.6d., the goods of James Vine.
JAMES Vine, salesman, Gloucester-place, Islington. About seven in the
evening of the 11th of Dec. I had some oil-cloth outside my shop-I missed
it two or three minutes after-this is it.
CHARLES RANDALL (police-constable N 172.) I stopped the prisoner in
Noel-street about a quarter past seven o'clock, on the 11th of Dec., with this
oil-cloth-he said he brought it from his father.
Prisoner. My father told me to look after a place; some lads said,
"Carry this just over the field; if you see a policeman say it is your father's."
Guilty. Aged 14.-Confined Three Months.
