467. WILLIAM DAVIS was indicted for stealing eight guineas, and a half guinea, the property of Nicholas Gotts , in the dwelling-house of Francis Morris, April the 27th.
 The prosecutor was called, but not appearing, the court ordered his recognizance to be estreated. NOT GUILTY.
 468.  WILLIAM BRANDFORD was indicted for stealing two pair of mens leather shoes, value two shillings, and 360 half-pence, the property of James Finch ; a three pint glass bottle, value six-pence, a pint of brandy, value one shilling, a tin canister, value four-pence, a pound weight of green tea, value eight shillings, a pair of half leather boots, value three shillings, and two pair of mens leather shoes, value two shillings, the property of William Kitchiner, March the 18th.
 "None of the things mentioned in the indictment
 "were found, but the bottle
 "and canister, in the lodging of the prisoner,
 "which the prosecutor could not undertake
 "to swear to, and there was no other evidence " to bring the charge home to the prisoner."
     NOT GUILTY.
 Tried by the Second Middlesex Jury before Mr. RECORDER.
 469.  WILLIAM NICHOLSON was indicted for stealing a silver watch, value forty shillings, a steel chain, value six-pence, a steel watch key, value one penny, and another steel key, value one penny, the property of William Tandy, May the 9th.
 "The prisoner was the brother in-law of
 "the prosecutor; the only evidence against
 "him was a confession that he took the watch,
 "but meant to return it, and it appeared his
 "brother had often let him wear it.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Tried by the Second Middlesex Jury before Mr. RECORDER.
 470.  FRANCES the  wife of ELIAS BARLOW was indicted for stealing three linen curtains, value six-pence, a linen sheet, value two shillings and six-pence, two copper saucepans, value two shillings, a feather bolster, value eighteen pence, a copper stewpan, value one shilling, and two flat irons, value one shilling, the property of Jacob Otto , being in a ready furnished lodging, let by the said Jacob by contract to the said Frances, against the statute, May the 5th.
   MARY OTTO sworn.
 I am the wife of  Jacob Otto; I let a ready furnished room, up three pair of stairs, to the prisoner, at half a crown a week; the things mentioned in the indictment were in the lodging; I went into the room and missed the things; I asked her what she had done with them; she said she had pawned them; I have recovered several of them.
 [  Robert Arlz, a pawnbroker, produced the curtains and sheet, which had been pawned with him by the prisoner, and they were deposed to by the prosecutor.]
 PRISONER's DEFENCE.
 I pawned them with an intent to fetch them out again, but my landlady took me to the watch-house and would not let me.
     GUILTY.
 Tried by the Second Middlesex Jury before Mr. RECORDER. [Branding. See summary.]
 471.  GEORGE GRACE was indicted for stealing a guinea the property of John Staneford, May the 10th.
 "It appeared upon the evidence that the
 "prisoner changed half a guinea with the prosecutor,
 "and had the guinea given him thro'
 "mistake among the silver."
     NOT GUILTY.
 Tried by the Second Middlesex Jury before Mr. RECORDER.
