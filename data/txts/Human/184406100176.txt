 1768. WILLIAM SMITH and THOMAS HIGGINS were indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 6 d  . the goods of Edward Murdon , from his person; to which
   HIGGINS  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Three Months . EDWARD MURDON. I am a pensioner at Greenwich—I was at Deptford fair on the 3rd of June, and lost a handkerchief out of my right-hand pocket—this is it. JOHN CARPENTER( police-constable R  84.) I was at Deptford fair—I saw Smith go behind the prosecutor, put his hand into his pocket, take out the handkerchief, and give it to Higgins, who had a short smock-frock on—he put it into his pocket—I took hold of him—he took it out of his bosom, and threw it down—another officer picked it up.
   Smith's Defence.  I went to the fair to get a job—I know nothing of it.
   SMITH  — GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Three Months .
 1769. WILLIAM GREGORY was indicted for embezzlement. MARY ANN RICHARDSON. I am the wife of William Richardson, and deal with the prosecutor—on the 4th of May I paid the prisoner 10 s . 6 d . for him. MARY ANN MARR. I deal with the prosecutor—on the 7th of May I paid the prisoner 1 l . for him. FRANCES RHODA PEARCE. I deal with the prosecutor—on the 8th of May I paid the prisoner 1 s . 9 d . for his master. JOSEPH DURBIN SNOW. I am a baker. I have not received 10 s . 6 d . from the prisoner for Mrs. Richardson—or on the 7th of May 1 l . from Mrs. Marr—I am quite certain I did not receive either sum—the prisoner was in my employ. GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined Nine Months .
