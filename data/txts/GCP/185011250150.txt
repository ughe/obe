MR. RIBTON conducted the Prosecution.

one o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner in Aldersgate-street with

purpose of stopping her-she ran back to the prosecutor, and began
pressing him to come along with her, catching hold of him-I told her to
leave off, and she said, “What, have not I a right to speak to my
husband ?"-I told the prosecutor I suspected she had been robbing him,
and asked him to see if he had lost anything, and before he could
understand, the prisoner began catcbing hold of him, and put her hand
