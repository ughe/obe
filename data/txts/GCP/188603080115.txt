MR. FILLAN submitted that there was no evidence to go to the Jury as against
Lord Hinton. Mr. HODsox contended that Alcock had merely acted as
Lord Hinton's agent. Mr. Jarvis argued that there was no evidenos of false
pretences or conspiracy as against Wright. MR. WILLIAMS having replied,
the COMMON SERJEANT directed a verdict of not guilty to be taken as to
Lord Hinton on the Counts charging an obtaining by false pretences, but
left to the Jury the false pretence Counts as against Wright and Alcock, and
the conspiracy Counts as against all three of the prisoners.
HINTONGUILTY on the conspiracy Counts. ALOOCK and WRIGHT-
GUILTY of obtaining goods by false pretences and conspiracy.

pretences as the other prisoners.
