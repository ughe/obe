watch the construction of the walls, and saw similar timber to this, forming
& casing to the concrete walls-I know that timber occasionally floats down
the river.

I went the following day, I was told that the wood had been taken away
from the walls by the police-I did not attend before the Magistrate actually
w a witness- I was here on the last trial, but was not called-Wooden iss
sawyer.
