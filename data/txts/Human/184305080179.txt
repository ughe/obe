 1670. THOMAS HOCKODAY was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of April , 11 penkives, value 16 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of James Cross . HENRY GLAISER. I am apprentice to Mr. James Cross, a stationer, at Greenwich. On Friday morning, the 21st of April, I came into the shop, and saw the prisoner there with a card of knives in his hand—he put them down, and asked how much they were each—I saw another card of knives under his coat, and asked what he had got there—he said, "Nothing"—I said "You have"—he put the knives down, and ran out of the shop—there was no one else in the shop—I called Mr. Cross, who ran after him—I fol lowed shortly after, overtook Mr. Cross, and spoke to a policeman, who ran after him—he got away in the grove—I am sure he is the person—these are the knives he had under his coat—they are Mr. Cross's. DANIEL MOYNAHAN( police-constable R  104.) I was on duty at Croom's-hill, Greenwich, on Friday, the 21st of April. In consequence of information, I pursued the prisoner—I lost sight of him in Blackheath-grove—I only saw his back. JOSEPH GREEN. I am a policeman. I apprehended the prisoner in the Broadway, Deptford, on the 21st of April, in consequence of information.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was returning home from my work, the police man stopped me, and said one of his men wanted me; the lad at first said he could not swear to me, and next day he said I was the man. GUILTY. Aged 10.— Confined Three Months .
