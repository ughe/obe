About 8 o'clock on the evening of 26th June, in consequence of what

-I went into the cabin, where I saw Waller and Walshaw-Waller was

broken, close to him; his right hand was over it as if he was endeavouring
to conceal it-I searched Walshaw, and found this full brandy bottle in
the outside pocket of his jacket-I asked him where he got it from
Walshaw said he had found it, and was about to hand it to his foreman
-Waller said nothing I retained it-Lleft them, and went on the quay
and met King, and ultimately Pinder, whom I asked to unlock the cabin
hatch of the Cochin-he hesitated at first, and then did 80-I requested
him to go into the cabin with me--he refused at first and said, "Mind, if

these 10 new meerschaum cases and pipes, tobacco pouches, and two
