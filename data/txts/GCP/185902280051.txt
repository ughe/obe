JONATHAN COOK (Policeman, P 254). The prisoner was given into my

her neck to prevent her swallowing it, but she made sueh a noise with her
throat that I was afraid I was doing her some barm, and eased my hands,
and then she must have swallowed it, for she opened her mouth and there
was nothing there I opened her hand with a great deal of force, and found
a bad half-crown in it and 8s. 24d.-I heard this florin drop from her, and
