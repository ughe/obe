 865.  DAVID STEWART was indicted for feloniously stealing, on the 1st day of June , one cotton gown, value 10 s. three yards of linen, value 4 s. one handkerchief, value 4 s. the property of Ann Lawrence.
   ANN LAWRENCE sworn.
 I am in service; I live now with Mr. Green; I have lived with him ten weeks, but I am going to leave him.
 Did you live with him as a  servant? - Yes; I took a hackney coach sometime back, but it is a long while ago; I cannot recollect how long, it is two sessions ago; the coachman addressed himself to me as a countryman, and he asked me to drink a glass of ale; he said, he knew the house, and I went in with him; and while I was in the house with him, he got acquainted with that unfortunate man at the bar; I was going to Charing-cross, to see a young woman that was going into the country.
 What time of night was it? - It was half past ten at night; I do not know who the coachman was; I was quite a stranger in London; the prisoner got sociated with the coachman, while I was in the house; we staid half an hour, or a quarter of an hour in the house; I got into the coach; the blinds were up; and the prisoner got on the box with the coachman, and they drove out of town in a strange land; then this young man and the coachman came to the door, and bid me get out, for there were some ladies that wanted to go into the coach; I said I would not get out if the King was there; I said, I hoped they would take me a little nearer the place I was going to; this young man came to me, and said his name was  John Smith, and I need not not be frightened, for he lived in the Strand; when they took me a little further, the coachman said, will you get out, if any body else comes in, you shall ride on the box; I got on, and they said it was not high my part of the town; but the coachman said they were going that way, and then they drove me out of town, another strange road; but I do not know the name of the road; I have been greatly censured in my character, and I have a right to stick up for my character as well as my property; my sister is tied up in service, or she would have appeared here; when I got out of the coach, I said I would enquire the way home; I had the bundle in my hand; the coachman said he would hold it, and he whipped it into the boot; and this young man set on the coach-box, and they went away with it; there was a black pursued the coach; I do not know him; I said, God bless you black, run after that coach, they have robbed me of my bundle.
 What was in your bundle? - The things mentioned in the indictment.
 Are you sure this is the man that was with the coachman? - I am sure of it.
 Court. Did you tell the company that were in the coach, how they had used you? - They went on; the prisoner had told me his name was  John Smith, and that he lived in Exeter-change, the second time they carried me in the coach to the other end of the town.
