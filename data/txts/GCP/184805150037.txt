1247. ALFRED BARNETT, stealing 66 chair legs, and 6 feet of tim-
ber, value 48. 10d.; the goods of William Norton, his master.
WILLIAM NORTON. I live at Cowley. I employed the prisoner to work
for me-these sixty-six chair legs, and this timber are mine--they were safe
about five o'clock on the evening of 13th May they were missed about six
-I was not at home till about seven-the prisoner was not authorised to sell
them.

make these chair legs? 4. Yes-he is a turner-he was in the habit some

last-I made an arrangement then, that he was to work for me, and I was
to pay him ls. a gross for working these chair legs up-we have not settled
for some time I was very anxious to get rid of him altogether-I let him
