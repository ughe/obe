EDWIN STORER. I am a cheesemonger, and live in High-street, White-
chapel. The prisoner was a customer of mine-he did not sell for me on
commission-he bought a great deal of butter of me, but we never knew where
it was to go-I think he bought some a few days before he was taken into
custody-we never sold him any bad butter- it was good at the price he gave,
which was 7 d., 8 d., and 94d.-it was all good-it was not mixed-he used
to buy it of us at 71d., and have a little bit of better put on the top-I asked
why he did so-he said the parties chose it so--the butter we sold him w
used to sell at 1d. and 14d. a pound more behind the counter-I sold it
him in pans, 10lbs. in each pan.
