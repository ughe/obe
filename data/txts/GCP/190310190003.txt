OLD COURT.--Monday, October 19th, 1903.
Before Mt. Recorder.
770. ARTHUR HEPBURN (28), PLEADED GUILTY to stealing, whilst
employed under the Post Office, three postal orders for 58., 1s. 6d., and
38. 6d., out of post letters, the property of the Postmaster-General. Nine
months' hard labour.—(771.) JOHN BONNEYWELL (33), to stealing,
whilst employed under the Post Office, a letter containing 50 sovereigns
and 20 half-sovereigns, the property of the Postmaster-General. Six months'
hard labour.—(772.) JAMES WILLIAM BAGNELL, to stealing, whilst
employed under the Post Office, twelve post letters, the property of
the Postmaster-General. Six months' hard labour.-(773.) HENRY
SHACKLEFORD, to feloniously marrying Ada Aldridge, his wife being
alive. Twelve months' hard labour.-1774.) JULIAN FREDERICK
WEEDON (40), to feloniously marrying Ella Harriett Biffen, his wife
being alive. Three years' penal servitude.-And (775.) GEORGE
RANDALL (26) to attempting to break and enter the warehouse of
William Henry Cheeseborough with intent to steal; also to being found
by night with housebreaking implements in his possession without lawful
excuse. Eighteen months' hard labour.
776. WILLIAM CECIL, Assaulting George Owen Dace and occa.
sioning him actual bodily harm.
MR. J. O'Connor Prosecuted, and Mr. HUTTON Defended.
During the progress of the case the prisoner said that he was guilty of
unlawfully wounding, and the Jury returned that verdict. The prisoner
agreed to pay the prosecutor £30 as compensation for loss of his eye, which
was destroyed by the butt of the prisoner's whip. Discharged on his own
Tecognisances.
