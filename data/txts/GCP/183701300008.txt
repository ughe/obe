JOHN LOVEGROVE. I am high-constable of Windsor. On Friday
morning, the 13th of January, I went to a public-house at Windsor-1
had not heard of this robbery, but I received information about the
prisoners, and went after them about half-past nine o'clock in the
morning I did not know either of them before - I found them sitting in
the public-house-Carter had a bundle of flannel in his lap, which has
nothing to do with this case I searched them both, and in Westwood's
pocket I found this telescope, but nothing else belonging to this car
found nothing on Carter-I asked Westwood, when he was before the
Magistrate, where he got the telescope-he said he had it from his brother
three months ago that his brother was a soldier, and had given it to hin
when he became a soldier.
EBENEZER WARD. I am a publican and live at Uxbridge. I know
the prisoners by sight-I saw them in my house on Tuesday, the day after the
robbery-they came in about eight or nine o'clock--there were more persons
in the house-I believe they came in by themselves, but I cannot say
did not see them do or hear them say any thing-my house is about hal
a mile from the prosecutor's--they remained there the biggest part of the
day, coming in and out-I have seen them together before in my house,
but not since.

MICHAEL POWELL. On the Monday night I saw the two prisoners in

ing up and down the street-it was between five and six o'clock-
