   COURT . Q.  Are the whole 70, 000 l . shown to be transferred to other persons? A.  The book is not completed—it is in an unfinished state—many items are only in pencil.
   MR. SARGOOD . Q.  With regard to the parties to whom they are trans ferred, does it disclose the consideration for the transfer? A.
