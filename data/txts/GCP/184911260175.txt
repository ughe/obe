1. I think I had taken refreshinent with him once or twice before; I think not

paid it before I think this time it was 1s. 2d. down and is, 4d. back,
because we came from Twickenham--I paid the fare both ways-I think I
rode in the same carriage with him down, but not up. I gave the young
ladies money to pay their passage--I have known them about eight months
I do not keep company with them I have never walked out with them in
an evening--they might have been at places where I have been, but I have
not seen them-what I did, I did by order of my superiors.

JAMES GILL HORSFORD. I am a ticket-collector at the Richmond station
-the prisoner was associated with me as collector--he and I were the only
collectors there for the last eight or nine months-each of us put down in
the excess fare-book what we receive this is the book-on the 25th Aug.
here are some returns under the thirty-five minutes past four-o'clock-train-
they are not my writing-I believe them to be in the prisoner's writing--here
is no return of 18. 4d. as excess fare by that train on the 2nd Sept. there
is no entry by the prisoner of 28. or 1s. 2d. by the ten minutes past four
train--it would be his duty to enter them if he received the money--that is
our instructions.

Q. Suppose you receive from one person 4s. 4d., from another 8d., and
from another 4d., might you not put them down in one sum, 28. 4d.2. A.
No; we put them down separately--when the line was first opened, they
might have been put down together-there has been a case where we have
been too late for the clerk, and we have paid the next morning we put down
each separate sum as far as lies in our power-there are no other money-
takers but ourselves--we receive on both sides, both from the up and down
trains, and account afterwards to the clerk-there is a good deal of hurry
sometimes we give change out of our own money-we seldom have ten or
twelve excess fares; we have two or three--it frequently has occurred that
the moment we let out passengers from one train we have to run to the
other to take them--we sometimes do not know the times of the special
trains; we do sometimes I have made mistakes.
