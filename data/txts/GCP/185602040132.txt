son in law, Mr. Meynell, had come to complain of having been charged
double fees, or that there had been an incorrect entry made-I was not pre-
sent when Malby begged them to take back the difference, but I heard of it
-it was not after I heard of that that I went and looked at the book; I
think it was previous to that it was their coming to make the complaint
that first caused the inquiry.
