625. ELLEN BARRY and MARGARET QUIOK, stealing 3 dresses,
a shawl, 10 yards of alpaca, and other goods, value 408.; the goods of Edward
Dutton.
MR. THOMPSON conducted the Prosecution.
EDWARD DUTTON. I am a butcher, and live in Well-street, Cripplegate.
In the afternoon of 30th April, I came out of one room into another, on my
first floor-I saw the prisoners there, and they had got these three dresses,
a shawl, about twenty yards of calico, and some other things each of the
prisoners had some of them in their arms—they saw me, and dropped them
down-I asked them what business they had there it was about half past
1 o'clock-my house is under repair and the painters had just gone-Quick
said that one of the painters had sent her up for half a crown-I had seen
these articles safe not ten minutes before, they were then in the further side
of the room-I never saw the prisoners before.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Was your street door left open!
4. I suppose the door was open--the house was under repair; the lock was
taken off to be repaired-Quick said that one of the painters had sent her
up for half a crown-the painters were just gone to dinner-I cannot say
which of the prisoners had any part of these articles, each of them had some
of them in their arms, and they were trying to conceal them under their
shawls--I left them in the room in custody of our workpeople while I went
to the door and got a policeman.
Quick. I had the bit of calico in my hand. Witness. I think you had
the calico and some other things, but Barry had the larger part of them.
HENRY SLATE (City policeman, 87). I was called in, and took Quick
into custody-as she was leaving the house, I saw her throw these three
keys behind the door-they are common keys, and are such as will open
