or about the 28th July last, I and Carter, another detective, received
information of a robbery from Messrs. Leet & Edwards, some brass fittings
had been lost-in consequence of information I apprehended two men,
named Eley and Floyd, who had formerly been in Leet & Edwards' employ,
and they were charged with breaking and entering the warehouse, and
stealing the property-from information I went on the 29th, to the
premises of the defendant Gale, at 69, Cow Cross, Smithfield, he carries
on the business of a dealer in second-hand machinery, and old brass and
metal—we saw Gale and had a conversation with him, he afterwards produced
the stolen property-we took him into custody, and were bound over Rs wit-

also committed for trial-I gave evidence before the Magistrate of the finding
