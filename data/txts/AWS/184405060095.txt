1461. WILLIAM MERREFIELD was indicted for stealing 1 milk-can,
value 48,, the goods of Richard Freshwater.
RICHARD FRESHWATER milkman, Kingsgate-street, Holborn. On the 25th
of April I left my can at the corner of Frederick-street, about half-past seven
o'clock, and I missed it at ten minutes to eight--this is it.
JAMES STEPHENS. I am a broker. I bought this can of the prisoner on
the 25th of April.

1462. WILLIAM HOOK was indicted for stealing 3 printed books, value
2s., the goods of Charles Couch.
ANN COUCH. I am the wife of Charles Couch. These three books are
ours-I missed them on the S0th of April,
SAMUEL SMITH. I saw the prisoner running, and Clark was running after
him-the prisoner dropped these books from under his coat.
ANDREW CLARK. I was running after the prisoner, from an intimation
that he had stolen these books.
Prisoner's Defence. A lad asked me to mind them while he went over
the way.
GUITy. Aged 7-Confined Three Months.
