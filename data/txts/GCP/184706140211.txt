it was got from the Well-house, at Sydenham, on Saturday, the 15th of May, by
the persons in the van-I saw four men in the van--they came to me as I was at
work there, between twelve and one o'clock, and said, "We have come for
this piping"- I thought my employer had sent them, and let them take it-
Conley was one of the men who came-- I cannot speak to the others,
Thomas Gore. I saw the van coming from Camberwell New-road to
Bowyer-lune--that would be the way to come from Sydenham-I am quite
sure Haines is one who was in the van saw a considerable quantity of iron
in the van-I did not take particular notice what iron it was there was a
quantity of water-pipe in it.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Did you not swear before the Magistrate
that you were quite certain Haines and Cockling were two of the men in
the cart? 4. No-this is my mark to this deposition-(the deposition being
read, stated, "I saw the two prisoners, Haines and Cockling, in the van
Cockling was driving-Haines was in the van-Haines knew me he put up
his arm to prevent my seeing him-I am quite certain Haines and Cockling
were the two men.")-I said there were four men-Haines was one I am
pretty well sure Cockling was the man who was driving-I saw the van
about four o'clock.
Me. PRENDERGAST. Q. You gave information of this ? 4. Yes, to
Dooley, whom I was driving rubbish for.
Alexander CHISHOLM. I received information of this from Mr. Plowman.
Cross-examined. Q. Who is Mr. Plowman? 4. He is foreman to Mr.
Hayward-he told me of this, and that some of our men had been pursuing
the thieves.
JOSEPH THRELFALL CARTER. This pipe is mine-I gave no authority to
any person to have it removed.
Cross-examined. Q. What is the value of it? A. 71. or 81.--there are
several lengths of pipe of a very remarkable sort-I suppose hardly any of
such a sort in the trade-if a person gaye 21. 178. for all mine, and 8cwt.in
addition, it is not anything like the value of the pipe-old iron sells
very low.
