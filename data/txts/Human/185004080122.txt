   THIRD COURT .— Saturday, April  13 th,  1850.
   PRESENT  —Mr. Ald. FINNIS  and Mr. COMMON SERJEANT .
   Before Mr. Common Serjeant and the Seventh Jury .
 815. EDMOND ANKINS , stealing 1 pair of trowsers, 1 pair of boots, and 3 shirt-studs, value 17 s  .; the goods of Joseph Ankins ; having been before convicted: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months.
 816. ELIZABETH OWEN , stealing 1 table-cloth, value 10 s.; also,  5 table-cloths, 4 sheets, 6 spoons, 4 forks, and other articles, value 15 l  . 9 s  .; the goods of William John Newington , her master: to which she pleaded GUILTY  .— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor.— Confined Four Months.
 (There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
 817. JOSEPH LEE , embezzling 20 s  .; the moneys of George Gooch , his master: to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Three Months.
 (There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
 818. SARAH SMITH , stealing 2 knives and forks, value 8 d  .; the goods of George Worrell ; having been before convicted; to which she pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Twelve Months.
 819. HARRIETT FAWCETT , stealing 2 pairs of boots and 1 petticoat, value 14 s  .; the goods of Peter Marsh Smalley , her master: to which she pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Six Months.
 820. THOMAS NEWELL and THOMAS BURTON , stealing 2 loaves, value 5 d  .; the goods of Henry Smith : to which they both pleaded GUILTY. Both aged 16.— Confined Eight Days , and once whipped.
 821. JAMES GARRATT , stealing 22 shillings and 5 sixpences; the moneys of Henry Fox , his master: to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Six Months.
 822. SAMUEL GRIEVES , CHARLES LOCKINGTON, and GOD  FREY WOONE , for a conspiracy to obtain goods and chattels of William Sampson Hodgkinson and others: to which they all pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Twelve Months.
 (Lockington received a good character.)
