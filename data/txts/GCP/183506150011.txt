one handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of William Chippendale, from his
person.

the afternoon of the 22nd of May, I came in at my gate, and my attention

my hand to my pocket, and missed my handkerchief-the prisoner was one
of them-he was quite close to me, and seemed to have something in the
breast of his coat-he ran off, and threw the handkerchief to another boy,
who gave it to me--the pot-boy followed, and brought the prisoner back-
he begged my pardon, and hoped I would forgive him.l v
