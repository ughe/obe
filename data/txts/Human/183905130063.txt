NEW COURT .— Thursday, May  16 th,  1839.
   Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1539. CORNELIUS MURPHY was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of April , 3 sovereigns, and 4 half-sovereigns, the monies of Thomas Jackson , his master. JAMES STARKEY. I live at Vincent-street, and am clerk to Mr. Thomas Jackson. The prisoner was in his employ as a carpenter's labourer—on the 22nd of April I gave him a note sealed up with an order to Mr. Jones to send me 5 l . back by him for Mr. Jackson—he did not return—I saw no more of him till he was taken. JOHN JONES. I am clerk to Mr. Jackson. The prisoner brought me the note from Mr. Starkey—I put up 5 l . in a piece of writing-paper, sealed it up, and directed it to Mr. Starkey—I told the prisoner to make haste buck with it.
   Prisoner.  I would have settled it with my master if he would have taken ten shillings a-week—I was tipsy, and whether I had it stolen or lost it I am not able to say. WILLIAM UNDERHILL. I keep the Crown and Thistle, in Great Peter-street. On the 22nd of April, between five and six o'clock in the evening, the prisoner came and had change of half-a-sovereign of me. JOHN CAMPBELL. About seven o'clock that evening I was at the Dog and Stile beer-shop—the prisoner was there—he called for a pot of ale, and paid for it with a sovereign. RICHARD SADD( police-constable B  80). I took the prisoner—he said he pad the money stolen from him, and had his pocket cut.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 40.— Confined Nine Months.
 1540. GEORGE PUCILL was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of April , 15 savoloys, value 1 s  . 3 d  .; and 3/4 lb. tripe, value 2 d  ., the goods of George Anderson , his master. GEORGE ANDERSON. I am a pork-butcher, and live in Whitechapel-road. The prisoner was in my employ a short time—I discharged him because I wanted a larger boy—he came again to assist me on Saturday the 13th of April, and as I was about discharging him he stooped to pick up something, and I saw some savoloys projecting from a hole in his smock frock—I then found fifteen more round his body and over his arms—I am confident they were mine—I gave him in charge—the officer found the tripe in his bosom. THOMAS ARNOLD( police-constable H  127.) I took the prisoner, and found the tripe and savoloys—he said he was going to take them home for his supper.
 (The prisoner received a good character, and the prosecutor promised still to employ him.) GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined Five Days, and Whipped.
