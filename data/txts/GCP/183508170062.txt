1719. WILLIAM PARSONS was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
July, 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of Daniel Donovan, from his
person.
Daniel Donovan. I live at Jubilec-place, Commercial-road. I was
"coming down High-street, Whitechapel, on the 20th of July, about eleven
o'clock at night; the officer tapped me on the shoulder, and told me some
thing-I felt, and my handkerchief was gone-I had had it safe about txo
minutes before ; it was produced to me; this is it, I know it by some holes
in it.
WILLIAM SAVAGE, (police-constable, H 50.) I was on duty, in plain
clothes; I saw the prisoner about eleven o'clock in the evening- I watched
him and another-I saw the smaller one go to the prosecutor, and draw
the handkerchief-the prisoner was close to him I went to the prose-
cutor, and asked him if he had lost his handkerchief-he said, "Yes" - We
then went into the City, and saw the prisoner and the other one looking at
it, with a girl-I took the prisoner; the other dropped the handkerchief on
the ground.
Prisoner. I was never in the county of Middlesex-I was in Aldgate
at the time-I never saw the other lad before.
GUILTY. Aged 20.* _Transported for Seven Years.
