510. FRANK FOLEY (19), Stealing in the night time in the dwelling
house of Frederick Thomas Smith a coffee-pot and other articles and 18
in money, and afterwards burglariously breaking out of the said dwelling
house.
MR. GEOHEGAN Prosecuted.
FREDERICK SMITH. I keep the Temperance Hotel, 97 and 99, South-
ampton Street, Russell Square—the prisoner was in my service two or three
months last year-on March 11th I came down at 6 a.m. and found the
front door on the latch and an old coat hanging in the hall which belonged
to no one in the house-I examined my secrétaire and missed 151, in cash
and notes, but no violence had been used-I also missed a tea and coffee
service, an opera-glass, some silver spoons, and three overcoats, two of which
I have seen in the possession of the police; one of them belongs to a gentle
man staying in the hotel.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. You left me suddenly-I did not make
anything-I induced you by subterfuge to come to the house, and then you
told me what Mrs. Smith had said to you—I have no doubt a room w
entered, where the person hidan old hat was found in the coffee-room-DO
bedrooms were entered-I did not think you a thief till you left, but I had
serious doubts about you on the evening you were arrested you were two
hours in a room with Mrs. Smith, but you had not an idea that the key
was turned on the other side that was because there was a difficulty
getting the police.
BENJAMIN FORDHAM. At this time I was a detective sergeant of the
division I went to the prisoner's house and told him the charge, he said "
know nothing about it, I left the place some time before"-he gave me his
address 21, Sturgeon Street, Walworth—I went there-Mr Wheatley
showed me his room, and I found in it 13 pawn tickets, one was dated
March 11th, and one March 22nd-they related to two coats-I took Mr.
Smith to the pawnbroker, he identified one coat, and a clergyman identified
the other.
THOMAS NEAVE. I am a pawnbroker's assistant at 56, Haggerston Road
-I produce a coat pledged on March 11th for 108., which was identified
by a clergyman at the police court.
WILLIAM POPPER. I am a pawnbroker's assistant at 25, Walwort.
Road-I produce a coat pledged for 88. on 22nd March, in the name of
George Foley-I gave the prisoner this duplicate (produced).
FREDERICK SMITH (Re-examined). This is my coat.
