bearer of these twelve notes and this ticket-I gave out the £200 note of
the number which the last witness called out to me--I have the note.
CUTHBERT GEORGE Brown. I am a clerk in the Bank. On the 21st of

quire the person to put his name and address on them, I did not see the

WILLIAM TAYLOR re-examined. I wrote " 5, Bethnal-green" on it, it

CUTHBERT GEORGE BROWN re-examined. Q. Did the person who pre-
sented that note receive any others in exchange for it? 4. Yes; one
