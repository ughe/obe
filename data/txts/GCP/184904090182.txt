1020. CHARLES THOMAS AUSTIN, stealing 413 lbs, weight of lead,
value 41. 108.: the goods of Ebenezer Tonkin.
RICHARD Cox. I am in the service of Mr. Glasbury, a butcher, of the
Terrace, Kensington. On Friday night, 12th Jan., I saw the prisoner out-
side the door, about ten o'clock, with five men-I had seen them about a
quarter of an hour before I saw the men putting some lead into a van
before Mr. Tonkin's door-it came from under Mr. Tonkin's window--I saw
the prisoner lifting up the tail of the lead into the van-another of the men
said to the prisoner, "He has treated us very well, shall we have another
pot ?"-the prisoner said, “No, we won't have any more"-that man then
walked off-the other four men then ran away, and the prisoner was left
alone with the van-he got up into it and drove the borse off towards London
-Fisk went in the same direction.
Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. How near were you to them ?
4. Two or three yards—the Magistrate desired that the prisoner should be
admitted to bail I did not hear the men desire the prisoner to give them a
lift up the prisoner's name and address was on the van-Mr. Tonkin's house
is the next house to the Adam and Eve-there is a court between there is a
large gas-light at the Adam and Eve-this lead was outside Mr. Tonkin's
shop-if a man wanted to go from there to Knightsbridge on an improper
errand there are plenty of bye-roads to go.
