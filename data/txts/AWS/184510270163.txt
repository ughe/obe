SURREY CASES
Before Mr. Recorder.
2142. EDWARD COPPEN was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd
of Sept., 2 pairs of leather fronts for boots, called grafts, value 58, and
1lb. weight of leather, 5s. the goods of Tobias Child, his master.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
JAMEs HOGAN. I am in the service of Tobias Child, leather-dresser,
Jobn-street, Dockhead. The prisoner was in his employ--ir consequence
of suspicion, I was directed by my master to watch him, which I did, from
four o' clock to six, in the afternoon of the 22nd of Sept., and at half-past
five I saw him in the warehouse-he staid there about a quarter of an
hour--the leather grafts are kept therehe went in again afterwards, and
remained there about ten minutes-] saw a bit of coloured leather in his
hand-he could not see me-I told Mr. John Child what I had seen-he
and Tyler went into the warehouse.
JOHN CHILD. I am the prosecutor's nephew. The prisoner was in his
service twelve or thirteen weeks the last time-the leather was kept in the
warehouse-Hoga gave me information-1 examined the warehouse and
missed two pairs of grafts-they are in imitation of French grafts for boots
-I got a policeman--the prisoner left the premises about twenty minutes
after, and was stopped about four yards from the gate-] saw the police-
man take his hat offhe found two pairs of grafts in it--they have our
shop-mark and my initials on them.
WILLtam NoKBs. I have heard Mr. Child's evidence-] found the
articles in the prisoner's hat.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going home, and on putting my hand on the
shelf for my things I found these leathers I took them down : T thought
I would bring them next morning; I did not know who they belonged to,
or I should have given them up.
GUILTY. Aged 2.-Confined Sic Months,
