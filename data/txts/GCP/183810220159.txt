shift, value 1s.; 1 night-gown, value 6d. ; 1 bonnet, value 1s. ; 1 cap,
value 1s. ; 3 handkerchiefs, value 4s. ; 1 tippet, value 38.; 1 pair of stays,
value 6d. ; 2 yards of dimity, value 48.; and 1 pair of boots, value 38.;

Frances RANCE. I am the wife of Alexander Rance, and live at Green-
wich—the prisoner was in my employ. On the 25th of October I got up
s usual, and called for the prisoner, but found she was gone shortly after
the policeman came and brought these things-they are mine, and had
all been safe the prisoner had no right to remove them--she had been
with me seven weeks-she had no character, but I knew her father-I had
her from the Refuge for the Destitute.
WILLIAM SMITH (police-constable R 49.) About six o'clock in the
morning I met the prisoner in Church-street, Greenwich, with a large
bundle containing these things—I stopped her, and brought these things
back to her mistress, who identified them as hers.
