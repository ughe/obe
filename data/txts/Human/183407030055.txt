 1008.  MARY BARR was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of May , 1 pair of trowsers, value 9s.; and 1 cap, value 3s.; the goods of John Ayres.
   MARY AYRES. I am the wife of John Ayres, and live in Rosemary-lane. The prisoner came to nurse me - I missed a pair of trowsers, and a cap, on the 28th of May - the prisoner had then left me - I told the policeman, who took her - the trowsers had been taken off the line, and the cap from a hat box.
   TIMOTHY TOOMEY(police-constable H 32). I took the prisoner. I received the duplicates of the property from Sullivan.
   ELEANOR SULLIVAN. The prisoner came to me for a night's lodging, and gave me the duplicates to mind till the morning. I gave them to the officer - there were three of them.
   WILLIAM SOWERBY. I am a pawnbroker. I have a pair of trowsers, pawned on the 28th of May - I cannot say by whom - this is the duplicate I gave for them.
   JOHN HOPE. I am a pawnbroker. I have a cap, pawned on the 28th of May - this is the duplicate I gave for it - I do not know to whom.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. The prosecutor has been in the habit of lending me things to pawn. I did not take them with the intention of stealing them.
     GUILTY. Aged 55. - Confined for Six Weeks.
 1009.  JOHN BARRETT was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of May , 2 shirts, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Abraham Wilson.
   JANE WILSON. I am the wife of Abraham Wilson - we live at Wapping- I had these two shirts on a line, which ties to my window - I have known the prisoner many years as a neighbour - I missed them on the 17th of May.
   DANIEL REARDON. I am a pawnbroker. The prisoner came and offered me a white calico shirt, but it was damp, I would not take it in.
   CHARLES TIBBY(police-constable K 144). I took the prisoner, and found this check shirt in his pocket - the white one has not been found.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1010.  ALEXANDER JENKINS and JOSEPH THORNTON were indicted for stealing, on the 24th of May , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of Jesse Chevers , from the person of Jessy Chevers.
 JESSY CHEVERS. I am nine years old. On the 24th of May, I was in  Dempsey-street, and had a handkerchief on- Jenkins took the handkerchief off my shoulders - I had not known him before - there was no one with him - he had loosened the pin before he took it off.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Was there no one with Jenkins? A. No, there was not.
   SARAH STRADLING. This girl told me she had lost her shawl - I ran after the prisoners, and found them in a dust-yard - I asked Jenkins for the shawl - he said he had not got it - Thornton gave it me from his person.
   THOMAS HULL. I heard the cry, and followed the prisoners - I saw Thornton give up the handkerchief.
 JESSE CHEVERS. I am father of this child - this is my handkerchief.
 (  Edward Wells, of Turner-street, Whitechapel; Edward Jones, of Philpot-street; Simon Ives, and James Dormer, of Norfolk-street, gave Jenkins a good character.)
 JENKINS - GUILTY. Aged 17. - Recommended to mercy. - Confined Three Months.
 THORNTON -    NOT GUILTY.
 1011.  MARY JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of June , 1 watch, value 1l., the goods of Mary Simpson.
 MARY SIMPSON. I am  single, and live with my parents, in Devonshire-street- I have known the prisoner three or four years. On the 11th of June, she came and drank tea with us - I was rather unwell, and went to bed at six o'clock, in the second-floor front room - I laid on the bed - my watch was hanging over the mantel-piece - I saw it when I laid down - I did not go to sleep - the prisoner came to the room and spoke to me - she was there about ten minutes - no one else came there - I missed the watch about a quarter before seven o'clock - this is it.
   WILLIAM MARTIN. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Little Earl-street. On the 11th of June, this watch was pawned in the name of Sarah Pearson.
   CHARLES LAKE(police-constable D 71). I took the prisoner on the 12th of June.
 Prisoner's Defence. There were a great many people in the house - I went to the threshold of the room door, but did not go in.(  Mrs. Varley , Mary Trimming, James Steele, and Ann Woodman, gave the prisoner a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 47. - Recommended to mercy. - Confined Three Months.
