1501. WILLIAM PERKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of
March, at St. Marylebone, 1 £100 bank-note, the property of James
Davies, his master, in his dwelling-house.
JAMES DAVIES. I am a linen-draper, and live in Oxford-street, in the
parish of St. Marylebone. I occupy the whole house the prisoner was
my shopman for five weeks-on Thursday, the 28th of March, I missed one
£100 bank-note from my cash-box-1 gave him into custody on the Mon
day fortnight afterwards--I had received, on the 19th of February, four
£100 notes, and three £50 notes from the Bank of England-my cash-
box was in my desk at the end of the shop-I believe I left the keys
in the desk, when I was attending the counter-I missed two £100
notes on Saturday the 30th, the day after Good Friday after searching
for them, the prisoner came and searched a drawer, and said, “Here is
one;" and delivered me a £100 note, No. 93,046-No. 98,045 was
still missing-after I had traced it, he was charged with taking it, by
the gentleman who was brought to the shop-I did not charge him with it
myself-he denied taking any thing.
Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. Where did he come from to you?
A. From Mr. Roberts, in the Borough I received a very good character
with him the officer has 691. and a gold watch, valued at 451.- I have
money or money's worth back.
CHARLES WARREN Cook. I am shopman to Benjamin Warwick, a
goldsmith in Regent-street. On Saturday morning, the 30th of March,
the prisoner came to the shop, between nine and ten o'clock, and wished
to look at some watches-1 shewed him some-he selected one, and a
gold guard, and a brigade chain-they came to 431. 188. all together-he
produced a silver watch, for which we allowed, I think, 38. he paid me
with a £100 note-I sent it out for change by Mr. Warwick's son, who is
quite a youth-he was not absent more than five minutes--he is not here.
Cross-examined. Q. What is the watch really worth ? 4. It cost us
between 261. and 271., and we charged him 321.-it is an English watch,
of the finest description--we guarantee it for twelve months.
