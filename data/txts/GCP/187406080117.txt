MARY ANN G. FOWLER, I live at 129, Windmill Street, Graveserid
about 14th November, Hammond and another person came for apartments
for a widow lady and her nephew -he engaged & bed room and sitting
room and told me her name was MoKenzie, and said that she would want
them for a month or two-on Tuesday, the 18th, Mrs. Easterby and little
Harper came-the other gentleman was with her he was a dark gentle-
man-Mrs. Easterby and the boy stayed at my house three months and
three days, and left me on the fourth day when they came, Hammond paid
one week in advance, and said that he would come and pay me the rent
every week, or see that it was paid, but he has never put in an appearance
to this hour-ho paid one week and Mrs. Easterby paid three-sho showed
a good disposition to pay when she had it gave her notice three times
but found it was useless-she had to money for food, and Thm sorry to my
that at times I kept her from starving she left of her own acord some
time in February, leaving her luggage there.
