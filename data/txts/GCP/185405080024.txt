JAMES JORDAN re-examined. Mr. Neal handed over 31. 168. to me
on 17th January, for two tons of coals-the bill is made out for 4l.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you remember whether there were three
sovereigns and 168., or three and a half sovereigns and 68. A. Mr. Neal
might have taken 201. that day for what I know-he hands over to me in a
lump at night any sums which he has taken, and I keep the book from the
counterparts of the receipts I cannot say what particular sum I received
for this transaction, only by the counterparts.
