SAMSON GENESE. My brother keeps an auctioneer's office in the Poul.
try—the prisoner came to me on the evening of Friday, the 16th of Febr.
ary, and asked if I had a dressing-case for about 11.-he wanted change for
a £5 country note-I asked him to let me look at the note-he produced
a £5 Ipswich and Needham Bank note- I took the date of it-it wa
12th month, 1834-this is the same note - I took such very particular na
tice of it-I took notice of the name of "Alexander" not being correctly
printed, because the man was anxious to have the change, and he wanted
me to give him 41. for it, and then wanted me to lend him 21.--the
next morning I sent our porter to the banker's to know if the note was
good one-the next morning the prisoner came, and our man went with
him to Barnett and Hoares, and he came back and showed me four sote-
reigns and two half sovereigns, and he went off.
Cross-examined. Q. Did he not go with the porter to the banker's!
A. Yes, he did—I do not know whether he was able to read-I suppose
80-I had seen the prisoner before I did not know his name till he was a
the Mansion-house-I had seen him in our shop before-he was perfectly
sober the night before.
HENRY STYLE. I am porter to Samson Genese. On the morning of
Saturday, the 17th of February, the prisoner went with me to Barnett and
Hoares, in Lombard-street- I went to show him the way-the note was
changed in a moment, and gold given for it, four sovereigns and two hall
sovereigns, by Mr. Cubitt.
Cross-examined. Q. The prisoner was known to your master before!
A. I do not know-he offered it in change himself-I think he was
drunk.
WILLIAM CRANFIELD CUBITT. I am clerk to Barnett and Hoares. I
gave change for this Ipswich note, which I have produced, on the 17th of
February.
GUILTY. Aged 52.-Transported for Seven Years.

840. WILLIAM COLE was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of Feb
ruary, 1 pair of boots, value 2s.6d., the goods of William Ovens.
CHARLES OVENS. My father's name is William Ovens, he is a boot and
shoe maker. On the 18th of February I saw the prisoner take my father's
boots from the board outside-he ran away-- I told my father-I followed
him-he was taken to Bow-street-I am sure he is the person-these are
the boots-(looking at them.)
WILLIAM OVENS. I am master of the shop-it is in Newton-stredt,
Holborn-my son called out “Father!"-I went out and saw the prisoner
-I ran after him and called " Stop thief-the policeman caught him,
and he dropped these boots from under his arm--they are mine.
JOHN LAURIERE (police-constable F 120.) I took the prisoner, and
have the boots.
