ROBERRT EAGLE. I keep the White Bear, Long-walk. Two of these pots are mine—I have lost a great number lately.
   Prisoner.  I had not taken the pots, nor cut them up—I was coming down by St. George's church, and turned up a gateway—I saw this bundle, and took it up to see what was there—I took them home—my wife told me to take them away—these two officers were standing as if watching me. GUILTY. Aged 25.— Transported Seven Years.

JOHN WILLIAMS, was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of October , 5 handkerchiefs, value 6 s  .; 1 pair of trowsers, value 10 s  .; 1 night-gown, value 1 s  .; and 1 shirt, value 6 d  .; the goods of William Flexman . MARY HARMON. I am servant to Mr. William Flexman, of Gloucester-street, St. George the Martyr. I saw the prisoner, on the 27th of Octo ber, go out of the passage—I followed him, and he dropped this bundle, containing these things, which are my master's—a young man took him to the station-house, and the officer fetched me.
   Prisoner.  I did not drop them. Witness.  Yes—I never lost sight of him. SAMUEL BUTLER. I live in Kingsgate-street, and am a porter. About seven o'clock in the morning I saw the prisoner running, and drop the bundle—I took him to the station-house. GUILTY.* Aged 14.— Transported for Seven Years.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, NOVEMBER  28, 1836.
