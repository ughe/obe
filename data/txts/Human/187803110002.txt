I turned him out of the house about four months ago, because he did not maintain me or his child—I was not told by Wilson, a cabman, before I married him that he was a married man, or I would not have had him—I did not say if the first wife did not mind I did not care—his brother did not tell me he was married—I was a servant at the Express coffee-house before my marriage—I have drank with the prisoner since those proceedings, but not at his expense; he ran after me and spoke to me when he was out on bail—I did not say I would go back and live with him. BENJAMIN CHARLES CARTER. I am first cousin to the last witness—I was present at her marriage with the prisoner on 7th May, 1876—her sister was also there. GUILTY — Six Months' Imprisonment.
