155. JOHN PINGRUM and JOHN FIELDER, stealing 60 pairs of
clogs, value 21.; the goods of Henry Deadman.
ELIZABETH GARRATT. I am single, and know Mr. Deadman, a clog-
maker, of Tanner's-hill, Deptford. I attended on his mother-I have
occasionally taken orders from him-on 24th Oct., about ten o'clock, I
saw the prisoners in the shop, Pingrum was behind the counter, and Fielder
on the customer's side; there was a female in the shop, and an old lady
who had sent for me-Fielder bad lived with Mr. Deadman, and I knew
Pingrum-I saw him take a basket of clogs from behind the counter-they
each took a basket-I asked what they were going to do; they said Mr.
Deadman had sent them, and they were going to take them away they
each went out with a basket of clogs; Mr. Deadman came home, and I
told him.
Pingrum. Q. It is not the first time you have given me clogs. A. I
have given them to you both when Mr. Deadman ordered me; and I have
received the money from Fielder.
