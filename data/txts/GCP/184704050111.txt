COURT. Q. You saw Wynn in the public-house before you were robbed,
had be the opportunity of seeing the 12. 88.? A. He had an opportunity of
seeing me with silver-he saw I had money I am certain.
Jous MURRELL (police-constable C 50.) On 22nd March, about one
o'clock, I was on duty, and heard a cry of police"-I went and found Jones
lying across the middle of the carriage road, bleeding from his temple--I lifted
bim up-be was speechless for a minute or two-when he recovered, he said,
"I have got the blunt all right"-I took him to the hospital-got his head
dressed, and went with him to a bouse in Church-lane Bingham went up
stairs with bim-I stopped below on the way to the station, the prisoners
both said they had not been in Oxford-street-about four o'clock in the morn-
ing, I picked up a packet at the spot where I had found Jones lying.
COURT. Q. Was Jones drunk or sober? 2. He might have been con-
fused by the blow, but I should say he had been drinking.
HENRY BINGHAM (police-constable E 53.) On 21st March, about one
o'clock, I saw the prisoners together at the end of Oxford-street-I bad seen
them in the Crown public-house, Crown-street, several times before that-I
saw them come out of that house, with twelve or fourteen men about a
quarter to four o'clock, from information, I went with Jones to a house in
Church-lane-he there identified Wynn the moment he saw him—we looked
round three rooms, there were four more females, one was very drunk-just
as Jones got out on the landing, Higgins came up stairs on to the landing, he
identified her directly he saw her-both the prisoners denied having been in
Oxford-street at all.
Cross-examined. Q. What did Higgins say, before Jones said she was the
person? A. I did not hear her say anything, he did not charge another
young woman first-one woman sat there very drunk-I roused her up for
him to look at her, but he did not charge her I did not hear Higgins come
up, and say to Jones, "You are drunk," or hear him then say, “You are the
person who robbed me"-I will not swear it did not pass- I will swear he
charged no one but her.
WYNN-Guilty. Aged 19. Transported for Ten Years.
