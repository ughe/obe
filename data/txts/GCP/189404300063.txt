of Hatton Garden--on January 18th Perritz called on me, and produced
this sample of platinum-we had a conversation about it, and he arranged

price-he knew nothing about platinum-he gave a card with the name of
his firm-there was no concealment; it was quite open-Mr. Matthey is a
well-known member of the Royal Society.
