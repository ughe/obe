with the agreement. There is no truth in the suggestion that the
banking account was to be kept in the names of Lemare and Furst
because defendant was an undischarged bankrupt. The reason given
was that defendant could not write. When he did sign anything it

January 15, 1904. Judgment was obtained on February 9 for

defendant, including £i 1s. for costs and the judgment. As a
solicitor I should know there was not much object in bringing an
