 2211. JOSEPH WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of John Phipps from his person; and that he had been before convicted of felony. JOHN PHIPPS. I live in Dorrington-grove, Kennington, and am a linen-draper. On the 31 s . t of July, between nine and ten o'clock at night, I was crossing Westminster-bridge—I felt a tug at my pocket, and missed my hand kerchief—I looked round—the prisoner and another young man were close by me—the prisoner was moving away from my side—I followed him, and in going across the bridge I saw him putting his hand into his breast pocket—I went up to him and said, "You have got my handkerchief"—he said, "Yes, sir, I have just picked it up"—I said, "I should rather say, picked it out"—I followed him, and gave him in charge.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  How far off were you? A.  When I felt the twitch I was on the pavement, and he was immediately on the curb—he was not looking at the handkerchief when I first saw him—when I first saw the handkerchief it must have been partly in his hand and partly in mine, as it was taken from his pocket—I know it by its general appearance, and swear to it—I had used it about ten minutes before. THOMAS BROWN. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former con viction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the man. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined One Year .
