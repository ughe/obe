JOSEPH Cheek, bricklayer, Commercial-road, Lambeth. I rent No. 11
arch of Waterloo-bridge, in which I keep horses and barness the prisoners
were in my employ-on Friday, the 10th of Nov., Westwood was employed
at the arch all day to clean the harness-he had no business to remove any
without orders-he left me on the 13th--I missed the things stated on the
12th-I saw the prisoners again in custody on the 18th, charged with
taking the harness--they denied it-I have never seen it since. fois
Cross-examined by MR. NEALE. Q. Have you always given the same
account of this ? A. Yes, I swear I have never said that the prisoners left
without giving me notice ---I asked them on the Saturday when I paid them
if they were satisfied-they said, "Yes" I did not tell them I did not want
them any more-I may have said that I missed the things on the 18th
missed them on the 12th and 18th too, and on the 13th I sent to make
inquiries about it--they were taken on the Friday--the arch is locked every
night at eight o'clock, and another gate outside is locked-there is a regular
watchman on the premises--I leave about eight o'clock there is only one
entrance-the other end is bricked up I had six or seven men at that time
I have all their names in my book- they had been working with me about a
month--the prisoners, I believe, worked the shortest-reight o'clock is the
time for closing the vault--I was there about five o'clock on Friday evening,
and told the prisoners to do them up I came back and found Jeffreys and
two others at work, but the prisoners were gone- remained there about
half an hour, seeing the work done--the hamess that was taken hung up on
the right hand as you go in-I keep horses and carriages there as well when
I left it at half-past six o'clock I left the door open, and left the men that
were there and my son--he always stops to close the door-beyond the door
are high outer gates, which are kept locked--a man named Griffin was in my
employ, and worked for me a week after the prisoners left-r he was at work
on Friday the 10th-he comes at half-past seven o'clock, and stops till eight
o'clock or my son are there to receive him, and take away the key
always look round the place, lock it up, and take the key--it is my son's
duty to look round as well as mine, but I do not look round on a Monday-
most likely my son took the key home on the Friday--I saw it next morning
at seven o'clock hanging on a nail by the fire-place in my front parlour no one
could get at it-I give it to the first that comes in the morning-I pay my
men at my house every Saturday night they do not come into the room
where the key hangs, and I do not think they knew where it was kept they
were paid at the door-I never told Griffin I would give him two guineas if
he would reveal the real thieves, nor was any sum mentioned by me or my
wife in my presence-I never asked him to try and find the thieves-Idio:
