Cross-examined. Q. It was about two months before you were before the
Magistrate ? A. Yes I had no motive in noticing it--the prisoner dis-
trained our goods for rent, and we left I would not have come against him
if I had not been compelled--this happened two or three weeks before that
-I was not there when it was found-I mentioned it to my brother and my
busband I went to the Police office about a week after I saw it once after-
wards in the shop-the prisoner deals in tools-I believe his father-in-law,
Mr. Barrett, lives in the country- he came about once a week, or oftener-I
have heard that he is the owner of the house--the name up is “Boxall, late
Barrett" - I do not know that Barrett was my landlord-I do not know that
he was there after the grindstone came--he may have been, but I have not
been down stairs much for two or three months together-I do not know
whether the prisoner slept in the house up to that time or not-I have heard
he slept out.
