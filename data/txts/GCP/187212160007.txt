LEVY PETTIT (Policeman E R 30). Mr. Ravell spoke to me- I watched
the prisoner and saw him go into Ramsey's shop and tender the token-he
then went to Mr. Campbell's shop and did the same there; he then went to
the Northumberland public-house, but he did not tender it there-I took
him in custody-he said “Not me, I am a hard-working man; I took it
for a sovereign"-I found the coin on him and 6s. and 28. 21d. in bronze.
Prisoner's Defence. I have a witness to prove that I sold a turkey for
a half-guinea and received this coin. I was intoxicated or I should not
have taken it.
Witnesses for the Defence.
ALFRED MILWARD. The prisoner is my father-I saw him sell a turkey
to a gentleman for 108. 6d. at the top of Sloane Street near the public-
house, and my father gave him 98. 6d. change.
Cross-examined by MR. COLERIDGE. This was about 1.30-I saw my
father receive what I thought was a sovereign, because I saw a head on it
-it was not like a sovereign, but I did not tell my father so-he put it in
his pocket, and did not look at it after I spoke to him.
GUILTY.-Twelve Months' Imprisonment.
61. WILLIAM WILLIS (27), was indicted for unlawfully uttering
counterfeit coin.
MESSRS. COLERIDGE and DE MICHELE conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN Howe. I am an oilman, of 97, Cambridge-road-on 2nd November
I served the prisoner with ld. of glue, he tendered 18.--I asked him if he
knew what it was--he said “A shilling "--I said "Where did you get it
from ?"—he said "At the corner of Holywell Lane in change for a half-
sovereign"-I bent it, and gave him in charge.
JOHN HOLLINGTON (Policeman K 586). I took the prisoner on 2nd
November, and received this bad shilling- he was remanded twice and
discharged.
ARABELLA MARY WILBY. I am shopwoman to Mr. Boxall, confectioner,
of Stepney-on 16th November I served the prisoner with a penny loaf-he
gave me a shilling– I tried it, bent it, gave it back to him, and told him it
was bad-he then gave me a half-crown; I gave him the change, but after-
wards thought it was not good, and told my mistress so, and he was given
in custody.
ELIZA JANE BOXALL. I thought the half-crown was bad, but it turned
out to be good.
WILLIAM BAGWELL (Policeman K R 65). I took the prisoner-this bad
shilling was lying on the counter-I took it up.
