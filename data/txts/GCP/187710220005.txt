754. HENRY CLARK (62), Unlawfully having in his possession
counterfeit eoin, with intent to utter it.
MR. LLOYD and MR. AUSTIN METOALFE conducted the Prosecution,
ALICE WILKINS. I am barmaid to Mr. Garnon, of 414, Oxford Street-
on 19th September the prisoner came in with three women and asked for
& quartern of gin and a glass of bitter beer, and tendered a shilling-the
liquor came to 7d.--Mr. Garnon spoke to the prisoner, who left the house.
THOMAS GARNON. I am a licensed victualler, of 414, Oxford Street-
the prisoner and two women came in on 19th September the last witness
gave me a shilling and said it was bad- I spoke to the prisoner, who ran
out-the women also ran out on the other side-I ran after him, and saw
him throw some packets down à grating-I gave him in custody-I went to
the house where the grating was, and a young man gave me a parcel which
contained 198.-I gave the bad shilling and the parcel to the constable.
WILLIAM PRIEST. I live at 12, High Street, Bloomsbury, and am a
porter- I was in the kitchen at Mr. Beaumont's I heard something
drop in the area, and found some shillings wrapped up I gave the parcel
to Mr. Beaumont.
JOHN HENRY BEAUMONT. I live at 412, Oxford Street-I received from
Priest a parcel containing bad coin-Mr. Garnon afterwards came, and
the parcel was given to the policeman.
PATRICK HACKET (Policeman E 436). The prisoner was given into my
custody-he said nothing these are the coins.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These two shillings are bad-here are also ten
bad shillings, five being of the same mould as the one passed; and four
bad florins.
Prisoner's Defence. I met the women and gave them some drink--they
asked me to have some more; and when we went into the prosecutor's
house they ran away-I know nothing about the transaction.
GUILTY—Judgment Respited.
