made out this draft on the head office : "London and County Bank, 6th
March, 1873. On demand, pay to James Esmart or order 3002, value
received."-I afterwards corrected it to " Jas. E. Smart " there is also on
it, Alteration in the endorsement requires to be confirmed by the payee"

found 301 sovereigns instead of 300—I asked him if it was exactly 300 he
wanted to draw for, he said that it might be one sovereign more or less-
I gave him the sovereign back-he paid 9s. for the commission, and I
