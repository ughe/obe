   Prisoner's Defence . On 18th December I found a small canvas bag in Whitechapel Road, containing the seven counterfeits produced. I met Reed in the evening, and lent him six out of the seven, telling him to be very careful, as they were very dangerous things for any one to have. I never told him to circulate them, and never circulated one myself. The seventh remained in my coat pocket till I was taken. GUILTY.†— Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
