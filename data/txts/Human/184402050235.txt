 814. PHILIP BOWDEN was indicted for stealing 1 pewter pot, value 2 s  ., the goods of James Gale ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. WILLIAM PEARSON. I am servant to Mr. James Gale, who keeps the Hen and Chickens at Walworth-common. This quart pot is his—I was going round for my pots last Friday morning, between nine and ten o'clock—I saw the prisoner pass, facing me, and he chucked  a bag on his back—I ran and caught him, and asked what he had got in the bag—he said, "Nothing"—I took the bag off his back, and shook this pot out, and asked where he got it—he said a boy gave it him to carry—it had been at a customer's honse—I had seen it safe at my master's on Thursday night. JOHN SMITH( police-constable P  60.) I took the prisoner, and have the pot.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A person asked me to carry it. JOHN GIFFORD. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former con viction, which I got from the clerk of the peace of Surrey—( read )—I was present at the trial—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined Six Months , and Twice Whipped.
