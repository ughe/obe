658. THOMAS SPITTLE (37), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin,
MESSRS. CRAWFORD and COOKE cooducted the Prosecution.
Ann GIDDINGS. My husband keeps the White Lion beer-shop at Acton.
On Friday, 7th July, the prisoner came between 8 and 9 in the evening for
2 pint of ale--I served him-he gave me half-a-crown-it came to three
pence, and I gave him two shillings and three pennyworth of halfpence in
change-be then called for another pint, and gave me a shilling-I bit its
little and bent it, and not having sufficient change, sent my daughter out to
change it-the prisoner afterwards bought two biscuits which came to two-
pence, and gave me a bad shilling-I bent it easily, and told him he might
pay me with coppers as I had given him plenty, and that was a bad shilling
-I gave it back to him-he said nothing, but gave me twopence and left-
I had put the half-crown in a basin where there was no other-I afterwards
looked at it, found it was bad, went to the station and gave it to a police-
man-on the following Sunday night Mansell, the policeman, brought it to
me, and I recognized it as I had marked it-this is it (produced).
JOHN MANSELL (Policeman, T 1). On 7th July, I went after the prisoner
aud overtook him about five or six hundred yards from Mrs. Giddings-I
told him he was given in custody for uttering counterfeit coin-he said, "It
is all right, I am a good fellow and work hard for my living "-I took him to
the station, searched him, and found on him half-a-crown, a shilling, three
sixpences, a 3d. piece and 1 d. in copper-I received this half-crown
(produced) from Mrs. Giddings, and this shilling from Mr. Tickner.
SUSAN GIDDINGS. I received a shilling from my mother on 7th July to
get change - I took it to Mrs. Tickner and left it with her, she gave me change.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These coins are both bad.
Prisoner's Defence. I received my wages on Saturday night, a balf
sovereign, two half-crowns, and a two-shilling piece—whether I got this bad
money in change for the half-sovereign I cannot say.
Guilty.-Confined Twelve Months.
