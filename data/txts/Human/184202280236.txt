 1120. SARAH EVANS was indicted for stealing, on the   28th of February , 18 sovereigns, 2 half-sovereigns, 3 half-crowns, 16 shillings, and 5 sixpences, the monies of Thomas Turner , from his person.
   MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution. THOMAS TURNER. I am a carpenter, and live at Cookfield, in Sussex. On the evening of the 28th of February, I was in Tooley-street—I bid eighteen sovereigns, two half-sovereigns, three half-crowns, sixteen shil lings, and five sixpences—I met the prisoner, and went with her to a house in Vine-yard—I gave her two half-crowns—we had some gin—I went to bed—I was sober then and my money was in my trowser's-pocket, which I put under my pillow—I awoke about one o'clock (the prisoner did not come into bed)—I saw my trowsers at the foot of the bed—the prisoner was then sitting on the side of the bed—she went down stairs—I examined my trowsers and missed my money—I gave an alarm, and she was taken by the policeman and searched—he told her if she had got it, she had better give it up instead of being searched—she said she had not got it at all—eight sovereigns were taken from her bosom, wrapped up in a bit of rag, and thirteen shillings and sixpence—another officer found four sovereigns on her.
   Prisoner.  I was very tipsy—I do not know how the money came into my possession. Witness.  I do not think she was tipsy. EDWARD CONLON( police-constable M  131.) I was called—the prisoner said she had no money about her but a little silver—I found thirteen shil ling's and sixpence in her hand—I searched her and found eight sovereigns in a bit of rag in her bosom—my brother officer found four sovereigns in a bit of rag. THOMAS KNIGHT. I am a publican, living in Tooley-street. At half-past ten o'clock at night the prisoner came to my house and said she was dying for a drop of gin, and had not got money to pay for it—she took this handkerchief from her neck, and I let her have a quartern of gin on it till the morning. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Six Months.
