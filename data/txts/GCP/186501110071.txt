186. GEORGE PHILLIPS (38), Feloniously stabbing George Kempt,
with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
MR. POLAND conducted the Prosecution,
GEORGE KEMPT. I am sub-warder at the House of Correction, Coldbath-
fields—the prisoner is undergoing a term of imprisonment there—on the
20th of December I saw him in his cell—I spoke to him abont his books
being wrongly placed on the shelf—the Lord's Book was on the top, and it
should have been at the bottom-be called me a s-d, and said I was always
making complaints on the following day I went to his cell between 9 and
10 o'clock-I went to take his stool away, as I wanted to sit down, having
& sick headache—the stool was not required by the prisoner- when in the
aet of stooping I received a violent blow ou the chin-I rose, and saw the
prisoner with a knife in his hand-I said, “Oh, man, what are you doing?"
he did not speak- I cannot say whether he struck me more than once-after-
wards I found two wounds on my face-I ran to another prisoner and told
him to shut the cell door persons came to my assistance, and I was taken
to the infirmary-I cannot swear that this is the knife ( produced) that the
prisoner used; it was one similar to that it was a shoemaker's knife.
DANIEL JOHN MILLER. On 21st December I was & prisoner in the

-I saw Kempt come out of the prisoner's cell—I went to the prisoner and

WILLIAM WHITOOMBE. I am one of the sub-warders at the House of Cor-
rection on the morning of 21st December I went to the prisoner's cell
- he had a knife in his hand—I wanted him to give it to me, but he would
not-be said he had nothing to say against me, and did not wish me any
harm-he gave up the knife whilst I was there, about a quarter of an hour
afterwards.
