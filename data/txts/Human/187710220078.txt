JAMES MASTERS. I was a seaman on this ship—I saw a row on the forecastle, and Cummings was hanging over—I caught hold of him—the prisoner wanted to fight Cummings.
   Cross-examined.  I do not know how the row began. ROSS WATTON. I was the second mate of this ship, which is an English ship, and was bound for Calcutta—I have not got the register—I heard a noise forward and went to see what it was—I met Cummings coming aft—he told me Hazel had struck him—I went forward, when Hazel abused Cummings and challenged him to fight—I told him there must be no fighting, or else he should fight with me, and I told them to obey orders.
   Cross-examined.  I do not know how it commenced—I had no report, and no complaint was made to the master. NOT GUILTY.
