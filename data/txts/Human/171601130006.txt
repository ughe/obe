   Mary Knight and Margaret Hopkins, of the Parish of St. Dunstan at Stepney, were indicted for privately stealing 9 Guineas, and 14 Shillings, from the Person of William Cane, on the 10th of December last. The Prosecutor depos'd, That going home to his Lodging, he met the Prisoner Knight, and ask'd his Way of her, being a little in Liquor, and not acquainted with the Town; That she answer'd him, she was going that Way; and to oblige him, provided he would make her drink, she would light him home: He complied, and away they went together; but instead of Home, he found himself conducted to the Ship in Church-lane, where he went to Bed, and was robb'd. The Prisoners confess'd the Fact before a Justice, and were both found   Guilty of the Indictment. [Knight: Death. See summary.] [Hopkins: Death -respited for pregnancy. See summary.]
