William PANTIN. I am a partner in the firm of William and Charles
Pantin, dry salters, of West Smithfield-on 20th April I paid 21. 88. 11d, to
a person who signed this receipt (produced), on account to the London and
South-Western Railway Company-to the best of my belief the prisoner is
the person--he signed the receipt in my presence.
BENJAMIN CHASTIN. I am clerk to Mr. John Bennet Lawes, of Adelaide-
place, London-bridge-on 17th April, I paid 11. 78. 6d. to the prisoner on
account of the South Western Railway Company-it consisted of two accounts
of 128. 6d. and 168.--the prisoner on that occasion signed these two receipts
(produced) for the accouuts.
CHARLES THOMAS BRADNOOK. I am one of the firm of Willan and Co.
of 21, Clink-street, Southwark, egg importers I produce s bill of the
London and South Western Company for 78. 10d, which was due by us to
the Company-it was paid on the 23 April in the present year it bears
receipt signed by R. T. Fry-I cannot swear that I paid the money myself,
but I know that it was paid to Mr. Fry, because he was well known to
every one in our establishment-I cannot swear that I saw it paid to him
I might have paid it myself-I know his handwriting-I am certain that
this is his writing.
Cross-examined by MR. RIBTON. Q. What is that 78. 10d. for 1 4. For
carriage of goods from Southampton-I was not in the habit of paying to
many other clerks of the South Western Company I cannot say how many
-Mr. Fry was generally the receiver of these goods-I was sometimes in
the habit of paying to other clerks-I cannot say to how many I have
generally paid money to Fry-the persons to whom I have paid money
have never brought receipts ready signed; they have always rigoed in my
presence-they were always signed in the presence of the person who paid
the money-I might have paid this money to the prisoner ; but I do not
know whether I did-either myself or Mr. Willan would pay it unless we
were going out and knew the day it would be called for; and then we should
leave it ont for the clerk to pay.
