John Green. I am a gardener, and live at Dulwich Common. On
Tuesday, the 8th of June, I left my cottage, about a quarter before three
o'clock in the afternoon-I left no one in the house my wife went with
me into the garden, which is about two hundred yards from the cottage-

me I went directly-I saw the window broken close against the button,
