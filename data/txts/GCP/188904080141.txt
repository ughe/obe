Police-station-he might have found four or five distinct bruises

dress, after going a little distance I was found there by a constable I
should not have fallen if I had not had drink-I was taken to the station

wounds) with a tin opener; I saw this knife in his hand as well as the
tin-opener”-I did not know what it was-I don't know what he struck
me with.
