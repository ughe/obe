155. ALBERT COURSEY (28), Burglary in the dwelling-house of
Thomas Harvey, with intent to steal.
MR. JAMES LEWIs Prosecuted.
WILLAM PEROIVAL (Policeman K 243). About 2 o'elock a.m. on 31st
December I was on duty in Boar Lane, Poplar-I heard a slight noise
like glass being broken, which seemed to come from Montagu Place, an
adjoining street-I went there, and saw in the area of No. 6 the prisoner
with his arms through the two broken centre panes of glass of the
window, apparently shoving the shutters inside to force them open-I
went up very suddenly and caught him pushing them-I asked him what
he was doing-as soon as he saw me he made a leap up the area, which
is eight feet deep-he missed his grasp, and fell baek-I asked him if
he would come out; he refused-1 blew my whistle for assistance-on
the arrival of two eonstables we pulled him up, and got him over the
railings--the windows were so brokenn his arms were through-his
coat was on the ground he was in his shirtsleeves, with his hat and
coat offon the way to the station he said I had no money to get any
lodging, but intended to get some."
