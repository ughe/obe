921. JOSEPH BENGE was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of March,
452lbs. of lead, value 41. 48.; the goods of William Rowley, upon a cer-
tain wharf, adjacent to a certain navigable river, called the Thames.
WILLIAM ROWLEY. I am a plumber, and live in Howland-street, Fitz-
top-square. The prisoner was in my employ-I had four hundred weight
and three pounds of lead, on Whitehead's Wharf, Earl-street, Blackfriars
-I saw it safe on the 24th of March
JOHN COOMBS. I am a clerk at Messrs. Whiteheads' Wharf. The pri-
soner came there on the 24th of March--I knew him to be in the prose-
cutor's employ, and the lead was delivered to him.
ROBERT CARTER, I am in the employ of William Bellamy, of Fleet-
lane, a dealer in marine-stores. The prisoner brought 7 cwt, of lead there,
which I bought at 78. a cwt.--I paid him 4 guineas-the lead was taken
to the Compter.
(The prisoner put in a petition, pleading extreme distress.)
WILLIAM ROWLEY. I had no reason to believe him dishonest, till now
-I believe his story is true.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined One Month.
