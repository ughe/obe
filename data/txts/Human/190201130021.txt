 126. WILLIAM SULLIVAN and WALTER JAMES FORD Robbery with violence on James Thompson , and stealing a watch and chain and a purse, his property.
   MR. ARNOLD Prosecuted. JAMES THOMPSON. I live at 25 Redmead Lane, Wapping, and am a watchman, employed by the Free Trade Wharf Company—on November 21st, between nine and ten p.m., I was at the wharf gate—the prisoners wanted to come out at my gate—and I would not let them—I knew them before—they are stevedores—they had no business to come that way—they started knocking me about with their fists—I was knocked down, and Sullivan struck me on my head with this bolt ( Produced ). One of them kicked me on my ribs—I became insensible, and remembered nothing more till I found myself in the London Hospital a fortnight later
