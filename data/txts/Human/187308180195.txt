   Cross-examined by MR. W. SLEIGH . I have had no money from the police, but Sergeant Croome has sent me 5 s . twice for my trouble in coming to the Court, and I have had; 10 s . from Brockweli, not more—I have not had 2 l . from him—he has not been paying all the witnesses—I have got into employment since this case began, with Mr. Purchas, of Wood Street, Golden Square—I got that for myself—I did not get a character; we don't require one in our line—I have been convicted of buying a ticket of a watch of a man who is doing seven years, but of nothing else—I got nine months—the police asked me to give them information as to who was buying this property, and I mentioned Ralph, and Castle, and Jewell—I did not mention King—I did not know that wax had been bought in large quantities by King, Edwards, Johnson, and others—I told the police that How was a receiver on the same night I spoke about Castle—I know Noble & Ross now—I never worked there in my life—I did not know from. Castle that Johnson and King bought largely—I saw Ralph at Castle's three times—I have no recollection of having said "I cannot swear whether or not I have seen Ralph at Mrs. Castle's three times"—I will not swear I did not—I said he had brought wax under his coat in a handkerchief—I cannot tell you the date—I did not; think it extraordinary—I mentioned, that before I heard of the reward—I have sworn that I should not mind receiving the reward of 20 l ., and that I did not say anything about it before I heard of the reward—I was then asked if I had told the police about it—It did not say I would not have the reward if it was offered me—I should like to have it now—I did not tell the Magistrate that I did not know the name of my employer—Mr. Lewis asked me to spell it; I could not do so till I looked at the paper—the name is Mitchener—I offered as an excuse that I had only been there a few days, and that I had a bad memory—I won't swear I did not say "I refused what was offered me"—I don't remember refusing anything—I came forward to assist the ends of justice—the matter about the ticket of a watch was eight or ten years ago—it was in Oakley Street, Lambeth—I am sure it was six or seven years ago—I was tried at the Surrey Sessions for receiving a stolen watch, the property of Mr. Moore, for whose father I. worked—I was never charged with stabbing my father
