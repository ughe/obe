1993. MARY SAWYER was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
July, I half-crown, and 2s., the monies of John Peek, from his person.
MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN PEEK. I am a sailor, and live in Leeds-passage, Greenwich. On
the 27th of July, I went to the Wheat Sheaf, Greenwich-there I had
some gin and water, for which I changed half-a-crown-I had a half-crown
and 25. about me- I drank all my gin and water-I fell asleep-I was
sober-I was asleep about half an hour, and the prisoner awoke me, by
pulling me into her lap under the table-my money was in my breeches
pocket-I felt her hand in that pocket-I found all my money was gone-
the half-crown I can swear to, but the two shillings I cannot-I had been
robbed once before, and I had marked my money after that-the half-crom
was marked - I had seen the prisoner before I went to sleep, and had spoken
to her-I had not given her any money-I had nothing to do with her.
Prisoner. We were drinking from one o'clock in the morning till nine
or ten o'clock. Witness. No, we were not.
Mark Burn. I happened to be in the Wheat Sheaf between nine and
ten o'clock that morning, when this happened, I saw the prisoner come in
and sit down by the side of the sailor- I saw her pull her hand out of his
pocket, but I cannot swear that she took any thing out-she put 1s. into my
hand, and I suspected she had done something wrong, I chucked it
under the form, and awoke the sailor, and told him to get an officer,
Prisoner. The money I had was my own I had 78.- this man was
so drunk he could not see, and he asked me to hand him 1s. Witness. No,
he did not.
John WALKER (police-constable R 182.) I was called in on the 27th
by the prosecutor-Burn was there they were neither of them drunk-
I took charge of the prisoner-I searched her and found a half-crown in
her mouth-this is it--I showed it to Peek-he said “That is mine-
bit it."
Prisoner. I told you I had no money from the man-I said I had gor
half-a-crown of my own, and I would give that rather than have any
bother about it. Witness. She said she had no money at all.
John Peek. This is the half-crown I had, and I had marked it.
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Six Months.
