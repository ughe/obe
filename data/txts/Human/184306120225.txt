 1987. DAVID WHITEHEAD , THOMAS LOMATH, and WILLIAM CASEY, were indicted for stealing, on the   14th of June , 1 penknife, value 2 s  ., the goods of George Howard and others; 30 cheroots, 3 s  .; 1 lock, 4 s  .; 1 printed book, 4 s  .; and 1 key, 2 s  .; the goods of John Lesiter ; to which
   WHITEHEAD  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 16.
   LOMATH  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Three Months . JOHN LESITIR. I am manager of a manufactory at Wapping. I lost a penknife belonging to George Howard and others, and some cigars, a lock, and a key, of my own—they were safe on Tuesday night, the 13th of June—these are them—the box was broken open in the counting-house. Daniel Sugg. I received information, and went to Whitehead's lodg ings, between one and two o'clock in the morning—he was in bed—I found this book at his lodging—I found Casey at his lodging—he denied knowing anything of the robbery—nothing was found on him or at his lodgings. ANTHONY COSS LLOYD. I live in Smith-place, Wapping. About a quarter to three o'clock in the morning of the 14th of June I was in my dressing-room, which gives a view into the prosecutor's premises—I saw the three pri soners come from the prosecutor's wash-house on to the water-butt, they jumped into the yard, and escaped—I ran down, and saw Whitehead come down—he was the last—I swear Casey was one of them—I knew him before by sight.
   Casey's Defence.  I was waiting for the steam-boats to come in; a lady asked me to carry a box; it was rather heavy; I asked these boys to help me; I left them, and went home to my lodging.
   CASEY  — GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Three Months .
 1988. GEORGE LOVELL and GEORGE BERESFORD were in dicted for stealing, on the   14th of June , 1 pair of boots, value 3 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Thomas Read . FREDERICK MILLS. I am salesman to Thomas Read, of Sydney-place. On the 14th of June I was in the shop, and saw the prisoners—they came up to the boots—Lovell cut a pair down, and gave them to Beresford—I ran out and caught hold of Lovell, and left him at the door—Beresford ran away, and dropped the boots—I took them up, and brought him back.
   Lovell.  I did not touch them; I had no-knife. Witness.  There was no knife found—he must have thrown it away in going to the station.
   LOELL  — GUILTY. Aged 15.
   BERESFORD  — GUILTY. Aged 12. Confined Three Months .
