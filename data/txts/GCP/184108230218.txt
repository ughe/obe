Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. Were you examined before the
Coroner? A. Yes--after the accident I asked the captain to change me
a half-sovereign--he referred me to the stewardess-she gave me change
but no sixpence, which I wanted to pay my fare, and was obliged to ask
for sixpence-the captain, the mate, and somebody else were standing by
-I asked for sixpence, not addressing myself to either, and the captain
took two sixpences out of his pocket, gave me one, and took my shilling,
from which I infer he was nothing like intoxicated I have always had the
same opinion.
