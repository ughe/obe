859. RICHARD MORLAND (21), Feloniously killing and slaying
Richard Morland
MR. DALY conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. WARTON the Defence.
CAROLINE MORLAND. I live at 5, Nelson-street, Stepney-the deceased
was my father--he was about forty-seven years of age, and was a rigger-
on Monday, 24th July, I saw him when he went out to work--he seemed
very well then he had had bronchitis last Easter very badly, and was in
the London Hospital --became home to tea about 7 o'clock-he was sober-
he went out between 7 and 8-I saw him again that night, when I ran out
in consequence of what I heard, and found him lying on the ground at the
top of Nelson-street, Stepney-he said his leg was broken--he was taken to
the hospital.
