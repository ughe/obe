MR. BODKIN, Q. If his attention was called to the Bawtry mark, he
would at once know it was a single letter? A. Certainly-a practical
inland-officer could not be deceived into a belief that this was a double
letter, but other persons in the post-office might, as there are many double
letters which bear the tax of eleven-pence-- the letters are separated on
the table, paid and unpaid, and franks also, but not at the stampers' table;
one pile is put on the stampers' table at a time--the stamper takes them
himself from the other table--a clerk examines them, and stands them by
the side of a small ridge-by accident paid letter might get mixed with the
unpaid-it was the prisoner's duty only to carry the unpaid letters to the sor-
ters' table--if paid letters were accidentally carried with the unpaid, they
would be sent back; but I can scarcely see how it was possible for him
to do that-it would be discovered by the examining clerk before it got
to the prisoner's hands; and if he did carry them to the sorter's table, that
sorter would carry it to the paid sorter-it has occasionally occurred in
error, that a stamper, who should stamp unpaid letters, has stamped paid
ones they go from the sorters' table to other sorters to be re-sorted, and
be told up-that is done in the inland-office.
MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. Is there any circumstance about these letters
to make a man mistake them for unpaid letters ? 4. Not the slightest-
nothing whatever-as often as there is a pile, it is handed over from one
side of the table to the other the same man who stamps unpaid letten
would take them to the sorter, but paid letters and franks are taken away
by other persons.

received such notes from my sister before, and they have been paid in
London-my sister's name is not on the back of the note--it is a Lyo
mington bank note, payable to the bearer.
ROBERT TYRRELL. I am an officer at the post-office. The prisoner
was delivered into my custody in Mr. Critchett's room, on the 18th of De
cember-I took him to another part of the office-he then burst into tears

no more, as it would be used in evidence against him..
