   Witness for the Defence. GUSTAVUS KALAN VON HOFE. I was employed by Rushton Brothers in their Londonhouse, 13, Lime Street, and have known the prisoner since 1866 or 1861—I knew of his being engaged on this code for a long time—the completion of it would take him an immense time—it would be of immense value in a business like Eushton'sbe a great saving—the prisoner has not been very well in health for some months, and I advised him to take a holiday—he told me he had come to an arrangement with Mr. Edward Rushton after Christmas—I think as far as I can recollect he was not in good health in November.
   Cross-examined.  I don't call that being "off his head." GUILTY. Recommended to mercy by the Jury on account of his having worked hard for his employers on the telegraphic code— Twelve Months Imprison ment.
