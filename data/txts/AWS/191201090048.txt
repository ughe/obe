On Mr. Forster Boulton stating that the only further evidence he
had to call was that of the landladies at the houses at which prisoners
resided, the Recorder stated that there was no evidence to go to the
jury, and directed the jury to acquit them.
Verdict, Not guilty. (Taylor was discharged.)
