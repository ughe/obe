   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT .— Monday, July  3, 1843.
   First Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 2067. JAMES SIMPSON and FREDERICK AKEST were indicted for stealing, on the   16th of June , 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Thomas Esberger , from his person. THOMAS ESBERGER. I am a licensed victualler. On the 16th of June I was going home from Blackfriars—I had a handkerchief in my pocket—I missed it when I received information—this it. JAMES BROOK( police-constable. ) I was on duty on Blackfriars-bridge, and saw Simpson take the handkerchief from the prosecutor's pocket, and pass it to Akest—I took Akest, and took the handkerchief from him—I laid hold of Simpson at the same time, and called the prosecutor.
   Akest's Defence.  I was looking over the bridge, as a woman had jumped into the water. I turned round, and the policeman seized me.
   SIMPSON *— GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined One Year .
   AKEST  — GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months .
 2068. WILLIAM WARNER was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of June , 2 half-crowns, the goods of Charles Gould , his master. CHARLES GOULD. I live in William-street, the prisoner is my son-in-law, and was in my service. On the 19th of June I gave him two half-crowns, to go to Mr. Knight's timber-yard, Saffron-hill, and purchase wood—he did not return—I found him, on the 22nd of June, in Leather-lane—he said he intended to bring the money back on Saturday night—I have never had money or wood. WILLIAM ARNOTT( police-constable G  39.) I took the prisoner into custody—he said, "I know I have taken the money, but I spent it. I intended to pay it on Saturday night."
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 19.—Recommended to mercy.— Confined One Month.
