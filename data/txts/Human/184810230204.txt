 2458. WILLIAM PARSONS , stealing 1 purse, value 6 d  .; 1 pocket, 6 d  .; 32 sovereigns, and 15 shillings; the property of Sarah Adams . SARAH ADAMS. I am single, and lodge at 103, Blackfriars-road. I have been in service in France—I came to London, and met the prisoner on 3rd Oct.—I asked him to show me a lodging—I had then thirty-two sovereigns—he took me to 61, White Horse-street—he told me I was quite safe, as if I was in my own house—he left me about an hour and a half—he came back, and took my pocket off the chair—I hallooed, and told him to put it down—he put his hand into it, and took the money—I got up and seized him by the wrists—he twisted himself from me, got away, and ran out of the door—I begged him to come back, but he would not—I returned, and called the per son of whom I took the room—she said she knew him—he was one of her father's clubmates—she took me to a house, but it was the wrong person.
   Prisoner. Q . What time did you meet me? A.  About twenty minutes to nine o'clock, you asked me to go into a public-house—I do not know what house it was—I was drinking there between eight and nine—I was not asleep when you took the money—there was a light in the room.
