Maggs on Dowgate Hill. I went back alone with the cart and the goods
were put into the cart. McCorkell told me to pawn them at Attenborough's,
I told the boy to drive to Temple Bar. I met him there. I told him to
drive to Catherine Street, Strand. I asked the pawnbroker, Sayers, to
make a loan on them. They would not take them in. I then told the boy
to drive to Attenborough's, in Charlotte Street. The goods were taken out
of the cart; about nine pieces. The pawnbroker advanced 441. on two
tickets for three months. He gave me the 446 and tickets, which I took
