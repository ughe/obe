 1758. WILLIAM JOHNSON was indicted for stealing 1 hay knife, value 9 d  ., the goods of William Henry Dean . CHARLES WORSTLEY. I am in the employ of Mr. William Henry Dean, at West Ham. I missed a hay-knife of my master's, which I had seen safe in the morning—the prisoner offered it for sale, and I gave him in charge. ROBERT KELLY. I am a milkman, and live at Stratford. The prisoner brought me this knife and offered it for sale for 6 d .—I knew it directly, and said if he would come home I would see to it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I found it in a ditch. CHARLES WORSTLEY re-examined.  I had left it stuck in the stack, and there were two or three notches cut in the stack to get up to it. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months .
 1759. ELIZA HARRIS was indicted for stealing 1 coat, value 10 s  .; 3 waistcoats, 10 s  .; 3 table cloths, 1 l  . 15 s  .; 1 sheet, 15 s  .; 1 hand-kerchief, 3 s  .; 1lb. weight of candles, 2 s  .; 2lbs. weight of soap,10 l  .; 3 doyleys, 3 s  .; 2 pillow-cases, 2 s  .; 4 wine glasses, 6 d  .; 1 tumbler, 1 s  .; 1 cape, 1 s  .; 2 cups, 6 d  .; 2 saucers, 6 d  .; 2 pairs of trowsers, 6 s  ., the goods of William Dodds , her master: and 1 coat, 10 s  ., the goods of Alexander M'Kee ; and FRANCIS HARRIS for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing them to have been stolen.
   MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution. WILLIAM DODDS. I live in West Ham-lane, Essex. Eliza Harris was my cook for two months—a policeman made a communication last Friday week, and I went to a house in Stepney the next day—the first thing I saw was a coat belonging to a friend, a shirt, and other articles which are principally marked with my name—I gave both the prisoners in custody—the policeman went to my house, and I gave Eliza in custody—she went down on her knees, and begged that I would excuse it—the policeman took a handker chief out of his pocket and shook it at her—that handkerchief had been taken out of Francis' pocket at the station.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  was Francis' hair dressed as it is now? A.  I cannot tell—the policeman took the hat out of Francis' hand, and the handkerchief dropped out—I know the handkerchief to be mine—there is no mark on it—I had used it, and had it in my pocket one day—my mother gave it to me.
