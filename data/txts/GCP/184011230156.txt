up securely in a flag basket--they were directed to Mr. Matthews, No. 47,
Great St. Andrew-street, Seven-dials, London.
HENRY MATTHEWS. I am in the service of my father, Henry Mat-
thews, No. 47, Great St. Andrew-street, Seven-dials. On the 24th of
October I received this basket containing twelve pairs of boots, it was
done up carelessly-it contained this invoice, by which I found that thirteen
pairs ought to have come, and I wrote on the bottom of the invoice,“One
pair short"-this is my writing on it-I have brought two pairs of the
boots that were in the basket, and the officer has produced one pair which
were found-these are the three pairs-(looking at them)--they are all one
man's work.
