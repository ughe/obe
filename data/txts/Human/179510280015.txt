   JAMES CHURCH sworn.
 I was not in the shop when this affair happened.
   SARAH CHURCH sworn.
 Q. Does your husband keep a shop? - I keep the shop in  Whitechapel, a haberdasher's.
 Q. Do you know the prisoner at the bar? - Yes.
