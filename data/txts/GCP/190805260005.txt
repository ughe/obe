1908.
CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT
BELL, MAYOR.
EIGHTH SESSION.
LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES.
BETORE THE RECORDER.
(Tuesday, May 26.)
MARSHALL, Edward Metham (38, journalist), who pleaded guilty
on March 31 of forgery and attering, and was ordered last Session
to find a surety of $100 (see Vol. CXLVIII., page 782, and page 5
of this volume), was again brought up. It was stated that he could
mot find the surety, but as he had been in custody for ten weeks the
Recorder said he would release him on his own recognisances in £50
to come up for judgment if called upon.
CUDDON, George John (71, agent) (see page 43), who pleaded
guilty last Session of uttering, and DELLANA, Frank (24, secre
tary), who pleaded guilty at the same time to forgery, were again
before the Court. It was stated that Dellana had executed an assign
ment of certain concessions to the prosecutor, John Chipchase Grey,
for which purpose sentence had been postponed.
Sentences: Cuddon, 12 months' hard labour; Dellana, 15 months'
bord labour.
ORCHARD, William Arthur (19, postman), pleaded guilty of
stealing one post letter containing one postal order of the value of
28., the property of the Postmaster-General, he being employed under
the Post Office.
Bentence, Eight months' hard tabour.
