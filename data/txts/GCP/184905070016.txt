RELSE Before Edward Bullock, Esq. and the Fifth Jury.
1084. ROBERT BARFOOT, unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin : to
which he pleaded
Guilty.-Confined Six Months.
1085. JANE DRISCOLL was indicted for a like offence.
Messrs. ELLIS and BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
Bridget DriscoLL. I am the wife of Cornelius Driscoll, of John-street,
Whitechapel. On Saturday night, 21st April, the prisoner came to my stall
for a halfpennyworth of onions, and gave me half-a-crown-I gave her change,
and put the half-crown into my pocket, wrapped in paper-I had no other
on the Monday evening I tried to pass it, and found it was bad-I afterwards
gave it to Brown.
GEORGE JOSEPH Brown (policeman, K 47). I received this half-crown
from Driscoll.
CHARLOTTE Noxes. I am the wife of Samuel Nokes, a cheesemonger
in Cambridge-road. On 21st April the prisoner came between nine and ten
o'clock at night for a quarter of a pound of cheese-it came to 27d.-she
offered me a 58.-piece--1 found it was bad, and told her 80-a policeman was
near the door--I gave it to him, and he took the prisoner.
WILLIAM COPPING (policeman, K 379). I was called to Mrs. Nokes's, got
this 58.-piece, and took the prisoner.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint. This
crown and half-crown are both bad--they have been cast in a mould and
been electro-silvered. Guilty. Aged 24.--Confined Six Months.
1086. ANN KELLY was indicted for a like offence.
