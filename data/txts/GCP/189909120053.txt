chestnut gelding, which I recognised as one of the horses stolen on August
3rd, 1898-it was sold I do not know what it fetched-it was sold to
Mr. Haylock-I gave information to Mr. Milton
JAMES HAYLOCK. I live at 243, Westerham Street, Old Kent Road
on July 24th a mare was sold to me at Aldridge's for £55, and a chestnut
for 25 guineas.
Cross-eramined. When I came to try the chestnut afterwards I found it
was lame ; if I had known that I should have given a great deal less for it
-I was communicated with by the detective, or I should have sent it
back.
Henky SAMUEL KINGSTON. I am a grocer, of East Durham, Norfolk
-on July 28th I bought a bay gelding from Mr. Warner, a horse-dealer,
who lives in the same town, for £20-it was not a good bargain.
ROBERT Scott Young. I am manager of the London and Provincial
Bank, Sydenham—the prisoner opened an account with us at the Edgware
Road branch on September 3rd, 1898, and it was closed on February
3rd, 1899.
The Prisoner, in his defence, said that he received the horses thinking them
to be genuine business ; that he did not know they were stolen ; that dealers
often sold horses without any previous arrangements, and that the telegram
was the first he knew of them.
