charge of the prisoner at the station - he said he lodged at 78, Oxford-street

prisoner's employers--I found something in it.
ALFRED HEATH. I am assistant to Mr. Young, a pawnbroker, in Princes-
street, Leicester-square. I have a piece of satinet pawned for 158., on 5th
July, in the name of John Williams- I have no recollection of the person
who pawned it.
WILLIAM Owex. I am partner with Mr. Joseph Willes Rumball, at No.

this duplicate of this satinet in it, which led me to the pawnbroker's-I saw
the satinet found there--the box was not locked.
