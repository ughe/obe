WALTER FISHER. I am a police-sergeant, in the employ of the Great
Eastern Railway Company-I apprehended Butler on 30th November, in
High Street, Whitechapel --he and Sherringham were together_Evans was
with me---they went along Whitechapel together-Sherringham turned
and saw Evans, and made off--he was pursued, but got away I took
Butler I told him the charge-he said "I did not take it; I was only
carrying it; it was another man that took it"-I found this snek on him,
at the station; it is large enough to hold a truss-this other sack was left
with the property--this hat was dropped from Sherringham's head when he
escaped.
