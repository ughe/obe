851. EDWARD PATTERSON was indicted for stealing, as the   12th of February , 1 saw, value 5 s  .; 2 planes, value 6 s  .; the goods of John Perry : and that he had been before convicted of felony. JOHN PERRY. I keep the Ship and Sailor ale-house, in Church-street, Greenwich. I lost several tools—I had left home for a fortnight in Fe bruary, and in my absence they were taken—I had used them the day before I left—the prisoner has frequently used my tap-room—I did not authorise him to pawn any of these articles. EDWARD BOLTON. I am an apprentice to Mr. Harber, a pawnbroker, at Greenwhich. On the 12th of February a saw and plane were pawned at master's by the prisoner—I recollect him—1 s . 6 d . was advanced for the two—I have the ticket. CHARLES WILLIAMS. I am a pawnbroker at Greenwhich. On Satur day evening, the prisoner came with this plane, which he wanted 4 s . on—I asked him whose it was—he said his father's—I said I should like to see his father; and on the Monday he came and said, "Mr. Williams, my father says he is unable to come, and unless you advance the money or restore the plane, he shall seek further redress"—I was taken off my guard, and lent him the 2 s . THOMAS LARKIN . I apprehended the prisoner at his mother's house, in Greenwhich, last Thursday—I told him the charge—he denied it, and said he knew nothing about the tools.
 (  Property produced sworn to .) JAMES WILD . I am a policeman. I produce a certificate of the pri soner's former condviction, which I got from Mr. Straight's office—I was present at Maidstone when the prisoner was tried and convicted there in March, 1834 ( read ). GUILTY. Aged 19— Transported for Seven Years.
   Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant .
 852. SARAH DABIN was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of Fe bruary , 4 bolsters, value 1 l  .; 10 s  .; 3 Pillows, value 1 l  .; 3 blankets, value 15 s  .; 4 counterpanes, value 16 s  .; 5 1/2 yards of carpet. value 10 s  .; 1 look ing glass and stand, 5 s  .; 1 set of fire-irons, value 5 s  .; 1 tea-caddy, value 7 s  .; 1 sugar-basin, value 3 s  .; 1 cork-screw, value 5 s  .; 1 hearth-brush, value 1 s  .; 4 yards of drugget, value 10 s  .; and 5 candlestricks, value 2 s  .; the goods of James Lester , her master. JAMES LESTER. I am an attorney, living at the Grove, Blackheath. The prisoner was employed by me from some time in October till the 6th of February—I have missed bolsters, pillows, blankets, a tea-caddy, sugar-bais and other things—this tea-caddy is mine, and some of these other things—the prisoner had on opportunity of getting access to these things—the looking-glass is mine. WILLIAM SPENCER. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Leonard-street, Greenwhich. I have produced the tea-caddy—I received it on the 6th of February from the prisoner. JOHN ABETHELL. I am a pawnbroker, and live on Blackheath-hill. This looking-glass was pledged on the 10th of February by the prisoner—this candlestick was pledged by her also. HANNAH WARREN. I know all the property as being Mr. Lester's—I lived in his house five months.
   Prisoner . I was in distress, and afraid of being ill-used by my hus band—I had no intention of stealing them—I had one of my children ill for ten months. GUILTY. Aged 45.— Confined Six Months.
