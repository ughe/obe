 346. THOMAS HOLMES was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of Dec ., 3 hearth-brushes, value 6 s  .; and 3 saws, 9 s  .; the goods of Lewis Lewis ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. LEWIS LEWIS. I am an ironmonger, and live in Conduit-street, Maryle bone. On the 9th of Dec. I missed three brushes from inside the door-post—these now produced are them. RICHARD OLDAKER. I live in Homer-street, Marylebone. About three o'clock on the 9th of Dec. I saw six persons coming up in one side of the street, and the prisoner and another on our side—I taw them with some brushes, and I watched them—six went round the New-road, and the other two went another way—I ran up Chapel-street, and saw the prisoner showing the other the three brushes—he saw me, dropped them, and ran away—I took up the brushes, ran, and took him—Mr. Lewis's shop is at the bottom of the street, about four doors from the corner.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I had the brushes given to me in Dorset-square. JAMES M'REATH( police-constable D  176.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Transported for Seven Years—Convict Ship .
 347. ANN LOWE was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of Dec ., 2 dead fowls, value 4 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Thomas Halton and another; and that the had been before convicted of felony. RICHARD STEEET. I am a porter. On Saturday, the 10th of Dec., I was employed at Mr. Thomas Halton's, in Newgate-street—Peek called me, while I was at the end of the shop, and I saw two women going out—I was told to follow them—they parted, one went to the right, and the other to the left—I took one, and took her back 'to the shop—she is not here—I then went and took the prisoner, and found these two dead fowls under her cloak—I took her back to the shop—they are Mr. Halton's fowls.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  Did you see her in the shop? A.  I saw two women going out—I could not swear it was her, I only saw her back. THOMAS HALTON. Street came back with the prisoner—these are my fowls—I have one partner.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How do you know them? A.  They were sent to we for sale—I never saw them till they were brought back. THOMAS PEEK. I am salesman to Mr. Halton. I missed two fowls—I saw them brought back by Street about a quarter of an hour after—they are just such as I missed them—I saw the prisoner in the shop. OLIVER DEATH( City police-constable, No.  201.) I produce a certificate oi the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY.
