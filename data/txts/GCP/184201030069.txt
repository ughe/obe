488. JAMES PATTEN was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of Au-
gust, at St. Marylebone, 3 sovereigns, and 2 101. Bank-notes, the property
of John Lucas, his master, in his dwelling-house ; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 33.-Transported for Ten Years.
489. AUSTIN POPE was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of De-
cember, 1 half-sovereign, the monies of Thomas Lansdown.—2nd Count,
stating them to be the monies of Luke Lansdown.-3rd Count, stating
them to be the monies of Thomas Goodwin,-4th Count, stating them
to be the monies of Her Majesty's Postmaster-General; to which he
pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Six Months.
490. MARY ANN WINTER was indicted- for bigamy; to which she
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Three Months.
491. CHARLES SIMS was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of Ja-
nuary, 4 candles, value 4d.; and 4 pieces of candles, value 2d. ; the goods
of Joseph Miller and others, his masters; to which he pleaded
GUILTY.-Confined One Month.
