OCTAVIVS FREDERICK HERITAGE. I am a surgeon—I attended the
deceased—he died on the 21st of October—the cause of his death was
rupture of his kidney and spleen, combined with the hemorrhage induced
by the bleeding in those two organs—the wheels of an omnibus going
over him would most probably produce that.
