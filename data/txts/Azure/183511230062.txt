ternoon, and the things belonging to it, I believe were all there *ben I
left—I had seen them about half an hour before I left the bed-room—I had
a padlock on it—there were a gold watch-chain and ral, and two keys at-

three bottles of scent, and several trinkets, rings, and other thite—l did
not lock my room door—I returned to my room about twelve o clock—I

—l have seen the box 'ince in the possession of Ballard, and some of the
