JOHN DAVIS. I am the husband of Clara Davis. The prisoner came on the 3rd of August for a quarter of a pound of beef—I cut it—I saw her pass the money over to my wife—when we closed the shop at night I discovered that it was a bad shilling—on the 9th she came again—I am positive she is the woman—she had a pint of table beer, and passed a shilling over the counter into my hand—I stopped her from going away—she cried, and fought, and used bad language—I got the officer, and gave him the three shillings. EDMUND MATTHEWS( police-constable M  68.) I received on the 9th of August two shillings from Mrs. Davis, and one from Mr. Davis—I produce the three. MR. JOHN FIELD. These three shillings are all counterfeit. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Six Months .
 2585. MARY ANN KING was indicted for unlawfully uttering counter feit coin.
   MESSRS. SCRIVEN and CHARNOCK conducted the Prosecution. ELIZABETH MOWAT. I am the wife of David Bennison Mowatt. The prisoner came on the 8th of July, and bought half a peck of peas, which came to 3 d .—she gave me a shilling, which I put into my pocket—I had nothing else there but two sixpences and a fourpenny-piece—she came again within half an hour for six pounds of potatoes—I served her—she gave me a shilling—I gave her 6 d ., and put that shilling into my pocket—I served no other person that morning—I went to a grocer's, and offered the two shillings—he weighed them, and said they were bad—I brought them home, and gave them to my husband—he gave them back to me, and I wrapped them in paper, and put them on the mantelpiece, into a little cup—on the Tues day following she came again for four pounds of potatoes, and gave me a shilling—I told her I had not got change—I ran over to my husband to get change—he gave me change, and followed the prisoner—I gave the other two shillings to Bush. DAVID BENNISON MOWATT. I am the witness's husband. On the 8th of July she showed me two shillings, which I bent—on the 11th of July she came over and showed me another shilling—I gave her change, and kept the shilling in my pocket till I gave it to the officer—I received the two bent shillings from Bush, and gave them to the officer. THOMAS BUSH. I received the two shillings from my mistress, and gave them to my master. JOSEPH KENT( police-constable L  164.) I took the prisoner and received these three shillings from Mr. Mowatt. MR. JOHN FIELD. These are all counterfeit, and cast in one mould.
   Prisoners Defence.  He said he took a great deal of bad money, and he would make me suffer for all. GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Six Months .
