To Mr. Justice Lawrence. With active shares it is not true that
the quotation ex-dividend is the same as cum. dividend. Rio
Tintos and Canadian Pacifies are very active ; variations occur every
hour or so. It must follow as a matter of course with active shares
that when they became ex-dividend they deeline to the extent of the
dividend declared. There may be extraneous reasons why the price
should not fall by the amount of dividend, but taking the example
which your lordship asked for, and which I turned up and put in,
the variation corresponds exactly. In my opinion it is less true with
international shares to say that the price does not change when they
are sold ex-dividend, because they are dealt with over such a large
area, and there are more people interested in their fluctuations.
