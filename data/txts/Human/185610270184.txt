   Cross-examined. Q.  Who gave them into custody? A.  A person named Kiddey, for trying to do people out of money.

I am the wife of Benjamin Holman, of the Plough tavern, Rotherhithe. On 3rd Oct., about 2 o'clock, Johnson and Hollowly came for 1 1/2 d . worth of bitters and a glass of 6 d . ale—( MR. LEWIS . objected to this evidence, it being the subject of another indictment. The COURT . having consulted MR. JUSTICE ERLE , decided that the evidence was admissible, but within certain limits )—one of them, I did not see which, laid down a sovereign—I went into the bar parlour, and brought out four half crowm, nine shillings and two sixpences, put one of the sixpences into the till, and took 3 d . out, which made their change right—I laid it down, and was in the act of turning away, when Johnson said, "This is not right, here is but 7 s . 6 d . here"—I said, "I am certain I gave you 9 s . 6 d ."—he said, "There is but 7 s . 6 d . here"—I said, "I see that, but I am positive I gave you 9 s . 6 d .," and then opened my till and gave him two more shillings, as I did not want a piece of work.
   Cross-examined. Q.  At the time you gave the extra two shillings did you or did you not believe that you had given the two shillings previously? A.  I believed I had given it right. GEORGE CADWELL. ( policeman, R  189). On 3rd Oct. I took the prisoners at a public house just by the Gibraltar gate, Deptford. They were given in charge by Mr. Holman—I found on Hopkins four half crowns and five 2 s . pieces nothing on Holloway, and eight shillings and two halfpence on Johnson.
 (  MR. LEWIS , on behalf of Johnson, submitted that as to the fast Count, there was no false pretence, as the prosecutor parted with the money knowingly, and that there was no evidence of a conspiracy to defraud Mrs. Bolton. The COURT . considered that it was a case for the Jury. )
   Holloway's Defence.  I met Johnson, and asked him for 8 s . which he owed me; I went into the public house, and he paid me; I was gambling with him afterwards, and he won it back again.
   Hopkins's Defence.  I met Johnson, and got drinking with him; he got tossing and gambling; a sovereign was changed two or three times, and as I won it, I got gold for it, not intending to gamble any more.
