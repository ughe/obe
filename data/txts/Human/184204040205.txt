I am in partnership with my son as wholesale cheesemongers—the prisoner Baker was in my service as carman for three years—there is a way of getting from our premises behind, at the stables, and you can come out into Irish-court, but the court is no thoroughfare—there is nothing beyond our premises—the constable brought Boughton to me, with a flat containing some cheese and hams—the cheese was ours—I can identify it—it was part of a lot I had just had in—the things in the flat were worth about 8 l .—Boughton had no business on our premises.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Have you the goods here? A.  Yes—my cheeses come from abroad—I believe the hams are Irish—I have no doubt of them—I have my mark on the cheeses—I had not sold any of them to the prisoners. GEORGE FREDERICK LARKING. I am the prosecutor's son, but not in partnership with him—my brother is. On the morning after this occurred I went into the stable, and found three Dutch cheeses cut in half, and a bottle of gin, in the cupboard.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Have not you some profit from the business? A.  No. CHARLOTTE BAILEY. My father was in Messrs. Larking's employ when this happened. On the 18th of March I saw Boughton going into the yard, out of Irish-court—he had nothing with him—I did not see him come out—I had seen Baker go into the stable with his horse about half-an-hour before—I had not seen him come out again—I have seen Boughton take a flat basket in there. JOSEPH VALLEY. I am carman to Messrs. Larking. I came home with my horse on the 18th of March, about a quarter past six o'clock, and went into the stable—I saw Boughton come in just after me—he did not say what he came for—I put my horse up, and before I came away Baker came in—his horses were in before mine, but he came in without them—Boughton was there then—they talked to one another before I left, to the best of my knowledge—they were not strangers—Baker seemed to know him—I left them together in the stable about a quarter after seven o'clock—Boughton conversed with me about the horses. WILLIAM SMITH. I live in Fashion-street, Spitalfields, and am a packing-case maker. The window of my workshop looks into the yard where Messrs. Larking's stables are—I have seen Boughton there fre quently, carrying a flat basket—I have seen him come out as well as go in. WILLIAM YOUNG. I am a policeman. I took Baker into custody-Taylor was present.
   BOUGHTON— GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Six Months.
   BAKER— NOT GUILTY.
