Cross-examined. Q.  How do you get your living? A.  In a shop in the general line—my house is next door but one to the stable—I swear to Elliis, and have every reason to believe Westwood was the other man—I saw the side of his face, and it was nearly covered with the saddle—I have heard nothing of any reward—I have not been dining with or treated by Mr. Cheek to-day. JAMES BROOK( policeman. ) I took Westwood, on the 18th, at Mr. Cheek's house—he said he knew nothing about it. WILLIAM GUEST( policeman. ) I took Ellis on the 18th of Nov., and told him what for—he said he knew nothing at all about it—when he got a short distance from the house he ran away—I pursued and secured him—in taking him to the station he said others ought to be taken as well as him.
   MR. CHEEK re-examined.  I went away twice—I went home to get my tea—I then came back and went away again—I was not there when the door was locked—the harness had not been taken home for the last fortnight—it was not taken home regularly, unless I ordered it.
 (The prisoners received good characters.)
   WESTWOOD  — GUILTY. Aged 20.
   ELLIS  — GUILTY. Aged 18.
   Recommended to mercy. Confined Twelve Months .
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, THE  11 TH OF DECEMBER .
