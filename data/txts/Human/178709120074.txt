 713.  EDWARD WARBY was indicted for stealing, on the 14th day of July last, one truss of hay, value 2 s. the property of William Dalton.
 The prosecutor's son saw the prisoner between two loads of hay, and saw him take a truss of hay out, and stopped him.
     GUILTY. Transported for seven years.
 Tried by the London Jury before Mr. RECORDER.
 714.  RICHARD CARR was indicted for stealing, on the 30th day of June last, two linen shirts, value 6 d. one pair of cotton stockings, value 12 d. two linen handkerchiefs, value 2 s. and one cotton ditto, value 2 s. the property of John Halsall. The prosecutor called, and not appearing, the prisoner was ACQUITTED.
 Tried by the first Middlesex Jury before Mr. RECORDER.

 Tried by the first Middlesex Jury before Mr. RECORDER.
