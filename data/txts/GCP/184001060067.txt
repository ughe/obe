CHARLES Bond. I am shopman to Mr. Bartram, & pawnbroker in
Princes-street, Soho. I have two yards and a quarter of buckskin, pawned
on the 6th of May, in the name of John Freer-I took it in, and the dupli-
cale was written by the boy-I do not know who pledged it--I have no
belief on the subject-it was a man--the prisoner bas often pawned at our
shop, and always in the name of John Freer I have some other property,
pawned on the 20th of May, about the same quantity, in the same name
cannot say whether it was pawned by the same person-the prisoner has
pawned many things similar to this, buckskin and cloth-- I know him by
sight, but do not know whether he pawned these articles or not he has
pledged buckskin on every occasion that he came the duplicate of the
pledge on the 6th of May is in the hand-writing of a person in our shop
it was affixed to the property I have produced the one produced by
Mr. Anson corresponds with it, and is the ticket which would be given to
the person pawning on the 6th of May. de
STEPHEN DALY. I am the prisoner's brother-I am a tailor, and
live in Dorset-place, Pall Mall East. My brother gave me this ticket, and
I gave it to Mr. Nussey, on the Thursday night that he was charged-1
received it from my brother at the latter end of May or beginning of June
- he wished me to take care of it-it was a duplicate, similar to this I.
cannot say this is the same---I have rather a doubt of it I did not look!
particularly at the ticket I gave Mr. Nussey, and cannot say whether this
is the ticket or not, I had it until December-I did not see Mr. Nussey
hand it over to Mr. Anson.
