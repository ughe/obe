£98 48. by false pretences.
MESSRS. C. MATHEWS and STEPHENSON Prosecuted, and MR. DRAKE
Defended.
FRANK Douglas PENNANT. I am a captain in the King's Royal Rifles
--the Hon. Reginald Cathcart is in the regiment he is at Malta, on
the Staff; he went in October—I was not living at 131, Jermyn Street,
on November 27th, and did not write or authorise this letter to be writ-
ten: “Gentlemen, I am up in town. Having seen your advertisement,
I shall be happy to have your terms. I shall not do more than £5 each
way”--this other is not Mr. Cathcart's writing. This stated: “I believe
Mr. Pennant has also written to you ")—this letter, addressed from Malta,
is Captain Cathcart's writing I have never authorised anyone to bet in my
name-I was not with Captain Cathcart last February; these telegrams.
were not issued by my authority; none of them are in Mr. Cathcart's
writing-I did not authorise the people at 131, Jermyn Street, to take in
letters for me.
