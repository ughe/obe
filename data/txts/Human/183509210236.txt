EDWARD TYRRELL. I am a policeman. I have been on duty in Oxford-street and Holborn for the last three years—I have observed Bray's omnibus going along—I have observed Trevett's, but I cannot speak with certainty about them—I have noticed Bray's going along, one before and one behind the other omnibuses—on one occasion I noticed the driver Mann, run against the other omnibus—there seemed to be oppo sition, one as bad as the other—on two occasions I have summoned Bray's omnibus for misconduct, such as obstructing—he was once fined 40 s .—the other time he had to pay costs—I have observed Bray's men abuse the passengers of the others, using blackguard language to the passengers or the coachman; that was Mann—the abuse was mutual, equal on both sides.
