FRANCIS POWER. I am chief cook on the London Belle—my duty is
to receive everything that comes on board, and to issue provisions f.om

chief steward—stock is taken every night—I did not miss anything from
the store the first week in August, nor the last week in July—Hawes's
duty was not in the larder, but to deal with the liquid refreshments—I
had two assistant cooks—one left.
