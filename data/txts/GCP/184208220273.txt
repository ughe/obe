2556. WILLIAM. BAVIN was indicted for feloniously assaulting
James Nash, on the 20th of August, and cutting and wounding him in
and upon the head, with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm.
Mr. Charnock condueted the Prosecution. The
JAMES NASH. I am a chair and sofa maker, and live in Broadwall,
Stamford-street, Surrey-the prisoner was my journeyman. On Satur-
day night last, about eleven o'clock, he came to be settled with he pro-
duced his account to me there was a difference between us as to the
charges, which were more than I thought I should pay-he called me a b
ferret-eyed looking b-, and a shuffling trickster, and said he should like
to give me something I then sent for a policeman, who heard me tell
him, what he had done I would pay him for, and when the other work was
finished, I would settle for that he then appeared more calm, and the
constable left-I proceeded to settle with him he went and sat at the
end of the room, and I was settling with the other men-while I was sit-
ting down writing, he got up, came behind me, and in an instant I felt a
tremendous crash on my head, three times; had I not put my hand up, to
save the second blow, I believe death must have been the consequence,
for I received that blow on my hand instead of my head-I cannot say
who struck the blow-I became insensible, and recollect nothing more till
I found myself reviving in the surgeon's shop-I was afterwards attended
by the surgeon of St. Thomas's Hospital - I managed to go out a little
way on Sunday evening, but felt so faint I was obliged to return.
WILLIAM BUCHANAN. I am in the employ of Mr. Nash-I was there on
Saturday evening while he and the prisoner were settling some accounts
- there was a dispute with the prisoner-a policeman was sent for, and
he became quiet-after that he sat down in the room by himself_the pro-
secutor began to settle with another man-I saw the prisoner get off his
seat, walk round behind the prosecutor, take an empty champagne bottle
off the drawers, and strike the prosecutor over the head with it as hard as
he could, apparently-master bad his hat off, and was sitting at the table
- he struck him three times with it-he said nothing the prosecutor got
up and went out-he staggered outside the door-his head bled a good
deal - the bottle was broken all to pieces- I think it broke with the se-
eond blow, and the third was given with the neck of it-I ran to master's
assistance, went over to the doctor's to him, and saw him sitting, bleeding
in a chair-he was sensible then-the prisoner was quite sober-I gave
him in charge.
