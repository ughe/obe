Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury.
193. ALEXANDER M DONALD, feloniously forging and uttering an
indorsement to a bill for 1601., with intent to defraud.
MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.is
WILLIAM JOHN Vian. I am secretary to the Agua Fria Gold Mining
Company, Old Broad-street. I produce a bill for 1601, dated 14th July,
drawn by the managing director of the Company in California, in favour of
Faulkner, Bell, and Co., our agents in California, and indorsed by them to
Donald Ross-it is drawn on myself, as secretary of the Company—it
would require the indorsement of Donald Ross before it could be paid it
bears my acceptance-it was brought to my office on 22nd Aug., 1 accepted
it, and on 23rd Aug., the prisoner called and fetched it away he asked
whether it would be paid at maturity-I said that it would most
undoubtedly-he asked me to discount it, but that was not in my power-
it was drawn at one month's sight, and was payable a month after I accepted
it, through the London and Westminster Bank-I made it payable at our
bankers, the Commercial Bank.
Cro88-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Do you see the indorsement of
Mr. Ross? A. "D. Ross"-I did not notice that when it was originally
brought to me-it is not usual for bills to be indorsed before they are
accepted, and therefore I did not look.
