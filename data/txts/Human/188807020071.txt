On 27th June I went with a warrant for the prisoner's arrest, to 92, Stoke Newington Road—I knew he was in the house—I first went into the parlour and then into the kitchen, but could not see him; I opened the small door, and found him there—I read the warrant to him—he said "I have done nothing"—I took him to the station.
