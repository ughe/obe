He had slippers on—he was taken in the slippers to the station—I did not hear anything said about his cutting the heels of his boots with his knife—I did not see the boots in the room—I did not see that he had one of the boots in his hand at the time I saw him walking up and down—I saw no boots whatever—I saw nothing in his hand but the knife.
