there several articles_< when there, Murphy spoke to Dufty-Dufty told me
to go on one side while they were speaking I did not hear anything that was

Ridley and Murphy there together. Murphy said "Won't you do anything

four times I then drove on to Jewry Street I saw Murphy and Ridley
there together—Murphy spoke to Dufty there I did not hear what was
mid-Ridley was a little way across the road-they went into a publie
