1127. SARAH SMITH was indicted for stealing 1 shirt, value 28. 6d.,
the goods of Thomas Wolstenholme and another; and that she had been be-
fore convicted of felony.
John KELLY. I am in the service of Thomas Wolstenholme and another,
pawnbrokers. On the 9th of March I saw the prisoner put her hand into the
shop door, take a shirt off, put it into her apron, and go offthe policeman,
who was in the shop, fetched her back with it.
John HURLEY (police-constable G 24.) I overtook her, with this shirt
rolled in her apron.
Prisoner. I was looking at another article on the under bar; the shirt fell
down, and I picked it up. Witness. She had got six or seven yards from the
shop.
JAMES YELLS (police-constable G 242.) I produce a certificate of the pri-
soner's former conviction, from Mr. Clark's office-(read)-she is the person,
GUILTY. Aged 44.
1128. SARAH SMITH was again indicted for stealing 1 pair of boots,
value 88., the goods of Thomas Wolstenholme and another.
JOHN KELLY. On the 8th of March I missed a pair of boots from the same
place where the shirt hung, the duplicate of them was found on the prisoner
-I went to Mr. Brown's, a pawnbroker, in Fetter-lane, and there I found
the boots--they are my master's, Thomas Wolstenholme and another.
CHRISTOPHER BROWN. These boots were pawned by the prisoner in ber
own name.
MARY ANN REDMAN. I searched the prisoner, and found on her two du-
plicates-one was for these boots pawned on the 8th of Marcb.
Prisoner's Defence. A strange woman came and asked me to pawn them
- I gave her the money, and she asked me to keep the tickets.
Guilty. Aged 44.-Transported for Seven Years.
1129. SAMUEL MATTHEWS was indicted for stealing 2 coats, valge
31. ; and 1 pair of trowsers, 108.; the goods of George Smith.
GEORGE SMITH. I keep the Olive Branch, in Marylebone. On the 15th
of Feb. the prisoner inquired for a bed- I said he could have half a bed for

were safe in the room when the prisoner went to bed-I missed in the morni-

evening before,
Prisoner. I spoke to you in the bar. Witness, Yes, but I saw nothing
with you; you could have come down without my seeing the whole of your
body; I was behind my beer-engine.
