ROBERT Newins. I am a baker, at Richmond. On 17th March the
prisoner came to my shop, about a quarter past 4 o'clock, for a 1d. biscuit-
I gave it him; he gave me a bad shilling; I gave him the change, but I
took it back, and told him it was bad-I asked him if he had got any more;
he said, "No"-I took back the change and the biscuit, and sent for a
constable--I gave him the shilling, and gave the prisoner in charge.
HENRY HERNON (policeman, V 297). I was called to Mr. Newin's shop
on 17th March, in the afternoon, and took the prisoner-I found nothing on
him-I received the shilling from Mr. Newins.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint. These
are both counterfeit shillings.

were bad. Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.
531. JOHN HENNESSEY, stealing 4s. 6d.; the moneys of William
Peter Holland.
WILLIAM Peter HOLLAND. I am an ironmonger, and live at Kennington.
On 29th March, in the evening, the prisoner came and asked for change for
a crown piece-I put the change on the glass case while he was feeling in his
trowsers pockets for the 5s. piece-he then handed a crown piece into my
hand; I perceived it to be bad, and said, “Stop!”-be instantly laid hold of
48. 6d. and ran away-I raised a cry of "Stop thief!" and went after bim for
about 300 yards; I overtook him, and charged him with it; he made no reply
-I walked with him some distance before I met a policeman, and on the road
he asked me to let him go-I saw him searched at the station; a half crown
and 28. were found on bim-I had put down on the glass case a half crown,
two shillings, and a sixpence-he took the half crown and two shillings, and
left the sixpence behind him.
WILLIAM PAYNE (policeman, P 138). I took the prisoner into custody-
he did not say anything-I found on him a half a crown and two shillings-
received this crown piece.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. This crown is a bad one.
Prisoner's Defence. I took it of a man for a dog ; I did not know it was
bad; I went on to find the man that had my dog; there was a cry of
“ Stop thief!" and I stopped.
Guilty. Aged 24.-Confined Six Months.
