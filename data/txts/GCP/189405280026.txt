MARGARET BOWEN. I have been in the employ of Mr. Nardie, senior,
for two years-I check goods with the invoice when they come in; I have
done that since Christmas-it was my duty to check with the invoice all
goods that came from Messrs. Stevenson; no one else ever checked the
goods--I have checked every lot that came in-I have never found more
goods brought in than were on the invoice, so that no goods could have
previously been given to the prisoner on the accommodation principle, as
it is called.
