 309. JOHN FORD , stealing 1 coat, value 12 s  .; and 1 pair of trowsers, 8 s  .; the goods of Edward Eastman ; having been before convicted. CAROLINE BEASLEY. I live at 17, Mitchell-street, St. Luke's, by the side of Mr. Eastman's. On the evening of 27th Nov., I saw there persons pase the shop—one pulled a pair of trowsers, which they took away—I went down, and saw the prisoner taking a coat down from the door—I said, "Stop a minute," and he gave me the coat—I called Mr. Eastman—the prisoner was going across the road. EDWARD EASTMAN. I heard a cry, and saw the prisoner having hold of one part of this coat and Beasley of the other, ( produced )—it is mine—the prisoner had no business with it.
   Prisoner.  It was on the ground. Witness.  No, it was not.
   Prisoner's Defence.  The stick fell from the top, and the coat was down; the woman said, "Stop a bit;" I said, "Here is a coat;" I picked it up. CAROLINE BEASLEY re-examined.  It was hanging up at the side of the door. EMANUEL WHEELER( policeman, G  36.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction—( read—Convicted August , 1846, and confined three months )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY . Aged 17.— Confined One Year .
 310. WILLIAM CLEMENTS , stealing 501lbs, weight of ham, value 6 s  ., the property of John Carter . ELIZABETH CARTER. I am the wife of John Carter, who keeps a beer shop. On 2nd Dec. the prisoner came for a quarter of a pound of ham—I took it to cut it—he snatched it and ran away with it to a coffee-shop—I went to the door, and called "Shop thief"—the officer came up and took him at the coffee-shop with the ham.
   Prisoner. Q.  Can you swear to me? A.  Yes—I recognised you from three others.
