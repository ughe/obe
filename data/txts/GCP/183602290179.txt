804. WILLIAM TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
March, 12oz. weight of indigo, value 8s.; 7 yards of linen, value 2.5
and 2 pieces of Indian-rubber, value 2d. the goods of the London Doek!
Company --2nd Count, stating them to be the goods of James Goddard.
GEORGE Dix. I am a Thames-police constable, and superintend the
London Docks. On the afternoon of the 3rd of March the labourers
were coming out of the docks the prisoner was one of them employed at
the docks--I stopped him at the gate, searched him, and found round his
ankles, within his stockings, tied round with tape, twelve ounces of in-
digo I found nothing else on his person.

JOHN GODDARD. I am manager of the indigo department, in the Lon
don Docks. The prisoner had been employed among the indigo that day

Cross-examined. Q. I think you know there are vast quantities of in-
digo of other qualities besides that? A. Yes ; brokers are employed
there they never give men portions of this by way of surplus have
known the prisoner about two years he bore an excellent character for
six years previous to my going therehe has been there eight years.be
had 188. a week-I have the sole management of the indigo in the dock,
and laying it out for the trade to inspect--nobody has any power there
but myself—this is worth about 58, a pound-the duty is 3d. a pound, to
be paid afterwards.
