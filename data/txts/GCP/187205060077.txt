JANDREW PEARSON (Detective). On the morning of 19th April, I
was near the Queen's Laundry, and found the prisoner drunk and incapable,
lying across the footpath in Kew Foot Lane, asleep-with Mr. Weston's
assistance I took him to the station, and found on him these two sovereigns
and 11. 68. 6d. in silver-I took off his boots, about 11 o'clock, and went
with Mr. Weston to Mr. Cottam's garden, adjoining where the Westons
sleep-I compared the boots with the footmarks, and they exactly cor-
responded; the iron is a little worn, and the nails are worn away-two or
three rows of nails on the toe left their print-I saw no barrows in that
part of the garden, it was planted, and he had walked to a sand-heap, and
we could trace the footmarks there and back again.
