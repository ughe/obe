   Cross-examined by MR. BROUN . When we went to Knox Road Mrs. Hill opened the door to us—she volunteered the statement that the plates were at Mrs. Carter's—she readily admitted us to the back parlour—the plates are printed in Russian characters. FREDERICK ABBERLINE( Police Inspector H ). On 27th September, between 4 and 5 p.m., I went to 15, Lavender Road, Battersea—I had learnt that Houlden was coming there—Mrs. Banks, the landlady, pointed out two parlours to me, and in the back parlour I found two large boxes which I opened with some keys which were on the mantelpiece, and found ten plates and blocks for the manufacture of five-rouble notes, and a black bag, also a large quantity of paper for the manufacture of between 6,000 and 7,000 notes—the boxes were taken to the station—Houlden pointed to the black one and said "That belongs to me"—I examined the other boxes; they contained articles which had nothing to do with this charge—next morning, about 10.30, I was called to the station, and Houlden said if I would give him a promise in writing that he would not be prosecuted he would give Queen's evidence—I said that I could not hold out any promise—he hesitated a minute or two and said "If you will go to 23 or 38, Knox Road you will find a man named Hill lives there, but you will not find him at home; he works at Messrs. Spottiswoode's, in New Street Square, in the room; if they have not disturbed them you will find the plates there—I had previously been with Mary Hill's sister to 64, Lavington Road, Battersea—she spoke to Mrs. Carter, who lodges there—she gave me a parcel, which I took to 28, Knox Road and opened it in Mary Hill's presence—it contained ten plates and blocks for the manu facture of 25-rouble notes—Hill said she hoped her husband would not get in any trouble—after she was taken to the station I went to Spottiswoode's with Moser and saw Henry Hill—I said "I am an inspector of police and this is an inspector; a man named Houlden is in custody, and your wife is detained at Battersea Station for having in their possession plates for Russian rouble notes, and you will have to go to the station"—he said "I will go with, you" on the way he said "Mr. Chester
