"Gleim, Brettauer, and Co.," for collection by Šmith, Payne, and Co., at their

24th December, I saw the prisoner in London—he said, “I shall not be
able to meet this acceptance at maturity; if you give me another acceptance,
I shall be able to discount it, and with the proceeds take up this bill for
4001. I received this letter, dated 24th December, from the prisoner, en-
closing this acceptance for 4386 128. 6d., asking me to return it accepted
I wrote this letter in reply, enclosing the bill accepted : We have your
letter of the 24th inst., and herewith return the enclosed draft for
