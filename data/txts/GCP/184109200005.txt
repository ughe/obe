man Maddox, and live in Smith-street, Marlborough-road, Chelsea. In
June last I had two ponies at my stable for sale the prisoner came about them
three times—I cannot exactly say the first day he came, but it was in June
- the last time he came was on the 26th of June-he at first said he cama
recommended by Mr. Hetherington, who he supplied with hay and straw,
to look at the pair of ponies-he did not tell me his name the first day-he
afterwards told me he was a farmer, living at St. Albans, that his name
Was Comber, and that he formerly lived at Silverton, in Northampton-
shire-he looked at the ponies-they had been looked at by a variety of
persons before he did not buy them the first day-I had other parties in

had offered-when he came on the 26th he said he had come to see
