 2446. CHARLES WELLER was indicted for embezzlement. HENRY GODFREY. I am the son of David Godfrey, a butcher, at Peck ham. The prisoner was his apprentice about twelve months—it was his duty to receive money on account of my father—it should be given to my father or me the same day. SOPHIA COLLINS. I am servant to Mr. Hardcastle. I paid the pri soner 6 s . 8 d . about twelve o'clock on the 9th of October, for a leg of mutton—he gave me a little ticket, but we did not keep it
   Prisoner.  It was the 2nd of October. Witness.  It was Monday the 9th, I believe. HENRY GODFREY re-examined.  The prisoner never paid us this money—my father was not at home—one day the prisoner came in for a shoulder of mutton for Mr. Hardcastle, and two chops with it—I did not like the look of it, and when the bill went in they said they had paid it—I spoke to the prisoner about it—he said he had had the money, and was sorry for it. GUILTY  . Aged 16 .—Recommended to mercy by the Jury. Confined Two Days.
