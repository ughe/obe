CHALLis, Mayor.
THOMAS BATEMAN MANNING. I am a licensed victualler, at Deptford.
On 5th March the prisoner called at my house, about 9 or 10 o'clock at night
-he asked me to lend him some money, 108. or 15s. on this order-he said
he was entitled to 151. 10s. or 181. 10s., as the last pay of a share of Her
Majesty's ship Phantom-th name of Broadley is there-| asked him whether
that was his name, and whether it was the order he received from the agent
of the ship-it is his own name, Samuel Broadley "-he said that he had
sold three-fourths of the prize money to a gentleman at Chatham, and this
was the remaining part to get, and that he bad been to the agent to receive
the money and he gave me this order to call on Monday at 10 o'clock to
receive it-he said the agent was Mr. James Buchanan- believed what he
said, and I lent him five shillings upon his stating he had lost two of his
shirts, and should get into trouble if he did not replace them-I was induced
to do it from his representation, that he would get into trouble for not having
bis shirts to produce-it was very foolish, I did it because I believed what
he said-I was induced to give bim money because believed I should have
it back on Monday-he said he should be with me at 7 o'clock to go to the
Admiralty-after I lent him 5s. he asked me to let him have some beer, and
I let him have five gallons, which be took away- did so because I believed
bis statement, and that he would pay me on Monday, when the order was
paid-a half past 9 o'clock I saw bim-he said he had met a comrade from
London, and would be with me at 10 o'clock on Tuesday to receive it-|
never saw him again till the 21st-I made a complaint to the Commandant
at Woolwich-the prisoner was there when I made the complaint-the
Adjutant told him he was charged with receiving money under false pretences
-he stated he had received the money, and was willing to be put on stop-
pages to pay it-he was given into custody the Adjutant requested me to
call the next day, and see the Commandant-he was given into custody on
22nd April-these are the two documents I got from him-| made every
inquiry of Mr. Buchanan in connection with this order, and could find no
such read-" Please allow the bearer to proceed to our ofice to
receive the last pay of a share in shares of Her Majesty's ship Phantom. I
remain, your humble servant, James Buchanan.")
(The prisoner's discharge from the ship Phantom was also read.)
