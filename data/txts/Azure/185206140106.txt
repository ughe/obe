TER, and JAMES EDE, burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-
house of Clarina Lonsdale, and stealing 18 cigars, value Is. 6d. ; her goods.
MR. RIBTON conducted the Prosecution.
ELIZA WEAVER. I live in James-street, Kennington, and am servant to
Clarina Lonsdale ; she keeps a grocer's and a general shop, and sells cigars.
On Wednesday night, 26th May, about 10 0'clock, I and my aunt were sitting
in the parlour—we heard a noise like the shuffling of feet under the window
—I went into the shop, and saw some one standing at the window—I only
saw one—I went to the door, and the person was gone—I examined the
window, and found it broken, and the cigars strewed on the pathway—they
bad been taken out of the case of cigars—the window bad the appearance of
being cut—I had seen the window safe and the cigars safe about ten minutes
before—the shop door was shut, and fastened by a latch—I helped to pick
up the cigars that were lying in the pathway—there were about eighteen.
