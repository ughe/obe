   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Was your husband unwell? A.  Yes—the prisoner did not manage the business—he did not make the bread—he ought to have paid the 8 s . on Thursday—he was taken in custody on the Saturday—there was a balance of 3 s . 6 d . then due to him, not a week's wages—he was not in the habit of deducting his wages from the money he received—I was not always present when he settled with my husband—he was going away a month before, and we increased his wages 1 s . a-week, I think, to stay. CHARLOTTE NEWBURT. I live at Lower Tooting. I dealt with the prosecutor for bread which the prisoner brought—on the 28th of March I paid him 8 s . on his master's account, between twelve and one o'clock in the day—he receipted the bill—it was 9 s ., but there was a mistake in it of 1 s ., which he took off. WILLIAM PEARSON. I am a policeman. I apprehended the prisoner at the prosecutor's house, on Saturday the 30th of March—I told told I took him for receiving Mrs. Newbury's bill—he said he had received it, and if his master would allow him a little time, he would contrive to make the money up.
   JURY to MRS. DOWLEY . Q.  Do you know whether he had received say other money on his master's account the same day as the 8 s .? A.  Not to my knowledge—we had overlooked other things which he had committed before?
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY  . Aged 21. —Recommended to mercy. — Confined Three Months.

JAMES JEFFRIES was indicted for burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Henry Bond , at   Bermondsey , about the hour of three in the night of the   1st of March , with intent to steal, and stealing therein 1 printed book, value 4 s  .; 2 snuff-boxes, value 15 s  .; 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  .; 1 sheet, value 2 s  .; 1 pair of shoes, value 4 s  .; 1 table-doth, value 1 s  .; 1 frock, value 2 s  .; and 1 pair of gloves, value 6 d  . his goods.
   MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution. HARRIET BOND. I am the wife of Henry Bond of George's-terrace, Grimscott-street, Bermondsey.
