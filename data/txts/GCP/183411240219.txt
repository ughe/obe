Fourth Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin,
196. SARAH COONEY was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
vember, 1 pair of shoes, value 38.; the goods of Joseph Wilkinson and
that she had been before convicted of felony.
JOSEPH Wilkinson. I live at Kent-street, in the Borough, and am a
shoemaker. I lost these boots from my door on the 18th of November
I had seen them safe about one o'clock-one of them was brought to my
shop on the Thursday following, to know if I had any larger,
MARY BRADBURY. I was coming from Covent-garden with a load, and
met the prisoner-I asked her to help me home, which she did she then
said she had a pair of shoes to sell, belonging to her husband, and she
would let me have the duplicate cheap, as she wanted a cup of tea, for she
had not broken her fast that day, I bought the duplicate, and got them out
--when my son came home, he tried them, and they were too small-I sent
one to the prosecutor's, to know if he had any larger-he stopped it.
ELIZABETH MANNING. The prisoner gave me the boots to pawn for her,
and I pawned them for 1s.
JAMES COPEMAN. I am an officer. I produce a certificate of the for-
mer conviction of the prisoner-I know she is the person.
Prisoner's Defence. I went to the Royal Oak-there were several per-
sons there, and one of them asked me to buy the duplicate of these boots
for 4d., which I did—I got them, and as they would not fit my husband, I
sent them to be pawned.
NOT GUILTY.
