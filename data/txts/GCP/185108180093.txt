Before Russell Gurney, Esq., and the Third Jury.
1684. MARGARET MURRAY and BENJAMIN MURRAY, stealing
1 501. Bank-note; the money of Mary Taylor, in the dwelling-house of John
Henry Wilson.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
John Henry Wilson. I am a bookseller and stationer, at 1, Watson's-
buildings, Kingsland-road. Mrs. Mary Taylor boards and lodges with me
she is eighty-four years old-tbe female prisoner was our servant, and left of
her own accord on 7th June-on 15th Jan. I went with Mrs. Taylor to the
Bank, when she received her dividend—she received a 501.-note, of which I
took the number-it was No. 9632, dated 9th Nov., 1850-on 27th April I
saw that safe in Mrs. Taylor's reticule, in the drawing-room- 1 missed it
about the middle of July-I went to the Bank to stop its payment, and found
it had already been exchanged - I believe the male prisoner is the female's
father, and is a porter in Cheapside-I gave information to the police, and
Went to 7, Sugarloaf-court, Thames-street, and there gave the female into
custody-she said she knew nothing at all about it..
