3059. GEORGE CHAPPELL was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of
Oet., 80 yards of cloth, called Alpaca, value 81., the goods of Christopher
Leaf.
THOMAS HILL. I am porter to Christopher Leaf, of Wood-street. Be-
tween two and three o'clock in the afternoon of the 17th of Oct., the prisoner

dealings with the salesman ? A. Yes.
