1983. JANE PHILIPS was indicted for stealing on the 28th of July,
2 geese, value 6s., the property of William Savill.
ESTHER SAYILL. I am the wife of William Savill. We lives
Layton-we had some geese on the forest on the 28th of July-two were
missing in the evening-I found them at the patrol's house they were
mine- know them by a private mark.
RICHARD CLANDENING. I am the patrol. I got information that there
was a young woman coming with property supposed to be stolen met
the prisoner, and found a duck and a drake in her apron-she said
she had bought them- I locked her up, and found the owner the
next day.
Prisoner. I met a friend or two, and got intoxicated-I don't know what
I had. Witness. She was perfectly sober.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 18.--Confined Two Months.
