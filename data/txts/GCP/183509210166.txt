WINCHESTER, Mayor.
Second Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
2083. ELIZABETH SPEARS was indicted for feloniously receiving
on the 5th of September, 1 cloak, value 58, ; 3 coats, value 1l. ; 2 pair o
trowsers, value 58.; 1 dressing-case, value 58.; 1 umbrella, value 2.
and 8 printed books, value 108.; the goods of John Jasper Boreham, well
knowing them to have been feloniously stolen, against the statute, &c.
LYDIA BOREHAM. I am the wife of John Jasper Boreham, and live
at Ratcliff. On the 4th of September, I delivered a box fo Lewis
Buckeridge, the Epping carrier, which contained the property stated
these are the articles.
Louis BUCKERIDGE. I am a carrier. I received the box froin this lady
to take to Epping-on the 5th of September, a person, named John Lay,
asked to ride in my cart, which he did when he was gone. I missed this
box from behind the cart-I do not know what had been in it-this is the
box.
Joun Emmerson. I received information, and went to the prisoner's
lodging at Woodford-I heard that Lay lodged there-I found the pri
soner-I asked if she knew any thing about the loss of the box, or the
contents of it-she said she did not-I found the box in a shed, a little
distance from her house-I went to her again in the evening, and asked
her if she knew nothing about what was in the box, as I had traced it to
her house, and likewise from her house---she said, she did not know about
it-I then went to the door, and heard her draw something from under
her bed, and she threw down stairs a bag, containing these articles.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. How do you know she did not
begin to search directly? A. I do not know that she did not-I traced
the box to her house by a man's footsteps I have looked after Lay, but I
cannot find him.
HANNAH ATKINSON. I live in the same house with the prisoner.
After the officer was gone, the prisoner took this umbrella from under the
bed, and gave it to me-she said she had forgotten to give it up--Lay had
lodged in the hay-loft, but he has run away.
Cross-examined. Q. Had she not a good character ? A. Yes; up to
this time-she is a widow.
Prisoner's Defence. I am not guilty.
Court to John Emerson. Q. Do you know Dr. Robinson's writing!
4. Yes; to the best of my belief, the signature to this deposition is his
writing. (Read. " The prisoner says, I found the umbrella and bag
under John Lay's bed- I took them, and put them under my own--when
John Emerson came the second time, I was gone into the coach-house-
chucked the bag down stairs."
NOT GUILTY.
