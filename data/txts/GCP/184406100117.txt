1657. JOHN BETTON was indicted for stealing 1 painting, value 30s.;
the goods of John Saunders.
Robert Acutt. I deal in paintings, and live in Holywell-street, Strand.
On the 20th of May, the prisoner came to sell this painting for 30s.-1 sent
for an officer, and gave him in charge.
John SAUNDERS. I am a picture dealer, and live in Holborn. This pic-
ture was safe in my shop at eleven in the morning on the 20th of May, and
about one, Mr. Acutt came to tell me it was at his house.
LUTHER CARTER (police-constable F 64.) The prisoner said, a gentleman
with a light coat on gave him the picture to sell — I could find no such
person.

1658. WILLIAM CRACK and RICHARD BURGESS were indicted for
stealing 1 shovel, value 28.; the goods of George Frederick Lambert; and
that Burgess had been before convicted of felony,
Thomas Good. I am slopman to George Frederick Lambert. This

the 11th of May, about half-an-hour before it was brought by the officer.
Samuel SPECK (City police-constable, No. 119.) I stopped the prisoners about
half a mile from the prosecutor's, about twenty minutes to four-Crack had
the shovel, and Burgess was by his side.
