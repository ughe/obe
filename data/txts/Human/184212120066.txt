 368. ANN CORSAN was indicted for bigamy; to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 29.— Confined Three Months .
 369. JAMES THOMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of Dec ., 6 cups, value 9 d  ., and 6 saucers, 6 d  ., the goods of Charles Turner . GEORGE RILEY. I am shopman to Mr. Charles Turner, of Oxford-street On the 12th of Dec., about seven o'clock in the evening, I was at the shop door—I saw the prisoner come and take six cups and six saucers off a shelf outside the shop—he put them under his coat and went on with them. JOHN MILLER( police-constable C  106.) I received information, and went and took the prisoner with these under his arm. GUILTY. Aged 35.— Confined One Year .
 370. GEORGE SPENCELY was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of Dec , 7 cigars, value 1 s  . 9 d  ., the goods of Leonard John Campbell . LEONARD JOHN CAMPBELL. I am a tobacconist, and live in St. John's-row, St. Luke's. I have missed cigars—I have reason to believe these are mine. ROBERT COLLINS. I do business for Mr. Campbell. On the 12th of Dec., on my return to his shop, I saw the prisoner outside the shop, watching a party who was in the parlour—I then saw the prisoner enter the shop, put his hand into a box of cigars, take some, and run out of the shop—I collared him, and asked what he wanted—he said he had been in for a pinch of snuff—I found these cigars in his hand.
   Prisoner.  The gentleman took the cigars out of the box himself. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Four Months .
