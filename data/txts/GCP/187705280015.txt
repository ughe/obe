EDWARD HENRY KICKHAM. I am & porter, and live at 8, Moor Street,
Soho--between 12 and 1 o'clock in the night of 12th May, I was in Earl
Street, Sevon Dials-I met the prisoner and another man; they opened
out and let me pass between them, and the prisoner put his left hand
across my throat, and the other across my back, and put his knee into me
to try to throw me down, at the same time I felt a snatch at my watoh-I
pulled my hands out of my pookets and caught my watch the other man
broke the chain in two places, knooked my hat off, and they both ran away
the watch was completely detached from the chain by the other man, while
the prisoner had hold of me-I retained the watoh--they ran up a court
close by-I picked up my hat and went about three paces after them,
when I saw a constable-he followed them and brought the prisoner back
in less than half a minute I had been drinking but was not drunk.
WILLIAM AUSTIN (Policeman E 73). I was on duty in Earl Street-I
saw the prisoner running from what the prosecutor said I followed him
and caught him and brought him back, and the proseoutor identified him
and charged him with stealing his watoh-he said nothing she knew about it.
Guilty. He also PLEADED GUILTY to a previous conviction at Clerkenwell,
in August, 1875**-Eighteen Months Imprisonment.
490. GEORGE GARDNER (17), Stealing 60 lbs. of butter of George
Wheelhouse
MR. BRINDLAY conducted the Prosecution.
SAMUEL SHEPHERD. I am a carman in the employ of George Wheelhouse,
a provision merchant, of Richmond-on 15th May I WAS with my cart on
the left side of the now Poultry Market, Smithfield-I left my cart for about
half a minute to look after my dog and when I returned I missed a firkin of
butter from the cart-I have seen it since, it is my master's property, and
worth about 41.
ROZOTT
HENRY WOODALL (City Policeman 351). About 4 o'clook on 14th May I
was at the west-end of the Poultry Market and saw the prisoner carrying a
firkin of butter on his shoulder-1 followed him about 400 yards into Hosier
Layehe there dropped the firkin into a truck-I went up to him and
asked where he was taking it to-he said he did not know and ran away
followed him, he struck and kicked me about the legs and said he would do
for me and would run a knife into me, but he had not got one upon him.
GUILTY. He also PLEADED GUILTY to a previous conviction at Worship
Street polico-court, in June, 1876.**-Eighteen Months Imprisonment.
