 2098. WILLIAM CURRY was indicted for stealing 1 clock, value 5 s  ., the goods of Henry Brunswick ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. SARAH BRUNSWICK. I am the wife of Henry Brunswick, of Brewer's-green, Westminster. I received information from Evans, and missed a Dutch clock which hung outside the shop—I have not found it. JOHN EAST. I am a bricklayer, and live at Westminster. I was pasting the shop—Mrs. Brunswick pointed out the prisoner running away with the clock—he was five or six yards from me—I am sure it was him—I followed and gave him in charge—he got rid of it while I was pursuing him.
   Cross-examined by MR. O'BRIEN . Q.  You did not overtake him, did you? A.  After he got rid of it I got up to him, walked a few steps with him, and then he took to running again—I did not speak to him, but walked within a yard of him at one time—he got round the end of the street, I and then run—I ran after him and lost sight of him for a few minutes—when I first saw him his back was towards me—he ran into a public-house—I took an officer, and gave him in charge. THOMAS EVANS. I saw the prisoner take the clock from the shop.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Where were you? A.  On the opposite side of the Way—I had never seen him before—I was taken to see him when he was in Custody—I know he is the man. GEORGE WETHERINOTON. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former Conviction—( read—Convicted Aug. , 1845, and confined four months )—he is the person—I have known him ever since. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined One Year .
