Rees's Defence. The prosecutor had been to the Mitre and raffled for
a fish-he lost his money and then said he had been robbed; a policeman
took me, and could not find the money on me; the prosecutor would not
give me in charge; I then went home, and at three o'clock in the morning
the officer took me.
Peat's Defence. Another man took us first, and the prosecutor said,
"I will have nothing to do with it, she has not got my money" - I came
out of the house with Allen, and the prosecutor struck me-he was drunk,
and so was Allen.
