By the COURT. The business in the sampling room is done thus : anybody
presenting this card would be entitled to take 3oz of tea out of every packet
on show, and he could get a pass which would take him out at the gate-
there might be 40 samples on show, and if so, he could get 40 307 samples,
but he would have to bring 40 in, and then he would get a pass—when the boy
paid 4d. and put a ticket into a little hole, that was for a private sale, the
other is a public sale—the man presenting this order would not go to Roberts
at all, he would go to Wenham--the boy would pay the 4d. to Holden if he
had this card-he would present a paper similar to these in Court-these
papers would give him access to the warehouse ; he could not get in without
--the dozen pieces of paper presented by the boy are all on our books.
