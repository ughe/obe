FREDERICK JAMES NORMAN. I am a labourer, living at 74, Dalmaine
Road, Forest Hill--the prisoner has been our lodger for ten weeks-on
11th February, this year, my wife was confined, and on that day, between
seven and eight, I bought a pair of shoes from the prisoner for 1s.-on
the 12th he gave me a flannel and some braces, and they were put in the
basket and perambulator in the kitchen he said, “You can have those ;
don't you take no notice of me"—these are the shoes—I do not identify
any other articles—he did not mention where he got them from, and I
did not ask him.
Prebs NORMAN. I am the wife of the last witness on the 11th
February I was confined on the night of the 10th February I heard the
prisoner, who occupied a room above mine, arrive home, after twelve I
think-I could not swear if it was after one, it was a little later than
usual—he used to come in about twelve, or a little after, sometimes
before.
The prisoner, in his defence, said that the things were his own property.
