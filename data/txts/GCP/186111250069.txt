Dear sir, I inelose herewith the returns which I think you will find by the
day-book are correct. I am sorry to find the sum is much deficient of what
I expected to reach ; my undertaking, therefore, if strictly carried out, falls
to the ground. There are, however, with diminished sales, some indications
of better prospects. The returns are made at no greater relative expenses
and I am inclined to believe there are less bad debts in the last six mouths
trading, than in the corresponding period. The greater of course the
returns, the less relative expense there ought to be ; and the fewer bad
debts, the less there is to be added to expenses in the shape of lose. Viewing
all things, I am till inclined to do in part what I would have preferred
doing entire, if the trade had come up to the amount. I therefore inclose
herein a cheque for 25to be disposed of thus of the amount, 151. for
yourself, and 51. for Miss Cawthorne, and the same for Miss Hill There
are many matters in the stook-books that will need great attention, more
particularly all the larger articles in musling these are, or have been, helpe
to the amount of returns, but your trade for them is gone ; the reason must
be found out. If it can be altered here or by you, so much the better
but if not, we shall want something to make up for what will be future
deficiency if not considered at once. The older articles, and in fact, every
article in needles must be considered. The fanoy box-trade is lost the
ridged eyes will be or should be extras I have a heavy stock yet of all
other goods, from six to eight millions of drilled eyed sharps; they are
nothing in the needle trade. Sixteen millions were ordered one day recently
in one order, I am told, at Redditch Large orders produce quick cash
returns ; small orders the contrary. In the one case many profits are made in
a year with the same capital; but with me I only turn anpital once a year
This is the old style of trading and wants your greatest consideration with

only made about once in five years, you sell so few; yet they are sold tog
larly, tons weight at a time. Hoping you will all feel satisfied with this
inclosure, and wishing we may succeed better in the next six months
