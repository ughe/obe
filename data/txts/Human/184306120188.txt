1946. JANE EVES was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of June , 2 breast-pins and chain, value 4 l  ., the goods of Charles John Willett , from his person. CHARLES JOHN WILLETT. I am an auctioneer's clerk. On the 7th of June, I was walking down Vinegar-lane with a rose in my button-hole—the prisoner asked me to let her smell it—I took no notice—she came behind me, put her hand round my neck, and took the pins out of my scarf—I looked round, and could not see any one—I did not miss the pins till she was gone—I lost sight of her for half an hour. WILLIAM BROOKS( police-constable K  364.) I went with the prosecutor to Vinegar-lane, and saw the prisoner standing at the door—we went past her, and she called to the prosecutor, and said she had got his diamond pin, that she had tried to take the rose, and had taken the pin by mistake—the prose cutor said he would give her in charge—she ran into the house, and through into another house—I went and took her.
   Prisoners Defence.  I asked him to let me smell his rose; he would not; I put my arm round his neck, and the pin caught the sleeve of my gown; I said I could not run after him, but when he passed I would give it him. NOT GUILTY.
 1947. ANN CHILTON was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of June , 2 table cloths, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; 1 shift, 1 s  . 6 d  .; and 2 handkerchiefs, 2 s  .; the goods of Margaret Collins ; and that she had been previously convicted of felony. MARGARET COLLINS. I live in St. George's-in-the-East. My servant was ill, and I employed the prisoner—I missed a sovereign, two tablecloths, two handkerchiefs, and a shift—these are them. FREDERICK JOHN WOOD. I am a pawnbroker, I took in these two table cloths of the prisoner on the 1st of June—she said she brought them for her sister—I have the other articles, but I do not remember whether I took them of the prisoner. TIMOTHY HERLEHY. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner—I asked her if she knew any thing about the property—she said she had pledged it.
   Prisoner.  I only pledged the two table cloths. ROBERT BYERS. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's conviction, which I got at Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY  . Aged 15.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Nine Months, nine Weeks Solitary .
 1948. THOMAS FINN was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of June , 6 yards of printed cotton, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Ellen Murphy ; and 6 yards of printed cotton, 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Catherine Sullivan . ELLEN MURPHY. I now live in King David Fort, Shadwell. Six or seven weeks ago I and Sullivan took a room at the prisoner's—on the night of the 2nd of June, between seven and eight o'clock, he came into our room very tipsy, and told us to be off out of his place—he broke down the line, and took two gown pieces, two shifts, and a pair of black stockings—he took them down stairs—next night I asked him if he would give me the things or the tickets of them—he had a candlestick in his hand, and he said, "If you don't be off, I will split your head open"—I gave him in charge—this gown piece is mine, and this other is Sullivan's—I owed the prisoner nothing—I owed his wife 2 1/2 d .
   Prisoner.  I took the gown-pieces out of her mother's hand, and stopped them for rent.
