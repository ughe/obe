 2472. HENRY APPLEYARD was indicted for stealing 1/4 of a pound of tea, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; and 1/4 of a pound of tobacco, 1 s  .; the property of Ben jamin Lance , his master. BENJAMIN LANCE. I keep a shop in Church-street, Greenwich—the pri soner was in my employ—I lost a sovereign, and desired to search him—he ran up stairs—I caught him by the coat and found on him two parcels—one was tea, and the other tobacco—I suppose they were mine—he told the Ma gistrate that he removed it with the intention of telling me about it—29 s . 6 d . was found upon him, and he had borrowed money of me the day before to pay for his washing.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q.  Had you a good character with him? A.  Yes—his wages were 5 s . a week.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY . Aged 24.— Confined Six Months .

 2474. SUSAN PARKER was indicted for stealing 1 sovereign, and 1 half-sovereign; the moneys of Richard Humphrey Nunney , from the person of Susan Nunney . SUSAN NUNNEY. I am the wife of Richard Humphrey Nunney. On the 15th of Oct., about eleven o'clock in the morning, I was a little tipsy—I laid a sovereign a half-sovereign, and a shilling on the table—the prisoner came in, and soon after the money was gone—I was a little too tipsy to see who took it. MARY ANN HUNTER. I was at Mrs. Nunney's, in Church-street, Dept-ford, at eleven o'clock in the morning, on the 14th of Oct.—she was a little the worse for liquor—the prisoner came in with two quartern loves from the parish, which she offered to sell for a shilling—Mrs. Nunney said, "Take them home to your children"—she said, "No"—Mrs. Nunney gave her a shilling for them—the prisoner said, "I will send for a pot of beer"—Mrs. Nunney said, "Do as you like about that"—the prisoner then moved and sat down by Mrs. Nunney—she said she had no more than what she had received with the bread. CHARLES WEBB. On the morning of the 14th of Oct. the prisoner came to me and changed a sovereign, and I saw she had a half-sovereign beside. CHARLES SEAWOOD( policeman, R  219.) I produce a bundle of new things, which the prisoner said she had bought of her sister—she said she had released the things from pawn with a sovereign she had received of her sister.
   Prisoner's Defence.  She has been in the habit of buying these loaves of me; I went and sold her these, and I said I would send for a pot of beer; I left her sitting by the fire as drunk as he could be, with three or four women round her. GUILTY . Aged 39.— Confined Four Months .
