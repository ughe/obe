JOSEPH DALTON re-examined. I found no other property on Sulffin-I
asked him two or three times for it, and he said he had not got it-I did not
expose his person I put my hand into his left-hand trowsers pocket, and

Sulffin's Defence. I bought the handkerchief in Petticoat-lane for 6d, four
or five weeks ago; I know Knight; he used to drive a team of horses.
Knight's Defence. If he saw me covering this lad in a crowd, why did
not be come and take us directly, and not let us walk 200 or 330 yards.
