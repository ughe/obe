HENRY SMITH cross-examined by MR. CARTEEN. I know Anthony
Cunningham very well—I have heard my mother say he is a relation of
mine, but I do not know it-he was at my sister's the day the prisoner
was there-I never said I would make away with her-I never said to
him that I would make away with her if it was not for the laws of my!
country- I swear that, nor anything to that effect, nor that if I did not make
away with her I would transport her--I never uttered such words--I never
said I would endeavour to transport her-I swear that-I have certainly
said I would endeavour to get rid of her by legal means, by the law of my
country, by applying to a Magistrate - I have applied to a Magistrate to
know whether I am justified in bringing her before him for saying she was
married to me—I do not know that I have used those words to Cunning-
ham--I have said it to my mother and other persons who have spoken to
me about the concern-I have even said it to the witnesses who have
been examined, that I would get rid of her by legal means, so far a
the law would allow-I never said I would transport her.
