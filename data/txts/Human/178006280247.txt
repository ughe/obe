 410.  SARAH COLLOGAN was indicted for stealing a printed cotton gown, value 20 s. the property of Elisabeth Murray, spinster, June 6th.
   JAMES HYDE sworn.
 I belong to the Rotation-office in Litchfield-street; I was at Lord Mansfield's house with the magistrates a little before four o'clock on the Wednesday morning on which the house was destroyed; at about half after four I had an information that some of Lord Mansfield's property was at the house of the prisoner. I went to the house, and told the prisoner I was come to look for some things which came from Lord Mansfield's. While I turned my back to look in the lower room she went to the stair-case; I followed her immediately up stairs.
 Who gave you the information? - I took two people who used to lodge in the house with some of the property on them; they gave me an information that there were more things in the house. I saw the prisoner put this gown under the head of the bed; I laid hold of her and the gown together. She said that was all she had; she said it was given her by a man she did not know at Lord Mansfield's house. This is the gown (producing it.)
   HENRIETTA CASSMAJOR sworn.
 I am lady's woman to Miss  Elizabeth Murray; I know it to be her gown, having made it myself; I saw it in the house at ten o'clock over night, it was locked up in a press.
 PRISONER's DEFENCE.
 I went that night to see the fire, as many more did; a man came down the stairs with an arm full of linen and this gown; he put the gown up to my face, and said if I would not have it he should throw it into the fire; he threw the rest of the things into the fire.
     GUILTY. Imp. 12 months.
 Tried by the First Middlesex Jury before Mr. RECORDER.
 411.  ELIZABETH GRANT was indicted for stealing a copper pot, value 30 s. a copper-plate warmer, value 3 s. and a copper dripping-pan, value 10 s. the property of the Right Honourable William  Earl of Mansfield, June 6th.
   JOHN DIXON sworn.
 I belong to the Rotation-office in Litchfield-street; I went to the house of one Mr. Gocart, in Hog-lane, three or four days after the fire at Lord Mansfield's, there I found these copper things (producing the several articles mentioned in the indictment). Gocart said he knew nothing of them. The prisoner said she bought them when he was out of the way. I believe she is a servant in the house; it is an old iron-shop; I found some on the compter and some behind it, and some were in the back shop; I took the goods, and left the prisoner. Two or three days after that, when Lord Mansfield's servants came to town, and saw these things, they said they were Lord Mansfield's, I went and told the prisoner she must come to the office. She said she would come as soon as she had put on her cap; she came there soon after me.
   JAMES HYDE sworn.
 I went with Dixon to Gocart's; we found this copper, some in the fore and some in the back shop.
 (  George Wilkinson and Henry Richards, two of Lord Mansfield's servants, deposed, that the several articles produced were the property of the Earl of Mansfield.)
 PRISONER's DEFENCE.
 I am servant to Mr. Gocart. Four or five fellows came in with blue cockades, and brought this copper; I said I did not buy any thing; my master keeps an open shop. They said if I did not buy them they would say we were papists, and would have the house down.
