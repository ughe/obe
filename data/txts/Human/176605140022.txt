THE WHOLE PROCEEDINGS ON THE
 King's Commission of the Peace, Oyer and Terminer, and Goal Delivery held for the City of LONDON, &c.
 Continuation of the Trial of  George Sutton.
 Mr. Carr.
 I Was present at the time the prisoner wrote these two papers, as they were dictated to him; I have made the same observations as Mr. Tyser has: I have seen them all, and believe them to be all the same hand-writing, upon my conscience.
   John Duck. The prisoner served constable for me, (he takes the letters in his hand) I have seen him write one receipt; I am apt to think that in some things, this is very much like his handwriting.
 Mr. Blunt. I have seen the prisoner write, (he takes one of the letters in his hand) on the direction of this, the letters r, t, and d, in the words Aldersgate-street, have a great resemblance to his hand-writing, which I have in my hand.
 Prisoner's defence.
 I never wrote none of them; I know nothing of them indeed.
 To his character.
   John Magriger. I live upon Lambeth-hill, near Doctors-commons: I am a fan-stick-maker; I have known the prisoner fifteen years; he always bore a very good character; an honest fair dealing man as I ever dealt with in my life.
   Richard Hopkins. I live in Little Britain, and am in a branch of the buckle-making way; I have known him between 15 and 16 years; his character was never tainted in any shape in the world, till this that he is charged with.
 Q. What business is the prisoner in?
 Hopkins. He is something in the fan way.
   Robert Rigles. I live in the Borough, and am a taylor: I have known the prisoner near 3 years; I have had opportunities of seeing him pretty often, he is a very honest good natured man.
   William Craven. I live in Black friars, and am a victualler; I have known him about 20 years; he is a very honest industrious man, I never heard to the contrary.
   John Hill. I live in Black-friars; I have known him upwards of 20 years; I never heard any other by him, then that of an honest sober man.
   Thomas Cocknell. I live in the Borough of Southwark, and am a plaisterer; I have known him ever since the year 1759; I have had dealings with him several times; he is a very honest man.
   John Kidman. I am an oil-man and perfumer, and live in Newgate-street; I have known him 7 years; he has a very good character, I never heard to the contrary.
   Hannah Kempton. I live in Barnaby-street, Southwark; I have known him ever since he settled in business, which is 17 years; he always dealt exceeding honest with me, and bears the character of an honest man, I believe him to be such.
   Anne Saunders. I have known him about 18 years; he keeps a chandler's shop; I always thought him to be an honest sober man.
   Elizabeth Cook. I have known him 20 years; he is an honest man.
   Mary Evans. I live in Pelican-court; I have known him 17 years; he has a very good character, and is willing to work for his living.
   Rebeccah Sears. I have known him 17 years; his character is that of an honest man.
   John Stredwell. I live in Pelican-court, and am a spectacle-maker; I have known him four or five years; I believe him to be an honest man.
     Guilty. Imp. [Fine. See summary.] [Provide sureties for good behaviour. See summary.]
