THOMAS HENRY DUGGIN. I am & green-meat dealer of 15, Palmer

ket purposes.
JOHN THOMPSON. I am a hawker, of Turnham Green-on 3rd February
I went to Bryant's house at Acton, early in the morning, and saw Mose
on the sofa-I said, “Get up," and he got up and had breakfast and we
went out, and when we came home at night Moss said to Bryant “Will
you buy the harness?" Bryant said " I don't know; how much do you
want for it?"-he said "I will take 10s. for it”-Bryant said "I will
have it”-he gave him 68. that night, and he went to a public-house and
spent it, and came down to the stable next morning and Bryant gave him
the other 48.-next day Moss said that he would sell the cart, and fetched
it from the yard, but I had not sufficient money to buy it—the shaft was
broken and the back-board-he said I will take 41. and trust you for
what you have not got-Bryant paid 108., and he was trusted 31. 10s.
I cannot read-I saw them both write on & piece of paper-Road:
“Walter Bryant has bought of F. J. Moss a set of pony for 41., and paid
108. on account."
