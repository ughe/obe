about eight o'clock in the morning; he parted with five of the sixpences and

141. EMILY TAYLOR was again indicted for stealing 1 sixpence, the
money of Richard Harvey, her master,
RICHARD HARVEY. On Thursday, 11th Nov., I had an interview with
the officer-I gave him some money, with certain arrangements-I was to
come down the next morning, by a signal, at half-past eight o'clock, and
come to the till-I came, and found, instead of 4s, only 3s. 6d.-one six-
pence, which had been marked, was missing--we were just sitting down to
breakfast, and I said to the prisoner, “Have you any money of the goose-
club?" --she said, "Yes, up stairs"-I told her to fetch it down, and she came
and laid the marked sixpence, and some other money, on the table.
Nor GUILTY.
