   MR. WILDE to MR. SCULTHORPE. Q.  Have you not known instances where sorters have neglected to sort the letters properly, that they have de livered them themselves? A.  Certainly not; it is contrary to the regulations of the office—I do not know of one instance in particular where a sorter took a cab for the express purpose of delivering it.
