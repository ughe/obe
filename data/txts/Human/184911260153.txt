 143. SARAH ANN MILLER , stealing 1 guard-chain, value 6 l  . 6 s  .; the goods of William Postlethwaite , in the dwelling-house of Louisa Hagg . FREDERICK ALEXANDER. I am a surgeon, of Stratford. I knew the prisoner in the service of Miss Hagg, where the prosecutor, Mr. William Postlethwaite, resides—I know that he lost his guard-chain—I heard that the prisoner was seen with one; I communicated it to him, and he requested me to see after it—I waited on the prisoner two days afterwards, at the residence of her uncle—I asked her for the guard-chain—she denied it at first, hut afterwards she said she had parted with the chain—I said, "You will have no objection to tell me where you parted with it?"—she said to a young man—she went up-stairs to her uncle, and he brought down the duplicate—I took it home to Mr. Postlethwaite; he is not here; he is ill, and not able to-attend—I have this certificate from another surgeon—this is Mr. Postlethwaite's chain; he identified it, and gave his deposition—I saw him this morning, and it is impossible for him to come here—(the deposition teas here read  —" William Postlethwaite, on his oath, saith: 'I reside at 3, High-street, Stratford. I am lodging with Miss Hagg. The prisoner was servant in the house. On the 4th inst. I missed a gold watch-chain from a drawer in my bed-room; I had seen it a week before; this is it. I never gave the prisoner the chain, or any authority to pawn it.") THOMAS RAWLINSON. I saw the prisoner with this chain round her neck—I asked her how she came by it—she told me her brother gave it her, who is gone to sea. JOSEPH MOLES( policeman, K  327). I took the prisoner—she told me that Mr. Postlethwaite gave her the chain for having connexion with her. FREDERICK ALEXANDER re-examined. Q.  What is the nature of Mr. Postlethwaite's illness? A.  Paralysis; he has had several attacks—his mind is affected to some extent—I think he would not have given her the chain—his irritation of brain has made him excitable, but be knows the value of property, and would know when he lost anything. NOT GUILTY.
 144. SARAH ANN MILLER was again  indicted for stealing 1 ring, value 5 s  .; the goods of Louisa Hagg , her mistress. LOUISA HAGG. I am single. I reside at 3, High-street, Stratford—the prisoner was in my service—I lost a ring, and had asked her repeatedly about it, when I had missed other articles—I said I had lost it from the mantel piece, and she must have known about it—this is my ring ( produced ).
   JANE  Low. The prisoner is my daughter. I have been married again—she gave me this ring.
