I took the prisoner and received this shilling—she said that she was not aware it was bad—a gentleman gave it to her the previous night with 10 s .—on the way to the station she took a good florin from her pocket—a good half-crown and shilling were found in her purse—Mrs. Sutton said in the prisoner's hearing that she was there some days before and tendered bad money, the prisoner made no reply. MR. JOHN WEBSTER, This shilling is bad.
   The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate. "I know nothing about it; I received it from a gentleman the night before with some other silver." GUILTY of uttering the shilling only.— Four Months' Hard Labour.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, DECEMBER  1 ST , 1882.
