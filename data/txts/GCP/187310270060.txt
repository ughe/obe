670. JOHN SMITH (40), Stealing a pair of shoes, the property of James
Double.
MR. LATIMER conducted the Prosecution.
James DOUBLE. I am a shoemaker, at 37, Mitre Street, Aldgate-
have a workshop on the ground floor-on the 9th of this month, I was
working there ; about 5 o'clock in the evening, the prisoner came and spoke
to me in a most familiar way as if he had known me all his days-he said
“Shopmate, how are you 1"-he was an entire stranger to me-he said
“How is business!"- I looked about as I rather suspected the man-
perhaps I told him how I was off for work-"Have you got a job P" said
he--"Not more than I can do myself," and I turned to take a little article
off a shelf when I immediately missed the shoes and the prisoner, too
the shoes were at the doorway, hanging near where the prisoner was-I called
out to him to stop-he was running away-I ran after him and overtook
him at Smith's Buildings, and saw the shoes lying close to the pavement-
when I got there I found them in the hands of George Howell, a curman,
who stopped him--the prisoner said nothing, but he tried to trip the police
and the man that took hold of him-I charged him with stealing the shoes,
he said nothing.
