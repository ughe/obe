twelve bags of linseed, which had not been landed-w had commenced

Cross-ecamined by MR. OBRIN. Q. What time on the 7th did the barge
arrive A. In the morning--I do not know how much it contained, but
about a hundred bags were taken out that day--I can tell what was
missing by the bulk--I counted the vacant places did not count how
many bags were left the night before, but we commeneed in the centre, and
these were taken from another part which had not been disturbed-the
wharf is closed at a certain time at night, when we have done work, some-
times 7 o'elock, sometimes 8there had been seven or eight men engaged
in unloading the barge-the bags that we took out were put into the granary,
and are safe there now.
MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Was it your duty to be last on the premises 3
A4: Yes, first and last-nobody had a right to be on the premises after I
left-when I left on Friday night the ridges fore and aft were all perfect,
we having begun in the centre, and when I came on Saturday morning ten
bags at least had been taken away from the fore part.
