   MR. PATER to JOHN GIFFARD Q.  Are there a great many parties who sign for the manager of the bank? A.  Yes; but they each sign his own proper name, and not one for the other.
   COURT . Q.  Do you know any of those whose signature is attached to this paper? A.  No; there are about 150 persons in the Bank—it is necessary for notes to be signed by the manager, by procuration of some body, and by the accountant, by procuration of somebody, and until it has those two signatures it is not a note, but only a piece of paper—this is not the signature of the person authorized to sign for the accountant, and he has no power to give authority to anybody else to sign a note. GUILTY  . Recommended to mercy by the jury believing her to be the tool of another person. Confined Eighteen Months.

   MESSRS. CRAWFORD and LLOYD conducted the Prosecution CHARLES JOHN SUNMAN.
