THOMAS BURCHELL. I am a policeman. I went to Mr. Dexter's

had them about three months, I found twenty-three duplicates on him.

shop-this spoon was brought to me by Mr. John James Purnell to have

did not come to work on the Monday or Tuesday-on Wednesday morning

sent it out-it was then shown me by the policeman.
