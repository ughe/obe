JOHN JOSEPH MARTINDALE I am a hat maker. The prisoner was my
town and country traveller; the terms of his engagement were, 18. in the
pound commission on country business, 6d. in the pound for town business, and
14 per cent. upon all cash he received, in addition to his other commission-
gave him no salary, and advanced no money for his expenses his duties were,
the greater part of the time, in town-I sent a boy out with him to take
his parcels, and it was his duty to account every evening, less discount, and I
put it into the book from his dictation—that was the town account he did
not make out an account himself-he did not keep a book—there was
another person besides me to whom he could account for the country
accounts-it is possible that that person might have received his account of
town transactions; he is here the prisoner never accounted to me for
41. 28. received of a person named Hill—he admitted it when I questioned
him, but did not account at the time-he never accounted to me for 31. 28.
received from Eastwood, or for 81. from a person named Horne-he has
never accounted for these four amounts in this account-I asked him if he
would make out a statement of the facts of what he had really taken, and
then he opened his mind-I laid before him a list of debts not accounted
for, and he said that he had received them, but did not account for them at
the time-some of them were not accounted for for two months after they
were received-31. 2s., which he received from Mr. Eastwood, was not
accounted for at the time he received it, but he did account for it-I have
not got my books, to say how soon afterwards; he has never been in the
habit of paying me till a month or two months afterwards, and has then
paid me with somebody else's money-it was his duty to pay me at once,
