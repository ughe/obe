THOMAS HILL I was an engine driver on the North Kent line, and was
the driver of the 9.15 train on the night the accident occurred our train
stopped at Blackheath, and after taking in passengers we proceeded on
towards Lewisham-I was on the engine-Hall, the fireman, was with me
--the guard gave me the signal to start from Blackheath-We were fourteen
or fifteen minutes late-we had been detained slightly at Charlton and
Woolwich-our train would go through Lewisham to New Cross-We were
stopped at Lewisham by the distance signal—it had a red light-I first saw
that about the first bridge from Blackheath, the Love Lane bridge--I
turned the steam off before I reached that bridge--I cannot say whether
the breaks were applied by the guards; they would not require it-
whistled, but not to put the breaks on-I do not think they were put on
our train all but stopped at the distance signal, and we proceeded between
the distance signal and the semaphore, or station signal-we went in past
the distance signal, and were between the two-the station signal was at
danger also, red we were just moving as we passed the distance sigual-
we could have stopped within two or three yards I did that for the pro-
tection of my train--it just went in beyond the distance signal, as we should
be safe there from anything following us.
