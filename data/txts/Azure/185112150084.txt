I examined the back-door, and found several marks on it, which bad been
made with some blunt instrument—between 4 and 5 1 went to the prisoner'.

house; I have measured it—if a person wanted to go from the fence to where

into Hare-street—the place where the prisoner was taken has been pointed

house, and found him lying on a bed with all his clothes on except a coat and
hat—it was then between half-past 4 aild cannot say the time exactly,
as I was delayed at the prosecutor's examining the place—I knocked at the
