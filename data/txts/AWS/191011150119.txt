the property of Frederick Brown.
FREDERICK BrowN, 20, Canonbury Street, Essex Road, Islington.
At 12 p.m. on October 8 I left my bicyele outside Finch's in Holborn.
I was inside five minutes and on my return I found it had gone. I
informed the police. On October 28 I identified it at the police
station. Its value is E7. I found the transfers and the shoe on the
front brake missing. I identify it by the left-hand fork being pivoted
by two holes cut for the purpose, which is unusual, also by a peculiar
fitment of baok mudguard. I have had it twelve months.

which shows that it is of the same make as mine.
