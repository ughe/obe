LIAMS, were indicted for stealing, on the 9th of May, 1 bag, value 2s.;
5 planes, value 31. 45.; and 6 saws, value 18.; the goods of Joseph
Shorley: 4 planes, value 21. 108.; and 6 saws, value 31. ; the goods of
Isaac Weller: aud 7 planes, value 31.; 2 saws, value 11. 58.; and 1 bevi,
value 38.; the goods of John Green.
JOSEPH SHORLEY. I am a cabinet maker. I lost some tools from a
workshop in Little Titchfield-street, Marylebone--I saw them safe about
half past six or seven o'clock in the evening on Tuesday last-they were
gone in the morning-the padlock had been broken, and some boards and
another part of the shop at the side.
John WILLIAM HAWKINS. (police-constable F 101.) On Tuesday night
last, at twenty minutes to eleven o'clock, I saw Dench and Wyatt crossing
the Seven Dials-Dench had got this carpet bag, and some articles in
it-and Wyatt was carrying a drugget with some more things in it-I
stopped them, not giving a satisfactory account, and I took them to the
station-house-on searching Dench, I found this lot of tools-on search-
ing Wyatt, I found some other tools in the drugget, and this small saw,
which comes to pieces, he had in his hand.
ISAAC WELLER. I was at work in these premises as journeyman.
This plough plane, and other things, are mine.
JOHN GREEN. I am a cabinet-maker. I was at work at this place-
part of these tools are mine, and some I lost, which have not yet been found.
WILLIAM CHAPMAN. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Long Acre.
About half-past ten o'clock in the morning of the 10th of May, Williams
offered two planes with the marks on, which we had received an account of
-I detained him and gave him in charge.
ISAAC WELLER. One of these is mine-the other is Green's.
RICHARD GABRIEL GABB (police-sergeant F 14.) I was sent for, and
took Williams.
CHARLES HENRY BAGNALL (police-constable P 31.) About eleven
o'clock on the 10th of May, I was in Long Acre, and saw some tools tied
up on a pitching block-I kept my eye on them-I asked a man about
them-he gave me information, and I went into the public house, but
could not find any one-I then kept watching it for three-quarters of an
hour-and then I took them and found it was four planes.
