ago I bought eleven shirts of one Weston-I saw them in my possession last
about twelve months ago, I cannot say when I had the scarfs safe, because I
have so many of the same description-I never sold the shirts.

the name of Gravel A. Yes, till last April-he robbed me to a very great

WILLIAM WESTON. I am a manufacturer of sbirts. About two years and
a half ago I had sbirts in my possession-I know this one-I sold it to the
prosecutor-there were eleven of this kind-this one has my own mark on it.
Cross-examined. Q. What is there particular about it? A. It is marked
outside-it has a calico body, with linen collar, front, and wrists-I may sell
200 dozen of these shirts in the course of a year-I know I sold this to Mr.
Mills, because I have referred to my book-my brother, who is dead, made
the entry-I took them in from Boswell, who was then the work-woman-I
know that, because I put the mark on it, and wrote “Boswell" on it--she
has not worked for me since, because she spoiled these goods-she had made
hundreds before-all the shirts that come from her do not bear her mark,
unless they are badly made-this is the only shirt I speak to.
