St. Pancras. Between 12 and 12.30 a.m. on January 9 I went to bed,

which is my husband's property, in a basket on the dining-room side-
board. I cannot say for certain whether the scullery window was
locked. On coming down at 7.15 the next morning the plate in the
basket was gone.
