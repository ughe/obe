 1302.  ROBERT COACH was indicted for feloniously receiving, on the 4th of June , 50 lbs. weight of coffee, value 3l., and 1 bag, value 6d., the goods of Sarah Wilkinson , which had lately before been stolen, he knowing the same: against the Statute.
   WILLIAM BARCLAY. I am a grocer, and in the employ of Mrs. Sarah Wilkinson. On the 4th of June, in the evening, I lost a bag of coffee from the door; this is it - a witness saw it taken, and we went and found it.
   JOHN BEST. I am a painter, and live in Holywell-street, Shoreditch. Between eight and nine o'clock in the evening of the 4th of June, I was in Shoreditch, and saw three people together; I do not know who they were, but they went to Mrs. Wilkinson's shop, and took a bag of coffee - they went with it to a chandler's shop at the hack of Shoreditch-church; I went and told Mrs. Wilkinson's people of it - they went to Worship-street; the last witness is her foreman - the officer found the coffee on the stairs.
 JAMES OSMOND PHILPOT. I belong to Bow-street. On the night of the 4th of June, I went near the Loggerheads public-house, and saw the prisoner come from his own house, which is near there; I went after him, down a passage, took him by the arm, and said I wanted him - I took him to his own house, and asked if he was the owner of it; he said, Yes - I said I had suspicion there was some stolen property concealed there; he said he knew nothing about any - I went with him into the back room, and shut the door; I searched, and in about a quarter of an hour, I found the bag of coffee about half way up the stairs - I believe Best was at the door, or in the shop; it is a chandler's shop, at the back of Shoreditch-church - I took the coffee to Worship-street, where the foreman owned it.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. The prisoner is married? A. I do not know; I saw a person I understood was his wife - I believe my expression to the prisoner was "Stolen goods," but I allnded to the coffee; he said he had no stolen goods that he knew of in the house.
 JOHN BEST re-examined Q. Did you say you saw some persons take some coffee from Mrs. Wilkinson's to a shop at the back of Shoreditch-church? A. Yes - it was about half-past eight o'clock, to the best of my recollection.
 Q. Did you see any thing of the prisoner at that time? A. No, Sir, I cannot positively say I did; there was some one in the shop - there was a woman there, but no man except the one that took the coffee; Mr. Wilkinson's two shopmen were in the shop; I did not observe any other persons - I was before the Magistrate on Thursday.
 Q.And now you state you did not observe any body else? A. Yes.
 Q. What did you say to the Magistrate - the same as you have now? A. Yes, Sir, to the best of my recollection; I saw a woman in the prisoner's shop.
 Q. No man? A. Not to my recollection.
 Q. Do not you recollect whether there was or not? A. I believe not, to the best of my recollection.
 Q.Then you do not recollect seeing any man but the shopmen and the thieves in Mrs. Wilkinson's shop. and no men but the thieves in the prisoner's shop? A. No.
 RICHARD FICKRIDGE. I am a Bow-street patrol. I went with Philpot to a house that I have understood since is Mr. Cauch's, in Virginia-row - the prisoner was there, without his coat, and his shirt sleeves tucked up; I asked him for half a pint of small beer, which I paid him for, and he said it was right; I turned to speak to my conductor, and the prisoner rushed by me, and ran up a passage - I ran and caught him by the right arm, and said, "This won't do;" I handed him to Philpot. and I stood there while my conductor and Philpot went to search the house; in twenty minutes I was called into the house, and there was this bag of coffee and a roll of cloth; I took the coffee, and Philpot took the cloth - the prisoner was gone then; he went out while I stood in the passage - the next morning I took him at the toll-bar near Shoreditch church.
   WILLIAM BARCLAY re-examined. Q. Did you look into the shop? A. Yes, when I had information from Best; I saw the coffee in a bag that night, but not in the same bag it had been in; I believe it was Philpot showed it me- I did not go up stairs; it was brought down: this is the bag the coffee was in, not this bag; this is Mrs. Wilkinson's bag.
 JAMES  OSMOND PHILPOT re-examined. The woman who was in the shop is not here.
 COURT. Q. This man allowed you to search very freely? A. Yes - he made no resistance; the witness went to the shop, but I do not think he said any thing in the prisoner's hearing.
     NOT GUILTY.
