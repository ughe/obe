   ROBERT TURLE. I am a Policeman. I saw the prosecutor and prisoner disputing: I went up, and he gave her in charge for stealing 2s. 6d. from his pocket - she denied it; no money was found on her.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1793.  ELIZA STEWARD. was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of August , 1 ring, value 15s.; 2 necklaces, value 30s.; 1 pair of bracelets, value 10s., and 3 yards of ribbon, value 2s.; the goods of Thomas Edenson , her master; to which she pleaded. GUILTY. - Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 1794.  DANIEL KEEFE was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of September , I handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of Thomas Cheshire , from his person.
 THOMAS CHESHIRE. I live with Mr. Graham, in Holborn. On the 13th of September, between six and seven o'clock in the evening, I was in  Holborn, about twenty yards from king-street; a boy gave me information - I looked, and saw the prisoner and another boy running away; I pursued, and never lost sight of the prisoner - he was stopped about three parts of the way down King-street, by Yates; I was calling Stop thief! - he said he was not the boy, and denied having any handkerchief; I found it concealed under his coat - it was safe in my outside coat pocket when I left home ten minutes before.
 Cross-examined by MR. FRENCH. Q. Was the other a tall boy? A. Rather taller than the prisoner; he ran behind the boy, but I noticed the prisoner most, as I noticed the position of his arm, and that he had the handkerchief under it - I felt my pocket the moment the boy told me; I did not feel it taken - it is not probable that it could have fallen out of my pocket; I did not feel it taken.
   JOSEPH YATES. I am a tailor. I heard the prosecutor call stop thief! and saw the prisoner coming along alone - I stopped him; the prosecutor came up and said he had stolen a handkerchief; he said, "I am not the person; I have got nothing about me;" but the prosecutor took it from under his arm, between his coat and shirt.
 Cross-examined. Q. He was alone? A. Yes, when I saw him; I caught him in my arms as he ran; he did not say he had picked it up, but that he had nothing about him.
   CHARLES FIELD. I am a policeman. I heard a cry of Stop thief! at the corner of King-street; I turned round and saw Yates take hold of the prisoner - I was at the further end of the street.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up - I denied having it, not knowing he was the right owner.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 14. - Transported for Seven Years.

 1795.  GEORGE SMITH and JOHN LEWIS were indicted for a misdemeanor.
 MR. PHILLIPS conducted the prosecution.
   BENJAMIN JONES. I am a slopseller, and live at No. 10, Glasshouse-yard, Minories. I had known the prisoner Smith five or six days before this transaction; I had advanced him money - I am in the habit of advancing seamen who come into port, money or clothes before they receive their wages, and selling my goods at a profit as others do - on the 19th of July the two prisoners came to my house; I was not at home when they came, but when I came in Smith said, "Jones, I promised you if I should fall in with any of my shipmates, I would bring them to you to let them have clothes till pay-day" - he said Lewis was Thomas Morgan; I am sure of that, and sure that Lewis is the man - he introduced him as wanting clothing till pay-day, which I gave him - I knew Smith belonged to the Lady Melville, an East Indiaman, according to his own account, and I wrote to Mr. King to know if any body named Thomas Morgan was a seaman on board that ship; I was satisfied that there was - Lewis put his mark, as Morgan, to the order for clothes, in the presence of Smith; I have it here - in consequence of Smith's representation, I believed Lewis to be morgan, and furnished him with a jacket, waistcoat and trousers, two pairs of shoes, a cotton shirt, a black silk handkerchief, a bat, and a cotton handkerchief; Smith said to Lewis, "Tom, you owe me a pair of shoes on the voyage, and while you are here you may as well let Jones give me a pair, and put them down to you" - I am sure he called him Tom; he had them, and I put them to Morgan's account - I had a few days before advanced Smith 30s., which he repaid me afterwards at the India-house; I have never been paid for these clothes.
