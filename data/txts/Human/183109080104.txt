 1750.  JAMES LEE was again indicted for embezzlement.
 THE  REV. JAMES MACDONALD. The prisoner was in my service - he received money on my account, and paid them to Warner, the book-keeper - Agnes Nicholls was a customer: he has not accounted to me for any sums received from her.
 Cross-examined by MR. ALLEY. Q. He did not account to you? A. No; Mr. Warner is the person he should account to.
   AGNES NICHOLLS. I keep a boarding-school in Kentroad. In December last my linen was washed at the Steam Washing Company - I paid these bills at the office in the Strand, but to whom I do not know; Mr. Warner and another were there, but which wrote the receipt I do not know.
 Prisoner. To save trouble I admit they are my writing.
   WILLIAM WARNER. I was in the employ of the Steam Washing Company. The prisoner did not pay me this 2l. 3s. 11/2d., but it has been accounted for since - he gave me some paper about February, and there is a memorandum about Miss Nicholls and others, but he never gave me the money.
 Q. When did he leave the service? A. He was in the habit of coming till February - I have not brought the papers here.
 MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. While he was in the office did he inform you of an error to this amount? A. He gave me the papers in February; the prosecutor had forbid his coming to the office then, but he was in the habit of coming and transacting business as usual.
 COURT. Q. Did he account to you weekly or how? A. He should account when he received it; it was in the latter end of February that he gave me the papers - he charges himself with this sum, but never paid it; I do not recollect the account being paid.
 MR. MACDONALD. The accounts should be communicated to Mr. Warner the same day, and entered.
 Prisoner's Defence. There was a great sum of money out - I received monies, and paid them; when Smith was discharged I took charge of the works - Mr. Macdonald came down and sent me to town to receive the accounts, and it is not likely I should account to an inferior clerk; I kept an account of what I received - I received this sum, and stuck the papers on the file, with a quantity of papers, and I overlooked it; in February I went to the file, and seeing these papers, I said, "Warner, I received from Miss Nicholls 2l. 2s. 6d., give me credit for it, I have credited Mr. Macdonald for it in my cash-book" - and the information he has got is from that very cash-book. If I intended wrong him should I have settled it at all?
 MR. MACDONALD. I desired Warner to receive mone when I dismissed the prisoner; that was about the 10th of January.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1751.  JAMES LEE was again indicted for stealing, on the 6th of January, 1831 , 1 order for payment of and value 7l. 13s., the property of James Macdonald.
 MESSRS. ADOLPHUS and CLARKSON conducted the prosecution.
   REV. JAMES MACDONALD. The prisoner was in my service in 1830 - on the 6th of January he applied to me for 7l. 13s. 4d. to pay to Halliday and Carter for some ironingblankets; he drew  the body of a cheque, and I signed it- this is it - the 4d. is omitted; I gave it to him to pay to Halliday and Carter - it is drawn in their name; on the 24th of April he again applied to me for a sum to pay to them - I think it was 8l. 11s. - I gave him a cheque for that - I did not know what the goods were, nor did I see any bill - I took it from his representation - he wrote the body of this cheque, and I signed it for goods had of the same persons; I discovered that I had paid these two sums on investigating my accounts.
 Cross-examined by MR. ALLEY. Q. I believe Messrs. Halliday and Carter live in Coleman-street? A. Yes; I discovered in April or May last that these two cheques had been received.
   JOHN PRIOR. I am clerk to Bosanquet and Co., bankers; I paid this cheque for 7l. 13s. on the 6th of January, 1830.
   JEREMIAH CARTER. I am of the firm of Halliday and Carter - we supplied blankets to the Steam Washing Company in 1830 - I have the books here; on the 2nd of January, 1830, we delivered goods to the amount of 7l. 13s. 4d. - I believe the bill of parcels went with the goods - on the 14th of January we supplied them with six pairs of cards, which came to 1l.; we had no other dealing with them till the 29th of May - we generally allow a discount for prompt payment - the first money we received was on the 23rd of April, 8l. 10s. discount 3s. 4d. - I received it by this cheque - this other cheque for 7l. 13s. I believe was never in my hand till now.
 Cross-examined. Q. You have known the prisoner many years? A. Yes - I entered his name in our book as the person to whom the goods were to be delivered, and made out the receipt in his name; this is it - it was given by my clerk; he stated it was on account of a Washing Company, and the gentleman did not wish his name to be known - I knew nothing of Mr. Macdonald, but he afterwards called on me and told me to make out the account to the Washing Company; the whole of the balance has not been paid to me.
 MR. ADOLPHUS. Q.Had you received any money for goods between January and April? A. No - the balance not yet paid is a subsequent account. (Cheques read).
 MR. ALLEY to MR. MACDONALD. Q. You had no account with this house till after these goods were purchased? A. No - I was arrested at the prisoner's suit, I think in March; it was before that I called on Messrs. Halliday and Carter to order the alteration to be made in the bills, as I had found out various things.
