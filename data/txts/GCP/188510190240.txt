before us--she was to give her experience, and, if possible, she was to
help in certain transactions of a detective nature, in order to bring to
light the existence of these crimes-she was not to go to children other
than those that were in the market-those instructioos were given her
first by Stead in my presence--they were not full instructions, they were
forced by me on Jarrett--Mr. Booth was a party to this arrangement a
well as Mr. Stead, and acquiesced in the arrangement-it was to be a
joint-purse arrangement between Mr. Stead, Mr. Bramwell Booth,
mysell, and Mr. Benjamin Soott, the Chamberlain of the City of London
