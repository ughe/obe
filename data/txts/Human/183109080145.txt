 1868.  THOMAS HOLLINGBURY was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of September , 1 handkerchief, value, 4s., and 1 snuff-box, value 2s., the goods of George Fitch , from his person.
   GEORGE FITCH. I live in Piccadilly. On the 8th of September, in the afternoon, I was in Parliament-street, and was spoken to by Sheppard, a Policeman - I missed from my coat pocket my handkerchief and snuff-box; my pocket was cut.
   WILLIAM SHEPPARD. Between three and four o'clock in the afternoon, of the 8th of September, I was in Parliament-street; there was a crowd collected at the Coronation - I saw the prisoner feel the pockets of several gentleman; I then saw him go to Mr. Fitch - he seemed very busy with his hands for about five minutes; I then put my hand on his shoulder, and his hand came from the prosecutor's pocket - I asked Mr. Fitch if he had lost any thing; he said he had; I found the snuff-box in the prisoner's trousers pocket, and the handkerchief dropped behind him.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you not see me pick it up and put it into my pocket? I said, "If there has been a robbery the man has gone along;" directly he moved I saw the box on the ground - you said, "Have you got any thing?" and I gave it you from my pocket; I knew nothing of the handkerchief. Witness. I saw his hand very busy about Fitch's pocket; he never stooped, and could not have picked it up - he was exactly behind Fitch.
 JURY to  GEORGE FITCH. Q.Did the prisoner give up the box, or did the officer take it from his pocket? A. The officer took it from his pocket.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. - Confined Twelve Months.
 1869.  ELIZA GILHAM was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of August , 1 medal, value 1/2d., and 19s., the property of James Shilton , from his person.
   JAMES SHILTON. I belong to a brig, which laid in Bellwharf tier. On the 30th of August I was at the Paviors' Arms public-house. I changed a sovereign, and laid out 9d.; I then had 19s. 3d., which I put into my pocket, with a medal of the late Duke of York - I saw the prisoner at the house, and gave her something to drink; I had a bad bowel complaint, and asked her to show me a bed - she took me to a house, and I laid down with my waistcoat on; I vomited, and then went to sleep - I awoke about half-past three o'clock, and found my pockets entirely emptied; I was alone in the room - I went and described her at the Police-office.
 Cross-examined by MR. BALL. Q. How much did you have at the house? A. A pint of beer, and half a pint of gin; I did not drink it all - I did not notice how many girls were there; I treated nobody but the prisoner - I merely went to out of the room - I did not send her out for gin, nor give her any money at all.
   PATRICK DUFFY. I am a Policeman. On the evening of the 31st of August, I found Shilton at the door of a noted brothel, in Isaacs-buldings, Bluegate-fields; in consequence of what he told me I apprehanded the prisoner, about five o'clock in the morning, at a coffee-shop; she asked me if the man had got up from the bed - I said Yes, and asked her for his money; she said she had none belonging to him; I searched her, and found 8s. and the Duke of York's medal on her - I took her to the house, and directly Shilton saw her identified her; she then said, all the money he had was 5s. 9d., and she could not have robbed him of 19s. - that he gave her the medal and 5s. 9d.
 Cross-examined. Q.Was he not very much intoxicated? A. Not at all; the first thing the prisoner said to me was," Is the man got out of bed."
 (Medal produced and sworn to.)
 Priosner's Defence. What he says is false; if he changed a sovereign it was before I was in his company- when I went in he sat there drinking; he asked me and ten other females to drink - he then asked me to show him a bed, which he gave 1s. 6d. for; he gave me 5s. 9d.
     GUILTY. Aged 34. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1870.  THOMAS GATES was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of August , 2 skates, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Mosely.
 THOMAS BOYCE. I am a constable of Hampsted district. On the evening of the 6th of August I saw the prisoner in company with another boy go into Mosely's shop- the other boy took down a cage, which had white mice in it, and the prisoner took down a pair of skates - I pursued him, and took him to the watch-house; I found the skates on him.
 Prisoner. The prosecutor took the value of the property, and here is the paper he signed for it.
   THOMAS MOSELY. I have not been paid for them - I know nothing of this paper, and never saw it before; I did not sign this paper - I know nobody who lives in Tothill-street; as far as I am concerned this paper is a forgery altogether; the prisoner's friends came and offered me money to keep from here, but I refused it.
   JOSEPH GATES. I am the prisoner's brother. Thomas Mosely signed that paper, and took 2s. from me, being the full value of the property; he told me if I would give him 5s. to pay his expences to save him coming here, he would accept of it - he took 2s., and we spent 3s. in drink; I, him, and another friend.
 THOMAS MOSELY. I never drank with this man in my life, or spent 3s. in his compary, nor received 2s. from him.
