Cross-examined by Mr. Price.. Q. Were you very intimately ac-
quainted with Mrs. Brown ? A. Yes my husband and I occupied the
froat parlour next door to her-I was in the habit of seeing her every
other day or Bor she got her living by washing and mangling- she was
very much at home, occupied with her business, the whole of the day
generally she had nobody to assist her-- she generally did all her work
hersell-I have seen the prisoner Greenacre I do not remember when I
first saw him it was some weeks before he took her away I believe he
come frequently to see her, but not being in the same house I did not see
him-- I was not present when he used to come to see her the lodger's
name is Mrs. Hawksworth, from whom I received the key--Mrs. Brown
told me that the reason she did not give me the key was, she had some few
things in the room which did not belong to her she had a right to occupy
the room till the Tuesday there was only one room she said she should
return on Monday, and then only to give up the key, and pay the rent-
I did not receive the rent till Wednesday my husband was the first person
who went into the room-she is here I went in with him, and found
nothing in the room but an empty birdcage, of no value—it belonged to
Mrs. Brown-she had offered it for sale for a trifle- I am quite certain
there was nothing else in the room,
Ms. CLARKSON. Q. You, did not live in the house where Mrs. Brown
resided? A. No-it was on the Wednesday after the Saturday she left
that I received the key from the lodger any one who had the key coula
have gone into the room without my knowledge, and taken any thing out
-while I knew Mrs. Brown she was a very sober woman.
WILLIAN GAY. I live in Goodge-street. The deceased was my sister
just before her death we were not on friendly terms-I saw her the
Thursday before her death, at my mistress's shop-I am a broker, doing
business for a lone woman, who keeps a broker's shop, No. 10, Goodge-
street--it was there I saw my sister on the Thursday- she was alone-I
know the prisoner Greenacre-I do not remember having seen him before
