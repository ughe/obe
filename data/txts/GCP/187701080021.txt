DAVID PEARSON. I live at 8, Three Foxes Court, Ratcliffe, and am a

at a public-house door-he came up and struck me with his fist on my face,
then he took a stevedore hook from his pocket and struck me across
the face with it-my face bled very much-this scar is from the wound;
my wife and child were present the prisoner then went to his own house-
I did not strike him-I never challenged him to fight or quarrelled with him
-Iknow him well by sight-the police doctor sewed up the wound.

not drunk-no blood came from the first blow-the disturbance only lasted
about a minute and a half.
