JOHN NEWELL (policeman, R 67). I was on duty at Woolwich on 20th
Dec., about half past 6 o'clock in the evening-I saw the prisoner going up
Powis-street, with a bundle under his arm-he went into a second hand
clothes shop-I went in a minute after him-I saw Mr. Hart, who keeps
the shop, looking at these trowsers-I asked the prisoner how he became
possessed of them- he said he was a tailor, and he made them--he after-
wards said he bought them of a recruit.
