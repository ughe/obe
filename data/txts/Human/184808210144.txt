 1960. JOSEPH FRANCIS , stealing 3 plants and 2 flower pots, value 1 s  . 7 d  .; the goods of William Lee . his master. EDWARD LEE. I am a market-gardener, at Hammersmith. I saw the prisoner on 26th May with a water-pot on his shoulder, and a jacket over it—he was in my son's service—when he saw me he did not come out of the ground to go to breakfast as the other men did—I went to him—he slipped the jacket off his shoulder on to the water-pot—I looked into it and saw these plants. WILLIAM LEE. These plants are mine—the prisoner had no business with them. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined One Month.
 1961. CATHERINE SPEEDE , stealing 1 watch, value 1 l  .; the goods of Peter Reitz , from his person; having been before convicted. PETER REITZ( through an interpreter ). I am a tailor, of Great Russell-street. On 1st Aug. I went to a house with the prisoner—she asked what time it was—I took out my watch and shewed it her—in about two minutes she took it and went away—there was no one there but her—I am sure she is the woman. CATHERINE FERGEUE. I was in a public-house drinking—the prisoner offered me something yellow—I would not accept of it. SARAH FREEMAN. I saw the prisoner offer her something—she would not take it, but told her to go and give it to the man—the prisoner offered me something yellow—I told her I would not take it—I did not know how she got it, nor what it was. JAMES EVES( police-sergeant, H  14). I produce a certificate of the pri soner's conviction—( read—Convicted May , 1846— confined three months )—she is the person—she has been in custody since then. GUILTY. † Aged 25.— Transported for Seven Years .
 1962. HENRY HUNTLEY , stealing 47 buckles, 8 ornaments, and other articles, value 19 s  . 4 d  .; the goods of Charles Fillingham , his master. GEORGE TREW( City-policeman , 26). I found a great many buckles and other articles at the prisoner's lodgings—the prisoner said the next day he was very sorry he had robbed his master, another man advised him to do so. HENRY WEBB( City-policeman , 258). I found a great number more bottles—the prisoner told me they were in tire cupboard. CHARLES FILLINGHAM. The prisoner was in my service—these are all mine. (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months .
