215. JOHN KENNY (25), Stealing 4 coats, value 208., of William Wood,
having been before convicted, to which he PLEADED Guilty. ***
Four Years' Penal Servitude.
216. JAMES THORN (27), Burglary in the dwelling-house of John
Atkin, and stealing his goods; to which he
PLEADED GUILTY.-Confined Nine Months.
217. MICHAEL ZINO (18), Stealing 158., 1 lb. of cigars, and 12 packets
of chocolate of Joseph Gatti, in his dwelling-house; to which he
PLEADED Guilty.-Confined Nine Months.
218. JAMES SMITH (53), Stealing 20 ounces of gun-metal, value 18d.,
of Edmund Pontifex and others, his masters; to which he
PLEADED Guilty.-Confined Twelve Months.
219. WILLIAM GLADWIN (36), Stealing 7 lbs. weight of lead, and
2 ounces of brass, of Edmund Pontifex and others, his masters; to which he
PLEADED GUILTY.-Confined Twelve Months.
220. JAMES HUDSON (29), Embezzling the sum of 91. 158. of the
Mayor, Commonalty, and Citizens of the City of London, his masters; also the
sum of 131. 10s. 3d, of his said masters; to both which he
PLEADED GUILTY.
He received an excellent character, and Mr. ROBINSON for the defence stated
that the money had not been taken for himself, but to meet the defalcations of his
father, a tax collector.
Confined Four Months.
221. RICHARD OLIVER (23), Unlawfully obtaining l shawl, value
31. 28. 6d.; also 2 shawls, value 51. 108., of William Morley and another;
also 2 other indictments for obtaining other goods of other persons by false
pretences; to all which he
PLEADED GUILTY.
Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutors.-Confined Eighteen Months.
