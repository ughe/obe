I was one evening at No. 1 1/2 South Conduit-street—I heard the prisoner tell Jane Allen to take care of the paper of nails he had brought there from his father—they were for a pair of sofas, and were tied up in a paper.

 1737. MARY ROACH was indicted for embezzlemont. CHARLES BENZONI. I live in Duke-street, St. James, and am a milk man The prisoner was in my service to carry out milk and receive money—Mr. Hill, of the Salopian Coffee-house, Charing-cross, was indebted to me. HARRIETT SMITH. I live at the Salopian Coffee-house. I paid the pri soner 11 s . 2 d . on the lst of June, for his master; on the 14th of June, 7 s .; and on the 20th of June, 7 s . more. CHARLES BENZONI. I did not get either of these sums.
   Cross-examined by MR. JONES . Q.  You employed her by the week? A.  Yes; I gave her 6 s . per week, and what she could get from customers—she had breakfast and tea with us—she has been about twelve or fifteen months with us—she was not at liberty to deduct her wages from the sums she received—her husband was out of employ for seven months—she has children, I belive. GUILTY  . Aged 29.—Recommended to mercyby the Jury. — Confined Ten Days.
