1948. MATILDA CONNOLLY was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd
of June, 1 watch, value 21.; 1 guard-chain, value 16s.; and 1 handker-
chief, value 48.; the goods of William Sprake, from his person.
WILLIAM SPRAKE. I am a pot-boy, and live in George-street. St.Giles.
On the 2nd of June, about half-past five o'clock in the afternoon, I called
at Bainbridge-street to see a friend--I saw the prisoner there, I asked be
if George was at home, meaning the person she lived with she said 10-
we had something to drink-I then went out by myself, for two hours and
a half or three hours- I then returned there, as I wished to see George-1
saw the prisoner again-she asked if my watch was safe-I said it was
she said it would be better for me to give it up to her, to give to the land
lady to take care of-I said I was quite capable of taking care of it mysel
-she would insist on taking it, and took it out of my pocket, took the guan
off my neck, and the silk handkerchief out of my cap, and went out-I had
had a little to drink, but I knew what I was about, and was capable of
taking care of myself-she returned to the room in two or three minutes
and I asked if she had left my watch with the landlady-she said, "Yes."
and I was satisfied-she then went out-I asked if she was going for
George-she said, “Yes," but, instead of that, she fastened me in the
room, and left word not to let me out-I afterwards gave her into custody
--my watch and handkerchief have not been found.
BRIDGET PRENDERGAST. I was staying with a person in the next room
at this house-the prisoner showed me a handkerchief the same evening
I heard her tell the prosecutor that she would take the watch round
Mrs. Sparrow, the landlady-the prisoner was drunk, and I called out
"Don't take the watch to Mrs. Sparrow"-she and the prosecutor bad
been together, and drinking-they passed as brother and sister to
her she had better leave the watch with Ann--that was the person I was
staying with
Joseph SMALLEY (police-constable K 175.) I took the prisoner on the
3rd of June- I told her it was for stealing a watch in St. Giles's--she said
she had not been in St. Giles's for a fortnight.
Prisoner's Defence. I have not seen it.
GUILTY. Aged 30.-Confined Four Months.
