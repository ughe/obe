garet's-road, Haggerstone on the morning of 15th April, I left home,
leaving the house safely locked; I returned about ten minutes past 9 in the
evening and found my bed-room door wide open, which I had left shut-I
did not miss anything, but I afterwards saw my daughter's room open-
I called her attention to it, and she missed things which were safe when
I left home.
