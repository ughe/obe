2257. THOMAS WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing I coat, value
48.; the goods of Charles Dean; having been before convicted of felony,
CHARLES DEAN. I keep a shop in Saville-row, Walworth-road. On the
31st of Aug., about two o'cloek in the afternoon, I received information, and
ran out-1 missed a coat, which had been outside my shop-I informed
the policeman--the coat has not been found.
CHARLES Cousing. I am a journeyman butcher, and live in Baker's-row,
Walworth. On Tuesday, the 31st of Aug., I saw the prisoner take a coat
down from the front of the prosecutor's shop-he folded it up, and ran away
with it-there was another man with bim-I am quite sure the prisoner
took it,
JAMES CASTLETON (policeman, P 33.) I found the prisoner running, and
took him into custody.
