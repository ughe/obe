66. JAMES HOY (26), Stealing 2 oz. of acetic acid, and other goods,
the property of Frank Dutch.
MR. KELLY Prosecuted.
WALTER GOODWIN. I was at Dutch's place, Leytonstone-I am a
greengrocer-I was standing outside the Two Brewers, near Dutch's
place, Leytonstone, on a Tuesday night in October I saw the prisoner
take up a hamper and get another man to help him I did not take any
notice of the other man the hamper was under Mr. Dutch's window-
have no doubt the prisoner is the man who took up the hamper.
Cro88-acamined by the Prisoner. It was about 8.30 or 8.45 there were two
orthree other little things outside the window, I cannot mention them-the
man was coming by who helped you-you went round the corner-I was
not far away from you--I was at the corner near the Two Brewers and the
harness-maker's--I did not take much notice of the man who helped you.
Re-examined. I was about ten yards off when the prisoner picked up
the hamper and took it round the corner--he asked another man to help
him-it was sufficiently light to see what took place.
FRANK DUTCH. I am a grocer in the High Road, Leytonstone I can
not remember the date of this Tuesday evening, but it was the early part
of October--there was a hamper on the pavement in front of my shop-
it was put there shortly before 8.30 p.m. by my carman-I saw it on
the van at the door when the carman was engaged in unloading the
hamper contained drugs and other articles I missed it about 8.45; 1
have not seen it since in consequence of information I received I com.
municated with the police I did not know the prisoner.
Cross-examined. I never saw you before I saw you at the police-court-
I never employed you.
FREDERIOK ŠLATER (Policeman 423 M). I am stationed at Leytonstone
--I received information that something was missed from Mr. Dutch's
shop on 5th October-I got a description of a person, in consequence of
which I stopped the prisoner on 12th October in the Harrow Road,
Leytonstone I said “You will have to go to the station with me"-
took him into custody and charged him with stealing drugs-- he said
nothing, but came with me to the station, and I fetched the witness Good
win-1 told the prisoner Goodwin had seen him take the hamper-
Goodwin identified the prisoner he was with seven others--he pointed
him out at once he touched him on the shoulder and said “That is the
man I saw take the hamper "--the prisoner made no reply--Mr. Dutch
was then fetched-he made the charge in the prisoner's presence-the
prisoner said not a word in answer.
Cross-examined. I could not see that you were drnnk-I did not tell
you the next morning that you were a little drunk-I had known you in
Leytonstone about two or three years—you were potman at one time at
the Plough and Harrow-I do not know what your character was-
cannot say that I have seen you mixed up with thieves.
Ro-examined. The prisoner gave me an address at the station-I could
find out nothing about him there.
Prisoner's Defence. I have a witness to prove that I was not near
Leytonstone on the night of the robbery, and that I was white-washing
and paperhanging, and did not leave till 9 o'clock on the Saturday--her
name is Matilda Hoy. (Matilda Hoy being oalled did not answer.)
NOT GUILTY.
