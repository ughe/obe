   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  I think you are rather uncertain as to whether any money passed between them? A.  Yes—I saw none; but it might have passed—I was eight or nine yards distance, looking out of a win dow—my shop has an open front—I can undertake to say, that these potatoes were not put in the scale at all, as the prisoners were standing in the middle of the shop, between me and the scales which were at the bottom of the shop; but I could see above them, and see the scales—my shop may be about four yards long; the scales are on the side, but near to the bottom—Tabraham was at the door of the house that I was in; she had not to turn her head to get out, which I had—Linge's general instructions were, not to give credit; if he had, I should have looked to him to have made up the deficiency.
   MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  Would you have allowed Linge, on his credit, to have served your customers? No—if these potatoes had been sold, they should have been weighed; that is the only mode of determining the price—I am certain that no weighing took place. SARAH TABRAHAM. I was directed by Mr. Jordan to watch his premises—I was at my own door, which is opposite his shop—I saw Donovan go into the shop; both she and Linge took the potatoes, and placed them in her apron—she then went out—the potatoes had not been weighed, and there was no money paid that I could see; I had a full opportunity of seeing, if any thing of the sort had passed.
   Cross-examined. Q.  The prisoners were standing close together when the potatoes were taken out, were they not? A.  Yes—they were some distance from me—I swear that no money passed between them—I was at the door, and could see plainly—they were standing side by side, and both faced the bin—Donovan stood nearest to me; her side face was towards me.
   MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  You were stationed there for the express purpose of watching? A.  Yes; and I have done so on other occasions; I was parti cularly attentive to see whether money passed, and am positive it did not. THOMAS SHEEN( police-constable K  187.) I took the prisoners at the shop—I weighed the potatoes which Donovan had; there were 11lbs. of them—Linge said he was very sorry that he had done so, and he would never do so again—Donovan said she had given him sixpence.
 (The prisoners received good characters.)
   LINGE  — GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined Three Months .
   DONOVAN  — GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined One Month .
