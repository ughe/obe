2228. JAMES SIMMONS was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of
August, 34lbs. weight of rope, value 4s; the goods of Thomas Clark. Ond
EDWARD PYNER. I was employed on board a barge at Barking, on
Tuesday the 8th of August-when I left work there was a quantity of
rope attached to the anchor-next morning I went on board to get my
money, which was refused, as the rope was missing-I found it on the 14th
at Mr. Clatworthy's, a dealer in marine stores at Barking.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. Was there an anchor at the end of
the rope? A. Yes-it belonged to Thomas Clark-I was not in charge
of the vessel, but only the rope and anchor--the anchor was gone as well
as the rope I took it away myself, by Mr. Clark's order it was not
pawned--I did not pawn it-I took it to Mr. Bryant's house by Mr.
Clark's order.
JOHN GRAY. I am a constable of Barking. In consequence of infor-
mation I took possession of the rope on the premises of Mr. Clatworthy
-I afterwards took the prisoner at Poplar.
