Wellclose-square. On the 1st of Dec., about half-past four o'clock in the
afternoon, in consequence of some information, I went to Mr. Lampson's
counting-house, in Queen-street-place-I made a communication to him
he gave me directions what to do, in consequence of which I watched the
two prisoners-about five the same evening I saw Bebell going into Mr.

in about ten minutes-he then had nothing--he crossed Queen-street-place

off to run, and looked round all the time-I do not know that he saw me

Cross-examined by Mr. ADOLPHUS. Q. What is your name? 4. Isidor
Sherwinski-my father's name was Sherwinski--I was sworn on the Old
Testament—my name is not Cohen, nor was that my father's name.I am a
cigar-dealer-that is my only trade-I have been here about four years-I
have been nothing but a cigar-dealer these four years I had a bazaar be-
fore that in Dublin, which I carried on in the name of Isidor Sherwinski-
I have never called myself a Polish count, or an hussar, and I never was one
-I never saw Bebell before that day in my life, but I have heard about him
-I knew nothing of him before I have heard since that his name is
