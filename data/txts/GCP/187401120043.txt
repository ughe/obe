JAMES APPLEBY (re-examined). In October or November last the prisoner
called on me and told me he expected some parcels down which would be
addressed to Mrs. Faulkner, Grove House, Halliford, but that the lady had
made a mistake in the address, and there was no such place, and he would
call for them-I afterwards received some parcels with that address, and
should have sent them on unless I had had those instructions-referring to
my book I find that on 22nd November & parcel arrived, addressed Mrs.
Faulkner, Grove House, Halliford-the prisoner called for it, signed for it,
and took it away-here are four entries here, all signed by the prisoner.
Prisoner. Had I had paroels before $ 4. Only one in the name of
Jackson---you signed Jackson for it--I had known you coming there several
times, but did not know you in the name of Jackson-I only knew that by
your signing for one paroel-you signed “F. F. for Mrs. F." for this paroel.
COURT. Q. How came you, if you knew him as Jackson, to give him a
paroel addressed to Mrs. Faulkner A. Because he gave notice that he
would call for them, there being no such address, and on the strength of
that we gave them up, which we should not otherwise bave done.
JEANETTE MARINER. I have lived at Weybridge twenty-five years the
prisoner lodged in my house from 13th May to 29th November, with his
wife and children, in the name of Jackson.
Prisoner. Q. Did I receive one or two letters ? A. Yes, but very
seldom-you owed me money, and said you would pay me as soon as you
got it-your wife was ill, and the doctor ordered her change of air, in my
presence-you paid me 6l. 68. 4d., and promised to pay the rest before
Christmas--you lived much worse than I do, and I am a poor woman
never saw you the worse for drink-you were, I believe, too kind to your
wife.
GEORGE CHALCROPT. I am post-office messenger at Mr. Champion's, the
post-master at Halliford-I know Halliford ; there is no Grove House there,
that I know of -I know the prisoner, he called at Champion's for letters in
the name of Faulkner, and took one in that name away with him.
WILLIAM OLIVER. I am manager to Robert Attenborough, pawnbroker,
of Duke Street, Manchester Square-I produce a sealskin oont, with sable
trimmings, pledged on 25th November, by the prisoner, in the name of
Bycroft, for 201.
'SAMUEL BUTTON. I am manager to Richard Attenborough, pawnbroker,
of Piccadilly-I produce some sable trimmings pledged on 27th November,
for 101., by the prisoner, in the name of Thomas Hankin.
HENR YARTHUR ATTENBOROUGH. I am manager to George Attenborough
and Son, of 93, Fleet Street-I produce a sealskin coat, a set of sable
trimmings, and 2 yards of sable trimming, pledged on 25th November, for
201, by the prisoner, in the name of Frank Williams, Wyon Lodge, near
Wimbledon--he said they were his wife's property, and had been bought of
Swan and Edgar-I will not say whether he said that her uncle had given
the money for the purchasé, or was responsible for it, but he said they
would have to be redeemed before the first week in January, as the uncle
would then be in town, and she would have to wear them.
JOHN BAKER I am assistant to Mr. Fleming, a pawnbroker, of Brewer
Street, Golden Square-I produce some sable trimming, pledged on 28th
November for 81., by the prisoner, in the name of J. Hawkins.
