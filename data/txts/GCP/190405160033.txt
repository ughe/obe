NEW COURT.-Wednesday, May 18th, 1904.
Before Mr. Justice Walton.
431. LOUIS PINEAUD (35) Unlawfully committing an unnatural
offence with Amelia Macdonald.
MR. BURNIS and Mr. Roack Proseouted.
GUILTY. Two previous convictions were proved against him, and he had
slated to the police that he had been sentenced to ten yeare imprisonment in
France for assaulting an officer. Eighteen months' hard labour.

433, EDWIN DOUGLAS and AMOS WILLIAMS (23), Conspiring
together to steal a bicycle, the property of Edward Henry Meek,
WILLIAMS PLEADED GUILTY.
Mr. Heddon Prosecuted; Mr. RANDOLPH defended Douglas,
EDWARD HENRY Meex. I live in the Brompton Road, and am a
naturalist-on February 29th I went to the Cadogan Arms, Sloane Street,
I was riding a bioyole-fleft itoutside the house, about 9p.m.-when I came
out the bioycle was gone-I immediately communicated with the police
I next saw the bicycle on, I think, April 21st-it had been painted, and the
mudguards changed-I have identified it by the number and other things
Joux HOWARD. I live at 5, Montpelier Road, Brompton Road, and am
foreman at Messrs, Huoklebridge's, cycle repairers, of Sloane Street-on
April 21st Williams brought a bicycle to me to be repaired I communi
cated with the prosecutor, who is a friend of mine-I recognised the
bicycle as my friend's.
AUSTIN DAVEY (Detective B.) About 4 p.m. on April 21st I went to
133. Sloane Street, and there saw Mr. Howard and the bioycle in question
I remained there till Williams came- I told him I was a police officer, and
asked him if the bicycle was his-he said, "No : George Jones brought it
to me this morning and asked me to look after it-I then told him that I
should take him to the station-from the station I went to 9, Pont Street
Mews and saw Douglas- I told him that Williams was in oustody for
stealing the bioycle--he said, "Oh, we have some mudguards here; I
believe they belong to the machine"-he went into the stable and said,
"Here is a lamp: I believe it belongs to the bicycle, " I returned to the
station and asked Williams if the mudguards were his-he said, No.
Did Douglas give them to you! He is as bad as I am; he told me to take
the machine; in fact, he kept a look out while I took it "-he also said,
"He was with me at the Cadogan Hotel "-Williams appeared at West-
minster the following day, when the Magistrate ordered Douglas to be put
into the dock and charged.
