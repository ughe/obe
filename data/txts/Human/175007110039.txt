495. George Lodge, was indicted for stealing two saws, value 5 s. the goods of Charles Burges, July 2.
 The prosecutor not appearing he was acquitted.
 496.  William Newman, was indicted for stealing seven pounds weight of lead, value 7 d. and one iron lock and key, the property of William John Andrews, against the form of the statute, June 16.
     Acquitted.
 497.  Mary Grant, spinster, was indicted for stealing one velvet cloak, value 3 l. and other things the goods of Amaza Cox, June 29.
     Acquitted.
 498.  Sarah Lord, was indicted for stealing one silver tea strainer, value 12 d. the property of Samuel Elston.
     Acquitted.
 499.  Thomas Clark, was indicted for stealing one silver watch, value 40 s. the property of William Shaw.
     Guilty. [Transportation. See summary.]
 500.  Susannah Shapter , spinster, otherwise    Shaslow , otherwise wife of William   Floyd, was indicted for stealing three linen shirts, two table cloths, and other things, the goods of Thomas Osborne, November 28.
     Guilty. [Branding. See summary.] [Imprisonment. See summary.]
