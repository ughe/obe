   Re-examined.  I never heard of any tender before the information was laid. FREDERICK SMITH. I am bailiff for the Croydon County Court—I arrested Mr. Dresel on September 18th—I went with him to Sydenham to see if he could get  the money and then we came to London—we went into the Boar's Head in Cannon Street—the prisoner came in—Dresel knew him and told him how he was situated and that he was in custody—he then suggested that the prisoner should write or communicate with Mrs. Dresel to try and procure the money to get his release—the prisoner said he would do what he could—I then took Dresel to Wandsworth prison—there were other actions against him at the Croydon County Court—the prisoner never came to settle any of them.

 66. ALBERT NORMAN  (27) Robbery with violence with George Plank upon James William Bannister and stealing £11 18s. his money.
   MR. LEYCESTER Prosecuted; MR. MILLAR Defended.
   The RECORDER said that he considered the evidence of robbery with violence was insufficient and directed the Jury to return a verdict of NOT GUILTY. This being done the prisoner PLEADED GUILTY to an indictment for assault ing James William Bannister . He received a good character. Nine months' hard labour.

 67. MARIO CAPACCI  (28) , Having in his possession a galvanic battery intended for making counterfeit coin.
   MR. WILKINSON Prosecuted and the evidence was interpreted. DOMENICO ESPOSITO.
