Henry WRIGHT. I am in the employ of Mr. Holmes, of Fore-street.
On the 11th of November, I was in Little Moorfields, a little after eleven
o'clock in the morning I saw the two prisoners, and another there, walk-
ing round a cart-the one not yet in custody, got up in the cart, and tried
to remove a truse it was too heavy for bim, and he got down again he
got up three times, and the third time he got it out, and with the assistance
of Thomas, it was put on Shears's shoulders all three went down New
Union-street--the carman came out, and I told him of it he went down
New Union-street-went by Thomas, and the others, and caught Shears
at the bottom, with it on his shoulders- I was at Guildhall, at Shears's
examination, and Thomas was brought in there he was found in Guild-
hall-yard, waiting there and I knew him to be the person. The
Cross-examined by Me, PHILLIPS. Q. Did you ever see them before ?
4. Never; there were not many persons about Shears was taken up in
about ten minutes I had not lost sight of him more than two minutes it
was not in the market-place, but in Moorfields, opposite the back ware
house of Mr. Battey—there might be ten or twenty persons in the street-
I believe Shears had on the same coat as he has now he had a brown
coat and dark trowsers, and a black hat or eap, I cannot say which my
attention was chiefly direeted to the man who went three times to the cart
-I thought he was doing wrong, but there was nobody I could give an
alarm to, except people passing the houses are re-building I thought
the carman would be out in a few minutes I do not know that I was
flurried-Mr. Battey's warehouse door was not open-I meant to tell the
carman--the man was in and out the cart three times I was watching
him altogether about five or six minutes. Shears turned round to have the
property put on his back-I did not hear what passed between themit
was a little after eleven o'clock in the morning any body could see it done.
Crossrexamined by MR. DOAN.. . Are you quite sure Thomas is the
man? Yes, I am certain I went with the carman before the Grand Jury
I do not recollect his saying, "Mind what you say you know we are
not sure Thomas was one of them" no one said to me, "Let us be careful"
-nothing of the sort was said to me, nor did I hear it said to any body.
