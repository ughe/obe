Crawford Passage, Clerkenwell. Prisoner has lodged at my house
regularly for five or six months down to the day before the policeman
came about Russi. I have not seen him since. Gaolano and another
man were also living with me at the same time as the prisoner.
The other man left about a fortnight before this occurrence, and
Gaetano left directly after it.

OLIVER FRANCIS PAYNES ATKEY, senior resident medical officer,
Royal Free Hospital. On November 12, at 10.45 p.m., Rossi was
brought to the hospital. I examined him and found a stab in the
abdomen about four fingers' breaaths below the ribs. It was a serious
wound, very dangerous to life. I came to the conclusion that an
operation was necessary, and performed one which was successful. I
found there were three incisions into the liver. The knife had passed
upwards. I can only explain the three incisions in the liver by sup-
posing that the stabber partially withdrew the knife and then restabbed
without taking the knite out. There were three jogs, but only one
external wound. The wound could have been caused by the knifa
produced. I did not see any other wounds. There were none of any
size, at any rate there may have been a scratch. Had I not operated
I think the result would have been certain death.
Prisoner's statement before the Magistrate : "I know nothing about
it. It was about 12 o'clock that night when I was with about twelve
others when somebody called me out. This party told me that Bavari)
Rossi had been wounded and they say it was you who stabbed him
They told me to look out as the police were after me. When I heard
that I ran away."
Verdict, Guilty of wounding with intent to do grievous bodily harm.
Sentence, Eighteen months' hard labour. Certified for expulsion
under Aliens Act.
