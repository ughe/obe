GEORGE ALDRIDGE. I am a labourer in the Regent's Park. On Tuesday
morning, 17th March, I was passing by the York bridge; I saw two leds
looking through the fence; I crossed over the road, and saw an old shawl
lying through the fence on the grass I got over the fence, and picked up
the shawl ; it contained a small child's hood I put them under the arch of
the bridge--I saw a park constable, I told him what I had seen-we
dragged the water for twenty minutes, and found the body of a male child
-I gave it to Willis, the constable I went with him to the workhouse,
and saw the matron there examine the child-I saw a mark round its neck
--we left the child there. this ad o rs

this child-supposing it had had a ligature tied round its neck, and been

round the neck-it was linen of some kind-it was twisted the cause of
death was not very obvious the appearances of suffocation were not well
