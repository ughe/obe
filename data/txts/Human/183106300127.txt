 1464.  THOMAS MUMFORD and JOHN HEMSON were indicted for stealing, on the 28th of May , 1 handkerchief, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Matthew Day , from her person.
   WILLIAM ARNEY. I am a Policeman. On the 28th of May, at eleven o'clock at night, I was in Piccadilly, near the Haymarket, and saw the prisoners in company; I had followed them from Regent-street - there was a considerable crowd, on account of the illumination; I saw Mumford put his hand into Mr. Day's pocket, and keep it there a considerable time - a coach then came up, which forced the people to go close; he then took his hand out with this handkerchief - I took hold of him; Hemson did nothing but stand by - he must have seen this done.
 MATTHEW DAY. This is my handkerchief; when my attention was called to it, I missed it.
 Mumford received a good character.
 MUMFORD -    GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Three Months.
 HEMSON -    NOT GUILTY.
 OLD COURT. THURSDAY, JULY 7.
 First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Sergeant  Arabin .
 1465.  HENRY SALMON was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of June , 1 watch, value 2l., the goods of James Brookman.
   WILLIAM SMITH. I am servant to James Brookman, an upholsterer, at Islington. On the 17th of June the prisoner came from Mr. Owen, a polisher, and was at work in our front shop for about two hours; this watch hung under the mantel-piece in that shop - it was missed about half an hour after he left; he had been alone in the shop.
   WILLIAM SHEEHY. I am a Policeman. I was on duty in Liquorpond-street on the 25th of June - the prisoner came and said, "Policeman, I am waiting for you;" I said, "What for?" he said, "I want to give myself up to you;" I said, "What for?" he said, "I have taken a watch and hat from Mr. Owen, of Liquorpond-street;" I took him to the watch-house - he said he had pawned the watch at Fleming's, Fleet-market.
   WILLIAM CLARK. I am shopman to Mr. Fleming. I have a watch, pawned on the 17th of June, in the name of Henry Bennett- I do not know who by.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1466.  THOMAS McNAMARA was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of May , 1 cash-box, value 12s., the goods of James Boyd, the elder, and James Boyd, the younger, his masters.
   ALEXANDER BOYD. I am journeyman to James Boyd and James Boyd, Jun., who are furnishing-ironmongers, and live in Wellbeck-street. The prisoner had been nearly two years in their employ, as a labourer- we did not miss this box till a pawnbroker brought it to us; it was kept in the shop.
   WILLIAM GOFTON. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Gilbert-street, Grosvenor-square. On the evening of the 21st of May the prisoner came to pawn this box; I asked how he became possessed of it - he said he was the manufacturer; he seemed confused - I saw a paper sticking out of his pocket, which I took out, and found a shop-mark on it; I took him into custody; he then said he hoped I would not give him in charge, and acknowledged stealing it from his masters.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1467.  ROBERT CLARK and THOMAS WOODRUFT were indicted for stealing, on the 16th of May , 24 yards of printed cotton, value 10s., the goods of James Veitch.
   JOHN THOMAS. I am a Police-constable. On the 16th of May, about a quarter-past twelve o'clock, I saw the prisoners in company in Ratcliff-highway - I watched, and saw them near the shop of Mr. Brown, a linen-draper; Clark stood on the pavement, with his face towards me -Woodruft had his back towards me, and his face to the shop, handling some prints in front of the window; they took nothing - they then went to two other linen-drapers, and handled prints at both of them; I at last followed them to Mr. Veitch's - a coach stood there, and I did not see them take any thing; they walked to and fro till two o'clock, and every time they came from the shop they had each an apron tied up round their middle, and as they came back their aprons were down - I at last saw Hennessey with Clark; I ran up to his assistance, and saw the property in his hands - Woodruft ran away; I know he is the same man - I had watched him for an hour and a quarter.
   MICHAEL HENNESSEY. I am an officer. I was on duty, and saw the prisoners in company; I understood Thomas had been watching them - I observed Clark's apron up and bulky; they were together then - I seized him with this property in his apron: he struggled, and dropped it -Thomas came to my aid.
   JAMES PORTCH. I am an officer. I was on duty in Cannon-street; my brother officer collared Clark, and pointed out Woodruft to me - I ran and took him.
