JOHN HURLSTONB (policeman, 37). I was with Porter on the roof of
the skittle-ground-I saw into and saw four persons-I took Collier about
4 o'elock in the morning in bed at his father's, 13, Tash-street, and told him
I wanted him for being at the back of the White Horse with three others
be said he was only in the yard-I saw them all four get into the tap-room,
and back into the yard again.
Cross-examined. Q. Were you looking through a hole in the roof? A.
Yes; there was a tile off; we both saw through the hole, and could see
them very distinctly-I am not mistaken at all, I am quite sure of them.
