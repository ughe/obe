HENRY DORCHESTER. I live in Harrisons-court, Ratcliffe. On Satur-
day night, the 13th of August, I was at Richard Kifford's shop at Shad-
well-I saw the prisoners coming by the shop-M'Allen took some beef
off the block and put it under his jacket-Harrington was with him
they went as far as the Coach and Horses-I ran and asked them for
the beef-M'Allen dropped it-I picked it up.
Thomas SHEPHERD. I am servant to Mr. Richard Kifford. I missed
the beef from outside the shop.
JAMES Rooke (police-constable K 245.) I went to the Coach and
Horses--I saw the prisoners-M'Allen was drunk-1 charged them with
taking the beef-they said they knew nothing of it.
Harrington's Defence. M'Allen asked me to go and have a pint of
beer; the officer laid hold of him; I said, “What do you want with him?"
he said, "I want you too ;" I know nothing about it.
M'ALLEN_GUILTY. Aged 18.
HARRINGTON-GUILTY. Aged 28.

2471. JOHN MCARTHY was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of
August, 19 lbs. of bread, value 38.; the goods of John Caswell.
Edward CATTAN. I am apprentice to John Caswell, of York-terrace,
Stepney. I went out to deliver bread on the 11th of August-I received
information, went after the prisoner, overtook him, and found he had
got four quartern loaves and two half-quartern-I asked whose bread he
had got-he said, his own I asked where he got it-he made no answer,
but put it down, and went off - I gave him into custody--they were my
master's loaves.
THOMAS STEWART. I was crossing Whitehorse-street-I saw the pri-
soner take the loaves out of the barrow, put them on the lid, shut it down,
take the loaves in his arms, and walk off.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined One Month.
2472. JOHN CARR was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of August,
21 lbs. weight of bacon, value 1s. 6d. ; the goods of Joseph Mason.
CHARLES CHRISTIAN MASON. I am in the employ of my father, Joseph
Mason. On the 23rd of August, I saw the prisoner and another. boy
come up together--the other took the bacon--the other stopped against
the stall--the prisoner ran up Bunhill-row, and was doubling something
up- I took him, with the bacon.
GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Three Months.
