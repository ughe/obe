820. JAMES TURNER (41), Stealing a chest of tea, the property
of Henry Hailey and another.
MR. HUMPHREYS conducted the Prosecution.
EDWARD FERN. I live at 229 Linton Road, Bermondsey, and am
a commercial traveller-on Saturday the 26th August, 1876, I was driving
along the Lower Road, Rotherhithe-I saw the prisoner walking behind
the van, which was laden with tea-he took a chest of tea off the tail of
my van and put it on another van, and put a low tarpauling over it, and
the driver of the other van drove away-I saw the prisoner get down-
followed the van and gave the other man into custody-he was tried, and
sentenced to nine months at this Court-I next saw the prisoner at Green-
wich police-court, on the 11th October, and identified him-I am sure he
is the man who took the tea.
JAMES WHEAL. I live at 70, Greenfield Street, Whitechapel-on Aug.
26th, 1876, I was driving & van along the Lower Road, Rotherhithe, of
thirty-six chests of tea, for the victualling yard, Deptford-when I got
down a chest of tea was missing.
WILLIAM Hunt (City Detective 795). On Tuesday, 10th October, I
apprehended the prisoner in Gracehcurch Street, for stealing a chest of
tea a twelvemonth ago he said “Middleton told me that was all settled "
I said I should take him to Rotherhithe to make further inquiries-the
charge was read over to him at the station-he said nothing.
