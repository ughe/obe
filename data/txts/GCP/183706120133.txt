the 23rd of May I went with a truck and two baskets, the property of Mr.
Rossi, to several places-I left my truck at No. 35, Coleman-street, and
went into the house when I came out the truck and baskets were gone
I afterwards went with Reed, and saw the axle-tree and wheels of the truck
in Duke's-place-one basket was found in Mitre-court, Cheapside, and
one in Tabernacle-row, Hoxton---they were my master's baskets.
