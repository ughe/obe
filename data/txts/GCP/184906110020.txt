1237. WILLIAM BROWN and CHARLES GILES, stealing 1 hand,
kerchief, value Is. ; the goods of William Thorpe, from his person: Bronz
having been before convicted : to which BROWN pleaded
Guilty. Aged 18.- Transported for Seven Years.
WILLIAM THORPE. I live at 18, Barnsbury-row, Islington. On 21
May, about four o'clock, I had my handkerchief safe—the officer gave me
information, and I missed it-this is it produced)-it is marked.
ALFRED GREEN (City-policeman 376). I saw the prisoners following Mr.
Thorpe-Brown took this handkerchief from his pocket; Giles was covering
him-they ran down Bow-lane-I overtook them, and took them to the
station---Brown pulled this handkerchief out of his pocket, and said it wa
his own-I had watched them following three gentlemen for half an hour.
GILES-Guilty.* Aged 15.-Confined Three Months.
1238. GEORGE WEBB, stealing 27 yards of cotton, 2 gowns, and
shawls, value 148.; the goods of Maria Barnett and another.
MARIA BARNETT. I am in partnership with Sarah Woolfe, at Shepherd's
street, Tenter-ground. On 22nd May, I employed the prisoner to carry
bundle for me I went with him-I just turned my back, and he was gone-
I could not find him for a week I have never seen the goods since-1
swear he is the man.
WILLIAM MOBBS (City-policeman, 265). On 26th May, I took the pri
soner, sleeping under some arches in Snow-hill-I told him the charge-he
said the bundle was of no use to him, and he laid it down in Finsbury-square,
and left it.
Guilty. Aged 24.-Confined Three Months.
1239. ANN DALE, stealing 1 watch and 1 watch-chain, value 25s. 4d.;
the goods of Emma Maria Warner.
ELIZABETH WARNER. I am a widow, and live at 27, Jewin-street. On
29th May the prisoner came-I saw her put her hand on the mantelpiece,
and directly missed my daughter's watch, which I bad seen safe three minutes
before-this is it (produced - I found it at a pawnbroker's.
Emma MARIA WARNER. This is my watch; it was in a mug on the
mantelpiece.
THOMAS NEAVE. I am assistant to Mr. Grant, a pawnbroker-this watch
was pawned on 29th May, for 15s., in the name of Ann Dale, I believe by
the prisoner.
WILLIAM GENGE (City-policeman, 122). I found this corresponding
duplicate (produced) at the prisoner's lodgings.
Prisoner's Defence. I did not go into the room, I was not further than
the door. If I had stolen it, I should not have put it in my own name.
Guilty. Aged 45.-Confined Three Months.
