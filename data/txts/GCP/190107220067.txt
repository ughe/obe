JOSEPH BROWN. I am a sergeant in No. 1 Company of the Irish Guards,
stationed at Chelsea Barracks--the prisoner with Evans returned to
barracks about 3.45 a.m. on June 18th-I was in charge of the guard-
they had no passes for after 12, so they were kept in the guard-room all
night-before they came I had had a visit from Police-sergeant Sim-
monds -he told me about the murderous assault upon the constable in
Hyde Park-I examined the clothing and accoutrements of the prisoner
and Evans--when they returned, in consequence of what Simmonds had
told me, I took off their belts and examined their boots and clothing-I
did not find any blood on either of their belts, or on their boots—I see
some marks inside this belt - they were not on it at the time, but I cannot
say if this is the belt Evans had on then—I did not take the number of
it-it looks like blood to me.
