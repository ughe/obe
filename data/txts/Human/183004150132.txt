 COURT. Q. What is your age? A. Twenty-one; I am a waistcoat-maker - I cannot tell whether both the bottles were alike; I drank part of their contents - it was poured into a glass: it was candle-light - it was dark coloured wine; I am not acquainted with wine - there was rum, not gin in the other bottle.
   HENRY RICHARD BUCKERIDGE. I took the prisoner at his father's, in company with Whaley; and in his righthand pocket I found this key; I took him to Mr. Brown's, and left him in the parlour while I went down - I found this key would open the spirit-cellar door rather better than Mr. Brown's key would; this is the key - I went to Lawrence's lodgings with Taylor; I asked if he had left any wine or bottles there - Lawrence's mother put her hand out of the window and gave me them out of a gutter; they had been fresh washed: I did not search the lodging; I had been to Taylor's lodgings, and he gave me a bottle which had contained Port wine.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did not he say his father-in-law, Godfrey had given it him? A. No. I saw the blood in the cellar; it might have been there a day or two - I examined the prisoner's arms and wrists, but found no cut on them.
 JURY to  ROBERT BROWN. Q. Was it blood or Port wine? A. Having been brought up a butcher I think I am too good a judge of blood to be deceived; I should think I have not less than 50l. worth of wine and spirits: I think it possible there might be another engaged in it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I never entered the place with the key in my pocket; I was not certain that it would have fitted it - it is not reasonable that I should have returned to the prosecutor's house if I had robbed him.
     NOT GUILTY.
 966.  MARY WARD was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of April , 1 sheet, value 1s.; 2 pinafores, value 1s.; 1 bolster, value 4s. 5d.; 2 pairs of boots, value 12s.; 2 shirts, value 2s.; 1 pair of trousers, value 1s. 6d.; 4 handkerchiefs, value 2s.; 1 shawl, value 1s.; 1 frock, value 1s., and 1 cap, value 1s., the goods of Joshua Flight , her master.
 JOSHUA FLIGHT. I am  bar-man at a wine-vaults. The prisoner was servant to me for twelve months; she left me without notice, and I missed this property.
   GEORGE LANCASTER. I am servant to a pawnbroker in Goswell-road. I have a number of these articles pawned by the prisoner.
   JOSEPH JOHN GOODGE. I have three pinafores and a frock, pawned by the prisoner, I believe; she is in the habit of coming to the shop.
   WILLIAM CRABB. I took the prisoner, and found the duplicates on her.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that the prosecutor engaged her as housekeeper, to take the charge of four children, but was too poor to supply them with the common necessaries or pay her wages; that she had pledged the articles to procure his children food.
 JOSHUA FLIGHT re-examined. Q. Have you been in such a state of indigence that you have employed her to pawn your property, or allowed her to do it? A. No - I allowed her money as a housekeeper; she has pawned her own property - I do not owe her any thing; I passed my word at a tally-shop, and a man has come to me for it.
 Four witnesses gave the prisoner a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 49. Recommended to Mercy by the Jury. Confined One Month.

 968.  JAMES DACEY was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of March , 1 handkerchief, value 4s., the goods of William Stevens , from his person; and that he had been previously convicted of felony.
 WILLIAM STEVENS. I am in the service of Mrs. Hitchins, Beaufort-buildings, Strand. I had a handkerchief in my pocket; a man ran against me and said I had been robbed - I felt and my handkerchief was gone; in about half a minute or a minute a young man collared the prisoner with my handkerchief.
   JOHN BRUNDLEY. The prisoner was given into my custody by Mr. Murray; this is the handkerchief.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   JOHN MURRAY. I saw the prisoner and another person with him; the prisoner took the handkerchief from the pocket - I sent a young man to tell the prosecutor; I took the prisoner who ran into the passage of a coffee shop.
 GUILTY.
   ABRAHAM HAINES. I have a certificate of the conviction of the prisoner on the 11th of June, in the 10th year of his present Majesty's reign; I was a witness on the trial - he is the man.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. - Transported for Life.
 969.  ALEXANDER FOX was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of March , 4lbs. weight of beef, value 1s. 8d., the goods of Frederick William Hilder ; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
   FREDERICK WILLIAM HILDER. I am a butcher. On the evening of the 6th of March, about half-past eleven o'clock at night, I was just going into the shop hanging up the meat; the prisoner walked by and took a piece of beef off the board - he laid a handkerchief on it and took it; I followed him about twenty yards, and gave charge of him.
   JOHN DANE. I took the prisoner into custody.
 Prisoner's Defence. A man said "I want you," took me to the shop, and said I had stolen a piece of beef; I did not know what he wanted me for. GUILTY.
   RICHARD MILLIN. I have a certificate of the conviction of the prisoner on the 15th of January, in the ninth year of his present Majesty's reign; I gave evidence - he is the person.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. - Transported for Life.
