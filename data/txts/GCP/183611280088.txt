54. JOHN GRICE was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of October,

31.; 3 pairs of drawers, value 15s. ; 1 shirt, value 38.; 3 handkerchiefs,
value 38. ; 2 collars, value 2s. ; 1 pair of braces, value 2s.: 1 pair of gloves
value 38. ; 4 boxes, value 11. 138. ; 2 inkstands, value 28.; 3 pocket-
books, value 28. ; 1 cigar-case, value 1s. ; 2 razors, value 58.; 1 razor-
strop, value ls. 6d.; and 1 hone, value 6d. ; the goods of Charles Gilman,
in the dwelling-house of William Pryor.

in Baltic-street, and live in the house. I kept my box in the back room

Mr. Pryor's-he did not sleep in the same room with me--he had nothing
to do with the room-one man could not carry the box-it would require
two-it was on the ground floor-I saw my things again next day at
Worship-street, and the lid of the box-the body of the box was broken up
