1866. FRANCIS PEACOCK was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief,
value 18., the goods of William Bibby, from his person.
WILLIAM BIBBY. I am a chemist, and live in Huntingdon-street, Kings-
land-road. On the 15th of June, a little before six o'clock, I was in Wilson-
street, Finsbury, looking at a house on fire-the prisoner and another lad
came and stood behind me they began pushing about, and I felt a pressure on
my pocket-in two or three minutes 1 felt a tug- I turned and saw the prisoner
with my handkerchief in his hand-he passed it to another-I seized the
prisoner, he struggled violently, and got away-he ran some distance, I fol-
lowed and gave him into custody.
HENRY COLE (police-constable C 126.) I took the prisoner.
Prisoner. There was a great crowd-the gentleman touched me on the
shoulder, and said I had got his handkerchief-I told him not to lay hold of
me.
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
1867. CHARLES MCARTHY was indicted for stealing 1 shawl, value
28.; 1 ball, 1d. ; 1 thimble, 1d.; and 1 sampler, 1d. ; the goods of Jane
Rowley.
JANE Rowley. I live in Charles-street, Drury-lane. On the 21st of
June, I met the prisoner in the street about twelve at night-I went with him
to a brothel-I awoke about five in the morning, and he was gone--I am sure
he is the man-he had not paid me anything I missed my shawl, which had a
blue middle and coloured border, a sampler, a ball, and a thimble, out of my
pocket-there was no one else in the room.
Prisoner. When she awoke she asked the woman who let us the room if
she had seen the shawl with me-she said no, and then she came and
gave me in charge.
ANN GRIPIN. I live in Carrier-street. At a quarter before five o'clock
that morning I met the prisoner--he offered to sell me a large sbawl for 6d.
it had a blue middle and coloured border.
EDWARD HARRIS (police-constable E 50.) I took the prisoner-the pro-
secutrix stated what she had lost-be cried, and said he knew where the shawl
was, and if Rowley would forgive him he would get it and give it her-
found this thimble and ball in his pocket.
(Properly produced and sworn to.)
Guilty.t Aged 19.- Transported for Seven Years.
1868. JAMES WARD was indicted for stealing two waistcoats, value 135.,
the goods of George Evans, his master.
GEORGE EVANS. I live in James-street, Holborn. The prisoner was my
errand boy-I discharged him three weeks since-I afterwards found a dupli-
cate of a waistcoat on my premises- I went to the pawnbroker's and found
two waistcoats--they were mine.
