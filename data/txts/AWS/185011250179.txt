I breakfasted with him-he breakfasted with me on the 15th with the

MaRY HAYEs. The prisoner came to lodge at my house on 13th Oet.,

Cross-examined. Q. You are an Irishwoman A. Yes; the prisoner
is Irish too-eight men were lodging in my bouse, all Irishmen-they all
breakfasted at home, some at half-past six, and some at eight-I went out
at a quarter before eight-1 have a clock it goes right, I keep it regular
for the men to go to work-I set it right myself-I was only gone five
minutes to the bakehouse and the shop-the prisoner was in my house
from the 14th till the 17th-I do not know where he went then.
