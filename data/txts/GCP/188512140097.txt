By MR. GRAIN. The hops arrived two or three days before
they were stopped, and I gave orders to my foreman to deliver no

afterwards the usual portion arrived in April this year, the market had
fallen very much the market fell quite at the end of 1884 and has
continued to fall till now-up to that time it was very firm, at one time
there was a rise-since 1881-1882, hop merchants have not kept any
considerable amount of stock--they can, unfortunately, always come to the
hop factors and borrow samples, and sell them, and then come to us and
buy-in fact they use our stock-they wanted to make out before the
Magistrate that I sold bad hops.
