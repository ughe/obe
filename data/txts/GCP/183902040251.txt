Before Mr. Recorder.
850. CHRISTIANA SPICE was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of
January, 1 tablecloti, value 10s.; 1 petticoat, value 4s, ; 1 handkerchief,
value 2s.6d.; 1 pillow, value 28.; 1 apron, value Is. ; 1 shawl, value Is. ;
1 pair of pattens, value Bd.; and 1 pair of stockings, value 3d.; the goods
of Sarah Grubb.
SARAH GRUBB. I am a widow, and live in King-street, Blackfriars-
road. I employed the prisoner to look after my children in my absence,
and paid her 6d. a day-she found her own food-on Friday, the 4th of
January, I left her in charge of the children, and on returning, about seven
o'clock in the evening, she was gone-I missed the articles stated, which
are worth more than il.--the prisoner boarded and lodged in the house
for three months I found her again on the 6th of January
WILLIAM WEST. I am shopman to Mr. Wharton, pawnbroker, Mount-
street, Westminster-road. I produce a tablecloth, pawned by the prisoner
on the 4th of January
ROBERT OVENDEN. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner in charge.
I found the shawl on her, the pattens on her feet, and the stockings on
her legs—I found duplicates of the other property on her, which the pawn.
brokers have given up.

851, JOHN PASSFIELD was indicted for killing and slaying John
Edward Passfield.
DOS
John BOULTER. I am a turner. I have known the prisoner five or six
years-he is a plasterer, bricklayer, and carpenter, a handy sort of man,
and got his living in that way-he has worked for me--- he did three weeks
work for me about five weeks ago he finished it on the 17th of Novem-
ber-the deceased died on the 29th of December--the prisoner had about
five days and three quarters' work from me between those times-- he had
two children, one girl and one boy, (the deceased,) who was between six
and seven years old-his wife has been dead about two years he had a
guinea a week when he worked for me he would have the means of sup-
porting his family, when in employment-I believe he sometimes got more
from other people during part of the time be worked for me, he lived in
Robert-street, or Ann-street-he never rented the place in Counter-alley
-he merely lived there on sufferance on the 28th of December, the day
