 963. JAMES MOORE and EDWARD THOMPSON were indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of March , 8 pieces of wood, value 9 d  ., the goods of Thomas Cubitt . DUNCAN GRANT. I am private watchman to Thomas Cubitt, at Thames Bank. About twelve o'clock, on the 2nd of March, I saw the two prisoners going towards a pile of deals—Moore took up this piece of deal, and walked away with it 200 yards—he then broke it into small pieces, and Thompson assisted him and tied it up in a bundle—I went and took Moore—he re sisted—I went and called a policeman—when I returned they were making away with the wood among the vacant places in the stones.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Do you know whether Moore's father is employed on the premises? A.  No—Thompson was walking close to Moore when he took the wood—I do not know whether Moore had been taking his father's dinner—it was after Moore began to break this up that Thompson assisted him—one of the masons employed on the place was helping them to put the wood away—I do not know his name—Thompson split up part himself—he did not attempt to get away—he said nothing at the time.
 (The prisoners received good characters.)
   MOORE— GUILTY.— Confined Two Months.
   THOMPSON— NOT GUILTY.
 964. HENRY CORTNEY was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of March , 1 gave, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 brush, value 6 d  .; the goods of Thomas Geeves . SARAH CLARKE. I am servant to Thomas Geeves, who keeps the Queen's Head public-house—on the 2nd of March I was up stairs, and saw the prisoner in the bed-room—I asked him what he was doing—he went away—I gave information to my master—I missed the gown now produced, which is my master's. THOMAS GBEVBS. I keep this public-house. On the 2nd of March I received information from Clarke—I began to see if all was right—the pri soner had no right in my bed-room—I found my gown was gone—I sent two people in search of the prisoner—I found him in a court in Maiden-lane—he had thrown down the gown and brush in the court—I did not see him do it—it was my wife's gown—not five minutes had elapsed between my finding the gown and the prisoner's going down from the room—it was safe at twelve o'clock, and this was nearly five—no one else had been in the room. WILLIAM MELEY. I live in Off-alley. I was in the public-house, and saw the prisoner coming down stairs with something under his coat—I could not see what it was—I ran and overtook him, and he threw the gown down.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was in a state of intoxication.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY  .— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Six Months.
