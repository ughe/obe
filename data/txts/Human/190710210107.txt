I was in my kitchen on Saturday, August 3, about 10.30. I was waiting to send breakfast up to a gentleman, and was sitting at needlework, and I distinctly heard the sound of a baby's cry coming from the bedroom which prisoner occupied. My daughter was in the front room cleaning, and the also heard the noise. I went to the bedroom door and knocked. Prisoner said, "Who is there?" I said, "It's me." She said, "You cannot come in; I am washing and dressing." I heard her go out about an hour after wards. She ran down the stairs. It is not a very long flight, but she ran very fast. She was out about an hour, or a little longer. Then she came in and asked me if Carter Peterson's had been for her boxes. She had told me on the Friday that she was leaving on Saturday. Carter Peterson's came while she was speaking to me in the kitchen; they took her boxes, and she went afterwards between one and two. She was only carrying an umbrella. She told me she was going to Guilford Street, Russell Square. After she had gone I went into her room and found the sheets were stained red.
