having been before convicted.

came in, and took tea with me- Walker said she had a bonnet to sell that
would suit me, and asked me to go to her house to see it-they left at four, and
I went to Walker's bouse at six, to see the bonnet-I did not fasten the
shutters, only the window and doors-I met Walker coming out of her own
house-] said I had come to buy the bonnet-she said she was going to the
pledge-shop-1 said, I will go and look at the bonnet -she said, 64 No,
come with me to the pledge-shop "I went, and waited outside- then
