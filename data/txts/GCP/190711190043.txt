POWER, George Gordon (30, manager); feloniously sending and
causing to be received by Arthur Henry Thompson certain letters
threatening to accuse him of having committed a certain infamous
crime, with intent to extort money from him.
Mr. Horace Avory, K.C., Mr. J. R. Randolph, and Mr. Barrington
Ward prosecuted.

Margate, where they had drinks together. In the following year he,
was in the Criterion Restaurant, when prisoner claimed aegasintance
with him. That night witness had too much to drink and went to an
hotel which prisoner suggested. Later on prisoner began to write
postcards and lotters asking for money, and witness lent him money
from time to time. (Witness proved numerous communications which
had been sent to him by prisoner, and which threatened to expose him
unless more money was lent. The letters were handed to the Jury.)
In all about £150 was given to prisoner, until at last the letter which
formed the subject of the indiotment was handed to a solicitor.
