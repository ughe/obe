THIRD COURT .— Wednesday, August  3 rd ; 1881.
   Before Mr. Recorder.
 687. WILLIAM CHARLES FREDERICK JONES  (25) , Embez zling various sums amounting to 108 l  . 13 s  . 6 d  . received by him on account of his master's, George Whitaker and another.
   MESSRS. BESLEY and TICKSLL Prosecuted ; MR. WILLIS , Q.C., Defended.
   The circumstances of this case showing a very great complication of accounts, the RECORDER suggested that it would be difficult to expect a Jury to convict; in order to prove embezzlement the appropriation of particular sums must be shown, and not a general deficiency. Upon this intimation MR. BESLEY did not press the ease, and the Jury found the prisoner. NOT GUILTY.

 693. RICHARD COOPER  (31) , Stealing a watch of Thomas Dickins , from the person of Caroline Malvina Dickins .
   MR. WILLES Prosecuted ; MR. STEWART WHITE Defended. CAROLINE MALVINA DICKLNS. I live at 1, Sussex Place, New Southgate—on Saturday, 23rd July, about 7 p.m., I was outside an omnibus coming to Farringdon Station—the prisoner sat next me—he got off abruptly and I missed my watch—the chain was hanging—I said "That man has got my watch"—I got down as quickly as possible and got hold of his arm—he said "You are wrong, I am not the man"—I said "You are; you have 'got my watch"—this is it ( produced )—it is worth about 30 s .—he was taken in custody.
