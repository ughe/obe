859. JOHN MILLER, stealing 8 loaves, and 91bs. weight of flour, value
28. 10d.; the goods of John White Samson, his master.
JOHN WHITE SAMSON. The prisoner was in my service three or four
months, as journeyman baker—he had to carry out bread—on 9th February
I found eight half-quartern loaves, tied in a handkerchief and put in a tub in
which yeast was kept—I stuck a piece of wood into the crumb of the bread,
and covered them over as before—at a little before six o'clock, after he
accounted to me for the bread he had taken out, I called him, and found be
was out—he ought not to have been out—I then went to the tub and missed
the bread—I went out, and saw him going towards Deptford with a basket—
I saw him untie a handkerchief, put it in his pocket, and leave the breadin
the basket—he went towards Blackhorse-bridge, and stopped at one of my
customers with two half-quartern loaves—seeing a policeman, I called out,
I have caught you at said, Oh Sir, if you will forgive me I wal
give you all I have found some flour on him as well—I gave him in
charge—he said he had only been doing it that week—the bread had bm
ordered by the customer—the policeman found the wood in the bread,
THOMAS Moscx, ( policeman, R 228). Mr. Samson gave the prisonerin
my charge—I asked him why he did it—he said he wanted to raise some
money to get some tidy clothes by Easter, and he had only been doing it
that week.
Prisoner. Q. Did not you ask the customer if it was ordered ? A. Ye;
they said you ought to have left it in the morning, and they were just going
up to see why you had not left it.
GUILTY. Aged 29.—Confned Four Months.
