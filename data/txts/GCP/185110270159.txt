1962. FREDERICK COLE and CORNELIUS SHEEHAN, unlaw.
fully uttering a counterfeit shilling, having others in their possession : to
which SHEEHAN pleaded Guilty. Aged 20.-Confined Six Months.
MESSRS. Scriven and CLERK conducted the Prosecution.
ADELIA JONES. On 1st Oct. the prisoners came to the bar of the Russell
Hotel, and asked for a pint of half and half-Cole gave me a shilling for it
I put it in the detector-it bent, and I gave it to my father.
WILLIAM Jones. I am last witness's father. On 1st Oct. I saw the
prisoners at my bar-my daughter brought me a bad shilling-I told them it
was bad, and asked them for a good one-they said they had not got one-
Cole then said he was a hardworking man, and wished me to give him back
the shilling--I would not, but sent for a constable and gave them into custody
and gave him the shilling the prisoners were searched in my house-Shee-
ban struggled very much, and I saw two other pieces of bad money full from
bis mouth-three others were afterwards taken from him.
HENRY COOMBER (policeman, P 302). I took the prisoner on 1st Oct.
Another constable said Sheehan had some more in his mouth-I caught him
round the throat, and took three pieces from his mouth-I received this one
from Mr. Jones (all produced)-I found 8s. 4fd. good money on Sheehan, but
none on Cole.
JAMES PLUMB policeman, P 89). I produce the two shillings which fell
from Sheehan's mouth.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These six shillings are all counterfeit, and all from
the same mould.
