five pieces of calico-I received twenty-eight of them at the house of Luck

GEORGE GILBERT. I am in the employ of Oliver, Dear, and Co. Mr.
Dear's name is James-Mr. Oliver is deceased--there are others--they are
consignees of all the calico that comes from the house of Gordon, Barron,
and Co., marked with the dove-it is not a common occurrence to sell as
many as thirty pieces to one purchaser.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Do you speak from your own
recollection or from books ? A. From both have referred to the books
to be perfectly certain-I could trust to my memory, but this was so sin-
gular a circumstance that I referred to the books.
Mr. Bodkin. Q. For the last four years have you sold forty pieces to
one person? A. Yes, on the 12th of December, 1840, to Mr. Amos, of
Bristol--that is the only instance in which forty pieces have been sold to
one customer these four years.
MR. PHILLIPS. Q. How many pieces do you dispose of in the course
of a year with the dove on? A. I do not know-I will not swear that
we have not distributed twenty thousand through England in the last
three years--we are the only persons who receive those with the dove on
them, and we distribute them-there are but few sold in London-I
should think not thirty pieces in a year.

226. GEORGE SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of No-
vember, 1 coat, value 158., the goods of George Thomas Beare.
George THOMAS BEARE. I am a seaman, and lodge at Mr. M'Carthy's.
I pulled off my coat and left it on the kitchen table on the 23rd of No-
vember-Mr. M'Carthy came and asked if this was my coat, and it is.
ISABELLA M'Carthy. I live with my brother John, who keeps a lodg-
ing-house for seamen. On the 23rd of November I saw the prisoner, who
had no business in the house-he had no coat on-he went into the kitchen
and put this coat on in my presenee-he went up stairs with it-I told my
brother, who stopped him in the passage with it.
WILLIAM ISAAC (police-constable K 223.) I took the prisoner-he
denied being near the house--he said he had been out and sold his mats
- he had got as far as Welclose-square.
Prisoner. I was along with a man-of-war's man-he said, “ Where is
the house?”-I showed him the house-he said, “There is a coat down
stairs, go and put it on"-I went down and took it-a drunken sailor went
down with me.
ISABELLA MCARTHY re-examined. A man came in with him who he
lodged there, but was not drunk.
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Confined Three Months.
