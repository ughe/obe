   Prisoner's defence.  I was going home, and saw two men running, and heard a cry of "Stop thief!" They separated, and I attempted to stop them; the man in front turned back, and in my eagerness to catch him I ran right up to the constable, and he took me. The prosecutor was drunk, and said I was not the man; but afterwards said I was. If I had taken his money I should have had more than fourpence on me. GUILTY.
