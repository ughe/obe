indicted for the wilful murder of Mary Ann Robinson. In a Second Count
Addison was charged as a principal and Boyce as an accessory after the fact

MESSRS. POLAND, MONTAGU WILLIAMS, and HORAOE AVORY Prosecuted;
MR. EDWARD CLARK, with MR. SAPPOBD, appeared for Addison, and
Mr. Fulton for Boyce.
The details of the evidence in this case (one of procuring abortion), care of
a nature unfit for publication.
