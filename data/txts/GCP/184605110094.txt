street, Shoreditch. About eleven o'clock at night, on the 4th of April, I was

round, and saw the prisoner and another person walking away from me I
put my hand into my pocket, and my handkerchief was gone-I walked after
them, and saw the prisoner pass a bandkerchief like mine to bis companion
--I laid hold of the prisoner-he struggled, but I kept him till the officer
