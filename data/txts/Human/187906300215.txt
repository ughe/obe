DANIEL HUNT( Detective-Inspecter P. ). I and Chamberlain had Work man in custody last September—I know his wife and Rogers, alias Dowthwaite—Workman made certain communications to me before his conviction, and subsequently his wife also made certain communications to me—I was at Slough on 26th March, when Davis and Harding were tried and convicted—Harding, alias Biker, made a statement to me before his conviction, and at three interviews after his conviction, in consequence of which I saw Mrs. Chandler—I obtained an order from the Home Secretary and saw William Chandler, and made certain inquiries, and then reported to Scotland Yard, and this prosecution was instituted—I obtained an order to see Workman, and saw him at Wandsworth on 24th May, and told him the object of my visit, and that Patterson was in custody with others on this charge, and said, "Now do you know anything about the case?"—he said, "No; I can have nothing to say in the case"—I said, "Do you know Patterson?"—he said, "Yes; I have known him about 16 years"—I said, "We are in possession of the whole facts of the case. We know that you can give evidence in this case if you like. Will you do it?"—he said, "No; I can have nothing to do with that job; I can't give evidence against my brother-in-law. Besides that, it was nearly two years ago it was talked about?—I said, "Do you recollect being in a certain public-house when the keys were shown to Baker by Patterson in your presence?"—he said, "Yes, I recollect being in a public-house with Patterson and Baker at the Father Red Cap, Camber well Green, one Sunday evening last May (1876), but Patterson did not show Baker the keys. In fact, I do net think Baker ever saw them. I went with my wife once about fifteen months ago, and had a look ever the place referring to Maudsley's. Patterson showed us over the premises and showed us the models"—he declined to give further testimony—on 23rd May with Inspector Chamberlain I saw Mrs. Workman at the Orange Tree Beerhouse, Avill Street, camberwell—I said, "Patterson is in custody charged with others conspiring to commit a burglary at Messrs.
