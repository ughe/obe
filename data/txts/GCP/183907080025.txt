2039. WILLIAM SWEENEY was indicted for stealing, on the 20th
of June, 1 cloak, value 10s., the goods of George Scott; and that he had
been before convicted of felony.
GEORGE SCOTT. I am eleven years old, and live with my uncle in
Vincent-square. I had a cloak on the 20th of June - my mama
bought it for me- I saw it safe that afternoon hanging out in the garden
-it was missing afterwards--this is it.
JAMES HOLT (police-constable B 124.) I saw the prisoner about twenty
minutes past three o'clock that afternoon with this cloak under his left arm
-he saw me and threw it away--I took it up and pursued him.
Prisoner. I never saw the cloak till he charged me with it-I was in
a public-house, and he came and called me out. Witness. I am quite
confident he threw it away-he was not more than thirty yards from where
the prosecutor lives when I first saw him.
WILLIAM WARDLAW (police-constable B 87.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's conviction, which I got from the Clerk of the Peace office at
Westminster-(read)-the prisoner is the man.
GUILTY. Aged 15.- Transported for Seven Years.—Convict Ship.
2040. JONATHAN DOBSON was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
July, 6 yards and gths of woollen cloth, value 5l., and 6 yards and gths of
kerseymere, value 21., the goods of James Morgan.
JAMES MORGAN. I live in Castle-street, Holborn, and am a tailor. On
the 3rd of July I lost this woollen cloth and kerseymere from my shop-I
saw it safe an hour before.
Thomas MORGAN. I am the prosecutor's son. On the 3rd of July,
about seven or eight o'clock, I missed this cloth I went to the door, and
the prisoner had dropped a piece in going out- I did not see him in the
shop, but I saw him at the bottom of the street-I pursued him to Fetter-
lane and gave him in charge, with these cloths on his shoulder.
John MANNING (City police-constable, No. 347.) I took the prisoner
with these cloths on him.
Prisoner. A man asked me if I would carry it, and follow him, and he
would give me 6d.
GUILTY.* Aged 20.- Transported for Seven Years.
