EDWARD TRUSTRAM WAND. I am a baker, and reside at Finchley. I
am one of the children of the late John Wand-he left six children had
an uncle named William-my father was the son of Elizabeth Wand-my
father's children were Jobn, Elizabeth, Martha, Mary, Sarah, and myself-I
do not know how many children my uncle William left, but I have been
given to understand four-I am not positive whether one of them was named
Ann- I was not much acquainted with the family--the prisoner was one of
his sons I am not positive whether he had a sister named Elizabeth-I
knew him as being my cousin-the signature to this deed is not my band-
writing-it was not written with my authority or knowledge—I had nothing
to do with it in any way.
