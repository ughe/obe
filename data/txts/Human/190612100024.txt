Mrs.  NICHOLSON  said that prisoner had been in her employment for three months; he bore a very good character, and was the best boy she had ever had. The letter was only a boyish freak. She would take him back into her employment, and she was sure he would not do anything of the kind again. She would become a surety for him. Prisoner was bound over in his own recognizances and those of Mrs. Nicholson in £10 to come up for judgment if called upon, and in the meantime to be of good behaviour. GAVIN  , Edward Hamilton (26, postman), and RANDALL  , John (37, tailor) ; Gavin stealing a post letter containing a postal order for 5s. and one other post letter containing a postal order for 21s., the property of the Postmaster-General , he being employed under the Post Office; Randall feloniously receiving two postal orders for 5s. and 21s. respectively, the property of the Postmaster-General, well knowing them to have been stolen. Gavin pleaded guilty.
