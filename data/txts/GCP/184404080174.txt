THOMAS BENJAMIN WALKER (police-inspector.) In consequence of in-
formation I went on the 15th of March to the London Docks, and saw the
prisoner-I asked if he had ever worked for Elliott's, the brewers, at Pimlico
-- he said, "No"-I said, "Well if you did, here is a gentleman here at
hand who will know you"-I then took him to Mr. Thomas, and said, "This
gentleman charges you with having absconded from the brewery after having
received money on account of Messrs. Elliott, is that so ?"-he said, “Yes"
-I said, “How was it?"- he said, "I got too much to drink, I lost the
money, and was ashamed to return."
(The prisoner received a good character.)

1239. WILLIAM SMITH and JAMES WILLIAMS were indicted for
stealing 1 purse, value 6d. ; 12 sovereigns, 8 shillings, and 3 sixpences; the
property of Charlotte Kingsmill, from her person.
CHARLOTTE KINGSMILE. I am a widow, living in Guilford-street, Rus-
sell-square. On the 9th of Feb. I left home at nine o'clock in the morning
- I was in Liquorpond-street, and saw a person named Beard-in consequence
of something I was told I searched my pocket, and missed my purse and
money.
(The other witness did not appear.)

MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.

Head-court. The prisoner was in the "Magnet" newspaper office, in the
Strand-on the 22nd of March I paid him 21. 73. 6d. for six quires and nine
"Magnet" newspapers, on the prosecutor's account.
Henry HALL. I am in the service of Mr. Brooks, a news agent, in War-
wick-square. On the 21st of March I went to the "Magnet" newspaper

and eighteen “Magnets"-I got this receipt from him.
Joseph HARRIBON. I am in the service of Mr. Thomas, news agent, Fincb-
Jane, Cornhill. On the 22nd of March I went to the "Magnet” office-I
saw the prisoner, and paid him 21. 78. 6d, for six quires and nine copies of the
"Magnet."
John Brown Bell. I am the proprietor of the "Magnet" newspaper,
in the Strand. The prisoner was in my employ in March, and had been two
or three years, as clerk and publisher of my newspaper-it was part of his
duty to receive money when persons carne for newspapers-when he received
money from the agents it was his duty to enter it in the daily cash-book,
which I have here-it was his duty to pay me every day as I might require it
