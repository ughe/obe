814. JOHN EDINGTON was indicted for stealing, on the Toth of
February, 7 pairs of socks, value 58. 6d., the goods of Mary Holder
Thomas.
Mary HOLDER Thomas. I keep a hosier's shop is Theobald's-road.
On the 10th of February, I saw the prisoner pass the shop, looking into the
window-I saw him lift this bundle of socks from a nail just within the
door, and run off with them-I pursued and saw him drop them-he was
secured without my losing sight of him.
Tuomas Moss. I heard the cry of "Stop thief," and saw the pri-
soner running-I stopped him, and the prosecutrix took up the stockings.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going through Red Lion-passage, and heard
a cry of “Stop thief"- I was running, seeing a man running before me-
I saw that man take the stockings.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Three Months.

thread, with intent to defraud Thomas Stokes. 2nd Count, stating it to be
with intent to defraud Thomas Goodacre.

gold lace-it means a quill of silver-gilt thread if a person produced an

the prisoner had been to me before, and I bad delivered him goods on
previous occasions for Mr. Starkie-I understood he was his apprentice-I

house? Witness. I am quite satisfied he is the person.
