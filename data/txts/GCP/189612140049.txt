85. SOLOMON MAGNUS and OSOAR MAGNUS were again in-
dicted, with JOHN SYDNEY (43), for stealing twenty boxes of sultanas
of JOHN KNILL, the master of Sydney. Second Count-For stealing
boxes of sultanas.
OSCAR and SOLOMON MAGNUS PLEADED GUILTY.
MR. BEARD Prosecuted, and Mr. MUIR Defended Sydney.
ROBERT PHILLIPS. I live at 20, New Tyson Street, Bethnal Green, and
am carman to Pickford and Co.-on October 19th I had instructions to
go to Fresh Wharf for goods, and saw the prisoner Sydney, the delivery
foreman-besides the goods I called for I received twenty boxes of
sultanas, which I had no delivery note for and no receipt-he said, "Take
these twenty boxes, and deliver them to Magnus, as the carrier has lodged
an order, and he could not wait”-I told him that we should collect any
goods, and take them to the White Swan Yurd to our depôt, and
our clerk, Mr. Pinket, made a note of it on October 22nd-I received
two half-boxes a few days afterwards, but I do not know the date-I did
not hand any delivery order for them; Sydney asked me to take thein
and deliver thein; I had no receipt for them; I took them to White
Swan Yard, and never saw them again.
