Court. Q. Who was afraid of being made away with ? A. John
Collins said he was afraid of being made away with; for he had fallen into
very bad hands, and wished to get away from some woman who he was
with-he said he was afraid of being burked; that was the word he used.
Q. How came you at the Two Brewers ? 1. On the 5th, the evening
previous to the Saturday, Owen Collins called with a person named Feeley,
at my house, to know if Dominick Feeley (the prisoner) was therehe
was there-it was as late as ten o'clock, and he asked him if he would go
and interfere on behalf of Owen Collins, on the morrow; to go and inter-
fere, to take his property from the hands of this woman.
Q. Then Owen Collins asked Feeley if he would go the following
morning to interfere about his property being in the hands of this wo-
man? A. Yes; there was another person with Owen Collins, of the
name of Michael Feeley-he was to interfere about John Collins's pro-
perty, who was afraid of being made away with-he appointed to meet
John Collins next day, at twelve o'clock-he went at twelve o'clock, and
met him; and Dominick Feeley called on me in the morning, about ten
o'clock, and asked me to be so good as to come to him at the Two Brewers
- I did not know what it was for, until I went.
MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. How long did you live with the prisoner as his
wife ? A. A very little while I do not think it was six months-it is seven
years ago since I lived with him as his wife-I have seen him frequently
since that time, but not to live with him-sometimes not for a month
together, sometimes six weeks, and sometimes two or three times a week.
Q. In all that time did you know of his having an old friend called John
or Patrick Collins ? A. I was not acquainted with his acquaintances-1
never heard the name, to my knowledgeI do not live in Marmaduke-street
now I have property of my own, and have lived on that since I have
parted from the prisoner-I believe the prisoner lodged in Ray-street,
and sometimes in his own house, in Gloucester-street, Commercial-road-he
was at my house when Owen Collins and Michael Feeley came to ask for
him--Michael Feeley is the prisoner's brother, and he married my sister.
Q. Then you were to walk from Commercial-road to Mutton-hill without
knowing what for ? A. I had other business to do- I was going to see
