Boldiers had not much money to treat friends with I did not see the note
you wrote to your sister at the police station-what was found on you is
entered in the book.
Witnesses for the Defence.
Mrs. CUNNINGHAM. I heard some one come home between 1 and 2 on
the 21st May—you went out again about 7.30 am. ; you came home between
12 and 1 every night, sometimes sooner.
Cross-examined. I told Detective Witham that somebody came in at
daylight, but who it was I did not know—I have four lodgers-you owe me
three weeks rent-you have been in no employment since you left Mr.
Hembrey-you told me you had lost your character.

from there to the Caledonian Road.
