HENRY ROLLASON. I am a surgeon, at the hospital. The prosecutor
came to the hospital on Sunday night, and had his wound dressed – I did
not see him till the Tuesday, when he was brought to me by the policeman
- he had a small wound over the right eye—I dressed it, and it healed—it
was about a quarter of an inch in length-it was such a wound as might be
done with a knife.
Cross-examined. Q. It was very slight, and nearly healed, when you saw
it? A. Yes, it was a cut, not a stab-it appeared to me to have been done
by some sharp instrument.
