Prisoner's Defence. I plead guilty to obtaining goods under false pretences
but I most distinctly repudiate the charge of forgery. The name from
whom that purports to come, has not been forged, it is an assumed name.
I do not attempt to deny that I obtained the silk and pledged it, but I am
not guilty of forgery. I left my situation and wandered about London
to get something to do : hunger pressed me, and, in a moment of desperation,
scarcely knowing what I did, I committed the act for which I stand before
you; and even then I swear I did not intend to defraud Messrs. White ; 1
trusted in the hope of obtaining a situation, and should have gone home and
received from my friends the money to liquidate that claim, befoto Mr.
Churchill could have known.
