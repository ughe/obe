between eight and nine, I never saw her.
Thomas White (Re-examined). The prisoner lived in one room besides
her shop.
GUILTY of manslaughter.-The prisoner had been several times convicted
of being drunk and disorderly, and of assaults. -Six Months' Hard Labour.
523. CHARLES TRUETT (60), Indicted for and charged, on the
Coroner's Inquisition, with the wilful murder of Augusta Caroline Truett.
Messrs. CHARLES MATUews and BODKIN Prosecuted, and Messas.
GEOGHEGAN and Hughes Defended.
Frank Głorge WAYLETT (176 K). I produce the plans of the ground-
floor of the Railway Tavern public house, Three Colt Street, Limehouse.

a little time before that he was living at Elstone, near Bedford, and my
aunt, Augusta Caroline Truett, was left in charge of the public-house-I
assisted her in the business-the prisoner is George Truett's brother
about a fortnight or ten days before May 23rd I saw the prisoner at the
