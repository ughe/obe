706. JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of January,
2lbs. weight of mutton, value 1s., the goods of William Chase.
William RAWLINGS. I live with Mr. William Chase, a butcher, in
St. Andrew's, Holborn. On the 18th of January, about a quarter to ten
o'clock in the evening, I saw the prisoner pass-he took this piece of mut-
ton off the board, put it under his arm, and went on-Twent after him
-I saw a policeman, and he took him.
