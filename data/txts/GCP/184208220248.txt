2528. MARY ANN DAVIS and ESTHER HOWELL were indicted
for stealing, on the 2nd of August, 9 yards of lace, value 21. 98., the goods
of Thomas Stroud and others.
THOMAS STROUD. I am in partnership with John Howell and others,
lacemen, in Regent-street. On the 2nd of August, the prisoners came to the
shop together--they asked to look at some Valenciennes lace for collars.
which I showed them, and they bought some, which came to 178. 6d.
they appeared to be both together--they did not pay the whole of the
money-they left the lace, and went out of the shop-I had shown them a
great many pieces that they did not buy, and I showed them a piece of
the pattern that is here produced-it is a new pattern.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did they leave any money!
A. Yes, I think 168.- I cannot say for a certainty-it might be 178.-it
was in silver-they had bargained for lace-both of them spoke-I am not
positive which paid me.
GEORGE BURTON. I am the prosecutor's assistant-I remember the
prisoners being there on the afternoon of the 2nd of August-Mr. Stroud
was showing them lace- I watched them, and when they rose from their
seats to leave the shop, the prisoner Howell dropped two pieces of lace
about a yard from the counter-I followed them out of the shop, and con-
tinued to keep them in sight till I was induced to give them in charge-I
could not see what they were engaged in, but it appeared to me they were
looking at some articles, or distributing something they were talking
they did not see me till I put my hand on the shoulder of one of them-
they were taken to Bow-street, and a piece of lace was there shown to
me by Taylor-this is it-I know it to be the prosecutor's property from
its general character, and from the pattern, which is peculiar--the lace that
was dropped in the shop was taken up and put into the stock.
WILLIAM TAYLOR. I live in Cannon-square, Chelsea. I was walking
in New-street, Covent-garden, on the evening of the 2nd of August-I met
the two prisoners in custody- followed them, and in Bow-street I picked
up this piece of lace about a yard from the prisoner Howell - I gave it to
the policeman.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. What are you? A. I am out of
business -I was attracted by seeing the prisoners go along- I was going
into the city on business-I followed them, and was close behind them!
