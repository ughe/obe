84. WALDEMAR WITCOWER (41). Unlawfully obtaining money by
false pretences from Isabella Wetzler, with intent to defraud.
MR. LEIGH conducted the Prosecution; and Mr. MONTAGU WILLIAMS
the Defence.
It appeared in this case that the money was obtained by a promise of some-
thing to be done hereafter by the prisoner; and the Court ruled that the false
pretence must be as to an existing fact. The Jury were therefore directed
to find a verdict of Not Guilty.
