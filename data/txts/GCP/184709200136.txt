NEW COURT.- Tuesday, September 28th, 1847.
PRESENT-Mr. Alderman Moon and Mr. COMMON SERJEANT.
Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Serjeant.
2194. WILLIAM BARNETT, THOMAS RUSH, and JOAN RUSR
were indicted for breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Frederick Job
Wood, at St. George's in the East, and stealing therein 36 rings, value 71.10.
his goods.
Messrs. O'BRIEN and ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY REED. I am in the service of Frederick Job Wood, a pour

Barnett and Thomas Rush were looking in at our window-there were ring
watches, and other things in it--they went away, returned, and stood i

bottom of the shop, and saw that a card of rings was gone from the windor
- I went to the door, looked towards Ratcliff-highway, and then looked
towards Shadwell, and saw Barnett-I caught hold of him, and brought hin
back to the shop-I told him on what account I brought him back--he said,
"Me! me! you are mistaken" - I have no doubt that he and Thomas Rush
are the persons I saw at the window-a small corner of the pane of the wit
