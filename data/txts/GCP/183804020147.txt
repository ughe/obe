Q. You have said you lived in the house of Mr. Miers from April to
December, is that true? A. Yes I was turned away on the 9th of No-
ovember, because I was saucy to Mrs. Midgley-my mother did not ask
Mr. Miers to take me back-he did take me back-he sent for me, at
eleven o'clock, one night, when I had been a fortnight out of his service.
