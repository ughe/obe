TAYLOR. John Henry, otherwise D. J. W. Tremayne (25, dis
mond setter) obtaining by false pretences from Francis Reyne, on
July 19, 1907, four diamond pendants, and on July 25, 1907, one
dismond tiara and other anticles, in each case with intent to defraud.
Mr. Tindale Davis prosecuted; Mr. Attenborough represented the
pawnbrokers concerned, with the exception of Mr. Martin, of
Brighton.
Inspeotor FRANK HALLAM. On August 1 I received warrant for
the arrest of prisoner, and on August 3 saw him at Snow Hill Police
Station. I read the warrant to him. and, in reply, he made a long
statement, in which he admitted that the jewellery had been pawned
temporarily. I found upon him a tioket relating to a bag left at
Vietoria Station, whither I went on the following day. From his
bag I produce seven pawnbroker's oontnact notes, which correspond
with his statement as to where he had pawned the things. I also
found in the bag two diamond brooches, a diamond and pearl ring,
a muff chain, and a bracelet. I showed them to prisoner, who said
they were the property of Mr. Reyne, the prosecutor. I also produce
a specimen of visiting cards which I found in his bag with the name
of the Right Hon. W. J. H. Tremayne, of New York. Prisoner's
real name is Taylor, I also produce some "appro." notes found in
Lis bag relating to some of the jewellery from the proseoutor. He
had on him six E10 notes, a E5 note and E2 156. in money, I
have been unsuccessful in tracing the notes. I also found a ticket
relating to a bag at Brighton. It contained a number of papers, but
nothing connected with this case. The date of the first of the
appro." notes is July 19.
