494. JAMES HANKIN, stealing 2 5l. bank-notes, and 5 sovereigns; the
property of Charles Scales.
CHARLES SCALES. I live in Lambeth-walk. The prisoner was my errand-
boy- he came to me on Tuesday, 18th Dec.; and on the following Saturday,
the 22nd, about a quarter to nine o'clock in the morning, I gave him two
51.-notes and five sovereigns to take to Mr. Scott, in Fetter-lane-he saw me
put the five sovereigns into the parcel-I asked him if he had a good pocket
- he said he had he showed it me, and it appeared good-he was to come
back- I did not see him again till he was in custody, a fortnight afterwards.
ROBERT Scott. I live at 44, Fetter-lane. I was to have received a parcel
from Mr. Scales on 22nd Dec.--the prisoner did not bring me any money
or any parcel, either on that day or any other.
THOMAS GARFORTH (policeman, L 151). I took the prisoner on 7th Jan.,
at his father's house, in Paradise-street, Lambeth-I said, “You must
consider yourself in my custody, for absconding with two 51.-notes and five
sovereigns"-he said, "I am willing to go; I am sorry I did not come back
before; I lost the money"-he said he was running after an omnibus; he
could not catch that, and he ran after a cab, and opposite the Horse Guards
he missed the money-1 asked which pocket he put it in-he said, “This,"
pointing to his right-hand trowsers pocket-I said, "Are these the trowsers
that you have on?"-he said, “No; I gave my old trowsers and 3d. for
these.” Guilty. Aged 17-Confined Six Months.

495. JAMES SMITH, stealing 2 pence and 2 halfpence; the moneys of
William Harley, from his person.
WILLIAM HARLEY. I live in Paradise-street, Rotherhithe, and am a
blocker. Between two and three o'clock, on the morning of 15th Jan., I went
into the Surrey Coal-hole--the prisoner and several others were there-two
young men were with me-something took place with the barman-I went
out, and came in again, when the prisoner caught hold of me round the waist,
and I felt his hand in my pocket, and at the same time two men caught hold
of each arm-I had two penny pieces and two halfpence safe in my pocket
two minutes previous to going into the house-I was let go-I then put my
hand into my pocket, and missed them-I went outside, and told my friends
I saw a policeman, and took him inside the prisoner was not there-- I took
him afterwards.

and-easy in Blackfriars-road--I had taken some porter, nothing else the free-

but the house was open-I was there till about two I had known one of my
friends two years, and the other six months-I met one of them at the free-

two and three-- there was a dispute between the barman and one of my friends,
and a quarrel between my other friend and the door-keeper--the barman
