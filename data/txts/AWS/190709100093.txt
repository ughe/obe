Cross-examined.: The men working at the table where the letters

Prisoner's statement to the magistrate: Sir, I am charged with
stealing a letter, which I did not, and also changing it at the Rail.
way Hotel, which I did not. The said letter was not found on me.
I think it hard to be accused of stealing when you have not. I am
a regular customer at the hotel; I go for my beer for dinner and
supper for my wife and me. I have also changed several postal
orders for my wife at the same hotel. There has been a great mis-
take there. The shilling postal order has been taken over the counter
from A postman and put aside; me being a regular eustomer I got
the blame, which I tell yon on my oath I did not, as 8 man to a
gentleman It is hard that I should have the stain on my chansoter
which I have not got. I have been 15 years in the Pont Office A8
an auxiliary postman. I have never taken one farthing piece from
them or anyone alse."
(Monday, September 16.)
(Defence.)
ERxEST JORN DAMANT (prisoner, on, oath). I live at 417, Hornsey
Rise. I have been an auxiliary postman for 15 years, and, as far a8
I know, hold an exeellent character. Previously I Wa8 for 12 years
in the Army as a musieian, leaving with A very good character. My
wife has from time to time received postal orders for 1s. and 1s. 6d.
I often changed them at the Railway Hotel," which is just across
the road from my house; I think that is nearer than the post office.
I was well known in that public-house. I often went in my private
dress and sometimes in uniform, at night, on my way home. I have
never seen the postal order, the subjeet of the charge. I did not
change any postal order at the hotel on the day in question. I might
have been in there that night, but not in uniform. I am not in uni-
form after four on Saturday. When I get home on Saturday, which
is about four, I go upstairs and play the violin. On this Saturday I
had my tea and changed my clothes. Then I went and had some
praetice on the violin. I never go out before about nine o'eloek.

then. I get them from my wife when I get home. I generally
