1119. FREDERICK WESTON (22), Embezzling and stealing 21.,
101. 118. 2d., and 11. 108., received by him for and on account of Messrs.
Hobbs and Co., his masters.
MR. WILLES Prosecuted.
RICHARD ORFORD LAMPREY. I am a medical man, of 62, East Hill,
Wandsworth--at the end of May last I went to Messrs. Hobbs and Co.
to order a carpet and some other things, which they sent on approbation
--a day or two afterwards the prisoner came to my house and received
the money for the goods, and gave me this receipt (produced).
Cro88-ecamined by the Prisoner. The first intimation I had from Messrs.
Hobbs and Co. that I had not paid, was about five weeks ago.
ULRIOH STEUR. I am an artist of Norwood-on the 7th April I paid
Messrs. Hobbs and Co. an account of 21., through the prisoner, for which
he gave me this receipt (produced).
STEPHEN COLLINS. "I am a contractor, of 10, Child's Road, Ilford-I
paid the prisoner on behalf of Messrs. Hobbs and Co. 30s. on account on
10th August; this is the receipt (produced).
THOMAS CHAMBERLAIN. I am manager of the furnishing department
of Messrs. Hobbs and Co., in whose employ the prisoner was up to
September last, as clerk and cashier-he had to keep the books, receive
moneys and account for them, and attend to customers—this cash book is
in the prisoner's handwriting (produced)—there is no entry of the pay-
ment of Dr. Lamprey's account, nor has the prisoner handed over the
money--the same applies to Stehr's account of 27., and to Collins' account
-I have also searched the ledger (produced ), in which, also, the amounts
are not accounted for.
Cross-examined. Messrs. Hobbs and Co. withdrew from certain charges
against you on account of your difficulties and for the sake of your wife
and children, but we didn't know then of these charges.
Guilty.-Eighteen Months' Hard Labour.
