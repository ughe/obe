95. JOHN STANLEY (36), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
Messrs. LLOYD and Cooke Prosecuted.
EMILY SHARPE. I keep the King's Head, Broad Street, Bloomsbury-
on 30th November, about 8.30 p.m., I served the prisoner with some
lemonade, which came to 2d.; he put down & florin, I bent it in the
tester, and told him it was bad-he said nothing, but put it in his pocket
and gave me a bad half-crown-I told him it was bad he said that is
not the one I gave you; you have got my good one "-I said “It is"
I noticed that it had a Queen's head on it-he went out with both the
coins, and was brought back by a constable this half-crown (produced)
is not the one he gave me; the other was black, and bent like a piece of
lead.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I sent the barman after you-I can
swear that this is not the half-crown.
WILLIAM THORPE. I am barman at the King's Hend-I saw the

it was dark-looking I did not observe whether it had a King's head or s
Queen's I tried it in the tester, and it bent like a piece of lead-I gave
it back to the landlady--the prisoner took it up, and said "I will carry
it back to the one I took it from "-I followed him, and found him standa
ing still in Museum Street, 60 or 80 yards off-I told him I wanted him, and
he ran away-& constable caught him, and I said that he had passed
