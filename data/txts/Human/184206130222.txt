JOHN STEVENS( police-constable R  55.) The prisoner was given into custody fifteen or twenty yards from the shop—she had these trowsers under her arm, exposed, so that any one could see them—she said they were given her by a gentleman—she appeared to be in liquor, but pre tended to be so more than she actually was.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A man gave me the trowsers; he told me to get my self a pint of beer, and come up the street; I cannot exactly tell the words, for I had been drinking. GUILTY. Aged 55.— Confined One Month.

 1982. ERASMUS WATSON and THOMAS ANGELINE were in dicted for stealing, on the   18th of May , 1 handkerchief, value 9 d  ., the goods of a man unknown, from his person; to which
   WATSON  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 12.— Confined Six Months. WILLIAM RUSSELL( police-constable C  113.) On the 18th of May I was at Greenwich fair, and saw Watson take this handkerchief from the right-hand pocket of a man who stood looking at Richardson's show, and give it to Angeline—I took Angeline—he dropped it—we called to the gentleman that he had been robbed, but he never came to the station—there was such a crowd, we were pushed away from him directly. WILLIAM ROMAINE( police-sergeant L  38.) I saw Watson take the handkerchief out of a gentleman's pocket, and give it to Angeline—I took Watson—the gentleman asked for his handkerchief—I told him he must come to the station—he made towards us, but there was a great mob, and we missed him.
