GEORGE WARDLE (City police-constable, No. 235.) I went with Gard-
mer and Powell to Seven Dials on Sunday the 9th-Martin was pointed

arms-he made a great resistance, but with the assistance of Gardner, we
secured him.
Cross-examined by MR. CARTEEN. Q. You, Gardner, Morris, and
Powell went together? A. Yes, we followed one behind the other on
different sides of the street-Powell was not more than two or three yards
from Martin when he pointed him out--Powell said, “That is him"-there
was another man with Martin at the time, and he struck at Powell with a
stick-Gardner rushed to seize Martin, and then he tried to make his
escape-we were all in plain clothes-it is a place of resort for thieves-I.
am not often there.

Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
2876. JANE DEDRIDGE was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of
September, 1 writing-case, value 18s.; 1 blanket, value 38.; 1 shirt,
Value 1s.; 1 pair of boots, value 10s.; 2 pairs of stockings, value 1s.;
1 petticoat, value 1s. ; 2 caps, value 12s. ; 1 pocket-book, value 1s.;
1 knife, value 18.; 4 pairs of gloves, value 2s. ; 1 bag, value 18.; 5 yards
of net, value 1s.; and i handkerchief, value 1s.; the goods of John Sidcombe
Hazard, her master; to which she pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 45.-Confined Six Months.
2877. JAMES DUKE was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of Octo-
ber, 2 scarfs, value 88., the goods of George William Watkins; to which
he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Confined Three Months.
2878. THOMAS SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing, on the 10th
of October, 1 tea-caddy, value 6s., the goods of William Wright, and that
he had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Transported for Seven Years-Convict Ship.
2879. CHARLES HARGRAVE was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd
of October, 1 sovereign and 1 shilling, the monies of Robert Norton, his
master; to which be pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 12.-Transported for Seven Years-Isle of Wight.
