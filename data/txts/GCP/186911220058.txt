THIRD COURT.-Thursday, November 25th, 1869.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
55. ROBERT CRAUFURD (45), was indicted for conspiring, with one
William Penny, to cheat and defraud Joseph Batchelor and others of their
moneys.
MR. GRIFFITHS conducted the Prosecution; and MR. METCALFE the Defence.
JOSEPH BATCHELOR. I live at 116, Stanley Street, Pimlico, and am an
omnibus driver on the 6th February a person named William Penny
brought me this cheque (produced) for 121. 108.-he said, "Batchelor, I
have come to pay you the 41. 10s. I owe you; I have only a cheque, which
I received from Colonel Craufurd, and when I got to the bank it was
closed, and if you can give me the difference of it, it is all right"-I told
him that I could not, for I had been out of employment sixteen months;
but I said, “I will take you up to my butcher - I took him to Mr. Croft,
in Lupus Street, and asked him if he would be kind enough to cash it for
me--he said, "Who is Penny; do you know him ?"--I said, " I have known
him twenty-five years as an omnibus driver" he said, "Where is he?"-
I said, “Outside "-I brought him in-he said, “Well, this is Saturday, I
am rather short, I will let you have 61. 108., and your 41. 108. will make
111., and if he comes up on the Monday I will give him ll. 10s.- I called

-I told him he had better keep it a few days, as perhaps he had overdrawn
