BUA TAYLOR. I am sister of Thomas Taylor, a tobacconist, in Eagle-
street, City-road. Between nine and ten o'clock, on 24th March, the pri-
poner came for 6d.-worth of cigars, and put down a 5s, piece I gave him
4.6d., and put the crown into the till-my brother came in immediately
after, and saw it was bad-there was no other crown in the till-I marked it,
and gave it to the policeman.

ROBERT JEYPREYS (policeman, N 165.) On Monday, 26th March, I was
at the Hoxton police-station-a young woman was brought there on some
charge—when she was there the prisoner came into the station and there was
some interference on his part, and he was taken into custody-he was
searched-I found a crown-piece in his right-hand pocket, and a sixpence-I
pave them to the inspector, who put them on the desk-in four or five
minutes the inspector called me, and told me the crown was bad-I put a
mark on it, and so did he-I told the prisoner it was båd --he said nothing
it has been in my possession ever since.
