JANE Scare. I live at Whitby, in Yorkshire. My brother was a clerk
at the shop of Mr. William Rotherham-I think I know his handwriting
this account of mine, in this book, is in my brother's writing-he was, for
any thing I know, the chief clerk, and manager at Mr. Rotherham's; and,
I believe, be kept his books I gave an acceptance for a bill of 1281.,
about the end of October, 1834- I did not pay 501. on the 16th of May,
1834 ; or 50l. on the 2nd of June, 1894 ; nor did I afterwards pay

JAMES WILLIAM BUCKLAND. I am a refiner of metals, and a manufac-
turer of British plate. I know the bankrupt-I recollect his coming to
me some time after his last examination-somewhere early in the year,
but I cannot tell the time it was soon after he became a bankrupt-I had
an interview, (but not at our house,) after he past his last examination-it
was at No. 18, Finsburr-square, at Mr. John Scafe's, who had been his
clerk--I learnt from the bankrupt that he had passed his examination he
said he was asked at the Court whether he had not a valuable gold watch
- he said he had a watch in his pocket: and he drew it out of his pocket,

was some observation which I cannot recollect; but he was told, if that
was the watch to put it into his pocket-the watch which he produced was
a metal one with a bit of string-he showed me the watch he produced
