1985. EMMA SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of June, 1
sovereign, the monies of Richard Owen, from his person.
RICHARD Owen. I am a tailor, living in Upper Whitecross-street. I met
the prisoner in Long Acre, and went and stopped all night with her, and went
to bed-I had 41. 108. in gold-I awoke about five o'clock in the morning
and had breakfast—we went to some friends of hers, and stopped a good part
of the day-we had some gin, and went home about four in the afternoon-
I sat down, and fell asleep-we went out together again about six to a public
house, and called for some drink-I then missed a sovereign and some silver
-I had had three sovereigns in my pocket-I gave her half-a-sovereign, and
gave her a sovereign to get drink-I had changed one about two o'clock-
accused her of robbing me--she said she had not- I cannot tell who came in
while I was asleep.
- William WYNCH (police-constable F 62.) I took the prisoner-she
offered the prosecutor a half-sovereign not to give her in charge - I found no
sovereign.
Not Guilty.
