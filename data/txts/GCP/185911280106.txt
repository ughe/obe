JOHN CATLIN. I live at Deptford-on Monday, 17th October, as near as
I can tell, between 8 and 9 o'clock in the evening, I was going for some
tobacon, through the alley leading to Brunswick-sqnare-I saw some persons
in the alley-I saw some men in the alley with a woman underneath them,
and I heard the cries of "Murder ! the woman is dead "-I came and
released the woman from a man who had her by the bair of the head-I
took her up, and then went out for a policeman, but when I came back,
there was a policeman there I cannot say whether the man is here that
had her by the hair of the head, because I did not know them at the time
I could not speak to any of them-there were three persons.
Cross-examined. Q. What o'clock was this? A. I do not know ; it was
between 8 and 9 o'clock.
ANN HEWLAND. I am married---my husband is a labourer-I live at
Deptford-on Monday night, 17th October, as near as I can say, between
11 and 12 o'clock, I heard a disturbance in Brunswick-square-I was in bed,
and I got out of bed when I heard the rou, and went to Scamel's door-1
saw John Ahearn and his brother go to Scamel's door, and ask if he was a
inind to come out and fight him-Scamel said he could not fight two--some
inan spoke and said he would see fair play--then the two men rushed at
Scamel's door, and knocked him down-the three were all down togetber-
I saw John Abearn and another man getting up-Ahearn's face was then
all over blood-I saw him go to his brother's house, and wash his face, and
then come to Scamel's door, and then his brother shoved him up before him
to Scamel's door-I saw Ahearn's wife come out to Scamel, and call him &
cuckold, and spit in his face, and make a kick at him he was then standing
in his own door Patrick Ahearn used a great many bad words I heard
some man call ont to mind the stick-Scamel went inside his door-the
Ahearns stopped outside, abusing him--I saw Ahearn's wife stoop down to
pick up a brick or & stone, I do not know which, and they told her to put
it down.
Cross-examined Q. Who was the other man getting up besides John
Ahearn? A. I think it was Scamel-when Ahearn was getting up, his face
was all bleeding-I saw him before this row began-his nose was cut then.
MR. HORRY. Q. Were there pebbles there ? A. Yes--he fell just outside
