SAMUEL TURNER (policeman, P 356). I was on duty at Peckham, on
31st May-some information was given to me, and I followed the two pri-
soners, who were together, from Peckham to New Cross I did not see
Browning go into Mr. M'Cormack's, but I saw him in the shop, and I went in
and took him into custody--I left him in eustody of some men, and I went
up the road to look for Burns-I found bim about 300 yards off, and brought
him back, and told him it was for uttering base coin-he denied all know-
ledge of it, and said he did not know Browning-I found on him 78. 4d. in
silver, and 74d. in copper; and on Browning, a half-crown, and 24d., and a
pot of bear's-grease--this is the half-crown I received from Wood-Burns
gave his address, No. 2, Broad-street, Golden-square-I went there, but
could hear nothing of him.
