 901. JOHN THOMPSON  , alias    John Tomkins, was indicted for steal ing, on the   5th of February , 1 bag, value 2 d  .; 601bs. weight of nails, value 2 l  .; and 31bs. weight of copper, value 2 s  .; the goods of Charles Thomas Rattenbury .
   MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution. CHARLES THOMAS RATTENBURY. My father was the proprietor of the Fore-and-aft-Dock, Bermondsey. On the 5th of February I purchased the ship Bolivar , of Greenwich, with the nails and copper—as the copper was stripped off, the nails fell out—I employed some boys to pick them up and put them into this bag, which I tied round and flung into the bottom of the dock, near the steps, which is fifteen feet deep from the wall—the vessel was in the dock at this time—I have not the least doubt that these are my nails—Brome gave them to me about an hour and a half from the time I threw them into the dock, but I saw them there half an hour before. WILLIAM BROME. On the 5th of February I was employed by Mr. Rattenbury to carry away some copper—between one and two o'clock I was left in charge of the dry dock—I saw a bag of nails at the foot of the steps—when I came round the bows of the vessel I missed them, and saw the prisoner with them on his back—he could not see me till I came round—I followed him and sung out, "Stop thief"—he went to East-end-stairs, and threw the nails into a boat, and got into the boat—I went and got them from him, and he got away—I saw him again in about a quarter of an hour—I am sure he is the man.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did I wrestle with you in the boat? Witness.  No; you seemed stupid. JOHN FARTHING. I work for Mr. Gordon, who keeps a shop near the Fore-and-aft-Dock. On the 5th of February the prisoner came and ordered a hook of me—this is the hook I made for him; and while I was at work I saw him go by with a bag of nails on his back—he was not tipsy when he gave me the order, but he was when he was taken. GEORGE MARTIN. I am a waterman. I saw the prisoner, on the 5th of February, heave a bag into my boat at East-end-stairs, Brome jumped into my boat after him, and took him. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Three Months.
