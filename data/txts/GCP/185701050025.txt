HENRY BINGHAM. I am a police officer attached to the Post-office. I
was fetched to Mr. Steel's room about 5 minutes to 8 o'clock on Thursday
evening, 18th, and was ordered by him to search the prisoner—as I was in
the act of doing so, he took from his right hand trowsers pocket this packet
directed to Nottingham, and gave it to me, saying, "Here is another"-I
searched him further, and from his coat tail pocket I took this packet,
directed to Norwich I asked him how he came to take these packetshe
said, “I took them for money, because I have not received any wages for
the last three weeks"-I found on him 128. 6d. in silver, and 2d. in copper,
in a purse in his pocket.
FREDERICK Cox, I am an optician in Newgate-street. On 18th Dec. I
made up this packet, addressed to Mr. Titchener, of Chelmsford—it contains
a pair of spectacles I put a stamp on it, and either posted it myself, or
sent it to the post.
ROBERT READ. I am assistant to Mr. Parsons, a chemist, of St. Mary-
axe. On 18th Dec. I made up this packet, addressed to Mr. Wilcoxon,
Nottingham: it contains a sample of castor oil in a small bottle-I gave it
to Sparkes to post.
John SPARKES. I am in Mr. Parson's employment. On 18th Dec. I
received this packet from Mr. Read, placed a stamp on it, and posted it in
Bishopsgate-street, between 5 and 6 o'clock that afternoon.
Cross-examined. 6. What do you mean by “posting" itt 1. I dropped
it into the letter box; I think it is at No. 54, Bishopsgate-street-it is a
grocer's, named Bridges—I posted another letter at the same time; that
had a 10d. stamp.
- MCRAY. I am assistant to Messrs. Bell, chemists and druggists,
of Oxford-street. On 17th Dec. I made up this packet, addressed to Mr.
Buck, of Norwich-it contains & quarter of a pound of lozenges I gave it
that same day to Edward Buck.
EDWARD BUCK (policeman, D 255). On 17th Dec. I received this packet
from Mr. M'Cray, and on the 18th I posted it in Upper Berkeley-street,
about half past 2 o'clock in the afternoon.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you put it into the letter box ? A. No, it was
too large to go into the box, and I took it inside, and gave it to the post-
master.
JAMES STEEL re-examined. This packet bears the Inland-office stamp-
that shows that it arrived that day at the General Post Office—the others
do not bear that stamp, I presume they must have been taken before they
went to the Inland-office-they do not bear the stamps of the district offices
--they ought to do 80-they appear to have been treated as newspapers,
which are not stamped.
GUILTY. Aged 23.---Six Years Penal Servitride.
