December, at St. Sepulchre, 1 ox, value 281.; the property of Jonas Payne.
-2nd Count. Stating it to be the property of Thomas Allcock.
Mr. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.

inghamshire. I have a brother, who lives at Charlton, named Jonas
- we are both farmers and graziers, and are in the habit of sending stock
