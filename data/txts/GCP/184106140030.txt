1573. EDWARD COSTELLO was indicted for stealing, on the 128
of June, 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of George Ward, from his
person.
GEORGE WARD. I live in Bread-street, Cheapside. On Saturday
evening, the 12th of June, I was on Blackfriars-bridge-I felt a slight tog
at my pocket, and turning sharply round I found the prisoner standing
behind me in the act of Ainging my handkerchief down- I took it up
his feet, and secured him immediately.
Prisoner. Two men were taken into custody? Witness. On our way to
the station-house, a boy came up, tapped him on the shoulder and said,
“Bill, I will tell somebody," who he named, and he ran down East-stres
-a person came up and said, “There are two of his companions on the
other side of the street, I advise you to take them"-one of my friends
walking with me went and gave them in charge, but we could not connet
them with the prisoner-I have a few iron-mould marks on the handker
chief by which I know it.
JOHN NICHOLSON (City police-constable, No. 372.) I took charge of the
prisoner, and have the handkerchief.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Six Months.
1574. WILLIAM WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the lid
of June, a pair of trowsers, value 28. 6d., the goods of Edward Gude.
EDWARD GUDE. I am a drover in the employ of Mr. Golding, a
butcher at Staines. On the night of the 11th of June I slept at hin
house-I left my trowsers on a nail in the wash-house, which was not
fastened except with a latch-next morning, when I came down, I missed
them-I went with the policeman, and found them at the station--these
are them.
Peter O'TOOLE (police-constable T 140.) I was on duty at Staines on
the night of the 11th of June-I met the prisoner in High-street with the
trowsers in his hand-when he saw me before him he tried to conceal them
under his coat-I secured him-he at first said he found them coming
from Ascot races, but at the station he said he took them out of a back
house, where he was going to sleep-I thought by his description it was
Mr. Golding's premises - I took him there, and he said it was the place –
he appeared in distress.
GUILTY. Aged 42.-Confined Two Months.
