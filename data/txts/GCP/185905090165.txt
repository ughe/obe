MR. RIBTON. Q. Whether it is a patent key or not, does it open the
door! A. It does I have been living in the house about eleven or
twelve weeks I left last Saturday I believe the prisoner has been mar
ried to Mrs. Waldron's son some twenty years she was not living in the

know-she did not sleep there—I have never heard her and Mrs. Waldron
quarrelling-I only speak of what my wife and the old lady have told me.

MR. DOYLE. Q. Are you and Ansell constant friends! A. I never

or twice a week, not oftener.
