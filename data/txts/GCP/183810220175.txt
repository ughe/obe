2495. JAMES SHARP was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of Septem-
ber, eight wheels, value 61., the goods of Thomas Kesterton and another,
his masters.
EDWARD KESTERTON. I am of the firm of Thomas Kesterton and
another, coach-makers, at Newington-causeway. We lost eight wheels
about the 7th of September--they were worth 61.—the prisoner was in our
service-these are our wheels-(looking at them.)
GEORGE HAMMOND. I am a furniture-broker, and live in Suffolk-street,
Borough. I purchased these four wheels on the 7th of September, of a
man very much resembling the prisoner, but I am not positive of him, for
286.- I gave 113. for the first pair, and 12s. for the second pair-I
rather expect the prisoner was the man, but I cannot positively say-I have
carried on business six years-I never saw him before the wheels are
new-the party gave his name and address-I knew if I had not bought
them, some one else would.
Thomas Mickel. I live in Great Surrey-street, and am a wheelwright.
I purchased six wheels of the prisoner for 16s.-not one of them were
new, and they were all odd wheels but two of them.
Prisoner. I only took two wheels from my master.
GUILTY. Aged 36.-Confined Six Months.
