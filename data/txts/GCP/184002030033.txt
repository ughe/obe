584. JANE AUSTIN was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of January,
1 dead fowl, price 48., the goods of Francis Gosden.
Francis Gosden. I am a fishmonger, and live in Gray's-place, Bromp-
ton. On the 14th of January I lost a fowl off a shelf in the window.
HENRY GREENWOOD. I am shopman to the prosecutor. About twelve
o'clock on the 14th of January, the prisoner came and asked the price of
some fowls-I told her she said they were too dear-she came back in
about an hour, and looked at the fowls outside, and I saw her take one and
walk away- I went after her, and found it under her cloak--she said she
had bought it of a person at the next shop, and gave 28. 8d. for it-I went
there, and they had not sold her any it was my master's I have no doubt.
Prisoner's Defence. I went to the next house, and bought this fowl for
28. 8d.
-
STEPHEN CHURCH. I live next door to the prosecutor. The prisoner
came to my house, and looked at some fowls, but did not buy one--the one
she had was not mine.
