856. ELIZA GOODRIDGE was again indicted for stealing 1 blanket,
1 sheet, and 1 table-cloth, value 108.; the goods of Frederick Inglis.
MR. THOMPSON conducted the Prosecution,
ELLEN INGLIS. I am the wife of Frederick Inglis, of 13, Bull-and-
Mouth-street. The prisoner occupied our second-floor front-room-she
took it of me—the furniture was mine-the policeman came with the key
of the prisoner's lodging; I then missed a blanket, sheet, and table-cloth,
worth about 10s.- I did not give the prisoner any authority to pawn them.
