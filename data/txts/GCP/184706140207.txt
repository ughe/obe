1538. JAMES HURLEY was indicted for stealing 1 coat, value 17, the
goods of Walter Crutchley; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
Thomas PARNELL. I live with Mr. Walter Crutchley, a tailor, in High-
street, in the Borough. On the 24th of May I received information about a
fishing-coat-a woman came in with the ticket which had the price of the
coat on it—we then missed the coat from the block.
Mary HOOPER. I am the wife of George Hooper-we live in Falcon-
court, in the Borough. On the 24th of May I was standing at the top of the
court, and saw the prisoner-he turned the corner very quickly-he had a coat
hanging over his arm-I could not tell what it was till he got by me, and
then he took the ticket off the coat and threw it away-I took it up, and
charged him with stealing it-he ran away- I took the ticket to the prose-
cutor's, and they missed the coat off the block, which was inside the door.
Prisoner. Q. Did you see me take the coat? A. No, I had a suspicion,
because you took the ticket off and threw it away-I saw your face--you were
closer to me than you are now-you went round to the right-I had seen
you so often, that I could not be mistaken in you-I can swear you are the
person-you bad the coat on your left arm.
HENRIETTA MASTERS. I live in Falcon-court. On the 24th of May I
saw the prisoner in the prosecutor's shop-he afterwards passed me with a
coat on his arm-I saw him take the ticket off it and throw it down-Mrs.
Hooper took it up-I am certain the prisoner is the man.
Thomas Hay (police-sergeant M 9.) On the 1st of June, after a long
run and a severe struggle, I took the prisoner, and took him to the station
he ran off immediately he saw me looking at him-he was identified by one
of the witnesses as the man- I have the ticket which I got at Mr. Crutchley's
shop.
MARY HOOPER re-examined. This is the ticket I picked up.
DE
THOMAS PARNELL re-examined. The coat that is missed had this ticket
on it, and it was the property of Mr. Walter Crutchley.
Prisoner's Defence. Does it stand to reason, that a person walking the
street with a coat, could be known by a stranger? I should have had wit-
nesses to prove I was at Stepney fair.
GEORGE WILD (police-constable M 94.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction at this Court-(read-Convicted 21st of Sept.,
1846, and confined six months)-the prisoner is the person.
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined One Year. La
