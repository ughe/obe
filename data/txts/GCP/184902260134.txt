778. EDWARD BAILEY, JOHN BAILEY, CHARLES OUTRAM,
and CAROLINE BAILEY, unlawfully assaulting Henry Cotton, a consta-
ble, in the execution of his office.
HENRY COTTON (policeman, R 118). On Sunday morning, at a quarter
to one o'clock, I was on duty in Garden-row, Greenwich, and was called to
quell a disturbance-Edward Bailey came up and struck me on the mouth
with his fist-I took him by the collar, and six men rushed on me-I was
knocked down, and had blows all over me-John Bailey and Outram were
there--they struck me about the body and head-I was kicked a good deal-
Jobo Bailey had a stick, and broke it on my head-I have been ill ever since.
Cross-eramined by Mr. PLUMPTRE. Q. What kind of night was it?
1. A fine moonlight night-the place is not much frequented--there are
houses on both sides.
Thomas BRIAN (policeman, R 184). I heard the row, and saw six or seven
people there, all on one man--they seemed to be trying to get at him, to
strike him I found Cotton on the ground-Edward Bailey had bis arm
round his neck, and his hand inside his stock-Cotton was bleeding very
much-I saw John Bailey run away.
Cross-eramined. Q. This was merely a drunken row? A. They did not
appear to be drunk.
Ann HUTTON. I heard screams of "Murder," and saw John and Edward
Bailey beating a constable---Caroline Bailey and Outram were there--I did
not see them do anything.
HARRIETT HUTTON. I saw Edward and John Bailey using Cotton dread-
fully-I saw Outram two or three yards off-Caroline Bailey was beaving
stones at the constable.
Cross-examined. Q. How do you get your living? 4. My father and
mother keep me, and I go out shoe-binding- I have attended two or three
fairs, serving out spirits at my grandfather's booth-it was a moonlight night
-there were several men and women about.
MARTHA FOSTER. I heard cries, and saw John Bailey with Cotton, on
the ground-two women were standing opposite him-one of them was
heaving stones.
