2883. JoHN YATES was indieted for stealing, on the 15th of August,
2 pairs of boots, value 308. the goods of Join Shaw, his master.
JOHN SHAw. I live in Queen-street, Grosvenor-square. The prisoner was
in my employment--ot the 15th of Aug. I left him in the shop while I went
to dinner-I came back in about twenty minutes, and missed him and three
pairs of boots-the two pairs produced are mine.
EDNUND SPEARING. I am a shoemaker, and live in Fulham-road, Chelsea.
On the 15th of Aug-, about four o'clock, the prisoner came with this pair of
boots on his feet, and this pair in his pocket-he said the pair on his feet
bis master had given him, and the other belonged to his father, who was dead,
and his mother gaye him them to disposo of-he wanted some victuals-he
sold the pair on his feet to a groom, who is one of my customers- am sure
these are what he broughthe sold the two pairs three weeks afterwards.
Prisoner's Defence. These boots were not sold for three weeks Mr. Saw
could not swear he saw me take them: he beguiled me from my master.
MR. SHAW re-eramined. I did not beguile him.

2884. CHARLES WEST was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of Oet.,
10 shirts, value 4l. 2 shifts, 108. 2 pairs of drawers, 28. 2 petticoats, 28.
collars, 1s.: 3 habit shirts, 1s. and 4 handkerchiefs, 38. the goods of
lohn Chester; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
ELIZABETH CHESTER. I am the wife of John Chester, of Dalziel-place,
a plumber. On the 19th of Oct., about ten 'clock, I fastened up the wash-
house, and left these things safe-about three 'clock in the morning I was
called up by the police, and found the washhouse in great confasion, and the
property removed, and placed in different places to where I had left it the
night before-the window had been opened by removing the fastenings-] am
sure it was fastened at night.
THOMAS EATON (policeman.) About two o'clock in the morning E was on
duty, and saw the prisoner go to the washhouse, try the door, and then go to
the window- square of glass was out, he put his hand in, removed several
things which were placed against the window, at last I stepped forward to the
window and opened it, having seen him go in--I did not see him remove the
linen-] found it just inside the window the prosecutrix being called up
she said it had been removed-I took him into custody.
Prisoner. The linen was taken out of a tub, and put into a basket, after I
was taken. Witness. I did not remove it till after the prosecutrix came
down.
TIOTHY WELLs (policeman.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner S
former conviction, from Mr. Clark's office-(read)-he is the person who was
tried and convicted
OILTY.-Transported for Ten Years.
