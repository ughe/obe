THOMAs ROBINsoN. I live at 6, Thurloe-place, Lower Norwood, and am
the owner in conjunction with my son of 5, Farringdon-street, in the parish

James Sivewright, for twenty-one years)-it being empty in November, I
engaged Mr Martin to let it, and in consequence of what he told me, I met
Perry at his office on 23d November-Perry proposed to take the house for

and that he should want several hundreds to get it ready-he said that he
was going to commence almost immediately-I ultimately agreed to take
1001. by three bills, and he signed this agreement (produced), in which he
