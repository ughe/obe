I was sitting on the terrace on Tower-hill—I saw the prisoner and another man running towards the Tower—the pri soner threw down the basket.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How near were you to him? A.  About twelve yards, but I was nearer when he was collared in the Tower—I saw him stopped by the watchman—I swear I saw the basket drop—he was taken into the guard-room—I am a night watchman on the river. JOHN WOODCOCK. I am a labourer. I went into the guard-room, and searched—I found this pocket-book behind the coal-box.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Who was. in the guard-room A.  Four guards and the corporal—the prisoner was not there then—he had been taken away—I suppose he had been in the guard-room half an hour. JOHN CRESSET. I stopped the prisoner, and put him into the guard-house—he resisted violently. GUILTY. Aged 35.— Transported for Seven Years.
 2136. ELIZABETH HAINES was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of June , 1 carpet, value 1 l  ., the goods of John Davies . JOHN DAVIES. I live in Shepherd-street, May-Fair, and am a job coachman. On the 27th of June, I left a carpet on the staircase outside my room—I lost it—this is it. MARY SAUL, I live in Shepherd-street. I was in a shop opposite the prosecutor's—I saw the prisoner go in and corns put with this carpet—I had known her before. RICHARD FISHER. I am a pawnbroker. I produce the carpet, which was pawned with me by a female on the 27th of June—I cannot tell who. SUSANNAH HOUGHTON. I keep the house—the prisoner came to me, and took a room—she came to me on the 27th, to know if she could come in—I went up stairs, and, while I was up, the carpet was taken. GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Three Months.
