value 11. 1 spectacle-case 4s. 1 saw, 28. and 12 glasses, in horn frames,
11.; the goods of Henry Pocock, her master.
HENRY POCocK. I am an engineer, and live in Long-lane, Ber-
mondsey. The prisoner was employed in my house oceasionally for two
or three days in a week-I missed a saw, and asked if she had seen it
-she said she had not-I lost some glasses set in horn, a pair of silver spee
tacles, and a pair of steel spectacles, from a mahogany box near the window,
in a room which she had access to-the prisoner said something to me at the
station, in consequence of which I went to a place where I found them
-] had not made her any promise, nor threatened her-she wished to see me
-I went, and she told me direetly that they were at an optician's shop in

policeman called there and got them.
THOMAs TAYLOR. I am a cabinet-maker, and live in Newland-court, St.

weeks ago yesterday-I saw a box of sights, and a pair of steel spectacles in
her possession.
ALEXANDBR M'KAY (poliee-constable M 265.) I took the prisoner-she

would forgive her-I produce the glasses, spectacles, and the saw.
