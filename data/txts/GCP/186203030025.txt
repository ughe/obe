282. WILLIAM DAVIDSON (57), Stealing & watch and chain, 2 seals,
and a watch key, the property of William Martin, from his person.
MR. LLOYD conducted the Prosecutional
WILLIAM MARTIN. I am an engineer of 7, Charles-street, Islington-
green-about half-past 2, on 29th January, I was looking into a toy-shop
window, in Cheapside- man came up to me, not the prisoner; I had some
conversation with him, and we went into a publio-house in Newgate-street,
where the prisoner introduced himself to the other man, and said that he
had just recovered an action in the Court of Chancery, and on the following
Saturday, he was to receive 5,000/- the other man said that he had some
houses to sell, would the prisoner buy them, and the prisoner agreed to do so
upon that, it was agreed that we should adjourn to another public house,
in Barl-street, I believe--we all three went there together, and the first man
that introduced himself to me, left the prisoner and me, and fetched a third
man-(they had said at the first publie-house that I was to be s witness to the
purchase of the sixteen houses) after they returned, gambling was the first
thing carried on, by chalking upon the table the prisoner and the other
man who was fetched in did that one pot of beer was had-I drank once
out of it this was up stairs one of them, I do not remember whicb, asked
me to pay for the pot of beer I said that I could not, I had only twopence
on me the man not in custody then asked me what the time was I
pulled out my watch and told him I had no sooner done so thau he
snatched my watoh from me and gave it to the prisoner I requested the
prisoner several times to give it to me, and it was agreed that I was to go
out with them and pawn it-I did not agree to pawn it as I had ordered no
beer-we all went off together, the watch being in the prisoner's possession
-I kept fast hold of the prisoner's arm-I asked him for it three different
times on the way, and he pulled it out in his hand, and said, "My good man,
your watch is safe," so I know that be bad it I never let go, but held him
fast till we got on the bridge, where we met a policeman coming towards
u8 ; and as soon as the prisoner saw that, I called the policeman he gave up
the watch-I did not see the other two men afterwards they were in front
of us, I gave 121. for the watoh some years back-I gave the prisoner in
custody.
