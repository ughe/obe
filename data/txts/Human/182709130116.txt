 1827.  JANE CHAPMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of August , 2 sheets, value 6s., the goods of Timothy Nolan.
   CATHERINE NOLAN. I am the wife of Timothy Nolan; we live in Charles-street, Drury-lane. I let the prisoner my second floor room - on the 20th of August, when I asked her for rent, she said I should have it in the afternoon, but she left, and I missed the sheets. I met her in Long-acre, and she gave me the duplicate of one.
   JULIUS HOCKLEY. I am servant to Mr. Newby, pawnbroker, of Drury-lane. On the 17th of August a sheet was pawned in the name of Ann Chapman. I cannot swear to the prisoner, but this is the duplicate I gave her.
   WILLIAM KING. I am an officer. I received her in charge on the 27th of August, and on the way to the office she acknowledged taking the sheets.(Sheets produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. A woman who took the room with me, took the sheets, and gave me the duplicate; I intended to redeem it.
     GUILTY. Aged 52. Confined Two Months.
 FIFTH DAY. - TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 18.
 Third Middlesex Jury - Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1828.  PETER DUBEY was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of August , 1 shirt, value 2s.; 1 pair of trousers, value 4s.; 1 handkerchief, value 1s., and 1 tin-box, value 1d., the goods of Henry Rose.
   GEORGE DEVEREL. I am an officer. I found this tin-box in the prisoner's jacket pocket at the office, on the 31st of August; Rose was there, and said he had been robbed of these articles and the tin-box - the prisoner said it was his own property; he had been brought on the 30th of August to the watch-house, on suspicion of stealing some other things; he said he had taken them from the house where his brother was lodging, to take care of them.
   HENRY ROSE. I lost my shirt, trousers, handkerchief, and snuff-box, on the 30th of August - the prisoner has my handkerchief on his neck now - I came that day from the India-house to Mr. Cole's. I saw the prisoner there, and he said, "I will cook for you, if you will give me a mouthful of rice;" I gave him a half-crown to go and get some beer; he went away, and did not return with it - he afterwards came and took my clothes away. I was a little tipsy, and went after him, but he got away - this was on Thursday night.
 COURT. Q. Did you tell any body of it that night? A. No; I was not so drunk as not to know what was going on. I saw him take the articles; I know this tin-box to be mine; I saw him take it along with the other things.
 GEO. DEVEREL re-examined. Q. When did you take this box from the prisoner? A. On the 31st of August; Rose came the next morning with another person, on another charge. I found on him a bag, which belongs to the other prosecutor and this box in it.
 Prisoner. The things I took I delivered to the officer. I did not steal them; Stevens gave me them to take care of.
   THOMAS STEVENS. Rose is a Lascar. There is a barrack for Lascars in Shadwell, and he was there; I saw the shirt and trousers, and other things belonging to Rose- the prisoner is a stranger. I never told him to take any of Rose's things, or to take care of them.
 JURY to HENRY ROSE. Q. How do you know this box? A. It has been black, and the black is rubbed off - that is my handkerchief round his neck; it is quite plain, and has no border; it is three-cornered shape.
     GUILTY. Aged 35. Confined Two Months.
 1829.  JOHN ARNOLD was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of August , 8 pieces of sole leather, value 4s., the goods of William Bailey.
 WILLIAM BAILEY, JUN. I am the son of William Bailey,  leather-seller, of Dean-street, Soho. On the 30th of August the prisoner came in as a customer - I saw him take four pieces of leather out of a binn and conceal them in his bosom, and some more in his pocket. I took it from him, and gave him charge.
   WILLIAM MORRIS. I am an officer, and took him into custody.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going to the counting-house, to ask if they would give me credit for it; it was under my arm, not in my bosom.
     GUILTY. Aged 63. Confined Fourteen Days.
 1830.  THOMAS EVANS was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of July , 7 spoons, value 2l., the goods of William Hobson.
   SUSAN KEATES PARFIT. I am servant to Mr. Wm. Hobson, who lives in Windsor-place, City-road. On the 14th of July the prisoner came into the kitchen; he went out in about eight minutes, and I immediately missed seven spoons, which were safe about five minutes before. I had been out of the kitchen while he was there - I knew him before; master had employed him to scour some empty houses down; we could not find him till the Monday following, when he was taken.
   HENRY CHERRY. I am an errand-boy to Mr. Hobson. I heard of this loss, and met the prisoner on the Thursday following. I asked what he had done with the spoons; he said they were sold and melted down, and they could not have them any more. I said Mr. Hobson would have him taken - he said he did not care; he could not give him more than six weeks, for he did not break any locks.
   JAMES TAYLOR. I am an officer, and took the prisoner into custody.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Transported for Seven Years.
