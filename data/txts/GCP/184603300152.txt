969. BENJAMIN GABRIEL LOCKYER was indicted for stealing 46
yards of flannel, value 21., the goods of James Joy.
ATKINSON SAMUEL NICHOLLS. I am assistant to James Joy, a hosier, at
Lambeth. This roll of flannel is his property-I know it by a private mark
-on the 1st of April, about half-past three o'clock, it was safe, four feet inside
the shop door-in about half an hour I missed it - I went immediately to the
station, and found it there, and the prisoner in custody.
HENRY Hart (policeman.) On the 1st of April, about four o'clock in the
afternoon, I was on duty in the Westminster-road-I saw the prisoner go up
Hercules-buildings, about forty yards from the prosecutor's shop, with this roll of
flannel on his shoulder-a gentleman stopped him-I overtook him, and took
him into custody- I asked where he got it-he said he had it given him by a per-
son at the top of the street-I asked if he knew who the person was—he said,
“No, the party went down the Westminster-road again"-ihis is the same
flannel - I have known the prisoner three weeks, but nevelknew anything
against him.
Prisoner's Defence. It was given me to carry, and I was glad of the job.
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Confined Four Months.

971. JOHN MAPTON was indicted for stealing 1 coat, value 31. ; 1 minia-
ture and frame, 1l.; 1 seal, 11.; and 1 handkerchief, 18.; the goods of Charles
Grote.
SAMUEL MEEK. I am in the service of Charles Grote, who lives in Clap-
ham New-park. On the 13th of March I was going up stairs, about a quarter
past four o'clock in the afternoon, and saw the prisoner in the hall, with my
inaster's coat on his arm-I called out, and he ran away-I ran after him-he
threw the coat down, and ran round the green-house, and made his escape at
the front gate-another man stopped him-he is the same person-I saw him
take a miniature and case out of his pocket, and throw it over the gate into
the garden I took him into the kitchen and sent for the policeman-this is
my master's coat--this is the miniature-I picked it up out of the walk where
I saw the prisoner throw it-it is the miniature of my master's youngest
brother-this handkerchief and pair of gloves were in the coat pocket.
William Marsh (police-constable P 92.) I was called to Mr. Grote's, and
found the prisoner in the kitchen-he pulled out this seal when he heard me
coming- I found nothing on him but some lucifers.
Thomas TOWNSEND. I am servant to Mr. Wilkinson who lives opposite
Mr. Grote--I saw the prisoner running, and I stopped him-he begged for
mercy, and that I would let him go I said I could not-he made a slight
resistance to get away-1 asked the prisoner what he had in his pocket-he
took this miniature from his pocket, and threw it over the gate- I went with
him into the kitchen-he begged we would let him go.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Six Months.
