Syndicate, for the sum of £2,000, payable as follows: £10,000 in cash and
£14,000 in fully paid-up shares of the British Electric Street Tramways,
Limited, £1,000 to be paid on execution of the agreement and £1,000 on
November 13th, 1900. The Board having perused this agreement, con
firmed the same "-it was not carried through, but the contract was
signed—the £1,000 was paid, but the purchase money was not paid

1900, for the purchase of the Street Car Company is the only business
done part of the consideration was shares in a company to be formed

profit could be derived from it-the Streets Company might have issued
