I recollect meeting the prisoner about six weeks ago, about a quarter before 8 o'clock in the evening—he stepped off the step of Howchin's Hotel, in St. James's-street—he touched me on the shoulder, and asked me if I would run with this note to No. 5, Dover-street, to Mr. Spiking, a baker—he told me to give it to him, and to make haste back, as there would be something in it, and he would give me a shilling, as he had no waiters in—I took the note to Mr. Spiking, and went back with Mr. Spiking to the hotel, but I did not see the prisoner there—I am quite certain he is the man.
