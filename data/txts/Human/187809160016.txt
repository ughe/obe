I am a police constable attached to the General Post Office—I apprehended the prisoner on a warrant on 26th July at Caleot, near Reading—I told him I was a police officer from the General Post Office, and I held a warrant for his apprehension—I read the warrant to him, and told him I should take him in custody for stealing a registered letter, also for detaining a number of letters the property of the Postmaster-General—he said he knew nothing about it—he asked me what the letter contained, I said four 5 l . Bank of England notes—I searched him, I found nothing on him.
