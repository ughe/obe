WILLIAM THOMAS WEELANDS, pawnbroker, Lambeth. On the 22nd of
May, Jenkins came to my shop for change for a sovereign—she asked me to
give her a half-sovereign, and the rest in silver—it was a good half-sovereign
—we always sound our money on the counter.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you speak to any distinct recollection of having
sounded this one, or only your general practice? A. From a recollection
of having sounded this one, I know, because Jenkins came in about half an
hour after, and asked if I had given her a good half-sovereign—a bad
sovereign will ring, but not like a good one.
