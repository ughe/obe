WILLIAM HOWARD ELMEY. I keep the Anchor and Hope, Westferry Road, Millwall—in October, 1877, I wanted a barman—Wood applied for the situation—he said he had been living with Mr. Humphries at the George by the Commercial Dock for about eight months—as he seemed a very likely young man, I asked him if Mr. Humphries would come over to me, as in consequence of an accident I was unable to get out—he said no; he would bring a note from him if I liked, and early after midday he brought this note.
