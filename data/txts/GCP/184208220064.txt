2260. JULIA MURPHY was again indicted for stealing, on the 11th
of June, 8 handkerchiefs, value 12s., the goods of Thomas Price.
WILLIAM MEEKING. I am assistant to Thomas Price, a linen draper,
in Tothill-street, Westminster-the prisoner was in his shop on the 11th
of June-l afterwards missed the handkerchiefs produced, which are my
master's.
WILLIAM PLATT (police-constable B 55.) I took these handkerchiefs
from the prisoner, in her apron, in Mr. Parton's shop.
Prisoner's Defence. They were put in my apron, but I never stole
them.
GUILTY. Aged 30.-Transported for Ten Years.
2261. ELLEN PALMER was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of
July, 2 sovereigns, 1 shilling, 2 sixpences, 1 penny, and 10 halfpence, the
monies of John Johnson, from his person.
John Johnson. I am a sailor-on the 11th of July, I met the prisoner
- she took me to a house in West-street, Limehouse-I paid half-a-crown
for the bed, and sent for a drop of drink-at that time I pulled my jacket
and waistcoat off, and put them on a chair back-while I untied my
handkerchief the prisoner took the money out of my waistcoat pocket, and
ran off-I called out to stop her, but she got out with it.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. What countryman are you?
A. A Swede-I arrived in this country on the 4th of July-my wife is in
London, living in Cornwall-street-I was with the prisoner about two
hours- I had been to two public-houses, with a woman named Stewart-
I was not drunk I saw when the prisoner took my money-I did not
give her any thing-she was not in the house many minutes.

the same house I was in bed when she came home that morning-she
said she took a sovereign out of the man's pocket.
