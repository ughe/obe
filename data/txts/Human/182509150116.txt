   CHARLES MATTHEWS. I am a constable. I was in the market on the 19th of August. Harvey fetched me to the pens - the prisoner was there. Harvey asked the price of the pigs; he said 5 l., or guineas. I said "They are stolen, and I shall take you." He said, he and another person bought them down below Brentford. I did not hear him mention the person's name whom he bought them of.
 Cross-examined. Q. Harvey was present at this conversation? A. Yes. Some conversation passed before I got up.
   THOMAS BRANSCOMB. I am an officer. I went with Matthews to the pen - the prisoner said they were his pigs - and after we took him to the public-house, I heard him say, he bought them near Brentford, of a man with a tilted cart, and a net over it, but did know his name or residence. He said there was another person with him, who saw him buy them. I had apprehended one Davis at that time in Smithfield, on suspicion of being concerned; he saw Davis - and said, he was the person who was with him, at the time he bought them. Davis was discharged.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you discharge him. A. He was examined three times. Brentford is about eight miles from Hyde-park corner.
 (Witness for the Defence.)
   WILLIAM LEE. I live in Canister-street, and have known the prisoner eight or nine months; he was in the habit of buying pigs and sheep, and selling them.

 Middlesex Cases, Third Jury,
 Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1553.  GEORGE CROFT and WILLIAM SULLIVAN were indicted for stealing, on the 3d of September , a desk, value 50 s., the goods of Robert Biggerstaff , in his dwelling-house.
 ROBERT BIGGERSTAFF. I live at  Thames-bank, in the parish of St. George's, Hanover-square. I had a folding mahogany brass-bound writing-desk, with brass handles and corners - the brass plate was off - it was an old one.
   ELIZA PIDDINGTON. I am servant to the prosecutor. On the morning of the 3d of September, I saw the desk, about eight o'clock, standing on a table in the front parlour, and a bird-organ standing on it; about one o'clock, I was cleaning the passage on my knees, and saw a man go out of the door and along the garden towards the Thames; he walked across the Green, and got to the path - he had on a black coat and dark trousers; he was about the size of Croft. I did not see any other person about the premises; soon afterwards, in consequence of what my mistress said, I went to the parlour, and found the door bolted on the inside; I got in at the window, which looks towards the garden; I found the little organ standing on a chair by the window, and the desk gone; I gave an alarm to Brown, who lives next door, and then went over to the counting-house to my master.
 Cross-examined by MR. BRODRICK. Q. How far was the person from you when you first saw him? A. I do not think he was more than twenty yards. I think it was a black coat he had on - I only saw one person.
   RICHARD HAWKINS. I am in the employ of Messrs. Cook and Co. the distillers. On the 3d of September, I went to dinner at one o'clock; I passed the door of Mr. Biggerstaff - I saw the two prisoners near there; Croft had a black coat on, and some crape on his hat: he had dark cord trousers on; he was sitting on the rails at the front of the house, and Sullivan was by him, with his hands full of apples; he had a blue jacket and trousers, and a seal skin cap; they had a piece of green baize by the side of them, but no desk; I went back in about twenty minutes; I met the two prisoner's again, and Sullivan had a desk on his shoulders, half covered with green baize, but I could see all the back part of it; it shut up in three pieces, and was bound round with brass, and had a brass handle at the end; neither of them spoke to me; they were coming towards Westminster; I saw Mr. Biggerstaff directly afterwards - he said he had lost a writing desk - I told him what I had seen.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you stop them to examine the desk? A. No; but I could see very plainly that it shut up in three pieces. I stopped and looked at it; Mr. Bickerstaff had not told me that it shut up in three pieces, till I told him what I had seen; I saw the end of it, and half way along it - I did not stop it because I had no knowledge of whose it was.
