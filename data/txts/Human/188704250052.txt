   Cross-examined by Quail.  You were not in the public-house—the two other men came in, but the barman refused to serve them in that com partment—you were not in the public-house to the best of my belief—I don't recognise you as being in the urinal—I did not say at the police-court that you were in the public-house—it was between 10 and 11, nearer 11, when I lost my watch and chain, I cannot give the exact time—after I lost my watch Regan and another one whom I cannot identify followed me down East Street, where I was kicked in the ankle—I don't believe you were in the Apollo.
   By the COURT . I was not robbed of anything but my watch and chain, I showed the Magistrate the half of my chain. ROBERT BARNES( Policeman D  75.)
