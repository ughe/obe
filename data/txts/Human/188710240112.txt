I was thinking of Thursday—I did not see either of the prisoners do anything, they were out at the time I did it—I got the piece of carpet from behind the fixtures in the basement, from off a short length of carpet which had come from upstairs—on the Thursday night I went to the fixtures—before the Magis trate I said, "I have seen Kerslake and Churchill cut up carpets, but not Baylis," that is so—this is also true: "On Wednesday 5th October the prisoners Kerslake, Churchill, Baylis, and myself each took a piece of carpet instead of putting it back, and we cut it up and each took a piece of it."
