   COURT . Q.  What were they wrapped up, in in the morning? A.  The desk and the cloak were wrapped in a red handkerchief, which does not belong to the prosecutor; and the other things were wrapped in one of the towels.
   Wells.  At half-past eight o'clock that morning, I was far away from that place. Witness.  I am sure I saw you there about half-past eight—I am certain of the exact time—it was about eight when I received infor mation, and then I went and got nay things on, and went to the prosecutor's house.
   Winkletop. Q.  Could I not be passing that way without being in their company? A.  You were in their company—you went up the gravel with them—I am certain there was nothing put with these things after I left them—what I put back, were the same, and no more, that I had left at the toll-house. ALFRED SPICE( policeman, V  47). I went with Gibbs to the premises—I have heard what he has stated, which I confirm—I followed Webb and Wells in company—they were joined by two more men, near Old Vauxhall-gate—Winkletop was not one of the men—they all four walked some distance, and then Webb took one of the strange men in company, and walked on, and Wells took the other—I followed Webb, as he had the bag under his arm, over Westminster-bridge to Charing-cross, and to the Seven-dials, where the man who was with Webb, went into a lodging-house, and Webb turned down Church-lane, and I lost sight of him about eight or ten doors down—in the evening I assisted Gibbs in carrying back the property and burying it—I then watched and saw the three prisoners come up the back of Bradley-terrace, and go to the heap—they were all in a stooping position together—it was just under a lamp—they remained a short time, and then turned away, and I saw Webb was carrying this property on his shoulder—Gibbs took him, and I took the other two prisoners—they did not say anything—this is the property.
   Wells. Q.  Did you see me in the morning? A.  Yes; about half-past eight o'clock, or it might be a little more—it was from half-past eight to nine o'clock. GEORGE WINGATE re-examined.  This is all my property, I have no doubt, except the red handkerchief and the wrapper.
   Webb's Defence.  I had not been there on the Sunday morning; it was I that took the things from the heap; as for anything more I do not know; these two prisoners are innocent of that.
   Wells's Defence.  I was passing by on the Sunday evening; I saw the men stoop; I went to see what it was, and was taken.
