FREDERICK SAVILLE. I was a seaman on board the Indian. I recollect
Abrams coming on board—be seemed to be in pretty good health then
seven or eight days after he came on board I saw the prisoner cut him across
the back with the rope's end ; he then had his clothes on the first time he
was regularly flogged was about three weeks after he came; that was across a
truss of bay-I believe his hands were held and his clothes were down--the
prisoner flogged him on the backside with a cat-I do not know how many
strokes he had; they bruised him I cannot tell what he was flogged for
from that time he used generally to be flogged every day-after he had been
flogged he dirtied the place where he slept--the prisoner generally flogged
bim, sometimes with a rope's end, and I have seen him get a crack with a
broomstick, or with a cat-I noticed that it had an effect upon his health
about a week before he died-he then looked very bad indeed -I did not see
him flogged the last week-I remember his being put into a tub of water, it
was in very cold weather indeed--the prisoner made him go in to wash his
body-I do not know whether he was dirty-after he came out he had to wash
bis clothes too I did not hear that he objected to go in- I did not see anything
when he went into the tub at the latter part of the time they said he was
flogged for his dirtiness--I never heard the prisoner say anything about it
Abrams did not get food enough-I never heard any cause stated for his
being flogged except his dirtiness-he slept in the lower forecastle when he
dirtied the sail - the hatch was covered over, which would make it dark below
-it was not battened down; be could easily get out. ass
