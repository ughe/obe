 1418.  DAVID BERRY was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of August , a carpenter's plough, value 5 s., the goods of John Hart.
 JOHN HART. I am a  carpenter. I had a plough stolen from a new house which I was building, in Hertford-road, Hackney; I had lent it some time before to Powel. I saw it again at the office on the 7th or 8th of August.  The prisoner had been in my employ, but not at that time.
   JOSEPH CARTER. I am apprentice to Mr. Hart. His plough was at the building, being used by one of the men; I saw it their on Monday morning, the 2d of August; it was missed after dinner, when the men came to work. I saw the prisoner at the lower part of the house that morning, between eleven and twelve o'clock; he was known to the workmen. I saw the plough at the pawnbroker's on the Wednesday following.
   JOSEPH POWELL. I was in Mr. Hart's employ. I had used this plough in the morning; we went to dinner at twelve o'clock; I left the plough up stairs. We returned at one, but I did not miss it till about three; I saw it again at the office, and knew it.
   WILLIAM HENRY MILLS. I am a pawnbroker, and live with Mr. Hewit, of Kingsland-road. I took in this plough, which was afterwards claimed by Mr. Hart, on Monday, the 2d of August; about twelve o'clock. The house from which it was said to be taken, is about half a mile from our shop. I am quite certain it was brought by the prisoner; I lent him 3 s. on it.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. Q. Do you recollect my dress - A. A pair of blue trowsers, black waistcoat, and fustian jacket. He was in the same dress when at the office.
 Prisoner to  JOSEPH CARTER. Q. On the day you saw me at the building what clothes had I on - A. The same dress as the pawnbroker has described, only he had a blue coat over his fustian jacket.
   GEORGE SMITH. The prisoner was brought to the watch-house on Thursday night - he was dressed to the best of my recollection as the pawnbroker has described.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had been talking to the carpenter at the building, but I had a different dress to what has been stated.
 GUILTY. Aged 45. Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
