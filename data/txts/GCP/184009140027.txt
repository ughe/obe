RICHARD HAWKINSON. I live with Elizabeth Jarvis, in Foley-street,
Marylebone. On the 21st of August I lost a piece of beef, which I had
seen safe between eight and nine o'clock that morning-I took it out of the
cart, and placed it on the block-I saw it produced by the officer-I be-
lieve it to be Mrs. Jarvis's.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. When did you miss it? 4.
About half-past six o'clock in the evening--this is the bone of the beef-I
took the flesh off after I had examined the beef-I swear to the bone, as
constituting a part of the meat.

2253. GEORGE HAMILTON was again indicted for feloniously re-
ceiving, of a certain evil-disposed person, 2 bottles, value 18. ; and 1 quart
of capillaire, value 48.; the goods of Thomas Masson and another.
HARRY Masson. I am partner with Thomas Masson. We are confec-
tioners, and live in Great Portland-street. I lost two bottles of capillaire
on the 21st of August-1 have examined these two bottles of capillaire-
am confident they are mine-my label is on them, and my name.
ROBERT LESTER (police-sergeant E 10.) I found these on a washhand-
stand in the prisoner's bed-room-I said, “What do you know of these ?"
---he said, "I can speak of them when I get to the place," meaning the
station.
NOT GUILTY.
