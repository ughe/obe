1643. GEORGE RICHARDSON was indicted for stealing, on the
28th of June, 3 waistcoats, value 28s., the goods of John Solomon
Ellis.
JOHN SOLOMON ELLIS. I live in the Minories. About two o'clock in
the afternoon on the 28th of June, three waistcoats hung outside my shop
-I saw a movement outside and ran out-I saw the prisoner moving from
the shop in company with one or two others-I secured him, and the
waistcoats fell from him I had seen them safe five minutes before-these
are them.
GUILTY.* Aged 18.- Transported for Seven Years.
