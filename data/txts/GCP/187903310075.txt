claims against me for expenses over here; he did not say what those claims
were—I think this conversation was on the Wednesday or Friday as he was
given into custody on the Monday.
Re-examined. I had nothing to do with the composition of this document
that has been produced; it was written out by the prisoner-I believe he
had got it ready written when he showed it to me--it was his proposition-
I would not sign it-I have never had a shilling except the private loan
when the partnership was entered into
