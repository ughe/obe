Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Did the prisoner take you to
the house at your request ? A. Not to my knowledge I think not
-I was intoxicated, and cannot say whether he asked me to go there, or
I asked him-I could see he was a soldier, and knew where to find him-
I cannot tell how far the house was from the Golden Anchor-there were
two women, the prisoner, and myself, in the room-I did not know the
sort of house I was going to-I was standing with my back to the door,
when this took place-the two women were going down stairs--they made
their escape up-stairs, when I was thrown down-I saw them in the lock-
up house next day-they were charged with pawning a Petersham great-
coat, which I had left there when I went to the Arsenal--my silver was in
my right pocket--I was thrown down on my back-I was not very drunk,
but being a stranger I wished the prisoner to accompany me home-the
women were on the oppostte side of the room when I was thrown down
-I did not see any body else in the room-it was a good large room-I
was not struck-I think I was thrown backward by his arm-I saw his
arm--I had not changed a sovereign or any thing at the public-house--I had
changed a half-sovereign the night before - I had not counted my money at
the public-house-I had not been to any other public-house.
