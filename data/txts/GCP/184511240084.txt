71. MARY ANN SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing 1 jacket, value
108.; 1 waistcoat, 58. ; 2 shillings, 3 pence, and 6 halfpence, the property of
James Lyons.
JAMES Lyons. I belong to a schooner, which was in Down's wharf, Wapping.
On the 22nd Nov., after having been drinking, I met with the prisoner, about
one o'clock in the morning, in the night-house-I asked for a bed -- she said
she would get me one-she took me to a house, and I went to bed-I had two
shillings and some coppers in my jacket-in the morning I missed my jacket
and waistcoat, and the money-I went down to the woman below, and she
said the girl had gone out to pawn them-I said, “What business had she to
pawn my clothes ?"-I waited till the prisoner came in-she said she had
pawned them for 48.- I looked at the duplicate, and it was 98.-I said it was
98., and she gave me abuse-I had not been in bed with her, I slept by my-
self.
Prisoner. I slept with him, and he told me to get up and pawn them; I
came back, and gave him the duplicate; he was drinking with me the next
morning; he told me he could not get any money till he went on board bis
ship; I went but two doors from the place where we slept. Witness. I did
not sleep with her, and I did not tell her to pawn these things- I tasted the
spirits, and that was all.
WILLIAM TAPPIN (police-constable K 234.) I took the prisoner, and asked
what she had done with the man's jacket and waistcoat-she said she had
b--y well pawned them-she gave no reason for it.
SAMUEL SMITH. I am a pawnbroker. I have the jacket and waistcoat
pawned by the prisoner, between eight and nine o'clock that morning, in the
name of Mary Ann Sullivan, for John Wilson-I knew her before.
Prisoner. He gave me the things to pawn, and remained with me till close
upon ten o'clock the next morning.
Guilty. Aged 27.-Confined Nine Months.
