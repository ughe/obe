1828. ALFRED DAVIS was again indicted, with DAVID DAVIS.
stealing 4 handkerchiefs, value 16s. ; the goods of John Orrick Kennard,
master of Alfred Davis: to which
ALFRED DAVIS pleaded GUILTY. Aged 14.-Confined Three Monthe
JOHN ORRICK KENNARD. Alfred Davis used to come to my shop
about an hour or two, morning and evening, to carry out parcels, open the
shutters, and clean the windows-he has been occasionally left in charged
the shop-David Davis is his brother-about the end of July, or beginnine
of Aug., I missed some silk neck-handkerchiefs, these are them (produced
there is my shop-mark on them- they are new, and not hemmed - this one!
can particularly swear to-I bought it on 7th Aug.
CHARLES WEBB. I am in the service of Mr. Nash, a pawnbroker-David
Davis pledged a handkerchief with me on 17th July-on 4th Aug. he pledged
two others-he said he brought them from his mother.
JAMES SHACKELL. I am in the service of Mr. Parry, a pawnbroker-
I produce a handkerchief pledged by David Davis-he said he bought it from
another party, he did not say who.
JOHN CARPENTER (policeman, R 38). On Soth Aug., in consequenced
information, I went to a house in Waterloo-place, and found David Davis-1
went with him to Florence-row, and found Alfred Davis-I then asked David
wbere he got the handkerchiefs he had pledged-he said he had none, and had
never pledged any-at the station I told Alfred he was charged with stealing
some handkerchiefs from Mr. Kennard-he said he had only taken six black
silk handkerchiefs, and those he had given to his brother-David, who wa
present, said nothing.
David Davis's Defence. A young man asked me to pawn them in my
mother's name, and gave me fourpence; I told Mr. Nash, and he put then
in my name; the man came to me another day, asked me to pledge another
and I did so.
DAVID DAVIS-Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
