600. WILLIAM SIMMONDS was indicted for stealing 1 gas-fitting.called
a main gay cock, value 38.; the goods of William James Shannon, and that
he had been before convicted of felony.
WILLIAM JAMES SHANNON. I am a hair-dresser, and live at No. 134. Uninn.
street, Borough. On Monday morning, the 19th of Jan., the prisoner came
to my house he said he had come from the gas company to put a cap on the
main pipe-he employed himself at the gas-pipe, and went away--I was with.
out gas some days, and when the inspector of the gas company came I missed
the supply cock.
ANN CORBETT. I am a gas-fitter--the prisoner brought me a brass supply
cock last Friday fortnight-he asked me to give him 9d. for it, I said I did nok
want it-he said had I got 60.--I gave him 6d.-he called again, and I said I
would rather not have it-he said he would bring the 6d., and have it back,
but he did not, and I gave it to the officer,
JOSEPH TAYLOR (police-constable M 56.) I received charge of the prisoner
- he said he was sent by an inspector to cut off the gas, but he did not know
his name, nor wbat company he belonged to-he told me I should find the
should u
cock at Corbett's-I produce it.
Prisoner. I was sent by an inspector; I passed the prosecutor's door
three or four times, he called to me, and I said, " I know what you want, you
want the brass tap that was on the pipe;" he said, "Yes, where is it?" !
told him, and I said, "You shall have that, or a new one;" he told me to stop
a minute ; and he brought a policeman.
Court to WILLIAM JAMES SHANNON. Q. Was anything the matter with
the gas-cock? A. No-there was a dispute about paying for the supply
gas in advance-the man called, and I refused to pay it-the prisoner was
waiting outside-he came in, and said he came from the inspector to take my
gas off-he did not say he came to take the brass supply-cock away-that
did not belong to the company-it is mine.
Prisoner. I worked for the Metropolitan Gas Company, in the factory, for
two years ; I cannot say whether the man belonged to the Metropolitan of
the Phænix, but I was called by the inspector, and told to put a stop-cap on
which I did. Witness. He brought this iron cap, took the brass main-tap
away, and screwed this on the pipe-I was eight days without a light.
ANN CORBETT re-examined. This iron cap that the prisoner left is worth
about 6d.--the brass supply-cocks cost 18. 8d. or 18. 10d.
Prisoner. I left it with her till such time as I brought the sixpence back;
I told her I would call and fetch it in a few days. Witness. No, he was to
call again for the other money, and when he came he said he would bring the
sixpence, and fetch it.
Prisoner. When I went to the prosecutor's house, he told me he was
going to have the gas put on in a different way.
WILLIAM JAMES SHANNON re-examined. Yes, I did, if the place suited my
business--the prisoner did not tell me he was going to cut off the supply-cock,
THOMAS CURTIS. I am an inspector of nuisances to the paving trust
produce a certificate of the prisoner's conviction at this Court-(read-Cox-
victed the 3rd of January, 1842, confined one year.)
Guilty. Aged 49.--Confined Six Months.
