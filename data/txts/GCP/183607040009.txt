Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. You say as you were at one
corner of the court, you saw the prisoner come out of the other corner,
and it was a very narrow court ? A. Yes that was the court in which
the man was lying the court I live in is right opposite-I crossed the road,
and saw him run into his own house the last person I worked for was
Mr. Rutter, as a paviour-that is four years ago I have worked at the
Docks since, at chopping wood- I worked at St. Katharine's Docks till
I jammed my fingers about a fortnight ago, I was only there constantly for
three days I have chopped wood for my mother-I worked for Mr.
Rutter before I went to the Docks, but I chopped turpentine-wood for
myself that is the way I get my living-that and working in the Docks
together-I bave only worked there twice this summer I was put to hard
labour for fourteen days once, at Lambeth-street, for gambling that is
the only time I have been in custody- I have got my living for the last

from Grange-road, Bermondsey-I bought barrels and cut them up, and sold
them myself I have been twice before a Magistrate, once for insulting my
sister, and again for gambling-I paid is. the first time, and was dis-
charged-those are the only times I was ever before a Magistrate
