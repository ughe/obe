ELIZABETH FENN. I am, single, and live with my sister, who keeps a
dyer's shop in Church-street, Greenwich-on 20th June, about noon, I was
in a rooom adjoining the shop, and observed the prisoner coming round from
the back of the counter where the till is, and making his way out of the
shop-I ran into the shop, and he ran out as fast as he could- a policeman
was outside, and be brought him back-I went and looked at the till, and
found it empty-I had seen it about ten minutes before-it then contained
Beveral sixpences and a shilling.
JOHN QUIN (Policeman, R 296). On 26th June, I was on duty at Green-
wich-I saw the prisoner run out of Mrs. Cowell's shop - I followed and
stopped him, without losing sight of him-he began crying- I took him
back to the shop, and found on him a pack of cards, and 18 24d. wrapped

penny in another pocket-he said the money belonged to his father.
