763. DAVID GOODMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of
January, 1 handkerchief, value 48., the goods of Martin Driscoll ; and that
be had been before convicted of felony.
JAMES DRISCOLL. I am the son of Martin Driscoll, at West Ham. The
prisoner lodged with us, I saw him open a drawer on the 14th of January,
take out a handkerchief of my father's, put it into his pocket, and go out.
ELIZABETH DRISCOLL. I am the prosecutor's wife. I charged the pri-
soner with stealing my handkerchief-he denied it, but after some time he
told me he had it.
PETER M'QUILLEN ( police-constable K 375.) I went after the prisoner
and charged him with stealing a handkerchief-he said he knew nothing
of a handkerchief-I was present when he made this statement in this
deposition, and the Magistrate signed it-this is his writing-(read.)
The prisoner, on being called on for his defence says, "I wanted some
money, and as Mrs. Driscoll was not at home, I took out of her drawer
the silk handkerchief, but I did not intend to steal it.-WILLIAM COTTON."
