CAROLINE WRIGHT. I live at 1, Belgrave Street, King's Cross, and let
lodgings-& gentleman named Thomas called on me on 1st December, and
took a parlour and bed-room in the front of the house for a friend named
Gorts-he gave no initial-Gorts never came to take possession--he was
coming from the country-I received some letters addressed to Mr. Gorts
the following day, which I gave to Mr. Thomas on his calling the letters
marked “W1," "W 2," W 3," and "W 4" I gave to the police on their
calling.
HENRY Piggott. I am manager of the London and South-Western Bank,
Uxbridge Road Branch--on 1st December, an account was opened there in
the name of John Gorts, of 1, Belgrave Street, King's Cross-he was intro-
duced to me by a customer-he paid 681, in at that time, and I gave him a
cheque-book-the letters (S 1 to 6) I received in his name I produced them
at the Mansion House--for the most part thoy contained cheques to be
paid in to bis account - I also received this letter, S 3. (This letter re-
quested that the pass-book and a cheque-book should be given to bearer.) I gave
them to the bearer, whom I saw at the Mansiot House-it was John Riches,
junior, the little boy-I believe I received a cheque for 401., drawn by Mr.
Austin, which I returned in a letter to Mr. Gorts, because it was crossed
"Union Bank of London," and we could not clear it-“W 1" is in my
writing-in that letter I sent cheques B B I to B B 4, 28th November,
1876, for 201., drawn by A. H. Austin, also endorsed “Union Bank of
