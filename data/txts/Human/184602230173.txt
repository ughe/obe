 897. MORRIS JOHN KNIGHT was indicted for stealing lib. of cigars, value 8 s  ., and 1/2lb. of cheroots, 4 s  ., the goods of James Bennett . ELIZABETH CAROLINE BENNETT. I am the wife of James Bennett, tobac conist, Belvedere-road, Lambeth—on the 11th of Feb., about half-past two o'clock, I was in my kitchen—I heard a bell ring which is attached to the shop-door—I went up and saw a young man in the shop—he asked for a cigar—I served him, and he left—I closed my gate after him and returned to my kitchen—I had not been there three minutes before I heard a noise—I went up and found the prisoner behind the counter—I am sure he is the per son—his handkerchief was on the counter with three bundles of cheroots in it, which had been removed from the window—he had got hold of 8 fourth bun dle in the window, and some of the cigars had been removed—I attempted to seize him—he knocked me backwards and ran out—I pursued—he was taken in Waterloo-road—I am sure he is the person—I never lost sight of him.
   Prisoner. Q.  Can you swear I stole those that were in the handkerchief? A.  They had been removed from the window into the handkerchief.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was half a mile from the shop; a man came and said, "You have knocked a woman down;" I said, "No", I have not, I will go back with you;" I went back and saw this woman; she said, "That is him."
   Witness.  I am positive he is the person—I have not the least doubt of it. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Six Months .
