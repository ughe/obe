NEW COURT.-Wednesday, February 11th, 1903.
Before Mr. Recorder.
227. CHARLES HENRY BLACKMORE (35), Stealing three silver
cups and a silver cruet, the property of Daniel George Collins.
MR. HUMPHREYS Prosecuted.
The prisoner stated that he pawned the goods, but had no intention of
converting them to his own use, upon which the Jury found him Guilty.
There were several other indictments against him for like offences, and he
had been convicted of unlawfully pawning. Nine months' hard labour.
