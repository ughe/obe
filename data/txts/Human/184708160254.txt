GEORGE DURANT was indicted for stealing 11 shirts, value 1 l  . 18 s  .; 4 pair of stockings, 8 s  .; 6 pairs of trowsers, 14 s  ; 1 pair of boots, 12 s  .; 1 pair of shoes, 3 s  .; 1 flannel jacket, 2 s  . I jacket, 8 s  .; 1 towel, 6 d  . and 2 flannel shirts, 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Reuben Whittle . REUBEN WHITTLE. On Thursday, 5th of Aug., I had my stocking and trowsers, and the other property safe in my bag at the Three Goats' Heads, in the Wandsworth-road—I left them there in the landlady's charged—I asked if she would know me again—she said, "Yes."
   Prisoner.  You gave them to me on Thursday morning. Witness.  I never gave them to him—these things produced are mine. THOMAS FOSTER. I was at the public-house—I recollect these thing being left—the prisoner and Whittle were sitting outside drinking—the pri soner came the next morning for the bag—I thought it right to give it him—he was looking about in the washhouse and kitchen—I asked what he wanted—he said, "The soldier's bag"—I gave it him.
   COURT to REUBEN WHITTLE. Q.  Are you a soldier? A.  I was dis charged from the Royal Marines—I was not drinking with the prisoner—I wanted to shift myself—a man took me into a stable and told me I could shift there—I went and shifted, came out, took the bag into the public-house, and gave it to the landlady, and some papers too—I paid two pots of beer to the man who let me go into the stable—I did not tell the prisoner to go and get the things. JOHN SAVILLE. I live at Bloomsbury. About one or two o'clock on Friday, the 6th of Aug., the prisoner came with the bag, and called for a cup of coffee—I saw him diving in the bag—he went out without drinking his coffee, which excited my suspicion—he said, "I will be here presently for the bag"—I turned it over, saw a government mark on it, and took it to the station—they told me to detain the boy when he came—he came, and I detained him—he said the soldier gave it him to mind, and he was going to take it to him.
   prisoner's Defence.  I met the prosecutor; he took me to a public-house; he gave me a pennyworth of gin; I went to several places with him, and then to the Three Goats' Heads; he gave me these things to mind. GUILTY . Aged 15.— Confined Three Months .
