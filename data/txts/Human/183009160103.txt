Cross-examined by MR. J.  ALLEY. Q. Is your name up at your shop? A. No; I borrowed my mother-in-law's name, as I sold a shop some time ago, and the agreement was, I was not to set up within a mile of it; the prisoner said she had taken the gown; my house is full of lodgers.
 COURT. Q. What did the prisoner say? A. She said she was in great distress, and was sorry she had taken it.
   WILLIAM FOWLE. I am a pawnbroker. This gown was pawned with me on the 12th of August.
   TIMOTHY RICHARDSON. I took the prisoner into custody; she gave me this duplicate, and acknowledged without my asking her any questions, that she stole this gown through distress; she then said she took it to go to a dance; she burst into tears, and said, "My brother beat me out of doors, the evening before, and I stole it through distress."
 Cross-examined. Q. Have you got any money or drink from the prisoner? A. Never.
 The prisoner pleaded distress, and received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 19. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined 15 days.
 1696.  JAMES GARDINER ELDRED was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of September , 4 half-crowns, and 4 shillings, the monies of James Lackington , his master.
 JAMES LACKINGTON. I  keep an eating-house. The prisoner was in my service; I went to look at the till, counted 1l. 17s. 6d. in it, and noticed four or five half-crowns - that was soon after eleven o'clock; I went again about one o'clock, and found only two half-crowns - I called the prisoner, and desired him to give me the half-crowns: he said he had taken none - I said, "If you don't I will fetch the Policeman;" he then burst out crying, and said he would fetch them - he went and got two half-crowns; I told him to go and get his things - I had spoken to the Policeman, and when the prisoner came down I said, "What have you got?" he said, "Not a farthing;" I had him searched, and the Policeman found two half-crowns and four shillings in his boots.
   SAMUEL SPEECHLEY. I am a Policeman. I found two half-crowns in one of his boots, and four shillings in the other - he had denied having any more; he acknowledged that it all belonged to his master, but 3s. he had received from his mother.
     GUILTY. Aged 14. - Whipped and Discharged.
 1697.  ELIZABETH GREEN and NORA GARVER were indicted for stealing, on the 11th of September , 1 watch, value 2l.; 4 half-crowns, 5 shillings, and 2 sixpences, the property of James Anderson , from his person.
   JAMES ANDERSON. I am a baker. I went to a house in St. Giles', on Saturday night, the 11th of September, between eleven and twelve o'clock, with the two prisoners, whom I had met in the street; I then had my money and watch in my pocket, and lost it - I took off my trousers; I had 16s. in half-crowns and shillings in them - neither of the prisoners were then in the room; I never got my money since - I am sure I had it when I went into that room.
   HENRY CURTIS. I am a Policeman. On the Sunday morning I took the two prisoners for being drunk and disorderly, between six and seven o'clock - I took them to the station-house, and found on Green this watch; they had no money, except one shilling - Green ran away, and I went and caught her with the watch.
   JAMES COBBINGS. At half-past twelve o'clock on Sunday morning, the prosecutor said he had been robbed, and described the room - I went there and found this key, which is the key of his trunk; he described the persons - I knew them, and told him to come down at five or six o'clock; Curtis brought in the two prisoners, who were drunk and had been fighting about the watch - they accused one another of having it; the prosecutor said he had given Garver 3s. for the room; she rented it of Mr. Grant, Lawrence-lane, St. Giles'.
   JAMES ANDERSON. This is my watch - the prisoners were both in the room all the time I was there; they blew out the light, and ran away together.
 Green's Defence. On Saturday night I heard a scuffle in the passage - I went for a light, and saw this young woman bleeding; she said she had had a tremendous blow with a pot - I took her to a doctor; coming back I picked up this watch in the passage - if the gentleman had returned I would have given it to him, but he did not; the door was locked, which it never was before - he said he could not swear to us.
 Garver's Defence. He could not swear we were the two girls who robbed him, but he went with us; he was very much in liquor, and gave me a blow on the head.
 GREEN -    GUILTY. Aged 19.
 GARVER -    GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Seven Years.
 1698.  JOHN HESTER was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of July , 1 watch, value 1l., the goods of Frances Hudson.
   FRANCES HUDSON. I am a widow. On the 29th of July I lost a watch from my mantel-shelf at ten o'clock at night - I had seen it not two minutes before; the prisoner was in the room - he has slept there two nights with my son-in-law; I called my son - he went and took it from the prisoner; this is it.
   JOHN REDMAN. I am the prosecutrix's son-in-law. On the night of the 29th of July she said she had lost the watch - I went after the prisoner, and caught him about fifteen yards from the door; he gave the watch up to me.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 28. - Transported for Seven Years.
