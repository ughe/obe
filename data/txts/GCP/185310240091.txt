ELIZABETH HIGGS. I am single, and live at 3, Peter-street, Berwick-street.
About eleven months ago I had a little boy. I suckled him till Tuesday,
4th Oct., and then put him out to nurse to the prisoner Nash, who I have
known, I should think, nearly three years- I knew her by the name of
Burch at the time I put the child out to her to nurse-she was then living
with the male prisoner--they passed among their neighbours as Mr. and Mrs.
Burch-I was to pay her half a crown a week for nursing the child, and I
