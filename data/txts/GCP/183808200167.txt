1961. WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of
August, 1 handkerchief, value 58., the goods of Robert Hill, from his
person; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
WILLIAM SANDERS (police-constable M 170.) On the 16th of August,
I was on duty in Palace-yard-I saw the prisoner there and watched him
for three hours- I saw him take one handkerchief out of a person's pocket,
and return it again-he stood by the prosecutor for three quarters of an
hour, and I was close to him the prisoner then took this handkerchief from
him-I closed his hand, and seized him directly with it-I was in plain
clothes.
ROBERT HILL. I am a gentleman's servant, I was in Palace-yard on
the 16th of August, and had this handkerchief in my pocket-I did not feel
it taken-this is it-(looking at it.)
Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up to give it to the gentleman, and the
officer took me-I had been talking to the gentleman a long time, and
telling him who the different carriages belonged to.
JOSEPH PYEFINCH (police-constable F 58.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's former conviction from Mr. Clark's office-(read)-the pri-
soner is the same person.

1962. BENTLEM DOUGLAS was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd
of March, 1 pillow-case, value 6d.; 1 table-cloth, value 6d. ; 1 scarf,
value 58.; and 4 spoons, value 78. 60.; the goods of Mary Douglas.
MARY DOUGLAS. I am a widow, and live in Albion-street, Waterloo-
town, Bethnal-green. The prisoner is my son-I have three boys and
one girl-the prisoner is the eldest-he lived at home with me on the
2nd of March I missed the articles stated in the indictment-I spoke to
him about them he said he had taken them away because he had got into
bad company--the property was found at the pawnbroker's.
WILLIAM RAWLINS. I live with Mr. Bradley, a pawnbroker, in Cable-
Street. I produce a scarf, pawned on the 4th of March, in the name of
John Douglas, I believe, by the prisoner.
WILLIAM SOWERBY. I am a pawnbroker. I have a table-cloth and
Pillow-case, pawned in the name of John Douglas, on the 2nd of March.
JAMES GIEL. I am a pawnbroker. I have four tea-spoons, pawned in
the name of Bentlem Douglas, but I did not take them in--it was on the
8th of March.
CHARLES JUTSUM. I am a pawnbroker in Whitechapel-road. I have
a brooch pawned for 1s. by the prisoner.
