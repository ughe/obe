JOSEPH MAHON. I am a wholesale stationer, and live in Blackman-street, Borough. I have a warehouse and stable-yard in Great Suffolk-street, about one hundred yards from my dwelling-house—on or about Tuesday, the 19th of April, there was about twenty-five tons of paper, tied up in quarter of hundreds, and about thirty tons tied up in bags, about seven tons of old millboard, and various packages of rags—I had made considerable purchases of waste paper from the Excise-office shortly before—I had seven stacks consisting wholly of that paper—it was worth 32 s . a cwt., in quarter of cwt. parcels—that in the bags was worth about 20 s .—the warehouse was secured by a padlock—you ascend a flight of steps from the stable-yard up to the warehouse door—no body lives in it—I merely send to it occasionally as I want things—in consequence of information, on the morning of the 27th, I went to the ware-house and missed about seven tons, or rather more, of the excise paper at 32 s . a cwt, from the stack, about twenty sacks of that packed in bags—the value of the paper stolen was about 210 l . or 220 l .—there were other things gone besides—I have seen some paper in the constable's possession—I never sell it in the state in which it has been found—I can undertake to say it never has been sold—I saw the warehouse safe when I was there on the 18th or 19th.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Is anybody authorised to sell besides you? A.  Yes, a man named Denny—he is not here—I can answer for it he never sold it in this state, nor any other person—Denny has nothing to do with the warehouse—he could not sell it without my knowledge. JOAQUIM JOSE DE OLIVEIRA. I am in the employ of Mr. Mahon, I went to the warehouse on the morning of the 26th of April—the padlock was on the stable door as if it was safe—I put my hand-to unlock it, and it opened—I found it had been forced—I sent the boy to call my master. PETER AUSTIN. I am a cheesemonger, and live in St. John-street, Clerk enwell. On the 22nd of April, about six in the evening, the prisoner and another walked into the shop, produced lcwt. 5lbs. of paper, and asked me to boy it—I believe they brought it in a truck—I agreed to give 24 s . a cwt., which was what he asked—he said he was selling it for Cohen, of Saffron-hill, that it belonged to Cohen—I did not know Cohen—I did not make inquiry how he got it—the officer came to me about a week after—I delivered what I had purchased, to him, three parcels—this now produced is a portion of it—I believe I paid the prisoner the money—I took no bill and receipt—I did not know where he lived—I knew him before, and had bought paper of him for some years before.
