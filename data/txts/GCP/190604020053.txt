PRISONER (not on oath). I can only say that I am innocent
of doing this. The woman jumped out herself. She has
attempted to jump out of the window before when she has had
these mad fits, and I have had to pick up something to try to
stop her temper, and prevent her aiming things at me. My
face is all cut about and scarred where she has thrown things
ab me, and sometimes when I have gone to work on Monday
morning the men have laughed at my scratched face. There
is all scars about my face now. The woman does not know what
she is doing when she has these tempers. She does not drink
much, but the least drop sends her off in these mad fits. I
have been at Brixton Prison over three months, and I declare
now, whichever way it turns, I am an innocent man; I never
diu it. The woman went out herself. There was nobody in
the room.
Verdict, not guilty.

knife was undoubted, and he had better leave her alone.
