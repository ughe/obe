2574. THOMAS CONNOLLY was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of
August, 1 counterpane, value 58.; the goods of Robert Garnham.
SARAH PRIOR. I am barmaid at the Nine Elms public-house, Battersen.
kept by Mr. Robert Garnham. On the 8th of August, between one and two
o'clock in the day, I saw the prisoner in the yard with the counterpane in i
basket-he went out into the road-I gave information, and saw him throw
the basket over some palings I am sure he is the boy- I saw him stopped.
SUSAN GARNHAM. I am the wife of Robert Garnham. I saw the prisone
going off with the basket, and throw it over the fence into a garden-this
counterpane, which is mine, was in it-it had been hanging out in the ter-
gardens.
Henry BROOKSON (police-constable V 60.) I received charge of the
prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. I was by the side of the railway, and I saw the train
coming in-I ran to get a job, and was stopped.
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.

2577. GEORGE TWINE was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of
August, 1 handkerchief, value 9d; the goods of William Ruffell Stevenson.
WILLIAM RUYFELL STEVENSON. I am in the employ of Mr. Russell,
a job-master, of Plough-yard, Clapham. On Friday night, the 11th of
August, I placed two handkerchiefs and something else on the footboard of
a fly in a coach-house in my master's yard-there are large gates, and room
for seven or eight carriages to stand- I went to work at half past six o'clock
next morning, and found an old handkerchief which I had left, but I missed
the one now produced-I saw it again in possession of the policeman-
mine-there is about an inch in one corner that has been hemmed, but it is
picked out now I can see the place where it has been hemmed-if it is held
up to the light you will see the holes - I will swear to it.
STEPHEN FARMER (police-constable V 154.) On the 12th of August the
prisoner passed me in Clapham, about twenty minutes before two o'clock in
the morning-I followed him for about 250 yards-when he got to the
turning to the back of Mr. Russell's stable I lost sight of him-I afterwards
went round and searched, but could not find him-I found the front gates
open, which five minutes before I met him were fast.
