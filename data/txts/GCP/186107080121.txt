THOMAS HENRY BRIGGS. I live in Arbour-street, Stepney I was in the
prosecutor's employ for two or three weeks, up to 5th June - I saw a box
brought to the house the prisoner took it in- I assisted him in taking it to
his own room on the first floor--two or three hours after it bad arrived he
asked me to help him up-stairs with it, and I assisted him with it up to the
second-floor back room-it was heavier then--when I took it up to the first
floor it was empty and unlocked; when I took it up to the second-floor it
was locked-I believe the box was paid for before it arrived, except a few
half-pence, and those the prisoner paid-] saw Mrs. Powter give the money
to the prisoner before the box was fetched-first of all she gave me 38. to go
and buy a box-) could not get one, and I came back, and gave the 38. t>
the prisoner, and the mistress gave him some more to go out and buy one-
she said, “Go and see if you can get one"-be went out, and after that the
box was brought as I have mentioned this is very much like the box-I
pointed out the box to Mr. Powter in the second floor back-room where I
had left it.
