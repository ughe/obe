EDWARD COX( policeman ), I took the prisoners at the Castle, and told them the charge—they said they had not done it—on Turton I found 2 s ., three sixpences, a 4 d .-piece, and 6 d . in halfpence, all good—Kelly put his band to his mouth; I heard something in his mouth, which rattled like money on his teeth—I put my hand to his throat, and he swallowed it, with difficulty—I found 6¼ d . in copper on him—I received this half-crown from Miss Lovelock ( produced. ) HENRY UNDERHILL( policeman ).
