 2154. LAWRENCE PHILLIPS and JOHN FELL were indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 3 s  ., the goods of William Lake Woodman , from his person; to which
   FELL  pleaded GUILTY.* Aged 19.— Confined Twelve Months .
   MR. WILDE conducted the Prosecution. GEORGE KEMP( police-constable N  82.) On the 16th of July I was on duty at Walthamstow—I saw the prisoners together—I saw Phillips pick a gentle man's pocket of his handkerchief, and pass it to Fell, who put it into his pocket—I seized Fell, and took it out of his pocket. WILLIAM LAKE WOODMAN. I live in Bedford-square. I was at Waltham-stow—I lost my pocket-handkerchief—Kemp spoke to me, and gave me this handkerchief—it is mine, and was in my pocket a few minutes before.
   PHILLIPS  — GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Six Month .
 2155. JESSE HAWKINS was indicted for feloniously receiving 1 hand kerchief, value 1 s  ., of a person unknown. GEORGE KEMP( police-constable N  82.) I was at Walthamstow on the afternoon of the 16th of July, at the races—I saw the prisoner selling pies, with a large board before him—I saw two reputed thieves try several pockets, and go backwards and forwards to the prisoner—I then saw them go to a re spectable gentleman, come back to the prisoner, and give him a handkerchief, which he put into his pocket—I suppose he was thirty or forty yards from the person who was robbed—I saw the other two try the pocket of a gentleman in a booth, and come from him, and go and give the prisoner this handker chief—I took the prisoner, and found this handkerchief and four others be tween his legs, and a gentleman's card-case. HENRY FRANCIS( police-constable N  227.) I was on the race-course—I saw two persons bring something out of a booth, and put it under the pri soner's tray.
   Prisoner's Defence.  The handkerchiefs were thrown under my tray; they asked me to mind them for them. NOT GUILTY.
