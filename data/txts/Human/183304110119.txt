NEW COURT. THURSDAY, APRIL 18TH.
 London Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 882.  ABRAHAM LUCAS SCOTT, THOMAS PROTHERO, BENJAMIN CHARLES MURRAY, ABRAHAM THOMAS PERKINS, and OWEN EDWARDS, were indicted for a conspiracy.
 MESSRS. ALLEY, GURNEY, and BULLOCK conducted the prosecution.
   WILLIAM LOWE. I live at No. 3. Greyhound-place, Kennington: I am a labouring man- I sell birds and fruit when I have nothing else to do. I saw Perkins, the prisoner, on the 17th of January; I did not know his name then - he came to my house that evening - my wife called me; I came to the door - he asked me if I had a cock linnet to sell; I said I had a very good one; he asked me to let him look at it - I got one, and gave it into his hand; he looked at it, and said it would suit him very well - he said he was going to take it to a friend of his in town, and asked me to walk with him, and he would pay me for it when he had sold it - it was then about six o'clock - he said he wanted to be in town by seven, to meet his friend whom he wanted to take it to, and if he was not there by seven he should be too late; he asked me if I had a cage or a little bag to put the bird into - I said I had a cage, but it did not belong to me, but I lent it him to put the bird into; he tied the cage up in his handkerchief, and we came out together - when we came out at the door, he said he had got a trifle of money for me to take care of till we had sold the bird; he gave it me wrapped up in a bit of paper, and I put it into my pocket; he said, "Don't put it into your pocket, we shall go into a public-house to have a pint of beer, and somebody may take it out" - I said I had no where else to put it; he said I could put it into my hat; I said, "May be I might lose it out of my hat" - he said, "Oh no, you won't lose it - they won't knock your hat off in a public-house;" I then put it into my hat (it was only one parcel), and I put my handkerchief over it - he said, "Let me look if you have put it safe;" I gave him my hat to look at - he said, "It is hardly safe, I will put it safer;" I did not look at it after he had placed it - I had two or three handkerchiefs in my hat, as I generally have; he placed it in, and said it was safe; we kept on the road - he walked on before me, and told me to wait at the Elephant and Castle till he came to me; I waited ten minutes - he then came, and said, "Come on, we have got to go a little further - I have not met the friend I thought of meeting here;" we then walked on till we came near Blackfriar's-bridge, on the left hand side; he there stopped at a gin-shop, and asked me to have something to drink - we each had a penny worth of rum; we then walked on to the corner of Fleet-market - he was there going into a gin-shop again, but he said "I shan't go in here, there is somebody here knows me;" we went on to a watering-house at the top of Holborn-hill; he stopped there, and asked me if I had any money - I said I had three-half-pence; he gave me another halfpenny, and told me to go and get a pint of porter, and stop till he came to me, and he would go to his friend to sell the bird (he had the bird with him); he returned to me in about ten minutes or a quarter of an hour, and asked if I was ready to go - I said I had not drank the porter; he took the pot up in his hand, and drank himself - I had paid twopence for it, the three-halfpence of my own and the halfpenny he had given me; he then said, "Drink that up, we will go home directly" - I drank it up in a draught, and in coming past the bar, he said, "Will you have any thing short to drink?" - I said, "I don't want any thing;" he said, "I have just had something myself, and you may as well have something" - I said "I will have a pennyworth of rum, if you please," and I had it; he went to the step of the door, and as he stepped into the street, he called out, "Come on," and as soon as I stepped out, Scott and Prothero came across the road, and collared me: they said, "You have got something you have no business to have, about you" - I said, "I have not done any thing to hurt any body, nor robbed any body - I don't know why you want to take me to prison;" they both said, "You have got something in your hat you have no business to have" - they said, "Don't let him put his hands to his hat," and they kept them down - I said, "If I have got any thing in my hat that I have no business to have, it belongs to that man," pointing to Perkins, who was close to us at the time - I said, "Take that man, it belongs to him - if it is wrong, take that man - what I have got in my hat belongs to that man - take that man;" they said, they had no business with that man, they had information upon me to take me; they then took me to the watch-house, and took off my hat the first thing they did - they pulled the money out, laid it on the bench, and I saw them mark a half-crown and a shilling with a pen and ink - whether there was any more I cannot say - I was then taken to prison, and the next morning before the Magistrate.
 COURT. Q. Did you see either Murray or Evans? A. Not till we were at Guildhall.
 Cross-examined by PRENDERGAST. Q.Prothero was a Policeman, was he not? A. He was in a Police dress - I do not know whether he was employed in that very part; the Magistrate ordered me to be sent to gaol on a charge of being in possession of bad money; I was there five weeks, and I think I was discharged eight weeks ago to-day, I was discharged at Guildhall by Mr. Alderman Wood; I have been a labouring man all my life - I do not follow birdcatching regularly, only when I have nothing else to do; I have been in regular work - I used to work for Mr. Fenderman while he was alive - I used to work in the brick way - I never was a groom; I have lived about that neighbourhood all my life; when Perkins gave me the parcel, he told me it was money, but I did not know what it was - it was in a bit of paper - I could tell by feeling that it was money, but I did not look at it; I have stated all that he said to me.
 Q. Upon your oath, did you not say before the Magistrate, that Perkins said, when he gave you the paper, that he would show you how to make these things? A. Yes; I forgot that - he said "some things," he did not say what; he told me that, not when he gave me the paper, but at the top of Fleet-market.
 COURT. Q. You have said some things, was not the expression such things? A. He said some things.
