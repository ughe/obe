ANN TAYLOR. I am a girl of the town. I was in Hart-street on, Sun day morning, the 10th of May, about half-pest one o'clock—I saw Allen and the prisoner there fighting—I had a good opportunity of seeing the prisoner—he had a knife in his right hand—I saw him stab Allen twice with the knife, and throw the knife in the street—I went to look for a policeman, but could not find one, and came back again—one cams up afterwards—I saw the policeman take up the knife—it was a little bloody on the handle.
   Cross-examined. Q . How long after you bad seen the fight was it? A . I should say about half an hour, bat not longer—I had left the spot, and came back again—I do not know what time in the morning it was—sometimes I live at No. 13, Stacey-street, and sometimes in Leg-alley, near Phcenix-alley—I have no regular lodging—I have seen Allen some times—he is no particular acquaintance—I sometimes go to No. 6—when I saw the knife I did not say, "He has got a knife in his hand"—I saw him stab Allen twice—it was not a clasp-knife, it was a long knife—I saw the handle and the blade—the handle was not in his hand when he threw it away—he had it in his hand about two minutes after stabbing the man, and then threw it away—I did not pick it up—I went away, and in half an hour the policeman showed it to me—when I saw the knife I said, he ought to be ashamed of himself—I went as far as Long-acre to look for a policeman, and turned back again—I was servant at the house in Leg-alley a few months ago—I lived once in Phoenix-alley.
