2131. JAMES WHITE was indicted for stealing 16 iron bolts, value
28., the goods of the Eastern Counties and Thames Junction Railway
Company.
WILLIAM DAVEY. I am agent to the Eastern Counties and Thames
Junction Railway Company—they are incorporated—the works are going
on, and are to be completed by Christmas.
JOHN WILLIAM MANNING (police-constable K 43.) On the 18th of Oct.
my attention was called to a bag, containing a quantity of iron bolts, con.
cealed in a yard behind some large gates, the property of the Eastern
Counties and Thames Junction Railway—I concealed myself some dis-
tance from it, with another constable—the prisoner came in a few minutes,
walked deliberately through the gates, directly to the bag—I saw bim stoop
down, but then lost him in tbe shade—I heard the sound of iron, as though
he was putting them into his pocket; and as be was passing through the
gates out, I followed him, and took him into custody—I found on his
person sixteen bolts—I went back, and found three bolts in the bag, cor-
responding with those on his person—after completing the search, I re-
marked there were only sixteen found on the prisoner—be replied, I
only took eight, and if get over this job I will go to sea again.'
MICHAEL CASEY (police—constable K 378.) I was with Manning—I
came up with the prisoner—he made an effort to get rid of the bolts—I
found be was loaded with bolts.

found on the Company's ground, in a different spot to where I sent two
bags of them to be used, which was at the back of the Bird in Hand public-
house, about eighty yards from where these were found—we use a great
