628. THOMAS KASLEY, stealing 58. 4d. in copper money, of John
Riley.
Ann Riley. I am the wife of John Riley, who keeps the Haberdashers'
Arms beer shop, in Milton-street. I keep my copper money in a bag in a
cupboard by the side of my bar-I had 12s. in copper in it on Monday, 17th
of May-I had counted it on the Sunday evening before I counted it again
that Monday morning, about half past 9 o'clock, after the prisoner was in
custody, and I missed 55. 4d.-I had not seen the prisoner before that, but
I heard his voice in my house about 20 minutes past 8-he asked my little
girl where her mother was, and said, “Will she let me have a pot of beer
till dinnertime?"
ELLEN RILEY. I am the daughter of the last witness. I saw the prisoner
in my mother's place on Monday morning, 17th May-he was inside the bar,
where my mother used to keep her copper-I saw him take bis hand from
the bag, and when he got outside the door he put his hand full of coppers
into his pocket-I called my mother, and ran after him-I saw him brought
back.
Prisoner. Q. Did you take some money out of that bag that morning?
A. Yes, 8d. for my sister and brother's school money.
