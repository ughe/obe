 1329.  WILLIAM MILLS was indicted for stealing, on the 22d of April , 2 yards of twilled cotton, value 1s., the goods of John Clark , his master.
 MR. PHILLIPS conducted the prosecution.
   DANIEL FORRESTER. I am an officer. On the evening of the 22d of April, I watched Mr. Clark's warehouse, and saw the prisoner come out; I followed him down East Smithfield, where he joined a woman, and went into a liquor-shop; I followed him, pulled off his hat, and found these two pieces of calico - he said, "You don't mean to take me for this, we will have a drop of something to drink; you don't know me, nor I you;" I took him to his master's counting-house, and he said, he took it to make a pair of drawers - he said this quite voluntarily.
   ROBERT BYFIELD. I am in Mr. John Clark's employ. The prisoner was brought back in custody; I did not see him until next morning - I saw the goods safe at four o'clock.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   HENRY PHILLIPS. I am in Mr. Clark's employ, and was present when the prisoner was brought back; no threat or inducement was held out to him - he said, "It is only a piece of brown cotton, which I took to make myself a pair of drawers;" the officer said, "Then you acknowledge you had it from Mr. Clark's?" he said, Yes; he has been in our employ one year and three quarters. GUILTY. Aged 49.
 Strongly recommended to Mercy. - Confined Two Months.
 1330.  WILLIAM BENTLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of May , 1 pelisse, value 6s., and 1 shawl, value 4s., the goods of Stephen Sandford.
 STEPHEN SANDFORD. I  keep an eating-house in Duke-street. I missed a shawl and a pelisse from my bed-room, on the 3d of May - I suspected the prisoner, and found him on the 6th of May, in custody; I did not know him before.
   JOHN LAWS. I was on duty in Newgate-street on the 5th of May; the prosecutor came and gave the prisoner into my charge.
   NATHANIEL ROUTH. I was at Guildhall when the prisoner was brought there; he was discharged, to be sent on board a ship - as I went along with him, I said, "It is a pity the man should lose his property;" he then cried, and said, he would show me where it was - he took me to the pawnbroker's; and said, he did not intend to go away without letting the man know where it was.
   HENRY WHARTON. I am a pawnbroker. On the 1st of May the prisoner pawned this pelisse, and on the 26th of April, this shawl.
 Prisoner. It was all through starvation.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.
 1331.  PETER SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of May , 2 looking-glasses, value 5s., the goods of Arthur Wilcoxon , and others, his masters.
 ARTHUR WILCOXON. I am a  looking-glass manufacturer, and live in Lombard-street; the prisoner was in my employ for about twelve months - on the 24th of May I lost these two glasses.
 Cross-examined by MR. BARRY. Q. You sell a great number? A. Yes; I cannot say I have not sold them; I had a nine years character with him - I have two partners.
   FRANCIS KEYS. On the 24th of May I was watching Mr. Wilcoxon's porters out; I saw the prisoner come out, and followed him, he went into a pawnbrokers in Houndsditch, and pawned these glasses - I then followed him to Southwark-bridge and took him.
   THOMAS CAPPS. I am a pawnbroker. The prisoner pawned these glasses.
   JAMES FOWLER. I went to the prisoner's lodgings and found another glass. GUILTY. Aged 44.
 Strongly recommended to Mercy - Confined 3 months.
