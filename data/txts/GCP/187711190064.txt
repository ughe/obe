42. WILLIAM JACKSON (33), Stealing 52 yards of cloth of
Francis McCarthy.
MR. ST. AUBYN conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM BUTTERICK (City Policeman 108). On Saturday afternoon,
November 3rd, I was in Barbican, and there saw the prisoner with
bundle of cloth on his back coming from the direction of Fore Street, and
going towards Aldersgate--I followed him to a house in Clerkenwell and
baw him go into the house and into the back parlour, and as soon as I got
into the parlour I saw the cloth behind the door-I told him I was a police
officer, and asked how he became possessed of the cloth-he said he had
bought it, and had given 71, 108. for it-I asked him where he bought it,
and he said at 35, Jewin Street-I asked for the receipt, and he said he
was a jobber and did not have receipts-be would not go with me to
Jowin Street, and I sent for assistance, and another officer arrived, and
he was taken to the station, and I took possession of the cloth-on going
to Guildhall he said a gentleman asked him to carry the cloth and he took
it home, and he should have taken it to the station.
ROBERT ANSOOMBE (City Policeman 112). I was called by the last wit
ness and helped to take the prisoner to the station--he said he had a
receipt in his pocket-that he had bought the cloth, and that we should
find it out on Monday-in the cell in the Guildhall justice-room he said
gentleman asked him to carry it for him and he went in a public-house
in Bunhill Row and had some beer and went home.
JOHN COLLINS DILLON. I am foreman to the prosecutor-this cloth is
his producod I saw it last on the 31st October, I should think two
days before it was lost.
FRANCES MOCARTuy. I am the prosecutor's wife he is unable to
attend through illness-between 3 and 4 o'clock on the day in question
I saw somebody with a roll of cloth on his back-I could not see his face
- he was just opposite.
Guilty. Ho also PLEADED GUILTY to a conviction on the 19th December,
1870, at Clerkonwell Sessions. Five other convictions against him wero proved.
Ten Years' Ponal Servitude.
