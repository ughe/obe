COTTON, Mayor.
• Cross-examined. My mother applied for a loan for me--I have not
made my cross on the guarantee paper, nor have I seen it-my mother
is in Australia-she went into the workhouse after-not when she was
applying for the loan-my brother had a loan and paid back monies to
Mr. Bell_I never saw Mr. Bell and had nothing to do with him I did
not live with my brother-proceedings were taken against me by the parish
authorities to support my mother at the time the money was due - I did
not keep out of the way--I paid-my brother appeared before the board
the first board day-I cannot say how long it was before he appeared.
