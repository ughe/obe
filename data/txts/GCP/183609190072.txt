Cross-examined by Mr. Bodkin. Q. What are you by trade? 4.1
am no trade, I am inspector of the watch, and act for my brother, who is

cart-nobody was in it with me I was sitting in the cart, with thirty
cwt. of goods- I was going gently--there were no carriages before me
there were a great many people about, and on horseback, and in carts,
usual-there were persons on the pavement, near the corner of Jewry-street
-I should think these persons stopped at the corner two minutes-that
was all the time I had to observe them I saw them before they sur
rounded Mrs. Spink-I stopped wben I first saw them surround her-
not before--the cart was going at about two miles an hour-I stopped
