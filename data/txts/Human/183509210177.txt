On the 31st of August, I saw the prisoner in the Broadway, Deptford—he went into Mr. Sharp's—I saw him come out, and Mr. Sharp came to the door—I asked him what the prisoner had got—he said a sheet that he suspected he had stolen—I followed the prisoner, and asked what he had got—he said a sheet that he had brough from on board the brig Charlotte;  and that his mother made it him at Kingston, in the West Indies—I took him to the station-house, and the prosecutor owned the sheet.
   Prisoner.  The woman where I slept robbed my bundle—I went the next morning, and took this sheet instead of my own—my ship is gone to sea now.
   COURT to MARY MORIARTY . Q.  Did the prisoner leave a sheet behind? A.  I did not see any—my sheet was clean in a drawer. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Three Months.
 2096. EDWARD GOUGH was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of August , 1 shawl, value 9 s  .; 1 handerchief, value 1 s  .; and 1 gown, value 4 s  .; the goods of Sarah Mary Ann Taylor . SARAH MARY ANN TAYLOR. I live servant at the Lord Wickham public-house, at Woolwich. The prisoner was in the habit of coming there—on the 24th of August I went to the town, on an errand—when I returned I went into my room—I found my box had been broken open; and I missed a gown, shawl, and handkerchief—I went to Mr. Davis, a pawn broker, where I found the shawl and handkerchief; and the gown at Mrs. Hoath's—these are the things—I had seen them safe between three and four o'clock. WILLIAM BOOTH. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Powis-street, Woolwich. I took in this gown of the prisoner, on the 24th of August. JAMES CHANDLER. I am a pawnbroker. I took in this shawl and handkerchief of the prisoner, on the 24th of August.
   Prisoner. Q.  Can you swear I am the person who pawned them? A.  Yes, certainly I can—It was between seven and eight o'clock in the evening. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined six Months.
   Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 2097. JOHN KEENAN was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of September , 1 candlestick, value 3 s  ., the goods of James Watson . JAMES WATSON. I keep the White Horse public-house, at New Charl ton, in Kent. On the 6th of September, about ten minutes before eleven o'clock at night, the prisoner and another came in—they called for some ale—they put down a sovereign, and paid for it—they then called for half-a-pint of gin—I saw they had had enough; and I told them, as soldiers, to keep sober, and go to their barracks—they went into my parlour, and drew the door to—when I would not draw them the liquor, away they went—I saw them go into the road—I then went into the parlour, and missed the candlestick—I had no suspicion that they had taken it till the next morning, when the watchman brought the candlestick to my house.
