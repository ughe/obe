drunk and disorderly, and as a rogue and vagabond, living on the
proceeds of prostitution.
The Recorder said he did not know what the jury thought of this
case, but if he were in their place he would not believe a word prose
cutor said. A man living on the prostitution of women and under
orders to be expelled the country was not worthy to be believed in
Court of Justice. He had very great doubt whether prosecutor was
robbed at all.
Verdict, Not guilty.
