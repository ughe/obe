I think it was in April—I took this order for two fowls to Mr. Smithers, the fishmonger—the prisoner gave it to me ( Read : "C. L. C, 13, 2, 75. RICHARD BLAKE. I am employed at the City Liberal Club as page—I was engaged by the prisoner on 24th October on 20th March the day of the boat race, the prisoner told me if I went up into his room I should find two notes there which I was to take to the parties addresses, and when I got the things they gave me, I was to take them to 278, Vauxhall Bridge Road—it was about 11.30 when he told me that, but I did not go till about 4.30—at 3.30 I was going up to us room when I met the chef, and he told me where I should find, the notes—I went in to the steward's room and found them; these are the notes marked C and D, one is addressed to Mr. Moss, the butcher, and the other to Boot & Davis the cheesemongers—I went first to. Moss and got a leg of mutton, and then to Boot & Davis' and got some eggs bacon and cheese—I then went to the prisoner's house and delivered them to Mrs. Dawson—I had, been there before, 'about a fortnight before as near as I can guess—Mr. Dawson told me to go on that occasion—I took a parcel that he had left in the hall to be. forwarded.
