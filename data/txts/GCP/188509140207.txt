JmE FLAXELL HINDLET (Interpreted). I am & wine and provision
merchant-in July last a Mr. Gull introduced Linden to me as a customer
I asked him what kind of goods he would want he said wines and
provisions he told me he had registry offices in Belgium, France, and
England-I asked him where his office was, and he said Near to your
house "-he asked me if I required a partner or manager, accountant,
book-keeper, or clerk, and he would get me one-I told him as the
business was getting on better now I should like to have a partner if I
could get a suitable person for the business—the next day Linden asked
me to come to his office the prisoners came to my place Launet said
If you will come at 5 o'clock to my place I wish to speak to you "-I
went with Gull to Noel Street-the prisoners and another man were
there.Linden said "I have written to France and other parts, and two
gentlemen will come, and I have no doubt that one of the two will suit
you; I will cause him to deposit 3,000 or 5,000 francs in my pocket, and
then I will send him to you to treat; once I have the money in my pocket,
and you have come to terms with them yourself, I shall prepare the
agreement," the terms of which were that the money was to be paid
As a loan at six months' interest in advance-I said M Well, if one of these
gentlemen call upon me and I find him suitable I will accept him " he
said "You call him a gentleman, I call him a type," meaning a man
not of great conséquence, or "one of the set "-I said "Well, if the man
suits me I will make an inventory of my stock, and will take him as a
partner; I shall then have an agreement drawn up by a solicitor, and it
we should dissolve partnership the money will be refunded to him " he
said "No, no, that must not be done like that; in a little while you will
pick a quarrel with him somehow or other, and turn him out of doors,
then send him to me, and I will just give him a touch of my boot to a
certain part, and send him back to his own country, and you need not
return him the money before the expiration of the contract in three or five
years accordingly"I did not fall in with the arrangement-I communi-
cuted with the police--this occurred the beginning of July.

Linden first it was because I never knew your names correctly, and made
a mistake; I meant the man in the middle (Launot)-the interpreter at
the police-court was so bad that he did not understand what I said, at all
events you (Launet) are the man I meant, and you must not forget I am
on my oath --I said Linden because I heard Linden so often that I made
& muddle of it-on the first occasion Gull introduced me to you (Larnet),
for on the second I saw you all three together-it was Linden who spoke
to me first at Noel Street.
