882. FRANK JEACOCK (28), Burglariously breaking and entering
the dwelling-house of Auguste Müller, and stealing a bag and goods
value 1207.
Ma. Grain Prosecuted: MR. THORNE Come Defended.
AUGUSTE MULLER. I occupy the second floor at 56, Eagle Street,
Holborn-I am a furrier-I work for, amongst other people, & Mr.
Phillips, of Newgate Street-on 6th August I had some sealskins in
the back workroom to make up for him into jaokets-about 7.30 I left
my premises safely locked up the skins were hung on a lino--they
could not be seen from outside they were worth 1201.I had employed
the prisoner's brother there, but discharged him a fortnight before the
robbery-the prisoner had come to see his brother late at night I had
seen him twice before 6th August- I came back to my premises between
one and two on Sunday morning somebody spoke to me, and I went
upstairs-the door had been locked with a key similar to this-I found
it open, and some marks on it as if an instrument had been used-I
missed the six jackets from the line-no inner doors had been locked-I
had left a calico bag on the table in the workroom where the skins were
-I missed that bag when I returned I found this hat in the small
room before I came to the workshop-it is not mine, and was not there
when I left.
