 Second Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1229.  JOHN HARWOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of September , one toast rack, value 7 s., the goods of James Shabot.
   ROBERT LONG. I am servant to Mr. James Shabot, a merchant, of Montague-street, Russell-square. On the 8th of September, about ten o'clock in the morning, I was at breakfast, the cook came down from the dining-room, and said I had not put the heater in the fire; I went into the pantry and saw the prisoner put his hand through the iron bar, the window was open, and take the toast rack - he had come down the area steps. I ran after him - he threw it down in Montague-street - I caught him in Bury-street, and gave him in charge - I never lost sight of him.
   WILLIAM WALTON. I was in the mews and saw the prisoner run across with the toast rack, he threw it down at my feet, I picked it up - Long was following and took him.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I heard the cry and ran into Bury-street after the thief.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years.
 Second Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1230.  MARY GRIFFITHS was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of August , one candlestick, value 2 s., the goods of William Hitchcock.
   WILLIAM HITCHCOCK. I am a publican, and live in Old-street. On the 14th of August, about half-past six o'clock at night, two gentlemen came into my parlour to write, and had a candlestick on the table. I went out, and did not miss it till the next morning; the prisoner was frequently at my house, selling buttons.
   SAMUEL BRADY. I am a publican, and live near Hitchcock. The prisoner came into my house on this night, about half-past ten o'clock, she said something, and two Scotch gentlemen said, "Turn that woman out;" I went to turn her out, and saw the candlestick sticking out of her pocket, I took it from her, she said it was her own; I sent for the watchman, who took her.
   JOHN TWEEDY. I am a constable. On the 14th of August, I took charge of the prisoner, and asked her what had become of the candlestick, she said she never saw it before.
   JOSEPH PRINCE. I was taking the prisoner to the watch-house, she said if I would forgive her, she would shew me where she took it from - I took her to several houses, and when we came to the Rum Puncheon public-house, where Hitchcock lives, she said "That is it."
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 (The prisoner put in a written defence, stating, that she had drank rather freely, and did not know she had the candlestick. till it was found on her.)
     GUILTY. Aged 63. Confined Three Months.

 1231.  JOHN GUEST was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of July , one basket, value 6 d., and twelve ounces of butter, value 6 d., the goods of John Dougan.
   JOHN BAYLESS. I am a watchman, at Islington. On the 21st of July, at twenty minutes past four o'clock in the morning, I was on duty in the back road; a boy gave me information, I went down Dougan's area, and found the prisoner with this basket, containing butter and candles - I secured him.
   MARY RUSSELL. I am servant to Mr. John Dougan, who lives at No. 6, Barnsby-place, Islington. The basket, butter, and candles, are his, and were safe on a shelf in the area when I went to bed.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Transported for Seven Years.
 First Middlesex Jury, before W. Arabin, Esq.
 1232.  MARY ANN GREEN, ELIZABETH HAYLETT, and JOHN DOUGAN were indicted for stealing, on the 22d of August , two handkerchiefs, value 2 s., the goods of Richard Garland.
   JAMES METCALF. I am a constable. On the 22d of August, about eight o'clock at night, I was in St. John-street, opposite Mr. Garland's, and saw Green and Dougan come up to this handkerchief, the prisoners were all three together, a few yards from the shop; one of them cut or tore down some handkerchiefs, which hung at the door - Haylett stood five or six yards off, the other two beckoned to her, she went up and took the handkerchief, and crossed the street, saw me, and set off running - I went and took them out of her apron, the other two were taken.
   RICHARD GARLAND. I am a linen draper, and live in St. John-street. These handkerchiefs hung at my doorpost, partly outside, and partly inside; I was called to the door, and knew them to be mine.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 GREEN'S Defence. I was looking in at the window, the other girl came up and said, "Are they not pretty prints."
 HAYLETT'S Defence. I saw these girls at the door, and I took the handkerchiefs.
 DOUGAN'S Defence. I know nothing of these girls.
 HAYLETT -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined Three Months.
 GREEN -    NOT GUILTY.
 DOUGAN -    NOT GUILTY.
