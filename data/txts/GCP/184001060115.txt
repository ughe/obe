Blizner's Defence. On the Friday when Carroll left her place, I was
at the Jolly Gardeners public-house--she came in, sat down along side of
me, and drank with me she asked if I would accept of that knifo-she
gave it to me, and afterwards took a snuff-box out containing a coral neck-
lace and said, "I will make you a present of this for your wife's little girl"
-I said, “Where did you get it?"-she said it was her own, and she had
bought and paid for it-she asked where I was going I said towards home
--she wanted to go that way, and came with me I went home, and she
went elsewhere ---next morning I was at the Queen's Head public-house-
I took the necklace out of my pocket-several women there wanted to buy
it of me- shoemaker said, "I will go and pledge it for you"_be did
take it, and pawned it for 18. 6d., and brought me the duplicato at night
I was taken for stealing from Carroll--the pawnbroker said I was the per-
son who pawned the necklace, but before the Magistrate he did not know
whether I was the person or not.
