939. ALICE COLLIS (30), Feloniously wounding Adelaide Frioschi,
with intent to do her grievous bodily harm.
MR. BLACKWELL Prosecuted.
ADELAJDE FRIOSCHI. I am the wife of Charles Frioschi, of 81,
Southampton Street, Clerkenwell--the prisoner was a friend of mine-
on the night of October 5th we had a few words, and she took out R
knite and stabbed me in my face, in two places, but I do not think she
intended it; I think she had had a glass of drink I went to the station,
and a medical man dressed my wounds.
Cross-examined. I did not hit you on your head with my umbrella-I
had an umbrella, but I did not raise it-I said, “Why don't you go
home?" and then you took out the knife and stabbed me-I do not want
to press the charge against you-I had not been drinking.
JOSEPH HUTCHINSON. I am a surgeon-on October 6th, at 12.45, 1
was called to the police-station and saw the prosecutrix; she had two
punctured wounds, one an inch from the outside angle of her right eye,
and the other an inch below the lobe of her right ear-they were slight
wounds - this knife might have caused them; it is an ordinary pocket-
knife.
Thomas RABBETT (296 G). On October 5th, at 12.45, the prosecutrix
gave the prisoner into my eharge for stabbing her-she said, “I did,"
and produced the knife, opened it, and said, "I will show you how. I did
it” -I took it from her-I think she had been drinking.
The prisoner produoed a written defence, stating that she had been drinking,
and that the prosecutrix struck her with an umbrella and broke it over her head;
that she had just been eating meat, and had a knife in her hand and stabbed hor,
but did not intend to do her any bodily harm.
Guilty of unlawfully wounding.--Recommended to meroy by tho JURY.-
