Cross-examined. That has no connection with value received on that bill
--there are no dots on the blotter, only one after “B.," not after Barry
I do not remember ever seeing two dots after an initial before, and it

received" - I call a dash writing - the two dashes look as if
they were made at the same time—there is a distinct difference in the
quantity of ink, but not in the colour—so far as the depth of colour is.
