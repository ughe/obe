 471. (M.)  JAMES MARTIN was indicted for ripping, cutting, and stealing 60 lb. of lead, the said lead being affixed to a stable, the property of Robert Adams and James Adams, March 22d. +
   Robert Lorrimore. I am a stone-mason: I was employed by Mr. Adams in a building in Mansfield-street; there was 60 lb. of lead stole from the stables.
   James Darnford. I was employed to watch the buildings: I heard a cutting, and saw the prisoner; as soon as he perceived me he endeavoured to make his escape; I pursued him and took him; I found that lead cut and rolled up.
 Prisoner's Defence.
 I went into the building to ease myself.
     Guilty. T.

 476. (L.)  MARY QUICK, spinster, was indicted for stealing a pair of linen sheets, value 3 s. the property of John Powell, Feb. 4. ++
   John Powell. I live in St. Ann's Black Fryers: the prisoner was one of the poor of the parish of St. Faith; I contract with the parish to take care of the poor: I delivered out the sheets to them to wash, in the wash-house in my house; I lost a pair.
   John Quince. The prisoner pawned this sheet with me. (Produced and deposed to by the prosecutor).
 Prisoner's Defence.
 I was in great distress when I did it. I leave myself to the mercy of the Court. Guilty 10 d. W.
