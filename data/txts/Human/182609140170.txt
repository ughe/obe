 1774.  JOHN COLEMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of July , 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of a certain person unknown , from his person.
   JOHN WILLIAM DIXON. I am servant to Mr. Jones, of Budge-row. On the 27th of July I was in the shop, and saw the prisoner lift up a gentleman's pocket, and take a handkerchief out; he put it into his own pocket - I ran out, and collared him; the gentleman claimed the handkerchief, but said he could not stop to prosecute.
   EDWARD JONES. My apprentice called me; I went out and collared the prisoner; he dropped the handkerchief behind him; the gentleman claimed it, but he could not wait.
 Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up.
 Two witnesses gave the prisoner a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined One Month and Whipped.
 1775.  MARY FOY was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of August , 2 shawls, 2l. 10s., the goods of Ann Solomons , in the dwelling-house of Nathan Marks.
 ANN SOLOMONS. I live at Carlisle. On the 7th of August I deposited these shawls, among other things, at Mr. Marks, in a reticule, in Mrs. Marks' bed-room - they are not worth much now.
 NATHAN MARKS. Solomon is my niece. The prisoner came into my service on the 31st of July, and on the 7th of August she asked leave to fetch her clothes; she never returned - next morning the shawls were missed.
   WILLIAM LOCK. I am a pawnbroker. On the 7th of August the prisoner pawned these shawls.
   RICHARD MORRIS. I am an officer. I apprehended her, and found the duplicate concealed in her shawl.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. Confined Two Months.
 1776.  JOHN FITZGERALD was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of August , 1 coat, value 2l., and 1 waistcoat, value 10s., the goods of Thomas Halliday.
 THOMAS HALLIDAY. I am a  waiter, and lodge in Chequer's-yard. On the 19th of August, in the morning, I left my coat and waistcoat safe in my box; the prisoner, who slept in the room, was then getting up; I went up after he was gone, and missed them; I followed him down Petticoat-lane, and got assistance; I then said to him"Where are my clothes?" he said "Here they are, I don't want to rob you; I was going to the tailor's to get a suit made like them;" he had them round him, under his coat.
   ROBERT BULLERS. I took the prisoner in charge. After I looked him up, I looked into the cell, hearing a bustle, and saw the clothes there; he had them wrapped round him under his coat.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 31. Transported for Seven Years.
 1777.  WILLIAM MONKS was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of September , 3 saws, value 20s.; 5 planes, value 15s.; 7 moulding planes, value 15s.; 1 nest of drawers, value 5s.; 1 pair of compasses, value 1s., and 1 square, value 1s. 6d., the goods of John Dyer.
 JOHN DYER. I am a  carpenter. On the 10th of September I found the prisoner in my shop with my tool-chest open, and these tools on his person; he said he did not know how he came there.
   JOHN BROWN. I received him in charge; he said he got in at the sky-light.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Confined One Year.

 1779.  GEORGE HOLMES was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of September , 1 shilling, the money of James Ward , his master.
 Mr. PHILLIPS conducted the prosecution.
 JAMES WARD. I live in  Jewin-street, Cripplegate, and am a bricklayer; the prisoner was apprentice d to my brother and servant to me. On the 3d of September I marked a shilling, with a scratch on the edge, and put it into a waistcoat pocket, on the floor of my room; the prisoner slept in the same room; next morning I missed it; he went out; I got up and followed him; he went to a coffee-shop, and returned; I got an officer, who found the marked shilling on him and some keys.
   WILLIAM TAYLOR. I am an officer. I searched the prisoner, and found the shilling in his waistcoat-pocket, with a few half-pence.
 Mr. WARD. This is the shilling I marked.
 Prisoner's Defence (written). I came home on Sunday with a shilling and a sixpence in my pocket, which I put on the drawers, to put into my other pocket in the morning - I was stooping to untie my shoe and picked up this shilling, which I threw on the drawers; and in the morning I took a shilling and a sixpence off the drawers, and thought they were my own.
 Mr. WARD. It was impossible that it could have fallen from my pocket; there was no shilling found on the drawers; I put it on the floor carefully; he was in bed before I put it there.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years. - (See 6th Session, page 498.)
