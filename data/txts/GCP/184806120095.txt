1567. WILLIAM PEACOCK, stealing 1 bag, value 1d.; 30 sovereigns, and
10 half-sovereigns; the property of William Shippey and another, his masters.
- 2nd Count, stating it to be the property of William Shippey.
Mr. Parry conducted the Prosecution.
William SHIPPEY. I am a licensed horse-slaughterer, and deal in horses,
The prisoner was in my service one night-I engaged him last Thursday five
Weeks, and agreed to give him ll. a week, and he drew 6s, in advance-he
positively swore before me on his knees that he would never rob me of a
farthing-on Friday we went to Hertford fair-I was taken ill, and could not
walk about the fair-I staid at the inn, and gave the prisoner 401, in gold, in
a bag, and told him to go about and see if he could buy some sound horses
Jor me, not to slaughter, but to sell again-he returned in about an hour, said
