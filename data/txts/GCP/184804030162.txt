1167. THOMAS HOWLETT, GEORGE PAYNE, JAMES TAYLOR,
and RICHARD WEBSTER, were again indicted, with HENRY BATES,
WILLIAM BARRETT, JOHN DENMORE, and SAMUEL MORGAN,
for breaking and entering the dwelling-house of David Jameson, at Camber-
well, and stealing therein 100 pairs of shoes, 80 pairs of boots, 20 necklaces,
20 towels, and other articles, value 25l. 18s.; his goods.
Messrs. BODKIN and CLERK conducted the Prosecution.

ampton-street, Camberwell—it is bis dwelling-bouse, and is in Camberwell
parish. On 13th March, between twelve and one o'clock, we had a good
many goods outside our shop, and hearing the mob was coming, we removed
the whole of them, except one jacket-we got our shutters all up but the
last one-we could not get that up- I could not get the bar up-my hus-
