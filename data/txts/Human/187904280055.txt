   Cross-examined.  I cash cheques for Mr. Stevens—the prisoner has brought me one or two cheques for him—as a rule I pay all cheques into the bank—he has brought one or two to Mr. Stevens's order, which I have cashed per sonally; they were endorsed—I have cashed (several for Mr. Stevens, and one or two very small ones for Mr. Reynolds—I never pay the cheques away to my customers, but will not swear I have not done so—I may have cashed 100 cheques for Stevens—my daughter assists me in the business.
   Re-examined.  It is not the custom to pay cheques to private gentlemen or customers instead of to the bank. GEORGE WILDBORE STEVENS. Joseph Reynolds, 12 l ."
