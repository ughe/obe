895. MARY ANN SMITH was indicted for stealing 32 yards of printed
'cotton, value 18s., the goods of George Thomas Minor; and that she had
been before convicted of felony.
HENRY EDWARD STREET. I am in the employ of George Thomas Minor,
a linen-draper, who lives at Lambeth. On the 30th of Jan. I put four pieces
of printed cotton outside the window-I saw them safe between eleven and
twelve o'clock - I went to take them in in the afternoon, and missed one of
them-this now produced is it-it is in two pieces now.
SOPHIA COLE. I am a pawnbroker. I took in this piece of print of the
prisoner on the 30th of Jan., between six and seven o'clock in the evening.
WILLIAM Amor. I am a pawnbroker. I took in this piece of cotton of
the prisoner on Friday, the 30th of Jan.
HENRY EDWARD STREET re-examined. These are my master's property.
Jury. Q. Are you able to swear to it? A. Yes- I believe the prisoner
was in the shop with another person, just before I missed the cotton- I swear
to this by the pattern, and it has been compared with a piece I cut off it, and
it corresponds exactly—I do not suppose only one person had the pattern-
this pattern I have in my pocket corresponds with the width of this cotton,
and it was torn off a dress that I cut off this cotton-there were
thirty-nine yards and a half of it, and I cut off six yards-1 should not like
to swear that the prisoner was in the shop, but I could swear to the person
who was with her.
The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that she had met her sister by
Bishopsgate Church, and received the cotton from her to make dresses, and
wanting to make a purchase at a bazaar, had pledged it.
