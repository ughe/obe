That the Prisoner at last confest the Cheat, and that one Conner (a Prisoner) put him upon it. Sheriff Billers deposed that the Prisoner served him such a Trick and got Two Guineas out of him. The Prisoner behaved himself very impudently to the Court, the Evidences, etc. The Jury found him   Guilty. And it appearing that he had been twice convicted for the like Offence before, and had been Fined, and Imprisoned for it, all which not being sufficient to reclaim him. The Court Fined him 10 Marks, and order'd him Three Years Imprisonment.
