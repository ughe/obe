1290. WILLIAM RICHARDSON, stealing 2lbs. weight of paint, value
88.; the goods of William Hurt, his master.
William Hurt. I am a wheelwright, at Iron-gate Wharf, Paddington ;
the prisoner was in my service. On 18th June I was in my loft behind a
cab, and saw him take zibs, of colour, worth Is. 6d. or 2s. a pound, and put
it into his pocket-he went down into the workshop-I followed him, and
told him to take that colour out of his pocket-he did not know what to say,
and at last he gave me the colour-1 gave him into custody.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'BRIEN. Q. How long has he worked for you?
4. Eight or nine months-he is a writer-he used to find his own paint in
working for other parties; I never saw any of bis in my loft-he had been
drinking- owe him 30s.- his wife has not applied to me for it.
WILLIAM BALE (policeman, D 225). I took the prisoner-on the way to
the station he said if Mr. Hurt had paid him what he owed him he should not
have done it.
