   Cross-examined by MR. SERJEANT PARRY . I saw the bows of the Fran  conid stove in; I could not say that I saw the sea rushing in, but I saw a fissure, I should describe it as a long, narrow hole; the sea must have been entering, I suppose; I did not see right through the fissure, I saw that the plate was broken, I saw the jagged ends—I only saw it for a few  seconds, I stood and watched the Franconia  back out of us.
   Re-examined by THE ATTORNEY-GENERAL . The Franconia  was an iron vessel, and I believe the Stratchclyde  also, but I could not say from my own knowledge. JOHN BEVAN.
