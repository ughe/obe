SLADE, Harry Hubert; unlawfully and designedly did falsely pre-
tend to the Société Générale du Credit Minier et Industriel de Paris
and others, that he, the said Harry Hubert Slade, had procured
divers persons to become subscribers to the sum of £10,000 sterling
towards the capital of a company, and that he, the said Harry
Hubert Slade, with intent thereby to defraud, was able and willing
to pay an instalment of £1,750.

KEELING, Mary Ann (48, no occupation), pleaded guilty of felo-
niously throwing upon Edward Keeling (her husband) a certain corro-
sive fluid with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
EDWARD KEELING. I irritated my wife by taking to drink and then
took up with another woman. We have been quarrelling for the last
two years. She kept the stuff in the house to clean a lamp. I would
rather not live with my wife; she has a very excitable temper; her
bad temper has driven me to what I have done. We have been
married 25 years, and have three children.
The prisoner was released on her recognisanoes in £50 to come
up for judgment if called upon.
