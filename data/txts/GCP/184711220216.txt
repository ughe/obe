Cross-examined. Q. How many beds were in that room 2 4. Five-

Davidson. Q. Is not the house kept open all night? 4. No, I never
allow my door open for anybody.
WILLIAM PENDER (policeman, M 278.) I took the prisoners--Mr. Middle-
