554. FREDERICK WYTHE (23), and WILLIAM METCALFE (25),
Burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Frederick Wells,
and stealing one coat, thirteen knives, two pieces of cloth, and other goods,
his property.
MR. GRIFFITHS conducted the Prosecution; and MR. PATER defended Wythe.
