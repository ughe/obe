2363. JAMES HUNT was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of July.
1 rule, value 2s., the goods of Philip Geoghagan.
Philip GEOGHAGAN. On the 22nd of July I and the prisoner were at
work at a house at Maida-hill, and I missed my rule- this is it.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Yon told the prisoner if he would
find it, you would forgive him, and he said he would get it in the morn-
ing? A. Yes, he did I should not have prosecuted him if I had not
been knocked about here, and lost my time-he bears a good character
-I think it was through his master not paying him, and he wanted a
dinner.
WILLIAM FAIRLAM Lorts. I am a pawnbroker. I took in this rule
of the prisoner for 9d.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Ten Days.
2364. ANN SANDERS was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of Au-
gust, 3 pairs of shoes, value 12s. 6d., the goods of Eliza Featherstone.
Eliza FEATHERSTONE. I am housekeeper to Mrs. Poole, of Wimpole-
street. A little before twelve o'clock last Sunday morning, I found the
prisoner in my room, where she had no business-I asked what she did
there, and she threw these three pairs of shoes of mine out of her apron
-I sent for an officer and gave her into custody- I never knew her be-
fore-she had come down the area steps, and got in.
Prisoner. I met a female in Wimpole-street; she asked me to go to
No. 26, for a person named Jane; I went; I saw two doors open; I
waited till I saw the housekeeper, and she accused me of stealing three
pairs of shoes-I had no shoes in my possession, but I saw them where
she had left them. Witness. We had a person named Jane in our house
- the prisoner had the shoes in her apron, and when I asked what she
had, she threw them on the chair.
GUILTY. Aged 49.-Confined Four Months.
2365. EMMA RIDOUT was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of June, 2
spoons, value. 8s.; and 1 handkerchief, value 18.; the goods of John Ben-
Dett, her master.
JOHN BENNETT. I live in Hoxton Old Town-the prisoner was my
servant. On the 18th of June I missed two spoons and a handkerchief-.
these now produced are them.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. How do you know the handker-
chief? A. It is torn, and I have worn it a long time-she had been with
me a month.
RICHARD GODWIN (police-constable N 217.) I took the prisoner for
taking these spoons-she said they were in pledge, and then she said she
would say no more about them.
Cross-examined. Q. Where did this conversation take place ? A. At
the corner of Pimlico-walk, in Hoxton-I said I took her for stealing a
handkerchief and two spoons-she first said she knew nothing of the
spoons, and then she said they were pawned, and refused to tell me
where.
