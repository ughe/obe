 1970. WILLIAM WEBB was indicted for stealing, on the l   7th of May , 1 pair of shoes, value 5 s  .; 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  .; 2 watchguards, value 6 d  .; 2 shillings, and 2 pence; the property of William Merritt . WILLIAM MERRITT. I live at Deptford, the prisoner lodged in the same house for a few days, and slept in the same room with me. On Whit Tuesday morning I missed the things and money stated, from my pocket in the bed-room—they were my own—I have no father—the pri soner was the first that went out that morning—he knew I had some money, for he had seen me give change with it—this watch-chain now produced is mine—I know it by a join in the middle—I kept it in my pocket in the bed-room.
