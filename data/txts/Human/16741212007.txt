Besides these nine ordered to receive Capital punishment, there were nine others that got off by the benefit of the Clergy, and carryed away the Law in their own hands. Twelve that prayed and obtained the favour of Transportation, and four sentenced to Shove the Fumbler, or receive the correction of the gentle Lash for several crimes respectively no less tedious, than impertinent here to be recited.
 There were likewise divers Romish Recusants Indicted for not repairing to their respective Parish Churches, and the Bills found against them according to the statutes in that case made and provided.
