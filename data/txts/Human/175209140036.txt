 499. (M.)  John Keeley was indicted for making an assault on James Glascow , putting him in corporal fear and danger of his life, and stealing from him one guinea his property in his own dwelling-house, Feb. 21. James Glascow did not appear. He was acquitted, and had a copy of his indictment granted him.
 The following Prisoners, condemned last Sessions, were executed on Monday, the 13th of July, viz.
   Daniel Macquin, and William Belcher, for footpad robbing.
