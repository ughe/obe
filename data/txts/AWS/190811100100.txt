RICHARD HAnRY KEEPING, timber importer, Fenchurch Buildings,
In January, 1907, I had my first communication from MaeCallum
Brothers, and after that some correspondence with them. Od
January 28, 1907, I received an order for timber, which I executed
and sent four delivery orders to MacCallum Brothers, which would
enable them to take the goods away from the docks whenever they
pleased. After I had sent the dock orders I asked for them to be
returned, but did not receive them. I then sent a letter with my
account, and enolosed a bill for acceptance, which was not returned,
and no answer whatever was made to my letter. I telephoned to
the works and to the house of Peter MaeCallum, but could get no
Answer. I then received a cireular letter informing me of a change
of address, and later on another cireular letter giving me notice that
they had deeided to file their petition in bankruptey. I visited the
Smallwood Road Works to see what had become of my timber, and
found that the name of the South London Joinery Company had been
put up there, but did not see either Peter or Andrew MacCallum. I
identified about wwo-thirds of the timber which I had sold, at the
works. I have not been paid any part of my account.
