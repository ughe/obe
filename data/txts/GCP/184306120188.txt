1946. JANE EVES was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of June, 2 breast
pins and chain, value 4l., the goods of Charles John Willett, from bie
person.
CHARLES JOHN Willett. I am an auctioneer's clerk. On the 7th of
June, I was walking down Vinegar-lane with a rose in my button-hole the
prisoner asked me to let her smell it-I took no notice-she came behind
me, put her hand round my neck, and took the pins out of my scarf-I looked
round, and could not see any one--I did not miss the pins till she was gone
- I lost sight of her for half an hour.
WILLIAM BROOKS (police-constable K 364.) I went with the prosecutor to
Vinegar-lane, and saw the prisoner standing at the door-we went past her.
and she called to the prosecutor, and said she had got bis diamond pin, that
she had tried to take the rose, and had taken the pin by mistake the prose-
cutor said he would give her in charge-she ran into the house, and through
into another house-I went and took her.
Prisoner's Defence. I asked him to let me smell his rose; he would not;
I put my arm round his neck, and the pin caught the sleeve of my gown ; I
said I could not run after him, but when he passed I would give it him.
BE Nor GUILTY.
1947. ANN CHILTON was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of June, 2
table cloths, value 2s. 6d. ; 1 shift, 13. 6d.; and 2 handkerchiefs, 28.; the
goods of Margaret Collins ; and that she had been previously convicted of
felony.
MARGARET COLLINS. I live in St. George's-in-the-East. My servant
was ill, and I employed the prisoner-I missed a sovereign, two tablecloths,
two handkerchiefs, and a shift-these are them.
FREDERICK JOHN Wood. I am a pawnbroker. I took in these two table-
cloths of the prisoner on the 1st of June-she said she brought them for her
sister-I have the other articles, but I do not remember whether I took them
of the prisoner.
TIMOTHY HERLEHY. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner—I asked
her if she knew any thing about the propertyshe said she had pledged
it.
Prisoner. I only pledged the two table cloths.
ROBERT BYERS. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's conviction, which
I got at Mr. Clark's office-(read)—the prisoner is the person.
Guilty. Aged 15.- Recommended to mercy.-Confined Nine Months, nine
She Weeks Solitary.
