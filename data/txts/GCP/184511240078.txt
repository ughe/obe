57. EDWARD CHANDLER was again indicted for stealing 1 breast-pin,
value 10s., the goods of William Walter.
WILLIAM WALTER. I now live in Tottenham-street, Fitzroy-square-I
before lived at Mr. Turner's—the prisoner lodged there, and slept with me-
I missed a breast-pin on Sunday, the 19th of Oct., this is it.
Prisoner. Q. What do you swear to it by? A. Because a piece of the
stone is broken.
ELIZABETH LONG. I am single. I know the prisoner-he gave me this
pin I am sure this is the pin he gave me.
Prisoner. Q. How long is it since I first came to your apartment? A. I
could not say precisely- I believe two or three months-you gave me this pin
soon after you came--not the first time--I think it was more than a fortnight
previous to your being given into custody-you gave it me to pin my hand-
kerchief-I have been at your lodging with it--nothing was said to me
about it.
