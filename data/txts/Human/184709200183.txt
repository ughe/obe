 2221. EDWARD ADAMS was indicted for stealing 3 shawls, value 3 l  .; 21 handkerchiefs, 3 l  .; 27 1/4 yards of stain, 5 l  . 10 s  .; 12 pair of stockings 2 l  . 17 s  .; 2 yards of cloth, 30 s  .; 1 1/2 yards of cambric, 12 s  .; 2 veils, 11 s  .; 2 scarfs, 7 s  .; 4 yards of ribbon, 4 s  .; 1 collar, 3 s  .; and 5 laces, 3 s  .; the good of Francis William Vant , his master. THOMAS DANES. I am in the service of Francis William Vant, draper, at Woolwich—the prisoner was his shopman—as I was shutting up at night, on the 12th Aug., I noticed a parcel on a shelf close by the warehouse door—I went up stair, and stated what I saw—I went down again, and the parcel was gone—I afterwards saw the prisoner take the same parcel out of his box—this is it ( produced )—these goods are Mr. Vant's. EDWARD MOODY. I was present when the box was searched—I have heard Davis's evidence—it is correct. GUILTY.  — recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor. — confined Nine Months
 2222. JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing 3 yards of corduroy, value 3 s  . 9 d  .; the good of the Guardians of the Poor of St. George-the-Martyr, Southwark , his matters. ELIZABETH SARAH O'BRIEN. I am matron of the workhouse of St. George-the-Martyr, Southwark, at the Lewisham—the prisoner was tailor there, at weekly wages—it was his business to cut out corduroy—he had no business to take any away—I have missed corduroy—I have seen some which is made into a pair of trowsers which Miller has on—I believe it is ours, but cannot swear to it—these other pieces I believe to be the same—they belong to the guar dians of the poor of that parish. CHARLSE MILLER. I am ostler at the Castle, at Lewisham—the prisoner made me this pair of corduroy trowsers which I have now on. EDWIN BOURNE. The prisoner asked me to carry a parcel to Price from the workhouse-yard—it appeared to be cord or jean—I did so. JOHN PRICE. I received these pieces of corduroy from the prisoner. CHARLES LLOYD( policeman, R  199.) I produce three pieces of corduroy, which I got from price.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I made two suits of clothes a week; I had two yards, or two yards and a half, according to the size of the lads; had I been short, I should have had to have made up the deficiency; I save pieces from the general cutting from one time to another.
   JURY to ELIZABETH SARAH O'BRIEN. Q.  was that the contract? A.  No, it was not—I bought everything that I gave him—I gave him out some times five yards, and sometimes six yards, to make a suit of clothes—if he had any over he would use it for repairing the old suits—he had no right to keep any himself. GUILTY . Aged 22.— confined Three Months .
