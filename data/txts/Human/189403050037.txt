OLD COURT .— Thursday, March  8 th , 1894.
   Before Mr. Justice Grantham.
 304. ANN NAOMI BERRY PLEADED GUILTY to feloniously killing and slaying her son, John Berry . She received a good character, by the inspector in the case stated that of late she had given way to drink. Three Months' Hard Labour.

   Before Mr. Recorder.
 305. CLAUDE AUDAIN PLEADED GUILTY to unlawfully converting to his own use an order for £195 1s., with which he had been entrusted.
   MR. BESLEY , for the prisoner, stated that he was willing to indemnify the prosecutor.— Discharged on recognizances.
