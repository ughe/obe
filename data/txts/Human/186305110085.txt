In the case of SAMUEL ROBINSON  (25) , charged wilful murder of Charlotte Hayes, Mr. John Roland Gibson, the surgeon of the gaol, deposed that the prisoner was of unsound mind, and unable even to challenge a Juror. MICHAEL ABRAHAMS( re-examined ). I am the solicitor for the assignees—I acted before that as solicitor for the petitioning creditor—I prepared the petition for adjudication, and attested the signature—I find my signature here on the attestation—I also attested the signature to the insolvency, and saw the prisoner sign it.
