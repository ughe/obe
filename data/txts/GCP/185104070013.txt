846. ELIZABETH JONES, stealing 1 watch, value 101.; the goods
of Robert Shipwright, from his person.
ROBERT SHIPWRIGHT. On Tuesday night, 11th March, about half-past
eleven o'clock, I went into the Mitre public-house-I saw the prisoner
there—I had never seen her before-I am sure she is the person—there were
two or three more girls with her-in consequence of something, I looked
for my watch, and missed it, when I had been there about twenty minutes
- I gave the prisoner into custody, in consequence of ber being close to
me at the time-this is my watch-I had looked at it when I went into
the house.

THOMAS LECHT (City policeman, 342). I was in Chancery-lane-Ship-

GEORGE COOKMAN. I went into the Mitre Tavern with Shipwright and

the prisoner put her arm out, as if to pass something to a female--the
policeman took her arm, and found the watch.
