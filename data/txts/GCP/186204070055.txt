Mr Lisle's you could get from No. 9 to No. 10 by the plank, and could
then very easily get to No. B_I got to the plank by getting up a ladder
half way, I then climbed up the wood-work of No. 10, drew the plank on to
the roof of No. 10, then returned, went to Mr. Lisle's, and called Mr.
Smithers up, it was then a quarter to 5-I went up to the trap-door and saw
