   Cross-examined by the Prisoner.  William Edwards offered me a shilling to get some drink and say nothing—he wanted me to go into another public-house; I would not, and he said "Here take a shilling." JOHN MOMAHON. I am a carpenter, of 17, Powis Street, Woolwich—on 7th July, soon after 11 o'clock, I went to the Prince Albert, and saw the prisoner outside the door—when I went in I heard a scuffle in the next compartment, and heard Mrs. Wheeler call her husband—I went out to go and see what the scuffle was, and saw the prisoner outside the door of the third compartment, with two hats on, one on the top of the other—I pushed open the door, and saw Thomas Edwards strike Ryan and rush past me into the street—the prisoner said "Come along," and I followed them into High Street—shortly before 2 o'clock I saw William Edwards, Thomas Edwards, and the prisoner—William Edwards and the prisoner were fighting—Ryan seemed insensible in the public-house, as if he had been drugged—he could not stand without help—I did not see the prisoner do anything to Ryan. ELLEN WHEELER. My father keeps the Prince Albert—on 7th July I served the prisoner with a pot of sixpenny ale and twopennyworth of rum—Ryan and the two Edwardses were there—the prisoner gave the rum into Ryan's hand, and they drank the ale between them—I saw them pulling Ryan about—the prisoner seemed to be rifling his pockets whilst the two Edwardses held him, and a little bag like this dropped from his pocket—Mrs. Smith picked it up, and one of the Edwardses snatched it from her and ran out, and the others followed—the bag was returned empty—the prisoner came to the door not five minutes after, with two hats on.
   By the COURT . The others held Ryan while the prisoner put his hand into his pockets—I did not see the prisoner hold a glass to Ryan's mouth. GEORGE BRENCHLEY( Police Sergeant R ). On 7th July, about 11.20, I met the prisoner and Thomas Edwards going in a hurried manner towards Greenwich; the prisoner had two hats on; they had something in their hands which they were sharing—I was palled to the Prince Albert, and saw Ryan without a hat, and in a stupid state, as if he had been drugged—William Edwards and Ogilvie were with him—Ogilvie and the two Edwardses were convicted at Woolwich Police-court about a week after—I took Ryan to the station and charged him with being drunk and in-capable—he was insensible up to 12 o'clock that night—he had no money on him—I took the other three men next night, but the prisoner ab sconded—the Edwardses got three months, and came out of prison at the time the prisoner was arrested.
