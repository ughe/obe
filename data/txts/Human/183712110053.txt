   Prisoner's Defence.  On the 12th, Wednesday, I was in company with Mary Clark, and it was raining—we went to a wine-vaults till it abated—an acquaintance of hers came into the house, and asked me to fetch him some tobacco, for which he gave me half a crown—I did not know it was bad, till the master cut it, and gave it me—I told him it was not for my self—I went hack to the man—he gave me another—I did not offer that till I had tried it with my key—T could not bend it with that, but I did, by fixing it in the iron railings—he told me to give them to him back, but before I could turn, he told me to run and throw them away—I had not long left him when a policeman came and tapped me on the shoulder, and took me to the station-house—he asked if I had been passing a bad half-crown—I said I had, and gave the two to him—I throw myself on the mercy of the Jury, as I never offered bad money in my life. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Six Months.
