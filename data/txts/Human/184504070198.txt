KENT CASES .
   Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
 985. DANIEL WASHBROOK and JAMES GURNEY were indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  ., the goods of Edward Evans , from his person. EDWARD EVANS. I live in Talbot-court, Gracechurch-street. On the 25th of March, about half-past three o'clock in the afternoon, I was at Greenwich, near the College-gate, looking at a man conjuring—I perceived nothing until the officer, who had both the prisoners in custody, showed me my handkerchief—this now produced is it—I know it was safe an hour before.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  There was a crowd round the conjuror? A.  Yes, perhaps 100, or 150 people. JOHN WHITE( police-constable R  108.) I was at Greenwich, and saw the prisoners by the College-gate—I followed them from the fair to the College—they were together—there was a crowd. I saw them go behind Evans, and saw Washbrook take the handkerchief from his pocket and hand it to Gurney—I secured them, and Glad win got the handkerchief. WILLIAM GLADWIN( policeman. ) I saw the prisoners by the prose cutor—White took Washbrook, I took Gurney, and took this handker chief out of his pocket.
   Gurney's Defence.  Washbrook told me he had picked up the handkerchief; he gave it to me and went away.
 (The prisoners received good characters.)
   WASHBROOK  — GUILTY. Aged 13.
   GURNEY  — GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined Two Months .
