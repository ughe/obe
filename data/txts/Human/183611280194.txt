I have got a good hand"—I said no, I would bet nothing, he said, "I shall bet for you"—I said, "Do as you please, but if you bet and win, or lose, I have nothing to do with it"—he threw down the shilling—we won the game, and, of course, the odd trick—he threw the shilling to me—I refused to take it, saying I had nothing to do with it—he attempted to put it into my waistcoat pocket—I put it on the shelf, and said, "There it is, I will have nothing to do with it"—I was then called down stairs—they rang the bell, and requested me to come up again—when I came up the glasses were charged—they said they were going to drink my health, and prosperity to me in my new house—I thought them very kind, and drank with them;
