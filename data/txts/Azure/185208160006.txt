COURT. Q. Was it bis duty to enter in the book what be paid?
Either myself or the clerk enter those amounts, but these sums are generally

called to pay money out of their own pockets.
Prisoner. Q. You say it was only usual to enter small sums in this

MR. CLARKSON. Q. Did he say on 29th June that he had lost the money!
A. No; he stated that he had left the money with Mr. Williams himself, and
this entry in the cash-book I made myself at the very period of bis making

me—he was employed in some Chancery suits, that required daily attention—
I knew that he lived in the neighbourhood of Goswell-street, but his direc$
