Cross-examined by Mr. Phillips. Q. Did you observe whether the
men in the cab were intoxicated ? A. I should say they were rather in
toxicated--they had been drinking.
Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Did the grey horse appear going
at speed at first ? A. It did, running as fast as the driver could make him
go-I saw the cabs for a distance of two hundred yards before it went over
the woman- I was within twenty yards of the woman when she was knocked
down.
Jury. Q. Was the driver of the cab drunk? A. He was perfectly
sober-I never saw him before-he was taken on the spot.
Court. Q. Could you form a judgment whether the cabs were both ar
either of them running away, or racing? A. The grey horse one was trying
to gain on the other, and when it knocked the woman down it had gained
ten yards on it--they were both driven by the drivers, urged on, I am der
tain-- I believe Nicholls was in the cab, but I will not swear to him.
