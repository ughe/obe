1189. JOHN REYNOLDS and ANN REYNOLDS were again indicted
for receiving, on 28th March, 200 squirrel tails, value 8s.; the goods of
Friedrich Freutel. MR. HORRY conducted the Prosecution.
BARTHOLOMEW SULLIVAN. I took some squirrel tails from my master's
stock twice. On 28th March, I took 200, and gave them to Welton at half-
past twelve o'clock the same day.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'BRIEN. Q. It was skins you sold to the pri-
soners? A. Yes ; I recollected at the station that it was a fortnight before
Good Friday, but I am not sure about the date-I say 28th, because I must
say some day- I took skins as well-I took some more a little while after
I took them from the workshop-Mendelson and some of the work-girls wera
there at the time-I met Welton in St. John's-square.
CORNELIUS WELTON. On 28th March I received 200 squirrel tails from
Sullivan, and took them to the prisoners- saw Mrs. Reynolds, and asked
her if she would buy them-Mr. Reynolds was in the back place, out of hear
ing-she paid me 53. for them, 2s.6d. a hundred-I had been in the babit
of going there for six months-on 30th March, I took another hundred tails,
and told her I got them from where I worked.
COURT. Q. How many dealings had you with Mrs. Reynolds ? A. I
have been there dozens of times, and I have told her in whose service I was.
FRIEDRICH FREUTEL. I went to the prisoners' house alone first, but
nothing passed about the tails--those tails are exactly like mine in colour, and
of the same quality--they are worth 4s. a hundred-some are worth a littlo
less—I lost some shortly before Easter.
Cross-examined by Me. PAYNE. Q. Are there hundreds of thousands of
these in the trade ? A. Yes, thousands alike--some of these are darker
than what I have lost-I bave had none like this smaller parcel.
