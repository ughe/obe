Cross-examined. Q. You did not find more than one wound? A. No
it was what I call a stab-if it was struck upwards or downwards,

inch and a half long there might have been two or four arteries in the neigh-
bourhood, and the knife running down might have wounded them.
Court. Q. Did you ever know a stab without cutting? A. There
must be a stab to be a cut--if I were to stab a man I should cut him if
the knife was not drawn out quite straight it would enlarge the wound.
JOSEPH KENDALL (policeman.) On the 19th of May I was on duty in
Gray's-in-lane--the prosecutor's sister-in-law came to me, and I went and
took the prisoner into custody, in Rose-alley, at his lodging, on Monday-1
took him to the station, searched him, and took this knife out of his right
band trowsers pocket- I told him the charge at his lodging-he said he
was there about six o'clock in the morning, but knew nothing of it-I have
produced the prosecutor's clothes--I took the coat and waistcoat myself
from his room-he was lying in the bed without his shirt-the surgeon
was dressing bis wound-the whole of the clothes were congealed with
blood.
(Caleb Robinson, of George-street, Lambeth, who had known the pri-
soner three years, deposed to his good character.)
Guilty.-Transported for Fifteen Years.
