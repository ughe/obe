2369. JOHN FRANCIS was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
August, 2 pairs of shoes, value 11s., the goods of Alexander Wilson and
others.
WILLIAM BOREHAM. I am shopman to Alexander Wilson and others,
shoemakers, in Crawford-street. On the 3rd of August, I lost these shoes
-in consequence of information, I went to Bryanston-square, and saw the
prisoner running with the shoes before him.
HENRY MITCHELL. I was in the balcony of the shop-I saw the pri-
soner and two others-one of them took the shoes while the prisoner was
on the pavement close by-he took them from him.
Prisoner. It is my first offence.
GUILTY. Aged 25.-Confined Three Months.
2370. THOMAS M'DANIELS was indicted for stealing, on the
2nd August, 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of Ricbard Shearing
from his person.
RICHARD SHEARING. I am a plumber, and work with my father, in
Rupert-street. About one o'clock in the morning of the 2nd of August
I was in Drury-lane-I felt some one at my pocket, I turned, and saw the
prisoner with this handkerchief in his hand-he threw it on the ground.
GEORGE GAITER (police-constable L 23.) I saw the handkerchief at
the prisoner's feet--the prosecutor charged him with it-he said, "I did
not do it"-he then said, "If I knew you were going to charge me with
this, I would bave done something else."
Prisoner's Defence. I never had it; it was two or three yards from
me.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
