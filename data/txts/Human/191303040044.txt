BEFORE JUDGE LUMLEY SMITH .
 (Thursday, March 6.) FRANKLIN  , John (41, nurse), and EASTER  , Harry (24, valet), pleaded guilty of committing an act of gross indecency the one with the other.
 Mr. Percival Clarke prosecuted; Mr. Curtis Bennett and Mr. Huntly Jenkins appeared for prisoners. Prisoners were released on their own recognisances each in £5 (Easter with one surety in £5) to come up for judgment if called upon.
