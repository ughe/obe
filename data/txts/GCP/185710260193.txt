Friday, 22nd Sept, between 7 and 8 o'clock in the morning Seers came for
8 half pint of gin; it came to 8d.; he gave me a sovereign, which I put into

him change, and he took the gin away in a bottle--Moore was present;

gave me a good sixpence he was in the habit of using the house on the
day before I had seen him with Welch, they went into the tap room toge-
ther--they used the house to breakfast, and sometimes they came in the after-
noon too on the morning of the 22nd, between 9 and 10 o'clock, Mrs.

an omnibus ; I am quite sure he paid for that gin with a sovereign, and not
with a half crown.
