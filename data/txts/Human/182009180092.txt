 1134.  THOMAS SHAW was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of August , one watch, value 4 l.; one ribbon, value 6 d.; two rings, value 6 d., and one key, value 6 d., the goods of Henry Cotton , from his person.
   HENRY COTTON. I am a cabinet-maker, and live in St. Martin's-street. About five o'clock in the afternoon I was at the end of Waterloo-place, the Queen was passing. My hands were pinioned down by a gang, who hustled me against the carriage, and I felt my watch go from my fob, About a quarter past six o'clock, I saw them taking a thief to the watch-house. I went in, and enquired for my property, I described it, it was produced to me, and I found the prisoner in custody. I remembered seeing the prisoner near me.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   GEORGE DORRELL. I am a brush-maker, and live in Broad-street, Manchester-square. On the 24th of August, I was in St. James's-square, I saw two suspicious characters leaving the square, and at the bottom of Whitaker-street, the prisoner joined them, and shewed them something. I followed them, met three constables, and pointed them out. The prisoner saw me, immediately ran off, and I followed him into St. Martin's-lane, pushed him into a coffee-shop, and asked what he had in his pocket, as he put his hand in. He said
 "Come none of your tricks. I have been robbed of 5 s. 6 d." He had got a watch half out of his pocket, I took it quite out, he said it was his own. I took him to the watch-house. The prosecutor claimed it in the evening.
 Prisoner's Defence. The watch was thrown down, a man scrambled with me for it. I intended to give it up.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Life.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before J. Vaillant, Esq.
 1135.  BENJAMIN MARSHALL and THOMAS CAMPBELL were indicted for stealing, on the 12th of July , one handkerchief, value 2 s., the goods of George Wentworth , from his person.
   GEORGE WENTWORTH. I live in Princes-street. I was looking at a print-shop in St. James's-street, Yates asked me if I had lost my handkerchief. I missed it, and he produced it. He had Marshall in custody.
   HENRY YATES. I am a constable. I had been watching the prisoners for three-quarters of an hour, I saw them go up to Wentworth. Marshall took his handkerchief out, he went round Rider-street. I secured him, and took it out of his hand. About an hour after I took Campbell in St. James's-street.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 MARSHALL'S Defence. He brought the handkerchief up, and said I did it.
 MARSHALL -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined Six Months, and Whipped.
 CAMPBELL -    NOT GUILTY.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1136.  CATHERINE MOORE was indicted for stealing, on the 22d of July , one watch, value 4 l.; one umbrella, value 2 s., and 10 s., in monies numbered, the property of Hendry Reusch , from his person.
 HENDRY REUSCH. I live in Mercer-street, Long-acre. On the 22d July, about half-past eleven o'clock at night, I went to wash my eye at the pump in Queen-street. I met the prisoner, and treated her with a glass of gin, as she asked me. I went home with her to  Charles-street, she left me in about three hours, we were in the dark. I found my property gone. I informed the watchman next day, and he took her.
   PATRICK CRAWLEY. I am a watchman. I saw Mr. Reusch in the passage of a house in Charles-street. He said he had been robbed, and described the girl; he was sober. I found his umbrella in a room on the first-floor, about four o'clock next morning with the prisoner. She lived on the first floor.
   WILLIAM READY. I am a watchman. I apprehended the prisoner on the second pair front room. The umbrella was not there.
   JOSEPH TRIMBY. I found the umbrella in the room; the prisoner was in bed.
 (Umbrella produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. He gave me the umbrella.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined One Year.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1137.  JOHN HALL was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of September , one watch, value 2 l., and one seal, value 2 s., the goods of Hezekiah Shepherd , from his person.
 HEZEKIAH SHEPHERD. I am an  assistant at a school. On the 13th of September, about ten o'clock in the morning, I was in the Commercial-road. A procession was going to her Majesty. I felt my watch drawn from my pocket, and saw the prisoner running two or three yards from me. I followed, called Stop thief! he ran about twenty yards off, without my losing sight of him, - he struggled to get away. The watch was dropped close by where I stopped him.
   HENRY WHITE. I am a ribbon-weaver, and live in Quaker-street. I was in the Commercial-road, and picked the watch up in the kennel, just by the prisoner, and delivered it to the prosecutor.
   JOHN STARKEY. I am an officer. The prosecutor gave the prisoner in charge for stealing his watch, he begged his pardon, and said,
 "You have got your watch, let me go."
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
