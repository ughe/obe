WILLIAM SALES. I am a labourer, and live in China-row, Stratford. On
Monday night, 12th Nov., I was at home I went to bed about 12 o'clock
I took my boots off abont 11 o'clook and placed them close by the side of
the fender in the back room—when I got up about 6 o'clock the next morn-
ing, the boots were not there I went into the back kitchen, the door was
standing wide open- a bird cage had been taken from the back room and
hung up behind one of the doors, and the bird had been taken out and killed
-I saw my boots at the station on Tuesday morning it was the pair that
I had lost.
