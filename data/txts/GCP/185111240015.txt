JESSE LAWRENCE (policeman, N 998). On 10th Nov. I apprehended the
prisoner in the street, at Edmonton-I told him it was on several charges of

the prisoner said he was very sorry be had done it, and if she would take him

a paltry sum, only 11s. 11d.

7. JOHN TEBBUTT, stealing 1 mare, 1 saddle, and 1 bridle, value
61. 108.; the property of Joseph Nutt.
Mr. RYLAND conducted the Prosecution.

I saw the prisoner in London-wall, on a grey mare, galloping as fast as it
could go-I saw him attempt to turn the corner of Coleman-street- the horse
fell down, and the prisoner endeavoured to run away--in a few seconds there

tleman, and went and stopped the prisoner-several persons came up and
said in my presence that he had stolen the horse-the prisoner said, " It is &
bad job; I got on the horse, and he ran away with me."
