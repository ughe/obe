FREDERICK GEORGE Woods. I am in the silk handkerchief department
at Puwson's—I was present on 6th September, when Davidson seized the
goods at Lawton's shop-I saw the handkerchiefs there, and identified
some of them as having been taken from Messrs. Pawson's--there are
14 dozen-I have a doubt about the others, but we have the same
pattern, and the same handkerchief in stock; the others I am quite
certain about-the average selling price of them is 258. a dozen-I have
also seen the handkerchiefs found at Wioks's; we have the same in
stook now.
