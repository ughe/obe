ANN DELL. I am servant to Mr. John Jones, who lives at Herne-hill.
On the 5th of October I heard a noise in the pantry, about half-past two
o'clock-I went and saw the prisoner standing with his back towards me
in the pantry, close by the window, which was a little way open-the
window comes down very near to the ground-he got out on his knees-I
pushed the window up, and jumped out after him and followed him-there
was a screw broken in the window.
Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Is your master here A. No
-his daughter lives in the same house, and four servants-nothing was
taken from the house-the prisoner had combs in his pocket for sale-I
have heard my master say the house is in the parish of Lambeth.
WILLIAM Jesse (police-constable P 135.) I took the prisoner in an en-
closed shrubbery, at Mr. Simpson's, which is eight hundred yards from Mr.
Jones's-be had no business there-he got over a six-feet fence-I exam-
ined the pantry-window-it had been forced open, and the screw was
broken-the house is in the parish of St. Mary, Lambeth.
Cross-examined. Q. Is there any such parish as Lambeth? A. Till
got to the office I did not know it was called St. Mary, Lambeth-I always
heard it ealled Lambeth.
WILLIaM HEMP. The house is in the parish of St. Mary, Lambeth-I

2794. WILLIAM TATHAM was indicted for the wilful murder of
Edward Hills, on the high seas, within the jurisdiction of the Admiralty.
Mr. ADOLPHUS conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM JoNes. I was steward of the Dido, which was at sea in Janu-
ary last, lying in Benin-roads, about fifteen miles from land, on the west
coast of Africa-Philip Carpenter was the master-the prisoner was chief
mate-Edward Hills was second mate-on the 12th of January Mr. Carpen-
ter went up the Benin river, leaving the prisoner in command of the ship-
Hills was also left-in the evening the prisoner and Hills were both very
much in liquor, and were quarrelling in the cabin-they appeared to be
reconciled, and very good friends before they went to bed-on the morning

Morgans, the surgeon, and Hills, the second-mate, were there-Hills came
