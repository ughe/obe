NEW COURT.-Friday, October 31st, 1873.
Before Mr. Recorder.
676. JAMES GREEN (50), and ALBERT MARTIN (34), were in-
dicted for a burglary in the dwelling-house of William Henry Beard, and
stealing 170 yards of oloth and other articles, his property.
Mr. PLATT conducted the Prosecution; and Mr. GRIFFITHs defended Green,
WILLIAM HBNRY BEARD. I am a tailor, and live at 103, High Street,
Kingsland-o Friday, the 19th instant, about 10.40, I shut up the house
before going to bed--the cloth and other articles were all safe then-I was
aroused about 4.45 in the morning, and on going down stairs I found the
house had been broken into, and I missed 170 yards of oloth and other
articles, to the value of nearly 1700.-there had been a ladder in the next
garden the night before, and in the morning that ladder was in the area,
pushed up against the back-parlour window that window was shut the
night before, but I don't know whether it was fastened-] found it closed
in the morning, but that was the way the thieves had got in-the parlour
was all in confusion-I sent for the police-these two pieces of cloth pro-
duced are mine, I can speak positively to them, and they were safe over.
night-two waisteoats, a writing-desk, a jacket, and other things were found
in my garden-this pair of trousers produced by Briden are mine-1I had
worn them two or three times, the buttons have my name upon them--this
waistooat had only been brought home on the Friday, and had not been
sent home the binding has been chipped and a few other things done to it
to make it appear old, but I am quite sure of it.
Cross-examined. saw some cloth in a bag at the station, about 4 yards
in one piece, and 1 yards in the otherI sell such oloths in made-up
clothes, and I sell a length if required-I identify both these pieces by
certain peouliarities in the outting--no doubt the manufacturers sell such
cloth to other persons--this piece is the remains of some from which I
made a suit for a gentleman's son; I can distinotly swear to it; I bought
it at Shoolbred's
WILLIAM CHaPMAN (Detective Oficer K). About 9.30 on Saturday 27th I

told him that I had received information that he had been buying stolen

1ook over my place if you "I searched the place-I found this piece

Cross-ecamined. The only cloth I found at Green's, was in the green-

years-I was going to mention that I wished to add something when my
deposition was read over, but I was told to stand down, that it was not
material.
