   Prisoner's Defence.  I say there was no such letter written; there was one written addressed to a female in Lamb's Conduit-street, to whom he was going to be married in Feb.; I left the house with him, and went to a public-house at the corner; I had half-a-pint of beer with him, and then left him; I came next day as usual, and shortly afterwards received a letter from my friends, to say that they had a situation for me; I applied for the situation and got it, which caused my absence from the place—I came back, having left the situation, and was taken into custody, charged with stealing the note; I know nothing at all about it; I have known the man two or three years; I have no friends in attendance; I could prove that that woman has been receiving trifles from inspector Campbell, and sergeant Wright, to come up against me. SAMPSON DARKIN CAMPBELL re-examined.
