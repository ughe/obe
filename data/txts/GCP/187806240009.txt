Re-examined. Mr. Gudgeon, of Storr Market, acting on behalf of the
creditors, told me on the morning of the sale not to part with the money,
and I gave it up to Mr. Humphreys.
WALTER BAKER, I am a machinist-on 31st August I threshed 77
cooms of wheat for Steele - I take the entries from my men-I never got
paid-the account is in my writing-I took it from the men who threshed.
JOHN DURHAM. I am in Mr. Baker's employ-on 31st August I
threshed 77.cooms of wheat on Steele's farm, and on 12th October 12
coons of peas, 18 of beans, and 57 of barley, and on 14th October 77
cooms of barley again.
EDWARD Fry. I am a corn and seed merchant at Ipswich-on 4th
September I bought 871. worth of wheat of Frederick Steele in Ipswich
corn market, and on 11th September Mrs. Steele called on me and brought
the receiving note, saying that the railway company had received 30
quarters of wheat; it was endorsed " 60 сooms of wheat, 871. Dear Sir,
Will you pay Mrs. Steele, my wife, the amount, as I am busy with
the barvest and cannot leave home?"-I gave her a cheque for 801.-I
declined to pay the whole, as I had not received the wheat--the cheque is
endorsed Elizabeth Steele," and has been paid by my bankers-61. 19..
was still due, which was paid next day to Mrs. Steele, as the wheat had
been received—the cheque is endorsed Elizabeth Steele."
JAMES GARNHAM. I am clerk to Messrs. Paul, corn merchants, of Ipswich
-on 9th Oetober I bought 110 cooms of barley of Frederick Steele, at 11.
& coom, and on 16th October 1 paid him 1121. by cheque, as 112 cooms
were delivered the cheque has been paid.
WILLIAM Moss BUCK. I am manager to Mr. Thomas Mortimer,
corn merchant of Ipswich I remember Frederick Steele selling them on
18th September 140 cooms of wheat, value 1471. 88.-it was delivered as
133 cooms-I gave his firm a cheque for 1001., and subsequently one for
471. 88.--those cheques have been sent in endorsed "Frederick Steele "
-on 9th October I bought some wheat of him for 631. 158., which I paid
for by cheque, which is endorsed "Frederick Steele."
ARTHUR CHARLES SEMPLE. I am one of the cashiers of the London
and County Bank, Knightsbridge branch-I produce the books-on 24th
October, 1877, Frederick Steele paid in 1921.--there were eight 101. notes
and 1121. in gold-on 29th October he paid in 6501. -he opened an
account, and on 39th October this cheque out of the book we gave him
was presented. This was for 8421. in favour of Mrs. Steele, dated 30th
October, and signed Frederick Steele)—that was paid with two 3001. notes,
90237 and 92581, both dated 14th February, 1877; a 2001. note, 22785,
dated 14th May, 1877; two 201.notes, 60516 and 60517, dated 12th May, 1877,
and 21. in gold-on 31st October, Messrs. Harvey and Nichols, customers
of ours, cashed a 3001. Bank of England note, No. 92581, dated 14th
February, 1877--we gave them three 100l. notes, Nos. 83227, 83228, and
83229, all dated 13th April, 1877.
Cross-examined by MR. KELLY. I did not pay this choque–I do not
know who it was paid to this scratch is the cashier's initial-it is payable
to bearer, and would be paid to anybody.
