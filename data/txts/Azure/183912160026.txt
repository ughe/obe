JOHN SAYER (police-constable N 34.) On Wednesday, at twenty
minutes past nine o'clock, I was going down Hoxton, and saw the prisoner
with a piece of lead, about five feet long, rolled on his shoulder—knowing
he was a painter and glazier, I did not think it was wrong.
JOSEPH LIDDLE (police-constable N 270.) At twenty minutes past nine

shoulder—he was tipsy.

JOHN ROBERTS (police-constable T 91.) About twenty minutes before
