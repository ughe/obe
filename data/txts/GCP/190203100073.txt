264. WILLIAM BISCOE (19), and JANE ALLEN, breaking
and entering the dwelling house of Thomas Henry Evered, and stealing two
suits of clothes, his property.
MR. METCALFE Prosecuted.
Thomas Henry EvERED. I am a coffee-house keeper, of 4, High Street,
Stratford—on Sunday, January 5th, I left the premises at 10 a.m. securely
fastened-nobody was left there--I returned about 10 p.m.-I live there
also I found the drawers had been opened and the contents scattered
all over the floor-a desk had been opened and the things thrown about,
and two suits of clothes were taken out of a chest of drawers-the entry
had been made through a back window, where there is a yard with a wall
--I communicated with the police-I have identified my clothes since
I value them at about £2 each suit.
JOHN BARBER (649 K.) On February 21st, about 2 p.m., I went to
227, Bow Road, which is a coffee-house, where I saw Allen-she is
employed there as a servant-I said to her, “It has come to my know-
ledge that you have in your possession a number of pawn tickets relating
to some stolen property, what have you to say about it?"-she said,
"I have no tickets, I know nothing about any"-I said, "I have positive
knowledge that you have some tickets"she then said, "Well, I will tell
you the truth, if you will come to our house I will give them to you"_I
accompanied her to 3, Water Court, close by-I said the tickets related
to some stolen propeaty—I did not say what it was she gave me two
tickets, one numbered 1038, on T. H. Downton, 190, Bow Road, for &
suit of clothes, pawned on January 6th, 1902, for 10s, in the name of
Phillips ; the other, No. 2552, pawned with the same pawnbroker on
December 10th for 38.--that one has nothing to do with the caseshe
took them from behind a picture which was hanging on the kitchen wall
she is 18 years of age when she gave me the tickets she said, “These are
all there are now, I gave the others to a chap named George, about 10 of
them, you know, Bił, who you saw me with that night”-she referred
to a night some six weeks previously when I had seen her with Biscoe
“He got me to pawn the stuff and asked me to mind the tickets for him
I wish I had never seen him now”-I took her to the police station-
on the way I saw Police Constable Carpenter with the other prisoner-
the female prisoner said "O God, here is Bill, they have soon got him”
--in answer to the charge she commenced to make a statement- she said,
- "I," and then left off.
