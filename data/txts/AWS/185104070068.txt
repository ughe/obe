JOsEPH WAUGH (City-policeman, 628). On Sunday, 23rd March, I
took the prisoners in Bell-lane, Spitalfields-I told them they were wanted
at the station for a robbery I did not know the particulars-at the
station the sergeant told Ellen she was charged with stealing twenty-four
pairs of trowsers-she said she was guilty-she was the worse for drink-
the man was sober.

-in consequence of what she said, I went to a beer-shop in Widegate
