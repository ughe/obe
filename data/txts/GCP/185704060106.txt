522. JAMES GREEN was indicted for bigamy.
MR. HAUTROKNE conducted the Prosecution.
MARY ANN CARTER. I am a widow, and live at Tottenham, and an
sextoness of St. James the Great, Bethnal Green. I saw the prisoner tar
ried to Emma Mitchell, last Easter Monday, 24th March, by the Rer.
Edward Cope-I signed, as they brought no witnesses with them.
GEORGE ŚMITH (Policeman, N 99). I produce a certificate of marriage,
which I got from Emma Mitchell I have compared it with the registeret
St. James the Great ; it is an exact copy-I have seen Emma Mitchell here
-I did not know her before I produce another certificate, which I have
compared with the register of St. Leonard, Shoreditch ; it is a true copy
I showed it to the Curate.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILIPPS. Q. How did you compare them 4.
I had the book and the copy in my hand, and first read one and then the
other, and the Curate and the Incumbent read them as well.
HENRY GARDINER. I am a sweep, and live at Norfolk Court, New Nor
folk Street, Islington. I know Emma Green, her maiden name wa
Mitchell—that is her; she lived with me, as my wife, for nine years, pre
vious to 24th March, and left me to go and marry Green--my wife has
been ten years away from me I have got the prisoner's wife back again,
because he ran away from her she was away from me from Easter Satur-
day till the following Thursday; and I took the second wife that lived with
bim, when he took mine away-directly he found that I took her home, hie
found another woman, and took her up to his place, and deserted her
immediately.
COURT. Q. When he took your's, did you take his! A. Yes, the day
after they were married I took her bome to my place --We were all intoxi-
cated; and as soon as he got her back he deserted her, and married the
other woman within a month.-(Certificates read: “St. James the Great
Bethnal Green ; James Green and Emma Mitchell, married by bands, 24th
Maroh, 1856."-4" St. Leonard, Shoreditch ; James Green and Margaret
Latlieff, married, 21st April, 1856.")
MARGARET LATLIEFF. I was married to the prisoner, by banns, on 21st
April last; but previous to our marriage we lived together for eight years
- he has always proved a very good husband to me, but this bad base
woman has got him away, and taken two rings off her finger to pay for the
marriage, to get him away from me- this man, Gardiner, has become a tet-
totaler on purpose to save up his money to punish him.
COURT. Q. Is he & teetotaler, out of spite, to get money to carry on the
prosecution ? A. Yes—his poor wife has become on the streets through
him, from his turning her out of doors.
GUILTY. Aged 32.-
