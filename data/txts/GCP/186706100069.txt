WILLIAM RADCLIPF. I am assistant to Mr. Harris, a pawnbroker, of
41, Aldersgate Street—this ticket (produced) is in my writing-it is in a
man's name-I cannot call the prisoner to mind-I took in the silk, and
advanced 81. on it.
COURT. Q. Are you in the habit of taking in unmanufactured silk,
without knowing the person who pledges it, and without asking questions?
A. I ask questions if I have any doubt in the matter, and I have been
able to detect thieves when they do not answer satisfactorily.
Prisoner's Defence. All these tickets came into my hands either by
being given to me by other parties, or else I have bought them. I have
never been in the habit of pawning silk.
GUILTY.-Twelve Months' Imprisonment.
585. WALTER GARRICK (16), Stealing 181 newspapers of William
Blankby Bull and another, his masters.
MR. MACCAFIE conducted the Prosecution, and MR. WILLIAMS the Defence.
WILLIAM BLANKBY BULL. I am one of the firm of Bull, Myddleton,
and Co., newspaper agents—the prisoner was in our service-it was his
duty to deliver certain newspapers in the City and West End-he got a
quantity of newspapers in the morning from Paddington Station and
Euston Square-he brought a certain quantity of them to Essex Street-
I had marked off the names for several days in May, of persons to whom
newspapers ought to be delivered—these papers (produced) are directed in
my writing—they are dated 18th May-these went into the prisoner's
possession from my office-it was his duty to deliver them to the persons
whose names are on them.
