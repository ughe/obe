Q. If you find, after death, the body entirely deprived of blood, can you
form an opinion whether a large quantity of blood must have flown before
life was extinct? A. I should think it very probable and possible, but
can scarcely say for certain-the time which blood begins to stagnate after
death, varies much, but in a very few minutes-in half-an-hour or an hour
after death the body could not be emptied of blood, but in some rare cases
the blood does not coagulate-in death from apoplexy, disease of the heart,
nor any thing else, should we find the body so empty of blood.
Cross-examined. Q. You concur with the medical gentlemen, that the
draining of blood was caused by an incision of some large vessel? 4.
Yes-there is sometimes a considerable flow of blood from the external

Mr. ATTORNEY-GENERAL Q. Could that occur without previous dis
