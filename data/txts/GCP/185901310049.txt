261. GEORGE HAYLER (22), CHARLES HORTON (22), and
THOMAS FULLER (23), Stealing a Bill of Exchange for payment of 201.,
2 pieces of paper, and 1 post letter, of Jane Coutts. Second Count of George
Allen. FÜLLER-PLEADED GUILTY.-Confined. Three Months.
MESSRS. GIFFARD and ORRIDGE conducted the prosecution. lado
ANN DYE. I lived in Addington-place, Edinburgh, in Decemberin
consequence of a letter I received I applied to the Royal Bank of Scotland,
and got an order for 201. -I enclosed it in this letter, and put both in an
envelope-I directed it to Quarter-master Walter Allen, of the 1st battalion
of the fusilier guards--I sent the letter.
CHARLES TURNER. I am cashier at Coutts' bank-on 24th December the
prisoners Hayler and Horton came there-Hayler handed me this order for
201., payable to Walter Allen-I observed that it was not endorsed, and I
asked Hayler if it was payable to bim-he said “Yes " Horton was
standing within about two yards, I have no doubt he must have heard it-
I gave the cheque back to Hayler, and said, "Write your name at the back
of it"-he wrote "W. Allen" on the back-I then paid him the amount in
two 51. notes, and the rest in gold.

Horton. Q. Was that the statement you made at the police-court A.

amended it afterwards.
THOMAS ANSCOMBE (Policeman, F 102). On the day before Christmas-
day I saw the prisoners Hayler and Horton come to Coutts' bank-I opened
the door to them-Horton said, "Is this Coutts' bank 1"-I said, "Yes, it
is," and he and Hayler passed in.
