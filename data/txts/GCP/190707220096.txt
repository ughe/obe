Rodini's story about my telling him that there were bronzes in this

gladiator, that on October 10 I was in possession of the two swords
and four of the bronzes, and that on the evening of Ootober 16 I was
selling the rest of the things to Mr. Letts. Those are unquestion
able facts. As to whether I received in all £72 for these things, I
have not added it up. I never told Rodini that I had melted a gold
