Yes, the prisoner was his labourer—Jones produced some lead from his pocket—I sent for a constable, but none came for some time, and I let them both go—we have not seen Jones since—the prisoner called next morning for his wages—he said what he had done was by Jones's direction—the bag and tools were Jones's, not the prisoner's. BONFIELD BRYANT.
