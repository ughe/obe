THIRD COURT.-Saturday, August 25th, 1849.
PRESENT-Sir John Pirie, Bart., Ald.; Mr. Ald. Hooper, and EDWARD
BULLOCK, Esq.
Before Edward Bullock, Esq. and the First Jury.
1616. JOHN CLARKE, stealing a watch, seal, and key, value 308.; the
goods of William Roach, from his person; having been before convicted to
which he pleaded Guilty.* Aged 24.-Transported for Seven Years.
1617. GEORGE EDWARD LAURECE, stealing two China ornaments,
value, 51.; the goods of John Squire, and others; having been twice before
convicted: to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 63. – Recommended to mercy.-Confined Four Months.
1618. GEORGE COLLINS and JOHN SMITH, stealing 1 purse, value
22., 2 shillings, 2 sixpences, 1 4dy.-piece, 8 pence, and 6 halfpence; the
property of William Robinson, from the person of Elizabeth Robinson ;
Collins having been before convicted: to which
COLLINS pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.-Transported for Seven Years.
SMITH pleaded Guilty. Aged 19.-Confined Six Months.
1619. JANE KENT, stealing a comb and brush, value 6s. 6d.; the goods
of Elizabeth Mary Stacey, her mistress : to which she pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 26.-Judgment respited.
1620. WILLIAM CHAMBERS, stealing 1 musket, value 21. 108.; the
goods of Isabella Ashton: also 7 steel grinders and 3 counter-sinks, value
22s.; the goods of Robert Walker: to both which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 19.-Judgment respited.
1621. JOHN MAJOR HALLETT, feloniously marrying Sarah Turner,
his wife Betsey being alive: to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 33.-Transported for Seven Years.
1622. WILLIAM HENRY JOHN WADE, stealing 22 grains weight of
gold-leaf, value 38. 8d.; the goods of James Barnes, his master : to which he
pleaded
GUILTY.-Confined Six Months.
1623. JOSEPH WARD and MARY MYERS, feloniously forging and
uttering a receipt for 251., with intent to defraud Charles William Hallett and
others.
MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
