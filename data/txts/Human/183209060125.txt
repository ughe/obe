2019. WILLIAM SMITH and JOSEPH PLUMMER were indicted for stealing, on the 30th of July , 57 live tame ducks, price 4l., and 44 live tame fowls, price 3l., the property of William Murphy.
 WILLIAM MURPHY. I  deal in poultry, and live in Caroline-place, Saffron-hill; I have a yard and stable fenced in, a few yards from my place - it has a wooden fence eight or nine feet high. On the 30th of July I had fifty-seven ducks and forty-four fowls there, in baskets, and between three and four o'clock in the morning they were all gone - it was all my stock except eleven geese; I gave notice at Newgate-market, and between eleven and twelve I was sent for, and saw some of them in possession of Bates.
   JOSEPH BATES. I am a patrol of the ward of Farringdon Within. I saw the prisoners selling chickens about the market for half their value; they sold six to a lad, and told him they had more at home - there was another person with them.
   JAMES COOPER. I go to the market for my father, and on that morning I saw the two prisoners and another person with them - they had eight fowls; they sold two to a woman for 1s. 3d., and then I bought the six at 8d. a piece; they said if I went to No. 8, Paradise-place, they would sell me thirteen more - I went there by order of the officer, and paid 4s. for six more, the officer took the prisoners.
   JOSEPH SWAINSON. Bates told me he knew where to find the prisoners; I went with him, and we sent Cooper in to bargain for the fowls, but he was not to pay for them till he brought the prisoners to a public-house where I was waiting - I then took them.
   WILLIAM MURPHY. I saw the fowls - they were mine; I got nineteen back; it has been the ruin of me and my family.
 The prisoners put in a written Defence, stating that they were employed by the other person to assist in selling the poultry, which they did not know was stolen.
 SMITH -    GUILTY. Aged 18.
 PLUMMER -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 2012.  THOMAS BENNETT was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of September , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of Robert Cuff , from his person.
   ROBERT CUFF. I live in Pall Mall East, and am a saddler. I was in Piccadilly on the 3rd of September, about twelve o'clock - I did not feel any thing, but received information; I turned, and saw the prisoner about two yards from me - I missed my handkerchief, and the officer produced it immediately.
   GEORGE STAPLETON. I am a public informer. On Monday, the 3rd of September, I was passing Half Moon-street, in a gig, and saw a female in a fit; I saw the prosecutor go towards a crowd of about twelve people - the prisoner went up to him, and appeared to hustle him; I saw the prisoner take the handkerchief out of the prosecutor's pocket - I took and held him by the collar till the Policeman came and took him; he took it from his near side pocket.
   ROBERT CULLY(Police-constable C 95). I took the prisoner, and took the handkerchief, which the prisoner had dropped down; I had been there assisting the person in the fit - I took him to the station, and found a crape shawl on him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was returning home about twelve o'clock on Monday night - I saw a crowd: I stood a few minutes, and the witness laid hold of me, and said I had taken the handkerchief - I denied it: another person said,"Here it is," and he said, "I suppose he has dropped it."
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 2021.  GEORGE CLAYTON was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of August , 1 pair of shoes, value 3s., the goods of Daniel Bamfield.
   WILLIAM HARRIS. I am in the employ of Daniel Bamfield, a bootmaker. On the 20th of August the prisoner was with me the whole day, employed as errand-boy- he had been employed four days; I missed him out of the shop for some time - I went out to inquire of a man selling fruit, and in consequence of what he said, I went and looked at the stock, and missed four pairs of boys' shoes; I went after him, and found him coming home - I taxed him with it, and he denied it; I called in a Policeman who found 1s. 6d. on him, and then he owned he had pawned them.
   WILLIAM BROWN. I am a pawnbroker. I have a pair of shoes, which were pawned by the prisoner.
 Prisoner's Defence. He was asking me all day long to buy some beer, and then I took the shoes.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Whipped and Discharged.
 2062.  WILLIAM DARBY was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of July , 1 hat, value 3s., the goods of Edward Morris , from his person.
   EDWARD MORRIS. I am a cooper. On the 10th of July I was passing through Bloomsbury-square, about three o'clock in the afternoon; I had walked a great way, and being hot, I sat down at a door, and fell asleep - I was not drunk; I had my hat on when I fell asleep - a gentleman touched me on the shoulder, and said, "Where is your hat?" I felt my head, and said, "I have lost it;" he took me to the station, and there I found it.
   THOMAS BATUTE. I am a gas-lamp lighter; I was opposite where the prosecutor was sitting - I saw the prisoner take the hat off his head, and walk to the corner of King-street, where he offered it to a Jew for sale - I followed him some distance, and when I saw an officer I gave him into custody.
   JAMES HAMILTON. I am an officer. I took the prisoner in the Bull and Gate-yard; he said this hat was his own.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 57. - Confined Three Months.
