   Prisoner.  I did not steal the pin, or I should not have pawned it in my own name; I found it; I met him in Westminster-road; he said he had been out all night, and asked if he could go home with me; I took him in, and said it was not my own place; we went into the bedroom, and he took off his coat and hat, and laid down, and the landlady came to the door, knocking, for the money. Witness.  It was not so; I had changed my dress, and left my purse in my other trowsers; there was nothing said about money—I had been out at a party—that might have been part of the conversation—I had not been in bed—I know I had the pin in my cravat the same morning, and I was in no company from the time I left the party till I got into the prisoner's house. THOMAS GRAMMAR. The prisoner asked me to purchase this pin. WILLIAM BROAD( policeman, L  82). I took the prisoner four days after wards—she said she had never seen the prosecutor, and only came out of Kingston that day. NOT GUILTY.
 175. MARIAN ALDRED , stealing 2 5 l  . Bank-notes; the property of Alfred Cooper , in the dwelling-house of Charles Cooper . ALFRED COOPER. I live at 7, Oxford-terrace, Clapham-road. The pri soner was living servant with my brother there—on 13th Oct. I lost two 5 l .-notes; they were in my left-hand waistcoat pocket when I took the waistcoat off—I spoke to the prisoner about them—she said she had not seen them, that I must have lost them out of doors—I afterwards looked into her work-box, and found two duplicates—I went to the pawnbroker's on 7th Nov.—I traced one of the notes from the Bank, and they paid it to Masterman's. HENRY WINCH. I live with Mr. Brown, a pawnbroker. I produce this 5 l .-note, which I took of the prisoner, who bought a gold chain, on 5th Nov.—the note was quite new and clean—she gave her address, "Dyer, 10, Rod ney-row, Kent-road"—this is the chain she bought ( produced ). ALFRED COOPER re-examined.  The 13th Oct. was a windy, rainy night—the notes were quite clean—it was not possible they could have got out while I was outside—I did not open my coat till I got in—if they had got out they would have been dirty—the roads were very muddy—this note is quite clean. CHARLES BURGESS GOFF( police-sergeant, L  8.) I took the prisoner.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I found the note near the gate as I was returning from an errand. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Four Months .
