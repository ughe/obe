and was winding up the affairs of Messrs. Gray Bainbridge and Co. in
June last-the account produced was then owing to us (for 291. 148. 40.)—
it was sent in in March–I did not ask the prisoner for an open cheque-the
firm had no banking account-most of our accounts are paid by crossed
cheques-I never received this cheque for 291. 148, 4d.-our account has
not been paid-I applied again for it in November—the initials" J.F. Y."
on this receipt are not minemine are "J. T. Y."-I did not authorise
any one to write that.
Cross-examined. I have known the prisoner about a twelvemonth-I do
not know anything against his character-I asked him whether his firm
would take up the patent of a printing-machine- I did not know that
the prisoner was the proprietor of several inventions.
