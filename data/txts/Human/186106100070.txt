THOMAS HUBBLE( City Policeman , 94). On Saturday night, 11th May, the prisoner was given into my custody by Mr. Weston—I found him in Meeting court, Miles'-lane, behind a sort of carpenter's bench—there is no thorough fare through the court—Mr. Weston gave met his half-crown ( produced )—I searched the prisoner, and found on him two shillings, a fourpenny-piece, and a halfpenny. WILLIAM WEBSTER. This is a had half-crown.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You have not cut it? A.  No; there is no occasion—I can feel it is bad—it is not what I call a fair counterfeit coin; it has not got the rim of avery good one—I do not think it would pass among labourers, if they were to be paid with it by their masters.
   The prisoner's statement before the Magistrate was here read as follows :—"I was persuaded to go into a public-house to pass it, by a tall chap."
   The prisoner's mother gave him a good character . GUILTY  .— Strongly recommended to mercy by the Jury. — Confined Six Months .
