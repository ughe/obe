two females and another man-he said, "Who was the other man?"-I
said, “Dippenham”-there is such a man, I know him well-he said,
"Dippenham was not there at all, the women were two lodgers of mine,
one has only been there a few days "-I took him to a cab, and in the

and I took him-he said, "I was there, but I had no hand in the matter,
I came here as a witness for Tucker."
