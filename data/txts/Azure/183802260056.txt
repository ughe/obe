value 158. ; and 1 table-cloth, value 158. ; the goods of Ellen Hill.
ELLEN HILL. I live at Tottenham. I hung some things out to dry,
on the 6th of February, at the back of the house—among other things, there
were two sheets and eleven pairs of socks—I was in the garden, and
they were all safe a little before four o'clock—I missed them a little befme
five o'clock—they have been found—I knew the prisoners—I did
see them near my premises—J saw my property again the next day—my
garden is surrounded by pales, and partly by a brick wall—whoever took
them must have got over the pales or the wall—the property is in Court
now—this is at it).
