678. HARRIET RAYCRAFT, stealing 2 sovereigns and other moneys;
the moneys of Job Fleetwood, her master.
Mr. Payne conducted the Prosecution.
Jane Hannau FLEETWOOD. I am the wife of John Fleetwood I am house-
keeper at Barge-yard Chambers. On 3d Feb., between two and three o'clock,
I sent Elizabeth Morton to Mr. Harrison to get 21. 138. 2d. I did not
receive the money-from information I went over to the Borough, and found
the prisoner in a house in Red Cross-street-she had been in my service, and
had left my place without bonnet or shawl the same day-I asked her what
she had done with the money that Betsy gave her to bring to me--she said,
“So help me God, she never gave it me"-I said, “It is nonsense to say
that, you know Betsy did give it you"-she then said, "I have not the
money about me, if you find it about me you may have it"-I said she
must go back-she swore she would not go over the water again that night-
I got a policeman and gave her in charge-she said she had put the money
behind the door in the coal-cellar-I looked in there, and in the water-closet,
and could not find it.
Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL. Q. Where did you have this conver-
sation with the prisoner ? 1. In the street partly, and partly in the house-
it was the first time I saw the prisoner after sending Morton for this money
-she had no right to receive money for me.
Mr. Payne. Q. How long had she been with you ? 4. Only a fort-
night-Morton had been with me two years, and had always received money.
ELIZABETH Morton. I am servant to Mr. Fleetwood, On Saturday, 3rd
Feb., I was sent for some money to Mr. Harrison- I went and got two
sovereigns, one half-sovereign, a half-crown, a sixpence, and four half-
pence- I had a bill—I gave the bill and the money to the prisoner as she
was going up stairs, and asked her to be so kind as to take it up to my mis-
tress and get the bill signed, and she did.
Cross-examined. Q. How much money had you in your pocket? 4.
None at all; it was precisely the same money I received that I gave to the
prisoner- I had a pain in my stomach, and was obliged to go to the
water-closet.
MARGARET DUFFY. I am the prisoner's aunt-I sell fruit at the bottom
of Barge-yard. On Saturday, 3rd Feb., she came to me-she bad got some
gold and silver; I do not know how much.
Guilty. Aged 17.--Confined Four Months.
