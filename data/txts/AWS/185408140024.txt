Prisoner's Defence. I am a man who work hard for my living I started
to work at hay making; I worked on Friday, and my master gave me 28.;
I earned only 6d. on Saturday, as it was wet I worked on Monday, Tues-
day, and Wednesday, and he gave me 28. in silver each day ; I same to this
woman's house and gave her a shilling, not knowing it was bad; she gave
me change, and I went and got my dinner I went to have a pint of beer
at 9 o'clock, and gave the shilling the woman gave me change, and in ten
minutes she brought the shilling back and said it was bad, and wanted to
have her change back I was not aware it was bad; I told her to send for
a policeman, and I pulled out my remaining money and gave it to him; I
always worked for my bread I worked for Mr. Ginger, of Stanmore.
