CLEMENT MACLEOD BURGHES (prisoner, on oath). I now carry on
the business which my father carried on for many years at 34, Pater-
noster Row. I put certain money in the business and took over the
assets in 1907. I knew nothing about this Ms. until I received a
letter from Messrs. Nutt in May last, stating that they had found the
MS. and wished to restore it to the original owner. I spoke to my
father about it. Messrs. Nutt returned the M8. to me. My father
told me he had purchased it for two guineas from Dr. Chatterton's
solieitors in 1906. I had no reason to doubt his statement. If I had
not thought it was my property I should not have sold it to Mesaro.
