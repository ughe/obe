722. WILLIAM CARROLL (17), Feloniously cutting and wounding
Peter McLeod, with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm.
MESSRS. WooD and COLLINS conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. LILLEY
the Defence.
PETER MCLEOD. I am a porter, of 4, Collingwood-street, Blackfriars-
road, -on 28th May, my wife and I were in Little Suffolk-street, Lant-
street, Borough, at a quarter or half-past 11-my wife was a little behind
me—the prisoner came up to me and said, “ Peter, how are you getting on !
I have not seen you for some time "-I looked at him, and said, "I do not
know you "-he came nearer to me and attempted to put his hand in my
trousers-pocket-I knocked it away, and attempted to hit him, but in so
doing I fell—be was underneath, and I was on top—I suppose he slipped-
I did not knock him down-my wife pulled me off him, and I saw no more
of him-I was the first to go away-I did not feel anything when on the
ground, but when I had walked twenty-five or thirty yards I felt sometbing
rather cold trickling down my side, and found two cuts there I had uot
been engaged in a struggle with anybody before that there was no means
of it being done but by the prisoner-we touched each other when on the
ground-the wounds were on my left side, through my coat and waistcoat
and two shirts- I was four days in the doctor's hands.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you been out spending Sunday! 4. I had
been out in the evening, and had taken some refreshment-I saw the
prisoner again eight or nine days afterwards in custody.
