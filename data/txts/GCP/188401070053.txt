213. OHARLES COURTNEY (42) and GEORGE JOHN HART
(60), Unlawfully obtaining by false pretences from Alfred Wheeler and
other persons divers valuable securities and sums of money, with intent.
to defraud. Other Counts for conspiracy.
After MR. POLAND's opening, Courtney stated in the hearing of the Jury
that he wan guilty on the whole indictment, and Hart stated that he was guilty
on the conspiracy counts. The Jury upon this returned a verdict of GUILTY.
COURTNEY then PLEADED GUILTY to a previous conviction in the name
of William Shaw in May, 1878, in this Court.-Five Years' Ponal Servitude.
HART received a good character.-Nino Months' Hard Labour.
