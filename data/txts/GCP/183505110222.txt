1358. THOMAS FARRER was indicted for embezzlement.
GEORGE RICHARD GAINSFORD. I live in the Borough. The prisoner
was in my employ for nearly two years-he was occasionally employed to
collect money-on the 4th of April he received 20s. from my partner, Mr.
Charles Goode-he was to purchase some stair-rods.
ROBERT HALL WRIGHT. I am in the employ of the prosecutor-the
prisoner came to me before he went out, and he had an I. O. U. for 4l. of
me-he afterwards brought me this bill for 31. 58., and 158., which made
the 41.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. When was this? 4. On Saturday,
the 21st of February--the I, O. U. is a piece of paper with those letters on it
-I have not got it here; Mr. Farrer tore it up-I gave him three sovereigns,
and 1l. of silver, of Mr. Gainsford's money--there are thirty-six people
in our shop-I give other persons money when they are going out I can
not say how often when they are going out with goods they come to me
for cash for change-I entered what I gave the prisoner in the book, but it
is not here--the prisoner brought me 158. and this bill for 31. 58.--I put
my initials on it, but they are partly torn off-I cannot swear that this is
the bill he brought me, but it was a bill for 31. 58.-I put my initials at
the Town-hall, about eight weeks after he had the money I had not seen
the bill from the time he gave it me, till I was at the Town-hall-I had given
it to my master.
Court. Q. The prisoner brought you a bill on the 21st of February, for
31. 58.? A. Yes; he did not bring me a bill of that amount from Mr.
Phillips on any other day.
GEORGE RICHARD GAINSPORD re-examined. Q. Do you recollect giving
the prisoner any directions about getting the goods ? A. He told me he
was going to get some rods--I did not give him the order that was on
the 20th or 21st of February-there had been several purchases of rods
about that time I had possession of this bill-it was given me on the 21st
of February, or the day following, as we balance every morning-if the
21st was Saturday, I received it on the Monday morning- I had no other
bill from Mr. Phillips amounting to 31. 58.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you keep the bill till it was produced at the
Town-hall? A. Yes, it was part of the time in the cash-box, and the
rest of the time in the desk-we purchased from the same person both be-
fore and afterwards, but not to the same amount-here is another bill,
which is 198., from the same person.
