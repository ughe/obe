933. MARGARET DORE, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 4d., the goods
of Mary Ann Pitt; and 1 pair of gloves, 4d. ; the goods of Benjamin Pitt, her
master.
WREY Ann Pitt. I am the daughter of Benjamin Pitt. The prisoner
Vus our servant-I lost a handkerchief about a month or six weeks before
she was taken--my handkerchief was found on her at the station--this is it
(produced)—it is mine—these gloves are my mother's--they were found
on the prisoner,
GEORGE BECK policeman, 164.) I took the prisoner to the station-I
found this handkerchief and gloves on her-she said she did not know any-
thing about them.
Prisoner. My mistress made me a present of the gloves, and I said I did
not want them; I lived six months with her, and she did not pay me my
rages.
MARTHA Pirt. I did not give her these gloves, but another pair
very much like them-she has got them in her bundle now-I owe her
some wages, but not six months' I owe her 1. Os. 6d.--the ladies I work
for will not tell me the value of all the articles.
Nor GUILTY.

CHARLOTTE RANDALL. I am servant to Mrs. Rann, of Upper Bedford-

GEORGE Beck (policeman, V 164.) I found this petticoat in the prisoner's

Prisoner's Defence. I made the petticoat, and brought it from Ireland.

235. GEORGE BROWN, aljas Dizon, stealing 15 sovereigns, 1 half
crown, and 10 shillings, the moneys of Michael Horden Peek.
Mr. Payns conducted the Prosecution.
MICHAEL HORDEN Peck. I am a dry-salter, at Southwark-bridge. The
prisoner was in my service about four months--on the 16th Oct. I gave him
fifteen sovereigns, a half-sovereign, and 108, in silver, to take to Spa-rond,
Bermondsey, to Mrs. Julia Adams, a manufacturing chemist-Mr. Barber
manages her business for her-I bave done business for them for years the
prisoner did not come back I did not see him again till the Tuesday fort-
night-I charged him with this-he made no reply-it was his duty to come
back, and he did not.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALDWIN. Q. Are you a manufacturing chemist?
4. No, a dry-salter-I supply muriate of lime and oil of vitriol-1 had
connexion in business about two months ago with a man named Bateman
-I know Marshall-street--1 bave no place of business there, Bateman
had--when I did business with him there was an illicit still there I had
nothing to do with it. I had no benefit from it-I used sometimes to get sweet
spirits and allum.

Mr. Peck, in Southwark-bridge-rond-he said he did not know Mr. Peck,
