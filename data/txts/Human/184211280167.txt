 248. JAMES OSBORNE and JAMES KELLY were indicted for steal ing, on the   17th of Nov ., 1lb. weight of bacon, value 6 d  ., the goods of Tho mas Atkinson . ELIZABETH SARAH ATKINSON. I am the wife of Thomas Atkinson, of Greenwich. I saw a piece of bacon safe about half an hour before it was taken—I saw a boy's arm through the broken window, endeavoring to take another piece—I then went to the door, and spoke to the officer—he took the prisoners, but there was no bacon found on them—I missed one piece. WILLIAM WRIGHT. On the 17th of Nov. I saw Osborne take a piece of bacon, and run up the road with it—Kelly was standing with him, and I had seen them together for a quarter of an hour—Kelly ran up to three other boys, when Osborne had taken it.
   Kelly,  What he is saying is false; some boys passed us, and we followed them. Witness.  I am certain I saw them together—I had watched the five together for a quarter of an hour—I work for my brother-in-law, who it a wood-chopper. JOHN BRADRICK( police-constable R  64.) I received information, and took Osborne on the Blackheath-road, running—Kelly was about 100 yards behind him, with three other boys, who were discharged by the Magistrate—Kelly gave his name as Mason.
   OSBORNE  — GUILTY. Aged 15.— KELLY  — GUILTY. Aged 14.— Confined Eight Days , and Whipped Twice .
 249. CAROLINE LAMB was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of Nov ., 2 pairs of stockings, value 2 s  ., the goods of John Goss Fleay . HENRY DALTON PATEN. I am shopman to John Goss Fleay, a linen draper in the Broadway, Deptford. On the 19th of Nov. the prisoner came in with a cloak on—she asked for some Orleans-doth of a colour which we had not got—as she went out, I saw her unpin some stockings which were at the door, and put them under her cloak—I followed, and found two pairs of stockings under her cloak—she begged I would forgive her, and not take her back—I took her back—these are the stockings.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Do her friends live at Deptford? A,  Yes—I do not know her circumstances—she was in the service of Mr. Smith about four years ago.
 (The prisoner received a good character, and a witness promised to provide for her.) GUILTY  . Aged 18.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury. — Confined Two Days .
