CAROLINE DAWSON Bruce WBTHERELL. I lodge at No. 6, Seymour-
place, Bryanstone-square. On the 8th of April I had a diamond ring safe
on a ring-tray, between three and four o'clock in the afternoon-I missed it
at eight o'clock in the evening-I acquainted the landlady of it immediately
- the only person on whom I could fix suspicion was John Simpson-be
used to bring coals from a person I have coals of, and he had brought me some
that day-his master was sent for, and then he was sent for-he denied all
knowledge of it-I had hand-bills circulated--I at last got to Mr. Thompson's
-I there saw my ring, and knew it-this is it-(looking at one.)
FREDERICK COLLINS. I am foreman to Mr. Thompson, a pawnbroker. On
the 14th of April, the prisoner Ann Simpson came to the shop-she wished to
redeem some things-she gave in three duplicates, and then she said she wished
to pawn a ring-she showed it me, and said she wanted as much as I could let
her have for it-I saw it was valuable, and I said would 6s, do for it?—she
said, "No" I said, would 78. do? she said, “ Yes"-I then told her to wait till
I sent to a lady-she said she could not, her children were at home she
left the ring, and I sent to the prosecutrix, who came and identified it-Ann
Simpson came again and she was given in charge she said the ring was
given to her by her mistress six years ago.
THOMAS HAZLEDINE (police-constable D 104.) I took Ann Simpson into
custody at Mr. Thompson's-she is the wife of the prisoner Joha Simpson's
brother.
Ann Simpson's Defence. My husband's brother brought it on Saturday,
and said he found it; we kept it till the Taesday following; my husband told
me to pawn it, and to say I had had it six years; I did not know it was
stolen.

1200. LOUISA WRIGHT was indicted for stealing 18 spoons, value 51. ;
and 12 forks, 5l. ; the goods of Susan Williams, her mistress.
Susan WILLIAMS. I live at No. 139, Strand. The prisoner was in the
habit of working as a char-woman on my premises on the 8th of March I went
to my plate-chest-I missed a quantity of plate-among the rest, eighteen
spoons and twelve forks I sent to the prisoner, and asked if she knew any.
thing about the plate—she said, "Nothing whatever"- the plate that is bere
is mine.
WILLIAM BONHAM. I am assistant to Mr. Luxmore, a pawnbroker, in St.
Martin's Lane. I produce eighteen spoons and twelve forks—they were
taken in pawn from the prisoner-we have known her a great many years-
she has brought these sort of things before.
Prisoner. I went to clean her apartments, and Mrs. Wood came to my
room, and asked me to take these things for her-she told me the property was
her own, and she had been a respectable woman.
William Pocock (police-sergeant T 14.) I took the prisoner at the
pawnbroker's-I found her lodging, but found no duplicates there-I have
not fourd Wood- I believe she is gone into the country.
SUSAN WILLIAMS re-examined. Wood was a servant in my employ
MARTHA SUTTON. I lived with Mrs. Williams before Wood came-I saw
