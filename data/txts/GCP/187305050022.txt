JOHN NEATE DYMOND. I am a member of the firm of Johnson & Dymond,
auctioneers-we keep the City Auction Rooms—on 16th December we
received a diamond brooch from the last witness, and sold it for 4201. on, I
think, the 16th December-before the 16th we showed it to Mr. Charles
Drayson, and we bought it for him-he left me the commission--he gave
me a limit to buy it for him at a certain amount
CHARLES DUPIN DRAYSON (re-examined). On 16th and 17th December
I was staying with Mr. Dymond-he showed me a diamond brooch, and I
gave him a reserve price for it, 4201.--I broke it up subsequently, and
showed Mr. Charman the stones-he identified them—these are them (pro-
duced).
WILLIAM JAMES BENGER. I am manager to Mr. Richard Attenborough, of
Oxford Street-1 produce a diamond plume, which I bought of Mr. Danby
on 12th December for 971, 108.
Cross-examined by MR. POLAND. I have been with Mr. Attenborough
25 years—971. 108. was a fair value.
Em WHITE. I am a widow, and carry on & jeweller's business at 128,
Great Portland Street-on 25th October the prisoner Rebecca came to me
and wanted to sell a diamond bracelet; she said that it belonged to her
mamma, whose ill health and reduced circumstances made her anxious to
sell it-I gave her 801. for it, and afterwards sold it to Messrs. Gilliam, of
Serle Street, for 1001.-on 29th October sho sold a diamond brooch to me
for 1601., she said that it belonged to her mamma--I sold it to Messrs.
Gilliam for 2001. the same day-on 10th December she brought a pearl
bracelet for sale; she asked 1501. for it, and said that she wanted the money
in about two hours; I did not ask why I did not purchase it then, as it
was not convenient-she brought it again on the 13th, and a diamond star
with it; I gave her 861. for the two, and sold them to Messrs. Field & Beer,
of Alfred Place, the same day, who gave me 901. for the bracelet and 402,
for the star--this is the bracelet and this is the star (produced).
Cross-examined by MR. M. WILLIAMS. I have not been in the habit of
dealing with Messrs. Gilliam recently, but I have known them for many
years- I sent my assistant to them with the things to make me an offer for
then— I have also dealt with Messrs. Field & Beer-I took them the arti-
cles myself, and they gave me receipts for them.
Re-examined. The receipts are all together; it is one receipt; I have not
got it now; it was delivered up at Marlborough Street, and I believe it is
pinned to my deposition-I wrote the receipt, and Rebecca signed it in my
presence.
JOHN GILLIAM. I am a jeweller, of 36, Serle Street, Lincoln's Inn-
I know Mrs. White-on 26th October I bought this bracelet (produced) of
her for 1001.—a month afterwards I sold it to a private customer on credit
for 1601., and since these proceedings I have got it back-on 29th October
I bought a diamond brooch of Mrs. White for 2001., and sold it to Messrs.
Field for 2501. on 22nd November-that is the brooch produced by Mr.
Field.
