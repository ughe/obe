MR. GIFFARD. Q. How long had you any one watch in your possession?
A. About six weeks; from 16th Aug. till 29th Sept.--I had no thought
of exchanging it.
John CARTER, Esq. The prisoner was in my service-it was his duty to
manage the shop in Tooley-street- it was his duty to keep books of the
transactions in that shop-if a customer bought a watch he should enter it
this is the book which he kept-here is no entry in it, from 16th Aug.
till 29th Sept., of any watch sold to Horspool, or to any one else, for
41. 143. 60.-I have carefully looked through the book-he has never
accounted to me for that 41. 148. 6d.-having received a communication, I
went to the prisoner on 14th Oct., and charged him with robbery and
embezzlement-I had heard he was in difficulties I upbraided him with his
conduct, and told him I should feel it my duty to give him into custody--he
begged me not to do so I reminded him that this was not the first time he
had been in difficulties-he left the room, went up stairs, locked himself in
his bed room, and cut his throat.
Cro88-examined. Q. Why was not this stated before to the officer, or to
any one else, that you had made a statement to him about his previous
difficulties ? A. I stated it to the prisoner, and told the servant to fetch &
policeman, and the prisoner instantly went up stairs and cut his throat-I
have occasionally been absent from that place for a month, but I believe not
since he has been with me it was not his duty to account to me whenever
I went there, but whenever I chose to ask him-I might be away a fort-
night, it was rarely three weeks I had the fullest confidence in him, and
thought my property was safe in his hands-I have been to him since 16th
Aug.-it might be once a week-I was there perhaps two or three times in
the month-the books will tell the sums I have received from him-I judge,
because I do not see the entry in this book, that the money has not been
paid-on some occasions money has been offered to me by him, and I said
it was not convenient to take it-I believe on the evening before there was
a balance of as much as 201., which I did not take.
MR. GIFFARD. Q. On the evening before, did you enter into an account
with him? A. I had occasion to suspect him the day before, and I saw him
on the night of the 13th-I did not enter into an account with him I was
engaged with the stock.
