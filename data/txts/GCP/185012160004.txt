198. JOHN MORRIS, stealing 5 sovereigns and 2 half-sovereigns ;
the moneys of William Strange, his master.
WILLIAM STRANGE. I live in Paternoster-row. On 28th Nov. the
prisoner applied to me for a situation, and I engaged him-my young
man gave him some money that evening, and he did not come back-
did not see him again till he was in custody.
WILLIAM BUCKNELL (policeman, B 190). I received information, and
took the prisoner in the Broad-way, Westminster, on 29th Nov., about
seven o'clock in the morning-I told him it was for robbing his master-
he said nothing-I took him to the station, and found upon him two
shillings, a comb, and a handkerchief.
WILLIAM JOHN Tuxe. I am shopman to Mr. Strange. On 28th Nov.
the prisoner was in my master's service-I gave him five sovereigns and
two half-sovereigns to buy some books; it was my master's money-he
left the shop, and never returned.
William Marsh CLARK. I am a bookseller. On 28th Nov. some
person came to my shop and asked me to let him leave this bag and
book, I cannot say whether it was the prisoner.
WILLIAM Gill. I am porter to Mr. Strange- I know this bag and
book, they are my master's.
WILLIAM JOHN TUKE re-examined. I know this bag and book- I gave
them to the prisoner at the time I gave him the money-this book con-
tairs a list of the books he was to get in.
Prisoner's Defence. I lost the money.
