HENRY BEARE. I am servant to Mr. James Strachan, of Teddington

the particulars.

for a warrant for Phillip Cork and John Stuckey - I went to Cork's house
on Twickenham-common- I told him I wanted him on a charge of felony
-I read my warrant to him-he said, “Why did you come at this late
hour of the day--why did you not come in the morning ?" -- I was about to
put the handcuffs on him--he said, “I will go without them" - I said,
"You must have them on-he then wanted to go into the next room for
his jacket--I said his wife could get it-a constable who was with me gave
it him-he seized this knife, and said he would not go, and he would stab the
first man that came near him-I seized him by the collar, and got him in the
corner of the room-he kicked the other constable, and struck me-we got
the handcuffs on one hand and took him-I went to get Stuckey-1 could
not find him-I returned to the lock-up place, and searched Cork-he said
"You want another person, don't you ?" he said it was Channell - I looked
him very hard in the face-he said, "I know I am guilty-I mean to plead
