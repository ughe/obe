bacco, value 2s.6d., the goods of William George Harrison, his master.
Mr. Darnell conducted the Prosecution.
RICHARD SILVESTER. I am in the employ of Mr. William George Harrison
tobacconist, Bucklersbury; the prisoner was in his employment. On Tue
day evening, the 5th of May, he left the premises about seven o'clock-
stopped him as he was leaving, and told him he had some property in
possession-he denied it-I insisted on having the property produced, and be
opened a handkerchief which he had in his hand, and this leaf tobacco was in
it-it weighs four ounces and a half-he went to the end of the room, and
put it into a box in which there was stalk tobacco-that would not !
mixed with leaf tobacco-on the following morning I took it out the prisoner

he is twenty-two years of age-- he has been married three years—he has no

MR. PARNELL. Q. You did not give him into custody till you had
