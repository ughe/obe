Rev. Stewart EXELYN FORSTER. I am a clergyman of the Church of
England, and reside at Lewisham. I had occasion to go into Lincoloshire,
where I had resigned two livings which I held there had a sister died a
short time before, (Mrs. Charles Forster,) and I bad a box which contained
various articles of hers, which were kept in Lincolnshire for a niece of hers
- I took the prisoner down for the purpose of packing up the wearing-ap-
parel, which I was removing from there to my present residence-she had
access to every thing-in consequence of what my servants told me, I sent
Duncan up stairs, and be produced these things I can speak to these stock-
ings very particularly, knowing them to have belonged to my brother--they
were marked "CF"-I can see that the C has been taken ont, and in
this instance the stalk of the “F" is of a different colour to the new work
-the box from which these were taken contained other articles of a
similar description-this handkerchief I can identify, as being a handsome
handkerchief-I have one from the box like it I cannot say how many
there were, but I can say that several have been abstracted-the box is
much lower than it was, in respect to the quantity of things it contains
these other articles I cannot speak to-here are handkerchiefs which have
been recently marked "MP"-they have been lying some time in the
box, and they correspond.

Cross-examined. Q. Which are the articles you identify A. These
three pairs of stockings, and the bottoms of these two pairs of ladies'
drawers--they were the bottom of a baby's robe- it was chiefly baby.linen
that was in that box, except pocket-handkerchiefs and stockings—I saw
them in the box on the 14th or 15th of June, and I left Lincolnshire
about the 20th-I did not take an inventory, nor count the articles in it-
I looked in, but did not ascertain whether any articles were missing-
never gave the prisoner any stockings for herself-I gave her some for the
nursery-maid, but they were marked with different initials--the box came
from Lincolnshire with the rest of my things--I saw all the articles as
8000 as they arrived at my house the prisoner was in my service eight
months, as cook and housekeeper-she came from Mrs. Barr's boarding-
house, at Cheltenham.
MR. DOANE. Q. Did you ever give her stockings of this description?
