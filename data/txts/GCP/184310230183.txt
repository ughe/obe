3058. WILLIAM BURKE was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of Oct.,
24 cigars, value 6s., the goods of Salmon Salmon.
Richard WITHERS. At a quarter to twelve o'clock on the morning of the
- 23rd of Oct., I was in Great Turnstile-I saw the prisoner and two others
standing at Mr. Salmon's shop-one of them said, "Go in, it is all right"
the young man was cleaning the window-the prisoner went in and helped
himself to some cigars on the counter--he put them into his breeches' pocket
and came out-the others followed him-they met together in Holborn, and
divided the cigars between them-I saw a policeman, and told him he took
the prisoner—the others escaped.
Eve Salmon. I am the wife of Salmon Salmon, a tobacconist, in Great
Turnstile. About eleven o'clock, in the morning of the 23rd, I went into the
back parlour while the men were cleaning the windows-no one was in the
shop-soon after the prisoner was brought back-I looked into a box on the
counter, which had had two dozen cigars in it, and found only three left-
these are the same sort of cigars.
JAMES Wright (police-constable F 54) I received information, and took
the prisoner-I found these two cigars on him.
Guilty. Aged 19.-Confined Two Months.

3059. GEORGE CHAPPELL was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of
Oct., 30 yards of cloth, called Alpaca, value 81., the goods of Christopher
Leaf.
Thomas Hill. I am porter to Christopher Leaf, of Wood-street. Be-
tween two and three o'clock in the afternoon of the 17th of Oct., the prisoner
came, and inquired if our salesman was in the warehouse-I said no, he was
at dinner-he said he would call again ; and, in going out, I saw him take
this piece of goods--I went and overtook him in Mumford-court, about 300
yards from my master's, with this cloth, which is ours.
Cross-examined by Mr. DOANE, Q. Do not you know that he had
dealings with the salesman ? A. Yes.
Guilty. Aged 52.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Four Months.
