HENRY WRIGHT. I am potman at the Duchess of Kent public-house, Devereux-street, Dover-road, where I have been seven weeks—it is about four miles from Mr. Templeman's cottage—I have known the prisoner from seven to eight months—I saw him on the 12th or 13th of last March, I am not positive which—I think it was the 12th, Thursday—he came to the Duchess of Kent public-house about half-past seven o'clock in the evening, and called fur half-a-pint of porter, and put down a penny, saying he had no more money—I did not take the money—I brought him a pint of porter—I then went out to deliver my beer—I returned about half-past eight o'clock—the prisoner was then standing on the outside, opposite the house, talking to a young man, a potman in the neighbourhood, named Richard Squires—there was no conversation between them after I came up—we had some porter together—as soon as we had had the pot of porter between us three, the prisoner and I walked up the street together—while we were walking together he said he had been ill, and was without money, and for want of money he was getting worse—he stated that he knew an old man who had got money—I asked him how he knew he had got money—he said he had heard he had bid 25 l . for a cottage, and he had flashed  a 50 l . note, and said if that was not enough he had got more—the prisoner said that he had put the notes into a drawer, and it would be like a gift to him, for he knew where to put his hand on it—he said he should like to have a right one along with him—I said, "A right one!"—he said, "Yes, a right one, or I could do it myself"—I asked him where the old gentleman lived—he said, "Oh, not far."
   Q.  Not far from when? A.  From where Gould lived.
   COURT . Q.  Did he say so? A.  Yes; he said, not far from him, that it, signifying where Gould lived—he said, "Not far from home"—those were the words be used.
   MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  Now, is that all you remember of what passed, or was any thing else said? A.  That is all I remember—when he was in the tap-room he named to me that he had been to a publican whom he once lived with, and that he should endeavour to get a situation, and try that man if he would give him a character, that he would send to him—that was before I walked with him—he said nothing further than I have stated with reference to the old gentleman's money.
