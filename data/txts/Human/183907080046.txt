 2070. MARGARET DONOVAN was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of June , 2 bonnets, value 1 s  .; 1 quilt, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 2 sheets, value 1 s  ., and 1 petticoat, value 6 d  .; the goods of George Yarnell . RUTH YARNELL. I am the wife of George Yarnell, and live in Coalyard, Drury-lane. I left the house on the 27th of June, about ten o'clock in the morning—I locked the door, and returned at six o'clock in the evening—I found the door forced open, and these articles gone—these are my bonnets—( looking at them. ) ANN WALKER. I am the wife of William Walker, and live in Monmouth-street. I bought these bonnets of the prisoner on the 27th of June. THOMAS FARRELL. I am a policeman. I got the bonnets from Walker. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Six Months.

 2073. GEORGE MONKS was indicted for a misdemeanor.
