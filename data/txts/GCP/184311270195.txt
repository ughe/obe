SABAH BALLS. I am lady's-maid in the service of Sir Richard Paul
Jodrell, baronet. The family were at Brighton in Oct. last, and left on the
28th, by an early train-1 assisted in packing up the luggage, particularly
Lady Jodrell's wearing apparel, in a large black leather trunk, with brass
nails--I put in some veils, some dozens of silk stockings, from thirty to forty
pocket-handkerchiefs, worth four or five guineas each--the whole was worth
about 2001.-Charles Crocker, the second footman, locked the box--I saw
the box in the drawing-room after it was corded I have not seen it since
I came by the same train as the family, and the servants were to follow two
men and two maids came by the same train as the luggage.
