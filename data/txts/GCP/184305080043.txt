Q. Do you know whether anybody struck the prisoner's arm before the
pistol went off ? A. There was some struggling the impression on my
mind is that the sparks were distinctly discharged from the pistol before the
prisoner was touched-though I had left my seat, I think I could see the
whole that was going on-1 kept my eye distinctly on the pistol, and on his
hand-he sunk bis hand, certainly, after raising it-he raised the pistol,
and discharged it--whether he discharged it at any person I cannot say.

Q. Did the muzzle of the pistol appear to be pointed so as to reach the
