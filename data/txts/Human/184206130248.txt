MICHAEL TOBY. I am a coach-trimmer. I have examined this leather, it belongs to the prosecutor—I cut it myself—I can swear to this piece—( looking at it. )
   Cross-examined. Q.  Was it you gave information about the prisoner A It was—I never stole any leather, or took any to make shoes of—I made a shoulder-strap of a piece of leather—this piece is of no value but to a coach-maker—my master does not permit us to take any, but it is a thing that is generally done—I have seen both of my shop-mates do it,
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 31.— Recommended to mercy.  — Confined Two Months.

 2017. SAMUEL KINSMAN was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of February , 1 jacket, value 8 s  .; 1 razor, value 3 s  .; 1 pair of gloves, value 6 d  .; and 1 comb, value 4 d  .; the goods of Thomas Allen . THOMAS ALLEN. I am a bricklayer. I lodged at the King's Head, Wandsworth—the prisoner lodged in another room—I missed masked containing a razor, a pair of gloves, and a comb—these are mine. THOMAS COWDERY. I bought this jacket of the prisoner. WILLIAM LEE I took the prisoner, and cautioned him not to say any thing—he said Cowdery was a fool to bring the jacket up there after haveing it. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months.
 2018. JOHN WELCH was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of May , 1 gelding, price 2 l  . 10 s  .; and 1 cart, value 1 l  . 10 s  .; the property of Henry Preston . HENRY PRESTON. I am a greengrocer, and live in Maynard-street, St. Giles'. I had a black gelding in a cart, on Saturday, the 14th of May—I left it in Hart-street, Covent-garden, about four in the morning, and some one took it away. THOMAS HALL. I am gate-keeper at Battersea-bridge. About five o'clock in the morning of the 15th of May, the prisoner drove the horse and cart—he came from Chelsea—there were two other boys sitting down in the cart—he paid the toll. ISAAC HUTCHINGS. On the 15th of May the prisoner left this horse and cart in the Battersea-road, and went down a turning—when he saw me he ran back, took the horse and cart, and drove off. GEORGE BERRYMAN( police-constable M  130.) I found the horse and cart in Battersea-parish—there were several boys round it. THOMAS DALEY( police-constable V  127.) I took the prisoner in High-street, Wandsworth—I found on him two toll-tickets—one of the 14th of May—he said he had had them six months—then he said he had been with a wagoner, who gave the tickets to him. NOT GUILTY.
