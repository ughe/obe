stealing from his person 138., his money.
MR. METCALFE Prosecuted.
JOHN HOLMES DOUGLAS. I am a fireman on board ship-the prisoner
lives at 14, Nelsen Tidal Basin-on December 7th I was indoors with
John Cox and Mrs. Morris in the back room downstairs-the prisoner,
whom I knew. came in between seven and eight, and asked Mrs.
Morris for some money; she said she had none to give him-he said that
he had a boarder just come, and then asked me I said that I had no
money; he struck me on my mouth, and rifled both my pockets and took
about 13s., which was in my right pocket-I kept it there because I
cannot use my left arm-I saw the money in his hand-I went out of the
house-the blow cut my lip-I had just receivod my wages, 188. 3d.-I
had had a drink, but I knew what I was doing, and made a complaint to
the police at once.
