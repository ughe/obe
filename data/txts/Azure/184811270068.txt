THOMAS PARKIN Corp ( policeman, N. 230.) I heard a noise in the

out at the front door—I sent to the station for a bull's-eye—I at last took
the prisoner out of a water-closet at No. 1 1, Montague-place—I had bad such
an observation of his person as to say he is the same man I saw in the kitchen
—this copper was behind the front door of the house, i had heard a noise in
the house.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Were you not outside the house?
A. Yes; I went to the door—if I had waited I do not know whether they
