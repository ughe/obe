John Woods (Police Sergeant M). On Saturday night, 4th August, or
Sunday morning, I was on duty in White Street, Borough there is a court
called Collier's Rents there, and through that a court called St. George's
Square, which consists of six houses, three on each sido-it is occupied
mostly by the lower class of Irish-in consequence of what I heard I went
to a court which runs at the back of this court, and heard voices as if
persons were quarrelling and fighting I was in uniform-[ went down the
place, and found a number of the inhabitants fighting and making a great
disturbance, both in Collier's Rents and St. George's Square it is a place
where there is always a special man on duty, and constables do not go down
there singly-Martin was the special constable on duty there that night,
and Dale was on the beat_I found them there the people seemed to be
fighting in different lots; it was a kind of free fight; we term them faction
