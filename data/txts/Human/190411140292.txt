I said, "Where are you going when your mate comes?" he said, "I am going to "work, I mean we are going to look for some"; I said, "Where are you going?" he said, "Over the water"; I said, "Where is your mate coming from?" he said, "From over the water"; I said, "What is his name?" he made no reply and twisted about—I said, "What have you got about you?" and I put my hand on the outside of his jacket and felt something—he said, "That is my spectacle case," and took out a spectacle case—I touched his right hand jacket pocket, and he said, "That is a piece of bread, Sir," and he put his hand in his pocket and brought out a piece of bread—I took hold of his hand and found underneath the bread this bag containing four keys and this piece of candle (  Produced )—I said,' I shall take you to the station"and I did so—at the station I searched him and found four skeleton keys, two in his right hand waistcoat pocket and two in his left; there were eight keys altogether with those that were in the bag—I also found this small jemmy ( Produced ), which would be used for forcing a drawer, these two pieces of wire, which might be used for picking a lock ( Produced ), and this box of matches ( Produced )—the prisoner said, "I picked them up in the gutter as I was coming over Tower Bridge; I saw it lying in the gutter; I kicked it and it sounded all right; I thought it was money so I picked it up and put it in my pocket"—he was speaking of the bag of keys—after making certain enquiries, I went to 48, Hough ton Koad, Bow, where I found in the room which he occupied this jemmy, and this piece of wire ( Produced )—I showed them to him after wards, and he said, "That piere of wire I use for cleaning anything; the chisekis mine, you can see where I have hammered it on the head. I only saw him go into one door—I could not see him try any doors—when he struck the matches, I take it he was looking at the keyhole; he stooped down to the level of the keyhole—he was smoking at the time, so he could not have struck the matches to light his pipe—it was quite dark at the time.
   The prisoner's statement before the Magistrate: "I picked them up in the gutter, as I have said, and these pieces of wire are merely pipe cleaners; this policeman did not see me do anything."
