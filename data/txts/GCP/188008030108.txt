15th July I was in the neighbourhood of the Blyth Hill Hotel, Forest Hill
driving a pony and trap, about 6 p.m.-I went into the hotel-I was then
about three minutes-when I came out the horse and cart were gone-in the
cart were a pair of boots, half a pound of tea, some cut flowers and two
pot-flowers, three handkerchiefs, and a whip--this handkerchief has my
name on it-I left it in the cart-the value of the horse and cart was about
£24 I have not since seen the boots nor the tea-I next saw the cart in
the possession of the police at Tunbridge.
