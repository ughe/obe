3099. JOHN ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of Oct.,
2 waistcoats, value 158.; 14 yard of fustian, 18. 9d.; the goods of Thomas
Nickenson ; and that he had been before convicted of felony: and ESTHER
CLEMENTS, for feloniously receiving the said ustian, well knowing it to
have been stolen ; against the Statute, &c.
THOMAS NICKENSON. I am a tailor, and live in Battersea-square. I saw
these waistcoats and fustian safe on the 9th of Oct., and missed them on the
10th-I cannot swear to the fustian produced, but can to the two waistcoats
- they were in my shop window.
EDWIN SOAMES. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Paradise-street, St.
Luke's. The male prisoner pawned these two waistcoats on the 11th of
October
FREDERICK LANGTON (policeman.) On the 9th of Oct. I saw the male
prisoner lurking about Mr. Nickenson's shop-1 afterwards went to the female
prisoner, and took her-I found a piece of fastian at the pawnbroker's-I
asked her how she came by it-she said her son gave it her to sell, and not
having money she pawned it, and gave him the money.
Robinson's Defence, I found them under the boards of a new house.
WILLIAM WALKER (policeman.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner
Robinson's former conviction-(read)—he is the person.
ROBINSON-Guilty. Aged 17.-Transported for Seven Years.
CLEMENTS-Not GUILTY.
