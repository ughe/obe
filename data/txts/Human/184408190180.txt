I am horse-keeper to Mr. George Stansby—he is a wharfinger, and lives at No. 9, Wharf-road. On Saturday night, the 3rd of Aug., at eleven o'clock, I left the stable all locked up safely—I went next morning, about five, and found the doors undone, and some beans scattered about—I went up to the loft, and found the inner door broken, and missed a sack of white oats, and some split-beans, which were my masters—the stable door was shut to when I found it, and it must have been opened from the in side—there is a wooden rack in the stable, and a person could get from there into the loft—I went with the officer to the boat, and saw Phipps and Williams—I saw some beans scattered from the stable to the boat—I com pared the white oats, and the split-beans with those which were in my master's loft—they corresponded exactly.
   Lovells Defence.  I was at the Red Lion with Bull—Phipps and Williams came; we asked where we could lie down; they said they had got leave to lie down on the deck, and we might lie there, which we did, and the corn was there.
   Williams' Defence.  I saw the sack there when we came back from supper.
   Phipps'Defence.  When we left work there was no corn; when we came back it was there—these men asked us if we could tell them where they could lie down, and we did.
   LOVELL  — GUILTY. Aged 20.
   BULL  — GUILTY. Aged 23.
   WILLIAMS  — GUILTY. Aged 18.
   PHIPPS  — GUILTY. Aged 25. Confined Six Months
 2140. JAMES DRISCOLL was indicted for stealing 1 breast-pin, value 1 s  .; and 1 pair of gloves, 1 s  . 3 d  .; the goods of Edward Barker , from his person. HANNAH MORRIS. I live in White Horse-street, Stepney. On the after noon of the 9th of Aug., I saw the prisoner leading the prosecutor, who was rather intoxicated, down White Horse-street—the prisoner beckoned to a female as he came up to our door—I followed them till I met a policeman—the prisoner asked the gentleman to go up Bear-street—he said it was not the way home—the prisoner said he would take him round the other way—they went a little further, and the prisoner leaned upon his breast, against the wall in Butcher-row, and he took a pin from his breast—I had noticed the pin before—they both fell down—the prisoner was rather intoxicated—they went up Bear-street—I followed them, and they went down a kind of court or alley—I went and asked the gentleman to come out of the place—the prisoner said he would fetch a policeman, and give the gentleman in charge for half-a-crown, for bringing him from Blackwall-stairs—I begged of him to fetch one—he went and fetched one—we came down the Com mercial road, down to Arbour-square—the gentleman, the prisoner, and I went to the station of our own accord, without a policeman—in going to the station the prisoner tapped me on the shoulder, and said to mind that he was halves—I saw the policeman, and gave the prisoner in charge—I saw some gloves and some silver taken from the prisoner.
