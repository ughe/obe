Joun LEGGETT. I live at 13, Little College-street, Chelsea, and am a
bricklayer. In Aug. and Sept., I worked for Coomer-I recollect in Sept.
being at work for him, building a wall in Europa-place--the stock of bricks
ran short on the morning Daly came; I had only about 300 remaining, and
at about half-past six I went and told Mr. Coomer 50 he said he would order
some, or had ordered some, I cannot exactly remember wbich ; and during the
time I had gone, the bricks came, and were shot on the ground-I did not
see who brought them-I went to Mr. Coomer about ten, I could not find
bim, and a little girl told me he was gone to Kilburn-I had been at work
at the wall seven days.
