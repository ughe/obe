WILLIAMS pleaded GUILTY. Aged 14.-Confined Three Months.
REBECCA HART. I am single, and live in Giffin-street, Deptford. On
the 24th of March, about six o'clock in the evening, I was in Greenwich
fair-a policeman asked if I had lost my handkerchief, and I found I had
-it had contained 10d.this is it.

out this bandkerchief-he was passing it to Gillett-I seized him, and it
fell on the ground-I took both the prisoners, and took up the handker-
chief, and found this money in it.
