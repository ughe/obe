entering the dwelling-house of William Woodward, about three in the night
of the 10th of May, at Lambeth, and stealing therein, 1 hat, value 11.;
1 spoon, 6s. ; 1 kettle, 21. ; 1 thimble, 6d. ; 1 top of a pepper-castor, 6d.;
and I candlestick, 4s. ; his goods.
