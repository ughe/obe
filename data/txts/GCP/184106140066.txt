1621. WILLIAM CHAPMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 13th
of June, 1 pair of gloves, value 2s.; and 1 penknife, value 6d. ; the good
of Samuel Case: also, 1 pair of trowsers, value 4s. 6d. ; 1 waistcoat, vale
3s. ; 1 pair of stockings, value 1s. ; 1 shirt, value 38.; and 1 shilling, the
property of George Cullem; to which he pleaded
GUILTY.-Confined Four Months.
1622. JOHN WYLD was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of May,
2 boots, value 148., the goods of William Hyland ; to which he pleaded
GUILTY.-Transported for Seven Years.
1623. MARGARET DOYLE was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd
of April, 2 shawls, value 21. ; 1 cape, value 10s. ; 2 pairs of stockings
value 98.; 1 handkerchief, value 4s. ; yard of silk, value 48. ; 2 pairs of
shoes, value 68.; and 1 pair of boots, value 4s. ; the goods of John Anthony
Tielens, her master.
John ANTHONY TIELENS. I live in Euston-square--the prisoner wa
my housemaid for a month-she left on the 10th of May-I missed these
things while she was with us--these articles produced are mine.
Cross-examined by Mr. Phillips. Q. I believe there is some reason
to suppose the prisoner has been made the dupe of a man named Doyle!
A. I have heard so.
ROBERT WOODGATE. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a table-cloth and
some other things which were pawned by the prisoner.
GUILTY. Aged 25.- Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prosecutor.
- Judgment respited.
1624. JOHN READ was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of May, 1
work-box, value 21.; 1 thimble, value 1d.; 1 tassel, value 4d.; 1 penny,
and 2 half-pence; the property of Elizabeth Norton.
SUSAN SMITH. I am servant to Elizabeth Norton, who lives in Cole-
brook-row. On a Saturday afternoon, at five o'clock, we lost this work-
box from the table in the parlour--it had a thimble, and tassel, one penny,
and two half-pence in it- I had seen it safe two hours before.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Is the prosecutrix single? A. Yes
- he house had been painted, and the door was left Open.
THOMAS WITHERS (police-constable N 211.) On the 15th of May I was
on duty, and saw the prisoner and another person-the other went into the
prosecutrix's house, and the prisoner followed--they staid about two
minutes-the prisoner came out with this work-box under his arm-I laid
hold of him-he struggled, and dropped it-a person took it up, and gave
it me- the other ran away.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Six Months.
