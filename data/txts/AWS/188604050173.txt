JOBEPH RICHARD PHILLIPs BBLLEN. I am assistant to my father,
Joseph Bellen, a chemist, at 86, High Street, Wimbledon- have known
Mr. Dyson about eighteen months-on the 28th December he came to
our shop about mid-day, and purchased some chloroform-about an ounce
and a half or two ounces, I am not sure which-it was pure chloroform,
and the price was 28. an ounce-it was in a small blue bottle, with the
words on it 44 Not to be taken on the glass, and 44 Poison and in
addition to that it had a label pasted on it, 46 Chloroform,' and one of
our ordinary trade labels with our name and address-I think there was
two ounces of ehloroform in it, but I cannot remember whether it was
full or not-he paid me for it and took it away.
