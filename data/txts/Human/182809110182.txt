 1581.  MARY ANN HARRISON and MATILDA HARRISON were indicted for stealing, on the 29th of August , 2 pillows, value 5s., and 2 pillow-cases, value 6d., the goods of John Sirkett.
 JOHN SIRKETT. I live in  Union-street, Somers-town, and am a coal-dealer. I let these two pillows and pillow-cases as part of the furniture of a room to the two prisoners, about the 28th of August; they were taken about the 8th of September - they did not leave the lodging, but they did not pay their rent; I took an officer, and missed these goods.
   FRANCIS PARKER. I am a pawnbroker. I have two pillows, and two pillow-cases, pawned by the two prisoners together; one on the 5th of September, the other on the 28th of August - the prisoners both came each time.
   JOSEPH CADBY. I am an officer. I took the prisoners in charge, and found these two duplicates on Matilda.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 MATILDA HARRISON's Defence. We did it through distress; we took home some work, but did not get paid for it: we wanted the prosecutor to wait half an hour, but he would not.
 MARY ANN HARRISON -    GUILTY. Aged 18.
 MATILDA HARRISON -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined One Month.
 1952.  ANN LESTER was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of July , 1 frock, value 3s., and 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of John Brickhell.
   ROBERT YOUNG. I am in the service of Mr. John Brickhell. On the 17th of July I was informed that a person had come into a box in his shop; I went round and found the prisoner there - I asked what she had in her apron - she said nothing but what belonged to her - I looked into her apron and found this frock and handkerchief belonging to us - they were both marked; she said she bought them in the market; I had seen them that morning before.
   THOMAS FRAMPTON. I am an officer; I took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that she had purchased the articles of a Jew, in Tottenham-court-road.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. Confined Fourteen Days.
 1953.  THOMAS PERKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of July , 2 pairs of upper leathers, value 1s. 6d. the goods of John Lawrence.
 JOHN LAWRENCE. I am a  shoe-maker, and live in Widegate-alley, Bishopsgate-street. On the 26th of July I went to Lambeth-street Office, and found those two pairs of upper-leathers which I had given to Ann Cole to bind, on the 22d of July; I know nothing of the prisoner.
   ANN COLE. I received this pair of upper leathers from Mr. Lawrence on the 22d of July; on the 23d the prisoner came and asked me for all the upper leathers I had done; I gave him three pairs, and this is one of them.
   GEORGE BURRELL. On the 25th of July the prisoner brought these upper leathers to me to sell - I asked him what he wanted for them; he said 2s.; I said that was more than they were worth - he said they were his father's; he gave me his name and address - I bought them of him for 1s. 9d.; in the course of the day I went to look for his father, and could not find him by the direction which he had given me - on the following Saturday I was going to the leather-cutter's, I met the prisoner, and gave him in charge.
   THOMAS SIMSON. I am headborough. I was sent for, and took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 13. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 1954.  CATHERINE ROGERS was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of July , 1 jacket, value 3s., the goods of John Cross.
   RICHARD EDWARD BARBER. I am in the service of Mr. John Cross, pawnbroker, of Clerkenwell. On the 15th of July - the prisoner came to our shop between four and five o'clock - she pawned an article, and then went away; a person then told me something - I went into the street, and saw the prisoner walking from the house; I asked her what she took from our shop - she said nothing at all; I lifted up her cloak, and found this jacket, which is Mr. Cross' - I had hung it up in the morning.
   JOHN BOWYER. I took her up.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I throw myself on your mercy.
     GUILTY. Aged 62. Confined Fourteen Days.
