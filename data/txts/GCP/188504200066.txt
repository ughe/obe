ELIZABETH BANKS. I live at 1, Hyde Street West, New Cross, and
am single on Tuesday morning, 24th March, the prisoner came and
engaged & lodging-he came back about 5 o'clock, sat down a little
while and talked he said he should soon go to bed, he was very tired,
and that he had not got any money-he had a cup of tea and half a slice
of bread and butter-I left the room, and he followed me almost directly
going out at the street door, and calling out "I shall not be five
minutes when I went back into the kitchen my watch was gone-1
had seen it few minutes before when he was sitting there-it was
worth about 21.- another young man was asleep in the kitchen-1
Aroused him and asked him the time he said half-past five-I afterwards
went to the police-station-I identify the priscner.
TEOMAS, PATTENDEN. I live at 1, Hyde Street West, New Cross, and
ama potman-I have been there nearly 16 months I think-on 24th
March, about four o'clock, I came home and went to sleep in the kitchen
the landlady aroused me I looked at the time-it was a little after
5.30-I never touched the watch-I know nothing about it-I saw
stranger there, but did not take enough notice of him to remember him
again.
JOHN BAKER (Policeman R 69). About 5.30 on the 6th inst. I took the
prisoner I said nothing to him about a watch-I was present at the
Station when Miss Banks identified him-she did so at once.
Guilty. There were two other similar case8 against the prisoner. Tholu
