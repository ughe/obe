641 JAMES KIRBY and THOMAS WEBB, stealing 70 lbs. weight

KIRBY pleaded Guilty. Aged 18.- Recommended to mercy by the
Prosecutor, who promised to employ him again.-Confined Two Daya.
GEORGE THOMAS WANGER. I am a farmer at Chiogford. On 23rd Feb.
my attention was called to the loss of some hay from my sbed, and I observed
marks of something being passed over my paling, near the shed-I traced
it across the fields about 300 yards, to Webb's garden, where I found
nearly a truss and a-half of hay, which I identified-in consequence of 1)
question I put to Webb, he opened a shed door and I took up some hay, and
said, “This is my hay, and I will swear to it”-I found Kirby lying dom
near the hay, and I asked him if he knew anything about it; he denied al
knowledge of it, and so did Webb-he said he did not know how it came
there-I sent for a policeman, and when he came Webb begged for mere,
wished me to settle it, and said he would pay me for it-Kirby has worked
for me several times.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. Was it Sunday morning when you
went to Webb's ? A. Yes; I asked him where he kept bis bay, and by
opened the shed, and showed me where it was-be said it was his at first, and
when I said it was mine, he said, “Then if it is yours, I do not know when
it came from"-when I sent for a policeman, he said, “For God's sake make
it up, on account of my wife"- I do not think his wife has very good healt
-- he was admitted to bail by the Magistrate, and has surrendered here.
HENRY WANGER. On 23rd Feb., I counted eight trusses of my father's bay
in his shed-about nine in the evening I missed some, and traced where some
hay had been put over some palings--I went to the hedge in the field adjoin
ing the road, traced some hay on the ground there, and on the hedge acro
the road, into the field adjoining Webb's premises--I noticed some hay bar
been put over there to his premises- I saw the hay that was found on the
premises-it is my father's.
WILLIAM BOWLER ( policeman, N 408). I was called to Webb's premises
and saw some hay in the shed, and Kirby lying on it-Webb said he did not
know how the hay came there, and Kirby said he had not brought it-M.
Wanger said he could swear to it-I said I should take him into custody, and
Kirby then said, "I took the bay from Mr. Wanger's stack, and broughts
to the shed, and Webb gave me 18. for it"-Webb did not attempt to deos
