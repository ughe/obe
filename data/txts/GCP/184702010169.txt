640. JOHN BONE was indicted for stealing 1 sovereign; the money of

GEORGE STEVENS. I am a carrier out of Kent. I put up at the Spur
Inn, in the Borough-I have been in the habit of employing the prisoner at
different times for the last four or five years. On the 28th of Dec. I gave him
a sovereign to go to Bishopsgate after a bag of shot, and to call at Mr.
Ruff's for some bacon-he returned no more till he was apprehended.
Prisoner's Defence. I lost it ; that is all I have to say,
