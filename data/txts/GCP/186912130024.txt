A. I wrote to all my clients in the same way, when I removed from South-
Ampton Buildings in the letter of November I asked him to bring me
business-he never did bring me any-I met him in the street, and told
him I should be happy to do any business he could get for me-he never
used my office, and no letters were ever addressed there to him, or legal

HENRY GUNNING MATTHEW. I live at 159, Vauxhall Bridge Road, and

days before, and in consequence of a conversation I had with him I went to

he had not been there that day, but he expected him every moment, and
he asked the nature of my business-I said if it had not been already
