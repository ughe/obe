Cross-examined by Mr. O'BRIEN. Q. How near did you come to the
person running away? 4. About thirty yards--I saw his back.
Ann West. I live at 2, Ship-gardens, York-street. On 9th Oet., about
two o'clock, I saw the prisoner, who I knew before, poll a coat down from
the two nails it was hanging on at Mr. Harding's door; he put it under his
arm and ran away through Horseshoe-alley-I told Nichols.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you know his name before ? A. Yes; I did not
mention his name to Nichols-Millerman came to me the next evening, I
told him I could swear it was the prisoner took the cont-he took me to see
him-I did not tell him his name at first, and he did not ask it-I knew where
he lived-I did not tell Nichols-I thought as he ran away, he would not
catch him; and I told him the evening after he was taken-I have known him
six or seven years I do not think I told the Magistrate that I knew him by
sight-(The witness's deposition being read, stated, "I am sure he is the person,
I know him by sight well")-I did not say anything about knowing him by
name, I was not asked.
William MILLERMAN ( policeman, B 95). I took him into custody; he
said he was selling walnuts all day.
Cross-examined. Q. Where did you take him? 4. In Rochester-row-
he had a basket of walnuts then.
