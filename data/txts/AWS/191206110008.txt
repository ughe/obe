Cross-examined. I did not tell Webster that I was going to steal
these letters I simply made arrangements to meet him with the bag,
and as far as I know he had no reason to suppose I was going to steal;
I had been honest up till this time. I arranged after I had given
it to him to call for the bag later on; I did not tell him I had put
anything into it. I was arrested half an hour afterwards, and I did
not see Webster again till about 8 p.m. He keeps a draper's shop,
and at Ohristmas he asked me to get up a slate club amongst the post
men to deal with him. I called on him about May 12.
Re-examined. I was going to see him again at his place at 6.30

ing these letters I did not say that he did not know anything about it ;
