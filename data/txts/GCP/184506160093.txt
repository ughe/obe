WILLIAM ROGERS. I am machine-boy to Bradbury and Co., printers,
Whitefriars. I was going past the Admiralty on the 27th of May-it had
jost struck nine o'clock-I saw the prisoner standing against the gate-he
said to me, "Boy, I will give you a halfpenny to go over the way, and get
me a quartern of the best gin"-he said he could not leave the gate--he
gave me a little stone bottle from his breast pocket, and gave me a half-
crown-1 went across to the Duke of Clarence I asked for the gin, and
gave the half-crown the prisoner gave me to Mrs. Jones--she spoke to me
about it, and told me to go behind the bar-I went, and took my two little
brothers with me--she then asked me to go into the parlour-I went across
the road to look for the prisoner, and could not find him-they then gave
me into custody.
