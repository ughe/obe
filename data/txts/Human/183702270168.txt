SAMUEL BROWN. I keep the Weymouth Arms. I supply beer at No 48, Harley-street—this pot is mine. JAMES MULLINGAN. I sweep the crossing at the end of Weymouth. street. I saw the prisoner take the pot from the door of No. 48, Harley-street—he placed it in a hag, and walked off with it—I went to the prosecutor's, and gave information, and the servant brought him back with the pot—he had got past the public-house. JOSEPH OCKLEV( police-constable D  83.) I went after the prisoner, and asked what he was going to do with the pot—he said he was going to take it to the landlord.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I picked it up, and was going to take it home—the witness ran into the public-house before I got in—I was going right past the house, but I did not know where it was. NOT GUILTY.
 853. GEORGE ALLEN, was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of February , 2 sovereigns and 2 half-sovereigns, the monies of Charles Howton Clarke . JOHN PARTRIDGE. I am shopman to Mr. Charles Howton Clarke, of Lime-street. On the 27th of February Chapman brought roe some money—I went into the shop to give him change, and found the prisoner an his hands and knees, and the till drawn out—I took hold of him, and he took two sovereigns and two half-sovereigns out of his mouth, and I took them. JAMES CHAPMAN. I saw the prisoner give the two sovereigns and two half-sovereigns out of his mouth—I took the till up, and put it into its place.
   Prisoner.  The sovereigns were in my hand—it is my first offence—I hope you will forgive me. GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined Three Months; One Week Solitary.
 854. ROBERT COOPER was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of February , 61bs. weight of beef, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Alfred Williams . JOHN DIXON. I am servant to Alfred Williams. On the 22nd of February I cut a piece of beef, and laid it on our board—I saw the prisoner take it, and wrap it in this cloth—I pursued, calling "Stop thief"—he dropped it on the stones, and I secured him. GUILTY  . Aged 20.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Nine Days.
