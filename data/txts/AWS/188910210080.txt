WHITEHEAD, Mayor.
I do not know of my own knowledge whether the prisoner received
any consideration for the promissory note beyond what Mr. Saw told me
-I do not know whether the money was borrowed to pay a bill on which
Cooper and Mr. Saw liable-I swore a joint information with Mr.
Saw against the prisoner at the Mansion House-I was asked there
whether the information was true -I never trusted Cooper; I looked to
the prisoner-I knew that the second transaction with Cooper was
no good-I wrote to him because I thought I was in duty bound to
write to all the parties on the note-I wrote to him and the prisoner to
the same address; they were living together-I posted the letters together
-I don't know whether the prisoner received his notice or not; I have
never spoken to him-I have had an action in which the prisoner was
successful I was successful in this other one-no one has got anything
in the case but the lawyers.
