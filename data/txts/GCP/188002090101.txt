Cross-examined. In making up the deficiency account, I have not
allowed anything for liquidation expenses--I cannot, because there is no
entry in the books for it; he kept no cash-book-the book debts turned
out 2211. better than they were estimated at; they were estimated at
1,1001., they realised 1,3211. 78. 3d.—the next item is a composition of
10% in the pound, 1,3811., which he did not pay; that is treating the old
debts as revived, because he failed to pay the composition-1,1601. 198. 10d.
is the amount of the unpaid composition to Mr. Garrett-Tuck and
Godfrey is 101.- I don't allow for the whole of their accounts there is
a proof of J. Tuck, sen., for 2521., subject to revision-Miss Skelton's revived
debt is 1771., the balance of her dividend; she held securities, but they
were not then realised—I have been an accountant about 20 years--my
office is in the same building as Mr. Garrett's—I did not hear the
prisoner complain that these questions had not been put to him until
eight months after his bankruptcy, and that his memory had been
shattered by his worries--I do not find the five rings in the stock sheet
- he kept no ledger-Jones's debt is 141. 108.---- he said that he had
borrowed 101. of Jones, and that he wished to hide him from being a
creditor-there are eight or ten creditors besides that, not entered on
the schedule--nearly 100 creditors are omitted-Mr. Pearce is not a creditor,
he is a debtor, a customer-I found out the eight or ten creditors four
or five months ago-I mentioned it to the solicitor-the prisoner nover
explained about the cheque for 151. from Pearce-I only knew of it from
his own accountant--the 1301. ring went out at various prices, 1152.,
127. 108., and 1301.4it was described as the same ring-I have no.
reason to disbelieve it—there were other ruby and diamond rings, but at
less prices, 301. and 401.-I have not made any inquiry as to where he got
the cats's-eye pin from--in the 2,0541. deficiency account which the debtor
claims, there is nothing about the 108. in the pound-I have allowed him
the 108. in the pound as if it was paid, and then charged it back again because
he has not paid it.
