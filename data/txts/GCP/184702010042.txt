514. GEORGE SAKER was again indicted for stealing 1 bird-cage, value
88.; the goods of Charles Johann Christian Jamrach; and that he had been
before convicted of felony.
CHARLES CLARK. I am in the service of Charles Johann Christian Jam.
rach. He is a dealer in foreign birds, and lives in St. George's street, St.
George's-in-the-East-on Wednesday evening, the 6th of Jan., I missed two
birds and a cage-the cage was worth 88.-I have seen the birds since, but
not the cage- I saw the birds at a shop in the Commercial-road-I swear
they are the birds we lost.
John Dollie. I keep Mr. Culionis' shop, in Nassau-place, Commercial.
road. I bought the two birds which Clark speaks to of the prisoner, on the
7th of Jan. - I asked him if they belonged to him-he said yes, he had
bought them of a sailor at Blackwall.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. There are a great many parrots sold
every day about Blackwall by the sailors? A. I do not know-I have been
to Black wall- I have seen parrots come from on board ship-these were nice
parrots—they were brought in a basket, not in a cage.
WILLIAM LEE. I am an officer. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's
former conviction at Clerkenwell-(read-Convicted the 5th of Nov., 1844,
and confined three months)-the prisoner is the person he has had three
months imprisonment since.
Guilty. Aged 20.- Transported for Seven Years.

515. GEORGE DAVIS and JOHN LONG were indicted for stealing
I pump, value 1l. ; the goods of Joseph Harris, and fixed to a building;-
2nd Count, not stating it to be fixed.
HENRY Hayhow. I am a plumber, and live in Vassal-road, North
Brixton. We fixed a leaden pump last Oct. for Joseph Harris, for three
dwellings adjoining the George the Fourth, at Hounslow-I am sure the pump
now produced is the one we fixed.
SARAH MILLS. This pump was brought to me by Davis, to sell, at
half past six o'clock, on the 15th of Jan.-no one was with him-I did not
buy it.
JAMES THORN (police-constable T 48.) On the 15th of Jan., about
twenty minutes to eight o'clock, I saw the two prisoners in company-Davis
was carrying this pump on his shoulder-I asked him what he had got-be
said, "Nothing"-I laid my hand on it, and said, "What do you call this?
-he dropped it, and ran away-I took him and the pump.
Davis's Defence. In coming home from Windsor I found the pump on the
pathway; I picked it up, and carried it.
DAVIS**-GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Nine Months.
LONG-Not GUILTY.
