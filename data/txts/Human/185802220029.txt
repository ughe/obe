JAMES BRANT re-examined.  I searched him, he had nothing in his pockets—his clothes were very ragged—he might have the shilling there somehow. RICHARD EDEN re-examined.  Stone had got his right hand rather behind him, and was very fidgety, that excited my suspicion—he got up, cast a side glance round, sat down again, and kept fidgeting—I told my suspicions to the constable, and he went and found the shilling in the spot where Stone had been sitting.
