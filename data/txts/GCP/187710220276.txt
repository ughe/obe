Cross-examined by Mr. SERJEANT BALLANTINE. When I first spoke to
him he said he would see me damned before we should have a farthing of
the money-I think he did not exactly know what he was saying-he did
not suggest that I was a thief, but he said he would not touch Mr. Freeth
with a barge pole-I went into the inner room because he made so much
noise-he followed me, and confessed that he was a party to the robbery,
but I do not think he knew what he was saying, he told me so many lies--
I have known him many years I do not think he had the knowledge that
it was a robbery-I do not think he looked upon it in that light-he said
the 81. he received was interest-I had no business with the 321. 128. 8d.,
but I wanted it for the iron delivered short at our works-I do not know
whether we received the full amount for which we paid-I only have to
do with the City office-I knew there had been a dispute about the amount,
and instructions were sent to me to collect it we went into liquidation
two years ago, and Mr. Parsonage bought the works and arranged with
us to carry them on and take a salary of 5001. a year, and according to
the letters we were to take it off his salary-his profits were to be 10 per
cent. on the little money that he had lent us from time to time-he was
acting as a dummy to enable us to trade-he purchased three parts of
the works--we considered ourselves his servants—we had 101. a week
regularly, which is entered in the wages book.
Cross-examined by MR. BESLEY. I was a commission agent before this
I took capital into the concern-no one has spoken to me outside since
Freeth was cross-examined, or said anything about the 101. & week--not
& soul has spoken to me, and the policeman knows it I did not try to
get the 321. 148. 10d. out of Porter or Douglas-I did not go after
Porter-he came to me,I did demand the money two or three days
after the 1st of May, and I went on demanding it till the 1st of June,
when I saw Douglas-I never saw Porter at the Lombard-he has not
complained of our weights to my knowledge-some one may have it is
one of the best weighing machines in London it has been there nine or
ten years I told Porter that there was & warrant out against him-
cannot say when. Porter and Clarks received good characters.
