- he began the conversation by asking me if I would join him in a

not say who-Thornhill was there I stated on that occasion to Bowen
and Thornhill, that I had got it of Howe, and that the brandy in the last
case, I had purchased of Everitt.

Howe? A. Yes, he knew perfectly well that it came from Mr. Goss's
stock-he had previously asked me.
