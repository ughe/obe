663. JAMES HURLEY (19), Robbery, with violence, with others,
Hermann Lopez, and stealing a watch and a cap, his property.
MR. KELLEY conducted the Prosecution.
HERMANY LOPEZ. I am a seaman, and live at 87, Pennington Street,
St. Georye's about 10.30 on 24th July I was in Ship Alley-some me
came up, knocked me down, and robbed me of my cap and watch, which
was worth 21.--the prisoner was one of them--they ran away-I ran after
them, and kept the prisoner in sight-I caught him-he kicked me, and I
gave him in custody- I have not found my watch.
COURT. Q. Had you been drinking! 4. Yes I was not drunk.
GILES PETTIT (Policeman H 23). On 24th July, about 11.20, the
prisoner was given into my custody by Lopez, who complained of being
knocked down, his watch having been stolen--the prosecutor's cap
taken, and the hat of a witness who has gone to sea-I said "If you know
the man run after him and catch him, and he caught hold of the prisoner
- I asked him if he was sure he was the person, and he said “Quite sure'
-I asked the prisoner if he had got the watch-he said no, he knew nothing
about it.
Prisoner. The policeman knows me as a hard working chap. Witmer
I have known him as working down the yard-when I went up there were
six or seven men about, but I did not see the prisoner. Nor Guilty.
664. JAMES JOHNSON (18), Stealing one watch and one chain
John Lynch, from his person.
MR. KELLEY conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN LYNCH. I am a tailor, of 30, Camomile Street-about 12 o'clock
on 31st July, I was in Duke Street the prisoner and another man followed
me-the prisoner snatched my watch and chain from my waistcoat pocket
-I followed him, and never lost sight of him till he was caught by a
policeman-this produced) is a portion of my chain-the value of the
watch and chain was thirty guineas.
Prisoner. Q. You say you never lost sight of me; what did I do with
your watch! A. I don't know, ask the man who was with you I did not
see you give him anything I was coming up Duke Street, and you were
following.
JESSE GRANT. I live at 5, Rutland Street, Whitechapel-about 11.30
o'clock, on 31st July, I was standing at the corner of the Minories, talking
to a friend-I saw the prisoner and another man, following the prosecutor
--they followed him into Duke Street-I afterwards heard cries of "Stop
thief !" and saw him running after the prisoner.
THOMAS VENESS (City Policeman 709). On Sunday night, 31st July, I
was at the corner of the Minories—I heard cries of “Stop thief l" apparently
in New Square-I went down a passage leading into New Square, and so
the prisoner running across the square towards the Minories- I stopped him,
and asked him what he was running for-he said “The boy struck me
first; the boy I was fighting with the prosecutor came up and gave him
in charge for stealing his watch and chain-I searched him at the station,
but found nothing on him-he gave a false address.
WILLIAM BALDWIN (City Policeman 735). About 2.30, on the morning
of 1st August, I was in Duke Street-I found this portion of a chain lying
in the carriage way.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going home, and the policeman took me.
GUILTY.-I'velve Months' Imprisonment.
