 215. JOHN SULLIVAN was again indicted for stealing, on the 20th of November , 1 saw, value 6s., and 1 square, value 2s., the goods of James Simpson.
 JAMES SIMPSON. I worked on the building, and lost this saw and square.
 MICHAEL COLLINS. I took the prisoner with this saw square, and the other tools.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Transported for Seven Years.
 There were two other indictments against the prisoner.
 216.  JOHN STEVENTON was indicted for embezzlement.
   RICHARD BOUCHARD. I am a publican, and am in the coal-business, in Chicksand-street. The prisoner was my weekly servant, and was employed to receive money, which he was to give me on his return.
   SARAH TIFFIN. I know the prisoner - I paid him three half-crowns and a sixpence on the 25th of November, for two sacks of coals, which he brought about eleven o'clock in the morning.
   RICHARD BOUCHARD. He never returned to his parents or to me, and never gave me any account of this money - he was away till the 3rd of December.
     GUILTY. Aged 13. - Confined Three Months.
 217.  MARY ANN TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of October , 2 sheets, value 4s.; 1 pair of earrings, value 5s.; 1 bolster, value 5s.; 2 pillows, value 3s., and 2 pillow-cases, value 1s., the goods of William Ring.
 WILLIAM RING. I live in  Greenhill's-rents, Smithfield-bars. The prisoner hired a ready furnished room at my house three weeks before she was taken; on the 28th of October, during her absence, I went into her room, and missed the articles stated - I found in the room the duplicate of a pair of ear-rings, which had belonged to my wife and had been taken from the kitchen; I went out, and gave notice to a Policeman, if he saw them about to take them; she represented herself as a married woman, and a man lived with her.
   ROBERT CUFFLIN. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Newgate-street. I have a pair of ear-rings, pawned by the prisoner on the 26th of October.
   WILLIAM IVEY. I am a pawnbroker. I have a pair of sheets, pawned by the prisoner.
   JOHN VARLEY. I have a bolster and two pillows, pawned by the prisoner.
   DENNIS HINDLE. I took the prisoner into custody - the prosecutor gave me two of the duplicates, which he found in her room.
 WILLIAM RING. These are my property.
 Prisoner. Q. How long ago is it that you had seen these ear-rings? A. It might be eight or nine months; in consequence of my father's death, they were taken out of my wife's ears to place black ones in.
 Prisoner's Defence. When I took the room I found these ear-rings and a stud laying in the room - I took them to a pawnbroker, who said they were of no value; I wore them some time, and the prosecutor and his daughter saw them, and did not claim them - the sheets I meant to redeem.
     GUILTY. Aged 31. - Confined Six Months.
 218.  WILLIAM ANSON was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of November , 34 Pence, the monies of John Fowle.
 JOHN FOWLE. I am an  oilman, and keep a shop in Anchor and Hope-alley, St. George's. On the 15th of November I was in my back premises, and saw the prisoner come into my shop, and lean over the counter; he put something into his trousers pocket - he leaned over a second time, and while he was doing so I ran up; he got out, but I caught him just outside the shop; I asked what he had taken from the till - he said he had not touched it; I took him round, and did not miss any thing from the till; I asked what he had in his pocket - he said it was nothing to me; I then recollected that I had counted 5s. worth of penny-pieces, and put them behind my counter - I went to them, and found only 2s. 2d. left; on the prisoner I found 2s. 10d., which made up the 5s. - the prisoner said he sold ducks about the street; the officer took him.
   CHARLES FALCONER. I am the officer. I took him, and have the money.
 Prisoner's Defence. He took me into the shop, said he had been robbed before, and he would make me suffer for all.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined Six Weeks and Whipped.
 219.  WILLIAM BRIGGS was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of November , 1 bed, value 1s.; 1 pillow, value 1s. 6d.; 1 bolster, value 1s.; 1 blanket, value 1s.; 1 piece of patch-work, value 1s., and 2 sheets, value 1s., the goods of William Moore.
   JAMES BARKWAY. This property was taken out of a shed adjoining my house in Wellington-place, Stepney; it belonged to Mr. Moore - it was distrained for rent, and put into my custody; I went out on the 5th of November, in consequence of information, and saw the prisoner about one hundred yards from my shed, carrying a bundle; there was an accomplice with him, but he got away - the prisoner dropped the bed, and I fell over it - I called Stop thief! the prisoner threw down the bundle.
