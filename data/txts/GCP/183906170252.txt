Cross-examined by MR. SERGEANT BOMPAS. Q. How long have you
belonged to the Police ? A. Nearly three years- I was not employed
listening at the cells-there were no other policeman in the yard--the jailor
was part of the time, and heard some part, and then went away--there was
a man who lives in the office came down to draw some water, and the noise
he made drawing prevented me hearing the whole conversation--the jailor
was there when that person came down I was requested to stay in the yard
--the jailor came to me and told me to stay in the yard to keep the door
shut-I do no know how long exactly I had been in the yard-when Caspar
said about the 14001. or 12001. Moses said something I did not hear, on
account of the noise made by the man drawing the water-he was drawing
it while I was listening- I do not know the person's name—he lives at
Lambeth-street office-I cannot exactly say how long the yard is-it is
not so long as this room-it is six or eight feet wide--the prisoners spoke
quite loud-there are no bars, so that they can look out there are four
short bars over the door--the tap is at the further end of the yard-I
cannot say exactly what time it was, it was from half past eight to half-
past nine o'clock - I think they were examined about twelve o'clock-I
waited in the yard a very short time after that conversation-a very few
minutes-I cannot say how long I was there before the conversation
began--I should say a full minute-as soon as I heard them talking, their
noise attracted my attention- I went to listen to what they were saying-
I cannot say how many policemen there were outside the yard at that time
-I cannot say how many were outside when I went out-I do not know
that there were any there they were not all there the door of the yard
is not locked, it closes with a spring-lock, which any body can open
from the inside, but nobody from the outside- I cannot say if there is a
key to open it outside-the jailor went away before I did, and came again
- he went away before this conversation was ended-I cannot say how
how long I was listening to this conversation-it might have been five
minutes- I swear it was more than one minute-I will not swear it was
five minutes-I went quite close to the door, and I went up the same day
to be examined- I have not seen my depositions since-no copy has
been given me since this was the last day they were examined-I have
had no opportunity of reading my deposition since it is the jailor's duty
to attend at the doors--he has the keys of the cell--nobody else can do
it while he retains the keys-I am not aware that there is any key to
the outer door.

been with Caspar-I heard him examined--that is all I have heard of
