   Cross-examined.  You may have asked me to take care of the place where you lived when it was cleared out.
   The prisoner, in his defence, said that a few years ago he had sunstroke. and after that a severe fever, which he continually suffered front up to  1900. that he believed that was the cause of his committing himself in this way, as he had no animosity against the prosecutor; that, while he was doing his work he felt quite sensible, but all at once a sort of darkness came over him, and he remembered nothing of what happened afterwards until he found himself in a boat, going ashore, when he was told what had happened; and that he had not been drinking. GUILTY  on the second count.
   He received a good character. Nine months' hard labour.
