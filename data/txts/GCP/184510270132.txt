2101. GEORGE HUGHES was indicted for bigamy.
Elias Brucass. I live at Brentford. The prisoner married my
daughter Mary Ann at Hanwell church rather more than twenty-three
years ago I was present and saw them married my daughter has been
living at Paddington for the last six or seven years-she is here now.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. It is not your daughter that
institutes this prosecution ? A. No, she kept out of his way--they had
not seen one another for thirteen or fourteen years-she did not trouble
him, nor he her-she never looked after him-she walked off without giv-
ing him notice.
Ellen HALL. I know the prisoner, and I know Sarah Fox-1 was
present at their marriage on the 6th of Nov., 1843, at Islington.

represented himself to me as a widower.

No-I have been living by myself these fourteen months.
