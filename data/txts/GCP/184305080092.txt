1519. JAMES MCARTHY was again indicted for assaulting John Smith,
with intent to rescue and set at large Hannah Moore, out of his custody,--
2nd Count, for a common assault.
Joen SMITH, I went to a house with Hannah Moore--I had seven sove-
reigns I went to bed, and when I awoke I missed them-I charged Moore
with stealing them--I came down, and was taking her to give her in charge
-the prisoner asked what I was going to do with her-I said to give her in
charge-he said I should not--I said I would give them both in charge; and
then he struck me above the eye, and on the nose--I had not struck him
before he struck me-I was sober then.
Thomas HENRY Ellis (police-constable K 30.) I saw Smith bleeding
very much.
Guilty of a common assault. Aged 20.-Confined One Month.
