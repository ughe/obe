1549. MARY HOLLIDAY was indicted for stealing 9 towels, value
4s. 6d. ; 1 table-cloth, 28. ; 8 pairs of stockings, 11. ; 6 gallons of wine, 4l;
30 bottles, 5s. ; 1 plate, 11d.; 1 inkstand, 6d. ; 1 pillow-case, Is.; the
goods of John Spittal Miller, her master.
Mr. Robinson conducted the Prosecution.
John SPITTAL MILLER. I live at Camberwell--the prisoner has been in
my service as cook about eighteen months. On the 29th of April, in conse-
quence of information from Ryan, I looked into my wine-cellar, and missed
nearly three dozen of port wine--the wine-cellar was kept locked- nobody
ought to have had access to it but myself and my wife, who kept the key-I
have since seen the key of my garden-gate, and find it opens the cellar-
was not aware of it before-on the 27th of April I called the prisoner up into
my room, charged her with having robbed me of my wine, and said if she
would confess wbat she had done, she might go that night-she denied having
robbed me at all, and denied having anything of mine- I called in a police
man, and she gave him the keys of her boxes-he searched them, and founds
table-cloth, towel, and inkstand, three shells, and other articles, which are my
property-no wine was found-she was present when the things were found,
and said two or three of them were hers—I identify these towels as my
property-they have no mark on them, but I know them-I have been in
the habit of seeing them for eighteen months or two years, and consider they
are mine-here is one marked "M"-I can swear to that as well as to the
others- I do not know that particular mark, but I know it is my towel-
recollect seeing this mark on it before-it is just such a mark as mine had
it is just such an "M" as other persons would have whose names begun
with "M" the towels were marked with red-this table-cloth is marked
“G.R. M."-that is my brother's mark--I know it was in my possession
this inkstand has no particular mark, but I can swear to it-it was kept 10
kitchen--this plate is mine, and this cream-jug--these stockings are my
wife's-here is a name on them which I wrote myself-the prisoner's box ww
locked.
