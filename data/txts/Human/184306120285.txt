 2066. GEORGE FREDERICK GWYTHER was again  indicted for stealing, on the   9th of February , 1 hearth-rug, value 6 s  .; and 1 account-book, 2 s  .; the goods of Harry Winterbourne and another, his masters.
   MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution. HARRY WINTERBOURNE. The prisoner was in our employ to work—he used to come to my premises generally every day—on the 7th of June I went to his room with the officer, and saw a duplicate of a hearth-rag found—this is it—I have no hesitation in saying so—it has our mark upon it—I find no entry of it—I found an account-book there, which is one of a lot I had ruled for my own business, and had not been used—there is one of the prisoner's entries in it—I have no doubt it is his handwriting.
   Prisoner. Q.  Is there not an entry of the rug in the book about five months back? A.  I have traced my books for ten months back, and can find no such entries. SOLOMON GARNETT. I searched the prisoner's premises on the 7th of June—I found a duplicate of this rug, and this account-book. WILLIAM BOYCE. I am in the employ of Reynolds and Field, pawn brokers, Stone's-end. This rug was pawned on the 9th of February—this is the duplicate I gave of it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I bought it there, and paid for it, about five months back, and likewise the book, and you will find in the book a bill that I paid 2 s . 3 d . for it. GUILTY.—( See page  484.)
