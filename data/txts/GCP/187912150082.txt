Cross-examined by MR. WRIGHT. I received the 481. 108. in notes and

statutory declaration on 16th June that my debts did not exceed 1201. -

on the 24th June I don't remember exactly whether I then returned my
debts at 4651. 16s. ld.--the declaration was false to a certain extent_the
