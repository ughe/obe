 599. WILLIAM MINTO was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of January , 8 plates, value 2 s  . 4 d  .; and 2 tea-pots, value 1 s  . 5 d  ., the goods of Elizabeth Stevens . GEORGE FREDERICK DUCKETT. I live in High-street, Marylebone, with my aunt, Elizabeth Stevens, who keeps a chandler's shop. On the 10th of January, about six o'clock at night, I heard a noise outside—I went to the door, and missed eight soup-plates and two tea-pots—I found a foreign boy at the door, who pointed to where the things had been taken from, and beckoned me to follow him—I did so, and saw the prisoner in Great Marylebone-street, and found the plates on him—I took him back, and met the policeman—I gave the prisoner into custody—these are my aunt's plates—the tea-pots have never been found. GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined One Month.
 600. JAMES PATMORE was indicted for stealing, on the   5th of January , 7 handkerchiefs, value 12 s  ., the goods of Thomas Vesper and another. GEORGE MARKHAM( police-constable K  96.)On the 5th of January, a little after eight o'clock in the evening, I saw the prisoner near some hand kerchiefs, which were outside Mr. Vesper's shop, in the Commercial-road—he drew some handkerchiefs out of the bundle—I went and asked what he had in his pocket—he said, "Nothing"—I said, "Let me feel"—he then said, "Only some handkerchiefs I have just picked up"—I took these seven handkerchiefs from his pocket ROBERT MORRILL. I am in the service of Thomas and William Vesper, pawnbrokers. These handkerchiefs are my masters'.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I saw a boy throw down these handkerchiefs—I took them up, and ran after him—I could not find him, and put them into my pocket GUILTY.* Aged 16.— Confined Six Months.
