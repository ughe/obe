WILLIAM WALTERS (Policeman M 393). I was on duty with Bannister,
and saw the prosecutor and prisoner speaking together-the prosecutor
shouted out, "Let me go "-I thought they were larking, but I saw the
prisoner throw him down and hold him down-I rushed up and pulled
him up, and money fell-the prosecutor was injured, and the doctor at
the station said that it was a very serious case--2d. was found on the
pavement, and I found 10d. on the prisoner.
HENRY BANNISTER (Policeman M 135). I was with Walters-I have
heard his evidence, and corroborate it.

CAROLINE SARAH RICE. I live at 11, Lordship Terrace, East Dulwich,
and am the post mistress there—on 9th September, about 10.30 a.m., I
was in the parlour, and saw through the window the prisoner behind the
counter-I went into the shop, and he had got his hand in the till-
I said, “What are you doing there?"-he came towards me, and I took
hold of him-he struck me a blow on my face which sent me back
against & case, and he got away-there was about 71. in the till, and
61. 10. in gold and silver was taken the prisoner was brought back,
and I am sure he is the man-I said at first that I did not think he was
the man, but afterwards I changed my opinion.
ADA OHARLOTTE STONE. I live in the same house as Mrs. Rice-1
heard her screams-I looked out of the window and saw a young man run
out-I ran after him-the prisoner is the man I have no doubt about
him-some men took up the chase and he was taken.
