and a letter signed "Mabel," which I showed to Harris and said,

(Friday, November 1.)
JOHN OTTAWAY, recalled. On June 27 I had warrant for the
arrest of Perryman. I saw him in Old Jewry, and asked him to go
to the police office there. I read the warrant, which charged him
with conspiring with Tyler and Harris to obtain money and valuable
securities by false pretences. He said, "Who had the temerity to lay
this information." I said "Upon my information the warrant was
granted." He said, "A police officer is not a very substantial person
to obtain redress from." I searched 2, Tokenhouse Buildings, and
found three letter-books containing letters, signed by Perryman,
letter-books of the Financial Observer." bundle of cheques, and
files of the "Mining Market Mirror and Mining Herald." Perryman
had no other office down to June, 1907. I found cheques on his
account at the London City and Midland Bank in favour of
Bleach; to George Harris, endorsed by Harris : to George Martin, in
the handwriting of Perryman; to C. George, endorsed by Harris, and
in his handwriting: one cheque drawn to Barnes; a number of
cheques payable to Herbert Smith and H. Smith, endorsed in Harris's
writing; one to H. Smith, endorsed by Perryman. I found the
cheques all together. There were a number of counterfoil paying in
slips in the handwriting of Mabel Long and Harris. The body of
some of the cheques is in Mabel Long's handwriting. I also found
cheques payable to Hawkes, some endorsed by him and others by
Harris ; also letter-books of Herbert Smith, containing letters written
from 43, Moorgate Street, and 27, Sun Street, many in Perryman's
handwriting, and containing a great many accounts in Perryman'
handwriting: one letter book commencing with Herbert Smith, at
Page 270, is continued in the name of H. K. Ablett, signed by
Harris. I found a large number of circulars in the name of William
Tyler and Co., H. K. Ablets, G. Martin, Chalmers and Cox, 4. M.
Noble, and Lombard Financial Corporation (signed by a stamp in the
name of A. R. Fraser). I found letter from Mrs. Butcher, addressed
to Ablett; green paper memorandum list of customers in Perryman's
writing, showing that they had been dealt with by Martin or Ablett,
including the name of Mrs. d'Altera, dealt with by Ablett, and
Mollins, dealt with by “G. M." (Martin); paying-in book of George
Martin with the Middlesex Bank, in Harris's writing. Some of the
circulars are altered in Perryman's writing. In one of H. Smith's
letter-books 30 consecutive pages are in Perryman's writing. There
