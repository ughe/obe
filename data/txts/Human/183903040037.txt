943. AMELIA SOPHIA EDWARDS was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of February , 1 half-sovereign, 2 shillings, and 4 halfpence, the monies of James Brown , her master. ELIZABETH ANSELLE. I am servant to Mr. James Brown, "of Porter-street, Long Acre—the prisoner was his servant. On the 4th of February she was sent to pay Mr. Brown's club money to the secretary, in Totten ham Court-road—I gave her 12 s . 2 d ., in one half-sovereign, two shillings, and four halfpence—she never came back—I never saw her till the Satur day morning following, when she was in custody. WILLIAM HENRY FAIRCHILD. I am secretary to the Imperial Loan Society. The money was not brought to us that evening—I do not know the prisoner. JOSEPH THOMPSON. I am an officer. I took the prisoner.
   Prisoner.  She gave me the 12 s . 2 d .—I put it in my bosom, and lost it with some money of my own—I offered to make it up—they would not take it. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Three Months.
   OLD COURT .— Thursday, March  9 th,  1839.
   First Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
