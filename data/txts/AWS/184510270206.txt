MR. HUDDLESTON conducted the Prosecution.
WILLAM SHAW. I am the son of Thomas Shaw-he is a linen-draper
the prisoner was his housemaid. On Satorday night, the 18th of Oct.,
I, with a policeman, searehed the prisoner's box- was not present, but
the policeman was, and Mr. Rogers- believe it was the prisoner's box
-it was in a room adjoining her room; which is merely used to hold boxes
and a chest of drawers-1 found in the bottom of the box this book,
which is mine- also found three lengths of new calico, containing about
ten yards-it was my father's, and had our private mark on it-I had
missed my book about a month.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. I suppose the mark was
on all the calico you sold ? A. Yes-we have not had any young men
left lately-the last who left was Mr. Thomas, about six months ago
he might have sold this calico-this is my book-the last time I saw it
it was in the bookcase.
MR. HUDDLESTON. Q. Has any other person left since the calico was
in the stock ? A. No-this was in the stock when Thomas left.

she had thrown them into a drawer-I found a chest of drawers in the
lobby, where the boxes were- found four keys in one drawer] tried

prisoner was in the station on the other charge-(se page 1033, No. 2177)

WILLIAM SHAW re-examined. Q. What was the cost of this book ?
A. I cannot remember- had not left it about that anybody might have
