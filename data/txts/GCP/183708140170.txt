SARAH CORNELL. I live at Stock, which is three miles on the other

water for his breakfast, and I saw him all that day in Stock-he did not
leave at all on Friday-be remained there nearly a fortnight-it was on
the 4th of July, that he came for the key-he was at home from the 4th of
July till the 4th of August-it was about a month ago that he came to me
for the key-I know it was the 4th of August-it was three weeks from this
time, I believe.

1989. WILLIAM HENRY HAIRS, ABRAHAM HAIRS, and
JAMES HOLGATE were indicted for stealing, on the 26th of July, 1
smock frock, value 2s., the goods of James Linsell.
JAMES LINSELL. I live in Chapel-row, Ilford. I had a smock frock
- my father's names is James- I left my 'frock against the hedge, on the
26th of July, when I went to work-I missed it about half-past five
o'clock-this is it.
GEORGE BIGGS. On Thursday, the 27th of July, I got my horse and cart,
after I received information, and drove about a mile and a half, and down
a bye lane-I saw these three prisoners- I got out and asked them if they
knew about any property--they said "No"-but I caught the two Hairs,
and they had each of them wet shirts on, and Holgate had the smock frock
-I took them into my cart, and gave them to the constable.
Holgate. I bought the smock for 1s. 3d., but I do not know the person.
WILLIAM SCARFE. I took the prisoners to the Magistrate, and the pro-
secutor came up and owned the smock.
HOLGATE-GUILTY. Aged 13.
WILLIAM HAIRS-NOT GUILTY.
ABRAHAM HAIRS-NOT GUILTY.
