 893. HARRIET GREGORY was indicted for bigamy.
   MR. CAARTEN conducted the Prosecution . MARIA GREGORY. I am the wife of John Gregory, and live at No. 38, Hart-street, Covent-garden. On 7th July, 1850, I was in the parish church of St. Ann's, Soho—the prisoner was there and was married to my son, Thomas Gregory—he is alive, I saw him a few minutes ago.
