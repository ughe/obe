SAMUEL LER (Police Sergeant II). On December 26th I saw the prisoner
at the King's Head, Abbey Street - I told him I was a police officer, and
was going to charge him with causing grievous harm to the prosecutor,
and blinding him--on the way to the station he said, "I was in a fight
at dinner time. I did not hit anyone with my stick "-he was sober-
when charged he said, “I do not know what you mean "-at the police-
court the next day on being removed to the cells he said, “I was not there
at all, I was at home with my father, and I can prove it."
CHARLES REASON (Re-examined). I am sure the prisoner is the man-
I next saw him at the Southwark Police-court in the dock-when he was
fighting I was looking at him five or ten minutes, and I saw him running
after the prosecutor.
The prisoner in his defence said that he was in doors having tea at the
time. GUILTY.--He was stated to bear a good character in his regiment.-
Eighteen Months' Hard Labour.
