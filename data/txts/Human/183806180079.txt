 1505. WILLIAM PEYTON was indieted for stealing, on the   15th of June , I handkerchief, value 3 s  ., the goods of George Melville Horton , from his person. GEORGE MELVILLE HORTON. I lived at No. 39, Hatton-garden, Be tween nine and ten o'clock, on the 15th of June, I was on Holborn-hitt—I felt a twitch at my pocket—I turned on my left, and saw the prisoner stepping from the footpath to the road—I saw him put something into his lift-hand breeches pocket—I went and took him, about two yards from the footpath—he pulled out the handkerchief, and dropped it at his feet—this mine—( looking at it )—it has my mark—I an quite certain the pri soner dropped it.

 1506. THOMAS COLLINS was indicted for stealing, on the   8th of June , 3 pewter pots, value 3 s  ., the goods of William Brown , I pewter pot, value 8 s  ., the goods of Zaccheus Wright . WILLIAM BROW. I am a publican. These pots are mine—( looking at them ,) ZACCHEUS WRIGHT. This is my pot—( looting at it )—I have lost such an one. JOSEPH THOMAS( police-constable S  53.) I found these pots on the prisoner on the 8th, under the Quadrant, at Regent-street—he had two in each hand—I asked where he lived, which he could not tell me—I saw there were different names on the. pots and took him—I found on him a counterfeit half-crown.
