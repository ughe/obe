267. JAMES BASSETT (24), Feloniously wounding Alfred
Worms.
Mr. Nolan Prosecuted.
ALFRED WORMS. I am a dealer, of 14, Leather Lane on Saturday,
January 20th, between 10.30 and 11 p.m., I was in the George public-
house--I had a quarrel with Mr. Carroll over 28.–Bassett came in, and
they left together-afterwards I met them in Baldwin's Gardens - I was
talking to Bassett's sister-Bassett ran up to me and struck nue with a
koife on my lip—both had knives—I saw the knives-I ran to the
hospital.

were not in the same bar—there is a door which is open - I went to the
back-I did not challenge you to the back to fight-I said, “Have a fair

a yard where people can bardly turn round-I said, "Jimmy, you can
stop all this row if you like " I said to your sister, "It is a nice thing
to quarrel, and your brother wants to take my life!"-all my relations
