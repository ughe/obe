a letter threatening to kill and murder her, he well knowing the contents
thereof.

the prisoner is my first cousin-last March we were keeping company,
but we were not engaged to be married; he had asked me to marry him
and I had said yes, but the death of my sister had prevented it from
being carried out-in August I saw him and told him I would have no
more to do with him since then he has been down several times to my
