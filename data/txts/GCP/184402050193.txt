762. SAMUEL SALE was indicted for stealing 10oz. weight of leather,
11d., the goods of Frederick Lennie.
Dowd
FREDERICK LENNIE, shoemaker, High-street, Bou. The prisoner worked
for me at home-I marked the materials 1 delivered him with an iron
stamp-on the 2nd Feb. he brought me some small boots-I examined
them and felt the soles of one, and found it was very soft, which it should not
be, knowing the stuff I had given bim-I said, “This is not filling enough"
- he said it was wet- I saw a stamp that was not mine-I said, "I suspect
you have been forging the mark, if I find you have I will prosecute you" -
there is one pair of soles and three pair of heels which are not mine-I had
given him out the materials for the shoes with my own mark-I let him have
fresh work, and he went away- I looked at these again and found they were
an imitation of my mark-in the evening I went with a policeman and gave
him into custody- I found all the leather that was not made up there-it was
worth about 108.

leather which you charge him with stealing? A. Between the 21st and the
22nd Jan. and the 2nd Feb.-I gave it out myself-I cannot say how much
I gave out between those times--this pair of shoes was made up by the pri-
soner with my leather--this coloured leather is different leather--he had
leather which in process of making would come to this colour.

763. JONATHAN BARTHOLOMEW was indicted for stealing 31 live:
tame fowls, price 31. 10s., the property of John Forster.
WILLIAM MASTERS (police-constable N 242.) About a quarter before three
on the morning of 1st Feb. I saw the prisoner and another person in Compton-
street, St. Paul's-place, Islington-there was another person about fifty yards
from him—the prisoner had a bag-1 asked what he had got in it—he said,
"Fowls" - they had just taken them from the Cambridge wagon-I said he
must go with me to the station-he said he would-be set the bag down, the
other took it up and carried it some distance they both followed me they
set them down, the third man came up, and he carried them a little way
they then put the bag down-the others ran away and left the prisoner - I took
him-I found twelve dead fowls and two live ones in the bag—the dead ones
were quite warm--the bag was covered with blood at the bottom-I found on
the prisoner a knife, and a piece of rope and some feathers in his hat, and a
handkerchief-he said he had been to see a shipmate, and in returning met
these men who asked if he would earn 1s., they took him to the bottom of a
lane and told him to wait there, and they brought these things to him to carry.
