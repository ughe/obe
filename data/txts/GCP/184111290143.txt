174. THOMAS BURN was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of No
vember, 1 pair of scales, value 13s. ; and 1 weight, yalue 18.; the goods of
Peter Mudie.
OLIVER ROGERS. I live with my father, John Rogers, a bootmaker, in
St. Martin's-street, Leicester-square. On the night of the 26th of November,
I was by the shop-door--I saw the prisoner unbook the scales from the
chain at Mr. Mudie's, which is next door-he put them under his arm, and
ran away with them-I went into the shop, and told Mrs. Mudie
Frederick Mudie ran out after him.
FREDERICK Mudig. I am the son of Peter Mudie. I ran after the
prisoner, and followed him as far as St. Martin's-lane, when I saw him
collared-he had the scales under his arm, which were what we lost, and
a weight in them.
Prisoner. The weight belonged to a place where I had been at work
that day. Witness. It is our own weight.
WILLIAM HILL. I am a policeman. I was on duty in New-street-I
heard a cry of “Police," and saw the prisoner stopped with the scales
under hi, arm.
Prisoner's Defence. I was at the corner of Orange-street, Leicester-
square-a man came and asked me if I conld tell where to sell the scales
I said I did not know-I asked what he wanted for them he said 48.-
I said they were not worth it to me--I had 3s. in my pocket, which I gave
him for them-I did not run-I walked slowly to the corner of New
street-the policeman saw me, and I gave myself up immediately.
