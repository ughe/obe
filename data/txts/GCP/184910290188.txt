2051. JOHN PURNELL was indicted for bigamy.
MR. WOOLLETT conducted the Prosecution.
JANE JONES. I am single, and reside at Langford, Somersetshire. I know
the prisoner and Mary Parsons--she is my cousin- I was present when they
were married-I believe it was four years ago, but I am not quite sure about
the date--I have no doubt the prisoner is the person-I believe the marriage
was at St. John's Church, Bristol, but I am not sure.
PHGBE SOPHIA LIGHT. I am a widow, and reside at Clifton. I remember
Mary Parsons being married in 1845, to a person named Purnell—the prisoner
is that person, they returned after the wedding to my house to breakfast --I
saw Mary Parsons last week.
MARY ELIZABETH Lucas. I resided at Tyleburst. I was married in
June, 1849, to the prisoner, in the name of James Richardson, at Christ
Church, Surrey-I believed at the time he was a single man; he told me so.
Prisoner. Q. In what name were we lodging at the time of our marriage ?
4. In the name of Smith; I first became acquainted with you, I believe, in
Nov., 1848, or a little before that ; I did not know you were married till after
I married you; I came to your house twice on business; I did not then see
a person who I believed was Mrs. Pure'l; I saw a person who you denied
was Mrs. Purnell; you were living with her as a wife, and had got one child
by her; I agreed to stand godmother for that child.
Court. Q. Did you live with the prisoner before you married him?
A. Yes; a short time, not longer than a month; we lived in the name of Mr.
and Mrs. Smith-that was under a promise of marriage- I did not know the
woman I saw was his wife.
