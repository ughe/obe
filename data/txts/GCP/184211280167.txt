ELIZABETH SARAH ATKINSON. I am the wife of Thomas Atkinson, of

249. CAROLINE LAMB was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of Nov.,
2 pairs of stockings, value 28., the goods of John Goss Fleay.
HENRY DALTON PATEN. I am shopman to John Goss Fleay, a linen-
draper in the Broadway, Deptford. On the 19th of Nov, the prisoner came
in with a cloak on-she asked for some Orleans-cloth of a colour which we
had not got-as she went out, I saw her unpin some stockings which were at
the door, and put them under her cloak-I followed, and found two pairs of
stockings under her cloak-she begged I would forgive her, and not take her
back- I took her back-these are the stockings.Y OOT YHAY.se
