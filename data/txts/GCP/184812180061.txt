294. FRANCIS MACERS and ELIZA STEVENS. stealing 1 scarf
value 10s. and 37 sovereigns, the property of Thomas Butcher, in his dwelling-
house.
MARIA ANN BUTCHER. I am the wife of Thomas Butcher; we live at 49,
Ironmonger-row. The prisoners came and lodged in my first-floor about five
months ago-they passed as man and wife my bed-room is on the same
floor with theirs- I went out of town for my health in Sept.--I left my keys
with Stevens-the places were all open-my business is the India-rubber and
brace work--the prisoners left me three weeks last Friday afterwards
missed a scarf, which had been kept in my bed-room, and 37 sovereigns in a
purse from my drawer-I went to Stevens with a policeman-he asked her
about the scarf, and it was found in her box in my presence-the prisoners
had the appearance of having a great deal more furniture than they had when
they lodged with me--they had scarcely anything when with me, and they
were old things--the reason they assigned for leaving me was that they wanted
a coalcellar-we said we would let them have one for 3d. a week more
Macers said he should lose as much as he gained by having to pay 3d.i
8-week more--I was told they paid 88. a-week where they went to--they
paid us 28. 8-week,
