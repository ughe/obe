here--I did not give him 58. at a time, only 1s. 6d.—Rumble is not wanted
on another charge, but the prisoners said so to get him into custody, and

Cross-examined by Mr. M. WILLIAMS. I have heard that Rumble was in
custody a week or so ago for stabbing ; I have heard since it was trumped
up- I have not been to the station to ask I have heard this morning that
two officers have been subpoenaed to be here--I have given no money except
1s 6d. or 2s. at a time for locomotion-I can't tell you how much altogether,
I kept no account; but Mr. Field gave me 201., which has all gone for the
witnesses and myself—I have been running all over England, and have
spent 51. or 101. more than the 201.-I expect that to come to me by-and-
by-I do not know how much Brockwell has had –Castle told me he had
had wax transactions with Jewell—he produced two receipts relating to wex,
but they had nothing to do with Jewell--I did not search King's premises,
but he showed us the wax he had bought of Jewell; some was in the store-
room, and some at the top of the house, in an ante-room-there was a bed-
stead in the room with only a mattrass on it, and I question whether it had
been slept on for some time there was a quantity of wax in the room-
Mr. Field asked to see his books, and he produced the invoices.
Re-examined. There are nearly forty-one witnesses in this case-it began
April, and there were eight or nine remands before the Magistrate.
MR. SLEIGH to J. K. FIELD. Q. Did you buy any wax of Mr. King for

bought wax of him at ls. 5fd., of the same quality that Creome gave
16. 8d. for.

live opposite Ralph and Soott-I know Mrs. Castle and George Castle-in
the latter part of last year I saw Mrs. Scott go out, with a bundle under her
arm and a shawl over it—that happened three or four times a week-I
have been to Mrs. Castle's to clean, and have seen Mrs. Scott there-I saw
her there on the Saturday that George Castle was married-she brought in
