EDWARD BARTON. I am a greengrocer, at Camberwell. On 8th April I
came from my house with a horse and cart, and left them at Compter-street,
Borough-market; I went backwards and forwards, and the third time I found
they were gone-my name was on a board nailed on the right-hand side-I
received information, and saw it at the green-yard with everything in it the
name was pulled off-the horse was worth 5l. and the cart 31.- I do not know
the prisoner.

1420. WILLIAM PAYNE, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 28. 6d., the
goods of Matthias Lingford, from his person.
MATTHIAS LINGPORD. I am warehouseman at 3, Devonshire-place, Old
Kent-road. On 26th April, between seven and eight o'clock, I was in the
Dover-road, felt a pull at my pocket, and missed my handkerchief--I saw
the prisoner, followed him, and saw him drop it—this is it (produced) it is
mine-I picked it up within ten yards of Kim-I kept him in sight-he was
secured.
ALEXANDER M'KAY (policeman, M 265.) I saw the prisoner running, and
Lingford behind him; I stopped him he said, "What have I done?"-I said,
"I will see about that directly" he tried to get away-I received this
handkerchief from Lingford.
