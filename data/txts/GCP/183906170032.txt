1761. JAMES WOODS was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of
May, 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of Isaac Wilkinson, from his
person, and that he had been before convicted of felony.
ISAAC WILKINSON. On the 13th of May I was in Duck-lane, West-
minster, walking along I missed my handkerchief-I did not feel any
one take it. I went back, and Furber said something to me--I have not
found the handkerchief-I know nothing of the prisoner--I had my hand-
kerchief just before it was at hall-past eight o'clock in the morning.
SAMUEL FURBER. I am a firewood-cutter, and live in Duck-laner
saw the prosecutor coming by, at half-past eight o'clock in the morning,
and saw the prisoner take a handkerchief out of his pocket, and cross over
-I do not know what he did with it I am sure he is the boy-I had
seen him there with other boys at the corner-before I went in and told
my father, and he told Mr. Wilkinson.
