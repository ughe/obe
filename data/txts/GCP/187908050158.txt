JAMES BADGER (Detective B). I was with the two last witnesses, and
saw the three prisoners together-I saw Wyatt leave Sheen and Smith
and go in the direction of the Clarence when they came out I followed
them with Shoobridge, and stopped them in the Meyrick Road-I took
Sheen and searched him, I found on him a counterfeit shilling wrapped
in paper and two half-pence.
WILLIAM WEBSTER (Inspector at the Mint). All these shillings are bad.
Wyatt's Defence. I passed a shilling, but could not say whether it was
bad or not.
Smith's Defence. I work for a fish-salesman in Billingsate- I went
into Mr. Sharman's to ask for some ale, and after that Sheen stops me to
ask if I had a lucifer—I put my hand in my pocket and gave it him;
two detectives came up, and said they wanted me for passing bad money
-I know nothing about it-I have been five years at work in one place,
Sheen's Defence. I left my place at a greengrocer's shop in Lambeth
on account of his son coming up from the country. I had been & work
there since 28th June. On Monday morning I went down to Kingston,
and then I went to Wandsworth, where I changed a 28. piece, and got
18. 9d. change. I went as far as the Currey Road, when I was stopped.
I had 8. 1d. left, having spent 8d. more. I asked Smith for a lucifer,
when Detective Badger came up and said, “I put your father away for
10 years, and now I will put the son away." I can swear my father never
done anything of the sort in his life..
Guilty. Wyatt also PLEADED GUILTY** to having been convicted at Now-
ington in July, 1878. WYATT-Two Years' Imprisonment. SMITH
and SHEEN-Twelve Months' Imprisonment each.
