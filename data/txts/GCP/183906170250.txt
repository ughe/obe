Cross-examined by MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. You spied Alice Abrahams?
A. No, I did not spy her, but I saw her cross -I did not see her make a
statement, she was up there for the purpose of making a statement-
saw Davis that day-I am not aware that I saw Davis after that I have
not seen him since Moses was in custody in Bow-street-I saw him there,
and Lea knew he was there-he was not taken.
