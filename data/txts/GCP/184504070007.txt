David DULLER (police-constable D 196.) I have a sheet which was
given up to me by a person named Burridge, shopman to Mr. Williams,
a pawnbroker-the prisoner was given into my custody at Paddington, on
the 11th of March - I told her what I took her for she said she knew
nothing of any of the property but the sheet, and that she had pledged at
Williams's, in the Broadway, Westminster she said that her mistress did
not allow her any coals, and she did it for the purpose of getting coals to
make a fire.
