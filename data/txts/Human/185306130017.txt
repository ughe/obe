I was on duty on 19th May, about half past 5 o'clock in the morning; Goldsmith came and showed me a crown piece; it was had—this is it—I went with her to No. 4, Tottenham-place; I there found the prisoner in bed—I held up the crown piece to him, and said, "Do you know anything of this?"—he said, "All right; I will go down with you"—he came down with me, and I took him to the station—as soon as he got in, I saw him stoop—there was a door close by him—I heard the link of money—I immediately turned round, and looked behind the door, and there found these two crown pieces ( produced )—when I found them, I said to the prisoner, "Halloo, George, you are getting rid of them!"—he did not say anything to that—I searched him, and found on him one bad crown piece ( produced )—I told the inspector in the presence of the prisoner what had occurred.
