1049. CHARLES PHILLIPS was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of Feb ., 25lbs. weight of cheese, value 1 l  ., the goods of William Knight . WILLIAM JOHN KNIGHT. I am in the service of my father, William Knight, who lives in the Strand—I saw this cheese at the door on the 24th of Feb., a little before half-past six o'clock—I then missed it, and ran out—Isaw the prisoner running with it under his arm—I pursued—he turned up a street and dropped it at the corner—he ran a few yards, and I caught him, and gave him to the officer.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  It was in the evening? A.  Yes—the place is rather crowded occasionally—I am quite certain as to the pri soner's person—I never was further than three yards from him all the time. RICHARD LATTER( police-constable F  51.) About half-past six o'clock that evening I was in Southampton-street—I saw the prisoner running towards me—Mr. Knight caught him—I took him, and have the cheese.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Are you acquainted with the Hammersmith Pett ? A.  Yes—I saw him at the Police-office, and he gave evidence against the prisoner—I heard him take his oath as to somethings he saw—he said the prisoner did not prig  it—after some time the Magistrate struck out his evi dence—I heard that he afterwards made a different statement—t did not make any inquiries into the Pet's  character—it did not want inquiries—I knew him—I heard him tell a story about the prisoner carrying the cheese away. GUILTY. Aged 31.— Confined Three Months .
 1050. CHARLES MURRAY was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of Feb ., 1 pocket-book, value 1 s  .; 2 sovereigns; 5 shillings; 2 sixpences; and 1 5 l  . Bank-note; the property of Patrick Hanton , from his person. PATRICK HANTON. I am baker on board the Solway  steam-vessel, board to the West Indies—she was lying in a place called Pilcher's Dock, opposite Black wall-stairs. On the 15th of Feb. I was in High-street, Poplar—I was intoxicated, and was taken to the prisoner's house, I think about nine or ten at night—when I was sober I had a 5 l . note, two sovereigns, and some silver—the note and sovereigns were in my pocket-book—I did not sleep above ten minutes or a quarter of an hour in his house, for I was taken to the station, and locked up for being drunk—I did not miss my money till the morning—the policeman went to some house, and got the remainder of the money—it is not likely I should have given it to the prisoner.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  You were drinking with a man of the name of Boatswain? A.  Yes—that was before I went to the prisoner—I was a good while with him; he took me to the prisoner to put me to bed there. ELIZA MASTERS. I live in Red Lion-court, Poplar—I was sitting by the fire with the prisoner on the night of the 15th of Feb.—Boatswain brought the prosecutor, and asked me to give him a bed; I said I would if he would pay me—Boatswain said he was going to sail the next day, and it would be all right—I told him to take the candle and go into the adjoining room; they went, and I saw him no more—next morning Boatswain brought me a 5 l . note—Boatswain got it changed at the Commercial public-house tap, and he gave me the change to take care of.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did not Boatswain say he was a shipmate of the young man, and he would make it all right? A.  Yes. THOMAS WATKINS( police-constable K  310.) I told the prisoner I wanted him on a charge of stealing a 5 l . note and two sovereigns—he said "Boat-swain did it, and gave me the money." NOT GUILTY.
