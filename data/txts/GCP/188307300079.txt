CHARLES BRETTLE. I am a provision dealer, of Forest Hill-I lost a
cheque-book about January with a cheque in it signed by my son-in-law,
but not filled up—I did not write anything on it, or authorise any one to
do so- I gave information at the bank.
JAMES NICHOLLS. I am a dealer, of Charlton Place, Deptford about

February 4th)-he said that he took it of a gentleman for some work, and
would I change it for him-he owed me money, and I gave him 258. and
took the cheque I took it to the Forest Hill branch bank, and they told

Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I have lent you money, and you always
paid me-you could have had 201. of me that night if you liked we have
had dealings since this cheque-I always found you honest.
FREDERICK FORTH (Police Sergeant R). On 30th February I served a
summons on the prisoner; I read it to him-he said, “All right, I can
prove where I got it from” -he was committed for trial at the April
Sessions, and admitted to bail-he surrendered, and the case was post-
poned to the May Sessions, when he was let out on bail again, and did
not surrender-& certificate of the indictment was issued, and I appre-
hended him when at Deptford last Monday morning-I read the warrant
to him-he said, "All right.”
