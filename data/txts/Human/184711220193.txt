   Cross-examined by MR. BALANTINE. Q.  I suppose hemp of other kinds might be of that value? A.  Yes—my foreman who made it is in court—I charged the market price—it was 2 s . extra, in consequence of the advance in hemp—Seager brought the current lists into the inner office, to induce Gardiner and Holliday to give me the 2 s . a cwt. in advance on the second order. JOHN PEARCE. On 4th Aug. I took ten packages to the Custom-house wharf; and on the 14th, ten; on 20th, twenty; and on 24th, twelve to Fresh wharf. GEORGE WILLIAMS re-examined.  On 6th Aug., ten packages were taken from the Custom-house wharf by Fisher's lighter THOMAS CLARK. I am export-clerk at the Custom-house quay. On 6th Aug., young Cusell brought an order for ten coils of rope—Fisher took them away. JOSEPH FISHER. I am a lighterman. On 6th Aug. I received ten coils of rope at the Custom-house quay—they put on board my craft—a person representing himself as young Cusell gave me directions—they were to be taken to Frost and Scheider's, at Wapping—I did not see them afterwards—I made out a bill for my expenses, at his direction, to James Ellis and Co. WILLIAM FROST re-examined.  On 6th Aug. I bought some rope of Cusell, at 36 s . a cwt.—I paid him and his son. JAMES ROBINS. I am a clerk at Fresh wharf. On 16th Aug. fourteen packages were delivered there—Mr. Glass, my fellow-clerk, received the delivery order, which was from Gardiner and Holliday to Cusell—I do not know where they went to—fourteen coils went on 20th, and twelve came in on 25th. PETER GLASS. I am a clerk, at Fresh wharf. On 20th Aug. I received a delivery order for fourteen packages of rope from Gardiner and Holliday to Cusell—I afterwards received one signed Cusell, directing me to deliver them to the bearer—on 20th Aug., fourteen were removed—on 24th, twenty packages were delivered at Fresh Wharf, and I received an order for them from Gardiner and Holliday to Cusell—on 1st Sept. I received a delivery order from Gardiner and Holliday to a person named D'John, and another from D'John to bearer for the packages—I only know of one man who took them away, that was Hazell.
