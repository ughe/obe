EMMA ELLARD was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of No vember , 1 workbox, value 1 l  .; 2 necklaces, value 15 s  .; 1 pair of ear-rings, value 15 s  .; 1 brooch, value 10 s  .; 1 bonnet, value 5 s  .; 13 sovereigns, 2 half-sovereigns, 19 shillings, and 1 sixpence; the property of James Wall , her master, in his dwelling-house; to which she pleaded GUILTY.— Transported for Ten Years.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, DECEMBER  16 TH , 1839.
