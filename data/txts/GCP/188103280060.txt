HENRY MORRIS. I am a solicitor of 1, Aldermanbury Postern--Big-
genden was my copying clerk and messenger-I paid him 11. a week,
which was a good deal more than he was worth, for he was worth-nothing:
I know his writing perfectly well; this is it in this identification book as
identifying Day.
Cross-examined by MR. PUROELL. I was clerk to his father for many
years; he was a highly respectable solicitor, and practised for forty years
I promised him on his death-bed that I would look after his son, as he
was quite incompetent to take care of himself-he is worthless as a lawyer
- he is half-witted, but I always found him honest and truthful he is just
the man that would be imposed upon.
Re-examined. He is not much better than half-witted-I don't believe
he knows the difference between an affidavit and a statement of claim-
he went up for only one examination, and was pluoked eight or twelve
times before he got through-he did get through.
MARTIN ROLANDSON WALTERS. I am a clerk in the Bank of England-
on 15th November, 1879, I cashed this cheque for 877. 98. 3d., and gave
for it six 101. notes, 3673 to 8678 consecutively, dated 19th April, 1879,
four 51. notes, 33571 to 33574 consecutively, dated 22nd April, 1879, and
72. 98. 8d. in cash.
WILLIAM JAMES READ. I am chief clerk in the London and County
Bank, Hackney-Hattersley had an account there in November, 1879,
and on 15th November he paid in six 101. notes, 3673 to 3678, dated 19th
April, 1879, and four 51. notes, 33571 to 33574—this is the paying-in slip
(produced)-& cheque was paid in on 11th November for 271. 108. in
favour of Preston, drawn by Hattersley on his account.
CHARLES JOHN WILLIAMS. I am a clerk in the Bank of England-I
produce six 101. notes, 3673 to 3678, and four 51. notes, 33571 to 83574,
