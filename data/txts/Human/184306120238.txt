2012. ELLEN LYNNES  , alias    Welch, was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of May , 2 half-crowns, and 1 sixpence, the monies of George Quigley , from the person of Mary Quigley . MARY QUIGLEY. I am the wife of George Quigley, a sergeant of the Marines, at Woolwich. On the 27th of May, I was standing in the market-place, and felt something—I turned round and saw the prisoner behind me—she lifted her hands from her side and folded them together—I took no notice, but felt her again at my pocket—I turned round, and she was close behind me, but went away—I missed two half-crowns, a shilling, and sixpence—there was another girl behind her. NOT GUILTY.

ELIZABETH FROST.  2014. JOHN LANNAGAN was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of May , 2 shirts, value 3 s  ., the goods of Spencer Martin . SPENCER MARTIN. I live in Old Kent-road—these shirts hang on a hedge in the garden to dry—I received information, and saw the prisoner running in the field—I saw him drop the shirts—they were picked up in my presence—I gave him in charge. GEORGE GULBRIDGE. I saw the prisoner in the field—I took up the shirts—I did not see him drop them. ELIZABETH MARTIN. I hung the shirts on the hedge, and I afterwards looked out of window, as the wind blew, and saw they were not there—I saw the prisoner come out of the water-closet, endeavouring to hide them under his coat—he asked for some bread—I said I would give him some—I went to call my son—when I returned he was gone—I saw him drop them in the field. GUILTY. Aged 55.— Confined Three Months .
