STEPHEN EMMERTON (Policeman K 483). On the 15th July, I received
directions from Caunt and other persons to look for the prisoner-Caunt

the House of Correction.
Cross-examined. Q. You went to identify him on behalf of Caunt 2 A.
I did; I did not identify another man first-my attention was called to

myself-Caunt said that the man was five feet six or seven inches, and that
he wore a dark coat and a round felt hat-that was the description he
gave me.
