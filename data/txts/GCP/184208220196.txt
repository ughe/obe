2467. WILLIAM PRICE and ALEXANDER DUNCAN were in
dicted for stealing, on the 18th of August, 2 pairs of shoes, value 5s., the
goods of Sarah Ann Silverside and another.
JANE SILVERSIDE. I keep a shop with my sister Sarah Ann Silverside
in Clerkenwell. On the 18th of August I saw the two prisoners near the
door, and soon afterwards I saw them go away-I got up and missed two
pairs of shoes--I saw the prisoners runnirg-one of them dropped the
shoes- I took them up-they are ours.
Cross-examined by Mr. Doane. Q. Are you sure Duncan was one
of them? A. Yes.
CHARLES DENIS. I heard a cry of “Stop thief," and saw the two pri-
soners running-Duncan threw down these shoes--I stopped him-he said
he had nothing to do with the shoes.
Price's Defence. I know nothing about them; I was not with Duncan
when they were taken.

2468. CATHERINE KELLY was indicted for stealing, on the 11th
of August, 1 pewter pot, value 18., the goods of Thomas Evan Gamble.
WILLIAM HAYNES. I live with Thomas Evan Gamble, a publican.
This is his pot-the prisoner came to the shop, on the 11th of August, and
had a pint of beer there.
THOMAS Wells (police-constable E 130.) I saw the prisoner in Ox.
ford-market, picking bones and rags-she had this pot in her basket.
Prisoner. I had had a pint of beer, and was going to take the pot
back. Witness. She was a mile and a half from Mr. Gamble's.
GUILTY. Aged 27.-Confined Three Months.
