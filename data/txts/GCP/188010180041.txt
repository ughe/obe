MR. PUROELL Prosecuted.
CHARLES TURNER. I am a cooper, of 29, Kinnerton Street, Pimlico-
I have known the prisoner about three months- I had lent him 108., and on
20th August upon my return to my place of business I found him waiting
for me-he said he wished to pay me, and said "I have not sufficient change
in my pocket, but I have a cheque from Mr. Watkins, who is a very large
tradesman in Battersea Park Road "-the cheque was for 31. 158. 6d. I
said "I have not sufficient cash; I have only about 21." he said " That
is not sufficient, as I am leaving here for Brighton to-morrow morning with
my wife and two children" I said that I could do no better the waited
some time, and asked if I could get a friend to cash it-I took him to
Mr. Coles, who cashed it-I handed the money over with the exception
of the 108.-—the cheque was on the West London Commercial Bank,
Sloane Square, by J. W. Watkins, in favour of Henry James, and dated
20th August-it was already endorsed-it was brought back to me next
morning by Mr. Coles, and we went to look for the prisoner at 22, Perk
Walk, Chelsea, at the address he had given-we did not find him there,
but I previously saw him in Kinnerton Street talking to Mrs. Hubbard
-I beckoned, and he held up his hand and said, "I am coming "-I
turned round, and when I turned back he was gone, and I did not see
him again till he was in custody-after that somebody called and wanted
me to make it all right-he gave no name--Mr. Coles kept the cheque.
