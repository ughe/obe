1736. MARY ANN DEVINE was indicted for stealing 1 watch, value 4 l  .; the goods of William Stubbs , from his person: and ANN WADE for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing it to have been stolen.
   DEVINE  pleaded— GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Nine Months . WILLIAM STUBBS. I live in Paragon-wall, Bermondsey. I was coming home at half-past twelve o'clock at night, on the 2nd of Aug.—I met a female, but I should not know her again—I had had rather more than I should—the woman promised to see me across the fields, and after a few paces she ran away—I directly felt, and found my watch was gone—this is it. ROBERT DEIGHTON. I am a pawnbroker. This watch was offered in pawn by Wade about twelve o'clock on Monday, the 3rd of Aug.—she said it was her brother's—she wanted 28 s . for it—while we were looking at it, she went oot of the shop and joined Devine—they were both given into custody—this handkerchief was picked up in the passage of the shop after they were taken. WILLIAM PARKER( police-constable M  255.) The prisoners were given into my charge—Devine said she had got the watch from a man on Saodaj night.
   WADE  — NOT GUILTY.

I am bar-maid to Mr. George Tranent—he keeps the French Horn, at Lambeth-walk. On the morning of the 3rd of Aug. I saw the prisoner behind the counter—the till was open, and his hand was in it—he had no business there—I ran to catch him—he got off, and I called the con stable Goose—he took him—I examined the till, and found it empty—there ought to have been eight sixpences and three fourpenny pieces in it.
   Prisoner.  She never called any policeman at all; she never saw me in tht house. Witness.  I found him behind the counter and bis hand in the till—he threw the money down. THOMAS GOOSE( police-constable L  160.) SARAH WEDDELL. I manage the business of Mr. Tranent's house. I had left eight sixpences and three 4 d . pieces in the till the night before.
   Prisoner's Defence.  It is false; I never was near the bouse. RICHARD ARTHUR( police-constable L  26.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction at Newington—( read—Convicted  13 th of April  1845; confined three months and whipped )—the prisoner is the person.
   Prisoner.  I am not the person. Witness.  I have not a doubt of it—I the officer who took him—I had known him before—I am sure he is the person. GUILTY † Aged 19.— Transported for Seven Years .
