GEORGE SMITH. I was at Hammersmith on the 19th-I saw the pro-
secutor take hold of the prisoner--the prisoner, and three more men
before that, surrounded Mr. Champion-one of them had hold of his arm.
and the prisoner knocked his hat over his eyes with his left hand, and
drew his watch out with his right-Mr. Champion caught hold of him, and
said, “You have got my watch "- the prisoner knocked his hat uft into
the road ; and after that, one of his companions came up, got the watch
out of the prisoner's hand, and ran away with it into the crowd-I saw
him take it out of his hand I had seen the prisoner take it out of Mr.
Champion's pocket-he kept it down in front of him.
Cross-examined. Q. Were you one of the procession ? A. Yes, I was going
to hear Mr. Hume make a speech I am a groom-I had nothing to do at
the time I was not in regular employ-I had left work about nine o'clock
that morning I was before the magistrate there was not an enormous
crowd the place was rather clear-Mr. Hume was just coming down
the prosecutor was surrounded by four persons--there were not many
people just at the time-not just by- I told the magistrate I saw the pri-
soner draw the watch out of the prosecutor's pocket--that was taken
down in writing, and read over to me I told him I saw another person
take it from him-I believe it was taken down, but I am no scholar.
DAVID HENRY DOWNEY. I was in the crowd at Hammersmith close to
Mr. Champion-it appeared there was a crowd round him--the prisoner
came up to him, knocked his hat over his eyes, then unbuttoned his coat,
and took something out-I could not see what he put his hand down by
his side-another man came up and took something out of the prisoner's
hand, and ran away--the crowd was so round Mr. Champion, I could not
see what was in his hand.
Cross-examined. Q. About how many people were round him? A. I
cannot say I should think about two or three dozen. de
TIMOTHY WINKUP. I am a policeman. The prisoner was given into
my custody by Mr. Maycock, of Hammersmith, on this charge--I found
no watch on him-Mr. Champion, I believe, had given him in charge be-
fore that I did not see him and Mr. Champion together--two men had
him.
Prisoner's Defence. I know no more about the concern than a child
unborn-I never saw the watch.
