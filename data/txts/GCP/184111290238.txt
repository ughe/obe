velvet, value 141., the goods of James Dickons Saltmer, well knowing it
to have been stolen ; against the Statute, &c.
Mr. Bodkin conducted the Prosecution.
Richard Evans. I am warehouseman, in the employ of Mr. Saltmer,
of Friday-street, Cheapside-the witness, M'Knight, was taken into cus-
tody, and made a statement. On the 19th of November I went to the
prisoner's shop, No. 2, Cumberland-row, Newington-butts, accompanied
by M.Knight and Mr. M'Lean--it is a second-hand clothes' shop-I and
Mr. Knight went into the shop together-I had a parcel--the prisoner

immediately went out-he was lame, and walked with a stick-on
