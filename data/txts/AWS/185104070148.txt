SURREY CASES.
Before Mr. Justice Maule.
1003. THOMAS STEVENS, robbery with violence, upon Benjamin
Parrott, and stealing 1 half-sovereign and 2 half-crowns, his moneys.
BENJAMIN PARROTT. On 31st March, between twelve and one o'clock
in the morning, I went to the Fishmongers' Arms-I came out about two
hours afterwards- had been drinking a little-I was not drunk-I knew
perfectly well what I was doing-1 had had two or three drinks of stout
and two glasses of rum-I went out to make water, and as I was returing
in again the prisoner caught me by the coat and waisteoat, and struck me
on the forehead- then received a kick over the eye which took away
my senses-I recollect nothing more-I had never seen the prisoner
before.
EMMA BURRELL. I am the prosecutor's sister-he went out with my
husband, about one o'clock on Monday morning, and finding they were
gone a long time, my sister and I went out after them to the Fishmongers'
Arms, and there found them drinking stout-between three and four my
brother went outside the house, and seeing him the worse for liquory I
followed him and saw him knocked down by the prisoner-I instantly
seized the prisoner by the hair and took his hand from my brother's right
hand pocket, it was clenched-I did not leave go of his hair till the policeman
came and loosened -there were several of the prisoner's associates round
him, and I received a blow in the mouth-I did not know him before-
he did not appear drunk.
PATRICK STACK (policeman L 123). The prisoner was given into my
custody by Mrs. Burrell-she had hold of him by the hair when I came
up-I took him a short distance, and found that the prosecutor was kept
back by a lot of the prisoner's companions-he told me that the prisoner
had robbed him-he was drunk-I told him at the station to search his
pockets and see what money he had lost-he said he had lost a balf-
sovereign, and afterwards he said he had lost some more-he could not
say how much-next day, before the Magistrate, he said he had lost two
half.crowns-he had a severe wound on the left eye, which was bleeding
very much.

both on the ground ? A. Yes you said, 4 Take me away from them,'
and so I did, and took you to the station.
