840. JAMES MACKEY, JAMES WRIGHT, and DAVID LYNCH,
were indicted for stealing 84lbs. weight of coals, value 10d., the goods of
Andrew Spottiswoode; and that Wright had been before convicted of
felony, to which
LYNCH pleaded Guilty. Aged 11.-Whipped, and discharged.
Austin EDWARD GANS (Thames police-constable No. 68.) I was on
shore at Vauxhall, near the gas-works, on the 22nd of Feb., about half-past
one o'clock in the afternoon-I saw the three prisoners go down a draw-dock
close to the gas-works-they went directly to a coal-barge called the Glasgow,
belonging to Mr. King--the property in it belonged to Mr. Spottiswood-I
saw Mackey looking about him-he then jumped on the gunwale of the
barge, took a large piece of coal, and put it by the side of the barge-he got
up a second time, and took another large piece-he got up a third time, and
then Lynch lifted Wright up, and they both took a piece, chucked them out of
the barge, and deposited them alongside of the barge-Mackey and Wright
got up a second time, both together, and took another piece—they then each
took a piece under their arms-Iran round the barge, and took Mackey-the
other two were taken by another officer.
Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q. Did you hear Mackey say he had
met these boys, and been induced to go with them? A. Yes.
JOHN WILLIAM TAYLOR. I was with Gans, and assisted in taking the
prisoners.
GEORGE YOUNG. I am clerk to Mr. Andrew Spottiswoode, a coal-merchant,
at Vauxhall--the coals in the Glasgow barge belonged to him.
__THOMAS LOCKYER (police-constable L 137.) I produce a certificate of
Wright's former conviction at this Court (read-Convicted 15th June, 1846,
and confined three months)- I had known him four times summarily convicted
previous to that.
(The prisoner Mackey received a good character.)
MACKEY-GUILTY. Aged 13.- Whipped, and discharged.
WRIGHT-GUILTY. Aged 11.-Confined One Month, and Whipped.
