I had considerable doubt as to the Counts based on the letter of July 28th. I have no doubt, if I may say so, that if there had been sufficient evidence of the receipt of an earlier letter by Broeksma, the case would be different, but the only reference to the earlier letter is in the letter of August 6th, and on the evidence before me, I cannot put the letter of July 28th on a higher position than the letters of August 6th. I am of opinion that the Counts alleging an offence under the Statute must be withdrawn from the Jury. No question of law arises as to the Counts alleging the attempt, and they must go before the Jury." COLONEL DAVIES( Re-examined by  Mr. ISAACS ).
