Second Count-on 3d January, 12 gallons of sherry, 4 gallons of brandy,
and 96 bottles and corks, value 211. Third Count--on 25th January, 1
cask, value 9s. 6d., of John Jones, his master.
MESSRS. GIFFARD and POLAND conducted the Prosecution.

man in Mr. Jones's employ-I was formerly in the prisoner's employ-I
remember some gin being fetched from Messrs. Booth's by a man named
Seares-it was at the latter end of December, but I do not remember the
day-it was brought into the cellar, and I packed it the same day-I
