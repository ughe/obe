I then found Burry in the custody of Stevens and they were taken

Cross-examined by Burry. I was about 12 yards away, right op-
posite you, when the affair happened. I was in front of you in Com.
mercial Road, and in Berner Street I was behind you. I might have
said to Stevens, “Where is the 'pros 'I" I asked prosecutor what
he had lost. He said, "I have lost my bacoa and some silver."
Police officers came up from Commercial Road when they heard the
whistle blow. When we got to the station the Inspector sent me and
the other witnesses out of the charge room. I do not know what pro.
secutor said to the Inspector.
Cross-examined by Brady. I was the first one to come across the
road to you. Stevens was close behind me. The women stood
the corner of Fairclough Street: they were dumbfounded. Stevens
said nothing about “Go for him." I only had my whistle in my
hand. You tried to dodge the constable when he caught you.
(Defence.)
TIMOTHY BURRY (prisoner, not on oath). On this night I was walk
ing towards home through Berner Street, when all of sudden I was
knocked down, and a man says, "Come on, get up." I said, " What
is the matter 1" He says, "All right, you will do," and picked me
up, taking me to Commercial Road, where he said to prosecutor,
"Have you lost anything 1" Prosecutor says, "No, I have lost
nothing." When we were taken to the station he said, "I have lost
nothing; what am I here for 1" Stevens says, "Oh, it's all right,
you will see." When charged at Arbour Square the magistrato asked
prosecutor, "Can you identify the man that put his hand in your
pocket 1". He said, "No." What Stevens says about the souffle is
a lot of lies.
Jown BRADY (prisoner, not on oath). On & Saturday night I was
walking along Äldgate. I had just left & friend and was going
towards home when I met this man whom I know. He was talking
to another man outside the "Wheatshef." We walked towards Com
mercial Road and towards the fire station. When there we saw pro
secutor and a friend with two women. Prosecutor was very drunk
and we were all looking at him. They walked along and we never
took any more notice of them. Afterwards we walked past them
saw a half-crown dropped on the ground, which Burry picked up.
and as soon as he did so he was knocked over by someone. Then
prosecutor and the others ran away, and the witness Finsong rushed
at me with something. I ran off, and then I was stopped by a con
stable. First of all prosecutor said he had lost nothing; then at the
station that somebody came behind him and put his hand in his
trousers pocket, and took out some pieces of silver; then he said it
was his 'bacca pouch and two knives.
Verdiet: Both, Guilty. Brady confessed to a previous conviction
on November 20, 1906. Other convictions were proved against both
men.
Sentences : Each prisoner, 20 months' hard labour.
