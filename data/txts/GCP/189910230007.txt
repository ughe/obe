651. WILLIAM STUART and JOSEPH WALLACE PAGE,
Conspiring to procure Alfred Piper to commit an offence against the
Official Secrets Act, 1889.
MR. MATHEWS and Mr. Bodkin Prosecuted; Mr. AVORY appeared for
Page, and Mr. Muir for Stuart.
MR. AVory submitted that the indictment was bad on the ground that
Piper was not in actual" possession" of the documonts required, and thera-
fore could not be convicted under the indictment. The RECORDER, agreeing,
guashed the indictment.
