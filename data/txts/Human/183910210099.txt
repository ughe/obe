JONATHAN NEWELL. I am a carpenter in the employ of Mr. Vine. About a fortnight ago the prisoner brought in a chimney-jamb—he asked me to show it to my master, and said he would fix them at 18 s . a piece—that was not one of these stones. CHARLES WELCH( police-constable N  241.) I took the prisoner, and have the stones.
   MR. VINE re-examined . I had no contract with the prisoner for any Yorkshire stone, but if he had put down this slab of Yorkshire, it would have been rather better than the contract, and I should have paid him for it. GUILTY. Aged 41.— Confined Three Months.
 2847. JOHN MILBANK was indicted for forging and uttering, on the   21st of June , a promissory note for the payment of 5 l  ., with intent to defraud Joseph Watts and others.
 The note not being produced, the prisoner was ACQUITTED.
 2848. ROBERT BANKS was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of September , 1 pair of boots, value 3 s  ., the goods of Francis Scoones . FRANCIS SCOONES. I live in Holywell-lane, Shoreditch, and am a pawnbroker. On the 25th of September I missed a pair of boots, which were hanging on a horse outside—I requested my brother to go after the person—the prisoner was brought back with them. JOHN WHITE. I saw the prisoner take the boots off the horse by the side of the door—I gave information—he is the man that took them. EDWARD BLACKFORD( police-constable G  70.) I went after the prisoner, and took him with these boots.
 (The prisoner pleaded poverty.) GUILTY  . Aged 52. —Recommended to mercy  . — Confined One Month.
   Fifth Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin .
 2849. WILLIAM UPTON was indicted for embezzlement.
   MR. JONES conducted the Prosecution . WILLIAM HENRY KNIGHT. I am a paper-stainers' flock-manufac turer. The prisoner was in my employ—he went round the town twice a week as my clerk aid collector for two or three years—he had authority to receive money, and was to hand it over to me on the following day, in September last—he did not account to me on the 14th of September, or thereabouts, for any money received from Mr. Evans, of Shoreditch, who owed me 23 l .—Mr. Isherwood, on Ludgate-hill, owed me some money—the prisoner did not account to me for any money received from him.
