532. WILLIAM ROW (33), Feloniously forging and uttering a request
for the delivery of two sacks of peas, with intent to defraud.
MR. LEIGH conducted the Prosecution.
John MOGGINNY. I live at 95, Belgrave Street, Stepney, and am fore-
man to Benjamin Boucher, a corn merchant the prisoner brought me this
letter to the warehouse, he was a stranger to me-Read: " Messrs.
Boucher. Please to deliver to bearer two sacks of split peas. Yours, &c.,
W. J. Whipper ")-I supplied him with the peas, as I knew Mr. Whipper-
they were worth 548.-he took them away on a truck.
Prisoner. Q. Did I say that I came from Mr. Whipper A. No.
WILLIAM JOHNSON WHIPPER. I am an oilman, of 134, Cable Street-I do
not know the prisoner--this note is not in my writing, or written by my
authority—the signature is not mine—I deal with the prosecutor.
ALFRED STIGANT (Policeman 90). I took the prisoner, and told him he
was charged with receiving two sacks of peas-he said, "I know nothing
of it."
Prisoner's Defence. On Whit Monday I was sent for the peas by a man
named Harrison; I was to receive 58. for taking them. I took them, and
the man gave me a half-crown. They were found at the place where they
were left.
COURT to John MOGGINNY. Q. Was the order made out to Harrison
A. No; the note was signed by the name of Ayres.
Prisoner. The delivery note was made out in the name of Harrison, the
clerk made it out. Witness. The two sacks were sold at 108. each, their
value being 548.--the prisoner and I went together to hire the barrow, I
told him he could get one opposite, and went with him; the man then
stated that he would do it for 28. 6d., the man wheeled the barrow and the
prisoner pushed behind-the costermonger is not here.
Prisoner. I told you where the barrow was engaged, where they were
going to, and that was the place I left them at, and where they were found.
I was sent by Harrison, the delivery note was in his name; I took the
peas where I was told to take them, and got 58., half-a-crown for myself,
and half-a-crown for the man.
Nor GUILTY.
