GEORGE HENRY PHILLIPS. I live in the Hampstead-road. On the 24th of September I sent the linen to Mrs. Charles to be washed—I gave the bundle to Sarah Graham. SARAH GRAHAM. I gave out the bundle of linen to the prisoner on the 24th of September—I do not know the contents, as my mistress had counted the things up the night before—I know it contained a table-cloth and some other things—I gave it the prisoner to take to Mrs. Charles. ELIZA CHARLES. I wash for Mr. Phillips's family—the prisoner had left my employ on Friday, the 20th of September, and he went for these things on the Tuesday following—he never brought them to me—I never saw him till he was taken up. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Transported for Seven Years.
   OLD COURT .— Monday, October  28 th,  1839.
   Third Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin .
 2915. ELIZABETH BENNETT was indicted for stealing, on the   8th of October , a sovereign from the person of Daniel Earith ; to which the pleaded GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Six Months.
 2916. THOMAS ROBSON was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of May , 9 shirts, value 18 s  .; a gown, value 5 s  .; and other articles; the goods of Jane Stodhart . JANE STODHART. I live in Anchor and Hope-alley, and let lodgings to sailors—the prisoner lodged with me eight weeks—I missed the property stated the day after he left to go to sea—this shirt ( looking at one ) was part of it, and was my husband's—I am a widow. CHARLES PATTEN. I am a policeman. I became acquainted with the prisoner about six months ago—about a fortnight or three weeks before he went away he asked me to go with him to a pawnbroker's, to get a few things out of pawn to take to sea with him—I went, and he got out six or seven shirts—while getting them out the clock struck twelve—he said it would be too late to get any more out now, and he told me to take care of two tickets till he came back—this was on the Saturday before he went away on the Monday—this shirt was afterwards found on him at the station-house. HENRY POTTER. I am a pawnbroker. I took in this shirt of the prisoner.
   Prisoner . I throw myself on the mercy of the Court. GUILTY.— Confined Three Months.
 2917. GEORGE CLARK  , alias    Wood, was indicted for unlawfully obtaining, by false pretences, 5 sovereigns and three 5 l  . promissory notes, the monies of Thomas Ashby and others; to which he pleaded GUILTY  . Aged 46.— Recommended to  mercy. — Confined Twelve Months.
 2918. GEORGE CLARK  , alias    Wood, was again  indicted , for feloniously uttering a forged order for the payment of 20 l  ., with intent to defraud Thomas Ashby and others.
 No evidence being offered upon this indictment, the Prisoner was ACQUITTED.
