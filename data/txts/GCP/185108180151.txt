unhappy, he was afraid he had offended me and Mr. Newton, his father's
executor - he had not offended me, or Mr. Newton that I know of - I said he
had not offended me, and what I had done, was done with the best intention
towards himself and his family, and I was sorry I had not accomplished my
wishes--that related to my negotiating the sale of an estate belonging to his
deceased father-I said, "Have you a holiday to-day?"-he said, no; he
