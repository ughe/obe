Johnson's Defence. I went to King William Street, and stopped to look
on. It was close upon 2 o'clock, and I turned to go away, and saw a lot of
people stooping. I saw the detective pick up something close to my feet
I did not know it was a watch. When I was taken he shouted out “Stop,

life. The prosecutor says he took the watch out of my hand, which is false.
They said they were determined to have someone that day.
