1396. GEORGE HAYNES was indicted for uttering counterfeit coin.
RICHARD SANDWELL. I am a publican, and live in High-street, Lambeth.
On the 2nd of March the prisoner came for some beer-he tendered a half-
crown-1 pat it into a bowl-there were a few sixpences there, but no other
half-crown- I gave him change and he went away-a few minutes after I went
to the till—there was no one there but me- I took out the half-crown, and
found it was bad-I marked it and put it into my pocket-on the 4th he came
again for a pint of porter-he tendered another half-crown-I saw it was bad
- he went into the tap-room-I did not say any thing, but sent for an officer
and gave him the two half-crowns.
ALEXANDER TAHOURDIN (police-constable L 57.) I was at Mr. Sandwell's
on the evening of the 4th, and took the prisoner- I found nothing on him-
I produce two half-crowns which Sandwell gave me.
Mr. John Field. These are both counterfeit.

Prisoner's Defence. I never was in the shop but once.
