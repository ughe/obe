MARY ANN METCALF. I lodged in this house--I was unwell at the
time, and confined to my bed-the child was crying from morning till
night.
William TAYLOR. I am surgeon of the parish of Clerkenwell. I was
called in by, desire of the parish-officer to see the child-it appeared to be
about eighteen months old-it was very much emaciated when first ad-
mitted into the house-the spine was curved, the radius of both arms bent
--the mesenteric glands were very enlarged-it appeared to me those sym-
toms were in a great measure caused by continued neglect and want of
proper sustenance and care-it being left alone from ten to twelve hours
without the attendance of any person would be very likely to produce
those appearances- there is a disease of the mesenteric glands to which
children are subject, which would cause similar appearances--in such a case
you may fill a child to repletion, and yet it would die of starvation, but
from the rapid improvement of this child when supplied with proper nou-
rishment, I think it was not the case here-in organic disease of the me-
senteric glands that improvement would not take place- I did not see the
child in the room.
Cross-examined. Q. When a child is brought up by hand, the mother
being insane and unable to attend to it, does it not sometimes get curvature
of the spine ? A. Sometimes I should recommend as a remedy that the
child should sometimes lie flat on its back on something hard-there are
peared to be no disease of the lungs- I could not detect any.
SARAH GREEN. I live in Exmouth-street. The house in which the
prisoner lived is my husband's--two or three days before the parish-officers
took the child away, she came to pay her rent, which was 1s. 9d. a week-
I asked her on that occasion how it was that the child cried so much, and
whether she left any one with it-she said she left a girl with it-I asked
if the child was ill-she said it was, that she had taken it to a doctor, and
it had eaten half a pound of meat the night before I said, “Half a pound
of neat ?" --she said the doctor had ordered nourishment-I said!
thought it was wrong to give it so much-I never saw the child.
Cross-examined. Q. You thought what she said to you very strange!
A. Yes.
