WILLIAN STONEMAN. I keep the White Lion Inn, Fleet Lane-on 19th

The Defence,

gave me his address, 183, Queen's Road, Dalston-I asked if he had a
factory there-he said, “Yes, behind the house, it is a manufactory, where
I have sixteen or eighteen men employed, and the trade is rather looking
up"-he said he had been in business there about six or seven years--we had
