FARNCOMB, Mayor.
1692. JESSE JOHN WESTERBY and CHARLES ISRAEL DIXON
were both again indicted for stealing 456 collars, value 101.; the goods of
Thomas Welch and others, to which WESTERBY pleaded GUILTY.
(MR. PARRY offered no evidence against Dixon.) DIXON-Not Guilty.
1693. JESSE JOHN WESTERBY was again indicted for stealing 140
pieces of linen, 50 pieces of cotton, llb. weight of wax, and I dozen shirt-
fronts, value 21s. ; the goods of Thomas Welch and others, his masters : to
which he pleaded
GUILTY. He received a good character.-Transported for Ten Years.
