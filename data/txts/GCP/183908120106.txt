2290. JOHN HARTNETT was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
July, 33 lbs. weight of tallow, value 10s., the goods of Frederick Taylor.
FREDERICK TAYLOR. I am a tallow chandler-I live in Kingsgate-
street now, I did live at No. 10, Orange-street, but my house has been
burned down. On the 27th of July, in consequence of information from
Mr. Harris, I went with an officer to the Orange-tree public-house, nest
door to where the fire was--I found the prisoner there, and gave him into
custody- I traced some tallow to Mr. Atkinson's—it is the same quality
and appearance as that on my premises- I know notbing further of the
prisoner than seeing him on the building as a labourer, pulling down the
ruins.
Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. Was this tallow yours!
A. Yes--it was in the house that was burned down--the house was in-
sured, but I took this among other things as salvage.
