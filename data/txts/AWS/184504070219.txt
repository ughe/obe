1021. JOHN BALL was indicted for stealing 1 101. bank-note, the mo-
nies of Henry Flower; and EDWARD LEWIS and SAMUEL LEWIS,
for feloniously receiving the same, knowing it to be stolen.
MR. ADOLPBUS conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY FLowER. I am managing clerk to Messrs. Goding and Co.,
brewers, in Belvedere-road, Lambeth. Ball was employed about their pre-
mises in Nov. last-he was to wait on the clerks, and go on errands or
messages for them-in Nov. last I had a 104. note of my oWD, which I
put into a drawer in the strong closet in the brewery--Bal had access to
that closet-the key of the drawer was on a bunch of keys which was in
the closet- missed the note on my return from Dover, about the 20th of
Nov.-i had been safe two or three days before.
ROBERT FENNINO (police-constable L 181.) On the 24th of Feb. I
went to Goding's brewery-) saw Ball--Mr. Goding sent for him to the
counting-house-I cautioned him not to say anything, and I said I was to
take him for some other things--I named the. 104. note-he said he had
not taken it-he afterwards said something, and I fetched Harvey to the
station-Harvey said in Ball's presence that Ball had taken a 101. note and
brought it to Lewis-Ball denied it-Harvey said, You know you brought
the 101. note and another note, and received 61. for it, you may as well tell
the truth, for I shall"-nothing further passed.
JOHN PETER PERRY. I am clerk at Lambeth Police-court. When
Ball was brought up for examination he said, (reading) 44 What he says
(meaning Harvey) is right they gave me 61. for the 1ol. and 5l. notes,
but asked me for 3l. back, because they said they could not change the
10/. note." Harvey says, 44 When Ball came for the 6L. I was there he
told me that he had a 5l. note and a 101. note, and sold them for 61."
Ball. James Halnut came up with Harvey, and told me to say what
Harvey said, but what Harvey said was not true.
WILLIAM HARVEY. I am fourteen years old. I was in the service of
Edward and Samuel Lewis till they were taken into custody- know Ball
quite well-I have seen him at Lewis's lodgings, at No. 3, Artichoke-
court, Lambeth-I have seen Ball there in a morning, and the Lewis's
were up in a corner whispering about notes, and in going up the Cut one
night Ball said to me, I sold Samuel Lewis a 5l. note and a 101. note for
61., and be made me give him 3l. back, he said there was one note bad, and he
could not change it"- heard the Lewis's say up in the room that Ball had
got a 5l. note and a 101. note, and "I will take them to Bill to change
them"-Edward and Samuel Lewis both said that-Samuel Lewis sent me
one night to Ball to get some money-h told me to tell Ball that Edward
was ill, and he wanted some money--Ball told me he got the notes from
Mr. Goding's brewery, and another thing he told me that he took a check off
Mr. Goding's table, and be said it was a gos shame that the Lewis's only
gave him 6l. for the notes, and made him give them 3l. back.
