 488. MARY ANN LEHO  (22), was indicted for feloniously uttering counterfeit coin.
   MESSRS. POLAND  and ORRIDGE conducted the Prosecution. GEORGE ROGERS( Policeman Z , 53). I produce a certificate ( Read: "Central Criminal Court, May, 1859, Mary Ann Leho was convicted unlaw  fully uttering counterfeit coin, and Confined Nine Months.") The prisoner is the person—I had her in custody. ALICE CASTLEDINE. My uncle's name is Thorn—he keeps a shop in Gravel-lane, Southwark—on 20th February I was in the shop—the prisoner came in—I served her a half-quarten loaf—she gave me in payment a half-crown—I looked at it, and told her it was bad—she said she was not aware of it—I went in to my uncle and gave him the half-crown—my uncle came into the shop and began to speak to her—she ran out of the shop.
   Prisoner. Q.  I did not run out of the shop. A.  Yes, you did, and my uncle ran after you. RICHARD THORN. I live at 36, Gravel-lane, and am a baker—on that day I was in the parlour; my niece brought the half-crown to me—I went in the shop to the prisoner, and asked her where she got it—she said she got it in payment for wages from a rope-maker in Holland-street—I said I would send for a constable to take her up—she ran out of the shop, and turned from Gravel-lane to Bear-lane—I followed, and brought her back, and gave her in custody—I gave the half-crown to the policeman. CHARLES TAYLOR. ( Policeman A , 500). On 20th February the prisoner was given into my custody by the last witness, with this half-crown—she was taken to the police-station, and she gave the name of Mary Drake—she was remanded till the 29th, and discharged. ANN MARCHANT. I am the wife of John Marchant, a news-agent, 10, Maze-pond—on 20th April the prisoner came to my shop and asked for "Bell's Life"—I told her I had not one, and she said, "I will take a 'London Journal'"—she put down a sixpence—I put it in my mouth, and found it was bad—I said, "O, dear, here is another bad sixpence!"—she ran out of the house—I had the sixpence in my mouth—I ran after her, and caught her by the back of the neck—I held her—she scratched my arm and made it bleed, but I held her till the officer came, and gave her in custody—I gave the officer the sixpence. HENRY SKINNER. ( Police Sergeant M  7). The prisoner was given into my custody—I received this sixpence from the last witness—the prisoner gave the name of Mary Parker—she had a purse in her hand, which contained one good sixpence. WILLIAM WEBSTER. These are both bad. GUILTY.— Four Years' Penal Servitude.
