 818.  ROBERT JOHNSON alias    ROBERT LAW was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of April , 1 gown, value 10s., the goods of John Holt.
 JOHN HOLT. I  keep the  Duke's Head public-house, in Old Gravel-lane. On the 1st of April, about a quarter before four o'clock, the prisoner and another came in - I served them with a pint of beer - they drank it, and gave me half-a-crown to give change; I sent my wife up stairs to get it, and in the mean time the prisoner went into the yard; my man had some suspicion, and missed the gown from the yard, where it hung just before - he followed the prisoner into the tap-room, and said, "He has taken something off the line in the yard" - I collared him, took off his hat, and found the gown in it - he said he was very sorry, and it was his first offence.
   WILLIAM DIXON. I am Holt's servant. I was in the kitchen when the prisoner went into the yard; I missed the gown - I followed him into the tap-room, and it was found in his hat.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I went backwards, and met a friend, who said, "Will you mind this gown for me."
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.

 819.  THOMAS MASON was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of March , 1 shawl, value 1s., the goods of Ellen Mullins ; and 1 jacket, value 2s., the goods of William Wilkinson.
   WILLIAM HALLETT. I am a cow-keeper, and live in Tyson-street, Clerkenwell. About half past five o'clock on the morning of the 24th of March, I went into my cow yard, and saw the prisoner coming out of one shed into the other, with a bundle under his arm: he turned into a stall where a chaff-engine was - he laid down his bundle there, went into another stall, and laid himself under the horse's head; I sent a man for the watchman, and the prisoner went into another stall; I then seat another man for a watchman; he attempted to make his escape, and I tried to seize him with one hand, having my light in the other, and some measures; he got from me, and ran to an open fence; I threw down what I had, and seized him by one leg; he then took a knife out of his pocket, and cut my two hands. One of the witnesses then came to me, and he cut at him in the legs; I tried to get the knife from him, but could not; he then cut at my legs, but with the back of the knife. I seized him by the throat - Ellen Mullins came, and got the knife from him - he was then taken to the watch-house - these articles were found in the manger.
   WILLIAM WILKINSON. I am in the service of Mr. Hallet. I saw the prisoner in a stall in his cow shed, on the 24th of March; I knew him very well. I went for the watchman. We found this jacket in the manger - he was quite sober.
 ELLEN MULLINS. I go to Mr. Hallett's to get milk. - I had left this shawl there five minutes before five o'clock that morning.
   THOMAS UNDERWOOD. I am a constable. These articles were brought to me by Wilkinson.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had been among the hay carts, trying to get some victuals. I got tipsy, and went to lay down in the manger, the shed being open.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. Transported for Seven Years. (See page 258).
 802.  JOHN HITCHCOCK, JASPER HITCHCOCK, and ISRAEL DUNNING were indicted for stealing, on the 5th of April , 4 live same pigeons, price 20s., the property of Charles Wighton.
 CHARLES WIGHTON. I live in  North-street, Poplar. - I missed four pigeons about four o'clock on the morning of the 5th of April - I had seen them safe about seven o'clock the evening before; the pigeon' house is about thirty feet from my house, in my garden. I found it broken open, and four gone out of the seven, which I found at the watch-house.
   JOSEPH CRUCE. I am a watchman. About half-past two o'clock in the morning of the 5th of April, the three prisoners passed me in East India-road, and a little before three I heard a woman crying for the watchman - I ran to North-street, from whence the sound proceeded, and she told me there were three men getting on the leads at the top of the door; I ran after them, but did not see them. About half-past three o'clock I took (with some assistance) the three prisoners, whom I knew to be the persons I had seen at first; I found four pigeous on Jasper Hitchcock.
   JAMES PURRY. I am superintendant of the watch. - Cruce brought the prisoners to the watch-house; I found four pigeons on Jasper Hitchcock: I made inquiries, and found where they belonged to. I had seen the prisoners pass the watch-house just before three o'clock.
 JOHN HITCHCOCK's Defence. I was going home to the Barking-road, over the iron bridge, where I work.
 JASPER HITCHCOCK's Defence. I bought the pigeons on the Tuesday evening.
 DUNNING's Defence. I was going to Blackwall-dock, to look for work. I never saw these young men before.
 JOHN HITCHCOCK -    GUILTY. Aged 25. Confined Six Months.
 JASPER HITCHCOCK -    GUILTY. Aged 16.
 DUNNING -    GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
