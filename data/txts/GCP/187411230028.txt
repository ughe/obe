doing a goodish bit of work for us—you made us some machines last year,

machines for me after that, six or seven--you made me three pleating

not take, that is four I never had three more from you after you made

guarantee that they were patent, and I got into trouble with you, and of
course I would not have the fourth machine the price was to be 301. or
352,- you wanted 401., and I paid you 51. deposit; you wanted more
