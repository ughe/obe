165. JAMES SAIT, Feloniously receiving a quantity of copper and
other metal, the property of Henry Levin & Co., Limited.
MR. WARDR Prosecuted: MR. ELLIOTT and Mr. Fitch Defended.
WALTER PEARCE. I live at 39, Chance Square, West Ham, and am
yard foreman to Henry Levin & Co., Limited, of High Street, Stratford,
metal merchants-on December 23rd I saw some bags of copper shell
and some ingots of lead at Plaistow Police Station, belonging to Levin
& Company-I left our yard on December 16th at eight o'clock-I
was the last to leave-I fastened up the premises properly, everything
being safe-I went there at 7.45 a.m. on the 17th - I found the back gates
and the warehouse door broken, and lead and copper shells missing; we
did not know how much at the time-those were what I saw at the Police
station-I have not the slightest doubt of that.
Cross-examined. We had about 16 tons of lead on our premises
-it was a mixture of antimony and lead-it is in ingots-we have
since weighed our stuff, and we are over two tons short-there is nothing
to identify one ingot from another-I daresay other people have similar
lead-I would not undertake to pick out our own ingots from others-
when I say I identify the lead I mean it is similar to what we had and
what others might have the copper has a backing of white metal to
keep it stift--the stuff we had was in a barrel-We had one bag packed,
and there is one bag the same as that at the station-I identifyt his (A
piece of copper.) by the bag, not by the metal--there are, I suppose, a
great number of similar bags in London-it is an old dirty bag, and has
no name or distinctive mark.
Re-eramined. I saw the bag that has been referred to on our premises;
it was full of copper--the bag I saw at the Police station was full of
copper-I say it was the same bag.
By the Court. What I say is that that bag is exactly similar to the
bag that was on the premises.
FRED EARL. I live at 79, Northam Street, West Ham, and am a
labourer in the employ of Levin, Limited on December 16th I had to
pack some copper in a barrel; there was a bit over, and I put it in a sack-
I left them in the warehouse - I have since seen the sack at the Barking
Road Police station- it was full of copper shell-it is the same bag that I
packed-I tied the bag up after putting the copper in, and it was tied up
at the Police station
Cross-eramined. We have some hundreds of bags on our premises-I
daresay others have got hundreds too- I do not identify the copper.
