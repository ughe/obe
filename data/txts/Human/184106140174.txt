 1789. WILLIAM BOSWELL was indicted for stealing, on the   30th of May , 1 pair of gloves, value 1 s  . 6 d  . the goods of James Cooper , his master. JAMES COOPER. I am a linendraper in Blackfriars-road—the prisoner was in my employ. On the 30th of May, before church time, he was in the act of going down stairs—I called him into my parlour, and said, "How do you spend your Sundays?"—he said sometimes he went to church—I then asked him what he had got in his pocket, having missed goods—he told me "Nothing"—I told him to take out what he had—he took out different papers, and said, "That is all"—I then told him to take out what else he had got, and after some time he took out these gloves—I asked how he came by them—he said he did not know how they came into his pocket—I took him down into the shop, and the paper was open that these gloves came from—I said they were my property, and I would send for a policeman—while the servant was gone for a policeman, he said, if I forgave him this time he would never do it again—I said I had been robbed so many times, I would make an example of him—he afterward said he intended to pay for them before he left the house—my servant have not the power to help themselves—this pair of gloves had been tied up on Saturday night.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  Did you search him? A.  I did, and there was not 1 d . found on him, nor in his box—about 1 l . was due to him for wages—I always pay my servants every quarter—I did not say that the cravat he had on was my property, nor that a pair of stockings he had were mine—there was a pair of braces in his box which I believed were mine, but the mark was rubbed out, and I could not recognize them—I did not say that he came to my house with a false character—he is the only person I ever preferred a charge against for robbing me—I have not preferred a charge against my apprentice—I charged him with robbing me of money, but I let it pass over—I did not send for a policeman, nor take him to the police-office—he is with me still—he has not brought an action against me, nor threatened to do so—I have had no letter from an attorney—I sent to his friends to tell them he had stolen two half-sovereigns—that is six months ago—I did nothing else—the money was found in the shop after I made a stir about it—the prisoner had not been with me a month—I had a good character with him from Mr. Roe of Oxford-street—the paper of gloves which was undone was on the shelf.
