CHARLES WARLAND. I am porter at the Swan-with-two-necks, Lad-

nutes-the coach was to start at eight o'clock.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. What was it? 4. A paper parcel

THOMAS Cook. I am a book-keeper. I booked this parcel to go by the
coach—I opened it at Guildhall-it contained a bottle and some gin.
George Edwards. I am horse-keeper at the Swan-with-two-necks. A
few minutes after eight o'clock, I saw a parcel behind the back of the coach-
wheel—the prisoner dropped a red handkerchief upon it, and then took it
up, parcel and all together-he made away with it -- I could not stop him
|--I halloed "Stop thief”--that is the sort of parcel he picked up.
Cross-examined. Q. Where were you? A. I was going across the road,
about two yards from it-I saw no one but him against the parcel-it all
happened in less than a minute.
