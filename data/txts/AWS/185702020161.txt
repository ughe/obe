a person unknown, upon Frederick Brownhill, and stealing a medal, value
58.; his property.
MR. SLEIGH conducted the Prosecution.
FREDERICK BROwNHILL. I am a private in the first battalion of Cold-
stream Guards. On Wednesday night, 21st Jan., I was in the Walworth-
road--a house was on fire there--I rendered what assistance I could to put

got about 300 or 400 yards I was seized from behind by two men, and

23rd in the possession of the police
