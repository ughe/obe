acknowledge I uttered the coin; I only had it half an hour before. A

Prisoner's Defence. I received the money from a man who was going of
board a ship to America the next morning; he did not tell me it was bad
He was going to see some friends, and said that he did not wish to take it
with him, and gave them to me. I put the other sovereign in my drawers
because I thought if one was bad the other must bave been bad. The knife
belonged to the other man ; he only lent it to me.
