933. WILLIAM STONE, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 2s.6d.; the
goods of John Lutwyche, from his person.
JOHN LUTWYCHE. On 18th Sept., between 3 and 4 o'clock, I was in
Fleet-street, and my attention was called by two persons in a chaise, who
pointed out the prisoner and told me something-the prisoner was running-
I pursued bim, calling out, "Stop thief!"-I saw him run down Bride's-
passage-I ran after him, but lost sight of him; I caught sight of him again,
and saw him stopped-I went up to him, and asked him for my handkerchief
-he denied having it-some person came up immediately, and said in his
presence that he had thrown it over the railing of the burial ground-I saw a
boy get over-he got it and gave it to me, and I gave it to the officer-this
produced) is it-it has my initials on it.
GEORGE LEGGETT (City policeman, 339). I took the prisoner into custody.
He said he knew nothing about it-Mr. Lutwyche gave me this handkerchief.
Prisoner's Defence. A woman said she saw a boy chuck it over the rail-
ings; she did not say it was me.
Guilty.**_Confined Twelve Months.
