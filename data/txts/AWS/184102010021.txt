November, 14 yard of cotton, value 9d.; and 1 purse, value 6d.; the
goods of Hugh Henry Ernsting, his master.

Charles-street-the prisoner was my errand-boy. On Wednesday morning
I took a yard from this cotton, and gave it to him to put round his neck
-in the evening I marked the cotton, and when he went home at night E
opened it, and missed the initials-it had been cut againI went after
him-he came back, and produced this cotton from his trowsers-he had
only been three days in my employ,

did not take the cotton with me, I left it behind me, and then took it
out and gave it him. Witness. He took it from his trowsers, and threw it
