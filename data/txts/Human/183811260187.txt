   MR. PHILLIPS called HENRY MOYCE. The prisoner was in my employ for a fortnight I was very much pleased with his conduct up to the present time—I gave him into custody from information received from Bryant—I had no alter native—I have a leaf here which I saw taken out of my book—it is dated, "October 27th"—here is 1 s . 9 d . entered as received by the person enter ing it—I cannot tell whether it is the prisoner's writing, but I believe it is—there are eight or ten entries after it—I dismissed Bryant for being out on Saturday, Sunday, and Monday nights—I said to him when he came back, "What do you mean by this?"—he said, "I was ill, and could not come"—I said, "Don't tell me so, for I have proof that you were at the theatre on Monday night"—I had received a message from a young woman on Monday night, about eight o'clock, that he was very ill in bed, and could not come, and that very night he was at the theatre—he wished to stay with me, but I said on no account would I have him, on account of his mis conduct—he had 25 l . a-year—the prisoner had 30 l .—these did not know what the prisoner had.
