   Cross-examined.  He said "I did not bring any bacon to the house, I saw it there on Sunday morning, and I and my brother carried it away and threw it aside on the line, and took it away in sacks." GEORGE NEWLAND. I am Superintendent of Police at Farnborough—on on the 14th last March I was in company with Potter, when he went to the prisoner's house—while George was being conveyed along he said "There were not fifteen"—I immediately cautioned him—he said "There were not fifteen sides, only twelve."
