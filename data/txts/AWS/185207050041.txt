736. THOMAS LAWRENCE, unlawfully having counterfeit coin in his
possession with intent to utter it. Mr. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM SMITH (police-sergeant, E 16). On 5th June I went to the
Royal Oak beer shop, in Chenies-street, kept by the prisoner-1 found him
in the back parlour-I told him I must take him into custody for having
stolen property in his house-he made no reply-I gave him into custody,
and searched the house-I found some property-ir going from the bar to
the kitchen I found one stair loose- removed it, and found under it a basket,
which I now produce, containing counterfeit coins-I looked further, and
found a tin box, or tray, in the washhouse containing coin-I put them with
those in the basket, and counted them all together-there were 28 sovereigns,
33 half sovereigns, 153 crowns, 198 half crowns, 15 florins, and 207 shillings
-I have known the prisoner living there about eighteen months-I said to
him, 44 How do you account for this '-he said, don't know how it came
there."

which led you to these steps| A. I had the prisoner has a potman and a
sister; he has a wife and, I believe, some children.
