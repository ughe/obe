On 6th June, about 10.30 I saw the prisoners running up John Street—a boy said "Stop those two men they have robbed me"—I stopped them and saw Abrahams' hand some thing to Lazarus, who passed it back again, but Abrahams would not take it—I said "What have you got there?"—he said "A sovereign," and gave it to me—the prosecutor came up and said "I charge those two men with robbing me of 6 l .—Abrahams said "We have not robbed him, we have been trading with him."
