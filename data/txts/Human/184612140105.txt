 329. EMANUEL WILMOTT and WILLIAM BEAVIS were indicted for stealing 1 loaf of bread, value 33 l  . 4 d  ., the goods of Samuel Hunt , the master of Wilmott. SAMUEL HUNT. I am a baker, and live in Tooley-street.
