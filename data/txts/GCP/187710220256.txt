Cro88-examined by MR. GORST. I believe Mr. Froggatt did sometimes
see clients at places other than his private office; the Scotch Stores are
in Oxford Street, about five minutes from Mr. Froggatt's office-I have
been there to fetch Mr. Froggatt from or to his clients, and have
seen him there conversing with clients, but never with Murray-I never
heard him say that he had met Murray there, and I never made an
appointment with Murray to meet him there-I believe he has often
been to the Pamphillion with his clients-I cannot say whether he
has met them there—it was about 4 p.m. when the Scotch note was
given to Mr. Froggatt at his office--Mr. Murray and myself were the
only persons present-I saw Mr. Froggatt hand the note to a clerk, his
brother Arthur, to pay into the bank-Murray said "I have no other
money, I shall give you this 1001. note on account of costs incurred, you
may have to go farther than Scotland"-Mr. Froggatt did not go to Scotland
