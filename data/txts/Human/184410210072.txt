 2447. CHARLES PERKINS was indicted for stealing 33 yards of doe-skin, value 5 l  . 16 s  ., the goods of Charles Wilson . SAMUEL KENISTON. I am assistant to Charles Wilson, a woollen draper, on Ludgate-street. I received some information about half-past eleven o'clock, on the 10th of Oct., and found the prisoner with this piece of cloth, belonging to my master—I had seen it a day or two before inside the lobby—I know it by a private mark—it goes by the name of doeskin—the prisoner said he was very badly off, and merely took it to be transported, he wished to be transported. JAMES RADCLIFF( City police-constable, No . 375.) I took the prisoner and the doeskin—he said if he had not stolen this he should have stolen something else; he wanted to be sent out of the country. GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Six Months .
