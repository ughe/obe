2007. MARIA BROWN was indicted for feloniously receiving 3lbs. of gun-metal, value 2 s  ., the good of John Bennington Blyth . JOHN BENNINGTON BLYTH. I am a brass-founder, and live in Guildford-street, Southwark. On the 13th of July Courtney came to my premises, and showed me some metal—it was mine, and had been stolen—we never sell it.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q.  Can you swear to it? A.  Yes—it was made on the 8th of July, and on the following Tuesday it was brought to me—my foreman knew of it on the Sunday—he has been bail for the prisoner—I have one of my sons in my employ—I have discharged one on account of this. THOMAS BROWN. I made this metal on the Thursday before. JAMES EVENS. On the 8th of July, about four o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoner came, and brought 3lbs. of metal to my shop—I bought it at 5 d . a pound—I bought some metal of her once before.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How long before? A.  About a week or five days—I am sure she is the person who sold it me—I do not recollect buying any other metal that day, I bought bones and rags—I should think I made twenty or thirty purchases that day—I sold the metal to Courtney—I saw it after wards at Mr. Blyth's. JOHN COURTNEY. I bought the metal of Mr. Evens—I told the foreman of it on the Sunday afternoon. JAMES BURTON( policeman M  272.) I took the prisoner for receiving the metal—she denied all knowledge of it, and said she had never sold any metal at all. NOT GUILTY .

 2009. GEORGE JORDEN was indicted for stealing 25lbs. of lead, value 5 s  ., the goods of Elizabeth Ashby , his mistress—2nd COUNT  , of Elisabeth Ashby and another. WILLIAM ASHBY. I am in partnership with my mother, Elizabeth Ashby—she is a cowkeeper, and lives in the Clapham-road—we had some lead on our premises—I saw it up about four days before it was missing—I have seen the lead produced by the officer, I am able to swear it is what we had on our house—the prisoner was in our service. WILLIAM GIBBS( policeman V  77.) I produce this lead—I saw the pri soner go to a marine store dealer's with it, and took him.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was going over a heap of dung in the yard, and saw this lead; I let it remain till dinner-time; I then took it, as I wanted bread for my children. GUILTY . Aged 39.— Confined Three Months .
