weight of iron, value 3s. 6d.': the goods of George Crawshay and another.
WILLIAM BLEACH (City-policeman, 363). On 24th March, about half-past
six 'clock in the evening, I was at Paul's Wharf-I saw Dismore on a barge,
and Lloyd standing by the side of it-Dismore took a bit of iron out of the
barge, passed it to Lloyd, and Lloyd went away from the barge with about
112lbs. of iron-be put it by the side of the pier, and covered it with a bag
-I went to get assistance, came back and took Dismore-Lloyd escaped I
took him on 3rd April.
Cross-ezamined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Where was the iron A. I
found one piece in a basket in Dismore's hand-] knew Lloyd before.
STEPHEN RUDD. I am private watchman to George Hawkes and George

that night I saw the prisoners in company had to stop to lock the gates,
and in the meantime they had done the robbery- saw Dismore after the
officer had taken him.
Cross-ezamined. Q. You were out of the way when this robbery was
