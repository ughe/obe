EDWARD PLATT. I am a greengrocer, of Thornton Terrace, Victoria
Road, Peckham Rye-on 4th May I was in a beer-house in Orb Street,
Walworth, about 6 p.m.-I saw Forman, and we had a conversation
about buying a pony-I had been in his company the greatest part of the
day---he said he knew some one who had a pony to sell he went to fetch
it-he came back in about 10 minutes with Stewart and Waldron--there
were five of us altogether-Stewart said he was brother to a taller man,
who asked me 61. for the pony-I ultimately purchased it from Stewart's
brother for 21. 108.-I paid him in gold-he said if I came down and
purchased a cart he would give me a receipt in full-it was the eart that
the pony drew-I said, "I don't like the look of these men, George "-
Stewart said, " It does not matter as long as I am with you, they won't
interfere with you, as I know the whole of them ”--they had some beer
after that I paid for two pots of ale--they all drank-I had not drunk
more than I do every day I was perfectly sober-I said to Forman, “I
had better separate my gold from the silver, and put the 41., if I buy the
cart, in the left-band pocket, because I do not like the look of those men,
although you say I am all right”-I told Forman in the morning what
I had got within a few shillings-I had come with him from St. Martin's
Lane-'I had over 81.- Forman saw what pocket I put it in--when I left
