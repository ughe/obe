206. WILLIAM DAWSON VIVIAN (20), Stealing two saws, the
property of John Bendall and another.
Mr. Wood conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN BENDALL. I live at 4, Abingdon Cottages, Lewisham-on 21st
of January I was working in an unfurnished house at Blackheath-I had my
saw, which I know by my initials-it was safe at twelve o'clock in the day
-I left it in the bottom part of the house-I gave information to the police
-the prisoner came and spoke to me about ten minutes before I missed
it-I saw it the next time at six o'clock at the police-station.
JOHN BROWN. I am a carpenter, residing at 3, Morden Street, Lewis. A
ham Road I was working in the next house on the same job that saw
was in my possession on the 21st-I used it at twelve o'clock-I missed it
at half-past twelve-I saw it next at the police-station.
THOMAS LANGRIDGE. I live at No. 2, Horsham Cottages, Cold Bath
Street-I saw prisoner at about twenty minutes past twelve on 20th
January-I afterwards heard a complaint.
JOHN SMITH. I am an assistant to Mr. Sharp, pawnbroker, at Dept-
ford-prisoner brought those saws to the shop between five and six-having
received information, I gave him into custody-he said they were his own.
EBENEZER HODD (Policeman 123 R). I was called into Mr. Sharp's shop
-I heard what the prisoner said about the property-I asked him what
account he could give of the saws-he said he bought them of a man who
asked him to help him on the road.
Prisoner's Defence. I bought them of a man for 3s., thinking to sell them
to a cabinet-maker I am lodging with. He would not buy them so, I took
them to a pawnbroker's. Guilty.—Sir Months' Imprisonment.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
