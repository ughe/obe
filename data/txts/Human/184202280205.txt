JOHN THING was indicted for stealing, on the   31st of August , at   St. Mary, Newington , 12 spoons, value 5 l  . 15 s  .; 1 coat, value 3 l  .; 1 pair of trowsers, value 1 l  .; 1 pair of boots, value 15 s  .; and 1 hat, value 5 s  .; the goods of Michael Meyers , in his dwelling-house: and 12 shillings, the monies of Elizabeth Banbury . MICHAEL MEYERS. I live at Kennington-common, in the parish of St. Mary, Newington, and keep the house. On the 26th or 27th of August last, I left my house and went to Aylesbury for three or four days, and on returning missed the articles named in the indictment, which I had left in charge of Banbury, my servant—I have found none of them except the hat, which the policeman brought to my house—it was one I had bought for my son Henry. ELIZABETH BANBURY. I am the prosecutor's servant, and have been so fourteen months—the prisoner paid his addresses to me—I saw him in August last—I remember master's going into the country—I had the care of the plate while he was away, and kept it in the locker, which I locked—I saw it all safe on the morning of the 31st of August—the prisoner was backward and forward—I missed the spoons about five o'clock that after noon—I also missed master's hat, coat, trowsers, and boots that evening, I did not see the prisoner with them on, but he left his own hat, coat, and boots in the house—I have never seen the plate again, nor the hat till the prisoner was taken, and the policeman brought it—it was Mr. Henry Meym's—I missed 12 s . of my own.
   Prisoner. Q.  Are you certain that is your master's son's hat? A.  Yes. BARTHOLOMEW HERRING. I am a policeman. On the 12th of Feb ruary, in consequence of information I received from the prosecutor, I traced the prisoner to St. George's-workhouse, and apprehended him—he denied the charge—I took this hat off his head, and asked where be got it—he said he bought it in Bishopsgate-street—Mr. Meyers identified it—I found nothing else on him. MICHAEL MEYERS re-examined.  This is my son's hat, which was lost—I bought it for my son, who lives with me.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was in the habit of visiting the female, and on Sunday evening another young man came there; several young men vi sited her, and I left the house—soon after she left the house with this young man; the hat I bought in Bishopsgate-street; it was then a very good one; it had none of the appearances it has now.
   MR. MEYERS . The hat was cracked here as it is now—the maker's name hag been torn out. GUILTY  of stealing, but not in the dwelling-house. Aged 21.— Confined Six Months.
