DR. WARD (Re-examined). I did not see any symptom of delirium
tremens either past or threatening-delusion as to people and things is
one of the first symptoms, or acute trembling of the frame generally-
that is continuous until by treatment they become better-it lasts for days
and nights-I saw no symptom of that.
