First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
 480.  ALFRED WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of August , 1 watch, value 2l. 10s., the goods of James Walker ; and that he had been previously convicted of felony.
   JAMES WALKER. I lodged in Edward-street, Soho- I am a tailor. The prisoner lodged in the same room, and in the same bed; he called himself a tailor- in August last I missed a watch; I believe I had put it on the bedside, or by the window, when I went to bed on the Monday evening - on Tuesday morning I got up, and dressed myself; I looked to see what o'clock it was, and left my watch in the same place - I went for some rolls, and went into the sitting-room, to breakfast; the prisoner came down, and said, "Good morning;" my landlord said, "Do you call this morning?" (it was then past nine o'clock) we went on with our breakfast, and in about ten minutes I went into the bed-room, and missed my watch; I afterwards found my watch at a pawnbroker's, in Charlotte-place, Lambethmarsh - I searched for the prisoner for a month, but could not find him; his week was not up at the lodging, and he ought to have returned - when three months had expired, I was in great want of my watch; I redeemed it for 2l. 4s. 8d. - I never found the prisoner till last Friday week; when I saw him he said, "You have compounded felony;" I said, "For what?" he repeated it again - I said, "For what?" he then said he took the watch through distress.
   GEORGE COOPER. I went to inquire at the prisoner's lodgings for a man he knew, and from some inquiry I thought he was the man who robbed the prosecutor, and took him.
   JOSEPH CLEMENTS. I am an officer. I took the prisoner - I made him no promise or threat; he admitted that he took the watch.
 -. I am an officer. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction - I am certain he is the man.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Transported for Seven Years.
 481.  GEORGE BAILEY was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of December , 2 garden hand-glasses, value 3s., the goods of James Young.
 JAMES YOUNG. I lost two garden hand-glasses from my garden, at  Middleton-hill, Spa-fields, on the 17th of December, and from information I found I had lost another - I do not know the prisoner.
   GEORGE PEARSON. I am a gardener, and live in George-street, Bagnigge-wells. I bought these two glasses of the prisoner on the 21st of December - I had seen him once or twice before; he asked me to buy them- I said I would not buy them of a stranger, I would come and look at them, and he took me to his garden, where there was a summer-house, locked up; I bought some things of him then; he brought the glasses afterwards, and left them at my neighbour's - he called the next day, and I bought them of him for 9s. 6d.
   WILLIAM REYNOLDS. I received the prisoner in charge- I have known him seven years; he has had a good character.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 24. Recommended to Mercy- Confined Fourteen Days.
 482.  WILLIAM FIELD and JOHN JONES were indicted for stealing, on the 8th of December , 1 coat, value 20s., the goods of Samuel Jones ; and that the said John Jones had been before convicted of felony.
   SAMUEL JONES. I drive for Mr. Norris, of Sion-hill, my waggon was in Marylebone-street on the 8th of December - I was unloading some potatoes, and my coat was on the waggon; I did not take any notice of the prisoners, but they might have been there; the officer brought me my coat - this is it.
   FRANCIS KEYS. I am an officer. On the 8th of December I was coming along Great Marylebone-street - I saw the waggon, and the coat on the sacks of potatoes; I saw the prisoners pass, and look at it - I hid myself, saw Field get on the wheel of the waggon, take the coat, and give it to Jones; they ran one way - I pursued, ran past Field, and took Jones with the coat; a man came up, and I told him to take Field, which he did.
   PETER HOGG. I caught Field in Queen Anne-street- he was running.
   ROBERT GOOSE. I am a Policeman. I have a certificate of the conviction of Jones, by the name of Bland, in September last, which I got from Mr. Clark's office; I was a witness, and know he is the man.
 FIELD -    GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Seven Years.
 JONES -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 483.  ELIZA FRENCH was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of December , 1 gown, value 2s., the goods of Ambrose Rotton.
 AMBROSE ROTTON. I live in  Playhouse-yard. On the 20th of December the prisoner came to my house, but I was not at home; on the 30th she came again - I was sitting in my back parlour; my son came in, and said the girl who had taken the gown on the 20th was in the shop.
   EDWARD ROTTON. I am the prosecutor's son. On the 20th of December the prisoner came to my father's shop, and asked for an old shoe; I had not one that would suit her - she asked if I should have one the next day; I said most likely - she said she would call again; I went round the counter after she was gone, and found a pair of trousers pulled down; I took them up, and then missed a gown, which had been safe a quarter of an hour before; I have not seen it since.
     NOT GUILTY.
 484.  EDWARD DRAYTON was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of December , 2 loaves of bread, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Amos Butts.
   AMOS BUTTS. I am a baker, at Endfield. On the 26th of December I saw the prisoner come and take two loaves off the counter; I knew him before by sight, but not by name; he has been on the parish lately - he was walking away with the bread; I collared him at the door, and he dropped it.
   HENRY BROCKENBOROUGH. I saw the prisoner take the loaves off the counter, and my master took him directly.
 The prisoner pleaded poverty.
     GUILTY. Aged 25. - Confined Three Months.
