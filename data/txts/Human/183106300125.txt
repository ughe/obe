 1457.  REUBEN ROY and SARAH BRADS were indicted for stealing, on the 13th of June , 1 coat, value 1l.; 2 handkerchiefs, value 4s.; 1 stock, value 6d.; 1 halfcrown, and 1 shilling, the property of James McDermot , from his person.
   JAMES McDERMOT. I live in Cow Heel-alley, and am a tailor. On a Monday in June I was at the Cock, in Golden-lane - I saw Roy there; I treated him to porter and gin, and he said he would take me to his place to have breakfast - there was another man with him; we then went to No. 4, Chequer-alley, kept by Mr. Rowley; we went to the back part of the house, and the other man said I ought to send for some beer - I said I had no money; Roy said I had - he put his hand to my pocket, and said he would have the price of half a gallon; he took out a shilling and a half-crown - I did not agree to it at the time, but it came in, and I drank some - Brads was there; the men then said my coat was soiled, and they would brush it; Brads went, and brought a scrubbing-brush - I said I would not allow my coat to be cleaned with that - they said they would have it cleaned, and they would take it off, which they did; I then turned to light my pipe, and the men were gone off with the coat - Brads said it was gone to the pawnbroker's; I saw Roy at the Pitt's Head, in Old-street, two days afterwards - I had him taken, and after he had had three hearings at Worship-street, the daughter of the woman he lives with brought the coat to my lodging.
 Roy. Q. Did you not come to me the next day? A. No; I went to your lodging, but you was not there - there was a stock and two handkerchiefs in the coat pocket.
   EDMUND HARDWICK. I am a Police-constable. I took the prisoners.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Roy's Defence. I never saw him; when I went home at night I understood that he and two more drunken men had been there; he called the next day, and asked if I knew whether Butt had been there - the duplicate of it was found two days afterwards in my landlady's house, and she sent it to him - he came to me a few days after, and wanted 10s. to make it up.
   JAMES McDERMOT. They came to me, and asked if I would settle it; I said if they gave me 10s., and paid for my time I would; but I was told I should be tried myself if I did.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1458.  MARY PAGE was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of June , 1 crown, 1 half-crown, 20 shillings, and 8 sixpences, the monies of Philip Hitchman , from his person.
   PHILIP HITCHMAN. I am a labourer, and live at Wantage, Berks. I was coming to town, and met the prisoner at Old Brentford - I had 33s. in my pocket; I went to two public-houses with her, and had two pints of cyder and one pint of beer - the second was the Running Horse, - my money was in a brown cloth bag in my pocket; I took it out there, and put it into my left-hand breeches pocket - I then went down a lane there, and while something was happening, I found her hand in my pocket; she then went off, and joined three men - I followed her to the Running Horse again, and accused her of taking my money, which I missed; she said she had not had it- a Policeman came, and took her; I had not given her any thing - my purse was safe in the lane, and I missed it directly she left.
 Cross-examined by MR. CHURCHILL. Q.What did you drink at the first house? A. A little gin, and a quartern of peppermint; I was about an hour and a half with her drinking - I had made no bargain with her; I felt her drawing my purse out - I did not tell her I had no money, nor that she might search me.
   JOHNSON ORR. I am a Policeman. I was on duty, and heard something about this - the prosecutor was sober, but much distressed; I saw the prisoner walking down the lane, and heard her tell a man she was accused of taking the money - I went by them, and saw them go to the Running Horse; the prosecutor came up, and went in - he came out, and took me in; I took the prisoner, and found one new shilling on her only - this was about ten minutes after I had seen her.
 Cross-examined. Q. There was time to escape then? A. Not after I took her - she came to the door and said she would send for a Policeman to search her.
 Prisoner's Defence. I never left him till I returned to the public house - as I came back I met several young men, and told them the charge he made; he said if I would give him 6d. or 1s., he would say no more about it.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. - Confined Six Months.
