 2428. HARRIET HASSELL was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of August , 1 sheet, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Charles Cossum . CHARLES COSSUM. I am a linen-draper, and live in Guildford-place, Clerkenwell. I missed a shirt, which hung at my door, on the 13th' of August—I had seen it safe about an hour before—this now produced is it. SAMUEL WOOD. I am shopman to Mrs. Cordwell, a pawnbroker, in Exmouth-street. On the morning of the 13th I took this shirt in pawn of the prisoner—in consequence of suspicions, I went to the prosecutor—he came to our shop, and gave her in charge. WILLIAM POWELL( police-constable G  184.) I received the prisoner—she said she would rather go to prison than be about the street.
   Prisoner's Defence . A man pledged it; he said he would treat me if I would go in with him, and when I came out he was gone. GUILTY.* Aged 30.— Confined Four Months.
