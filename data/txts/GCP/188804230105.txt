490. CHARLES MULLALEY (24), Stealing & watch chain of Francis
Aaron from his person.
MR. MUIR Prosecuted.
FRANCIS A ARON. I am a railway porter, of 21, Colpin Road, Custom
House-on 17th April about 9 p.m. I was in Liliput Road, and saw
three men, and the prisoner stepped out and seized my chain, tore it from
my cuat, and ran away: I pursued him: I lost sight of him for two
three minutes, and then two boys called out, and I saw him lying on the
ground in Church Road, he got up and got over a wall, I went rou
and met him running. I called "Stop him!”-a young manran frond
a shop and stopped him, and they fell down-the prisoner is the man-
there were plenty of lamps.
WILLIAM CALVERT. I am a pawnbroker's assistant-on 17th April,
about 8.30, I heard cries of "Stop him!,” saw the prisoner running and
stopped him-he fell but I did not. Aaron then came up.
