681. OWEN CALLAHAN (30), Feloniously killing and slaying Thomas
Lexton.
Mr. McDONNELL conducted the Prosecution.
MARY LEXTON I am the widow of the deceased Thomas Lexton-he WAS
a labourer, and fifty-four years of age-on Monday, 20th May, I returned
home from my work between 10 and 11 o'clock, and found him in bed
bleeding right down the fingers, and down the bosom of bis shirt-his health
before that was very good indeed-he never complained.
Cross-ecamined by Mr. SLEIGH. 0. How long have you been married to
him 4. Fourteen years-he was never ill for an hour the whole of that
time-he has never suffered from any accident or blow that I know of-this
was only his finger which was cut-the fourth finger of the left-hand-it
appeared as if it had been broken by glass-it happened on Whit-Monday,
and last Sunday six weeks he was buried-1 am no scholar.
