CHARLES GRAY (Policeman, K 332). I went with the last witness to the
prisoner's house-I found this coat there-I searched the pockets, and in the
right hand inside pocket I found a quantity of feathers, which, I believe, are
duck's feathers, and here is a quill, which, I believe, is that of a duek--there
were white feathers about the prisoner's bed room-I went with the last
witness to the Forest-I saw the prisoner-he walked a little distance, and
then ran the best part of half a mile I was with the other constable when
he compared the marks of the shoes.
COURT to ROBERT NOBLE. Q. What age were your ducks? A. Two
years old-these feathers in the coat pocket are duck's feathers, and this I
believe is a duck's quill.
