DAVID ABRAHAMS. I was at that time a City police-constable, No. 150. I took Edwards into custody at No. 8, Jewin-street—in going to the station, he said his name was Mr. Whiffin, and that he was an agent, at Chelmsford—I asked him if he knew a person named Madell—he said he had been in company with a friend who had made a dupe of him—when he got to the sta tion he said his name was Edwards—I said, "You told me your name was Whiffin"—he said he had not.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  How comes it you are not in the police now? A.  I resigned—I was ordered to resign—I had been in the police before—Edwards and I were alone when he told me his name was Mr. Whiffin. JOHN HARVEY. ( police-constable G  118.)
