2032. JAMES TOHEY and JAMES GEORGE were indicted for stealing,
on the 6th of June, 1 handkerchief, value 4d., the goods of a man unknown,
from bis person. schon
JAMES Brannan (police-constable N 69.) On the 6th of June, I was in
Greenwich fair, and saw the prisoners together-I saw George put his hand into
several gentlemen's pockets, and at length he took a handkerchief from a
gentleman's pocket, and passed it to Tohey, who was behind him-I endea-
voured to get to the gentlemen, but the crowd came between us and he got
away-I took the prisoners afterwards—I found two handkerchiefs on Tohey,
and I believe this is the one that George took from the gentleman-I found a
small knife in George's hand, which was open--I had been watching them
through the fair for half an hour.
Tohey's Defence. The handkerchiefs found on me by the officer were my
own; the white one my aunt gave me, and the other I had had above nine
months.

2033. THOMAS CASSITY was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of May,
1 gun, value 10s.; 1 coat, 58.; the goods of William John Ferguson.
William John FerGUSON. I am a gardener, and live at Deptford. On
the 13th of May, I had a coat and a gun in a shed in my garden, which was
not locked—I saw them safe that night, and missed them about six o'clock
the next morning—this is my coat-the gun has not been found.
John JONES. I am a labourer, and live in Workhouse-lane, Deptford. I
have known the prisoner a little while-on Sunday morning, the 14th of
May, he said to me, "I bought a coat and a cap for 1s. 6d., of a man in
Church-street, last night, and if you like I will sell it for the same"-I said I
did not want them--he offered it to several, and in the afternoon I gave him
Is. 6d, for them-on the Wednesday I heard Mr. Ferguson had lost a coat-
I ran to him, and he owned it.
