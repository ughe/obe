1437. CHARLES CAPLIN was indicted for a misdemeanor.
Ann RANDALL. I am the wife of William Randall, who keeps the
White Lion public-house, in Clare-market. On Sunday night, the 5th of
April, the prisoner came for a pint of porter- it came to 2d.- he gave me
half-a-crown, and I gave him 28.4d, change--I put the half-erown on the side
of the counter--there was no other money there it did not remain there
two minutes—when I examined it, after the prisoner was gone, I found it
was bad, and put it into a quartern measure I afterwards took it up stairs,
locked it in a tea-caddy, and kept the key till I opened it and gave the
half-crown to the policeman-on the 17th of April the prisoner came
again, about half-past eleven o'clock, and asked for a pint of beer-he
gave me a shilling, which I bent and gave to my husband I thought it
was bad-1 asked where he got it-he ran away he was pursued and
brought back-I mentioned to my busband about the prisoner being there
before, and the prisoner said he had not been there I am sure he is the
person- he has the same clothes on.
Prisoner. Q. How do you know me? 4, I know you well; you
live at Hungerford-market.

WILLIAN RANDALL. I received a bad shilling from my wife in the
prisoner's presence, and she said that he had passed a bad ball-crown to
her before on Sunday week-I reached to lay hold of him, and he imme-
diately ran out--this was on Good Friday night, the 17th-he had come
before on the 5th of April-he got out of my sight-I kept the shilling in
my band till I marked it at the station-house and gave it to the officer.
CHARLES ATTYIELD (police-constable F 131.) I got the shilling from
Mr. Randall-I heard the cry of “Stop thief," saw the prisoner running,
and stopped him he begged me to let him go - I said I could not.
John RIDDICK (police-constable F 10.) On the 17th of April I went
to Mr. Randall's house-I received this half-erown from Mrs. Randall-I
searched the prisoner at the station-house, and found on him two playing
cards and a penny.
MR. JOHN FIELD. These are both counterfeit.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury.
