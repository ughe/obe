OLD COURT.-Wednesday, December 19th, 1838.
Before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
296. GEORGE BAKER was indicted for stealing, on the 11th
December, 1 handkerchief, value 58., the goods of John Wynne Strettle:
to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Confined Three Months.
297. AMY WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of Sep
tember, at St. Botolph without Aldgate, 6 forks, value 31.; 13 spoons,
value 21. 148.; 1 pair of breeches, value 11. 108.; 1 coat, value 11. 10.;
2 shirts, value 158.; 5 gowns, value 31. 185.; 2 yards of silk, value 178.;
5 sovereigns, and 2 £5 Bank-notes; the goods, monies, and property of
Samuel Hawkins Jutsum, her master, in his dwelling-house.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
SAMUEL HAWKINS JUTSUM. I am a wholesale butcher, and live in
Whitechapel, in the parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate. The prisoner
has been in my service about eight months--on Saturday, the 8th of Sep-
tember, I went to my wife and family into the country, leaving the prisoner
in charge of every thing I returned on the Monday following, and found
she had left the house she had given me no notice whateverwe imme
diately examined the house, and found all the plate gone-I went to the
secretaire and book-case, and missed 2 £5 notes, and 51. in gold, which
had been placed there on the Friday evening, and in the prisoner's room
were three chests, one of which was quite emptied the lock of one box
was hampered-other things were gone.
MATILDA JUTSUM. I came to town with my husband-I examined the
drawers, and missed the plate, money, and my dresses--I had no idea of
the prisoner's intention of leaving the house.
GEORGE JUTSUM. I am the prosecutor's son. On Friday evening, the
7th of September, I placed two £5 notes and five sovereigns in a drawer
in the book-case, in the front parlour-on Sunday evening, the 9th of Sep-
tember, I went out, leaving the prisoner in charge of the house.
WILLIAM ARGENT. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into cus-
tody on the 7th of December, at the prosecutor's house-she told me her
husband had done the robbery, and that she went out to look for bim.
