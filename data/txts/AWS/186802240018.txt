HENRY PRIoR. I am warehouseman and manager to Mrs. Burt, of 10,
Aldermanbury ; she is in the stuff and fancy dress trade-on the 31st
January last, the prisoner came into the warehouse, in the usual way that

goods for Mr. Johnson gave it to him it contained fancy mohair
