 1366. HENRY GREENFIELD was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of May , 1 coat, value 1 s  .; 1 pair of trowsers, value 7 s  .; and 2 pencil-cases, value 3 s  .; the goods of George Hurst ; to which he pleaded. GUILTY.— Confined Two Days.

 1367. JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 3 s  ., the goods of John Bond Ratcliffe , from his person. JOHN BOND RATCLIFFE. I was going through Union-street, South-wark, on the 20th of April, about half-past four o'clock, and stopped to allow some carriages to pass—I felt my handkerchief drawn from me—I turned and saw the prisoner crossing the road with it under his arm—I took it from him and gave him into custody. JOHN FEAR( police-constable M  42.) I was called and received the prisoner and the handkerchief.
   Prisoner . There were a mob—a young man and woman were passing—I saw this handkerchief drop—I picked it up, and was putting it into my pocket, and the prosecutor seized me. JOHN BOND RATCLIFFE. There was no one nearer me than him—I know it was him took it from me, and he owned to it afterwards. GUILTY.* Aged 25.— Transported for Seven Years.
 1368. WILLIAM RATCLIFFE was indicted for stealing, on the   29th of April , 1 sovereign, the monies of Peter Annand , his master. PETER ANNAND. I live at Bridge-road, Lambeth, and am a confectioner. The prisoner was in my employ for about ten days—I saw this sovereign safe in the till about nine o'clock at night on the 29th of April, and missed it before ten o'clock, I immediately made inquiries of the prisoner—he denied having it—when he had got his great coat on, and was going away, I called him back—he waited in my parlour till I fetched a constable—he still denied having it, but the constable found it in his waistcoat pocket—he then said he picked it up from under the counter.
   Prisoner . I found it under the counter—I did not know whose it was—my master did not ask me if I had got it till the policeman came. Witness. I did—he said he had not got it, that he had seen it in the till, but he did not know where it was.
