EDWIN RITCHINS. I keep the Cambridge Arms-on 6th June, about
10 p.m., the last witness came and gave my wife a sixpence for half a pint
of ale-she handed it to me, and I found it was bad, told the child so,
and detained her-I went outside, and saw the prisoner oome out of her
house, 57, Pollard Row-she looked into the Westminster Arms, 30
yards from my house, then into the Oxford Arms, and then hesitated a
few minutes, and looked into any house-I said "Who are you looking
for P are you looking for a little girl ?"-she said “Yes”-I said "I
have been looking for you for the last two months,” caught hold of her,
and took her into the bar, and said “Is that the little girl you are
looking for?"-she said “Yes”-I said “We have taken a bad six-
pence from this little child, who you sent for beer," and gave her in
charge with the coin-I had frequently seen her in the house within the
last seven months, and we have had several bad sixpencee, which we
threw into a drawer, and they were lost.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. You live opposite me, and if I had
