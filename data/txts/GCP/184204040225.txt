Court. Q. Although the child was in this emaciated state, did it ap-
pear to be clean, tidy, and wholesome? 4. It was not clean, nor yet
out of the way dirty-it was dressed, and lying on the bare floor--there
was a bundle in the corner-it appeared like a bed with a kind of coverlid
throwo over.
Cross-examined. Q. Was there any bedstead? A. No-the prisoner
was very respectably dressed, in a bombazeen dress- I heard before the
Magistrate that she comes from Clifton.
WILLIAM MALLETT. I am chief clerk at the police-court, Clerkenwell.
I attended at the examination of the prisoner on this charge-she made a
statement, which I took down accurately in writing-it was not signed by
her-this is it-(reads)—“The prisoner says the child was christened
Selina Emily Highton, and I never called it "brute or devil' in my life ;
it was christened at Clifton Church."
