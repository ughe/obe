0907. GEORGE GATES was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
October, 1 half-crown, 4 shillings, 2 sixpences, 7 pence, 10 halfpence, and
3 farthings, the monies of William Waddington.
ELIZABETH WADDINGTON. I am the wife of William Waddington, who
keeps a beer-shop in Phillip-buildings, Wilsted-street, Somers-town. On
the 20th of October, about ten o'clock at night, I was sitting in front of
the bar, I looked through a window, and saw the prisoner at the til, and
saw him take his hand out of the till-I went out, seized his hand, and
called my husband-be resisted as much as he could, and said he had
nothing but 2s. in his hand-my husband forced his hand open, and found
the money-there were 16s, or 178. shillings in the till before
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. He had been drinking there?
4. Yes, two or three hours-he was rather the worse for what he had
drunk I am sure his hand came out of the till, which was shut, but he
opened it-two shillings were in his band.
WILLIAM WADDINGTON. I am the landlord of the house my wife
called me, and I found her holding the prisoner's hand-he said, "It is
all right"-I requested him to open his hand, he refused, but after I had
put my hand in his pocket, and taken one half-crown, four shillings, two
sixpences, and I think 117d. in pence and halfpence, he opened his hand,
and there was in it two shillings, but I know there was a half-crown in
the till, and considerably more silver than when I came to it.
WILLIAM HAWKE. I was in the house when the prisoner said he had
lost sixpence-- he turned out all his pockets, and he had one half-crown,
two shillings, a sixpence, a halfpenny, and a farthing.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you see the contents of all his pockets? 4.
Yes, I had had a glass of ale just before I did not stay till this took place.
WILLIAM DAVIES. I am a police-sergeant. I was called and took
him into custody.

2908. THOMAS PEARTON was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd
of October, 7 tame ducks. price 148.; and 1 tame goose, price 58.; the
property of Charles James Taylor.--2nd COUNT, for stealing 7 dead
ducks, value 14s.; and 1 dead goose, value 58.; the property of Charles
James Taylor.
HENRY Creed. I am a police-sergeant. On the 23rd of October I was
on the Uxbridge-road, near the top of Ealing-I saw a cart travelling to
London at three o'clock in the morning—there was a bundle behind it, and a
man walking five or ten yards behind- I asked if the bundle belonged to
him-he said no, it belonged to that man, pointing to the prisoner I went
and asked the prisoner if it belonged to him-he said, “Yes," and he had
two or three ducks in it-that he bad bred them and killed them, and was
going to take them to London market-I took him and the bundle, in
Which I found seven ducks and a goose, which was warm.
