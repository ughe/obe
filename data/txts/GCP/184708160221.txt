1958. WILLIAM HENRY THOMPSON was indicted for embezzle-
ment.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY BLAKE PARSONS. I am clerk to H. and J. Johnson and Co., of Bush-
lane-on the 13th of June, 1846, I received this check inclosed in this en-
velope and this letter-1 know the prisoner's writing-this check for 501. and
letter are his writing-in exchange for the check inclosed in this letter, I filled
up this check of my employers'-it was signed by Mr. Butler, one of the
partners of our firm-I gave it to the bearer of the letter from the pri-
foner--the prisoner had a letter of credit from the house of Nathaniel
Johnson and Son, on our house-it was in consequence of that letter I gave
this check,
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. You had received this letter
of credit before? A. Yes, I should have parted with the check if the note
had not been sent-I thought that was totally unnecessary.
MR. BALLANTINE stated that he should be unable to resist the evidence, and
consented to a verdict of
GUILTY, Aged 23.-Confined Nine Months.
1959. CHARLES CAHILL was indicted for embezzlement.
Mr. Charnock conducted the Prosecution.
Joan CRIDIFORD. I am a perfumer, and live at Bristol - I deal with
Mr. Burton, of Greek-street, Soho-on the 19th of Jan. I paid the prisoner
21. 18. 3d., on his account.
Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON.Q. How long had you known the
prisoner as a traveller? A. About six months -- he appeared an active dili-
gent person, pursuing his master's interests as well as he could-be received
this money a few minutes after nine o'clock at night-the debt I owed was
121. Is. 3d.-I arranged that I would pay the 21. 18. 3d., and send him a
bill for 101. to Reading
JAMES ALDER. I am a perfumer at Windsor-I know the prisoner, as
traveller to Mr. Burton-on the 21st of Jan. I paid him 41. 158. 8d., on Mr.
Burton's account.
Cross-examined. Q. Was there any discount allowed to you? A. Yes,
5 per cent.--it was paid in the evening.
