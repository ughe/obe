MARY ANN DAY. I keep the Magpie Inn, Northampton-on 14th
August the prisoners, a third man, and Brown's wife, came into the yard
with a living van, a cart, harness, and a chestnut horse--they staved &
week and went on the 21st-Brown lived with his wife in the van and
Greenaway lodged at my house-on the 28th they came again with B
third man, and remained half an hour—the third man asked for some
note paper; I did not see what they did with it. .
Cross-examined. I am quite sure the third man came with them on 14th
August-I saw on the Sunday the horse they brought, it had white legs
and face; they tell me it was the horse that was stolen-I bad seen the
third man some four years ago when he came with Greenaway's father
and mother into my yard.
ARTHUR BENT. I am a butcher at Harpole, near Northampton-on
18th August I saw the prisoners and a third man-they asked me
whether I wanted to sell the cob I had in the market-I said, “I don't
mind”-they asked me whether I would make an exchange for the
cart-I said I did not mind, and I exchanged my cob for the cart and
71. 108.-next day I exshanged my old cart for this cart and set of harness
and gave 318.-all three men were present on both the 18th and 19th
August—I had never seen the third man before, I don't know who he
was; I have seen him in the market since-on 4th September Small saw
these things in my possession-I saw a horse at the Magpie; I don't
know what became of it.
Cross-examined. The third man paid me the 71. 108.-I knew the two
prisoners were with a travelling van--I saw them about the town several
days after 19th August-I saw the third man at the Magpie; he was not
going about with the van.
SAMUEL PEARSE. I am groom to Henry Ward, Hyde House Farm,
Kingsbury, Middlesex-his bay mare was in a field close to his resi-
dence at eight o'clock on 30th August-next morning it was gone
I communicated with the police, and on 7th September I went to
Northampton, where I saw it in the possession of the police and
identified it.
JOSEPH SPENDER CAMPION. I am an auctioneer, of 5, Dorngate, North-
ampton-on 4th September, Greenaway brought me this letter-( Requesting
the witness to sell a bay mare without reserve-signed, " G. Dennis, Towcester")
with a bay mare, which was put in an auction shed-I put her up to
sell in consequence of the instructions in this letter; there was no bid for
her, and she was taken back to the auction shed, and, there left till she
was fetched away, and I saw no more of her.
Cross-examined. I never heard of a Dennis of Towcester-I have been
at Northampton all my life-I know Towcester well, and deal much with
Towcester people.
ALFRED GREEN (Detective Officer, Northampton). On Saturday, 4th Sep-
tember, about 6.30, I saw the two prisoners at Wootton, nearly two miles
from Northampton- I told them they would be charged on suspicion of
stealing a horse, cart, and harness, from the Old Kent Road, on or about
9th August-Bent had accompanied us, and he said, “These are two of
the men; that is my cart and harness, they had it in their possession"
Greenaway said he bought that in the market of a man, he did not
know who-Brown said he did not know who the man was-they
had a van and cart, too, and a bay mare which was drawing the van-
