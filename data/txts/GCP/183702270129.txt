805. JOHN FIRMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of Febru-
ary, at St. Leonard, Shoreditch, 1 saddle, value 21. Tos, ; 1 bridle, value
76.; and 1 gelding, price 201.; the goods and property of Thomas Harvey.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES HARVEY. I am the son of Thomas Harvey, a farmer at Wicks-
comb, Essex, about fifty miles from London. On the 20th of February,
I saw his bay mare safe in the stable, at seven o'clock in the evening
there was no lock to the stable-door--the prisoner is my cousin-I went
to the stable next morning, about six o'clock, and the mare was gone, and
the saddle and bridle also, which I had seen overnight I saw the mare
again last Tuesday, the 28th of February, in Mr. Dyer's stables, at Wor-
ship-street, and the bridle also, but I have not seen the saddle since I
knew the mare when I saw her she is worth about 101.
Cross-etamined by MR. JONES. Q. You can make no mistake about
having seen this particular mare that night? 4. Not at all-it was my
business to look after her--the prisoner has not been to my father's for
several years past-I have seen the mare this morning.
HEZEKIAH FAIRHEAD. I keep a beer-shop at Wick-green, Essex,
about half a mile from the prosecutor's. On the 20th of February I was
out, and came home about three o'clock, and found the prisoner at my
house having a pint of ale-he asked me several questions about Mr. Harvey
-he staid from half-past twelve o'clock till about twenty minutes after eight
O clock in the evening-he told me be was going to Bewhurst that night
that is about three miles off, in Suffolk,) and he was going from there to
Sudbury.
