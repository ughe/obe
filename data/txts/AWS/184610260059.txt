Cross-eamined by MR. PARNELL. Q. All this did not take many mi-
nutes? A. Not very long-there was a hedge on both sides of the road-
there were houses about 100 yards off-my eyes were covered nearly all the
time they had hold of me-I had never seen Greeney before-I was taken
to the station to see whether I could identify Greeney-the door of the cell was
open-1 looked at him, and said he was not the man, but he was not brought
out of the cell-I have not heard of the money since- do not know how
much there was.
Lampert. I never asked you a question in my life, for I never knew you;
I have been on the gates three years myself, and know better than you about
the money. Witness. You did.
Mr. CLARKSON. Q. You never had a doubt of Lampert A. No-I first
saw Greeney after this took place, at the police-station, between eleven and
twelve 'clock in a cell-I will not be certain whether it was light or dark-I
said then I thought he was not one, but when he was brought out to be taken
to Hammersmith I said to the policeman I was sure be was one who came
into the road-seeing him at this bar I have not the least doubt of him.
