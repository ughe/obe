   Prisoner  Mr. Harris told my mother, if I would confess every thing, he would try to do something to get me employment. BARNETT HARRIS re-examined  I did not—I never saw him from the time I missed them till he was at the station; nor did I see his mother—she occupies my third floor, and is very industrious.
 ----  LATHAM . I am the prisoner's mother.
