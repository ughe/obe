   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT .— Monday, October  22 nd , 1866.
   Before Mr. Recorder .
 882. HENRY NEWBURY  (36) PLEADED GUILTY to stealing 2625 pairs of shoes and six wooden casks, also 3850 pairs of shoes and twelve wooden casks of William Austin .
   Recommended to mercy by Prosecutor.— To appear and receive judgment when called upon.
 883. JOHN DONALDSON  (71) was indicted for a libel upon Mary Ann Deans , alleging that she (a nurse in the Middlesex Hospital) had been guilty of indecent and immoral conduct with a patient in one of the wards.
   MR. SLEIGH conducted the Prosecution, and MR. RIBTON the Defence . NOT GUILTY.
 884. HERBERT RYMELL  (32) was indicted for inflicting grievous bodily harm on George Turnham .
   MR. M. GIFFARD , Q. C., for the Prosecution, offered no evidence . NOT GUILTY.
