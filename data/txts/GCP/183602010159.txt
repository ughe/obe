540. EDWIN LAWRENCE was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
January, 2 pairs of shoes, value 6s. ; and 1 pair of half-boots, value 4s.6d.;
the goods of John Masters.
LEPA
PORN MASTERS. I keep a shoe-shop in Field-terrace, St. Pancras.lt
had the care of some shoes from Mr. Reeves--the prisoner is a carpenter
he frequently came to my shop-I lost the shoes, and found them in pawn,
Cross-examined by Mr. Parne. Q. Where does Mr. Reeves lives
A. In Leather-lane--he supplies me with the shoes to sell for him—I do
not purchase them of him-I am answerable for them- it is my shop, but
the goods are his. I employed the prisoner to put a partition up in my
shop, and I have worked for him.
But
EDWARD Pope. I am in the service of Mr. Button, a pawnbroker, at
Battle-bridge. I have three pairs of shoes pawned by the prisoner on the
Ist and 9th of January, and the 21st of December.
JOHN MASTERS re-examined. These belong to Mr. Reeves. a per

the stamp on them I have put no mark on them myself I have had them
in my hands hundreds of times-Mr. Reeves supplies no other man be
buys his goods of manufacturers--I know these have not been sold they
have not been worn.
Jurr. Q. Are they Northampton shoes ? A. Yes--they are the shoot

COURT. Q. They are not cleaned up in the way you prepare them when
