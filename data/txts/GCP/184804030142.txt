1157. JAMES MAY, stealing 36lbs. weight of metal and 17lbs. weight
of copper pipe, value 31s. 2d. ; the goods of George Rennie and another, bir
master.
GEORGE BARKER. I am in the service of George and Sir John Rennie, of
Holland-street. The prisoner was their trimmer-on 9th March I left 88lbs.
of copper and two or three pieces of copper pipe safe at half-past five o'clock.
on a grating at the back of the furnace-I knocked this piece of pipe about,
and can swear to it (produced)- I had it in my band that morning.
SAMUEL WISE. I am gate-keeper at Messrs. Rennie's. On 9th March,
about eight o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner coming from the brass.
foundry to the gate, with a key in his hand, and a handkerchief and this
metal under his arm-I followed him about twenty yards into John-street,
and said "May, I want to speak to you"-be said, “What do you want!"
-I said, " Come, and I will tell you "-I touched the handkerchief, and
said, “I want to know what you have got there "-he said, "It is the first
time I ever took anything, let me go, and I will make it all right in the
morning"-be leaned on a window-ledge, as if he was faint, and I afterwards
found this piece of pipe there-I have compared it with the rest; it corre-
sponds.
John Mills (policeman.) The prisoner was given in my charge-I
found this small piece of pipe in his pocket-he said, "Don't shew him that"
(meaning Wise).
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Recommended to mercy-Confined Six Months.

window was broken-I missed a 5-in. box and a flanch, value 21. 38.--I had
seen them safe ten minutes before these are them.

broken? A. No; I received information in five or six minutes- I was at
a butcher's, ten or twenty yards off-I did not hear the crash, it was whole
when I left-this is a brass union flanch-it is tinned over-it would be
flanch if it was not tinned - this is a brass box-it has leather to it-it
worth 1l. 53.
