2454. WILLIAM GABELL was indicted for embezzlement.
WILLIAM NELSON. I am a publican. The prisoner was my pot-boy,
and had been three weeks and three days—it was his duty to settle
every night before he went to bed, which he did till the day he ab-
sconded.
THOMAS MANSELL. I take beer of the prosecutor. On the 15th of Oc-
tober I paid the prisoner ninepence for beer which I received that day.
WILLIAM FRICKLEY. I deal with Mr. Nelson. I paid the prisoner
eightpence on the 15th of October for two pots of beer.
THOMAS THATCHER. I deal with Mr. Nelson. I paid the prisoner
twopence on the 15th of October, for some beer which he brought me
that day.
WILLIAM NELSON re-examined. He absconded with 158. 8d. He
brought the tray of pots in, and went away-I paid him weekly-his week
ended on Wednesday, and he went away on Sunday night.
Prisoner. He left me to find the customers-some I took the money
from, some I did not I had to pay for those that did not pay me that
night I got drinking with a young man who was going on board a ship, and
drank rather too much I went to go back on Monday, and met the same
young man again-1 had no wages while I was there,
