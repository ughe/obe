 261. THOMAS NEWMAN , stealing 22lbs. 15 oz. of copper, value 16 s  ., the property of Thomas Henry Maudsley and other, his masters.
   MR. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution. WILLIAM DYKE. The prisoner was an engine-driver at Messrs. Maudaleys'—I was in their employ—on 4th Nov., in consequence of missing copper and brass, I watched the prisoner, and saw him look up to the top of the engine—I afterwards saw him on the top of the engine, pulling something about like—I watched till he went to tea, then went on the top of the engine, and found three bars of copper; I left it there—he came back, and went to work till the men left—he then set to to clean the engine—he put the lights very low, then reached the bars of copper down, tied them with some strings to his pockets, and then went and washed himself—I saw him leave, followed, and never lost sight of him, not half a second—I walked up and asked him to have some thing to drink, as I could see no policeman—I asked a labourer to have some thing to drink, and told him to fetch a policeman—the prisoner heard me say so—I said I was going to gave him into custody—he said, "For God's sake do not, for the sake of my wife and family; I have got a lot of copper upon me"—I said, "I know you have"—I gave him in charge—this is the same sort of cooper as was on Messrs. Maudsley's premises ( produced )—we have it in large quantities—we should not miss 100 pieces—six pieces were found on the prisoner, tied with string—they are eight or ten feet long, but the pri soner had cut them to carry them away. JAMES SHERIFF. I am in the service of Messrs. Maudsley—this copper is worth 16 s . or 1 l .—it is the same as we have on our premises. DAVID PEGRAM( policeman. ) The prisoner was given in my charge—I searched him, and found six large pieces of copper on him. GUILTY . Aged 25.— Confined Twelve Months .
