Mr. WARBURTON contended that the offence charged being an attempt to
obtain, it was distinguishable from the case of the Q. v. Larner, (Q. v. Brown,

MR. BURNie, in reply, argued that if in law a full offence could not
be committed, the attempt to commit it was no offence; although the attempt
to commit an offence might be made out, even if the full offence was
physically impossible.
The RECORDER stated that after the decision in Q. v. Larner, although he
personally felt no doubt in the matter, he would consider whether he should
reserve the point.
