drove up, and two or three dozen empty bags.
Cross-ecamined. I have not been a witness before in this case-I
drove in at-the City Pre8s gates in the usual way; I did not begin to

van came up-I did not hear Mr. Benny say he wanted the stuff

did not see him speaking to Yates.
Re-eramined. I was at the Guildhall, but was not called-I was with

the one-horse van my van was 200 yards away from it.
