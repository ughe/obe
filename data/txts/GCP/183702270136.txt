CORNWALL BARRY Wilson. I am a solicitor, and lrave chambers
Furnival's Inn. I know the two prisoners--they were arrested on a bill
in which I was concerned, and were sent to Whitecross-street in the early
part of August, last year--they were discharged the latter end of the fol.
lowing term, about the latter end of November-I saw them in prison in
August, but never saw them again-the discharge was handed to me by
the attorney in November.
Court. . Q. Are we to understand you that they were not out of
custody from August to the beginning of November? A. They were in
custody the whole time-I can undertake to say they were in custody in
November--they were both in custody on the same matter-one was the
drawer of the bill, and the other the acceptor-they were both arrested the
same day, I believe--I saw them both in prison about the 12th of Auguk
- I did not see them in September.
Mr. Bopkin. Q. Were you concerned in the action? A. No-Iwu
employed to negociate with the plaintiff for them, but I could not-there
were no papers--the writ was not sent to me--they had no attorney-
ultimately arranged for their discharge with the plaintiff's attorney, Mr.
Melton, who lives in Surrey-street, Strand, I think I never saw the
writ, nor any of the papers--the discharge was given to me.
