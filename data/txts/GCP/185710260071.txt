TENRY WEBB. I am & detective officer of the City police. On 17th
Oct. I apprehended Bolyne at the corner of a street, in Foley Street,
Kitaroy Square-I told him I wanted him for uttering & forged note, at a
coffee shop in Holbord, on or about the 1st Sept.---he said " I know nothing
about it; I thought you wanted me for a 302. bill"- I took him to the
station-he there gave the name of John Bolyne, but refused his address :
Cross-examined. Q. Was he searched ! A. I searched him I found on
him a betting book; he said he had been engaged in betting on the turf for
some time he did not say that he had received any money lately due to him.
