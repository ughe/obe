536. HENRY WRIGHT (50), PLEADED GUILTY to fraudulently con-
verting £3 10s., the property of Isaac Taft, to his own use, having been
before convicted of felony. Six months' hard labour.
537. ARTHUR HENRY GRAY (20), Attempting to carnally know
Maud Mitchell, a girl under 13; Second count, indecently assaulting her.
MR. HUTTON and Mr. FORDHAM Prosecuted ; Mr. Roots Defended.
GUILTY. Judgment respited and point reserved for the consideration of
the Court for Crown Cases Reserved.
