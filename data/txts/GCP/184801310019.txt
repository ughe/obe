CALEB EDWARD POWELL. This is half of a counterfeit shilling, and this

Prisoner's Defence. I and a young man went to see a man walk; we
went to have a little beer, and some gin in it; we both won a little money at

half-crown; the woman might have been mistaken at half-past five o'clock
in the evening. Guilty. Aged 23.-Confined Six Months.
