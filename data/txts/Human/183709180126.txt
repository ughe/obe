 2169. JOHN WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of September , 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  ., the goods of Stephen Stuart Curtis , from his person. STEPHEN STUART CURTIS. I live in Coleman-street. On the 21st of September, I was in the Strand at one o'clock in the day time—I felt some thing at my coat pocket, and put my hand behind, and my handkerchief was gone—I tamed sharp round, and saw the prisoner with my handkerchief in his hand—I secured him, and he threw it down.
   Prisoner . He took it off the ground—I never had it. Witness . He threw it behind him when I seized hold of his arm—I saw it quite plainly in his hand. LEWIS CURD( police-constable F  108.) I was on duty in the Strand, and received the prisoner into custody with the handkerchief.
 (  Property produced and sworn to .) GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months.
 2170. THOMAS EDMUND WYATT was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of September , 1 blanket, value 7 s  .; and 2 sheets, value 5 s  .; the goods of Goulding Collins . GOULDING COLLINS. I live in Gloucester-street, Queen-square. I lost a blanket and two sheets—the prisoner came to lodge with me last Tuesday week, the 12th of September—I did not miss any thing, as he took the key of the room, till about three o'clock on the Saturday, while he was out, and when he came home I gave him in charge—he immediately gave up the duplicates of the property, and said he intended to restore them. ELLEN HATCH. I am servant in the house—( looking at the property )—I know this, it belongs to my master—I am certain of it. GEORGE WILLIAM WILSON. I am in the service of Mr. Barber, a pawn broker in High-Holborn. I produce a blanket, which the prisoner pawned in the name of John Simpson—I know him to be the man—I gave him the duplicate produced. GEORGE JOHN RESTIEAUX. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into custody last Saturday night, and he gave me thirteen duplicates out of his fob pocket.
   Prisoner's Defence . I meant to restore them on the Saturday evening, but not getting my money, I was not able to do it—I told him I would pay him on the Monday. GOULDING COLLINS re-examined . That is correct what he says—no money was found on him. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Three Months.
