NEW COURT.-Saturday, January 17th, and OLD COURT.-Monday,
January 19th, 1887.
Before Mr. Recorder.
197. SAMUEL VICTOR MORLEY, otherwise SAMUEL MARKS,
Unlawfully obtaining by false pretences from Henry Thomas Lodge an
indenture of lease, with intent to defraud. 37 other Counts varying the form
of charge.
MESSRS. MOYSES and RICHMOND Prosecuted; SIR CHARLES RUSSELL, Q.0.
and Mr. POLAND Defended.
The defendant in this case was acting on behalf of persons claiming property
under the will of one Joshua Mobbs. The RECORDER, who in the course of the
case intimated more than once that there was no proof that the defendant acted
fraudulently, after hearing Counsel on both sides held that there was no case to
go to the Jury, the defendant having acted in the matter under the advice of
eminent Counsel, and directed a verdict of
