Cross-examined. When I saw my son's bed on the Friday morning
it had been slept in, but it was not much rumpled because he slept by
himself, and he never kicked about much I did not tell Detective
Gillan on the Friday that the bed had not been slept in. I know
nothing of what happened between a quarter past 10 on the Thursday
night and seven o'clock on the Friday morning.
Re-examined. It was the detective who started the conversation
about the bed not being slept in. I took him upstairs and showed
him Charlie's room. I said, “This is his room; there is his bed just
as he got out of it; he could not have kicked about much, the bed
is not very much rumpled." He said, "No." (To Judge Lumley
Smith.) I was the first to come down in the morning. I came down
about seven ; I did not know that Charlie had gone then, but he was
in fact out of the house.
Verdict, Guilty.
Previous convictions were proved against bola prisoners. Sentence,
both, Five years' penal servitude.
