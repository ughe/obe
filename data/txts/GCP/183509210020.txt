that to be correct—he took off his stock, inside which his name was written
-when I mentioned this to Mr. Reynolds, at the Elephant and Castle,
thought he appeared rather agitated-he appeared rather surprised.
Q. A little confused? A. Very trifling-he did not thank me for har
ing traced his watch-we had very little conversation-Mr. Reynolds ap

in my presence of what had passed between him and me, to the gentlemen
with him—there was an appointment made for him to meet me at Bow.
street next morning, but not at that precise moment.
MR. REYNOLDS re-examined. This is my watch and the one I parted
