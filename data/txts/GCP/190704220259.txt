BEFORE THE RECORDER.
(Tuesday, April 23.)
HOLMES, John Noel Wendell (24, engineer); breaking and enter
ing the dwelling-house of Thomas Foster and stealing therein two gold
brooches and other articles, the property of Florence Foster, and one
platinum and gold ring, and other articles, the property of the said
Thomas Foster, and feloniously receiving same.
Mr. Curtis-Bennett prosecuted. Mr. Symmons defended.
FLORENCE FOSTER, wife of Thomas Foster, 12, Sheen Gate Mansions,
Mortlake. On March 30, 1907, I left my flat at 5.45 p.m., securely
fastening the door, and returned at 9.45. The flat is on the second
Boor, and as I neared the door I heard men's voices inside. I saw
that the light was full on from my bedroom. There are five rooms
obe sitting-room, kitchen, and three bedrooms. Thinking my husband
had returned, I tapped the letter-box; the gas was then turned out
and no one opened the door. I hurried down and sent my niece for
the hall porter, who lives opposite. He came immediately, and I
went for a constable, whom I found at the corner, and returned to the
ball door with him. The hall porter was arguing with two men who
had come downstairs, one of whom was the prisoner and the other &
man named Mott, who had since died. I knew Mott as having had
business relations with my husband two years ago. I had never seen
prisoner before. I asked prisoner, “Who are you 1” He told me his
name-Wendell Holmes and said he had come to see Mr. Foster.
The constable arrested them. Mott said nothing. Mott was intoxi-
cated, and the prisoner appeared excited with drink. I accom-
panied them to the police station. Mott ran away on the road. I
went back to the flat with a detective. We examined the door of the
Hat and found it had been forced. There were splinters of wood on
the mat, and part of the book was on the ball table. On searohing I
hissed the articles mentioned in the indictment, part of which I iden-
way. A watch and ring have not been found. The value of the
articles lost is $7 108 Cigars had been taken from a box in the
