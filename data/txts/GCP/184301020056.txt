465. JOSEPH BARNETT was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of Dec.
1 coat, value 108.; the goods of Richard Reeks.
John STOREY (City police-constable, No. 414.) I was on duty, on the
6th of Dec., in plain clothes, in Farringdon-street, with David Storey-I saw
the prisoner and two others cross the road - one took the coat out of the
cart, and gave it to the prisoner- I pursued them all three, and stopped the
prisoner with the coat in his possession-I took him to the station--this is
the coat.
David Storey. I saw this, I have heard what John Storey has said
is true.
Richard Reeks. I am a licensed victualler, and live at Roehampton.
This is my coat--I left it in my cart in Farringdon-street that evening whilst
I went into a shop.
Prisoner's Defence. A person stole the coat and threw it to me; he made
his escape.
GUILTY. Aged 19.- Recommended to mercy.--Confined Three Months.
466. MARY ANN MALEARAM was indicted for stealing, on the 29th
of Dec., 2 blankets, value 2s.; 2 pillows, 58. 6d.; I sheet, 28.; and 1 coun-
terpane, 28. 6d. ; the goods of John Hoddy.
CHARLOTTE HODDY. I am the wife of John Hoddy, a shoemaker, in
Carr-square, Moor-lane. In consequence of information, on Thursday the
29th of Dec., I went up into one of my rooms, and missed two pillows, one
sheet, two blankets, a counterpane, and a patch-work quilt off the bed-1
went into Carr-square, and saw the policeman-I went with him to the sta-
tion, and saw the property there, this is it.
BENJAMIN SEXTON (City police-constable, No. 161.) On the 29th of
Dec., about five o'clock, I saw the prisoner coming out of Carr-square-se
had this bundle in her arms- I apprehended her.
Prisoner's Defence. I met a woman ; she asked me to carry the bundle
for her to Union-street, and she would give me sixpence; the policeman
stopped me, and asked where I got the things ; I told him.
Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Six Months.
