Before J. A. Rentoul, Esq., K.C.
   Fourth Court, January  15 th , 1906.
 196. BENJAMIN POPE  (30) , Together with other persons unknown feloniously assaulting Henry Goddard and robbing him of 5s. 6d. with violence.
   MR. WRIGHT Prosecuted ; MR. RODERICK Defended. HENRY GODDARD. I am a dealer and live at 6, Loughborough Street, Kennington—on Saturday, December 23rd, about 7 p.m. I was outside a fish shop in Chester Street, Lower Kennington Lane, buying some fish—the prisoner came up to me with two other men and said, "Are you going to buy some beer?"—I said, "I haven't got no  money for beer "—just then my carman came up and handed me 11s. 1 1/2 d.—the prisoner then said to me, "Now you have sot some money, give us a shilling or else we shall snottle  you "—he put his right hand in my left hand and punched me on the nose with his left hand—one of the other men with him punched me in the back of the ear—I received two punches and was knocked almost senseless—when I recovered I counted up my money and found I had lost 5s. 6d.—my carman picked me up—I saw the prisoner running one way and the other men another—on December 28th I found the prisoner in custody and charged him—I knew him by seeing him outside public-houses—I have spoken to him—he has asked me for some coppers for breakfast, as he said he had been out all night.
   Cross-examined.  I knew they called him "Pope "—I have not known him for four years, only about six or seven months—he has never worked for me—I take men on for odd jobs occasionally, but never had him—I should have known if I had taken him on—I know one of Guest's public houses—I might have been in there, but I never drank with him—there was no quarrel between us—I did not hit him—I have lived in the neigh—bourhood for four years—I do not know the other men who were with the prisoner.
   Re-examined.  I had not seen the prisoner that day before 7 o'clock—I have never had drink with him at any public-house.
 By  MR. RODERICK . I am a peaceable man—I have had six months' imprisonment for knocking a man down with a poker—I found him in bed with my wife.
 By  MR. WRIGHT . When he came out of the room he struck me, and I hit him with a poker. THOMAS SAUNDERS. I am carman to the last witness and live at 5, Gundulf Street, Kennington—on December 23rd I saw my master and handed him 11s. 1 1/2 d.; that was change out of a sovereign—I saw the prisoner and two others talking to him—when I came up I said to the prosecutor, "Here is your change, sir "—the prisoner then said, "You have some money now; we want some beer. I want a shilling; if we don't get it we shall snottle you "—with that he struck him on the head—one of the others struck him behind the head—I was struck on the jaw—my master was knocked nearly senseless—I picked him up—I did not what went out of his pocket—the men ran away—on Christmas morning
