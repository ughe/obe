 1959.  MARY SCREEN was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of July , 1 cap, value 8d.; 1 shirt, value 1s.; 3 sleeves, value 6d., and 8 gloves, value 6d., the goods of Robert William Henderson.
   SARAH HENDERSON. I am the wife of Robert William Henderson - we live in Queen-street. The prisoner came to my room, and offered to help me work, on Thursday, the 23rd of July; she helped me, and I gave her tea - in the course of the afternoon, she asked me if I would let her sleep with me; I said I did not take lodgers, on account of my husband coming on Saturday night - she said she hoped she should get a situation before that; I gave her her victuals while she staid - I missed these articles on Saturday morning, and named it to her - she was very angry about it; I have since seen the articles in her box.
   WILLIAM ELLIOTT(Police-serjeant D 18). The prisoner was given into my charge at No. 36, Queen-street; I found a bunch of keys on her - I asked where her boxes where, and she told me they were at the Hop Pole, Edgware-road; she took me into the two pair front room, and sat down by two boxes - I asked if they were hers; she refused to answer - I then asked a woman if she had slept there; she said Yes, two or three nights - I asked if she had any boxes - she said, "Yes," these are them -I then found these things in them; the prosecutrix asked where her other box was - she refused to answer; we looked and found a black box, in a cradle, and in it I found the cap, gloves, and habit-shirt; the three gloves were in the first box.
   SARAH HENDERSON. The prisoner had one box at my house with the hinge off, but there was nothing in that - it was covered with leather; my cap was found in that box, and the other things in a hair trunk.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence stating, that the articles were placed in her box without her knowledge.
     GUILTY. Aged 58. - Confined Three Months.
 1960.  MICHAEL MULLINS was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of August 1 handkerchief, value 4s., the goods of Michael Ryan , from his person.
   MICHAEL RYAN, M.D. I live in Hatton-garden. On the 16th of August, I was in Hatton-garden, at a few minutes past six o'clock; a person tapped me on the shoulder, and said my pocket was picked; I turned, and the prisoner was pointed out to me; he was taken, and I saw my handkerchief found on him.
 Prisoner. Q. What part was it? A. Near Holborn; I did not miss it till I was told of it; I saw you down Charles-street, within a few yards of the turning.
   RICHARD AUST MALTWOOD. I am a constable. I was called, and found the prisoner making a very violent resistance; I saw this handkerchief produced at the office.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I had been with my father and a few friends; in going home (being intoxicated) I stumbled against a man - I begged his pardon; he struck me, and I returned it; he said he would give me in charge; I tried to get away, and did not know where I was till I found myself in the watch-house next morning - I never saw the handkerchief.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Confined Six Months.
 1961.  JAMES SUTTON was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of August , 8 live tame fowls, price 14s., and one sack, value 1s. 6d., the property of William Atley.
   JOHN RYAN. (Police-constable L 142). On the morning of the 29th of August, I was in the Westminster-road, at a quarter before five o'clock; I saw the prisoner going towards the Marsh-gate with two small bags, and eight live fowls; I asked what he had got - he said some live fowls; he did not know what they were, but I found they were five cocks and three hens - I stopped him.
   WILLIAM ATLEY. I live at Park Farm, Hanwell, about ten miles from the Westminster-road. My fowls were safe on the 28th of August - I have a good many about my hedges; I had employed the prisoner a great part of the summer, and he saw the fowls from time to time; this sack is mine, he made it into two to bring away the fowls.
   AMELIA ATLEY. I know these fowls - they are my husband's, and were safe on Monday night, the 28th; the prisoner had been discharged on the Saturday before.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Confined Six Months.
 1945.  CHARLES MORRELL was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of July , 1 hat, value 15s., the goods of Richard Watson.
   RICHARD WATSON. I live in Buckingham-street, Fitzroy-square. On the 16th of July I went to a skittle-ground; I put my hat on a bench - the prisoner was sitting within a yard of me; I missed him in about five minutes, and my hat was gone.
 Cross-examined by MR. LEE. Q. What are you? A. A  tailor; there were seven or eight people there- I was quite sober; I gave charge of a young man named William Doe, for receiving the hat - I did not receive 6s. from any person.
