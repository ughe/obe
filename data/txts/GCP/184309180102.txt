2730. ANNETTE CHARLTON was indicted for stealing, on the 194
of Sept., 2 half-crowns, the monies of John Sedgwick Williams, from
person.
John SEDGWICK Williams. I live in South-street, Marylebone. About
two o'clock in the morning of the 12th of Sept., I was going along the New
road-the prisoner caught hold of my collar, and wished me to come home
with her-I said I was going to my own home-I had two half-crowns in my
waistcoat-pocket before I met her-she lifted up the corner of her gown, and
one half-crown dropped-I picked it up-I felt in my pocket, and missed
both my half-crowns- I called a policeman, and gave her into custody-1
know my half-crowns were safe three minutes before.
GEORGE JAMES (policeman.) I was in the New-road-I saw the prisoner and
the prosecutor standing together opposite, in close conversation--the prisoner
then lifted up the corner of her gown, as if putting something into her
pocket, and a piece fell, which sounded like silver-the prosecutor accused
her of stealing two half-crowns, and called "Police "- I went over, and be
gave her into custody-she denied it, and said it was her own money, she
had none of his.
ELIZABETH CHAPMAN. I am the wife of Edward Chapman, a policeman.
I am employed to search females at the station- I found on the prisoner i
sixpence, a key, and a thimble, and in the foot of her right stocking a hall
crown-she said, "Don't take it from me, it is my own; it will be very
unkind if you do."
Guilty. Aged 40.-Confined Four Months.
