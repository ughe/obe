1030. JAMES AGEN was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of March,
7 lbs. weight of beef, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Edward Gower Burton.
EDWARD GOWER BURTON. I live at Chelsea, and am a butcher. On
the 1st of March, between seven and eight o'clock in the evening, I saw
the prisoner come up to the front of my shop, reach over the board, un-
hook a piece of beef, and walk away with it-I ran after him, and sav
him in a corner with two or three more I collared him, and held him till
the policeman came-just before I collared him, I heard something fall,
and the beef was found there,
RICHARD WORSFOLD. I live at Chelsea. I saw the prisoner chuck the
beef into a dark corner-the prosecutor took it up.
Prisoner. I never went near it.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
