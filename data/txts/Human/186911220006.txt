   COURT . Q.  Can you tell at what part Poynter left off reading, and Mr. Cook began to read? A.  No I really cannot, I was in such trouble at the time, my little boy was dead.
   MR. M. WILLIAMS . Q.  Did you understand what Mr. Cook read, after he began to read it? A.  Certainly I did not understand it as I understand it now, or I never should have been so silly as to sign such a deed as that, I am quite certain. FRANK MOORSON.
