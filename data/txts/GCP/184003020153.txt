Louis Krezor. I am a clock and watch manufacturer, and live in
Tottenham Court-road. Last Monday evening the prisoner came to me
and said, " Mr. Kyezor, I want to sell this," handing me this gold chain and
seal and key– I have known him three years—I said, "Where did you get
this?"-he said he had brought them from Bombay with him-he said he
wanted 71. for them-I said, “ Have you not a wateh?"-he said, “No, I
sold that five years ago, and the chain has been in pawn several times, and
I have taken it out to-day"-he said they had lent him 5l. on it-I was
going to weigh it, and he said it weighed two onnces and a half, which I
found it didI said, “The pawnbroker has given you the full value for
it" - he said, “If I pawn it again, will you give me to-morrow 11. on the
ticket?"--I said, “No-he came to me the next day, and said he would
take 51. for it-I said I would go to the pawnbroker with him, and if they
would now lend him 5l. on it I would buy it-he then hesitated, and I
said to him, “Now, I am convinced you stole it, and if you don't tell me
the truth, I will give you in charge"-he then said, “I will tell you the
truth, it belongs to Judge Cracroft"-I said if he did not go and fetch him
I would give him in charge.
