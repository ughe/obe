   Prisoner's Defence.  It was my wish to pay for the goods as I had paid before. I was locked up three weeks, and the case was abandoned, but they got another case. It was the last of my thoughts to do Mr. Bassett out of a farthing. We were like brothers together. He gave me a good character when I left. ALFRED BASSETT( Reexamined ). I gave him a good character because he had not robbed me then. GUILTY  . Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor. — Nine Days' Im prisonment.
