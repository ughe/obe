Q. What character did the prisoner bear while he was with you? A.
While he was with me, he was a well-conducted young man in every respect;
man of a kind, humane disposition-I never heard of his getting into
rows, or committing any assaults-when he left on the Thursday morning,
he bade me good-bye he told me what vessel he was going in, the Vidoria,
from the London Docks to New York-I had heard of his going to Americk
& fortnight before-it was made quite public amongst his friends and persons
living there.
