1809. JOHN JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of May,
one tame duck, price 38., the property of George Trumper.
PETER MITE (police-constable T 142.) At half-past six o'clock, in the
evening of the 19th of May, I met the prisoner carrying a duck, about six
hundred yards from the prosecutor's house-I asked where he got it-he
said he bought it for 18.-I said he must go to the man he bought it of
we went, and could not find him-I took him to the station, and then
found the owner-the duck was warm, and had not been dead five
minutes.
Prisoner. I was half a mile from the place. Witness. You were not.
JOSEPH WBEDON. I am servant to George Trumper, he lives at Nor-
wood-this duck is one of my master's—they were at a distance from
the house, at the end of the lane-I missed two of them this is one-I
have seen the prisoner, but know nothing of him.
