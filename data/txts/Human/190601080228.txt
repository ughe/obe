   Cross-examined by MR. MORRIS . Not very much money was found on Tennant and no postal orders.
   Cross-examined by MR. WALSH . The extracts from Ross's diary were all selected by Mr. Becher and his clerk in my presence; I presume they were selected for the purpose of showing some evidence of guilt against him—at his premises were found bills that showed a general printing business was going on by him for Puddefoot, and an agreement between him and Puddefoot, by which the basement was let to Puddefoot for printing; Puddefoot sub-contracted work for Ross—Exhibit 166 contains the addresses of business firms and telephone numbers and notes with reference to various matters of daily life—the two entries of "Willetts" have numbers against them which appear to be sums of money—in his diary there are entries about going to see people who are ill—on July 8th there is an entry, "Very hot; worried about money "—I do not know anything about Jinks; I take it from you that it is spelt "J-e-n-k-s" in the diary—I cannot tell you what the suggestion is about his going to see Jinks off by train—I have not made inquiries who "Jinks" is, and I do not know who has—it may be he has no connection with this case—copies of entries were made, but what importance attaches to them I do not know—I do not know if there was any raid at Stamford Street—I was not there—I cannot say if the summonses found were ever proceeded with.
   Cross-examined by MR. BENNETT . In the invoices referred to by Mr. Walsh, which were found as showing printing business between Ross and Puddefoot, there was no item of music; in one there was an entry of so many thousands of music catalogues. JOHN ASHLEY( Police Sergeant ). On November 3rd with Inspector Morgan I went to 41a, Farringdon Street, and there saw Ross—I told him I had a warrant for his arrest for conspiring with others to print and sell pirated music—he said, "All about music?" and I said, "Yes "—I took him to Bow Street, and he when charged said, "I have never published or sold a piece of music in my life, and in reference to the selling and exposing of the music I have never done such a thing.
