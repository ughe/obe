219. HENRY BAILEY and JAMES GAUNTLETT were indicted for
stealing, on the 10th of November, 1 pair of trowsers, value 58., the goods
of the Directors and Guardians of the parish of St. Marylebone.
ROBERT DANLY. I am a pauper in the Marylebone workhouse. On
the 10th of November I lodged in a room with Bailey in George-street,
Lisson-grove-about half-past nine o'clock on that morning I went with
Bailey to the long-room at the workhouse for relief-Bailey then went into
the yard and got on the wall-Gauntlett came and spoke to him-Bailey
remained on the wall a quarter of an hour I told him to come down-he
said he would not, no one could see him I then went and asked him
again to come down-he did so, and had a pair of trowsers under his jacket
- he told me that Gauntlett gave them to him--he went across into the
men's yard, and put them on-he had not trowsers on when he went there-
he did not get over the wall-they must have been given to him by some-
body, as he never got off the wall these are the trowsers-he showed
me a pawn-ticket from Gideons's-it was like this one I was admitted
into the workhouse afterwards, and Bailey came in in a quarter of an hour
aftur me-Gauntlett came up to him and said, “If you have got the ticket,
do away with it, there has been a row about it, and all the men know it."
JAMES JOHN MESSER. I live at Mr. Gideon's, a pawnbroker, in Stafford-
street, Lisson-grove. I produce the trowsers, which were pawned by a
young man, but I cannot swear to the prisoner.
ROBERT FORD. I am foreman of the tailors, in Marylebone workhouse.
These trowsers belong to the Directors and Guardians of the poor of that
parish-they had their buttons on them, but they have been cut off,
and others put on.
JAMES BAKER. I am a pauper. I missed a pair of trowsers like these
from under the bed on the 6th of November
EBENEZER BOWYER (police-constable D 143.) I was present when the
prisoners were charged with stealing these trowsers-Bailey said he knew
nothing about them.
BAILEY-GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Three Months.
GAUNTLETT-NOT GUILTY.
