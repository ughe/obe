into her room-she was in bed, alone and undressed--she appeared to me
as though she was dying-she spoke low, but was quite sensible--I ob-
served her hands-her fingers were contracted, and were quite black, and
her face was as black as her hands-I laid hold of one of her hands, it was
as cold as could be I could not open her fingers-I asked her what was
the matter with her---she said, "Mary, I am dying "-I said, “Jane, what
have you been drinking ?"-she told me she had not drunk anything except
the brandy-bitters-she requested me to send for her brother-there was

room afterwards-I sent her for the deceased's brother-soon after Russell
