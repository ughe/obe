Only as clerk to Edwards and Radcliffe—I believe he it managing clerk—about that time he called on some other matter, and inquired if Serjeant Channell was retained in two causes, mentioning their names—upon my stating he bad no retainers in the matter, he said directly he got back to the office the retainers and fees should be sent; and he left a memorandum for me of the plaintiffs' and defendants' names—I am sure he only mentioned two actions—it was some time in April—I do not know whether it was in Easter term—if it was in term-time, managing clerks have a great pressure of business—the cause in question has never been tried.
