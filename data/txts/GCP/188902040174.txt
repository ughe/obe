JOHN CHRISTOPHER Cox. I am manager to my father, John Cox, of
the Blackfriars public-house, Queen Victoria Street-I cannot remember
the transaction, but I must have cashed a cheque for £3 58. 6d. for the
prisoner, because my father was in the country-I remember receiving a
cheque dated 22nd September for £2 10s. from the prisoner (On the York-
shire Banking Company, signed by two directors of the Hull Cement Company)
-I believed it to be good, and gave £2 10s. for it-I paid it into my
father's account on 19th October—it was returned marked "Refer to
drawer”-I have never been paid that amount-I don't think I have
seen the prisoner since except at the Police-court.
Cross-examined. The prisoner had cashed two cheques of the Hull
Cement Company, and one of the Tallow Company with us before they
were honoured.
Re-examined. One was returned, and the prisoner brought the money
for that.
