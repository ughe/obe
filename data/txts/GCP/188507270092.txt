789. JOHN PARKER and ALFRED BAILEY, Unlawfully assen-
bling and taking part in a prize fight.
MR. POLAND Prosecuted.
JOHN BOUSTEAD (Policeman P 434). About 7.30 p.m. on 2nd July I
received information, in consequence of which I went along the bank of
the Brighton and South Coast Railway at Brockley-in a field adjoining
I saw about 250 persons standing round in a ring, and in the centre I
Baw Parker and another man having a stand-up fight they were stripped
to the waist, with no gloves on, pummelling each other-I climbed over
the fence when I got to within 60 yards Bailey was fanning Parker
with a shirt-when I got near to them there was a cry of Police," the
fight stopped, and they all ran away-I apprehended Parker, who ran
away, about three-quarters of a mile off-he had his coat on, but nothing
underneath; his hat was on-he had & cut over his right eye, it was
bleeding, and a number of bruises about his body-I saw Bailey caught
by another constable, he was carrying two shirts and a waistcoat-the
other man escaped, we have not been able to catch him—the fight might
have been going on a couple of minutes.
Cross-examin,d by Parker. I saw you fighting at 300 yards distance-I
never saw a man faoned with a shirt before.
Cross-examined by Bailey. I could see you distinctly-the 250 were of
the ordinary size I could see you as I got over the fence, and when in
the field I could see you distinctly.
WILLIAM ROFY (Policoman 538). I was with Boustead, and saw Parker
fighting with the other inan, stripped to the waist-Bailey was fanning
him with a shirt--they went across the field-I went after them sod
caught Bailey after about three-quarters of a mile-I took him in custody,
he was carrying a shirt, under-shirt and waistcoat-at the station they
were given up to Parker, as they were his-I told Bailey I should take
him in custody-he said he was only having a look on.
Cross-examined by Purker. The fight was not far from Brockley Jack,
a public house and place of amusement where holiday people go-there
are sometimes 200 or 300 people outside.
Cross-examined by Bailey. I came up hill to you, but when you were
fanning Parker the people opened out.
