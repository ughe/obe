2183. SAMUEL WARD was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of
August, 2 printed books, value 48., the goods of John Hawkins Chilcott,
his master.
John Hawkins CHILCOTT. I am a laceman, and live at Islington. The
prisoner was in my service seven or eight months-I bought half-a-dozen
Bibles in Coventry-street, these are two of them. RX
GEORGE HAWKINS CHILCOTT. I went to the prisoner's apartments on
the 17th of August, and found these two Bibles on the table.
GUILTY. Aged 14.-Confined One Month.
