SARAH GARBUTT. r am the wife of Robert Garbutt—we live in the
parish of St. John, Wapping—he is the householder—the prisoner livd
servant with us for four months. On the 22nd of Dec. I went to chapel

past eight—I first missed my cap off my bed—I went up into the prisoneis
room, and seeing her clothes I thought there was something amiss—she
was not there—I then went to my room again, and missed from my drawers,
which were open, the articles stated—these now are what I
missed, and are my property—they had been in separate drawers in the
same room, except a half-sovereign, which was taken from my son's room
—that is not included in the indictment—my drawers were not keptlocked
—l gave notice to the police, and saw my things again at the police-offce
—the value of the property is about 51.—1 never gave the prisoner any
of the things, or gave her any authority to take them—I bad a good cha•
