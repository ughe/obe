I received from Ford and Rix, I apprehended the prisoner-I told him

The prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate :-“Wilson came round
to our house at four o'clock in the morning, and asked me if I would buy
& coat. I asked him what sort of coat it was. He asked me to come and
look. He took me to this girl's house. I looked at the coat, it was too
big, and put it back. He left two pairs of boots, and took the other things
out, tied up in a tablecloth, and took them away about half-past four.
Guilty. -Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
