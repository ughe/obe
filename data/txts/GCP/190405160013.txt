ALFRED BOWLER (10 D.) A warrant was granted by Mr. Curtis Bennett
for the prisoner's arrest for using threats-I arrested him on April 21st at
85, Southan Street-I told him I was a police officer and had a warrant
for his arrest for using threats towards his sister, Mrs. Carter-he said,
"I have not spoken to my sister these six years; she has ruined me;
the threats I made use of were not meant for her, they were for a man
-at the station, when the charge was read over to him, he described
himself as a painter and decorator not in work.
Evidence for the Defence.
HENRY BALLS (261 8.) I was called by a lady and gentleman on

The prisoner, in his defence on oath, said that he first wrote to the prosecutriz
protesting against her conduct with this gentleman those name he did not know,
but did not say anything vulgar or rude in the letter, that he then wrote saving
sho ought to help him, as he was out of work and had a lot of children, that
he had only written one vulgar letter to her ; that after receiving the solicitor's
letter he wanted to keep away from the house, and only went there to pay
Greenslade the hall sovereign which he owed him, that he did not intend
to make a scene , that he had not threatened his sister, and did not intend
to injure her.
Guilty. The Jury said they thought it was a pity that the case was
brought to this Court, and that the prisoner was not bound over at the police
court. Twelve months' hard labour.
