   Cross-examined.  Several men were about, but you were with the one I saw you with on Monday and Tuesday—I have tried to find the man, but had not sufficient information to take him upon. WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. This florin and half-crown are bad—these five are all counterfeit, from three different moulds—these coins are rubbed with lampback and wrapped in paper, and then before they are uttered, they are taken out, and the lampblack rubbed off.
   The prisoner in her defence stated that a man asked her to hold a parcel while he crossed the road to speak to a man, and immediately the constable came and took her, and that she knew nothing about the other two cases.
