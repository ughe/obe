EDMUND ROBERT MILLER. I am a clerk to Mr. Lubbock, solicitor to the
defendant in the action of Hall v. Todd--the deponent's signature to this
affidavit is the prisoner's--there was an oversight in filing it, but directly I
had an intimation I placed it on the file-tbe action has been discontinued
and the defendant's costs taxed--they amounted to 51. 148. 100.--the de
fendant's wife was a material witness - 1 drew up this affidavit myself; the
defendant's statement was made to me in an ordinary and proper manner,
and I then drew it up and immediately I received the summons I wrote to
him to call at the office-be called the following day; I dotted down his
answers in pencil and asked him to call the next day when I would have it
epyrossed, and he could swear it-the effect of the affidavit is not to prevent
the plaintiff from recovering his money but to enable the defendant to put
before a jury the real version of the story.
JOSEPH TRAVERS. I have been book-keeper to Mr. Hall, the prosecutor,
nearly six years-1 produce his day-book and ledger-the prisoner has dealt
with Mr. Hall since 18th July, 1871-on 26th May, 1874, the prisoner gave
me this acceptance for 181. 68.--it is his writing; it is entered to the
prisoner's account as a payment on 26th May in the ledger-it next appears
in the books on 29th September, 1874, when it was dishonoured--this (pro-
duced) is the delivery-book, by which I find that seven hides were received
by the prisoner on 17th August, price 428., and on 18th August, five hides
and six dozen of straps 161. 168. and on 25th August, five brown hides
and five enamel hides 461, 118.- they are all signed by Todd-on 28th
Augnst, here is " J. Todd, nine dozen straps 31. 78. 6d., and on 29th one
shoulder 108., signed J. Todd-on 31st, one gross of straps 178. 6d.-on
September 9th, four hides 91. 48. signed J. Todd-I had prepared the
previous accounts before August 17th, and sent a statement of them to the
prisoner, ending 14th and 15th August, 1874, which show a balance-I did
not post the Accounts myself. The account showed a balance of 611. 48. 3d.
up to 28th September, 1874). Acting on Mr. Hall's instructions I drafted out
this bill of exchange produced-this is his signature as the drawer, and
Todd's as acceptor-the bills when I received them were generally paid into
the banking account-this bill was paid in with three or four others-I
afterwards received it back from the bank dishonoured-it came by post-
it was drawn on 7th December, the letter contained nothing but the bill —
after that further goods were supplied to Todd-I never knew of an execu-
tion being put into Mr. Hall's house.
