it was merely a conversation—they were appointed to meet Ritchie at the Orange Tree, from five to seven—they were called luminaries  by Mullins—there was to be a pass-word—Ritchie was to say to them, when they came in, "Do you want me?" and if they an swered "Justice" he would know they were the men sent by the delegates-nothing was said about what Ritchie was to provide, but that he would give instructions to these men; but each man was advised to come armed, if he could—we met at the Orange Tree, Red Lion-square on the 14th—there wert between twenty and thirty present—Mullins, Payne, Brewster, Ciiffey, Ritchie, Crookshank, Allnutt, Fay, Johnson, Pearce, Simmons, Fleming, two Granshaws, (brothers,) and a person named Ford;
