MR. SLEIGH, Q. Did you authorize the prisoner to do anything without
your knowledge ? A. Never.
EDWARD PEEK (junior). I am one of the firm of Peek, Frean, and
Peek--the prisoner was in our employ-I never gave him authority to sell
or shoot any of the horses, or deal with them in any shape or form-I never
saw him before.
MR. LILLEY submitted there was no case to go to the Jury, as there was no
proof that Mr. George Hender Frean had not given the prisoner authority to
sell the horses. MR. SLEIGH contended, that having called one of the partners
and the manager of the firm to prove that the prisoner had no authority to
sell, that the case ought to go to the Jury, especially as it appeared, by Mr.
Mead's evidence, that the prisoner denied selling them at all.
The COURT considered that in an indictment for false pretences in which
the authority of tro persons would be necessary, it was not sufficient to prove
the authority of one, and therefore directed a verdict of
NOT GUILTY.
