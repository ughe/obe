882. WILLIAM TODD was again indicted for stcaling, on the
Sept., at St. Andrew Ilolborn, 1 silver salver, value 141., the goods of
Francis Paget Watson, in his dwelling-house.
MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
LETITIA GREEN. I am servant to Francis Paget Watson, at No. S,
Thavies'.inn, in the parish of St. Andrew, Holborn. On the 10th of
March, a little before twelve o'clock, I went on an errand—I left the street
door ajar—there was a washerwoman's cart at the door, and Smith was
with it—I was gone about ten minutes—my mistress stood at the door,
and said some man had been in aud stolen the salver, which I had seen safe
on the cheffonier in the drawing-room, on the first floor, about a quarter
before twelve o'clock, and had dusted it.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. Are you sure you saw it? H.
Yes—I left the street door ajar for the washerwoman—I had been in the
kitchen a few minutes after I went down from cleaning the drawing-room.
EMILY FRANCES WATSON. I am the wife of Francis Paget Watson.
The servant went out on the 10th of March—while she was gone I thought
I heard a person come down stairs—I ran up, and asked the woman at tbe
door if anybody had gone out—she said, Yes, that young
looked out, and saw him—he was then about as far as No. 6—he looked
very like the prisoner—I then ran up stairs, and missed the silver salver
—there was no one in the house who could have taken it—it was worth
about 141.
ROBERT VILE SMITH. I live at Walworth. I was with the laun-
dress's cart in Thavies'-inn, Holborn, on the 10th of March, about twelve
o'clock, to take care of the linen—Mr. Watson's servant came out, and left
the door a little ajar—a young man, very like the prisoner, went after her,
but he had a lightish brown coat on then—I saw his face—I am sure the
prisoner is the man—be came back again, and slightly knocked at the door
—he then walked round the cart, and, after two or three seconds, pushed
the door open—he looked round towards Holborn, and then walked in—
in two or three minutes he came out again—his coat was buttoned with
one button at the top when he went in, and when he came out it was but-
toned up—I did not notice whether anything was under it—Mrs. Watson
came almost directly to the door, and my mistress said there was a man
come out—be was then about as far as No. 6.
Cross-examined. Q. Which way did he go ? A. He turned out Of the
inn, and I lost sight of him—he walked rather fast—I have no doubt he
is the person, from his dark hair and eye-brows, and he looked of a Jewish
complexion—I saw him go and listen at another door that was ajar, and
then to Mr. saw him with another person afterwards, and I
knew him.
