 244. CHARLES CURTIS was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of November , 44lbs. weight of lead, value 5 s  ., the goods of Benjamin Dra per ; and JAMES WATERS , for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing it to have been stolen. BENJAMIN DRAPER. I am a plumber, living at Hounslow. On the 20th of November, I went to my melting shop—I found the window broken, which led me to suspect the place had been entered—I missed half a cwt. of old lead which had been left in my shop—I informed the police-man —the lead now produced is mine—it was brought to me by Waters on the Tuesday following, the 23rd—he asked me if I would buy some lead—I found it belonged to me, end the officer took him.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDEROAST . Q.  Half a cwt. is rather heavy for inch a lad as Curtis to carry? A.  Yes. CHARLES JEFFS( police-sergeant T  20.) I received information on the 23rd of November, that the lead had been offered at the prosecutor's house, I took Curtis into custody on the 20th—I saw some footsteps—I compared them with Curtis's—I took him on suspicion. NOT GUILTY.
 245. ELIZABETH JONES was indicted for stealing, on the   30th of November , 9 1/2 yards of printed cotton, value 5 s  ., the goods of Edward Jenkins ; and that she had been before convicted of felony. HENRY GIBBS. I live with Edward Jenkins in New Church-street, Marylebone. About eleven o'clock on the 30th of November I heard something snatched down—there was some printed cotton hanging inside the door—I went round to the door and saw the prisoner standing there—as soon as I went to the door she left it—I missed one piece of cotton from the place—I followed the prisoner, brought her back, and took this print from her. THOMAS HASELDINE( police-constable D  104.) I took the prisoner, and have the cotton. THOMAS HARRISON( police-sergeant D . 14.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—she is the same person—and she had been before convicted of felony. GUILTY.* Aged 21.— Transported for Seven Years.
