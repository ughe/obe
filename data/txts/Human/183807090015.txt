   Q.  Do you recollect when it was you first heard of a publication on the part of Reitterhoffer, how soon after the decease of Lady Mary Hill? A.  I cannot state any particular time—there was some claim immediately after her death—I do not recollect any claim being advanced by Reitter hoffer, as connected with Lady Mary Hill, while she was living—I am not aware of any publication with reference to Lady Mary Hill, till after the decease of the late Marchioness—there were proceedings in Chancery, taken by reitterhoffer.
