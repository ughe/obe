1819. JAMES TALLANT was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
June, 1 sovereign, the monies of John Thompson, from his person.
JOHN THOMPSON. I am a coach-wheelwright, and live in King-street,
Drury-lane. On Sunday, the 9th of June, 1 was in front of the bar in the
Rose public-house, Great Wild-street--the prisoner was sitting near me-
took a sovereign out of my pocket to pay for half a gallon of beerthe
prisoner snatched it out of my hand-] went out after him, but he was
gone--] saw him at Bow-street on the Tuesday after am sure he is the
man.
Prisoner. Q. Was I near to you, or did I speak to you 2 A. Yes-
when you were at Bow-street you said you were in liquor.
TMOTHY DEsMOND. I was at the Rose public-house on the 9th of
June, with another man-we heard the prosecutor call for half a gallon of
beer, and we thought he had no money to pay for itaI asked him where
was the money-he said, 64 Here is a sovereign," holding it up betwixt his
fingers-the prisoner snatched it out of his hand.
Prisoner. Q. Why did you net take me A. That was not my

THOMAS POCOCK (police-sergeant R 18.) I apprehended the prisoner
two days afterwards at the Red Lion public-house, in Great Wild-street
-he said he knew nothing about it.
Prisoner's Defence. I went home from the house to go to bed on the
