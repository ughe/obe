 1065.  GEORGE KNIGHT was indicted for feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Sarah Grubb on the 15th of February , at St. Bridget alias St. Bride, and stealing therein, 3 gowns, value 30s.; 1 shawl, value 10s.; 8 tea-spoons, value 20s., and 1 table-spoon, value 10s., her property.
 SARAH GRUBB. I am a  widow and lodge in a house in the parish of St. Brides. On the 15th of February, at ten o'clock in the morning I went out - I left my room door locked; I returned at six o'clock in the evening and found my little boy there. I missed eight silver teaspoons, one silver table-spoon, three gowns and one shawl; they had been taken from my drawers - I had seen my black gown in the drawer the night before, and the spoons the day before; I had seen the other two gowns in the course of that week, and the shawl I had worn; I have seen them all since, they are in Court - I have looked well at them, and I know them to be mine.
 Prisoner. Q. Was not this on the 10th? A. No; it was on the 15th. - I am certain of that.
   FRANCIS JAMES GRUBB. I am the son of Sarah Grubb; I came home from school to her lodgings on the 15th of February, a little before one o'clock; the door then was ajar," and the bolt of the lock shot out, and the drawers were open, and four of the bolts of the locks were up; I did not miss any thing.
   THOMAS HARRISON. I am an officer; on the 15th of February I was in Whitecross-street, at a quarter-past eleven o'clock in the morning: I saw the prisoner turn out of Red Lion-market into Whitecross-street; he had this bundle under his arm, tied up in this handkerchief - he went about twenty yards, and into a clothes shop. I followed him, and saw the bundle on the counter; I asked what it was in it; he said some clothes, which he had bought in the lane; I told him to untie it, which he did; I saw these three gowns and this shawl in it - I told him to tie them up again; I took him to the watch-house with it - I searched him, and found eleven skelton and latchkeys, and eight tea, and one table-spoon in his trousers pocket, and this one skeleton key in his waistcoat pocket; he said he bought them all in the lane; I took him before the Magistrate, and traced the owner of the property.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 GUILTY - DEATH. Aged 19. Recommended to Mercy by Prosecutrix on account of his Aged Parents.
 1066.  JAMES JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of February , 42 lbs. of pork, value 20s. the goods of Thomas Taylor.
 THOMAS TAYLOR. I am a  salesman of Newgate-market. On the 27th of February, when I came to business in the morning I looked among my legs of pork in my shop, which is opposite my dwelling-house in Rose-street, and missed two legs; I had seen it safe the morning before at nine o'clock; I sent for the beadle who brought the two legs of pork to me, and I knew them to be mine from some mark upon them, and they were from a sty hog, not particularly fat, but very large; my clerk had ticketed those two legs of pork, and I saw the tickets on them.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Is the paper here that was on them? A. No, it had been tucked into the fat of the meat, and was so rumpled, that if we had attempted to dry it, it would have melted almost; but it was a sty hog, which carries the mark with it almost; the man is not here who killed it - they were sent to me to be sold - the paper was put into them by some of my people - I cannot say whose writing it was.
 COURT. Q. Could a person acquainted with pork be able to swear to a piece as belonging to a sty hog. A. Yes.
   CHARLES HART. I am in the prosecutor's employ. On Tuesday, the 26th of February, after the market was over, I took in the legs of pork which were not sold - the next morning there was a report that a man was taken up for stealing two legs - I looked into our shop, and two of my master's were gone; this was about half-past seven o'clock; an officer afterwards came to my master's shop - I described the legs of pork to him, and about nine o'clock, I saw what I believe were Mr. Taylor's legs of pork, in his possession - I have not the least doubt of them.
