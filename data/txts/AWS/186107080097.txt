ROBERT TAYLOR. I am a wholesale fish dealer, residing at 7, Mill-lane,
Lambeth-walk, Surrey, and have a shop in Hercules-buildings, Lambeth.
On Tuesday, 18th June, I locked up my shop at 2 o'clock in the morning
and went home-the policeman came and called me up at 4 o'clock-I re-
turned, and found the place broken open-I missed a watch and clock,
butter pot, two coats, a hat and black bag, and two knives (produced)--thes
are the fragments of the butter pot-these things are all mine-I found the
door broken open-I had securely fastened it before I left know nothing
about the prisoners I think I saw them in my shop that night, but I will
not be sure.
