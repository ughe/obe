 924.  WILLIAM JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of March , 1 silk umbrella, value 18s., the goods of Francis Moor.
   EDWARD WILLSON. I am shopman to Mr. Francis Moor, of St. Martin's-court. On the 9th of March I missed an umbrella from the shop, which I had seen not long before; the officer brought in the prisoner with it.
   JOHN GROOM. I am an officer. On the 9th of March, about nine o'clock in the evening. I observed the prisoner and another lad loitering about this shop; I watched them, and saw the prisoner take something from his pocket - he immediately stepped into the shop, and brought out this umbrella; he came down the court with the other -I tripped up the heels of the other, and took the prisoner with this umbrella and knife.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going home - I saw the umbrella lay, and took it.
   JOHN GROOM. There was no dirt on it; I saw him reach into the shop, as if to cut a string.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years.
 925.  CATHERINE KELLY was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of April , 6 yards of printed cotton, value 5s., the goods of Thomas Benjamin Sowerby.
   JOB VALENTINE WATKINS. I am in the employ of Mr. Thomas Benjamin Sowerby, pawnbroker, of Chiswell-street. On the 3d of April, the prisoner came in, and went to the pledge side; I suspected her, and saw her roll something up and go out - I followed her, and found this cotton under her arm; I had seen it while she was in the shop.
 Cross-examined by MR. CHURCHILL. Q. Are you sure it is your master's? A. Yes.
   JOHN BROWN. I took the prisoner; I found upwards of eighty duplicates on her - half of them were for linen and cotton.   GUILTY. Aged 28. Confined Two Months.
 926.  DAVID LEWIS was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of March , 1 pair of boots, value 5s., the goods of Samuel Swoolston.
   JOSEPH HOTCHKISS. I am shopman to Mr. Samuel Swoolston, a boot-maker. On the 14th of March a young man told me these boots were gone, and I missed them from the nail I had seen them on but a few minutes before, when I was at the door - I went across; I saw the prisoner at the length of the street, but could not go after him - they have not been found.
   JOSEPH CHURCH. I was coming past the door at a quarter after ten o'clock in the morning; I saw the prisoner stoop and creep under the front of the window - he put his hand and took the boots off a nail in the shop; he ran into High-street, Bloomsbury - I went to the house where he delivered them, and should have had them, but there were so many ruffians about; I swear I saw him take them - he ran to what is called Rats' Castle.
   THOMAS PLUME. I took the prisoner on the 14th of March - I found nothing on him; he said the boots were in Rats' Castle, but said another boy took them.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was walking past the shop - another boy stooped and took the boots; the gentleman knocked me right under the horse's feet - they afterwards came and took me: I said I knew nothing about them -I ran home; they came again and took me.
   JOSEPH CHURCH. There was no other boy with him, nor any one near enough to take them but him; I could not catch him, he ran so fast.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Whipped and Discharged.
 927.  GEORGE OAKLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of March , 6 lbs. weight of pork, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Seth Barber.
   SETH BARBER I am a cheesemonger, and live in Baldwin's-gardens. On the 21st of March I heard a call of Stop thief! and ran out; Flemming brought the prisoner and this pork in - I knew it to be mine, and had seen it about ten minutes before.
   JOHN FLEMMING. On that evening, between eight and nine o'clock, I was passing the prosecutor's shop; I saw the prisoner snatch the piece of pork, put it in his apron, and run off - I pursued; he was stopped in Kirby-street, Hatton-garden - I am quite sure he is the person.
   JOSEPH COGGER. I am a publican. I found this piece of pork in my passage, which is about one hundred yards from the prosecutor's; I did not see who put it there - several persons ran by the house.
   MATTHIAS WELDHEN. I am an officer. I heard a cry of Stop thief! I saw a person run from the prosecutor's shop, across Leather-lane; I secured him in Kirby-street- I did not see at first who it was; the prisoner said he did it, because he was in distress, and had nothing to eat.
 GUILTY. Aged 20. Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor. Confined Fourteen Days.
 Before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
