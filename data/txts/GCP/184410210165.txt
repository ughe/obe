JOHN JENNINGS. I am a gold-beater, and live in Margaret-street, St.
George's, Southwark--the prisoner was my apprentice for about six months.

gold from Mr. Alcock's, in Little-Britain I did not see him again till the
23rd of Sept., 1844, when he was in custody.
