Setu KITCHEN. I keep the White Horse public-house, in West-street,
Carnaby-market. On the day this happened I was passing down the Bag.
nigge Wells-road, and saw two persons in Guildford-place--the prisoner was
one of them-I knew him before by the name of Franklin-I am sure he is
one of them, I saw him come round the corner of a public-house; he made a
stand to see if anybody was following him, looked round, saw the prosecutor
following him, and made a stop-Mitchell came up and passed him—the
other party had this bag with him-I saw the property taken from it--when
Mr. Mitchell came round the corner, the one with the bag made his way on as
fast as he could I kept my eye on Mitchell, and lost sight of the prisoner for
two or three minutes--I am quite certain I saw him with the other man with
the bag-he remained there-he did not run away-when I had done looking
after Mitchell I saw the prisoner on the other side of the street, going in a
direction towards the other man.

CHARLES Cliff. I live next door to the prosecutor-on the day in ques-
