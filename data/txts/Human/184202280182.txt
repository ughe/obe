1069. WILLIAM TOWNSEND was indicted for breaking and enter ing the dwelling-house of George Toms , on the   28th of January , at   St. Dunstan, Stepney , and stealing therein 1 table-cloth, value 9 s  ., the goods of George Toms . ESTHER TOMS. I am the wife of George Toms, and am a laundress, and live in Gloucester-street. I work for Dr. Bennett, in the Commercial. road—I had a table-cloth hanging on a line in the parlour-some one knocked at the door, and told me something—I ran into the parlour, and the table-cloth was gone—I had seen it safe about a quarter of an hour before, when I had shut the window—I dwell in the house—it is in the parish of St. Dunstan, Stepney. MARTEN WELBROCK. I live with my father, in Gloucester-street, Com mercial-road. At half-past nine o'clock on this morning, I was going along Gloucester-street—I saw the prisoner come to the prosecutor's window, and lift it up—he walked as far as the Bricklayers' Arms public-house—he then came back, and went to push the blinds open, but I suppose be saw some one—he went by, and came back again, pushed open the blinds, pot his knee on the table, took the table-cloth, and went away—I had known him for three years by sight. CHARLES STEEL. I have known the prisoner some time—a little after eight o'clock I saw him pass my father's house, No. 1, Gloucester-street while we were at breakfast.
   Prisoner.  I am innocent. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Nine Months.
