 2064. ELIZABETH SALE was indicted for stealing 1 napkin, value 3 d  .; 1 pillow-case, 6 d  .; 1 apron, 6 d  .; 1 pair of stockings, 6 d  .; and 1 snuff box, 6 d  .; the goods of Daniel Emmerson , her master. JANE EMMERSON. I am the wife of Daniel Emmerson—we live at Shore ditch—I am a laundress—the prisoner was employed by me—this napkin, and the other things produced, are mine.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  How long had the prisoner been en gaged by you? A.  Nearly four months—I cannot state when I saw these things last—here is a mark on this napkin in my daughter's needlework—I swear this is mine—I am sure all the things are mine—most of them are marked.
