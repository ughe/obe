754. ELIZABETH HICKTON was indicted for a misdemeanor.
JOHN HOLLINGSWORTH. I live with Mr. Joseph Smout, a butcher, in
Upper North-place, Gray's Inn-road. On the 3rd of February the pri-
soner came to the shop-I had not known her before-she said, 64 I want
two pounds of rump steaks and one pound of mutton chops for Mr. Dixon,
87, Baker-street"- gave credit to that, and served her with the meat-
she did not pay me for it.
ROBERT DIxoN, Esq. I live at No. 37, Baker-street-I deal with
Mr. Smout--I never knew the prisoner, and never authorized her to go
for meat on my account.
Prisoner. I am very sorry, I throw myself on the merey of the Court.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 2Recommended to erey.Confined -Confined One Day.
