 1538. JAMES HURLEY was indicted for stealing 1 coat, value 1 l  ., the goods of Walter Crutchley ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. THOMAS PARNELL. I live with Mr. Walter Crutchley, a tailor, in High-street, in the Borough. On the 24th of May I received information about a fishing-coat—a woman came in with the ticket which had the price of the coat on it—we then missed the coat from the block. MARY HOOPER. I am the wife of George Hooper—we live in Falcon-court, in the Borough. On the 24th of May I was standing at the top of the court, and saw the prisoner—he turned the corner very quickly—he had a coat hanging over him arm—I could not tell what it was till he got by me, and then he took the ticket off the coat and threw it away—I took it up, and charged him with stealing it—he ran away—I took the ticket to the prose cutor's, and they missed the coat off the block, which was inside the door.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you see me take the coat? A.  No, I had a suspicion, because you took the ticket off and threw it away—I saw your face—you were closer to me than you are now—you went round to the right—I had seen you so often, that I could not be mistaken in you—I can swear you are the person—you had the coat on your left arm. HENRIETTA MASTERS. I live in Falcon-court. On the 24th of May I saw the prisoner in the prosecutors' shop—he afterwards passed me with a coat on his arm—I saw him take the ticket off it and throw it down—Mr. Hooper took it up—I am certain the prisoner is the man. THOMAS HAY( police-sergeant M  9.) On the 1st of June, after a long run and a severe struggle, I took the prisoner, and took him to the station—he ran off immediately he saw me looking at him—he was identified by one of the witnesses as the man—I have the ticket which I got at Mr. Crutchley's shop. MARY HOOPER re-examined.  This is the ticket I picked up. THOMAS PARNELL re-examined.  The coat that is missed had this ticket on it, and it was the property of Mr. Walter Crutchley.
   Prisoner's Defence.  Does it stand to reason, that a person walking the street with a coat, could be known by a stranger? I should have had wit nesses to prove I was at Stepney fair. GEORGE WILD( police-constable M  94) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction at this Court—( read—Convicted  21 st of Sept. , 1846, and confined six months )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined One Year .
 1539. ELLEN TAYLOR was indicted for stealing 1 penny, the money of Charles Sutton , her master. CHARLES SUTTON. I keep the Union Arms, Union-row, Clapham—the prisoner was in my service—I put some marked money on the top of my till—the prisoner cleaned the bar, and I found a marked penny in her drawer up stairs, that was lost last Monday morning—I missed that penny immedi ately.
