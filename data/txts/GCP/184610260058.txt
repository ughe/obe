2057. CHARLES HOWARD was indicted for a rape ; and WILLIAM
STEWART and WILLIAM LEWIS, for feloniously aiding, assisting, and
abetting him in the said offence.
Mr. HUDDLESTON conducted the Prosecution.
HOWARD-GUILTY of an Assault.
LEWIS-GUILTY of an Assault.
STEWART-Not GUILTY.
(Lewis and Stewart were also indicted for a rape on the same person, upon
which no evidence was offered.)
