during the week, amongst which this proposal appeared. Upon it
prisoner would be entitled to credit himself with 3s. 4d., and he
credits himself with 10 times the premium on the sheet. Another
proposal purports to be signed by Beatrice Hutchinson effecting in-
surance on the life of Martha Floyd, the wife of William, the pre-
mium in that case being 3d., prisoner being entitled to 2s.6d, if

of Martha Floyd appears in the list of new business with that of
William Floyd, and prisoner signed the following certificate: "I
hereby certify that the above mentioned industrial business has been
legitimately obtained, that I have received the first premium in each
case from the assured, and that this certificate and summary are
correct." On the other side of the weekly return there is a list of
premiums collected during the week under numbers corresponding
with numbers in the collecting book and including the premiums pur-
porting to have been paid in respect of William Floyd and Martha
Floyd. The collecting book is kept by prisoner and he hands it in
each week. At the bottom of the statement of cash received there is
a summary and balance struck, in which 25 per cent of the total
premiums collected during the week is credited to prisoner. Besides
the insurance of William and Martha Floyd, there was genuine new
business during the week to the amount of 1s. 11d., representing
several policies, making the total new business 2s.6d., on which pris-
oner would be entitled to 10 times 2s.6d., equals £1 5s. In crediting
himself with that amount prisoner credited himself with 5s. 10d. more
than he ought to have done. He also credited himself with 7s. 8d.,
being 25 per cent. on the total amount of premiums collected. Instead
of crediting himself with £1 12s. 8d, he should only have credited him.
self with £1 6s. 8d. I handed over to him 1s. 11d., which was app&-
rently due to him.

signs those forms as to which it is thought necessary to make in-
