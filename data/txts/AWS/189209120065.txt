tried on the 17th February, 1892, at the Royal Courts, before Mr.
Jastice Grantham-it was an action against Morgan and Tomkins, the
City of London Publishing Company and the Authors Alliance; I was
the plaintiff-none of the defendants appeared; they were not repre-
sented by counsel or solicitor-a verdict was returned in my favour for
$40, and E200 damages, or the return of the MS. and the costs of the
action-I never got any fruits of the judgment, no costs, no MS., nor
the $40; somebody paid my own costs for me-the facts of the case got
considerable publicity in the London and provincial press.
