ALFRED BARNARD. I live at Norwich and am a member of the firm
of Barnard, Bishop, and Barnard, ironmongers there—I attend to the
business daily and know most of the customers—I pever saw the prisoner
till he was at the Mansion House—he has not been a customer of our
firm for thousands of pounds—there is no such name on our books,
Belding is a Norfolk name.
Cross-examined. I am 31 and have been in the firm 16 or 17 years
we have a house in London but no agents in the country-we deal largely
with ironmongers in market towns.
ROBERT KEYS. I am an assistant at Messrs. Barnard's and was present
when the prisoner saw Mr. Morris-I went to the station with him on
29th May, when he was given in custody-he dropped these two papers
(pawn-tickets) out of his hand in Queen Victoria Street, which a boy
picked up and gave to me.
SAMUEL KELL. I am assistant to R. and C. Bowman, pawnbrokers,
of 68, Goswell Road-on 25th May, before 3 o'clock, the prisoner pledged
a set of ormolu fire-irons and a screen and rest—the foreman took them
in and gave him the ticket, but I was present.
