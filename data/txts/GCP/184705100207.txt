Ann Mack. Ann Sheen came to me on 11th April, and dined with me
she went out, returned with two bundles, and said her sister had made her a
present of them-I did not see what they contained-I saw some silk in one
of them--this is the silk, and I saw a velvet cloak-the bundles were left at
my house-I gave them to the officer the next morning.
JAMES BURTON (police-constable M 272.) I went to Mrs. Mack's on the
12th April, and got these two bundles-they contain all the articles stated.
Ann Sheen's Defence. My sister came and asked me to come to her place,
as she was going to leave; I went to fetch her things, not knowing there was
any stolen property amongst them; she told me they were her clothes; I did
not know any difference until I was taken into custody- if she will speak
the truth she will tell the same.
ANN SHEEN. GUILTY.-AGED 21.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury.

1307. JAMES BENNETT was indicted for stealing 4 bushels of oats,
value 15s., and 1 sack, 28.; the goods of Henry Dunkin, in a barge on the
navigable river Thames.
SA
MR. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.
DANIEL DAVIES. I live in King's-row, Horsleydown. I am a watchman
to a brewery. On the 20th of April, about nine o'clock in the evening, I
saw the prisoner and a man named Cook coming up a turning leading to
Horsleydown Old-stairs- I knew of a barge of Mr. Dunkin's lying there-
Cook was carrying a hat and a bag, and directly after him came the prisoner
with a sack - I went to take hold of him, and put my hand on the sack-he
dropped it, and Cook turned back and asked what I wanted to do, and showed
fight--the prisoner got away, but was stopped by some persons - I took the
sack which was in my fellow-servant's possession, and found the name Henry
Dunkin branded on it- this is the sack, it is full of oats.
DAVID DAVIES. I was standing there I heard the last witness call out
"Police," and saw him seize the prisoner-I saw the sack on the prisoner's
back--he dropped it, and I took it inside our gates.
MICHAEL MAHONEY (police-constable M 143.) I received charge of the pri-
soner-as I was taking him along he asked me how I thought he would get on
-I said I did not know, I knew nothing of the case--he said, “He did not
see us take it, but he saw me with it" - I asked who was with him he said,
"Young Cook."
