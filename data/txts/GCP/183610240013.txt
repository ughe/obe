stealing, on the 24th of September, 2 leather straps and buckles, value
: 1 lamp, value 11. ; 2 door-plates, value 158., 60lbs. weight of lead
nine, value 108.; 6 door locks, value 12.; the goods of John Wilks
Hill: and 1 copper, value 21.; and 30lbs. weight of lead, value 6s. ; being
fixed to a building. o
JOHN WILKS HILL. I live in Warwick-place, Peckham, and am the
owner of the houses, Nos. 2 and 3, Cooper's-row, Crutched-friars, they
have been unoccupied a considerable time. On Saturday, the 24th of
September, I went to No. 2, and attempted to get in with the usual key
that I have always been accustomed to use in opening the door I put the
key into the lower lock, and found it in a very different state to what I had
int it in a few days before when I came out; I had double-locked the door
-when I put the key in I found it on the single lock-I pushed against the
door, and made a considerable noise-I conclude the bolt at the top of the
door was fastened inside-I immediately suspected some persons were
inside the house-I requested my eldest son, who was with me, to go to
his house, which was nearly opposite, to get the keys of No. 8, to get over
the back premises, and see what was going on--he went, and returned in
1 minute or two, as he was approaching with the keys, I saw White
come out of No. 8, and as he was passing down the lower step of the door,
I took hold of him by the arm- he was going up the street, towards
Trinity-square-I said, " My good Sir, what have you been about in this
house!" - he said, “Nothing, I have not been in the house at all, I was
mly passing"-I said, “ You must stop til I have better proof of that," and
just at that moment four or five persons gathered round the door-one of
them said, “There is another man come out of the other house that
turned out to be Poole -I immediately turned round and took hold of him
-he said, “What are you going to do with me?" I said, “You have
been in that house, I must know what you have been about "--he said, “I
have been passing down the street"-I said, “That is impossible"-on
that a young woman came over, and said, Mr. Hill, I saw that man come
out of your door this instant, and I also saw that short man (White) come
out of the house the day before yesterday, with a large copper on his
shoulder, containing a considerable quantity of lead "-I then sent for an
officer and gave them in charge - he took them to the station-house I
then opened the door, which I could not do before, with the same keys
went into the back kitchen of No. 2, and saw it in a deplorable state
found the brick-work, which contained the copper, broken away, the cop-
per gone, and the lead-work in the sink was loosened all round, ready to
moved-a pump was also removed, and a large pipe, which supplied
the cistern with water, turned up to remove the lead I then went to the
sation-house, and they searched the prisoners- I went back to the house
-the officers soon followed-We examined, and found the box staple of
the lock of No. 2 broken-We went into the kitchen, and found things as I
