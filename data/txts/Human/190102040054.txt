BARRY HOLTHAM( Constable, G.P.O. ) I produce post-office receipt for a registered letter delivered at 59, Liverpool Road, N., signed "J. Sparrow," and addressed to Sparrow. JOSIAH SPARROW. I am a builder, of 59, Liverpool Road, Islington—Roberts, who has pleaded guilty, stayed with us last year, from June till November—he did some writing—I have no manager—he made out estimates, and I filled in the quantities and prices—I owe him nothing, and never did—I never had a contract with the Wimbledon Local Board, nor any money coming from them—Roberts never advanced me any money to complete a contract at Woolwich—he never lent me anything—I never wrote or authorised anyone to write, to Kingsford, Dorman & Co.—Mr. Lacy, or his assistant, came to see me, but I never received a letter from him, to which this letter of December 5th is an answer.
   Cross-examined by MR. ABINGER . Roberts married my sister 15 years ago—I have only known him two years next month—I have heard him say he was accountant to the District Messenger Company.
   Cross-examined by MR. EDMONDSON . I have been in business three years—I have not had large contracts—I was eight years a general foreman—Roberts was staying is the house, and did not charge for his writing—he told me Child was a house agent and surveyor, and said, "No doubt I could get you some work"—he did not tell me his business—I knew nothing about his buying property till I heard of it at Bow Street. ARTHUR HENRY JOHN WALKER.
