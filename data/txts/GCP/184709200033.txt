his service. On the 22nd of May I gave him a sovereign to fetch some
vegetables-I sent him to Mr. Edwards, but he went to Mr. Hills-he came

sovereign-he told me had paid for the articles, and he had had them of Mr.
Edwards--this is the bill of the things.
GEORGE HILL. On the 22nd of May the prisoner came to me for some
broccoli and other things-they amouted to 28. 1d.- this is my bill-he did
not pay me he had them on Mr. Hare's account, the prisoner was in the
