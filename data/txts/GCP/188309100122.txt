CHARLES McCOMBY. I am manager to Mr. Che cross, a merchant, of
Mincing Lane-on 11th May we received this memorandum from Alfred
Bixton, of 17, Tower Hill. (Asking for samples and prices of essence of lenson)
--we sent them, and on 16th May we received an order for two 20 lbs. at
58. 3d. --we sent two coppers and an empty copper, value 111. 118. 10.-
on 18th May he wrote: “Gentlemen,- I will take the remaining ait
coppers"-I then asked for a reference, and he gave me the name of
Rogers, of 41, Peter Street, Islington-I wrote there and got this answer.
(Stating that Mr. Bixton had not been in business more than three months, but
he would consider him good for 1001.)—I did not execute the second order,
but wrote on 4th June: "Sir, -As your account for 111. 118. 1d. is over-
due, unless it is paid by 12 o'clock to-morrow I shall enter proceedings
against you "-I have never been paid, and never saw my goods again.
Cross-examined by Bizton. The account was due on 2nd June, but from
what I saw and heard I complained to the police before that I had
applied for payment and was put off with promises--I have witness
who put my letters into your box.
Cross-examined by MR. BLACKWELL. I had not made up my mind to
have nothing to do with Bixton before applying to Rogers--I made
private inquiries about Rogers at the same time, as my suspicions were
aroused.
Re-examined. I did not know that Rogers only used a friend's bed at
41, Peter Street.
