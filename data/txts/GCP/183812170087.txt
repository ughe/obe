365. THOMAS HARRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of
October, 1 wheelbarrow, value 16s., the goods of William Filkins; and
that he had been before convicted of felony.
WILLIAM FILKINS. I live at Ickenham. I had a wheelbarrow on the
30th of October, and missed it the next morning. I found the wheel on
the 8th of December, at the prisoner's house, on another barrow-I asked
him bow he came by it-he said he bought the wheel of a man who came
round gathering old iron and bones, and he had made the bartow himself-
I have examined it-the barrow is not mine, but the wheel is it was
andexed to another barrow when I lost it.
John LARKIX. I am an officer. I went with the prosecutor, and he
identified this wheel—the prisoner said he had bought it of a man who
went about buying bones and rags.
Prisoner's Defence. I bought it of a man who deals in old iron-I was
at home one day at dinner, I saw he had this barrow wheel, and I bought
it for half-a-crown.
John LARKIN re-examined. He stated that, and said the man he bought
it of lived at Acton-I said I would take him there in my gig - he then
said, “Never mind, on the trial will do." I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction, which I got at Mr. Clark's office (read)—the
prisoner is the man.

THOMAS HALSEY and WILLIAM HALSEY, for feloniously receiving
the same, well knowing it to have been stolen; against the Statute, &c.
Messrs. BODKIN and BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
