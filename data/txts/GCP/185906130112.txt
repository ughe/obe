649. WILLIAM JONES (26), Burglariously breaking and entering the
dwelling-house of William Potter, with intent to steal.
MR. ORRIDGE conducted the Prosecution.
FRANK POTTER. I am the son of William Potter, who keeps the Grey-
hound, Clapham-road-on 21st May, about 2 o'clock, I was disturbed by a
noise over the leads, and afterwards by the window opening- I listened
some time, and then thought I heard a noise in the passage leading to the
house-I got out of bed and awoke my brother—we both went down, and
I heard footsteps in a room which was locked outside-I opened it, went in,
and found the window open-I went on to the leads, and saw a man get-
ting over the adjoining premises into the road-I raised an alarm, a police-
man sprung his rattle, and several persons came and said that they had
caught the man.
