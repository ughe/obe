JAMES PATMAN (108 J). I was called to Dalston Junction on
October 27th at 6.48_I arrived in the ladies' waiting-room, where I
found the station-master, who pointed out to me, in a water closet, the
dead body of a child ; lying near its head there was a clinker brick-I
did not disturb the body or anything till Dr. Fennel came just after he
came a police-sergeant, named Birch, came, and after the doctor had
seen the position of the child the police-sergeant took charge of the
brick-a black shawl partly covered the body.
