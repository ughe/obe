2430. JOHN TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of As-
gust, 1 pair of braces, value 18., and 2 pairs of gloves, value 2s.6d.: the
goods of Samuel Morgan Lewis, his master.
SAMUEL MORGAN LEWIS. I am a hosier and glover. The prisoner
lived with me for nine months-he was occasionally behind my counter, and
at other times had to carry out goods-I missed things from the shop and
questioned him about several things that had been missed-I gave him in
charge on Saturday, and on Sunday his box was searched by the police
man, and these braces and gloves found--they are mine, and were not
taken with my consent.
Cross-examined by Mr. Phillips. Q. Are not gloves often left to be
cleaned by persons who do not call for them? 4. Yes, that happens in a
great variety of instances-it is not unusual for persons in the shop to
wear them-I have some that have been left three or four years--I hate
worn them myself—the prisoner was in the habit of buying things of me, and
having a set-off for them in his wages-he gave me an account of them in
writing- I was not very intimate with the prisoner-We were not acces
tomed to lend things to one another.
Q. Now consider, have you not ? A. He lent me a coat once, which
I returned the same evening—it was a coat to go to the Opera in-ke
brought it down, and it happened to fit me, and I put it on-I never leat
him any thing that I am aware of.
WILLIAM West (police-constable F 106. When the prisoner was
brought to the station, he asked if I had searched his box-I said I had
not-he said "You will find a pair of braces and two pairs of gloves, which
ought to be down in master's day-book."
NOT GUILTY.

2432. EDWARD FLYNN was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of
September, 1 spoon, value 78., the goods of Sir John Beresford, Bart.
ANN MERCER. I am servant to Sir John Beresford, who lives in Har
ley-street, Cavendish-square. Between nine and ten o'clock in the mori
ing of the 6th of September, the prisoner came and swept the kitchen
chimney, the housekeeper's room, and another--the plate was in the
housekeeper's room-it was all right before-a policeman came on Tues-
day morning, to know if we had lost any plate-we counted it, and missed
this spoon.
WILLIAM DANIELI. I am in the employ of my brother, a silversmith,
in Oxford-street- I bought the spoon of a girl that came in to sell it, or
Monday evening--she is not here.
CHARLES WEBB (police-constable C 61.) On the night of the 6th
was on duty in Oxford-street, and saw two girls fighting- I took them
into custody-on the road to the station I heard one say to the other,
“This is through the silver spoon your brother stole and gave you;
sold it at a shop in Oxford-street" -I then went to Dameli's, and found
the spoon- I went to the prisoner, and asked where he was at work
said, "At Harley-street"--that he went into the scullery for a necessary
purpose, and took the spoon out of the gutter, at Sir John Beresford's.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 14.- Confined One Month, and Whipped.
