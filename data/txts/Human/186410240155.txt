   Prisoner's Defence.  I used to live at home with my mother and lather. This young chap  induced me to come away and live with him, and go part ners in wood-chopping. His wife is a prostitute, and used to go out of a night to keep him. I was with them three weeks; we had a row, and I left My father took me back again, and I heard no more of them till the police man came. I had nothing to do with the things. He told my father if he gave him 3 s . for the things he would be satisfied, and I told him not to give it. WILLIAM CUMMING( re-examined ). I had no instructions to take the pri soner until 27th September—nothing was done till the prosecutor came out of prison, after serving his three months. NOT GUILTY.
