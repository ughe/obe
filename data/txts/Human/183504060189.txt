   Prisoner . There are no railings facing the house. Witness . They are about twenty yards up the street—I did not beat or ill-use her—I went to catch hold of her frock behind, and she scratched my face and fell down—I did not see her bloody—I did not strike her. SARAH TREASURE re-examined . These are all my property, and are worth about 5 s . I had no quarrel with the prisoner—I am certain I was sober—I had had one glass of gin with the prisoner when I went out to market, about half-past eight o'clock—I met her outside the street door, and we went down the town together—I parted with her about half-past eleven—I went home and put my market things away, and left her in the street—I went to get some corn-beef for supper—I was gone about half an hour I think—I went about one hundred yards—Jacob was going to partake of the supper—married people live in the house—I believe the prisoner is sin gle, and so am I—I felt the door catch when I pulled it to—it if to near the street door, I always see that it is fast—the street door was only latched; it is common to all the lodgers—my door if kept on the latch, unless I only go into the yard.
   Prisoner . Different people come out of the house at all hours of the night—it is a very bad house—the street door was open until three o'clock in the morning. JACOB EARNEY re-examined . I came home in company with the prose cutrix—she had come to the Coach and Horses public-house after me—I had left the lodging about seven o'clock.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I went to my mother's about seven o'clock on Sa turday evening—I left her house to go down the town—as I went out at the street door Treasure called to me, "Where are you going? stop a bit"—I waited for her while she put on her bonnet and shawl—she came out, and we went to High-street—we met a woman whom she knew, and we went into a public-house, and had a quartern of gin—we went into four or five different houses, and had gin and ale—I was in liquor, and so was she—that man used me dreadfully—but I do not believe I am guilty—I cannot remember any thing about it—when I found myself in the cage, I asked as watchman what I was there for; he said, Treasure had given me into cus tody—I am told Jacob Earney had brutally ill-used me. JOHN ALSFORD re-examined . Earney appeared sober when he cam—I saw him holding the prisoner in the house—it was done with violence—I did not see any thing of the transaction myself but her nose was bleeding at the time.
