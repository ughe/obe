   The prisoner's statement before the Magistrate was here read, as follows : "It was not me; I was coming from Tooley-street, and was going towards the bridge, and I saw the prosecutor and another man fighting together." THOMAS BLENCOE re-examined.  I was not fighting with another man; no one was near me but the prisoner.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was walking along, heard cries of "Police!" and was collared; the gentleman said, "That is the chap that struck me;" I told him I was not. GUILTY.* Aged 23.— Confined Nine Months
