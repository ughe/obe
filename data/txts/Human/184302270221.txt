 1056. JAMES M'KENZIE was indicted for stealing on the   23rd of Feb ., 5 3/4lbs. weight of pork, value 3 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of John Joseph Mason . CHARLES CHRISTMAS MASON. I am the son of John Joseph Mason, a cheesemonger, in Chiswell-street—on the 23rd of Feb. I saw the prisoner going out—I stopped him, and found this pork on him—It was my father's, and I had seen it safe a quarter of an hour before—I had not sold it to the prisoner.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A man came and said, "Will you carry this pork? "—I said I could not, as I was going up Bunhill-row, but I would carry it as I came back—I came back and he gave it me. GUILTY. Aged 14.— Confined Eleven Days and Whipped.

JOHN PARMAM. I am a coachman out of place—I lodge in Shepherd's-court, Hanover-square—I missed the articles stated from my room on the 27th of February—I saw them safe at nine o'clock that morning—these are them. JOHN STANLEY( police-constable D  48.) I received information from a little boy, and found the prisoner about seven that evening in the Cock and Lion public-house—I found on her this coat, waistcoat, cork-screw, and two old brushes—she appeared to have been drinking. ANN COLE. I keep the house where the prosecutor lives—about four or five o'clock I saw the prisoner come down into my passage—I asked who she wanted—she said, her brother—I said, "Who is your brother?"—she said, "That is my business"—I said, "How did you come here?"—"That is my business," said she—she had a bundle, and went out.
