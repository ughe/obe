 1687. JOSEPH HOLLOWAY , GEORGE ROBINSON , THOMAS BUXTON , JOHN BENNETT, and WILLIAM PAGE , were indicated for stealing, on the   4th of May , 4 tons of stone, value 20 s  . the goods of Thomas Cubitt ; to which they pleaded GUILTY  .— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor, believing they acted under the erroneous idea that they were entitled to the property. Confined Two Days .
 1688. JOHN BULMER and MARY BULMER were indicated for stealing, on the   12th of April , 5 decanters, value 12 s  .; and 4 plates, value 10 s  .; the goods of Robert Passenger , the master of John Bulmer. THOMAS SPENCES. I am clerk to Mr. Robert Passenger, a potter and glass man, in William-street. I John Bulmer was his servant—we have lost some decanters—we searched the prisoner' house in Dyer's-buildings, Gravel-lane, after they were in custody, and found these two decanter in a band-box in the front bed-room, and three smaller ones between the bed-clothes in a back-room—I know these plates to be my master's—we have missed such—I cannot tell when we lost them—they were found in the bottom room front of the prisoner's house.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Do you undertake to you have missed these five plates? A.  I missed them after seeing them there, not previous—we have stock on the premises of the same pattern—I have not counted them since to see if any are gone—I know them from the pe culiar patter—no one else has the pattern—I can only speak to the decanters from their general appearance—it is a common pattern—I could not swear to them—I do not know how many children the male prisoner has got—the female prisoner is his sister, and lived with him—he had been about ten year in the prosecutor' service.
 (John Bulmer received an excellent character.)
   JOHN BULMER .— GUILTY  . Aged 42.— Strongly recommended to mercy.
   MARY BULMER .— NOT GUILTY.
