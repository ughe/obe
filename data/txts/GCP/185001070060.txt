John Joseph Wickers, with intent to disfigure him: 2nd Count, to do some
grievous bodily harm. MR. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM JOHN JOSEPH WICKERS. I am an appraiser of 16, Sussex-
place, Old Kent-road. I produce a distress warrant for rent from Mr.
Roberts, which authorized me to enter the house 22, Thanet-street- I went
in the middle of the day, when the prisoner was not at home-he was a
lodger there. I told all the lodgers they must take their goods away-I
first saw the prisoner between four and five o'clock, when I was in the kitchen
seeing the lodgers clear out their things-he came down to me and said,
"Are you the broker?" -I said, “I am "-he said that was a

he might take the rest away-his wife was engaged in removing them-he
