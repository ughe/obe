Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
651. HENRY WRIGHTSON was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd
of January, 1 balf-sovereign, 4 half-crowns, 11 shillings, and 7 pence, the
monies of William Barnes, his master; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 12.-Confined Five Days.
652. SARAH MILAND was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
January, 1 bonnet, value 158., the goods of Ralph Hunt; and I gown,
value 10s., the goods of Mary Miles; to which she pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 51.-Confined Three Months.
653. ABRAHAM HARRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of
January, 1 coat, value 5s., the goods of John Wisbey; to which he
pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Six Months.

her master; to both of which she pleaded
