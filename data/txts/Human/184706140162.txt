LOUISA MOORE. I saw the prisoner come in with a Knife, the day before he said he would take away Mr. Cook's life—he said none of us should see Whitsuntide—I saw him come up stairs on the day this occurred—I saw Mr. Cook bleeding from the back part of her head—I went to the doctor's. ARTHUR KIRKMAN( police-constable F  20.) I took the prisoner—I asked him how he came to stab his wife in the back of her head—he told me he did not do it—on the way to the station he told me he did do it on account of going home and finding another man in bed with Mr. Cook—I found this Knife on the ground and this stick on the table.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did he not say that Mr. Morgan did it A.  Yes; he did afterwards—I found him sitting on the top step of the stairs. JOHN PHILLIPS. I am assistant to Mr. Walker, a surgeon in Drury-lane, On the 20th of May Mr. Cook came to me; her face was smeared with blood, and there was a small scalp wound at the back of her head—it was such a wound as this Knife would have made—it had left off bleeding when I saw her. GUILTY of an Assault. Aged 45.— confined One Year , and to enter into recognizances for his good behaviour for five years.

 1493. MARY GORDON was indicted for stealing 1 sovereign, 3 half-crowns, 1 shilling, 1 sixpence, and 1 piece of foreign copper coin, value 1/4 d  .; the monies of John Harber Barber , from his person; and that she had been before convicted of felony. JOHN HARBER BARBER. I live in Brewer-street, Pimlico. On the 12th of June, about twenty minutes past ten at night, I met the prisoner at Buck ingham-gate—she asked me if I would give her a drop of gin—I took her into the White Horse—we had a quartern of gin, came out together, and came down St. James'-street together, walking side by side till we came to William-street, when she turned up a dark passage—I put my hand in my pocket, missed my money, and followed her—she met me, and said there was no thoroughfare, I could not go through—I accused her of robbing me—she denied it—a policeman came, and I gave her in charge—I had had one sove reign, three half-crowns, and one shilling and sixpence loose in my pocket—it was my wages which I had received, and an old coin which I had found three weeks ago—this now produced is it—I swear to it by this figure "7" on it.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did not you want to take improper liberties with me? A.  No—I did not offer you sixpence, or pull up your clothes.
