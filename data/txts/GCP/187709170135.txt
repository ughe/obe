JOHN POLLARD LOVERING. I am a public accountant, of 35, Gresham
Street-I have acted as trustee in a great many liquidations and bank
ruptcy cases with wholesale warehousemen of London-I was concerned in
the liquidation of the Thompson's -I know De Chastelaine, I am a trustee
under his bankruptcy, but I have not the date with me when he stopped
payment-he did not attend the appointment for his examination, he
absconded I have not the date with me -I acted for the wholesale house in
the liquidation proceedings of North; I attended the first meeting of
creditors at Mr. Parry's office--list of creditors who I represented was
taken down at that meeting it included the names of Seof, Hunt, and
others; I had a copy of the list, it was produced to the meeting by Mr.
Benton--this on the file is either the original' or a copy of it; it sets out the
amount of book debts without giving any names, there is no other list of
debtors-there was no explanation at the time the books were lost, and I
requested North to give me the names of the debtors to his estate-there
were no assets whatever-at the liquidation he stated that he had lost his
books and could not give any account_I asked him to give me a list of some
of the persons to whom he owed 51, or 101., and he gave me about half &
donen-the debts were estimated to amount to 4001. or 5001., and the cash
in hand was 108.---no disclaimer was made at the time of the liquidation,
on 28th August, of Walter and Edward Thompson being indebted to North
-no discovery was made of the dealings with North of Edward, Walter, or
Alfred Thompson I was made acquainted by North, or by the Thompsons,
of the disposition of goods obtained from wholesale houses in February
March, and May, 1876—I have atteded the preliminary inquiries before the
Magistrate, and have been present during this trial, having in my hands the
exhibits from the wholesale houses, and also the sheets marked R, Islington,
R1, 31., and 51. ; I find goods bought of the London Warehouse Company on
15th February, 191, 178. 11d., and sold to Roberts, Islington, on 23rd
February, for 131. 68., being a loss of 61. 113. 11d., and that is excluding the
luss for carriage from the London Warehouse Company to Tottenham,
Hoddesden, and Islington, which is a loss as well—I give the loss without
the cost of travelling, without the working expenses--by Exbibit T, I
find goods bought of Tucker and Co., on 18th February, 11l. 88., and sold,
on 25th February, to Roberts, of Islington, for 121. 6s. 6d.-by K 1,I find
goods bought of Kynaston and Son, in February, for 22. 168. 2d. and sold
to Roberts, of Islington, for 161. 98. 74d.-by this invoice of Rotherham and
Co., I find goods sold by them, on 18th February, for 311. 138. 1Cd., disposed
of to Roberts, on the 24rd, for 22. 138. 10d., being a loss of 91.-I also
find goods sold by Caldecott, on 14th February, for 131. 118. 2d., disposed of
to Roberts, on 23rd February, for 91. 148. 11d., being a loss of 31, 168, 3d.,
and goods bought of Hitchcock, Williams, and Co., on 21st February, for
206.58, 1d., are sold, on 23rd February, to Roberts, for 192. Os. 104d., being
a loss of 71. 48. 21d., irrespective of carriage. (The witness went through the
