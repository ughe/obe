2230. ROBERT JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of Aug.,
1 handkerchief, value 38., the goods of John Furnifull, from his person.
JOHN FURNIFULL. I am a clerk, and live on the premises of Messrs.
Leaf, in Wood-street, Cheapside. On the 29th of Aug., about three o'clock
in the afternoon, I was in Cateaton-street-I felt something at my coat pocket
-I turned round, and saw the prisoner running away with a handkerchief
in his hand, which was mine, and had been in my pocket just before-I pur-
sued, and saw him throw it down- I took it up-it is my property.
JAMES TRIM (City police-constable, No. 415.) I heard the cry of
"Stop thief," and saw the prisoner running-I pursued, and stopped him in
Lombard-street.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Four Months.

Before Mr. Justice Wightman.
2231. HENRY FOY and DANIEL GRAINGE were indicted for stealing
2 sheep, price 41., the property of Thomas Symons.--2nd Count, stating
them to be the property of William Roadnight.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS TRUMPER. I live at Harefield, and am bailiff to my uncle, Wil.
Jiam Trumper. On Friday morning, the 30th of Aug., about seven o'clock,
I saw the prisoners going across the pasture fields belonging to my uncle, at
Harefield-they went into a blind ditch, which has a hedge growing over it
-I went round, and came up to the spot where they were when I got near
them I heard what I thought was the sawing of meat-I got assistance, and re-
turned with William Trumper and George Holt to the spot where I had seen the
prisoners-they had then left-I examined the place, and found two loins of
mutton in the ditch they have not been preserved—they were covered with
a bag- I saw Foy going away from the spot-he was from seventy to a hun-
dred yards off when I saw him I sent for a policeman, showed him where
I found the mutton, and delivered it to him--when I saw the prisoners in the
ditch there was nobody else about the mutton was partly in and partly out
of the bag.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'Brien. Q. When you first saw the prisoners,
were they not going across the footpath ? A. No, crossing the fields where
there is no footpath-they got into the path-there are hazel-nut trees in
the hedge.
