 1340. WILLIAM JOHNSON was again indicted for stealing, on the 24th of April , 1 hat-brush, value 2s., the goods of William Roberts.
   JAMES BARRON. The prisoner came to my house on the 24th of April with this brush - he was dressed in black and had a blue cloak on his arm, and his spectacles as before - I looked at him, as I had received information but half an hour before - I said to him,
 "You sold me a book?" he said, "I did;" I said, "I have had information, and I must detain you;" he said, "For God's sake, don't do that, I shall be ruined;" I said, "I must do my duty;" I sent my servant for an officer, but Wray was going by, and he took him - I looked at the brush, but did not particularly observe it.
 ELIZABETH ROBERTS. I live with my father,  William Roberts- he lives in St. Martin's-court, and is a brush-maker. On the 24th of April a man came into our shop, and asked to look at a clothes-brush; I took out the drawer- he said he wanted one with a handle; I turned, and got one out of the window - he said the hairs were not long enough; I said we had no longer, and he went out - we had several brushes on a tray in the shop; I did not miss one, but I have seen the one produced - it has my father's name on it, and had been in our shop; it is worth 3s. - I had not sold such a brush on the 24th of April - the person had a cloak on his arm, a stick in his hand, and a crape hat-band - I did not observe his face or his voice; it was between six and seven o'clock in the evening.
 THOMAS WRAY. I am the officer. I took the prisoner about nine o'clock in Mr. Barron's shop - I took the brush from his hand at the station; he had this cloak and this stick - I do not know whether he had any spectacles on, as I did not like to take hold of him - I thought he was certainly a nobleman; he then had whiskers, and his hair was all about his forehead, but he was shaved his whiskers off.
 Prisoner. I have a witness that I bought that brush in the beginning of Passion-week, in St. Martin's-court - I believe it was of Miss Roberts herself, though it was only the affair of a moment.
 ELIZABETH HASLET. I live in White Lion-street, Pentonville - the prisoner lodged with me. I know he had a brush of this kind - he showed it to me, and said he gave 2s. 9d. for it; he lived with me five or six weeks - I cannot say when I first saw the brush, nor when I last saw it- it was a small hat-brush.
     GUILTY. Aged 32. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1341.  RICHARD GIBBS was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of May , 1 bottle, value 3d., and 1 pint of rum, value 2s., the goods of Charles Temple.
   ALGERNON SMITH(Police-constable S 22). On the night of the 7th of May I stopped the prisoner in Skinner-street, with this bottle under his coat; he appeared partly intoxicated - I asked where he got it - he said he bought it of a boy for 4d., at the corner of Brill-row.
 CHARLES TEMPLE. I  keep the  Somers'-town coffee-house, in Charlton-street. I have lost two or three sample-bottles of spirits - this is one of mine, and had rum in it; it has the mark of the ship on it: it was safe on the Friday, when we cleaned the window.
 Prisoner. He could not swear to it at the office. Witness. My young man could not swear to it, but when I went I could - he was taken nearly a quarter of a mile from my house.
 JURY. Q. Are you satisfied that no other publican has such a private mark as you have? A. It is not my mark, it is the ship's mark, and my number, which is 63; the numbers go regularly.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1342.  JAMES EPPS was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of May , 1 fish-plate, value 2s., the goods of John Walden.
   ELIZABETH WALDEN. I live with my father, John Walden, at Somers'-town- he deals in china. I saw the prisoner outside the shop, on the morning of the 9th of May; he asked the price of something - I said I could not tell, as my mother was out; I then saw a fish-tray under his arm; I went and overtook him in Seymour-street, with it - he said he had bought it of a girl, but gave it to me; it is the same size and pattern as ours, and we missed one - it has no mark on it.
   FREDERICK HILES. I took the prisoner - he told me he bought the plate of a girl for 3d. - he did not say when.
 Prisoner's Defence. I bought it in Petticoat-lane.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1343  HENRY COTTON was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of May , 1 handkerchief, value 4s., the goods of Thomas Jackson , from his person.
   THOMAS JACKSON. I live in George-street, Minories. On the 5th of May, about half-past ten o'clock at night, I was walking along; I felt something - I turned and missed my handkerchief; I saw the prisoner a few yards off, throwing it down - I pursued him, and he was stopped; I had not lost sight of him - this is it.
   JOHN DAGLEY(Police-constable G 171). I stopped the prisoner at the corner of Greville-street, Hatton-garden- I got this handkerchief from a young lady, in the prosecutor's presence: I saw the prisoner throw two handkerchiefs down, and I got another from him at the station.
 Prisoner's Defence. I never had the prosecutor's handkerchief in my possession; I did not throw any away- the gentleman raised his umbrella to strike me, and I ran away.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
