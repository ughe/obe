JOHN STEPHENS. I am a cheesemonger, opposite Mrs. Trafford's. I
heard screams of "Murder !" and "Police I went and saw the prisoner, Mrs.
Trafford, and her daughter struggling in the road together, the prisoner gave
Miss Trafford four or five very savage kicks about the lower part of her person
- he had a little hair clinging to his fingers-I interposed-it was I that
tore his coat-I did not see Miss Trafford strike him--the prisoner had a
black eye, but one of the witnesses gave him that I had a great deal to do
to keep the people from punishing him-he was given in charge.
Prisoner. Q. Did not you strike me? A. No-you were not knocked
down—it is a respectable house.
SEN
Prisoner's Defence, It is the result of vindictive feeling, through my
having left off lodging at her house, and gone to a house opposite; the
daughter said she would serve me out.
GUILTY. Aged 32.-Confined Six Months.
