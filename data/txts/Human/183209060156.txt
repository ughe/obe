RICHARD SILVER. I am a silk-weaver, and live in Hare-street, Bethnal-green; the prisoner was sent from Bethnal-green workhouse to nurse my wife in her confinement. On Sunday, the 12th of August, when I came home to dine, there was no dinner cooked, and she had left my wife in bed unable to help herself - I missed these things; she came again on Monday; I said, "Where are the things you have taken away, belonging to the Benevolent Society-box? tell me, or you will get me into disgrace;" she then told me, and I found them.
   SARAH BAILEY. I keep a clothes-shop near Bethnalgreen. The prisoner brought me a bed-gown, five napkins, and a jacket; I gave her 1s. 3d. for them - they are very old; I did not observe the society's stamp on them till it was shown to me.
   JOHN CARPENTER. I am a Policeman. I have the property; some of it is very old.
 Prisoner's Defence. His wife asked me to go and pawn something belonging to the box - I refused; she sent her boy to pawn them - the following week she asked me again, and I pawned some for 3s.; I was taken ill, and asked this woman to lend me 1s. 3d. on these things - I was to return it again; I told his wife what I had done - she said I was welcome.
     GUILTY. Aged 52. - Confined Six Months.
 2107.  ANN BRADY was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of August , 2 pairs of boots, value 10s., the goods of Joseph Hester.
 JOSEPH HESTER. I am a  boot and shoemaker, and live in Crown-street, Finsbury. On the 24th of August, about two o'clock, the prisoner and another came into my shop to buy a pair of shoes; several pairs fitted them, but they objected to them - the other went out, and just as the prisoner was going out, I stopped her, and asked what she was going to do with the boots she had got, as I missed two pairs off a rail; I found one pair under her arm, under her shawl - she said she was going to pay for them- she had before denied having them; I have not found the other pair.
   WILLIAM DAMAN. I am a Policeman. I received her in charge; she said she had money in her pocket to pay for them - I found 6d. in silver, and 6d. in copper, on her.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2108.  JOHN BROOKS was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of September , 1 pair of shoes, value 3s., the goods of Thomas Clark.
   THOMAS CLARK. I am a shoemaker, and live in Upper Marylebone-street. The prisoner was nearly eleven months in my employ; I gave him the materials to make four pairs of shoes, on a Saturday, and on the Monday he was taken in the act of selling a pair of shoes, which are mine, and have my mark on them - I cannot say whether they were what he had to make - I never knew any thing against him.
   PETER CANE. I am a Policeman. I saw the prisoner on the 3rd of September, offering these boots to a Jew for sale - he folded them up in a handkerchief, and walked off, on seeing me; I followed him to Manchestersquare, and asked how he came by them - he said he made them for a tailor's wife; they did not fit, and he wanted to sell them; he said he lived in Clipstone-street; I found he did not live there - he begged me not to take him, and then said it was work his master gave him, and when finished, instead of taking it home, he wanted to sell it - when I got near the station, he ran off, but I never lost sight of him; he said at the office that he stole them from his employer in Tottenham-court-road, but did not know the name or number.(Property produced and sworn to).
 The prisoner pleaded poverty.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. - Confined One Month.
 2109.  WILLIAM CASTLE and JOSEPH SKELTON were indicted for stealing, on the 6th of September , a skittle-ball, value 2s., the goods of John Willis.
   CHARLES HOLLAND. I live with John Willis, a turner, in Kingsland-road. I missed this skittle-ball out of a little carriage, in the shop.
   RICHARD HAWKES. I am a Policeman. On the 6th of September I saw the prisoners together; they went up Kingsland-road, and stopped at a public-house next door to Willis'; I afterwards stopped them, and found a skittle-ball on Skelton; he said he found it - I had seen him take it(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoners received a good characcer.
 CASTLE -    GUILTY. Aged 16.
 SKELTON -    GUILTY. Aged 17. Fined 1s., and delivered to their former employer.
 2010.  CORNELIUS DRISCOLL was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of August , 1 shawl, value 1l., the goods of William Henry Pearce.
   WILLIAM HENRY PEARCE. I live in Rosamond-place, Limehouse. On the 21st of August, early in the morning, the shawl was on the neck of my child, which a friend was carrying - he is not here; I had occasion to stop behind, and as I came on I saw the prisoner take the shawl off the ground; I said, "My friend, this is my property;" he said, "I wish you may get it," and run across the road-I followed; he said, "You may run, and be d - d;" I cannot say that he saw it fall from the child, which had gone on about thirty yards.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Where is your friend? A. Not here; I have not seen him since - I told the prisoner it was mine, and he ran off with it; I do not believe he saw it dropped - I had asked him about a minute before what he stood there for; this was before he took the shawl.
   JAMES PYE. I am a Policeman. I took the prisoner in Penny's-fields, Poplar, running; the prosecutor was following, calling Stop thief! I took the shawl from under his jacket.
   JOHN BENT. I am a Police-constable. About two o'clock in the morning I was with Pye, and heard the alarm; I received the prisoner - he said he had picked the shawl up in the road; I asked if nobody claimed it - he said Yes, there was a man claimed it; I took him back, and met the prosecutor.
 Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up in the road.
     GUILTY. Aged 36. - Confined One Month.
