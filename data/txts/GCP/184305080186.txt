1678. GEORGE RIXON was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of April,

Before Mr. Recorder.
1679. JOHN GLENNING was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
April, 1 paper-bag, value fd., and 1lb, weight of gingerbread, 18., of a man
whose name is unknown, from his person.
ROBERT BURNETT (police-constable C 120.) I was on duty at Greenwich
fair, on the 18th of April—I saw the prisoner in company with some other
boys—I saw him follow a gentleman, and take out of his pocket a bag of
gingerbread nuts-1 took him directly—the gentleman came part of the way
to the station, but he had a ticket to go by the steam-boat-it was just getting
dusk, and when I got near the station, he was gone-1 had not asked his
name, as I was not aware he was going - this is the bag of nuts-I asked the
gentleman if he had lost anything of value, and he turned round, and said this
was his property.
'Guilty. Aged 13.-Confined Three Weeks.1
1680. CHARLES NICHOLSON and CHARLES ANSLOW were in
dicted for stealing, on the 18th of April, 1 handkerchief, value 6d. ; the goods
of John Foster Hillman, from his person; to which
NICHOLSON pleaded Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Fourteen Days.
John Foster HILLMAN. I am clerk to Mr. Hillman, a merchant in the
City. On the 18th of April, between nine and ten o'clock at night, I was in
Greenwich fair-I heard some one call-I turned, and saw my handkerchief
in the constable's hand-it was safe about a minute before this is it.
HENRY FOWLER (police-constable E 111.) I was at Greenwich fair, and
saw the prisoners following the prosecutor, and talking together in an under
tone-I saw Nicholson take this handkerchief out of the prosecutor's pocket
and give it to Anslow, who had been covering Nicholson, and was near
enough to see what he did I took him, and took the handkerchief from him.
Anslow. I had it not in my possession at all. Witness. I took it from
him.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
ANSLOW-Guilty. Aged 17.- Confined Fourteen Days.
