1466. LOUISA FERGUSON was indicted for a misdemeanor.
Messrs. BODKIN and DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
ANN CHEsNEY. I am the wife of Joseph Chesney, a stationer in Ber,
mondsey-street. On the morning of the 3rd of March the prisoner came
in for a 46 Paul Pry," which came to 3d.-she gave me 2d. for it-I said I
could not let her have it-she said she had been in the habit of having it
every week for that-I said it had been raised to 3d. for three months, but
perhaps she meant the 44 Town"-she then said, Let me have the
Town gave her a Town," which is 2d.-she gave me a sixpence,
and I gave her 4d. change-I laid the sixpence on a shelf behind me-
after she was gone I looked at it, and thought it was a bad one-I showed
it to my daughter-I then put it by itself in a purse, which I have nowi
my pocket, and threw it into the till-it remained there till the prisoner
came-on the 17th of March, when my daughter was in the shop, the pri-
soner came in, I heard her voice-I came into the shop, and then she
wanted a 40 Paul Pry" for 2d.-I told her she could not have it for that-
she had the 44 Town" again, and offered a bad sixpence to my daughter,
who did not give her change, but kept the sixpence in her hand, and
showed it to me-I told the prisoner it was bad, and that she had passed
one to me that very day fortnight-she said no, she had not been in the
shop before-my husband sent for the policeman, and gave him the two
sixpences.
LOUIsa CHEsNey. I am the prosecutrix's daughter. On Sunday, the
17th of March, the prisoner came to my mother's shop-she asked for a
twopenny 46 Paul Pry"-] told her it was 3d.-she then changed to the
Town," and offered me a counterfeit sixpence-my mother came in, and

ROBERT ROSOMAN (police-constable M 158.) I was called in on the
