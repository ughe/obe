ELIZABETH METCALF. I am the wife of John Metcalf. The prisoner
lived servant with me for nearly three months-I missed these articles at
different times- I missed feathers from the bed - I had her boxes searched

was a very good servant-these things were all taken rough from the wash
-they were washed in the house-the shirt was taken from a drawer.

gown in particular, and she denied it-it was found in her box.
