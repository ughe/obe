1693. GEORGE GARDNER was indicted for stealing 4 pairs of gloves,
value 108.; the goods of William Eveling, his master.
WILLIAM EVELING. I keep a linen-draper's shop at No. 20, Richard-
street, Woolwich-the prisoner was in my service-I missed several pairs of
gloves, and some other things—these gloves produced are mine.
Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL. Q. Have you any partner? A. No
- Mr. Davey is my landlord- he has no interest in my business—he keeps
a wholesale house-I deal with him-the prisoner had been with me since
the 3rd of April; I have four persons besides in my shop-I found a large
quantity of gloves which had been worn, and out of them I found these four
pairs, which belong to me-- the prisoner acknowledged taking these-it is
not the custom in my house for young men to take goods and put the money
into the till-that has not been the custom in any shop that I have been in-
have been a linen-draper twenty-four years, and never knew it to be the case
-if he wanted a pair of gloves he would be served by a person in the shop-
if he paid for them, they would put the money into the tillif he did not pay,
an account would be taken-I keep my accounts myself, I do not take an
account of every article that is sold, it would be impossible--when this
pair was missed, I made inquiry if any one had sold any white kid gloves-
had eight pairs of them in my shop-these were missed on April 24th.
WILLIAM GLADWIN (police-constable R 122.) I searched the prisoner's
box, and found these four pairs of gloves, some lace, and some socks.
Cross-examined. Q. Did yon say to him, "It would be best for you to
confess all ?" A. No, certainly not-I did not say, "If you do so, they
may remand you for a week or so, and then you will get off"-I said
nothing of the kind merely asked him where he got these gloves-he
said he had taken them out of his master's shop without his knowledge.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prosecutor.-
Confined Three Months.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
