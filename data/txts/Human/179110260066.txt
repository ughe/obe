Mr. E. HODGSON,
 SHORT-HAND WRITER to the SESSIONS at the OLD BAILEY, more than Eighty Sessions,
 RESPECTFULLY thanks the Gentlemen of the Law and his Friends for their Patronage and Support, and informs them, that for the greater Convenience and Dispatch of Business, all Orders will be instantly attended to, at No. 22, Fetter-Lane, opposite Rolls Buildings, where Mr. HODGSON may be seen at all Times.
 TRIALS taken as usual, and Copies made out with the greatest Expedition.
 Mr. HODGSON flatters himself the Number and Importance of the Causes he has been employed to take for many Years past (including the Impeachment) will be his best Appeal for requisite Ability.
 A new Edition of his SHORT-HAND TREATISE, Price only 1 s. 6 d. is in the Press.
 Short-hand taught as usual, in Four Lessons, at home or abroad.
 Mr. HODGSON has a compleat Set of Sessions Papers, for the last ten Years, to dispose of at the usual Price; or any Person wanting any particular Trial, may have a Copy at Six-pence per Folio.
 N. B. The Sessions Papers, sold as above, at No. 22, Fetter-Lane, and sent to any part of London or the Country, as ordered.
 *** Various antient Sessions Papers, and Ordinary of Newgate's Accounts, for many Years, &c. &c.
