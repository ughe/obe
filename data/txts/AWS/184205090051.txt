SAMUEL EVERETT. I live in Silver-street, Bloomsbury. I missed a
metal cock and ball and a piece of lead pipe from outside my yard-I
know the lead produced belongs to me-it was fixed in the place which
supplies the kitchen with water.
RICHARD PHCEBE. I live in King's Arms-yard, Silver-street. About
ten minutes past nine o'elock, on the 14th of April, I was in my stable, I
heard a noise, in consequence of which I went out into Mr. Everett's yard,
and saw the prisoner coming along with something under his arm-I
stopped him, and found it was a piece of lead and a ball and cockhe
gave it me, and said he was very drunk-I took the pipe from him, and
let him gohe was very drunk-the pipe was wrenched off.
Prisoner. I was very drunk. It was this man's place to give me into
custody, not let me go home. I was at work next morning, and did not
know any thing about it. He could not swear to me; he said it was a
man in a red night-cap. Witness. I have not the least doubt he is the
man- never said I had any doubt-I did not know bim before.
GUILTY. Aged 20.Confined Two Months.
1488. FREDERICK RALPH and JAMES JONES were indicted for
stealing, on the 13th of April, 1 cloak, value 3l., the goods of William
Grange Pater.
JOHN M'PHERSON. I am errand-boy to Mr. Reed, a linen-draper,
On the 13th of April I was standing at my master's door, and saw Ralph
take the cloak out of Mr. Pater's phaeton-] called 44 Stop thief"
passed it o Jones-they both ran away down Vinegar-lane-I ran after
them, and lost sight of Jones-he had the cloak-I am sure Ralph is the
person I saw take it.
SAMUEL SOUTER. I was walking in Back-lane, Lower ShadwellI saw
Jones with the cloak under his arm, and Ralph close behind him-they
were walking up Back-lane-I saw Ralph give Jones a white handker-
chief, in which Jones wrapped the cloak-I passed them, and walked as
far as Devonport-street--I saw a number of persons running] asked a
groom there if he had lost any thing-he said, 44 Yes, a cloak"- went
to the first turning in Devonport-street, and saw Ralph in Vinegar-fields
-we ran after him-he was taken--this is the cloak 1 saw Jones with.
JAMES WHITING. I am groom to Mr. William Grange Pater. This is
his cloak-I lost it on Wednesday, the 13th of April, from his phaeton,
between twelve and one o'clock-it was missed for about half an hour.
Ralph's Defence. I was going down the Commercial-road to look after
work, and heard the cry of 46 Stop thief." I ran, they ran after me, and
stopped me. I know nothing about the cloak. I do not know Jones; E
do not think I ever saw him before.
RALPH"GUILT Aged 20.--Transported for Seven Years.
JONES-GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Three Months.
