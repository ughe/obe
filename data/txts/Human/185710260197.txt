 1132. GEORGE PRITCHARD was indicted for a like offence.
   MR. CLERK . conducted the Prosecution. JAMES WILLIAM BAKER. I keep the William the Fourth beer house, Old Kent Road. On 19th Oct., about five minutes before 10 o'clock, the prisoner came for a pint of porter and put down a counterfeit shilling——I broke it in two with my mouth, and asked him to pay for the porter—he said that he had not got any more money—I marked the pieces and put them on the counter—he took them away, and I took back my beer—this ( produced ) is one of the pieces. JOSEPH BROWN. I keep the Builders' Arms, Bermondsey New Road, not two minutes walk from Mr. Baker's. On 19th Oct., about 10 o'clock, the prisoner came for half a pint of, porter, and gave me a shilling—Mr. Baker had sent a man, who came in behind him and said, "Look at that shilling; he has just come from Mr. Baker's "—I tried it; it was bad, and I give him into custody. JOHN BRYANT. ( Policeman, M  212). I took the prisoner—Mr. Brown gave me this shilling ( produced )—I searched him in the house, but I found nothing—on the way to the station he said, "I expect I shall get remanded, and then get turned up;" that means discharged—on the following morn ing, about six o'clock, I searched between Mr. Baker's house and Mr. Brown's, and picked up this piece of a broken shilling on the pavement. WILLIAM WEBSTER. This is a bad shilling; and this is a fragment of another.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was at Mr. Brown's house, but I know nothing of the piece which was picked up; I may have taken it When I was out with or barrow. GUILTY. ** †— Confined Twelve Months .

EDWARD DAVIS  (17), Burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling house of Samuel Spencer , at St. George the Martyr, Southwark and stealing therein one clock, one sheet, one candlestick, and one tobacco box, value 17 s  . 6 d  ., his property. REBECCA SPENCER. I am the wife of Samuel Spencer, of No. 5 Alford Place, Old Kent Road, in the parish of St. George, Southwark; it is his dwelling house. On 8th Sep. I fastened up the house about 10 minutes to 11 o'clock; all the windows and doors were safe; the kitchen window was shut down, but I cannot say whether it was fastened I placed this pair of trowsers ( produced ) op a chair in front of the fire, as they were damp, and I looked up and saw this candlestick and tobacco box safe—I was not disturbed in the night, but missed the things in the morning. MARY FOOT. I live with Mrs. Spencer. I came down at half past 5 o'clock, and found the kitchen window partly open, and the kitchen and garden doors—there is no shutter; the bolt of the garden door was not broken—I cannot say whether there was any fastening to the window; if anybody opened it they could get in—I informed Mrs. Spencer, who came down.
