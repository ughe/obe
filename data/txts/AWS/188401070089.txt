Cross-eamined. He had not been boxing, but the boxing was to take
place in the evening-] had the barman and my wife to assist me in the
bar-I was in the bar the whole time-I had to stop it because they got
singing-10 or 12 persons were in the bat-my house is about seven
minutes from Great Alie Street-I had my eye on him all the time
and never missed him-there were about seven of them, and I was
getting out of temper with them beeause they wanted to dance-
MeCarthy came about twice a week of an evening to box-he went away
to his tea and came back about 8.30, and stayed the whole evening.
Re-eramined. The public-houses are closed on Christmas Day from
3 to 6 as on Sundays-I noticed nothing extraordinary in his manner,
or any indication that he was frightened, but the reverse, for he was
rather jolly.
