 1602. EDMUND HARRISON was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of May , 10 pairs of trowsers, value 3 s  ., the goods of William Sumshon Hayes ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. JAMES LLOYD. On the 9th of May, about seven o'clock at night, I saw the prisoner near Mr. Hayes's window, in Farringdon-street, with the trowsers on his arm—I went in and inquired there, came out and followed the prisoner—he dropped the trowsers, and went into Shoe-lane, where the policeman met him—the prosecutor took up the trowsers. ADAM SPRAY( City police-constable. No.  38) I heard an alarm, and saw the prisoner running—I followed him to New-street—he ran into a cellar, I secured him, and brought him back. WILLIAM SUMSHON HAYES. These are my ten pairs of trowsers—they had been on a chair near the door. WILLIAM DARBY( City police-constable, No.  9.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office ( read )—the prisoner is the person who was convicted. GUILTY.* Aged 21.— Transported for Seven Years.
 1603. JOHN THOMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of April , 1 portmanteau, value 1 l  . 5 s  ., the goods of Thomas Moore . GEORGE KEDGE. I am shopman to a pawnbroker in Long-acre. This portmanteau was pawned by the prisoner on the 24th of April—he said he was a maker, and pawned it to raise money to make two others. GEORGE DAVIES( police-constable C  33.) I stopped the prisoner on the 26th of April with another portmanteau, and found on him the duplicate, of this one. ANDREW COXHEAD. I am shopman to Thomas Moore, a trunk-maker in St. Martin's-lane. This is his portmanteau—it was stolen on the 23rd or 24th of April off the counter. GUILTY. Aged 19.
 1604. JOHN THOMPSON was again  indicted for stealing, on the   26th of April , 1 portmanteau, value 8 s  ., the goods of John James . GEORGE DAVIES( police-constable C  33.) I met the prisoner on the 26th of April carrying this portmanteau—he was turning out of Oxford-street into Berwick-street—I saw him offer it for sale for half-a-crown—he then went to a pawnbroker's, and offered it there for 6 s .—I then took him. WILLIAM STAITE BREWER. I am shopman to John James. This port manteau is his, and was taken from the front of his shop—I missed it about half-past four o'clock on the 26th of April. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Transported for Seven Years.
