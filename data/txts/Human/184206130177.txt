 1902. JOHN LINEHAM was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of June , 8lbs. 6oz. weight of nails, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Thomas Cubitt . JOSEPH MARKQUICK. I am in the employ of Lewis Cubitt, who lives at Chelsea. About twenty minutes before six o'clock in the evening of the 4th of June I saw the prisoner get over a wall and building belonging to Thomas Cubitt, which is opposite where I live—the workmen had left about a quarter of an hour—I saw the prisoner come back with a cap in his hand, which he had on his head when he went over—I followed, he saw me, and ran away—I took him, and found in his cap about 1000 nails—he said he picked them up among the rubbish—I said, "What, new nails?"—he said "Yes"—I gave him to a policeman, and went to the building, and saw the nails had been shifted—I found some nails in the premises similar to those found on him—part of them were scattered on the floor—I compared those in his cap with what I found, and they cor responded—they were Mr. Thomas Cubitt's. JOHN DENMAN( police-constable B  69.) The prisoner was given in charge—be said he took them to sell for a halfpenny a pound, as he had done before—he told me he took them out of the building.
   Prisoner's Defence.  Mr. Cubitt's foreman said if I told the truth he would let me go; they were nails that were lying on the floor. GUILTY. Aged 11.— Confined Three Months.
 1903. THOMAS POTTER was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of June , 1 handkerchief, value 6 d  ., the goods of Philip Hemery , from his person. THOMAS HARE( police-constable A  103.) I was on duty at Westmin ster on the morning of the 7th of June—I saw the prosecutor listening to the band, and the prisoner close behind him—I watched, and as the sol diers were coming out of the archway I saw the prisoner get closer to the prosecutor, and turn from him with this handkerchief in his right hand—I took him—he dropped the handkerchief—the prosecutor turned round, and owned it—the prisoner said he had not had it. PHILIP HEMERY. I live in St. James's-place, Hampstead-road. I was in the Colour-yard, at St. James's, about eleven o'clock in the morn ing of the 7th of June—I put my hand to my pocket, for my handkerchief, and missed it—I turned, and saw it in the policeman's hand—he was in the act of seizing the prisoner—it was safe in my pocket two or three mi nutes before—this is it.
