I now produce the prisoner's pass-book—it has been received made up and sent to him in the ordinary way—I do not know when it was last made up—one book was left February 2nd, 1876—I would swear it was returned to the prisoner before Christmas last, made up to March, 1876—there are marks in the book from which I could tell when it went out; for instance, the entries being made in another clerk's writing would show me it had gone out in the meantime—pass-books are not usually made up every night, the same clerk generally makes up the pass-books—I feel certain the prisoner's book was sent out in February, but won't swear it without referring to another book, which is not here. GUILTY — Five Years' Penal Servitude.
