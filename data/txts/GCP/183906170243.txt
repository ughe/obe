GEORGE GRIFFITHS. I am clerk to Messrs. Ransom and Co., bankers,
in Pall Mall. Benjamin Woolf, the last witness, banks at our house, on
the 2nd of last April, we paid a cheque in favour of Henry Solomon, drawn
by Woolf, for 1001.
Q. Tell me whether among the notes you paid one of £5., No. 81,406,
dated the 3rd of March, 1838 ? A. I paid one of that number, but the
date I did not take-I also paid a £5 note, No. 71,342-(these two notes
were numbered 16 and 17.)

it does not say to whom it was paid.

hand-writing-(looking at note No. 16—81406) - this is also his hand-

Cross-examined by ME. SERGEANT BOMPAS, Q. Do you know Mr.
Abrahams, the attorney ? A. Yes; I cannot tell who found me out-I bad
no communication with him on the subject of the hand-writing I have
seen him here--I should say it was eight years since I have seen Moses
write-it was at my father's chambers, No. 17, Clement's Inn.
