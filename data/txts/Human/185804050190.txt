 492. MARY HUNT , Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
   MR. SCOBELL conducted the Prosecution. JANE FRIEND. I am the wife of William Friend, a corn chandler, of No. 70, New Palace Road, Southwark. On 17th March, about 2 o'clock, the prisoner came for a pint of split peas—my man served her, and I saw her tender a bad half crown in payment—I put the change down, she took it up in a harried manner, and left; I found it was bad; my husband went after her aud gave her in charge. WILLIAM FRIEND. I am the husband of the last witness. I Went after the prisoner, and gave her in charge. GEORGE BARR( Policeman, L  34). I took the prisoner—she was take to Lambeth police court, and discharged. ELIZABETH KING. I am the wife of Henry King, of Evelin Street, Dept ford, a china dealer. On the 27th March, the prisoner came, between 6 and 7 o'clock in the evening, for two plates, at a 1 d . each, and gave my daughter a half crown—she gave it me, and I found that it was bad—I marked it; and gave ber in charge. JOHN LOCKER( Policeman, K  295). I received the prisoner, and this half crown—she said she did not know it was bad; a gentleman gave it to her down the road. WILLIAM WEBSTER. These half crowns are both bad. GUILTY.— Confined Six Months .

 493. JAMES HOLLOWAY , Breaking and entering the dwelling house of Timothy M'Carthy , and stealing therein 4 s  . 6 d  ., his moneys. TIMOTHY M'CARTHY. I keep a beer shop in Providence Street, Rother hithe, I reside there alone; I padlock the door when I leave, and take the key with me—I go out on Sundays to divine service, and for some Sundays when I came home I have missed money—on 28th March, I missed a half crown, and a florin from my trousers' pocket, in my bed room—I had left home about half past 10 o'clock, and came back about 11 o'clock—I afterwards saw it at the station—the prisoner has been in and out like other customers, and has done several things for me—if I wanted a pint of beer taken out, he would take it, but he was not in my service—this money ( produced ) I had marked.
