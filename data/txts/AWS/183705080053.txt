1203. EDGAR ROBERTS was indicted for stealing, on the 28rd of
March, 4 books, value 1l., the goods of the trustees of the British Museum.
-2nd COUNT, stating them to be the goods of Sir Henry Ellis, Knight.
3rd COUNT, stating them to be the goods of our Sovereign Lord the King.
-Also on the 1st of February, 3 books, value 61., the goods of the trustees
of the British Museum.-Also on the 16th of March, 6 books, valne 2L,
the goods of the trustees of the British Museum.-Also on the 3rd of April,
8 books, value 2l., the goods of the trustees of the British Museum; to all
of which indictments he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Transported for Seven Years.
1204. ELIZA MARCHANT was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of
April, 6 handkerchiefs, value 3l., the goods of Sophia Matilda French.
MR. ADOLPHUS conducted the Prosecution.
SOPBIA MATILDA FRENCH. I live in Portmanrplace, Edgeware-road.
The prisoner was my servant for a fortnightaI knew her before-she was
very much distressed-I missed 2 handkerchiefs on the 13th of April, and
6 in all-I told the prisoner that it would be better to tell about them-
she pretended that she had not seen them--I afterwards gave her into custody
-the handkerchiefs were taken from the pawnbroker's -these are them-
I know them by marks of my own making.
JOHN ROBBRTS (police-constable T 91.) On the night of the 17th of
April, I received information of this robbery-l went to Mitchell-street,
Edgeware-road and apprehended the prisoner, but she was not detained
that night-the next morning I traced 2 handkerchiefs to a pawnbroker's
and the next morning I again took hershe said she hoped Miss French
would forgive her, that she had taken them, and given them to a person
who had pawned them for her, of the name of Flower-I took Flower,
but the bill against her has been thrown out.
GEoReg SPELLER. I live with Mr. Wallis, a pawnbroker-these 2
handkerchiefs were pawned with me on Monday the 17th of Aprilbut
not by the prisonerthey are very good and very valuable ones.
Prisoner. I was just out of my illness, and was in great distress.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged S,wReconimended to merey by the Jury and Prosecutor.
Confined Three Months.
1205. JOHN WILDEY was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of April,
1 wateh, value 158. 1 seal, value 28. 2 watch keys, value 28. and 1
wateh ribbon, value 1d. the goods of Thomas Miller.
THoMAs MILLER. I live in Prospect-terrace, St. Pancras, and am a
shoemaker. About nine o'clock at night, on the 8th of April, I saw the
prisoner walking out of my front room, and he had got my watch under a
sack that he had in his hand--he had not got out-this is the wateh-it
had been on the mantel-piec in the front room-he said he hoped I should
not be hard with him-I was in the back parlour at work when he came
in- gave him in charge.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going up to Gray's Inn-road, and a man
asked me to do a little job-he showed me this wateh, and told me to go
and feteh it for him-I refused, but he gave me some drink-I went in,
and did it--I told the prosecutor the man was outside, but he would not
go after him.
GUILTY. Aged -Recommended to mercey--Confined Six Months;
Three Weeks Solitary.
