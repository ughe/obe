I was called and saw Gregory; his face was bleeding, and his hat broken—the prisoner was pointed out to me by the Rockingham; I took hold of him and said I should take him for assaulting a man—he said, "I never touched the man; you are taking me for nothing"—on the way to the station he told Lock that if he came against him he would wait on him when he came out—Gregory opened his coat at the station, and this bar of the chain fell from his waistcoat—the prisoner refused his address; he said he had just come from Bristol.
   The Prisoner produced a written defence, stating that the charge was made up against him owing to his coat and handkerchief, and to his having just come out of prison, and contended that if he had stolen the locket he would not have come back to be given into custody. GUILTY. He was further charged with a conviction of felony at this Court on  8 th April , 1889, but as it appeared that the charge was misdemeanour and not felony, the RECORDER directed a verdict of
