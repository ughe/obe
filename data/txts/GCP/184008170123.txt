2079. DENNIS GLYNN was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of
July, 2 pence, and 2 halfpence, the monies of Thomas Appleyard, from
the person of William Henry Appleyard ; and that he had been before
convicted of felony.
THOMAS APPLEYARD. I live in Dartmouth-row, Westminster-my
son William Henry is between nine and ten years old. On the 31st of
July he was sent out by a woman who was working in my house, but
who is not here, to buy some bread-he had the money with him I was
asleep at the time,
WILLIAM HENRY APPLEYARD. I am between nine and ten years old.
I was sent with a shilling for a loaf - I got the loaf, and bad 3d. in change
-I was going home, and saw the prisoner-he told me to open my band
-I said I would not-he then forced it open, took the 3d. out, and ran
away-I know he is the boy- I did not know him before.
ROBERT SMITH (police-constable B 97.) On the 31st of July I heard
a cry of "Stop bim”-I saw the prisoner running, and he said it was not
him that took it-I took him, and the little boy came up directly, and
said he had robbed him of 3d.- I looked in the prisoner's hand, and
found the 3d.
WILLIAM UPCHURCH (police-constable B 25.) I produce a certificate
of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got at Mr. Clark's office-
