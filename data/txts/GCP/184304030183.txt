1370, FREDERICK STEVENS and GEORGE WOOD were indicted
for stealing, on the 7th of April, 5lbs. weight of bacon, value 2s., the goods
of Robert Edward Wiltshire.
2 to
ROBERT EDWARD WILTSHIRE. I live in Cross-street, Wenloek-road, and
am a cheesemonger. A little after twelve o'clock on the 7th of April, a young
man gave me information, and I missed three pieces of bacon-I ran out, and
saw the prisoners turning the corner-I sent my boy after them the police-
man, in the mean time, brought the prisoners and two pieces of the bacon,
which were safe ten minutes before. de

Stevens throw down a piece of bacon, and Wood dropped another piece-I
took them back to the shop, and one of them dropped another piece I found
a knife on Wood.
