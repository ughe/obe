574. NORAH MAHONEY was indicted for stealing, on the 4th No-
vember, 1 bedstead, value 28., the goods of Richard Burn.
RICHARD Burn, I am a fruiterer, and live in Little Shire-lane. In
October last the prisoner lodged in my house, and continued there till the 7th
of December- I missed a bedstead, and charged hen with having taken it-
she said she did not expect I should take her up for so trifling a charge-I
told her, after living rent-free for so many weeks, and committing such a
number of depredations, I must-I went to Cornelius Mahoney, in Wild-
court, Drury-lane, and there I saw the bedstead.
Prisoner. I had it six weeks before I went to his house it is my own
and not his. Witness. It is mine I am sure.
Alice LILEY, I live at Mr. Burn's. I saw the bedstead in the cellar
in October, before the prisoner came to the house I have seen it since it
is his, and was so before she came it had a sacking on then.
BRIDGET MAHONEY. I am the wife of Cornelius Mahoney, and live in
Wild-court, Drury-lane. The prisoner gave me the bedstead that was
found at my house nine weeks before Christmas, and said her brother gave
it to her before she went to live there she lived on the third floor, in
Wild-court-she bad it in the room I am no relation to the prisoner
-I always knew her by the name of Macarthy she lodged in the room
before I rented it-she told me she was moving to Mr. Bam's there was
a bedstead in the room, and she did not want that, so she let me have it
there was no sacking to it then.
.
JAMES NOCKALL. I am a policeman. I charged the prisoner with this
-she said she did not think Mr. Burn would prosecute her for so sim-
ple a thing.
TITIE
Prisoner. I did not say any such thing: it is false; tell the truth.
Witness, I swear that is the truth.
Prisoner's Defence. I owed him 88. rent, and he said he should give me
in charge because I ran away with his rent.
.
RICHARD BURN re-examined. She does owe me rent, but I freely for-
gave her when she left the house I have no object in this prosecution but
justice, nor had I the least idea of punishing her--I should not have thought
of finding this bedstead, only on 26th of January my bed-room door was
broken open, and my drawers ransacked of every thing; I had reason to
suspect the prisoner, and I had proofs of two instances, but I told her I
would forgive her for that.
