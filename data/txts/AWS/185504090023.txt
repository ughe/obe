ELIZABETH ANN SPEARS. I keep a cigar shop, in Kingsland-road. The
prisoner came there about 10 'clock on 19th March-h asked for 6d.-worth
of cigars-] asked which he would like-he looked towards the Pickwicks
-I picked out seven, and wrapped them in paper, and he gave me half a
crown-I gave him 28., and he went away-I put the half crown in the
till-I had no other half crown there-when I closed the shop I took it out
and put it in a small box, and took it to my bed room-the next morning
I sent it by my servant to the buteher's-ish brought it me back-I asked
two persons in the shop if it was good-one said he wished he had a pocket
full-the other said it was bad-I afterwards took it to the butter shop and
they broke it.
Cross-excamined by Mr. SLEIGH. Q. Did you ever see the prisoner before 7
A. No-I saw him again on the Tuesday week following, but I deseribed
him to the policemanwher he came out of the van, the policeman told me
to pick him out-I did not hear the Magistrate say he was not satisfied with
the identity of the man, and he should discharge him-I am positive to him
by his features and the leer of his eye-I described him to the policeman.
Mr. BODKIN. Q. Where was the van when he came out of it A. At
the police station-] cannot tell how many men I had seen before I picked
out the man that passed the bad coin-ino one pointed the prisoner out to
me; I said it from my own recollection-al noticed his features, and de-
seribed them to the policeman-I have not the slightest doubt about his
being the person.

WILaM FINcH. I keep the Admiral Keppel, in Hoxton. On 19th
March the prisoner came to my house, at a little after 11 o'clock, for a
quartern of gin; he put a bottle on the counter-I served him, and he put
down a bad half crown; I weighed it, and found it was bad-I told him I
thought it was bad, and I was justified in detaining him and the half crown
-I gave him into eustody, and gave the half crown to the policeman.
Cross-eaamined. Q. Did he not tell you he did not know it was bad
A. Yes he said he got it from his employer on Saturday night, and that

other persons round, but not my family-I knew most of them; they were
neighbours-I have not any of them here.
