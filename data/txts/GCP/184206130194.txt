1926. HENRY JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of
June, 1 purse, value 18., 6 half-crowns, and 1 sixpence; the property
Rachael Wilson, from her person.
RACHAEL WILSON. I am a widow, and live in Little Compton-street
On the 10th of June I was in Lincoln's Inn-fields alone, about nine o'clock
in the evening-it was on the same side that Turnstile is--perceived
two persons behind me-I had not proceeded far before I missed a weight
from my pocket-I then turned and saw two persons so near me as almost
to touch me, and the prisoner was next to me I saw my purse in his
hand, and saw him pass it to the other man who was with him I went up
to the prisoner, and told him he had robbed me--the instant I said that,
the other man ran away-the prisoner said he had not robbed me- mob
collected round us, and the officer came up I gave him in charge-I had
in my purse six balf-crowns and a sixpence--it was a brown silk purse!
am certain I had it safe when I turned into Lincoln's Inn-fields-I know by
the weight of it.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. Was not this later than nine
o'clock in the evening? 4. It might be a little later-the prisoner did
not offer to run away-the mob gathered round-I had not noticed those
persons behind me till I got into the square-there were other persons
coming towards us, but not going the other way-the prisoner had my
purse in his hand-it was not dark, it was twilight-I do not know how
they got at it-my pocket was not cut-I actually saw my purse in the
prisoner's hand.
ADOLPHUS LONSDALE (police-constable F 110.) I was on duty, and
saw the prisoner, who was detained by the prosecutrix and a gentleman-
the prisoner said he had not taken the purse.
GUILTY. Aged 30.- Confined Six Months.
