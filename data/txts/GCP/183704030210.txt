against any body? 4. No, I had one person summoned about three
months ago-there were three of them--their names were Cripps, Barton,
and another I went as far as a bench-warrant, and during that time two
of them got committed for exposing their persons in Covent-garden market,
and I got 11. from each not to proceed-their wives begged me to do 80-
