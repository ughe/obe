JOSEPH Hows.--I am a boot and shoe maker-on 28th July, about 10.30
or 11 o'clock, I was in the Globe public-house, seven or eight doors from the
prisoner's house - I heard a great noise, went out and saw a crowd in front
of the prisoner's shop-I saw Hibbert, he was decidedly drunk, and said
“You hounds, I will wait till I do have it out with one of you, and if
I don't have it out now I will meet you in the market to-morrow"-the
prisoner was then under his blind, which extends nearly across the pave-
ment-he wished Hibbert to go home, and Hibbert said that he would not
go until he bad had it out with one of them.
