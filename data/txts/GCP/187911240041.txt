33. GEORGE BLUNT (26), Embezzling the suns of 21. 108. and
11. 28. 6d. received by him on account of Headly Bankhouse and another,
his masters.
MR. BESLEY Prosecuted ; MR. GEOGHEGAN Defended.
THOMAS SAUNDERS. I am manager to Mr. Bankhouse, who trades as
Roberts and Co. at 76, New Bond Street, Chemists I was with then
in the Paris house three years, and only knew Blunt as an assistant
when I came to manage the London shop about seven months ago
his salary was 651. a year and board and lodging-in consequence of suspi-
cions I saw Sergeant O'Dee at Vine Street Station, and he marked some
coins in my presence with a letter-punch-I received from him live half
sovereigns marked "J," which I placed in the till at New Bond Street
just hefore 11.30, removing all other money except between 68, and 98. in
silver unmarked-I had sent Blunt upstairs we keep this cash-book (pro
duced) at the shop, and also a cash-sheet—the assistant who receives money
for shop sales enters the amount and the article sold, on the cash-sheet, and
puts the amount in the till—--while Blunt was upstairs I made a fictitious
entry in the book, so that he might not suspect that the half-sovereigns
had been put into the till the page was blank-the sheet was in its place
by the till the entries are in pencil; there were three, and I had marked
the last-Blunt came down in a few minutes-it was his duty in my
absence to serve customers and receive moneys—I had commnnicated with
Mr. Williams, the other assistant-I then went away for about half an
hour-Detective Fergeant O'Dee was near the shop by arrangement when
I returned, and Blunt Wos serving—I do not remember if Williams was
there-I stood at my bureau, and saw Blunt write the last entry in the
cash-book “48. 6d., Miss Stuarton "-"Pinto" is the fictitious entry-the
268. is Blunt's, and “Sands" is mine" Sutton" and "Stnarton" are his
writing-"Livier" and "18. 6d. cash-book" are mine-I then cleared the
till of all the gold, viz., five half-sovereigns marked "J," one half-sovereign
marked "F,” and three unmarked—there was about 108. silver-I found
three entries had been written in the cash-sheet by Blunt "Anti-fat,
68. 6d.; Mrs. Ellis, 68.; Maddison, 168."-after communicating again with
O'Dee, I came back to business and waited for him-I said nothing to
Blunt-O'Dee came in about ten minutes and spoke to me in a private
room-Blunt was then called in from the shop, and O'Dee asked him if he
had any money; he produced two half sovereignsafter examining them,
O'Dee said "These are marked coins; I caused them to be paid over the
counter"-he replied “You have me"-I give him in custody—I had seen
the two half-sovereigns stamped "T" by the officer.
