GEORGE BEEDY. I am'& bootmaker, at Waterloo Place, Woolwich-on

saw a man running as hard as he could-I ran after him, and after a short
time I came up to the prisoner he was stopped by a crowd-I saw a man
there with the shawl, and I said, "Give me that shawl," thinking he was
the man who had taken it-he said, “ All right; I am a police officer"-
be was holding the prisoner at that time.
