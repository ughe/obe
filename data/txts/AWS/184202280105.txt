989. EDWARD HOLT was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of
February, 1 shovel, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Thomas Gardner.
THOMAS GARDNER. On the 22nd of February, I was at work at the
back of the New Prison, at Islington-I lost two shovels and a spade-
this shovel is mine.
ALFRED BEzANT. I am apprentice to a pawnbrokerth shovel was
pawned on the 22nd of February, by the prisoner, in the name of John
Holt.
ROBERT EvANS. Last Tuesday week, I was in the road and saw the
prisoner with the shovel under his arm, and go into the pawnbroker's shop
with it-I knew him before, and worked with him.
Prisoner's Defence. I was ill in bed at the time of the robbery.
GUILTY. Aged 21 --Confined Three Months.
