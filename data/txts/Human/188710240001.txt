 991. JOHN LESLIE WESTON was indicted for unlawfully obtaining, by false pretences, 153 l  . 10 s  . from Alfred Piers Payne .
   MR. KEMP , Q.C., and MR. MONROE Prosecuted ; MR. GEOGHEGAN and MR. HUTTON Defended. ALFRED PIERS PAYNE.
