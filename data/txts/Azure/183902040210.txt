WILSON, Mayor.
MR. PRENDERGAST conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES GRIFFITHS. I am a brewer, and live at Islington. The prisoner
was engaged by me as a collecting clerk, at ll. a week, and IS. for each
new customer he brought—these terms were afterwards changed to 308. a
week—he was to receive the money for the ale and beer delivered to the
customers, and to pay me that money on the same night, when hereturned,
and was to enter in a book what he had received in the dap—I have that
book here, his own hand-writing—on the 15th Of November here is no
sum entered by him as received of Mr. Leach, of Broad-street ; nor did be
on that day pay me 58. as so received; nor has he at any time paid it me
—be entered other sums as received that day, and paid them to me, but not
that—it was his duty to take out, for the purpose of giving receipts to the
customers, a little book, containing blank forms of receipts—be ought
to receive money without giving one of these forms, or a bill with a printed
head—be is not my partner.
Cross-examined by MR. DO ANE. Q. How long had he been in your
vice? A. About three months—he received 51. or 61. or or 101. a day
—there are several entries by him on the 15th of November—I think be
left me on the 24th Of January—I am not aware that any customers left
me when he did—his brother came to me shortly after he left—I think I
found out about this 58. al. out three weeks after he left—his brother did
complain that I had been spreading reports against the prisoner's honesty—
he said, if there was any thing wrong he would make it right—another bro.
ther of the prisoner told me where I could find him—when his brotbe
called on me I knew of one charge, but not of this.
ROBERT LEACH. I am a plumber, and live in Broad-street. On the
15th of November I paid the prisoner five shillings on account of his master,
Mr. Griffiths—I have his receipt here—I saw him write it—I knew it
the custom to give printed receipts—I looked at this receipt when he gave
it me—I forget the words I made use of, but I know the prisoner said, "l
have given you this on a blank piece of paper, as I have not any printd
receipts with me," and he said, I am a partner with Mr. Griffiths."
NOT GUILTY.
