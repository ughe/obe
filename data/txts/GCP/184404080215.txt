1289. ELIZABETH BEST was indicted for stealing 1 parasol, value
88. 9d., the goods of Frederick Tomkins.
FREDERICK TOMKINS, linendraper, Charles-street, Middlesex Hospital.
On the 11th of April, between twelve and one o'clock, the prisoner came and
asked for a gentleman's hat-band-I referred her to Colo in about three
minutes Cole came to me, and stated something-I went round the counter,
and saw a parasol under the prisoner's shawl-I said, “I must detain you,
you have some of my property"-she made no reply- she was standing near
the door-in a minute the parasol slid down on the floor-she said, "Oh dear
me, it has hung to my shawl" I replied, "That is impossible, because you
have got it underneath your shawl " it had stood between five and six yards
from where the prisoner stood,
JOSEPH COLE. I am shopman to Mr. Tomkins. The prisoner came and
asked for a hat-band--I served her-it came to 60.she gave me half-8-
crown-we were proceeding to the other end of the shop to fold up the hat-
band and give her change, and about half way down the shop I observed her
halt-I instantly turned round, and saw her hand on a parasol there were &
great many lying together on a paper on the counter, about half way down.
the shop -- they might project an inch or two, so that a person going by might
brush them if they went near enough to the countershe would not have to
pass the spot close to where they were lying there was a thoroughfare of
about four feet-there was a counter on one side, and rolls of flannel the
other side, and four feet space to walk-I observed her halt and put the.
parasol under her shawl on her left side-I did not make any observation to
her-she continued her walk to the end of the shop --I communicated what I
had seen to Mr. Tomkins--the prisoner had then arrived opposite him-he
went round to her, and said, " I must detain you, you have some of my pro-
perty on your person" -Mr. Tomkins requested me to go after a policeman
-I did so I observed the parasol fall on the floor- I saw her place the shawl
quickly over it-she had not time given her to apply for the purchase of it
she was immediately charged with taking it she was charged with stealing it
about fourteen feet from where it was lying-she stood still a minute with it
under her shawl, to give time to Mr. Tomkins to come round the counter
--she observed him coming, and instantly turned her head the other way he
charged her with having it-he did not say "Have you got it?"
