open it"—it was then battered up—I found this other piece of lead, and Robinson said, "I thought it came from a builder, and I gave 5 s . 10 d . for it"—he went behind me, and I turned round and saw him at a desk in the office—a book was lying on the desk, I cannot say whether this was it—I asked him to come to the station with me, but did not take him in custody—I then told him he would be charged with receiving the lead knowing it to be stolen—he made no, reply—the tank was 34 pounds—Mr. Knott has identified it.
   Cross-examined.
