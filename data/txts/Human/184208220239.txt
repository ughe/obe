 2509. JOHN HUGHES was indicted for stealing, on the   29th of July , 6 knives, value 1 l  .; 6 forks, value 1 l  .; and 1 spoon, value 1 l  .; the goods of William Henderson . HENRY KNAPP. I am groom to Colonel William Henderson, of Ham mersmith. On the 29th of July, I placed these knives and other things en the table in the dining-room—I missed them in about twenty minutes—these now produced are them—they are my master's. GEORGE CHAMBERLAIN. I am a bricklayer, and live in Wells-lane, Hammersmith. On the 29th of July, I saw the prisoner, and three com panions, near the water side—one of them went to Colonel Henderson's, and got in at the window—the prisoner and the other two were outside watching—when the prisoner saw the policeman he whistled—I gave in formation, and he was brought back in a few minutes—the prisoner or one of his companions threw these things down, and I took them up. JAMES HAMON( police-constable T  149.) I took the prisoner, and received the property from Colonel Henderson's servant. MART ANN WEARING. I received this plate from Mr. Chamberlain, and gave it to my fellow servant.
   Prisoner's Defence . I was walking along the shore, and there were three young lads—one of them got in at the window, I believe, but I did not see it—I heard a cry of "Stop thief"—I ran to stop them, and two men stopped me. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months.
 2510. BENJAMIN PEDIGREE was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of August , 1 pair of boots, value 4 s  ., the goods of Charles Wright , from the person of James Wright ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. JAMES WRIGHT. I live with my father Charles Wright, in Essex-street, Hackney. On the 13th of August, I was playing in Bishop Bon ner's field—the prisoner came to me, laid me on my back on a bank, and took my boots off. ALFRED COPE. I am nine years old. I was playing in the field—I saw the prisoner running down the bank with a pair of boots in his hand—I am sure he is the person. JAMES FREDERICK TROVELL. I am a pawnbroker. I have the boots—they were pawned with me by a boy, on the 14th—I cannot say who it was.
