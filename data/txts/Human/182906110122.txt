1273. GEORGE SANSOM was again indicted for stealing, on the 4th of April , 9 legs of pork, value 2l. 17s. 6d., the goods of Joseph Collins.
 JOSEPH COLLINS. I am a  butcher, and live in Watling-street. On the 4th of April I bought nine legs of pork of Mr. Hubbard, but never received one of them: I do not know the prisoner - I never authorised him to receive them.
   DANIEL LEE. I am a servant to Mr. Hubbard, a salesman, of Newgate-market, Mr. Collins purchased nine legs of pork and twenty-seven pieces of meat; two of his men took the meat away; they left the legs of pork in my care, and in five minutes the prisoner came and asked for them for Mr. Collins, of Watling-street; I immediately delivered them to him, supposing he was his servant.
 Prisoner. Q. How came you to let me have them? A. Because you came with a tray across your arm, and asked for them - I had seen you on the Friday, but never before.
 WILLIAM HUBBARD. I am a salesman. I sold Mr. Collins nine legs of pork, among other meat; I never authorised the prisoner to go and fetch them - I never saw him before.
 The prisoner, in a long address to the Court, positively denied ever having had the property.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years.
 1274.  HENRY WILKINSON, JOHN BROWN, and JOHN PERRY , alias    GEORGE SHEEN, were indicted for stealing, on the 4th of June , 3 loaves of bread, value 1s. 3d., the goods of Henry John Baber.
   ANN FRITH BABER. I am the wife of Henry John Baber, baker, of London-wall. On the morning of the 4th of June I was in the shop; the prisoner Wilkinson came in, took three half-quartern loaves, and ran out of the shop with them; the other two prisoners were standing outside - they all three tore them in pieces, divided them, and ran away; I did not see them tear them myself.
   PETER KELLY. I am a constable. On the morning in question I saw three men run round the corner of Circus-place, pursued by the call of Stop thief! I and another officer, ran and took the three prisoners with two loaves and a half - they had each a loaf divided in two, and were eating them very voraciously; I asked why they took so many loaves; they said they were very hungry - they made a deal of resistance; we found nothing on them but a small bag and a spoon.
   ABRAHAM HAM. I was in the Circus at half-past seven o'clock in the morning, and heard the cry; I pursued, and took the prisoners - they were eating some of the bread, and had the rest under their arms.
 WILKINSON'S Defence. I told him not to take the bread from me - I was starving with hunger.
 WILKINSON -    GUILTY. Aged 21.
 BROWN -    GUILTY. Aged 21.
 PERRY -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 The prisoners have all been previously convicted of felony.
 1275.  JOHN ROBERT SPENCER and JAMES COSTON were indicted for stealing, on the 9th of June , 1 1/2 bushels of coals, value 18d., the goods of John Thomas Pocock and another.
   MARK FULTON. I am clerk to Messrs. Pocock and Son, coal-merchants, of St. Bride's-wharf, Whitefriars. On the 9th of June, about twelve o'clock, I saw the prisoners taking coals out of the Joseph barge, which laid on the shore - they reached them out of the barge, and put them into a bag on the side; neither of them were in the barge; they must have pushed one another up to have reached them - there was about a bushel and a half in the two sacks: I have seen them there frequently before - the coals could not have tumbled overboard, for they had not been in the mud, and there was a vacancy in the barge where they were taken; I had seen the coals safe the night before - I saw them both take coals out of the barge; I saw Spencer taking them out of the barge - he reached them by standing on his toes; there were five in all - Spencer was the biggest; I apprehended them directly; I have warned them at least fifty times.
 JAMES YATES. I am a beadle. I was sent for, and found the prisoners by the side of the barge; they took up their bag, moved on the puddle-dock, and I secured them.
 SPENCER'S Defence. I came along the shore all the way from the Temple, and picked the coals up at several places.
 COSTON'S Defence. I say the same.
 SPENCER -    GUILTY. Aged 18.
 COSTON -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined One Month, and Whipped.
 1276.  GEORGE SPINKS was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of May , 8 smelling-bottles, with silver tops, value 3l. 16s., the goods of Apsley Pellatt, his master.
   JOHN ALSUP. I am in the service of Mr. Apsley Pellatt, a glass-manufacturer, in St. Paul's church-yard. The prisoner has been two years and a half there as porter; in consequence of suspicion, on the 7th of May, I secreted myself in a corner of the shop, at eight o'clock in the evening; he had received my orders to lock up the front door, but before he did that I went and hid myself - he locked the door, and went into the glass room, and took these bottles; I went and let myself out at the front door unknown to him- I watched him out at the front, then returned to the shop to see what was gone, and left my fellow-servant to follow him; I followed, but he was out of sight, but in Aldersgate-street we found him in a public-house - I got an officer to take him - he was searched, and 8 smelling-bottles found on him, which are worth 3l. 16s. with the tops -I missed them from the room.
 Prisoner. The firm is Pellatt and Green. Witness, Master has no partner now.
   FREDERICK MOSS. I am servant to Mr. Pellatt. I went out with Alsup, and found the prisoner at a public-house- the property was found on him.
 JOHN  WILLIAM HARRISON. I am an officer. I found the bottles on the prisoner, between his shirt and skin.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I entered the prosecutor's employ with a ten years character, and had I been a depraved character, I certainly could have robbed them to a considerable amount.
     GUILTY. Aged 38. Transported for Seven Years.
