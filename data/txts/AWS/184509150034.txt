2 sovereigns, the monies of Jobn Emblin, his master.
JoHn EMBLIN. I live in Leather-lane. The prisoner was in my service
-n the 25th of Aug. I gave him five sovereigns to purchase goods at
market to that amount-he brought me back goods to the amount of not
quite 3l.-he did not bring me the two sovereigns back-he said he had
spent it, and that was the best account he could give me.
Cross-examined by Mr. HoRRY. Q. I believe he was in the habit of
buying meat for you A. No, it was sheep's heads and plucks-he has
been in my service four years-he has purchased other things for me-he
was to account to me whenever I asked him- settled his wages on Saturday
nights-i was not the usual course of business for him to settle every Satur.
day-he was to settle every day, or when I asked him for the account-i
I did not ask him he would account on Saturday-he received a guines
a-week-] found him drunk, and then I asked what he had done with the
5l.--he pulled out 3s. 4d.-I asked him about the rest-he said he had
spent it, and that was the best account he could give me of it-I gave him
the money on the Monday-I expected him with the goods immediately-
it was towards the stock of the shop-he brought a portion of it on the
Monday, and some on the Tuesday, and he ought to have rendered me the
balance-he was to use his own discretion in the purchase of the goods.
GEoRGE MARRIOTT (police-constable G 31.) I took the prisoner-h

1829. EDWARD ROGER JONES was indicted for stealing 1 coat,
value 2l. 10s. and 1 handkerchief, 3s.; the goods of William Forge.
WILLIAM FoRGe. I am a fish salesman, and live in Rood-lane. Be-
tween ten and eleven o'clock in the morning on the 3rd of Sept., I was in
my first floor room-my daughters called me down-I saw the prisoner
he had been up stairs to my second floor room, which is used as a counting-
house-] did not see him there, but saw him on the stairs- asked what
he wanted-h said he wanted to speak to me-I invited him up stairs to
my second floor room to inquire his business-he said he did not want to
go up again, he had been up, he wanted a situation-I said, 40 If you will
wait a bit I will get you a situation"- sent for the policeman, and in my
own great coat, which the prisoner had got on his back, he found my silk
handkerchief-I had not discovered that he had my coat on-this is my
coat and handkerchief-they had been in the second floor front room, which
was open- had seen them there the day before.
JEREMIAH MAHON (City police-constable, No. 513.) I was sent for,
and took the prisoner-I found this coat on him, and this handkerchief in
the pocket-he said he was out of employ for eight months, and was very
hard up.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged D.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury.-Confined
Three Months.
