FRANK Mabe, for three years an agent of the Music Publishers'
Association. On October 1, 2, and 3 I saw Boken ham and Clarke
come out of the premises, 15, Wellington Road, On October 3
Bokenham brought out a parcel of the shape and size of 500 copies
of music. On October 4 Bokenham brought out two paroels. Clarke
was also in and out. On Ootober 7 and 8 they were there again, and
on the latter date Bokenham brought out a parcel. On October 9
and 11 the same thing occurred. On Ootober 11 Bokenham had
another large parcel. I then went to Moorgate Street Station, and
shortly afterwards Clarke and Bokenham entered it. Bokenham was
carrying the parcel, which he took to the parcel office. From there
he went to the passenger booking office and took two tickets, and
he and Clarke walked down the steps to the platform. I waited about
an hour and three-quarters, and then I saw a man whom I knew to
be associated with dealers in pirated music come up from the plat-
form, go to the parcel office, hand in a ticket, and obtain the parcel
which Bokenham had left. This he put into a sack, and went down to
the platform by the same steps that Clarke and Bokenham had gone
down. On October 16 and 17 I again saw Clarke and Bokenham at
15, Wellington Road, On the latter date Bokenham took a parcel
of about 500 copies to a receiving office in Stoke Newington · Rond.
He came out without the parcel, went across the road, and
waited. In about five minutes Carter Paterson's van drove up to
the receiving office, the parcel was brought out, and put in it, and
Bokenham walked away past Wellington Road in the direction of
the North London Police Court. On October 19 I saw Bokenham
only at the premises. On October 21 and 22. they were both there
On the latter date I saw Inspector Martin meet Bokenham. On
August 7 I was watching Myers' receiving office and saw Walsh
go in and carrying the sack produced about half full and come out
with it empty. Walsh afterwards met Kidd and McCarthy in Chapel
Street, On August 8 I was again watching Myers' office and sa
Walsh coming from the same direction with parcels in a sack, which
he left at the receiving office, He afterwards walked to Chapel Place,
where he met McCarthy and then went into Kidd's house, No. 2,
Chapel Place. On August 19 I went to Brighton, and there wrote
the following letter, which I registered, to "Bob, 50, Liverpool Road,
