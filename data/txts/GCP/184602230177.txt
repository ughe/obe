ANGELINA POPE. I am a widow and live at Peckham Rye-Mary Ann
was in my service-she came on the 20th of Oct.-Fanny is her mother-on
the 12th of Feb. I had a party, and Fanny was there, assisting her daughter
in the kitehen-I had only employed her on that, and on one former occasion,
but I have known her to be there on Sunday evenings, to see her daughter-
these are my articles--I do not know when I lost them I know I had some
of them since Mary Ann has been with me I kept the grocery goods and
candles in a kitchen cupboard--it is usually locked and my daughter keeps
the key and gives out the things-on the 12th of Feb, the cupboard was open
most of the day-Faany was at the house that day-she left at night and the
policeman brought her back.

THOMAS WHITCOMB (police-constable L 89.). On the 3rd Feb. about seven

then got through an empty house, No. 59, Park-street-I fell over a grate in
the yard—I got over to the next house and found the prisoner coming out of
the back door with this grate on bis shoulder, which I produce-I asked what

iron-another officer came up-I secured the prisoner and took the grate to the
