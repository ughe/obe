ALFRED JOSHUA REED (Police Sergeant H 3). About 11 o'clock on the
night of the 28th, I saw this broken window- I was aware the church had
been entered I saw the window again in the morning, and the hole was
much larger, and there were some burnt matches-a few minutes after 4
o'clock I heard the cry of “Stop thief!"-I saw the prisoner and the con-
stable running across Commercial Street, into the market-I followed the
prisoner, and caught him-he was very much exhausted-I said, “What
have you been up to 1"-he said, “Nothing ; I am going home - I said,
“ You must go back along with me, and see what is the matter"--I took him
back into Spitalfield's Market, and there met Cable, who said, “That's him ;
I saw him jump over from the churchyard into Church Street"-he produced
this piece of velvet, and said, “I saw him throw that away"--I took him
to the station, and he gave the address of some coffee-house in the White-
chapel Rond—when I saw him running he was going in a contrary direction
to the Whitechapel Road- I went with Sergeant Saunders and the sexton,
and examined the church.
JOSEPH SAUNDERS (Police Sergeant H 17), I was on duty at 4.15 this
morning in Wood Street-I saw a boy pick up these two velvet covers-I
handed them to Sergeant Reed.
