RIOTT were indicted for stealing, on the 8th of October , 2 shoes, value 3s., the goods of Mary Squires.
   MARY SQUIRES. I keep a shoe-shop, at Chelsea. I lost two pairs of shoes on the 8th of October, while I was out.
   HANNAH INCE. I live with my aunt, the prosecutrix. On the 8th of October the shoes were lost, but I did not know who took them; I was told they were taken.
   JOSEPH WILKINS(Police-constable B 100). Between seven and eight o'clock on the 8th of October, Goose and I were coming up the King's-road, and saw the two prisoners near the prosecutrix's house; we watched them -Marriott came from the door with something, which he showed to Wren; it appeared like the shoes: they all walked away - there were four of them together; we followed them to the other side of Sloane-square, and there I caught hold of Marriott - he sprung into the road, and threw something from him; I pursued, but he got into a court - we afterwards found him; this wire and this purse were found on him - the shoes were found thrown down an area.
   EDWARD WEATHERLEY(Police-constable B 20). I went on the beat at nine o'clock; my brother officer told me that something was thrown away - I went the next morning, and found the shoes down the area.
   ROBERT GOOSE(Police-constable R 55). I was with the other officer, and saw Marriott come from the prosecutrix's door; go to Wren, and show him something - they ran off, and we followed them; I found one shoe in another area - I took Wren, but nothing was found on him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Marriott's Defence. I was running home, and this man called Stop thief! I had not been near the shop.
 WREN -    GUILTY. Aged 15.
 MARRIOTT -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Whipped and Discharged.
 2418.  ROBERT WORLEY and JOHN AUSTIN were indicted for stealing, on the 17th of September , 12 lbs. of soap, value 5s., the goods of Henry Edwards.
   HENRY EDWARDS. I am a grocer, and live in Great St. James'-street. On the 17th of September I lost this soap - I did not see it taken; I heard a cry of Stop thief! and run up stairs - I overtook the prisoners about thirty doors from my shop; one run one way and one the other; one was stopped, and the other was taken the next morning - the soap had been hanging on the door-post.
   WILLIAM HORSFORD(Police-serjeant D 6). I saw the prisoners pass the shop twice - they then went and looked into the window; Worley unhung the soap, and gave it to Austin - they run different ways; I pursued Austin up William-street - when I got near him he threw down the soap; I took him turning into George-street -I said, "I want you for taking the soap;" he said,"I did not take it;" I said, "No, but Worley gave it to you;" I went, and took Worley in bed the next morning; I said, "You know what I want you for;" he said, "No, I do not;" I said, "For taking the soap;" he said he took none - I said, "When you were in company with Austin;" he said, "I was not with him."
 Worley's Defence. I awoke about half-past nine o'clock, and Austin went to get something for supper - I went out, but could not find him; I then went home to bed, and about two o'clock in the morning the officer came and took me; I am quite innocent.
 Austin's Defence. I was going up the street - a man stopped me, and the officer came and took me; he said,"I want you, for having this soap;" I said, "I did not take it, I never saw it."
 WORLEY -    GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
 AUSTIN -    GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Six Months.
 2419.  GEORGE GARDNER was indicted for that he, on the 11th of August , did forge a certain request for the delivery of goods, which said forged request for the delivery of goods was as follows - that is to say:
 GENTLEMEN, - Please to send by bearer, two reams of demy, at 20s., for Messrs. Cunningham and Salmon.
 August 11, 1832. with intent to defraud  John Hodge and others; against the Statute, &c.
 SECOND COUNT, for uttering the same.
 THREE OTHER COUNTS, stating his intent to be to defraud other persons.
 MR. PHILLIPS conducted the prosecution.
   HENRY BLUNT. I am in the employ of Messrs. Hodge and Co., stationer s. The prisoner came to their shop on the 11th of August, and produced this order; I gave him two reams of paper, worth about 36s., and he went away with them.
 COURT. Q. Why did you give him the paper? A. He produced an order, which purported to come from Messrs. Cunningham and Salmon - I believed it to be genuine and good; he said nothing.
   JOHN BROWNING. I am a pawnbroker. The prisoner pawned these two reams of paper with me on the 11th of August.
   MORRIS SALMON. I am one of the firm of Salmon, Cunningham, and Co. The prisoner lived in our employ as porter, and left us in July last - I did not send this order to the prosecutors', nor give any direction; I have no doubt this is forged.
   JOHN CUNNINGHAM. I am in partnership with Mr. Salmon - we are printers. This order is not in my writing; I know nothing of it - there is no other partner.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 There was another indictment against the prisoner.
