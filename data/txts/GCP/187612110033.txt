with Lambert in the Holloway Road, in uniform--a woman spoke to us,
and from what she said I went to the Derby Arms—the Pied Bull is on the
left of the Holloway Road coming from the Archway, and is nearer London
-St. George's Road is on the opposite side of the road, opposite the Derby
Arms-it was then about 12 o'clock, and the Pied Bull was shut up about

here" --some one in the crowd said "You can't take two, you b-
