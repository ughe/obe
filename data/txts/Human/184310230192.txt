 3073. CHARLES BLACK was indicted for embezzling 3 s  . 1 d  ., the monies of his master, James Trunk : also, for obtaining money by false pretences; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Four Months.
 3074. SAMUEL FULLBROOK was indicted for a misdemeanor. PETER HAYNES. I live in Oxford-street, Commercial-road. I am the father of William Haynes, who is committed for the murder of his wife—the Jury sat on her body—the prisoner came to my house, and wished to see the body, because, he said, he was one of the Jury—I let him do so—he then asked me for some vinegar to wash his hands, and asked when I intended to bury it—I said the next day—he said, "Where?"—I said, "At Haggerston church"—he said, "I am the parish clerk"—I said, "Perhaps you will be so kind as to take the warrant for interment, and see to the grave"—he said he would, and said, "There are dues fot the interment"—I said, "Very well, what are they?"—he said, "18 s . 6 d .," which I gave him for the dues—when I began to reflect in the evening, I thought I had been deceived, and waited on the person who I found to be the parish clerk the next morning—I should not have given the prisoner that 18 s . 6 d . if he had not told me he was the clerk—if I had not believed it I should not have given it him—I did not per ceive that he was drunk. SAMUEL ROUSE. I am parish clerk of St. Mary, Haggerston; I have been there four years. The prisoner has never acted as parish clerk while I have been there—he had no authority to take any money for burials or anything.
   Prisoner.  I had been drinking in the morning. GUILTY. Aged 48.— Confined Nine Months .

 3076. WILLIAM COOPER was indicted for unlawfully breaking and entering the shop of William Shrub , with intent to steal. WILLIAM SHRUB( police-constable D  49.) About ten minutes past three o'clock in the morning of the 4th of Oct. I heard a smash of glass—I looked, and saw the prisoner standing with his back to me, and his arm through a pane of glass that was broken in the shop window—he pulled out his arm, and ran off—I pursued—he was secured without ray losing sight of him—I swear his arm was through the window. CHARLES HALL. I am a watchmaker. I left my shop, in the Edgware-road, safe on the 3rd of Oct., fastened with a bar and four shutters—I came the next morning, a shutter was then down, and the glass was broken—there were watches, clocks, and other property in the shop.
