   Prisoner.  I was never in the shop; the female gave me the paper. Witness.  To the best of my judgment she is the woman. CATHERINE MATILDA EDWARDS. I am the daughter of Jabez Edwards. On 4th March, about half-past eight in the evening, the prisoner came for half a quire of note paper and a stick of sealing-wax—they came to 4 d .—she gave me a half-crown—I sounded it and gave it to my father—I saw him put it by the money scale—no one touched it while he went out. HENRY WADLOW( policeman, R  267.) I went to Mr. Edwards's shop, got this half-crown, and took the prisoner—she said she had never been in the shop—I asked her if she had any paper about her, or wax—she said "No; nothing of the kind"—I took her to the station, and found in her apron three packets of paper, some wax, envelopes, tobacco, and other articles—all new. CALEB EDWARD POWELL. I am inspector of coin to the Mint—these half-crown are both counterfeit and have been cast in the same mould. GUILTY . Aged 19.— Confined Six Months
 1156. ELLEN ORMOND , feloniously receiving 4 combs, 1 bottle, and 1 pot of pomatum, value 1 s  .; the goods George King Temple . GEORGE KING TEMPLE. I am a hairdresser, at High-street, Deptford. On 1st March I went with an officer to the prisoner's house—I charged her with receiving goods from a person in my employ—she said we were welcome to search her place—I searched, and found a pair of combs, part of another pair, a bottle, a pot of castle oil, and pomatum—I believe them to be mine—I missed the combs a few days previously—I had not missed the other things. ANN BROWN. I was in Mr. Temple's service—I took these articles from my master's shop and gave to the prisoner—I told her I had got some things she had told me to get—she said she was very glad of them, (she had told me to get them), she was very much obliged to me, but I might as well have got the best combs out of the window—I said, I knew my master knew what he bad got in the window, and I could not get them. JOHN WHITE( policeman, R  180.) I charged the prisoner with receiving these things—she said, "How was I to know they were stolen"—I searched and found the things.
