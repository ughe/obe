was still beating her when I and Thomas went and took her from her

U JOHN WILLIAN BIED. I am a surgeon, and live in Ratcliff-highway.
The prosecutrix was brought to me on the night of Wednesday, the 8th,
to have her face dressed her nose was cut across, at least it was divided
-the bridge was broken, and her lip divided in two places they were
contused wounds, not such as a person would receive by a fall in the
street--there are three distinet wounds--the one across the nose is a
transverse one, the other longitudinal, and the other oblique the cut on
the upper lip was oblique and flat over, so that the force must have been
applied upwards, and there is an excoriation on the nose upwards, which
would not occur in a fall-it was certainly not done by falling on the
ground—this key would produce such appearances it must have been
applied with considerable violence-the wounds correspond with the key,
and the key is stained with blood now. Sugoy bio
