I was in the house at a quarter after eleven o'clock; Mrs. Oldham had been saying she had lost some money and a tea-spoon - I said, "Who have you had in your bar?" she said, "No one but Mr. Brooks;" soon after Mr. Brooks came into the tap-room, and laid down - Mr. Oldham's brother-in-law was there: Mr. Oldham then came in, and said he had lost the money and the spoon; he saw the prisoner there, he rubbed his hand down him, and found these bottles and this spoon on him - I had a very severe struggle with him; he made very great resistance; there were three half-crowns, two shillings, one sixpence, 3s. in copper, and four farthings on him.
 Cross-examined. Q. Had you been there the night before? A. I do not recollect; I had reason to recollect that evening, as I got a brace of very handsome black eyes; I gave him as good as he sent, and a little better: I cannot say whether he was drunk the night before.

 883.  MARY BARRETT was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of March , 1 shirt, value 1s., and 1 frock, value 6d., the goods of John Barker.
 JOHN BARKER. I live in  New Gravel-lane, Shadwell; I deal in earthenware and old clothes. About nine o'clock in the evening of the 16th of March, I was opposite my door, talking to a neighbour, and saw the prisoner come out of my house with these things under her arm - I went into my house to ask if she had been there; they said No - I pursued, and took her, twenty yards from my door: she was in a stooping position - I asked what she had been in my house for; she said she had not, but when she moved, these things fell from her; they are mine, and had been in my warehouse - she was quite a stranger.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 50.
 884.  SARAH BISHOP was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of March , 1 counterpane, value 2s.; 2 handkerchiefs, value 1s., and 2 caps, value 3s., the goods of Henry Hewitt.
   SARAH HEWITT. I am the wife of Henry Hewitt, a shoemaker- we live in Wentworth-street. I have known the prisoner a year and a half or more; she came to lodge with me on the 29th of March, but she had no money, and I refused to let her in - she went away, but was brought in again between ten and eleven o'clock with the property, which is mine; she had been in my house about a quarter of an hour.
   MOSES JACOBS. I live in Wentworth-street. On the 29th of March the prisoner came between ten and eleven o'clock, and wanted me to buy these articles; I said I would not have them - she said she wanted 1s. for them; I said I would not buy them if she wanted but 1d.; she then said she had brought nothing in, but wanted a shift - I called the officer and gave charge of her.
   GEORGE CRAWLEY. I was on duty and took the prisoner; I heard the witness say they did not suit him - he would not have them.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. They were given me by a woman who lodged in the house with me; this wicked woman has been swearing falsely against me - the officer knows I was in such a state I did not know what I was doing; she is the ruin of hundreds - her door is always opened at seven o'clock in the evening, for poor creatures to be brought in to be ruined; one of her favourite lodgers now lies in a cell.
     GUILTY. Aged 36. - Transported for Seven Years.

   JOHN CARVER. I live at No. 5, Years'-court, Carey-street- I let part of the house. On the 9th of April, at six o'clock in the morning, I heard an unusual noise - I got up and found my street door open; I went to the back of my house, looked down the kitchen stairs, but seeing no doors open there, I returned, shut the street door, and went to bed - I got up at seven o'clock, went down stairs, and missed the linen from off the lines, a carpet, and a copper from the place where it had been fixed the night before; I missed four aprons, a shawl, a waistcoat, and the other articles stated - I cannot say how the parties got in; the door had been secure at one o'clock in the morning - this is the property.
   THOMAS THOMPSON. I am a Police-officer. On the 9th of April, about twenty minutes before six o'clock in the morning, I was on duty in Great Queen-street, about three hundred yards from the prosecutor's, and saw the prisoner with something on his arm; I said to my partner,"Come here - here is something wrong;" the prisoner was then in Little Queen-street - I went and said, "What have you got here?" - he put this copper on my head and ran off; I sprung my rattle, and my partner took him - I found this linen in the copper.
