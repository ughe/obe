 1293.  ANN HALL was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of August , a watch, value 2 l.; a watch key, value 3 s.; a seal, value 6 s.; a chain, value 6 d.; a pocket-book, value 6 d., and two handkerchiefs, value 1 s., the goods of Robert Smeaton.
   ROBERT SMEATON. I belong to the Revenue service. On the 30th of August, about eleven o'clock at night, I went into a court in Cable-street, for a necessary purpose, and the prisoner came up the court, and invited me into her room in the same court - another woman came for 2 s. for the use of the room - I stopped till four o'clock next morning. The room door was locked - my watch was under the bolster, and my trowsers and property also. I missed my watch, and accused her of taking it - she denied it. I was perfectly sober.
   FRANCIS JACKSON. I am a constable. On Sunday morning, the 31st of August between four and five o'clock, the watchman brought the prisoner to the watch-house - she had left the house. I went to the room between eleven and twelve o'clock, and found the handkerchief, with carrots and turnips tied up in it. I found the prisoner in bed there, with a man she lives with; she was much intoxicated, and the prosecutor was rather fresh. She said she gave the watch to another girl, or that another girl had it. There is no lock to the door.
 Prisoner's Defence. I met him very drunk; he sent me out for drink, and when I came back he was asleep, and the door open. I awoke him, and he drank most of the liquor. Another woman came up, and drank with him.
   ROBERT SMEATON. There was no liquor in the room. I thought she locked the door.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1294.  JOHN WILLIAMS and WILLIAM HIGGS were indicted, for stealing, on the 26th of August , a waistcoat, value 2 s., and a piece of cloth, value 1 s., the goods of William Crow , from his person.
   WILLIAM CROW. At the time of the robbery, I lived at Ashford, near Staines. On the 26th of August, about eleven o'clock at night, I was coming from Hounslow to Ashford, I was not intoxicated - I had a bundle, containing a waistcoat and an apron. There were four young men on the road in liquor; they snatched my bundle from me; the prisoners are two of them; they snatched the bundle out of my hand. I knew two of them before, but not the prisoners - Page, who I knew, snatched it and gave it to Williams, he delivered it up to me, and Page snatched it from me again; I got the waistcoat back the same night It might be a joke.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1295.  RICHARD LOVELL was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of September , one shilling, one sixpence, and five penny pieces, the monies of Maria Gilham , from her person.
   MARIA GILHAM. I am an unfortunate girl. I lived in Twine-court, Shadwell. Last Friday fortnight, about half-past eight o'clock at night, the prisoner came into my room - I had seen him in the street before - I refused to give him my company - he insisted on it, and put the light out, and pushed me on the bed - while I was on the bed, he accused me of stealing a five shilling piece from him. I denied it - he then said, I had stolen half-a-crown. I said, I had not got one - he turned up the bed, and said he knew where the money was, and took a shilling, a sixpence and five penny pieces from under the bed; he said, it was part of his money. I knew him before: he ill used me, and I charged him with an assault.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1296.  SOPHIA FOX was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of September , a pair of boots, value 6 s., the goods of Joseph Hodges.
   JOSEPH HODGES. I am a shoemaker, and live in Mary-le-bone-lane. On the 11th of September, about half-past six o'clock in the evening, these boots were stolen. I went out at ten minutes after six, and they were safe - I returned and found the prisoner in custody with them.
   JOHN CHUMLEY. I live opposite to Hodges. About half-past six o'clock in the evening, I saw the prisoner go up to the prosecutor's door, and take these goods down, and walk away with them; I ran over and called Mrs. Hodges, who pursued, and took her with them.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   SARAH HODGES. I followed her and took them from her.
     GUILTY. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 1297.  CHARLES FULLER was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of July , three hundred nails, value 18 d., the goods of Mark Cohen.
   MARK COHEN. I am a builder, and live in John-street, Bedford-square. On the 10th of July, about eight o'clock in the morning, I filled a box with six hundred nails, and left them in care of a boy, while I was sent for to breakfast; I returned in half an hour, and missed half of them. The prisoner was employed as a plaisterer at some buildings in the neighbourhood - he was taken four or five days after, and brought to me - my boy is not here.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1298.  ROBERT GOODISON was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of September , five loaves of bread, value 3 s. 6 d., the goods of James Vyse.
   JAMES VYSE. I am a baker. On the 11th of September I sent the prisoner out with sixty-four loaves and a half of bread to serve the customers with. He brought ten and a half home, and booked all the rest to customers, but has not accounted to me for the money.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1299.  RICHARD PHILLIPS was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of September , four books, value 10 s., the goods of William Bowles.
   WILLIAM BOWLES, JUN. I am the son of William Bowles, a bookseller, and live in the New-road, Somers-town. These books were on a board in front of the shop. I saw the prisoner at the board, and in consequence of what I heard, I went and overtook him with four books which are my father's. He pleaded distress.
