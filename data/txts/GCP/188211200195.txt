97. RICHARD RICHARDSON (51), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit
coin.
ELETTI
MESSRS. POLAND and CHARLES MATHEWS Prosecuted.
WILLIAN STEVENS. I keep the Alliance public-house, at Peckham-on
the afternoon of 22nd November the prisoner came in and called for half
a pint of stout and mild-he put down a shilling-I tried it, and found it
was bad-I said to the prisoner "This won't do, this is bad. You must
give me my change back"-he said "I will pay you in half pence; it is
very unfortunate. I had taken a two-shilling piece a fortnight ago that
was bad "-I said "Give me my change back; you must take this to where
you got it"-he gave me sixpence and fourpence-halfpenny, which I had
given him in change, and 15 d. for the stout and mild-there was a lot
more money in his hand-I do not generally give counterfeit coin back-
when I had given it back he wrapped it up in a piece of paper, and put
it in his pocket-my son had observed him, and followed him.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I only tried the shilling with my teeth-
I recognise it.
