Court. Q. At the time you heard and saw the baby coming on the bar,
had the prisoner hold of it? 4. Yes--I saw the pole of its head coming on
the bar, and the head slipped down-I stooped down, and picked the baby up
---the prisoner held it round the hips at the time its bead came against the bar,
as far as I could understand.
MR. PAYNE. Q. How did the head come against the bar ? A. I cannot tell
whether it was his good will or accident, or how-I do not think I saw anything
more than I have said, I do not recollect it if I did—before he left the tap-room
to go to the bar, he said a good many things I could hardly make any meaning
of-1 did not hear him say anything while he stood at the bar, that I can re-
member now-before he left the tap-room he said a good many things I do
not recollect, and some I do recollect-be had his finger on some beer that
was spilt on the table, and was writing figures, or something-I could bardly
understand what-he talked about starvation, or something-be said, "When
you see the jaw-bones moving, it is very queer;" and after that he said blood
on a brick wall looked very funny, and if there was a bullet on a brick wall
there would be blood, and blood on a brick wall looked very funny-as far as
I recollect, that was all this was before the child's head came against the bas
-I think it was better than half an hour after the mother had gone out that
the child's head came against the bar.
Court. Q. What was he doing when he stood at the bar with the child
in his arms, was he doing anything with the child ? 4. No more than taking
care of it, as far as I could expect a man to take care of it-he appeared to be
cuddling it at the time, and I saw him take it suddenly by the thigh-I saw
no change in his position.
Cross-examined by MR. WILKINS. Q. How long had you been in the tap-
room before the wife and mother came in? 4. I think about half an hour-the
child's mother was carrying it-she came in in a great passion, and put it into
bis arms, not steadily-I cannot say she threw it into his lap-she was sitting
down, and put it into his arms-it was asleep when he stood with it at the
bar, it was quite quiet-I had heard it screaming about ten minutes before-it
was in the prisoner's arms at the time the mother was striking him he did
not offer the slightest violence to her all the time--if she got the child back
from him again, I did not see it-the mother-in-law did not strike him-she
went to strike him with a quart pot-I would not allow her-all the blows
the wife gave him were on his face, and as hard as she could hit- I cannot say
whether the old woman, when she came the second time, called the child &
little nasty faggot--I saw her hit him-she abused him, and came in in rather
a bad temper-I am quite sure almost immediately before the child's head
came against the bar he was cuddling it close to his bosom-he cried bitterly
two or three times in the tap-room-he seemed to me to be wandering when in
the tap-room, saying, when a man's jaw was moving it was a queer thing, or
something like that.
