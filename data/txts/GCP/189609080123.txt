Jones and Mears, and saw the prisoner follow us in; we had just reached

policeman came-he knocked two glasses over in trying to get away.

721. GEORGE FREEMAN (61), Breaking and entering the shop of
Lucy Munn, and stealing 73 watches and other goods. Second Count
-Receiving tlie same.
Mr. Lyne Prosecuted, and Mr. PURCELL Defended.
HERBERT SMITH. I live at 74, St. John's Road, Clapham Junction, and
am manager to Mrs. Munn-on July 23rd I found, on going to the shop-
in the morning, that the shutters had been forced, and entrance gained
from the street, and a large number of gold and silver watches, gold
chains and bracelets were missing--we had lost about £500 of goods the
shop was in general confusion -on the previous day I saw the shop was
fastened up and all secure at 2 p.m., because it was the early closing day
-I have been shown eight or ten watches, which I identify; the stock
numbers have been altered. (MR. PURCELJ, said that he did not question
the identity of the watches.)
Cross-examined. We lost seventy-three watches, twenty-eight chains,
forty bracelets, 150 brooches, forty-one links and solitaires, fifty studs,
etc., over 100 articles altogether--since then I have only seen ten of those
articles, and they are all watches.
THOMAS CHOPPIN. I am assistant to Hurland, a pawnbroker, of
Clerkenwell—these two silver watches were pledged with me by & woman,
who gave the name of Green. (Margaret Webb was called into Court.) That-
is the woman.
Cross-examined. One of them was pledged on August 5th, and the
other on July 31st-I advanced 8s. on each.
