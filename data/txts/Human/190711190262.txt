 Sentence, Eighteen months' hard labour. VINCENT  , Albert (30, labourer) . Stealing the sum of £1 10s., the moneys of James Campin , from his person.
 Mr. W. S. M. Knight prosecuted. JAMES CAMPIN, 144, St. Mary's Road, Plaistow. On October 1 I received my Army Reserve pay. About ten o'clock in the morning of October 1 I came out of the Post Office, went across the road to the "Black Lien" public-house and stood outside, when prisoner came up. I had known him to speak to for about two or three years. I asked him to have a drink; we both went into the house and had two glasses of ale, for which I paid. I put 30s. in piece of paper in my waistcoat pocket—a sovereign and half a sovereign. Then we walked down to the "Green Gate," and had another drink. After that we went on a tram to the "Boleyn" tavern. We had one glass there, came out, and walked up Green Street. Prisoner asked me if I had my money all right I said, "Yes." I took it out of my waistcoat pocket, opened the paper, and showed it to him. He took it out of my band and put the 30s. in his pocket; then he put a halfpenny and a farthing into the paper and gave it to me. I took it, and said, "Thin won't do; I want my money." He said, "I have not got it." I asked him for it several times, and he said he had not got it. We got up to the "Queen's" public-house, and be said, "Come and have a drink." He went in, and I followed him. He had a glass of beer and offered me one. I refused. We then cams out, and I asked him for the money again. He would not give it to me, so I knocked him down and tried to get it that way; but his friends got round and got him away—three men named Hill, Stowe, and Ait. Prisoner went round some back turnings and I chased him, but lost him round by the Broadway, Plaistow. I gave information to the police, and the next time I saw prisoner was at Barking Road Police Station about a month after; he was amongst about ten other men and I picked him out.
 Cross-examined. I was not drunk. I had two drinks in the first public-court and one in each of the others. I gave information to Detective-sergeant Stephens about it and gave him prisoner's name.
 Detective Sergeant STEPHENS, K Division, stationed at Plaistow. Prosecutor came to me some days after the offence took place. I made inquiries, and arrested prisoner on October 30.
