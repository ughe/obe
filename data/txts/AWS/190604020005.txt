1906.
CENTRAL ORIMINAL COURT
MORGAN, MAYOR.
SIXTH SESSION.
LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES.
OLD COURT; Monday, April 2.
(Before Mr. Recorder.)
OLACK, William (55, labourer), pleaded guilty at last
Sessions (see p. 5) to feloniously marrying Margaret Sybilla
Woodward, his wife being then alive. On stating that he in-
tended to marry Woodward, his wife being now dead, he was
then released on his own recognisances in 2100 to appear at the
present Sessions. He had now married Woodward. Sentence,
one day's imprisonment
Prisoner was accordingly discharged,
RICHARDSON, William (48, rubber worker), pleaded guilty
at last Sessions (see p. 11) to feloniously marrying Paulette
Mary Boyoe, his wife being then alive. Sentence, four weeks'
imprisonment.
Prisoner was accordingly discharged.
SCHANDEL, O1to (27, waiter), pleaded guilty to feloniously
having in his possession a chain, the goods of Francis Hackel,
which had been stolen outside the United Kingdom, knowing
the same to have been stolen. The police stated he had been
discharged by his employers as lazy and impertinent. Six
months' hard labour. Certified for expulsion under Aliens Act.
EYRES, Robert (39, mail van driver), having charge of a
mail van did by (1) wanton (2) and furious driving thereof cause
certain bodily harm to Walter John Plowman
Mr. Torr prosecuted.
WATER JOHN PLOWMAN a fireman in the employ of the Lon-
don County Couneil. On Saturday, October 14, about 7.30 a.m.y
I was up a ladder at the Redeross Street fire station. The
ladder was upset, I came to the ground, and was taken to St.
Bartholomew's Hospital where I remained for two months. I
am still unable to walk. Prisoner came to see me in the hos.
pital and expressed regret at the accident.
