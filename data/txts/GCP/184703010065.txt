725. CHARLES PHILLIPS was again indieted for embezzling 58., the
money of George Whittle, his master.
MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution
Henry HEMMING. I am landlord of the Queen's Arms, Holborn-I
owed Mr. Whittle 13s., which I paid to the prisoner for his master on 4th
February
Cross-examined. Q. What was it for? 4. To advertise in two news-
papers, 88. for one paper and 58. for the other-I do not know what papers
they were to be in-the advertisement was about letting a house, and was to
be put in when he thought fit-the 5s. advertisement was to be delayed for a
few days.
COURT. Q. Did you know that after you gave him the money or before?
A. When I gave it him I said I thought one advertisement should be a few
days after the other, but left it to his master.
George WUITTLE. The prisoner was in my service. On 4th Feb. I sent
him to Mr. Hemming to receive 138.-he came back, and said he had only
received 8s., which he paid me, and I entered it in my cashbook- he did not
pay me 138.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you send him to get 13s.? A. Yes-for the
purpose of advertising I had stopped two sums of 58. out of his wages, for two
sums which he bad robbed me of, and I had forgiven him, but this 58. was
not one of them-he promised it should not occur again-one advertisement
was to be in a Sunday and one in a daily paper.
MR. DONE. Q. Did you receive 8s. from him or 13s.? A. Eight-I
never got the 58.
