ciously damaging by night a plate glass window, the goods of John

monds with intent to do her some grievous bodily harm.
Prisoner confessed to having been on September 27, 1910, con
victed at London Sessions and sentenced to 18 months for purse
snatching. Other convictions were proved.

concurrently.
