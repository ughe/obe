Before Mr. Recorder and the Fourth Jury.
618. EMILY BARTRUM (23), and JOHN MACKLIN (27), Robbery
upon Charles Walters, and stealing from his person 1 watch, value 2l., his
property.
Mr. Best conducted the Prosecution..
CHARLES WA/TERS. I am a warehouseman, and live in St. Helen's-place
-n Saturday evening, 21st May, I was in St. Mary Axe, between 10 and
11 o'clock, on my road home-the prisoner Bartrum came up and spoke to
me; she said she thought she knew me, and had seen me before-] told her
she WaS mistaken-she continued talking to me, and said she knew some-
body very much like me-she followed me to near Great St. Helen's-it was
there a narrow part, and I did not like to go through that part with her-
while we were there the constable passed, and he had hardly got out of sight
when she made a grab at my wateh--I seized her hand with the watch in it
-she made a hem, and immediately I was seized by another man, not the
prisoner Macklin-he got his knee in my back and tried to get me down,
and used very vile language-I called the police, and another woman came
and held my wrist back, and Bartrum got the wateh off the swivel and ran
away-the man who had hold of me tried to stop my mouth-I struck himn
and he let me go-I lost Bartrum, and Macklin was standing by-Bartrum
ran by him, and apparently put something in his hand, and he put his hand
in his pocket-the policeman came and took Bartrum and I seized Macklin,
and said I thought he had got my wateh-he said directly, 46 That woman is
my wife"-the other two that had had hold of me came up, and one seized
Bartrum's arm and pulled her away from the policeman-sh ran towards
the square, and there is no getting ont there-I seized Macklin, and the
policeman came and took him-Bartrum was taken by another person-
am sure the prisoners are the persons-wher we got to the station they both
said they had not seen me.
