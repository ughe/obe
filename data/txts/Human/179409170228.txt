 559.  JOHN BROWN was indicted for feloniously stealing, on the 2d of August a wooden hogshead, value 2s. the goods of Nicholas Gainsford.
   MICHAEL WEBBER sworn.
 I am a porter. On the 2d of August, I stood at the end of  Fleet-market, to ply for labour, and I saw this man come across the pavement and go up where two hogsheads stood and took one out of the two; there were two stood at the door of Mr. Gainsford, they were standing nearly to the curb, a little on the curb, and the rest off, and this man, Godden, came out and asked if any body see a man take an hogshead away? and I said yes, there is the man going along the street with it now. Accordingly he followed him, and took him with the hogshead, just up at the top of Snow-hill, he was rolling of it up, rather bearing to Smithfield, to the turn, when he took him.
 Q. What time of the day was it? - Between four and five in the afternoon.
   JOHN GODDEN sworn.
 I am servant to Mr.  Nicholas Gainsford, he is a cheesemonger, he has no partner, he lives on Holborn-bridge; I missed the cask between four and five in the afternoon; I had two standing at the door, and there was a person came in and gave information that a man had gone off with one; I went out into the street and enquired which way he was gone; and I was informed, by this man and many others, that he had gone up Snow-hill with it. I followed him and asked him who gave him authority to take it away? he said a man employed him to wheel it away for three-pence; I asked him if he knew the man? he said, no; I asked him if he had no objection to wheel it back again? he said, he should not; and then I took him by the collar and brought him back again, and had him taken to Guildhall, where he was convicted.
     GUILTY. (Aged 22.) Imprisoned fourteen days in Newgate and fined 1s.
 Tried by the London Jury before Mr. COMMON SERJEANT.
