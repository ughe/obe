HENRY WAINWRIGHT. I am landlord of 563, Caledonian Road-in
April, 1889, a man took the house, who gave his name George Smith, at
£42 a year, and gave as a reference Mr. Firth, a solicitor, of Waterloo
Road-I do not think he took possession, but he took the key, and let
the house out in lodgings, and came there weekly for the rent-I took
forcible possession six weeks afterwards, and have never seen him since
-I do not know the prisoner.
Cross-eramined. I find I have seen the prisoner before, but he only
had a moustache then, now he has a beard-Smith introduced a fair man,
who he said was bis brother, who wished to take the next house-I wrote
to Mr. Firth, but did not see him.
