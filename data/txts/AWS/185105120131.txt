CATHARINS CHURCH. I am a widow. I carry on the business of a
painter and glazier, at Blackheath-the prisoner lived five years in my serw
vice-his wages were 188. a week-he was in my service up to the 29th
April-from time to time things were missed out of my shop-in consequence
of something that oceurted I took a policeman to the prisoner's house on
29th April, about eight o'clock in the evening, and gave him in chargehe
was not employed by me to paint the house next to where be lived-he had
been working for me that day, and had left about five-the policeman searched
his house, and produced these articles--I believe these suckers are mine-
this hose belongs to my pump-I had not missed it--I forget when I hed
seen it last-there is no mark on it. but my son-in-law put it into the pump.
and found it exactly fitted-I can speak to this ghue-potI was looking for
it for some time-I had asked the prisoner to look for it for me, and he told
me he could not find it-1 thought that a person who worked in my employ
had borrowed it, and told the prisoner to go there and look for ithe went,
and said he could not get it-the prisoner had not the smallest authority from
me to take any of my things to his house.
Cross-eramined. Q. He came into vour employ at 108. a week A. Yes
he then got to 128., then to 148. or 168., and then to 188.--I believe he is
recently married-he bas been treated with great confidence-he sometimes
had the management of my business- had no one else to depend on-the
articles I am able to speak to are worth 258. or 268.-I could not give much
for them in my cireumstances-I do not know the value of this glue-pot-I
