John OSBALDISTON. I am a clerk in the London and County Bank-
the prosecutor opened an account with our branch in Hanover Square on
2nd July last-he gave his address 93, Regent Street ; no other that I am
aware of-possibly he might, but we should only use that one-on 3rd
October he had not 1501. to his credit; nor had he quite that at any time
in October.
Cross-examined by MR. SERJEANT SLEIGH. He had transferred his account
to us from Cox & Greenwood--on several days in October he had ample
to meet a cheque for 801.---we had no securities of his in October—we had
some afterwards.
Prisoner. Q. Did you hear him swear positively that his cheque for
1501. would have been honoured ? A. I understood him that arrangements
might have been made, that he would provide for it.
GUILTY.-Six Months' Imprisonment.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, 9TH JANUARY, 1871.
