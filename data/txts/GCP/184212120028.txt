298. RICHARD MAHONEY was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of
· Dec., 1 yard of canvass, value 4d. ; yard of calico, 4d.; 14 yard of woollen
cloth, il. ; 24 buttons, 1s.; and 6 yards of silk cord, 6d., the goods of Ben-
jamin Prew, his master.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
BENJAMIN PREw. I am an outfitter and tailor, and live in Aldgate, High-
street-the prisoner was in my employ for about two months as cutter, at
30s. a-week and his keep I have missed articles from time to time-
Tuesday evening, the 6th of Dec., while the prisoner was gone to tea, at
eight o'clock, I examined his great coat-pocket, which hung in the cutting.
room-there was nothing in it then, and at nine, when he went in to supper
I went into the room again, his coat laid on the table, I found a piece of
canvass and other articles in a sort of private pocket at the back of his coat
--when he came from supper he put his coat on as usual, and was about
leaving- I said, “You have something which does not belong to you" he said,
" Why do you think so ?"-I said, "I am satisfied of it," and called a policeman
in, who found these articles on him-he said he hoped I would forgive him,
he had only got a coat ready cut out for himself-the policeman found on him
a coat ready cut out, with buttons, trimming, and everything.
