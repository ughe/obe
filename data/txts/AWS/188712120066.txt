JAMES HENDERSON. I am a journalist, of 45, Olarenee Road. Kentish
Town-on lst December, about 2.80 p.m., I Was at the corner of Olarence
Road coming from an omnibus and was making my way to my lodgings-
had a top-coat on, which was partially buttoned, and an umbrella in one
hand and a bag in the other-I saw a great erowd and I was huatled and
knocked about by the erowd, and I found myself in the middle of the
street with my top-eoat torn open and my hat knoeke over my eyes-a
hand then snatehed at my gold albert chain, but did not sueceed in
removing it-I was hustled again and tripped up, and then a hand
foreibly got hold of my chain and tore it away-Smalley tripped me up-
the other two prisoners were there then, but they did not do anything to
me at that time-the party who snatehed at my chain is not to be found
-other snatches were also made, but I could not say whether it was by
the same party-I would identify the party who took my chain but he
has disappeared-before he disappeared he was in momentary contact
with Whales and Smalley, he went up to them and Whales I believe took
the chain-Whales also made a snateh at my wateh-there was a very
large crowd moving up the street, and as they moved I followed, ealling
out Police! and a man, who afterwards turned out to be a police
officer in plain elothes, asked me if I had lost anything, and he followed
up the crowd-I could not point out the individuals, but I afterwards
saw Whales and Smalley in the eompany of the officers-I never lost
sight of them from the time this oceurrenee took place--I was not much
hurt, but exceedingly knoeked about-I was only down on the ground
once, but nearly twice- this (produced) is my chain-I next saw it upon
a desk at the police-station, before which the prisoners were.
Cross-eramined by Bradford. I first saw you a little way from where
the assanlt took place, and you were one of the persons who hustled me
and prevented me from following-you did not knoek me down, you
pushed me, I believe you did it intentionally-I did not see you in the
erowd before the chain was snatehed, but afterwards did.
Cross-ecamined by Mr. KEITH FRITH. I see well enough, but I use
spectacles at night and during the day-I had not got spectacles on at
this time, I do not wear them in the street-this was all over in a few
minutes-] am not near-sighted, but I wear spectaeles at night when
reading documents-I did not see the process of arresting-this was on
