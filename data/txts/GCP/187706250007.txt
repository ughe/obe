518. WALTER WARNER (24), Unlawfully having in his possession
eight counterfeit sbillings, with intent to utter them.
MESSRS. CRAUPURD and E. LLOYD conducted the Prosecution; and Mr. W.
WRIGHT the Defence.
WILLIAM WALKER. I keep a refreshment house at 63, Broad Street,
Bloomsbury-on 28th May the prisoner bought a penny pipe, for which he
tendered a bad shilling to my wife-he said he got it in Holborn-I gave
him into custody.
TV
Edmund Brown (Policeman 169 E). I took the prisoner into custody
he said he had the bad shilling from a house in the Seven Dials-the
prisoner dropped a packet on the way to the station, the prosecutor's son
picked it up-it contained eight counterfeit shillings, with paper between-
I found 86. 3d. on the prisoner, 11d. in bronze.
Cross-examined. There was no crowd-it took place in Broad Street.
WILLIAM WALKER. I am the son of the first witness the prisoner came
to our house on 28th May and passed a bad shilling-I followed him-near
Brownlow Street he dropped a packet-I picked it up and gave it to the
constable.
Cross-examined. There was no crowd--the prisoner is a stranger to me.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These coins are bad-four are of the same mould
as the one tendered.
The Prisoner received a good character. Guitry-Judgment respited.
