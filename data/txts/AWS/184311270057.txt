68. JANE FLACK was indicted for stealing 1 pair of ear-rings, the goods
of John Frimley.
MARY FRIMLEY. My husband, John Frimley, keeps the Rainbow public-
house, Liverpool-road, Islington. The prisoner was a week in our service-
I had no character with herwon the 23rd of Nov. I missed these ear-rings
from a looking-glass drawer, which was not locked- had seen them safe the
evening before-next morning I asked if she knew anything of them-ghe
denied having them-she was dressing, and I found them in her pocket-
these are them.
