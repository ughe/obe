526. ELIZABETH HOLMES was indicted for stealing, on the 6th a
Jarruary, 9 lbs. weight of pork, value 58., the goods of William Guustoo.
HARRIET AUSTIN. I know the shop of William Gunston, he is a cheest
monger. On the 6th of January I was in the shop, in Exmouth-street
and saw the prisoner take a hand of pork from inside the door-I told
Mr. Gunston's young man of it-he went and fetched her back, and, on
opening her cloak, found she had also a loin, which she took from the shop
as well.
CRANLEY BRITNELL. I am in the service of Mr. William Gunston.
The young woman told me of this, and I took the prisoner three or four
doors from the shop with this pork, which is my master's.
