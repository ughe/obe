 2581. WILLIAM KELLARD was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of August , 4lbs. weight of nails, value 1 s  ., the goods of John Scovell and others, his masters. ROBERT BINES. I am shipping clerk in the service of John Scovell and Sons, at their wharf, in Tooley-street; the prisoner had been a cooper in their employ for twelve years. On the 21st of July, about four o'clock in the afternoon, I desired him to go into the counting-house, and told him to take off his hat, which he did—I found these nails in it—there are 4lbs.—they had come from the cooperage, which the prisoner had possession of—he had no authority whatever to take them away—they were precisely the sort of nails that we use in coopering tallow-casks, which was his occupation—he was going off the premises. WILLIAM THRUSSELL( police-constable M  148.) I was called to the pro secutor's premises, and received the prisoner in charge, and these nails.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I went into one of the warehouses, and saw them loose on the head of a cask; I threw them into my hat, and the witness called me; I put my hat on my head inadvertently, and he asked what I had in my hat; I showed him them; I was going to put them where I generally keep them. NOT GUILTY.
 2582. JAMES JENNINGS was indicted for unlawfully having in his possession counterfeit coin; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Six Months .
 2583. THOMAS RIPPON was indicted for unlawfully uttering counter feit coin.
   MESSRS. CHAMBERS and SCRIVEN conducted the Prosecution. DAVID DE LANEY. I am a cheesemonger. The prisoner came to my shop on the 12th of August for a quarter of a pound of cheese—he gave me a shilling—I found it was bad—I asked him where he got it—he said he was not aware it was bad—I gave him and the shilling to the officer. JOHN CONSTABLE( police-constable M  43.) I took the prisoner—as I was taking him to the station, there is an open ditch near to the shop—he put his hand to his mouth, and threw a quantity of coin into the ditch—two shillings rested on the side of the ditch—I kept my eye on them till I saw them got by the street-keeper—these are them, and this is the one I got from Mr. De Laney. MR. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to the Mint. These shillings are all counterfeit, and cast in the same mould. GUILTY.* Aged 19.— Confined Twelve Months .
