Eliza LANGRIDGE. I am the wife of James Langridge-we keep the
George public-bouse. On 5th Nov. the prisoner came for three halfpenny-
worth of gin, and a pipe of tobacco-I served him-he gave me a half-crown
- I looked at it, and suspected it was bad- I called my husband, and gave

my husband asked if he had any money to pay for what he had had-he

change-my husband kept the two half-crowns in his hand, and gave them to
the policeman-as soon as I saw the second half-crown, I said it was bad,
