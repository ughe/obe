WILLIam BOLTON. I live at 84, Coburg Buildings, Westminster-on
the night of Friday, May 30, 1884, I was gatekeeper at the Junior Carlton
Club, Pall Mall; the back gate is in St. James's Square-I was sitting
in a glass box abutting on the area-about a quarter past 9 o'clock p.m.
a very violent explosion took place which injured four or five of the club
female servants (they were sent off to the hospital) and threw me off my
stool; I was partially deaf, I have not recovered since-Elizabeth
Horsfall who was there at the time, injured her arm-considerable
damage was done to the building.
