   COURT . Q.  When you gave the information to the Grand Jury about the hole, did they show you the coat? A.  No, I had seen it at the office, and had it at my house—I saw the hole while he was talking to me—I was not asked about it before the Magistrate—I was asked to describe the man and his dress. WILLIAM CUMMING( police-sergeant D  3.) I took the prisoner into custody on the 1st of March, and saw this coat fitted on him—it fitted exactly.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Are you a good judge of the fit of coats? A.  I can form an idea—it fitted him as far as I could see—I did not observe whether it was tight or loose—I considered it a good fit—it was not but-toned—I should say it seemed to have been made for him—it is a frock coat—I went to Mrs. Robins's—I believe it was before she was examined at the office—I have a doubt of it—I believe it was after she went to the office—I went there to tell here what time to be there the following day—I do not think she was examined more than once—she came to the office, and the prisoner was remanded on one charge, to be brought up on another, and she was not examined—I went to her before she was examined—she bad been told the prisoner was in custody before that—I sent a man for the clothes—the Magistrate did not tell me to be quiet, and not prompt the witnesses, nor any thing of the kind—nor to go away from any wit ness—I took the prisoner in Great Marylebone-street.
