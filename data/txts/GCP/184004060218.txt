Mr. BABLANTINE. Q. Were you compelled to go before the Magistrate,
or did you give evidence voluntarily? A. I saw it in the paper, and I
went up to Hatton-garden the same day, but there was no Court then-it
was not the same day I saw the account of the murder, but the same day
I saw my name in the paper that was on Monday, and I went up the
same day, but the examination was not on it took place the next day, and
I attended and gave evidence-I stated all I knew I have been on
friendly terms with the prisoner, but I have not seen him above twice for
two years.
Court. Q. When you went to be examined you knew Johnson had
been examined ? A. I was told he had been the week previous, but I did
not see Johnson till after I had been examined-I think I saw his ex-
amination in the newspaper before I was examined, but I will not be
positive.
