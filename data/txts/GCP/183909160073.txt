0519. MARY BROWN was indicted for a misdemeanor.
Susan FRANCES LITTLE. I am the wife of Robert Little, and keep a
Shaker's shop in Holywell-lane. On the 7th of September, about one
o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoner came and asked for a penny loaf-
she gave me a bad shilling-I asked where she got it--she said she took
it of a man in Shoreditch, and if I would give it her she would get it
changed-she said, “What is the matter with it?"-I sent my son for a
policeman-I marked the shilling, and gave it to him.
HENRY KIDNEY (police-sergeant G 6.) I took the prisoner, and re-
ceived this shilling-she was taken before the Magistrate, and discharged
about six o'clock in the evening-she went by the name of Sarah Smith.
ELIZABETH Brown. My son keeps a butcher's shop, in Union-street.
On the 13th of September, the prisoner came for a lb. of beef, and
gave half-a-crown-I showed it to Symons-it was bad—he went after
her and could not find her she came again about a quarter-past nine
o'clock, and asked for 1 lb. of pieces-Symons knew her, and she was
detained- I gave the half-crown to Symons-be gave it me back, and I
gave it to the policeman.
WILLIAM JAMES SYMONS. I saw the prisoner come first at eight
o'clock in the evening-I saw the half-crown was bad- I went after her,
| but could not find her- gave the same half-crown back to my mistress-
the prisoner came again about a quarter past nine o'clock-I am sure she
is the person who came at eight o'clock--I had her taken.
ROBERT M'GOVERN (police-constable H 53.) I received the half-crown
from Mrs. Brown- I took the prisoner to the station-house-she had 33d.
in copper on her.
