WILLIAM MILLAN (police-constable B 95). Soon after the door was opened
I saw the prisoner with a spoon in her hand I saw her take it off the fire I
had before that seen her put half-a-crown into it.
Court. Q. Then you saw the spoon first on the fire ? A. Yes-she had
put the balf-crown into it which she took off the shelf-she then took the spoon
off the fire and put it on the hearth-Inspector Penny took it up, and took the
balf-crown out, all in a fluid state-one end of it was melted--the metal in
the spoon was in a fluid state I produce a piece of a counterfeit shilling,
which I found on the hearth-there was a piece of carpet near the fire all
over melted metal-the prisoner was taken to the station - I went there after-
wards-next morning she called me, and said she bad swallowed a good half-
crown while we were breaking the door open and that she was very ill-she
appeared very ill, and was sent to the hospital.
Cross-examined. Q. What did she say when you entered the room?
4. If we had been half an hour sooner, we should have got her to rights, she
