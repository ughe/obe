The prisoner, in a very long address, entered into an explanation of the
various transactions alleged against him, which at the outset amounted to a
charge of misappropriating some thousands, but which had dwindled down to
the amount of 471., received as fees at Regent Street, and which he alleged was
received by him rightfully, ho having continued to carry on that business after
it hail been given up by the bank, who had not sustained any loss whatever upon
any of the transactions in question.
