JOSEPH THOMPSON (policeman.) Os 5th May I took the prisoner in
charge, in Stafford-street, Lisson-grové-I held the two bills produced, in
my hand, and asked her where she got them, as I thought they were not
all right; she said from Mrs. Douglas- I asked her how she got them;
she said, she received them by post from Watford-I asked her wbere Mrs.
Douglas lived; she said, 34, Charles-street, Berkeley-square; if I looked in
the Directory I should find it there-she said Mrs. Douglas was her sister,
and the wife of Admiral Douglas, who was a very old man, and she always trans-
acted his business for him-I searched her room and found these papers marked
E. and F., in a writing case, and also a letter when I found the paper marked
E, I asked her whose writing it was, she said it was bers—I said, it is a bill
of acceptance; she said, "Yes, I was merely scribbling at the time I wrote
it"-I found this paper at the house written in pencil, and a letter, not
opened, directed to Coutts and Co.-I asked her who wrote that; she said
she bad, and that she was about to send it to Coutts'.

Mrs. CATHARINE ANN DOUGLAS. I am the wife of Admiral J. E.
Douglas-he is advanced in life, and in infirm health–I do not live at
Watford, but near Bushey-we have a house, 34, Charles-street, Berkeley.
square, but have not occupied it for four or five years—the prisoner is my
sister-I have an account at Coutts' banking-house in my own name-I am in
the habit of drawing on the house myself-neither of the two bills produced
are my writing, nor written by my authority or sanction—this note is not my
writing- I had rather not say whose it is believe it to be the prisoner's.
Court. Q. How long is it since you have had communication with the
prisoner? 4. I have not seen her since 1830- it was not at her marriage
that we ceased to have communications--I do not know her husband he
lives in the West Indies—she has ehildren by him, who live with him
I do not know how long she has ceased to live with him-she came
home from Jamaica in ill-health-she has applied to me for help-(the
bills were here read as follows:-"Watford, 4th May. Fifteen days after
