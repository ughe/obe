2388. ELIZABETH ATTFIELD was again indicted for stealing, on
the 2nd of October, 1 pencil-case, value 1s., the goods of William Nathaniel
Wortley, her master.
NOT
WILLIAM NATHANIEL WORTLEY. I lost a pencil-case about a fortnight
previous to the 16th of October I did not speak to the prisoner about it
--the officer found it in a little black bag which she used to take to church
with her bible-I lost it from my bed-room-it might have been in my
pocket, or on the floor.
Jous Hull. I am an officer. I found the pencil in the prisoner's
bag with her bible--she said she picked it up, and forgot to put it back,
Prisoner. I picked it up as I was going to church, and it was too late
to put it back.
