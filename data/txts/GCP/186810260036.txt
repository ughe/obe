ANN LOWMAN. I live at 11, Buck's Row-on Saturday, 19th Sep-

to mind a bundle for half an hour--we did not know what was in it-the
prisoner did not get up, but said to his wife," Jane, take them"- she took

them in the bottom drawer-I gave evidence before the Magistrate - I did
not know the young man, but I thought he looked very deplorable-I had
A pot at my side, and I gave him a drop-I am not a charwoman-I had
been helping to clean the house.
