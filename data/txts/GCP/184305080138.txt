1600. JOHN MORRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of May, 1
pair of boots, value 21s., the goods of William Davies.
John LECKIE. I am shopman to Mr. William Davies, a boot-maker, in
Lamb's Conduit-street. On the 6th of May I saw the prisoner stooping
before the counter-the boots were in front of the counter, on the floor--he
ran away immediately-I ran after him-he was stopped, and begged me to
forgive him, and let him go-I returned, and found these boots about halla
yard from where they were before-some other boots had been knocked down
-none were taken away.
BENJAMIN GUEST. I live in Little St. Andrew's-street. I saw the pri-
soner go into Mr. Davis's shop-he stooped down, and took a pair of boots,
came out of the shop with them, and threw them down when he got about two
yards from the shop door-I saw a lad pick them up, and take them into the
shop, and put them there-Leckie ran after the prisoner.
John NUTTALL (police-constable E 92.) I saw the prisoner running in
Devonshire-street, Queen-square-Leckie was following- I took him back to
the shop-he begged me to let him go. P

1601. ROBERT WATTS was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of April,
1 brush, value 2s., the goods of Henry Goddard Watling.
EDWARD WRIGHT. I am in the service of Mr. Henry Goddard Watling, a
butcher, in Marchmont-street. This brush is his—it was safe in the manger
in the stable, in Compton-mews, on the 28th of April-I left the prisoner and
two boys in the stable-I received information from the boys- I went after
the prisoner, and found him in Harrison-street- I called to him three times,
and said, “You have got my brush”-he said, "I have not got it"-I said
he should either come back to my master's, or I would give him in charge-he
was coming back, and a woman said, “Your brush is over the wall" - I got
on the walì, and could not see it--the prisoner said, " There it is."
WILLIAM CHARLES BRANCH. I was in the stable with another boy,
sweeping the dung, and I saw the horse-brush in a little manger-the prisoner
went out, and then I missed it-I told Wright of it
Prisoner. Q. Did not another man come in to fetch an oil-can? 4. No
--he stopped in the other stable while Wright came in.
Prisoner's Defence. The woman said it was over the wall; I got up, saw
it, and said, "There it is;" another man who had been in the stable was
standing at the corner at the time.
Nor Guilty.
