 201.  SAMUEL BUSHELL was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of November , 1 coral-drop, value 7s., the goods of John Garritt.
   JOHN GARRITT. I live in Ray-street, Clerkenwell, and am a lapidary- the prisoner was my servant. On the 20th of November I had a large sized coral-drop in a box in my shop; I discharged the prisoner, and then missed it; this is it.
 Prisoner. Q. Are you not in the habit of looking your coral over almost every day? A. Just as my customers may require it; I know this piece by a hole, about half an inch from the top of it, where a branch has been broken off; there are frequently holes, but not like this; I know it from its being an odd drop - I had tried to match it, and could not; it is the largest piece that was left in the box; it weighs about 4 dwts. 16 grains.
   WILLIAM WHITELOCK. I am a working-jeweller, and live in Northampton-street. I bought this coral-drop of the prisoner for 3s. 6d.; I asked him if he worked for Mr. Garritt; he said No - (he had before brought me a pair of drops;) I sent to Mr. Garritt to know if he worked for him, and he said he did; he then brought me another pair of drops, and I sent to ask Mr. Garritt if he had sold him any drops - he said he had sold him three pairs; I bought three pairs, and this odd one of the prisoner; on the Monday Mr. Garritt called, and I told him I had bought this drop of the prisoner - he said it was his, and one he had been looking for.
   ANDREW LLOYD. I am an officer of Hatton-garden. I apprehended the prisoner; he wanted to know what I charged him with - I said, "With taking a coral-drop;" he said he had sold three pairs to Mr. Whitelock, but he knew nothing about the odd one.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that he was in the habit of purchasing goods of the prosecutor, and selling them on his own account.
   JOHN GARRITT. I believe the prisoner had one pair of me some months ago, and three pairs lately, but I never sold him this; I am quite positive it is mine.
 GUILTY. Aged 48. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined One Month.
