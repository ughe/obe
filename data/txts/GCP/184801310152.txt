CHARLES HENSHAW. I am a labourer, in the employ of Mr. Hicks, a
builder. I locked up bis place on 30th Dec., and had the key-I found the
place broken open in the morning, and all the things gone.
Joun CROUCHER, I am a carpenter, and work for Mr. Hicks. I left work
on 30th Dec., and left my jacket and cap safe they were gone at six o'clock
next morning-these are them-I saw the prisoner at the Police-court with
my cap on.
John Perett. I left an apron in the shop-I lost it-this is it.
CHARLES BURGESS Gorr (police-sergeant, L 8.) On 5th Jan., the pri-
soner came to me at Tower-street station-he had a jacket and apron on which
answered the description of those stolen-he said he bought them in Petti-
coat-lane, about a week before.
Prisoner's Defence. I was waiting near my father's; a young man asked
me if I wanted work; I said, "Yes ;" be led me to Petticoat-lane ; ve
went and had a pint of beer; he went out and brought in these things; he
said, “You put them on, they will make you look fit for work;" I did so,
he took me to Ratcliff-highway, and sent me to pawn some tools; they
would not take them; they told me to send the man who sent me; I went
out, and he was gone ; I went to the station, and told the inspector; if I
had known the things were stolen, I should not have put them on,
Nor GUILTY.
