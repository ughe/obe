WILLIAM Young (City policeman, 443). I took the two prisoners into
custody, at the White Swan-it was stated that they were charged with
passing bad money-they did not say anything-I searched them at the
station; I found on Jones 48. 4d. in good money, and James had a half
crown, a sixpence, and a 4d. piece, good-they had each of them enough to
pay for what they wanted without changing--these are the two shillings I
received from Mr. Saunders.
