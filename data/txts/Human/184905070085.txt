 1178. JAMES HILL , stealing 9 feet of wood, value 1 s  .; the goods of John Brogden the younger . JAMES STELFOX I am in the service of Mr. John Brogden the younger—we had some boards on 17th April—I have seen the boards, which are here—I cannot swear whose they are, we use such; but there is so much timber about there—I believe they are not my master's. JOHN EDWARDS( policeman, R  176). About two o'clock in the morning, on 17th April, I saw the prisoner take a board from a heap of others belong ing to Mr. Brogden, and place it inside his garden—he went back and fetched a second, and then a third—he came out again; he saw me and slammed the door in my face. ROBERT WHITEHEAD I was in Mount-street that night—the policeman told me something—I went in front of the prisoner's house, and saw a board in his garden; where it came from I cannot tell. NOT GUILTY
 1179. GEORGE EDGERLY , stealing 1 pair of boots, value 8 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of George Beaver . THOMAS BARHAM I live at Greenwich. On 20th April, about eight o'clock, I was passing Mr. Beaver's shop—I saw the prisoner take the boots off the nail—he put them under his arm—I told the prosecutor's boy. MARK DRAKE I was serving a female—she told me the prisoner had taken the boots—I went and stopped him with them under his arm—he said he had bought them at a shop, he did not know where—I gave him in charge. GEORGE BEAVER These are my boots—I missed them from a nail outside my shop.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I bought the boots at a second-hand shop; I did not take notice of the shop; I was rather too much gone in liquor. GUILTY Aged 22.— Confined Six Months .
