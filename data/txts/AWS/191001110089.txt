The Foreman stated that the Jury wished to commend the oonduet

The Recorder said he was glad to hear the Jury say SO. He was
sorry to say that the police did not get much assistance in the streets,
and it was refreshing to find a young man with the courage to follow
up these two men in the early morning: He quite concurred in the
commendation of the Jury, and thought Harding deserving of the
thanks of the public for courageously pursuing these men and bring
ing them to justice, and he would direct that Harding receive a
reward of E2 in addition to his expenses.
