9067. JOHN WILMOTT was indicted for stealing 2 shillings, 3 pence,
and 5 halfpence; the monies of Richard Cannandine, bis master.
RICHARD CANNANDINE, Jun. I live with my father, Richard Cannandine,
of 21. Great Smith-street, Westminster-on the afternoon of the 28th of Sept.
the prisoner, who is in his service, came into the back shop, and asked me to
lend him some money-soon after John Harding came and asked me, in the
prisoner's presence, if I had taken 28. 5 d. off the side-table in the parlour?
I said, "No"_I asked the prisoner if he had been in the parlour ?--he
said, "Yes"-I asked if he had seen the money 1-he said, "No"-I said,
"You must go down into the parlour," and I asked him if he had any objection
to my searching him he said, "No"-he began searching himself-he found
nothing- then searched him, and found 2s. 9d. in his fob pocket I said,
"If you had no money, what is this?"-he said, "3d. is mine, the other is
yours; I took it off the side-table."
Prisoner. I saved up 2s. 9d. to buy some clothes.
JOHN HARDING. I am in the prosecutor's service. My mistress told me
to mind the shop-a lady asked me the price of four window-curtains they
came to half-a-crown--the lady said she had only 28. 540.--the money was
laid on the corner of the parlour table-I missed it-I saw the prisoner in the
room-no one else could have taken it.
Prisoner. I asked for 6d. to get something to eat, as I did not like to
break into my money that I had saved,
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Confined Three Months.

2069. MARY GRANT was indicted for embezzlement.
ALEXANDER PETRIE. I am a sack-manufacturer, and live in St. George's-
street, St. George's-in-the-East. The prisoner was in my employ-on the
24th of Oct. I gave out 61. to settle with my work-people--the prisoner
presented an account to me of what she had paid she stated she had
paid Jane M'Guin 78. 4d., Ann Buckley 88. 8d., Sarah Fousee 78. 4d., and
Ellen Collins 48. 8d.-if she has not paid them that she has kept it-that was
what I gave her the money for.
Cross-examined by Mr. HORRY. Q. In the course of business
the prisoner had to attend to some of your manufactory? 4. Yes: I
gave her the sack-work, and she gave it out to do-she had paid the people
for some time-I never knew that she charged too much against herself-
there has been no mistake in the account.
JANE M'Guin. I received payment from the prisoner for my labour last
Saturday-she paid me 68., and no more on the Monday after she called me
down and said, “ For God's sake say you received 78. 4d."
ANN BUCKLEY. The prisoner paid me only 68. 8d.
SARAH Fousee. She paid me 6s., and no more.
ELLEN COLLINS. She paid me 88. 4d.
Guilty. Aged 42.-Confined Six Months.
