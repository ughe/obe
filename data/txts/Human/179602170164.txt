 If 'midst the busy scenes of life, We seek to calm internal strise And snatch a cheerful hour, The jocund tale, the flowing bowl, Will shed oblivion o'er the foul, And check reflexion's pow'r.
 But when dark solitude pervades The midnight scene - and silent shades Are wrapt in awful gloom, Where is the mind impair'd by GUILT? Where is the hand that BLOOD has spilt, That shrinks not at its doom?
 Ah! what avails each grand parade By the proud hand of fashion made, And scenes of splendid riot? What charm can health or wealth impart? What tranquil moment knows the heart When CONSCIENCE is not quiet?
