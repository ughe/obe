be ready for master and mistress on the following Wednesday-it was
Barnes's holiday as well; she was going home at the same time-when I
was taken to see the men in the station yard the prisoner was the tallest
man there, I did not notice that there was one other tall man, and that
all the rest were of small stature-I did not notice how the other men
were dressed--I don't know what class of men they were, or whether
they were labourers--the prisoner was not dressed the same as he was
that night, I looked at all the men-I can't remember whether one was
tall and dressed in black-I identified the prisoner at once by his
features, and I went and touched him-I did not identify him by his
dark short beard, I identified him because he was the man; I had no
doubt whatever I heard him speak at the police-court.
Re-examined. This is the description I gave of the man at the station

beard and moustache; dress, short grey coat, dark trousers, black hard
