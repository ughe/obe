HERBERT PAYNE, clerk, 62, Walnut Tree Walk, Lambeth, About
7.30 a.m. on December 11 prisoner called on me and asked for Mr.
Fitzgerald, the late landlord of the house, saying he had left a tin
box with Mr. Fitzgerald. I gave prisoner & tin box which had been
left in my charge to be called for. Prisoner put two pardels into the
box.
Cross-examined. I had never known prisoner before. I did not
investigate his right to the box. It was the only box there.
FREDERICK LINIB, 212, Camberwell Rosd. I have been jobmaster.
I was in Billingsgate early in the morning of December 11. A man
offered me something for sale. He had some bottled whisky And
brandy in a fish basket, with something like a towel on top of the
basket over the bottle. I did not buy. About a quarter of an hour
after I left the man I went to have my coffee in the only public house
in Billingsgate, and saw the same man offering something from his
basket to two other men in the public house. I think the prisoner
was one. The other was a dark man whom I have seen two or three
times in the market. Except on that occasion I had never seen the
prisoner before. Reading of this case in the newspapers, it occurred
to me that it might refer to the man whom I had seen selling bottles
of spirits. You can buy pretty well anything at Billingsgate
Verdiet, Not guilty.
