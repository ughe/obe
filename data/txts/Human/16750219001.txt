NEWS from ISLINGTON, OR, The Confession, AND EXECUTION, OF GEORGE ALLIN BUTCHER, Who now Hangs in CHAINS Near TO ISLINGTON,
 With a full and True Relation of his whole Confession before several Godly Ministers who came Dayly to visit him in Newgate.
 With the Tryal of one Stevens for Breaking open a House at Branford.
 LONDON, Printed in the Year, 1674.
