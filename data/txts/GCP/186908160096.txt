780. JOHN FINNAGAN, (a soldier) (32), Stealing one ring, one chain,
and one cross, of Jessie Penfold.
MR. STERNE conducted the Prosecution.
JESSIE PENPOLD. I live at 15, Mulgrave Place-on Sunday night, 4th
July, I met the prisoner in Artillery Place-he went home with me about
6 in the morning, when I awoke, I saw him leaving the room with my chain
in his band; I called for assistance, but he vanished-I met him again on
the 18th, the worse for drink-he said, “ Are you coming home”-I recog-
nized him, and said “Yes"-he said, “Will you fetch some beer!"--I sid
“Yes," but instead of going for the beer I got a policeman and gave him
in charge the value of the things I lost is about 47. 10s., and there was 14
in money--when the policeman charged him, he said " Oh!"- this chain and
ring (produced) are mine-I also lost my watch and a cross.
Prisoner. (. Did you see me take the articles? 4. I was awoke by
your unlocking the door-I saw you leaving the room with my chain in your
hand—I did not know you were & soldier, you were in officer's clothes
could not give notice to the police, because I was undressed I awoke some-
one in the house, but they were all undressed the front door was open all
night.
JOHN HESSEY (Policeman R 180). About 11 o'clock on the night of the
18th, the prosecutrix spoke to me I went with her to a house, and took
the prisoner into custody--he was dressed in civilian's clothes, and was lying
on the bed, very drunk-I asked if he knew anything of the charge-he said,
"No"-he was very violent-I had to get assistance to get him to the
station-1 searched him there by main foroe, but only found things belong.
ing to himself-I gave information at the barracks, and searched his quarters
next morning, and, on the top shelf of a dresser, I found the chain and ring
produced.
John McGRANERY. I am a corporal in the Royal Artillery-on 19th July,
I received instructions to go with the policeman to the prisoner's quarters
and make a search he is employed there as an officer's servant, and on the
top shelf of the dresser I found this guard, cross, and ring-he keeps his
property there, and his regimentals and plain clothes.
EMILY Mussos. At 11.30 or 11.45 on this night, I saw the prisoner in
