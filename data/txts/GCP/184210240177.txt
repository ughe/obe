Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. How long have you been a widow?
A. Not above a year and a half-my theatrical name is Lesliemy real
name was Rogers before I was married I have not gone by any other
theatrical name-I get my living by attending the theatre the first time
I saw the prisoner was when he came to look at my apartments--I did not
meet him in the street-I never saw him in Waterloo-place or Regent-
street, or any other place, before he came to my house-I bad not a boy
in my service when the prisoner called on me- I had been my own servant
for ten days--I have children of different ages-the eldest is twenty-one-
I have one, who is a lad, about sixteen-he is out every day from half-past
six o'clock-he did not open the door to the prisoner-I did myself—I
did not talk to the prisoner about the Italian Opera-I never told him I
performed in the chorus there, nor that I wore a particular pink gown-1
