 275. WILLIAM SNOWSWELL was indicted for stealing, on the   29th of Nov ., 20lbs. of coals, value 3 d  .; the goods of Stephen Finney , in a vessel on the navigable river Thames. HENRY JOSEPH KING( Thames police-constable. ) On the 29th of Nov. I saw the prisoner row his boat alongside the barge Emma,  and get on the gun wale, and throw some pieces of coal in them—he went again, sat on the gun wale a quarter of an hour, and threw some more coals into his boat. JOHN HOWES. I am a lighterman, of the Emma,  in the employ of Joseph Finney—I only knaw his name by what the lighterman say. NOT GUILTY.
 276. ROBERT BARFOOT and MARY BARFOOT were indicted for a misdemeanor.
   MR. CHAMBERS conducted the Prosecution. JOSEPH MOUNT( police-constable C  115.) At half-past seven in the even ing of the 2nd of Nov. I and Russell saw the prisoners and two others in Oxford-street—I watched them down to Charing-cross, and took them in Vine-street—I found no coin on these prisoners—they were remanded till the following Thursday, and then the other two were discharged, and the prisoners kept in custody—I went to a house, No. 122, Cornwall-road, Lam beth—I saw the landlady of the house, Elizabeth Thorn, and her husband—he conducted us to a room in that house—he opened the door—I searched and found under the hob these five counterfeit shillings. WILLIAM RUSSELL( police-constable C  113.) I was with Mount, and went to search the room—I found a pipkin, and some metal in it on a cup board, and some plaster of Paris—there was no one in the room then but the landlord—the prisoners were in custody. ELIZABETH THORN. I live in No. 121, Cornwall-road—I let the back room first floor of No. 122, to the prisoner, Mary—she lived there eight weeks—she said her husband was a tailor—the officer went into that room. ROBERT THOMAS. While Mary was lodging at No. 122, Robert used to come there as her husband—I conducted the officers to that room—I saw them find five bad shillings under the hob, and this pipkin and plaster.
   Robert Barfoot. Q.  When did you see me? A.  Repeatedly—on my being brought to Marborough-street, you were sitting down, and I could not posi tively swear to you, but when you turned round I knew you. MR. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to the Mint. These 5 shillings are all counterfeit, and all cast in one mould, not finished and have not been circulated—this metal is of a similar kind to that the counterfeit money it made of.
   Robert Barfoot's Defence.  I was never near, the room—Thorne stated very different at the office to what he has now.
   Mary Barfoot's Defence.  The lock of the door was not quite sound; any thing might have been put in the room.
   ROBERT BARFOOT  — GUILTY. Aged 22.
   MARY BARFOOT  — GUILTY. Aged 23. Confined One Year .
