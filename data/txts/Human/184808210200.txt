 2021. JAMES BARNES was again  indicted for stealing 1 pewter pot value 1 s  .; the goods of Charles Evans . CHARLES EVANS. I lost a pot—this is it ( produced ). GEORGE WADE. On Sunday evening I put a pot by Mr. Dowsett's wharf—I did not see it again till it was at the station. JOHN TAYLOR( policeman ). I saw the prisoner come from his house at five o'clock, and take this pot, wrapped it in his apron, and took it into his forge. NOT GUILTY.
 2022. ELIZABETH STYLES , stealing 1 waistcoat, 2 pillow-cases, and other articles, value 20 l  .; the goods of John Frederick Copland , her master. MARY ANN COPLAND. I am the wife of Frederick John Copland, of Willow-terrace, Charlton. I lost these things, and charged the prisoner with stealing them—she was my servant—I went with a policeman, searched her lodgings, and found twenty-six duplicates, by which I found these articles at the pawnbroker's—they were all in my charge. EDWARD SCOTT( policeman, K  47). I went with Mr. Copland, and found these twenty-six duplicates ( produced ) on the table, in the prisoner's room. THOMAS MARSON. I produce a sheet, table-cloth, and two pillow-cases, pawned in the name of Elizabeth Smith—I gave the person this duplicate.
 (There was another indictment against the prisoner.) GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Nine Months .
 2023. CATHERINE HERRION , stealing 1 half-crown, 11 d  ., 2 half-pence, and 1 farthing; the goods of James Figg ; having been before con victed. HARRIET FIGG. I am the daughter of James Figg. On 30th June, about nine o'clock, the prisoner and another young woman came and called for two glasses of porter—my father brought it—I saw the prisoner put her hand into the till, take some halfpence, and put them into her pocket—I missed half-a-crown from the till—I saw her take some more halfpence off the counter—I told my father.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLERK . Q.  Was she drunk? A.  I think she was the worse for liquor—she seemed to know what she was about. SARAH EVELEIGH. I searched the prisoner at the station, and found a half-crown, eleven pence, two halfpence, and a farthing—she was very tipsy. HENRY BURNS( policeman, R  261). I produce a certificate of the prisoner's conviction—( read—Convicted Sept ., 1847, and confined two months )—she is the person. GUILTY.— Confined One Year .
 2024. WILLIAM JOHNSON and THOMAS WILLIAMS , stealing 1 purse, value 1 s  .; 4 shillings, 2 sixpences, and 3 groats, the property of John Rees ; from the person of Eliza D'Oyley Rees . ELIZA D'OYLEY REES. I am the wife of John Rees. On 14th July, I was at Sir Thomas Wilson's fete, at Charlton—an officer spoke to me—I missed my purse from my right-hand pocket—there were shillings, sixpences, and fourpenny pieces in it—this is it.
   Cross-examined by MR. METCALFE . Q.  How do you know it? A.  I have had it two years—I looked at it before I left my house.
