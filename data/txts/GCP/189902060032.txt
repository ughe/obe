WILLIAM SMITH (The prisoner). I was called by Mahoney to go with
him and earn ls. 6d., and stopped in the lobby-he said, "See to
the van"-there were 25 cwt. bags and nine 2-cwt. bags, making 34

refused-We went to Queen Victoria Street, and then to High Street,
Whitechapel, where we found we had 4 cwt. over, and were going to take
that back to the yard--the driver asked what we had got, and I said,
“4 cwt. to go back to the yard "-be said, "It is a little bit on your
own!"_I said, “No"-I went with the van back to the yard, and
there was no stealing whatsover.
Cross-examined. I never said to the driver, "Yes; I done them out of
4 cwt. in Queen Victoria Street; it is sure to be two months for it." -
Mahoney received a good character.
