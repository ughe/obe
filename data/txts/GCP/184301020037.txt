HENRY FANTHAM. I am a boatman, and live at Wendover, in Bucks. 1.
had a boat lying in Paddington canal basin on the 28th of Dec., and had a
chain about ten yards long on board, and another on another boat-one was made
fast, and the other loose ---Seymour came to me before daylight on the morn-
int of the 29th, and said, had not I a chain-I said, for anything I knew I
had, on the forward part of the boat-I said I did not want it, if he liked to
have it he should have it for 108., which was the value-I sold him that one
for 10s. I did not deliver it to him-I told him it was there, and he might
take it-he paid me the 105.- afterwards found the other chain down at the
station produced by a policeman-that was not the chain I meant to sell for
105.- I do not know how it got out of my possession-I was in the cabin
getting up when Seymour came to me I did not go out to look after it-T
was not dressed the chain was on the forward part of the boat, towards the
head- I told him it was there.
