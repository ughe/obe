Mk. MAGUIRE conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE WINGATB. I reside in Spring-place, Wandsworth-road, Lam-
beth. On 2nd Nov., I went to bed at a few minutes past eleven o'clock;
I bad fastened my doors and windows-I. am satisfied every thing was
perfectly fast-the next morning I came down at seven-I saw a light in
the back-parlour, and found the window-shutters had been forced open, and
the window was open- missed sheets, counterpanes, and other things,
which had been in the front-room, and which were brought into the back-
parlour-I went to the station and came back-I then missed a writing-
desk, which had been standing on a dining-table in the back-parlour-I had
seen it safe the night before and the counterpane, sheets, towels, an apron,
and a watch-the watch was in my writing-desk, the other things were in
the boxes which were under the the counter in the shop-they bad been
brought into the back-room, I had seen them safe the night before-any
person gould get from the back-parlour to the shop, without breaking
any locks.
