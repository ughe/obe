WILLIAM MARSHALL. I am a baker, and live in New Gravel-lane-
Mr. Talbot is my master. On the 8th of April, between ten and eleven
o'clock, I saw the prisoner and prosecutrix come out of the King William
public-house-I was standing at my shop-door, which is next to it they
both came out-the prosecutrix first--the prisoner followed her--the pro
secutrix fell down on the curb without any body touching her she came
running up against the prisoner--there were three or four persons coming
out-the prosecutrix ran up to the prisoner to make a blow at her, and
fell down on the curb-I saw Thomas lift her up.
Court. Q. Did the prosecutrix come out of the house quietly by her-
self, or was she lifted along? A. She walked out alone--nobody helping
her on one side or the other— I was first asked to be a witness to-day-i
do not know who applied to me-I was at work, and one of the witnesses
came up for me-I do not know her name I do not know any of their

MARY ANN TOOMEY, I am single, and live in New Gravel-lane. Last
Wednesday night, between ten and eleven o'clock, I was in the long room
at the King William public-house-I use the house as an unfortunate girl
--the prosecutrix was there—the prisoner came in afterwards, came down
the room, and spoke to me and another woman she was standing speak
ing to me, and the prosecutrix came down and said "B- Cumberland Poll,
there is no law for pinebiog noses or kicking" . and pulled her nose
--that was before any thing was said to her the prisoner said nothing to
her-the waiter came up and said he would not have a row there, and the
prisoner went outside the door-the prosecutrix followed her out, and
going out, pulled her cap and beads off, gave them to Thomas, and as she
got out at the door, sbe said, "You b Cumberland Poll, now I will give
you what you have wanted for a long time," with that she was going to hit
the prisoner, and Thomas said, "Don't strike, she has got a key in ber
hand”--the prisoner said, "I don't want to hit with the key, you can take
the key," and gave it to her out of her bosom-she then struck her, and
both fell down on the curb they were both the worse for liquor Thomas
said, “Your face is bloody"-she said, “Never mind a little blood, it will
do me no harm”-they fell to fighting again, and fell into Mr. Talbot's
passage, and when sbe got up she was all over blood..
