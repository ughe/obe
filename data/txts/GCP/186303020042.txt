JONATHAN BELL I am landlord of the Bell beer shop, Church-street
Bethnal-green-on the night of 28th December, I went to bed, leaving my
house secure; next morning, I found it foroed open-it is in the parish of
St. Matthew, Bethnal-green.
WILLIAM THOMAS (Policeman, 141 H). About half-past 6 on the mornine
of 29th December I was on duty in Bethnal Green-road, near the Bell beer
shop, and saw the prisoners Attill (that is Goodland), Crane, and Lawrence
I cannot speak so confidently of Wright, because he stood in the shade
but I can swear to the other three-I had never seen them before-when I
first saw them they were about 200 yards off, close to the beer shop; and
when I went along they divided into twos, just before I got opposite them,
on the opposite side-I went down as far as Abbey-street, crossed the road
and they got together again, but divided before I got up to them. I went
up as far as Tyson-street, thinking to meet some plain clothes men, but did
