798. ALFRED BROOKS (27), Forging and uttering an order for the
payment of 151. 108., with intent to defraud.
Mr. HUGGINs Proseouted.
ROBERT AIKEN. I am an undertaker, of 5, Whitechapel Road-on
6th June the prisoner owed me 7l., and offered me this cheque (Signed
R. Snow," for 151. 108.), and said that if I would change it for him he
would settle my account-I gave him the change, and paid the cheque
to my account, and it was returned-I saw the prisoner several times
afterwards; he could not give me Snow's address-I made inquiries
and then accused him of having forged the cheque-he said 46 You seem
to know all about it "-I said Yes, I have found out all about it it is
not the first one you have done, and if I do not have my money to-morrow
I shall apply for a warrant for your arrest"- then said that he had
forged it, and if I would only let it stand over he would pay me the money.
Cross-eramined by the Prisoner. It was returned marked No account.
ARTHUR JOHNsoN. I am cashier at the Putney branch of the London
and South-Western Bank-we have no customer named R. Snow-If find
that the cheque form came from our Bow branch.
