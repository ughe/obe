824. ELIZABETH TUCK, stealing 4 ozs. weight of silk, 1 watch-
guard, and other articles, value 178.; and 4 sovereigns and 2 shillings ; the
property of Charles Lompech, her master.
MARTHA ANN LOMPECH. I am the wife of Charles Lompech, of Fore-
street, City. The prisoner was in our service up to 26th March-she then
Jeft of her own accord-five days after that, I missed four sovereigns, a
cornelian snap, a watch-guard, and one yard of black silk from my bed-room
-the money was in a piece of paper in a trunk, and the other things in a
honnet-box-the trunk was locked, and the key was in the pocket of a dress
that hung up in the same room-when I missed the things the key was still
in the same place I communicated with Sergeant Teakle, and on 3rd April
the prisoner was taken into custody- I went to the station, and found the
watch-guard and some silk. Yo Gott i cosito an
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. How long was she in your service ?
A. Eleven weeks-she was nurse-maid-we have three other persons in our

was allowed in the bed-room-she did not sleep in the house, balta
GEOROB TEAKLE ( police-sergeant, H 8). On 3rd April I went to a house

ber mistress suspected her, and I was come to search ber box-sbe opened the

Cross-examined. Q. Do you know her father? A. Yes; he is a respectable

Mrs. LOMPECH re-examined. This watch-guard and silk are my property.
Mr. Payne. Q. How do you know the silk? 4. It was dyed expressly

(Strongly recommended to mercy by the Jury.)-Judgment respited.
