Cross-examined by the Prisoner. You were not in bed at 12 o'clock,
you were dressed-you struck me with the tongs at 12 o'clock, and with
the poker at 4 o'clock - I did not shake you when you were in bed, and
ask you to get up and give me a drink, I had money of my own.
John BETHEL (21 YR). On November 26th shortly after 4 p.m. I was
called to 31, Johnson Street, and found the prosecutrix in the front room,
ground floor, bleeding from severe cuts on her head-a surgeon was sent
for, and she was sent to the hospital—I went back to Johnson Street,
looked about the room, and found the tongs in the front room, and the
poker in the bedroom which belongs to another woman—the prisoner was
in custody for being drunk-I charged him-he said nothing.
Mary Ann SAUNDERSON. I am attached to the Temperance Hospital
-the prosecu rix was brought there suffering from seven scalp wounds-
blunt instrument such as a poker or tongs would inflict them - these fira-
iruns would do so—they were serious wounds, very severe scalp wounds-
she had lost much blood.
MARY BROWN. I live at 31, Johnson Street-on November 26th I
saw the prisoner at a little after 12 coining from the back parlour
with the tongs in his hand following the prosecutrix-an old lady lived
in the back parlour—he called the prosecutrix a foul name and struck
her-I had heard him say at 6 o'clock that morning that he would swing
for her before long-I saw him at 4 o'clock that afternoon with a poker
in his hand and he said that he had done for her.
Ann Jay. I take care of No. 31, Johnson Street-on a Saturday a
fortnight ago the prosecutrix came out of the back parlour into my room
for a shawl-the prisoner ran after her I went to my room to take a
baby which I nursed, out of the cradle -I said, “ George, you will kill
her"-he said, “Yes and I will kill you too," and struck me across my
shoulders, a woman 75 years of age-he struck the prosecutrix seven or
eight times, and each time made a wound.
A statement made by the prisoner at the station was here put in in which
he said that he met the prosecutriæ in Old Street, roho stated that she was
in great distress and he took hor home to his landlady, stating that she wae!
his cousin, and that he would pay for her keep, but afterwards told her
to obtain another home; that on the day in question she knocked at hie
door at 6 40 a.m., and told him to look out for himself before the day was
out; that she gave him troo black eyes and five wounds, and that she had
told him that she roas on the streets of Liverpool before sho was sixteen
years old.
ELIZABETH CUNNINGHAM (Re-examined). I was at Liverpool some time-
I have been convicted no less than twenty-seven times of assaults on the
police, receiving stolen goods, shop breaking, and other offences, chiefly
assaults, on each of which I have been sent to prison for various terms.
BENJAMIN NORMAN (Detective, G). I received a communication from
Holloway yesterday morning - I received this list, stating that the prose-
cutrix has been convicted twenty-seven times.
