87. EBENEZER MARSHALL was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of November , 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the property of George Buck , from his person. GEORGE BUCK. I live at Mr. Cox's, in the Poultry. On the 22nd of November, in the evening, I was in Bridge-street—a person spoke to me, and I missed my handkerchief—this is it—I had had it in my pocket just before. CHARLES THORP. I am an officer. I was in the street, and saw the prisoner, whom I had known—I watched, and saw him take the handker chief from the prosecutor, he was in the act of passing it to two others—I took him.
   Prisoner's Defence . I was crossing from Fleet-street to Farringdon-street, and the officer took me—this handkerchief was on the ground—I had not touched it. CHARLES THORP re-examined.  I had been following him, and saw him take it, just opposite Mr. Sangster's window. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Transported for Seven Years.
 88. JAMES LINDSAY was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of No vember , 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  ., the goods of Richard Samuel Hadwen Ward , from his person. RICHARD SAMUEL HADWEN WARD. I live in Queen-street. I was coming up Fleet-street, on the 24th of November, and felt my handker chief drawn out—I turned, and saw the prisoner with it—he dropped it, and his own handkerchief too—I took it up—this is it—he was secured.
   Prisoner.  I saw two young fellows behind him who took the handke chief and dropped it on the ground—I was going to tell the gentleman. Witness . There were no persons near. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Transported for Seven Years.
 89. HENRY LLOYD was indicted for stealing, on the   5th of Novem ber , 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  ., the goods of Samuel Hanbury , from his person; and that he had been before convicted of felony. SAMUEL HANBURY. I live in John-street, Bedford-row. On the 5th of November, I was in Gregory-court, Holborn—I felt a twitch at my pocket—I looked very sharply back, and saw the prisoner close behind me, and my handkerchief on the ground—I charged him with stealing it—he denied it, and while I was stooping for the handkerchief, he ran away—I followed him, crying, "Stop thief"—he ran across Brooke's market, and I lost sight of him, but on turning a corner, I found him in custody of a man who keeps a coal-shed—I have no doubt he is the boy who took it, but I should not like to swear to it—there was no other boy running away but him. EDWARD ASH. I was in Gregory-court. I saw the prisoner put his hand into the prosecutor's pocket, and pull out the handkerchief—the prose cutor turned round—there were several boys with the prisoner—I followed him, and did not lose sight of him. THOMAS CLARK. ( police-constable G  88.) The prisoner was given to me by the coal man. WILLIAM HOWELL. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction—I know he is the man. ( read. ) GUILTY. Aged 17.— Transported for Seven Years.
