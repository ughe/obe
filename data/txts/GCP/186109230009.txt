735. WILLIAM JOHN NEWBY (25) Stealing 30% in money, the
property of John Kearns and others, his masters.
MR. GIPPARD conducted the Prosecution
TIMOTHY MURPHY. I am a labourer in the employment of Messrs
Kearnis and Co.--the prisoner was a clerk in their service on 16th August
I was on board the ship Ada in the London Docks--I saw the prisoner
there-he told me that he was there to pay the freight of the goods, and to
check the weights he told me to go to the prosecutor's office for 801. to
pay the freight-I went, and when I got there I found that the money had
been sent-I went back to the ship, and the prisoner then said that he had
received the 801.--I did not see him at the prosecutor's premises after that
--he remained on the ship till 7 o'clock that evening-I saw him pay away
the 501. note after we left the ship I was in company with him till we
came to Cannon-street, City, and after that I did not see him till he was
taken in custody-it was a little after 7 o'clock in the evening that I last
saw him.ARDO
