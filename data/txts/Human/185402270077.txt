Mr. Mackinnon's.—They were directed to parties from that room? I think some of the friends took some few of the notes, and directed them from there; I think they did to the best of my recollection.—Some of your friends took upon themselves to direct the notes there? To invite some of their own friends.—Then the invitations did not proceed direct from Mr. Curteis?
