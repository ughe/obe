   COURT . Q.  Supposing that the child had had a dose which had a narcotic effect upon it, on the Tuesday, do you believe that it had entirely recovered from that, and that it died from convulsions unconnected with that? A.  I believe it had recovered from that if it bad taken it, and then these convul sions supervened. NOT GUILTY.
