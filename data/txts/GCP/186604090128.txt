ROBERT LAWSON (Policeman, L 9). I took Emmerson, and told him he
was charged with others in stealing some beef-he said, "I know nothing
about it"-there was a small parcel on him wrapped up, containing about
3 lbs. of beef.
Cross-examined by MR. SLEIGH. . Did you ask him where he was
taking it? A. No-he did not say he was taking it by orders-he went
quickly-I only know where the prisoner lived by hearing them give their
addresses at the station.
