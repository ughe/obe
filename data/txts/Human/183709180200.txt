   Mary Collins, Q.  Do you mean to swear I attempted to rob you?—Were we not dancing Jim Crow  about the house, and, of course, you turned round and round with the dance? A.  No, I did not—I did not lay on you bed—Mrs. Glenister did not say to me, "You faggot  you have robbed me, nor did she strike me—she did not offer to strike me, to my know ledge—when I went to the station I certainly was not in a state to speak through the gin that was given me—I might be going round the room—do not dispute that—I have spoken nothing but the truth. ELIZA GLENISTER.    James Collins, Q.  On the Sunday before this happened were you up in my room? A.  I do not recollect—I might be—I did not say any thing about a bricklayer serving a warrant on  you, and that if he did I would swear any thing for you.
