ELIZABETH ANNE TURNER, widow, 37, Coppermill Lane, Summers
Town, S.W. Prisoner and his wife lived at No. 35. I knew her as a
neighbour. At 10 a.m. on January 9 I saw her hanging out some
washing. I had not seen prisoner for over a week. At about 2.20 p.m.
that day I was at my street door when I saW the side blind in the front
room downstairs of their house drawn down.
Cross-examined. They had been there about 12 months. They were
very quiet neighbours and seemed to be on very good terme- most
happy couple. The children were well looked after. Prisoner seemed
a very quiet man.
