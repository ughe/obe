82. ALFRED SHEA (19), Unlawfully having implements of house
breaking in his possession; to which he PLEADED GUILTY.
83. ALFRED SHEA was again indicted for burglariously breaking and
entering the dwelling-house of Henry Faulkner, and stealing therein 1 box,
6 spoons, 1 scoop, 1 case of drawing instruments, his property; and 1 shawl,
the property of Rachel Newton. Second Count, Feloniously receiving the
same,
MR. HORRY conducted the Prosecution.
Rachel NEWTON. I am servant to Henry Faulkner, of 7, Bedford-row,
Claphan-rise that is his dwelling-house-on Sunday evening, 220 Septem-
ber, I fastened the house up—the back drawing-room window was quite fast
-I put the latch to-I and the family went to bed about half-past 10
o'clook- I left this shawl (produced) on a small table in the kitchen I came
down next morning about half past 6-I had left a deak also standing on
the table I found it on the floor in the kitchen-the things were turned
out-I missed my shawl-I called up the family the drawing-room window
was half open--the blind was half up at one end, pulled up antidily as if
it had been hastily drawn up
Cross-examined by MR DICKIE. 9. Do you swear to that positively, as
being your shawlt 4. Yes, because there is a mark in one corner where
the box was shut on it and went through it,I have no mark by needle
work--the shawl was put in the box and the bolt of the lock went through it.
Court. Q. How long were you up before you came down! 4. Not long,
about a quarter of an hour-I did not hear the clock strike 6_ I was the
first down.
EXILY LUQY FAULKNER. I am the wife of Henry Faulkner, of 7, Bed-
ford-row, Clapham-on the morning of the 230 September, about 7 o'clook,
I was called up by my servant-I found the drawing-room window wide
open, a pane of glass cut out of it, and the lateh put back I found the
inside of the drawing-room door very much cut-it had been locked securely
on the outside on the night before, and it was forced and very much out
about and disfigured-they succeeded in opening it-T missed a silver suff-
box, and a set of drawing instruments I have never heard anything more
about them-my house is only separated at the back, from the fields, by a
low wall.
COURT. Q. What parish is it in! 4. Lambeth, I believe.

know.
