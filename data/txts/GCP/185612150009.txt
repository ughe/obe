94. DANIEL WILLIAM RUFFEY was again indicted for stealing,
on 3rd June, 108.; the moneys of John Hodge and others, his masters
2nd Count, on 7th June, 48.-3rd COUNT, on 11th June, 12; the moneys
of his said masters,
MESARS, GIPTARD and SLEIGH conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS SAUNDERS. I am one of the directors of the United Patriots
Benefit Insurance Society. The prisoner was the secretary, at a salary-
amongst his various duties, he had to send to the country agents of the
society, paroels containing circulars and prospectuses, and he had authority
to pay the expenses of their transmission, and had to enter the sums in the
society's expenses book- this is it (prodriced).
GEORGE HOLMES. I am one of the booking clerks at the Green Man and
Still, Oxford-street. The prisoner was in the habit of bringing parcels there
to be booked, to be sent into the country-we sign for the paroels
(looking at the paroels book) here is an entry on 3rd June, of thirteen parcels
brought by him on that day, the amount paid for their transmission to the
country was 198. 5d., and no more, and which was paid to me—the book is
signed by me-on 7th June he brought four parcels; my signature is
attached to the entry, for 78. 3d. paid, and no more on 11th June here is
an entry of thirteen parcels, 11. 48. 8d. ; that amount was paid to me, and
no more, by the prisoner.
