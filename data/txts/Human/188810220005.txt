   Re-examined.  There was no liability to pay the prisoner 3 l . a house; he says he was entitled to that—he had over 1,000 l . belonging to the parties in the suit—300 l . was the balance due on 27th November; since then he has received more—he has never paid one penny of that balance—there are orders of the Court to pay 1,000 l .—six months after he was appointed receiver 189 l . was advanced to him to do some repairs, he only spent about 20 l . of that, I know that by having had to pay—75 l . was what he was ordered to pay—I have paid that, he has got the rest—I did not know him by the name of Raven until after this was discovered; I did not see him again until after his arrest—this is a press copy of a letter I addressed to him ( Read: "Dear Sir, Fry v.
