go to Mr. Sewell and get an emetic, for I thought she had poisoned herself as
well as the child-on my return, I found her in the same place in the yard
she did not continue so more than 10 minutes-Mr. Sewell's assistant gave
her an emetic and she recovered, and was delivered to the police I gave
