 876. WILLIAM TURVEY was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of February , 1 pair of reins, value 6 s  . the goods of George Boreham . GEORGE HALE. I am in the employ of Mr. George Boreham of Bomford, a straw-carter. I was returning home on Saturday evening the 11th of February, about half-past six o'clock—when I got into the lane I felt a blow as if somebody hit me on the shoulder—I looked up, and saw somebody running away with the reins—they had been on the fore-ladder of the cart—it was in taking the reins that he hit me on the shoulder—I took it to be this prisoner—I knew him before, and his name—I went to his house on the Sunday morning after, and asked him for them—he said he had not got them—I went to the constable, who went, and he gave them to him—these are them.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  Where were you coming from? A.  London—I cannot say how long I had left London—I only stopped to bait the horses at Bow—he did not accompany me from Bow—he was coming away from his baiting-house as I passed, about six miles from Bow, and about a quarter of a mile from the lane where I felt this rap—I did not accompany him—I did not speak with him—he was with his cart before me—when I got to the end of the lane, his road led another way—he turned off there, and then I got this blow on the shoulder. GEORGE HOLT. I am the constable. Hale gave me information—I went to the prisoner's house, and asked for the reins—he said, "I have not got them," I said, "I am given to understand you have, if you do not give them me I shall look for them"—he went into his room—I followed him, and he gave me the reins.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Have you always given the same statement? A  Yes; I do not know to the contrary—what I said was taken down I expect—it was read over, and I signed it.
 (  The witness's deposition stated , "I went to the prisoner, and asked him if he had any reins belonging to George Boreham, he replied he had, but he was going to take them to Mr. Hale, the father of the witness George Hale, that evening.")
   Witness.  That is true, but he at first said he had not—it appears that I did not say any thing about it, but he did say it to me. NOT GUILTY.
