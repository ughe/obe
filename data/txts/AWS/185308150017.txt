856. JOHN LACEY, feloniously wounding Join Elliott, with intent to
do him some grievous bodily harm.--2 COUNT, feloniously shooting, with
a like intent.
MR. RIBTON conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN ELLTOTT.
I have been two years and a half in the prisoner's
service, on and off. On 2nd Aug. I went home with him to Pimlico in the
horse and cart; we had stopped on the road, and got home about 8 o'clock
-I went to the stable for a quarter of an hour, and took the harness off the
pony I then came out, and met Mr. Lacey, as if he was coming into the
stable-he was talking to Hillier-he told me to go and put the cart in I
was going to do so, and he began to call me a lazy vagabond; I said I was
not, and he told me to go away from his place went away, and sat with
Hillier on the bank in front of the garden, about six yards from the palings
-while we were sitting there, the prisoner came out of the garden two or
three times, and wanted me to go in I told him I would not go in till he
was in a better temper-] had been sitting there some time, and he came to
the gate-1 did not see anything in. his hand but he stepped back a step
or two, and fired; the shots came into me like a lot of stinging-nettlee,
and knocked me down the bank; and as I was rolling down I saw the flash
of the gun-I found my neck was all blood-Hillier was struck to0; he
ran down the road, and I after him-I gave information to the police.
