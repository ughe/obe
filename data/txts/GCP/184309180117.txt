then to Herring- I went to the shop, and saw the foreman--some minutes
after I saw Herring- I said to him, Did you not go to a public-house and

man came to the door, and asked me whether I would change a 31. note for
him" I said, “Who was the man?"-- he said, “I don't know-I said,
"How was he dressed ?"-he did not know-I said, “Did he purchase any
goods ?"-he said, “No, he did not”-I said, “What did he give you ?"
