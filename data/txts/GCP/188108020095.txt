EDWARD KNIGHT. I am a painter, of Penrose Street, Walworth
about 12.10 a.m. on 24th July I was in Blackman Street, Borough, and
saw the prisoner and two others—the prisoner caught hold of me by my
left shoulder, and asked me for a penny; I said I had none to spare
he struck me on my mouth and knocked me down-I got up, he snatched
the chain from my waistcoat, left the watch in my pocket, and ran away;
I caught him in Lawrence Street and gave him into custody-he was
quite sober-he said “Old man, I know nothing of your chain”--the
other two got away. ba
