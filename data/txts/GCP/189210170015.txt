926. THOMAS CHRISTIAN (23), Unlawfully uttering a counterfeit
half-crown, and within ten days a counterfeit crown. Other Counts.
MR. PARTRIDGE Prosecuted.
PETER APPOLLINARI. I am barman to Messrs. Gatti and Rodesano,
of 166, Strand-on Saturday, 1st October, the prisoner paid me this half-
crown for a glass of bitter, 2d.-I put it in the till—there was no other
half-crown there-I gave the prisoner 2s. 4d. change-I put no other
half-crown in the till—the next morning I found it was bad-on 6th
October the prisoner tendered this other crown for a glass of lager, 3d.
-I recognised him, and took the coin to Mr. Rodesano-he found it
had no weight-he went to the prisoner, and said, "It is bad, and you
have been here with a half-crown, which was no good "-the prisoner
said, "Don't take it if it is no good; I shall change it; I will give you
another "-Mr. Rodesano sent for a policeman, who took the prisoner
into custody.
Cross-examined by the prisoner. I am sure you gave me the half-crown
-you had a black eye.
By the Court. The 1st October was the first time I saw you.
JOSEPH RODESANO. On 6th October, about 11.30, Appollinari brought
me this 58. piece - I took it to the prisoner-he said, "If it is bad I will
give you another"-I sent for a policeman-I gave the coin to him.
ALBERT PORTER (405 E). I was called to this restaurant--the pro-
prietor said, “This man called for a small liquor, and tendered this
coin; he called on the 1st and tendered this coin”- the prisoner made no
answer-I took him to the station-I searched him first-I found on
him & purse containing a 28. piece, 1s., two sixpences, and ijd. in
coppers.
WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. I am Inspector of Coins at Her Majesty's
Mint-these coins are counterfeit.
Guilty.***— Twelve Months' Hard Labour.
