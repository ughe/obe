THOMAS BUTLER. I am a gheesemonger, of 12, Eleanor-road, Woolwich-
my attention was called to the prisoner by Mr. Oram--I saw them all
loitering abont, taking their turns walking np and down past the prosecutor's
door--at last I saw Price out the string of the boots, and Hart received one;
one fell on the pavement, the other was picked up they then made off-I
immediately followed and secured Price Chapman and Hassell were loitering
at the corner at the time bystander picked up the boots and handed them
to me about thirty yards from the shop, in the direction in which the
prisoners ran.
JAMES MARGETSON (Policeman, 122 R). I received the prisoners in custody
-Hassell said he did not steal the boots--he did not cut them down--Hart
and Price did not say anything-Chapman said he did not cut the boots
down, it was Hart and Price.
