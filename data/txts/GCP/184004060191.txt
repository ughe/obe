RICHARD HOOKE. I am a baker, and live in Shoe-lane—the prisoner was
my servant-on the 13th of March I sent him, among other places, to Mrs.
Norie, in Bear-alley, with fifteen loaves-he brought word back that they
wanted ten more, and in the afternoon I sent him with ten more the
came to the counter and had them booked-here is the book in which they
are entered-on the Monday morning he was to take their bill on the
Monday I gave the bill shortly after breakfast Mrs. Norie sent to my
shop and said they had not got the bill-I questioned the prisoner, and he
said he supposed he had left it at another customer's I made out another,
and in the course of the day they came and said there were ten loaves too
many-the first opportunity I bad, I questioned the prisoner about the
loaves, and he said, "I suppose I forgot to book them" said, "Very
well, go in the evening and set it right he went out and did not return.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. Had he to take loaves out
to other customers ? A. Not at that particular time--I am quite sure he
said Mrs. Norie wanted ten more loaves--I am sure he did not merely say
that he wanted ten...
