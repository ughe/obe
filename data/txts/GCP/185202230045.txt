318. DANIEL MUNRO, was indicted for embezzlement.
THOMAS MILLS. I am a baker, at Poplar. I know the prisoner-in the
early part of Jan. J paid him 171. 8s. 6d. on account of Mr. Penson of New-
gate-street- he gave me this receipt-I saw him write it (produced.)
THOMAS WILKIE ADAMS. I am a baker. On 29th Jan. I paid the pri-
soner 21. 11s. 9d.; he gave me this receipt (produced)-I saw him write it.
Cross-examined by Mr. ROBINSON. Q. How long have you known him?
1. About eighteen months- I never heard anything against his character.
DUNCAN CLARK. I am a baker, at Charlotte-street, Fitzroy-square. On
7th Jan. I paid the prisoner 77. 68. 10d. on account of Mr. Penson-be gave
me this receipt produced).
Cross-examined Q. How long have you known him? A. About eighteen
months I believe him to be of a very exemplary character.
John Wilcox. I am cashier to Mr. George Penson, wholesale cheese-
monger. The prisoner was his town, traveller-it was his duty to receive
money, and to pay it over to me, and no one else he has paid me sol. of
this 171. 8s. 6d. paid by Mr. Mills-he entered 10% in the cash book, and
paid it over to me, but said nothing about the rest - he has not paid me
21. 11s. 9d. received from Adams, or 71. os. 10d. from Clerk.
Cross-examined. Q. Was the prisoner paid by commission ? A. Yes; I
do not know what is due to him that is settled by Mr. Penson, who is not
here, I am occasionally away from the business-Mr. Mylne is the head of
the establishment, after Mr. Penson - I believe the prisoner was engaged by
Mr. Mylne; he is not here I was examined before the Magistrate-Mr. Wood,
the prisoner's solicitor, desired that Mr. Mylne might appear at the trial.
COURT. Q. How do you know he only paid you 101. out of the 171. 88. 6d.?
4. By the cash book (produced) I find an entry in his own writing, 101.,
dated 8th Jan.-I put down all sums that he paid me.
Mr. ROBINSON, Q. If Mr. Penson received any money, would he enter it
in this book? A. Yes, and pay it over to me-all money goes through my
hands.
(The prisoner received a good character.) Guilty. Aged 22.- Recommended
to mercy by the Jury.--Confined Four Months.
