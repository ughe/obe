SIDNEY, Mayor.
OLD COURT.-Wednesday, June 14th, 1854.
PRESENT.-The Rt. Hon. the LORD MAYOR; Mr. Justice COLERIDGE ;
Mr. Ald. Moon; Mr. Ald. FINNIS; Mr. Ald. MUGGERIDGE; and Mr. Ald.
ROSE
Before Mr. Justice Coleridge and the Third Jury.
724. HENRY CHAMBERS was indicted for feloniously forging and
uttering, on 9th May, an order for the payment of 101. 78. ; also, on
30th May, one other order for the payment of 181. 53. ; with intent to
defraud : to both of which he
PLEADED Guilty. Aged 20.-Six Years Penal Servitude.
725. HENRY COPE, feloniously forging and uttering an order for the
payment of 301. ; with intent to defraud : to which he
PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 20.—Six Years Penal Servitude.
726. EDWIN BENJAMIN VIDLER, alias, JOSEPH SAUNDERS,
feloniously forging and uttering an order for the payment of 41. 138. ld.;
with intent to defraud : to which he
PLEADED Guilty. Aged 34.-Ten Years Penal Servitude.
(MR. BODKIN for the prosecution stated, that the prisoner had been dis-
charged on a ticket of leave before the expiration of his previous sentence, that
the ticket contained the signature of Colonel Jebb, and the prisoner had found
out the colonel's banker, and had imitated the signature to the ticket upon the
forged cheques in question.)
727. GEORGE RAY, feloniously forging and uttering an order for the
payment of 5l. ; also, one other order for the payment of 101. ; with intent
to defraud : to both of which he
PLEADED Guilty. Aged 59.---Confined Two Years.
728. ANDREW WEBB, feloniously stealing a post letter, containing a
half sovereign, and 12 postage stamps, the property of Her Majesty's Post
Master General: to which he
PLEADED Guilty. Aged 58.-Confined Two Years.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
(The above five prisoners were sentenced by MR. BARON ALDERSON, on
Thursday.)
729. JOHN CHARLTON, feloniously and knowingly uttering a forged
note for 101. ; with intent to defraud : to which he
PLEADED GUILTY.
730. JOHN CHARLTON, was again indicted, for feloniously and know-
ingly uttering a forged note for 5l. ; with intent to defraud.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
