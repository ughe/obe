 106. FRANCES BARRETT, was again  indicted for stealing 1 gown, value 8 s  .; the goods of Frances Lee ; upon which no evidence was offered. NOT GUILTY.
 107. CHARLES HUTCHINSON and FREDERICK FIELD , stealing 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  .; the goods of John William Foster : Field having been before convicted. ANN CASE. On 2nd Nov., between twelve and one at noon, I was at my bed-room window, which looks into Mr. Foster's garden, and saw Hutchinson get over the wall, walk half-way down the garden, take a handkerchief which was hanging up to dry, and give it to Field, who was stooping down at the wall—they both ran across a field—I went down and got close behind them, and heard Hutchinson say, "I wonder what the lady would give us if we told her who stole the handkerchief"—Field put it into his breast-pocket—I followed them into the Ball's-pond-road—they turned up a court and ran—I called, "Stop thief!"—they were stopped and taken to the station—I have not seen the handkerchief since. GEORGE LANGDON( policeman, N  265). I searched the prisoners at the Kingsland station, but iound nothing—Ilutchinson said the parties that took it in away. RACHAEL FOSTER. On 2nd Nov. I had a handkerchief hanging out to dry—I missed it between twelve and one—it was my husband's, John Wil liam Foster.
   Field's Defence.  I saw two boys take it and give it to another boy. WILLIAM MONAGAS( policeman ). I produce a certificate of Field's con viction—( read—Convicted July , 1848, confined three months )—I was present—he is the person.
   HUTCHINSON  — GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Six Months .
   FIELD  — GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Twelve Months .
