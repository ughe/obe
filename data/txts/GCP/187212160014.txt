not called on me constantly for weeks, and been paid pound by pound-I
paid the 201, all in a lump at my place in Portobello Road, I believe in

written--we had settled the 401. loan before that I had lent him money
between his giving me back the IO U and the time of this forged bill
being drawn-I took no acknowledgment of it-it was about 71, ; he came
to me for it.
