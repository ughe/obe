 2135.  MARY ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of August , 1 bed, value 15s., the goods of John Ord.
   ELIZABETH PEARSON. I am a widow, and live in Shakspeare's-walk, Shadwell. On the 27th of August we lost a bed from No. 1, Glasshouse-place, Stepney; it was Mr. Ord's property - the prisoner's daughter had been in charge of his house; I asked the prisoner what was to be done about the bed; she said, "What's that to you? I took it, and shall keep it as long as I please."
   JANE READ. I am the wife of James Read - we live within three doors of Ord's. On the 27th of August I saw the prisoner go in there, and a person named Berry came out with the bed on his back - the prisoner came out just after him.
   JOSEPH MICKLEFIELD. I am shopman to a pawnbroker in Ratcliff. The prisoner left this bed with me as security for a monthly note which she had left with me.
   GEORGE HARRISON(Police-constable K 64). I took the prisoner between eight and nine o'clock in the evening; I found on her the key of Ord's house.
   MARIA ORD. I am the wife of John Ord. The prisoner's daughter learned work of me; I have pawned things for the prisoner, and have lent her things to pawn, but I never had any of the money; I never allowed her to take things without my lending them to her - I did not allow her to take this bed; she had taken my fire-irons and other things.
 Prisoner's Defence. My husband has been away two years and five months; when I took the bed, and got the monthly note, I had to wait till half-past seven o'clock before I got the money for it.
   GEORGE HARRISON. The note was found on her, and I was informed she had received a month's money that day.
     NOT GUILTY.
 2136.  BENJAMIN SHARPE was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of August , 1 scarf, value 4s.; 4 shoes, value 5s.; 1 reticule, value 1s., and 3 yards of ribbon, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Thomas Sharpe.
   MARY SHARPE. I am the wife of Thomas Sharpe, he is at sea; I live in White Horse-street, Stepney. On the 14th of August I was at Bell-wharf, Shadwell, talking to my sister; the prisoner crossed over, and asked me for a bed; I said if he would come with me he should have my bed, and I would sleep with my landlady - he did so, and next morning I let him out at half-past four o'clock, and went up immediately; I then saw a new bonnet destroyed on the table; I then missed the shoes, reticule, and scarf - I have seen them since.
 Cross-examined by MR. DONNE. Q. When were you married? A. On the 26th of June, six years ago; I am a dress-maker and tailoress - I had not known the prisoner before, but we both came from the north; he had had a little drink, and I said if he would go home with me, I would give him a bed; I took him to a room up stairs, and left him there; I was not in the room with him long - I did not attempt to go to bed with him; he did not complain of losing any money.
   TIMOTHY DONOVAN. (Police-constable K 154). I met the prisoner at Bell-wharf, about half-past five o'clock in the morning of the 14th of August, about half a mile from the prosecutrix's - I had received the information soon after four o'clock; I found this scarf in his hat, and the shoes and reticule in his pocket; he said he would give me 5s. and the articles to let him go, as he had a large family at Shields; that he had had 10s. in his pocket the night before, and had been robbed of it.
   PETER COSTELLO. (Police-constable K 90). I asked the prisoner if he slept by himself - he said Yes; the prosecutrix is a respectable person.
 Prisoner. I had been in company with my brother, and had a good deal of drink; this woman came and took my arm, and asked where I was going - I said,"On board my ship;" she coaxed me to go home and stay with her; I had four half-crowns, and some other money - she pulled off my things; I said, "If I can't sleep by myself, I will go out of the house;" when I awoke in the morning I had not a farthing, and I took these things out of vexation.
 MARY SHARPE. I did not pull off his clothes; he pulled off his jacket and trousers, and left on his drawers and shirt; I did not lead him from where I saw him; he took hold of my arm, and spoke to me while I was speaking to my sister; I had been to the doctor for a bottle of stuff for my cough - I did not take him home for improper purposes; when I awoke in the morning, I heard a footstep, and I said I thought it was that man; I asked my landlady for her shawl, I put it on, and opened the door for him; he said,"Good morning," and went away.
 GUILTY. Aged 53. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined One Month.
