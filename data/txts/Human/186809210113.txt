I live at 10, Conduit Terrace, Green Lane, Green wich, and am the widow of the deceased, Henry Pogmore—on 2nd Septem ber, my husband was brought home about midnight—he complained of his head, and he said he had been cruelly used, and had got his death blow—I bathed his head, and did what I could—about 4 o'clock in the morning I went to fetch Dr. Ryder, but before he came he was dead—he was thirty-three years of age—he took a little drink, but he had not taken any for a fortnight before this—he was quite sober when he came home; but after asking me to bathe his head, he could not speak another word—he had been in the 1st Dragoons, but was doing nothing at that time—he had no pension—I had had some money left me lately. FRANCIS JAMES RYDER.
