I was out five or ten minutes—I saw them, as the privy door was open. JAMES APPLEBY. I am a labourer, living at Feltham. The prosecutor came to me, and said, "You must go along with me to the Magistrate, and say you saw Newman and Hatch follow me down to the privy"—I was drunk, and so was he—the next morning he came to me, and said, "You must come with me to Sunday"—I said it was of no use, as I was asleep—he still pressed me to go, and said he would pay me for my day's work GEORGE LITTLEWOOD. He was at work, and I told him it was no use for him to work for me, as I was not there, and could not pay him.
