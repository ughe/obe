FILBEY, Albert (30, labourer), pleaded guilty of feloniously caus-

work, and the latter struck Allerton about the head with a heavy iron
bar.
Sentence Fifteen months' hard labour, the Recorder stating that it
was a most unprovoked assault on an old man.
