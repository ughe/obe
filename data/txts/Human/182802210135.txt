 748.  CHARLES TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of February , 1 piece of mahogany, value 5s.; 1 cap, value 2s.; 1 plane, value 2s.; 1 oil-stone, value 1s.; 1 chisel, value 6d.; 1 guage, value 6d.; 1 hammer, value 1s., and 1 pair of saw-handles, value 1s., the goods of Richard Hewett.
 RICHARD HEWETT. I am a  cabinet-maker, and live in King-street, Hammersmith. The prisoner worked for me; I lost these tools at different times.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Does he not work at home? A. Yes, I have borrowed tools of him; they are at my place now - some of them have been lost - the wood was found on him, in the shop.

 749.  JAMES SHEA was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of February , 1 coat, value 5s.; 1 waistcoat, value 2s. 1 pair of trousers, value 5s.; 1 shirt, value 1s., and 1 handkerchief, value 2d., the goods of John Bell.
   JANE BELL. I am the daughter of John Bell, shipwright, of Hermitage-street, Wapping. On the 16th of February, about three o'clock in the afternoon, when my father was out, the prisoner came and asked if Mr. Bell lived there; I said Yes; he said he came from the ship for these articles; my father was working on board the Atlas; he said he came from Mr. Bell for them, as he was going out with the captain; I delivered these clothes to him, believing that he came from my father.
   JOHN BELL. I know nothing of the prisoner, and never sent him for any thing; I have not found my clothes.
   JAMES JEFFREY. I called at the prisoner's house - his brother came and gave himself up; I sent for the witness, who said he was not the man; I said if the prisoner did not come I should come and take him; he came, and she said he was the man.
 The Prisoner called -
   JULIA CALNAN. I live at No. 2, George-street, Foley-place, Regent's-park. Last Saturday week I was at the prisoner's father's apartment from half-past one o'clock till half-past four - his father is my brother; I went there in consequence of a letter I had from Ireland - the prisoner was there; I believe his brother knew Mr. Bell; the prisoner was not sitting with his mother and me - his brother is older than him.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined Three Months.
 750.  JAMES NEEDS was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of February , 3 bushels of potatoes, value 8s., and 1 sack, value 1s., the goods of William Hobbs.
 WILLIAM HOBBS. I am a  farmer, and live at Enfield. The prisoner was my carter; on the 19th of February I sent him, and some more men, to drive some potatoes home from the field: I ordered the men to load ten sacks in each cart, and no more - I went home and told my sister to mind what came in - I then went to market.
   GEORGE ROBINSON. I am in Hobb's employ; he ordered me to put ten sacks into each cart; the prisoner drove one cart: I saw that every cart was loaded with ten sacks- none of them had more nor less, I am certain; he had to take them to the yard, which is three-quarters of a mile from the field.
   REBECCA HOBBS. I am the prosecutor's sister: he told me to watch every cart; the prisoner came into the yard several times - I went out each time to see him unload, and one time he brought nine full sacks and one empty - I said nothing to him, but told my brother when he came home.
   THOMAS COREHAM. I am in Mr. Hobb's employ; he told me to assist in unloading the potatoes; the prisoner came with one load of nine sacks full and one empty - all the other loads were ten full sacks; I said nothing to him about it.
   JOHN WILSON. I am an officer, and took the prisoner; he said there was not a sack lost - that he shot a few potatoes over a gate, but who had them he did not know.
 GUILTY. Aged 24. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Three Months.
 751.  JOHN PHILLIPS was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of February , 1 pair of shoes, value 3s., the goods of Thomas Blizard.
   JAMES GIBBS. I am a butcher, and live at Hollywell-lane. On the 16th of February, about half-past five o'clock, I saw a boy go and take a pair of shoes from Mr. Blizard's shop; I ran out and pursued the prisoner and another - I caught the prisoner in Shoreditch, and saw the shoes close by him; I cannot swear he took them.
 THOMAS BLIZARD. These are my shoes - I know nothing of the prisoner.
   SAMUEL STUDD. I heard a cry of Stop thief! ran out, and saw the prisoner drop these shoes - I am sure he is the boy.
     GUILTY. Aged 12. Whipped and Discharged.
