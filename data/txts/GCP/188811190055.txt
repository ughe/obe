Smith's Defence. Mrs. Lucas is entirely mistaken in regard to my
having gone to her place; I never saw her till I saw her at the station

that house, but I never wrote one with the name of Sylvester attached,
and never heard the name-there are plenty of people in London who
know me and my writing well, and the police have got no one here to
prove it.
