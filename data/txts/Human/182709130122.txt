 1848.  JOHN LINDSAY was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of September , 1 handkerchief, value 1s.; 1 pair of buckles, value 3s.; 1 pencil-case, value 2d.; 1 key, value 6d.; 1 pair of shoes, value 5s.; 1 knife, value 5s.; 1 shilling, and 2 sovereigns, the property of Robert Thomas Griffiths , from his person.
 ROBERT THOMAS GRIFFITHS. I am a  servant. On the 12th of September, about half-past two o'clock in the morning, I was at the corner of Pickett-street, Strand, sleeping; the watchman awoke me, and asked if I had lost any thing; I missed my shoes from my feet, and these other things.
   MICHAEL BOWER. I am a watchman. About half-past two o'clock I passed the prisoner, but said nothing to him - he passed me again shortly after - I asked where he was going; he said home - soon after, I saw Griffiths laying down without his shoes, and asked the prisoner where they were - he said he did not know - I took hold of him and found them on him, also the handkerchief and all this property.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had been to the play, and found the handkerchief with the things in it - the shoes laid by the parcel.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Confined Two Months.
 1849.  WILLIAM WILLIAMS and JAMES WILSON were indicted for stealing, on the 14th of August , 1 half sovereign, and 22 shillings, the monies of James Murphy , from his person.
 JAMES MURPHY. On the 14th of August, between six and seven o'clock, I was at the corner of  George-street, looking at a mob, and had some money in my breeches pocket - Williams came and seized me by the shoulders; they thrust me into a passage of a house in George-street; Williams got my purse out of my pocket - I struggled to get it; he bit a hole in the purse and took out a half sovereign, which I have not got back - I cannot tell how much I had.
   FRANCIS THOMAS GATES. I apprehended the prisoners, whom Murphy pointed out - he charged them with an assault - I said he had better get a warrant, but he gave charge of them for a robbery in about five minutes after.
 WILLIAMS' Defence. He was quarrelling with a woman whom he struck, and I struck him - he pushed me into a passage; Wilson was chief evidence against Murphy, and he gave charge of him, because he should give no more evidence against him.
   JAMES MURPHY. I gave charge for an assault, as I did not then know I had lost my money; I struck nobody.
 WILSON's Defence. I saw the prosecutor quarrelling with a woman; he struck her, and I then struck him; he ran in and shut the door; he came out and gave charge of Williams for an assault. I was going as a witness for Williams - he then said I had assisted in the robbery.
   MARIA MINNS. I was in the street - Murphy came up and struck me - I had quarrelled with him before, because some coiners were taken up in his house, and he thought I had told of them - he struck me in the chest, and then in the side - I was three weeks in the hospital; I saw no robbery - he keeps nobody but thieves and coiners in his house.
   MARIA WHITE. I was coming up Church-street, and saw several people running - Murphy hit and kicked this woman - Williams took her part; Murphy then said he would have his spite on him.
 FRANCIS THOMAS GATES re-examined. Williams gave this account at the time I took him - Murphy keeps a lodging house in Church-lane - I afterwards saw his purse - there was a piece torn or bit out. I saw Minns that evening; she said Murphy had struck her, but said nothing about a kick - I found Williams in a room with Minns.
   JAMES MURPHY. She cohabits with Williams.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1850.  WILLIAM TOBIN was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of August , 4 half-crowns, and 8 shillings, the monies of Timothy Morgan . from his person.
 TIMOTHY MORGAN. I am a  labourer. On the 5th of August I went to Saffron-hill watch-house, to see a man whom I knew - the prisoner was sitting close to me, and I felt his hand come out of his pocket - I felt and missed my money. I then got up and caught hold of him, and was going to serve him out; then he and another got up and struck me; I lost six half-crowns, some shillings, and sixpence.
   JOHN BARNLY. I am constable of the night; I saw the prosecutor at the watch-house. I was told he had been robbed; I searched them all, but found no money on any of them; I was looking about, and behind the door of the lock-up place, I found this handkerchief with three half-crowns and four shillings in it.
   JOHN WATSON. I am a watchman; I took morgan to the watch-house to see a man; he was in liquor; he took out four half-crowns, and said, "This is my money, nobody shall have it;" he put it back again.
   CHARLES VINCENT. I was constable of the night; I took the prisoner in charge before Morgan came in; I searched him he had no money; but in his hat I found this handkerchief, in which the money was afterwards found; I know it was his, for I had him in custody a night or two before, and he had the same handkerchief then.
 Prisoner's Defence. The handkerchief came with bread and butter in it, to a prisoner named Sullivan.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Fourteen Years.
