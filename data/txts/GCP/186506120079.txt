Cross-examined. Q. Were you living at Salisbury in 1842 1 A. No;
I was then living in Hackney-road, London-I visited Mr. and Mrs. Saunders
perbaps twice or four times a year after I went to Salisbury in 1846, I did
not come up above once or twice-I should think I saw Mrs. Saunders
twenty times after her husband's death-in January, 1855, I did not want
to stop with her, nor did my wife-my wife did not propose that I should
stop in her apartments-I know the letter of the 22d January, 1865; "I
am sorry I have no convenience for Kiddle"-I did not want to go and stay
there when in London-Mrs. Kiddle would write and say I was coming, and
she might write to Mrs. Kiddle and say it was not convenient.I do not re-
member over having proposed to stay at her apartments and been denied-
I do not know what the letter of the 17th November, 1868, is in reply to
-I do not believe I ever suggested that I should stay there. I think it
probable Mrs. Kiddle might be writing that I was coming up, and she might
reply, "I have no acoommodation" - I had not constantly invited Mrs.
