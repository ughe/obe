three months-I have seen the prisoner's children ; they had not
enough to eat-I could not afford to give them much--they
had vermin on their heads, and I saw sores on Walter's head
I remember one of the twins being ill he had milk once
--the father bought a can of milk-I thought the child was going to die
I did not see that he was treated in a different way by his father-the
prisoner's room was comfortable, and he had a nice bed; but there was
only one small bed for the six children, and it was filthily dirty, and the
room was dirty too-the children fell away after the mother's death-
when I found them they appeared hungry; they caught hold of the
bacon; anybody could see that they wanted food I have seen the

coffins, as they were worth more dead than alive.
