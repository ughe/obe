 1993. MARY SAWYER was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of July , 1 half-crown, and 2 s  ., the monies of John Peek , from his person.
   MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution. JOHN PEEK . I am a sailor, and live in Leeds-passage, Greenwich. On the 27th of July, I went to the Wheat Sheaf, Greenwich—there I had some gin and water, for which I changed half-a-crown—I had a half-crown and 2 s . about me—I drank all my gin and water—I fell asleep—I was sober—I was asleep about half an hour, and the prisoner awoke me, by pulling me into her lap under the table—my money was in my breeches pocket—I felt her hand in that pocket—I found all my money was gone—the half-crown I can swear to, but the two shillings I cannot—I had been robbed once before, and I had marked my money after that—the half-crown was marked—I had seen the prisoner before I went to sleep, and had spoken to her—I had not given her any money—I had nothing to do with her.
   Prisoner.  We were drinking from one o'clock in the morning till nine or ten o'clock. Witness.  No, we were not. MARK BURN. I happened to be in the Wheat Sheaf between nine and ten o'clock that morning, when this happened—I saw the prisoner come in and sit down by the side of the sailor—I saw her pull her hand out of his pocket, but I cannot swear that she took any thing out—she put 1 s . into my hand, and I suspected she had done something wrong—I chucked  it under the form, and awoke the sailor, and told him to get an officer.
   Prisoner.  The money I had was my own—I had 7 s .—this man was so drunk he could not see, and he asked me to hand him 1 s . Witness.  No, he did not. JOHN WALKER( police-constable R  182.) I was called in on the 27th by the prosecutor—Burn was there—they were neither of them drunk—I took charge of the prisoner—I searched her and found a half-crown in her mouth—this is it—I showed it to Peek—he said "That is mine—I bit it."
   Prisoner.  I told you I had no money from the man—I said I had got half-a-crown of my own, and I would give that rather than have any bother about it. Witness.  She said she had no money at all. JOHN PEEK. This is the half-crown I had, and I had marked it. GUILTY.* Aged 22.— Confined Six Months.
   Before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
