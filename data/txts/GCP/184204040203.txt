1846. JAMES WARREN was indicted for feloniously assaulting and
wounding Thomas Tyrrell, on the 10th of March, with intent to maim and
disable him.
Mr. BODKIn conducted the prosecution.
THOMAS TYRRELL. I keep the Green Man public-house, Muswell-hill,
and have known the prisoner about a year and a half-he is a navigator
or labourer. On the 10th of March, about half-past six o'clock in the
evening, he came to my house accompanied by Page, who is not in custody
- it was about a quarter of an hour before dark- I did not see them come
in-I found them in the skittle-ground, making use of very bad language
and conducting themselves in a disorderly way-they did not ask for any
liquour-I desired the man not to take any light in when it became dark,
until these parties bad gone--they came into the house, and while there, a
man in my employ brought in a pewter pot bent up and destroyed—when
I went into the tap-room the prisoner had got one of my tap-room tables
turned bottom upwards, and himself on the top of it, I asked who of these
parties had been villain enough to destroy my property in that manner
-the pot had been sent into the skittle-ground with beer--they both made
use of most filthy language and conducted themselves so, that I ordered
them out of the house they would not go, and I sent for a constable
-I gave the prisoner and his companion in charge they used the
most filthy language, and said I might look out for myself, they would
do for me, and they were taken out of the house after some difficulty
-I afterwards told the constable I did not wish to give them in charge
if they would go quietly away-in about a quarter of an hour or twenty
minutes they returned and went to the skittle-ground again I told them
to go away--they would not--I sent for the constable again and gave them
10 charge-as soon as the policeman proceeded to take them, I received a
fall in some way, a blow which deprived me of my senses--I do not know
where it was struck, and knew nothing more till four o'clock the next morn-
ing, when I recovered--I was kept in bed three weeks, and am not quite
recovered yet-I found I was injured on the top of my head the skin
was cracked very lightly.
Prisoner. Q. Did not you serve me with a pot of beer which was on
the table? 1. No; the table was bottom upwards, and you standing on
it-I suppose your intent was to break the legs off.
