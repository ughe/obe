Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
710. WILLIAM PUGH was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of Feb-
ruary, 1 pair of shoes, value 43. 6d., the goods of Ralph Wilcoxon, bis
master.
THOMAS SOUTHERBY. I am shopman to Ralph Wilcoxon, of Oxford-
street, a shoemaker. The prisoner was his errand-boy, and he had been 80
twelve days-on the 18th of last month I ordered him to take in the goods
from outside-he took some, and the other boy took some-I went out,
and saw the prisoner with his hand by his trowsers, tucking them up!
followed him in, and said, "You have got some of your master's property
-he said, "What do you mean?"--I said, “You have got a pair of shoes
-- he said he had not-I sent the other boy for a policeman-the prisoner
said, "For God's sake don't send for him" - I found one shoe on each side
of his trowsers -those had not been out that night, but were on the sben
inside between twelve and one o'clock in the day-he must have taken
them from inside.
WILLIAM FLATTERY (police-constable C 73.) I searched the prisoner,
and found a pair of shoes concealed in his trowsers.
Prisoner. I intended to pay for them as soon as I could.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
