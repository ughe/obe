to give the testimony I have to-day.

RICHARD BAILEY. I am a printer, in the employ of Messrs. Haddon and
Co., of Castle-street. I was so on the 30th of Jan.--I know Fay--he has a
sister, Mrs. Mary Ann Alliston-I went with Fay to his sister's, on the 30th
of Jan.- he came down to me at half-past six o'clock, and I suppose it was
twenty minutes past seven when we got to Mrs. Alliston's—we remained there
till eleven.
