   JOHN HAZELL. I am an accountant, and lodge at the prisoner's mother's, in Well-street, Oxford-street. On Sunday morning, the 23d of July, I left my apartments to go to Epping-forest. Jones took him three days after, in bed. He lived with his mother, who is a widow.
   JOHN JONES. The prisoner's uncle came to me, and told me I should find him in bed at a public-house, in Clipstone-street, Marylebone. I was up there all night, and at five o'clock in the morning, a man said he was in bed at home. I went up three pair of stairs, and saw him in bed, and I told him I wanted him about a watch, he denied knowing any thing of it. I locked him up at the watch-house; his uncle wished to speak to him. I brought him out, and he said he sold it for 13 s., at No. 13, Crown-street, Soho; for three half crowns, 6 d., and 5 s. He took me to the shop, kept by Carey, it is a rag-shop. I did not find it. He told me as he went along that he had taken his cousin's shawl which cost two guineas and a half, and sold it at the same shop for 5 s. He belongs to very respectable parents, but has had connexions.
 Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing whatever of it.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years.

 1263.  GEORGE GRUDGINGTON, JOHN BOSWELL, and CHARLES SEAWARD, were indicted for stealing, on the 1st of September , one milk pot, value 1 s., the goods Timothy Greated.
   TIMOTHY GREATED. I am clerk to the London Insurance Company. I know nothing of the transaction.
   GEORGE TAYLOR. On the 1st of September, about eight o'clock in the morning, I saw all the prisoners in company together, coming down Pentonville. Boswell had an apron tied before him, he went up two garden walks, came out and joined the other two. He then went up the walk of No. 18, Pleasant-row, and got in at the window which was open, and came out with the milk-pot in his hand, and was stuffing it in among some shavings which he he had in his apron; the other two waited for him two doors off - he joined them. I took him and pulled the pot out, and asked where he got it, he made no answer. I took him to the house, the servant claimed it. I kept all three in custody.
   JOSEPH CADBY. I saw the prisoners coming down, I secured Grudgington and Seaward, they made great resistance.
   JOHN HUTT. I assisted Cadby in taking them. I had seen them all three together the morning before.
 GRUDGINGTON -    GUILTY. Aged 19.
 BOSWELL. -    GUILTY. Aged 19.
 SEAWARD. -    GUILTY. Aged 20. Confined Six Months, and Whipped.

 1265.  JAMES GEE was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of September , one shovel, value 3 s., the goods of James Hall.
   JAMES HALL. On the 5th of September I saw the prisoner lurking about a building of mine, at Limehouse, I watched and saw him take this shovel up and go into the next building, he was concealing it under the floor when I secured him, he begged forgivenness.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. A boy stole it and gave it to me.
     GUILTY. Aged 13. Whipped and Discharged.

 1266.  CAROLINE GOODWYN was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of August , one cloak, value 2 s.; one petticoat, value 1 s.; and one gown body, value 6 d., the goods of David Airey.
   DOROTHY AIREY. I am wife of David Airey, who is a carpenter. The prisoner was stopping at our house till she could get a situation. A young woman gave me information - I missed these things out of my chest and suspected her. They were at a gentleman's house which I had the care of - the family had given her leave to be there.
   MARIA HAMMON. The prisoner came to tea with me and gave me a white cloak - she said it was one of her little girls. Airey claimed it.
   MARY JUDGE. The prisoner gave me a white gown body, and said it was an old one of her own - I gave it to Airey.
   WILLIAM WESTCOAT. On the 10th of September, Mrs. Airey gave me information - I went to North-end, Fulham, to Mr. Kent's, where the prisoner was in service - searched her trunks and found a petticoat and part of a gold seal.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. On the Thursday before the robbery, I was next door and returned about eight o'clock, the street door was open, and I lost some shifts, stockings, and four sovereigns. Another box was robbed of several things, and a counterpane of my master's - they said next day they had lost a purse, containing twenty guineas, and hoped I would keep it secret, or they would be discharged, I agreed. As to the things, I do not know how I became possessed of them, unless they got mixed with mine at the mangle. The prosecutrix was much embittered against me for not conniving at her changing some feathers out of the bed - she said she would be my destruction, unless I gave her a sum of money.
   DOROTHY AIREY. I never had a dispute with her.
     GUILTY. Aged 32. Transported for Seven Years.
