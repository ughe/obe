2952. THOMAS HUNT was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of Octo-
ber, 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of Robert Banfield, from his person.
ROBERT BANFIELD. I am a servant, living at Norwood, in Surrey. On
the 7th of October I was in Holborn looking at some birds-I felt som
thing at my pocket and missed my handkerchief-I turned and saw the
prisoner close to my side, with it in his pocket-I asked him for it-
denied having it-I said I could see it in his pocket-be then ran away
I pursued, and caught him-be gave it to me this is it.
Prisoner's Defence, I saw the handkerchief down by my feet; 1 pack
ed it up, and put it into my pocket; when the prosecutor asked me
had got it, I said yes, and asked if it was his.
GUILTY.** Aged 26.-Transported for Ten Years.
