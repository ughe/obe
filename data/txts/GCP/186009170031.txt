-I have said that I had been injured -I am quite sure that I never told

before the Magistrates, told anybody that I mentioned all these circum-
stances to Varney-I was questioned upon that point-Varney had
acquainted me about his death, but I turned away when I saw he wanted to
enter into the discourse of what they had done I was asked if I had told
Varney what had happened, and I said no; I had spoken upon other
things, but not on that, that was on 2d August—I said I was not aware I
had told Varney what had happened about the kicks and blows-I am
pretty well certain that I did not tell him.
