485. MARY HURLEY, stealing 1 pewter pot, value 18, 10d.; the goods
of William Sanderson.
WILLIAM Burn. I keep the King's Arms, Shadwell. On the evening of
27th Dec. the prisoner and two other women came and had a pint of beer-
the two women became disorderly, and I put them out-the prisoner remained
about an hour and a half-she became disorderly, and the waiter was about
to put her out-the barmaid said, "She has got one of our pots"-she denied
having any-I searched her, and found a quart pot under her apron and
another in her pocket--this one is mine (produced) I know it by the marks
-it was in my house that evening.
WILLIAM SANDERSON. I keep the Duke of York, High-street, Shadwell,
a short distance from Mr. Burn's. This other pot is mine (produced) I have
seen the prisoner at my house once or twice.
