THOMAS NORMAN ( policeman, L 23). I was sent for, and saw the prisoner
and deceased - they were standing in the passage when I got in-blood was
running from the back part of deceased's head, and her face was beaten all
across-she gave the prisoner in charge for robbing her of 101., and for
assaulting her-as I was taking her to the station, she said, “Ah the d-
old wretch, I will have her life now before I have done with her."
SARAH HEMMINGS. On 30th Aug. I went into the passage, hearing a noise,
and saw the deceased lying on the floor with her head on the step, and having
hold of the prisoner's hair--they remained in that position five minutes-I
said to the prisoner, "Shall I send for a policeman, or come to your assist-
ance!" she said, "Pray send for a policeman "-I went to look for one, and
when I came back they were up-the deceased's face was very much swollen,
but that it always was-it was more swollen than usual, and a little black.
Cross-examined. Q. I believe she was constantly drank? 4. I never
saw ber sober; she fell about very much she had a very awkward fall a
week before-she was drunk at nine o'clock this very morning-I heard her
secuse the prisoner of robbing her of 101.- I never saw the prisoner drunk-
she was always kind and attentive to her mother.
Baran Cress. I live at No. 12. On 30th Aug. I came through the
passage, and saw the prisoner and her mother there--the prisoner asked her
for the key of her room-the deceased said, "No, I will not give it you, you
you bave robbed me enough " she was drunk-the prisoner then
pushed her down-she fell back, and her head came against the step-the
prisoner said, “Now will you give me the key ?" --she said, "No," and called
bera again several times the prisoner then stooped down, and beat
her across the face and eyes with her double fist-Mr. and Mrs. Hemmings
were in and out of the passage at the time, but they did not stop long-a
young man who stood there, said, "Don't beat your mother any more" --tho
prisoner said, "Shall I strangle her, and rid the world of such a monster as
she is !"-she was standing at that time, and her mother was lying on ber
buck-she bad her bonnet on, and when she got up I observed blood on it-
her face was very much beaten and bruised, and her eyes were black
quarter of an hour afterwards.
