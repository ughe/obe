1 sheet, value 28.; and 1 pillow-case, value 4d. the goods of Sarah
Burgess.
SARAH BURGESS. I am a widow, living in Bluegate-fields--I let lodg-
ines. The prisoner came and slept with a person who she represented as

out fast-I opened the parlour door, and missed the things I followed
ber-she turned round Angel-gardens, and I lost her-I did not see her

13th of April, in the name of Shand-I cannot tell who by birds
