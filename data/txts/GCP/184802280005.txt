748. HENRY BISHOP and GEORGE ARNALL, for unlawfully ob-
taining money by false pretences.
Messrs. BODKIN and ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution,
WALTER SEARLE. I am a clerk at Messrs. Barclay and Perkins-I know
the prisoners. On the 20th Aug. Bishop came and brought a cask, which he
said came from Elliott, of Bookham-he wanted the money for it, which
was 11.- I wrote a receipt-he presented this porterage-ticket--- produced)
-I then paid him 4d., and wrote this receipt for 11., which I told him he
was to receive in the other office-it is the custom for casks to be brought
back empty, and when we see the name of the customer, we pay the money
-casks are frequently received at the Talbot-inn.
Cross-examined by Mr. METCALPE. Q. If Bishop bad brought the barrel
without the porterage-ticket, what should you have done? 1. Sent him
back for it-I should have bought the barrel without the ticket being pro-
duced, but should not have given him any porterage-if he brought the
ticket I should not have given him the 11. without the barrel the porterage
would go to the person who keeps the inn-I filed the ticket and receipt, as
vouchers.
MR. BODKIN. Q. Should you take in a cask, and pay 11. for it, without
knowing what customer it came from? 4. No.
