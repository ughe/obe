OLD OURT-Saturday, June 16th, 1855.
PRESENT-Mr Ald. FARNCOMB; Mr. RECORDEB; Mr. Ald. WIRE; and
Mr. Ald KENNEDY.
Before Mr. Recorder and the Second Jury.
644. THOMAS GROVE, unlawfully obtaining of Frederick Abud and
another, the sum of 201., and an order for 421. 58., by false pretences.
MEssRs. BALLANTINE and POLAND conducted the Prosecution.
FREDERICK ABUD. I am a gold and silver refiner, of St. James's-street,
Clerkenwell, in partnership with my brother. On Saturday afternoon, 5th
May, about half past 4 o'clock, a man, whom I have not seen since, came
with this assay paper, and asked me to purchase this bar (produced)
(Paper read May 6th, '55. Mr. Anderson, parting assay, 2 ozs., 6 dwts.,
6 grs.; 7 ozs., 3 dwts., 12 grs., in lb. Troy S. palladium. Stamped,
Hatton-garden, London Office.")- knew that the persons who issued this
paper were assayers, and I knew the writing on the stamp by seeing it
oceasionally ; it is Mr. Staples, clerk to Johnson and Matthey he makes the
assay for them-I weighed the bar, and just rubbed it on a stone to test it,
but did not cut itit weighed 66 des. 8 dwts., and I purchased it of the
man for 621. 58.-I gave him twenty sovereigns, and a cheque for 42l. 58.
