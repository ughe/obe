FOURTH COURT Thursday, August 19th, 1875.
2
Before Robert Malcolm Kerr, Esq.
446. CHARLES GOUGH (23), and HENRY EUŜTACE (45), Stealing
fifty-five cases of condensed milk, value 701. of Francis Maybury, the master
of Gough.
MR. FRITR conducted the Prosecution.
No evidence was offered against Bristace-Nor GUILTY.
FRANCIS JOHN MAYBURY, I am manager to my father, who is a master
carman, of 7, St. Mary Axe--the prisoner was in his employ as ourman-on
12th August, about 7 o'clock in the morning, I sent him to Custom House
quay for fifty five cases of milk, he was to take them in two londs, fifty to
Wilkinson, of Stratford, and five to Ibbeson-he was then to come baok and
take fifty cases to Mr. Chalon, of Stoke Newington, get the receipts, and
bring them baok to me signed the value of the cases was 701. -I next
saw the prisoner about 7 o'clock in the evening in Whitechapel, where
Childs went to look after him.
WILLIAM CHAPMAN. I am a delivery foreman at Custom House quay-on
the morning of 12th August, between 10 and 11 o'clook, I delivered to the
prisoner, fifty casos of condensed milk, in one load, and fifty in another-
they were marked G and Z--this (produced) is a case of the same kind.
WILLIAM MORGAN. I am a grocer and cheesemonger of 6, Lancet Place,
Church Street, Stoke Newington-on the afternoon of 12th August a
Mr. Eustace came to my shop, my niece was there-I never saw Gough
till he was at the Mansion House--in consequence of something my niece
said to me, I went to Mr. Maybury's with Mr. Eustace-I found the case
produced along with fifty four others in my cellar.
