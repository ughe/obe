Cross-examined. I thought prisoher had the stock to sell, but I
cannot point to anything in his letter that should lead me to think
that. The letter from Tilling, which "Earle" forwarded, does not
convey that the two men were old friends. It conveyed to my mind

it is not a recommendation, but a reference. If I had not stopped the
deal it would not have resulted in a profit.
