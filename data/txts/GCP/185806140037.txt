WILLIAM HUNT. I live at No. 4, Burlington Mews, and am a carman.
On 7th May, Mr. Rutland came to my house, he spoke to me, and I went!
out with my child-I put two shillings in paper into her hand; I sent her
first, I followed her, and hid myself in a doorway I saw her standing on the
pavement, and all at once I saw a short person, with a white shawl on, come
from a passage ; the child ran up to.her-I ran or walked fast, the woman
made two or three steps from the kerb to receive the money, and moved her
hand to her; I got up just in time to take the money from the child.
Cross-examined. Do you swear that you saw her move her hand ?
A. I will—I was hiding in a doorway, with my head just outside I swear I
saw her put out her hand I saw it very plainly.
