came out again I tried to catch her, but could not, and the officer took her
at the corner of Short-street with the print.

45. EDWARD NICHOLLS was indicted for stealing, on the soth of
October, 1 bag, value 1d. ; 1 sovereign, 1 half-crown, 1 shilling, and
6 pence; the goods and monies of Henry Brown; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months; Three Weeks Solitary.
46. GEORGE LOVERIDGE was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
November, 1 oz. of rhubarb, value 1s. ; 1 plaister, value 1s. ; 6 vials,
value 158.; oz. of gum, value Is.; and 1 bottle, value 2d.; the goods of
Joseph Freeman, his master; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 26.-Confined Three Months.
47. JOHN VINCENT was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of Novem-
ber, 1 cloak, value 10s., the goods of Thomas Henry Veale Lukey.
JOHN BRADLEY. I was in the Strand, near Temple Bar, on the 2nd of
November-I saw a chaise opposite a door a young man was standing
behind it, and the prisoner on the sidejust as I got to the horse's head
I saw the prisoner take a cloak out of the chaise—there was no one passing
just at that time I stepped round the horse and caught him-he threw it
down, and the other, who was much bigger than he, ran away I took the
prisoner into a shop, and asked if a gentleman had lost a cloak-they said,
"Yes"-I left him there, and went on-I met a policeman, and told him
the prisoner asked for forgiveness.
Thomas HENRY VEALE LUKEY. I live at Milford-Jane, and am clerk
to a coal merchant. My gig stood in the Strand while I stepped into the
shop for half a minute-I came out, went on to Temple Bar, and then
missed my cloak- returned, and thought I had left it in the shop-and
the prisoner was there in custody.
