985. DANIEL WASHBROOK and JAMES GURNEY were indicted
for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 28., the goods of Edward Evans, from
his person.
EDWARD Evans. I live in Talbot-court, Gracechurch-street. On the
25th of March, about half-past three o'clock in the afternoon, I was at
Greenwich, near the College-gate, looking at a man conjuring-I per-
ceived nothing until the officer, who had both the prisoners in custody,
showed me my handkerchief--this now produced is it-I know it was safe
an hour before.
Cross-examined by Mr. Horry. Q. There was a crowd round the con-
juror? A. Yes, perhaps 100, or 150 people.
John WHITE (police-constable R 108.) I was at Greenwich, and saw
the prisoners by the College-gate-I followed them from the fair to the
College - they were together there was a crowd. I saw them go behind
Evans, and saw Washbrook take the handkerchief from his pocket and
hand it to Gurney--I secured them, and Gladwin got the handkerchief.
WILLIAM GLADWIN policeman.) I saw the prisoners by the prose-
cutor-White took Washbrook, I took Gurney, and took this handker-
chief out of his pocket.
Gurney's Defence. Washbrook told me he had picked up the handker-
chief; he gave it to me and went away.
(The prisoners received good characters.)
WASHBROOK-GUILTY. Aged 13.2
GURNEY-GUILTY. Aged 16. S
