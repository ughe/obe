THIRD COURT.-Wednesday, May 26th, 1880.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
529. FREDERICK FOGHILL (27) and HENRY HUDSON, Stealing
two portmanteaus and a wooden box, a quantity of linen, and other property
of Edward Effingham Lawrence.
MESSRS. POLAND, GRAIN, and RAVEN Prosecuted; MESSRS. BESLEY and
MORICE defended Foghill; and MR. PURCELL defended Hudson.
EDWARD EPPINGHAM LAWRENCE. I am a stockbroker, of 3, Craven Street,
Strand--my chambers are on the third floor; two rooms—I shall have been
there 14 years in August-I had two portmanteaus in the office, one was
full of my property, linen and wearing apparel, the other was empty-the
door was locked--the key was usually kept in my room on the mantelpiece
I was in the room about three weeks previous to the 19th of March, and
saw the portmanteau there, but did not open them--the contents were worth
about 31.-I also had some black boxes in the attic containing household
linen to the value of 151. or 201.--on Friday, the 19th March, in consequence
of what the housekeeper said to me, I went to the attic, about 7 p.m.;I
found the two portmanteaus had been taken away as well as a portion of the
contents of one of the boxes, and the whole of the contents of the other
-both boxes were burst open--the attic door was unlocked and opened-I
also had a packing-case on the third-floor, on the landing outside my room---it
contained cotta ornaments and other articles to the value of 207. : that was
gone-I saw it safe on the landing that morning; it had been there a long
time-I had packed it myself-I left the house that morning about 10.30-
I locked my rooms, and took the key, so that the key of the attic was locked
