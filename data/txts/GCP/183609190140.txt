SAMUEL PIERSON. I am an oilman, and live at Hammersmith. The
prisoner was in my service a week previous to the robbery-I kept my
cash-box in my counting-house-it was locked, and I had the key in my
pocket-on Monday, the 5th of September, there was about 208. in
it, in silver, as near as I can recollect-there were five half-crowns, seved
shillings, and a sixpence; also a canvass bag, containing a few pieces
old coin, and a half-guinea-I left it safe about eight o'clock-I did
examine the box again till Tuesday, at about ten o'clock-I then opened it
with my key without any difficulty-it was locked—I missed the silver,
and the canvass bag containing the old coin and a half-guinea- I have
seen two of the old coins since, and know them to be the same-one of them
I bad worn on my watch for a long time I have had them these three
years.
