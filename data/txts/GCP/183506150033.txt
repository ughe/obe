ELIZABETH POOLE. I am the prisoner's mother. He has been guard to
an omnibus for three years- I was present when he was married to Emma
Louisa Hawke, at Leyton Church, Essex, but did not notice the year-I
knew he lived with Mary Blazby-she passed by the name of Poole--they
said they were married, but I did not know that.
WILLIAM ACTON EVANS. I am deputy clerk to the parish of Leyton.
