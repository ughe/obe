"JOHN MARSHALL. I live about fifty yards from Mrs. Pollett.
On the 7th of June I was standing at my door, and hearing a cry
of "Stop thief," I looked, and saw the prisoner creeping under the
Malings I caught hold of him he was coming against our house from
Vrs, Pollett's-be pushed me on one side, and at the same time dropped
bundle from under his arm I heard a sound in the bundle like
plate--I picked it up, and ran after bim-I caught him again between
Parry-street and Paul-street--I had not lost sight of him-he made a bit
of a stop, and knocked me down, saying, "You b , what do you want?"

who took him--I never lost sight of him he was taken to the station--I
