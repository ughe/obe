 1274. JOHN MONTEITH and ANN MONTEITH were indicted for feloniously receiving, on the   24th of March , of certain evil-disposed per sons, 31bs. weight of mutton, value 1 s  ., the goods of Thomas Seabrook , well knowing the same to have been stolen; against the Statute, &c. THOMAS SEABROOK. I am a butcher, and live in Harrow-road, Padding ton. On the 24th of March a breast of mutton was stolen from me, about half-past twelve o'clock in the day—I was informed of it by a neighbour—I went out, and saw some boys—I followed them—I then went to the pri soners' room with Ridley, and asked if they had not got a boy there concealed with some meat—they said they had not—I said I was convinced that they had—they said if he was there he must be in the area—I went, and there was no one there—I looked about the room, and found the boy Young in the turn-up bedstead and this meat—John Monteith was sitting by the fire smoking his pipe, and did not say any thing.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Did both the prisoners speak to you? A.  No, only the woman—I have heard that they are husband and wife. JOHN YOUNG. I am fourteen years old, and live with my father in Little Carlisle-street—he is a policeman. I know the boy Ridley who stole the meat—I was with him and two others—Ridley took it from the shop, and gave it to me—I was two yards off when he stole it—the prisoners live in Devonshire-street—I took the breast of mutton there—I had not seen the prisoners before, but the other two boys told Ridley to take it there, and I took it—I found the two prisoners there—I set it down, and asked if they would buy it—they said they would—I said I wanted 10 d . for it—they said they would give me 8 d .—I said I would take 9 d ., I would not take less—they said they would not give me more than 8 d ., as they could buy it at a shop for 1 s .—the male prisoner was putting his hand into his pocket to pull out the money to pay me 8 d ., when Ridley and the prosecutor came in—(the female prisoner had dealt with me, but the man was going to pay me)—I saw the policeman have hold of Ridley, and I said to the prisoners, "Here is the butcher, where shall I get to?" and the woman told me to get into the bed—I went into the bed directly, which was turned up.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You have been tried for this, have you not? A.  Yes, and convicted—I was fined 1 s ., and discharged. THOMAS SAVORY( police-constable D  58.) I took the prisoners in charge.
