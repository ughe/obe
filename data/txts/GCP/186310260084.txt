MR. Sleigh to STEPHEN STEPHENS. Q. Do you remember Mr. Barnard
being present when the prisoner paid you the 11. 168. A. Yes-I put
down 128., 12., and 128., on the dates it was due- I received it about 9th
or 10th July-it was then that I let him have the cab back again.
MR. COLLINS. Q. Were all these entries made by you at once? A No;
at different times the last entry is " June 6th., 128."—that was the follow-
ing week from the time he had it-whenever be paid me any money, I
applied it.
MR. COLLINS to R. BARNARD, Q. Did you see the prisoner with Stephens
more than once? A. Only once before I saw him at the court-I was
then in Mr. Stephens' counting-house, and was about settling the matter
with bim to take his business.
