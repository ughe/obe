1917. WILLIAM ARNOLD was indicted for stealing 2000 canvas bags,
value 25l., the goods of Charles Sawing.
Mr. Doane conducted the Prosecution.
CHARLES SAWING. I live in Gibraltar-walk, Bethnal-green - I have
known the prisoner about eighteen months-he dealt in sacks-he came
to me about the middle of Aug., and asked if I had any sacks to sell-
said I had about 2000—they are called gunny-bags, and are made of hemp-
he asked what I could sell them for- I said, when mended, at not less than 38.
a dozen-he told me to give him three as a sample, and he thought he could
get an order for 2000-he called again next evening, and said he had got an
order for them from Mr. Marshall, of Cross-lane-he was to give me 38. a dozen
- they were to be mended—I said I had not money to pay for mending them-
he said he would lend me 71., and when I got my money I could pay bim
the 71. back, and pay him for his trouble-I did not have the 71.-1
might have had it, but I requested him to keep it to pay the women for mend-
ing them—they were completed, and on the 31st of Aug. they went in ons
wagon which I hired-the prisoner went with me with them to Mr. Mar-
shall's stores, at Limehouse-they were delivered there- I did not get any
money then I saw the foreman in the prisoner's absence-I went again the
same afternoon, with the prisoner, to get a receipt for the bags, because he
said Mr. Marshall would not pay it without the receipt from the foreman-the
prisoner told me the foreman produced the receipt-I went with him to Mr.
Marshall's counting-house, in Cross-lane-he went in and came out again,
and told me he had to go again in two hours' time-he wished me to walk with
him to his apartment to have some refreshment, I went, and he asked if
would take a drop of porter-he left the room, and said he was going for :
pint of porter-he left the house-I waited half an hour, but he did not return
-I then went to Marshall's counting-house, and learned that the money bad
been paid to the prisoner-they came to 251., out of which I was to have
paid him 77., which he paid for the mending.
