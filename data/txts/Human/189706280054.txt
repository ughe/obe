Prisoner's Defence : Detective White caught me. If I threw anything the constable had leisure to pick it up. I was completely led into this, I never robbed the man, nor do I know who did rob him. GUILTY .** — He then PLEADED GUILIY to a conviction at St. Mary's, Newington, on March  10 th , 1896, of uttering counterfeit coin.— Nine Months' Hard Labour.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, JULY  26 TH , 1897.
