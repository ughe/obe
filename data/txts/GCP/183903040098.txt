o'clock I went out, and when I came back my shopman told me he had

WILLIAM HAYDON (police-constable C 41.) On the 23rd of February
I was in Gravel-lane, and met the prisoner, about a quarter to twelve o'clock
at night, with two pieces of bacon, with this ticket on them-I asked
where he bought them--he said, in Piccadilly, but he could not tell me
what shop- I took him to the station-house.
Prisoner's Defence. I bought it in Piccadilly-I did not notice the
ticket on it-I took it just as it was- I think I bought it in John-street,
but there are so many shops, I do not know where.
GUILTY. Aged 32.-Confined One Month.
1020. ROBERT THEOBALD was indicted for stealing, on the 25th
of February, 2 carriage cushions, value 11., the goods of Daniel Keen.
ROBERT WILLIAMS. I work for Mr. Daniel Keen, a coachmaker in
North Audley-street. I took a carriage to Grosvenor-mews, on the 25th
of February-it had two cushions in it-I left them safe-I afterwards
saw them in the hands of the policeman - they were those I had left in the
carriage.
Thomas WELLS (police-constable C 99.) On the evening of the 25th
of February, I met the prisoner in Grosvenor-market with these two
cushions under his arm-I followed and stopped him, and asked where
he was going with them--he said to Newman-street, Oxford-street, to :
coachmaker's shop, and he had brought them from a wheelwright's shop,
down a mews, which he did not know the name of-he took me down the
mews, and there was a phaeton there-I asked him if he took them from that
- he said he did not, but a man gave them to him - I took him about three
hundred yards from where the carriage was.
