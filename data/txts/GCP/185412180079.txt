WILLIAM BARNES. My father is the toll keeper at Hammersmith-bridge,
Last Wednesday I saw the three prisoners on the bridge, between 2 and
3 o'clock in the afternoon; they were going towards Barnes-I saw them
come back about 10 minutes before 6 o'clock Jane Drennan paid me a bad
shilling—I gave her 10 d. change-I suspected that the shilling was bad,
and I put it in the detector and bent it-the officer Tilbury came up; I
gave him some information he told me to mark the shilling, and give it to
him, which I did, and he followed the three prisoners,
