Re-examined. Immediately a proposal was declined it was my duty to
enter that in this book-I entered it in pencil when it was declined to
save time, because I was entering others, but afterwards I went carefully
through, and entered in red ink, and rubbed out the pencil—I have
no power to reconsider proposals, being only a clerk-Mr. Owens had
nothing to do with anything I have had to do or say to the prisoner-1
did not lead him to suppose that any of the three proposals charged had

prisoner in a similar manner to the three prosecutors, because the prisoner
