the monies of John Carroll, her master, in his dwelling-house.
JOHN CARROLL. I am a stone-mason, and live in Short's-gardens,
Drury-lane, in the parish of St. Giles. I occupy the whole house, and
pay the rates and taxes--1 week before Christmas the prisoner came to
me as servant-I agreed to give her 18. a-week, but never gave her any
money-she had her board and lodging I only had her to mind the place
and my children-on the 27th of January I went to my desk, and there
was 91. in it--the same afternoon I gave my wife four sovereigns and &
hall, (not out of the 91.,) and gave her the key to put it to the rest

the desk locked; I forced it open, and all the money was gone-the key
was gone as well, and I never saw it afterwards-I apprehended the pri-
soner on Friday-I did not see her searched.
