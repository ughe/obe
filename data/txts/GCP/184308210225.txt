Prisoner's Defence. I was formerly in the dragoons, and went to the bar-
books frequently, to see some comrades. I was very kindly treated, both by
officers and men ; they subscribed for a suit of clothes for me, and told
me on the day in question, that if I went to the tailor, I should get them,
and also some money. I was about getting into the police again and it is
not likely I should take these things, after such kindness. As I left the
harracks a woman met me, gave me the clothes, and told me to say I had them
from my wife, and I was to pass the night with her-she was a stranger to
me; she told me to go to this shop.
EDWARD PALMER (police-constable R 231.) I heard one of the tailors say
that he was very much surprised the prisoner should commit a felony, for they
had collected for a suit of clothes for him, for some religious institution, and
likewise a little money, but they returned it, on hearing what he had done.
GUILTY. Aged 40.-Confined Four Months.
2557. JOHN SCULLION was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of July,
1 shirt, value 28. 6d., the goods of Henry William Mecham.
HENRY WILLIAM MECHAM. I am a gunner in the Royal Artillery. On
the 28th of July I had a shirt safe, about seven o'clock in the evening-I
missed it next day-this now produced is it.
John Wilson. On the 28th of July, between eleven and twelve o'clock,
I saw the prisoner coming towards the barrack-room, where Mecham and I
slept-I followed him, and saw him putting&the shirt in his breast.
John Ross. This shirt was offered to me for sale by the prisoner, on the
27th or 28th of July.
Prisoner. Q. Do you swear that is the shirt ? A. It is, I gave it to the
policeman a few hours after, when he applied for it-I paid you for it-I have
some dozens of shirts in the shop-I gave 9d. or 10d. for this-I did not put
it among other shirts under the counter-it laid on the counter when the po-
liceman came in- I had never put it by--I never bought anything else of you.
Prisoner's Defence. I did sell him a shirt, but it was not this one.
Guilty. Aged 32.--Confined Four Months.
Before Mr. Recorder.
