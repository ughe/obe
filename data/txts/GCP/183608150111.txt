THOMAS MANSEL. I am shopman to James Porter, a pawnbroker, in
Upper Park-place, Dorset-square, about a hundred yards from Edward -
street. The prisoner and his wife were in the habit of pawning things with
us-on the 29th of June the prisoner came to our shop between three and
four o'clock in the afternoon, and brought 21. 108. in silver, and asked me
to give him two sovereigns and a half for it, which I did his wife had
before that, brought 21. 10s. worth of silver as well--it contained half-
crowns, shillings, and sixpences--I gave her gold for it that was very
near four o'clock -- a few days afterwards, a female came and took some
things out of pawn--she is the prisoner's wife, I believe I cannot say the
amount she paid, as I did not deliver them myself.
