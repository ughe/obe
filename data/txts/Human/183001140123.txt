 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that she had bought the ribbon in question at the prosecutor's shop on the 19th of December , and on the day in question had taken it back to get it' changed , when she was charged with stealing it. She called three witnesses to her character, all of whom denied any knowledge of her having been in custody before .

 496.  JOHN LEARY was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of January , 1 cap, value 5s., the goods of William Turnell , from the person of Joseph Turnell.
   JOSEPH TURNELL. I am brother of William Turnell. On the 13th of January I was in Lamb-street, Spitalfields and the prisoner came behind me and took my cap off my head - I saw him with it in his hand; I ran after him, but could not get it - he ran down Wheeler-street; I saw him cross the road - I went to him, he turned , and said he would give me a punch in the month , - he hit me, and said if I did not go away he would knock me down to the ground.
   JAMES NEWTON. I am a shoemaker , and was in my master's shop in Wheeler-street. I saw the prisoner and the prosecutor about twenty minutes before nine o'clock - I heard Stop thief ! repeated ; I went to the door, and saw the prisoner, and the prosecutor challenging him, and desiring him to give him his cap - I heard the prisoner say, "I will give you a bl - y punch in the mouth;" the prisoner was walking fast, but not running - the prosecutor was in the road by the side of the curb - I left my door when I heard the threat repeated, and said to the prisoner, "Give the boy his cap:" the prisoner said he had not the cap, and had not taken it, but as the snow had fallen the day before, I saw something like a cap , but I cannot say it was that - it was gone in a moment.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going down the street , and saw the prosecutor and some other boys playing at snowballs; the prosecutor then came and asked me for his cap - I said I had not his cap, and had not had it; I told him I would hit him in the mouth - the witness said he saw me throw the cap down; he went away, and some other persons came up - the witness then returned , and said he did not see me throw it down.
     NOT GUILTY.
