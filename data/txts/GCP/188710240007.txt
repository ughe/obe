GEORGE WILLIAM Pussy, I am & mechanic employed in the telegraph
instrument office at the General Post Office--on 15th September, about
6 o'clock in the evening, I was on my way home from the Post Office with
& young man named Buchanan, also employed in the Post Office-in
Angel Street I saw the two prisoners-I never saw them before-Sumner
tapped me on the shoulder and asked me for a party named Wilkins or
Wilcox; he said they were working in the telegraph instrument workshop
-I said there was no one of that name there-he said "He must work
there, as he promised to meet us here to-night to get some platinum for
us"-while Sumner was speaking to me Beckett was standing about &
yard a half off-I asked Sumner to describe the man--he described him
as a little taller than Beckett, and of a fair complexion-he said he was
& married man with three children, living in Holloway, and he used the
Eaglet public-house-I asked him if they wanted him very particularly
he repeated that he had promised to meet them with some platinum ; that
they had had four pennyweights, and he was to bring some more; that
they paid at the rate of 158. an ounce-I said "Good night," and walked
away-as I turned to go Sumner said “Do you think you could get me
any of the stuff?”-I said “No”-he said “Do try and see what you
can do for us "-I then saw their motive-he said he would meet us
to-morrow night at the same place-I then went away-in the morning
communicated what had taken place to my superior officer in the Post
