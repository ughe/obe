   Prisoner's Defence.  I owed Girling 9 s .; he asked me for it; he stid, "Take your things away;" I said, "No, I will not take them away till I have paid you; you cannot afford to lose the rent more than I can;" with that his wife took two chairs, and a tea-board outside the door; he ran In and took the table up, and was in the act of turning it out; I took up the hammer, and was going to strike him on the arm, but it slipped out of my hand.
   MR. DRY re-examined.  There was an inch between the two wouods, and you never get a longitudinal and a transverse wound at the same time—one must have been done when he was on the right side, and the other when on the left, and he had a contused wound on the right arm, so there must have been three blows—the wound on the arm was fresh. WILLIAM GIRLING re-examined.  I had received no wound on my arm before I entered the room—all the injury was done after I entered the room. GUILTY  on the 3rd COUNT  .
