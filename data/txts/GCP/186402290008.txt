CHARLES WEBSTER (Police-inspector, M) I took Abbott and Cameron,

WILLIAM WEBSTER. These coins are all bad ; these found on Harding are
five florins of 1858 from one mould, and two of 1862 from another mould-
these two half-crowds of 1842 from Harding are from the same mould-
this florin found on Eveson is from the same mould as Harding's-the florin
of 1860, which was picked up, is counterfeit—the forin of 1858 found in
Cameron's pocket is from the same monld as the three thrown away by
Baker, among which are three counterfeit crowns of 1816, all from one
mould.
The prisoners' statements before the Magistrate :-Harding says, “ Abbott
gave the coin to me." Cameron says, “I did not know it was bad; Abbott
gave it to me." Abbott says, " It was me who gave this girl the piece; it
was me that throw the three half-crowns away."
Harding's Defence. I had them given me to mind wrapped up in paper ;

Baker's Defence. I did not throw the pieces away.
