1610. WILLIAM GRACE was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of
May, 1 shovel, value 18., and 1 grafting tool, value 1s., the goods of John
Simmons.
John SIMMONS. I work on a branch of the Western Railroad-the
prisoner worked with me there-I left a shovel and a grafting tool in a
shed, on the 2nd of May, and on the Monday they were gone--the shed
was not locked—the prisoner kept his tools there--I found him at work,
with my grafting tool, about half a mile off--these are my tools--the
shovel was at his lodging-he said they were his own.
