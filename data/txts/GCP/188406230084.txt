appetiser-I received this order on 19th September from J. Robinson,
grocer, 162, Maryland Road, Stratford, for one case of twelve bottles

that address—that was an order through Hisoock-these goods are
signed for in the delivery book on 20th September by J. Robinson-
Hisoock brought me two orders from J. Robinson, 10, Tyssen Place, one
for one case at 318., and one on 11th October for one case at 318.-we
had the receipt for those goods—I had an order in writing from Fiscock
on September 20th for two dozen for Waters, 57, Kender Street, New Oros
Road, of the value of 31. 28., and on 17th October another order for the
