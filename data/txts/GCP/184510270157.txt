Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
2132. MICHAEL KERRIGAN was indicted for stealing, on the 18th
of Sept., 1 watch, value 71., the goods of Chillman Avery, in the dwelling-
house of James Avery.
CHILLMAN AVERY. I live with my father, James Avery, at the Gib-
raltar public-house, Lower-road, Deptford. The watch produced is mine
-I saw it safe at a quarter to five o'clock, on the morning of the 18th of
Oct., at the head of my bed- I left my room, went up again about half-
past six, and it was gone-my door had been closed, but not locked-the
prisoner was billeted at our house, with four other soldiers.
THOMAS Barnes THOMPSON (police-constable R 12.) I took the pri-
soner, about half-past seven o'clock, on the morning of the 18th, and found
the watch on him.
Prisoner. Q. Was I sober? A. Yes.
(The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that he was very much in-
toxicated, and did not know how the watch came into his possession.)
Not Guilty.
Before Lord Chief Baron Pollock.
2138. CHARLOTTE MACKIE was indicted for feloniously assaulting
John Mackie, and cutting and wounding him in his cheek, with intent to
do him some grievous bodily harm.
John MACKIE. I live at No. 119, New-street, Deptford. The prisoner
is my wife-on the 15th of Oct. we had a few words before this matter
happened, which was between one and two o'clock in the day-she was
very much the worse for liquor--I had my dinner, and was just going
to return to my work, when she stabbed me in the cheek with the
knife she was using- I had been persuading her to stop in, and not go
out, and we had had a few words--we have been married five years--we
have no family-we have lived pretty bappily-when I found I was stabbed,
I went to a chemist's shop, and she went with me I do not think she
intended to do me any mischief-she did not say anything at the time
she had been using the knife at her dinner-I cannot say how much she had
been drinking-she was very tipsy-I do not think she knew sbe had the
knife or that she used it-the wound was about two inches long-it passed
slightly through the cheek-- I did not give her in charge---she was taken
by Sergeant Fenn in the chemist's shop at Deptford-I had been there
about half an hour-she was there all the time--it is about 100 yards from
my house.
