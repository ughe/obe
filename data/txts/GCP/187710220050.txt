783. ARTHUR EDWARD FREDERICK BOULTON, Feloniously
attempting to discharge a loaded pistol at Solomon Levy with intent to do
him grievous bodily harm.
MR. MILLWOOD conducted the Prosecution.
SOLOMON LEVY. I am a wholesale clothier, of 38, Upper East Smith-
field-on 18th October, about 3 p.m., I was in the billiard-room of the
Woolpack, Minories—the prisoner came in, made & remark upon the
manner in which some one made a stroke, and then he said that he would
play any one for a half-sovereign or a sovereign-Mr. Arnett answered
him in a joking kind of way ~ All right, I will play you!"-after the
game which was being played was over, the prisoner took up a cue and
attempted to strike a ball, and Mr. Arnett and the prisoner appeared
about to commence—they both made two or three ineffectual strokes,
knocking the balls about, and the marker said "I will have none of this
nonsense, there are gentlemen waiting to play," and took the cue out of
the prisoner's hands—the prisoner then walked across the room to &
young man on the opposite side, saying "I will have my half-sovereign
I have staked ”-to my perfect knowledge, he had never drawn any
money out; in fact, the marker said that he would not have any gambling-
I am positive he never staked any money-several persons said at once
"You never staked any money!”-he then attempted to strike the young
man—the marker got between them and said “You shall not fight!"-
he attempted to catch the marker by the throat, but he was too quick for
him, and caught him by his throat with one hand and by his wrist with
the other, and pushed him towards the door-I opened the door, and the
marker put him out, and told some one to ring the bell—the potman came
up, and the prisoner was put downstairs-hearing that he was making &
disturbance downstairs, I saw him outside on the pavement, and some
coppers rolling about which were handed to him, he picked some up himself,
and from a stooping position he turned towaads the door, looked round
deliberately at everybody standing there, drew a pistol from behind him,
and presented it at my head I was about two feet from him I had
never spoken to him-I ducked my head to avoid the shot, and the next
thing I saw was that Mr. Brown had collared him on one side by the
right wrist, and Detective Hutt on the other—the pistol was in his right
hand-on the way to the station I saw blood on Mr. Brown's hand, and
at the station I saw the inspector draw seven charges from the pistolthe
prisoner said to me “I am sorry I did not shoot you, you son of a bitch,
