809. DANIEL HENDERSON and JAMES TURNER were indicted
for stealing 180lbs. weight of lead, value 11. 3a., the goods of Augustas Ray-
mond, in a certain wharf, adjacent to the Thames.
MR CURwWOOD conducted the Proaecution.
HAMOND JOHN GRIFPIN (Thames police-constable, No. 83.) On the 13th of
Jan. a man came and spoke to me, and in consequence of what be said I went into
the shop of Bourne, a marine-store dealer, and found the prisoners with some
lead- told Henderson I wanted him--he said, $4 What for said, The
pig of lead"-they were standing before the counter, apparently in the act of
rolling the dealer was in the act of looking at it-I called my brother
officer in, and gave them into custody, and I carried the lead--they said they
picked the lead up on Chureh-stairs shore.
Cross-eacamined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. Who gave you the informa-
lion A. I do not know- was a man in Wapping Dock-he did not tell
me where the men got the lead- was going by, and he spoke to me.
GEORGE BOUBNE, marine-store dealer, Green Bank, Wapping. Between
ten and eleven 'clock at night I was called down, and told somebody wanted
to sell some lead--my shop was shut-when I got down I saw two persons
in the shop-1 cannot say whether they were the prisoners- directly after-
wards saw two Thames police-officersthe two persons were taken--there
was some lead in a seale on my counter] cannot say who put it in-my w wife
let the men into the shop, and called me down-I cannot swear whether it
was such a pig of lead as this that was in the scale-] would not swear to it.
Cross-examined. Q. Was your wife in the shop A. No, she came down
when the knock came, and she direetly called me-I had no sooner got
down than two police-officers came into the shop-I expect my wife did not
put this lead into the scale-I saw nobody put it into the scale-this was be-
tween ten and eleven 'clock on Saturday night.

-I missed one on the Sunday morning which I know was there on the Sa-
