out of my pocket, while others held me—they ran away—I got up and

other, and I felt my money being taken from me—I lost 188. 6d. from
my small watch-pocket—I called Murder ! ' '—the men ran to the gate—
I next saw the prisoner in the custody of the constable I had spoken to.

the young man stopped," and kept the prisoner in conversation till a
policeman came and took him—the Other two men had run away.
