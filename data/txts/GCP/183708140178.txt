Before Mr. Recorder.
2005. HENRY RUSSELL was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd el
August, 1 purse, value 3d. ; 1 sovereign, and 38.; the goods and monies
of Josiah Attwell.
Josiah ATTWELL. I am in the employ of Joseph Reeve-I was en-
gaged on board the ship Excel, which laid at Carpenter Smith's wharf.
On Thursday, the 3rd of August, master gave me a sovereign to pay some
ballast-men-I put it into a purse, with 38. in silver, and put it into my
waistcoat pocket-immediately after I went down to have my tea- I took
the purse out of my pocket, put it on the cabin table, and left it there-
came on deck after tea, and sent the prisoner down to have his tea-he is
steward of the vessel-I afterwards missed my purse, and said to him,
“Steward, I have lost my purse with a sovereign and 38. in it"-- he said,
“It is a bad job"-I afterwards saw the policeman take it from him.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. Are you quite sure you left it on
the table ? A. At first I was not-nor before the Magistrate, but I hate
studied about it since, and am now certain of it-I told the Magistrate I
thought so, but I am positive now-I searched for it in the cabin, and
every where I could think of, and down the hold, as I was not sure then that
I had left it on the table- I did not say I thought it might have fallen
overboard when I was hauling up the fender--I was not asked that ques-
tion before the Magistrate-I searched among the ballast-I was down
there about a minute, and said to the prisoner, “ It is no use looking here,
I think I left it on the cabin-table-I saw it again on Saturday, the 5th-
the prisoner. had been steward about three months-I believe he comes
from Deal-his father is a Deal pilot-I had a quarrel with the prisoner
ten days before this-I told him to do his duty-to mop the deck, and he
said, "I shan't"-he was cooking the dinner-I did not strike him-he
threatened to strike me with the tormentors, and I took them from him-1
did not say I would fight him for it-nor any thing of the sort-I never
said that I struck him-he was aggravating me, and I said, "Now take
and drop it, there's a good fellow-you want to vex me to strike you, and
get me into trouble," and that was all that passed- I can swear to this
purse-looking at it)- I bought it at Cardiff-there is one very small
black spot at one end, where I put the money in, and I know it by
the ring- I had had it about six weeks.

he had-I asked if he had a purse, and he drew this purse from his pocket
with four sovereigns in it-1 handed it to Attwell, and he said it was bus
