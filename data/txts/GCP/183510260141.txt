Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. This was the usual time of going
to work? 1. It was about seves o'clock-there was nothing to conceal
the lead-I found he worked where he said.
Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. I suppose you searched
Easton at the station-house ? A. Yes; and found 58. 11fd. on him
found nothing particular on Anderson-I knew him before he brought
the lead forward when I desired him the station-house is about 200
yards from where I took them- returned to the house in about a quarter
of an hour I took Anderson in his own house-I found no nails on
Baston.
Court. Q. How far was Easton from his master's shop? 2. Not
above thirty yards.
MR. CLARKSON. Q. Did you say you saw the lead in the scale?
A. Yes; I saw Anderson taking it from the scale-Easton must have been
in the shop about a minute and a half before I saw him there-I saw no
money pass.
MR. DOANE. Q. You swear you saw him lifting it out of the
scale? 4. Yes; it was in the scale-Anderson was hoisting it out
of the scale I saw him in the act of handing it out of the scale he
had not got the weight of it in his hand-he had just got hold of it to lift
it out, just as I was looking into the window-the scale was quite clear of
the counter, in the centre of the shop-Anderson admitted before the
Magistrate that it had been in the scale-the small piece of lead was rolled
up inside the large piece.
Jury. Q. Did the prisoners seem to be acquainted ? A. I did not
hear any conversation between them.
John Howe re-examined. The lead cost me about 4s. or 4s. 6d. -
the selling price is about 158. or 16s. per cwt. I mean, to be sold
as old lead this is new leadit cost me from 198. to 195. 6d. per
cwt. I do not charge 4d. a pound -a small quantity would be
charged higher than a large one if it was cut out of a sheet, I must
charge inore than for the whole sheet, as it would be spoiled in cutting out
--the thirty pounds did not cost me as much as 58. 114d.-I should
charge 248. or 25s. per cwt.
Easton's Defence. On the 25th of September, I was employed by Mr.
Howe to do a job at the Three Tuns-he told me to put some lead round
the back as flushing, and new pipe-I measured the piece for the back,
which was seven feet four inches, which I cut off a whole sheet-I took
some pipe to the job on Friday afterndon in cutting the old pipe, I saw
the bottom of the sink would not stand, and I thought I would take this
piece of seven pound lead, as it laid there, thinking to cut a piece out, to
put in the bottom of the sink-the other piece I took to cover some wood-
work, which I thought would complete the job, although Mr. Howe did
not tell me to do so.
JOHN WOODHOUSE re-examined. He was not going in a direction to the
tee Tuns-he had passed the place he ought to have turned down to
go there he was not above four or five yards out of the direct road.
(William Baker, cabinet-maker, Redcross-street, Borough, gave Easton
a good character.)
EASTON-GUILTY. Aged 28. Transported for Seven Years.
