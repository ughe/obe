defended Plume. Fin
DAVID WUTELAW. I am manager to Messrs. Eyre and Spottiswoode-
Bick has been in their employ about twenty-six years as book mounter--he
had the opportunity of taking Church Services if disposed- I do not know
Plume- I called Biek into the room from which the books had been taken,
and the result was that I gave him in custody-here are, I think, twenty-
four Church Services produced, varying in price from 10. to 256) all quite
new, and the property of the firm--their entire value is from 201. to 261.
Cross-examined. We may sell thousands of these Church Services in a
year-I say that these have not been sold, first, because they have no mark
in them of the retail bookseller's, and second because I have referred to the
books in each case, and find no entry of their being sold two other men
had access to them, but Bick confessed it--we never had cause to doubt his
honesty before.
