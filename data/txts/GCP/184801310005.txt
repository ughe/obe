555, JAMES SHAW FORSTER, unlawfully obtaining goods by false
pretences.
Messrs. BALLANTINE and ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM Fears. I am a clerk to the Eastern Counties Railway Company,
The prisoner was engaged by the Company in the granary department-it
was his duty when goods came in, consigned to persons in London, to send
them out by the carman-I have the order-book here (produced)--here are
fifteen sacks of peas, nine sacks of potatoes, and sixteen and a half quarters
of beans, entered as consigned to Messrs. Noble on 16th Oct., and on 18th,
twenty-three and one-eighth sacks of peas--the entries are in the prisoner's
writing-he should send a bill of this kind (produced)-it ought to specify
the right amount of cartage charged-he should also send out two tickets, one
to leave with the consignee, and the other to bring back signed by him, one
would also be left in the book as a check-I produce a ticket-book-here is
the counterfoil check of the twenty-three and one-eighth sacks of peas on
18th Oct., and 58, 9d. is entered on it as the amount of cartage payable by
Messrs. Noble--that is in the prisoner's writing these two tickets are what
be sends out-it was his duty to write the right amount on the front of the
ticket before giving it to the carman-on the back of the ticket there appears
to have been 8s. altered to 58. 9d.-the prisoner was to pay the amount of
the cartage to me--this is his petty cash-book, 58. 9d. is there entered as the
cartage received-I received that 58. 9d. from him, and no more on 16th
Oct. 38. 9d. was the proper charge-5s. has been the amount at the back of
the ticket, and it has been altered to 38. 9d.--the prisoner paid me 88, 9d.
