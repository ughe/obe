143. JAMES WALLACE (28), Robbery, with violence, with others, on
Moses Morris, and stealing a watch and chain, a hat, and umbrella, his pro-
perty. MR. THORNE COLs conducted the Prosecution.
MOSES MORRIS (Interpreted). I live at 2, Samuel Street, Spitalfelda,
and am a skin-dresser-about 12 o'clock on this day week I went into &
beer-house in Spitalfields, and saw the prisoner there--I left, and he followed
me-he pushed me, and I fell—he laid on my breast, and beat me I am
perfectly certain the prisoner is the man--there were several others there,
but I did not observe any one else but him--one of the other men laid
across my legs whilst the prisoner was on my breast-the prisoner struck
me on the head, and wanted to take my watch away-I struggled-then
he tore two buttons from my coat, and I became unconscious-before I
became unoonscious I missed my watch and chain, hat, and umbrella--I
valued the watch and chain at 21. 10s, the umbrella Bs., and the hat lo.
I went home when I came to myself, and the next day I went to the police-
station, where I saw the prisoner.

signed it-it was read in English, and then some one told me in German
