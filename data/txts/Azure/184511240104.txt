WILLIAM FRANCIS. I am an ironmonger, and live at No. 21, Chapel-street,
Islington. On Saturday night, the 22nd of Nov., I slept at No. 16, West.
Street, Smithfield—I had with me four sovereigns, and 4d. in copper—I put
the sovereigns into my waistcoat-pocket, and put my waistcoat under my
pillow—the copper was in my trowser's-pocket, on the bed—Alfred Knight,
the prisoner, and three others, also slept in the room—six altogether—the
prisoner slept in a bed about a foot from mine—I awoke between six and
seven in the morning—I found my waistcoat, but the sovereigns were gone,
also the coppers from my trowsers—one halfpenny was marked, and I could
swear to it—the officer has it—the prisoner was charged with this, and pro-
duced some money.
Prisoner. Q. Did you gay you missed anything before a handkerchief
belonging to another person was found under your head? A. No, I did not
——1 found a handkerchief near my waistcoat—every person in the room was
called upon to show their money, and the marked halfpenny was found
among the money you produced the second time—it is beaten with a hammer
—I had noticed it the night before—it is marked all over.
ALFRED KNIGHT. I slept in the room—the prosecutor complained of losing
his money—every person was called upon to show his money, which they
did—the prisoner was the last to do so—I saw a halfpenny among his money,
which I knew.
FEANCIS TAYLOR. I am landlord of the house. I was called up on the
Sunday morning, by Knight, who said he had lost some money—I went up
into the room, and told them if they were honest, to show what money they
had about them ; and they all voluntarily did so—the prisoner •howed his
money—among it was 6d., and a halfpenny, which were identified by the
prosecutor and Knight—the halfpenny was battered very much—I took tbø
money into my possession, sent for a policeman, and gave it to him.
Prisoner. Q. Did not I show my money directly you asked me? • A.
Yes.
THOMAS BROWN (City police-constable, No. 218.) I took the prisoner—I.
received some money from Taylor, which I produce.
WILLIAM FRANCIS re-examined. This is the halfpenny I know.
ALFRED KNIGHT re-examined. This is the halfpenny that I saw among
the prisoner's money.
Prisoner's Defence. I had 48. 8d. found on me. which was my own

THOMAS BROWN re-examined. I found 48. 8d. in money altogether—I
