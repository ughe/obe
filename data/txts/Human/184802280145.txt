   Prisoner. Q.  Did not you strike me? A.  Never—I did not say I would serve you out and get you a lodging, because you went to a house in oppo sition to mine—I did not follow you into the street and knock you off the kerb-stone. JOHN STEPHENS. I am a cheesemonger, opposite Mrs. Trafford's. I heard screams of "Murder!" and "Police!" went and saw the prisoner, Mrs. Trafford, and her daughter struggling in the road together—the prisoner gave Miss Trafford four or five very savage kicks about the lower part of her person—he had a little hair clinging to his fingers—I interposed—it was I that tore his coat—I did not the witnesses gave him that—I had a great deal to do to keep the people from punishing him—he was given in charge.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did not you strike me? A.  No—you were not knocked down—it is a respectable house.
   Prisoner's Defence.  It is the result of vindictive feeling, through my having left off lodging at her house, and gone to a house opposite; the daughter said she would serve me out. GUILTY . Aged 32.— Confined Six Months .
