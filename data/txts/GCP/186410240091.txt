Mr. Benett, notwithstanding the remonstrances of Mr. Marshall's attorney,
sent Mr. Byrne away, and be returned to Loudon before the cause was called

communication with the defendant's attorney, whom he supplied with invoices,
letters, and other documents for use in the cause. To the surprise of Mr.
Marshall, Mr. Benett entered the box as a witness for Captain Blakely. The
counsel for the defendant did not, however, ask him a single question, and
the jury without hesitation found a verdict for the plaintiff for 4761, which,
with the costs, has since been paid. The following correspondence subse.
