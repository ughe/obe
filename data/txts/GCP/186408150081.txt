818. ELIZA WILLIS (15), Unlawfully obtaining certain goods from
Samuel Francis Sheppard, Stephen Fullalove, and Joseph Cohen, by false
pretences.
MR. DALEY conducted the Prosecution.
MARY ANN SHEPPARD. I am the wife of Samuel Francis Sheppard, &
china dealer, of 95, Bartholomew-close on Tuesday, 5th July, about 11 in
the morning, the prisoner came to our shop-I had not seen her before-she
asked me if I let things out on hire for weddings I said, “Yes," and she
selected a dozen cups and saucers, a glass butter dish, a glass sugar bnsin,
two large meat dishes, and other things-she said that they were for a wed-
ding, for Miss Collins, No. 1, Cloth-fair, and asked me if I could let her
have them in the evening-I said, "Yes," and she went away-about an
hour afterwards she came again, and asked me if I could let them bave the
things directly, as they wanted them before 2 o'clock-I said I could not, as
Mr. Sheppard was out-when he came home he poaked up the things, and
took them to 1, Cloth-fair, before 2 o'clock-I knew that a Miss Collins
lived at that place— I thought the things were for her ; if I had thought they
were for the prisoner I should not have let her bave them she told me
that if we rang the bell she would answer the door, which she did.
SAMUEL FRANCIS SHEPPARD. I am the husband of the last witness-on
Tuesday, 5th July, about 2 o'clock, I took these goods to 1, Cloth-fair-I
saw a female there I did not take sufficient notice to swear to her-I left
the goods with her; they were worth fourteen shillings-I left them on the
first floor landing I have never had them back, or any money for them.
