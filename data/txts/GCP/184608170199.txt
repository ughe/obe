1705. JOHN LARKINS and ANTHONY BARRETT were indicted for
stealing 1 coat, value 108., the goods of James Pincott Cornwall Reynolds.
JANE REYNOLDS. I am the wife of James Pincott Cornwall Reynolds, of
Leyton in Essex. On the 3rd of Aug. I saw the two prisoners in the hall of
our house--they had no business there-Larkins took my husband's coat and
ran away-Barrett did not come in, he stood on the steps-Larkins went to
him with the coat, and they ran away together -- they dropped it in
the garden.
Larkins. It was not me took it. Witness. I am quite sure of his person.
MARY WOODFORD. I saw the prisoners going over the gate-I am quite
sure they are the two persons. "
Larkins Defence. A person was standing at Mr. Reynolds's gate; the
bell was broken; he asked me to get over, which I did ; the coat was near the
door, I did not touch it ; it was Charley Evans took it.
(Larkins received a good character.)
LARKINS-GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Two Months.
BARRETT*-Guilty. Aged 16.--Confined Four Months.

1706. JAMES LANGFORD was indicted for embezzlement.
WILLIAM Bangs. I live in New-row, Deptford, and deal in fire-wood-
the prisoner was in my service- it was his duty to make bundles of wood,
and to go out with it occasionally, and receive money for the wood when he
sold it--on the 17th of July I sent him out with wood-his own boy went
out with him, with a horse and cart--the boy came back with the horse and
cart--the prisoner did not return--he was taken on the 31st of July-be
ought to have returned on the evening of the 17th of July, and if he had
received any money he was to pay it the same night, when he came home--he
was to sell the wood by the hundred-if he got more than the price I charged
he was to keep that to himself-the wood I sent him with that day was worth
16. 168. 91d.-I produce a note which he sent to me when he was taken-
some man brought it to me-I do not know the writing.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not lend me the horse and cart ? A. No.
