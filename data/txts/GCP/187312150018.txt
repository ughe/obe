71. EDWARD WILLIAMS (54) was indicted for feloniously marrying
Sarah Jones, on 23rd December, 1868, his wife being then alive.
MR. MILWOOD conducted the Prosecution, and MR. WARNER SLEIGH the Defenc.
HENRY Dawson. I apprehended the prisoner on the 11th-I said I
charged him with marrying Surah Jones, his first wife being alive—he said
“She will have to prove that "-I said "She will have ample proof, and will
be able to do so "-he said “She was a wicked, designing woman, aud this
is the result of it"-I said "I have been informed that you were married
in 1840, at the Abbey Church, Bath"-he said “Yes, my first wife left me
twenty-two years ago, and I have not seen her since, and I believe she is
dead - produce certificates of both marriages.
PETER CHARLES Buxton. I am & smith, and live at Bath--the prisoner
was married to my sister, Elizabeth Buxton; I was not present at the
ceremony-I saw them go to church und return, and I was present at the
marriage supper at his father's

23rd December, 1868, I was present at the Register Office, Piccadilly, when
the prisoner was married to Sarah Jones-he said he was a widower.

known her fourteen years.
