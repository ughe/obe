CHARLES WOOTTON (policeman, 276 R.) I went to the prisoner's house
on the 29th Sept., I found Fisher there-I searched part of the premises, and
behind some brick work, under the rafters, I found a great quantity of brass
and iron articles concealed-I found a few nails in a basket in the small
cellar place---part of the things I found were old, but they are mostly new.
Cross-examined. Q. Was this lock found there ? A. Yes-it was behind
the brick-work which supported the rafters under the stairs--I put my arm
in and there was a lower place where these things were—it was a dark place.
CATHERINE CLAYTON. I have lodged in the prisoner's house about three
months, I have one room entirely, and the use of the kitchen when I want
it to boil my kettle-nobody else lived in the house but Mr. Ellis, and his
wife and three children- I think his father has been dead five years I never
knew of this property till I saw the officers find it.
Cross-examined. Q. You never saw the chest opened ? A. No-it stood
in a dark place --I never saw any use made of it-there was a mate of the
prisoner's lived there, but he went away, and I had his room--I have known
the prisoner six years, he bore an excellent character-his father was a
builder-I have heard that the chest was his father's.
DAVID Roy, I am a clerk in the store-keeper's office in Woolwich Dock-
yard. Some of these articles are old-these others are new-these nails
and this lock have never been used, nor these other articles-these are all
marked with the broad arrow as being the property of Her Majesty, and are
all new--I have known the prisoner four or five years -- I believe he has been
seven or eight years in the establishment-it is no part of his duty as a
carpenter to take away any of these things-if he had occasion to use articles
of this description, he was bound to use them in the yard.
Cross-examined. Q. These things would not be in the carpenter's depart-
ment? A. Yes--all of them.
Mr. Hall, of the Dock-yard, Woolwich, gave the prisoner a good
character.
GUILTY. Aged 41.-Confined Six Months.
2480. SARAH WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing 43lbs of bacon,
value 38. 6d.; the property of Stephen Winder.
STEPHEN WINDER. I am a grocer and cheesemonger, at Greenwich. On
the 20th of Oct. I saw the prisoner take a piece of bacon of 4 lbs weight
from the window, and put it under her cloak-she went across the road-
I went after her, and in taking the bacon from her she dropped it this is it
-it is my property, and the piece I saw her take.
Prisoner. I hope you will have mercy on me.
GUILTY.-Confined Three Months.
