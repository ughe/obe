   Cross-examined by MR. GILL . This was my first visit on this occasion—I saw the club about 1891—the posts of the ring have cushions on them—I do not know if the men were protected with cotton wool; I was not present—these are 6oz. gloves—it has been the practice for years past that notice is given to the police when competitions and contests are taking place at the club—there are no boxing competitions in the Police Force. GEORGE SHEPHERD. I am the glove attendant at the National Sport ing Club—these gloves ( Produced ) are what are known as 6oz. gloves, and are the actual ones used in the contest on April 22nd.
   Cross-examined by MR. HALL . Four-ounce gloves used to be used these were made 2oz. heavier—I think the change was made about 12 months ago; I do not know why; I suppose because the club thought they would be on the safe side.
