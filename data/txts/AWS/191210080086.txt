Police-constable JAMES SMITH, 143 H. On July 24 at 3.15 p.m.
I was patrolling in front of Free Trade Wharf and assisted to clear the
streets I did not draw my truncheon. I saw MeGregor with 20 to 30
others opposite the wharf entrance he called out, Come on, boys,
get your hooks out, let us have their f-ing guts out."' I saw no
hook in his hand He was arrested by Wakefield, 69 KR, became very
violent, and I assisted to get him through the wicket gate into the
wharf. At about 3.35, when the streets were cleared, I assisted in
taking McGregor to the station.

Police-constable Smith and I had got MeGregor inside the wharf he
said, I I have done nothing." He was afterwards charged with in-
