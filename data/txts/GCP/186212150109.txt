JOHN WILLIAMS. I am a working carpenter, and live at 4, Hamilton-
terrace, High-road, Lee-on Saturday, 15th November, I was possessed of
some tools, and was at work in a workshop in a field belong to Mr. East, of
High-road, Lee--we left off work at 4 o'clock in the afternoon, collected on
tools together, put them in a basket, and left them in the shed, which was
properly locked and secured-these are the tools (produced)--this plane and
this square belong to me--my name is stamped on them ; also on two gouges
and two chisels--1 also identify this rule and compass -I know the compass,
because it opeus on one side of the joint-it does not close in the joint-
there is a defect in the workmanship of it-I know the rule by its having
two small holes in it, made by a bradawl-it happened to be under a piece of
wood that I was boring-I went to the shed at half-past 8 on the next
Monday-morning, and found my tools gone--the foreman had been there
before me, and found the tools gone-l reckoned the lot lost between us to
be worth about 76
