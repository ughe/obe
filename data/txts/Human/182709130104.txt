 1777.  JOHN WELCH was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of July , 4 half-crowns, and 15 shillings, the monies of Robert Goodson.
   ELIZABETH GOODSON. I am the wife of Robert Goodson - he lives in Rosemond-street, Clerkenwell. Between two and three o'clock in the afternoon of the 25th of July, I was sweeping the parlour, and on turning round I saw the prisoner on the wrong side of the counter; he appeared to be returning from the till; I was frightened, and let him go: I then called Watkins out of the bake-house, who pursued - he ran down Corporation-lane, but was taken; he had taken two handsfull of silver, and left the till open: there was 8s. 6d. found on him, but I missed more than that; I had seen the till safe not two minutes before. The prisoner was a stranger to me.
   THOMAS WATKINS. The prosecutrix called me - I pursued the prisoner, and caught him in Ashford-street; he had 8s. 6d. in his hand; he offered it to me, but I refused to take it.
   GEORGE HEFFER. I am an officer. I received charge of the prisoner, and produce the money, which I have had ever since.
     GUILTY. Aged 14. Transported for Seven Years.
 1778.  JAMES WHITE was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of August . 5 handkerchiefs, value 10s., the goods of Thomas Dexter.
   WILLIAM COVENTON. I am in the employ of Thomas Dexter - he is a pawnbroker, and lives in Whitechapel-road. On the 4th of August I saw the prisoner take these five handkerchiefs off the ground - I cannot tell how they came there; I had hung them up in the morning on a book, about two yards within the shop; it was between two and three o'clock; he stood just outside and reached in - he had not been in the shop; I followed him, stopped him, and asked what he had got in his hat - he said nothing. I told him a person had seen him put some handkerchiefs into his hat, and desired him to come back - he said he would; he turned, and came back a few steps - he then gave me the handkerchiefs out of his hat, and wished me to leave him - I said"No, I will take you back;" he kept dodging me about, and then ran away. I pursued, called for assistance, and some gentleman stopped him, and took him to my master's, who gave him in charge.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 30. Transported for Seven Years.

 1780.  SAMUEL SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of July , 1 handkerchief, value 2s. 6d., the goods of John Burge , from his person.
 JOHN BURGE. On the 23d of July I was at the  Camel public-house, Kingsland-road; I had been playing at Skittles, and pulled my jacket off - my handkerchief was in the pocket; I missed it - I made inquiries, and no one knew any thing of it; I had the doors closed, and every body searched. The prisoner had been there, but was gone - he returned in about a quarter of an hour, and was told what had taken place; he came to me, and said, "I beg your pardon - I know where your handkerchief is, and if you will go with me you shall have it;" we went to a stall in Huntingdon-street, just by - he asked Archer's wife for the handkerchief which he had left there; she said her husband was gone out, and had got it with him; he then said to me,"It is all right - you shall have your handkerchief;" I said,"When I get the handkerchief I shall say it is all right - but you must go back with me;" Constantine came, and took him, and as we were going to the office we met Archer, running with the handkerchief. My jacket had been off a quarter of an hour or twenty minutes - I do not know whether it was taken while my jacket was on or not.
 Cross-examined by MR. J. ALLEY. Were not many persons about? A. Yes, and many coats and hats were off- it might have been put into the prisoner's hat. When I first missed it I offered half-a-gallon of beer to any one who would give it me, if they had done it for a joke. The prisoner told me he knew where the handkerchief was, but the officer had then been sent for.
 COURT. Q. Did he know the officer had been sent for? A. Yes - I told him myself, when he returned, but at the time the officer was sent for he was not there. When Archer came, he said he had brought the handkerchief, which the prisoner had left with him, and told him he would call again presently; neither Archer nor his wife said he left it with them to find the owner - he said he did it for want - he had had no work for a long time.
   JOHN ARCHER. The prisoner put this handkerchief into a little basket under my stall, and said he would call for it presently. As soon I heard what had happened I went and took it down to Constantine.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that he had put his hat on the ground while he played at skittles, and after putting it on again he found a handkerchief in it, which he left with Archer, to take care of while he went to inquire who had lost one. He received an excellent character.
 GUILTY. Aged 19. Recommended to Mercy. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.
