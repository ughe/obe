I mean this particular sovereign, it is a George and Dragon sovereign of 1824"—the prisoner hesitated for a few seconds, and then said, "I received it from the bank with other money"—Mr. Sculthorpe asked him what bank, and he said, "The Provident Bank, St Martin's-lane"—Mr. Sculthorpe said, "When?"—he said, "About three or four months ago"—he was then given into custody—I took possession of the sovereign—I have had it ever since, and now produce it—from half-past 6 o'clock, when I went to relieve Peak, down to the time when Blackburn, brought these papers from the closet, no one had been to that closet but the prisoner.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Was not what the prisoner said, that he got the sovereign from the bank last month? A.  No; I did not try to ascertain from the Provident Bank, in St Martin's-lane, whether he had drawn out any money the month before, I am not aware that any one has done so—I had no instruction to go—there is a savings bank there, called the Provi dent Institution—he did not say he got it out of the savings bank last March—he said "three or four months ago," he never mentioned any month—my deposition was read over to me before I signed it—this is my signature ( The witness's deposition being ready stated : "The prisoner than stated he received it from the bank in March last")—it is my impression that he said "three or four months ago"—I will not say that what I said before the Magistrate was not true—I think he did say "March last)" I cannot say positively, it is some time ago—when Peak first spoke to him about the sovereign he said, "I received it last week for my wages"—he did not say he had saved it from his wages and drawn it out from the bank in March last—he said nothing
