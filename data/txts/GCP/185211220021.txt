HENRY WILLARD. I am a licensed victualler, at 31, Coleman-street; I
reside there, and carry on my business there I went to bed on the night in
question about 12 o'clock-the fanlight was then safe--I always examine the
house every night before I go to bed-there are three doors to my house
opening into White Horse-court, and one into Coleman-street-I saw the
treacle on the sides of the doorpost, and on the prisoner's shirt-I have no
doubt that it was treacle; I smelt it-I do not know that I examined the
coat, but I did the shirt-it was pointed out to me at the station.
