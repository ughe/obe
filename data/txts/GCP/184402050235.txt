GUILTY. Aged 24.-Confined Nine Months.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
814. PHILIP BOWDEN was indicted for stealing 1 pewter pot, value
28., the goods of James Gale; and that he had been before convicted of
felony.
WILLIAM Pearson. I am servant to Mr. James Gale, who keeps the
Hen and Chickens at Walworth-common. This quart pot is his-I was going
round for my pots last Friday morning, between nine and ten o'clock-I saw
the prisoner pass, facing me, and he chicked a bag on his back-I ran and
caught him, and asked what he had got in the bag-he said, “Nothing" - I took
the bag off his back, and shook this pot out, and asked where he got it-he said
a boy gave it him to carry-it bad been at a customer's house--I had seen it
safe at my master's on Thursday night.
JOHN SMITH (police-constable P 60.) I took the prisoner, and have the
pot...
Prisoner's Defence. A person asked me to carry it.
John GIYFORD. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former con-
viction, which I got from the clerk of the peace of Surrey-(read)—I was
present at the trial—the prisoner is the person.
Guilty. Aged 15.--Confined Six Months, and Twice Whipped.
