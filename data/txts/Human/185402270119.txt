   MR. LILLEY . Q.  Why is it you recollect it better now; did the wedding call it to your mind? A.  Yes; when I was before the Magistrate I was asked the date on which this happened—it was with respect to the exact day that I could not tell—I recollected it afterwards, from other circum stances bringing it to my memory—it was in consequence of a day of my own, which I had to receive next day; I was to go to work on the 13th at another place—I recollected about the wedding, that assisted me in the date—I did not count the money.
   COURT to THOMAS BUSHELL . Q.  Did Wilkins ever speak to you about returning the 6 s . to the prisoner? A.  Not to me; he never asked my leave to return it; he never told me he had received it, nor did I know he had agreed to return it.
   COURT to GEORGE STUPPLE . Q.  Did you receive any answer to that letter of yours? A.  No; when I received the bill I enclosed it to my brother; it was not receipted, as it is now.
