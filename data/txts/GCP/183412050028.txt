219. GEORGE WATKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of De-
cember, 1 handkerchief, value 28., the goods of Samuel Osborne, from his
person.
SAMUEL OSBORNE. I live in Bishopsgate-street, and am apprenticed to
my father. On the morning of the 22nd of December, as I was passing
along Bishopsgate-street, I felt something at my pocket-I turned round
and saw the prisoner with my handkerchief in his hand-I collared him,
took him to Gracechurch-street, and gave him into custody of an officer,
with my handkerchief.
Prisoner. I picked it up. Witness. I saw nobody near enough to
take it but him-I turned round directly, and found him close to me with
it-instantly
JAMES SOUNDY (City-officer No. 53.) I took charge of the prisoner,

for work, and saw the handkerchief on the ground-I took it up, not
knowing who it belonged to-Henry Gilbert, of No. 7, Globe-road, was
here yesterday to give me a character, with several others.

BAUMER, were indicted for stealing, on the 4th of December, 83lbs. of
cotton wool, value 21.; and 1 bag, value 3d.; the goods of William Wilson
and another, the masters of the said Stephen Jordan

court which leads into Golden-lane-after Leonard placed it on Baumer's
back, I saw Jordan drop from the gates-I came down stairs to the door,

came by, and they joined company--I went out at my front door, down
