 459. RICHARD SMITH and JOHN BROWN were indicted for stealing, on the   19th of December , 6lbs. of figs, value 2 s  .; and 1 box, value 3 d  .; the goods of Edward Stead . CHARLES SMITH. I live at Westham, in Essex. On the 19th of De cember, at half-past two o'clock in the afternoon, I saw both the prisoners close to the shop of Edward Stead—I watched, and law Smith go into the shop, take out a box, put it under his coat, and come out to Brown, who was waiting at the window—both went away together with the property into Grove-field—I went and apprehended them in the field—I saw them hiding the property by the side of the ditch, behind a post—they then came towards me, and I took them into custody—I went to the spot, and found the drum of figs. HENRY CLARK. Smith gave me information—I saw the prisoners con cealing something, and found the drum of figs at the spot—I took Brown, and Smith took Smith. EDWARD STEAD. I live at Westham. This is my drum of figs, which was taken out of the window, and is worth 2 s . 6 d .
   Smith's Defence.  If the officer saw me come out of the shop with them, why did he not take me?
   Browns Defence.  I know nothing of the transaction—I went to the field to ease myself.
   SMITH— GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months.
   BROWN— GUILTY.* Aged 21.— Confined Six Months.
