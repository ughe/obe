A. I believe he had twenty-five years ago—I do not know whether there

MR. RIBTON to JOHN BERT. . Do not you know that at the time of

(MR. RIBTON submitted, that as regarded the three first counts of the
indictment, there was no evidence to go to the Jury that the defendant ever
received a farthing of the money obtained from Mrs. Burton, and in the recent
case of Regina v. Garrett it had been held that the party making the false pretences
must obtain the money for himself, and that it would not be sufficient for A
to make a pretence by which В obtains something; that as to the charge of
conspiracy, there was no evidence to show any conspiracy with Crutchley, the
letters that had been read only showing a connection between Wright and the
defendant. The COURT considered that the case was for the Jury.)
GUILTY. Aged 37.-Judgment Respited.

118. CHARLES YOUNG, stealing 41., in money, of William Baynes
Netherwood.
MR. SLEIGH conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM BAYNES NETHERWOOD. I am a retired officer from the East
India Company's service. I live at the Yorkshire Hotel—in June, 1852, I
had two dogs in my possession; they were at Harlsdon-green, five miles from
town-during my temporary absence I lost those dogs-on my return the
prisoner introduced himself to me, and said he could and he would find them
and return them to me, if I would give him 4l.-I parted with 41. to him
on the faith of his restoring the dogs- I gave him four sovereigns in presence
of an officer of the police he went away with it the officer followed him,
but he gave him the slip, and got away-when I gave him the money
he said he would restore the dogs in ten minutes, or a quarter of
an hour.
