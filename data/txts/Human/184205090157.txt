Between eight and nine o'clock in the morning, on the 3rd of May, I was in the parlour at the back of my shop—I saw the prisoner in the shop—I saw him take two pounds of leather off a nail, wrap it up, and put it under his coat—the leather produced is mine—it is marked—part of it was cut up.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  He threw it back into the shop, I believe? A.  Yes, after he got outside—a person named Thomas, a shoemaker, has part of the shop, and I have the other part—we are not partners in the business, only in the shop—it was my leather the prisoner took. JOHN COLEBY. My master called me, and I saw the prisoner with the leather under his coat, outside the door—I asked him what he wanted—he asked if there was a man named Harris lived there—I told him no—he then threw the leather back. LUKE NIXON. I am a policeman. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got at Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the man. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Transported for Seven Years.
 1618. JOHN COSGROVE was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of May , 1 jacket, value 15 s  ., the goods of Lewis Joseph Hart ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. JOEL JOSEPH HART. I am shopman to my brother Lewis Joseph Hart, of Ratcliff-highway. I hung a jacket outside, the door on the 9th of May, about eight o'clock in the morning—I missed it about five minutes afterwards—this is it—it is my brother's. JAMES ALLEN. I work opposite Mr. Hart's—I saw the prisoner jump up and take the jacket down—I gave information, and he was pursued—I did not pursue him myself. WILLIAM ARUNDELL. In consequence of information I received, I went after the prisoner—he was stopped by t policeman—I saw him drop this jacket. GEORGE CLARK. I am a policeman. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—I am quite sure the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined One Year.
 1619. EDWARD WEIR was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of May , 1 handkerchief, value 4 s  ., the goods of Edward Halton , from his person. EDWARD HALTON.
