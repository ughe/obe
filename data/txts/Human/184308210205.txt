 2524. JOHN LUMSDEN was again indicted for stealing, on the   13th of June , 2 marble chimney-pieces, value 14 l  . 14 s  ., the goods of James Rogers , and another. JOHN CHRISTIE. I live in Lower Queen-street, Rotherhithe, and am in partnership with James Rogers. On the 13th of June I missed two chim ney pieces from Princes-street, Rotherhithe—they were worth fourteen guineas—these are them. JOSEPH BENJAMIN AVILA. I am a pawnbroker, and live in the Mile End-road. These two chimney-pieces were pawned on the 17th of Aug., by the prisoner, for 2 l .—I have no doubt about it—I gave the money to him.
   Prisoner.  I was in Whitechapel-road—Jackson called me, and asked me to lend him a hand to shove the truck down to the turnpike, and he would stand two or three pots of beer—I said, "What have you got?" he said, "Marble chimney-pieces to pawn, at Mrs. Avila's"—I lent him a hand, and he pawned them. GUILTY. Aged 28.— Transported for Seven Years .
 2525. MARGARET ALLEN was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of Aug ., 5 flat-irons, value 5 s  .; 1 tea-kettle, 9 s  .; 1 purse, 6 d  .; 7 sovereigns, and 1 half-sovereign, the property of Sophia Woodden , her mistress. SOPHIA WOODDEN. The prisoner lived with me for a month as a weekly servant. On the 12th of Aug. I put my purse behind my pillow—it con tained seven sovereigns and a half—I missed them after breakfast next morning—she was very poor indeed when she came to me—I lent her 1 s . on the Friday to buy a second-hand pair of shoes, as all her shoes were gone. JAMES BRADLEY( police-constable B  134.) I took the prisoner on the Friday following the robbery, and asked if she had got any money—she said she had none—she had a bonnet-box at her lodging, in Welborough-place, Chelsea—I found two boxes there, filled with good articles, which appeared to have been released from pawn, and in the large chest which she had not told me of, I found four sovereigns. ELIZABETH KNOWLES. The prisoner came to lodge with me at Mrs. Foley's—I did not see her bring the boxes—I did not say that the boxes searched by the policeman, were brought there by her—I believe them to be hers. ELIZABETH ARNOLD. I am searcher at the station. The prisoner told me the had got no money—I found on her 9 s . 2 d .—she asked me what would be done with her—I said I did not know—she said seven sovereigns and a half of the old lady's were lost, but they would not find the purse—if they went to her lodging they would find some money. MARY ANN GRAHAM. I was at Mr. Perkins's, the pawnbroker's, on the 16th of Aug.—I saw the prisoner there—she gave some tickets in—I saw her change a sovereign, and take two parcels out, and then I saw her give an other sovereign.
