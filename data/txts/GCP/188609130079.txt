Before Mr. Recorder.
935. PAUL FELIX KÜHNE (82), Forging and uttering an endorse-
ment on an order for the payment of 61., with intent to defraud.
MR. HUGGINS Prosecuted; Mr. OVEREND Defonded.
JOHN DESIRÉ ENGLAND. I am & dry plate manufacturer, of 21,
Charles Street, Notting Hill — the prisoner has been my clerk
and occasional traveller between three and four years-he received
money for me, and it was his duty to hand it over to me about
May last Frederick Hollier, of 9, Pembroke Square, owed me 67.
for some dry plates, and it was the prisoner's duty to call upon
him-I have never received that money or any cheque for that
amount the endorsement on this cheque is not written by me or by my
authority-I have seen the prisoner write, and it is his writing-this
letter “From J. D. England, manufacturer of dry plates, &c. May 13th.
To F. Hollier, Esq. Dear Sir,- In calling upon you this morning I
forgot to tell you I have discontinued to deal with the Union Bank,
kindly cross your cheques and Co.,' and oblige, pro J. D. England,
Kühne" was not written with my knowledge or consent.

week-my father employed him before I did ; he did not pay him his
wages up to the time of his arrest-my father gave me money to pay the
prironer's wages with the prisoner did not pay me my salarymy
books are here there is no wages book-the prisoner used to make out
roughly on & slip of paper at the end of the week the amounts to be paid
to the people I used sometimes to look over it, or leave it to him the
money to pay them came from my business-all the printing is done in
my name I swore at the police-court that this business was entirely
mine, and that my father had no interest in it-I repeat that here to-day
I have no banking account--the profits of the business were paid over
