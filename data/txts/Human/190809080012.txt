   BEFORE MR . JUSTICE PICKFORD .
 (Wednesday, September 9.) THOMAS TEMPLEMAN  ,(55, plumber) , pleaded guilty of sending a letter to Walter Avery Bird , a solicitor, threatening to murder him, but that he never intended to carry out his threat.
 Mr. Eustace Fulton prosecuted. Prisoner was released on his own recognisances to come up for judgment if called upon. JAMES EVANS  ,(29, labourer) , pleaded guilty of feloniously set ting fire to a haystack, the property of John Bell , of the value of £250.
 Sentence, Eight months' hard labour. ARTHUR JOHN SMITH  , (37, grocer) , pleaded guilty of feloniously sending to Thomas Richard Allinson a letter threatening to kill and murder him. Prisoner was released on own recognisances to come up for judg ment if called upon. VALENTINE ORLANDO  , (24, correspondent) , pleaded guilty of wounding Marie Orlando , with intent to do her grievous bodily harm. Judgment respited. ELIZABETH JANE OVERTON  , (43, nurse), was again indicted (the jury not having been able to agree on two former occasions—see pp. 314 and 573), for using an instrument upon Esther Grover with intent to procure a miscarriage.
 Mr. Travers Humphreys, for the prosecution, now offered no evi dence, and a verdict of Not guilty was returned. JASPER STUART MARTIN  , (33, carman) ; feloniously and carnally knowing Elizabeth Williams , a girl under the age of 13 years.
