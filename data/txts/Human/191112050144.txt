These two sheets were found in the envelope. Exhibit 84 purports to be a list of liabilities and assets on December 31, 1905, and January 1, 1906. It is in two parts. In the first part there is a list of the liabilities of various branches on current and deposit accounts and in the second of the assets of the various branches. The deposits at Bedford Street Branch come to £482,000 and the current accounts £18,000, and at the Bishopsgate Street Branch within £121,000. The depositors at branches come to £43,000, and there are two similar items of current accounts coming to about £9,000. The liabilities total up to £675,196 and the assets to £981.616.
