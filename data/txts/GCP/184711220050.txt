PRESENT-Mr. Ald. Lucas; Sir CHAPMAN MARSHALL, Knt., Ald.; Mr.
RECORDER; Sir James Duke, Knt., Ald. ; Mr. Ald. WILLIAM HUNTER ;
and Mr. Ald. Moon.
Before Mr. Recorder and the Sixth Jury.
77. WILLIAM BAILEY, stealing 1 sovereign, the money of John
Smith, his master; also obtaining 3lbs. of bacon, llb. of butter, and 16 eggs,
value 58.; the goods of John Taylor, by false pretences; to both of which
he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 14.-Judgment Respited.
