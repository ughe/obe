   Q.  Mr. Barnes night have walked into his private room to secrete any thing he chose, as you were not busy? A.  It is not allowed for the pre sidents to go out of the office—they have done it, but it is contrary to rules—he might have gone up to his private room—I missed a sovereign—I counted the money by design.
   Q.  Was there any thing whatever to prevent you, if you thought he had taken the sovereign, to have had him searched on the spot, and called in assistance? A.  There was an objection—there was nothing to prevent my doing so if I chose—I was desired by Mr. Wagstaff not to mark the money—there was no search made—the sovereign was mentioned to Mr. Barnes himself by the solicitor next morning—i do not know whether Mr. Young was present—i think he was there was no search made for the letters that night.
   Q.  If Mr. Barnes had taken letters that must they not have been detected on his person, if he was searched? A.  No, they might not, because he might have sorted them off to their different destination—there would not be say postage inquired for—Mr. Barnes charges the postage to the junior—he might have left them if he contermplated a fraud; but it could not have been discovered that the had taken them; he might have sorted them off without it being discovered that the had taken them—it would have concealed his part in the transaction—the sorting of the letters is done at the left-hand side of the of the room from the fire—they are sorted off in two boxes on the left-hand side from fire-place—they are two boxes, with the separate countries written on the different boxes—Mr. Barnes having got the letters, might have sorted them off in these boxes, without its ever being known that he had that them.
   COURT . Q.  If he had been followed at the moment he took them, they would have been found on his person? A.  Yes, certainly.
   MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  In what room are the letters sorted? A.  In the Foreign Office—not in the window-room—Mr. Barnes must have taken them out of the window-room—I was not a minute in the room—I do not know where Mr. Young was—if he had been by the fire, he must have been the prisoner sort them; but he is called frequently to the window—Bailey could have been him—he might not be paying attention—I called Mr. Young to me, leaving Mr. Barnes behind, because I wanted to speak to him, and with the intent also of leaving the prisoner behind, if possible—this was by desire, and by design—it was a plan—I did not know that Mr. Campbell would come to the window at that hour—I am not aware that Mr. Barnes knew of his coming to the window.
