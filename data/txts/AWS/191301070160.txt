SURREY CASES
BEFORE THE RECORDER.
(Thursday, January 9.)
DOLLING, Frank How (49, painter), pleaded guilty of unlawfully
obtaining credit from T. S. and C. Parry for E2 13s. from the An
thracite Association, Limited, for E12 10s. 2d. from Stratton Gentry,
Limited, for E2 18s. from the Gwauncae-gurwen Colliery Company,
Limited, for E12 4s. 9d. from Lipton's, Limited, for E1 12s. ofd. and
8s. 7d., under false pretences and by means of fraud other than false
pretences, and obtaining from George Alfred Shave 12s. 8id. by false
pretences with intent to defraud.
The police stated they had received many complaints of similar
frauds committed by the prisoner.
Sentence Nine months' hard labour.
