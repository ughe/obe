   Cross-examined by MR. WILLIAMS . Q.  Which one do you think it was that came behind you? A.  Drage—I do not know that Samuel Bowman gave a person in custody for stealing some time ago—I do not know that Samuel Bowman was so much injured that for some time he was an inmate of St. Bartholomew's-hospital—I charged him with being drunk and disor derly, as I was not aware of the nature of my injuries—I have every reason to believe it was Drage that threw me down, because after the Magistrate came to take my deposition, Drage shook hands with me, and said that I was the first man he had ever hurt, and he was sorry for it.
   Cross-examined by MR. MARRIOTT . Q.  Did he not say, that if he had hart you, you were the first man he had ever hurt? A.  No; his words were, you are the first man I ever did hurt—I assisted in taking Samuel to the station—I was not assisted there. WILLIAM GAZE( Policeman, N  93).
