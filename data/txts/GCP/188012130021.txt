Luoy NEWPORT. I am a servant of Mr. Bailey, of 16, Summerford
Grove, Stoke Newington-his house was broken into on the 24th
October, again on the 2nd November, and again on the 8th November
on the morning of the 9th I found a life-preserver outside the scullery
window attached to the house, covered by a water-butt-Oliver was
apprehended thereSummerford Grove is about five minutes' walk from
Victoria Grove.
Cross-examined by Seymour. The life-preserver could not have been there
from the time of the second robbery, because I had cleaned the window.
ALFRED MORAN. I am an assistant to Mr. James Russell, pawn-
broker, of 10, Shoreditch - I produce a coat and four plated ash trays,
pledged for 10s., on 13th October, by Seymour, in the name of John
Foley.
Cross-examined by Seymour. I asked you your address and whose
property it was-you said it was your sister's you did not say you
purchased them the same morning.
THOMAS NEAVES. I am a pawnbroker of 56, Haggerston Road-
I produce a handkerchief pawned on 22nd October for 18. 6d., in the
name of James White-I do not recognise any one.
ROBERT WELLS. I am assistant to Mr. George Wells, pawnbroker,
of 18 and 14, Duncan Place, Hackney-I produce a coat pledged on the

who by.
MICHAEL DEANE (Poljoeman N 177). I took Seymour in charge at a
coffee-shop in consequence of information I received, with a lad named
Merritt, who was afterwards discharged at the police-court-I said "I.
am a police officer; I shall take you into custody for entering the
dwelling-house 16, Summerford Grove "--I searched him at the station
And found five purses, one locket, a chain, a knife, 24d. in bronze, 8
meerschaum pipe, three pencil cases, two suits of clothes, and twenty-six
pawn tickets some of the tickets relate to the property produced
at the station Seymour said "This boy knows nothing about it, I met
him in the street and asked him to have a cup of tea” Mr. Bailey
identified the clothes Seymour was wearing, boots, top coat and all-on
the 10th November, in the passage to the Court, he said "I am sorry I
have not given my proper name, Frank Foley, if I had given Seymour I
should have been an alias."
Cross-examined by Soymour. The name of Foley was on the tickets,
but I did not speak to you about it I did not converse with you, you
made & voluntary statement.
