1187. WILLIAM ROWLAND was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd
of January, 1 plane, value 18.; and 1 hammer, value 6d.; the goods of
Joseph Rumble.
CHARLES LLOYD. I am a bedstead-maker, and live in New North-
place, Holywell-mount. The prisoner was two or three months in my em-
ploy-I suspected something, and gave him in charge-I recollect having
a bammer in my shop, which he claimed ; and when he was taken into
custody he said he bought it at an old iron shop.
SALOME
JOSEPH RUMBLE. I am an upholsterer, and live in Windmill-street.
The prisoner was about three months in my father's employment-Lloyd
brought this hammer to me, and I knew it to be mine--I had not missed
it-I saw this plane at the pawnbrokers, which is mine,
CHARLES JOHNSON. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Providence-row
- this plane was pawned by the prisoner on the 24th of January for 4d.
