Q. When that account was rendered did you agree with the prisoner
striking a balance between you, that he was to give you security for
the balance due to you? A. He offered me security for that amount
-I did not agree to accept it-I sent him to Mr. Jennings the solicitor
-a draft deed of assignment was drawn up, by which I was to be se-
cured for this balance, but not the other-if Mr. Horsnaill's account had
been there I should not have noticed it probably-I should not have
struck that out; you may say that the other amounts that he received
have been treated as matters of account between us, but not the 8l. 188. 6d.
- if that had been there it would have been so, but it is not down, it is one of
the robberies- I should have compromised it had it been down-I bave not
treated it as matter of account-Mr. Jennings has not to my knowledge had
constant communication with the prisoner-not more than three or four
times—most likely that was sufficient to draw up the deed of assignment-
the deed was not executed-if the prisoner would give me security I should
object to take it now-I am not aware that the deed of assigment was nego-
tiated as late as Dec. last-I have not heard Mr. Jennings state 80-I never
saw Mr. Jennings writing--I sent to him but he never had anything to do with
me, neither did I know him-I do not consider that I employed him-he is
doing this business-I believe he drew up the deed -I never saw it till the
day before yesterday, when it was in Mr. Clarkson's hand-my brother-in-
law is named Cross-he is one of my travellers- I sent him with the pri-
soner to Mr. Jennings.
MR. PARNELL. Q. I suppose the mode in which the prisoner would have
to account was this--he should have remitted the money up within two or
three days after he received it, and on his return he should bave given the
account in by word of mouth ? A. Yes-in October the negotiation was with
reference to a sum of between 2001. and 3001. that he was found to bo
deficient, and in that this sum of 81. 18s. 6d. was not included-it was after
that I discovered he had not included in it the sum he had received-it was
discovered by a letter from Mr. Horsnaill - I did not see the prisoner between
October and the time of his apprehension-I never saw the deed till the other
day-I believe the truth was, the prisoner would not execute it.
COURT. Q. When was the time he ought to have given you some account on
the receipts? A. Within three or four days of the receipt of them-he mostly
remitted the money up in lumps, and the statement of the accounts he had
received-I had some negotiation with him about a deficiency of 2001. or 3001.
-he owned that, but this sum was not in that account.
