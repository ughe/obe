THIRD COURT.-Thursday, February 6th, 1851.
PRESENT-Mr. Ald. Challis and RUSSELL GURNEY, Esq.
Before Russell Gurney, Esq. and the Fourth Jury.
516. HANNAH DAVIS, stealing 1 bottle and 1 pint of brandy, value
5 s. 1d.;the goods of George Morris, her master : to which she pleaded
Guilty. Aged 38.-Confined Three Months.
517. EDWARD NAUGHTON, stealing 2 books and 1 gown, value
25s.6d. ; the goods of Mary Fielder: and 1 pag, 2 pairs of boots, and
other articles, value 198.; the goods of John Headfort, his master;
having been twice before convicted: to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 21.- Transported for Ten Years.
518. JAMES BYE, stealing 1 shilling; the moneys of John James
Batsford and another, his masters : to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Confined Six Months.
519. THOMAS WARNER, embezzling 91. Os. 6d.; also, 11. 108.
and 51. 12s. 9d.; the moneys of Samuel Turner and others, his masters : to
which he pleaded Guilty. Aged 39.-- Transported for Seven Years.
520. THOMAS VOSE, stealing 1 candlestick, value Is.; the goods
of Abraham Myers: and DRUSILLA WILLIAMS, receiving the
same: to which
VOSE pleaded GUILTY. Aged 15.-Confined One Month, and Whipped.
MR. HORey conducted the Prosecution.
ABRAHAM MYERS. I am a broker, at 55, Exmouth-street. On 30th
Jan. I had a candlestick like this produced, outside my shop; I missed it
about half-past five o'clock-it cost me ls.
Cross-examined by MR. PARRY. Q. It was on the board for sale ?
4. Yes; I should have sold it for 1s. 3d.-it is only tarnished--I had had
it about ten days-it was new, and had never been used; it appears to
bave been used now-this produced) is the fellow to it.
EDWARD BAXTER. I live at 5, Brook's-gardens, Baggnige-wells-road.
On 30th Jan., at half-past five o'clock, I saw Vose and another boy at the
bottom of Exmouth-street, with a candlestick-Vose went with it to Mrs.
Williams's eating-house, and came out without it.
Cross-examined. Q. Are you a companion of his? 4. No; I know
him by sight-I sometimes had my meals when he did, at this cook's-shop
- he was a customer there-I am a greengrocer's errand-boy-I once
offered Mr. Williams a dinner-knife for sale, when I was in want of some-
thing to eat; he did not wish to buy it--I afterwards sold it to a cats'-
meat man-he was not charged with receiving it.
