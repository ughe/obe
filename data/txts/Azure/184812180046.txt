great deal of trouble. A. No.
Thomas Toms's Defenee. I, my three sons, and my daughter, have worked
for him during the summer; the first week he brought my money short 25'.,
and he said, You had better draw '0 much a-week, and have the remainder
at the end I did so, and when it was done, there was 71. 18. 2d. due to me,
but I could not get a halfpenny of him; he said, I think it will do you a
great deal of good in the winter-time."
