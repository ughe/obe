2287. CHARLES BELL was indicted for "stealing, on the 8th of
August, 1 ring, value 12s. ; 1 breast-pin, value 58.; 1 ink-stand, value
18.; and 1 printed book, value 28.; the goods of Jemima Daniels.
JEMIMA DANIELS. I am single, and reside in Prospect-cottages,
Shooter's-hill. On the morning of the 8th of August, I found my
bureau had been broken open-it stood in my wash-house, and these ar-
ticles were missing—the ink-stand and book produced are mine.
Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. What day did you miss
them! A. On Sunday, the 8th of August, they were taken-I did not dis-
cover it till Monday morning.
MARIAN ESTHER DANIELS. I am single, and live with my sister--the
bureau was secure on the Sunday.
SUSANNAH MARTHA PICKETT. I am servant to Miss Daniels--the pri-
sonet used to work in the house on this Sunday, I saw him putting the
ink-stand into his pocket-he was then at the bureau-he had the lid of
the bureau in his hand, and a chisel was on the top of the bureau-I said,
"Charley, what are you doing?"-he held up his finger, and said,
"Hush"-I said, “What are you doing?"- he said, “I shall have this,"
and put the ink-stand into his pocket.
Cross-examined. Q. What age are you! A. Twelve-the prisoner is
servant to Mrs. Phillips, who lives in the next house-she was repairing
the lower part of her house, and the prisoner came into our wash-bouse to
clean his shoes I did not say any thing about this, as I was afraid the
prisoner would beat me he used very often to beat me-he once hit me
on the arm with a hammer-he did not threaten to beat me for this-I
told my mistress on Monday.
