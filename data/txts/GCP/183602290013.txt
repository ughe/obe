ANN HILL. I live at Bishop Stortford, in Hertfordshire. On the
Wednesday before the 20th of February I was at Epping-I stopped there
at Mrs. Clarke's, at the White Lion, and had my dinner, and then came
od to London-I was alone, and had a bundle with me about nine
o'clock the prisoner overtook me-he said nothing to me, but hit me on
the left side of my head he had been at the public-house-1 had left him
there, and had got above a mile from the public-house before he overtook
he had not spoken to me in the public-house, nor I to him I had
stopped at another public-house before he came up, and had half-a-pint
beet; I then went on my way-when the prisoner came up, he passed
me and went into a public-house-I passed that house, and he overtook
my and gave me a blow on the head--I fell into the ditch-I got up,
and went and rang at a gentleman's house he did nothing else to me, only
wok the bundle-I did not see his face--he had a blue coat, black trow-
sérs, low shoes, white stockings, and a paper cap-I had a gown, shawl,

out of the ditch-I saw he had a paper cap when I was in the public-
house-I saw him the next day at Lambeth-street, before the Magistrate;
ud the articles were produced to me there I was coming to London to
see my father and mother.
