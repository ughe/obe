118. WILLIAM BAKER, feloniously uttering a forged request for the
delivery of goods. MR. CockLe conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE HAOUE. I am in the service of Mr. George Coben, an outfitter,
of 66, Royal Mint-street, Whitechapel. Last Saturday, the prisoner came to
our shop with this order, which he said Mr. Cohen had given him-(read-
"Please to let William Baker have one pair of trowsers and one pair of
boots, G. COHEN "-I said Mr. Cohen could not write, and he said Mr.
Cohen got some one to write it for him-I gave him a pair of trowsers and a
pair of boots-unless I had received that order, and the prisoner had made
that statement, I should not have given him the goods.
GEORGE COHEN. I am an outfitter, of 66, Royal Mint-street-Hague is
in my service-I did not write this order, or authorize any one else to
write it.
Prisoner's Defence. The prosecutor came to the Sailors' Home ship, and
asked me if I wanted any clothes ; I said, "A pair of shoes and a pair of
trowsers ;" he said I could have them, and pay for them when I received my
wages; I received 101. 2. 3d. wages at one o'clock, had a little to drink, and
was given in charge before I had time to pay: I left a pair of shoes and trowsers
at the prosecutor's till I paid for these things, which were worth double.
Mr. Cohen re-examined. I did not go to the ship and ask him.
Not Guilty.
