MR. METCALPE. Q. At the time that was sent was there any intention to
prosecute! - A. No; when we made up our minds to prosecute we ab-
stained even from examining the prisoner in the Bankruptoy Court-bank-
rupts are very frequently examined with a view to their prosecution-I
thought that unfair, and did not examine the prisoner-he was very much
confused when I charged him with pawning-it was about a fortnight after
that that I sent this document and received this answer.
