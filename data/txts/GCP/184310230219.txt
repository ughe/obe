WILLIAM COOPER. I am a builder, and live at Tulse-bill-the prisoners
were in my service, about February last, as plasterers--I believe they were
working in Clapham New-park-1 had a rule there I was there almost every
day-I saw that rule there afterwards, and it had been altered it had been made
narrower at each end, which was for some purposes more convenient than if it
was a regular size all the way through, as I had had it made this is it-(look-
ing at it) —it had been as wide at the ends as it is in the middle-I bad it made
about twelve months ago I observed "EB" was cut on it, which had not
been on it before I saw Byfield when he came to the building-I asked how
he dared to take and put his name on my rule-he said it was not my rule,
that a person named Wren made it for him, for doing a job just above--I said,
every man on the building knew it was my rule, by a singular place on it,
that Wren never made it, and every person in the building knew it to be the
truth-he then said if Wren did not make it for him, it belonged to Haines
-I said, if he dared to take it away, or Haines either, I should lock
them up wherever I found them, for stealing the rule, as it was mine-this
was in the latter end of May-I was at the building, and observed the rule
was missing in July, and I sent to the prisoners, by a boy who they employed
to work for them--I sent three different messages ---Haines at last came to
