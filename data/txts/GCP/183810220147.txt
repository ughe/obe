Henry MURRAY. I am a stationer, and live in Coventry-street, Hay.

on me, representing himself to be John Timmings, of Uxbridge in con-
sequence of a reference he gave me, I sent to Mr. Lloyd, a tailor, and he
sent me a good account of the person about whom I inquired-on
the same day, the same person called again, and after some inquiry, I de-
livered to him a day book, a ledger, an account book, six reams of
paper, and three map files-the bill amounted to 72. 38:-- he took the goods
away in a cart the next day-on the 4th of August, I received by hand
an order to the amount of 21. odd, which I forwarded on the 7th of Au-
gust, the prisoner Thompson called, and brought an order-he took the
invoice of the goods with him, and I believe I sent the goods to the coach

the pawnbroker's-on the 14th of September I received another order for
