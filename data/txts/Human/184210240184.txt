 3013. JOHN LEE was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of October , 3 1/4 lbs. weight of flour, value 7 d  ., the goods of Samuel Lancaster , his master. SAMUEL LANCASTER. I am a baker, and live at Kensington, the pri soner was in my employ. On the 20th of October I gave him twenty one half-quartern bags to fill with flour, he brought me but twenty back—I waited a little while—he went out with his bread—I then looked round the bakehouse, and found two half-quartern bags filled with flour, behind three tins—I said nothing—the prisoner came back from serving a few customers, and went into the bakehouse—in about five minutes he went out again—I looked, and the bags were gone—I told the officer to go and take him—the bags were found on him. RICHARD TRIPP( police-constable T  84.) I took the prisoner, and found the bags of flour in his hat.
   Prisoner.  I went to the bakehouse, and came out directly. I was not there two minutes. I was nearly a quarter of a mile from the shop when he found them in my hat. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Six Months.
