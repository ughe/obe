have been employed under Mr. Barnsby in the Postal Stores-] began
work there on March 5th, and have been continuous with two exceptions
-on Mareh 8th I left Mr. Barnsby and went downstairs into the
Stationery Department, but returned on the 17th-I1 left again on April
3rd or 4th I cannot say for how long-I left on a Tuesday and returned
on a Tuesday, but whether it was the following Tuesday or the Tuesday
week I cannot tell you-when I was in Barnsoy's room on the first
occasion my duty was to cut straps off old pouches- had no duty in
connection with string - had to bag up plain and also coloured caavas;
they used to go together- made up the canvas into weights of 56 lb.-
then I used to take them over and mark out the name on the outside of
the bag, put a slit on either side about 1. ft. long, and then stow them
away in a corner of the bag-room, where Newham was employed-about
twice a week more bags would come on a van the man in charge of the
van would pass them down a shute into the bag-room, and I was told off
to assist Newham in receiving them-at first I did not know what
they contained they were not opened, but stowed up just behind us out
of the way-on one occasion I stowed a number of bags from by the
shute into another part of the bag-room-I had been sent down to New-
tham's room by Mr. Barnsby--Newham showed me the bags, and where
he wanted them shifted to LI put a slit in some of the bags when they
arrived, as they had none when they came-I only slit them on that one
occasion- cannot say who did it when I did not do it-I did not see
anybody else put a slit in them-when I was there I never packed
canvas and string together.
