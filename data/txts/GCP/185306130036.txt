JOHN Lron KITCHEN. I am inspector of the Independent Gas Light
Company at Haggerstone. I know the prisoner very well, he is an umbrella
and parasol maker, and lives at No. 46, Hackney-road-he has a shop there,
which is supplied with gas by the Company-it is my duty to collect arrears
and payments for gas for the Company-amongst other persons I had to col-
lect from the prisoner-on 7th May I went to him to collect for 11. 58. 2d.;
I wrote this receipt (looking at it), in his shop-here is a memorandum wbich
was given me by the collector previously, 11. 58. 2d.; it is scratched through
as being paid--the prisoner paid me 11. 58. 2d. ; I gave him the receipt for
the amount-this is the receipt with the exception of the alteration of the
figure 1 into a 2- wrote this in the prisoner's presence-I find on the
receipt the word "one" that has not been altered, but the figure 1 is made into
a 2-that has not been done with the same ink--this is a sort of brownish
ink--this is the counterfoil taken from the book, and here is the book it is
taken from (produced)-I made this memorandum on the counterfoil at his
shop before I wrote the receipt-I think I saw the prisoner again on 9th
June-I was in the private office I had had some communication previously
from one of the officers- I asked the prisoner the purport of his visit to the
Company's office, and he told me be had paid me 21. 58. 20.- I had not the
receipt with me then-it had been left by him with Mr. Monteith-he said
to me, "I paid you 21. 58. 2d."- I told him he was not robbing the Com-
pany, he was robbing me of a sovereign-it wa, in consequence of some
conversation I had had with Mr. Monteith that I used that expression-
said to the prisoner the recipt had been altered-he said he had not altered it.
Cross-examined by Mr. Sleigh, Q. On the day previously to that the
prisoner had been at the Company's office? A. Yes.
MR. METCALFE. Q. After the 11. 58. 2d. was paid by him, was there any
other claim for 1l. made on him? 1. No, only for 58. or 68.; the claim I
am referring to was what he claimed back from that 28. 58. 2d.
Thomas RUTHERFORD MONTEITH. I am one of the clerks of the Inde-
pendent Gas Company. On Monday, 6th June, the prisoner came to the
office, between half-past 1 and 2 o'clock-he brought with him two receipts
for an explanation of the charges-he produced this receipt to me-at the
time he produced, it, it was in the state it now is--I saw it was given for
21. 58. 2d. in appearance-be said, “I have called for an explanation of the
difference between this receipt and the amount of gas I have consumed-I
referred to our books, and found it was 11. 58. 2d. I told him there must be
some error in the case, and if he would leave it with me I would get it
explained he gave this receipt into my hands to keep in my possession-
said it was very much out of the usual course for persons to give their
receipts up, but I locked it up, and thought nothing of it till the next
morning, when Mr. Kitchen came- I called him in and spoke to him.
Q. Did the prisoner call for the return of any money? 4. No, but merely
for an explanation-I asked him what he had paid-be said 21. 58. 2d.-I told
him to wait, and I got the receipt-I told him I discovered that it had been
altered-1 pointed out the change in the figures and in the ink-he said it
was false, and said he did not do it, and did I suppose if he had he would
have returned me the receipt-I told him it was a very barefaced attempt
at fraud, and gave him into custody-he did not ask for any money of me.
