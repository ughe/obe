I could not say whether this cheque came from our book unless I had it here—I did not miss a cheque—at the end of September, 1888, our premises were levied, they belonged to Mr. Turtle, who went bankrupt—an execution was put in, and they cleared us out at once, and took all our papers; I cannot even produce a receipt—they took everything out of my desk; the execution was for a quarter's rent, they said that if that was paid we could have our papers—they took the cheque-book among other things—I know a builder named Nicholls, but his name is not spelt the same as on the cheque. NOT GUILTY. There was an indictment on the same facts for false pretences, upon which no evidence was offered. NOT GUILTY.
