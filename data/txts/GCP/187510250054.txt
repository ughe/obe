the money should be paid to me-he said as he had made the arrangement
it had better stand as it was, and he would call on me the following day,
Wednesday, and settle with me the costs of the writ that I had issued against
him-I never got a farthing---on 1st October I received this letter from him.
(This merely acknowledged the receipt of the writ, which he stated he had taken
to his solicitor.) I bad served bim personally with the writ in consequence
of some information I had from another client at Notting Hill-subsequently
to the receipt of that letter I found he was under remand at Hammersmith
and I attended before the Magistrate.
