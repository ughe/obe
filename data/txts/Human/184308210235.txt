JAMES TRENATE , JAMES HUGGETT, and WILLIAM JONES were indicted for burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Alphonso Macintosh , on the   13th of August , at Lambeth, and steal ing therein, 4 spoons, value 1 l  . 10 s  .; 1 pair of sugar tongs, 1 s  .; 4lbs. weight of cigars, 5 l  . 4 s  .; 4 sixpences; 60 pence; and 96 half-pence; his property; and feloniously beating and striking the said Alphonso Macintosh, in the said dwelling-house.
   MR. CROUCH conducted the Prosecution. ALPHONSO MACKINTOSH.
