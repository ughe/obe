GEORGE HOLLOWAY. I am a constable of East Ham. Aldridge brought
me a warrant against Bradshaw, Morris, and Monk, on Saturday night,
the 19th of March-I apprebended Monk and Bradsbaw the same night
I told them it was for a highway robbery on a man named Aldridge-
they said nothing, except that they did not know any thing about it. y lo
Cross-examined. Q. Do you know where the prisoner's father lived ?
A. Yes, I believe the prisoner lodged with him I have known him ever
since he was a child I have seen him frequently, for three weeks or a month
before, up and down the place - I do not know that he ever went away.eu
Prisoner's Defence. When I left him at the Duke's Head, I went home,
and never saw him till I saw him at the Cock.

SOPHIA Mons. I am the prisoner's another the day before he was
taken up, I was at the Cock public-house, about four o'clock in the after
noon, as near as I can recollect-my husband came after I got there whe
is very ill indeed at present-Joseph Duckel was there the prosecutor,
Bradshaw and Morris, and my son, were there the prosecutor did not
charge my son or Bradshaw with robbing him he said Morris had robbed
him, and my son and Bradshaw were present he offered to settle it for 11.
it was not given him-he then offered to settle it for 10w., and then for
50., but it was not given him-I said I would make it up with him if they
were guilty, but they all said they were not-neither me nor my husband
said if my son was guilty we would pay him whatever he had lost my son
and all of them said he was not guilty, and would not let my husband of
me pay him any money I had not offered to pay him money.my
husband said if they were guilty he would pay it, and my son told the
prosecutor, he would not allow his father to pay a farthing, as he wall not
guilty --Duckel was present when he said that my son lodged with us
he was at work as usual for three weeks before the meeting at the Ooek,
and sleeping at home at night, so that the constable might have found
him.
