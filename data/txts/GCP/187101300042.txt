THIRD COURT.-Wednesday, February 1st, 1871.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
175. THOMAS GREGORY (46), was indicted for feloniously wounding
Georgina Gregory, with intent to murder.
Upon the evidence of JOHN ROWLAND GIBSON, Esq., Surgeon of Newgate,
the jury found the prisoner insane, and unable to plead. Ordered to be de
tained till Her Majesty's pleasure be known.
176. GEORGE BELL (34), was indicted for unlawfully obtaining on
credit, within four months of his bankruptcy, 36 tons of potatoes, of John
Read. Second Count-For a like obtaining of 30 tons of potatoes from
Jonathan Read. Third Count-For obtaining the said goods under false
pretences. Fourth Count—For obtaining the said goods by means of fraud
other than false pretences.
Messrs. METCALFE and Collins conducted the Prosecution; and
MR. SERJEANT SLEIGH, with MR. BESLEY, the Defence.

when application was made by the defendant's counsel, before plea taken, to
quash the indictment.
