745. WILLIAM SMART (33), stealing a coat, the property of James
Collins.
Mr. BRINDLEY conducted the Prosecution.
MARY HENDRY. I live at 8, Three Dragons Court, Fore Street-about
1.30 in the day, on 17th August, I went into Mr. Collins' shop, at 10,
Huggin Lane, Cheapside-I saw the prisoner in the shop, putting on a cont
- he had his right arm in one sleeve- he was standing about a yard and a half
in the shop-I caught hold of him, and asked him what he was going to do
with the coat-be said "Nothing, let me go "-he dropped the coat,
and ran down Huggin Lane as fast as he could.
CHARLES COLLINS. I assist Mr. James Collins, a tailor, of 10, Huggin
Lane-about 1.30 on this day I was below, in the basement--I heard a
noise in the shop, and ran up-I saw Mrs. Hendry at the shop-door, with a
coat on her arm-she pointed down Gresham Street, and I saw the prisoner
running-I ran after him-I spoke to a policeman at the corner of Aldermati
bury, and I saw him stopped and brought back.
JAMES DOUBLE (City Policeman 180). I was on duty in Gresham Street,
and saw the prisoner run down Aldermanbury-I stopped him, and took
him back to the shop.
Prisoner's Defence. I went into the shop, and I picked up the coat,
that I should not tread upon it. The woman said “What do you want !
and I ran away. I went in to get a situation as porter.
GUILTY. He further PLEADED GUILTY to having been before convicted in
January, 1870—Nine Months' Imprisonment.
746. THOMAS SMITH (19), Robbery, with violence, on Mary Ann
Hanlon, and stealing a chain and locket, her property.
MR. COOPER conducted the Prosecution.
ALFRED WINKLER (Policeman C 162). On Saturday morning, the 17th
September, about 12.30, I was in Leicester Square-I heard a woman
scream, and saw the prisoner knock down a woman-he ran across the road
and hid himself between two cabs--I ran after him, and took him back to
the woman-she got up, and I asked her if that was the man that
knocked her down-she said “Yes; where is my chain ?"-he said " If you
will let me go I will give up the chain "-he took the chain out of his left-
hand coat pocket-I took it from his hand-I found a knife and 38. 6d.
on him.
MARY ANN HANLON. I live at 3, Argyle Street, Euston Road - this chain
is mine-it is worth 61.-on the night in question I was the worse for liquor,
and I don't remember what happened, I had this chain on when I was
sober.
JAMES SPENCER (Policeman C R 30). I saw the prisoner knock the last
witness down-she screamed-he ran away and was taken-I am sure he
the same man-I was with Winkler-he was charged with stealing the
chain, and he denied it.
