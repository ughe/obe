   Cross-examined.  Among the letters I found upon her there was one from Lord Worcester's country seat—I do not know if it was from him—this letter was found on her ( produced )—I do not know who signed it—it is from one of Lord Worcester's servants ( saying that Lord Worcester's servant was being sent to Bath to make arrangements about the prisoner being sent to Scotland )—I have spoken to the prisoner on two occasions only—I spoke to her again at the Police-court, when she spoke about having a claim on Lord Worcester—she seemed to be in earnest—she appeared to believe in her claim. GUILTY — Six Months' Hard Labour.

   MR. BIRON Prosecuted, and MR. GEOGHEGAN and MR. HUGHES Defended. CHARLES UPCHURCH.
