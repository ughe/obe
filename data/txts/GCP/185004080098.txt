PETER HAYS. The last witness was my barman-about 19th March I
marked some money--before that I had received two or three communications
from Taplin upon the subject of what had been suggested to him the first
was on the evening of the 15th-he told me from time to time what he has
stated on the 19th, abont half-past nine o'clock, I was in the bar-parlour-
had before that handed two shillings, marked, to Taplin, and the police were
in the house to watch - I saw the prisoner come in, apparently for some
beer-I knew him as a neighbour-I had no opportunity of seeing
the transaction.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not say Taplin suited you very well? A. Yes,
he did, with the exception of one thing, that he was addicted to drink-I had
not contemplated discharging him, he gave me notice himself; I was very
sorry he did he was a most excellent tradesman.
