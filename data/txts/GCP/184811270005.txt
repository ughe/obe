Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury.
1. JAMES ALLEN, stealing 30 yards of cloth, value Bl., the goods of
James Wood, in his dwelling-house ; to which he pleaded GUILTY.
2. JAMES ALLEN was again indicted for stealing 40 yards of cloth,
value 151.; the goods of Thomas Tillman, in his dwelling-house.
JAMES BATTLEBURY. I am in the employ of Thomas Tillman, a tailor, of
200, Whitechapel-road, in the parish of St. Mary, Whitechapel, it is his
dwelling-house. On 1st Nov., about half-past six o'clock, the prisoner
came in, said, "Good evening," took up eight lengths of cloth, and ran out-
I am lame, and before I could get up he was half way out of the shop-I
called, “Stop thief!" but could not find a policeman-a parcel of boys sur-
rounded me-I afterwards recognized him at the Mansion-house I know
him by his cap and dress, and also by his features--this is like the cap
(produced)-I was discharged in consequence, as it was thought I bad a hand
in it, but have been taken back.
WILLIAM LOCKYER (City policeman, 264). On 10th Nov, the prisoner
was in my charge at the Mansion-house in the other case; Battlebury was
brought there and identified him.
Guilty. Aged 20.--Transported for Seven Years.
