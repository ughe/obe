ELIZABETH TWIsT, widow, 3, Short Road, Stratford. Prisoner has
lodged with me for three years. This dagger (produced) belonged to
him, and was kept in my work-box. I saw it for the last time about
a week before May 29. I did not know he had it in his possession on
May 29. He left my house about 9.30 p.m. on May 28, saying he was
going to see his son, and I saw no more of him.
FREDERICK HENRY HARRIS, 12, Globe Crescent, Stratford. I am
the stepson of the prosecutor. At about 8.30 p.m. on May 28 pri-
soner called at our house, told mother he wanted to see prosecutor, and

must wait, if I wait till six to-morrow morning." He waited in the
passage as he wished to do so, and sat on a chair which I had brought

About two minutes after I heard the prosecutor cry, He has stabbed
