220. THOMAS INGLETON was indicted for stealing, on the 29th
of October, 1 coat, value 31., the goods of George Read.
GEORGE READ. I am ostler to Mr. Bottom, of Windmill-street, West-
minster. On the 29th of October I was returning to town with a horse,
and stopped at the Red Lion tap at Ilford-I gave my horse to the land-
lady's son to hold - I had a coat attached to the saddle-I came out in a
quarter of an hour, and it was gone--I have not seen it since, but I saw
the prisoner with the horse coming up to the door-he had taken the horse
from the boy that I gave it to-he gave the horse to the boy, and the boy
ran into the house, and said my coat was gone - I am sure the prisoner
is the man.
