446. ELIZABETH CARR was again indicted, with CHARLES
CARR (39), for a like offence.
MR. POLAND conducted the Prosecution,
ELIZABETH ALDERTON, CHARLES VITALIS JONES, RICHARD HOOPER,
and WILLIAM WEBSTER repeated their former evidence.
Elizabeth Carr's Defence. I would not go in with him, as he had had a
drop to drink. The money I had was paid to me. I am an unfortunate
girl. I never denied my husband; I said I did not know anything about
the bad money.
Charles Carr's Defence. I was not aware it was bad, or I never should
have tried to have passed it.
ELIZABETH-GUILTY."--Confined Nine Months. CHARLES-
GUILTY.-Confined Twelve Months.
