BENJAMIN HUREN. I am a butcher, of Park-lane, Dorset-square. On
16th Feb., about 12 o'elock in the day, the prisoner came for half a pound
of beef steak I cut nine ounces, which came to 4id.he tendered me
bad shilling, I bent it nearly double with my teeth, gave it to him again, 8
and he left without the steak, not being able to pay for it followed him
up the street, and he joined a woman, Ann Dillon (See page 608), about 100
yards off-they went together to a public house in Upper Park-place, and I
informed the police-a few minutes afterwards I saw them in Mr. Webb's
shop, a few yards from the public house they came out, and went up the
street-some time afterwards I saw them in a public house next door to
my house, and they were taken into custody.
Prisoner. Q. How long did you see me with this woman 4. Half an
