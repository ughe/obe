SAMUEL LILLY. I am a tallow-chandler, and live at Walthamstow.
On the 28th of September as I was going to bed I heard a noise in the
street, and thought I heard a cry of murder-I opened my window, and
heard the cry repeated—I went into the street and saw a great number
of people, about twenty, a great many of them using violent language-
saw Frederick Hagger beating Lawrence on his head on the ground with
athick walking-stick, and kicking him, and William Hagger was beating
Reynolds, who was retreating from the mob to avoid the blow-he had his
class up, but did not use it-I saw Frederick Hagger come to assist bis
brother, who was beating Reynolds--they both beat him violently-Rey-
noldo got over a little fence, and I went to his assistance Reynolds's wife
was singing round William Hagger, begging him not to murder her hus-
band-Reynolds got out of the yard, and they struck him again I ran up
and put my hand on William Hagger I said, " For God's sake, what do
you mean to murder this poor man? I will not allow it"-I assisted them
some, and sent to the station-house they appeared very much hurt, and
Reynolds was covered with blood.
Cross-examined. Q. You saw him with his cutlass ? A. Yes-I did
see him cut at the man-he kept it up, and warded off the blows-
not see him snap a pistol, but I heard him say to his wife, "Stand off,
stress," and I judge from that that he did—there is a great feeling against
the police among the lower orders of persons.
