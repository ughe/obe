We let the pony go as he liked, and he went to  the mistress, who gave him a bit of sugar, and he licked her; he will take his round to my customers with anybody who goes with him—he was taken home on Saturday, and I have brought him here—I have had him back a week last Sunday—the Magistrate let me take him away, on my paying his expenses at the greenyard.
