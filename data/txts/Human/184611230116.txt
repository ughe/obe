 157. HENRY FROUD was indicted for stealing 1 coat, value 8 s.  , the goods of William Fryer . WILLIAM FRYER. I am a carman, and live in Devonshire-cottages, Wal-worth. I had a coat on the 3rd of Nov.—I had some business at the Coach and Horses public-house—I went into the house, and left my coat in my cart outside—when I came out I missed my coat—this is it. HENRY MOSS. I deal in coats. I bought this coat at Mr. Martin's house, in Mill-lane, Deptford—it laid on the table—the prisoner was there—Mr. Mar tin sold me the coat and two hats. JAMES MARTIN. I sold this coat to Moss—the prisoner brought it to my house on the 3rd Nov.—it laid on the table.
   Prisoner.  This man was in bed when I went in there. Witness.  I was in bed, but I was awake.
   Prisoner's Defence.  He did not say that I brought it in; he said it laid on the table, but he did not know how it came there; it was not there the same day, it was the next morning. GUILTY .* Aged 27.— Confined Three Months,
