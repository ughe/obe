down for nothing I went over it with you and the Captain-you asked
me 15,0001., if my memory serves me rightly-I did not consider it worth
the money– I did not agree with Mr. Groome-he was purchasing for
development, and I was purchasing for re-sale, that makes all the differ-
ence-the Captain's wife called on me on the business after I had written
to decline it, and I suggested that a less Bum would be taken if I had
purchased it I should certainly not have paid down all the money-it is
usual for persons to buy land and to negotiate a mortgage to a certain
extent, not up to the hilt though-it is within my experience that persons
may sign & contract to buy property with a view to negotiate a mortgage
to complete-it is very usual.
