To Prisoner You were taking the bedstead down, and walked
into the kitchen to get the hammer to knock the side of the bedstead
out; we started nagging and jawing again, when you struck me.
To the Judge: We have been married 13 years next March. I
have had six children, of whom five are living. Prisoner has treated
me with every kindness and respect; he has been an honest, hard.
working man, and has worked for the Borough of Paddington for
10 years; the is a decent, kindly man when not drinking. He does
not drink generally. I have nothing to say against him in may
respeet. I think he lost himself. If he had not been drinking I do
not think he would have touched me.
