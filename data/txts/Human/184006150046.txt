JAMES MATTHEWS. I recollect the Saturday night before my brother was taken into custody—he slept at home that night in the same bed as me—he got up about eight o'clock—I was not awake when he came to bed.
   M. PRICE. Q . What time did you go to bed? A . About eleven o'clock—my brother William was not in bed then, he came to bed soon after I was in bed, soon after eleven o'clock—I was not asleep when he came to bed—he came to bed about an hour before Thomas—Thomas came to bed about twelve o'clock—he did not say any thing when he came to bed—I did not hear him tell my brother where he had been—I was not awake at twelve o'clock—I know he came to bed about twelve o'clock, because I was awake then—I was awake when he got into bed—I saw him—he had no light—I did not keep awake long after he came to bed, about half an hour—I did not talk at all during that half hour—neither of us did—not a syllable passed on either side, I will swear that—William asked Thomas what kept him out so late, and Thomas said that was nothing to him—William requested him not to be so saucy—I remem ber all that distinctly, and then some words passed—they did not get to fighting, but they were very near it—William told him to go to sleep and hold his tongue—there was a quarrel between them about a red waist coat, Thomas told him to let the waistcoat alone.
