 1972. FREDERICK THORNTON was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of May , 6 sovereigns and 10 shillings, the monies of Joseph Cole , his master. JOSEPH COLE. I am a stationer, and live in London-street, Green wich—the prisoner was in my employment upwards of two years—I had him from his parents. On Saturday afternoon, the 14th of May, I gave him six sovereigns and ten shillings, to go to town and purchase some Sunday newspapers at the different news-offices—he generally brought them by the railroad—he did not return—next Monday he was brought to my house by my son—I asked what he had done with the money—he said, "I have none"—he had 12 s . a-week—he always behaved well up to this time. DANIEL MONYAHAN( police-constable R  184.) I took the prisoner into custody in London-street, Greenwich, and brought him to the station—I found on him a silver watch, a gold ring, a sovereign, five shillings, a fourpenny-piece, and 7 d . in coppers.
   MR. COLE re-examined.  These do not quite account for the money—I understand he got some of his clothes out of pawn—he was bailed, and has surrendered to take his trial.
   Prisoner.  I am extremely sorry for what I have done, hut I had no felonious intent; I intended to make up the money in the course of the week, and pay my master. Witness.  I understand that he belonged to a 6 l . money-club; they pay so much a-week or month, and have a draw. (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 19.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor and Jury. Confined Three Months.
