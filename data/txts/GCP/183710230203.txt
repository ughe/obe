2408. THOMAS LANE was indicted for a misdemeanor.
THOMAS Fletcher. I live at Stratford, in the parish of West Ham, and
am an ironmonger. I did not know Mr. Young personally, but the prie
soner had been several times for goods in the name of Youngon
the 11th of April he said he came from Mr. Young for two coppers, the
largest copper tea-kettle we had, and an iron boiler-he brought a string
for the size of the coppers--we had none of the size, but he took an iron
boiler-I gave him the boiler, and said I would go to Mr. Young, and
speak about the coppers, intending to do so, but was prevented by some
customers coming in-I believed the story he told me I gave it him,
conceiving that Mr. Young would be my debtor for it-I received informa-
tion that he was selling the boiler, and went and took him.
Prisoner. Q. Did not I pay ls. on the article ? A. No.
MATTHEW YOUNG. I have seen the prisoner-I never sent him to Mr.
Fletcher, nor did I authorise any one to get these things.
Prisoner. I offered to pay for the things, if he would look over it.
(The prisoner received a good character.)

Before Mr. Recorder.
2409. GEORGE SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
October, 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of James Smith, from his
person.
JAMES SMITH. I live in George-place, Greenwich. On the afternoon
of the 19th of October I was in Charlton-fair, between two and three o'clock,
looking at a show-an officer came up and spoke to me-I put my hand
to my coat, and missed my pocket-handkerchief-I told him so, and the
prisoner was seized instantly and searched-a crowd gathered round, and
a man produced the handkerchief to us—this is it-(looking at it.)
JAMES ADAMS. I am Superintendent of the Lewisham and Lee police.
I was at Charlton-fair on the 19th of October, and saw the prisoner stand-
ing close behind Mr. Smith-I saw him put his left hand into Mr. Smith's
night-hand coat-pocket, and take a handkerchief out--then put it into his
nght hand, and attempt to shuffle it up his coat-I took bold of him
immediately-I tapped Mr. Smith on the shoulder, and asked if he had lost
any thing--and while I was searching the prisoner the handkerchief was
produced-I am positive the prisoner is the person who took it-Mr. Smith
moved a little forward while he was taking it, and I saw the handkerchief
--it was the same colour as the one produced.
Prisoner's Defence. I was looking at one of the shows, and the policeman
collared me I had no knowledge of the handkerchief-he searched me,
found nothing on me--and when we moved about a hundred yards, 4
person brought the handkerchief up, and asked if he had lost any thing.
