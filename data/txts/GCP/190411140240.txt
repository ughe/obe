he took them back and gave me what I gave for them I have not sug-
gested that he has defrauded me-I do not suggest it now, I am perfectly
satisfied with the way in which he carried out this transaction; the estate
was well worth what I gave for it; I had it valued; there was no sus-

I never made any suggestion against him at all.
