ROBERT WOODHAMS. I am manager of the St. John's Wood branch of the
London and South Western Bank-Wetherall opened an account with us in
1873; the account is still open, it is overdrawn about 51.- about 1873 it
was overdrawn-in October, 1876, about 501. was paid in, and I took a pro-
missory note for 501, the balance of the overdraft : that note was not met.it
is still due, and the 51. is overdrawn.
Cross-examined by MR. STRAIGHT. I had known Wetherall about three
and a half years as far as I knew of him I believe he was honest and
respectable-he has had a balance in our bank of more than 8001.- I do not
know anything of his connections or associations--I have received a draft
against the promissory note and the overdraft which I believe will be paid at
maturity.
