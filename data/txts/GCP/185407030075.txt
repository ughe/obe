ANN TURNER. I live at the corner of Wooten-street, Cornwall-road,
Lambeth ; I keep a shop. On the evening of 9th June, the last witness
came to my shop for some dripping-she gave me in payment a bad half
crown-I asked her some questions and detained the half crown, and told
her to send the man in for it-she went out and some one with her, and
they could not find the man-I marked the half crown, and gave it to the
policeman-this is it.

and found was bad-I asked him where he got it, and he told me I put
& quartern of water in the bottle, and told him to take it over to the man
-I stood at the door, and saw the prisoner cross over to the boy...the

the policeman caught him the man the policeman caught was the same
that I saw speak to the boy.
Prisoner. Q. If you saw me speak to the boy, what did you speak to
