434. THOMAS HUMBER was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of
August, 36 pairs of stockings, value 21. 11s., the goods of Joseph Wilson
and others, his masters.
MR. PHILLIPS conducted the Prosecution.
Isaac Wilson. I am in partnership with Mr. Joseph Wilson and
another-we carry on business in Milk-street, and manufacture at Notting-
ham and Derby-the prisoner was in our employ for about two years and
a half as warehouseman. In August last Inspector Waller brought me a
pair of brown cotton hose-in consequence of what he said I accused &
porter named Townsend-he caused Lemon to be brought to me, who
made a statement to me-I spoke to the prisoner in consequence of that-
I asked him how he could account for these goods-he said he was em-
ployed by a man to sell them, but he would not give his name-M'Lean
then said he must take him into custody.
Ephraim LEMON. I am a general dealer, and live in Beer-lane, Spital-
fields--I have known the prisoner twelve or fourteen months. In the
beginning of August he asked me to buy some stockings—be showed me
a sample-I told him I did not understand them-he gave me two pairs,
and said, “May be I could get something by them to buy them of him"-
these two were for samples—I showed them to one and another, and
next day bought them for 10s. a dozen-I bought seven dozen-I sold
four dozen and seven for 10s. 6d. a dozen-he gave me credit for the last
188.-I afterwards sold some to Mr. Dewey, a publican--M'Lean called
on me, and I went to Fetter-lane with him-the prisoner was found
there.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. What does "general dealer"
mean? A. Buying any thing I can get hold of in an honest way-I first
met the prisoner in the Nottingham Castle public-house-every person
that comes into that house knows I am a dealer-I have lived where I do
for three years and a half-I know Townsend now-he found me by means
of Mr. Dewey-I do not know whether stockings are to be sold cheap at
sales- I never bought such things before.
Thomas Dewey. I am landlord of the tap of the Swan-with-two-
necks. In August Lemon came to me to sell some brown cotton
stockings- I bought a pair from him for 1s.- I considered it very cheap
-in consequence of that I sent for Inspector Waller as I thought they
were stolen-Lemon came to me again, and bought twelve pairs the second
time--in consequence of my suspicion I detained the twelve pairs while 1
sent round the neighbourhood-Mr. Wilson has got one pair of them--the
others I used- I am sure this pair is one of the pairs out of the dozen-
gave them to Waller.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you known Lemon before? A. Yes I had
seen him before.
JAMES WALLER (City police-sergeant.) I got a pair of stockings from
Mr. Dewey- I took them to Mr. Wilson-I am sure these are them.
