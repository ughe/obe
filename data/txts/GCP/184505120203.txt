Q. On your oath, did not you go into the George between the time you left
the Anchor and Hope, and your going to the Elephant and Castle ? A. No,
I swear I did not go near the George--we were doing nothing between
leaving the Anchor and Hope, and going to the Elephant and Castle-we
walked right along the road - I cannot say how long it took us, but I can
swear we never went in anywhere after we left the Anchor and Hope--we
went from the Duke of Clarence at the corner of Southampton-street right
away up to the Elephant and Castle, straight up the Walworth-road-the
prisoner had shoes on--that I swear-Barton had on a white jacket, a
pair of moleskin trowsers, and a cap-I will not be certain whether I had
a pipe-I do not think I had - I believe the prisoner had a pipe-he bad
a cap on-I swear that--and his shoes also- I heard nothing said about
malleting anybody-we saw a policeman against the Duke of Clarence-I
said nothing to him, but Barton did I do not know what he said-I
cannot swear which policeman it was- I did not see another policeman
near Addington-square as we were going along.

in his company till seven o'clock next morning-during the whole of that
time he was not in the George at all-I parted with him at Mr. Rofy's,
where he works, opposite the Waterloo Arms, Camberwell-we went
through the George to get there-it is a thoroughfare - I have known the
prisoner as long as I have known myself, I know he had his shoes on
while he was in my company-I do not know what particular shoes he had
on--this cap is not his never saw him with a cap at all like it-I never
saw it before that I am aware of -I went before the Magistrate to give
evidence about the prisoner's being with me I was sworn.
