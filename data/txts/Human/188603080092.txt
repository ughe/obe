JOHN COUTTS ROSSALL( Re-examined by MR. HALL ). I am paying cashier—I do not think I should have paid a cheque bearing the signa ture to this 1,500 l .—I should have referred it—I should have said so before the bank manager said it—I think this is not the same signature; there is a difference, I cannot say where, in the word Noel—I should have left it in the manager's hands and not have taken the responsibility—if a forgery came to me I should refer it.
