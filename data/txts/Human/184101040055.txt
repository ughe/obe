NEW COURT .— Thursday, January  7 th , 1841.
   Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant .
 488. DAVID MURRAY was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of De cember , 1 till, value 2 s  .; 2 half-sovereigns, 1 crown, 6 half-crowns, 27 shillings, 8 sixpences, 4 groats, 15 pence, 52 half-pence, and 66 farthings, the property of Robert Ambrose Hall ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Five Days.
 489. GEORGE KIRBY was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of De cember , 1 pair of shoes, value 4 s  ., the goods of James Bagley , his matter; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined One Month.
 490. FREDERICK DUNBAR was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of January, 1 till, value 2 s  .; 2 groats, 6 pence, and 17 halfpence, the pro perty of Isaac Spooner ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 18.— Transported for Seven Years.—Parkhurst.
 491. Aged 5 l .— Confined One Year.
 492. JAMES O'DART was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of De cember , 2 pewter pots, value 3 s  . 3 d  ., the goods of William Morgan ; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Six Months.
 493. JOSEPH MATTHEWS was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of January, 20 shillings, the monies of Thomas Peachy , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months.—The last Week Solitary.
 494. THOMAS WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of December , 14lbs. weight of beef, value 8 s  ., the goods of Henry Oxley ; and that he had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 16.— Transported for Seven Years.—Convict Ship.
 495. THOMAS MIZEN was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of Ja nuary , 1 sheet, value 3 s  ., the goods of Mary Flannagan ; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Three Months.
 496. SAMUEL JORDAN was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of De cember , 5 pairs of stockings, value 5 s  . 2 1/2 d  ., the goods of William Laws ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 39.— Confined Three Months.
 497. ALFRED KING was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of January , 2 half-crowns, 9 shillings, and 11 sixpences, the monies of Jacob Watson ; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Three Months.
