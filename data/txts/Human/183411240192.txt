First Jury, before Lord Chief Justice Denman.
 185. BENJAMIN BRYAN and WILLIAM GRAY were indicted for burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Thomas Long , about the hour of one in the night of the   27th of October , at Richmond , with intent to steal, and feloniously and burglariously stealing therein 5 silver spoons, value 1 l  .; 1 tea-caddy, value 1 s  .; and 1 cork-screw, value 6 d  .; his goods. ANN STEWART. I am in the service of Mr. Long, a builder, living at Richmond. On Monday, the 27th of October, I shut up the house at night, and on Tuesday morning I was the first person up—I came down at a little after six, and observed a decanter and a piece of chewed tobacco on the stairs—it was not there the night before—I went into the back parlour, and found a cupboard there broken open—a bookcase there was opened, and the bills scattered all about the place—I had seen these places safe overnight—the cupboard had been opened with some instrument—I saw marks on it of some instrument—I know it was locked over night—the bookcase was fastened the night before—I did not notice any violence on that—the larder window, which I had seen safe over night, had the wire grating pushed down, and it appeared as if some body had pushed themselves through the window bars—nothing was dis placed but the wire grating, but the paint was rubbed off the bars—they are wide enough to admit a person through them—I missed four table spoons and two tea-spoons, from the kitchen cupboard—I put them altogether the night before, when I went to bed—the kitchen cupboard was shut, but not locked—master missed other things.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  At what time the night before did you go to bed? A.  Nine o'clock—I left my master up—the larder window is in the garden, at the back way to the house—I could reach it with my hand from the ground—the wire grating was inside—that was put up with two or three nails round it—I saw that before I went to bed—I was there the last thing before I went to bed—they are two upright bars—the window is not outside—there was a nurse girl in master's employ—she was gone to bed before me.
   COURT . Q.  Is the larder window in the wall of the house? A.  Yes; it is part of the house. THOMAS LONG. I am a builder, living at Richmond.
