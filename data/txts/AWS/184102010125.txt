743. HENRY GOODIN was indicted for stealing, on the lst of Febe
ruary, 2llbs. weight of lead, value 4s., the goods of George Adam Young,
his master.
GEORGE ROBINSON. I am foreman to George Adam Young, a builder,
in Harper-street, Red-lion-square-the prisoner was in his employ for about
two months. We had a job in Gracechurch-street-on the 1st of February
I called to the prisoner to stop as he was about leaving those premises
he pretended not to hear me, he went outside-I went out, and found
him just turning round the hoarding-I said I suspected that he had some
lead about him-be said he was sure he had not-I said I was pretty well
sure he had, and I must search him-I told him to go back-he went up
stairs-I followed him-he went partly up the second flight of stairs-I
saw bim take a quantity of lead from his person, and drop it down the
well-hole which is there-he took it from about the waistband of his clothes
-he had got it out in his hand before I saw it said I had seen enough,
there was suflicient-I got a policeman, and then I picked up this lead,
which turned out to be a piece of three-quarter lead, and some service pipe
which we were using in the basement of the premises-part of it was for a
water-closetwhen I returned back from going for the policeman the pri-
soner was goneI saw him next day in a public-house in Tooley-street,
and gave him into custody-h refused to go, and threw the policeman
down.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Why did you not take him yourself
A. I thought I had better get a policemanI was not more than sixteen
inches from him when he threw the lead down the hole-1 was on the
step below-the well-hole admits light on the stairs--I saw him take the
lead from his person-I believe he unbuttoned himself as he was going up
stairs, as when he turned round his clothes were all undone.
JoHN TOoLEY. I am a plasterer. I was at work there--the prisoner
passed me with Robinson behind him-I heard a noise when the lead fell,
and Robinson called out for me to look out that it did not fall upon me+
it fell perhaps five feet from me-there was about 21lbs.-the prisoner
escaped at that time-I went with Robinson and the officer to the publie-
house the next day--there was some scufllingy and I was struck by the
prisoner's wife.
Cross-examined. Q. About how much was the lead worth A. About
4s.-the prisoner was employed as a plumber's labourer by Mr. Young or
the foreman.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 3.-Reconmended to merey by the Jury.
Confined Three Months.
