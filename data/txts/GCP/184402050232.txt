AUGUSTUS RAYMOND. I live at Limetree-cottage, Rotherhithe--my brother
is proprietor of the wharf-I am the owner of the lead-the wharf belongs
to my brother-goods are landed there, and I have the carting of the pro-
perty, and am answerable for it--some lead was landed from a barge in Jan.,
and I cart it as I think proper--there were between 700 and 800 pigs of lead
there, and they were all marked the same as this one is-on the Sunday
morning the foreman gave me information, and I missed one pig.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you see this lead? A. Yes, on the Saturday
night, between six and seven o'clock- cart the lead for my brother-he is
the person who contracts with the owners of the lead-he pays me half-a.
erown a ton for the cartage-this lead was lying on his premises--I have
nothing to do with the owners- I should pay my brother if anything was gone,
MR. CURWOOD. Q. Your brother is the wharfinger, the lead was landed
on the wharf, and you have the contract for the cartage? A. Yes--if it is
lost, I am responsible to my brother--if property is stolen from the wharf,
my brother is answerable-I have to pay my brother, and he has to pay the
party-my servant has to watch the wharf, and if he loses it, I have to pay
--my brother's name is Mead Terry Raymond-this lead came for Pontifex's
-directly the barge comes alongside, I put a watchman in, and I am answer-
able for it.
MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. Did you see the whole of this lead on the wharf!
4. Yes-the original parcel was 900 pigs--I saw it landed-1 did not see it
put into the barge-we have had several pigs since, which were different to
this-here is the name of Grassington on one side of this pig, and I think
there is an F on the other, and the coat of arms- I do not think there is a
great quantity of lead of this description.
EDWARD EVEREST (Thames police-inspector.) I know both the prisoners.
On the night on which the lead is said to have been stolen, I saw Turner
near the prosecutor's premises, from five to ten minutes before ten o'clock.
(Henderson received a good character.)
HENDERSON-GUILTY. Aged 18. Recommended to meroy.
TURNER-Guilty. Aged 19. Confined Three Months.
