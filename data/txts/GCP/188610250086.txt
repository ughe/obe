hat on-he told me to drive to Portland Road Station-he was in an

me 18. fare, and walked across the road, through the station, and acruss
the road over the way-he wore a long, dark, ribbed coat, tight;
buttoned up.
Cro88-examined by MR. PURCELL. He had got in and shut the door

HENRY THOMAS Norrox. I am a hatter, of 43, Hampstead Road-
I was in my shop on 25th March-between 9 and 10 o'clock Palmer came
without a hat-he said he was a cabman, and had lost his hat the
previous evening, when he was out late, at Haverstock Hill, he must
have gone to sleep on his box-I was taking the goods from the window
when he entered on the Thursday morning, I never do so any other day,
the hats were strewn about-he bought one very quickly, he paid, and
went-I saw him next in custody.
Cro88-examined by MR. PURCELL. He was a stranger-my son was in
the shop--Sergeant Fraser called the next week-I saw Langrish at the
police-court-I did not see Palmer's photograph.
Re-examined. My shop is avout 500 or 600 yards from the Portland
