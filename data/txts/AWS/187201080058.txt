STANLEY AUGUBTUS GILL. I am one of the house surgeons at the
London Hospital--on 10th December, between 12 and 1 a.m., Frederick
Errington was brought there, suffering from a lacerated wound of the scalp,
at the back of his head-it was not dangerous, but he had lost blood, and
was rather faint-he also had a black eye-I do not think he had been
drinking.

and then a man came and gave me a blow on the back of my head with a
