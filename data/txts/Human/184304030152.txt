 1310. MARY ANN WARD, was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of March , 1 shilling; and 1 jug, value 1 s  .; the property of William Stevens , her master. MATILDA STEVENS. I am the wife of William Stevens, a baker, and live in Memel-street, St. Luke's—I make shirts—the prisoner came to work for me for a week—about two o'clock in the afternoon of Monday, the 13th of March, I gave her a shilling and a jug to get a pint of beer—she was to pay for it and bring me the change and the beer—I never saw her till the next day, when she was at the station—she said there she bought two rings with the money; and I heard her say at Worship-street that she went to the theatre with the money—that a boy fell against her and broke the mug. WILLIAM TILLIER. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner—she said she took the jug, and had gone to the theatre, and bought two rings with the money.
   Prisoners Defence.  I should have come back with the money and jug but I had the misfortune to break the jug. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Judgment Respited.
