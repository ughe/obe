NEW COURT .— Saturday, August  24 th , 1844.
   Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 2070. JOHN MOORE was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of James Adkins , from his person; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Three Months .
 2071. JAMES HUNT was indicted for embezzling the sum of 7 s  . 6 d  .; the monies of his master, Jacob Beisiegel ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Four Months .
 2072. JOHN MULLINS was indicted for stealing 1 waistcoat, value 2 s  .; and 1 pair of trowsers, 3 s  .; the goods of Jeremiah Mullins ; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Six Months .
 2073. MARY WHITEHEAD was indicted for stealing 2 knives, value 2 s  .; and 1 fork, 2 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Richard Rouse , to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 50.— Confined Seven Days .
 2074. EDWARD HOWE was indicted for stealing 1 shawl, value 16 s  . 9 d  .; 12 scarfs, 6 l  .; 7 s  .; 42 handkerchiefs, 5 l  .; and 3 shawls, 2 l  .; 10 s  .; the goods of James Martin , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined One Year .
 2075. LOUISA GORDON was indicted for stealing 1 frock, value 2 s  ., the property of William Lee ; to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 29.— Confined Three Months .
 2076. RICHARD RAWLINSON was indicted for stealing 3 pairs of shoes, value 9 s  .; and 1 pair of boots, 3 s  .; the goods of Joseph Clarke , his master; to which be pleaded GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Six Months .
 2077. MARY EYRE was indicted for stealing 1 gown, value 1 s  .; and 1 apron, value 6 d  .; the goods of Elizabeth Stonebridge : also 1 shawl, 5 s  .; the goods of John Shapley ; also 4 shirts, 12 s  .; 2 table-cloths, 3 s  .: 1 frock, 1 s  .; and 1 petticoat, 1 s  .; the goods of William Ward Miles ; to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 46.— Confined Nine Months .
 2078. WILLIAM ROBINSON was indicted for stealing 64lbs. weight of iron, value 1 l  .; the goods of William Lucas ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Nine Months .
 2079. ANN STEPHENS was indicted for stealing 1 gown, value 2 s  .; and 1 shawl, 1 s  .; the goods of John Mahoney ; to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 40.— Confined Three Months .
 2080. BENJAMIN SCULTHORPE was indicted for stealing 1 handker chief, value 1 s  ., the goods of Thomas Creason Killock , from his person; and that he had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Aged 25.— Transported for Ten Years .
 2081. WILLIAM DUNN was indicted for stealing 2 coats, value 10 l  .; 1 waistcoat, 1 l  .; 4 shirts, 4 l  .; 1 breast-pin, 5 l  .; and 2 pairs of boots, 2 l  .; the goods of John Nelson Bonitto , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined Four Months .
