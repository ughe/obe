 575. CAROLINE HODSON was again  indicted for stealing 2 hand kerchiefs, I table cloth, 1 pint of gin, and other articles, the property of Charles Davey , her master.
   MR. COOPER conducted the Prosecution. EMMA DAVEY. The prisoner was in my brother's service as housemaid—on the morning of 22d April, I found her putting some gin into a soda water-bottle—in consequence of that she was given in custody—her boles were searched in my presence, and this tobacco and other things taken out—there were several parcels of tea and sugar done up in this way, and a table-cloth—my name is on this handkerchief—the tablecloth is my brother's—she had no business with it in her box. CHARLES DAVEY. I saw the things taken from the prisoner's box—I know the table-cloth; this tray-cloth, and these gloves are mine—I had some cigars, and tea, and sugar, like these—she had no business with these things in her box—she used to get the breakfast of a morning—I have worn these gloves once, and pulled the buttons off them—there were thirteen cigars, I think—these are of an exactly similar kind to what I had.
   Prisoner's Defence.  The gloves were given to me. GUILTY.— Confined Four Months.
