 NOTE.
 On the 5th day of the Session,  John Bailey alias    Donald M'Craw, convicted at the April Session (see pages 250 and 252) was brought to the bar, when Mr. Justice Bayley stated that his case had undergone a full consideration by the Twelve Judges, who were of opinion that the house was properly described as the dwelling-house of Richard Ryan; for although the staircase window, at which he was supposed to have entered, was common both to Mr. Ryan and Mr. Choice, yet he had broken into a shop which was part of the house in the sole occupation of Ryan. The prisoner, accordingly, received sentence of DEATH with the other capital convicts.
 The Learned Judge also delivered the decision in the case of  James Walsh- (See January Sessions, page 140) - which was that the felony was complete, as the property was entirely removed from the place on which it stood. The prisoner was sentenced to be Transported for the term of Seven Years.
