THIRD COURT .— Thursday, October  26 th , 1854.
   PRESENT  —Mr. Ald. FAREBROTHER ; Mr. Ald. KENNEDY ; and Mr.
   COMMON SERJEANT .
   Before Common Serjeant and the Seventh Jury.
 1131. EDGAR GREEK , embezzling 16 l  . 19 s  . 6 d  ., the moneys of Richard Swift , his master: also  , stealing 2 warrants for the delivery of 16 cases of caoutchouc manufactures, and 1 other warrant for the delivery of goods of the said Richard Swift, his master: to which he PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Six Months .
 1132. MARY BROWN , stealing 1 shirt, and 1 bed gown; the goods of Patrick Nicholls : to which she PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 39.— Confined Three Months .
 1133. HENRY SELF , stealing 1 printed book, value 1 l  .; the goods of William Burch : also  , 1 printed book, value 1 l  .; the goods of Robert Schofield and others: to which he PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Four Months .
 1134. JOHN RANCE , unlawfully placing an iron hook on the   London and North Western Railway , with intent to overthrow a certain engine And carriages travelling thereon: to which he PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 14.
 (  Henry Beresford, superintendent of the railway police, stated that the luggage train was stopped by the hook in question; but that, had it been the express train, which was due, the consequences would have been very serious. ) Confined Fourteen Days .
 (  The prisoner's father gave him a good character. )
 1135. JOHN WHITTAKER , robbery on Sarah Butler , and stealing from her person 1 brooch, and 1 purse, value 1 s  .; and 2 s  . in money, the property of John Butler : to which he PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Twelve Months .
 1136. PHILIP MARKS , burglariously breaking and entering the dwell ing house of Thomas Collis , at   St. Margaret, Westminster , with intent to steal: to which he PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 41.— Confined Four Years .
 (  He was stated to have been the ringleader of a notorious gang for the last twelve months. )
