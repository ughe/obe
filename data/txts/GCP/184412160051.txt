216. MARGARET DAVISON was again indicted for stealing, on the
3rd of Dec., 1 scarf, value 21. ; 1 shawl, 1l. 108. ; 1 ring, 78. ; 3 collars,
86.; 2 aprons, 28. 6d. ; 1 purse, 38. 6d. ; 5 handkerchiefs, 4s. ; 1 veil, 23.;
3 neck-ties, 3s. 6d. ; 1 fan, 1s. 6d. ; 3 pairs of gloves, 25.; 1 pair of
mittens, 1s. ; 1 pair of cuffs, 58.; and 5 neckerchiefs, 2s, bd.; the goods
of Elizabeth Collins.
ELIZABETH COLLINS. I am single, and reside at No. 14, in the
Poultry, with my brother, who is a miniature painter. On the 3rd of
Dec., about seven o'clock in the evening, on going into my chamber, I
observed my drawers open, and one quite empty, from which I missed the
articles stated-I had seen them safe a few hours before I saw some of
them again on Saturday the 14th.
