sers, 1l.; 1 waistcoat, 78.; and i handkerchief, 68.; the goods of David
Watson: and 1 coat, 21. 18s.; 1 pair of trowsers, 11. ; and 1 handkerchiel,
38.; the goods of John Watson, in his dwelling-house, and that Williams
had been before convicted of felony.
AGNES WATSON. I am the wite of John Watson, and live in Mary Ann's
place, Shadwell. On the 7th of June, about six o'clock, I missed two suits
of clothes, with the exception of one waistcoat, all the property of David
Watson, and a pair of trowsers and handkerchief of John Watson's, which I
had seen safe on the sofa in my dwelling-house, between ten and eleven in
the morning-I found a key in the parlour when I came home, and the things
all gone I have not seen any of them since.
Cross-examined by Mr. PARNELL. Q. I believe you left a little boy in
the house? A. Yes, my youngest son, William Bailey-he is eleven years
old, he is not here.
