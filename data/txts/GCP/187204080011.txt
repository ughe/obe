319. ANN, EDMUNDS (30), Feloniously uttering counterfeit coin.
Messrs. COLERIDGE and De MICHELE conducted the Prosecution.
MARY ANN TAYLOR. I am barmaid at 96, Cannon Street-on 15th March
I served the prisoner with a half-quartern of rum, which came to 3d. - she
gave me a florin, and I gave her the change-I saw that the florin was bad,
and gave it to Mr. Bell, who broke it-I said that it was bad-she said sho
was not aware of it, and picked the pieces up, and gave a good one-Mr.
Bell followed her.
Hoe
ELIZABETH GRAHAM. I am barmaid at 109, Upper Thames Street on
16th March I served the prisoner with a glass of alo-she gave me a florin,
I put it in the till, where there was only. some small change, and gave her
16. 6d. and coppers in change-she left and Mr. Bell came in- I looked at
the coin and found it was bad-he marked it, but did not take it away-I
gave it to him later on.
EDWARD BELL. I assist my father, who keeps a public-house at 96,
Cannon Street-I remember the prisoner ooming there-the barmaid handed
me a bad coin-I broke it and put the pieces on the counter, and the
prisoner took them up she said that she got it for doing some washing-
she paid with good money and left-I followed her into Thames Street,
where she met Jones—(see next case) --she left him and went with her child
into the Shakspeare - I went in and spoke to the barmaid -I then
followed them to Allhallow's Pier, and went on board the steamboat with
them to Hungerford, where they got out and got into a small row-boat-I
followed them on the Embankment--they got out at the Temple, and I
gave them in custody at Essex Street, with the coin.
GEORGE PARSONS (Policeman E R44). On 15th March Mr. Bell gave
the prisoner into my charge at the top of Essex Street-this bottle was
found on her--Mr. Bell gave me this marked coin.
