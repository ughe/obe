THOMAS BALCHER ( City police-constable, No. 618.) I was called on
on the morning of the 26th of March to take the prisoner—I asked him
who and what he was—he said he did not know—I asked him to give some
account of himself—he hesitated, and I said if he did not give an account
he must go to the station— I took hold of his collar, and he turned and
struck me a violent blow on the mouth, and knocked out two of my teeth.
Prisoner. Q. Did you produce the teeth before the Lord Mayor? A.
I swallowed one, I could not produce that—I produced one piece of a
tooth—I was under the doctor's hands for six days—I drank one glass of
ale next day with your master.
