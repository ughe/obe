58. ; 2 table-cloths, value 123.; and 2 pairs of sheets, value 78.; the
goods of Jane Hibbert.
ELLEN Welch. I am servant to Mrs. Jane Hibbert. I know the pri-
soner-she came on the 15th of November for the linen-I gave her some
linen, but I cannot say what articles they were.
NOT GUILTY.
398. WILLIAM STROUD was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of
November, 2 sovereigns, and 10 shillings, the monies of George Ryder
Peppercorne.
MR. PHILLIPS conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE RYDER PEPPERCORNE. I am secretary to the Vauxhall Water
Works Company. The prisoner was in their employ the room in which
he worked is divided from my room, in which I kept my money, by
door-I was out of my room on the 13th of November, from five to ten
minutes, and in the evening of that day I missed some money-when I
left my office that day, the prisoner was in the outer office, and when I re-
turned he told me that no one had gone into my office-(no one could
have gone in without his seeing them)-in consequence of something
heard afterwards, I called the prisoner to me-I asked him whether the
statement made by his uncle was correct, as to his taking certain money
from me he said it was so.
