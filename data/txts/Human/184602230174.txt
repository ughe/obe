 799. ELIZABETH WISEMAN was indicted for stealing 1 shawl, value 8 s  .; the goods of John Bassett . CAROLINE BASSETT. I am the wife of John Bassett, of Upper Marsh, Lambeth. The prisoner lodged with us, and left while I was ill—I missed a shawl—this now produced is it. CHARLES RUSSELL. I am a pawnbroker—I took in this shawl on the 30th of Jan. I believe of the prisoner.
   EDWARD DIXON ( police-cotistable L  85.) I took the prisoner—she told me I could find the shawl at Mr. Stevens's, where Mr. Russell is shopman—I did so. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Two Days .
 (The prisoner's mother promised to take charge of her.)

 800. JAMES WEST was indicted for stealing 2 pianos, value 5 s  .; 1 stock and bit, 8 s  .; and 1 gauge, 1 s  .; the goods of Thomas Mellor . THOMAS MELLOR. I am a carpenter, and live in Ely-place, South Lam beth—on the 13th of Feb. I was working at a building which I locked up at five o'clock—I left there a stock, a plane, a gauge, a trying plane, and some other things, which I have not found—I missed them all when I went on the Monday morning—the plane, stock, and gauge produced are mine. CHARLES RUSSELL. I am shopman to Mr. Stevens, a pawnbroker, at Newington. This plane was pawned on the 10th of Jan. for 1 s .—I do not know who by. CHARLES FRANKLIN. I am shopman to Mr. Fletcher, a pawnbroker. This stock and gauge were pawned on the 17th of Jan. by the prisoner—I am able to swear to him. WILLIAM MORTON( police-constable P  289.) I took the prisoner—he denied it.
   Prisoner.  I pawned them for a man I met in London-road—I did not know him.
 * GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Six Months .
 801. CHARLES WEST was indicted for stealing 1 gate, value 8 s  .; the goods of the London and South Western Railway Company .
   MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution. JOHN CUNNINGHAM( police-constable V  195.) I was on duty about a quarter past ten o'clock at night on the 18th of Feb. in the Wundsworth-road, and met the prisoner with this gate—I asked where he got it—he said he had brought it from St. John's Wood, he had bought and paid for it—I said if he would come with me I would show him where he brought it from—he went back with me about half a mile to Belmont-place, Wandsworth-road—I showed him where he took it from, and told him I had seen it there a quarter of an hour before—he said, "I did not take it from there, I took it off the path."
