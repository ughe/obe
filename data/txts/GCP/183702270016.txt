Q. Had you written any other letter on the subject of bills, except

other transaction--I had not accepted any bill besides the four I hare
mentioned, before I received Gardiner's letter-I had accepted one a long
time before that for 251.--those were the only bills I ever accepted in my
life-after that I received an application from a gentleman named Simpso
by letter, and desired Mr. Keily, a friend of mine in town, to go to Mr.
Hance-I authorized him to take this letter to Mr. Hance (read.)
"Marine Barracks, Portsmouth, December 17th, 1836-Sir, This let.
ter will be handed to you by Mr. Keily, who will pay you the 100.,

pensation that appears fair to him for the period which the loan has ex-
