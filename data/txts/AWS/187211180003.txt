LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES.
OLD OURT.-Monday, November 18th, 1872.
Before Mr. Deputy Recorder.
1. WILLIAM VICKERY (30), WILLIAM LINSEY (24), and EDWARD
PARKER (29), were indicted for feloniously wounding William Hammond,
with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
Mr. A. B. KELLY conducted the Prosecution MR. STRAIGHT defended Parker,
and Mr. RIBTON defended Linsey.
WILIAM HAMMOND. I am in the employment of Mr. Boughton, the lessee
of Hounslow Heath- am paid wages by him for keeping the Heath, to look
after the fences and the cattle, and to prevent trespassersabou 5 o'elock
in the afternoon of 13th October the three prisoners came on the Hesth,
and were proceeding to walk across the Heath-I told them they were
trespassing, and they had no right there.; they said, with an oath, that they
were there, and they meant going-I told them they would be liable to be
turned back again, and while I was telling them that, Linsey drew up olose
to me, and hit me a violent blow in the mouth with his fist, and was
knoeked down immediately, and seized by all three prisoners; they all three
closed in, and kioked and hit me with violence, and said We have got you
here in a good place now. we will do for you"--they all three said that
they kicked me in the eye, which bled most fearfully 1 have the scar, and
you can see the place noon of them, I don't know which, said 46 Well,
he has got enough, we have given him enough; we won't give him any more
and another said shan't take any further part in it". don't know which
it was said that; I was almost insensible at the time- got up on one knee,

done anything to them before they assaulted me; I had not put my hands
to them, or attempted to: I merely told them they had no right to pass
I got up, and proceeded home as well as I could, and lost about a pint and
