2472. HENRY APPLEYARD was indicted for stealing of a pound
of tea, value 1s. 60.; and of a pound of tobacco, 18.; the property of Ben-
jamin Lance, his master.
BENJAMIN LANCE. I keep a shop in Church-street, Greenwich-the pri-
soner was in my employ-I lost a sovereign, and desired to search him-he
ran up stairs-I caught him by the coat and found on him two parcels---one
was tea, and the other tobacco I suppose they were mine-he told the Ma-
gistrate that he removed it with the intention of telling me about it-29s. 6d.
was found upon him, and he had borrowed money of me the day before to
pay for his washing.

2474. SUSAN PARKER was indicted for stealing 1 sovereign, and 1
half-sovereign ; the moneys of Richard Humphrey Nunney, from the person
of Susan Nunney.
Susan NUNney. I am the wife of Richard Humphrey Nunney. On the
15th of Oct., about eleven o'clock in the morning, I was a little tipsy-I laid
a sovereign, a half-sovereign, and a shilling on the table--the prisoner came
in, and soon after the money was gone-I was a little too tipsy to see who
took it.
MARY ANN HUNTER. I was at Mrs. Nunney's, in Church-street, Dept-
ford, at eleven o'clock in the morning, on the 14th of Oct. she was a little
the worse for liquor--the prisoner came in with two quartern loves from the
parish, which she offered to sell for a sbilling-Mrs. Nunney said, " Take
them home to your children"-she said, "No"-Mrs. Nunney gave her &
shilling for them the prisoner said, "I will send for a pot of beer" Mrs.
Nunney said, "Do as you like about that" - the prisoner then moved and
sat down by Mrs. Nunney-she said she had no more money than what she
had received with the bread.
