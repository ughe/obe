1806. BENJAMIN PRIOR was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of
June, 2 sovereigns, 1 half-crown, 3 shillings, and 2 sixpences, the moneys
of Willam Hawkins, his master.
WILLIAM HAWKINS. I am a biscuit-baker, and live in Tooley-street-
the prisoner was my journeyman-I sent him to a wharf to get a sack of
flour-I gave him two sovereigns, a half-crown, and 3s. 6d.-he went out,
and did not return, nor did the flour come.
Henry Peckham. I am a constable of the Victoria Theatre-I re-
ceived information, and took the prisoner on the stairs of the theatre.
Prisoner's Defence. There was one sovereign, nineteen shillings, and
three half-crowns; I got tipsy, and lost a sovereign and a half out of my
pocket.
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Confined Six Months.
1807. WILLIAM ASPIN was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of
May, 1 jacket, value 158.; and 1 handkerchief, value 6d. ; the goods of
Henry Pearcy.
HENRY PEARCY. I am captain of a Gravesend steamer. I was lying
at Hungerford Pier on the 29th of May, about half-past eleven o'clock at
night, I left my jacket and handkerchief in the cabin, and went to bed-I
missed them about five o'clock the next morning--the handkerchief is now
here—the jacket has not been found-three of the crew's pockets were
picked while we were asleep.
THOMAS GRIMSTON. I am a Thames police-inspector. In consequence
of the robberies on board the steamers I was induced to watch-on Mon-
day night last I was in ambush, and saw the prisoner pass in a boat be-
longing to the Westminster Pier Company-I followed in about two
minutes, and saw the boat lying alongside the Nymph-I went on board,
and hallooed out to the crew-I turned on my light, and saw the prisoner
crouching, with his shoes off, close to where the crew were lying-I said,
“What are you doing here?"-he said, "I am the fireman"-the crew
awoke, the captain said they knew nothing of him-I searched the pri-
soner, and found two silk handkerchiefs, a cotton handkerchief, a knife, a
brush, and a purse-I said, “How did you come by these ?"-he said,
“They all belong to me, I have had them twelve months "-this handker-
chief belongs to the prosecutor.
Prisoner. I throw myself on the mercy of the Court.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Transported for Seven Years.
Before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
