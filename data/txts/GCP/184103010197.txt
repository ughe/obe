998. SARAH PETERS was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of
February, 1 sheet, value 2s. 6d.; and 1 blanket, value 28.; the goods of
Joseph Wilkinson, her master.
JOSEPH WILKINSON. I am a shoemaker, and live in Kent-street. The
prisoner was in my employ for a month-I keep a lodging-house, and she
had the care of the beds-on the 22nd of February I missed a sheet and a
blanket-these are them.
SAMUEL HOBELL. I am a pawnbroker. This sheet and blanket were
pledged by the prisoner, on the 22nd of February.
Prisoner. I was sent by my master to pawn a bandkerchief about halfs
past eight o'clock, and I was not in the shop after that. Witness. I am
sure he is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 42.-Confined Four Months.
Before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.

1000. THOMAS TRIMMER was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
February, 1 loaf of bread, value 44d., the goods of James Starmer Brett.
JAMES STARMER BRETT. I lost this loaf from my cart, which was at
Mrs. Graham's gate, at Clapham, on the 19th of February-this is the
loaf.
JOSEPH Wain. I saw the prisoner take a loaf out of the cart—he walked
a little way, and then ran- I told Mr. Brett, who took him.
Prisoner. I was not there, I was working in a garden.
(The prisoner received a good character, and his master engaged to em-
play him again.)
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Eight Days.
