866. GEORGE PIERCEY was indicted for a like offence.
MESSRS. BODKIN and ELLIS, JUN., conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY FAYLE. I am landlord of the Victoria public house, No 5, Pre
vidence-row, Limehouse. On Sunday, 7th Sept., the prisoner came to my
house-he had a glass of ale, it came to 1 d., he gave me a shilling-1 gav
him 104d. change, and put the shilling into the till, there was no other
therehe went to the door, and spoke to some one--he returned, and asked
for a bottle of soda water to take away he gave me a 58. piece I gavo
him 48. 8d. change--he was just going away, and was caught and brought
back-after he gave me the crown, I dropped it into the till where the
shilling was, and there was no other crown there—a gentleman spoke to
me, I took the crown out of the till, and went after the prisoner, and
brought him back--he gave me a good erown, and said he did not know the
crown was bad-he wished very much to be let off, said he did not know it
was bad, and that he took it at Deptford—I gave the bad crown to the
policeman, and the shilling also-when the prisoner was going down the
road, he said he gave me 11d. for the glass of ale, he did not give me
shilling.
Prisoner. Q. Did not I give you 11d for the glass of ale ? A. No, you
gave me a shilling I did not say I could not swear that you gave it mo-
that was a shilling that you gave me on Saturday night I had taken & bed
shilling then, but I could not swear that you gave me that shilling.
MR. BODKIN. Q. What interval was there between his giving you the
shilling and the crown piece A. Not more than two or three minutes
the prisoner had been there on Saturday night, and he had given me s
shilling I afterwards found that I had a bad sbilling, but I could not
swear that the prisoner gave me that one-he had a glass of ale on Saturday
night, which came to 14d.
JAMES HUNT (policeman, K 384) I was called to the house of the last
witness, and took the prisoner-I produce this crown pieee that the witne
gave me the prisoner said he came from Deptford, and he took the bl.
piece there that day-on going to the station this shilling was produced
and the prisoner was charged with uttering it-he said he had paid with
1 d., he had not passed that shilling--he was asked his address, he said it
some street in Aldersgate-street, and at the station be refused to give his
address.
Prisoner. Q. Did I not say to the proseoutor, “Can you swear that I
gave you that shilling ?" and did he not say, "I can't swear that he gave
me that" ? A. I did not hear him say anything about it.
MR. BODKIN. Q. Did the prosecutor express any doubt about the pri-
soner passing the shilling on the Sunday ! A. No, he said he was almost
sure it was the same as the shilling he had passed on the Saturday night.

are both counterfeit.
