GARRATT MUNRO PEARCE (Policeman, M 59). I took Mrs. Frazier into
custody on 20th June, at a quarter-past 10, P.M.- I told her I should have to
take her for being concerned with other parties in robbing Mr. White of 971;
she said she knew nothing about it-I said she would have to accompany me
to the station she then said that she would not be screwed up for any person;
she did know who committed the robbery and she would round on them, it was
Rollicky and Sailor-I then asked her whether she had any money-she
said she had none-I afterwards searched her drawers, and in a shawl in the
drawers I found 188. 6d. in silver-I then took her to the station- I did
not say anything more about my information; I said, "From information
I received"-I then said, " At all events the money has come through your
place, through your bands"-she made no reply to that.
