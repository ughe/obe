smaller notes, and gold - I then left, and went to Smith, Payne, and Co.
which is close by the Mansion House-I had an appointment there with
Mr. Goodchild, who had not arrived, but in a short time he came up, while

and cashed a cheque for 3001., for which I got a 3001. note-I delivered that,
and the 1001 note I got from the Union Bank, to Mr. Goodchild-it was
the only 1007. note I had I had waited at Smith, Payne's, at least ten

ticular standing there, so many people stand waiting there for busses and so
