858. THOMAS M'CARTHY, JOHN M'CARTHY, and PATRICK
MALONEY, feloniously cutting and wounding John Rourke, with intent
to do him grievous bodily harm.
MR. RIBTON conducted the Prosecution.
JoHN ROURKE. I live at Isleworth. On Sunday evening, 16th March
I went from Isleworth to New Brentford, between six and seven o'clock-
I went into the Red Lion-yard to see my brother-there. were people
walking about the yard-I did not see the prisoners there-at a little after
seven, I was standing out in the road, and Maloney came up and hit me
with bis fist-then four women came up, and pulled my hair-I afterwards
saw John M'Carthy-he hit me with a brick on the front of the head; be
flung it at me-I could not get away-I saw Thomas M'Carthy there, and
he flung a brick and hit me on the side of the head with it, after I had
been struck with the other-they were pulling and hauling me about-)
made my way to the Town Hall, and put my band out- do not recollect
anything more.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. There was a row in the street? 4.
Yes, a great one; I could not say bow many people were there--] was stand
ing looking did not interfere-I do not know whether there were retwod
different counties-] do not know that they all appeared nearly drunk
I was not drinking at all-I do not know that my brother belonged aby
faction- did not challenge Maloney to fight outside the house-he did
not refuse-I did not knock him down-I did not hang my coat on the Tow
Hall railing and when Maloney got up, attack him again- took off my coat
to save myself, and gave it to a little girl, but not that I might fight better.
Q. Were you not fighting with Maloney, and was not he down unde
neath you A. No, Sir; Maloney and I fell together when be first hit
me he was not under me-Thomas M'Carthy did not come up and get
Maloney away from under me-I did not strike Thomas M'Carthy and knock
him down- saved myself as well as I could-I struck back again after
he struck me-I do not know whether there were thirty fights in the street,
or whether they made a great noise; I heard a seream-I do not know
whether there were as many as 300 persons, or whether Thomas M'Carthy
came up after I had been returning the blow to Maloney I did not see
him-M'Carthy did not touch me at all till after Maloney had bit me and
I had hit him again-] was not above five minutes at the place-I had
been ten minutes or a quarter of an hour with my brother in the house-
he lives in the Red Lion-yard-it does not belong to the public-housef
is at the back.
