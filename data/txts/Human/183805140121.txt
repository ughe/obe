 1303. ELLEN MADDEN was indicted for stealing, on the   8th of May , 1 watch, value 4 l  ., the goods of John Woodhouse . JOHN WOODBOUSE. I am a seaman, belonging to the Percy , of Durham. On the 8th of May I went with the prisoner to a public-house in Ratcliff-highway—I pledged my watch to a man named Bush for 5*.—he is not here—the prisoner was present at the time, and saw me give him the watch!—she had part of some drunk with the money, and so had Bush, and I went away and left them—this is my watch—( looking at it ,) ROBERT SMITH. I keep a cook's-shop in Ratcliff-highway. On Mon day night Bush came to me, and asked me to give him change for a sove reign—I said I could not—he asked me if I could lend him 5 s . till the morning—I had got but 4 s ., and was going to get him shilling's worth of halfpence—he said "No, I have got 1 s . "—and he said, "I am going to lend 5 s . on this watch till the morning"—he left the watch with me—he came again and took it away, and left me a sovereign. JOHN BONHAM. I am shopman to Mr. Fleming, a pawnbroker, in Whitechapel. This watch was pledged with me on Tuesday afternoon, the 18th of August, by the prisoner. JOHN MITCHELL( police-constable  K 161.) I took the prisoner, and asked her if she knew of a watch left with Mr. Tavener's bar-man—she said, "I have pledged it, and here is the ticket"—I said, "Was the seaman with you?"—she said, "No"—I said, "Did he give you leave?"—she said, "No."
   Prisoner'8 Defence . I pawned it for 5 s . and gave the man 1 s .—I was looking for the prosecutor to give him the money, and was taken up. GUILTY. Aged 32.— Confined Three Months.
