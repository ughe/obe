1704. CHARLES RYAN, alias Charles Callaghan, was indicted for
burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-bouse of Nathan Smith,
about the hour of 1 in the night, on the 24th of April, with intent to steal,
and stealing therein, 1 looking-glass, value 10s., his property,
NATHAN SMITH. I live in Bridge-street, in the parish of Bermondsey.
On Wednesday, the 24th of April, I went to bed about half-past ten o'clock
-I was the last person up-I thought all the doors and windows were
fast-I had been out all day, and came in at that time I did not examine
them-I saw that the back-door was fast, and went up to bed directly
- fastened the front-door myself I got up just before eight o'clock in
the morning, and went out directly I saw the back-door was opet, but
went out without taking further notice, thinking some of my daughters
had been down and opened it.
CAROLINE SMITR. I am the prosecutor's daughter. I went to bed on
Wednesday, the 24th of April, at ten o'clock, before my father came home
-I bolted the back-doorI got up just before eight o'clock in the morn-
ing, after my father---this looking-glass was on the sideboard the night be
fore, I am sure-I missed it in about ten minutes after I came down
found the back-door open the bolts were broken the back-room door
was broken open also, which was locked when I went to bed the glass
had been in that room.
