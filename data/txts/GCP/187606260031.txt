WILLIAM BALOHIN. I am a forwarding agent and merchant at 2, Fen-
church Street Buildings in May, 1872, Mr. William J. Brown came to my
office respecting a sale of wineshe married my sister-I had known the
| firm of Saul, Godfrey, & Brown many years before this interview I had
I bought their wine for private use I had seen Brown before with reference
Ito & sale ; I saw him somewhere about March or April, 1872I recollect &
sale of wine by Mr. Godsall in April, 1872--I had seen Mr. Brown about
11th January, 1872; when he brought me wine warrants-the wines they
represented were sold in bond, partially by Mr. Godsall I delivered the
warrants afterwards to someone else about 11th January Mr. Brown
called on me and stated that he had a lot of family bills coming due, and
had not the money to meet them, and not liking to expose the affairs of
the firm to money-lenders, he had arranged for a privato advance on dook
warrants, using my name, for which I should have a commission-I agreed

in a list-it is in Brown's writing-lhe handed me a cheque for 7681., which

-I don't remember by whom it was signed that is all that took place on

uoming I put them in Godsalls hands for sale---I put in for sale from 397

values are given "-I then allowed invoices to be sent from Saul, Godfrey,
& Brown, Aldersgate Street, to me-_1 paid this firm for the whole of the
