   Cross-examined.  I got you down on the deck and hit you hard. EDWARD PLUMMER( Thames Police Inspector ). I was called to the Baron Osy on 29th September and saw the prosecutor bleeding from a slight wound under his right eye and on his nose—he told me in the prisoner's presence that the prisoner had assaulted him—I took the prisoner.
   PRISONER'S DEFENCE . This mate told me I was not working hard enough—I said I could not work harder—he asked me a second and a third time—I told him to shut up, and he told Mr. Nye, who called me up, and then this mate snatched the hook and tore my jacket, which saved my flesh, and I thought it was time to defend myself—I closed with him and Mr. Nye came to his assistance—they got me down and thumped me, and called another man, who also tumbled on me—my teeth are loose now—the hook accidentally caught the mate in the face—I did not intend to hurt him, and he did not know he was hurt till somebody told him he was scratched—I have worked for him twelve years, and never had a quarrel before. GUILTY of unlawfully wounding. Recommended to mercy by the Jury— Twelve Months' Imprisonment.
