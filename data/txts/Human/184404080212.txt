At half-past eight o'clock that night I went through my trap door to the top of my house—I saw Daniels in the corner of the gutter, and asked what he did there—he said, "Pray forgive me this time, I will never come here any more"—I took him to the trap door to take him down—he insisted on going down first, and when he got down he took away the ladder and left me there—I found the lead was cut and rolled up from the coping and the tiles.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How many months before had you been up there? A.  I may have been there about six weeks ago—I do not know whether Daniels removed the ladder by accident—he did move it, and I dropped down—the lead was entirely separated from the other. JAMES HAYWARD( policeman. ) I saw Watts drop from a shed about seven houses from Bones' in Adam and Eve-court—he was endeavouring to conceal this knife, and dropped it behind him—I asked him where he fell from—he said, "From the top here," pointing up—he was stunned. HENRY LEE( policeman. ) I saw the lead—it was cut off so that it could have been carried away without using a knife again—the cutting appeared to be quite fresh.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Can you swear whether it was cut a week or ten days before? A.  It was quite fresh—to the best of my belief it was cut at that very time—it was cut away, and the coping of the wall was taken off—the lead was rolled up and entirely cut off from the other. (Watts received a good character.)

 1282. MARGARET MINTON was indicted for stealing 4 sovereigns the monies of Thomas Brown , her master. SELINA BROWN. I am the wife of Thomas Brown, of North-street, Red Lion-square. The prisoner was in my service for six days—I put fourteen sovereigns, and two 5 l . notes into the cash-box on the 1st of April, and took it up stairs—I brought it down to the parlour with me the next morning—I did not leave the parlour till about three o'clock in the afternoon, when I left the prisoner to sweep the room—I went to the parlour at four o'clock and missed four sovereigns from the box—the prisoner was out then—when she returned I accused her, and she denied it—I gave her in charge, and four sovereigns were found in her hair—I had left the key of the box in the parlour. HARRIET FOWLER. I searched the prisoner and found four sovereigns in the back of her hair.
