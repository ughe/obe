First Session, 1862–63.
M RS. POLAND and Besley conducted the Prosecution.
John RUTHERFORD. I am in the employment of Mr. Bewley and another,
who carry on business as “The Gutta Percha Company," in Wharf-road,
City-road-I have been in their service between nine and ten years I have
known the prisoner about eighteen months-I first beaume acquainted with
him at his shop in Gray's-inn-lane it is a gutta percha shop, and he sells
boots as well—I went to his shop first, with four pounds weight of gutta
peroba--that was about eighteen months ago--I had obtained that from the
masticating room of the Gutta Peroba Company's Works, where I was em
ployed-I had no authority to take it; I stole it-I asked the prisoner
whether he bought it-he said, “Yes," he gave 8d. a pound-I sold it to
him-I think he gave me 26. 8d. then--that was the first time I saw him
I did not tell him who I was or where I lived; nothing was said by him as
to that-nothing was said about my returning to the shop again I went
there again, about a fortnight afterwards, with sixteen pounds weight of
gutta percha, which I obtained in the same way I sold it to the prisoner
for the same price-after I had been there once or twice more, the prisoner
said to me he knew where I worked, because he bad see me come out of
the frotory gate I had some gutta peroha at the time be said that that
was some time last summer I did not see him till about three weeks after
that, I think, when I met him accidentally at the corner of Edward stret
-that is about 250 yards from the gutta pereha factory-- he asked me if I
had got anything by me I told him no; I would get him some
afterwards went to his place again, with about eighteen pounds weight
my lodgings were at Vaughan-terrace, Shepherdese-walk-I lodged there
two years ago I have left about four or five months. I saw the prisoner
near my lodgings after I had sold him the eighteen pound he asked
me again if I had anything by me I told him no; I would bring him
some down-I afterwards went to his shop in Gray's-inn-lane, and took
about twenty pounds that time that was before last Ohristmas, at the
latter end of Ootober or the beginning of November, I can't say which
-I went to his shop twice after Christmas the last time was in April
then took eighteen pounds of gutta perche sold that at 8d. a pound_
sold it all at 8d-it was all stolen by me from the factory.
Cross-examined by Mr. Ribon, 0. How long have you been a thief
4. Eighteen months ; not before that I began to steal the gutta peroba
about April-that is the only thing I have stolen I am in the employment
of the Gatta Peroha Company-I am not there now I have not been dis
charged—I have not been tliere this week-- I do not expeot to go back again
-I should say they will not take me back; I am not sure of that was
employed up to last Friday-I sold the prisoner altogether about eleven lote
- have sold to two other persons besides the prisoner or not tell
whether they will be here next sessions - I have only sold to two others!
that is all I gave their names up to the Company
