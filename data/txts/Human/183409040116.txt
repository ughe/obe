 There were two other indictments against the prisoner.
 1433.  JOHN DUNKIN was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of August , 1 timepiece, value 50s., the goods of Richard Palmer Green.
 RICHARD PALMER GREEN. I live in  Bath-street, City-road, and am a watchmaker. I missed this timepiece on the 8th of August, at eleven o'clock - I had seen it half-an-hour before.
   EVAN DAVIES(police-constable G 192). I was on duty in Whitecross-street. I saw the prisoner with a bundle under his arm, going along Long's-buildings, between three and four o'clock - he saw me, and ran off - I pursued and took him with this timepiece.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was selling some books and garters - a young man asked me to carry this timepiece to Whitecross-street, and said he would give me 3d. - he saw the policeman, and ran off.
   - WOOD . I am a cutler, and live at No. 32, Bird-street. The prisoner has been in my employ, and I will employ him again.
     GUILTY. Aged 14. - Confined Six Days.
 1434.  WILLIAM COOK was indicted for embezzlement.
   CHARLES HORNE. I live in York-place, St. Luke's, and am a shoe-maker. The prisoner was in my employ, and it was his duty to receive money for me when I sent him with goods - on the 18th of July, I sent him to Mr. Campion, in Bridgewater-square - he was to receive 11s. 8d. for me - he did not return to me.
   JOHN CAMPION. The prisoner received 11s. 8d. of me on the 18th of July, for his master.
 (  JOSEPH MARTIN, of Bowl-court, Playhouse-yard, gave the prisoner a good character, and promised to employ him.)
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Confined Three Months.
 1435.  ELIZABETH JONES and SARAH SMITH were indicted for stealing, on the 11th of July , 18 yards of printed cotton, value 9s., the goods of William Spooner.
 The prosecutor did not appear. NOT GUILTY.
 1436.  CHARLES WARREN was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of August , 1 basket, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Robert Houghton.
   SARAH HOUGHTON. I am the wife of Robert Houghton - we keep a shop in St. Marylebone. On the 23rd of August, I was coming home in the evening, and saw a man looking at the window, next door but one to our house - the prisoner came from our door with this basket in his hand, and an open pen knife - I caught him, and told him he should not move, but he got from me - I called, "Stop thief," and the officer took him - he was not tipsy - this basket is my husband's.
   HENRY GARNETT(police-constable D 148). I heard the alarm, and took the prisoner - the lady had the basket - the prisoner said, "I should not have cut it, but some one told me" - I did not perceive that he was tipsy.
 Prisoner's Defence. Another persuaded me to do it.(  John Newcomb gave the prisoner a good character, and promised to employ him.) GUILTY. Aged 17. - Recommended to mercy. - Confined Six Days.
 1437.  CHARLES KNATCHBULL was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of April , 5 spoons, value 2l.; 2 glass decanters, value 6s.; 2 sheets, value 10s.; 2 pillow-cases, value 1s.; and 1 table-cloth, value 5s.; the goods of Maria Warren.
 The prosecutrix did not appear. - NOT GUILTY.
 1438.  JOHN PHILLIPS was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of August , 2 gowns, value 10s.; 1 frock, value 2s.; and 1 shawl, value 5s.; the goods of Mary Ann Cox.
 MARY ANN COX. I live in  Peel-street, Kensington Gravel-pits. The prisoner lodged at my house - he left on the 5th of August, and did not pay his last week's rent - I then missed these articles from my drawers by his bedside.
   WILLIAM GOLDER. I am a pawnbroker. These articles were pawned on the 5th of August, in the name of John Parker - I cannot say whether the prisoner pawned them, but his face is familiar to me.
   THOMAS JOSEPH AVIS(police-constable 39). I took the prisoner, and he produced this duplicate of the property.
 Prisoner's Defence. My brother-in-law said if I would give up the duplicate he would say no more about it - the officer said the same.
   THOMAS JOSEPH AVIS. I never mentioned such a word, nor heard it mentioned.
     GUILTY. Aged 38. - Confined Six Months.
