1567. JOHN HEDDERWICK was indicted for stealing 1 101. Bank-
note, the property of Thomas Eady, his master.
Thomas Eady. I am a solicitor, and live in Clement's-inn; the prisoner was
in my employ as clerk for three months. On the 6th of July I had a 101. note,
which I received from Twining's-I cut it in two, and inclosed one half,
directed to Mr. William Allen Jones, in Dublin-I gave the prisoner that and
the other half to enclose, telling him to put one in the post-office, and give the
other to the postman--this is the note I gave him-five or six days after the
6th of July I asked if he had posted the letters, I not having heard from Mr.
Allen Jones-he said he had I asked him for a copy of the letter be bad
sent, and he handed me this (read)—" Clement's-inn, 6th July, 1846.
Dear Sir, In this and another envelope I send you a Bank of England note,
No. 59142, 4th May, 1846, for 101., the receipt thereof be pleased to acknow-
ledge."-- afterwards made inquiry about the note, and ultimately found it.
WILLIAX SPIERS. I keep the Grosvenor's Arms, in Holywell-street, West-
minster. I have known the prisoner two or three monthshe was in the babit of
using my parlour-) saw him about half-past seven o'clock in the evening on
the 6th of July-be asked me if I could give him change for a 101. note-
this is the note-I bave no memorandum on it, but I never took a note that
had been cut in half since I have been in business before I took the note
up, and put it into my cash-box-I paid it to Mr. Langley with other money
-I cannot say whether it was 201. or 301.--I am quite sure I paid the note
the prisoner gave me on the 6th of July to Mr. Langley on the following day,
Prisoner. Q. Can you swear that is the note you took of me? A. No;
but I never took a 101. note cut in half before.
THOMAS EADY re-examined. This is the note I had-I cut it in a parti-
cular way, that the date may appear on each half of it-it is L. B. No.59142,
dated 4th May, 1846.
