could do you good, and that you could supply me with goods which would

CHARLES RICHARDS, I trade as Charles Richards and Co., cycle
manufacturers, at Heath Town, Wolverhampton--in December, 1892, I
got this letter from Newman and Aston. (This had a printed heading :
Newman and Aston, from Lasson Grove, Tailors, Linen and Woollen
Drapers, Rockliffe Street, Islington ; Boots, Hats, Wringing Machines,
Bicycles, Pianofortes," and asked for terms upon which bicycles could be
supplied)—I wrote stating my terms, and then I got this letter asking for
a bicycle to be sent, as a customer wanted it for the holidays after
a further communication about the size, I forwarded a bioycle to that
address, value £7 38, net-that was not paid for on January 3rd I got
another letter from the same firm, and after correspondence, on February
10th we sent the machine, value £7 15., to the same address that has
never been paid for I have never seen the bioyoles since when I parted
with them I believed that Newman and Aston were carrying on &
genuine business as stated in the billheads, and that there were customers
who wanted the bicycles-on 7th February I got this order for bicycles
with the heading : "M. A. Lewis, wholesale and retail manufacturer
- I wrote and had this reply. (This requested a sample machine, and stated
that last year they had sold a great quantity of machines of various makers,
and that should his machines suit their customers they would be able to do
considerable trade) I looked at Newman and Aston's order and noticed
the resemblance in the writing, and in consequence I declined to supply
Lewis with any goods-we wrote to Lewis for references, and did not
think they were satisfactory; and independently I made inquiry through
& confidential agent, and the answers were not satisfactory and I declined
to send.
Cross-examined by Aston. The order for the first machine was on a
printed memorandum form, I believe, received on 20th December -I asked for
a reference, and for cash for the first order—I got no reference in the first
instance, as you had called at my stand at the Stanley Show, and my fore-
man saw you and considered from what you said to him that you were
bond-fide traders,
Cross-examined by Wilson. I did not know Mr. Hedges, a traveller,
personally-he has no authority from me to go about saying he is getting
orders for me-I do not produce the references I got from Lewis-I sent
invoices for cash; there was no reply-I had no further correspondence
with you.
