JAMES OSBORNE (Police Inspector H). On 14th instant, about 9.30, I
went to the London Hospital, and found the prisoner there-he was taken
to the bedside of the deceased--I asked the deceased man how he felt, and

the bedside at the time the deceased looked round, and pointing to the
prisoner, said "That is the man who struck me "-just at that moment
