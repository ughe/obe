611. JAMES WALLIS and JOSEPH FIELD were indicted for steal-
ing, on the 30th of October, 10 lbs. of onion-seed, value 16s., the goods
of John Biggs, their master.
John PAYNE (police-constable V 11.) In consequence of information, on
the Ist of January, I went to Mr. Draper's, a gardener, in the parish of
Richmond, and he produced 10 lbs. of onion-seed, which I now have in my
possession-I took the prisoner Wallis into custody on the 2nd of January
I said I wanted him-he said, “You want me for that onion-seed" I
had not said any thing to him about onion-seed.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. Where does Mr. Draper live?
4. Between Kew and Richmond.
