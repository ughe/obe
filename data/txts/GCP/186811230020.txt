39. MICHAEL AARON (22), and ELIZABETH ARNES (21), Bur-
glariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Isaac Mercer, and
stealing therefrom one shawl, two jackets, and other articles, his property.
Second Count-Receiving the same.
MR. GRIFFITHS conducted the Prosecution ; Mr. M. WILLIAMS appeared
for Aaron, and MR. STRAIGHT for Arnes.
CHARLOTTE MERCER. I am the wife of Isaac Mercer, of 116, Sidney Street,
Mile End-on 1st November, about 9 p.m., I fastened up the house - I found
the shutters open at 1340, and missed two jackets, a shawl, and a skirt-
this is the skirt ( produced)-it was safe when I went to bed-it is partly
made-I am a dressmaker, and it was left for me to make.
ALFRED Page. I am assistant to Mr. Fisher, a pawnbroker-a fortnight
or three weeks ago Arnes brought this skirt to pawn—we had received
information from the police, and a pattern, the day before, and stopped it.
Joshua SOLOMON (Policeman). "I took Arnes on 3rd November, and
secured this skirt from the pawnbroker the same day-she said, " I bought
'it in Petticoat Lane yesterday"-I took her to Fisher's, and as we were
leaving Aaron came up outside the shop, and said, “What is the matter 1"-
she said, “I am taken in custody for pawning this"-he said, "I gave her
58. yesterday to buy it"-I took him in custody.--Not GUILTY.
