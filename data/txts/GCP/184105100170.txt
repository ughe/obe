lar Samuda and another, his masters.
JOSEPH D'AQUILAR SAMUDA. I carry on business with my brother
James, as engineers, at the Southwark Iron-works; the prisoner was in
our employ. On the 21st of April I saw bim go into the Fox public-hour
-T followed, and noticed him go into the back-yard I went and told

wait and search for them-he ran away-I pursued, and took him to the
station - I found this article on his person-I did not see him leave my
premises.
