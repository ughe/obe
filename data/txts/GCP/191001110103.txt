with £15 by Beatrice Carroll, £3 by Edwin May, £15 by Mabel
Edwards, £8 by Molly Gunn, in order that he might apply the same
for a certain purpose, did fraudulently convert the same to his own
use sud benefit ; obtaining by false pretences from Agnes Reed £25,
from Samuel Smith £50, and from Edwin May £3, in each case with
intent to defraud.
Mr. M. Newberry prosecuted ; Mr. G. St. John McDonald defended.
