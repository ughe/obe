Inquisition with the wilful murder of Lydia Mary Joyes.
Mr. Muir and Mr. Graham 1Campbell prosecuted.

SIDNEY REGINALD DYER, Principal Medical Officer Brixton Prison.
Prisoner has been under my observation since December 22 and I have
had several interviews with him. He is suffering from bullet wounds
in the head portions of bullets have been extraeted. As the resul:
of my observation and of his family history supplied me I am of opinion
