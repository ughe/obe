and went out with him; when he came back about 11 o'clook he only
missed the bill then. I took it, and only kept it to frighten him. When I

proceeds between us. I could not get it back from him, and so I thought
I would let him change it, and then try and get the money from him, and
bring it back to the prosecutor.
