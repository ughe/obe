THOMAS WHEELER was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of August , 1 purse, value 2 d  .; 1 sixpence, and 7 pence, and 7 half-pence, the goods and monies of Charles Simpson , from the person of Rebecca Simpson ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. REBECCA SIMPSON. I am the wife of Charles Simpson, of Brothers-row, Lambeth. On the 1st of August I was at Lambeth-stairs, looking at the rowing match, and had a red purse in my pocket containing the money stated—a gentleman came and told me something—I was my purse on the ground—a gentleman seized the prisoner—this is my purse—it has the money in it now. GEORGE VICKERS. ( police-constable L  54.) About seven o'clock in the evening I was on duty, and saw the prisoner standing close behind the pro secutrix—I did not see him take the purse out of her pocket, but I took his hand from her person, and took this purse out of his hand—he let it fall on the ground. WILLIAM ROWE( police-sergeant M  10.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, from Mr. Clark's office ( read )—the prisoner is the man. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Transported For Fourteen Years.

   MIDDLESEX CASE .
 2015. WILLIAM MATTHEWS was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of August , 12 umbrellas, value 2 l  ., the goods of Thomas Evans . ELIZA BRUCE. I am in the employ of Thomas Evans. On the 12th of August, I was taking twelve umbrellas to him, Silver-street, at eight o'clock in the evening—I put them in the passage, where we always put the work, by the warehouse-door, and sat down about half a yard from them—my attention was called by a young woman, and they were gone—I went after a man in white trowsers, who had come into the passage—I spoke to him, and said, "You have got my work"—he said something—I saw the prisoner in Addle-street, and saw him drop my umbrellas—he was running at the time—I hallooed out, "Stop thief"—he was stopped, but not in my presence—the umbrellas were picked up, and given to me—I was about four yards from the prisoner, on the other side of the road, at the time—I saw him again in about ten minutes. THOMAS FREESTON KIRBY. I live in Addle-street. On the evening of the 12th of August I saw the prisoner in Addle-street, with a bundle of umbrellas—he was running—I saw him drop them—I heard the prosecutrix cry, "Stop thief," and ran after him till he was taken, by a person, at the end of Philip-lane, and given to an officer—he had run about two hundred yards after dropping them, and turned a corner—I was so close to him, and did not lose sight of him.
