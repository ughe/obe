2684. WILLIAM BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 25th Of
October, 4 pieces of carpet, value 28., the goods of John Wood and others,
bis masters.
JOHN BELL J am in partnership with John Watson and John Wood,
carpet-manufacturers, in Old Bond-street—-the prisoner was one of our
porters. In consequence of suspicion, I called bim into the counting-
house on Monday, tbe 25th, and found there pieces of carpet on him
secreted under his waistcoat and in the waist of his trowsers—l gave him
in charge—these are our property.
Cross-examined by ME. PHILLIPS. Q. Are-they cuttings? Yes—
they are of great service in preserving our patterns—we have a standing
rule in our house that nothing is to be taken—our house expends several
hundreds ip a year in producing patterns—we told bim when we engaged
bim he was not allowed to touch anything—I cannot say I said so to him
myself—I do not think I engaged him, but we always tell our servants so—
they would be valuable to other as patterns—they could not
copied without the pattern.
STEPHEN ISAACS. I am a policeman. I took him in charge, and have
the carpet.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 21.—Recommended to mercy.—Confined One Month.
