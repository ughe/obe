Q. Would he have any authority, according to your regulations, to have
his gun loaded with ball that day? A. None-the muskets are loaded with
ball when the men are stationed on convict guard, but there is no conviot
guard on the Sunday.

Q. When the muskets are loaded for the men on convict guard, are they
loaded from the ball cartridges which are regularly given out to the men,
or are they supplied with ball cartridges for that particular purpose? 4.
They are supplied with ball cartridge for that particular purpose there is
merely one charge given for the musket-it is the duty of a man after
coming off convict guard to draw the charge--it is drawn before he leaves
the guard-room, and delivered over to the sergeant of the guard--the men
have two packages given them for their pouches-each package contains
five cartridges - I believe the prisoner has been in the battalion twelve
months--the deceased had been there many years I do not know how long.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. The men had not fallen in? 1.
No-the men were scattered about all over the parade when this happened,
but I did not notice any one near the deceased when this happenedhe
was not on any military duty, standing on one spot more than another-
the prisoner had no business to have the ball cartridge in his musket that
day-it is delivered over to the sergeant, before they leave the guard-room
-- he had no business with it at all, not being on conviot guard, neither in
or out of his musket.
MR. BODKIN. Q. When a man goes on convict guard he has a parti-
cular ball cartridge given him to load his musket with ? A. Yes, and be-
fore he leaves, it is taken out of his musket, and given back to his superior
officer, and the musket is examined, so that it would be impossible to leave
the room with a cartridge in it-he would have the ordinary ten in his
cartridge box-every man is always served out with ten rounds, but that
he is not to use.
