431. WILLIAM FARRELL (38), Stealing 78., the moneys of Henry
Groves.
MR. A. METCALFE conducted the Prosecution ; and Mr. PURCELL the
Defence.
HENRY GROVES. I am a navvy and live at Bristol-I came up to town
with my brother on 2nd April-we arrived at Paddington about 2.80
p.m.we met a man in the road and went with him to the Victoria
public-house, where we saw the prisoner, but no conversation took place
for a while the man asked us if we would have anything to drink-W
had & quart between us and the prisoner had a glass of gin-about five
minutes after we went in the prisoner asked the other man whether we
were relations-I told him “No”--they were on each side of us—the
prisoner said that he was up in London about a lawsuit with a railway
company, and if he spent 501. to-day he could get 501. more to-morrow
-he showed us & purse which appeared to have bank notes and
sovereigns in it-we did not know that they were medals-we all four
left the house together and went into Hyde Park, and the other man said
" I shall wish you good day" and went up the first turning the prisoner
then said "If you are hard up, if you can show me ll. I will give you
another 1l."-I said "We have only 78."-he said "As you are hard up!
will give you 11. if you will lend me 58.," and then he wrapped a coin upin
a handkerchief and gave it to my brother and I gave him a five-shilling
