Cross-examined. 0. The prisoner is a good seaman A. Yes; one of
the best I ever crossed the water with he has been at sea forty-three
years he has been with me nine months I have had several cargoes, and
many thousands of pounds worth of cargo-I do not know whether the
prisoner was drunk that day-I gave them 108. apiece, the day before he
was taken-I left the money with Pye-I left the whole confidence to this
man and the mate—the mate is not here—the mate ought to have been on
board that day-the prisoner had a good character for anything I know-
his friends live at Sunderland—the mate would not be entitled to give
away any of the property.
