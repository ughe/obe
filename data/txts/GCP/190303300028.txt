SNOW DON KIRK. I am a chemist at 6, Crisp Street, and 89, Upper

* To test metals" I said, “You don't want fourpennyworth, I will

- he said, "That is no use to me; are you afraid I am going to poison

that is all right "-I said, “Will you promise me that it you don't 1188

Cross-examined. The prisoner held up the bottle and said, " Is this
