 221.  EDWARD ROSE was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of November , 1000 bricks, value 1l. 13s., the goods of William Mountford Nurse.
 MR. ALLEY conducted the prosecution.
   JOHN DARKE. I am a wharfinger at the Junction-canal, Paddington - the prisoner was in my service; Mr. Nurse, the builder, employs me to receive his bricks, which are burnt at Action. On the 17th of November, I received one thousand for Mr. Knott, and delivered them to the prisoner, about a quarter before six o'clock; they were all in one cart, with two horses - they were Mr. Nurse's; I saw about one thousand bricks in the Alpha-road, on the 22d; Mr. Nurse has two marks on his bricks - one is W. N., and the other N.; I never authorized the prisoner to take any bricks there.
 Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN. Q. It was not light at that time? A. No - there are fifty or sixty men employed on the wharf, and about thirty carts; there are two moulds; the bricks are all marked so at that place - there are millions made there; I have sold a great many.
   JONATHAN HILL. I live in Little James-street, Pomona-place, Lisson-green, and keep a horse and cart. The prisoner applied to me, on the 17th of November, to go round by the Nightingale public-house, and fetch up some bricks, and bring them to Steven's-buildings, for Mr. James - I removed them at two loads, as I had but one horse.
 Cross-examined. Q. I believe you were taken up? A. Yes - when the Magistrate was about to commit me, I told them; there were about one thousand bricks; I showed Mr. Darke the place I took them to - I do not know that Mr. James was there when I shot them; when I took Mr. Darke there, the building was carried up - there was no building when I went.
 MR. ALLEY. Q. Did you take any bricks out of the wall? A. Not then, but we have since, and I have them here; they have the mark on them.
   WILLIAM HILL. I am the son of the last witness. The prisoner called at my father's house, and paid me 3s. 6d. for the removal of the bricks - I helped to load one load; my father carted them.
   PETER CARROLL. I am a labourer, employed by Mr. James, to whom these bricks were sent - these bricks were taken out of his wall.
 COURT. Q. Was the wall fresh built? A. Yes: I cannot tell how long ago - I am not very often there; Mr. James lives in York-mews, nearly a mile from the wall - I was not there when the bricks were delivered; I was there about a fortnight before - I do not think the wall was built then; the foundation was laid, it was level with the ground; I took this brick from the wall this morning and washed it, to bring it decently to Court.
   SAMUEL HUNTER. I am contractor for Mr. Nurse's bricks - these are made for him.
 Cross-examined. Q. Do you know whether Mr. James has bought any of him? A. No.
 MR. DARKE re-examined. Q. Had any of these bricks been sold to Mr. James? A. No: they all pass from my wharf - one thousand would be worth about 33s. - Mr. Nurse's name is William Mountford Nurse.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. Confined Three Months and twice Publicly Whipped.
 222.  THOMAS FISHER DODSON was indicted for embezzling, on the 20th of September, 1822 , 40 sovereigns and 20 half-sovereigns, which he had received on account of Frederick Woodbridge and others, his employers.
 This offence having been committed before the passing of Mr. Peel's Act, in the absence of all proof of any specific coin having been paid, the prisoner was    ACQUITTED.
