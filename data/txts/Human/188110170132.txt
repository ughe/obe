 937. JOHN WENDOVER  (42) , Feloniously cutting and wounding Louisa Shaw , with intent to murder her. Second Count  , with intent to do her some grievous bodily harm.
   MR. RIBTON Prosecuted; MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS Defended . LOUISA SHAW.
