niously forging a certain request for the delivery of goods, to wit: for 1
two-guinea cake, 4 dozen mince-pies, and also characters, with intent to
defraud George Miner.-2nd Count, for uttering the same, well knowing
it to be forged.

January I received this twopenny-post letter (read.)

Gray requests Mr. Pontin will send him up one of his very best two-guinea
cakes, four dozen mince-pies; also characters. No. 1, Salter-street, Can-
non-street-road."
