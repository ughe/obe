 653.  WILLIAM BAXTER was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of February , 1 pair of boots, value 5s., the goods of George Starr ; and 1 pair of boots, value 5s., the goods of William Smith.
 SECOND COUNT, stating them to be the goods of  William Webb.
   WILLIAM WEBB. I am porter at the Cross Keys, Wood-street. On Saturday morning, the 16th of February, I was called up about three o'clock, by the watchman, who said a man had come over the gates with a pair of boots: I dressed myself, and came down - two watchmen had hold of the prisoner, who I saw take one pair of boots out of his breeches; they belong to my master, George Starr- I followed them to the watch-house, and they took a pair of boots off his legs, which I identified as belonging to Smith, a lodger; they were in the boot-room up in the gallery.
   BENJAMIN JACKSON. I am a watchman. I saw the prisoner coming over the gates of the yard; I stopped him - he had two pairs of boots and a pair of slippers in his jacket; he said they were his own - I rang the bell for the porter, who came down and claimed them; he had no money - the gates are nine feet high.
   FRANCIS EARL. I received the prisoner in charge at the watch-house - one pair of boots were on his feet, and the others were produced.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner pleaded poverty.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. - Confined Three Months.

 655.  GEORGE HENRY MILTON was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of February , 1 seal, value 2l. 10s., the goods of John Banfield.
 JOHN BANFIELD. I am a  silversmith, and live in Cheapside. On the 11th of February I was called into the shop, about one o'clock; my young man, said the prisoner, who was in the shop, had stolen a seal - I said,"We must search him;" I sent for an officer - he pulled his handkerchief out of his coat pocket, and with it he threw the send on the floor; it has my mark on it - it cost me 2l.
   EDWARD FOSTER. I am shopman to Mr. Banfield. - The prisoner came into the shop alone, and asked to look at a few neat gold seals; I showed him some in a tray; I saw a seal in his hand, and missed one from the tray - seeing him keep it in his hand I watched him very closely, and saw him put it into his pocket; he had not asked the price of it - I sent for my master, who came down; I said I suspected he had stolen a seal - he said, "If you think I have robbed you, you may search me," and at the same time drew she handkerchief out of his pocket, and the seal came out with it.
 Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Can you take on yourself to say there might not be some mistake? A. Yes - I am quite sure I saw him put it into his pocket - I never said any thing about his having flape to his pocket.
   THOMAS DAVIES. I am an officer, and received him in charge.
   EDWARD FOSTER. This is one of the seals I showed him.
 Prisoner. I am unwilling to add to my crime by denying it; I have hitherto borne a good character, and throw myself on your mercy.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 24. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined 14 Days.
