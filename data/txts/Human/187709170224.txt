This rep was used for bonnets—I have no marks upon it—a piece could not be produced the same colour, as it has faded—it was manufactured in Spitalfields—other houses might have been supplied with it at the time that was made—if I said I had had it fifteen years, that is a mistake; it has been made fifteen years—I corrected that mistake before the Magistrate—I said I had it to sell for a friend, a neigh bour—I was not asked his name.
   Re-examined.  It could not be died that colour—I have not the slightest doubt that it is my property—I never knew silk like that sold on barrows in the street. ALEXANDER ONSEY. I am manager to the prosecutor—on 11th June I was in the warehouse, and saw the property which was stolen on 29th or 30th May—the prisoner Harris' father came to me on June 11th and offered me some patterns of silk—I saw at once that they were the goods we had lost, and made arrangements to see him again the following morning—he gave me this card. ( M. Harris, wholesale shirt manufacturer and ware-houseman , 8, Great Prescott Street, London. ) I saw the bulk at his place of business the next morning at 11 o'clock, in Great Prescott Street—he showed me a piece at a time, till I saw the whole of them; there were eleven pieces—he brought them from under the counter, where he was writing—he said the price would be 1 s . 2 d . per yard all round, and it amounted to 68 l . 5 s .—the value of the white silk is 2 s . 2 d . to 2 s . 6 d . a yard; the white sarsnet, 3 s . 3 d . to 3 s . 6 d .; the coloured crimson, 3 s . 3 d . to 3 s . 9 d .; the Italian cloth, 1 s . 10 d .—I got a cab and said "Will you put them in the cab? we may as well take them to the warehouse, and Mr. Spiers can decide the purchase"—they were put into the cab, and we drove a certain distance—when we went down Great Prescott Street the prisoner Harris got into the cab—we stopped at a public-house where the police had arranged to meet me, and from there the police took Harris and the silk to the station—this is the memorandum of the quantities shown to me by Harris—he did not write it in my presence. RICHARD KIMBER( Detective Officer ).
