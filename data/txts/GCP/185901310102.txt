WIRE, Mayor.
Cross-examined. Q. What name did you know him by there ? A. I
never took notice he was in the habit of dealing at my shop, and when
the fire destroyed my place I went and took two rooms of him at Hampton-
street-I do not know where he went to from there I did not know him
at Chelsea in the name of StantonI did not know him in the name of
Stanley-I lodged with him two months.
GUILTY.-Confined Twelve Months.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, FEB. 28, 1859.
