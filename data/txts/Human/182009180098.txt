 1164.  ALEXANDER READ was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of August , one watch, value 3 l.; one chain, value 1 l.; two seals, value 1 l., and two keys, value 3 d., the goods of John Jones , from his person.
   JOHN JONES. I am a milkman, and live in Tower-street, Westminster-road. On the 31st of August, about half-past eleven o'clock in the morning, I was in Parliament-street, her Majesty was coming past; I was pressed and pinioned. I put my hands down, missed my watch, and found the prisoner in custody. My watch was produced.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   WILLIAM JONES. I am an officer. I saw the prisoner in company with two others, whom I knew before. I watched them from the corner of Parliament-street, they attempted to take every gentleman's watch who came near them. I saw the other two get on each side of Jones, the prisoner stood in front, and drew his watch out; I seized him and took it from him as he was putting it into his pocket. About forty thieves surrounded us, and if it had not been for the Bow-street officers we could not have kept him. I found a handkerchief on him, which he said was marked, but it was not.
   WILLIAM MASON. I was with Jones, and confirm his statement.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Transported for Life.

 1165.  RACHAEL SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of July , one watch, value 4 l.; one seal, value 3 l., and one slide, value 5 s., the goods of Henry Godwin , from his person.
   HENRY GODWIN. I am a land-waiter, and live in Mile End-road. On the 6th of July, about ten o'clock at night, I was in Stepney-fields, the prisoner came up and asked me to accompany her across the fields, as she was fearful - I did so. She said she was a servant out of place, and was going to Southend, but the woman where she lodged would not let her have her clothes as she owed her 2 s. 5 d. I was going to give her 2 s. 6 d., when she asked the time? I said I thought it was a quarter past ten o'clock. She said,
 "I must wish you a good night, as I have an appointment at half-past." She immediately ran away, and in about ten minutes I missed my watch. I met a watchman, and told him. I found her at the watch-house with my watch.
   ROBERT BLAKE. I am a watchman. About a quarter before eleven o'clock the prisoner came to me in Fieldgate-street, and said she had a watch, which she meant to pledge - I had known her by her walking the streets. She said she got it out of Stepney-fields. I asked her for it, she gave it to me, and I took her to the watch-house.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. The watchman wanted me to sell it to him.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Judgment Respited. - Sent to the Refuge for the Destitute

 1166.  CHARLES SAMUEL SAVORY was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of August , two pair of trowsers, value 35 s., and one handkerchief, value 1 s., the goods of Charles Read , from his person.
   CHARLES READ. I am a tailor, and live in Newman-street. On the 9th of August, a little after eleven o'clock at night, I was at the corner of George-street, St. Giles's, with two pair of trowsers in a bundle. I saw the prisoner and two others at the opposite corner; he crossed directly over to me, snatched the bundle from under my arm, and ran off with it - I followed, calling Stop thief! His companions crosed between us to hinder me, but I kept my eye on him - he ran away with a number of them. I was afraid to go in after him - he was stopped in a minute. My bundle was conveyed away. I am positive he is the man, for I did not lose sight of him.
   LEWIS ROBERTS. I am a watchman. I heard the cry, saw a crowd running, and went in a direction to meet them, the prisoner was the foremost; I laid hold of him, Read appeared immediately, and said he was the man.
 Prisoner's Defence. If he saw me take it he can say what I did with it.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Life.
