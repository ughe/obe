765. HENRY BODMIN was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of Ja-
nuary, 1 coat, value 21.; and 1 waistcoat, value 48.; the goods of John
Wright.
INTRO
MARY WRIGHT. I am the wife of John Wright, of Buxton-street,
Clerkenwell. The prisoner is my brother he was at our house for a
week or two-I went out when I came in he left the room, and I missed
my husband's coat and waistcoat in about an hour--the coat was worth 2.
THOMAS CALVER. I am in the service of Mr. Wells, pawnbroker,
Broad-street, St. Giles's. On the 21st of January, the prisoner pawned
this coat-he said he pawned it for his brother, James Bodmin.
HENRY NEWSTEAD. I am in the service of Mr. Bradbury, pawn-
broker, London-road. I have a waistcoat pawned in the name of John
Wright, No. 4, Marshall-street, for 18.-I do not know who by.
NICHOLAS MEARS. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner in charge
--he said nothing.
GUILTY. Aged 14.-Confined Three Months.
766. ROBERT JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the 18th
of January, 1 coat, value 58., the goods of Charles Henry Gilbert, his
master; and 1 cloak, value 158., the goods of Henry Thomas Mason,
and that he had been before convicted of felony.
CHARLES HENRY GILBERT. I am a surgeon, and live in Raven-row,
Spitalfields. The prisoner was my errand-boy for four or five days-on
the 18th of January, he asked leave to go out for half an hour-he did not
return-I missed a coat worth 58., and a lady's cloak worth 158. I found
him in custody afterwards I had a good character with him--the cloak
belonged to the wife of Henry Thomas Mason.
