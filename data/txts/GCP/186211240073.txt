cannot say whether I saw him on the Sd or not; I saw him on the 4th, I

not any entry in my books--this (produced) was the invoice made out when

to the goods leaving the place, when I requested the amount of the ball cash
- am quite positive I did not receive the order until the 4th Novem-
ber-I did not receive the order on the 3d I did not then receive

to send it to his place I said I could get the use of Britten's cart to send

--lie did not then say he would give me a cheque for the 8th November
