Williams's Defence. I went to Walker's house, and the police came and
called for Walker; I went up-stairs with them; they said they wanted some
bonnets ; Mrs. Walker wanted to know who would take them; I said I
would ; I carried them; I was taken to the Hackney station, and locked up;
I can prove I had no connection with the prisoners; I had been only out of
confinement five or six days; I was in Giltspur-street Compter from the
July till 29th Oct.
(A messenger was sent to Giltspur-street, and after the Deputy-Governor
had spoken to MR. PARNELL, he withdrew Williams's case from the Jury.)
Laws's Defence. I was in the employ of my master till 27th Sept., there-
fore I could not be connected with these things.
