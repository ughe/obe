OWEN YOUNGMAN. I am a tobacoonist, at 29, Villiers Street, Strand
on 27th May, about 11.30, I served the prisoner with a oz. of shag,
which came to 11d.--he handed me & florin-I thought it was bad, but
gave him the change-he ran out as hard as he could I tested the coin,
and followed him-he dodged me up several courts, running as fast as
he could, and came into Villiers Street again-I caught him in the Strand,
and gave him in custody--my tobacco was found on him-I gave the
florin to the constable. XOS
GHORGE FLETCHER (Policeman ER 41). Youngman pointed out the
prisoner running by Charing Cross, and gave me this florin-I took him,
and told him the charge-he made no answer-I found on him, ls. 10 d.,
the change for the florin, and nothing else.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These two florins and this shilling are bad.
The Prisoner, in his defence, stated that he was not in Mr. Cook's house
on the morning the coin was passed, being at Epsom at the time, as could
be proved by a witness, George Sorby, who, however, did not appear; and
that he did not know that the florin uttered to Mr. Youngman was bad.
