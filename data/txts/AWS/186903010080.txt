WALTER ROPER. I am a decorator, and live at Lewisham-or Wednes
day, 27th January, I missed from outside my shop a roll of lead, about

WILIAM TowNER. I am in the prosecutor's service missed the lesd

JAMES DIGNUM. I live at Walpole Street, King's Cross, and am in the

weighed it, there was 1 owt.i agreed to give him 158. for itI did not
see him paid-he said he was a neighbour, and lived close by, and that it
was his own property.
DAVID SBAMAN. I am assistant to Mr. White, a marine store dealer, ofs

White-he gave the name of Smith, and said he lived in South Street, and
it was his own property.
JAxEs HAEELDINE (Policeman R R 21). On 25th January, I received
