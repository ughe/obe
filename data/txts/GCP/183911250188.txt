268. WILLIAM CLARK was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of
November, 1 pair of breeches, value 88., the goods of William Moores.
WILLIAM Moores. I am ostler at the Bald-faced Stag public-house,
at Putney. On the 12th of November I lost a pair of cotton-cord breeches
out of the settle in the tap-room, where I had left them when I went to
bed-these are them.
JAMES HERRICK. I am landlord of this house. The prisoner went
out last on the night in question-he had something under his smock-frock
- there was a traveller sitting with him, who slept at my house the pri-
soner left at a quarter to eleven o'clock-no one left after ten o'clock but
him.
ABRAHAM Howell. I am bailiff to Colonel Wemys, of Kingston.
On the 13th of November I went to an out-house belonging to my master,
and found this pair of breeches—the prisoner slept there that night.
GUILTY. Aged 39.-Confined Three Months.

269. CHARLOTTE SHEARER was indicted for feloniously receiving,
on the 25th of October, 3 yards of sackcloth, value 28., the goods of John
Murray; well knowing the same to have been stolen; against the Statute, &c.
ALEXANDER PATTERSON. I live with my uncle, John Murray, of
Church-street, Minories, a hemp and sackcloth-manufacturer. This sack-
cloth is his-we have not sold it-I cannot say whether we have lost it-
we employ from thirty to forty hands---it might have been removed from
the premises.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did I understand you that it
had not been sold ? A. This piece had not--here is three yards and a foot
of sacking-our sacks are made chiefly by women--they are given out to
be made-I suppose this is a piece given out to make I believe the pri-
soner is married.
