THOMAS AMISS re-examined. These waistcoats are mine-here is & mark
on the breast of one of them I have had one eight, and the other twelve
months this book has my name in it, in my writing.
Prisoner's Defence. There is part of the neck-tie ripped at the end, and
it has been so for some time, and if it was the prosecutor's he would have
known that; I wore it better than a month; I bought the other things in
Petticoat-lane, of a tailor; if I had stolen any of the property, it is not
likely that I should leave the name in the book; on the day before, I bought
three other books, and they have been taken away from me, it was in fair
daylight that they saw me come out of the room, and I had nothing in my
possession; it is a made-up plan because I had the misfortune to sleep in
the house; three or four men slept in the house, and perhaps they com-
mitted the robbery and gave me in charge for it; I pledged the things in
my own name, and with a man who knew me.
Guilty.
(The prisoner was further charged with having been before convicted.)

present the prisoner is the person.
