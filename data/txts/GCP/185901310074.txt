MR. Best conducted the Prosecution.
SARAH FABLING. I am in service at Perry-hill, near Sydenham. On
Thursday, 18th November, about 4 o'clock, I was coming from Catford-bridge
towards Ladywell, Lewisham-I went across a footpath there—as I came
to the third stile I saw the prisoner sitting on it-he got off the stile and
came towards me, and when he got close to me he drew a thick stick from
under his coat, and said "I demand what money you have "-he flourished
the stick over me-I said " Oh, yes, you shall have my money"-I gave
him 48. and 3d. or 4d. worth of half-pence—he then threw my cloak over
my shoulders and searched me all over my bosom for my watch-he said
"Where do you carry your watch ? "-I said "I have no watch "-he said
“Then let me look if you have not," and he searched me all over-he
examined my pocket-he also tried to throw me down-he would not let
me turn back again, but made me go on the way I was going, and he went
the other way, I went on for about a mile before I came to a house, and I
went in there I am quite sure he is the man.
Prisoner. In the indictment I am charged with striking her- I did not
strike her. Witness. I did not say you struck me.
GUILTY.

MESSRS. BEsT and LANGFORD conducted the Prosecution.

evening, 18th January last, about 12 or a little after, I went to a coffee-shop
kept by Mrs. Masters, in Church-street, Woolwich-the prisoner Moon was
there I had a tobacco-box in my pocket, containing an Arctic medal and
two sovereigns-I fell asleep there for about an hour or an hour and a half-
when I awoke my tobacco-box was gone-I gave information to the police
I saw a woman named Bowden in the room while I was there when I woke
up I told Masters that I had been robbed of a tobacco-box containing &
medal and two sovereigns-she said, “I know nothing about it.”
