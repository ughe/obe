Thomas HITCHEN. On the 4th of July, I saw the prisoner and Black

of the way, Eliza, and don't you interfere"-I don't consider it was a blow-
it was not a violent blow that might have occasioned her death-he pushed
her away with the back of his hand.
Prisoner. He is afraid to say more for fear he should be turned away,
