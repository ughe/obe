WILLIAM FLETCHER (policeman, R 308). The prisoner was given into
my custody with these articles (produced), she said that she bought them of

GEORGE CHAPMAN (policeman, R 208). I was at the station when the
prisoner was brought in I knew that she lived at No. 3, Mill-lane, Dept-
ford-I went there and found this petticoat (produced) hung on the bed-I

brought it there.
MARY ANN COLE re-examined. This petticoat is one which I missed
these two other petticoats, and this day shirt and night shirt are also
mine.
Prisoner's Defence. I bought them, not knowing they were stolen.
Guilty on 2nd Count. Aged 32.-Confined Six Months.

379. ALICE NEALE, stealing 2 gowns, 1 jacket, 1 mantle, and 2
shawls, value 31. 88., the goods of Elizabeth Ann Reed.
ELIZABETH ANN REED. I reside with my brother, at Brockley-lane,
Deptford. The prisoner was in my brother's service-she left in the early
part of Jan., and I afterwards missed two gowns, three jackets, a pair of
cuffs, a petticoat, three shawls, and a satin mantle, which I had seen safe
about a fortnight before she left--they were kept in a chest in the front
kitchen which was not locked.
JAMES BUCHANAN. I am shopman to Mr. Sharpe, a pawnbroker, of
Deptford. I produce a lustre dress, pledged on 26th Dec.-I cannot say
that I was present—it had a new ticket on 8th Jan., but I was not present
to my knowledge-no other person from the shop is here-I produce a
shawl and scarf, pledged with me, one on 30th Dec. and the other on 18th
Jan., I cannot be positive who by--I have known the prisoner some length
of time, and likewise her mother-I have received articles of both.
GEORGE SPENCER. I am shopman to Mr. Tye, a pawnbroker, of Deptford.
I produce a black satin mantle, pledged with me on 23rd Dec., I cannot say
whether by the prisoner or her mother-I know them both.
JOSIAH TURNER policeman, R 307). The prisoner was given into my
custody by the prosecutrix's brother on, I believe, 4th Feb. I told her that
she was charged with stealing a quantity of wearing apparel from her
mistress, and pointed to this dress, which she was then wearing, and said,
“You have now one of the dresses on " --she said, Yes, it was I then read
over the list of missing articles to her, and she said that she had taken the
lustre dress and pledged it, also the shawl, at Mr. Sharpe's I went there
and got them—she said that the black mantle was pledged at Mr. Tye's
the other things she said she knew nothing about she said she should not
have done it if the other servant had not persuaded her to do it.
ELIZABETH REED re-examined. These are the articles I lost.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury on account of her
youth.-Confined Three Months.
