209. HENRY DRAKERS (22), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MESSRS CLERK and POLAND conducted the Prosecution.
ELLEN TOWNSEND. I am barmaid to Alfred Jobn Wrangham, who keeps
the Gregorian Arms, Tooley-street-on Saturday, 19th January, about 4
o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoner came there and asked for half a pint
of porter-I served him, and he gave me a penny in payment-he drank the
porter and went out-he was absent about two or three minutes-he then
returned, and asked for three penuyworth of ginger brandy-I served him,
and he gave me a five-shilling piece-I took it up and went into the bar
parlour and gave it to my sister, Mrs. Wrangham, in order that she might
give me change-I did not give any change to the prisoner-I left the
crown-piece with Mrs. Wrangham.
ELIZA WRANGHAM. I am the wife of John Wrangham-I was in the bar-
parlour on the afternoon of 19th January—the last witness brought me &
crown-piece-I bit it, found it was bad, and gave it to my husband—be
came out into the bar and spoke to the prisoner about it—the prisoner was
then at the bar.
