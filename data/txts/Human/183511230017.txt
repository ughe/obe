I am a clerk, I know Rice—I am not connected with the police now—I was once—I was going to call on Rice—I arrived at his house about half past eight o'clock—when I got near his house I observed him bringing the prisoner along, as if from his house—Rice gave him into custody while he went into my the house and while I hold him by the door something seemed to fall from him—I do not know what it was—I did not take it up.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I had been down to Billingagate market on Friday afternoon to see what fish was coming there—I left about five o'clock and came towards shadwell, and into globe lane to go home I turned up the turning to go across the fields and saw prosecutor's door open—thinking it an improper time for it to be open, I pushed the door in and Rice followed me in, and took me.
