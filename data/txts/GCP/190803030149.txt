(Thursday, March 12.)
Verdict, Guilty.
William Johin Church, on the advice of Mr. Eustace Falton, now
withdraw his plea of Not guilty, and pleaded Guilty.
In regard to Mrs. Church, Mr. Muir said that he desired to take
the opinion of the Attorney-General as to whether she should be pro
ceeded against.
Against Church several minor conviotions (one an assault on his
wife and on the police) were proved.
Sentence: Both, Nine months' hard labour.
ESSEX CASES
BEFORE THE RECORDER.
(Tuesday, March 3.)
PEARCE, Harry (21, baker), pleaded guilty of burglary in the
dwelling-house of Thomas Meek, and stenling therein one overcoal,
one pocket-book, and other articles, his goods.
Sentence, Nine months' hard labour. An order for restitution was
made.
BEFORE THE RECORDER.
(Wednesday, March 4.)
HART, John (39, painter), pleaded guilty of stealing one donkey
and one set of harness, the goods of William John Wood. Previous
convictions were proved.
Sentence, Nine months' hard labour.
DAY, Frederick (35, labourer), pleaded guilty of stealing three
bruss weights, the goods of John Ellis. Thirteen previous convie-
tions were proved.
Sentence, Six months' hard labour.
