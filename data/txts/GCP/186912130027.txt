WILLIAM FREDERIOK London. I am a plumber, of 19, Stewart Street,
Old Artillery Ground-on the night of 24th February, I shut up my house
safe a little after 11, when I retired to rest-I rang the bell at 6 o'clock in
the morning, for the servant, and she went down stairs first-I went down
afterwards and found that the house had been broken into, and missed two
coats, five table cloths and other things—the kitchen window was open-it
was shut down the night before, and to the best of my belief, latohed, but
the shutters were not shut-I afterwards saw my things at the station-house,
they were given up to me.

with two men and a female, the prisoner had a bundle in his hand-when
