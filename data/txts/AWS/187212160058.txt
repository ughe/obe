99. FREDERICK RICHARDSON (21), Robbery, with violence, on John
Hendenham, and stealing his water.
MR. COOPER conducted the Prosecution and Ma. STRAIGHT the Defence,
JOHN HENDENHAM. I am a carpenter, and live in Hinton Street,
Camberwell-on the night of 8th December, about 12.30, I was walking
quick down the London Road to the Elephant and Castle just as I got
near Garden Row there were three or four men standing on the pavement;
as I tried to pass on the houses side of them one of them laid hold of my
great coat, which was unbuttoned, the under coat was buttoned with one
button, at the top ; it was pulled open by the force of the pull, and the
prisoner snatched at my wateh chain-he was in front of me, the others
were on my left side-my guard broke, and the prisoner went off with my
watch-] pursued him round the corner of Garden Row, where I saw a
eonstable, and told himrwe chased him through Garden Row, across St.
George's Road, and I saw him brought back by a policeman- had only
lost sight of him just as he rounded the comner of West Square, and that
was not for more than half-a-minute at most-I am positive he is the man
-I never recovered my wateh.
Cross-examined. It was not more than two minutes from the time it
happened till the constable had him-I don't know what became of the
others, they all ran away-nothing happened to me except losing my
watch.
JoHN PAGE (Policeman L 170). On this night, at 12.20, I was on duty
at the corner of Garden Row, London Road--I saw the prisoner run round
the corner, out of London Road, and a moment afterwards the proseoutor
came up, and said 44 That man has robbed me of my wateh"-we ran after
him into West Square, and as we turned the corner another constable had him.
C'ross-eamined. I first saw him running in the London Road-there
were a great number of people passing-after the proseoutor spoke to me I
ran after the prisoner and shouted 44 Stop thief saw persons running
behind him, not in front.
FREDERICK STEBBING (Policeman L R 17). I was in West Square-I
heard the ery of 44 Stop thief p" and saw the prisoner coming down West
Street, leading into the square-there was no one before him-I caught him
by the arm he got away from me-I chased him into the square, and
there caught him-he said he was in pursuit of the thief, the man who stole
the wateh-I did not at that time know the charge-as I was taking him
back I met the others-he was out of breath from running.
GUITY of robbery without violence.-Nin Months' Imprisonment.
