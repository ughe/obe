2140. GEORGE JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of
September, Alb. weight of leather, value 1s., the goods of William Grint
his master.
WILLIAM GRINT. I am a shoemaker, and live in King-street, West-
minster. The prisoner was in my employ for two months-I sent him, on
the 14th of September, to Clarence-terrace, York-road, with a pair of boots
-I lost some leather--this is it-it was brought to me by the officer.
WILLIAM ISBESTER. I am an officer. I saw the prisoner, on the 14th
of September, with this piece of leather in his hand, offering it to a Jev
salesman for sale-I took him, and found his master-he told me he lived
with Mr. White, but I could not find any such person.
