THIRD COURT.-Friday, October 28th, 1853.
PRESENT-Mr. Ald. CUBITT ; and Mr. COMMON SERJBANT.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant and the Eighth Jury.
1112. DANIEL SKINNER was indicted for unlawfully obtaining money
by false pretences. MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
MOSES LAZARUS. I am a grocer, and am a member of the Working
Man's Equitable Building Society, of which the prisoner was secretary; our
meetings were held every Wednesday evening at No. 71, Old-street, St.
Luke's, for the purpose of receiving the contributions of the members. On
22nd June, I was indebted to the society 151. for my rent, and the prisoner
called on me in the middle of the day, and said that my rent had become due
that day, and he should be very thankful if I would let him have it, and
accordingly I gave him a check, and he signed my books ( produced)-each of
these three books refers to 51.; they are books furnished by the society-he
said my rent was due, and said how much was due, 151.-it was due to the
society- I considered when I paid him that he had authority to receive it.
John Murray. I am a licensed victualler, of Old-street, St. Luke's. I
know the prisoner-I gave him cash for a check for 151., which I paid into
my banker's- I cannot say whether this produced) is the check, or when I
crushed it.
FREDERICK HART. I am one of the trustees of this Building Society.
The prisoner has not accounted to me for this 15l.-I was not on duty at the
time: nor has he accounted to the society to my knowledge.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. Nor for any part of it? 4. I
am given to understand not.
Edward John THOMPSON. I am another trustee of the society. The
prisoner has never accounted to me for this money-the books of the society
have been investigated, and there is no account of it.
Cross-examined. Q. Is there any trustee here to whom he might have
accounted ? A. They are all here.
(The Court considered that no false pretence had been made by the prisoner,
but that he merely requested payment of the rent, and may have intended to
make it good to the society.)
Not Guilty.
