Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
491. LEWIS HENRY AYLING (24), PLEADED GUITY to feloniously
marrying Jane Doery, his wife Emma being alive.
Mr. WooD, for the prisoner, stated that, having applied for a divorce, he had
been deceived by a person who he employed, and who had shown him a document
with the seal of the Court attached, purporting to be a dissolution of his mar
riage, and which turned out to be a forgery.-Judgmen Respited.
