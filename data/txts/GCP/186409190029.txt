ANN CHADWICK. I am the wife of Thomas Chadwick, of Baker's-row,
Coppice-row, Clerkenwell—I knew the prisoner and deceased on Sunday
night, 28th August, about 8 o'clock, the deceased came to my house, and
remained some two hours with me—she said her husband had struck her in
the eye in Farringdon-street, and that was the reason she had left him-
after that I went home with her.
Cross-examined. Q. I believe you had known the prisoner and his wife
about two years A. Longer than that his conduct to her was always
kind and afectionate when he was sober.
