Third Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
1733. THOMAS SHARPE was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
May, 5 sovereigns, the monies of Rebecca Lane and another, his employers.
Louisa LANE. I am single, and live in Ludgate-street. I also have a shop
in Sidney's-alley-I am in partnership with my sister, Rebecca—the prisoner
was our errand-boy. On the 19th of May, about half-past seven o'clock in
the evening, he came to me at Ludgate-hill, with a message from my sister-
I was serving some customers, soon after, and he went into the back shop.
where I had a cash-box on the desk with sixteen sovereigns in it-I had
been to the box five minutes before, in the prisoner's presence-I turned the
key, and, as I thought, locked the box, but I had not-the sovereigns were
there then--I left him in the shop with the box ] returned to the back
shop to write a note to my sister-I took the box up to put it into the desk,
and the lid came open-I immediately counted the money, and missed five
sovereigns-the prisoner was then in the front shop-I asked a person, in his
presence, if I had paid any money away.
Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. I believe you found all your
money? A. Yes-I found the five sovereigns on the floor, by the prisoner
-be had only been with us three days—I had turned the key, but the hasp
had not caught.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 17.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prosecutrir.
Confined Seven Days.
1734. ROBERT PAIRMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of
May, 2 bottles, value 6d. ; and 3 pints of brandy, 98.5 the goods of George
William Colley Gregory, his master.
GEORGE WILLIAM COLLEY GREGORY. I keep a tavern, in Rupert=street,
St. James's—the prisoner lived two years with me. On the 10th of May, at
eight o'clock in the morning, I was in my passage, which leads to the street
door-the prisoner came up to me, and asked leave to go out-I said, “You
were out on Sunday night, you had better remain in and save your money"
- he said, "I must go out”--I saw his pocket handkerchief, and he was covering
something under his jacket-1 asked what it was he said, “Not anything"
I asked again-he said he had nothing, advancing towards me--I said I in-
sisted on seeing-1 stopped him from going out as he ran towards the door-
we struggled— I threw him on the stairs, and found a bottle of brandy on
him-he then said it was smuggled brandy-I went to the shoe-house, and in
a cupboard, which he kept locked, I found a pint of brandy-- they were in
champagne-bottles, which he had access to.
WILLIAM CAWTHORN. I am a wine and brandy merchant, and live at
Salisbury-wharf, Strand—I have tried the strength of this brandy with the
hydrometer, and tasted it--it is the same as the prosecutor's stock. 33
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. I suppose you serve other people
with brandy? A. Yes-brandy often differs in quality, 2017
JOHN WARD. I took the prisoner with the brandy.
Not GUILTY.
