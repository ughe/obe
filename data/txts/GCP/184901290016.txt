SARAH Powell. I am a widow. I keep the Blue-lion-the last witness
is my sister-on the night of 10th Jan. she gave me a 58.-piece-I put it into
my desk-I gave it to the officers in the morning.
James GROVE (policeman, N 142). I received from Mr. Powell this crown-

SARAH POWELL. I got these three half-crowns from my sister, which I
gave to the officer.
ELIZABETH DANES. I live at the Orange-tree public-house, at Barnet.
On 10th Jan., about eleven at night, the prisoners came to our house with
another man-they asked for a pot of beer-one of them gave me a half-
crown-I put it into the till-after drinking that, Ellen Jones asked for
a half-pint of rum-I gave it her-she gave me a half-crown, which I laid on
a shelf beside the till-they afterwards asked for another half-pint of rum-
John Jones gave me a half.crown to pay for that-I took it to my mother-
I then went back to look at the other half-crown on the shelf, and it was a
bad one-I then went to the till, and took out the same half-crown that I had
put in-that was bad- I gave them all to the officers.
SARAH DANES. I am the mother of Elizabeth Danes. She gave me
a half-crown- I found it was bad- I went to the door and the man
ran away.
