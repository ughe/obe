8.30 p.m., I was in Neale Street, Long Acre, with Sergeant Gregory-in
consequence of information I went into the Two Spies public-house, T
saw the prisoner sitting down there-I told him to get up, I was going

down in the corner, a small packet containing four florins, which I
produce they were done up in a slip of paper and a piece of paper
between each coin-I then searched further and found in the inside
- Breast pocket of his coat a brown-paper parcel containing 17 half-
crowns, 14 floring, and a shilling, done up in packets with a slip of
paper between each coin-I told him they were bad-he said nothing-
took him into custody,
