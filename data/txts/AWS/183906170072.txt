BEN CREECY, for feloniously receiving 8 half-sovereigns, part of the
said property, well knowing the same to have been stolen; against the
Statute, &e.-2nd COUNT, for feloniously harbouring and maintaining the
said James Creecy.
Mr. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
