transfer of certain mining shares, with intent to defraud. Other County,
varying the manner of laying the charge.
MR. F. H. LEWIS conducted the Proscontion.
ELIZABETH COLVILLE I reside at Norfolk-square, Brighton-I have
known the prisoner about three years in 1859 and 1860, he was acting u
clerk to a Mr. Clements an appraiser at Brighton, who is my nephew -- I gave
the prisoner authority to purchase some shares for me in mining compania
---about the year 1859 a cheque was given to Mr. Clements in payment for
them, and he gave the amount of the cheque to the prisoner-he purchased
for me five shares in the Tineroft Mines, five in the Tanner's, five in the Hing
ston, five in the Wheal Harriet, five in Kelly Bray, and five in Peuden
- this is the cheque (produced) which I gave for the payment of those share
to Mr. Clements I received the transfer of those shares from the prisone
himself-I still have those shares--this signature to the transfer of the five
shares in Kelly Bray is not my writing, or signed by my authority-I should
say it was the prisoner's writing--this signature to the transfer of five share
in the Hingston Down Mine is not my signature, or written by my
authority--my belief is that it is the prisoner's--this signature, Elizabeth
Colville, to the transfer of Pendeen Consols Mine is not my writing, or
written by my authority-my belief is that it is the prisoner's writing this
one to the transfer in Wheal Harriet, is not my signature, or written by my
agthority--my belief is that it is the prisoner's writing.
Prisoner. 2. How do you know Mr. Clements gave me the money .
You gave me an account-I have not got it---I do not know what has becomie of
it-you gave me an account at the time, of the expenses--you got the shares in
my name I knew nothing of mining shares--I had to receipt for the money
I paid, only your word—I once lent 401. or 501. to some advertising people
who were offering 51. bonuses, through you-I do not know whether you
came up to London to inquire about that you were in partnership with
Mr. Clements from September, 1859, but you were his clerk at the time
the shares were purchased-I came to London with Mr. Clements in Mayo
June, 1860, and called at No. 3, Old Broad-street, between 8 and 9 o'clock, one
morning-I did not remove all the furniture from your office I had nothing
to do with that I did not give you any authority to sign those transfer-
every paper taken from your office was returned to you—they were sent to
No. 3, and given to a man there-I did not give them--I was present when
they were given-I do not know that you received them--there were other
things sent back besides papers, that were in the drawers I had nothing
to do with it-they were given to a man at the Bricklayer's Arms to take to
your office.
