2485. PETER WILKINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 5th
October, 1 watch, value 5l. ; 1 watch-guard, value ll. ; 1 watch-key, value
1d.; 1 bag, value 1d.; 4 sovereigns; 2 half-crowns; and 2 shillings; the
goods and monies of Peter Williams, his master, in a certain vessel on the
navigable river Thames.
Peter WILLIAMS. I come from Newport in Monmouthshire, and am
master of a vessel. The prisoner was in my employ for fifteen days—the
vessel arrived in the river Thames on Friday night, the 5th of October-1
went into my cabin between ten and eleven o'clock, took off my clothes,
and hung my watch on a little pin by my bed-side-my money was in my
trowsers' pocket in a bag --when I awoke in the morning I missed my watch
and money-my mate went for the prisoner to the forecastle where he slept
and he and his clothes were gone-this is my watch and bag-(looking a
them the money has not been found.
Edmund North. I am a pawnbroker in the Borough. The prisoner
offered the watch and guard at our shop on the 11th of October, and I
stopped him from suspicion- I had not received any account of it.
George Havill (police-sergeant M. 2.) I went and found the prisoner
-I found this bag on him and 10d., that is all.
GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Six Months. Three Weeks solitary.
