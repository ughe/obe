1895. JAMES JOSEPH FINCH BROMLEY was indicted for stealing
1 half-crown, the monies of Thomas Brown, his master.
THOMAS BROWN. I keep a glass and earthenware shop- the prisoner was
in my service-on the 17th of Sept. I received information, and asked if he
had taken any money of mine-he denied it-I threatened to send for a
policeman unless he acknowledged it - I sent for one the prisoner
said, “You don't give me a chance to find it"-he crept under a desk and
appeared to be taking off his shoe-I took the shoe and found the half-crown
in it.
Cross-examined by MR. MELLER. Q. He was in the act of taking off his
shoe? A. Yes- I took hold of it, and the half-crown fell from it-he said
he took it to buy an accordion.
Q. Have you not said before, that the half-crown fell from the boy? A.
Yes-it fell from him, and he said he took it to buy an accordion, and after
that he said he meant to give it back to his mistress.
V. Did you ever give that account before? A. Yes, before Mr. Ballan-
