190. THOMAS JONES was indicted for stealing 1 sixpence and 17
groats; the goods of Joseph Edward Chaddock, from his person,
JOSEPH EDWARD CHADDOCK. I am a baker's boy, and carry bread about
for Mr. Savory, of the New Cut, Lambeth. On Saturday, 7th of Nov., I met
the prisoner and another boy--they offered to help me to carry the bread-
had received my wages just before that, a sixpence and seventeen groats--they
asked whether my master paid me, or paid my mother-I said he paid me-
we got on to Bridge-walk, Lambeth, and the prisoner said, “I will lay you
sixpence I will put you down in wrestling"-I said, “No, I shall lose my
money "-he then threw me down and got on me when I got up, my money
was gone and my trowsers onbuttoned - I told him he had it he ran away,
and I did not see him any more till he was taken on Sunday morning-I am
sure he is the person-I felt his hand about me, but I did not feel it in my
pocket-no one else could have taken the money,
Prisoner. There was a man came by.
HENRY MOTLEY (police-constable L 31.) I took the prisoner-he said,
"I have not had the money, why is not Jin here ?"-he said, “Jim was with
us at the time, he ought to be here as well as me."
Joseph EDWARD CHADDOCK re-examined. Q. Did Jim wrestle with you?
4. No, he did not touch me-I am quite sure of that-I knew the prisoner
by sight before-they did not go away in the same direction-Jim went with
me I knew him by sight, and the prisoner too- I do not know how he gets
his living.
Prisoner. We were both wrestling, and fell down ; a man came by and
picked him up.
Guilty. Aged 15.-Confined Six Months,

GEORGE HORNSEY. I am shopman to a pawn broker, in High-street,
