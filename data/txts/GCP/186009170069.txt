ELIZA RIGG. I am the widow of Mr. Samuel Rigg-I live at.6, St.
James's-terrace, Notting-hill-my husband died in September, 1858, leaving
& will appointing Dr. Gourley executor and two others, James Horton and
William Parnell, and I was named executris--the will gave me a life
interest in the property, and at my death it was to be divided amongst
different persons—the other gentlemen renounced the probate and Dr.
Gourley disliked to act at that time-he afterwards accepted the trust, and
he and myself proved the will-we have acted as executors under the will
-before my husband's death the defendant had attended us professionally
