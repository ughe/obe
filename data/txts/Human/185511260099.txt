   Cross-examined Q.  As soon as you took hold of the prisoner what did you say to him? A.  I was so excited, I should not like to swear what I did say, my recollection will not carry me back so far—I think I said some-thing—I do not think I told him why I took him—I merely took him across the road till I saw an officer—when he resisted, I thought discretion was the better part of valour, and I called a neighbour—when I called the neigh bour the prisoner went quietly—the neighbour had no occasion to help me at all—the prisoner went quiet then.
