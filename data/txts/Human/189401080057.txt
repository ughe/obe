   MR. PURCELL Prosecuted and MR. A. GILL Defended. JOHN WORTHEM. I am a clerk in the bankruptcy department of the High Court of Justice—I produce the file of proceedings in the prisoner's bankruptcy—his petition was filed on 9th April, 1890; his ad judication was on 14th April—the statement of affairs discloses liabilities £11,173 14s. 10d., and assets £213 15s.—application for order of discharge was made on 28th November, 1890, and suspended for two years till 28th November, 1892—he would then have got his discharge by applying for his certificate; but he has not applied—he could get it at any moment.
   Cross-examined.  He has been entitled to the certificate since 28th November, 1892—there has been no order of the Bankruptcy Court to prosecute in this case. MINNIE TRUSCOTT.
