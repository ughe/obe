I was a prize-holder in the Art-Union, in 1847, to the amount of 40 l .—I went to the Suffolk-street Gallery—I selected a pic ture, price 50 guineas—the prisoner was in the room—I dealt with him for it—I gave him a check for 12 l ., the difference—this is the check I have since received from my bankers. WILLIAN WINTER.
