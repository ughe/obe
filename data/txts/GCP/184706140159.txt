Court. Q. Did you mean to export these ? A. Yes: I got them for an

MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN FINLAYSON. I am out-of-door superintendent of the goods depart-
ment of the Eastern Counties Railway, in Brick-lane. The prisoners were
coal-porters there-they were ordered into the granary to sweep it up-on
the 21st and 22nd of May there was a great quantity of flour there belonging
to different consignees—I ordered Dyson to watch the prisoners.

behind a sack of flour in the flour-warehouse-after remaining there about

house to find a broom--they could not find any-they went to the upper-
