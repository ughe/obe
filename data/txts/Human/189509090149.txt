Prisoner's defence: "I have known this man a long while—he is the utterer of counterfeit coin. He said to me, 'I know you, I will put you away'—he swears I did not tell him it was bad, and the detective says I did. I was not with them; I refused, and told them I had a situation and wanted to get an honest living. He says that I gave it to him in order to ruin his character." NOT GUILTY.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, OCTOBER  21 ST , 1895.
