MR. BODKIN. Q. From the beginning, when he joined you, down to the
time he left, did you pay for whatever he bought ? A. Yes, everything was
bought and paid for with my money-I paid the whole of his wages; I was
paying them at the time he went to Kingston-when Mr. Peters sued me,
I called, under the advice of my attorney, and paid the money.
