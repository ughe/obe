Cross-examined by MR. LEE. Q. I believe the debt was paid that same
night? A. I cannot tell that, he was discharged, I believe, that same
evening- I cannot tell whether he was discharged - I did not see him-I did
not know it till I was told of it the next day- I was not there the next day,
to my knowledge-I cannot say whether I was or not-not to my know-
ledge— I heard he went out the next day- I live in St. John's-square-I
have got my living for the last three years by going with the Sheriff's
officers-that is the only way-I am married my wife is alive-I have no
family--I shall not answer the question whether I am living with my wife
- I do not think it is a legal question-I think it is not proper to answer
it, it is bringing family affairs into publicity.
