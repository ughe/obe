JOHNSON, Mayor.
ROBERT SUTTLE (police-constable B 97.) I remember the prisoner beine
tried-he is the person named in the record.
JAMES Pickett. I am a market gardener, and live at Egham. On the
morning of the 22nd of Sept. I saw the prisoner in Covent-garden market
I had walnuts to sell-he bargained for half a bushel for 2s. 3d., and gave
me a half-crown to pay for them-I bit it, and put my teeth into it-in doine
that he caught it out of my hand, and two policemen seized him and took i
from him.
Prisoner. You gave it back to me yourself. Witness. No; I am certain
he caught it out of my hand.
RICHARD MOORE. I am beadle of the market. On the 22nd of Sept. I
saw the prisoner there in company with another man who I knew I watched
them, and saw the person who was with him give him something, which
seemed to be a crown piece--they walked round the south side of the market
-I saw the prisoner rubbing the crown with his fingers-he went up to
Pickett, and gave him the crown-I stood twenty yards off, and saw Pickett
put it to his mouth, then sound it on the ground, and take it up again-the
prisoner then snatched it out of his hand-I am certain of that-I immedi.
ately seized his right hand-he tried to throw it away, but after a severe
struggle I took it from his hand-he could not throw it down, because I kept
his hand closed-I was obliged to have Johnson's assistance to secure him.
Prisoner. I was not in the market more than half an hour. Witnes. I
had seen him there from half-past six to a quarter to eight o'clock.
FREDERICK JOHN JOHNSON (policeman.) I saw the prisoner in company
with another man, and watched them for a short time I came up when Moon
called me, and saw the prisoner snatch the crown out of Pickett's hand-
succeeded at last in getting him to the station-he put his left hand into his
left waistcoat pocket-I prevented his getting it quite in-I afterwards found
a counterfeit shilling in that pocket.
JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to Her Majesty's Mint-this crow
piece and shilling are both counterfeit in all respects.
Prisoner's Defence. I sold a pair of trowsers for 6s. to a man; he gave
me the money.
Guilty. Aged 24.-Confined Twelve Months.
