 2222. HENRY RANDALL , WILLIAM COBBETT, and RICHARD RANDALL , stealing 8lbs. weight of beef, 1 3/4 lb. of veal, and 1 1/4 lb. of mutton, value 5 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Mark Wade , the master of Henry Randall and Cohbett. MARK WADE. I am a butcher, of Crown-street, Walworth. Henry Randall and Cobbett were in my service—the meat produced is mine. SAMUEL WRIGHT( policeman, P  172). On 10th Sept. I was set to watch Mr. Wade's shop—about seven o'clock in the morning I saw Henry Randall and Cobbett go in—Richard Randall followed them—in two minutes Richard came out with a blue apron under his arm—he went to Turner-street—I stopped him, and asked what he had got—he said he bought the meat for 4 d . a pound—I asked what it weighed—he said he could not tell—I asked what he gave for it—he said he had not paid for it—I took him to the station, and took the meat to his master—there was 8lbs. of beef, 1 3/4 lb. of veal, and some mutton. EDWARD AUGUST( policeman, P  206). I saw the prisoners go into the shop—Richard Randall came out, and was followed by Wright—while he was gone Cobbett, came out with a white cloth and some mutton in it.
   Richard Randall's Defence.  The meat was given to me to take to a person.
   Cobbett's Defence.  I had paid 5 s . for the meat. MARK WADE re-examined.  Cobbitt had a right sell meat—the money that was there was marked—I had sent a woman to buy some meat with it—there was no other money there.
   HENRY RANDALL  — GUILTY. Aged 20.
   COBBETT  — GUILTY. Aged 26.
   RICHARD RANDALL  — GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Nine Months .
