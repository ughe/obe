 2312. JAMES SAUNDERS was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of October , 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  ., the goods of Thomas Dodds , from his person. THOMAS DODDS. I live in Adam-street, Adelphi. On the morning of the 3rd of October I was on the London side of Temple-bar—in conse quence of somebody speaking to me, I missed my handkerchief—the pri soner was pointed out to me—I went and took my handkerchief out of his pocket—this is it—I know it by some marks and stains upon it. ROBERT NUNN( City police-constable  17.) I was on duty, and saw a mob collected—Dodds had got the prisoner by the collar—I took him.
   Prisoner.  I was going down Temple-lane, and picked it up on the pavement—I put it into my pocket.
   MR. DODDS . I could not have dropped it—it was quite safe in my pocket—I had felt it not five minutes before. GUILTY.* Aged 19.— Transported for Seven Years.
 2313. SUSAN EUSTACE was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of September , 1 smock-frock, value 2 s  ., the goods of David Hopkins . SOPHIA HOPKINS. I am the wife of David Hopkins, of Burn-street, Marylebone. The prisoner is my sister-in-law—we were removing, on Monday the 26th of September, and she wished me to let her help me, on account of my giving her her breakfast, as she was so distressed—I missed a smock-frock, and charged her the next day with taking it—she denied it—I told her to give me the duplicate, and I would get it out—I did not wish to prosecute her, being a relation; but when he policeman came about another case, he said I must—my husband had denied her the house, but I employed her unknown to him.
   Prisoner.  I did it through distress. GUILTY  . Aged 48.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined One Months.
