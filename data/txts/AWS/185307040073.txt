815. AMELIA DIBLE, stealing 1 glove, value 6d.; and 3 sovereigns
of Thomas Bowen and LAVINIA DIBLE, feloniously receiving 1 some.
reign, part of the said money
MARTHA BowEN. I am the wife of Thomas Bowen, of Charlotte-street,
Plumstead. On 24th June, I employed Amelia Dible to assist in moving
my goods from one house to another was changing my residence, my
things had to be carried three quarters of a mile, or not quite So far--I had
amongst other things, this hand basket--there were some gloves in it, and in
one of the gloves there were three sovereigns- had put them in the glove
about a quarter of an hour before in the front room in the house which I
was moving out of-there was nobody in the house but my husband and me,
and this girl-when I came out I had this basket on my arm, and a clothes
basket with some croekery in it-I put both the baskets down in the street,
and took them up again after we had changed hands-Amelia Dible helped
me to carry the large basket-when we got to the new house she said she
was ill, and must go home-she had had plenty of opportunity of taking
the money out of the small basket, when I put it down at the corner of a
street-she had opportunity of taking it without my knowing itmrmy arm
ached, and I was not looking at her-after the basket had been about a
quarter of an hour in the new house I then looked, and missed the glove and
money- went to see if I could find her-I found her in the street, near
her own home- asked her for the money, she said she had never seen it,
