   MR. RYLAND . Q.  There was a great deal of other property lying about; where did it come from? A.  From No. 44, Compton-street—I found a basket amongst other things, and it was at the station, with all these tools in it. ALFRED GREEN( City-policeman , 376). I found this card on Hoare when I searched him in the station, on 9th Aug.—it is, "James Hoare, wholesale iron and metal-dealer, 44, Compton-street, Clerkenwell"—I accompanied May to search that house—I assisted him in finding these carpenters' tools—I was present at the station, and heard May say, "How do you account for these tools," showing him the tools and the other things—Hoare said he had had them for years, and purchased them at different times—I heard a similar question asked of Downer, and he said he had had them for years, and had purchased them for his son, and they cost him 70 l .—in going down to the cell, I said, "Your son?"—he said, "Yes, Hoare is my son, he is a car penter"—I had been to the shop before with Meredith—I found Downer standing behind the counter—I asked him if he was master of the shop—he said, "Yes"—I asked him if his name was Hoare—he said no, but he was at my service—Hoare came up a trap-door in the shop—I asked him if he knew anything about the lead—he said yes, he had lent money on it.
   Cross-examined by MR. PARRY . Q.  Were you aware that there was a charge about these articles being stolen? A.  No—I went about for two days, to make inquiry about them.
   HOARE  — GUILTY. Aged 39.— Transported for Twelve Years. DOWNER  — GUILTY. Aged 53.— Transported for Ten Years .
