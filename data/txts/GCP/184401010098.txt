510. WILLIAM BLAKELOCK was indicted for stealing 1 sweeping-
machine head, value 58., the goods of Samuel Brown, from the person of Ed-
win Thomas Loveland.
SAMUEL Brown. On the 14th of Dec. I had a machine safe at ten o'clock
in the morning-I was engaged sweeping Mr. Loveland's chimneys-I after-
wards found it in the soot, in a shed, in the prisoner's back premises, No. 25,
Charles-street, Westminster-road.
Cross-examined by Mr. WILKINS. Q. You are a sweep? A. Yes--I sent
the little boy Loveland for this machine-head, as the one I had with me was
not big enough-I know the prisoner, and he knew me he is a sweep-
know this machine by having it in use twelve years, and repairing it.
EDWIN THOMAS LOVELAND. I reside with my parents, in Cornwall-rond.
About eleven o'clock, on the 14th of Dec., the prisoner met me, and asked me
who that machine belonged to, and took it away from me I told him it was
Mr. Brown's.
Cross-examined. d. Did you know the prisoner? A. Yes, by his going
past my father's—I do not think he knew me.
Nor GUILTY.
