   MR. WIGHTMAN . Q.  Was that a real letter or a sham letter? A.  It was a sham letter, purposely for an introduction, to get an interview with Mr. William Rotherham, and his name was in full on the letter—he was the person we want to arrest—the prisoner is the man. WILLIAM WILLIS, JUN. I am the officer to the sheriff of Middlesex. I went with the last witness to arrest Mr. William Rotherham, on the 15th of November—I went to the shop after Benfold had been in some time—he took a letter with him—I went into the shop and inquired if Mr. William Rotherham was at home—the young man said he did not know—while I was there I saw Mr. William Rotherham and Benfold pass the door in the street—I walked out after them, and saw them together—Benfold said, "Never mind; I am not the person you want, and at your peril take me"—he was then going to walk away—I said, "No, stop a moment; I must know who you are before I take you, if your name is not William Rotherham"—I told Benfold to go into the shop and tell one of the young men that Mr. William Rotherham wished to speak with him outside—I said that in the presence of William Rotherham—we were all three standing together—Benfold went into the shop and fetched out a young man—when he came out he came towards the prisoner, and the prisoner said, "That will do, "I think he said Edward; "I am the man you want"—he said to the young man, "You may go back;" "I am the man you want"—I then took him to the lock-up-house.
   Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN . Q.  What lock-up-house did you take him to? A.  Slowman's in Cursitor-street-we generally take them there if they wish it—he wished to go there, because Mr. Slowman would give an undertaking for him—Benfold was in the habit of going there with me sometimes, and by himself sometimes—I believe he did not go with me there the next day—I did not go myself—I herd that the prisoner was discharged the next day—that was not through Benfold going to make in quiries—I heard it from my brother—I did not write this letter—it might be Benfold or my father—I could tell if I saw it—it was sealed up in the usual way—he denied that he was the person we wanted, and said his name was not William Rotherham—I then went into the shop—not a word was said about Leaf's house in my presence—I can give you a notion of the contents of the letter. THOMAS EDWARD STUBBS.
