 2171. PETER BUSHELL was indicted for stealing 1 copper, value 10 s  ., the goods of John Evans , and fixed to a building. JOHN EVANS. I live in Prospect-place. I lost a copper from the kitchen of No. 3, Hertford-place, West-street, Camberwell—I saw it safe on the morning of the 20th of June—it is the house of Mr. Cox—I was the tenant—I missed it on the 4th of July, and saw it on the 8th of July—the police man brought two coppers, one of which fitted my place as near as possible. JAMES COX. I saw a copper at Union Hall, doubled up, and the bottom cut out—there were three coppers doubled up and. quite disfigured—they were beaten out, and I saw one of them tried to this house—it fitted, except leaving a margin in the cement—there was no cement on the copper. WILLIAM BURTON( policeman. ) I stopped the prisoner on the 1st of July, at half-past six o'clock, in Kennington-road, about a quarter of a mile from this house, with a basket containing the greater part of three coppers.—as we were going to the station he said, "You think you will do a great deal now, but I can prove where I got them,"and at the station he told the inspector the same, but said he should defer that to another place—one copper was beat out by a smith, and fitted the cement so at to leave a very slight vacancy—the bottom was cut out. NOT GUILTY.
