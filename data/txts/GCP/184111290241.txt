287. RICHARD HITCHMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 12th
of November, 1 purse, value 1s. ; 10 shillings, and 2 sixpences; the pro-
perty of Joseph Limperani, from the person of Christiana Judith Limperani.
CHRISTIANA JUDITH LIMPERANI. I am the wife of Joseph Limperani;
we live in Albany-road, Camberwell. On the 12th of November, at five
o'clock in the afternoon, I was going home with my daughter from Wal-
worth-gate- I got to the corner of Albany-road, between the wall and the
watch-box, exactly at the angle of the road, I felt the proximity of three
young men who had been following us--I felt myself touched, and one of
the men immediately passed me---seeing him put his left-hand into
his pocket, and connecting that with having been touched, I put my own
hand into my pocket, and immediately missed my purse--I saw a man
pass me who I thought had put his hand into my pocket-I told my daugh-
ter I had been robbed, and my purse was gone-a policeman being in the
immediate vicinity I gave information immediately-the man who passed
me, and put his hand into his pocket had on a brown Taglioni coat, with
pockets in front-I lost a purse containing, as nearly as I can recollect,
11s.-next morning the prisoner was produced to me-from his personal
appearance he bears a resemblance to one of the three men who were there-
I did not turn my head to look in his face-I had got change from Mr.
Townsend, and one of the shillings was excessively black between the head
and the rim-I have since seen one which I believe to be that-to the best
of my belief this is it-(looking at a shilling)-the purse has not been found
--this shilling is the only one I can identify.
Cross-examined by MR. Puillips. Q. I believe it was the shadows of
the men you saw? A. I saw the men sideways, by a side glance-I cer-
tainly did not see them from top to toe-as I turned my head I saw two
heads on one side, and one on the other-I did not see their faces suffi-
ciently to identify them--my money was in a green purse.
