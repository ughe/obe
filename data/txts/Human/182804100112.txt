 1041.  JAMES BRIAN was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of April , 2 seals, value 3l.; 2 rings, value 1l.; part of a watch-chain, value 10s, and 1 watch-key, value 10s., the goods of Henry Westley , from his person.
 HENRY WESTLEY, Esq. I am a  gentleman. I was at Covent-Garden Theatre on Friday evening the 11th of April; I was handing some ladies to a carriage when the play was over; I had not shut the carriage door, intending to get in myself, but a man came up and snatched at my watch-chain; he got the seals, the rings, and a portion of the chain; he snatched with considerable force; I made an effort to take him, but he escaped under some coaches; and the prisoner knocked me down on one knee, which prevented my seizing the thief - I have no doubt whatever that it was done intentionally - I took the prisoner; he struggled very much, but I gave him in charge.
 Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. You did not see the prisoner near you at the time your property taken? A. Yes, they were both together, and the other took the seals; it did not occupy a minute; I did not pursue the person who took them; the prisoner did not say it was accidental; he denied that he pushed me at all; he did not say his eyes were bad, and he could not see.
   EDMUND DONOVAN. I am a watchman. I took the prisoner; nothing was found on him.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Life.
 1042.  WILLIAM JONES and GEORGE WARWICK was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of April , 6 brushes, value 9s. the goods of John Sears.
   GEORGE ANSON. I live with my parents in Weston-street, Somers-town. On the 12th of April, at twelve o'clock in the day, I was playing at shuttlecock; I saw the two prisoners go up to Mr. Sears's house; Jones took these brushes off the door-post; Warwick was close by him, and had a basket in his hand; Jones put the brushes into the basket, and they went away - I told Mrs. Sears; she and I went in pursuit; we took them about 100 yards off; the brushes were found in the basket.
   ANN SEARS. I am the wife of John Sears; he is a tinman, and lives in Weston-street. Anson gave me information; we pursued the prisoners; I found these six brushes of my husband's in the basket which Warwick was carrying.
   ROBERT TEASDALE. I am an officer, and took the prisoners.
 Warwick's Defence. I heard a cry of run; I ran, and was taken.
 JONES -    GUILTY. Aged 14.
 WARWICK -    GUILTY. Aged 14. Confined Three Months, and Whipped.
 1043.  THOMAS JOHNSON, EVAN JONES, and JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of February , 1 cap, value 1s., the goods of James Tyer.
   JAMES FOGG. I am an officer. I was with my brother in Rosemary lane on the 27th of February; I saw Johnson and Smith together; and Evans soon after joined them; they attempted several shops in Rosemary-lane; they went to a coppersmith, and two of them tried to lift up a copper from there, while the other looked in at the window; they were there about half an hour, when they left and went down the Commercial-road, and Johnson and Smith went into a shoemaker's shop, and looked at some shoes, but there were several persons there, and they came out again; they then went to Mr. Tyers, - Johnson and Smith went in; Jones remained outside, as he had done before; they staid there a little while, came out, and crossed the road; I and my brother took them, and found this cap in Johnson's hat; he said his mistress, who kept a shop in Whitechapel, had sent him to Limehouse for it.
   JOHN FOGG. I was with my brother, and confirm his statement - the prisoners were all in company together.
   CHARLOTTE TYER. I am wife of James Tyer. On the 27th of February I came into the shop; my sister said, some suspicious characters had been there; I then looked and missed this cap, which the officers brought in with the prisoner.
 JOHNSON -    GUILTY. Aged 18.
 JONES -    GUILTY. Aged 17.
 SMITH    GUILTY. Aged 15. Transported for Seven Years.
 1044.  JOHN LUTY was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of February , 1 mallet, value 2s., the goods of William Dance.
 WILLIAM DANCE. I am a  carpenter, and worked with the prisoner at an empty house in Holborn. On the 26th of February, I left my mallet there, and missed it in the morning; I found it in the room where the prisoner lives a fortnight after; he was apprehended by my master for robbing him, but the bill was thrown out - I lost some other tools about that time - another man worked there with us.
 Cross-examined by MR. BARRY. Q. Were you on the premises at the time you lost the mallet? A. No: after he was apprehended, his wife came there for some tools of his.
   FRANCIS FAGAN. I apprehended the prisoner on the the 29th of February. Dance afterwards came to my house, and said he had lost a mallet; we went to the prisoner's room, and found a great many tools - Dance picked this mallet out.
     NOT GUILTY.
