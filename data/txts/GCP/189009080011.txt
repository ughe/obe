Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MR. WILKINSON Prosecuted, and Mr. K. FRITH Defended Blaby.

Monarch public-house, Chalk Farm Road-on 15th August, about half-
past five, the two prisoners cảme in with a woman, and Stanley called
for some liquor, price fourpence halfpenny, and put a florin on the
counter-I gave him 1s. 7 d. change, and put the florin on a shelf where
there was no other florin-shortly afterwards my sister Maud, who was
serving, said in the prisoners' hearing, “They have given me a bad one"
-I did not hear either of them say anything.

when I came back-I am positive it was a florin I took, and not a
shilling-my sister saw you give it to me.
