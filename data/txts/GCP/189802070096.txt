196. AMELIA HARDING (49), Indicted for and charged on the
Coroner's Inquisition with the wilful murder of Charles Levy.
MR. C. F. GILL Prosecuted, and MR. WARBURTON Defended.
The prisoner, in the hearing of the Jury, stated that she was Guilty of
manslaughter. The Jury found that verdict and recommended her to mercy,
believing she had acted under great provocation.--Eighteen Months' Hard
Labour
