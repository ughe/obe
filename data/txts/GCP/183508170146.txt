friend of it, he advised him to go to the respectable hotels, and that the gentle.
men who came there would relieve him—the Frenchman occasionally visited
him at these hotels, and on one occasion, when he called, he asked him to
direct him to the water-closet-upon his return he stated he had received 301.
from Liverpool, and the prisoner begged him to lend him 31., which he pro-
mised to do, and gave him the two 5l. notes to get changed that he
changed them at the shops in question, and gave the Frenchman 77. as the
change.)
Three witnesses gave the prisoner a good character.
