617. ANNIE MARIA CUTHBERT (20), Unlawfully abandoning and
exposing her new born child, whereby its life was endangered.
MR. KELLY conducted the Prosecution.
It appeared by the medical evidence that the child died from injuries caused
during birth, the prisoner having delivered herself without medical assistance,
and that it would have died independently of the exposure. Nor Guilty.
Before Mr. Justice Hannen.
