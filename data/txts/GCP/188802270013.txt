BOSWELL CAPPER. I am Mr. Carr's clerk--he handed over Bruce to
me to take the numbers of the bonds he brought, which I did. (Roading
them.)
ROBERT HALL. I am clerk to Messrs. Glynn, bankers-I produce my
book, which shows on 19th October & cheque for 1021. by Carr Brothers,
which I cashed with 10 51. notes, Nos. 74456 to 74465, and 521. in gold-
on 15th December I cashed a cheque for 2081. 158., with 10 51. notes,
58777 to 53786, 20 51. notes, 53757 to 53776, inclusive, and gold.
FRANK GEORGE MANNING. I am a cashier at the London and South
Western Bank, Fenchurch Street Branch--on 20th December & cheque
for 7201. 58. on Messrs. Glynn was paid in with this paying-in slip by J.
Bruce to the credit of Mrs. Catherine Bruce-that was advised the same
evening to the Camberwell Branch-we had no account of Mrs. Catherine
Bruce.
GERALD MAURIOR. I am a clerk in the London and South Western
Bank, Camberwell Branch-on 19th December Bruce came there with a
man named Vallance, who said that he wished to introduce Bruce to open
an account in the name of his wife, and Bruce produced a letter from
Mrs. Bruce, requesting us to open an account in her name—this, " Wit-
ness to signature, H. L. VALLANCE,” was written at the time-Bruce paid
in 11 51. notes, 53757, and 53761, and 53765, and 53780 to 58784-I gave
him a cheque-book-he asked me to change three 51. notes, 53777 to 53779,
which I did-on 21st December I was advised from the head office to
credit the account 7201. 58., and on the same day, a little before noon,
Bruce came and presented a cheque for 7001., and I paid him 24 101.
notes, 88997 to 39000, 85576 to 85595; three 201. notes, 62433 to 62435;
four 501. notes, 52697 to 52700; and two 1001. notes, 00754 to 00755—
that was the only cheque paid on the account-another was drawn, but it
was stopped.
HENRY FLETCHER VALLANCE. I am a solicitor, of 55, Chancery Lane
I am not doing much now I have been in practice at Camberwell some
time-I live at 155, The Grove, Camberwell-I have known Bruce many
years- I took him to the London and South Western Bank, Camberwell
Branch, and introduced him to open an account-my wife has her
account there I did not see this letter written, but I know the signature
-Bruce asked me to go to the bank on the same day that I went-he
told me that Mrs. Bruce's cheque had arrived I was aware that Mr.
Alexander Bruce was in the habit of paying her dividends on some funds,
and he said “Will you introduce me to your bank?"-I said “The
account should appear in her name, as the money, being hers, it is for the
family"-Bruce introduced me to Ross on, I think, December 21st-I
don't think he mentioned his name-he said “My brother-in-law”-that
was at Bruce's house in Champion Grove-I don't think I ought to
disclose what I was introduced for-I was consulted as a solicitor by both
Bruce and Rogs on that Monday-I had never seen Mr. Ross before-I
have got a certificate, but I have not got it here—they consulted me as a
solicitur on that 21st December, and I left England with Ross that night
-I think that was the same day that I was introduced to him we went
