 1795.  ANN BROOKER was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of July , 1 purse, value 6d.; 13 sovereigns, 17 shillings, and 1 sixpence, the property of William Barton , from his person.
 WILLIAM BARTON. On the 27th of July I met the prisoner in the Haymarket, and went to a house in  Union-court, Westminster; I went to bed with her - I had thirteen sovereigns, and 17s. 6d. in my purse; I put my trousers on a chair by the bed-side - I did not go to sleep - she complained of being thirsty; in about three-quarters of an hour she said she would get up, and go and have a pint of beer - she went away, but did not return. I got up and missed my trousers and money - I was sober; I saw her at Bow-street afterwards, and am certain of her person.
   GEORGE BLACKMAN. I am a patrol. I apprehended the prisoner, in consequence of information, on the night of the 7th of August, at No. 2, Union-court, Orchard-street, Westminster - I had been looking for her several days, and she had been absent from her lodgings.
   ELIZA BOND. I live at No. 16, Union-court - the prisoner lodged at No. 2. On the 26th of July, I had the care of her child, which is now in her arms; she left it with me on that day, and I did not see her afterwards - she used to call daily to see it before.
   WILLIAM BARTON. I am sure I did not go to sleep - that nobody else came into the room, and that the money was safe when I undressed.
 Prisoner's Defence. I never saw the man.
 GUILTY. Aged 20.
 Of stealing, but not from the person. Transported for Seven Years.
 1796.  ANN MILLER was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of August , 4 half sovereigns, and one 20l. Bank note, the property of Francis Steward , from his person.
 FRANCIS STEWARD. I am a  sailor. On the 4th of August, about twelve o'clock at night, I was in Whitechapel, and had a 20l. note and four half-sovereigns in my breeches pocket; the prisoner spoke to me, and I went with her to a house, which I could not find afterwards. I had the money in my hand when I went to her room. I wrapped the sovereign in the notes while we sat there, and put them into my pocket; the prisoner took them out, and ran away directly. I met her in the street three or four days after - I was not above five minutes with her, but am certain of her.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. What information did you give the officer respecting the street you was robbed in? A. I do not know the street; I never mentioned the name of it, or the number of the house - I never said it was No. 20, White Lion-street, or that it was at Mrs. Clark's. I met her again on the Wednesday following, in the same place - I did not give charge of her; and, on the Friday after, she was brought to me by a shipmate - and was secured.
 COURT. Q. Why not take her on Wednesday? A. I was afraid she would run from me.
   JOHN NORRIS. On Saturday, the 4th of August, the prosecutor came to me between five and six o'clock, and said he had been robbed; I went with him - he could not point out any house. On the Friday he sent for me, and said he had seen the woman, and his shipmates were going to bring her down; she was taken, and he swore positively to her - she was remanded twice, but he had given us information of quite another person altogether.
 Cross-examined. Q. Has not the other person absconded? A. We have seen her since, but he swore positively to the prisoner.
   JAMES HANLEY. I am an officer of Worship-street. The prosecutor told me he had been robbed at Mrs. Clark's, No. 20, White Lion-street - he took me there, and showed me the room.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1797.  EDWARD PURSEY was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of July , 1 watch, value 30s.; 2 seals, value 6s., and 1 key, value 1s., the goods of Thomas Larke , from his person.
 THOMAS LARKE. I live in Union-place. On the 14th of July, between eleven and twelve o'clock at night, I was going home with Thomas Dean, my landlord - the prisoner ran up against me, and said, "I beg your pardon, Sir;" and as he said that, he drew my watch out of my pocket. I caught it with my left hand, and caught him with my right hand.
   THOMAS DEAN. I was with Larke; his account is correct; I was about six feet before him; he called me; I turned, and saw the prisoner in his hands; he gave me the watch; I took it to the office - Larke was sober.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going along - this gentleman was in a mob, and seemed in liquor; he took me, and said I had robbed him; he took the watch from his pocket, and gave it to the other witness.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Fourteen Years.
