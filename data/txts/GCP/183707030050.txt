REBECCA SHILLINGFORD. My husband lives in Hatfield-street. The

o'clock.
EDWARD Price. I live at No. 68, Golden-lane, and am a broker On
Monday, the 12th of June, about eight o'clock in the evening, the prisoner
came and asked me to buy a flock bed-I asked where it was-she said,
"Over the way"-I went with her to Cherry-tree-court, and bought the
bed for 38.--this is the bed-the prosecutrix was not there the prisoner
was sober.
