A little after 11 o'clock on Wednesday night, the 23rd, I was called to the Hercules, and saw there the prisoner and prosecutor—the prosecutor said "This woman has robbed me; I charge her with stealing 5 l . 10 s .    Cross-examined.  The prisoner said it was her own money—the pro secutor had been drinking, but was quite capable of knowing what he was about—the gold was loose in the bag; there was a handkerchief and gloves over it, but if it was put in and shaken the gold would fall to the bottom of the bag.
