done very quickly, and without pain-she told me herself that she had
not any pain-she afterwards told me she could see--she was blind when

were going to give one to Mr. Ralph that was after the second operation
-I do not know Mr. Ralph's writing-these instruments produced are
like those I have seen, with one exception.
