SAMUEL EDGE (City police-constable, No. 264.) I took the prisoner at
the White Hart public-house, Bishopsgate-street, on the Sunday evening, at
nine o'clock-I said, "I want you"-he said, “I did not take the wo-
man's money" - I had not said any thing to him about money--this bundle
was given to me at the station-house-it contains straps.
Cross-examined. Q. How did you know the prisoner was at the White
Hart? A. The prosecutor told me where he was-he stood at the door
and pointed him out.

1385. BARNETT JOSEPHS was indicted for feloniously receiving of
a certain evil-disposed person, on the 6th of March, 500lbs. weight of lead,
value 30s., the goods of William Miller, well knowing the same to have
been stolen ; against the Statute, &c.—2nd Count, calling it the goods of
John Clay.--3rd Count, of the Right Honourable and Right Reverend
Father in God, Charles James, Lord Bishop of London.-4th Count, of the
Right Honourable and Most Reverend William, Lord Archbishop of Can-
terbury.---5th Count, of divers persons unknown.--6th Count, of certain
persons unknown.

M R. PHILLIPs conducted the Prosecution.
Tuomas RAWLEY. I am gate-keeper to Messrs. Pontifex, of Shoe-
lane, they are coppersmiths. On the 6th of March the prisoner brought
40 cwt. and 12lbs. of lead-it was weigbed-I saw a piece of it opened-
it appeared to be coffin lead a part of it is here now-some of the plates
were cut out in my presence, and are here.

left it for not doing my duty-I was not charged with being concerned
in carrying on a private still there was an illicit glass-work, and because
I did not report it to my superior officer, I got discharged the prisoner had
dealt with Messrs. Pontifex before-I do not know whepher be buys metal

marks on the lead in the last truck that was being unloaded-that might
be about twenty minutes after the prisoner had left.
