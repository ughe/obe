1821. SHADRACH MITCHELL was indicted for stealing, on the
5th of June, 681bs. weight of potatoes, value 18, 6d., the goods of Elis
beth Parrott and others; and that he had been before convicted of felong.
John PARROTT. I am a farmer, and live at Harmondsworth-there is
small farm belonging to my sister Elizabeth and two other sisters-1
manage it for them. On the 5th of June they had a pit of potatoes-1
examined it on the 6th of June, and found it had been opened, and be
tween a bushel and a sack of potatoes stolen--I had seen it safe within
week-I received information, and gave information to the policeman-
have seen some potatoes since, and they correspond exactly with those it
the pit-they are a particular sort, called "Regents."
Thomas DUGGAN (police-constable T 28.) I received information, in
consequence of which I went to the prisoner's house, about ten o'clock
night, a short distance from Cranford-bridge I found him there--I asked
if his name was Mitchell-he said it was- I said I came to apprehend bin
for stealing potatoes from Miss Parrott, and asked him if he had any po-
tatoes-he said he had he took me down stairs, and took out a sack which
contained about five pecks of these potatoes--I have matched them will
those in the pit, and they seem the same--I asked how he came by them
he said he purchased them at the door of a hawker whom he did not know
- HENRY PETERS. I am a labourer, living at Harmondsworth. At 17
