like to tell an untruth I did not deceive Mr. Coronel I have dealt with
Jones at Newington-I am a witness against him-it was out of sorrow for
my sins that I turned witness, no other reason I do not exactly under-
stand you-I wished Mr. Saltmer to have his property back- I told of this
before I was threatened to be prosecuted.
Q. On your solemn oath, did you tell one word until you were promised
not to be prosecuted yourself? A. Yes, I did-Cuthbert told me that my
master would not prosecute me if I told-I answered you before without
thinking--my memory came back after seeing Cuthbert-I was not afraid
he would contradict me I said it quite in mistake-I understand the
question- I do not know that it was to get off myself that I told the story
-Cuthbert told me, over and over again, that I should be prosecuted unless
I gave evidence-it was after that I told the story against the prisoner-
did not tell Cuthbert a word till he told me I should not be prosecuted-it
is to save myself that I have come to give evidence, and also from sorrow
for my sins, both I do not think about being safe-I should not have
told any thing unless I had thought I should be safe-I believe I should
have done so now, because I am very sorry for it.
Q. Do you care how many you convict, provided you get off yourself!
A. I do not wish them to be hurt-I do not wish them to be convicted-
if the Jury do not believe me, I think I should run the risk of being pro-
secuted-I wish to convict the prisoner that I may save myself-whether
I am prosecuted or not depends very much upon whether he is convicted;
and the same with respect to Jones, if the Jury do not convict, I think I
run the risk of being tried myself.
