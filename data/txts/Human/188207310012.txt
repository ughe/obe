   The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate. "While with the cart I had to go to the urinal, and when I came back it was gone. I afterwards found two men with it in the Strand; they said they were going to sell it. I said that they had better not do that. They said they knew the owner, and told me to go to Golden Lane with them; we did so and went to a public-house."
   Prisoner's Defence.  When I was left to mind it I got in the cart and fell asleep. These two chaps  said they knew the owner; they took me to a public-house and I went towards the Strand with them. They said "All right, we know the owner; you come along with us," and I drove about with them trying to find the owner out, till the constable stopped me. GUILTY  .— Recommended to mercy by the Jury on account of the temptation. — Three Months' Hard Labour.
