2016. THOMAS WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 27th
of June, 1 coat, value 41., the goods of the Earl of Warwick : 2nd
Count, of John Corner.
-
JOHN CORNER. I am coachman to the Earl of Warwick, who lives in
Carlton-gardens. On the afternoon of the 27th of June I returned with
the carriage to the stables in Carlton-mews, and left my livery coat on the
carriage-I missed it shortly afterwards, and my helpmate gave me in-
formation-I met the prisoner in the street with it on his shoulder, about
two hundred yards from the stable--this is the coat-(looking at it.)
JOHN YOUNG. I am helper in the prosecutor's stables--I saw the pri-
soner go out with the coat-I followed, and called out “Stop him." olo
John Dawson (police-constable A 119.) The prisoner was given into
my custody-I asked him whether he was guilty of the charge-he said
he was.
Prisoner. I throw myself on your mercy-it is my first offence.
GUILTY. Aged 52.-Confined Six Months.
