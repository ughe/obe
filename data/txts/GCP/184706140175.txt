Lucy BIRD. I am the prosecutor's wife-on Friday, the 7th of May,
I was in Searle's-place, Temple-bar, and saw Charles Farr going into a
house there in which his mother lodges--I waited outside some time, and at
last he came out-he walked very fast, bolding his head down-he might
have seen me—I was in a position for him to see me-I kept up with him-
he went round Ship-yard into the Strand, then came again through Temple-bar
to the bottom of Searle's-place-he went up to a cab, spoke to the cabman,
