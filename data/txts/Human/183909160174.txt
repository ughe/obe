 2683. GEORGE THOMAS was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of August , 1 sack, value 2 s  .; and 4 bushels of oats, value 15 s  .; the goods of Emanuel Cooper , his master.
   MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution. EMANUEL COOPER. I am one of the Society of Friends, and am a hat manufacturer, and live in Castle-street, Southwark. I have stables at Jackson and Ranson's yard, in Worcester-street, and one at the back of my house—the prisoner was in my service as carman, and had the care of the horses food in Worcester-street—a man of the name of Langford has the care of the horses in the stable at the back of my house—on the 15th of August, I had about ten sacks of oats from Thomas Bax—I was informed is was delivered at the stable in Worcester-street—I directed the prisoner that the corn should be kept in the stable in Castle-street—on the 27th, from information I received, I called the prisoner into my counting-house—I asked him where he had taken the sack of oats to—he said, "What sack of oats?"—I said, "You had better tell me the truth, you see I know about it"—I did not know Redford personally, but he had been employed to cart some loads of rubbish away—I gave the prisoner in charge in the afternoon—I went before the Magistrate, and he asked where Redford was—he was not in custody—he directed a warrant to be issued, and he was taken, but before he was taken he came to my counting-house—in conse quence of what he said, I went to his place, and there found a sack, which was Thomas Bax's, and I have no doubt it was one in which my corn had come—the prisoner had no authority to deliver one of these sacks to any person, but to take the corn to my stable in Castle-street.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  The prisoner was desired to take it to Castle-street from Worcester-street? A.  Yes, they are adjoining streets—Redford's house is three or four hundred yards from there—it would have been nearer to take it to Castle-street than to Redford's. JOHN ELLARD. I am warehouseman to Jackson and Ranson, in Wor cester-street—our counting-house is on one side of the yard, and my house on the other.
