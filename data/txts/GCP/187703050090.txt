November, when he left suddenly I cannot trace the likeness in the photo
graph produced-I knew Francis very well-he gave me as reference Mr.
Prendergast, 129, Commercial Road, E., a cheesemonger-I have seen Parker
at Francis' premises more than once, and Gardner frequently-Aarons came
with Francis when he took the premises and was there frequently, I know
Gentles, but not by that name-he went in the name of Lindsay and Belfoy, but
not Blyth, to my knowledge-- he was there frequently they all appeared to be
engaged in the same business-Aarons and Francis appeared to be the prin
