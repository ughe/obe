HENRY Brown. I am a lamp manufacturer, in Westminster Bridge
Road-I was a creditor of Mr. Weston's for 431. ; I was at the premises on
8th September, and saw Mr. Weston point out two black bags; Homewood
was present ; Mr. Weston asked Homewood what they contained, he said
"Tools"-Weston said to me, “ Just feel these "I did feel them, and I said,
“They are cut-glass work of chandeliers, 1 should imagine"-Homewood
said nothing to that-Weston said he would call the police in, and the
police were sent for- I did not see the policeman there; I did not see the
bags opened, but I saw them afterwards and they contained miscellaneous
chandelier work, out drops, star pans, and prisms, and a good many-I
have been acquainted with such articles for many years I could not say
the value of them.
Crow-examined by MR BESLET. . Are these the bags that have been
produced ? 4. Yes, I saw no tools in them, nothing but the glass-work-I
am a creditor under this bankruptcy- I was a creditor for 1841. under
former deed, I did not get any thing, I have no guarantee-I have been
promised to be paid in full, by a partner of Weston's; I expect the whole of
my money-I have been in business all my life; I began at four years of age.
MR, METCALPE. Q. Have you been promised your money by Mr. Abra
hams 4. Yes; Mr. Weston said Abrahams should pay me-my attention
was called to a large quantity of glass in the cupboard under the staircase,
by Mr. Weston.
