REGINALD ARTHUR CUTTING, clerk to Attree, Johnson, and Ward,
solicitors. Mr. Ward has an account with the London and County
Bank, Holborn, where I do my work in the same office as the cashier,

Leonard Archibald McQueen, son of prisoner MoQueen, and identi.

knew where I was employed, but I have not seen him there. I know
his father, and I think he knew my name and that I was a solicitor's
clerk. I know nothing about Exhibit 75, with the name "Richard
Cutting."
