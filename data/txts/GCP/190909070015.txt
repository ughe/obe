CORNELIUS DERKSEN, house surgeon, Hampstead Hospital. Pro-
secutor was brought in to me on July 20 suffering from a cut across
the throat about four inches long, which had been sewn up; it was
non deep. He also had a swelling around the left eye, causing pro-
tasion of the eyelids; there was also some bleeding by the tissues
of the eye; the swelling would have required a hard knook by a
blunt instrument. I kept him in the hospital for eight days; he
was seriously injured.
Inspector ARTHUR NEIL, Y Division. I arrested prisoner on
July 20. On my telling him the charge, he said, "Yes, I did it, but
it did not oocur quite like that. I went to the shop yesterday. I
asked him if the Hitchin Market was a day or half-day market. I
mo pedlar. He advised me not to go to Hitchin but to peddle
Hampstead. I left my bag with some goods at his shop. I peddled
Hampstead but did no good. I was walking about all night and this
afternoon. I went to get my bag. He allowed me to go to the
lavatory. I afterwards went into the room at the back of the shop.
We were looking at the pictures on the wall. Seeing the knife on
the table the temptation overcame me, and I picked it up. I do
not know what I did. We had a struggle and (referring to a slight
injury on his hand) I must have done this. I do not know what
made me do it. There was no need to do it. I am respectably con-
sected. I have only had a bun and a glass of milk since yesterday,
and it is very hot to-day."
SIDNEY REGINALD DYER, medical officer of Brixton Prison, said he
had had prisoner under observation since July 21, and had gone
into bis history. Having carefully considered the case, witness
expressed the opinion that at the time of the alleged offence prisoner
was really not responsible, and did not know the nature and quality
of his acts.
Verdict, Guilty, but that prisoner was insane at the time, 80 m
not to be responsible for his acts. He was ordered to be detained
during His Majesty's pleasure.
