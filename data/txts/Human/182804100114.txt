 1049.  GEORGE BURLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of March , 1 copper boiler, value 12s., the goods of William Linnet.
   WILLIAM HUBBARD. On the 6th of March, I saw the prisoner in Northampton-street, with a copper boiler, running from Mr. Linnet's house - I went and told him; he went in pursuit - I did not see him take the boiler.
 WILLIAM LINNET. I live in  Northampton-street, and am a broker. Hubbard told me, on the 6th of March, that the prisoner had gone off with a boiler - I pursued about 150 yards, and saw him carrying this boiler very leisurely along - I took him back - the boiler had been chained to the door - this is it.
 GUILTY. Aged 14. Recommended to mercy. - Confined Six Weeks.
 1050.  BARNET ABRAHAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 22d of March , 11 books, value 36s.; and one quilt, value 8s.; the goods of John Jones Price.
   CHARLES BROWN. I am in the employ of Mr. Price, a pawnbroker of Wentworth-street. On the night of the 22d of March, about a quarter before eleven o'clock, I observed our inside window open; I was behind the counter opposite to it - I saw the prisoner with the window in one hand, and a bed quilt in the other, tied up in a handkerchief, which had been in the window; I gave an alarm - he went to the door and tried to escape; the shop was full of people - I went out and seized him; he dropped the quilt on his left side, the moment he got out at the door.
   SUSANNAH SMITH. I was at Mr. Price's; I saw Brown jump over the counter, and go to the door; he took hold of the prisoner, and I took hold of him - I saw him drop the quilt and several books at the same time.
   JOHN POWELL. I am a watchman; I took the prisoner down to the watch-house - I searched him, and found a pair of shoes on him.
   JOHN DUNGATE. I produce the property which was brought to the watch-house with the prisoner.
   JOHN JONES PRICE. I keep this shop; I was at home, and heard a bustle - I told Brown to jump over the counter, and see what was the matter, which he did; these articles are my property - they were brought in by different people out of the street; he came in by the door, and there are folding doors to the window, fastened with a button, eight feet high - I should not have thought a person could have reached it.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I went to pawn a pair of shoes that night; they accused me of having the property - but there was none on me, it was out at the door.
     GUILTY. Aged 25. Transported for Seven Years.
 1051.  BENJAMIN SPENCER was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of March , 4 sows, price 16l., the property of Thomas Jackson.
 THOMAS JACKSON. I live at  Rose-well, Mile-end Old-town; I had four sows in a sty, in a cart-shed, which shuts with a pair of folding-gates - I locked them up at eight o'clock at night, on the 26th of March, and at five the next morning I missed them - I cannot say whether they had broken out of the sty, or whether it had been broken down; but the gates were wide open, and the lock had been forced off with a piece of iron, which I have in my pocket - I had had them about seven weeks, and fed them there regularly; they were all in pig, and I suppose about half their time gone, what farmers call, heavy in pig; I went round with my milk in the morning, and made enquiries, and in the afternoon a person said, I should find them in Green-street, Twig-folly - I left my milk, and went there; I saw Mr. Fernley, and gave him a description of them; he said it was perfectly right, and showed them to me directly - I knew them to be mine; his house is about three-quarters of a mile from mine - I have seen the prisoner about the neighbourhood carrying meat in a tray, as a hawking butcher.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did you describe them as two white sows? A. Certainly; the other two were black and white; the prisoner lives in Devonshire-street, about three quarters of a mile from my place.
