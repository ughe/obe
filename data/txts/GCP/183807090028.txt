1671. JAMES SMITH was indicted for embezzlement.
WILLIAM BLACKMORE NOBLE. I live in the parish of St. Andrer,
Holborn, and keep a dairy farm. I am in partnership with John Mead, of
Peckham Rye-he does not interfere in the business at all-Martin, my
clerk, receives money for me--the prisoner received money for me--he
has not accounted to me for 18. 2d., nor for 78. 1d.
JAMES MARTIN. I am clerk to the prosecutor. The prisoner was in his
service, and used to receive money for him I made out these two bills-
(looking at them)-the prisoner never brought me the money for them-
ought to have brought it on the day he received it-the words "Paida
these bills are the prisoner's writing.
