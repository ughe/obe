 1992. JOHN BILL was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of August , 2 pints of brandy, value 5 s  ., and 1 pint of wine, value 2 s  ., the goods of the London Dock Company , his masters.
   MR. BALLANTINE . conducted the Prosecution . GEORGE DIX. I am a policeman. In consequence of information I went with a man named Clements to the East Vault Office, London Docks, on the 14th of August—the prisoner was employed in that office as a clerk—I asked Clements to examine the prisoner's desk—it is a large desk which extends from one side of the box to the other—I should think it is about eight feet—there are others sit there besides him—there is a small cupboard under his desk from the ground up to the desk—the prisoner un locked the door of the cupboard—I searched it all round, and I found a small bag, but nothing of consequence—I perceived that the inside of the cup board did not extend so high as the outside-by pulling a drawer out in the desk, he could get to the top of this cupboard-while the drawer was closed I could not see any opening—I pulled the drawer out entirely, and saw a board which appeared to have been recently planed—I pulled up that board, put my hand in, and found three bottles containing two pints of brandy, and one pint of wine—I examined the cupboard well—there was no means of getting to the place where I found the bottles without opening the drawer—the desk does not open—it was by pulling the drawer out, I got to the top of the cupboard, and the prisoner unlocked the drawer—I asked whether he could give any account of these bottles—he said he knew nothing of them, nor yet of the place—he had been in the employment of the Docks a long time—he came there when quite a lad.
