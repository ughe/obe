SMITHERS, George, feloniously marrying Eliza Ann Chamberlain,
his wife being then alive.
No evidence being offered that prisoner had seen his wife for seven
years, the jury were directed to acquit him if they thought he bona fide
believed his wife was dead.
Verdict, Not guilty.
BEFORE THE COMMON SERJEANT
(Tuesday, March 15.)
HOFLER, Wilhelm (53, merchant), pleaded guilty of unlawfully
publishing a malicious libel of and concerning Carl Kleinhaus: also
publishing a false and malicious libel of and concerning Carl Klein-
haus, Alexander George Lee, and Ernest Haensel.
(Prisoner, having undertaken to pay the costs of the prosecution,
was released on his own recognisances in E50 to come up for judgment
if called upon.)

live at Ashburton, St. Aldrey's Road, Barking. In March, 1909,

ber. I went back to my work for about a week and then had a re-

must separate owing to doctor's orders, as he was suffering from a
certain disease, and that if I was found in the house with him he would
