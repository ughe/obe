Before Robert Malcolm Kerr, Esq.
693. WILLIAM JOHNSON (20), Robbery with violence on Patrick
Leehan, and stealing a handkerchief, his property.
Mr. WARNER SLEIGH conducted the Prosecution.S PORTE
PATRICK LEEHAN. I am a labourer, and live at 7, John's Place-about
9.30 on the night of September 4th I was in Brick Lane the prisoner
stopped me, and asked me to give him a penny for him and his mate, as
they were going reaping in the morning-I told him I had not got one-he
caught me by the waistcoat, and put his hand into my right-hand pocket
I told him I had not got a penny in my possession-he struck me, and put
his hand in again, and pulled out my tobacco box, knife, and a pipe-I said
"You are going too far with that; I will give you six months the struok
me again, and I was dragged into Flower and Dean Street - I was knocked
about and kicked fearfully—the prisoner struck at me all the while-I
heard some words pass to have the handkerchief I was wearing on my neck,
and that was taken the prisoner was choking me at the time I was down,
and he took the handkerchief from me-be put the tobacco box and the
knife in my pocket again-1 was insensible for a few, minutes after-I
waited till I saw a constable, and I went then to the police station---the
sergeant told me to go, and if I could see the prisoner, to give him into
custody-I met a constable in Brick Lane, and went in search of the
prisoner, and gave him into custody I was not capable of performing
daily labour for some time after.

up, and then I went to the station.
