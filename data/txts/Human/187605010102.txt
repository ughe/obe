   Cross-examined.  I cannot tell in how many months last year he was engaged in repairing the machinery—I am there every day from 10 till 5 o'clock about the works, superintending—I may have had instructions to shorten hands for some months, as soon as the work in hand would enable us to do it—the prisoner worked by the hour—there was no arrangement as to notice, and I should not give him any until he came to the table to be paid—I could not say whether the moulding machine worked properly it was in motion such a short time—he was very abusive, he was the worse for liquor—he sent on Sunday to say that he wanted to come and put the machinery right; I was not there. DANIEL BROWN.
