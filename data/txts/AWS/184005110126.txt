JAMEs DIxoN. 1 am a sailor, and belonged to the Phenic. On the
13th of May I was at a public-house near the Tower-r] met the prisoner
and another soldier there named Davis-Davis was in the public-house
before me, and the prisoner came in after me-I had not known either of
them before-y sat down and drank togetherwe remained there about
half an hourI had a watch, a chain, and a key in my waisteoat pocket,

he said I should not, I shonld go in again-I said I would nothe

-we went to the canteen, and found the prisoner--I have no doubt that

A. The North of Scotland-] came here about three weeks ago-my
