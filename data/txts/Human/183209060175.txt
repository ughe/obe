 2180.  JOHN WELSH was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of July , 1 watch, value 2l., the goods of Daniel Goode , from his person.
   DANIEL GOODE. I am a farmer's labourer, and live at Hornsey. I was in Canonbury-fields; I felt tired, and fell asleep - I awoke in a few minutes, and found the waist band of my breeches open, and my watch gone from my fob; it was in the day-time.
 Prisoner. Q. Do you recollect being in my company? A. I was not - he is a perfect stranger to me; I had been up all night at master's business with his cows, as I went along a person represented himself as a sailor in distress, and asked me to give him a quartern of gin, which I did; but whether it was the prisoner I do not know.
   THOMAS HOBBS KING. I am a Policeman. I was with my brother officer in Canonbury fields; we went to relieve two other officers, and as we stood talking together, I saw the prisoner laying on his belly by the prosecutor - knowing the prisoner, I watched and saw him get up and leave, and just as he got by us, he threw away a stick, which belonged to the prosecutor, and ran up the field - I ran and asked the prosecutor if he knew the man who had been laying by his side - he said nobody had been by his side; his trousers were undone - he felt and missed his watch; I ran after the prisoner: my brother officer ran one way and I the other, and in the Lower-road I met him in custody.
   JAMES CANTY. I am a Policeman. King told me of this robbery; I went and saw the prisoner running out of the field; I secured him opposite the Queen's-head, and found the watch on him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that he had found the prosecutor laying on the ground at Islington, and after assisting him, he took him to a public-house, and gave him gin, then desired him to go on with him; on getting into the fields, he gave him his watch to mind, and sent him for a coach, which he was going for when the Policeman stopped him.
   THOMAS HOBBS KING. The prosecutor was in liquor, but knew what he was about; it was church time, and we let him remain at the station - he went to sleep there; the prisoner did not say the watch was given to him, but denied knowing any thing of him.
   CHARLES HAYWARD. I keep the Two Brewers, Lower-road, Islington. I recollect the two men coming to my house on a Sunday morning; after I served them the prosecutor used bad language, which I told him not to do, or I should call the Police - the prisoner said, "Leave him to me, Mr. Hayward, I will take care of him;" the prosecutor paid 6d. for what was drank, and the prisoner took him away - they were about ten minutes in my house.
   DANIEL GOODE. I never gave him my watch to take care of.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2181.  JAMES ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of September , 1 shirt, value 2s.; 2 handkerchiefs, value 6d.; 2 bags, value 1s.; 1 brush, value 3d.; 1 pair of scissors, value 6d.; 1 tea-canister, value 6d., and 1 towel, value 2d., the goods of Thomas Goslin.
 THOMAS GOSLIN. I live in  Philip-street, St. George's. I have known the prisoner some time; he came to me, stating that he was in distress for a lodging; I let him sleep in my room - next morning he asked me to let him have the key to let himself in; I did so - I returned about eight o'clock in the evening, and could not get in; I was going to break the door open, and the key fell from the top of it - I opened it, went in, and missed these articles; all the rest of my things were packed up, ready to be carried away.
   JAMES HARRIS. I am a pawnbroker. I have a shirt, which was pawned by the prisoner.(Shirt produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I only took the shirt, as I had no bread to eat.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Confined Three Months.
 2182.  RICHARD WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of September , 1 sovereign, the money of William Brown.
 WILLIAM BROWN. I am  apprentice to a jeweller. Last Saturday-night I gave the prisoner a sovereign, telling him to get it changed and bring it to me; he is a pot-boy- he went away and never returned; I found him in Covent-garden watch-house on Monday.
 Prisoner's Defence. I met a young man and drank with him; I was tipsy, and made away with the sovereign.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Confined Six Weeks.
