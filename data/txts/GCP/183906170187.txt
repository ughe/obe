Q. When had you first any communication about Solomon? A. The
first communication we had about Solomon was from Messrs. Bult-it
was some time at the latter end of April or the beginning of May, I
am not sure which-it was before he was examined as a witness-I
remember his being examined at the police office-he was first sum-
moned to the police-office to give an account of the property he had
sold to Messrs. Bult-he was examined as a witness and sworn-that
was very early in April, I do not remember the day-I believe it was on
the 6th, it was on a Saturday I know-I think Solomon's house was
searched about a fortnight or three weeks after the examination-it was
not searched more than once to my knowledge - I was not there when
it was searched at any time-Solomon went about his business after he
was first examined-he was taken up, I should say, nearly three weeks
after-it was after that that the first negociation took place about his
being admitted as a witness- I never spoke to Solomon in my life-I
never spoke to my attorney about his being admitted as a witness-I was
not aware that any proposition was made by Solomon--I was not aware
that any offer was made of his being made Queen's evidence-I only knew
it about three weeks or a month ago, I think about a month ago I knew
it before he was examined the second time as a witness—a few days before
-how I recollect it was at the commencement of May, the first three
or four, or five or six days of May-I learnt it from Lea the officer.
Q. Was it then agreed on with your consent that he should be admitted ?
4. I gave no consent about it, I left the matter to the discretion of the
magistrates- I gave no assent or dissent about it-I left it to the magis-
trates and my legal advisers--I heard that he was to deliver up a large
portion of the property which he had got possession of I mean the five
bars of gold which he had they were worth about 24001., we understood
at the time it was near 30001.--I believe his house had been searched
before that by the officers—I do not know that it was arranged that it
should be found in any given part of the house, nothing of the kind was
said to me--I know nothing at all about it, I did not even know in what
part of London it was to be delivered up, nor any thing about it-at the
time Lea communicated to me about Solomon being admitted as Queen's
evidence, he did not say any thing about his house being searched again-
I am sure of that I do not recollect it-Solomon was taken into custody
some time in April, he was examined as a witness the second time-I do not
recollect that he was sworn more than once-he was examined more than
once or twice-he was first summoned and sworn when the first conversa-
tion took place about Messrs. Bult-he was afterwards in custody and
examined as a prisoner, and afterwards he was examined as a witness to the
best of my recollection, that was at the latter end of May, but I am not certain
about dates-it was some time after the conversation between myself and
Lea-I do not recollect that any one was present when Lea mentioned it
to me.
