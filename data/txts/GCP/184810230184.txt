worth 4s. 1d., my husband's goods.

2429. JOHN BROWN, was again indicted for stealing 2 waistcoats, value
68.; the property of John Tighe, since deceased.
JAMES DRY. I lived in the service of John Tighe, of Deptford, twelne
months; he was a pawnbroker. On Saturday night, 21st Oct., about nine
o'clock, I saw a man dressed like the prisoner, in the passage of the box
about eleven o'clock-Butcher produced two waistcoats to me, which wen
Mr. Tighe's ( produced)-I saw them safe at seven.
• Cross-examined by Mr. HORRY. Q. There were a great many people in
the boxes ? A. Yes.
WILLIAM BUTCHER policeman, R 188). I took the prisoner, searched
him, and found on him a pig of lead, and these waistcoats, which he said
bought in Deptford.
Cross-examined. Q. Was he in liquor? A. Yes.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty.-Confined Four Months.
