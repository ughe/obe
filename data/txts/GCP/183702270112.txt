780. GEORGE JENKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of
February, at St. George, I £10 bank-note, the property of Peter Clark.
in the dwelling-house of John Plowman.
PETER CLARK. I am a sailor. The prisoner was a sailor in the same
ship-I lodged at the house of John Plowman for two nights before this
happened-I came to the King William in New Gravel-lane, and fell in
with him there-he told me he had lost his money, and said he had no
place to go to-I said I had some money, and would give him part of it
I treated him at my own lodging, and he slept in the same room-1 had a
pocket-book with a £10 note in it-it was in my inside jacket pocket-
looked at it when I went to bed, and it was safe-- I awoke in the morning
at half-past seven o'clock, and he was getting up-I told him it was too son
-- while he was dressing I fell asleep-I awoke again at nine o'clock, and
my pocket-book and £10 note were gone-I went down, and the prisoner
was gone- I have never seen my note since I have known the prisone
three years-he bore a good character.
GEORGE HUNT (police-constable K 136.) I apprehended the prisoner
at the buuse of his sister in the Liverpool-road-I waited outside, while his
sister went and called him-I asked what he had done with the £10 note
he had taken from Peter Clark-he said, “I have changed it, but I have got
81. left- I am sorry for what I have done"-Plowman's house is in the
parish of St. George.
EDWARD DUNTHORN. I am a linen-draper. The prisoner came to my
shop about ten o'clock in the morning and bought three pairs of stockings,
and gave me this £10 note-I gave him 91. and some silver in change.
Prisoner. It was done in a state of drunkenness.
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor.
Transported for Life.

781. ELIZABETH BAILEY was indicted for stealing, on the lot of
March, at St. Marylebone, 2 cloaks, value Bl., the goods of Letitia Miller
in the dwelling-house of William George.
LETITIA MILLER. I am a widow, and live in Castle-street, in the house
of William George, in the parish of St. Marylebone. I occupy the second
and third floors-on the 1st of March about half past six o'clock I was go
ing out of one room into another, and saw a woman pass me on the landing
of the second floor--1 said, “What do you want?"-she made no answer
I repeated my question several times, and followed her down--we have two
doors, she slammed one against me, and went out-I ran and called her back
--but I fainted away, and do not know any thing more--I had missed two
cloaks from the work-room at the top of the house I had seen them safe,
hanging up, about five o'clock-if finished they would be worth 121. or 186
I should say they were worth 81.
GEORGE DENHAM. I am turncock to the West Middlesex Water-works.
I saw the prosecutrix go up to the prisoner, and catch hold of her, and
something chucked over the area. I went to it, and it turned out to be
cloak.

saw the prisoner turn the corner of the Castle publie-house in Regent
