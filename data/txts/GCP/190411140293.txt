68. OLIVER KINGSLEY (34), PLEADED GUILTY to the manslaughter
of Eliza Jane Williams. He received an excellent character. Six months
hard labour.
59. HENRY PAYS (19) and WILLIAM JONES (24) (who is deaf and
dumb), committing an unnatural crime with each other. PAYS PLEADED
Guilty.
Mr. Elliott and Mr. Fitor Prosecuted,
During the evidence the Court considered that Jones should be tried as
to whether he was fit to plead to the indictment. The Jury were therefore!
discharged from giving a verdict, and were again sworn to try as to
whether the prisoner could plead. On the evidence of Dr. James Scott, the
medical officer at Brixton Prison, they found that the prisoner was not in
a condition to give proper instructions for his defence or to defend himsel
To be detained during His Majesty's pleasure. PAYS, against whom a
former conviction was proved. Three years' penal servitude.
