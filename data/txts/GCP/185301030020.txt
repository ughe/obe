MR. CLARKSON, Q. Let us understand you say you made a purchase

Oct. down to the present time were known to the police--these things that
have been produced are a portion of what I purchased on 9th Dec.-- this is
the mark which I made myself, it is merely the price and date-I also marked
the salacine-I made a mark on all the bottles.

induced me to communicate with my wholesale druggist; it was the repe-
tition of the thing.
