   Cross-examined by MR. PATNE . Q.  Did you send for the prisoner next morning? A.  Yes—her sister came to my house for two bonnets which I had to clean for her, and I asked her to send the prisoner to me, which she did—when she came I sent for a policeman, and she told me in his presence, that she had pawned it—her mother charred for me, and is a very respectable woman—the prisoner had no wages—I took her out of compassion, and provided her with clothes. SAMUEL FROST. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a sheet which was pledged on the 7th of April—this duplicate produced is the one I gave for it. MART DOBAR. I am a searcher—I searched the prisoner at the sta tion, and found this duplicate on her. GUILTY  . Aged 17.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutrix and Jury. — Confined Eight Days.
