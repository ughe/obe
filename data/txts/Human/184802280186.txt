   Cross-examined by MR. PARRY. Q.  Was not Slaughter a considerable distance from Cheshire? A.  Not more than two yards—directly I stopped Cheshire, Slaughter came up, took a piece of lead out, and said, "We are allowed to take the old lead away." GEORGE MARKS. I am foreman of the Iron Steam-boat Company's works, at Nine Elms. The prisoners were in the company's employ—they were repairing a barge, which had old lead in it—I saw it safe a day or two before—I received information that the prisoners had been taken about eight o'clock on the night of 17th Feb.—I examined the barge next morning, and missed the lead—it was similar to this—( produced ) I believe this to be it.
   COURT. Q.  Are the men allowed to take the old lead? A.  No—William Chaplin is one of the proprietors—there are others.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q.  How long had Cheshire been in the company's service? A.  Four or five weeks—his wages were 5 s . a day—I used to pay him—Slaughter was the senior hand, and in my absence he might give orders.
   Cross-examined by MR. PARRY. Q.  Do not the men have perquisites? A.  No; they have choppings of wood—they do not take lead—I have been nine or ten years in the company's service, and Slaughter ten or eleven months—I do not know that any man could take 28 lbs.  of lead by mistake, NOT GUILTY .
 972. JOHN CANE , stealing 9 lbs.  weight of copper, value 6 s  .; the goods of John Steavens and another, his masters. JOHN STEVENS. I have one partner—we are engineers, at Southwark Bridge-road—the prisoner was in our employ occasionally—I missed a quan tity of property from our premises from time to time, but not lately—this pipe, produced by the officer, is gas-fittings for devices for illuminations—it is drilled with holes—here is where the small pieces have been detached—it is ours—other persons make this article, but I can identify it as made on my premises. THOMAS HAY( police-sergeant, M  9.) On 22nd Feb. I went into a marine-store dealer's shop in Montague-street, Southwark—I saw the prisoner and another man there—the other man made off—I saw a quantity of pipe in the scale—I asked the prisoner where he lived—he said in Spitalfields, and he brought the pipe from home—I said, "Could you not find a marine-store dealer's nearer than this?"—he said, "I was coming this way, whether or not"—I took him, and found in his pockets a quantity more pipe—I found on him his employer's card—I went there, and the pipe was identified.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I met a man, who said, "Come with me; I have some copper pipe to sell;" I went with him; the marine-store dealer would not buy it; the man said to me, "Put it in your pocket, and come along;" and just as we were coming out, the officer stopped us; plenty more makers make the same as this.
 (The prisoner received a good character; and a witness engaged to employ him.) GUILTY . Aged 26.— Confined One Month .
