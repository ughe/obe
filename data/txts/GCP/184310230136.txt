2972. DANIEL JACOBS was again indicted for stealing, on the 26th d
July, 1 jacket, value 88.; and 1 quadrant and case, 12s. 6d. ; the goods of
Abraham Raphael.
ABRAHAM RAPHAEL. My wife lent the prisoner this quadrant-he said
he could not go to sea till he learnt his lesson-he gave me this duplicate of
the quadrant, pledged in the name of Barns.
John THOMAS LEECH. I am a pawnbroker. I produce the quadrant, and
have a duplicate corresponding with the one the prosecutor has.
GUILTY.
2973. WILLIAM FIELDER was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
Oct., 36 cigars, value 38. 6d.; and 1 cigar-box, 3d.; the goods of Henry
Osborne Thompson.
HENKY OSBORNE THOMPSON. I am a tobacconist, and live in Upper
Queen-street, Pentonville. On the evening of the 19th of Oct. I was in my
back parlour—the prisoner passed the shop two or three times, then crawled
in on his hands and knees, took this box of cigars from the shop board,
and ran away-I ran after him, and stopped him-he stopped before I took
him, and put the box down at my neighbour's door-I am sure he is the
person.
Prisoner. I was passing; the prosecutor took me, and said, "Where are
those cigars ?" I said, “What? I saw none;" there were three or four other
boys before me. Witness. I saw no others.
Guilty. Aged 23.--Confined Three Months. the n
