EMMA HUGHES. I am the wife of William Hughes; I live at No. 77,
Brook-street, West-square, Lambeth ; Mrs. Howes is my brother's wife.
On 22nd April, she brought a woman to my house in the name of Betsy
Wood—I had seen her before, about twenty years ago, when I was quite a
child-she was then Betsy Wood-I had not seen her within the last year
or two have seen her since, and have been told she is Mrs. Horne-when
Mrs. Howes brought her, she wanted to know if Betsy Wood could remain
with me a little while I had no room, and I took her to the last witness's
house--we all three went together on the following Monday I saw Betsy
Wood leaving Mrs. Reed's house, and the prisoner, Fennell, was with her
they got into & van and went away-I went with them they took with
them a box and a carpet bag—the box was a great deal like this box that is
here I did not see any card on it--I saw Mrs. Howes when these persons
were going-we all walked to the public house at the corner together—then
I went with the van, but Mrs. Howes did not to be
