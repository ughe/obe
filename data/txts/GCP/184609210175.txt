Robert THOMAS BARNES. I am superintendent of the police. I took
the prisoner in Hertfordshire - I told him what I took him for-he said he did
not know such a person as Mr. Davis, I was mistaken in the person-he
repeated that two or three times I asked his name, and he refused to give
it-I said, from the description in the Gazette, I believed him to be the man
--I searched him, and found this 5l. note and a Post-office order on him.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you say one single syllable to him about Mr.
Davis before you apprehended him? A. I did- I told him I took him for
taking these notes from Mr. Davis-he said he did not know him, I was
mistaken in the person-I believe I said that before the Magistrate I have
every reason to believe so-what I said was read over to me--I see it is not
mentioned in this deposition.
Charles Good. I was with Mr. Barnes--I searched the prisoner after he
had searched him-I found this 102. note in his fob-Mr. Barnes told him
he apprehended him on suspicion, a description having appeared in the
"Police Gazette"-he said he was mistaken in the person-we read the
"Gazette" to him-it states it was the property of Mr. Davis-we had seen
the prisoner on the Monday before we took him-he was spending money
very lavishly-we watched the “Gazette" every day, and waited till this
appeared in the “Gazette."
Cross-examined. Q. Did not he say he knew Mr: Davis? 4. He did
afterwards-he was sober on the Monday night, and on the Thursday night
when we took him-I did not see him between those times-he said he would
come before the proper authorities, and account for the notes.
MICHAEL COCKLING. I am a shoemaker, and live in Baldwin's-gardens-
the prisoner bought a pair of boots of my father-he brought a 5l, note-Mr.
Low changed it for him.
William Low. I changed a 51, note for Mr. Cockling-I believe the pri-
soner is the man who was with him-this is the note.
CHARLES BURGESS Gorr police-sergeant 8 L.) I took this 51. note from
the Bank of England to Mr. Low, and he identified it as the one he changed.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
