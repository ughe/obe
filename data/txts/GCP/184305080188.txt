on the 18th of April - I saw the prisoners in company, attempting several
gentlemen's pockets—I saw Mr. Walker and another gentleman walking along
arm in arm-the prisoners followed them close, and put their hands into each of
the gentlemen's pockets-Cockran took up Mr. Walker's pocket, and Goodman
the other-Cockran got a handkerchief out of Mr. Walker's pocket, and put it
behind him for his companion to take it - I was behind- I took hold of him and
the handkerchief-I found two other handkerchiefs on him-Goodman was
quite close to him, and could see what he did.
