ERICK WILLIAM FARR, M.R.C.S. I practise at 175, Kennington

on the back of the head, distinct from the wounds, and caused by a
different instrument—the wounds were triangular in shape, widest al

large quantity of blood—she was in a state of great shock from the
assault, and faint from loss of blood—she was under my care till the
Friday—I anticipated there might be erysipelas ; there was none.
