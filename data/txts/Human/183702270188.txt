 885. THOMAS ELLIOTT was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of February , 1 copper, value 10 s  ., the goods of Thomas Lee , and fixed to a certain building; against the Statute, &c. THOMAS LEE. I keep the Royal Oak, New Road, Woolwich. I saw my copper safe a little before eight o'clock, on the 9th of February, and missed it at twenty minutes past ten o'clock—it was taken out of the brick-work—I have since seen it fitted in the place—it fits exactly—this is it.
   Cross-examined by MR. JONES . Q.  Were you in the habit of using this before? A.  Yes—I have had it three years—I had it taken down and reset—it has never been taken out since I had it reset—there was a thoroughfare through the place where it was, to go out the back way—my customers went in and out that way sometimes—I am quite sure I saw it that same evening—I cannot say how many customers we had that night—perhaps there might be from twelve to twenty-four. CHARLES STEWART WARDEN. I am a constable of Woolwich. I went to the prisoner's lodgings, about half-past eleven o'clock on the night of the 9th of February—I knocked at the door, and called the name of Elliott twice—he opened the door—I told him I was a constable, that a copper and some lead bad been stolen from Mr. Lee's, the Royal Oak, that night, and suspicion had fallen on him, and I should not be satisfied without searching the room—he said I might—I searched, and found this copper under his bed—I asked if it was his—he said, "No"—I took him—the next morning I fitted it to the brickwork—it agreed perfectly, and Mr. Lee identified it—the prisoner said I should have a difficulty to prove that it was the property of Mr. Lee—that was while he was dressing himself in his own apartment.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Was he the only person who lived in the house? A.  I cannot say—it is a small cottage, in some back premises—he was the only person there at that time—two cottages join each other, and there is one room in each—there is a passage between the front houses, down to this little cottage, which stands in the rear—there are not two apartments in the cottage, only one room and one entrance—I found this under the bed on which the prisoner was sitting—he did not seem to be perfectly sober nor perfectly drunk—he was able to walk—he refused to have the handcuffs on, and appeared to be under the influence of liquor.
   Q.  Will you swear he did not say you would have great difficulty in proving he stole it? A.  It was as I stated—he said they would have great difficulty in proving it was Mr. Lee's—I took the copper, and fitted it at Mr. Lee's myself. JAMES DENTON. I am a watchman, living at Woolwich.
