John Ralli (prisoner on oath). My real name is Israel Israel
I have been known by the name of John Ralli from 15 to 20 year
I am a tailor and outfitter and a commission agent in bill discount
ing. If the transactions are risky I get a little more on them.
was introduced to W. H. Cross and was with him for about fort
night at Fulham. When he opened the business in Willesden Gree
I was manager at 35s. a week and 1 per cent. commission on the
turnover. Mr. Maurice Adler was Mr. Cross's successor, and after
wards Mr. Coleman. I acted as manager all through. Afterwards
it changed hands again to Mr. Berry, and I was with him in the
same capacity. I received my salary from those four men. I did
all the work in the shop-138. 6d. a week I paid for rent I have
paid on an average from 10s. 6d. to 13s. 6d. a week for the last
six years. While managing the business at Willesden Green I had
control of the money till the proprietors came for it, when I had
to hand it over. Prior to going to Willesden I was employed by
Braun Brothers, Mr. Hermann Braun, at 30s. per week. That
changed hands to Shepherd, and I got the same salary under him
After that I had no business with Braun. I have no recollection
writing any letter in the name of Ralli. I did ask Goldstein, at the
request of Clifton, to give the latter further time for the payment of
rent. I had no connection with Steward Street. I used to introdne
clients to Clifton for shirts, for which I got a commission. Im
never known in any name but Ralli. I called on Roberts at the
quest of Adler, who was my master. I called on Beckett in respect
of the fixtures on behalf of Paul Berry. I bought the fittings
Willesden Green for Coleman, on monthly terms. I gave him the
reference given to me to give him by Coleman, who was then the
proprietor. It is a deliberate lie when Mr. Gray says that when it
became a question of paying I told him we did not do little people,
but only did big firms. I did not say so. I said, "These are not
little people; you will get your account." That was after I had asked
him to send for a police officer, and if I was doing anything wrong to
give me in charge. I did not benefit in any way by the goods which
have been obtained. The name "Ralli" was given me by J. R. Dale
and Co. when I was working for them in Westbourne Grove fifteen
years ago.
Cross-examined by Mr. Leycester. My connection with Goodman
dates from 1901, when I was director of Robinson and Co., a com
pany promoted by him. I was not in business with him as Wreford
and Co. I was then in business with another form. I do not think
I have been actually Goodman's servant, nor have we been employed
by the same people. He was not in the service of Braun Brothers
He was acting in the next office as the Merca Society, with which I
had nothing to do. I left Braun Brothers when they were ejected,
when the firm ceased to exist. Mr. Braun had sold the business to
