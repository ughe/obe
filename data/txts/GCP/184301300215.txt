John CHARLES WEAVER LEVER, M. D. I reside in Bridgehouse-place,
Southwark. I was acquainted with the late Mr. Richard Constable-
attended him professionally up to the time of his death-be died the last
day of 1840-at that time he lived in West-square, Southwark-I also
knew Joseph Stanley-on the eve of Mr. Constable's death Stanley came to
my house to fetch me to see Mr. Constable; in consequence of what he said,
I accompanied him back to Mr. Constable's house --we got to Mr. Con-
stable's house a few moments after the clock struck seven--the clock was
striking seven as we entered West-square-when I got into the house Mr.
Constable was just dead--I had seen him on the previous day and on that
morning alive, I should think between eleven and twelve o'clock in the
forenoon—I cannot exactly state the time he was then dying--he was
decidedly not capable of retaining a pen in his hand-he was not sensible.se
Court. Q. Was he so far in a dying state as not to be likely to recover
his senses? A. Certainly he was not likely to recover them-i considered
him a dying man from the 28th or 29th of the month.
MR. CLARKSON. Q. What state was be in on the night of the 30th ? A.
Unable to answer questions put to him-I am speaking within bounds when
I say that for thirty-six hours before bis death he was incapable of answering
a question-it was both a physical and mental incapacity-he had inflamma-
tion of the lungs, and the brain was affected as well-there was no defect of
voice-he could speak before his death, but he was more or less delirious
from the 24th of December-for twenty-four hours before his death he de-
cidedly, could not have written bis name, or retain a pen in bis hand I know
his handwriting--I have often seen him sign his name--the signature to
this deed is certainly not his handwriting--I was the attesting witness to
one will which he executed, not the will the probate of which has been
produced-1 afterwards heard of Stanley's death---Mr. Birch told me of it
in consequence of something which I heard I afterwards called at the office
of Hale, Boys, and Austen, and had some conversation with somebody
there.
