2378. WILLIAM WYATT was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of
September, 1 handkerchief, value 18., the goods of Frederick Kenyon, from
his person.
FREDERICK KENYON. I am a linen-draper. Between three and four
o'clock, on the 8th of September, I was in St. Martin's-court-the witness
asked if I had lost anything-I felt in my pocket, and missed my handkerchief
- he told me the prisoner was gone on into St. Martin's-lane-I went and
took him by the collar, and saw it in his left-hand pocket-this now pro-
duced is it.
HENRY NORTON. I saw the prisoner take the handkerchief out of the
prosecutor's pocket, and put it into his own-he turned round, looked into
a shop window, let the prosecutor go on, and then he went on.
Prisoner's Defence. I bad no work; I came down St. Martin's-court; I saw
the handkerchief lying down, took it up, put it into my pocket, and walked to
the bottom of the court; the gentleman caught hold of me, asked me for
it, and before I had time to take it out, he took it out.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Transported for Ten Years.
2379. JAMES LLOYD was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of
August, 1 handkerchief, value 4d., the goods of William Miller Scotchmore,
from his person.
WILLIAM MILLER SCOTCHMORE. I am a clerk. I was in Palace-yard
on the 30th of August, about five o'clock-I felt my handkerchief ab-
stracted from my pocket- I had a pocket-book in the same pocket-1 feb
it going-I turned, and saw the prisoner give the handkerchief to a female,
who had hold of his arm--she put it under her shawl-they walked
little way I went to them, and said, "You have taken my handkerchief
and this female has got it"-they said they had not-I called a policeman
--the female gave me the handkerchief-I gave them into custody.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. What were you doing then! A
I was merely standing, seeing the Peers going down-I had my hand in
my pocket just before, and as the Duke of Wellington passed, I took my
hand out to cheer him-I could not have dropped it.
John RORKE. I am porter to the House of Commons. I was 1
Palace-yard, and saw the prosecutor speak to the prisoner-I turned and
saw the handkerchief in the prisoner's hand-I saw him hand it to be
female.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Six Months.
