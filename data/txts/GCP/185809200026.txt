WILLIAM LIDDON. I am house-surgeon at King's College Hospital,
-the prosecutor was brought there about 12 o'clock on Saturday night-
he was drunk ; his face, chest, and right arm were scalded; his right eye
was very much inflamed and swollen-he remained there about a month-
his right eye is completely destroyed—the other eye is quite right, I think.
