HARRY BENHAM. Mr. Forrestier Walker is dead he was a publican
and horse dealer. I was out driving with a friend of Mr. Walker's
named Martindale, who told me he had heard it reported that Miss
Dare was dead. I knew Miss Dare and her sister very well. They
were not alike. I believe one of them is dead. I do not know for
certain.
Verdict, Guilty.
Previous convictions were proved.
Sentence, 12 months hard labour.
