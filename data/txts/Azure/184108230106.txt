2125. JOHN WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for embezzlement.
JACOB BEISIEGEL. I am a baker, living in York-street—the prisoner
was in my employ. On the 29th of July he took some bread to Mr.
Stevens—if be received any money he ought to have paid it on the evening
of the 30th—he did not pay it.
WILLIAM STEVENS. I am private in the Grenadier Guards. On the
29th of July I received some bread of the prisoner—on the 30th I paid him
58. 3d. for his master.
Prisoner. He paid me 38. 6d. on the 30th. Witness. No, I paid him
5'. 3d. on the 30th, and 38. 6d.on the 31st.
GUILTY. Aged 18.—Confined Three Months.
2126. MARTIIA KEANE was indicted for stealing, on the 12th
August, 1 ale-glass, value 7d., the goods of Henry White.
HENRY W HITE. I keep the Masons' Arms public-house, in Watney•
street. The prisoner and a man came to my house on the 12th of August
—the man left a stick behind him—the prisoner came back for it—she
went into the parlour for it—as soon as she was gone I missed the ale•
glass—I went after her, and she came back with me, took it out of bet
pocket, and put it on the bar.
Prisoner. The man gave me the glass when I took him the stick, and
told me to take it if I liked, as very likely he might break it; he was a
stranger. Witness. She had got to the end of the street.
GUILTY. Aged 40.—Recommended to mercy.— Confined One Month.
