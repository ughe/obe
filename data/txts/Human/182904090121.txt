 947.  THOMAS GOULBOURN and PHILIP NEWMAN were indicted for stealing, on the 8th of April , 7 lbs. of beef, value 3s. 6d., the goods of Thomas Eden Frenholm.
   THOMAS EDEN FRENHOLM. I am a butcher, and live at Stoke Newington. This beef was in my shop on the 8th of April, about ten minutes before six o'clock in the evening- I saw the two prisoners walking backwards and forwards two or three times, and heard one of them say it would not do; I had seen Goulbourn before, lighting the lamps in the road; Goulbourn then came and took the heef - Newman was close by him; I was at the back of the shop; I went and took Goulbourn - Newman made off, but was taken the same evening.
   JAMES CLARK. I am an officer. I was sent for, and took Goulbourn.
   JOSHUA FREDERICK CLEMENTS. I am an officer. I took Newman in about an hour and a half; he made great resistance.
   FRANCIS MOORE. I was in the shop. I saw the two prisoners come up together; Newman drew  the beef to the edge of the window - they then went into a public-house; I then saw one of them come and take the beef - I cannot say which.
 Prisoner NEWMAN. Q. Where did you see me? A. Coming along the pavement, and you  drew  the meat to the edge of the board.
 Prisoner NEWMAN. This prisoner never saw me at all.
 GOULBOURN'S Defence. This man was not with me; I never touched the meat.
 GOULBOURN -    GUILTY. Aged. 33. Confined Three Months.
 NEWMAN -    GUILTY. Aged 24. Transported for Seven Years.
 948.  JULIA McCUEN was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of April , 2 copper pots, value 12s., the goods of Richard Herbert.
   RICHARD HERBERT. I am a boot-closer, and live in Adam and Eve-court. I lost two pots from my back kitchen - the prisoner lodged with me; I had information, gave her in charge, and on the Monday morning I found this pot at Jackson's, in Gee's-court.
   THOMAS JACKSON. I live at No. 9, Gee's court, and deal in marine-stores. I bought this pot of Mr. Dobson, in Berwick-court.
   JONATHAN DOBSON. I am a broker. On Saturday, between two and three o'clock, the prisoner brought this pot, and asked me if I would buy it - I told her to untie it, and asked what she wanted for it; she said 7s. - I said it was too much, which it was - it weighed about 14 lbs., with the iron; I gave her 5s. 6d. for it, and sold it for 6s.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I met a woman, who asked if I would oblige her by selling this pot, and she would allow me 1s.; she said her landlord was seizing, and her children were starving - she told me to ask 7s.; this gentleman said he would give me 5s. 6d. - I came out and told her; she said,
 "Take it."
     GUILTY. Aged 34. Confined Six Weeks.
 949.  THOMAS MANSFIELD was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of April , 2 bit-halters, value 18s., and 1 crupper, value 2s., the goods of Charles Chamberlain.
   CHARLES CHAMBERLAIN. I live at Alridge; I am a carman, and drive hay and straw to town; I lost these two bit-halters and a crupper from my stable in my yard- I left them there on the night of the 9th of April, and missed them the next morning; the door had not been locked.
   SAMUEL JOHNSON. I am a saddler, and live in Turnmill street. The prisoner came and offered two bit-halters for sale, for 4s.; I gave him no answer, and he went away - I informed the constable, who took him.
   JOHN ROBINSON. I am the constable. Johnson told me, and I took the prisoner - he said he had brought up two horses to the Ram inn, in Smithfield, and was going to take them home to Hertfordshire; I said if he would go there I should be satisfied, but he would not; I was forced to call in another officer - we found this crupper on him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I found the harness near Chigwell.
     GUILTY. Aged. 30. Transported for Seven Years.
