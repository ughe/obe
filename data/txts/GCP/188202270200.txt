Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I was a ready-money customer at the

Ro-eramined. I got the receipts at my own house.
EDWARD HAYWARD. I carry on business at Spencer Road, Forest Hill
-the prisonerſcame into my employment at the end of November-he did
general work in the shop, and solicited orders out-doors-money taken at
the counter would go into the till without restriction-money taken out of
doors would be paid in the evening to Mrs. Hayward or me, and a signa-
ture given for it-there is no entry of 168. 9d. on 6th January, or 11. 10..
94d. on 13th January received from Mr. Grub-I did not know Mr.
Grub.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. You were about three months in my
employ– I received a five years' character with you—my gross takings
from 9th to 14th January were 571. 198. 10d.--181. 118. 9d. was in bills
paid--the Saturday's takings were 171. 188., leaving 201. for the other five
days—I said before the Magistrate that my grocery ready-money
averaged 21. 108.-if a person pays the same day no entry is required; it
paid the following day the cash is entered in this book, and credited after
office routine-Hilton left me on 11th February because he was associated
with you—except that I dismissed him I have no part in his non-appear-
ance to-day-I did not know he was to give evidence.
Re-examined. From inquiries I made about the prisoner's character
through Messrs. W. A. Gilby of their agents in Hull, Messrs. Catley
and Percival were in business in a small way for four years, and only
twelve months ago were partners—they kept no assistant—the prisoner's
character purported to be for five years with that firm--Mr. Grub's name
does not appear in my book at all; nor Mr. Dye's—they were secret
transactions.
By the Prisoner. The goods were delivered surreptitiously at meal
times, against the rule of the house--you were in the shop the whole
day once a week-I did not offer to withdraw from the prosecution before
the Magistrate; I said I should be glad if the Magistrate was able to
deal with it, whereupon your Counsel said "Perhaps you would like to
withdraw?" and my answer was "Certainly not."
GEORGE KIDD (Policeman P 403). I took the prisoner into custody at the
Charing Cross Hotel on 27th January—I told him he would be charged
with embezzling sums of money amounting to 41. from his employer, Mr.
Hayward-he said "I paid all the money in."
WILLIAM SAMUEL Dye. I live at 3, Stanley Villas, Forest Hill, and am
a grocer-on a Tuesday in January I had from Mr. Hayward through
Braye a hundred-weight of sugar-he was round soliciting orders-I
afterwards purchased a half hundred-weight of sugar-that came to
11. 178. 60.-my wife paid il., and I paid 178. 6d. to the prisoner.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I paid it at the shop over the counter-
I took no receipt-I considered it a cash transaction-I saw you put it in
the till 10 one else was in the shop-I know Hilton-he has said he
saw Mr. Grub pay moneys, and I understood him to say he had seen
moneys paid into the till that had been received from Mr. Grub.
By the Jury. The day I paid the 178. 6d. was not the day I had the
goods.
