CHARLES BURGESS Gore (police-constable L 83.) I apprehended the
prisoner at Folkestone, and found this watch on him.
Prisoner's Defence. I expected to have witnesses to prove I had the watch
a long time prior to entering Mr. Bayfield's service; Pinkn ey has been shown
the watch before, so as to know the number.
JOHN JAS. PINKNEY re-examined. I had not noticed the number before I lost
it, but I went to Mr. Carter, the chronometer maker, on Cornhill, who cleaned
it for me three years ago, and had it in his hands two or three times, and
he gave me the number from his book-they always take the numbers of the
watches they clean-I have had it seven years, and am quite positive it is
mine-I can recognize it from the dial, and from having it in use so many
years, independent of the number.
GUILTY. Aged 20.

798. GEORGE GIFFORD was indicted for feloniously assaulting
Henry Howard, and stabbing and wounding him on the left side of the
chest, with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm.
MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY HOWARD, corn-porter, Dale's-place, Stoney-lane, Tooley-street.
On Thursday night, the 18th of Jan., between twelve and one o'clock, I had
been taking a drop of porter, but was not the worse for liquor-I met a
female, and went with her to a house in Vine-yard, Tooley-street, kept, I
believe, by the prisoner-we went into the top room-1 took up the poker,
joking-like, and swung it round in my hand-I was talking to the female
when the prisoner came up, and asked me for the money for the room-I re-
fused to give it him just then, and said I would send it down by the young
woman in a minute or two (I had not been in the room three minutes when
the prisoner came in be asked for the money again, and I said again that I
would send it down-I had the poker in my hand, and was swinging it round,
and singing a bit of a song, "The Sprig of Shillalah and Shamrock so Green"
-the prisoner then said, “Oh, is that what you mean?" and left the room,
shutting the door after him-he came up again with a drawn sword, and
said, "Now, you go out of my house" -- he made a cut over my head-I
held up my arm, and happened to catch the flat part of the sword-he then
stabbed me in the breast-I was so irritated I did not exactly see how it was
done--I had on this coat and waistcoat-my breast was cut, and bled very
much-the poker was at that time in the fender-I put it there while he had
gone down for the sword-I did not think he was going to fetch it, or I would
not have put it there--I had nothing in my hand at the time I was stabbed
-I instantly ran down stairs, and found a policeman-I was taken to Guy's
hospital, and had my wound dressed-I remained there till the Monday
morning-I was hardly able to move a limb-I then went to Union-hall-I
was not able to leave the hospital sooner-I had pains all over my body, limbs
and back, and it made me bad all over-I could hardly fetch my breath-I
saw the wound-- it was a kind of stab-it struck right against my breast bone
I expect--I had on this coat-I had made no attempt to strike the prisoner
before he made this blow at me.
