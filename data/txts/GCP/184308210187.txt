9480. JAMES M'GOUVERIN was indicted for stealing on the 16th of
Augs., 1 handkerchief, value 2s.6d.; the goods of Edward William Downes,
from his person.
ROWARD WILLIAM DOWNES. I live in Chenies-street, Tottenham Court
road. About half-past nine o'clock, on the 16th of Aug., I was in Plumtree-
street, St. Giles's-a man passed me, I felt, missed my handkerchief, and
called “Stop thief"- the prisoner was taken, and it was found three or four
yards from him-he had gone over that ground-it was about fifteen yards
from where I missed it-no one was near me but him—this is my handker-
chief.
JOSEPH Hughes (police-constable E 114.) I heard the cry of " Stop
thief"-I stopped the prisoner, and found the bandkerchief in the road.
Prisoner. I was crying “Stop thief." Witness. I saw no one before him.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going up the street ; a gentleman turned, and
cried "Stop thief;" I turned, and saw a lad running into the oyster-shop.
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Nine Months. gnybar en

2482. WILLIAM BENNION was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of
Aug, 1 parasol, value 18.; the goods of James Richardson Cody..
JAMES RICHARDSON Copy. I live in Philpot-terrace, Edgeware-road. On
the 16th of Aug. I was in a field at Paddington, at Father Mathew's teetotal
meeting--this parasol was in my boy's hand-he slipped close to a mob, and
dropped it, and it was gone in a moment I do not know who took it-this
is it, but it has been altered since I lost it-I know it by the handle-the top
has been cut off and made shorter, and the hook has been altered I took it
to pieces myself once, to repair it--I believe it to be mine,
CHARLES TROWSDALE. About half-past five on the afternoon of the 16th
of August the prisoner brought this parasol to sell at my place--the stick was
broken and one of the ribs - I altered it for sale.
John Lure (police-constable 137 D.) I took the prisoner-he said rather
than go to jail he would get the parasol, which he had sold..
Not Guilty.
2483. GEORGE WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of
August, 1 handkerchief, value 28. 6d.; the goods of John Jenkins, from his
person.
JOHN JENKINS. I live in St. John-street-road. On the 11th of August;
about nine o'clock in the evening, I was at the corner of Wilderness-row,
taking some oysters- I felt a pull at my pocket-I turned and saw the
prisoner, and my handkerchief was in his hand, and partly out of my pocket.
I collared him, and then my handkerchief came quite out and fell down-I am
sure no one could have taken it out but him.
