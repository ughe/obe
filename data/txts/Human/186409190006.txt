 836. ELIZABETH EVANS  (27) , Stealing a watch, the property of Robert Robertson , from his person. ROBERT ROBERTSON. I am a sail-maker, living in Orchard-street, Broad-street—on the night of 23 d  August, between 12 and 1, I was in Cheap side—I met the prisoner, she asked me to treat her, and I went with her to where she said there was a public-house—as we were going, she pushed against me—I heard something go click;  put my hand to my waistcoat-pocket, and found my watch was gone—I called out for a policeman—a man came up, and said he would hold the prisoner while I went for a policeman—he saw a policeman's lantern coming, and he ran away, and the woman put the watch back into my hand—this is it ( pro  duced)—it has my initials on the back of it—the bow is broken.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you not promise me half a crown, and would not pay me? A.  No, nothing of the kind. JOHN DIXON( City-policeman , 416). I heard a cry of "Police!" and went up to where the last witness and the prisoner were—he gave the prisoner into custody, and gave me this watch—I found the bow broken.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I met the prosecutor, and he asked me to go down this turning, and he would give me half a crown; he went away without paying me, and then he said he would give me his watch, and I took it to pledge it for the half-crown. GUILTY.— She was further charged with having been before convicted of felony at the Mansion House, on  3 d June , 1863 : to which she
   PLEADED GUILTY .— Confined Twelve Months .
