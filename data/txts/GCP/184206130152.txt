dicted for stealing, on the 19th of May, 9 yards of printed cotton, value
4s. 61.; and 1 pair of stockings, value 9d. ; the goods of James Clover
Tayers.
Éliza Tayers. I am the wife of James Clover Tayers, a draper, in
High-street, Bromley. At half-past five o'clock, on the 19th of May, I
was called by Dykes, and missed this cotton-I saw Brooks about three
yards from my shop-I caught her, and asked if she had come into my sbop,
and taken a gown-piece-she said she did not-I gave her in charge, and
went after Ellis, who had got about twelve yards- I took her back-I
found Dykes had taken it back—it is mine-Brooks said, "I did not take
it"- these stockings were taken from the window.
ELIZABETH DYKES. I am the prosecutor's servant. I went into the
shop, and missed the cotton- I had not seen any one in the shop-Brooks
was brought back by my mistress--before she returned I found this colton
just outside the door-I had seen it safe about ten minutes before--I had
seen the two prisoners and another together, a little while before, sitting on
the steps next door.

ELIZABETH MARWOOD. I am cook to Miss Atkinson, of Russell-square
