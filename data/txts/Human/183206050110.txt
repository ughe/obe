 1691.  RUTH LOVETT was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of June , 1 spoon, value 3s.; 1 pair of sugartongs, value 5s.; 1 gold chain, value 3l., and 1 ring, value 12s., the goods of Ann Cofield.
   ANN COFIELD. I am a widow, and live in Lincoln's Inn-fields; the prisoner was my servant for a fortnight. On the 11th of June I went out to Lambeth, and while I was gone, she stripped my place; I have seen some of my things since.
   BENJAMIN CATMULL. I am an officer. I saw the prisoner in Shoe-lane at a quarter before nine o'clock, on the 11th of June; she had received a very bad cut from a fall, and being in liquor, I was taking her to the watch-house - a tea-spoon dropped from her; I set her down, and saw a bundle in her bosom - I took it out, found an old purse, these silver-tongs, and a gold chain; I then thought I heard something drop, and picked up this gold ring; I found three sovereigns on her person.
   ANN COFIELD. These are mine. I lost a great deal more.
 Prisoner's Defence. I lived with her a fornight, and she came home drunk every night, with a mob about her; she sent me to a woman to tell her fortune - these things were not taken with a design to rob her, but to return them to her before a witness.
     GUILTY. - Confined Twelve Months.

 1693.  GEORGE MONEYLAWS was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of June , 1 handkerchief, value 1s.; 1 towel, value 8d.; 1 basket, value 2s., and 6 lbs. weight of beef, value 4s., the goods of James Ramage ; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
   THOMAS FARRANT(Police-constable C 42). On the 21st of June, at nine o'clock at night, I saw the prisoner in Brewer-street, carrying something in his apron - I followed, and asked what he had got; he said a piece of linen, to make himself an apron - I took him to the station, and found the prosecutor, who claimed the property.
   JAMES RAMAGE. I am a tailor, and live in Little Compton-street. These are my property, and were taken from my kitchen - I do not know the prisoner.
   JAMES RICHARDSON. I am pot-boy at the Duke of Argyle. On the 21st of June I saw the prisoner in the tap-room, with the parcel.
   THOMAS HOBBS(Police-constable C85). I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office - I know he is the man.
 Prisoner's Defence. I bought the things in Clothfair, and intended to make myself an apron.
     GUILTY. Aged 33. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1694.  MARY NICHOLLS was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of July , 1 decanter, value 3s., the goods of John Wrigglesworth.
   ALBERT MARTIN(Police-constable N 72). On the 3rd of July I met the prisoner at the end of Shoreditch - she bad something concealed under her apron; I thought it was something she ought not to have - I pursned, and asked what it was; she said a bottle which had been given to her, but she did not know by whom; I said she must go to the station, and on the way there she said it was given her by her mother, who had borrowed it the day before, to get some wine, as she was ill - I at last found the prosecutor.
   JOHN WRIGGLESWORTH. I am a publican, and live in Shoreditch. The prisoner was in my house that morning, and when she was gone I missed this decanter from my sideboard.
 Prisoner's Defence. It was given me outside the door.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1695.  THOMAS NUNN and THOMAS PRIDHAM were indicted for feloniously receiving, on the 16th of June , 1 pair of trousers, value 2l., the goods of John Lloyd , well knowing them to have been stolen.
   THOMAS ANDERSON. I am in the service of Mr. John Lloyd, a tailor, of Conduit-street. On the 16th of June he gave me a bundle, containing a pair of trousers, to take to Mr. Pontifex. at Holloway - there was a direction on the parcel, but I cannot read or write; Mr. Roberts put it on - I was looking in at a picture-shop in Oxford-road, and the prisoner Nunn spoke to me; I did not know him, but he asked why I did not make haste on with my errand, and if my master was at home; I said No - he said he had lived with Mr. Lloyd for thirteen months, and he had taken three waistcoats and a coat to Holloway - he walked with me as far as Portland-place, and then he left me; he had looked at the direction on the parcel - after he had left me some time, Pridbam came up, and asked if my master was at home; I said No - he then asked where I was going: I told him - he gave me a halfpenny, and told me to make haste there; I went on to Camden-town, and then another person came to me; he asked whether I lived at Mr. Lloyd's; I told him Yes - he then asked if the things I had were for Mr. Pontifex, at Holloway; I said they were - he said he came from there, that Mr. Pontifex was waiting for them, and he wanted to be measured for some more on Monday - he took the parcel, gave me 1d., and when he got some distance form me he told me he would give me 6d. on Monday.
 Cross-examined by MR. CHURCHILL. Q. How far had you got after you spoke to Pridham, before you saw the third person? A. A good way - I did not see what was in the parcel; the housekeeper gave it to me.
