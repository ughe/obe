959. GEORGE CHARLES CUNNEIU was indicted for stealing, on
the 3rd of March, 80 yards of silk lining, value 4s., the goods of John
Cheek.
Anne SHENTON. My husband is shopman to Mr. John Cheek, of the
Strand. I was on the stairs about eight o'clock in the morning, on the
3rd of March, and saw the prisoner come into the shop, put his hand on
the glass case, and take out this silk linings-he put them into his left-
hand pocket-there was no one in the shop-I told my husband when
he came up—he collared the prisoner, and told him he would give him in
charge--the prisoner took the linings out of his pocket himself, and put
them down.
Prisoner. I asked a boy the price of them; he said his master would
be up directly and tell me when the man came I asked him the price,
and he collared me, and said if he could he would send me to Newgate.
Witness. You had not seen my husband; they were in your pocket when
he came; you had ordered 71. worth of canes a few days before you
asked the boy if they were done, and while the boy was gone' you took
these.
EDWARD SHENTON. I am the husband of the last witness, My wife
told me of this-I collared the prisoner, and told him to take these out of
his pocket, and he did so.
Prisoner. Did I not ask you the price, and offer to pay for them?
Witress. No.
GUILTY. Aged 25.-Confined Four Months.
