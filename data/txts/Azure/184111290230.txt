283. JEREMIAH DELAY was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of
November, 521bs. weight of lead, value 8s., the goods of James Easton,
and another ; and CHARLES MAYHEW for feloniously receiving the
same, well knowing it to have been stolen.

Easton and another, engineers, wheelwrights, and lead merchants, in the
Grove, Great Guildford-street, Borough. This piece of lead, now produced,
weighing 521bs., resembles the lead on my master's premises—it has no
particular mark, but it is cast in a particular way—the prisoner Delay
employed as a labouring bricklayer on the premises—he had access
lead—he was desired to remove it outofthewaywheretheywere building.
boiler—I found it at a marine store-shop in Redcross-street—l was after.

that Delay gave it him in the Grove, Great Guildford-street, and thatit vu
handed out at the door of the factory to him.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Did Mayhew say he got it from
Delay ? A. Yes—I have no doubt I told the Magistrate of it—I meant
to do so if I did not.

Cross-examined. Q. Who was with you? A. Mr. Timewell—l askd
