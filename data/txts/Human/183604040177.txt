GEORGE CARTER was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of April , 3 saddles, value 40 s  .; 1 pair of traces, value 5 s  .; 1 pair of hames, value 5 s  .; 1 breeching, value 10 s  .; 1 bridle, value 10 s  .; 1 pair of reins, value 4 s  .; 1 martingale, value 1 s  .; and 1 horse-cloth, value 4 s  .; the goods of Louis Panormo . LOUIS PANORMO. I am a musical-instrument maker, and live in High-street, St. Giles. I lost this property from a stable which I rent in Falcon bery-court—I had seen it safe in the morning of the 4th of April—I was sent for by Mr. Fox, at a quarter before nine o'clock in the evening—I found the padlock had been broken, and the stable was stripped—there were marks of an instrument by which it had been broken—this is my property. WILLIAM FOX. I am a barman. At a quarter before nine o'clock last Monday evening I was in Falconberry-court, and saw a man coming out of the stable with a whip—the prisoner then came out with a bag of harness—I called to him and asked what he had got—he made no answer—I then collared him, and took him to the Three Tuns, where I reside—I sent for the prosecutor, who claimed the property. SAMUEL GODDARD( police-Sergeant F  13.) I took the prisoner and the property.
   Prisoner's Defence . I was quite in distress—I really did not know what to do—I had been out of a situation for some time. GUILTY. Aged 27.— Transported for Seven Years.

MATILDA DE BOILET was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of March , 1 knife, value 2 s  .; 1 fork, value 2 s  .; 2 spoons, value 6 s  .; 1 brooch, value 10 s  .; 1 towel, value 6 d  .; 1 petticoat, value 1 s  .; 1 pair of trowsers, value 18 s  .; and 5 yards of merino, value 10 s  .; the goods of William Jenner . WILLIAM JENNER. I live in Grove-street, Commercial-road. I have known the prisoner about two years—she lived in my house for six or seven weeks, like one of my family—on the 23rd of March, I and my wife went to a sale opposite Whitechapel church—I left the prisoner at home with a young man, who works in the room—I returned about five or six o'clock in the evening—the prisoner was then gone, and did not return at all—I missed a pair of trowsers from a box, and from the table drawer, which had been locked, and of which my wife had the key in her pocket, I missed the other articles.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  How often have you allowed her to pawn things for you? A.  Never—not once—when she came to me, she came as a binder, I am a shoemaker—I got her work, but she was discharged, as she did not to work enough—I work for Mr. Kirby, opposite Whitechapel church—I have no houses—I sold them at Lady-day—I had some in King-street, Commercial-road—that street is full of "accomodation houses"—I kept the houses as such, but I did not know what they were when I paid the deposit-money—I went to the Pavilion one night with my wife and the prisoner—the prisoner pressed me to send my wife home, and as I was rather fresh, the prisoner took me unawares—I do not know of any other time that she pressed me to leave my wife and family—I never had much to do with her since—I never went with her to one of my own houses.
