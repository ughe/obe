1866. JOHN BARRELL was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of
Mach 2 nose-bags, value 38., the goods of John Edgington and another;
and that he had been before convicted of felony.
WILLIAM BOSWELL. I am carman to Mr. John Edgington and an-
other. coal-merchants, at Blackfriars Wharf. On the 12th of March I was
unloading my wagon, in Wentworth-street, Whitechapel-had four nose-
heos banging on the side of the wagon- left it for a short time, and
when I came back I missed two nose-bags-these now produced are them.
and are my master's property.
WILLIAM BROOMCROSS. I am a policeman. On the 12th of March,
about six o'clock in the evening, I met the prisoner in Shoreditch, offering
these nose-bags for sale to a cabman-I took him into custody.
Prisoner. I was tipsy at the time.
George COMARFORD. I am a policeman, I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's former conviction, from Mr. Clark's office-(read)—I was
present at the trial-the prisoner is the person.

1368. ANN COUTTS was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of April,
2 pillows, value 28. ; 1 tea-caddy, value 58.; 2 pictures, framed and
glazed, value 28.; and 2 paintings and frames, value 28. ; the goods of
Francis Joseph Green.
FRANCIS JOSEPH GREEN. I am a carpenter, and live in Berwick-street,
St. James. On Monday, the 4th of April, the prisoner came and took a
furnished room in the name of Coutts, representing herself as a married
woman-a man came as her husband on the Sunday following-she was
there alone on Friday and Saturday, as far as I know-I did not see any
one else-I saw the articles stated safe in the room between seven and
eight o'clock on the Friday evening, when she came on the Thursday
morning following I looked through the window and missed the carpet-
the door was locked, and the prisoner had the key, and also a latch-key
of the street-door-I went into the room on the Thursday morning with
my wife, and missed the articles-I sent for a policeman, and gave the
prisoner in charge-she said she knew nothing about them she afterwards
delivered up three duplicates to the officer, and said she intended to re-
deem them- the things now produced are mine-I have never seen the
caddy or picture-frames since.
JAMES Moody. I am in the service of James Aldous and Son,
pawnbrokers, in Berwick-street. This carpet was pledged at my masters
by the prisoner--the duplicate now produced is what I gave her.
JOHN CARVER. I am shopman to Mr. Benton, a pawnbroker, in Tot-
tenham-court-road. One of these pillows was pledged at my master's, (I
believe by the prisoner,) for 18., in the name of Ann Rogers, lodger, No.
10, Rouse-street--this is the duplicate I gave.
THOMAS MARCHANT. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into
custody-I told her what it was for-she gave me these three dupli-
cates, one for a carpet, and the others for one pillow cach.
