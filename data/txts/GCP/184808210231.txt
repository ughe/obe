his evidence, he found the trunk containing these papers unlocked ; many of

minutes of any particulars produced at any meeting. With respect to the
sympathy we held with the people of Ireland, it is quite clear they were in
i state of disturbance at the time I presided at this meeting: and are we on
this side of the channel to be deprived of the rights of humanity, and be
come amenable to the laws for sympathizing with those on the other side ?
for who among you, if he has not drained his veins of the milk of human
kindness, would not sympathize with an oppressed people, standing on the
threshold of civil war, and unable to say how soon the same horrors might
surround your own dwellings? Does it not make your blood run cold, when
you think that the maladministration of our internal affairs has driven the
people to such a course, leaving them no other choice than open insurrection
or unregretted starvation? But do not imagine that the coercive measures
lately enacted were framed for the purpose of suppressing the spirit of free-
dom that is rising in the bosom of the honest mechanic will be laid aside
when a few convictions have been obtained ; on the contrary, when you have
occasion to complain of ministerial oppression, and I do not think that many
of you will say that the present Cabinet are doing that which is best calculated
to promote contentment and prosperity in the country, that moment will you
Decome subject to the same persecution, and those Acts of Parliament that
have been framed to suppress us, will be unscrupulously used for your
oppression; you may read a lesson of your future fate in the subterfuge
which the present ministry resorted to on 10th April last, when the working
classes of the metropolis attempted to exercise what has never been disputed
to be a constitutional right, the right of meeting and going in procession
with the petition of their grievances to the Legislature. But, though they
were determined to make no concessions, they were aware that there were
ample reasons for complaining, and they felt no hesitation in raking up an
obsolete act,--an act that had been consigned to oblivion for more than a
century and a half, and one that had only been passed to prevent a
debauched monarch from being interrupted in his royal revelries by the con
gratulations of his loyal subjects;- allude to the act of Charles II., which
suppressed a constitutional right possessed by the people at that period :
