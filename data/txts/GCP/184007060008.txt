MR. PARE re-examined. Q. How was the prisoner to be remunerated
for collecting these sums? A. In the shape of extra service, as it says in
the agreement-I should bave treated it as if he had a written order.
Q. Why not give him a written order? 4. I considered the entering
in the book and giving him the account was sufficient authority-in the
first six months he did not receive any money-I am not aware that my
partner knew I was allowing him to receive debts due to the firm without a
written authority-he knew I had given him instructions to receive the
accounts, but I do not suppose he knew whether I had given him a written
authority or not-I was not aware that it was necessary to give him a
written authority to receive it-he never made any objection to it.
MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Do not you know that since the prosecution has
been instituted Mr. Young has given the prisoner the shelter of his
house? A. I do know it.
JURY. Q. Does this book contain the first collection he made for the
firm ? A. Yes I did not on that or any subsequent occasion deliver to
him any other instruction than the book and the bills of account.
