-she has always conducted herself respectably have known her in no
other character my husband is a glove-outter.
Cross-examined. I know Mr. Fox-I knew Mrs. Fox when she was
lodging at Mrs. Bowyer's—I have been there after her for work. I was not
in the habit of visiting her I have been often into her room I did not
know Hermitage at that time I knew no one in the house but Mrs. Fox
and Mrs. Bowyer--my visits at the house were merely for the purpose of
inquiring after Mrs. Fox upon matters of business I was not at all familiar
with her private life and habits-she has worked for mother and me on
several occasions, and we always found her a most trustworthy woman in the
business—I did not know Lester-1 have heard of him-Mrs. Fox conducted
herself respectably in my house--I have met her on several occasions going
to work as a work woman-she appeared to get her living 80-I am quite
certain of it I have met her on several ocasions going to and coming from
her work, always neat and clean.
