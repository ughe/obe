ANTHONY BURNHAM and MARTHA BURNHAM were indicted for stealing, on the   7th of May , 1 bed, value 3 l  .; 1 bolster, value 6 s  .; 2 blankets, value 13 s  .; and 1 sheet, value 6 s  .; the goods of Thomas Seers , the master of Anthony Burnham. DOROTHY SEERS. I am the wife of Thomas Seers, the master of Anthony Burnham. These articles were in a cottage, which was not ours, but the articles were—the servant saw them there safe last November—they are here, and are ours.
   Cross-examined by MR. JONES . Q.  I believe you and your husband kept the Tiger's Head at Lee? A.  Yes—we left It two years ago, and then we removed these things to the cottage, which we hired for the pur pose of putting these things in—we had the key generally, but Anthony Burnham had it occasionally, to go there for different things—he and his wife lived near there—this bolster is my own making, and this bed was made by myself and servants—here is a small hole in this bolster, about sufficient for a pea to pass through—I made the bolster perhaps fourteen or fifteen years ago—I know the ticking of this bed perfectly, and part of my own work is on it—Anthony Burnham has been in our service about twelvemonths. MARY WINCE. I am in the prosecutors, service. I saw the property safe in the cottage about six months ago. JOHN CARPENTER( police-constable R  84.) I went to the prisoner's house, and found Martha Burnham there—it has been rumoured that she is not the wife of Anthony Burnham—I found this bed and bolster there—she said she bought it seven years ago; after that, that it was given her by her mother; and after that, that she bought the tick of a tally man, and the feathers were given her by her mother. WILLIAM KIRBY( police-constable R  22.) I went to the prisoner's house with Carpenter, and saw Martha Burnham attempt to conceal something in a black bag—I took the bag from her, and found in it six duplicates, one of which is for a blanket pawned on the 7th of May, which has been identified by Mrs. Seers—I then went after Anthony Burnham—I asked if he had heard any thing from Lee—he said, "No"—I told him Martha Burnham was taken, and cautioned him as to what he said—he said, "Pretty doirgs these are"—he said he had had the bed some years, that he bought it of a man named Distant, who had lived in Theobald's-road some years ago, but was since dead. EDWARD NASH. I am a pawnbroker. This blanket was pawned by Martha Burnham, on the 7th of May.
   MRS. SEERS . This is my blanket—I am able to swear to it.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How do you know it? A.  By its being like the one I lost—I have no mark on it.
   ANTHONY BURNHAM  — GUILTY  . Aged 40.— Recommended to mercy.
 — Confined Three Months.
   MARTHA BURNHAM  — NOT GUILTY.
