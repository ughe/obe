428. JOHN JOHNSON was indieted for stealing, on the 20th of De-
cember, 1 umbrella, value 48., the goods of John Green.
ELIZABETH PAPWORTH. I am married, and live opposite the prosecutor's,
who keeps an umbrella shop. About eight o'clock at night, on Friday, the
20th of December, I was looking through my window-I saw two men, and
a young man in a butcher's frock, who had an umbrella, looking at it, un..
der the gas, about two doors from the prosecutor's shop the two men
went back to Mr. Green's shop, and the butcher went on to White Lion-
street, with the umbrella up-a man came out of Mr. Green's shop, who
disturbed the two men, who were lurking about the door-I went out and
told Mrs. Green on Sunday the prosecutor sent for me, and said he had lost
an umbrella-I cannot swear that the prisoner is the person I saw with
the umbrella.
