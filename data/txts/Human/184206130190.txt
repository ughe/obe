   MR. CHARNOCK . Q.  When were you applied to come up? A.  When the prisoner was taken up—I have known his wife twenty-two years, and she said to me, "Dear me, father is taken up for selling a mangle, and they have brought the wrong mangle"—I was sitting with his wife when he was taken, and they took me—I had two hours imprisonment—I used to have the mangle to mangle my things, and pay so much a dozen to Mrs. Seat herd—I remember no other mangle there but this—I had not been at the premises before the prisoner came—I am sure there was only one mangle. MR. CHARNOCK called PETER HILLS. I know the mangle that was on Mr. White's premise, in Three Cups-alley—I had known it nine or ten years—I hare pot it up ten or twelve times—it belonged originally to Mr. York Haltom, who is deceased—I went to Dawson's and saw it there, and identified it among many others—I have no doubt of it—I know it, because I repaired those places in it
   Cross-examined. Q.  When did you put it together last? A.  About two years ago—I have not put up many mangles since that—I was fore man to Mr. York Halton—I have put it up in Three Cups-alley, in Kent-street, and Thames-street, City—it had been much used, but was standing by for years—the bed of it was oak—a person who works for Mr. White, asked me if I should know it—I said, yes—I met him and the policeman and went and identified it—when it was put up the last time, it was for Mr. Halton. ANN TOSSETT. I was present on the 28th of April, at seven o'clock in the morning, when the cart was at the door—there were other peoun there—I said to the men who were removing the mangle, that they were removing the mangle belonging to Mr. White of Newington-causeway—I followed the cart to Mr. Dawson's, and told them the same as I did at the house, that they were taking away stolen property.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Do you live in Three Cups-alley? A.  Yes—I pay no  rent—I have been there two years, and have paid no rent during that time—I was at my own door when I spoke to the men—I cannot tell whether they gave any answer to me—they paid no attention to what I said.
