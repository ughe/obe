172. FANNY JACOBS (44), and MICHAEL DE HOZIZIER (37),
Stealing 21 yards of satin, and 12 yards of silk, the property of Robert
Robinson, and another, in their dwelling-house. Second Count, receiving
the same.
JACOBS-PLEADED GUILTY.
The prisoner being a foreigner had the evidence interpreted.

another woman, and a man, came to our warehouse, and asked to look at
some velvets I rang the bell for some one to come and attend to them-
