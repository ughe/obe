634. JOHN MATHER, stealing 4lbs. weight of tea, Value 168.; the
goods of Francis Thomas Clarke and another, his masters,
Messrs. CLARKSON and ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS TUCKER. I am porter, in the service of Messrs. Clarke and
Rowe. The prisoner was their warehouseman-on the 15th Jan., he came
and asked me if some tea that was there was Congou tea of the ship Sarah
Muir-he took a large scoop full of it, went to the counter, and made a parcel
of4lbs. with it, then came into the warehouse and put it into his bag-in twenty
minutes after. I saw a man come into the warehouse, the prisoner gave the
parcel to him, and he took it away-I looked in the book that evening, and
that tea was not entered--the book is here.
Cross-examined by MR. MELLOR. Q. What were the prisoner's duties?
4. He was only a warehouseman-he had not to sell tea, nor the weighing of
it-be bad bought a chest of tea on his own account, some time previous-he
was not in the habit of taking samples of tea-he had not procured orders, or
extended the number of our customers-he had not benefited the firm, that
I am aware of-there was only myself present when he took the tea-Beckett
