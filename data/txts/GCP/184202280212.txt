WILLIAM JOHN LLOYD. I am the prisoner's brother--he lives with
his mother, at No. 6, William-place, Prospect-row, Walworth-road-
has been latety engaged in transacting business for Mr. Staples, an ul-
holsterer at Chatbam, making purchases for him, and sending them down
by van--I remember Thursday the 17th of February, the day before my
brother was apprehended-I got to my mother's house that night about
half past nine or a quarter to ten--my mother was dangerously ill at that
time-my brother was then gone in search of a medical man, and returned
in five minutes after I arrived-Mr. Prior, the doctor came in less than
five minutes after my brother returned-my brother let him in- I was in
the passage--my wife was with me, I live at No. 2, North-street, Eust-
street, Walworth--I had occasion to go home, and told my wife 80-1
looked at my watch then, and it was half-past ten-I had left my house on
the latch, and no person in it-I went out of the door about a quarter to
eleven-at that time I believe my watch to have been ten minutes too fast,
but I did not know that till Saturday-when I went out of the house i
wanted less than a quarter by my watch-I then walked up and dom
the place for about ten minutes with my brother-he and I came out o
the house together, and we had some conversation while I was waiting
for my wife-I think it must have been eleven o'clock when I parted with
my brother--within five minutes, either way-I parted with him at the
corner of William's-place, which is about three-quarters of a mile from the
King's Arms--my brother wore very light drab trowsers that day!
never knew any charge made against him in my life-when he left
me he turned to the right-there is a beer-shop at the corner, and be
turned, I believe, to go there--that is away from the King's Arms-I be
lieve he turned into the house--he said he had some instructions from
Chatham-we walked about 100 feet from my mother's house.

I remember that Thursday--the prisoner was at home all day, till be went
for the doctor in the evening, which was, as near as I can guess, between
nine and t'en o'clock-he returned in about a quarter-of-an-hour, and had
been unable to find the one he went for-he then went for another, and
was gone about ten minutes-he then returned, and did not leave tk

ously ill-we were in very great fear of her till the doctor came--the pri
soner was very much hurt to see her so-he appeared distressed, and eren
