(Defence.)
JORN CHASEY (prisoner, on oath). In 1905 I was living with my
father and mother at 18, Francis Street, Tidal Basin. Prior to that
I had been working with 8 costermonger selling rabbits. I was not
in regular employment. When I was employed I made about 5s.
a day. That Was not enough to keep me, and I wanted to get away
to sea. I had been trying for five or six weeks before this time
to get a ship. I went to the shipping ofhiees and went about the
dooks trying to jump a ship. That means that when a man who his
signed on does not turn up you get a change to take his place. You
can get on without signing If the man who has signed is not there
at the time the ship is leaving anyone with a character can go, In
moving about in that way I got to know Maxwell, I had not known
him before. On this parwcular day in November, when with Man
well, I met prosecutor. He asked Maxwell to have a drink, and I
being his friend went too. We went to the Peacock," then Brown
invited us to go on the tram to the Swan." When I got on the tram I
saw some other men I knew, and I thought I would go with those I
knew best. We went to a publichouse called the Albbey Arms.' I had
been drinking mild and bitter and old and mild. but there I had
whisky. I went outside because they refused to serve me any more;
the other men took me home, and I did not know any more till the
next morning, when I found myself in bed. I did not go into the
Swan." Maxwell went with Brown and the others. I stopped at
the Peacoek about half an hour or an hour. I think we had four
or five drinks. When I got utp next morning I went out to look for
a ship. When I went home again my mother said, My son, there
has been a deteetive here for you." I said, What for She said.
He wants you there has been a robbery with violenoe." I said,
Well, mother, I am not going away for what other people have
done." The next day I went to Tilbury. I went from there to New
York till the boat got back. Then on Jannary 11 I went away and
came back on the same ship in 1907, a 12 months' voyage, and I
have been sailing out of Tilbury ever since I Was away one
month on the first voyage. Then I was in England 11
days and then went away for a year. I went round Cape Horn
to San Franeiseo. I Was there about the time of the earthquake.
