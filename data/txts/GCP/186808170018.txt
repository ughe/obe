PATRICK MAHONY, I am one of the Irish Constabulary-I went on board
the City of Cork steamer, at Queenstown—I there found the prisoner and
Prince - I told the prisoner I had a description of her, and had a telegram
to arrest her and Prince on a charge of forgery- I told her she was charged
with forging on the bank for the amount of 6001., giving the number of the
notes and all she refused giving up the money there until she came ashore
-she said she could not allow herself to be searched on board ship-after
coming ashore I searched Prince and her she handed me over 6001. in
1002. notes--it was in this bag, which she handed to me from inside her
small-clothes- I opened it in her presence and found the six 1001. notes-
she also handed me her passage ticket—there were also two 101. notes, and
fifteen 5l. notes- I made a copy of the numbers of the 1001. notes, and have
them here; they are 64,496, 72,072, 62,904, 72,798, 72,074, and 72,800–I
