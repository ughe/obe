 2006. MARY FRANCIS was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of July , 1 handkerchief, value 4 s  ., the goods of Joseph Stockes , from the person of Richard Searle Stokes .
   MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution NANCY STOKES. I am the wife of Joseph Stokes, and live in Belvidere-place, Southwark. On the 6th of July, about half-past three o'clock, I sent out Mary Jones with my little boy who is five months old—the child had a silk handkerchief on—I found it at the work-house, about half-past eight o'clock—my husband had gone to the work-house before me—the child then had no handkerchief on—this is the handkerchief that was on it child when sent out.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS Q.  What day in July was this? A.  On Wednesday, on the 7th of July, to best of my recollection. MARY ANN JONES. I received the child that Wednesday in July, near about half-past five o'clock—I went to Southwark-bridge-road—the pri soner came up to me—she hallooed after me, "Little girl"—I turned round—she said, "I wish you would go of an errand for me"—I said I could not, I was going home—she said, "You are going past the door, won't you take this ring to my husband?"—I again refused—the child was crying—I had it across my arms—the prisoner came to me, reached over my shoulder, and snatched the child from me—I was very much alarmed, and went into a tobacconist's shop, and told the people—the child had this handkerchief on.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What hour in the day was this? A.  About half-past five, to the best of my knowledge, when she came up—I cannot speak to her clothes, but she had a green stuff—I never stated she had on a straw bonnet tied with brown ribbon, nor a green gown with boots but toned inside, only a green gown—when I came home I told Mrs. Stokes so—I only looked at her face—she had a white bonnet on—it was straw—it was white—I did not say it was straw—I cannot tell what kind of boots—I never said she had boots—I cannot tell what shawl she had on—I never attempted to describe it to any one—I never said she had a red shawl—I did not tell my mistress what kind of bonnet she had on—I do not recollect that I told you she had a straw bonnet.
   MR. DOANE . Q.  To the best of your belief, is the prisoner the woman who took the child? A.  Yes—I described her to Cowlin, the officer as fresh-coloured and red hair, and her hair braided and a green gown. MARY FITZPATRICK.
