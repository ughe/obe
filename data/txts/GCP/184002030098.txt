680. JOSEPH WILKINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
January, 1 quart of beans, value 4d., and 2 pieces of iron called keys,
value 1s. 6d., the goods of John Benbow, his master.
S
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
-John BENBOW. I am proprietor of Cowley Hall-mills, in the parish of
Hillingdon, and reside there. Previous to the 18th of January, the pri-
soner had been one of my grooms-I had another groom-in consequence
of some information, I caused Biggs, an officer, to be stationed to watch
the prisoner's movements-after the officer had made a discovery, some
corn was shown to me, some beans, and some iron-they are my pro-
perty.

Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. Were your beans a mixed sort? 4.
Norwhen these were shown to me they were they were a parcel I had
selected some months ago of a particular sort, the best I could get for
my own horses-these were mixed with oats-I know the prisoner was in
the employ of the Hon. Mr. Tollemache.
SMS
MR. CLARKSON. Q. Were yours Tartary oats? 4. Yes—they were
mixed in my bin, and when they were found they were mixed, and the
oats found were Tartary oats.
