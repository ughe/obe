I live at 149, Pemberton Street, and am a carpet-beater—on 19th June, about 5.45 a.m, I was going down Clayton Street, and saw Marshall come out of Mr. Archer's house—the prisoner ran after him from my master's (Mr. Williams's) stable door, which is about 10 yards from the house—they went on together about 200 yards—I went up to Marshall and said "What do you do in that house?"—he said "Nothing"—I took him back, and the prisoner said "You bleeding long s—; if you don't let him go we will kill you"—I said nothing—I could not get near the prisoner—he went away, and I took Marshall to Archer's house—I have known the prisoner 10 years, and cannot be mistaken in him—he lives in Stroud Vale, Maiden Lane.
