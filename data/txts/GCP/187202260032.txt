259. EDWARD MANEY was again indicted for a robbery on James
Watson, and stealing from his person 81., his money.
MR. MOODY conducted the Prosecution; and MR. MEAD the Defence.
JAMES WATSON. I live at Stephen's Road, South Bow on the 6th of
January I had a horse and van to sell, and was going to Islington-I met
the prisoner and gave him a ride, and Hill and Lowe went with us—I sold
the horse and van for 81. which, I believe, the prisoner saw me receive--
he said that I had promised him 58. which was false, but I gave him a half-
crown--and I thought he would go away, but he did not-he said he wanted
a half-crown for the two companions, fifteenpence a-piece-and he struck
me in the mouth and knocked me down, and two others who I have not
seen since fell on top of me--my money was loose in my right hand
trowsers' pocket--the prisoner kicked me in the back and went away,
Cross-examined. I could not run after him because he kicked me in the
back-I knew that my money was lost because my pockets were turned in-
side out I felt them take it when I was down I saw nobody about
there were two or three people at the Roman Arms—I paid for a pint of
half-and-half hardly five minutes before I was knocked down.
THOMAS HILL. I am a cooper— I went with Watson to sell his horse and
van at the Cattle Market, and was present when the horse was sold-the
prisoner asked him for 58., but he would not give it, and the prisoner struck
him on his nose and made his nose bleed - 1 said, “Maney, if you were to
do the same thing to me I would lay hold of you and break you in half,"
and I went away and did not see him till he robbed me.
WILLIAM CHAPMAN (Detective Officer K). I took the prisoner -- but at
that time I knew nothing of the charge--the prosecutor was sent for and
picked him out from six or seven or others.
GUILTY* -Five Years Penal Servitude.
