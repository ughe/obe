WILLIAM HOLLOWAY (Police-inspector H). On Thursday, 14th November
I took Gillett-in custody about half-past 6 or 7 in the evening, in the
Whitechapel-road-I knew him before he keeps a little broker's shop in
Church-street, Milo-end New-town-I said, “Your name is Gillett, I believe",
he said, "Yes, Sir"- I was in plain clothes I told him I was an in-
actor of police belonging to the H division, and my name was Holloway
that he must consider himself in custody for receiving ten bales of stolen
silk about the middle of March last- he sid, “I do not know anything
abont it"-I said, "You must go with me to the police station "--when
