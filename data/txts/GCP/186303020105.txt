JANE Yates. I am the wife of John Yates, who carried on business as a
boot and shoemaker, at 86, Wheler-street, Spitalfields; we afterwards re-
moved to No. 24, in the same street, of which Mr. Dear is landlord I know
the prisoner-the first time I ever went to his house my husband sent me
to change some boots for different sizes; that was just before the beginning
of August-I afterwards weut with my husband to buy some; that was in
August, it was in Bishopsgate-street-my husband purchased nine pairs of
women's and children's boots of the prisoner; he paid 4s. 3d. for the women's
and 2. 5d, and 28. 9d. for the children's-I am a boot-binder—there was no
shop at this place ; it was a second floor front-room-I went there again with
my husband in the same month; he then bought twelve pairs of boots ;
thirteen pairs were given in a mistake—the prisoner took them from under
the bedstead, hid in a basket; they were side-springs women and children's

cate, and my husband gave her back one pair the prisoner came to our
