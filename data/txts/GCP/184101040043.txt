Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. I dare say you thought that

Q. Have you ever said to any one whether you thought Scalp guilty or
innocent ? A. I should think he was innocent-I have said so-1 was at
the Thames Police when they brought Scalp up on a charge of smuggling
this-I saw Mr. Eyres, his master, on the day he was bailed-(the wagon
and horse had been seized for smuggling)—I was coming in the evening
by where Mr. Eyres was, in Thames-street-I knew him before I do not
believe I said to him, “You have got a van and horse seized," but I will
not swear I did not-I cannot recollect whether he said, yes, he had-I
might have said, "I suppose you want to get it back again as soon as you
can"-I do not recollect it--I said to him, “I can tell you who put the
brandy into your van"-he said, "Can you? I shall be very much obliged
to you"-I said, “You know them both”—be said, “Do I, who is it?"
I said, "One is Peter Gann, who was in your employ, and the other is
Barber, which is a nick-name for Dalton"--Mr. Eyres then said, “ Has
my man Scalp any thing to do with it?"
