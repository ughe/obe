WILLIAM MARSHALL. I live in Trafalgar-road, Greenwich, and am a
wheelwright and smith-un the morning of 22d June last, I had the care
of some fields near my residence-these fields adjoin a by-road which leads
to Woodland-terrace - I saw three persons in the fields on this morning,
about half past 5 or twenty minutes to 6; Archer was one of them-I don't
think they had coats on; I can't say exactly-I waited at the gate of the
field till they came up—I asked them what business they had in the field,
and they said they had been nesting- I positively identify Archer--there
was no public thoroughfare through the field; it was a by-way-there was
no proper way-they could come that way direct from Woodland-terrace
by trespassing.
Archer.Q. What day was it. A. The 22d, Wednesday-I don't identify
you exactly by your blue Guernsey ; I have seen your face in Greenwich-I
believe Thomas said you had been nesting.
JOHN WILLIAM CROUCH (Policeman, 118 R). I have been investigating
this case-on Wednesday week last I received some information of the
burglary, and on Thursday I weat to the gaol, and there saw the three
prisoners-suspecting them to be the men I went on the following morning
to the gaol ; Reddick accompanied me I told the prisoners the charge, and
they said nothing I did not know either of the prisoners before.
