   Cross-examined. Q.  Are you able to identify these stockings? A.  Yes—I have seen them in my drawer before—I know them by this little blue mark on the top—I had noticed that on them previously—they are my mother's—they have only been washed once—I do not know that this is a common mark—it was on them when they were bought—this watch is not mine—I lost a watch, seal, and key—when I first saw this piece of leather at Liverpool it appeared then to have the impressions of spoons having been in it—it was used to wrap the spoons in—I know it by the shape—it is not exactly a square—I told the officer before I saw it that there was a piece of leather which contained the spoons—I have seen it for some yean, and had it in my hand—there is no particular mark by which I know it—I have no mark on these braces—I know them by the size, and the exact position in which the buckles are—they are exactly the; length, I wore them with the pair of trowsers to which they were attached when taken away—I can swear to them—this knife I found in my room—it had no business there—it belongs to my mother—this piece of paper, which I found in the prisoner's room, was taken out of the drawer where I had left it locked up, on the first-floor—the spoons were in that paper when it was lost, and were also enclosed in these pieces of leather—I do not claim this diamond—I left my house a little after six o'clock, and re turned at a quarter to ten—the prisoner was then gone, and all the things—I gave information immediately—the prisoner was not indebted to me for rent or anything—he paid weekly—he had paid on the Saturday, and this occurred the next day—I went up into his room, and found the few things in his room gone—he had no watch of his own, and scarcely any clothes, merely a bundle—I have not found any of my property but this now produced. PHOEBE ROBINSON. These stockings are mine—I left the house on the 8th of May, about ten minutes before six o'clock—my son was last in the house—I missed two shawls, four handkerchiefs, and a variety of other things, which I have not found—I can swear to this mark on the stock ings—it is wove in—I have not a doubt of them—I went to my drawer for them, and they were gone—they have only been washed once.
