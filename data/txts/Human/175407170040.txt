 BRACHYGRAPHY: OR, SHORT-WRITING, Made easy to the meanest Capacity.
 Wherein the persons, moods, and tenses, are comprized in such a manner, that little more than the knowledge of the alphabet is required to the writing hundreds of sentences, in less time than spoken.
 The whole is founded on so just a plan, that it is wrote with greater expedition than any yet invented, and likewise may be read with the greatest ease.
 Improved after upwards of thirty years practice and experience, and is of itself a sufficient Instructor.
