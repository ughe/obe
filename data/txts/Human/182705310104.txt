 1278.  BENJAMIN RAY was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of May , 3 pewter pots, value 2s. 8d., the goods of Thomas Meecham.
 THOMAS MEECHAM. I  keep the Duke of Cumberland public-house, Red Lion-street, Spitalfields; the prisoner was brought in on the 10th of May, with a pint pot under his coat; I fetched a constable, who found two quart pots on him.
   JOHN FITZGERALD. On the 10th of May I was at my house, No. 3, Red Lion-street; I saw the prisoner go up the steps of No. 2, and take some pots from the window cill; he had sweep's clothes on; I secured him, and took him to Meecham's; he said he was going to take them to the public-house, but he was going the wrong way - he dropped one.
   THOMAS HART. I am a constable, and took him in charge.
   THOMAS GOODLAD. I am a baker. I saw the prisoner and witness going along; I saw the prisoner take one pot from under his clothes, and put it on a potatoe sack; I took it to the watch-house.
   ELIZABETH PRATT. I live in Red Lion-street. On the 10th of May I put some pots out of the window.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.
 1279.  THOMAS LAWSON was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of April , 3 pewter pots, value 3s., the goods of Richard Taylor ; 1 pewter pot, value 1s., the goods of Edward Smith ; and 2 pewter pots, value 2s., the goods of John Alford.
 RICHARD TAYLOR. I  keep the Earl St. Vincent, public-house, Torrington-street. On the 9th of April Green brought three pots of mine in. I know nothing of the prisoner.
   JAMES RUSSELL. I am servant to John Alford of the Calthorpe Arms, public-house, Gray's-inn-road - these pots were put out at our door. - I missed two about half-past ten o'clock. Green afterwards brought them to the house.
   JOHN GREEN. I am a beadle. I stopped the prisoner in Russell-square - I had seen him loitering about; he went into Torrington-street three times - I went up and found these pots on him.
 EDWARD SMITH. I  keep a public-house in Torrington-street. This is my pot.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that he had found the pots in Bloomsbury-square, and representing himself to be in great distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 46. Confined Two Months.
 1280.  SARAH JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of May , 12 books, value 4l., the goods of Lewis Duval, Esq.
   WILLIAM ANGERS. I am shopman to a pawnbroker in Liquorpond-street. On the 17th of May the prisoner pawned this book. On the 25th she brought this other, which is Rowe's Cases of Bankruptcy - when she was gone I saw the erasure of a name in it - and when she came again I stopped her.
   JAMES KING. I am a pawnbroker. I have some books which were pawned with me in March last, at different times; I believe by the prisoner - but I cannot swear to her.
   WILLIAM CREE. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Gray's-inn-lane; I have some books, pawned by the prisoner.
   ELIZABETH PARK. I am laundress at Mr. Duval's chambers, Lincoln's-inn. I employed the prisoner there under me - I gave her a few half-pence now and then.
   JAMES EDWARD WATERHOUSE. I am clerk to Mr. Lewis Duval, in Lincoln's-inn- those books are his, and were in his chambers.
   WILLIAM LEE. I am an officer. I took up the prisoner.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in great distress, and this lady employed me.
 GUILTY. Aged 26. Recommended to Mercy by the Jury. - Confined One Month.
 1281.  HENRY HOLMES was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of April , 2 live tame pigeons, price 3s. 6d., the goods of Roger McDonald.
 ROGER M'DONALD. I  keep  the Harrow, public-house, at Poplar. On the 16th of April I went into the tap-room. Fordham pointed out Holmes to me - he then opened his coat and the pigeons dropped down - they were taken from the house in my yard.
 Cross-examined by MR. QUIN. Q. Was any body else in the tap-room? A. Yes; I swear they are mine.
   WILLIAM FORDHAM. On the 16th of April, in the evening, I was going in at McDonald's back gate - and saw the prisoner on a ladder, reaching the pigeons out of their house. I went down to the stable, and when I returned he was on the ground - I asked what he did there, he then went into the tap-room - I went and told McDonald - it was dark. I was going to feed the horse.
 Cross-examined. Q. Was he sober? A. Yes.
 GUILTY. Aged 18. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Nine Days.
 1282.  JOHN GEORGE was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of May , 2 handkerchiefs, value 5s., the goods of John Turney ; and 1 pair of shoes, value 5s. 6d., the goods of Patrick Turney.
   MARIA TURNEY. I am the wife of John Turney, and live at Hackney; my brother Patrick missed his shoes out of a box, and I missed two handkerchiefs.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   JOHN GODDARD. I am an officer. On the 14th of May I took the prisoner in Essex, and found these shoes on his feet - he took me to a house and produced the handkerchiefs.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined Three Months.
