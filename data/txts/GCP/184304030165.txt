1338. JANE DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of March,
2 half-crowns, 4 halfpence, and 1 farthing, the monies of William Ashley,
from his person.
CON
WILLIAM ASHLEY. I am servant to Sir Robert Cumming, of Curzon-
street. About twenty minutes past twelve o'clock, on the 31st of March, I
was in Jermyn-street with two men-the prisoner came up to me, caught me
round the waist, and turned me round two or three times I felt her hand in
my pocket where I had the money stated-I missed it, and charged her with
taking it-there was no policeman-I led her to St. James's church-she got
abusive, and would not go till the policeman came.
THOMAS CHAPEL (police-constable C 140.) I took the prisoner-she put
her band into her bosom, and said, “That is the money I bad of him," pro-
ducing it-she said, "That is the money he gave me" the prosecutor was
sober-he said he had lost two half-crowns, four halfpence, and she had a
farthing.
Prisoner. I was very much in liquor. Witness. She had been drinking.
Guilty, Aged 38.-Confined Three Months.
