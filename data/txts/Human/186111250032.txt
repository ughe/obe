MR. METCALFE . Q.  When did you make that note? A.  Next day when the book was brought to me to sign, not when the prisoner brought the money.
   MR. SERJEANT BALLANTINE . Q.  Do you recollect the prisoner bringing the book the following day? A.  No, he did not come, and I then made a memorandum—I am able to say on looking at that memorandum how much money the prisoner brought on the preceding day.
   COURT . Q.  Did you at that time remember the exact amount he had brought? A.  I referred to my books; finding a deficiency of 100 l . by referring to my books and to the docket, I could tell the amount he brought to me—I had no other means—I presume the books are at the bank; they are not in my custody—I did not make an entry in the pass book of the money I received—that was done by another party.
   The Court was of opinion that in the absence of the books, there was no case to go to the Jury. NOT GUILTY.
