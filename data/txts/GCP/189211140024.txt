JOHN GIDSEY (Police Sergeant V 2). On the early morning of 2nd
October, I went to 10, Charlotte Street, and there found the last witness
in a room with his throat cut-I assisted to take him to the Middlesex
Hospital - the prisoner had been to the station, and it was from what
she said that I went to the room.
MARSH WEST. I am house surgeon at the Middlesex Hospital-I
was there on the morning of 2nd October, about half-past six, when the
prosecutor was brought there by Sergeant Gidney-he was suffering from
an incised wound in the right side of the neck, about four inches in
length-none of the large vessels were severed it was not a dangerous
wound-he remained under my care for six days and then left, and I
saw him no more.
