 561. ANNIE REEVE  , alias RICKABY (24), was indicted for feloniously marrying Robert Mills , her husband, Charles Julian Reeve , being alive.
   MR. SERJEANT BALLANTINE , with MR. POLAND conducted the Prosecution ; MR. RIBTON , the Defence . MATTHEW A. PIERPOINT. I am a clergyman of the Church of England—in June, 1863, I resided at Cardiff, Glamorganshire—on 2nd June, 1863, I performed the marriage ceremony between two persons—this ( produced ) is a copy from the marriage register of the parish church; the ceremony was performed by me—( This certified the marriage of Charles Julian Reeve and Annie Rickaby, bachelor and spinster, of full age, on  2 nd June , 1863, the bride's father being described as "Robert Rickaby, merchant )—the prisoner is the lady that I then married—I have seen the husband—as Julian Reeve, since the marriage, on 24th April, this year, at Exeter—I had seen the prisoner and Mr. Reeve at my house, before the day of the marriage, about making the arrangements—they called together, and made arrange ments for the marriage on the following morning.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you know Mr. Reeves before? A.  No, he was a perfect stranger—I don't know whether he lived in the neighbourhood—I never saw him afterwards till 24th April, this year, at Exeter—I can't say for how long I saw him—perhaps an hour or two.
   MR. SERJEANT BALLANTINE . Q.  Had you any conversation with him? A.  Nothing more than might happen in an ordinary way of meeting a person—I had conversation with him—I have not the least doubt about his being the person—I can't say his age; he had neither whiskers or beard; he might be about thirty, but I can't say exactly.
