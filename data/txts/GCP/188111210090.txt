RICHARD LEGDON (Policeman N 361). I was on duty about 3 p.m., on
9th November, at the corner of the Loughborough and Brixton Roads-
the prosecutor complained to me of a robbery and pointed out the
prisoner, who was standing in the Brixton Road looking towards
Robsart Street--the prisoner could see the prosecutor and me following
- he walked from one side of the road to the other he then walked
quickly through Robsart Street into Clark's Road, into Ingleton Street,
and into the Brixton Road again-then he began to run-I jumped on
& tram-he ran about 200 yards—I got off the tram and told the
prisoner he would have to go back to the pawnbroker's, who said he
had been robbed, and I said " He said you have been there to look at a
clock”--the prisoner said "I went to look at a cloek, but as to the
robbery I know nothing about it "-he was taken to the station and
charged-he said nothing-I searched him I found nothing on him-
the cash tray was found in a garden the next morning.
