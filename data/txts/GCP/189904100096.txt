By Dr. O. F. OGILVIE. I am a retired lieutenant-colonel in
the Indian Army, a medical officer in the service with the rank
of lieutenant-in 1896 I met Mr. Goodman in the office of Mr.
Baslewood, the broker-I cannot recollect the month-I afterwards
called on him at his place of business at Ludgate Circus, and in the curse
of conversation he mentioned to me this West Australian Gold District
Trading Syndicate, and drove me to the offices of the company in Basing-
hall Street, and introduced me to Mr. Bernard, the secretary-to the
best of my recollection Mr. Goodman said that the company was doing &
good business, and advised me to buy some shares in the syndicate-I
heard Mr. Goodman make & speech at the statutory meeting that was
held on June 18th-I do not recollect his saying that they had already
shipped £60,000 worth of goods; I think he said that they had made
profits equal to more than twice the amouut of their capital—Sir Edward
Lee was there, and I heard hlm make a speech-I purchased in the
syndicate 250 ordinary shares and 15 founders' shares from a Mr. Ford,
I believe, before the meeting; I think that transaction took place &
day, or a few days, after Mr. Goodman drove me down to the office-
in giving my evidence before, I took my evidence froin my cheque book
-I think that first transaction in shares was before the statutory meeting,
otherwise I should bave had no locus standi there; I should not have
been admitted-I paid a cheque for that amount of shares, £446 158., at
the company's offices- I have a sort of idea that I paid it to Mr.
Bernard -my next purchase was not long afterwards- I took my
evidence from my cheque book on the former occasion-I think it
was 600 shares--I am not quite sure whether it was after the
statutory meeting : unfortunately it is in the other cheque book-
the second shares I bought were after June 18th-that was a pur
chase of shares from & solicitor. £600, I think it was that trans-
action also took place in the office a day or two afterwards Mr.
Goodman asked me to go into the market and buy all the shares I
could get in the syndicate, and accordingly I instructed my stock.
