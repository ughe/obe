NEW COURT.-Saturday, February 8th, 1845.
Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
508. GEORGE SMITH was indicted for stealing 1 shawl, value
98. 6d.: the goods of Stephen Dew and another; to which be pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 27.-Confined Nine Months.
509. FREDERICK SHEPHERD was indicted for stealing 1 spoon.
value 12s.; and 1 breast-pin, 10s.; the goods of Thomas Killick Hur
lock, his master; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 20.-Confined Three Months.
510. JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing 1 jacket, value
108.; the goods of Henry Edwards, in a vessel on the navigable river
Thames; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 27.-Confined One Year.
511. GEORGE BROWN was indicted for stealing 1 pair of trowsers,
value 38.; and I waistcoat, value 28.; the goods of Michael M'Casey, in
a vessel in the port of London; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 29.-Confined Four Months.
512. CHARLES CHAPMAN and RICHARD PARKER were in-
dicted for stealing 5 carpet bags, value 14s.; the goods of Henry Fielder,
their master; to which they both pleaded
GUILTY.-Confined Three Months.
513. ELLEN CONWAY was indicted for stealing two blankets, value
4s. 6d. ; the goods of James Swaine ; and that she had been before con
victed of felony; to which she pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 48.-Transported for Seven Years.
514. JOHN THOMAS was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of Feb,
1 cask, value 18.; and 44lbs. of lard, value 158. : the goods of William
Were ; to which he pleaded
Guilty.--Confined Three Months.
515. THOMAS SIMPSON was indicted for stealing 36 plates, value
88.; 6 cups, value 4d. ; 6 saucers, value 4d.; 1 tea-pot, value 6d.;
jugs, value 8d.; and 1 milk-pot, value id. ; the goods of Joseph Cor
thorne, his master, in a vessel on the navigable river Thames; to which
he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 28.--Confined Two Months.
516. RICHARD TOWNSHEND was indicted for embezzling 81
the monies of William Watkins Welton, his master; to which he pleaded
Guilty.-Corfiried Six Months.
