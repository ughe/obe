WILLIAM HOUGH. I am a servant, of 5, Little Queen Street, Camber-
well-on 27th June I saw a man with a mare, which had a rope round her
neck, going along-he asked me to give him a leg up-I did so, and he
went towards Dulwich-I cannot say whether it was the prisoner-when
I saw the mare again she was in the prosecutor's possession, and I recog-
nised her.
JOSEPH RIBBINS. I live in Broad Street, Greenwich, and am assistant
to my father, a horse slaughterer-on 27th June the prisoner brought me
8 mare with black feet, white off feet, and white face-I gave 31. for it
to slaughter-the prisoner said that he bought it behind a straw waggon,
and gave 31. 108. for it-next day I sent it to be killed, but the prosecutor,
coming in, saved it.
COURT. Q. Had you ever seen the prisoner before ? 4. Yes, and
knew him by sight-I saw him again about five months afterwards.

COURT. Q. Was it between four-and five ? A. Yes,
Court to WILLIAM Hough. Q. Did you say that it was between four

THOMAS FOULGER (Policeman 234 R). I was in search of the prisoner,

I took him to the station-he said that he had sold several horses at

from Greenwich.
