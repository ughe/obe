at Lambeth, and stealing therein 1 decanter, value 2s. 6d. ; 3 pictures,
framed and glazed, value 2s.6d. ; 1 tea-pot, value 6s. ; 1 table-cover, valued
3s. ; 1 work-box, value 10$. ; 1 ink-stand, value 6s.; 1 cloak, value

WILLIAM NICKELLS. I am foreman at King and Co.'s India-rubber ma-
nufactory, and live at No. 6, Belvedere-road, in the parish of Lambeth-T
keep the house. On the night of the 13th of February, I was lying ill in
the back parlour in bed about a quarter to eight o'clock, and saw a light under or
the door in the front parlour-I heard a rustling and whispering in the room
- I got out of bed, put my hand on the handle of the door, and heard a voice
say, "Make haste"_I opened the door, and saw the prisoner standing by the
fire-place-he immediately rushed to the window, which was open-I caught
him in the act of getting out-I also saw somebody getting out of the
yard gate, and heard glass break, which was the glass of the pictures the
prisoner struggled very hard, and got out of the window, but I went after him,
and never let go of him-I afterwards examined the premises, and missed
a table-cloth, inkstand, and the other articles stated in the indictment,
from different parts of the room-I had seen them safe about two o'clock
I went to bed about three o'clock-my wife and family were in the kitchen,
