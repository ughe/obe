of Samuel John Rathbone. MR. RYLAND conducted the Prosecution.

Ground, Westminster. Last Sunday morning, about half-past twelve, I was
in Petticoat-lane--I had a silver watch in my right-hand waistcoat pocket,
with a silk guard attached to it, which went through a button-hole and round
my neck-I was surrounded by three or four men, and afterwards I felt my
guard come tight round my neck--I put my hand to my pocket and found
my watch was gone-- I turned and saw the prisoner with a part of my watch-
guard in his hand I went and drew the guard out of his hand, and asked
him wbat he had done with my watch-he turned round and said, “Do you
hear this chap accusing me of stealing his watch ? "-I saw the officer, told
him, and gave the prisoner into custody.
