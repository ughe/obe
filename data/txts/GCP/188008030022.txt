484. ROBERT POWELL EVERETT (22), Burglary in the dwelling
house of Henry Dick, and stealing 34 watches and other property.
MR. MILLWOOD Prosecuted.
HENRY DICK. I keep a jeweller's shop at 128, Camden Road, St.Pro
-on 25th May last I went to bed a little after 11-before doing so I val
into the shop myself, and the place was all square and the property ther-
the following morning I was called by the police a little after six o'clock-
I sleep on the grond-floor- I went into the shop; it was all in oonfusion
and the window stripped—the shop door was shut-I missed 35 watch
86 rings, and other property, worth about 3001.— I have only recovered
pair of eyeglasses produced- & square of glass had been taken from the bat
window which looked into the yard -the shop door had been opened on the
inside.
WALTER BRISCOMB. I am assistant to Messrs. Archbutt, of 201, West
minster Bridge Road on Saturday, the 12th June, the prisoner brough
these glasses and asked 38. or 48. for them-I asked him whom they belonged
to-he said, “My brother gave them to me," and that he lived in Wedde
Row-that is near the Waterloo Road—I asked him to come round to the
front-he went out and down the Westminster Bridge Road, leaving the
glasses behind- I followed him into a public-house-I sent a boy for a co
stable, and gave him into custody.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I cannot say whether I was waiting a
customers when you came in.
DANIEL MUNDEN (Policeman Y 544). On May 26, about 6.15 am, 1
was passing the prosecutor's shop; I found the door open-I entered
premises; I found the shop in great confusion-I awoke Mr. Dicks;
searched the shop, and found the implements produced (lantern, jemmy, and
knife)-& pane of glass had been extracted from a back window 10in. by
14in. this knife was lying on the work-bench inside the window, and the
stock bit as well (jemmy)--the lantern was on the counter.
THOMAS LUCAS (Police Sergeant Y). I took the prisoner in charge
Lambeth Police-court-I told him the charge was for committing a burglary
at 128, Camden Road—be said, “Oh, you are dreaming"-I conveyed him
to Kentish Town-I said, “How do you account for the eyeglasses -
said, "I bought them of a man "-he was searched, and nothing was found
on him.
Prisoner's Defence. I bought the eyeglasses in the Meat Market. I
not have them till the 12th June, and the robbery was committed som
where about the Derby Day. I left them at the pawnbroker's because I
been sleeping at a common lodging-house, and did not want it known,
they asked for my address. The witness was serving a customer, and a
not ask me a question.
Guilty of receiving.--Fifteen Months' Imprisonment.
