MARJA KING. I am the wife of William King, of Leather-lane, a bone
button manufacturer-I was with my husband on the night of 26th De-
cember at the Golden Anchor-we were in the bagatello-room-Michael
Harrington, the deceased, was there, and several others, English persons, in
the same room--there were no Italians in that room; they were all English
-I left the room, and proceeded to come out ; that was from 6 o'clock to
balf-past-I was leaving to go home--my husband was there with Harring-
ton and the other English persons in the room-I was coming out at the
bagatelle-room door to pass through the passage---when I came to the door
the prisoner knocked me down-he knocked me down with his fist, in the
mouth.
