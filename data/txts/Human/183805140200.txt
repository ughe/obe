   Q.  Was it the same person said all this? A.  There were different void—I cannot tell which said which—there was more said, but I did not dis  cern it myself, I could not comprehend what it was-upon that I went into the room, and saw Mr. Miller and Mr. Mason in there, and Mr. Miller threw himself backwards on the bed—Mr. Mason rushed at us, and pushed us out of the room, and offered us half a crown, but we would not have it—we were in the lobby—we came out of the room—Mr. Mason came out with us, pushing us before him, and offered in half a crown, but we would not have it—I knew Mr. Miller and Mr. Mason before that day—I was sober that night, I never touched any drink all the day—I went from the Castle to the Globe-Gallimore and Plant went with me—there were a good many persons there in the room—Mr. Brostock was one—we mentioned at the Globe the conversation we heard at the Castle—I have been in custody on this charge six weeks last Friday on this charge.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  you expect to be tried on it, don't you? A.  I am on trial now—I mean to tell the jury that I think 11 am on trial now—I am as a witness for one of the party—I do not know the rule of the Court—I saw a man tried once—I never knew there were assizes at Newcastle—I saw Mr. Mason go up the stairs—he had to pass us in the lobby before he got to the door—I cannot tell how far I was from the door at that time, three, or four yards—I saw nobody else with Mr. Mason—there was somebody else went in before him—I saw him go in by himself—there was a light in the lobby so that I could see anybody that passed—I believe some one passed me shortly before Mr. Mason-yes, there did—I did not see that person, my head was turned the other way—that was not half a minute before I saw Mr. Mason—somewhere about that time—I heard Mr. Mason come up the stairs, when the other passed—they did not come up together—they left the door open—they spoke up, not very loud—I could hear distinctly—the first thing I heard was some thing about 900 l ., I believe—he asked where the 900 l . was that had been already advanced-to the best of my knowledge that was the first thing I heard—after that he said he wanted 700 l . to buy seven-score burgesses with—I am sure of that.
   Q.  Did you ever swear it was 500 l . he said he wanted? A.  I believe I did, but it was a mistake—I did not swear to it a second time, that I know of—I will not swear that I did not—I was close to the door-Plant and Gallimore went along with me close to the door.
