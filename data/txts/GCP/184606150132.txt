Johnson, Mayor.
JULIA ELIZA WALKLEY. I am the wife of William Walkley. We live at
Charlton. I had a flannel shirt hanging on a line in the yard on the 5th of
June-I missed it-this is it.
ELIZA EDWARDS. This is my frock-it was hanging on a line in the same
yard, and I missed it.
Patrick Dee (police-constable R 236.) At half-past one o'clock at night
on the 5th of June I was on duty in Woolwich-road-I saw the prisoner com-
ing down Antigallican-lane with a bundle-I asked what he had got-he said
only his coat and cap- I took him, and found the articles produced and some
others on him-he said they belonged to his sister, who lived at the Mortar
Tavern-I went, and there was no such a person.
Prisoner's Defence. I came down Antigallican-lane and saw a man asleep:
I spoke to him, and about twenty yards further I found this bundle.
Guilty.* Aged 30.-Confined Three Months, without hard labeur, being lame.
1371. CHARLES LONGMAN was indicted for stealing 1 brooch, value
68.; and I scent-bottle, ls.; the goods of William Hollinden ; to which he
pleaded
Guilty. Aged 22.-Confined Six Months.
1372. JOHN WALTER FLEMING was indicted for stealing 2 saddles,
value 41. 108.; and I pad, 108.; the goods of David Valentine, his master.
DAVID VALENTINE. I am a harness-maker, and live at Greenwich--the
prisoner was in my employ. On the 27th of May I was busy-I left the shop
for about a hour at eight o'clock in the morning-I returned, and three saddles
were gone, and the prisoner also I have never seen the property since
it was the prisoner's breakfast time--he ought to have come back, but
did not.
EDWARD SPILLER. I saw the prisoner at five minutes before eight o'clock
in the morning, on the 27th of May, coming out with two saddles and
a pad.
Prisoner. I never saw the saddles.
WOOD
Guilty. Aged 30.---Confined Nine Months.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
SURREY CASES.
Before Mr. Recorder.
1373. GEORGE BOWSER was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief,
value 58., the goods of Thomas Tatlock, from his person and that he had
been previously convicted of felony: to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 21.- Transported for Ten Years.
1374. ELIZABETH BAXTER and LOUISA STEVENS were indicted
for stealing 24 yards of printed cotton, value 128., the goods of George
Bucknall Picken; to which Baxter pleaded
Guilty. Aged 18.- Confined Six Weeks.
WILLIAM JOHN PICKEN. I saw the prisoner Stevens in company with
Baxter- I did not see her take anything-when they left the shop they went
in different directions--the property was found on Baxter-Stevens did not
buy anything-she did not come into the shop-the goods were in the
doorway.
STEVENS-Not Guilty.
