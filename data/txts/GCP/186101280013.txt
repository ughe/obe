WILLIAM Wooly. I am a draper, of 80, High-street, Shoreditch about 6
o'clock on the night of the 12th I was standing at my shop door saw the
last witness coming-I saw the prisoner and two others with him--they
were standing at the end of Eyre's-alley-directly the last witness came
opposite the court, two pushed her into the court, and the prisoner caught
hold of ber bag-he succeeded in getting it from her the other two ran
away, and he rushed up the court - I sent one of my assistants for the police,
who went up the court and brought the prisoner back. I am quite sure he
is the man.
