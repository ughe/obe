 1128.  JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of July , one shawl, value 15 s., and one watch, value 15 s., the goods of John Wright.
   JOHN WRIGHT. I am a shoemaker, and live in Samuel-street, St. George's in the East. On the 27th of July, about half-past five o'clock in the morning, I hung this watch by the fire-place and went next door, leaving my door open, but kept my eye on it, and saw the prisoner come along the street smoking his pipe. I came out in a minute and a half, and saw him about twenty yards off; I went in, missed my watch, and saw some tobacco on the boards smoking. I ran out, he saw me, and ran. I called Stop thief! a constable collared him, and I took the watch out of his right-hand pocket - the shawl was found on him.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before J. Vaillant, Esq.
 1129.  MARY ANN MATLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of July , two sheets, value 7 s., the goods of James Blake , in a lodging-room.
   JAMES BLAKE. I live in Park-street, Regent's-park. The prisoner took a furnished lodging of me on the 15th of June, on the 19th of July I missed a pair of sheets, and found them in pawn, she gave me the duplicate.
   CHARLES VAUGHAN. I am a pawnbroker, the prisoner pledged the sheet with me.
 Prisoner's Defence. I intended to replace them.
     GUILTY. Aged 30. Confined Three Months.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before J. Vaillant, Esq.
 1130.  MICHAEL KELLY was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of August , one snuff-box, value 2 s., and five thimbles, value 3 s., the goods of Thomas Potter.
   THOMAS POTTER. I am a sword-cutler, and live in Vere-street, Clare-market. On the 23d of August I was at tea in my back-room, and heard a noise at the window, which had been broken, and a plate put against it. I perceived the plate had been knocked down, and a quantity of thimbles gone. Next morning a little boy came to sell me a thimble which I knew to be one of them. He said he lived at No. 9, Steward's-rents, I sent him home. A girl came in and said, a boy was there with a quantity of thimbles. I went there, and found the prisoner laying on the floor on a blanket, and charged him with it. He produced the thimbles and snuff-box.
     GUILTY. Aged 14. Transported Seven Years - Penitentiary.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before J. Vaillant, Esq.
 1131.  MARY EDWARDS was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of August , three pint pots, value 4 s., the goods of Henry Bell ; two pint pots, value 2 s.; the goods of Samuel Cordeaux ; and one pint pot, value 1 s., the goods of Abraham Redway.
   RICHARD GARDENER. I keep the Manchester Arms, Adam-street, Manchester-square. On the 9th of August, the prisoner ran into my bar, produced three pint pots, and said,
 "Here is three pint pots of yours." My name was not on them, but Mr. Bell's. I asked where she had them, she said
 "Baker-street", and ran out. Bell immediately came in, and enquired for her. I pursued, and found her in Portman-square, I found three more pots in her apron.
   HENRY BELL. I keep the Westmoreland Arms. I was in Adam-street, I saw the prisoner pass me, and suspecting her, followed her into Gardener's, she saw me and ran out. We searched her, and found three more pots in her apron.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 34. Transported for Seven Years.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before J. Vaillant, Esq.
 1132.  JAMES CLUTTERBUCK was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of August , one suit of boy's clothes, value 10 s., the goods of George Morley.
   JAMES HUNT. I am servant to George Morley, who is a clothes-salesman, and lives in Whitechapel. On the 18th of August, these clothes hung on the door-post, pinned to a cloth. I heard an alarm, ran out, and a gentleman stopped the prisoner. He immediately dropped the clothes. I saw him running with them.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 26. Transported for Seven Years.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before J. Vaillant, Esq.
 1133.  SUSAN RICHARDSON was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of July , one handkerchief, value 3 s., the goods of Thomas Pizzey , from his person.
   THOMAS PIZZEY. I am a cabinet-maker, and live in Old Nicholl-street, Bethnal-green. On the 1st of August, about half-past one in the morning, I was in Shoreditch. I was very tired, and fell asleep on a step. I was quite sober, I was awoke by feeling somebody at my neck, jumped up, and saw the prisoner in the act of putting my neck-handkerchief into her bosom. I seized her, and took it from her. I found my coat-pockets turned out, she cried out Murder! and I was surrounded by about a dozen men, who struck me, and told me to let her go. I held her till the watchman took her. They then made off.
