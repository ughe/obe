WILLIAM HENRY WARD. I am a tobacconist, and live in Maiden-lane,
Covent-garden. On the 16th of May, about ten minutes to one o'clock, I
missed from my counter a jar containing nearly 2lbs. weight of tobacco-they
have not been found.
Eliza Young. I live next door to the prosecutor About one o'clock, on
the 16th of May, I was standing at the door, and saw the prisoner come up
the court, walk into Mr. Ward's, and in about two minutes walk out with
the jar of tobacco under his arm, and go up the court-I am sure he is the
man.
