 1098. JOHN McKILLICK was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of March , 1 gown, value 6 d  .; 1 frock, value 3 d  .; 1 flannel petticoat, value 9 d  .; and 2 aprons, value 2 d  .; the goods of Sarah Robinson . SARAH ROBINSON. I am a widow, and live at Greenwich. On the 26th of March, I hung some linen to dry in front of the house; among which was a gown, a frock, and two aprons—I missed them the same evening. JAMES WILD( police-constable R  141.) On the 26th of March, I went to East-lane, Greenwich, and saw the prisoner at Mr. Smith's. He escaped from there, by getting over a wall—I went immediately to his lodging, and waited in the passage till he came—I then told him I took him for that wet linen out of Hogg-lane—he said, "Say nothing about it, and I will give you all I have about me"—I said, that was not the way I did my buis ness—I went into his room, and found these articles in a box—he wanted me to put them back.
   Prisoner . This wall is twelve feet high, and has glass bottles on the top—I could not get over it—I did not rent the room he went to. Witness . He showed me the room, and said it was his box—he said he took the linen for a lark , and did know it belonged to this poor woman.
   Prisoner . I expected it was Sarah Nichols's. JOHN SAVIOUR. I am a labourer, and lodge in East-lane. On the night of the 26th of March, I was at Berry's house, in Hogg-lane—the prisoner came in there, and said he was going to the other side of the College—after he had been there a little while, he trod on my toes, to make me go out with him—he said there were some handkerchiefs hanging in a court, and he went and took them down—he offered me some, but I would not have them—he then told me to follow him, which I did—he went home, and left them there—he wanted me to sell them, but I would not—we then came down together—he went one way, and I went and told the policeman—I have had no dispute with him—he said, while he was unpinning the things, that he took them for a lark .
   Prisoner . I went to Mr. Berry's, to inquire if he had got the piece of baize. Witness . That was the night before—two boys told me that you had made inquiry about an old rug—I had sold it for old rags, and it fetched 2 d .—when you took these things, you said, "Never mind about the piece of rug"—we had no quarrel about it.
 (  Property produced and sworn to. ) NOT GUILTY.
