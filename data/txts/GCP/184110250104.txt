MR. CLARKSON called
JOHN HARVEY. I am a tobacconist, and live at No. 13, Bear-street,
Leicester-square. I am landlord of the marine-store shop in Tower-
street, to which the officers have alluded the man Goodlad is my tenant,
and has been so for six or seven years—I seized on him for the last quar-
ter's rent, due last Michaelmas--I saw him on the premises-as far as I
know he has been the party in possession of the shop, and carrying on the
business--I let it to him, and he paid me the rent-I have known the female
prisoner all that time, and always supposed her to be his wife-she has
lived with him, and passed as his wife.
(Goodlad received a good character.)
