242. MARY ANN MILLER, Forging and uttering a receipt for the pay-
ment of E9, with intent to defraud.
Mr. ARTHUR GILL Prosecuted.
BENJAMIN MEACHAM. I am a French polisher, of 8, Trifina Place, Bow
Lane-I was formerly a Reservist in the 18th Hussars-I was discharged
from the Colours on August 10th, 1902, and went to 14, Abbey Street,
Plaistow, where the prisoner, who is my aunt, lives-I only stayed a few
hours-on August 11th I decided to leave that address, and told my aunt
that letters might come for me, and that she was not to open them- went to
Bow Lane-I did not tell her the addresg-about three months after August
11th I went to see my aunt-I asked her if she had any letters for me-
she showed me these opened envelopes-one contained my discharge, and
the other a money order for E10-the postmarks are November 15th and
17th, and both are addressed to Private B. Meacham, 14, Abbey Street,
Plaistow-] told her that she ought not to have opened them, and it was
quite likely she would get into trouble over itshe said, 46 Don't make a
fuss about that,' and that she had already changed one order for E9, and
would pay it back at E1 a week after Christmas, and that a man came into
the shop who said he had been a soldier for fifteen years, and had shown
her the way to change the order, and that she had paid E4 rent, and bought
herself a pair of boots with the money-I reported the matter to Aldershot
-the signature Benjamin Meacham on this order for E9 is not mine,
nor written with my authority-i is dated December lst, SO that the inter
view with my aunt must have been subsequent-I never told her that she
