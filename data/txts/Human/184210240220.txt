I was on duty in the Old Kent-road on the 27th of September, and saw the two prisoners and another boy coming up together towards the gate—when they came near the gate Kelly and the other boy crossed over the road and ran away, and Moulden went up towards the gate—this was a mile or more from Mason-street—I ran after Kelly and the others, and caught them—I asked what they ran for—they made no answer—I took them to the station—Kelly there said that he and Moulden went into a field together, and there found the pro perty, along with a knife-box and some knives, which they left behind them—that he and Moulden were in company together, and the other boy had nothing to do with it—no knives were found.  3060. GEORGE BENTLIFFE was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of October , 1 coat, value 5 l  ., the goods of John Smith ; and that be had been before convicted of felony. JOHN DARTNALL. I am coachman to Mr. John Smith. On the 8th of October my coat was on the box of my master's carriage, in the London-road—about twenty minutes to seven in the evening I had occasion to get off the box to buy some glass, and left my coat quite safe—I got on my box again, in about two minutes, and did not miss it till I got into Hart-street, Long Acre—this is it.
   Prisoner.  He said at the office, that he did not know whether it dropped or not. Witness.  I am certain I never dropped it—it could not have dropped off the box. JAMES BROOK. I am a policeman. I was on duty in the Waterloo-road at a quarter before seven o'clock on Saturday evening, 8th of October, and saw the prisoner with this coat on his back—he crossed the road walkmg—I asked where he got it from—he made no answer—I took him in charge.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I saw the coat lying in the road, took it up, and put it on, and was going to take it to the station.
