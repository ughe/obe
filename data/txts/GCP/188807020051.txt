indicted for the wilful murder of Michael Lewis.

the request of the Court also for Warne.

I had some zine in a garden at the Vale, 336, King's Road, Chelsea,

was gone-I saw it at the station the same evening-Warne had worked
for me one day at the place where the zine was, within a day or two of
this--the Vale is only open to the tenants living there-I do not know
Wood at all.
