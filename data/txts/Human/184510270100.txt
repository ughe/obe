 2048. HANNAH COFORD was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of Oct ., 1 pair of shoes, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 1 petticoat, 1 s  .; and 1 shift, 1 s  .; the goods of John Wordsworth Robson , her master. JOHN WORDSWORTH ROBSON. I am an artist, and live at Wapping-Wall. The prisoner came into my service about fifteen weeks before my wife's death, which was on the 24th Sept., and continued so since—I missed some articles after my wife's death, and spoke to the prisoner about it—she offered to swear on the Bible that she had not made away with any of my property—on the 10th of Oct. I went to Mr. Williams, a pawnbroker in New Gravel-lane, and there found this shift and pet ticoat, and a pair of shoes, which are mine—I gave her into custody.
   Prisoner.  The shoes are mine, and the other things I was ordered to pledge by his wife. Witness.  I bought the shoes myself for my wife, and am sure of them—a quantity of property was pawned before my wife's death, which I have not indicted her for; but these were pledged on the 9th of Oct., and my wife died on the 24th of Sept. JAMES GOSLIN. I am in the service of Mr. Williams. These shoes were pledged on the 6th of Oct., and the other things on the 9th, by the prisoner. JAMES MALIN( policeman. ) I took the prisoner—she said Mrs. Rob son allowed her to pawn the things.
   Prisoner.  When she laid ill she had not necessaries, and wished me to pawn her things; I did, and gave her the money and tickets; two days before she died, she called her husband to her and gave him the duplicates; the shoes are mine. JOHN WORDSWORTH ROBSON re-examined.  What she states is false—there was always money in the house, and my tradesmen serve me, and send in their bills quarterly—she could have what she liked. GUILTY. Aged 42.— Confined Three Months .
