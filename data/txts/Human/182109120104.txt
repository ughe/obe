14th of September, between two and three o'clock, I was in the kitchen, my mistress said there was some woman gone out with something. I ran out, the prisoner was pointed out to me going up Portland-place. I went up and asked if she had not been in the poulterer's shop, she said Yes; she had made a mistake, and took the wrong coat, and was bringing it back, that she had left her own there for 1 s., then she said, she went in and saw this coat, and thinking it was hers, she took it. I said she took it without seeing any body; she said she saw the good lady. She had left no coat in our shop - I do not know her. It was taken from the parlour - it could not be seen without coming into the parlour behind the shop.
   HENRY BUCKERIDGE. I am an officer. I was sent for, and took her in charge; she said she left her coat at a poulterer's for sixpence, and could not tell the shop. I went to several poulterer's, nobody knew her.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I bought sixpenny-worth of giblets, and left my coat. I went into the shop, nobody was there but a lady reading. I took the coat and said, "Thank you;" it rained, and I put it over my head, found it was not mine, and was coming back with it.
     GUILTY. Confined Six Months.

 1258.  SAMUEL MIDGLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of September , one bedstead, value 10 s.; the frame of a four-post bedstead, value 10 s.; one leather bag, value 3 s., and one sack, value 2 s., the goods of George Mitchell.
   GEORGE MITCHELL. I am an upholsterer, and live at Tottenham. On the 10th of September, between seven and eight o'clock in the morning, I missed these things from my back warehouse. When I came to town, the officer informed me that the prisoner was in custody - he is a shoemaker at Tottenham.
   WILLIAM TURNNARD. I am an officer. About ten o'clock on the night of the 10th of September, I went to take the prisoner - I believe he was in bed, he came down. I told him I took him for stealing some property from Mitchell; he acknowledged taking it, and took me to an out-house adjoining his own house, where he had concealed it. Mr. Mitchell claimed it - he fell on his knees and begged him not to prosecute him.
   HENRY MITCHELL. I employed the prisoner to clear the warehouse out, and sent him out with a four-post bedstead; a few days after, I heard he had robbed my father.
   THOMAS BENSON. I am a gardener, at Tottenham. I saw the prisoner take these things out of the place, and put them in a hay-cart; he said "Mr. Mitchell employed me to take them." It appeared two bedsteads tied up.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. The witness,  Henry Mitchell, employed me to fetch his cart from the wheelers, after that he set me to clean the horse and cart to go to Barnet races, and to keep it a secret from his father; he said he would give me something handsome if I attended him on his return. He told me to keep the horse and cart out of the way till his father was gone; he then set me at different jobs, and asked me to buy one bedstead and to take another to another person, and told me to put them in the hay cart, and if his father met me I was to say I was going to take them to Murray's; he was in the habit of selling things unknown to his parents; he said they would not miss these; he used to pay people in furniture for work they did, unknown to his father.
   HENRY MITCHELL. It is not true.
     GUILTY. Aged 42. Transported for Seven Years.

 1260.  EDWARD JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of September , one pistol, value 5 s., the goods of John Creamer.
   JOHN FRANKLIN. I am servant to Mr. Hunt. On the 6th of December, my master sent me and the prisoner into the country to work, he said he should not work, but go and shoot sparrows. He went and fetched the pistol which he shot them with.
   MICHAEL MORRIS. I produce the pistol which I got from the office.
   JOHN BINDER. I am a carpenter. I repaired Mr. Creamer's house twelve months ago. A pistol was there then. I think I can swear this is it.
     NOT GUILTY.

 1261.  LYDIA HILLSEY was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of September , one plane, value 3 s., and one chissel, value 1 s., the goods of George Simkin.
   GEORGE SIMKIN. I am a carpenter. On the 12th of September, about nine o'clock in the morning I was next door to my shop, and was told these tools were stolen off my bench. They had to go through the shop into the back room to get them.
   HENRY COVENTRY. As I came from my father's I saw the prisoner go into Simkin's shop. She went into the back room, moved several things, then took something and put it under her arm, she came out and asked if I wanted to buy a shovel, which she had. I said "No." She went on. I told some men who went and took her.
   WILLIAM COX. I am a constable. I took her in charge with the plane under her arm, and found the chissel in her pocket.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up.
     GUILTY. Aged 53. Confined Six Months.
