THOMAS DELANTLEY (City policeman, 493). I was on duty on that day
in Cheapside-the prisoner was brought to me by the omnibus conductor,
and given into my custody for stealing a purse-I took him to the shop
where the lady was; she said, “ You villain, you have robbed me"-he said,
“Mother, what do you mean?" in broken English or French-she said,
“You know that you did "-he seemed to wish her not to give him into
custody, but he spoke such bad English I could not understand him.
JAMES QUINN. I am a conductor of a Royal Blue omnibus. On the
day the lady got into the omnibus I remember the prisoner coming, but I
do not exactly remember the place-it was probably between the Bank and
Cheapside-he sat the second on the left hand side of the omnibus at the
bottom of Cheapside the lady called to me to get out-I saw her get up
from her seat she could scarcely get up, she was entangled with something
-when she got up she turned round and looked on the seat, and after she
set her foot on the step she looked at the bottom of the omnibus—I looked,
but I did not see the purse-the lady stepped on the ground, and the pri-
soner immediately got up, pushed by the lady, and ran out, and at that
instant I saw the purse on the floor of the omnibus-I chased the prisoner
round St. Paul's Churchyard, and caught him.
