Prisoner's Defence. Last Monday five weeks I had been with a young
gentleman my father worked for in former years, he lives in Bishopsgate-
street, and is a wine-merchant; I went with him to take care of a cart and
assist him with the goods he had in it; on returning home in the evening
coming down the City-road, I missed him; I went back to look for him
and saw these things lying down by the public-house; I took them up,
and as I crossed the road another policeman came up, and this one asked
what I had there, I told him dirty linen; he asked where I was going
to take it, I said home; he took me to the station-house, went to the
coffee-house and brought this young man there.
(John Baker, of Butler's-buildings, Bermondsey; and Rosetta Cabby, of
Dun's-alley, Bishopsgate-street, gave the prisoner a good character.)
