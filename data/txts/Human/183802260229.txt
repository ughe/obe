891. WILLIAM HEATH and WILLIAM CROWLEY were indicted for stealing, on the   1st of March , 1 plane, value 10 d  ., the goods of John Coan Ives . GEORGE SYMES. My father is a blacksmith and farrier. I saw the two prisoners together on the 1st of March, by Atkins, the doctor's—they came up to me between seven and eight o'clock in the evening—I passed into the road, as I thought they were going to hit me—they said, "Do not be frightened," and asked me if I would go on an errand, and they would give me three pence—I said yes, I would, and they gave me this plane—Heath spoke to me—they were both together—Crowley told me to ask 5 s ., and he would give me 3 d .—then Heath gave it to me—I went to the pawn broker, and I heard them say, if the pawnbroker stopped me, one was to come in, and say it was his tool—they were dressed as they are now, like soldiers—they had caps on—I went to the pawnbroker's, and he was stopped—I took the pawnbroker to the two prisoners, and they said, "I will come and have a talk to you," but instead of coming up, then ran away—they were taken on the Friday.
   Crowley. Q.  Was it me gave it to you? A.  No, Heath gave it to me, from under his cloak. DAVID WILLIAM BUNNEY. I am a pawnbroker. The last witness, Symes, came to me to pawn this plane—he said he would show me the person who gave it him—Heath came into the shop first—I asked him if it was his—he said yes, he bought it in London—he went and fetched the other prisoner—I said "Is this yours?"—he said, "Yes"—I said, "Does this belong to you?"—he said, yes, he bought in King-street, of an auc tioneer—I asked him who—he said Jackson or Johnston—I called for the police, and they then ran away—I am sure they are the same persons—I picked them out of the regiment on Friday morning. JOHN COAN IVES. I am a carpenter, and keep a beer-shop at Dept ford. The two prisoners were in company at my house on the 1st of March—I do not think they came in together—they asked me for a pot of beer, which I served them with—this plane was in a cupboard—I had not time to lock it in my chest—it has my name on it, back and front—I lost two table knives, and a smoothing plane, at the same time.
   Crowley. Q.  Were there not from ten to fifteen soldiers there? A.  That is true, but you two were there, and when you were gone I missed the things. WILLIAM COLLINS( police-constable R  18.) I took the two prisoners from the barracks—they belong to the Rifle Brigade, lying at Deptford dock-yard—I had previously seen Crowley in bed, before I took them—I asked him if he had sent anybody to pawn articles—he said, no, neither had he been himself; and when I took the two, I asked if they had been or sent anybody—they both denied it. DAVID WILLIAM BUNNEY re-examined.  Symes first said that his father sent him with it, and that it was his property.
   HEATH  — GUILTY.—Aged 28.
   CROWLEY  — GUILTY.—Aged 28. Confined Three Months.
