Oetober, 2 pairs of boots, value 128., the goods of George Fowler, his
master.
GEoRGe FOWLER. I live in Caroline-place, Kingsland-road, and am a
ladies boot-maker. On the 15th of October the prisoner came to me for
wor'I left him at work next morning when I went down to breakfast-]
came back in about five minutes, and he had left, and two pair of boots were

they could not get in unless they came into the room where I was sitting
