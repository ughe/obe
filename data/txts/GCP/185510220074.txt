handkerchief was picked up by some person, and given to the gentleman,
and the officer snatched it out of his hand, and said, “You must come along
with me for your handkerchief;" he said, “I will," and he went to the
station, and accused me of picking his pocket; he said, he never saw me
take it ; nor saw me with it ; nor saw it picked up : I never had it in
possession ; I think that I stand before this bar quite innocent of that; the
punishment I have gone through for a month or five weeks, I think suf-
cient; I know I assaulted the policeman, but I was used in a most shameful
manner; he got me down and trampled on me, and tore my trowsers al
to pieces; he took his staff out and said, “I won't let you get up till you

barrow; I had no person with my barrow, and it is gone; I called on the
public to know if this was treatment I ought to have, and two or three
gentlemen said it was a shame, but I could not get their names: I tried W
get away to mind my barrow; this witness put his hand over my mouth to
prevent my hallooing out; I bit his finger, and I believe I bit it severely
to save myself from being choked by his hand; I got to the station, and
there they used me ten times worse ; they got me down and twisted my
hands back, and said they would twist my arms clean off, I went quiet
to Arbour-square, and I asked to have the gentleman produced to give up
the handkerchief, but the Magistrate would not allow it.
