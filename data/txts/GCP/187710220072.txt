ARTHUR BARTON (City Policeman 889). About a quarter to one on the

John MINES. I am the prosecutor's potman-I asked some people to
leave the compartment about 12.30, but they did not, and my master
went to them-I saw him dragged into the street, holding the prisoner-I
heard him say that he had lost his watch I ran for a policeman, and as
I came back I saw the prisoner running and stopped her--my master gave
her in charge.
CHARLES KING (City Policeman 933). I took the prisoner—the charge

watch-I never had it."

797. EUGENE JOHNSON, Stealing 188. 7d., the moneys of the
church wardens of St. Ann's Church, Westminster, and afterwards break-
ing out of the church.
Mr. Gill conducted the Prosecution.
RICHARD WILEY (Policeman C 250). On Saturday morning, 22nd Sep-
tember, I was in Dean Street, and saw the prisoner come out of St. Ann's
Church, close the door after him, and walk away-I followed him and he
ran-I ran after him-he got twenty yards in advance of me-I sprang
my rattle, and another constable came towards me-before I got up to the
prisoner he threw away this piece of steel (produced)—I took him in
custody, and at the station he tried to throw away this other piece of steel
—both pieces have some sticky substance still attached to them-he said
that he picked the steel up on the church steps-I found on him six
florins, six shillings, a sixpence, and two halfpence—they have a sticky
substance on them, and have evidently been wiped with this duster, which
I found on him.
