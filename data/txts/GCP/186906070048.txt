custody"-he said, “What for 9"--I said, “For being concerned in removing
this cochineal" he said, "I know nothing about it"-I took him up to
John Baldwin, who was in custody, and left him with 57 1 while I went to
John Baldwin's place, Falstaff Road, Kent Street, Borough-I had not
asked him for his address I locked the prisoner up, returned to the railway
station, and then took the two Baldwins in a cab to the police-station-
