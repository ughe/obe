2718. CHARLES ROBINSON and WILLIAM IVES were indicted
for stealing, on the 15th of September, 1 cullender, value d.; 1 sauce-
pan, value d.; and 6lbs. weight of kitchen-stuff, value 8d.; the goods
of John Jackson ; and that they had been before convicted of felony. :)
THOMAS HENWELL. I live with my father, in St. Pancras-fields-John
Jackson lives in the next cottage. At half-past five o'clock on the morn-
ing of the 15th of September I was in my father's garden--I saw the two
prisoners coming by--they stopped just at the corner of the gate of Jack-
son's yard—Ives broke in the gate, went in, and took the fat, the saucepan,
and cullender off the pig tub by the back door-Robinson was standing
outside five or six yards from the fence-I then saw Ives come out with
the cullender and saucepan in bis hand-Robinson came to him, and took
them from him, emptied the fat from the cullender into the saucepan, and
chucked the cullender into the ditch-Robinson took the saucepan into his
band, and they both went away together-I van round another way, and
met them-1 saw a policeman, and he took them-I knew them very well
by sight about Somers-town--they told the policeman they were going to
work in the brick fields.
