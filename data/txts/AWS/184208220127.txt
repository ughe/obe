2350. HENRY BROWN was indicted for a misdemeanor.
Mr. EsPINASBE conducted the Prosecution.
SARAH MITCHELL. I am the daughter of Samuel Mitchell, who keeps
the Crown public-house, in Broad-street, Golden-square. The prisoner
came on the 6th of August, between four and five o'clock--I served
with half-a-pint of beer-he gave me a shilling, which I showed to him my
brother- then went for my father-when I came back the prisoner was
on the step of the door-b ran down the street when he saw me, and
across the road.
WILLIam MITCHELL, Junior. On the 6th of August my sister gave me
a shilling-) marked it, in the prisoner's presencewhe said, 44 Don't mark
it"'-he said he would take it to a baker's in Wardour-street--he wanted
to go, and wanted it back-I would not give it him-he went to the door,
and stood there-when he saw my father crossing the road he ran away-
I followed him to the top of Berwick-street--I met Beresford- joined
in pursuing-before he was stopped he put something down Mrs. Lowe's
area-I afterwards saw it was a bag, with six bad shillings in it.
ANN MARGARET Lowe. I am the wife of John Lowe, a cabinetemaker,
in Berwick-street. On the 6th of August I was in the front kitehen-|
heard something fall in the area-I picked it up--it was a linen bag, con-
taining some silver gave it to Beresford.
HENRY BERESFORD. 1 am a police inspector. I assisted in pursuing
the prisoner-a soldier stopped him-I received from Mrs. Lowe this bag
and six shillings, and from Mitchell this sbilling.
MR. JOHN FIBLD. I am inspector of coin to the Mint. These are all
counterfeit, and all from the same mould.
Prisoner's Defence. I found the money in Middlesex-street; not
knowing it was bad, I went to change one, and then I threw the bag
away.
GUILTY. Aged 7.-Confined Six Months.
