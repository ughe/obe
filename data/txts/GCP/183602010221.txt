THOMAS Tossin. I live in Cherry-garden-street. I was passing by
last Thursday morning, between eleven and twelve o'clock, and saw fire
in the front room window-I ran over into the shop, and said, “Mrs.
Fisk, your place is on fire"-she asked me where I said, “In the front
room, up-stairs"--she said, "My dear, mind the shop, wliile I run up-
stairs and see "-she was going up-stairs--I said, "You had better let
me go up, you cannot put it out"-she made no answer; but I ran out of
doors-a policeman was standing next door I called him in; and at that
time the wind blew the door too, and we could not get in I did not go
up-stairs till it was all over.
