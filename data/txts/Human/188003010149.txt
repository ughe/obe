   Cross-examined by MR. METCALFE . I had known young Gourlay for about three weeks—I was not at school with him—I made his acquaintance at Stettin—I only knew from him that his name was Gourlay—I knew that his father was trading under the name of Laird and Co.—young Laird came to me at Glasgow for the purpose of paying me the 5 l ., to compromise the matter—it was then I got the two promissory notes, and I gave a receipt in full.
