1183. WILLIAM JOHNSON was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchiel
value 6d., the goods of Hannah Rogers, from her person.
HANNAH ROGERS. I live in Somerset-place, Rotherhithe. On the 124
of May I was at Greenwich-fair, and lost my handkerchief from my
pocket-it was safe about a quarter of an hour before-this now pro
duced is it.
John WHITE (police-constable R 180.) I was at the fair, and saw the pri-
soner put his hand into the prosecutrix's pocket, and take from it this hand-
kerchief-I caught him by the arm and he dropped it-I picked it up.
Prisoner's Defence. A boy standing before me took the handkerchief,
and threw it down by the side of me.
Guilty. Aged 16.-Confined Four Months.
Before Mr. Recorder.
1184. THOMAS HAYS was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of May,
1 cigar-case, value Is. ; 2 cheroots, 4d.; and 3 cigars, 6d., the goods of
Charles Watson, from his person.
CHARLES WATSON. I am a sergeant of the City-police, and live in
Green Arbour-court, Moorfields. On the 14th of May I was in Greenwich
fair, in plain clothes I heard a scuffle bebind me, and on turning round
observed my cigar-case in the hands of a man in plain clothes, who after-
wards turned out to be an officer.
JAMES RAPSEY (police-constable R 102.) I was at Greenwich on the
14th of May, about four o'clock-I saw the prisoner and another go up
towards Watson, lift the tail of his coat, draw the cigar-case from bis
pocket, and pass it to the other, who I immediately laid hold of, and took
it from him-Watson turned round, and seeing the case in my hand, res-
cued the prisoner, and took the cigar-case from me, thinking I had
taken it.
John Wuite police-constable R 180.) I did not see the prisoner take
the case-I saw him lift the prosecutor's coat tail, and took him into
custody.
Prisoner's Defence. I was looking at Richardson's show; there was a
crush of people, and I saw a boy taking the cigar-case out of the gentle-
man's pocket; the officers caught hold of him and took it from him; he
ran away, and the officer got hold of me.
Guilty. Aged 23.-Confined Six Months.
