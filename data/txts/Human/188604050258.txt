JOHN MILRUP( Policeman W. ) On 27th February I saw three men standing by the shutters of 21, Wands worth Road—I don't recognise the prisoner—I had another man in custody—I blew my whistle and chased them and caught another man named No it, who has been dis charged—ten minutes after I had him in custody the prisoner was brought back by Baker, who had heard my whistle and stopped the prisoner, who was wearing this coat over his own—I did not see the coat picked up. SAMUEL BARKER( Policeman  551 W. ) On 27th February, about 1.20 a.m., I heard a whistle, and saw the prisoner run into Archer-street from Bord-street from the direction of Wandsworth-road—he ran by me; I tried to stop him, and then ran after him—he ran into Miles-street, and in a dark place threw himself into a garden and was going to crawl into the area—I took him; he was wearing this coat—I made inquiries and found the shop had been forced open—I took him to the station, where I found another man in Milrup's custody—I went back and found this coat doubled up by the side of the kerb where I had first took up the chase.
   The Prisoner, in his statement before the Magistrate and in his defence said that he picked the coat up. GUILTY.†— Six Months' Hard Labour.
 493. JOHN MORGAN  (25) , Robbery with violence on Elizabeth Sharpe , and stealing a bag and other articles her property.
   MR. WILLIS Prosecuted. ELIZABETH SHARPE. I am a widow, living at 9, Freeschool Street—on April 1st, about 8.30, I was walking along Tanner-street, Bermondsey—I had a bag in my hand containing post-cards and, I think, 1 l . 2 s . in silver—just as I went down a narrow street a man hit me with his fist between my shoulders and sent me to the other side of the road—he then seized my bag and ran away—I screamed "Stop thief!" and he threw it down—somebody picked it up and a detective caught him—the prisoner is like the man; he is about the height—he had to stoop almost as low as I was before he tore it away, and I saw him sideways.
   Cross-examined by the Prisoner.  You look like the man—I had a clear view of you. ALFRED HILL.
