Mr. PANE conducted the Prosecution.
CHARLES BLUNDELL. I am a boat and barge builder at Strand-on-the-
Green, next door but two to the City Barge publie-house. On Friday
erening, the 11th of Sept., about six o'clock, I was standing in front of my
E
house and saw the two prisoners and another enter the public-house did
not see them come out.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Had you ever seen Barnett
before A. No, my attention was called to him from his resemblance to a
young man I knew very well.
Cross-ezamined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. You were not so certain of the
others, were you A. I recognised them all before the Magistrate--] felt
more certain of Barnett than anybody else.
