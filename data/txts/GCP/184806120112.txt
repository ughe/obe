1586. JOHN BUCKLEY, unlawfully obtaining by false pretences 1h
tea, llb. coffee, 20 eggs, and other articles, value 1l. 2s. 11d.; the goods of
John Watkins.
JOHN WATKINS. I am a grocer, at Richard-street, Woolwich. On 14
April, the prisoner came and said he was the steward of the Investigator, and
came from the officers of the gun-room for some grocery-he brought a book
with the order written in it for tea, coffee, sugar, &c.-I supplied them
knowing there was a vessel fitting out at the time-he said the officers would
settle the book every week, and asked me for 28. to purchase a basket to take
them in-I lent it him-he returned with a basket, and took away the goods
-I afterwards met him in Parliament-street, and spoke to him about the
goods-he said he was very sorry he had obtained them, he had used them
- I gave him in charge.
BENJAMIN FISHER ( policeman, R 272.) The Investigator was at Woolwich
on 14th April-she sailed on 8th May.
WILLIAM BUCHANAN. I am a grocer, at Wellington-street, Woolwich.
On 14th April the prisoner came and asked for some goods for the officers of
the Enterprize -- there was a vessel of that name lying above-1 offered to
send the goods-he said I could not get on board with them, and unless I let
hiin take them he would not have them-he wanted 28. to buy a basket to
take them with him-I did not supply them.
Guilty. Aged 52.- Recommended to mercy.-Confined Four Months

office, who sealed it in my presence-read-John Magee convicted 15th June,
1840, of desertion, and transported for fourteen years.)
John CARR. I was drummer in the 29th regiment-I am now dis
charged. The prisoner was a private soldier in that regiment in May, 1840-
we were in the same company, and were at the Royal Artillery-barracks,
Woolwich I heard sentence of fourteen years' transportation pronounced on
him on the garrison parade, for desertion, and saw him marched from the
parade.
