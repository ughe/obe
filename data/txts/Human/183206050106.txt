 1672.  JOHN BURGIN was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of June , 5 brushes, value 6d., the goods of James Banks Harraden.
   WILLIAM BLAIN. I was in Islington-road on the 30th of June; I saw the prisoner and two others loitering about the prosecutor's house - I watched, and saw him lean over a rail and take the brushes off a nail, outside the shop; he put them into his apron, and ran away - I pursued, and took him with them.
   JAMES GODFREY. I am a Police-constable. I took the prisoner.
   JAMES BANKS HARRADEN. These are my brushes, and were taken from my shop.
 Prisoner's Defence. Another boy gave them to me.
     GUILTY. Aged 12. - Whipped and Discharged.
 1673.  JOHN BOWLES was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of June , 1 watch, value 30s., the goods of Benjamin Holley.
   BENJAMIN HOLLEY. I am a sweep. The prisoner was employed by me on the 23rd of June; I left him in bed, and my watch was in my bed-room; when I returned it was gone - I found him on the Brentford road; he was secured.
   JAMES ALDOUS. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Berwick-street, Soho. I received this watch from the prisoner on the 23rd of June, in the name of John Bowles.
   HUGH SANDILAND(Police-constable T 80). I took the prisoner, and found the duplicate on him.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1674.  JAMES CULLENS was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of June , 1 pair of shoes, value 10s., the goods of Michael Elbell , from the person of John Elbell.
   JOHN ELBELL. My father is a shoemaker. On the 27th of June I was carrying some shoes home - I stopped at the corner of Titchfield-street, and the prisoner snatched them from under my arm; I called Stop thief! but he got away, and the shoes have not been found - he was taken in four days afterwards; I had seen him about the street with a butter basket.
   THOMAS LOCK(Police-constable S 35). I took the prisoner at the Hare and Hound tavern.
 Prisoner's Defence. He was playing at buttons, and put his bag on the step of a door - he then missed the shoes, and he knew no one but me to charge with it.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1675.  ELIZABETH CLARK was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of May , 6 pence and 14 halfpence, the monies of George Green , his master.
   GEORGE GREEN. I keep a general shop- the prisoner was in my employ for three months, as servant of all work. I missed some money; on the 20th of May I marked 13s. worth of copper, and put it into my till - I went out as usual on Sunday, from eleven till one o'clock; when I came back I found the till had been opened, and missed 1s. 1d.; I taxed her with it; she denied it - I got an officer, who was about to search her, and she produced seven 1d. pieces, but none of them were marked; I asked if she had any more; she denied it - she was taken to the station-house, and 2s. 4 1/2d. was found on her; two halfpence and three 1d. pieces were marked, and were what I had put in my till.
 Cross-examined by MR. DONNE. Q. Was not she in the habit of paying for little articles? A. No; she had no more right to go to the till than a stranger had.
   THOMAS CALLOWAY(Police-constable H 79). I took the prisoner, and Francis searched her.
   SUSANNAH FARNCIS. I serched the prisoner, and found on her two half-sovereigns, eight half-crowns, some small silver, and 2s. 4d. in copper - some of it is marked.
   GEORGE GREEN. This is the money I marked.
     GUILTY. Aged 60. - Confined Twelve Months.
 1676.  PETER DAVIDSON was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of June , 3 sovereigns, the monies of John Neale , from his person.
   JOHN NEALE. I am a servant- I have known the prisoner between two and three years; I believe he is a groom. On the 24th of June I had been out with him drinking - he was out of place; I went home with him to another man's lodging - I pulled off my coat and my boots, laid down on the bed, and went to sleep; it was then about one o'clock in the morning - I was sober; I had a purse in my fob, containing three sovereigns - I awoke between seven and eight o'clock in the morning; I found my clothes were all unbuttoned, and my braces undone, which I had not done myself - I felt, and thought I had lost some silver from my pocket; I then felt my fob, and thought the three sovereigns were safe, but I found afterwards that they were three shillings - I did not give any alarm, but asked the man who keeps the room permission to go to the water-closet; the prisoner said he would show me the way - when I was there I examined my purse, and found they were only shillings in it; I went up again, but said nothing to the prisoner; we then went out together, and the first officer I met I gave charge of him.
 Cross-examined by MR. DONNE. Q. Had not the prisoner asked you to go to give bail? A. No; he asked me to go with him to a Frenchman, to speak on behalf of two women and a man, for robbing a man at a public-house; I had drank with the prisoner the night before - we began about ten or eleven o'clock, and left off about twelve; I was not drunk - I did not ask him to take care of my money; I was capable of taking care if it myself.
