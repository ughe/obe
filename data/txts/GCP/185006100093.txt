1144. SUSANNAH BREESON and JOHANNA WILLIAMS, stealing
8 51. bank-notes, and 3 sovereigns; the property of George Tait, from
his person.
To sobo
GEORGE TAIT. I am boatswain of the ship Manchester. I received four
51.-notes and two sovereigns, on board the ship, on 31st May, about one
o'clock-I rolled them up, and put them into my wateh-fob. I went on shore,
and met Williams at the White Hart, between two and three--I went home
with her to where Breeson lives, and her family-I saw Breeson and Williams
there I went with them both to a pawnbroker's Breeson said she had a
monkey-jacket there to sell I took out my four 51.-notes there--I changed
one of them, rolled the rest up, and put them into my fob again--the prisoners
were present-we went back to the White Harti bad a glass of rum and
two glasses of wine there--I sat down the prisoners were one on each side
of me-- I got stupified-I came to myself at half-past six the prisoners were
gone-I went to see a friend, and found all my money was gone; my fob
was turned inside out-I went back to the White Hart, and made inquiries
I then went to Breeson's, and did not find her-I stopped there till half-past
twelve at night--neither she nor Williams came I went to the police, and
they told me to go the next morning to the ship, and get the numbers of
the notes.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'BRIEN. Q. What drink had you had 14.
Only one glass of rum--I had met Williams before I had it-I treated her
with one glass: I treated no other woman I did not change a 5-note at
the White Hart--I had the two sovereigns when I changed the note when
I went to Breeson's I had two cups of tea; I had no rum there; I might have
had a glass of gin; I did not have two or three glasses I did not send out
for any; they treated me I did not order gin-I gave Breeson half-a-crown
-I then went to the pawnbroker's, and purchased this monkey-jacket-
