 209.  SOPHIA SMALL was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of December , 4 pairs of boots, value 16s., the goods of Ralph Wilcoxon.
   THOMAS BAKER. I am a police-constable. On the 4th of December, at half-past eleven o'clock at night, I was in Tottenham-court-road - I saw the prisoner walking at a moderate pace; I saw she had a bundle, and I saw the ticket of the price of these boots outside - I asked what they were; she said a pair of shoes she had taken from a shop merely to have a lark - I asked where she had them from; she said she would show me - we went to several shops, but could not find which it was till we came to Mr. Wilcoxon's, who owned them.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. How far was she from the prosecutor's shop? A. About three hundred yards - she was not in liquor; I cannot swear she had not been drinking - she gave me her right name and address at the watch-house.
   WILLIAM PARDY. I am in the employ of Mr. Ralph Wilcoxon. These are his boots, and were safe in his shop at half-past eleven o'clock, on the 4th of December-I did not see the prisoner till she was brought back.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did she not appear as if she had been drinking? A. I did not notice her particularly - she gave her address, and that of two women who had gone away.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was very tipsy, and am sorry to say it - I never was tipsy before.
 The prisoner received a good character - one witness stated that his mistress would employ her.
 GUILTY. Aged 34.
 Recommended to Mercy. -    Judgment Respited.
 210.  JOHN ONSLOW was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of December , 1 cloak, value 14s., the goods of James Pincott.
   JOHN CONNOR. I am a Police-officer. On the 6th of December I saw the prisoner run by me very quick with a bundle under his arm; there was a blue apron tied round it - he said it was a cloth pelisse; I said I would examine it - he then said it was a lady's cloak; I took him to the watch-house, and then found the prosecutor, who keeps a silk-mercer's shop at the top of Tottenham-court-road, and it was at the bottom that I took him.
   WILLIAM MEADE. I cut this coat out, and made it for Mr. James Pincott - I can swear to my own work.
   HENRY JAMES COOK. I hung this cloak at Mr. Pincott's door, about eight o'clock in the morning of the 6th of December - a young man gave me information, and I missed it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had to carry it.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Transported for Seven Years.
 211.  THOMAS RICKETT was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of November , 6 half-crowns, 20 shillings, 2 sixpences, and 4 1/2d., the monies of Joseph Barrett.
   MARY BARRETT. I am the wife of Joseph Barrett - he is journeyman to Mrs. Wilson, who is a baker, and I deal with her. On the 3rd of November the prisoner came to me, and said Mrs. Wilson would be glad if I would send her all the money I had, as the miller was in the shop; I gave him the money stated, and he went away.
   CATHERINE WILSON. The prisoner had lived with me about a month - I saw him go out of the shop; I never authorized him to go for this money - he did not return to me.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Confined Three Months.
 212.  GEORGE ROSE was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of November , 1 coat, value 2l.; 1 pair of spectacles, value 10s.; 1 gown, value 4s.; 2 yards of cloth, value 2s., and 1 basket, value 1s., the goods of Elias Griffiths , his master.
 ELIAS GRIFFITHS. I live in  King-street, Golden-square, and am a tailor. The prisoner was my apprentice, and had been so for five months - on the 24th of November, I went out for half an hour, leaving him at home; when I returned he was gone - I missed a coat, a pair of spectacles, a gown, two yards of cloth, and a basket; he was taken early the next morning - he always behaved very well before.
   THOMAS LUCAS. I have a coat and gown, pawned by the prisoner on the 24th of November.
   GEORGE SEAMAN. I am an officer. I was on duty on the 25th of November, and saw the prisoner come down White Lion-street; I took him, and found these spectacles on him - I found this basket, which he had left at a public-house in Whitechapel, with two linen shirts in it.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 GUILTY. Aged 16. Recommended to Mercy. - Transported for 14 Years.
 213.  JOHN SCARBOROUGH was indicted for embezzlement.
   JOHN KING. I am a baker, and live in Woburn-buildings, Tavistock-square. The prisoner has been twice in my employ, about six months each time; it was his duty to receive money, and to account for it every evening - I keep a book, and put down what he pays me; Jane Pope is a customer of mine.
   JANE POPE. I paid the prisoner several sums of money for his master; on the 6th of November I paid him 5s., and he gave me a receipt on this bill.
   ANN SIMMS. I paid the prisoner 3s. 9 1/2d. on the 6th of November - he gave me no receipt, as I always pay him ready money; I am cook in a family.
   JOHN KING. He never paid me either of these sums-I asked him about them, and he said he had not received them; I never went from the Saturday till the Monday without settling - he owes me 9s. now.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Confined Three Months.
