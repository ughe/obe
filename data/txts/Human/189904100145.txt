On March 1st I found the prisoner detained at Bow Street Police-station; he had been arrested by Sergeant Pedder—I told him I was a police-officer, and should arrest him for stealing a van, value £8, with a man named Guest, then in custody—he said, "All right"—he was conveyed to Leyton.
   Cross-examined.  I was not present when Guest pleaded guilty—I do not know why sentence was postponisd. FRANCIS LOVE.    Cross-examined.
