things-Abigail Abrahams was with him-the prisoner was at home-I asked
Mr. Dashwood to go to her, but he refused - I went and told her that Mr.
Dashwood was there--she said, "If Mr. Dashwood has anything to say, let

been robbed-she said, "I know nothing about it" -Mr. Dashwood went out

tell me how you came by it?"-she said, "I bought it; but I have a sister
who has been keeping a gentleman's house; I did not like you to know it ;
I have been calling her my cousin ; I have been the means of getting her
away, and this is got up out of spite."
Cross-examined. Q. Did you see Mr. Dashwood again? A. Yes, he came
back, and my husband would not let either of the sisters go out, and that was
the reason they were all found the next morning-I said to Mr. Dashwood,
" When she came home she showed me a piece of satin, but she says she can
give a very good account of it"-he said, “I am very much obliged to you
for the information, we will search the room"-he took the policeman in, and
between two beds he found the satin-he said he did not know it, but his
wife did—I do not know whether Mr. Dashwood went to the station--only
one of the sisters lodged with me, but the other two came to see her on the
Sunday, and my husband would not let them go away till Mr. Dashwood
came--we waited till two o'clock for him that night, and he did not come
he came on Monday.
JOSEPH William BROOKS. I am a pawnbroker, at Battle-bridge, I pro-
duce a scarf that was pawned with me by the prisoner on Saturday evening,
21st July, for 38.
02E
Cross-examined. Q. You never saw her before? A. I might have seen
her, I cannot say-I am sure she is the person who pawned it-I recollected
her from the Saturday night till the Monday, when I went to the Police Court
-this scarf has been worn.
WILLIAM CLARK. I am assistant to Mr. Burgess, a pawnbroker. I have
a scarf pawned by the prisoner on Saturday evening, 21st July, for 78.
GEORGE COLLINS (policeman, N 59). I took the prisoner on Monday,
23rd July, in the back-room-I found this piece of satin between the bed and
mattress-I asked the prisoner, who was present, where she got it-she said
she bought it of a man in Gray's-inn-lane, a bargain, on the Saturday night.
Cross-examined. Q. Did Mr. Dashwood go to the station ? A. Yes-he
said he could not give her in charge, it was not his property, it was his
housekeeper's-Simkins has the blue dress, but he is ill-he is not here.
ABIGAIL ABRAHAMS re-examined. These scarfs and other things are my
property.
Cross-examined. Q. What house do you keep beside the one in Thornhill-
place ? A. A cigar-shop in Wych-street-I was there that night--there are
four rooms in that house besides the shop-Mr. Dashwood is servant to his
father, at 2, Holywell-street, and he is there all day-at night he goes to
Thornhill-place-there are two bedrooms at Wych-street-we have a lodger
there, and the other bedroom is mine-I am there now and then-these
articles were in the drawers in the room when the prisoner's sister went
to lodge.
