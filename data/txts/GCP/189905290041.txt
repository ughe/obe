WILLIAM PONSFORD (653 K). I was stationed at Canning Town, and
at 1!.15 on May 13th I was in Barking Road, and heard a police whistle
I was vut of uniform-I ran into Burnham Street, and saw the
prisoner running towards me, followed by Collins-I stopped him, and
asked him what he was running for—he said nothing-Collins came up,
and said that he had stabbed Frisby-I assisted Collins to take the
prisoner to the billiard saloon--we took him out by the back way, to
avoid the crowd, and took him to the station-I returned to Burnham
Street at 12.30 and found this pair of scissors (Produced).
Cross-examined. There was a crowd about when he was running towards
me - he was about 10 yards off when I saw him—I did not see him drop
anything.
