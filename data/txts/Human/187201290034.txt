I have been on them in the day, and have allowed the accountants on the part of the prisoners to work there—Mr. Moss has a representative there, I do not know his name, I have been there myself, and have requested the policeman to keep the key, because he said he could not give possession till this trial was over—I was not allowed to look at any books, only the com mon day-book—I have not searched for invoices of these transactions, I did not know they were wanted—I do not know Mr. Walmsley.
