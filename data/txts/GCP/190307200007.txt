Breaking and entering the shop of N. Thierry, Limited, and stealing &
pair of shoes and other articles their property. MOGUIRE PLEADED
Guilty.
Mr. FULTON Prosecuted.
CHARLES EAMES (468 H.) On June 23rd, about 10 a.m., I saw Brown in
Hanbury Street, Spitalfields, carrying a brown paper parcel-the paper
was torn, and I saw a new boot protruding-I stopped him and looked
in the parcel, and found three left boots with lasts in them-I asked him
where he got them from, and he said he picked them up down the street-
I was not satisfied with that, and took him to Commercial Street Police
Station-I afterwards received information that a window had been
broken at Messrs. Thierry's shop, 7, Eastcheap, and some boots stolen-
Brown was then taken to the Minories and charged with the offence.
Cross-examined by Brown. You did not tell me you got the boots from
a man down the street.
