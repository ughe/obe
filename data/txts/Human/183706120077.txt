ANN SUTTON, JUN. I am the daughter of Samuel Sutton, and live in Corbet's-court, Spitalfields. On the 18th of May I was going up to my mother's room, the prisoner was in the passage, and inquired for a dress maker—I looked out of the window and saw her go out of the street door about a quarter of an hour after I saw her outside the door with my shawl la ing behind her—I told my mother, and went and found her with my shawl. ANN SUTTON, SEN. I am the wife of Samuel Sutton. I lost two shawl and a shawl from my bed-room on the second floor—I found the gown lying in the passage, and the shawl the prisoner had outside the door—she begged to be let go. NOT GUILTY.
