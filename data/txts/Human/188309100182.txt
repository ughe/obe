   Cross-examined by MR. FRITH . Nothing relative to this charge was found on Thomas or John Taylor—I saw Thomas Taylor in London at the rail way station with half a dozen to a dozen racing men going down, and afterwards with Cooper at Croydon—John Taylor I never saw before in my life—when I commenced this series of questions he was not in custody, I did not intend to take him then; Brushwood had not given the informa tion then—I was investigating the matter, and thought it a proper act—I did not argue with him.

WALTER DENNY( Detective Sergeant, Criminal Investigation Department ).
