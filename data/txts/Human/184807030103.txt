WILLIAM HAWES. I am a soap-maker, at Lambeth—I am partner with my brother. We have a banking account at Messrs. Lubbock's—our prirate number is "I. 172," which is printed on the check-book given to us—every check in that book is marked "I. 172"—this check is not out of our book—it is not signed by me or my brother, or by our authority.
