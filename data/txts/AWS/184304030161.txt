2 sovereigns, the monies of Jane Russell, her mistress.-2nd COUNT, stating
them to be the monies of John Holmes.
MARTHA HOLMES. I am the wife of John Holmes, a plumber, at Turn-
ham-green the prisoner was my mother's servant. On the Ist of April
she brought me a veil which I had left at my mother's on the previons even-
ing, and said she was going to her grandmother's- told her to call for a
parcel as she came back-after she was gone I put two sovereigns in a paper,
put that into a piece of net, and inclosed the whole in a large sheet of paper,
I put a pin at each end, and a pin in the middlewhen she came back I
gave it her, and told her to go straight to her mistress with it, and take care
of it, as there was something in it of consequence.

JANE RUssBLL. I am the mother of Martha Holmes; I live at Hatnmer-

there was something of great consequence there.
