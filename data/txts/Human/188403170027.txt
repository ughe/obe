   The Prisoners' Statement before the Magistrate. William Baker says: "I am innocent of stealing the birds. I bought them at the Feathers for 6 s ., and being very small birds I thought it was enough to give for them." Ware says: "I was not in this man's company. I went with his brother John to sleep at 11 o'clock in the loft, and at 12 o'clock William Baker came in." John Baker says: "I had been at work till 6.15, and went over to the Bull and stopped there playing. Then I went straight up the loft with Ware at 11 o'clock. I am quite innocent of any stealing. I was at work from 9 till 6.15. I never saw either one of the men. It was 10.57 when I met Ware."
   William Baker's Defence.  I am innocent of the fowls.
   John Baker.  I know nothing of the fowls. GUILTY.
   WARE then PLEADED GUILTY to a previous conviction of felony in December , 1878.— Eighteen Months' Hard Labour. JOHN and WILLIAM BAKER  — Six Months' Hard Labour each .

 418. ROSE LEVESLEY  (35) was indicted for the wilful murder of Maud Levesley .
   MESSRS. POLAND and MONTAGU WILLIAM Prosecuted. Upon the evidence of Mr. William Orange , Fellow of the College of Physicians of London, and Medical Superintendent of Broadmoor, the Jury found the prisoner was of unsound mind and unable to plead. —To be detained during Her Majesty's pleasure.
 419. EMANUEL TRUMAN  (75) and THIRZA ELIZA TRUMAN  (43) were indicted for the wilful murder of Elizabeth Ann West , by pro curing her abortion.
   MESSRS. POLAND and MONTAGU WILLIAM Prosecuted; MESSRS. LOOKWOOD , Q.C., and AURAHAMS defended the prisoners.
   The evidence is unfit for publication. GUILTY of manslaughter.
   EMANUEL TRUMAN .— Fifteen Years Penal Servitude.
   THIEZA ELIZA TRUMAN .— Twelve Years' Penal Servitude.
