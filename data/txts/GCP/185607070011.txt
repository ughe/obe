MARY ANN JOHNSTON. I was chambermaid at the Royal Hotel, Man-
chester, on Tuesday, 15th April. I recolleet Kingston sleeping there that
night-he slept in No. 40-I saw him that night examining various doors,
and I asked him what he was looking for he said he was looking for
No 40, his own room-I told him it was a flight of stairs higher up on the
Thursday morning, the day after he was given into custody, about 9 o'clock,
I was cleaning on the second landing, and found this pair of pliers
produced they were under the mat at the foot of the stairs coming down
from No. 40; I was lifting the mat up, and picked them up under it-
Mr. Edden's door was the second door on the left hand side from it; in
going from Mr. Edden's room to No. 40 you would pass over that mat.
Cross-examined by MR. COOPER. C. There were a good many persons
sleeping on the same landing, I supposef A. Yes, there are ten bedrooms
on the same landing all of them were ocoupied there were not many
strangers there there were ten persons on the landing above there is one
bedroom on the left of Mr. Edden's room, and two just opposite his door-
person going to either of those rooms would not be seen going to the left-I
did not hear the alarm that night.
