1321. JOHN ANDERSON was indicted for stealing 2 hammers, value
110.; and 1 gimlet, 1d. ; the goods of Edward Newman, his master.
John STIMPSON (police-constable K 50.) On the evening of the 10th of
June, I saw George Jones go into a marine store dealers shop in the Back-
road, St. George's, and offer there two hammers and a gimlet for sale-the
man refused to buy them--when I came out I met the prisoner about a hun-
dred yards off-in consequence of what Jones told me, I asked the prisoner
where he got them from-he said he found them in his master's yard, in the
dust.
