young man for some work, and would fetch him-I told him to go and be
left the shop-I went a short distance after him-he turned up Booth-street
--I saw a constable and directed him to take him-I gave the constable
the bad shilling and sixpence.
MICHAEL MAHAN police-constable H 79.) I took the prisoner, and

MR. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to the Mint. This sixpence

Prisoner's Defence. On Saturday evening I was not near the shop; I
