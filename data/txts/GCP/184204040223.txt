SABAH THRIFT. I am married, and live on the second-floor at No. 14,
Easton-street, Clerkenwell, in the next room to the prisoner-she lodged
there with the child for about nine or ten weeks I have heard the child
cry repeatedly-the prisoner used to go out between six and seven o'clock
in the morning, and return about the same time in the evening, and some-
times went out again after that I believe no one was left in the room with
the child while she was out-I have tried about three times to look into
the room when she has been going down stairs, to see whether there was
any one there, but she always shut the doorshe did not take the key
with her when she only went down stairs- I did not open the door to look
in-when she went away for the day she locked the door, and I had no
means of looking to see what was the matter with the child--I never re-
member any person being left with the child when she went out I never
heard her speak kind to it, whenever she did speak she called it a little devil
--that was when she has been going into the yard-it was a female child
-I remember the parish-officers coming to the place the child was in the

asked if I knew any thing of a little girl that would take care of a child-
old her I did not-I afterwards went with the child to the workhouse.
