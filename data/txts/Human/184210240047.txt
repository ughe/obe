FRANCIS KIRBY was again  indicted for stealing, on the   6th of January , 1 plank of wood, value I s  ., the goods of Charles James, Lord Bishop of London ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. CHARLES BALDWIN. I saw the prisoner in the Bishop's ground, carry ing this plank—it could not have been washed away—he was carrying it off the premises—it is the property of the Bishop Charles James Bloomfield. SAMUEL HAT. I was with Baldwin, and saw the prisoner carrying the plank off the field with another man—it could not be moved by the wash ing of the water.
   Prisoner's Defence  I did not get it off any where; I picked it up two hundred yards from the Bishop's premises; it had washed on the bank; I saw two or three more people picking up some pieces. SAMUEL HAT. It was nailed to piles driven into the ground—I had teen it not an hour before. ALEXANDER M'CASKERY. I am a policeman. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction—( read )—I was present at the time, and know him to be the man—I got it from the Clerk of the Peace for Middlesex. GUILTY.
