MICHAEL DOHERTY. I live in Bell-alley. I had the articles stated, in my box, I broke the key of it a fortnight or three weeks before the 6th of April, and I gave the articles stated, in charge of the prisoner's wife, who lived in the same house—I missed the trowsers, the coats, and other things—they are now here—the pawnbroker has them. JOSEPH JOHN GOODE. I am a a pawnbroker. I have two coats and a pair of trowsers, which I took in of the prisoner on the 6th of April, which he said were his own property—it was between four and six o'clock.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I pawned them; I went out that morning at six o'clock to work; the prosecutor took my wife and made her drunk; and when I came home I found him and my wife in the act of adultery. I went to the fire, took up the poker, and gave him a pretty good share of it. I went out, and when I returned I asked my wife for some money; the prosecutor said I might take some clothes, and make money, which I did.; and in the evening he got sorry about letting me have them; he asked me for the money, and the money was scattered between them. MICHAEL DOHERTT re-examined.  I did not permit him to pledge them, I did not know he had them. GUILTY. Aged 63.— Confined Three Months.

ANN PARKER. I am the wife of William Henry Parker, and live at Plais tow with my brother, Thomas Gethin. I have a daughter named Mary Ann, who is fifteen years old. Lothbury, to take to Plaistow—they were the property of my brother—I am not living with my husband, he is in Wales—this is the box and other articles which I deliver ed to Wood ( looking at them. ) JAMES WOOD.
