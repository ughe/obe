THOMAS ROBINSON. I am a director of the Robinson Printing Company,
of 1, Lombart Court-Lawson is my brother-in-law-our company has done
printing for him and for the Construction Company-Exhibit 27 was
printed by our company--the order to print it would be given by the
Construction Company; I cannot specify any one in person--we do not
appear to have any correspondence with regard to the order; many of
these instructions came through upon the telephone-I remember very
well that we printed this—the order was given, not to me personally, a few
days after the meeting-finding that on September 24th, 1901, we are
writing sending the proof personally to Lawson does not recall who
gave the order; most likely we should have instructions through the tele-
phone to print a certain article which appeared in the "Financial News"
of a certain date to put it into type--it is to Lawson we send it, and of
Lawson we make the inquiry as to what is to go on the front page with
regard to capital-after supplying a few preliminary proofs we printed
a thousand copies; the first lot sent were 250 copies, part of the thousand
--there was some error in them, in consequence of which the remaining
750 copies were not delivered ; as a matter of fact, they were put amongst
out waste paper, 250 copies being delivered about the end of September or
the beginning of October--we have our order books where the orders were
entered; we have a consignment book ; I have not that with me, but from
that consignment book I find that only 250 copies were sent; I cannot
tell you when to a day; we made a further delivery upon October 3rd of
50 copies, and evidently it would be between September 23rd and
October 3rd-we sent a proof on the 24th-there was a further consign-
ment of 225 copies sent a few days later, between the 5th and the 10th-
the 50 and the 225 which were afterwards sent were a second edition, with
the error in the first 250 corrected-the total delivered is 525—1 held
50 shares in the Construction Company.
