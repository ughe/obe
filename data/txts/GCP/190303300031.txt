Cross-examined. I gave the deceased the solution of potash about
fifteen minutes after she came in-I had difficulty in getting her to take

of the pain--the dose I gave her was one-sixth of a grain, which is &
medium one-the effect of the dose would last two or three hours--I do
not think one dose would cause hallucinations--people who take morphia
for some time get hallucinations, after systematically taking it I did not
