2373. MARGARET WATSON was indicted for stealing 1 book, value
1s. 3d., the goods of James Foulkes; and that she had been before convicted
of felony.
Thomas PHIPPS, I am in the service of Mr. James Foulkes; he lives in
the London-road-on the evening of the 27th of July I was standing at the
book-stall outside his door--I saw the prisoner take a book from the stall,
put it under her shawl, and walk away with it, I went after her-she stood
up against the Blind School, and threw the book over the palings into the
yard-I got over the palings, and got the book--this is it-it is my
master's.
Cross-examined by MR. CROUCH. Q. Have you other copies of this work?
A. Only one more-my master sells the books- I look after them outside-
the prisoner went away with the book in a contrary direction to the shop-door.
William PURNELL (police-sergeant M 5.) I took the prisoner, and produce
the book.
SAMUEL EASTMAN (police-constable V 182.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office-(read)-the
prisoner is the person.
Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Six Months.
