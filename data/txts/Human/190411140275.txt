ISAACS submitted that there was no case to go to the Jury against Hooley, as there was no evidence of a conspiracy between Hooley and Lawson to defraud the shareholders in the Construction Company; that there was no evidence that Hooley had aided and abetted Lawson as manager of the Construction Company to make false statements, for it could not be supposed that Lawson was manager de jure or even manager facto, and that all the witnesses directly connected with the Company had declared that Hooley had had nothing to do with its affairs. Lawson also submitted that there was no case to go to the Jury against himself, there being no evidence of his having conspired with Hooley to defraud Mr. Paine.
