 1699. SAMUEL GIBBONS was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of May , 25lbs. weight of brass, value 1 l  .; and 2 metal cocks, value 4 s  .; the goods of Thomas Lambert and another, his masters. THOMAS LAMBERT. I am in partnership with one other person in Chal croft-terrace, Lambeth. I have lost a quantity of brass and two metal cocks lately—to the best of my belief the brass and metal cocks produced are mine—I have no doubt whatever of it—I know some of the articles were made by us, and have never been sold—they must have been removed off the premises by some person—the prisoner hat been in our employ for three years up to the present time.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  I believe you have a number of workmen? A.  Between thirty and forty—here are several articles which I know were made by us—the whole produced are worth about 20 s .—I do not apprehend they could possibly be taken at the same time—I do not say it is impossible. SAMUEL HARRIS. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into custody—he gave his address—I went to his house and found the property pro duced.
   MR. BALLANTINE called SAMUEL WEBB. I was in the brass line—the prisoner was at that time my partner. We have had patterns of all these description of cocks—when I left the business of gas-fitting the prisoner bad part of the stock—it was about 1 cwt. at that time—I believe a great many of these articles are part of that stock.
   COURT . Q.  How long is it since you separated t A.  Between four and five years—the things I had were similar to these, all odds and ends—during the time we were partners he conducted himself honestly.
   MR. LAMBERT re-examined.  One of the things the witness speaks to is an article which is an invention of my own, and has not been in exist ence three years—the value of this is about 4 s .—if that alone had been found on his premises I should have charged him with stealing it (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 28.— Confined Six Months.
 1700. MICHAEL was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of March , 1 candlestick, value 1 l  ., the goods of John William Church ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. JOHN WILLIAM CHURCH. I am a retailer of beer, and live in Penton-street, Newington. On Saturday afternoon, the 12th of March, about three o'clock, the prisoner was in my house—I saw him go into the kitchen, deliberately take a candlestick off the shelf, and conceal it under his jacket—I immediately collared him—the policeman came up, and I gave him in charge—this is my candlestick.
   Prisoner.  I picked it up in the skittle-ground, where it was lying—I not capable of going about. Witness,  I have no skittle-ground.
