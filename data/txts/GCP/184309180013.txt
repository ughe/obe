2010. MARTHA CUMMINS was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of
September, 36 yards of printed cotton, value 158. 6d., the goods of James
Crickett.
THOMAS KAY (policeman.) On the 2nd of Sept., about eleven o'clock
in the morning, in consequence of information, I stopped the prisoner
in High-street, Whitechapel, with this bundle, containing this cotton-- I asked
what she had got-she made no answer-I asked where she got it-she made
no answer, but turned very pale--I asked where she was going with it, and I
believe she said she was going to get it made up-I took her to the station,
and asked if the apron it was tied in was hers-she said yes, and that she
had come out of prison that morning, where she had been committed from the
workhouse, and had no where to go.
