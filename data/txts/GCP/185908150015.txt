722. PERCY ILLINGWORTH (25), Stealing i handkerchief of
Richard Davis, from his person.
RICHARD DAVIS. I am & clerk-on 27th July I was walking along
Eastcheap about a quarter past 2 o'olock in the afternoon—I felt a sudden
tug at my side coat pocket; I put my hand and found my handkerchief was
gone-I turned quickly round, and saw the prisoner walking behind me with
my handkerchief wiping his nose-I immediately seized him by the collar
and said, “You have got my handkerchief "-he replied, " It is not your's; I
picked it up "-I detained him till a policeman came, and gave him into
custody.
Prisoner. Q. From which pocket did you lose your handkerchief: A.
From my left hand pocket-I can't swear what distance you were from me
- I don't know whether there were three men behind me I saw you wiping
your nose with the handkerchief-I may have asked some men if they saw
the handkerchief taken-I saw some men after I detained you. HE
NOAH ANDREWS. (City policeman, 542). I took the prisoner in charge, and
got the handkerchief; this is it.
RICHARD DAVIS. This is my handkerchief.
Prisoner's Defence. I picked the handkerchief up. I was about four yards
from him. There were three men standing facing him not a yard from him;
be asked them if they saw the handkerchief taken from his pocket; they
said, "No." They must have seen me take it if I did take it, he told them
to hold me, and I said I would stand till the constable came, and I did so.
GUILTY.-He was further charged with having been before convicted.
CHARLES BAKER (City policeman, 653). I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's conviction, (Read : Central Criminal Court; James Lawman,
convicted, July 1858, of stealing a locket and other articles, Confined Nine
Months.") The prisoner is the person.
Guilty.- Four Years' Penal Servitude,
