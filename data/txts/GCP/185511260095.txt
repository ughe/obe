WILLIAM QUINTON. I am a butcher and live in Leather-lane. I have
dealings with Messrs. Larner and Greatrex, meat salesmen-on 11th Nov.
the prisoner called on me and I paid him 6l. 178. 11d. on their account
this is the receipt; he signed it in my presence I had had former dealings
with him as the servant of Larner and Greatrex.

Cross-examined by Mr. ROBINSON. Q. You paid him with two country
notes 1 A. Yes, and he gave me the change he did not tell me he had lost
some money-he went away and came back in about an hour and wanted
to speak to me--he said he had lost a 101. note some time ago, and he wanted
to make the money up, and if any body spoke to me about this money, he
asked me if I would say that I should pay it on the following week-I said,
“No, but I, perhaps, shall not go that way till Saturday morning: if any
one should ask me, of course I shall tell them yes" -- this was on a Thursday
-I went to the market on Saturday, and I inquired whether my bill was
paid in they said it was not I did not ascertain that the two notes I
gave the prisoner were paid in---I have known the prisoner many years in
the service of Larner and Greatrex.

GEORGE BALL. I am a butcher, and live in Old North-street, Theobald's-
road. On 1st Nov. I paid the prisoner 102 on account of Larner and
Greatrex-he signed this bill-I paid him in gold.
Cross-examined. Q. I believe, some time ago, he told you he had lost
some money? 4. Yes, about four or five months ago that he had lost 101.
- he came to me the night before to borrow 51.-I said, "I can't do that, I
have not enough to pay my own bills, and, moreover, there is money still
back”-I told him I should pay 101. off the bill to-morrow morning--but,
before that, he asked me whether I would allow him to pay 74 to Mr.
Greatrex's clerk, and when he came in the morning, to allow him to have
101.—that was, in fact, to lend him 31.—I told him I would not, but I
would pay him 101, and he might do as he liked I have known him a great
many years, and never heard any thing against him before I have lent him
money-he now owes me about 21. 108., I believe-he borrowed that to make
up his account
