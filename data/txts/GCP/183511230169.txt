181. JOHN SAMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
November, 1 handkerchief, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Edmund Yalden
Knowles, from his person.
EDMUND YALDEN KNOWLES. On the 19th of November, I was walking
with a friend, near St. George's Church, Southwark; as I was stepping
off the pavement, I felt a tug at my pocket-I turned, and found my hand-
kerchief was gone-I accused the prisoner of taking it -- he was stooping;
I had caught sight of the colour of the handkerchief in his hand-
he denied it, and drew back- I requested my friend to call an officer-the
prisoner then ran offI followed him; and after a short chase, I came up
to him, and just as I was about to take hold of him, he pushed the hand-
kerchief down an iron grating, near the Town Hall --A woman took it up,
and gave it to me this is it.
Prisoner. Q. Did you see any person running besides me? 4. No; I
saw it in your hand.
JAMES ANDREWS (police-constable M 45.) I assisted Walsh, with the
prisoner, to the station-house-I found on him two handkerchiefs, a
tobacco-box, a knife, and some duplicates.
Jonas WALSH (police-constable M 111.) I produce the prosecutor's
handkerchief-it has been in my possession ever since.
Prisoner's Defence. I was walking along, and felt the handkerchief
thrown against my feet-I picked it up the prosecutor came, and said I
had taken his handkerchief-I denied it, and walked away---he called
"Stop thief," and I walked a little further.

182. ELIZABETH WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the
14th of November, 2 pairs of half-boots, value 11s., the goods of William
WILLIAM SAD. I am a boot and shoemaker, and live at Wandsworth.
On the 14th of November, I lost two pairs of half-boots out of my window.
SAMUEL JAGO. I am a police-constable. On the afternoon of the 14th
of November, I saw the prisoner near the prosecutor's shop-I watched
and saw her go in with two pincushions, and come out again with only one
- I did not see any boots-- I then received information from the prosecutor
that he had found a pincushion in his shop-I had suspicion, and followed
the prisoner- I took her, but did not find any boots on her.
Prisoner. Q. Where did you take me? A. In the public-house, about
five o'clock.
