Q. How much money did you pay under that? 4. 61., and 11. 18. for

Cross-examined by Mr. CHARNOCK. Q. Do you know how much was
paid for engrossing this document? A. He told me 1l. 1s. was paid-I
never saw this paper (looking at one)I do not recollect that he told me he

ing-boards, and other things-the signature to this paper (looking at one)
looks like my son's I know my son brought home a book called "Salisbury
Cathedral" - I do not know whether he was charged 21. 10s. for it-it was
not worth that I did not see the receipts.
Q. Did he bring home some architectural precedents? A. Yes; and
some 2d. books-he brought home a drawing-board- I do not kuow whe-
ther be had any Indian rubber-he had no pencils of Chadwick, he had
bundles of pencils of his own.
