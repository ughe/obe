1422. ELIZA WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
May, 1 gown, value 178.; 1 petticoat, value 1s.; 1 quilt, value 6d.; 1
candlestick, value 3d.; 1 knife, value 2d. ; 1 fork, value 1d. ; 1 cup, value
1d.; 1 saucer, value 1d. ; 1 pair of shears, value 4d. ; 1 pair of suffers,
value 3d.; 1 veil, value 1s.; and 2 sheets, value 1s. ; the goods of Maria
Farmer.
MARIA FARMER, I am single, and live in St. Giles. The prisoner
lived with me-on the 3rd of May she desired me to go for some tea for
her--when I returned, my door was locked, and the prisoner and key gone
-I missed the articles stated in the indictment-I gave information to the
police.
SARAH Power, I am the wife of Thomas Power, of No. 3, Bow-yard.
I have known the prisoner about a fortnight-on Monday, the 3rd of May,
she came to my room with the prosecutrix's property, and asked me to
take care of it till next day, as she was going to see her uncle-next day
the prosecutrix came and claimed them.
WILLIAM West (police-constable F 106.) I took the prisoner-I found
the gown, pocket, and bonnet, on her person.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
GUILTY. Aged 22 --Confined One Month.
