three witnesses in Court, but they only heard one I did not wish to drive the
case for the Magistrate to send it to trial-I wished him to give them a repri-
mand-he did not discharge them-he said, "I do not discharge them, I leave
it to you to indict them at the Sessions."
Q. Did they immediately afterwards bring an action against you for false
imprisonment? 4. Yes, but that was only to bounce it over me, that I should
not go to the Sessions--the Magistrate said, " You can take the rule, and do
as you like"-I said, "No, I won't, they may contend that I altered the rule,"
and the rule has been in the hands of the policeman ever since the pri-
soners served me with a process- I dare say that was done to get rid of me-
the solicitor came and tried to laugh me out of it.
