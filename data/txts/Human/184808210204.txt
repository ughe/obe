WILLIAM BOOKER. On 26th July my sister gave me this half-crown—I saw it was bad—I went in pursuit of Leary—I saw him and Cooper, about ten rods off on the right, on the road to Lee—I followed and cave them in charge at the Tigier's Head—on going along the road they fell in conver sation with another man—I gave the half-crown my sister gave me, to the sergeant. JAMES JENKINS( police-sergeant, R  10). Booker came to me—I went to the Tiger's Head, and saw the prisoners in conversation—the other man had left hem—Leary was about to enter the Tiger's Head—I took him and Cooper to the station—I found this half-crown in Leary's hand—I was about to search Cooper—he took his right hand from his pocket, and in it was this half-crown—I bent the two half-crowns between my teeth, and found they were bad—I found a penny on each of the prisoners—this is the half-crown I received from Booker. CALEB EDWARD POWELL. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint. These three half-crowns are all counterfeit, and have all been cast in the same mould.
   LEARY  — GUILTY. Aged 37.
   COOPER  — GUILTY. Aged 44. Confined One Year .
 2027. HENRY WILSON , stealing 2 shillings, 4 sixpences, and 4 groats, the moneys of Frederick Andrew Hall ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months .
 2028. ANN BROWNE , stealing 2 sovereigns and two half-sovereigns, the monies of Francis William Thorn . MARTHA THORN. I am the wife of Francis William Thorn—the prisoner lodged with us in Plumstead-road. I had two sovereigns and two half-sovereigns in an egg-cup—the prisoner came on 22nd July—I was ironing—she asked me to let her iron a shirt, but I did not—my sister and her husband were up stairs—they did not come down—I went out for about ten minutes—when I came back the prisoner was gone and the money. JOHN JONES( policeman ). The prisoner came to the station, and said she could not rest easy; she had robbed her landlady of two sovereigns and two half-sovereigns.
   Prisoners Defence.  I took it and gave myself up; I had no home to go to; my husband said he would pay the money back again. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined Six Months .

About four o'clock, on 25th July, I missed these boots—I saw them safe at eight that moruing. GEORGE WILLIAM WATERS.
