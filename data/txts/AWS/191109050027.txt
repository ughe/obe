BENHAM, Frederick (38, labourer), pleaded guilty of feloniously
marrying Daisy Hannah Elizabeth Pusey, his former wife being then
alive.
It was stated that prisoner after passing through the form of
marriage with Mrs. Pusey (a widow), had lived with her as a lodger,
and had not cohabited with her. He had allowed his wife 16s. a week.
Sentence, Three days' imprisonment.
