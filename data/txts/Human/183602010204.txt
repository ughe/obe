 580. JULIA CONAN and ELIZABETH POTT was indicted for stealing, on the   29th of January , 1 pair of shoes, value 2 s  ., the goods of Charles Casey . HARRIET CASEY. I am the daughter of Charles Casey, a dealer in shoes, in Brick-lane, Old-street. On the 29th of January, the prisoner came to our shop to book at some books. I showed them some—they tried a pair on, which were too narrow, and while I went to get another pair I missed a pair, which were found in Conan's basket—I saw them found on her—these are them—I had not sold them.
   Cross-examined by MR. ALLEN . Q.  You did not see Conan take them? A.  No—the other one came to buy the shoes—Conan had a pocket-handkerchief in her hand, and a basket on her arm, which she took off and put on a chair—when the shoes were found, she said they were put into her basket—she did not say they came there by accident. WILLIAM DUGDALE. I am the shop-boy. I saw the prisoners come in—I saw Conan take a pair of shoes from off the mail behind her—she blew her nose, and while she was doing it, put them into the basket—Pott was trying on boots at the time, with her back towards her.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Then she could not see what the other was doing? A.  I do not think she could.
   Cross-examined by MR. ALLEN . Q.  She took them down openly before you? A.  No—Pott came to purchase shoes—Conan did not want a pairs—he had a basket in her lap as she was sitting in a chair—I did not see her put the shoes down to see how long they were for her feet—she did not offer 4 s . for them—one of the young men told Conan she had a pair in her basket—she said she had not—the basket was opened, and the shoes found in it.
 (Ellen Trussell, of Clerkenwell; Mary Aldwin, Shoreditch; Elizabeth Letherdale, Golden-lane; John Brown, marble-publisher, White Cross street; and Patrick Herbert, labourer, Duke-street, Manchester-square, gave the prisoner Conan a good character.)
   CONAN— GUILTY. Confined Three Months. POTT— NOT GUILTY.
