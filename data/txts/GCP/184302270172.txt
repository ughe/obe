HUMPHERY, Mayor.
980. JAMES BOND was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of Feb., 2 na
bacco pouches, value 1s., the goods of John Evans.
JOHN EVANS. I am a tobacconist, and live in Munster-street, Regent's
park. On the evening of the 21st of Feb. the policeman brought the prisoner
into my shop-I examined, and missed two tobacco pouches-be produced
them to me-these are mine.
THOMAS KENDALL (police-constable S 69.) I had been watching the
prisoner. I took him into the shop, and found these pouches in his trowsers'
pocket.
Prisoner. I bought them of a young man at the window. Witness. There
was no one with him.
GUILTY. Aged 14.-Confined Three Months.
981. GEORGE TABRAR was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of Feb.
1 wheel, value 10s., the goods of Daniel Coales.
DANIEL COALES. I live in Gray's-inn-lane, and am a smith. I missed
wheel from a store-cellar-I found it at the station--this is it-I have the
mill belonging to it.
John RUSSELL (police-constable G 165.) I found this wheel in the pos-
session of the prisoner on the 11th of Feb.-he stated that it belonged to
a lathe he had.
Prisoner. He did not find it on me. Witness. He was wheeling it
along the street-there were three of them, and they took it by turns to
wheel it.
Guilty. Aged 23.-Confined Three Months.
982. JANE WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of Feb,
26 yards of lace, value 21., the goods of John Nott.
ANN CHANDLER. I am in the service of Mr. James Nott, who keeps a
lace shop in Upper-street, Islington. About four o'clock on the 16th of
Feb. the prisoner and another woman came into the shop-the other woman
asked me to show them some quilling net, and I showed it them in a box-
cut off a yard and a half at one price, and three yards at a halfpenny a yard-
the boxes were then put away-the other woman then asked to look at some
lace-I showed them a variety of lace the prisoner and the other both looked
at it, but none of it suited-while they were deciding, the prisoner took some
lace-I saw her make a movement, and put it under her shawl-I am sure
I saw the lace under her shawl, and I missed three cards of it, but I said no-
thing—the other woman then decided on one, and bought a yard and a half
at 18. a yard-- 1 then cleared all away, and the other woman offered me :
sovereign to give her change, and take 28. for what she had bought-there
was not sufficient change in the till, and I gave it out of my pocket--the
prisoner remarking that she had nothing but notes and gold about her, or she
would lend the other a small sum-after I had made up the parcel, and
given the change, I desired the lad to take the three cards of lace from under
the prisoner's shawl, and I saw him do so-the prisoner said, “I humbly beg
your pardon, Miss," and then I let them go-she had taken the cards of lace
from the counter-she had a bad hand-it was wrapped up in a handkerchief
-she leaned that on the counter, and her shawl was over that hand-she
made a movement and took them-if the cards had been knocked down ac-
cidentally they would have fallen-they remained under her shawl about a
quarter of an hour.
