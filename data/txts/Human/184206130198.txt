 1932. JAMES STEWART was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of June , 1 copper, value 7 s  ., the goods of John Abbott , being fixed to a building,—2nd COUNT, not stating it to be fixed. JOHN ABBOTT. I keep the Harrow public-house, at Poplar. I missed my copper—I believe this one now produced to be it—I have no marks on it—it exactly fits the brickwork—I gave the prisoner no authority to take if. SUSANNA ABBOTT. The copper was fixed at our house—I did not authorize the prisoner to remove it, or mend it—I saw it safe last on Tuesday, the 31st of May.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you not admit before the Magistrate that you might have given me leave to take it? A.  No—I cannot recollect that I ever did—you have several times done small jobs for me—yon have sometimes taken tins when I told you. CHARLES AGAR( police-constable K  211.) About ten o'clock at night on the 1st of June, I stopped the prisoner with this copper on his shoulder—he said he bought it for 1 s . 6 d ., of a lad—I said he must go to the station—he then said it was a job, Mrs. Abbott knew he had got it, and had spoken to him about two months ago.
   Prisoner*s Defence.  She told me three times within the last six or seven months, that she wished the copper to be done; I said I meant to take it away that night, and I understood her to say, "Very well." GUILTY. Aged 32. —Recommended to mercy.  — Confined Nine
 Months.
 1933. ANN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of June , 7 pence, and 5 halfpence, the monies of George Rossiter . SARAH ROSSITER. I am the wife of George Rossiter. Between seven and eight o'clock, on the 7th of June, the prisoner came into the bar, and called for half-a-pint of porter—I served her, and put some money into the till—I then went into the bar-parlour, and heard money jingle—I went back, and saw her hand in the till—she dropped a halfpenny on the counter, and put her hand into her bosom—I sent for a policeman, and 9 1/2 d . was found in her bosom. SARAH INGRAM. I heard a noise of money—I came and searched the prisoner, and found 2 1/2 d . in her bosom, and 7 d . in her hand.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was very poorly, and met a woman who gave me half-a-pint of beer; a woman put the money into my bosom, which I had in change for a shilling. GUILTY. Aged 62.— Confined Three Months.
