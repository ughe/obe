October, 12 knives, value 11s. ; 12 forks, value 53.; 60 spoons, value !

knowing them to have been stolen ; against the Statute, &c.
CHARLES WEATHERLEY. I keep an ironmonger's shop in High-stret
Islington. Thomas Goulding was in my service- received information
and gave him into custody-I then proceeded to his mother's lodging
and, after searching some time, we found these various articles in her cup
board--the apartments were occupied by the prisoner as well as his mother
-I found, at Mr Smith's, six tea-spoons, one pepper-box, one flat-iron,
and 2 pairs of scissors - I am able to swear to the whole of them.
Thomas Goulding. The curling-irons are not his--I bought them and the
spoons- I paid for them at so much a week. Witness. He had two or thre
articles, but I do not recollect that he paid for any of them-the pepper
box, and scissors, and knives and forks, I can positively swear to as a
having been sold.
HENRY ALLAN (police-constable N 250.) I went with the prosecutor te

I believe the prisoner's daughter-she ran back directly-I followed ha

me there was nothing in that cupboard - I had known her so many years,
and she pretended to search herself, which put me off my guard - I told
her I must search, and then I found these things under some sauce-pans-
I found some duplicates in the place, relating to other property, some unde
pots and kettles.
