   Cross-examined by MR. RIBTON . Q.  You know this place? A.  Yes—I go on duty at ten at night, and I ought to leave at six—this was half an hour before my time for leaving—I was about that neighbourhood all night—I came down Catherine Street and down to the canal—I went into Cathe rine Place, but not down Little North Street, that is not in my beat—the deceased's head was under this cross-stock of the chair, like this: the head was resting on the rail, the forehead part—I mean under it more than against it.
   COURT . Q.  How far does your beat lead you from this house? A.  I do not go nearer than Catherine Street, about 200 yards from the cottage—the extreme distance I go at night is 300 yards from the house, but my beat is a good deal longer working in and out—I never go further than 300 or 400 yards from the house—I heard nothing during the night. JAMES HORTON, M.R.C.S.
