 203. DAVID GREEN and JOSEPH GREEN were indicted for stealing 118 lbs. weight of fat, value 15 s  .; the property of John William Messenger , their master.
   MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution. JOHN WILLIAM MESSENGER. I carry on business in the Borough-market and in Rochester-street.
