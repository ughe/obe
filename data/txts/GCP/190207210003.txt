the property of Sir Robert Drummond Moncreiffe, from his person, having
been convicted at Clerkenwell on May 20th, 1895. Thirieen other con-
victions were proved against him. Five years' penal servitude.-(524).
JOHN THOMAS HATCHER (22), to forging and uttering an order
for the payment of 200 francs with intent to defraud. Six months' hard
labour.-(525). ALFRED CHARLES JOLLY (24), to stealing, while
employed under the Post Office, & post letter containing six stamps and
an order for the payment of 78. 6d., the property of the Postmaster General.
Nine months' hard labour.-(526) WILLIAM FREDERICK WHITBREAD
(26), to marrying Elizabeth Lily Livermore, his wife being then alive,
