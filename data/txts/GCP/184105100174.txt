MARY ANN BILL. The prisoner came to my house on the 18th of April,

1531. HENRY HYAMS and SAMUEL MARKS were indicted for
stealing, on the 8th of May, 1 purse, value 18. 6d. ; 4 sovereigns, and
shillings; the property of William Petheram, from the person of Charlotte
Petheram.
CHARLOTTE PETHERAM. I am the wife of William Petheram, and lite
in West-square, Lambeth. On Saturday night, the 8th of May, I went to
a fishmonger's stall near the Marsh-gate, to make a purchase I saw the
two prisoners standing by me, I had my purse out to pay for what I had
bought-it contained four sovereigns and three shillings-Marks looked
very hard at me-he put his hand across me, and took up the crab that
I had bought, and felt the weight of it—he then put it down, and offered
6d, for three small ones- I tied up my purse, and put it into my pocket-
1 turned from the stand, and in a moment I felt Hyams's hand in my
pocket- I turned round and told him he was robbing me—the prisoners
were standing together-I did not see them talk-Hyams said, "I am not"
-I felt my pocket, and my purse was gone-he ran from me, and the mob
secured him in a shop-Marks came over, and tried to defend him, and get
him from me-he said he was not the person who had robbed me-I am
sure his hand was in my pocket-the purse has not been found there
were several more with them- they are the only two I can swear to.
Cross-examined by MR. Doane. Q. This is a great thoroughfare, is it
not? A. Yes-- it was about eight o'clock in the evening-there is a kind
of market there I never went to any other stall-there were more near to
that I did not talk to the prisoners- I thought they both looked suspi-
ciously at my purse, and I tied it up, and put it into my pocket-Marks!
hand was not in my pocket--I never lost sight of him-Marks vindicated
Hyams, and said I ought to let him go-I did not consent to that.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Were there others standing
by? A. There were, but I did not notice them-I felt Hyams's hand in
my pocket, and turned and spoke to him I had a shawl on, not a velvet
mantle-my pocket was behind-I saw Hyams's hand inside my dress.
-I did not take him-I was in hopes he had not taken my purse till
found it was gone.
