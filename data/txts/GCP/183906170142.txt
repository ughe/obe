1939. JANE JESSEY OWEN was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of
June, 2 blankets, value 11s.; 1 pillow, value 5s.; and 1 sheet, value 45.;
the goods of Oliver Horton Smart.
CAROLINE SMART. I am the wife of Oliver Horton Smart-we live in
Ann's-court. The prisoner took a ready furnished lodging of us for three
weeks--on Tuesday evening the servant said something I went up, and
all the things were gone-the prisoner was sitting in a front room-she
owed me some money.
John Unwin. I am a pawnbroker. I have a pillow pawned by the
prisoner on the 12th of June, in the name of Jane Owen.
ALFRED WALKER (police-constable E 43.) I was called on the 19th, and
took the prisoner to the station-house--nothing was found on her, but I
took her bag, and found the ticket of the pillow in it-I came down, and
asked her for her keyshe gave it to me- I found three duplicates relating
to this property, besides the one in the bag.
ROBERT MITCHELL. I am a pawnbroker. I have two blankets and a
sheet pawned by the prisoner.
(The prisoner pleaded poverty.)
GUILTY. Aged 33.- Recommended to mercy by the Jury--Confined
One Month.
1940. THOMAS FIELD was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of May,
1 ring, value 6l., the goods of James Hedgman, his master.
MR. THOMPSON conducted the prosecution.
James HEDGMAN. I am proprietor of the National Baths, High Hol-
born. About five or six weeks ago I was taking a tepid bath-I put an
emerald and diamond ring on a seat of my box-I swam about ten mi-
nutes or a quarter of an hour and returned to my box, and when dressing
I looked for my ring-it was not to be found my room was quite separate
from the other rooms, there being a bolt, of which I availed myself-the
prisoner was in my employ, and being the principal man in care I called
him, and told him to search for it-he came and searched for it, and said be
could not find it-he might have entered the room while I was in the water
without my seeing it-neither of us could find it-I heard nothing of it
except in some matters of condolence or sorrow on the part of the pri-
soner several times-he remained in my service for some time-- I called
him into a private room and charged him with embezzling some money-
and Mr. Ball began searching him--we found a knife and a purse-I saw
him take something wrapped in paper from the purse-he turned his back
on us, put it into his mouth, and made a violent effort to swallow it-Mr.
Ball collared him, and pulled him across his thigh to endeavour to chake
him-the prisoner then took the ring from his mouth with his own hand and
dropped it.
