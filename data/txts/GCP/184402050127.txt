659. JOHN BROWN was indiqted for unlawfully uttering counterfeit
coin.
Messrs. Ellis and HUDDLESTON conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES Bryson. I keep the Two Brewers public-bouse, Brewer's-green,
Westminster. About three, on the 11th of Jan., the prisoner came for a glass of
gin--I gave it him-he fumbled about his pockets, and pulled out a penny-I
said it would not pay for it-be then felt all his pockets, took up the penny,
chucked down a bad shilling, and said, “That will pay for it" -I took it up,
bent it, and flung it at him-it fell on the floor-he picked it up, and went
out-he came again, about ten minutes after, for a pint of beer-1 drew it-
be took another counterfeit shilling out of his pocket, and threw it down on
the counter-it was a straight one-I took it in my hand I called my man
to get a policeman-the prisoner went out-I followed bim, and he was taken
-I saw the officer search him, and a good many counterfeit shillings were
taken from his mouth-I gave the officer the second shilling that the prisoner
gave me.
RICHARD COLLIER (police-constable B 110.) The prisoner was given into
my custody-I found he had got something in his mouth-1 pinched his
throat, and got nine shillings out of his mouth, and got this one shilling from
Mr. Bryson.
Mr. John Field, I am inspector of coin to the Mint. These shillings
are all counterfeit, and all cast in one mould.
GUILTY, Aged 52.-Confined One Year.

GEORGE BUTCHER. I am servant to Mr. Marshall. I took the coals, and
4s. 6fd. change, to Wilstead-court-they said it was not there I was coming
back-the prisoner called after me from a little court close by, and said he
made a mistake, I had better give him the change, and he would give me the
five shilling piece, he wanted to go and get a box of congreves--I gave him
the change, and he gave me a bad five sbilling piece-I told him I must try
it, which I did with my teeth, and a piece came off-I said it was bad-he
took no notice, but ran off-I took it home, and my master held it, while I
marked it.
