 1350.  SUSAN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of May , 9 stockings, value 2s.; 1 box-iron, value 1s., and 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of Joseph Prince.
   FRANCES ELIZABETH PRINCE. I go out charing; my husband's name is Joseph; I have known the prisoner for two years - I have employed her the last two months to take care of my children while I was out; I missed some odd stockings out of a box to which she had access - I spoke to her about them; she said she had taken them; I never allowed her to take them - I think she took the best of them; I told her if she would give me them back I would not hurt her - she told the officer where they were.
 RICHARD COLLINS (Police-constable G 124). I went to a house in Playhouse-yard, by the prisoner's direction, and found these stockings and this box-iron.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. The stockings I sold.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1351.  WILLIAM ASPREY and WILLIAM EDWARDS were indicted for stealing, on the 4th of May , 4 1bs. of lard, value 3s., the goods of William Pitt.
 SARAH WOOD. Mr.  William Pitt keeps a butter-shop in Kingsland-road. On the 4th of May, about half-past nine o'clock in the evening, I saw the prisoners loitering about his shop - I watched, and saw Edwards take a bladder of lard; Asprey stood on the other side of the shop - they ran off together; they did not go into the shop; I gave information, and they were taken with the lard - I am quite sure of their persons.
   JOHN PITT. Wood gave me information - I went out, and saw Asprey running; I took him - this lard was brought back, which is my father's.
   WILLIAM SMITH. I am an officer. I was on duty, saw Asprey running from the shop, and took him into custody.
 FILDER HARRIS (Police-constable N 228). I saw Asprey and another, who I believe was Edwards, standing near the prosecutor's shop - in a few minutes they were gone, and the lard likewise.
   JOHN COLLINS(Police-constable N 241). I took Edwards with the lard a quarter of a mile from the shop.
   WILLIAM PITT. I know this bladder of lard by a mark on it.
 ASPREY -    GUILTY. Aged 18.
 EDWARDS -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 1352.  JAMES BRANGROVE was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of April , 2 spoons, value 18s., the goods of Francis Gray, Lord Gray in that part of the United Kingdom called Scotland.
   JOSHUA BEALE. I am in the employ of my father, who is a watchmaker, at Twickenham. The prisoner brought a dessert-spoon to us on the 21st or 23rd of May; as soon as I found whose it was, I took it to Lord Gray - he came again on the 25th, with part of a spoon, which I gave to the constable; I know the prisoner, and am sure he is the person - I had seen him several times.
   RICHARD BEGEN. I am an officer. I took the prisoner; I asked what he had done with the remainder of the spoon; he said, "Buried it in Mr. Williams' garden;" we went there, and found this part of the table-spoon.
   SAMUEL PATTERSON. I am butler to Francis Gray, Lord Gray; he lives at Twickenham; the prisoner's mother was in his employ - I have seen the prisoner there; I know this property to be Lord Gray's,
 Cross-examined. Q.What was his mother? A. She works in the garden, and sometimes comes into the kitchen- she may have taken them; the crest has not been erased.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Confined Three Months.
 1353.  JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of May , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the property of Thomas Slater , from his person.
   THOMAS SLATER. I am a cabinet-maker. I was at the corner of Great Queen-street, at half-past eight o'clock, on the 6th of May - I did not feel my handkerchief taken, but the prisoner passed me - I saw my handkerchief in his hand; I pursued, and took him with it - this is it.
   DANIEL HONE. I am an officer. I took the prisoner.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going down Queen-street, behind this gentleman; his handkerchief fell down - he came and took me.
     GUILTY. Aged 14. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1354.  JOHN BRADBEAR was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of April , 5 saws, value 23s., and 1 plane, value 3s., the goods of Joseph Skinner ; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
   JOSEPH SKINNER. I am a carpenter. The prisoner lodged with me in April - I went into the workshop when he left, and found three duplicates; I then looked over my tools, and missed a number of them; I had taken the prisoner in, and lodged him, and given him a few jobs, as he was in distress - he gave himself up to the officer.
 Prisoner. Q.Whose tools had I when I worked for you? A.Some of mine were lent to you, but you returned them.
