BURRELL, Richard (39, tinsmith), and JOHNSON, Robert (35,
dealer), pleaded guilty of breaking and entering the shop of Joseph
Harrison and stealing therein one safe and other articles, his property,
Burrell confessed to a previous conviction of felony at the Newington
Sessions on December 11, 1907, and Johnson to a previous conviction
of felony on February 21, 1907. Eleven previous convictions were
proved against Burrell and ten against Johnson. It was stated that
they had done a little work since their last discharge.
Sentence (each), Three years' penal servitude.
