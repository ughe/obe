399. WILLIAM BONE was indicted for a robbery on John Mills, on
the 5th of September, at Friern-Barnet, putting him in fear, and stealing
from his person, and against his will, 1 pair of scissors, value 2s.; 1 pair
of speetacles, value, 1s.; 1 tobacco-box value 4d.; 1 knife, value 1d.;
3 shillings, 1 sixpence, and the sum of 21d. in copper; the goods and
monies of the said John Mills.
JOHN MILLs. I am a drover, and live at No. 5, Broad-yard, Turnmill-
street. On the 5th of September last, about a quarter before six o'clock
in the morning, I was at Friern-Barnet-I was coming out of the park,
between Whetstone and Barnet-it is a field adjoining the road, called the
park had a lot of beasts there-before I was pulled down I saw a whole
lot of people standing against the gate-I knew none of them but the pri-
soner- have known him by sight for twelve months before-I had just
crossed into the middle of the high road, and was thrown down five or six
yards from the gate- had passed the men, and the prisoner followed me,
took me round the neck and threw me down right in the middle of the
road-he threw me in the road, and held me by the neck while the others
undid my things-he came from behind-I am certain he is the man, from
seeing him before-he blinded my face-I saw him as he caught hold of
me, and I saw him as I lay on my back-thirteen or fourteen of them came
round me-the prisoner knelt on my neek, and held his hands over my
eyes, while the others ransacked me, he choked me so, that I could not breathe
