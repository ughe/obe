SAMUEL DOBLE (Policeman, D 147). At 9 o'clock on this day, I went to
the back of the bouse, 12, Howley-place-I found a window bad been forced
up and the catch forced back, and I found marks of a knife right under
neath the window-I also found marks of feet without shoes in the closet,
and traced them over the walls at No. 18, I traced them to a place
where he had evidently been sitting down-I put my hand down in the
ground, just where the mould was loose, and found these two skeleton-keys
(produced).
Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing about them; I was coming down
the street at the time the constable took hold of me; the other one came
up and struck me over the head, and I said I would go quietly; they were
knocking me about so.
Guilty.--Confined Twelve Months.
