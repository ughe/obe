his going out early one Monday morning to fetch the second parcel of flour-
I was at home on the Sunday night before, and my husband was out--while
he was out, Mr. Peacock came; I believe about ten o'clock-1 had gone to

left an order with me, for my husband, for twenty sacks of flour-he said my
husband was to sign the name "J. Roberts," as before-he said he was to
go early in the morning-I told my husband, and he went in the morning.

PEACOCK-GUILTY of Uttering-Aged 40.-Recommended to mercy by

1056. JAMES JONES and JOHN KEEFE, stealing 1 pair of trowsers, and
1 coat, value 10s; the goods of James Rayford ; and 1 coat, and 1 pair of
trowsers, value 108.; the goods of Edward Rayford; Keefe having been
before convicted.
James RAYFORD. I atn mate of the schooner Richard Hill, lying in the
river. On 17th March, between two and three o'clock in the morning,
I got out of bed and went on deck-I tried to push the state-room door
open, 1 found a man stood in the doorway; he went on deck and went over
the stern-I sung opt "Police!" the police-boat went after them-I saw
them heaving some things overboard-I sent a boat and picked up this coat
and trowsers, across a chain in the water-they belong to Edward Rayford,
the captain--they were hanging in the cabin-these others are mine.
JAMES GIBBON (Thames police officer 61). I heard a cry of “ Police!"-I
took the prisoners in a skiff, they had nothing in the boat-I saw the
prisoners in the midships of the vessel, and saw them shove off from it-
there was no other boat there.
Jones. We were going across the water in a boat, and when we got half
way the police came and charged us; we had nothing.
JOSEPH SMITH (Thames-police-constable 17). I produce a certificate of
Keefe's conviction (read_Convicted April, confined six months.) - he is the
person.
