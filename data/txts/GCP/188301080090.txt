Cross-examined by Mr. GRAIN. I am only selling goods for Mr. Hume,

verbally to divide expenses to pay for the 'goods-I have not borrowed
money of him, only what I have paid back; 21., I think I have nothing
to complain of as to Stratford, because his bill has been paid by Mr.
Knight, the acceptor.
