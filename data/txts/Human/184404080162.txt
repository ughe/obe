 1213. JOHN WINTER was indicted for unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
   MESSRS. ELLIS and DOANE conducted the Prosecution , JOHN MORGAN, baker, Great Peter-street, Westminster.  On the 4th of April the prisoner came for half a quartern loaf—he gave me a shilling—I saw it was counterfeit, and bent it—I called a constable—while he was searching him, I heard something drop close by the prisoner—the constable picked it up—it was a counterfeit half-crown—I gave the shilling to the constable, and marked it. ROBERT JACKSON( police-constable B  30.) On the 4th of April I went to Mr. Morgan's, and took the prisoner—while I was searching him he dropped this half-crown close to his feet. MR. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to the Mint. These are both counterfeit. GUILTY. Aged 28.— Confined Six Month .
