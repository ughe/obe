JAMES MCARTHY. I am assistant to Mrs. Baun, a bootmaker, of No. 27,
New Bond-street. On Monday morning, 14th March, the prisoner drove up
in a Hansom cab, between 12 and 1 o'clock he came into the shop, and
asked for a pair of shooting boots-I served him with a pair-be tried them
on, and kept them on, and left his old ones-he told me to take them to his
lodgings, and a bottle of waterproof varnish as well he gave me a 5l. note
in payment. I went into the counting house to make out the bill-I asked
the prisoner for his name and address-he gave me the name of Charles Clif-
ford, Esq., No. 43, Essex-street, Strand-I did not write down the address
- I did not take down the number of the note; I gave him three sovereigns
and 48. change - I looked at the note, and saw the name of "Job Wilson
