Cross-eramined by Mr. O'BRIEN. Q. How long have you kept the
house? A. Not exactly thirty years, but I have been in the neighbourhood
that time Gill had nothing to drink with me when she called in the early
part of the day I took some brandy-and-water with her in the evening-I
did not have any gin and half-and-half-it might be eleven o'clock when they
came the second time they stopped some time in the kitchen with me-there
are a number of candlesticks in the house-I found the outer door shut on the
single lock in the morning-I always double lock it-the kitchen is on a level
with the outer door, and with my bed-room, and with the stair-case-it never
happened before that any one went into my bed-room-there is no sofa in the
second floor room-my bed did not appear to have been used, except by
putting the cash-box in it-I had no drink that evening but what I had with
them- this is the only candlestick of the kind that I have-the others are
tall large brass ones-persons who come to my house sometimes bring the
candlestick down stairs with them, but nobody brings them to my bed-room
-it is usual to leave the candle outside on the stairs--I am not in the habit
of using glasses--I never used these spectacles at all they do not suit my eyes
-they were left in my care by a gentleman ten months ago-he never came
for them-I do not know whether they would suit the sight of a young person
-Jones was taken on the Saturday after the robbery-I never mentioned the
loss of the spectacles before that-I never knew they were gone till they were
shown me I know them because one of the glasses is cracked, and by the
old case I know it by the mark on it-this piece of leather has been torn off
since I saw them last-it hung down ragged, but was not torn off-I never
noticed any stamp on it-it was a case as old as this-my servant drank a
drop of the brandy-and-water and did not like it, she chucked the rest away
she only tasted it I found ber fast asleep at six o'clock in the morning-I
shook her to awake her-she had not been drinking before that I know of
it was very difficult to awake her-she had not brought in anything to drink
-I do not know how many couple were in my house that evening-there
were a very few-they do not come without my knowing it-I receive the
money from all the parties-there is a fastening to my bed-room-it was shut
that evening with the key in the lock.
MR. BALDWIN. Q. Had any person who came that night been in your
bed-room? A. No, I am confident of that-they could not go there without
my knowing it, unless I was asleep-I was not asleep before, that evening-
there are not two entrances to the bed-room--the entrance is in the passage,
it is not necessary to go into the kitchen to go to the bed-room.
SUSANNAH WOODWARD. I am servant to Mrs. Barkley-on the 17th of July,
about ten o'clock in the evening, or a few minutes before, Gill and Jones
came to the house together-Gill said to me, what will you have I said
nothing-she said, "Have something"- I said, "If I have something you must
fetch it"-she fetched spirits and some half-and-half, which we drank together
-we all four partook of it--they went out together and returned in about half
an hour-about eleven o'clock they said, “What will you have to drink"-
somebody said brandy-and Mrs. Barkley herself fetched half a pint, 28. worth,
from the public-house-it was mixed by Gill and Jones--a person called me
to the street-door, and when I came back mistress had a glass by the side of
her—she drank some of it and said how sweet it was-Gill gave me a glass in
the kitchen--I said, “ Jane, what have you put in this brandy"-she said,
"Nothing" - I swallowed a good mouth full of it, and chucked the rest away
