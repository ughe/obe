WILLIAM LUMLEY (Policeman M 305). I was on duty on Wednesday

JAMES SMITH (Policoman M 206). I was on duty on the night of

239. JAMES HUMPHRIES (27), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit
coin.
Mr. POLAND Prosecuted.
ATHUR KENNEDY. I am 11 years old-on 11th December about 9 p.m.
I saw the prisoner at the top of Hale Street, outside the Prince of Wales
public-house--he told me to go and get half an ounce of tobacco next
to the chemist's shop, and wrapped something up in paper-I don't know
what it was-he said, "Don't lose it, Tommy ”- the tobacconist's was close
by-I went there and gave Catherine Edsall the florin in the paper-she
gave me the tobacco and 1s. 10d. change-I saw the detectives there in
plain clothes--when I went out the prisoner had gone about thirty yards
from where I left him I went and gave him the tobacco and change,
and he gave me a halfpenny--two detectives then came up and took him
- I did not know him before.
