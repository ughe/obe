that time I had gone in the name of Walter Thomas--I was sentenced

Sessions, for breaking and entering a counting-house at Brockley Railway
Station--in August, 1895, I was released on licence-in January, 1896,

the garden before I was, and standing against the fence when I got there
-I ran after her with the object of stopping her, but she reached the
garden before I got there—I brought her back, I did not throw her ; I
placed her on the ground-I placed a revolver to her bead to frighten
her; it was not loaded, and the trigger was broken, it was only an old
thing: I could not have fired it-it was broken before that night; it was
a rusty black one-I said I would shoot her, but only to frighten her-I

revolver in the first garden I came to after I fell down, and the watch
