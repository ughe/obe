 JURY to GEORGE ARNOLD. Q. Did you see the prisoner on the day mentioned? A. I have not seen him these three years; there is no truth in what he has stated- a young man brought a parcel one Sunday morning in October to go in my parcel to Torrington - I do not know what it contained; I do not know the young man's name.
     GUILTY. Aged 29. - Transported for Seven Years.
 246.  MARY BARRET was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of December , 1 sheet, value 3s., the goods of Robert Fox.
   CHARLES JAMES FOX. I am the brother of Robert Fox- he keeps the  Star, in Old Gravel-lane; this sheet hung out to dry, and I saw the prisoner, on the 11th of December, come down the passage, take it off the line, and walk away with it - I pursued, and took her with it, folded up under her cloak - this is it.
     GUILTY. Aged 55. - Confined Two Years.
 247.  THOMAS BETTS was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of December , 1 pair of trousers, value 1s.; 1 Guernsey frock, value 1s.; 1 plane, value 1s.; 2 books, value 1s.; 10 yards of ribbon, value 1s., and 1 shell, value 1s., the goods of Joseph Titford.
   JOSEPH TITFORD. This is my property, and was in my chest on board the ship Roslyn, in the West India-dock- I had not seen the prisoner on board that ship; he did not belong to it - I missed this property on the 2nd of December, and I saw it found on the prisoner at the Police-office; he was a stranger to me.
   JOHN BISHOP. I am an officer. I stopped the prisoner coming out of the dock with these things; I found on him one plane, two printed books, ten yards of ribbon, and a shirt, wrapped up in a pair of trousers - he said they had been given him by a lad on board the Roslyn; I took him there, and found the ship-keeper and a boy - they said he had been sent there, and was desired to go on shore again- I took him to the office, the prosecutor appeared, and identified the property.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 GUILTY. Aged 16. -    Judgment Respited.
 248.  MARY ANN BLOUNT was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of December , 7lbs. weight, of bacon, value 3s., the goods of Charles Clark.
   JANE CLARK. I am the wife of Charles Clark- we keep a chandler's-shop, in Marylebone. On the 9th of December the prisoner came in to sell a 7lbs. weight; I would not buy it, and then she took this bacon from a board by the door - my husband went, and took her six doors off with the bacon.
     GUILTY. Aged 36. - Transported for Seven Years.
 249.  JOHN CLARK was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of December , 1 pair of trousers, value 3s., the goods of Henry Gardner ; 1 case of mathematical instruments, value 6d., the goods of James Stride ; and 1 saw, value 2s., the goods of George Joad and others.
   HENRY GARDNER. I am a mariner. This pair of trousers are mine, and were taken from the steerage of the Jane and Catherine, in the West India-docks- the constable brought them to me on board the ship, and I knew them to be mine - I know nothing of the prisoner.
   ROBERT TAYLOR. I am a watchman. John George stopped the prisoner, and brought him to me at the West India Export-dock - I asked how he came in possession of the trousers - he said he took them from on board the Jane and Catherine, and the case of mathematical instruments likewise - this saw, he said, he found by the side of some logs of timber; he is a labouring man, but does not belong to the dock.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. - Confined Six Months.
 250.  GEORGE COATS was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of December , 4 pairs of shoes, value 10s., the goods of Joseph Johnson.
 JAMES ODWELL. I am a shopman to Mr.  Joseph Johnson, of Tottenham-court-road. I saw a hand take hold of the shoes - I ran from the further end of the shop, and took the prisoner with the shoes in his possession - I took him by the collar; these are the shoes.
     GUILTY. Aged 14. - Whipped and Discharged.
 251.  GEORGE CHAPMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of December , 2 bushels of onions, value 10s., and 1 sack, value 6d., the goods of Charles Richardson.
 CHARLES RICHARDSON. I am a  market-gardener, and live at Isleworth. I lost these onions from a shed belonging to my house, on Friday last - they had been transferred from where I had left them, into a hay-loft, put into a sack, and partly concealed under hay-bands and other litter; the prisoner was my carter, and was going to town the same evening with me.
   HANNAH SMITH. I am servant to Mr. Richardson. A little before one o'clock on Friday, I saw the prisoner put a prop against the hay-loft door, which I never saw before - I told my master I thought there was something wrong in the loft; I had seen the prisoner loading onions on the cart at five o'clock in the evening.
