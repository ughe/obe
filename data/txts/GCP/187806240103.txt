WALTER CALLAGHAN (Policeman M 134). I saw the prisoner running
very fast, about 150 yards from the Sun-it was raining fast and I had
my cape on-I stopped him; he caught hold of my cape and gave me &
twist in the leg and tried to throw me down-I ran him to another
turning, got hold of him again, and he tried to trip me up again-he
twisted me round but I did not fall-I took my cape off and took him in

florin in his hand and id. and two halfpence in his pocket, and a pawn
ticket-he said that he got the florin at the Elephant-I received these
two florins from Mr. McGwire.
