EDWARD WILLIAM ROGERS. I live at Norfolk House, Arundel Square,
Barnsbury--my mother is the owner of 288, Essex Road, Islington-no
person named Dixon was living there in January, February, or March last
the last tenant was not a satisfactory one, named Glover-he went away
on the night of 25th March, after clearing the house of the furniture, bell-
handles, and everything he was there from December to March-he had
taken the house for three years-he furnished it himself-I was never in
the house while he was there.
TIMOTHY CRONIN (Policeman Y 62). I took the prisoner into custody

charged with, and brought him to Moor Lane Station-he made a statement
to me about another matter, but said nothing about the bills of sale,
JOHN BREWER. I am a clerk in the Registry Office of bills of sale-I
have not referred to the register in reference to these two bills of salo-
it is for the public to search the books—it is not my business to do so.
The prisoner, in his defence, stated that the two transactions in question

Weston, and that both Dixon and Dyer were seen by Mr. Brown before the
money was advanced.
