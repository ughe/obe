SIEoN. I am a boiler-maker. I was with Southwick when
he took the prisoner-I saw the prisoner throw three keys into the gutter
and put his foot on them-the prosecutrix picked them up.
JOHN Cox. I am a policeman. I was fetched to Colchester-street about
a quarter before four 'clock that evening-I saw the prosecutrix, the pri-
soner, and several persons round him-the prosecutrix charged him with
being in her house, but she did not know whether he had taken anything
-somebody called out about the prisoner's left hand pocket-I searched
it, and found in it two keys and a chisel, and in his other pocket this
empty bag- have these other keys, which were given me by the prose-
cutrix-the prisoner tried to get away, and the mob was against his being
taken to the station-I tried those keys to the prosecutrix's room, and one
of them opened the door-I fitted this chisel to the marks on the box,
yhich had been broken open-it corresponds exactly.
Prisoner's Defence. I saw people running, I was behind them this
man put out his foot, and I fell down.
