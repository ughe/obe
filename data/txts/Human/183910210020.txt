2720. ABRAHAM BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of October , 1 handkerchief, value 3 s  ., the goods of Joseph Pollock from his person. JOSEPH POLLOCK. About a quarter-past two o'clock in the afternoon of the 17th of October, I was in Cheapside—I observed some one near me, and I found my handkerchief was gone—I saw the prisoner about two yards from me with it in his hand—he ran off, and I followed—he dropped it, I took it up, and secured him. WILLIAM FULCHER( City police-constable, No . 54.) I heard cry of the end Stop thief," and took the prisoner—he said, "I hope you will look over it this time."
   Prisoner , I had my own handkerchief in my hand. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Three Months—One Week Solitary.

   MR. PHILLIPS conducted the Prosecution . RICHARD GARDNER.
