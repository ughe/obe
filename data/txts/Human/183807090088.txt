 1755. GEORGE WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of June , 65 yards of printed cotton, value 30 s  ., the goods of John Goss Fleay . JANE SMITH. I am the wife of Samuel Smith, and live at Lewisham, in Kent. About four o'clock on the 26th of June, I was near the shop of John Goss Fleay, and saw the prisoner and another standing at his door, and presently the prisoner had this bundle—the other, who was a very re spectable looking man, took it off a bundle of prints in the lobby, and gave it into the prisoner's apron—they went off, one, one way, and the other, the other. JOSEPH JAMES WALTERS. I am a coachman. I was standing at the Centurion about four o'clock that evening, and saw the prisoner come along with a bundle—soon afterwards I saw the prosecutor's young man and the policeman—I gave them information, and took the prisoner with this bundle—it contained this print. JOHN CARTLEY( police-constable R  85.) I took the prisoner—the bundle was by his legs on the ground. CHARLES AUSTIN OWEN. I am shopman to John Goss Fleay. These are my master's—( looking at them )—I missed them when I received infor mation. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Transported for Seven Years.
   Before Mr. Sergeant Arabin .
 1756. ANN FREEMAN was indicted for stealing on the   3rd of July , 1 blanket, value 4 s  ., the goods of William Trodd , and that she had been before convicted of felony. WILLIAM TRODD. I am a tinman, and live at Woolwich. The prisoner took my lodging with her husband a month ago last Saturday—they passed as man and wife—they hired a ready furnished room at 4 s . 6 d . a week, and occupied it—the man started away on Saturday morning, but the woman remained till Sunday evening at half-past ten o'clock—I never received a farthing from them—the prisoner went without notice—I went into the room and missed a counterpane, a blanket, and a sheet—the prisoner was taken last Sunday about twelve o'clock—this blanket and sheet are mine. WILLIAM GREEN. I am a watchman. I received information that the female had run away from her lodgings—I heard she was at a house—I went and found her there—she delivered me up three duplicates.
