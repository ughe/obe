 2450. GEORGE NEWMAN was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   MESSRS. CHAMBERS and SHEA conducted the Prosecution. ELIZA PORLETT. I am the wife of John Porlett, a baker, living in Mint-street, Southwark. About half-past ten o'clock at night on the 22nd of September, the prisoner came for 1 d . worth of bread—I served him—he gave me a bad shilling—I gave him the change, and then saw it was bad—I told him so—he said it was not a bad one—I told him it was—he contradicted me several times—he said he was not destitute of a shilling—he took up the change, and was going to put it into his pocket—I told him not to put my change into his pocket; I could not afford to give him good money for bad—he then gave me another shilling—I said that was bad—he said it was not; that I did not know good money from bad—I said, "Give me back my change?"—he would not—I told him to go, or I would charge him with the police—he said he would not; I might do as I thought proper—there was no policeman—I beckoned to a neighbour out of the window, and was going to send him, but some other person fetched the policeman. WILLIAM HUMPHREYS( police-constable M  47.) I went to the shop—Mrs. Porlett gave me two shillings—I took the prisoner, and found on him a sixpence and 5 d . in copper—he said, "It serves me right, if I am hanged; I had a chance to get away, and would not take it."
   MR. FIELD . These are both counterfeit—I cannot say they are from the same mould.
 (The prisoner pleaded poverty.) GUILTY. Aged 65.— Confined One Year.
