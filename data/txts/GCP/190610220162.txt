ELIZABETH WELLINGS. I am daughter of the last witness and
sister of Benjamin and Joseph. On the night of August 25 I
was standing at the outside of the block. I saw Joseph strike
the constable with the gas barrel which has been produced. I
was close to him when he did it. As the constable was looking
at Seymour he struck the blow and dropped the gas-pipe and
ran away. He was in his shirt sleeves, with no boots on, and
he had a blue shirt with narrow white stripes in it. He ran
indoors, took his trousers off, and got into bed. I went in five
minutes afterwards and he was in bed. At the time Benjamin
was indoors; they were holding him down in the chair bathing
his head when Joseph hit the constable. When I found my
brother in bed Benjamin had gone upstairs, but I did not see
him go. I was trying to get the gas barrel out of Joseph's
hand, but he swung it around. He said the constable had my
brother on the ground, but he knew differently; he knew that
my brother was inside having his head bathed. He said,
