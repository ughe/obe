1178. MARY BARNETT was indicted for stealing, on the 12th
November, 11 sheets, value 41. 78. ; 2 shifts, value 12s.; 3 towels, value
2s.6d. ; 1 handkerchief, value 4s.; and 2 pillow-cases, value 68.; the
goods of William Clarke, her master.
ELIZABETH CLARKE. I am the wife of William Clarke, a tobacconist,
in Upper Seymour-street, St. Pancras. The prisoner was our charwoman
from November to March-I missed the property stated from a drawer, in
a box in my bed-room, to which nobody but her had access-I told her
what I had lost-she said she had not taken any thing-she afterwards
said she had taken and pawned them at Mr. Bailey's- I went there, and
found them-these are them-(looking at them.)
Thomas Philip PAYNE. I live at Mr. Bailey's. I took in some of
these things from the prisoner, and other persons took in the others.
GUILTY. Aged 40.-Confined Six Months.
