 1379. JOHN THOMAS was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   MR. CHAMBERS conducted the Prosecution . WILLIAM HAYMAN( police-constable B  136.) I went to Mr. Stanford's shop in Pimlico, and took the prisoner—Mr. Stanford said he had offered him a bad shilling, which be gave to me—he was taken to a police-office and discharged—Mr. Stanford is not here, but he charged the prisoner with it in his presence. CHARLES BUNDOCK. I live at Mr. Priest's dining-rooms, Lowere Marsh, Lambeth. On the 16th of April the prisoner came there, about twenty minutes past ten o'clock—he asked for 1lb. of buttock-of-beef—a young woman cut it, and he gave her a crown-piece—I saw it was bed—it was returned to him—he had no more money, and went out—I followed him, and saw him go to another shop, eight or ten house down York-street.
   Prisoner . I never was in the shop at all. Witness. I am positive he was—he had a snuff-coloured coat on. SARAH MOSLEY. I am the wife of Mr. Mosley, of Granby-street. On the 16th of April, the prisoner came for half a quarters loaf, and half a quartern of flour—he gave me a five-shilling-piece—I gave him 4 s . 4 d . change—I laid the five-shilling-piece down, and the moment he went out, I looked, and found it was bed—a man ran up to the door, and asked if a man had passed had money—I kept the prisoner in sight till I gave him to a policeman. WILLIAM BURROWES. I am a carpenter. I met with Mr. Priest's servant—I followed the prisoner and another man—they went on to Mr. Mosley's—I saw the prisoner go in there, and assisted in stopping him till the officer came. JAMES NEWMAN. I am called by Burrowes, and took the prisoner—the flour and loaf were on the counter—he chuched 4 1/2 d . on the counter, and said, "I sha'nt take that, I have passed 1 s ."—Mrs. Mosley gave me this crown-piece. SARAH MOSLEY. I gave him 4 s . 4 1/2 d ., and when he came back he threw down 4 1/2 d ., and said he had passed 1 s .—I do not know what became of the shillings I gave him, unless he threw in the road—it is a very dirty place. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to the Mint. This is a very bad crown-piece.
   Prisoner . I went into the shop for half a quartern loaf and half a quar tern of flour—there was another person came out of the shop just as I went in—I gave but 1 s ., and had but 4 1/2 d . change GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined One Year.
