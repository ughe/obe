 249.  RICHARD HART was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of December , at St. James, Westminster, 2 sheets, value 12s.; 2 gowns, value 2l.; 9 shifts, value 20s.; 1 pelisse, value 2l.; 1 pair of stays, value 5s.; 1 coat, value 30s., and 2 table-cloths, value 1l., the goods of Samuel Mitchell , his master, in his dwelling-house.
 SAMUEL MITCHELL. I am a  publican, and live No. 22, Broad-street, Golden-square, in the parish of St. James, Westminster. The prisoner was in my employ, and had slept in my house for about six weeks. On the evening of the 4th of December, between five and six o'clock, he came to the bar and asked for a light, to go up stairs; I thought it was to go and clean himself, as usual; he took the light, went up stairs, and I never saw him afterwards; in about an hour I went up stairs to clean myself, and found my bed-room door open - I went in - my wife followed me, to give me some clean linen - she opened the drawer, and missed her gowns; I then looked with her, and we missed the sheets and some damask table-cloths - she then missed some shifts; the prisoner never came back; next day we got most of our things back; I think no one of them is worth 5l. - the pelisse cost six guineas, when new, but is not worth that now.
   WILLIAM BROWN. I am apprentice to a butcher, of Tottenham-court-road. The prisoner came to my master's shop about nine o'clock on Tuesday night, the 4th of December, with a bundle; he called me out of the shop, and asked me if Phoebe Joyce was at home - she is my master's servant - he said he was her brother, and had come out of the country, and he would be obliged if I would give her that bundle; he pulled out a handful of silver from his pocket, and gave me 6d. for myself.
   JOSEPH COTTON. I am a pawnbroker, and live at Somer's-town - I produce some table-cloths and sheets, pawned on the 4th of December, between eight and nine o'clock at night - I cannot exactly say the time.
 Q. Are you sure it was not before six o'clock? A. I think it was about that time - I forgot myself before - the prisoner pawned them.
   BENJAMIN WEBB. I am an officer. I heard of this robbery, and went to No. 9, Ogle-street, and found this coat at the house of a man named Hampton, who was at the office, but is not here; on the next morning I went to Tottenham-court-road, and got this bundle from Phoebe Joyce - it contains nine shifts, two gowns, a pelisse, a pair of stays, and several other articles; I left the shop about ten minutes, and saw the prisoner on the other side of the way - I knew him, went up, and took him - he went quietly with me; I asked what he could think of himself for robbing so good a master; he said he was very sorry, but he could not help it; I found on him two duplicates - one of Mr. Cotton's, and another, and three keys, one of which easily opened Mr. Mitchell's bed-room door.
   PHOEBE JOYCE. I know the prisoner. Brown gave me this bundle; I gave it to the officer.
   ROBERT YOUNG. I am a pawnbroker, and produce a pair of sheets, pawned by the prisoner, on the 4th of December, in the evening.
 SAMUEL MITCHELL. My bed-room door was locked before; all this property is mine - it is worth 15l. or 16l. in all, and was all safe that day.
 GUILTY - DEATH. Aged 18. Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prosecutor, supposing it to be his first offence.
 250.  WILLIAM LONGMAN and THOMAS HOUGH were indicted for feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Henry Whitaker , on the 9th of November , at St. Leonard, Shoreditch, and stealing 1 hat, value 15s.; 1 coat, value 15s.; 2 waistcoats, value 16s.; 1 pair of trousers, value 12s.; 10 handkerchiefs, value 12s., and 1 set of harness, value 7l., his property.
   HENRY WHITAKER. I am ostler at the King's Head, Kingsland-road, in the parish of St. Leonard, Shoreditch. Mr. Milledge keeps that house; I rented a house of my master, adjoining the public-house. On the 9th of November my place was broken open - I had fastened it about nine o'clock over night - I was called out of bed between four and five in the morning, and found the door broken open; it appeared to have been kicked open, and the lock was forced off the door; I missed my coat, hat, two waistcoats, a pair of trousers, ten handkerchiefs, and a gentleman's harness, which was in the same room, under my care; I had seen them all safe the night before; I know nothing of the prisoners, and have found none of my property.
 Cross-examined by MR. BARRY. Q. Is your house in the public-house yard? A. Yes; it is next to the public-house, but there is no entrance from it to the public-house - I knew Hough by his coming to the public-house - I have drank with him - he was not a particular friend - I had seen him before. After locking up my house at nine o'clock the night before, I went to the public-house, and had a pint or two of beer, and I slept at my master's that night, as I had not a bed at my house; I had never slept in my house.
 COURT. Q. When did you begin to rent it? A. Two or three weeks before, but I had not got the things ready to go into it.
 Prisoner LONGMAN. Q. Were we not drinking together the night before? A. Yes, we were.
