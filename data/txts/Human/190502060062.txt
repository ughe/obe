I was instructed to touch the man who came to the bank—if the detectives had not said, u Which is the other man," I should probably have said, "Well, I am not absolutely certain that this is the man"—I should not have said that there were two men I was not quite sure about if I were certain of either of them—I will not swear that when touching the first man I did not say, "This is the man; "probably I did say that.
