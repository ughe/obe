Josiah LAWRENCE. I am a watch and clock-maker, and live in the
parish of St. Mary, Newington-on the 19th of June, about twenty minutes
after eight o'clock, I went into my shop, and saw the prisoner and another
standing at my window -I am quite sure the prisoner was one of them-
I saw him in the act of drawing two fingers from a hole previously made
in the glass, which had been broken-I saw it all secure a quarter
of an hour before-I had watches lying in my window-I went out
immediately, and they walked away the moment they saw me-I followed
them, and secured the prisoner-the other instantly ran away-I took
the prisoner back, and missed two watches, and afterwards another-
have never recovered them-I saw them there at five minutes after eight-
the watches were worth 301.
THOMAS WILLIAM RATLEY. I am shopman to the prosecutor-I left
at about four minutes after eight o'clock that night--the window was
perfectly safe and secure that night, and the three watches in the window,
I am positive.
Prisoner's Defence. I had oceasion to go to Camberwell, and was
returning home about a quarter past eight o'clock--the gentleman came
over, charged me with breaking the window, and took me to the station-
house-he said he had lost a watch, and about a week afterwards he said
he had lost two; at the next hearing he said he had lost three.
GUILTY of stealing above the value of 51. but not of breaking and entering,
Aged 19.-Transported for Life.

of Thomas Bax; and CHARLES MANN for feloniously and maliciously
inciting the said William Mann to do and commit the felony aforesaid.
