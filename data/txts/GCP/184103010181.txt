Court. Q. Is the shovel you have what you found on the premises ?
A. Yes I could discover no stains of blood on it-I found it in Mrs.
Hambrook's room, close by the stairs, packed away under a table.
Josian Smith re-examined. Q. You have heard what the policeman
says-you told us you got in by means of the policeman ? A. I did not
rightly understand what you said-Wiseman had gone home before that
I got in twice-it was the second time when the policeman came they
had locked the door after we got Wiseman out, and we waited till the
officer came--before we got Wiseman out I did not hear Carter say, “I
will have your life, or you will have ours."
MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Have you not been talking to Carter since you
left the box? A. No, I only said one word-I was asking him whether
he thought we should be able to go away to-day?
FREDERICK COTTON FINCH. I am a surgeon. I saw the prosecutor on
the morning of the 6th of February-he had a compound wound-there
was contusion and laceration of the globe of the eye, and loss of sight
altogether--the sight was gone when I saw it.
Cross-examined. Q. Did he attend to your instructions to take care of
it? A. On one occasion, four days after, I found him over a fire instead
of lying in bed.
MR. PHILLIPS called
ANN SULLIVAN. I am an unfortunate woman. On the night in ques-
tion I was at Mrs. Hambrook's house with Wiseman-I came down with
him, he wanted to pick up a quarrel with the prisoner, and made use
of very bad expressions, and said he would knock his eye out the
prisoner, being in liquor, got up, and said, “You had better knock my
eye out”-I went out, and when I returned I saw the prisoner and pro-
secutor hustling together-they forced the door open, went out, and tried
to break the fence down to strike the prisoner-they took a prop, broke it,
and Smith came out with one piece in each hand, and said, “Dean, come
out, you shall have my life, or I will have yours"Wiseman had been
struck, but I was not there then.
Court. Q. Why did you not stay to see what happened? 4. I had
occasion to go outside the door-I was there when the policeman came,
sitting by the fire-side-I was not there when they took Wiseman away-
I was coming up the court when they were bringing him down-that was
about twelve o'clock--the policeman came about half past twelve-they
came and knocked at the door, as if they wanted to come in again, and I
nearly had a blow on my head with the prop-they had been drinking there
nearly all the evening.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
