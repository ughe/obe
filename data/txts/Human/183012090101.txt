203. ELEANOR CONNOLLY was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of November , 15 yards of shirting, value 5s., the goods of Nicholas Morritt.
   JAMES SPENCER. I am in the employ of Mr. Nicholas Merritt, a pawnbroker. On the 23rd of November the prisoner came into his shop while the lad was busy taking down articles, and folding them up on the counter - I was busy at the end of the counter; I turned, and saw the prisoner -I said, "What do you want?" she said, "Do you take in beads?" I said Yes - she said, "I will go, and fetch them;" I had seen her two or three times in the course of the day, and suspected her - I looked through the window, and saw something under her pinafore; I sent the lad to bring her back, and found this fifteen yards of shirting under her pinafore - she told me a woman had given it her to pawn for 1s.; I know it to be my employer's property.
     GUILTY. Aged 12 - Transported for Seven Years.
 204.  JOHN FINNESSEY was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of December , 1 piece of wood, value 2s. 6d., the goods of John Joseph Hickmott.
   JOHN JOSEPH HICKMOTT. My father and I were taking down some premises, at the corner of St. Martin's-lane- this wood belonged to us.
 EDWARD CARPENTER. I saw the prisoner take a piece of timber from the corner of the ruins; he carried it out, and I followed him - he laid it down in Castle-court; I went down Tothill-street, and gave information-the officer took the prisoner, but the wood has not been found; there were eight or nine feet of it.    NOT GUILTY.
 205.  THOMAS HUNT was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of October , 4 coppers, value 3l., the goods of George Levett.
 GEORGE LEVETT. I am a  copper-smith, and live in Whitechapel-road. On the 22nd of October four coppers were taken from inside my door - I left them there safe at six o'clock when I went out, and when I returned they were gone; I did not see the prisoner till he was in custody.
   SAMUEL PRENDERGRASS. I took the prisoner on the 4th of November, about three hundred yards from the prosecutor's, between six and seven o'clock in the evening - the coppers have never been found.
   WILLIAM WARNER. On the 22nd of October I was coming up a yard, and saw the prisoner and two others standing at the corner of Mr. Levett's premises - I heard the prisoner say, "B-g-rd, if we don't have it;" and he went in and brought out the copper articles before him - the others lifted them on the prisoner's back, and he carried them off; I could not see how many there were of them-I knew the prisoner before; he did live with a butcher then - he sold fruit, and then he carried on a rum game.
 Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing of the coppers - the officer said a woman and a boy came, and said they saw me take them.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
 206.  MARY LONG was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of November , 1 hat, value 10s., and 1 box, value 6d., the goods of James Whitehead.
 JAMES WHITEHEAD. On the 24th of November I went into a shop, and put down a hat and a hat-box, while I transacted my business - I went out, and left them behind me, not recollecting them; I called at another place in the neighbourhood - I then remembered them, went back, the lady in the shop told me a person had taken them, and she could not detain them, not knowing whose they were; I have never seen them since - I know nothing of the prisoner.
   MARY ELIZABETH READ. I did not see the prosecutor leave the hat and box in my shop, but a few minutes after he was gone the prisoner came in and took it away in a moment - I concluded she had left it; she had been to my shop several times - the prosecutor then returned, and I found my mistake: the prisoner came to my shop about a fortnight afterwards, and I had her taken.
 - BERESFORD. I took the prisoner in Mrs. Read's shop.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had been to the shop several times, and she never said a word about it till the day I was taken.
     GUILTY. Aged 49. - Confined Three Weeks.
 207.  DENNIS McCARTHY was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of December , 2 planes, value 2s., the goods of William Bryan.
 WILLIAM WHEATLEY. I am an officer. On the 3rd of December I saw the prisoner carrying these planes in Brick-lane, Spitalfields: I asked where he got them; he said from a sawyer, who had borrowed them of his master. and he was going to take them back - I took him to see if it were right; he told me of different people, but could not find any one - the next day I found the prosecutor.
 WILLIAM BRYAN. These planes are mine. I lost them from  Essex-street, Whitechapel: I had them at work last Friday week, and missed them; the prisoner lived in that street.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had them given me.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. - Whipped and Discharged.
