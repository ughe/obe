ROBERT BOULTBY (Dotective Sorgeant G). About 4 o'clock on the even-

saw the prisoner standing outside the Hat and Feathers public-house
we both walked up, and I told him I should take him into custody on
suspicion of uttering-he said "I haven't put one down, but you will
find them on me; you have got me by rights for possession, but not for
uttering"-I took him to the station and saw Merrony search him and
take from him five bad florins, separately wrapped in paper-he said
"You have got me to rights; I give you credit for it" -I saw no one
in company with him--we had been waiting at that corner some time, as
a great number had been passed in that neighbourhood. T t
