Inspector Brand took the charge—I have not seen him here—the Police-station in Commercial Street is about 800 yards from the place of arrest—the prosecntor walked there with me and Jeremiah—Jeremiah went quietly—I had no conversation on the way—the prosecutor had been drinking, but was not drunk—he gave an intelligent account of what took place—I heard him say of the other two men, "I know them."
