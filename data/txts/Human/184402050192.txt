 759. WILLIAM CLEMITSON was indicted for embezzlement.
   MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution. THOMAS BORRITT, butcher, King-street, Brick-lane, Spitalfields.  A person named Brashier owed me 5 l .;, money lent—I have known the prisoner for twelve years—he has been in the habit of collecting money at different courts for tradesmen—seven or eight months ago I applied to him to collect this money from Mr. Brashier—I said, "William Brashier owes me 5 l .;, and the son 2 I. ; I with you to try and get it; if you get it I will give you a sovereign; I do not know where they live, but the son is in the Post-office"—the prisoner told me he had inquired, and found out where Brashier lived, but he could not get the money; he must summons him—I said, "What will that cost?"—he said the 5 l .; would cost 12 s . 6 d ., deposit money; and the son's 2 l .; would be 7 s . 6 d .—he brought me a little book, which he said was the Act of Parliament, I might see it—I consented, and gave him the 12 s . 6 d . and the 7 s . 6 d ., about the end of July—when I saw him again, he said he had sum moned them, and told me the day he was to go to the hearing—he did not bring me any money—I received a summons from Mr. Brashier—I gave the prisoner the summons and the letter from Mr. Brashier—I said, "I shall leave it all to you"—I saw him again in August, three or four days after the hearing—he said he had summoned them to the Court; the younger one was to pay 10 s . a month, and the other 9 s . a month—he had my authority to receive these sums—in the first instance I engaged him to go and get money from Brashier—I did not contemplate taking proceedings in the Court—when he came back he said there was no other way to get it—I saw him in September, and he did not then account for either of these sums—I did not see him again till he was in custody—I went to his house several times, but could not find him.,
   COURT . Q.  Was it his business to collect debts for different tradesmen in different courts? A.  Yes, and knowing that I sent to him to collect these. NOT GUILTY.
