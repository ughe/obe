upon the state of Mr. Toker's affairs, upon his mortgages and the interest
being demanded -Mr. Toker wrote to me at last to hand over the deeds to
Mr. Glover-I remember Mr. Glover coming to my office three times; twice
with Mr. Toker, and I think once alone I can tell you generally what

encumbrances on his property-I cannot state the particulars of the converse

of Mr. Toker-I remember particularly that complaint being made-I am
