 909. DAVID GREEN was indicted for a misdemeanor. CHARLES BURGESS GOFF( police-constable L  31.) I saw the prisoner on the 16th of February, in the New-cut—I took him into a baker's shop—he said he was ready to be searched—I felt something bulky at the bottom of his trowsers—I then cut open the lining, and found fire bad shillings, and one bad sixpence, which Gardner caught when they fell I took two other bad shillings out of the lining—he said he had given 2 1/2 d . a piece for the shillings, and 1 1/2 d . for the sixpence. RICHARD GARDNER( police-constable L  159.) I was with Goff, and kept the shillings that fell out of the lining—these are them.
   MR. FIELD . These are all counterfeit, and all alike. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Six Months.
 910. GEORGIANA TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of December , 1 shawl, value 30 s  .; and 1 pair of stockings, value 2 s  .; the goods of Charles Green . ELIZABETH GREEN. I am the wife of Charles Green, of Camberwell I have known the prisoner some years—she came to my house and said she was very much in distress, and her appearance indicated distress—I allowed her to come to do needlework on the 1st of December—she continued there all day, till the evening, when she went out for the supper beer, and never returned—I then missed my shawl and stockings—no other person could have taken them—I had seen the shawl at three o'clock, and she left at nine o'clock. GUILTY. Aged 20. (See page 713.)
 911. JOHN M'PHERSON was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of February , 2 boxes, value 1 s  .; and 89lbs. weight of raisins, value 2 l  ., the goods of Thomas Strange and another. JOHN RUNACRES. I live in Houndsditch, with Thomas and Samuel Strange—they are ray masters, and deal in raisins. I packed up two boxes of raisins together on the 22nd of February—they were worth 2 l . each—they weighed 89lbs.—I marked them, and saw them loaded in the cart, in the City—these are them—I can swear to the boxes by the marks on them—they have not been opened. GEOROE HOOPER( police-constable M  170.) I was on duty between seven and eight o'clock in Bermondsey-street, on Wednesday, the 22nd of February—a man came and told me something—in consequence of that I went after the prisoner, and found him, with these two boxes on his back—I asked where he got them from—he immediately threw them down and ran away—I took them. JAMES TIDMARSH( police-constable M  51.) I heard the cry of "Stop theif"—I pursued and took the prisoner—he was the person that had the boxes. WILLIAM MARSH. I am a carter. I had the boxes in my cart, and was going to Stanton's Wharf, at the bottom of Morgan's-lane—I saw the prisoner and another at the foot of London-bridge—they seemed to stick pretty close to the cart—I looked as sharply as I could, and just as I turned Morgan's-lane the prisoner was very close to the cart, and I missed the boxes in two minutes, and the man also.
