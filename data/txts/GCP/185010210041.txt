John HAYDON re-examined. This is my watch, and this piece of chain
that I have was broken off it-these other things are mine.
Prisoner's Defence. I went to this prosecutor; be was drinking with
another man; his friend went away, and he made me drink; he took me to
another house, and made me have some more; I was in liquor; he asked me
to carry his bundle, and I do not remember anything more.
GUILTY. Aged 33.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury.-Confined
Three Months.
