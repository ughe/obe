the street-I pursued the man, calling 4 Stop thief did not see his
face, only his back, but I never lost sight of him till I left the constable in
pursuit of him, and then both the man and the constable got out of
my sight-to the best of my belief, the prisoner is the man he is the
man that was brought back-I could not swear to him, because I did
not see his face, but he resembles the man in size and dress-I after-
wards examined the window, it was open, the lower part slides up, two
