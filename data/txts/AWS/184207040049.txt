1 watch, value 4l. 1 chain, value 21. 108. 1 seal, value 14. 108. 1 key,
value 11.; 2 drawings, framed and glazed, value 14: 58. 1 table-cover,
value 7a.; and 1 pair of boots, value 58. the goods of Charles Stedman
and EDWARD CONROY, for feloniously receiving the wateb, chain, and
seals, well knowing them to have been stolen against the Statute, &d.
CHARLES STEDMAN. I am an ornamental and decorative painter, and
live in Charles-street, Hampstead-road. On Monday morning, the 13th
of June, I missed a wateh, chain, and seals from the front parlour mantel-
piece, a drawing, by Sir Thomas Lawrence, from over the chimaey-
piece, and the cover off the table--they were all safe at nine o clock on
Sunday evening found the parlour in great confusioni was left in
order the night before-the window was down in the morning, from the
top] cannot tell whether it was down in the evening- gave informa-
tion-this is my watch and seals--they are worth 84. or 9l.
JAMES LOCKYER. I am shopman to Mr. Dempster, a pawnbroker, in
Blackfriars-road. On Friday, the 17th of June, Comoy came to our shop;
and offered this wateh, chain, and seal for 808.4] asked if it was goldwhe
said it was-I said I should go and try it-I had seen some handbills, in
consequence of which I had suspicions, and got an officer, who took him-
he said he bought the watch of a boy for 14.
RICBARD WHEATLEY (police-constable M 161.) I was brought to
Lockyer's shop on the evening of the 17th of June-Conroy was given
into my custody-he said he bought the watch of a boy named David for
1.-as we were going along he wished to say something- told him to go
quietly, and not to say anything that might be used against him-he said
David was lodging at his house in Buckeridge-street-I there, waited
some time, and took Cranewhile taking him over to the Sutrey side,
he asked where Conroy was taken- told him-he said, while he was

a wateh ; he said no; the boy asked him to put the wateh into his pocket;
he consented, and they were together three hours he lost the boy, and
sold the watch to Conroy for 11.
