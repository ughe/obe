MARY FINLAY. I am the wife of John Finlay : We keep this house.
About two months ago the female prisoner came to lodge there I had a !
cloak to sell, and she bought it for 23. 4d. it was in pledge for 3s. she
bought the ticket of me--she told me she had received some money from
her husband, which he had received from home that was on Saturday, A
week before this money was missing- she paid me 18. rent, and I believe
on the Monday she bought the cloak she paid me 23. and a 4d. piece
-she kept her clothes in the room--the prosecutrix came down and
asked me to go up stairs—I went-she was at the table-drawer, and said,
"Here is the purse that had my money in it."
