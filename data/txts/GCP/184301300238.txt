in her presence-I cannot say whether I saw them produced by the prisoner.
COURT. Q. Then have you told the truth when you said that the prisoner
brought down the receipts? 4. I was in the lower room-the prisoner stepped
down from the sitting-room, where he was in conversation with Miss Constable,
and brought them-he produced the receipts to me in the kitchen, where I
copied them- there were various receipts-I should say ten or fifteen or more
than that..
MR. CLARKSON. Q. Take that account in your hand, and be cautious how
you answer; it contains a list of items in your own handwriting? A. Yes
-I see some writing at the bottom.
Q. What part of that writing will you on your oath swear you saw Miss
Constable write? 1. “Elizabeth Hollis Constable"-I will not swear to any
thing else I wrote the paper through-after I had written it I gave it, in the
presence of the prisoner and Miss Constable-I cannot say to whom I gave
it-I might have laid it on the table they were both in the room-I do not
know whether I banded it to the prisoner or Miss Constable, but I saw Miss
Constable write this “Elizabeth Hollis Constable," in the room-I saw her
write that-I put the paper down, or gave it to the prisoner or Miss Constable,
I do not know which.
Q. How soon after did you see her name to it? A. I should say from
twenty minutes to half an hour I am not quite certain whether I was in the
room all the while I was in the room with Miss Constable I should say
after this paper was signed-I was in the room until she signed it-I saw her
write those words- I will not swear who wrote what is above it-I do not
remember.
Q. Why you never left the room until she signed it ; on your solemn oath,
did not the same person that wrote this name write that which is over it? A.
I cannot swear- do not remember-I do not know why the prisoner did not
put his name to it as a witness as well as me I did not ask him I was not
acquainted with these things-I put my name because I was asked by the
prisoner to do so I am not certain whether Mrs. Black well or Miss Blackwell
were there, or who was there-- I know Miss Constable and the prisoner were
present.
Q. But as you were a person to be benefited by the deed, why did not you
ask them to witness it? 4. I did not know I was to be benefited by the
deed when that paper was signed-it was a transaction between the prisoner
and Miss Constable-I cannot say what it was signed for I was waiting
there to get my account, for goods supplied to the prisoner in his trade.
