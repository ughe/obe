1250. VALENTINE GANLY was indicted for stealing 1 penny, 3 hall
pence, and 2 farthings the monies of George Collis, from the person of Eliza
beth Collis; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
ELIZABETH COLLIS. I am the wife of George Collis, of Clifton-street,
Fitzroy-square. On the 6th May, about nine o'clock, I was with my mother
in Tottenham-court-road, I felt something at my pocket and saw the pri-
soner draw his hand out-my pocket was turned inside out and my money
gone-I called out, and followed the prisoner across the road-I called out,
"Stop thief;" and he was stopped, I did not lose sight of him-I am sure he
is the man-I had some halfpence in my pocket-that was all I had.
ELIZABETH JUPE. I was with last witness in Tottenham-court-road when
she lost the money-she pointed out the prisoner to me, he was secured.
RICHARD NEALE ( policeman.) I received the prisoner in charge, took him
to the station, and found nothing on him.
Prisoner's Defence. I passed the woman and she ran after me, and called
stop thief; I had nothing belonging to her, and did not touch her
ELIZABETH COLLIS re-examined. There was nobody else near that I no-
ticed-I had not put the halfpence into my pocket two minutes.
ANDREW WILLIAMSON (policeman.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's
former conviction-(read-Convicted, August 1846, confined six months) -
was present at the trial- he is the person.
Guilty.t Aged 22.- Transported for Seven Years.

1251. ALLAN WEALE was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value
18.; 1 collar, 6d.; 1 habit shirt, 6d.; and 1 reel of cotton, 1d. ; the goods
of James Barnard, from the person of Lucy Barnard; and that he had been
before convicted of felony.
Lucy BARNARD. My husband's name is James, he is a printer. On the
11th May, between three and four in the afternoon, I was on Holborn-hill,
felt something at my pocket, I put my hand down, and caught hold of the
prisoner's arm-his hand was in my pocket-1 missed my handkerchief and
a small parcel-the handkerchief was found on him, and the parcel was lying
on the pavement, close to him-it contained a handkerchief, collar, and reel
of cotton-these are them, they are my property.
