 2074.  JAMES PARSONS was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of October , 1 waistcoat, value 4s., the goods of Abraham Solomon.
 ABRAHAM SOLOMON. I am a  clothes-dealer. On the 28th of October, while I was at a neighbour's, this waistcoat was taken from my shop; I saw it at Eagle-street watch-house, and know it - I had seen it safe half an hour before.
   JAMES ISAAC. I saw the prisoner running up Hatton-wall - I came up to him, and he was going to throw this waistcoat down an area; he was about three hundred yards from Solomon's - I took him to the watch-house; the prosecutor came, and said if it was his there was a figure of 8 on the collar, and there is one; the prisoner said he found it in the middle of Great Saffron-hill.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going down Saffron-hill. and saw a young man running, who I suppose dropped it; I was running away with it, and was taken.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.
 2075.  WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of October , 1 shawl, value 3s., the goods of Richard Smale.
 RICHARD SMALE. I  keep a coffee-room. On the 24th of October my wife came in and threw this shawl across the wainscot, and went up stairs; the prisoner was there, and called for a cup of coffee - he drank part of it, and went out suddenly; in less than five minutes my wife missed her shawl: on Friday last she saw him in the street, and brought him home - I questioned him about it; he at first denied it - I told him I thought it was his first offence, and he did it through distress, and if he could tell me where I could go for a good character, I would be as lenient as I could with him - he said he did it through distress, and he worked at a dyer's near Finsbury-square.
   LEONARD CLARE MATTHEWS. I have a shawl, pawned on Saturday week, by the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I took it up, as I thought there had been some woman in the place; the prosecutor said if I would give it up, he would not appear against me.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined One Month.
 2076.  SUSAN SURETEES was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of October , 3 pairs of leather soles, value 1s. 3d., the goods of Eusebius Smith.
   JOHN ANDREW COTTON. I am shopman to Mr. Eusebias Smith, a leather-cutter, of Kingsland-road. Last Saturday the prisoner came and bought a pair of soles, which she paid for, then stooped down, and put these three pairs into her basket, and then the pair she had bought - she was going out; I told her Mr. Smith wanted a word with her, and I rang the bell for Mr. Smith - she found there was something amiss, and went to take the soles out of the basket; she took out two pairs; I said."That will not do - you must let me look into the basket; "Mr. Smith said he would send for an officer, for she had been doing it some time; these three pairs of soles were taken from her basket - she said she was very sorry.
   GEORGE SMITH. I am a constable. I went to the shop- the prisoner was delivered to me, with these three pairs of soles; she begged forgiveness.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I deny putting any into the basket - I looked out some, and gave the witness to put by for me till I returned; I had not put them into the basket, and he knows it well.
     GUILTY. Aged 46. Confined Six Months.
 2077.  ANN MORE was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of October , 1 blanket, value 2s., and 1 sheet, value 3s., the goods of Dennis Slater.
   MARY SLATER. I am the wife of Dennis Slater - we live in St. Giles'. On the 10th of October the prisoner, who had lodged with me about a month, left - I missed a sheet and a blanket; she came to my house this day week, and was intoxicated - she could not stand; I gave her no leave to pawn any thing.
   THOMAS FISHER. I am a pawnbroker. I produce the articles; the prisoner pawned them with me on the 10th of October.
   WILLIAM WILSON. I took the prisoner - she gave up the duplicate.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I expected money to redeem them.
     GUILTY. Aged 37.
   Confined One Month.
 2078.  BURDETT ROGERS was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of August , 1 sovereign, 1 half-sovereign, and 1 shilling, the monies of George Morgan.
 GEORGE MORGAN. I live at a club-house in  St. James'-street- the prisoner was employed there as a painter's labourer. I came home about five o'clock on the 24th of August, and asked him to clean my shoes; I took off my trousers, with this money in them, and put them on a box in the dressing-room - I went down stairs, and in about five minutes I came up again, to take the money out of the trousers; I then missed upwards of 30s.: my fellow-servant came in, and I sent for the prisoner - I asked him if he had picked up this money; he denied it - I told him I would make him pull all his things off; he still denied it, and my fellow-servant went for an officer - while he was gone, the prisoner said if I would not hurt him he would give me the money; he took some of it out; I said that was not all - he then took out the rest from his inside trousers pocket, and laid it down; I went out for a few minutes - when I returned he was gone; he was taken soon after, for something else.
 Prisoner's Defence. When the prosecutor came home that day he was quite intoxicated - I was called to go on an errand, and when I returned I went to finish the shoes; I let one of the brushes fall, and in picking it up I saw the money - I should have given it him, but he had some friends at tea. GUILTY. Aged 17.
 Recommended to Mercy by the Jury. - Confined 1 Month.
