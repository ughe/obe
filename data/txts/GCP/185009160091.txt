Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. You could see the purse in
Sounders' hand perfectly well? A. Yes; I did not make a catch at it-I
should have done so if I had not been stopped by one of their confederates
the man that came in with Saunders stood before me with a stick-I was
making my way towards Saunders, to get my purse-the man was alongside
of the others, and turned round, and stood before me, and with that all of
them got away-I called out, " That is my purse "-I do not know the name
of the man that had the stick; he remained behind-I did not give him into
custody, because he said he knew where to take me to-he did not use the
stick-he had it on his shoulder, or rather in his mouth-he might have hurt
me with the stick if he had liked, and I have not the least doubt that was his
intention-1 did not expect it then; but he got first on one side of me, and
then on the otber, to prevent me getting out-it was quite evident he was
enabling the others to get my purse-1 was foolish to walk about with him
afterwards, but I did it because he said he could take me where the man was
--Anderson was tried, I think, about a week or a fortnight afterwards-I
forget whether I told the landlord that Saunders had robbed me-I ought to
have done so I dare say, but I did not-I gave this man that stopped with
me my address, I thought he was a respectable young man- did not
