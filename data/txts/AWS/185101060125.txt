GEoRGE BRODIE. I live in the Old Kent-road, and am a gas-lighter.
About 5th Sept. I wrote to the prisoner, and he came the following day, and
said he was an officer belonging to the High Court of Chancery had heard
he was an officer, and asked him so when he came, and he said, Yes"-
told bim I had some property in the Court, and he said he would draw
me up a petition, which would be 5a. 6d., and I should hear from him in
a few days-he afterwards came, and brought me an answer. to the
petition- have not got it, he took it away with him-1 signed my name
to each of them. but did not keep a copy-| signed my name to the
petition and the administration, I think he called it-be said the expense
of the last was 22s. 6d., and the 5s. 6d. was for the petition-he said I
could not have had a better answer if I had paid 100 guineas for it-he
called several times but when he brought the administration, he did not
call for three or four weeks ; and I wrote to him, and he did not come;
I
wrote again, and he came, and said the property would be settled by
22nd Dec.-I paid him the 22s. 6d. (I do not know the coin) for the

in custody-th last time he came he said he thought mine would be put
