168. CHARLES WEBBER (20), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit
coin.
MR. DAVIN conducted the Prosecution.
MARY BUTT. I am bar-maid at the Red Cow, Long Lane-on Saturday
evening, the 2nd January, between 8 and 9 o'clook, the prisoner came in
and asked for a glass of six ale, he gave me a shilling-I took it to the till,
tried it and bent it-I gave it to Annie Florence, she was in the bar-I did
not say anything to the prisoner.
· Annis FLORENCE. I am head bar-maid at the Red Cow-on the 2nd
January, the last witness gave me a bad shilling the prisoner was in the
bar-I told him it was a bad one, and asked him if he had any more like it!
- he said, " What do you mean t-I said, “That is a bad one, and if you
have any more, let me see them" he said, "I shall not be dictated to by
you"-he gave me a good florin, and said that was all he had got-I sent
one of the customers for a policeman, and in the meantime the prisoner ran
away without his change-I followed, caught him, and brought him back to
the house,
JOHN RIDGEN (City Policeman 310) I took the prisoner into custody-I
found on him two sixpences and sixpence in ooppers-I received this le.
from the last witness.
Prisoner. You went to my mother Witness. I did-I asked her if she
would come and see you-she said you had gone to buy some things, and
she had given you a half-crown.
ANNIE FLORENCH (re-examined). I gave this shilling to the policeman, it
is the one that Mary Butt gave to me.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. This shilling is bad.
Prisoner's Defence. My mother gave me the half-crown to buy a piece of
meat-I got the shilling in change, and I went past this house. I put the
shilling down and found it was bad.
GUILTY-Twelve Month's Imprisonment.
