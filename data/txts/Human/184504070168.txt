 948. JAMES FREEMAN was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of April , 1 handkerchief value 1 s  . 6 d  . the goods of Thomas Yeakell , from his person. THOMAS YEAKELL. I live at Kennington-cross. On the 9th of April, between two and three o'clock in the afternoon, I was passing through the Temple, and in Dorset-street felt a sensation at my pocket—I put my hand, and missed my handkerchief—I turned, and saw the prisoner at my left, and accused him of having taken it—he coloured up very red, and denied it; but I saw him endeavouring to force it up his coat—I held him for tome few moments—when his hand came away the handkerchief fell on the pavement—I picked it up, and gave him in custody to the policeman, with the handkerchief—this produced is it. CALEB GROVES( City police-constable. ) I took the prisoner and the handkerchief.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I am not guilty; several young men were passing at the time; as for seeing me shove  it up my coat, it is false.
 (William Freeman, the prisoner's brother, a publican, in Strutton  ground, Westminster, deposed to his good character, and undertook to pro vide for him.) GUILTY  . Aged 19.— Strongly recommended to mercy. — Confined Fourteen Days .
