245. FREDERICK WILLIAM LE POIDEVIN was again indicted
for stealing 380 spoons, 44 kniven, 61 forks, 1 pair of fish carvers, and other
articles, the property of William White and another.
MR. METOALFE conducted the Prosecution, and MR. GENT the Defence.
WILLIAM WHITE. I am in partnership with Mr. Johnstone we placed
the prisoner at 10, London-wall, to carry on our business-he oumnienced
with a stock sent up from Sheffield, consisting chiefly of electro-plated goods
--I examined the stock after he went away, and found it upwards of 4002.
deficient that was for things we could not account for, that were not
entered at all-I have seen goods produced by several pawnbrokers, and
what I have seen are our goods--the prisoner had no right to pledge goods
in any way, and if he made a sale it was his duty to send us an account of
it the same day.
Cross-examined. Q. Don't you know that an agent would be entitled to
keep goods for any commission owing to him? A. No; I saw these goods
at Guildhall—the prisoner paid Scollard, I believe, up to last December.
MR. METCALFE. Q. Is there money owing to the prisoner by you, or is
there money owing to you by him A. Money owing to us by him I be-
lieve 1,0001.-there is no pretence for saying that we owe money to him.

-I produce two which relate to goods pawned at Harrison's—those goods

and silver goods.
