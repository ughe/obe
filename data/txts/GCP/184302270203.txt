JOHN KNIGHT. I am clerk to Mr. Piper on the 27th of Oct. I went to
No. 20, Bread-street-hill-I saw Hamor, or a person that answered to that
name-the warehouse was then quite full of crates, and apparently it was a
place of considerable business-I saw plenty of crockery-ware and sauce-
Dans, and other things strewed about-I said I had been sent by my em-
ployer to inquire about a man named William Clark, that was opening a shop
at Epsom-he said he knew him for some years he gave as good an account
as ever I heard, and I gave him a good character to my employer.
RICHARD SWIFT. I am a shoemaker, and live in Greville-street, Hatton-gar-
den—in Nov. the prisoner came to me-he represented himself as William
Clark, of Epsom-he wanted about 401. worth of boots and shoes, as he was about
to open a shop- I told him my terms were cash, and at last asked for a refer-
ence; he referred me to Mr. Hamor—on the following day I saw him again,
and on the 4th of Nov. I parted with the goods, to the amount of 391. 158.-
I received 141. in cash-I gave an invoice of the goods--they were in a
hamper, and at the bottom of the hamper was a paper, stating the hamper
was 123. if not returned-he promised to bring 121. more on the following
Tuesday morning-he did not do so-I heard of some of my goods being in
the hands of Mr. Davis, but did not see them I have never been paid.

John HOLLAND. I am a shoe-manufacturer in Cheapside and St. John-
street-road. About the 8th of Nov., a man who has been indicted, by the
name of Davis, brought some articles to my shop from having dealings with
Mr. Swift, I recognised them as his-Davis showed me an invoice, and I reeog-
nised it as Mr. Swift's handwriting the heading of it was cut off, but at the
foot of the invoice it was stated that the bamper, if not returned, would be
125-I waited on Mr. Swift-Davis took me to a place near the Jews' sy-
nagogue, and showed me the goods-I made an appointment, and went again
with Mr. Swift, but no goods were there, nor Davis neither I went to Mr.
Hamor's, and saw Horner-he said he was acting as clerk--he introduced
Mr. Hamor to me as Mr. Hamor.
