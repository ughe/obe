ANN WARE. Between two and three o'clock on 26th July the prisoner came to my house—he had a double handful of halfpence—he tied them in a handkerchief, and wanted to leave them at my house, but I would not allow him—I went into the garden to call my husband, and when I came back the prisoner was in the coalhole—I asked what he was doing—he said he was doing no harm—I saw that he was hiding his halfpence—I found this half-penny —I made him take the others away—he left this knife there. JOSEPH GOSTWICK( policeman, T  206). I took the prisoner—I found on him two shillings, and one penny in copper.
   Prisoner.  I got that halfpenny in change for a sixpence.
   MRS. ALLEN . I did not change any money for him. JOHN DENTON. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's conviction at Clerkenwell—( read—Convicted Nov., 1849, and confined three months )—I was present—he is the person. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined Twelve Months .
