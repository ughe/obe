Cross-examined. 6. Who gives out the goods to him? A. One of the
counter men-ho is not here-Barker, & clerk, makes out the bill for him to
take-he is not here-when the prisoner leaves goods he leaves the invoice
for them at the same time-those are to be paid at the end of three months,
and at the end of three months a statement is made out by Mr. Rolfe--he is
not here-he does not give it to the prisoner, it is sent by post, but the
prisoner is in the habit of calling weekly, and Mr. Smith might say, “Here
is a bill of yours, I will pay you"-he does not receive any list of arrears
which he is to collect--if they are in arrear, it is put into other hands to
collect-when a man takes to & van there are certain amounts on the way.
bill which we consider equa Ito cash, and we advance him to the amount of
501. in cash; if the bill amounted to 201. we should advance him 301.--that
was not each time he went out, only on the first onset when he has the 50%.
he must pay cash on the following morning for all the goods he takes out-if
he gives credit it is at his own risk-if he pays cash to the amount of 30%.
we do not make up the 501. again ; he has got to the end of his tether then,
and would be discharged-if, when he comes home, he has the 201, and 30%.
cash, he has not to pay us both; he is always to keep the 50%, in hand-ho
must pay in the whole amount that is on the way-bill
MR. METCALFE. Q. He pays in the amount, and next morning he receives
perhaps 301., that would make 601., then he would have to account for 602 1
A. Yes; he has to pay in the cash items on the way-bill each time he
returns-he would not have to account for the cash difference-we leave the
money in his hands as a cover for himself-we always leave a fixed sum in
his bauds-it is an insurance fund-there is a way-bill every day-this
(produced) is the way-bill for the next day-besides this insurance fund
there are two sureties in 501. each, who enter into a bond for 1001.-we
also deduct 68. a week from the wages; that forms a deposit account for the
half of bad debts or accidents-we have three funds to fall back upon-the
68. a week has nothing to do with accidents--the bond is for surety in case
he should be in arrears—I have not seen him since he left on 10th October
-be did not ask me for a statement of his accounts before he left, nor did I
ask him for it-I did not see him from 27th August till 25th September-
was away the whole of that time-he ought to have accounted to me on
25th September-Mr. Rolfe, the ledger keeper, did my duties in my absence
---any account of 20th September belongs to Mr. Rolfe-he is not here--I
was not there on the 25th-I was there on Friday and Saturday, the 26th
and-27th-the money was always paid to me in the morning—the running
account of 501. is only settled up when a man leaves, he then has to account
for exactly what he receives.
Court. Q. It is only to put him in funds to pay ready money for what
he does not get ready money for? A. Yes-the way-bills are filed in the
office, and he has access to them to see who did not pay-I suppose that
those who are left have not paid.
