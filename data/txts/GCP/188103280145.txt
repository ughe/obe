ROBERT OUTRAM (Detective Sergeant). On 16th Feb. I followed Clarke's
van from Aldermanbury to Finsbury Circus, where he stopped for a minute
or two, and Hook went up and spoke to him and then left, and Clark
turned his horse round and went into Bloomfield Street --I followed
him to the docks and saw him deliver his load-later in the evening
I took him into custody, and Sergeant Boultbee told him he would be
charged with two others with stealing a case--he said. “I know nothing
about it "--on the way to the station he said, "All that I know about it is,
a young chap asked me if I knew whether Filer had gone to the docks, and
I said that he was probably in Wilson Street, where he got his dinner.”
Cross-examined. by Mr. Hicks. He had delivered his load all right at
the docks—he went just as I should expect a carman to go.
Hook's Defence. Clark is innocent as far as I know.
