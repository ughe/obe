HENRY BAKER. I am employed by Mr. Smart—I was prevent at Bott's two liquidation meetings; I listened to his evidence and took notes all through—I took shorthand notes of the first meeting, but they have been lost—Mr. Jacobs asked Bott a question about the Queensland Bonds, and I understood him to say that he had not seen them since Mr. Brown went away; I am sure he said so, but I don't recollect the exact words—I attended as shorthand writer, but beyond that I had nothing to do With it.
   Cross-examined.  I do not remember Without my notes, When Bott said that he missed Mr. Collins's bonds, but I distinctly remember his saying that he had not seen them since Brown went.
