book, there may be some mistake.
MRs. MoRGAN (Re-ecamined). Nothing was left behind with me.
Mrs. FLEMING (Re-ezamined). The prisoner left behind her a woollen

By MR. GEOGHEGAN. I saw the mother and child going the shawl

everything-t mother took a bundle with her; I don't know what that

JOHN ALLAN BRIDGE I am a pawnbroker, of 20, John Street, Mary-
lebone-between 9 and 10 a.m. on Monday, 26th September, someone (I
could not swear it was the prisoner) came and pledged this child's hood
for 38. in the name of A. Sweetman-she did not have a child with her
-to the best of my belief the prisoner is the person, but I would not
swear.
