MARGARET FLACKMAN. I am servant to Mr. Clementson, of Coleman.
street. On 16th May, about seven o'clock in the morning, 1 found the stain
case window open, and the parlour door, and the back door leading to the yard
-I had bolted that door the night before-I went to bed at half-past eleven
the night before; the window was then shut-the front gates were locked, a
I left them the night before-no one could get access to the premises through
the door that was open, unless they were in the bouse before-if anybody bad
been in the house they could have let others in easily.
