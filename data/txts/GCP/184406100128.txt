1683. ALEXANDER WARDROP was indicted for stealing 1 gown,
value 28.; 1 petticoat, 1s. ; and 2 shifts, 28.; the goods of Sarah Ann
Hanks.
SARAH Ann Hanks. I am single, and live in Phænix-place, Somers.
town. The prisoner was married to my cousin-on the morning of the 21st
of May I went from my apartment at nine, and left the door locked-1
returned at ten at night, and found part of the partition of my room broken
down-I had left a box of clothes in the room--I missed these articles-the
prisoner lived in the same house-next morning I asked him if he would give
me up the tickets-- he said the things were quite safe-I asked if he would
give me the money-he said he had got no money-I asked if he would give
me the tickets-he said he tore them up-these are my things.
GEORGE FINNIGAN. I live with Mr. Griffiths, a pawnbroker. One of
these things was pledged by the prisoner-I am sure he is the person.
ROBERT Dixon. I live with Mr. Griffiths, a pawnbroker. This gown
was pawned by the prisoner.
WILLIAM ELMER (police-constable S 187.) I took the prisoner-on the
way to the station he said he had done it, and would replace the things in the
course of the day.
Prisoner's Defence. The prosecutrix owed my wife 78. ; we have been in
the habit of pawning her things at her own request; my wife had permission
to pledge these things, and told me to take them as I wanted to make up some
money; I intended to get them out the same day.
· SARAH ANN Hanks re-examined. I never gave leave to his wife to pawn
these things—I did not owe her 8s.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
3Guilty. Aged 33.-Confined Three Months.
1684. ESTHER LEWIS was indicted for stealing 2 sheets, value 4s.; 1
bolster, 158. ; 1 blanket, 6s. ; 1 counterpane, 58.; 1 pillow, 1s. ; 2 quilts, 38.;
1 table-cloth, 2s.; and 1 pillow-case, 6d. ; the goods of John Glanville Dur.
bridge.
ELLEN DURBRIDGE. I am the wife of John Glanville Durbridge, of
Charles-street, Stepney. The prisoner hired a room at my house--she was
there three months--a week ago I went into the room to take the dimensions
of a bolster—the prisoner was there-I missed the articles stated—these are
them--they were let to the prisoner with the room,
MICHAEL KELLY (police-constable K 144.) I took the prisoner-she was
charged with stealing these things-she said she was sorry for what she had
done, and delivered up eight duplicates—some more duplicates were found on
her.
Jane Scott. I am the wife of Sergeant Scott. I searched the prisoner
and found nineteen duplicates on her, three of them belonged to this
property.
Thomas VESPER, pawnbroker, Sydney-place, Commercial-road. The
prisoner pawned these articles with me.
(The prisoner pleaded poverty.)
Guilty. Aged 38.—Confined Three Months.
