xx MUNDAY, wife of Richard Munday, labourer, Clapton. On
August Bank Holiday I had a bay horse at grass in Reader's field,
close to Les Bridge. It was safe at five o'clock in the evening. Early
mert morning, when I went to look for it to take it to Spitalfields
Market, it was gone. I am a costermonger, and help to get my own
living and the ohildren's. When next I saw it it was at Dorking on the
9th, and 18 inches had been out off its tale. I know prisoner Riley.
He came to me to buy some fowls and a set of harness, but I could
hot say for certain when that was. He did not buy the fowls as the
price was too high. Riley saw the cob on two occasions and asked
whether it was for sale. The value of the horse was £14. It had
on, in the field, a leather head stall, which was found in the trap at
Barking. There was also found in the cart a collar I had sold to
Riley. The collar was too small to go over my horse's head. Later
on I saw Riley at Stoke Newington Police Station, and charged him.
I value the halter at a shilling. I was shown some of the harness
which I did not recognise. I knew nothing of the harness except
the collar. There was a complete set and some head pieces. The
Earness was found at a livery bait stables, and I paid 7s. 6d. for
the feed of the horse on 6th, 7th, 8th, and 9th. When I saw the
horse it was in the custody of the police.
