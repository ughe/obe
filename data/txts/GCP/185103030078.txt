753. JOHN FITZGERALD, burglariously breaking and entering the
dwelling-house of William Knight, and stealing 1 pair of boots and 1 pair
of trowsers, value 38.; his goods.
William KNIGHT. I am sehoolmaster at a ragged-school, to which
the prisoner came for about seven months. On Friday, 21st Feb.,
went to bed about eleven o'clock, and locked up the house securely-
was aroused at a few minutes after four, and found a pane of glass taken
out of the kitchen window, which was pushed up, so that a person could
get in a box in one of the rooms, which had been locked, had been
opened, and some wearing-apparel taken out-I missed a pair of trowsers,
which I had seen a month before, and a pair of boots, which I had seen
outside the box the morning before.
HAINES ( policeman, A 420). I took the prisoner on 24th Feb.
in the Standard Theatre-he had on these trowsers and boots (produced).
WILLIAM KNIGHT re-examined. These are my trowsers-these are not
the boots; they are a pair which have been taken since. -
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Confined Two Years.
