GEoRGE MoRRIs. I am a jobmaster, of Tunbridge Wells-on November
3rd the prisoner Legge came, and said he wanted a trap for his master to
drive to Sevenoaks-] asked him who his master was, and he gave me
the card of Sidney Graham-I took the cart out about 2.30, and he gave

CHARLES WINGROVE. I am a livery-stable keeper, of Cambridge Road,
