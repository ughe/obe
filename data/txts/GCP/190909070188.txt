liability whatever on the part of the corporation, either moral or

obligations of the late syndicate to its customers; resolved that in
the event of any case of this nature arising it should be investigated
by Mr. H. Lee Dillon, to report and advise the board as to the
course to be adopted." On May 18 Petty resigned as a director,
and the board passed a vote of thanks to him " for his valuable ser-
