 736. ANN JOHNSON  (38) , Feloniously killing and slaying William Onslow ; she was also  charged on the Coroner's Inquisition, with the like offence.
   MR. BRINDLEY conducted the Prosecution; and MR. TAPLIN the Defence. ELIZA ONSLOW. I am single, and am now living as wet nurse with Mrs. Barales, at Sydney Cottage, Walthamstow—I was confined in the Isling ton Workhouse, on 27th March of a male child, which I suckled for three months and a fortnight up to 16th July, when I—made an-arrangement with Mrs. Barales, who arranged with the prisoner to take my child at 5 s . a week and keep it—my sister, Mrs. Smith, was to send clothes, and the prisoner was to give it the necessaries of life—she said she would do a mother's part to it—my sister introduced the prisoner.; mine was a fine healthy child—I paid the money regularly to the prisoner by post-office order every week, which I sent to my sister for her—a fortnight, afterwards I heard that my child had diarrhoea, but I afterwards heard from my sister that it was better—I then heard no more—I got anxious about the child and informed my mistress, who went to see it—on 3rd September, I received a letter from the workhouse saying that it was dead—four days afterwards I saw it and identified it at the inquest—I could not recognise it at first, but the second time I went to see it—I recognised its toes—two of the toes on each foot were joined together.
   Cross-examined.  I never knew the prisoner till she took my child—no doctor had ever seen it—when I heard that it was suffering from diarrhoea, I paid 2 s . for medical treatment for it, because my sister said that it had to be taken to a doctor; I then beard that it was better, but never that it was quite well—I never saw it again-after leaving it with the prisoner.
