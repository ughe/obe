   MR. CUNNINGHAM conducted the Prosecution , MARGARET COSTELLO. I am the wife of Mark Costello, and reside at Nile-street, Woolwich—the prisoner lodged with me in September last—I used to employ her to assist me in doing some cleaning—on the 8th of September I sent her to fetch some soda—she did not return—from what a lodger said to me, I went and looked over my things, and I missed seven counterpanes, a child's petticoat, and a cape—I saw some of the things at Mrs. Hake's afterwards. CAROLINE HALSE. I am the wife of James Halse, and reside at 29, Church-street, Woolwich—my husband keeps a wardrobe-shop—I remember the hopseason—about that time the prisoner came into my shop—she asked me to purchase these things ( produced ), which I did, and paid her 5 s . 10 d .—she had been to my shop on previous occasions—Mrs. Costello came to me and I showed her the things—I did not see the prisoner again until she was in custody. RHODA MAINE. I am the wife of Robert Maine, and reside at 29, Church-street, Woolwich, with my sister, the last witness—I was in the shop on the day the prisoner came in and brought these things—I am sure she is the woman. CHARLES CARTWRIGHT( Policeman, R  101). On the 30th of March last, I took the prisoner into custody—I told her she was charged with stealing some things about September last, she said, "I never took any of the property, but I would rather pay for it than to go prison." GUILTY.— Confined Six Months .
