 364. JOHN GREEN was indicted foe stealing, on the   30th of De cember , 1 pair of shoes, value 2 s  ., the goods of John Fines . GEORGE ELLIS( police-constable C  66.) On the 30th of December, at half-past eleven o'clock in the forenoon, I met the prisoner in the Seven Dials—I observed this pair of shoes under his jacket, and asked where he got them—he said he bought them of a lad for 10 d .—I took him. MARY FINES. I am the wife of John Fines, who lives in Crown-court, Windmill-street. These are his shoes—I saw them safe about half-past ten o'clock and missed them soon after.
   Prisoner's Defence . I earned 1 s . for carrying a gentleman's luggage; I then went on, and young lad said I might have these shoes for 10 d ., they were his own making. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Six Months.
