 622. JANE DOORS was again indicted for feloniously receiving, on the   30th of November , 1 pair of shoes, value 5 s  ., the goods of John Matthews , well knowing them to have been stolen. WILLIAM ROBERTS I live at Messrs. Walmsley's pawnbrokers, in Drury-lane, I have a pair of new shoes pawned by the prisoner on the 28th of November in the name of Hutchings. JOHN MATTHEWS. This prisoner came to my house about two months before Christmas—these are my shoes; I have lost such—I have not sold any of this make—they have my private mark on them. JAMES ATFIELD I am a police-constable. When the prisoner was apprehended on the other charge, she told me she had taken a pair of shoes from Mr. Matthews, and pledged them at Mr. Walmsley's in Drury-lane.
   Prisoner . No; I said I received them. WILLIAM HENRY BARRETT. I am a police constable. The prisoner said she took this pair of shoes from the shop, and pledged them in Drury-lane.
   Prisoner . I had only had those which the boys gave me—they gave me these—they told me they might take a hundred pair from the shop, and nobody know it, as the master was in the habit of going out at seven o'clock every evening, and not coming home till two o'clock in the morning, and then quite or nearly drunk.
   MR. MATTHEWS . The prisoner had access to these shoes—she used to pass through the shop many times a-day—my shop is very full of goods—any body passing might take a pair here and there. NOT GUILTY.

 624. SARAH POPE and JANE SCOTT were again indicted for feloniously receiving on the   1st of December , 2 pairs of boots, value 5 s  ., the goods of John Matthews , well knowing them to have been stolen. WILLIAM HENRY BARRETT. I am a police-constable, I apprehended the two prisoners at the Victoria Theatre—I understood they had two pairs of boots given them by Freeman—I took them to the station-house and got the boots from their feet. NOT GUILTY.
