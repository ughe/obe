days; you did not pay me half a crown of it-you came in afterwards, and

JAMES BISHOP. I am a greengrooer, of 76, Osnaburgh Street-on
21st November the prisoner called and gave me this seamen's allotment
note for 31. 108. on the Glen Nector-he said it was payable to his wife,
And this was half of a month's pay note and represented his wife's
brother's pay-I lent him 156. on it-he said it was payable on the 16th
-I afterwards met him in a public-house, accused him, and got 58. out
of him.
Cross-examined. You did not bring the money to my shop; I got it
form you outside the publio-house.
