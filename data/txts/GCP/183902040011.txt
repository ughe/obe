RICHARD CROUCH. I am a packer in the prosecutor's service the prisoner
was the same. About seven o'clock, on the evening of Jan. 23, I went to the
place where our great coats were hanging to get some cord, and as I was tak
ing some out of the box, I knocked my hand against something bard in the
prisoner's great coat pocket-I felt, and found one of these beakers--Nash,
my fellow-servant, came up, I told him of it--we called the prisoner up,
and told him he had got something he said he had--we advised him to
put them back--he said he would, but he did not-he put on his coats
to come away at half-past eight o'clock-I and Nash followed him down
to the door, and said, “Have you put them away?"-be said yes he had
--Nash felt in his pocket, and said he had not the prisoner then ran up
stairs, and the policeman was sent for, and these beakers and slippers were
taken out of his pocket in my presence.
