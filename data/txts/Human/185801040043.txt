 198. JAMES BROWN  (30) , Feloniously killing and slaying Mary Coney , the younger.
   MR. W. J. ABRAM conducted the Prosecution. JAMES BELL. I live at No. 5, Chequers Alley. I knew the deceased little girl, Mary Coney—I saw her in Chequers Alley, about three weeks ago, play ing against some bricks; several little children were there at the time—the prisoner was there cleaning some bricks—he told the little girl to go away several times; she would not go, and he struck her with a leather belt on the right side of the head; it had a buckle to it—her head did not bleed—she went home crying—I did not see her afterwards—I am quite certain that the prisoner is the man who struck the blow.
   Prisoner.  It is false what he has been saying, I never hit the child at all.
