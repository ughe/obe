365. ALDEN WESTON (30), THOMAS HILL (43), ROBERT
DAKER (61), GEORGE JOHNSON (51), and EDWARD FAIRCHILD
(26), Unlawfully conspiring to defraud Alexander Brown and others of
30,0001.
Mr. Cock, Q.C., MR. H. Avory, and MB. ABRAHAMS Prosecuted:
MR. GRAIN and MR. ABINGER appeared for Woston, MR. GEOGHEGAN
for Hill, MR. FULTON for Daker, Mr. Gilt for Johnson, and Mr.
PURCELL for Fairchild.
NATHAN KYSER. I am manager of the stock and share department of
Messrs. Kaulb and Co., foreign bankers, Cornhill-on 20th May, 1887,
the prisoner Johnson came there to purchase a Hamilton and North
Western Railway bond of 1001., but we had not one in stock-he agreed
to pay 1111. 128. 6d. for it, paid 51. deposit, and came again on the 26th
or 27th, when we handed him the Bond and he paid the balance; the
number of it was 1954-this (produced) is one of the coupons which was
attached to it when we sold it to him, and was payable in December
1887–he gave his address 16, Ayr Street, Regent Street.
AUGUSTUS SMALTZMAN (Detective Officer C). On 27th May, 1887, I was
watching No. 16, Ayr Street, Piccadilly, a stationer's shop, where letters
are sent and received I saw Johnson enter-I was expecting him, and
followed him to Mr. Kaulb's, 16, Cornhill.
