 1484.  MARY BURKE was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of August , twelve yards of muslin, value 15 s., the goods of John Noble.
   JOHN NOBLE. I am a linen-draper, and live in Brewer-street, Somers-town. On the 27th of August, about twelve o'clock I lost twelve yards of muslin - I had just brought them home and marked them. I left them on the counter, and went into the garden - my wife called me back, I came, and found some of my goods were gone. I followed the prisoner, and overtook her about fifty or sixty yards off. I charged her with the robbery; she said she had not got any thing. I told her to come back, and the piece of muslin fell from her in walking - it was one of those I had just brought home. She broke from me, I called Stop thief! and she was taken in my sight.
   JAMES FOLKES. I am a milkman. When the prisoner got from Mr. Noble, I stopped her.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. A woman came up and asked if I would have any thing to drink, I said No; she said
 "Will you come with me to get a couple of shirts made." I took hold of the article, and this gentleman came and took me. I never denied having it, but gave it him directly.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Six Months.
 1485.  MARY ANN BARNES was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of September , a shawl, value 2 s., the goods of Thomas Windsor Allen, and Charles James Maynard.
   FRANCIS BYAS. I am in the employ of Thomas Windsor Allen and Charles James Maynard, who live in Clare-street, Clare-market; I had shown this shawl to a customer er about four o'clock in the afternoon, it was taken about eight; it hung about a yard from the counter, the prisoner had got outside the shop; I went out and caught her with it; I had seen her in the shop before, she was talking to a person at the corner of the court, I brought her in and asked her to deliver up what she had stolen, or I would send for an officer, she said,
 "No, no, you wont," and gave up the shawl; she had come into the shop to take out a small piece of cotton which had been pawned for 4 d., she appeared to be in liquor.
 Property produced and sworn to.)
   RICHARD GARDNER. I am a constable - I was sent for to take her.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had been out with two persons, and was very much intoxicated.
     GUILTY. Aged 57. Confined One Month.
 1486.  MARY APPLEBY was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of September , a pewter pot, value 9 d., the goods of Charles Freake ; and a pewter pot, value 9 d., the goods of Thomas Pugh.
   WILLIAM BEST. I keep the George public-house, Queen Anne-street, Cavendish-square. I was informed that the prisoner had taken one of my pots; I went and found her about one hundred and fifty yards from my house, she was going backwards and forwards; I asked her what she had in her apron which was under her arm, she said only her shawl and a piece of baize; I felt round her and hear some pots chink; I delivered her to the constable, who searched her and took two pots from her, with the names of Freake and Pugh on them; she made great resistance.
   CHARLES FREAKE. I keep the Turk's-head, Harley-street; this woman was brought to my house; I had seen her about the neighbourhood before, but she had not been at my house that day; I do not know where she had taken my pot from; I believe it had been sent out with some ale at noon.
   THOMAS SMART. I took the pots from the prisoner, they were in a pocket behind her, not by the side of her.
   THOMAS BUCKRIDGE. I am a constable - I found these two large pockets on her, one before and one behind.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in great distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 64. Confined for One Year.
 1487.  JOHN SPARKES was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of September , eight copper plates, value 20 s., the goods of John Mackey Langham, his master.
   JOHN MACKEY LANGHAM. I am a stationer, and live in Red-lion-street, Holborn; I missed nine copper plates on the 8th of September; the prisoner had been at work for me, but was not then; I made enquiries, and heard the prisoner had pledged some things with Mr. Brown, at the corner of Neville-court. I went there and found two of the plates; I suppose them worth about half a crown a piece; I have seen six others since; he had frequently been in my house; they were kept in the work room at the top of the house, he worked in that room.
   JOHN GRINDLEY. I am in the employ of Mr. Townley, Russell-street, Covent-garden; I have six copper plates, two of them were pledged on the 6th of September, two of the 7th, and two on the 8th; we advanced 2 s. on each of them, in the name of John Sparkes; I believe they were brought by the prisoner, but I cannot be certain.
   RICHARD FAIRHAM. I am in the employ of Mr. Brown, a pawn broker. I took in two copper plates on the 6th of September, for 1 s. 6 d.; I am quite positive they were pawned by the prisoner, in the name of William Spicer.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I throw myself on the mercy of the Court, I did not do it with a felonious intention.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined for Fourteen Days.
