 1901.  THOMAS WARD was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of August , 1 cooper's adze, value 3s., the goods of James Garrett.
   JAMES GARRETT. I am a cooper. I lost this adze from No. 4 shed, in the West India-docks, where I worked, on the 31st of August; the prisoner is a journeyman there - I had not lent it to him.
   THOMAS WILKINSON. I work in the docks. I went into No. 4 warehouse, about eleven o'clock in the morning, and saw the prisoner shaving the handle of an adze- when he saw me be laid it down; I took it up, and asked whose it was - he said one he had brought in to put a new handle to; I knew the prosecutor had lost one, and told him.
   BENJAMIN DAVIS. I am employed in No. 4 warehouse. Wilkinson told me to look at this adze; I saw J.G.  marked on it - I went to No. 8, and asked the prosecutor if he should know the adze again - he said Yes; I told him to come up at beer time, and I would find it for him.
   HENRY SHIP. I am an officer. Garrett came to me with the adze; I took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I found it at No. 4, and it had no handle; I took it out and put a handle - I did not mean to take it out, but to work with it till it was owned.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1902.  WILLIAM WARREN was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of July , 1 veil, value 5s.; 1 crape handkerchief, value 2s., and 3 yards of net, value 1s., the goods of Ann Harrison.
   ANN HARRISON. I am unfortunate, but work at the dress-making when I can get it, I met the prisoner, on the 3rd of July, in Regent-street, between twelve and one o'clock at night; I had never seen him before - he asked where I was going - I said home; I then lived in Phoenixcourt, Long-acre- he went home with me, gave me half a crown or 3s., and we went to bed; I fell asleep, and awoke about three o'clock, he was then gone - I missed a veil, a crape handkerchief, three yards of net, and the duplicate of a gold pin; the veil had been on a bonnet in a chair, the net on a table, and the duplicate in a small chest - I met the prisoner, on the Saturday following, in Cranbourne-alley; I asked him for my things - he said he was very sorry he had done so, he had had them, but had not got them then - I gave him into custody.
   WILLIAM NIGHTINGALE. The prosecutrix charged the prisoner with robbing her, and I took him in Lisle-street; he said he had the things, and pawned them for 1s. - I said, "A man of your years I should have thought would have known better;" he said "I ought to have done so."
   JOHN TYLER. The prisoner was a shopmate of mine; when he was in custody a letter came to me, and I visited him in Newgate, as a brother-in-law - I said, "Don't deceive me, I know where the handkerchief is - tell me where the other things are;" he said, "Tyler, I will not deceive you, if you go to such a house, Susan Jones will give you the other things;" I went and got them - I sent to the prosecutrix to say I knew where the things were, and I would produce them at the trial; I was taking some work home, the Policeman took me, and I gave them the things.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I went home with her; we went to bed - recollecting I had business to do in the morning, I left her, took my hat, and went home - when I got home I found the articles in my hat; I was very much intoxicated.
     GUILTY. Aged 44. - Transported for Seven Years.

 1903.  THOMAS MOYLEN was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of August , 10 sovereigns, the monies of Samuel Walker.
   SAMUEL WALKER. I am a publican, at Bromley, Middlesex. On the 27th of August, the prisoner owed me 27s.; he produced a cheque to me, and said, "Is that of any use?" I said "Yes, it is a cheque of Mr. Hale's for 14l. 12s.;" he said, "Take your reckoning, and give me change;" I gave him eleven sovereigns, three half sovereigns, and 15s. - I said "Here is 13l. 5s., and your bill is 27s.; the cheque is 14l. 12s." - he said very well, it would save his master going to town; he stood talking some time, then went away, and next day I found the cheque was only for 4l. 12s. - I found the prisoner at Poplar, and told him of my mistake; he said he was no scholar, and had put the money into his pocket without counting it.
     NOT GUILTY.
