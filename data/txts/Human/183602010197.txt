 572. JOHN JUDAH  , alias    Jones, was indicted for stealing, on the   28th of January , 1 cheese, value 14 s  ., the goods of James Pearson . THOMAS JOHNSON, I am shopman to Mr. James Pearson, of East-street, Marylebone. About two or three o'clock, on the 28th of Janu ary, a neighbour opposite came and told me something, in consequence of which I missed a cheese—I had seen it safe hall an hour before—the prisoner was pointed out to me by somebody—I went and brought him back—when the prisoner was in the shop, he said, "I do not want to have any bother about it, I know where the cheese is; I will return it, and make you a handsome present"—I said I could not it, and gave him into custody.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Did you tell this to the Magistrate? A.  I did—Mr. Thackray was present when this occurred, and two wit nesses—I could have brought a dozen—he was pointed out to me directly after I missed the cheese—he did not say he would rather give me a cheese than have any bother about it—he said he knew where it was—the cheese was taken within a hundred yards or so, just round the corner, where he was lost sight of, and it was cut up into four pieces—I was in formed of that. PHILIP THACKRAY. I am a carpenter, and live opposite the prosecutor's shop. I was at the window of my first-floor, on the 21st of January, and saw the prisoner take the cheese—I am sure he is the man—I went and gave information to Mr. Pearson—I saw the prisoner again within five minutes—I caught him and took him into the shop—he positively denied it at first, and when I came to be positive with him, he wanted to com promise with Johnson, and said he wanted to make it up.
