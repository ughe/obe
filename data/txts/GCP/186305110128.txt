Q. A great number of letters have been read, which I confess are very
unintelligible to me; do you allege that any of those letters refer to trans
actions in which Crowther was acting improperly upon his own account!
A. Yes, almost every one of them I often called Barber's attention to the
heaviness of his account-I frequently did that, down to the termination of
the dealing; the explanation he gave me was, that the market was likely
to get better, or something of that sort-he told me the account was well
secured by warrants; I swear that: he told me so several times when we
sold up, there was only one weight-note, worth about 1001.
