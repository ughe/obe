done with my cheque and money; I received a reply, and in consequence
I called the prisoner into my room and asked him what he had done with
my money ; he replied, " It is all right, it is at the bank "--I replied,
"I have information to the contrary, I have a telegram from the bank :
you have paid my money to your personal account "-after some excuses
he stated that that was so, but that he could repay me I said, "Whose
signature is that on the contract, is the Duke of Rovigo really your
partner ?" -- he said "No "and he asked me to save him for the sake of
his wife and mother-I did not charge him then--he said he would repay
me-I left him towards the end of the month, but about January 19th
I called at the police station where I found him in custody on another
charge-I then charged him.
