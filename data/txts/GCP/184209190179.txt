2764. EVAN DAVIS was again indicted for embezzlement.
WILLIAM JAMES DAILEY. I employed the prisoner as a traveller on the
1st of June to go round to publicans and other tradesmen with show-
bills-I gave him 149 bills, worth between 37. and 41.-he never returned
to me, nor paid me any money-he never paid me 1s. 6d. on the 4th of
June, which be ought to have done the day he received it.
Cross-examined by MR. JONES. Q. On what terms did you engage
him? 4. On a commission of one third of the amount of the bills dis-
posed of -he would have been entitled to 6d, out of this ls. 6d. if this had
been the proper price for these, it should have been 25. 10d., he would have
been entitled to nearly Is. on them.
FREDERICK MATTHEWS. I bought half-a-dozen bills of the prisoner
on the 4th of June, and paid him Is. 6d. for them.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 38.-Confined Six Months.

stealing, on the 8th of September, 1 bag, value 28.; 100lbs. weight of
bacon, value 178.; and 35lbs. weight of pork, value 38.; the goods of
Thomas Dare and others, their masters.
MR. PRENDERGAST conducted the Prosecution.
Thomas DARE. I am a partner in the firm of Richard Wilson and
another--we are wharfingers in Southwark-Byrns was in our employ, and

they are our property exclusively, and Byrns, who has been eight or nine
years in our service, knew this-it is our custom to sell these cuttings at
different times, and at the end of the year we make our men a present out
of the produce of them that is at our discretion, and that is all the inter-
est the men can have in them.
