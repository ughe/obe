half inches wide, with sharp edges, and flat--it was very smooth the
edges were sharp-it was to set seams with-it is a heavy wood-1 used it
in my business.
Q. Have you told us all the conversation, as far as you recollect, that
took place between you and the prisoner on the subject of the murder !
A. As far as happened, except part that my wife was speaking to him
there was a conversation on Scripture-I recollect in one part Richard said,
if the ten commandments were just, or the Mosaic law was right, he bad
broken them all, for adultery he had committed many times, and drunken-
ness and then that conversation dropped with me, and went on with my
wife--I got up to go into the yard-that was at the time we were talking
of the murder it was all in the same conversation-I saw some money
produced in a stocking at the station-house at Islington-neither the
money or the stocking was my property-I believe one farthing was all
the money in the house at all that day-I have never been in the habit of
keeping money in the wash-house or privy-we have not had any to do it
