not been in the habit of gambling-the only gambling I ever transacted was
in the house which I used to keep, where it was usual for us to play at 3d. a
corner, and sometimes 6d. or 1s.- I know a man named Hartly-I swear
that the extent of my gambling has been Is. 60.--I never won in one night
at my house upwards of 301., nor any such sum-Hartly has a situation in
a banking-house-there was no raffle in my house for a horse--there was at
Mr. Hartly's house-I met Mr. Briant that night at Mr. Hartly's house - I
and Briant did not divide between us what we had won that night-I cannot
say whether I and Briant won that night-I made some little bets, but I
