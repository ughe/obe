WILLIAM BOYELL (Police Sergeant E). I took the prisoner in custody
on this warrant, which I read to him—he said, " It is nothing of the kind,
I have given IOUs for what money I have borrowed."
The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrates. I gave Mr. Vanderfield
an IOU for the money, and had not any idea of defrauding him.
The Prisoner in a written defence said "I will repay Mr. Vanderfield
every farthing of the money I borrowed. I will bind myself with the
strongest tie of the law to this statement. Op 22nd October, 1878, I saw
Mr. Vanderfield at Long Acre, and he asked me how I got on. I told him
and that I could get employment at the Freemasons' public-house if I had
better clothes. «Come along with me,' he said, and I went and he gave
me 78., and the rest of the money in small sums afterwards."
