1448. THOMAS STRATFORD was indicted for stealing 1 pair of boots,
value 58.; the goods of Sophia Beckwith.
WILLIAM SMITH. I am footman to Miss Sophia Beckwith, of No. 52, Eden-
place-about a quarter past nine o'clock, on the evening of the 12th of May,
the prisoner came there and brought a parcel-I had a pair of boots on the
board there-I missed them the next morning these are them--they are
Miss Beckwith's-the prisoner had an opportunity of taking them.
Prisoner. Q. How long was I in the house? A. About two or three.
minutes, while I was gone to get the money to pay you—these boots were
on the board where you put the parcel down-no person was there after you
-I locked the gate up after that the boots were there when you came with
the parcel -- you must have seen them.
