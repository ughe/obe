ARTHUR ALLEN (Detective, G) I produce a copy of the certificate of
the prisoner s marriage in December, 1872-I have compared it with the
register in Somerset House, and it is correct-I also produce a certifi-

from his wife-I have compared it with the original at Somerset House
- it was made the day after the weduing-the second woman gave the
information, and I arrested him on a warrant-he has six children-she
gave him in custody after living with him 23 years.

Park Street, Halifax-I know the prisoner-on December 31st, 1872, I
was at the Parish Church, Halifax, when he was married-they lived in
Halifax about two years-be would not work, and he ill-uged her, and
she left him and went to her mother, and I have never seen him since---
she has lived in Halifax ever since, and he could easily have found her if
he liked-I visited them several times after they were married-there
was quarrelling between them.
