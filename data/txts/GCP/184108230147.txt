2192. JOHN RUSH was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of August,
1 scarf, value 108.; and 1 shawl, value 58. the goods of James Richmond
Folkard.
EDWIN HAM. I am shopman to James Richmond Folkard, a pawn-
broker, in Upton-place, Commercial-road. At one o'clock in the morning
on the 25th of August I was sitting up in bed watching the door-I saw
the prisoner putting his hand through the fan-light, and dragging this
shawl and scarf through the window-I went out of a side door, went
round, and saw the prisoner and another man-I presented a pistol at
them--they both ran- I ran after them--the other crossed the road-I
followed the prisoner, and Murphy stopped him-I was behind him-I found
this shawl and scarf at the foot of the door under the prisoner's feet-they
are my master's.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not say that the other got away with the shawl ?
4. No.
EDWARD MURPHY. Between one and two o'clock in the morning I was
coming up Sutton-street-I met the prisoner running as fast as he could-
I caught him—there was a cry of "Stop thief" - when I took him he said
he had not robbed any of them-I said it looked very likely by his running
away.
FINLEY WENTWORTH. I was in bed in the shop-I ran out after Ham
- picked up the shawl about three feet from the door-I saw the pri-
soner running.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going home; this man came and stopped
me; there was another man running over the way; I had been robbing no
man: the shopman came up, and put a pistol to my head, and said I had
robbed him; I asked of what; he said he did not know; they took me
to the shop, and showed me a small place over the window, about two
inches wide and four or five long.
