   MR. CLARKSON . Q.  What did Tarrant say upon that observation of yours, and before you saw that Jacobs was in sight? A.  He said, "I have robbed you of that handkerchief, Sir"—(that was another handkerchief)—and then he said, "Jacobs has robbed you as much as I have"—Jacobs then came from a back warehouse, and said, "You know you are a liar; I have not; I have only robbed you of one silk-handkerchief; "and he took a key from his pocket, and took the handkerchief from his box in the presence of my son, who came up-stairs at that moment.
   Cross-examined by MR. PARRY . Q.  Had you asked him any question about Jacobs before? A.  No—after he told me he had robbed me of this handkerchief, and he had said this of Jacobs, he called to Jacobs and said, "Come forward, you have robbed master as well as Í have;" Jacobs said, "You are a liar; I have only taken one handkerchief."  1699. JAMES CLEAR and PATRICK SULLIVAN , breaking and entering the warehouse of Richard Wills and another, and stealing 1 quart of brandy, value 2 s  .; their goods. GEORGE BRIGHT. I am in the service of Richard Wills and another, of Southwark; the prisoners were in their employ. Last Wednesday evening I was in their warehouse—there were two vessels off there, loading for Devon-port—I came out about six o'clock, and shut the door, which shuts with a spring-latch—I left everything safe—I went to another warehouse, and returned about seven—I then smelt a strong smell of spirits, and saw the warehouse-door half open—I looked in, and said, "Who is here?"—a person answered—I looked in with a short piece of candle, and saw the prisoners, and a cask was running from two holes—I knew it was spirits, and said to Clear," For  God's sake, James, what are you doing? don't let the spirits run away; put your fingers on the holes, while I get another candle"—I went to get another candle, and saw Dyton—we returned to the cellar, and there was no one there.
   Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON . Q.  Whose property was this? A.  The spirits are supposed to be the property of the wharfingers.
