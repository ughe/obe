Cross-eramined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Cogan was not in the tap-room
above half an hour A. No; I did not see him go--I did not observe

on a settle.
Cross-examined by MR. METCALFE. Q. Was Wooderson lying on a
settle part of the evening A. No; he does not lie when he gets drunk,
he is constantly going about-I did not have any wine-I thought the
bottles were two medicine bottles, because I had removed two a day or
two before-I did not know till next day that any wine was lost:
Mr. ROBINSON. Q. What time did you go to sleep A. I slept a

saw me several times ; he will know whether I was drunk.
