Before Edward Bullock, Esq. and the First Jury.
1932. HENRY LEE, for a burglary in the dwelling-house of Richard
Palmer, and stealing therein a hat-brush, value 6d.; the goods of John
Kendard.
MR. THOMPSON conducted the Prosecution.
MARY PALMER. I am the wife of Richard Palmer, of 9, Charterhouse-
street, in the parish of St. Sepulchre. On the night of 22nd Aug. I shut the
staircase window about half-past nine o'clock.
John KENNARD. I lodge in the prosecutor's bouse. On the morning of
23rd Aug. I was awoke about half-past four o'clock by a noise in the house,
and when I came down I found the prisoner in the custody of the police-
this brush is mine ; it was in my apartment the evening before--I found
some articles of clothing removed from my drawing-room to the staircase-
went to bed about eleven.
HARRIET ROBERTS. I lived as servant at 35, Charterhouse-square, the
back of which looks out on the back of Mr. Palmer's. About half-past
four o'clock, on 23rd Aug., I was called by my mistress, and I saw the
prisoner get on to the leads of Mr. Palmer's house, open the first staircase
window, and get in- I gave information to the watchman of the square,
Prisoner. Q. Was the window open ? A. No ; you opened it with some
instrument- I did not see you break the window.
THOMAS DIMENT (policeman, G 64). From information I received on the
morning of 23rd Aug., about half-past four o'clock, I went to the back part
of Mr. Palmer's house-I saw the staircase window open-I got on the
leads, and saw the prisoner with a pair of shoes in his hand-he saw me, and
ran down stairs, and was stopped by another policeman-I searched him, and
found this brush on him, this screw-driver, penny, key, and duplicate,
Thomas GUNDRY ( policeman, G 178). From information I received I went
to the front door of Mr. Palmer's house--the prisoner opened the door, and
ran out on the step, and I took him.
GUILTY.-Transported for seven Years.
