116. FERDINAND CHEVROLAT (36), Stealing a banker's cheque
for £13 188. of the Glass and Bottle Works Company, his masters.
Mr. Gill Defended the prisoner.
CHARLES Hunt. I am a wine merchant, trading as Lowenthal and
Co., at 6, Duke Street, Adelphi-on 10th November I owed the Glass and
Bottle Works Company £18 188., and sent them this cheque (produced)
the prisoner, who is their traveller, called on the 14th and said, "I have
come for the cheque "-I said that it had been sent, and showed him &
copy of the letter sent with it-he said that it had never arrived-he
said, "I had better go to Price's bank and stop it”-I said, “You can-
not do better, and in case it has been paid, what will the endorsement
be ?”-he said, “Per paid the Glass Works, managing director"-he
left-I communicated with Messrs. Coutts, got the cheque back, and
sent my clerk to see the prisoner, and communicated with the police-I
got back just as the prisoner was coming out; my partner saw him.
