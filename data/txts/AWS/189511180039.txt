MARY ADELINE MAGORRERY. I am a governess, and live at 53, Talbot
Road-I had a room on the second floor, on the same landing as the
prisoner and his wife-on Tuesday night, August 20th, about eleven,
was in my room, and heard voices, just a buzz; I could not tell whether
there was more than one voice-it went on till nearly two-I could not
distinguish the voices at allon the following morning (the 21st) I saw
the prisoner go outI heard him come back in about half-an-hour.-he
went upstairsI did not see where he went-a few minutes after he had
gone up I heard four bangsfirs I heard two, then, quickly after, another,
then, after a short interval, two more-after hearing those four I went
up to my room, and while there I heard anotherthat might have been
nearly a quarter of an hour after I had got into my room-it was the
noise of a pistol shot-after that I heard a fall, as of a heavy body going
overI then went for Mrs. WillsI saw her try the door-I afterwards
