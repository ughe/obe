1235. FREDERICK COOLING was indicted for embezzlement.
GEORGE BENNETT. I am a baker, and live in the Kingsland-road.
The prisoner lived with me it was his duty to take bread to my cus-
tomers, and to receive the money from them—he ought to settle his ac-
counts every evening-if he received 51. from Mr. Massey, be has not paid
it to me-he did not bring me any account from Mr. Massey-he abscond.
ed on that day.
EDWARD Massey. I deal with Mr. Bennett-I cannot recognise the
prisoner-I paid 51. on the 4th of September.
ANN DINNIGAN. I am servant to Mr. Massey--the prisoner came for
Mr. Bennett's bill-I know him perfectly-he used to bring the bread-
introduced him to my master to be paid.
Cross-examined by Mr. Payne. Q. Do you know what day it was?
A. No, I did not see my master pay him-he never came after that day.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Six Months.
