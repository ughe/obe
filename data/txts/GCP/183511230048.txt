37. GEORGE TIMBRELL was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of
November, 1 handkerchief, value 38., the goods of Joseph Howse, from his
person.
Joseph Howse. I live at Cirencester-I lodged at Cooper's Hotel,
Bouverie-street. On the 7th of November, I was in Wood-street, passing
along-on arriving at a place where I was stopped by a carriage, I was in-
formed something had occurred, and missed my handkerchief from my
pocket-I looked round, and saw the prisoner in custody-while I turned
round, some one handed my handkerchief to me--it has my initials on it.
John Howell. I live in Aldersgate-street. I was in Wood-street that
morning, at half-past eleven o'clock, and saw the prisoner put his hand
into the prosecutor's pocket, and take the handkerchief out-secured him
with it in his hand.
Prisoner. I had no handkerchief in my hand when he took me. Wit-
ness. He dropped it the instant I took him-I secured him the moment
his hand came out of the pocket with the handkerchief in it.
Prisoner. He struck me on the forehead. Witness. I deny that.
Prisoner's Defence. I went after a situation, and on coming along Wood-
street, that gentleman took hold of my collar, and said I had picked a
gentleman's pocket, and had got the handkerchief about me-I never saw
any handkerchief till I was at Guildhall.
