ELIZABETH FIELD. I live with my father, George Field, who is a body
coach-maker, and carry water-cresses out. In October last, I went to the pro-
secutor's with some water-cresses, and saw the prisoner-I know her to be the
woman-she gave me some broken victuals, and told me to bring the
towel back the same day, but my father would not let me, and I took it
back next day-she sent me to fetch a coach to the door, which I did about
three o'clock in the afternoon-I saw nobody but her in the house she put
a large hair trunk and a small box into the coach-the coachman helped her
in with the large one-she went into the coach with them, shut the streets
door, and told me to go down the kitchen steps, and wait there till she
came back-she should not be long I waited there three quarters of an
hour-I found nobody in the house, and went away, leaving the servant
next door in care of the house.

MICHAEL O'NEILL, I am a sergeant of the police of Cork. In consequence
of information last March, I went to Mr. Duggin's house at Cork, and found
the prisoner in the first floor room there-1 asked her dame- she said.
"Maria Williams"-I told her to consider herself my prisoner, and to be
cautious what she said, for if she said any thing to criminate herself, I should
have to state it there was a large box, and a small trunk on the floor
asked if they were hers-she said, “ Yes" I asked if what they contained
was hers-she said “Yes”-I asked for the key she gave it me-I
opened one-I found three gold chains, a gold key, three gold rings, a gold
brooch, a pair of ear-rings, some silver spoons, a reticule, eight shawls, a gold
watch and case, and a variety of other articles-1 asked if she bad got a
watch-she said she had a brass one, and produced it from her bosom
I also found some parts of tea spoons, with the initials T. A. B., which

perty to the prosecutor-I found a quantity of plate at Mr. Bennet's, in

the way, she asked me whether it would be better for her to plead guilty
or not-I told her 1 should give her no advice on that subject.
